# 386YE Autumn Equinox Military Council orders

## Overview

The Military Council is the gathering of Imperial [generals](../../mixed/titles/General.md) and [admirals](../../mixed/titles/General.md#admiral). Individually, the generals have responsibility for the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) given to the [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) under their command, while the admirals provide the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy_orders.md) for the [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md). A general commands a single [Imperial army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md), drawn from volunteers from their nation. Once a general is given their command, they have sole authority to [order](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) the army to [move](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#movement) or [attack](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#issuing-orders) as they choose. After each summit, the Civil Service quickly circulate the orders each general has given among Imperial citizens, allowing [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) captains to decide if they wish to commit their own force to [support an army](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#support-army), and if so which army to support.

With the Autumn Equinox ending, the Empire remains engaged with barbarians in several locations, summarised in an in-character way in the mini-wind of war **[After the Harvest](#after-the-harvest)**. It's important to note that it *only* takes into account the orders of Imperial generals; it doesn't predict what the barbarian armies will do. It's our intention to provide a similar summary going forward after each event, to help lend context to the orders submitted by the generals.

Following that, there is a list of the orders that were submitted by Imperial generals, as well as where they are deployed, and whether the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has decided that military units supporting them will receive the guerdon or not. We'll be doing this after every event going forward as well, bringing the Imperial Military Council into line with the Senate, Synod, and Conclave in terms of making their decisions public.

## After the Harvest

As the last flames lit at the Autumn Equinox flicker and die, we look across an [Empire at war](../winds-of-war/386YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md), battle-horns echoing from one side to another.

In the Mournwold, an immense force of Jotun seem set to bring the whole Marcher territory under their command. As the Equinox dawned, tens of thousands of Jotun warriors had [seized](../winds-of-war/The_wild_wind_rises.md) [Southmoor](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#southmoor), [Greensward](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#greensward), [Ore Hills](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#ore-hills), and [Chalkdowns](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#chalkdowns), reinstating Jarl Haakon as ruler of the Mourn. Now a dozen Imperial armies stand against them, the oath-bound [Tusks](../../mixed/government/military/Tusks.md) at the fore. The people of the Mourn, human and orc alike, rise to meet the challenge of the Jotun, inspired by the [victory](../winds-of-war/The_wild_wind_rises.md#sixty-years-gone-battle-opportunity) of Imperial heroes in the Ore Hills. If the Jotun press their claim or stand their ground, the dour hills will again be steeped in blood. Can the Empire hold their line, or force the Jotun back?

Further south, the future of the Brass Coast remains uncertain, but for the moment Imperial forces have pulled out, armies needed elsewhere. In their absence, Madruga remains split between multiple factions, a great force of Grendel camped along the coast of what were once the Siroc Plains now [transformed](../winds-of-fortune/Where_the_river_rises.md) by cataclysmic magic into mysterious fens. Feroz has been freed, but Commander Gallum Fiersach still [commands](../winds-of-war/Glass_and_flame.md#game-information-moras-rock) the fort of [Mora's Rock](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#moras-rock). The Children of Wrecks have [raised their navy](../../mixed/government/military/The_gathering_storm.md#objective-stop-the-wrecks-forming-a-navy) and stand poised to spread their power beyond scattered islands; where will their first furious hammer-blow fall?

Along the south-eastern coast [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) is [wracked with storms](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Foam_and_Spittle_of_the_Furious_Sea.md). Amid tumultuous rumour of an attack by the Children of Wrecks,
three Imperial armies hurry to defend the Urizeni coast, to reinforce the garrison at the [Court of the White Fountain](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#the-court-of-the-white-fountain). A force of that size should be more than enough to see off the Wreckers, perhaps the Council has reason to believe something worse is on the way? The Empire's probing strike into Ayereed has fallen back, and only the [Winter Sun](../../mixed/government/military/Winter_Sun.md) remains to defend [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md). Will the Grendel armies they [fought in the petrified forest](../winds-of-war/Steel_and_salt.md) follow them, and can they stand against them if they do?

And then [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md). During the Autumn Equinox, the heroes of the Empire fought the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) here, a desperate battle to [prevent the ghulai](../winds-of-war/Blood_and_mire.md#all-along-the-lakeside-battle-opportunity) from not only destroying Feverwater Steading but rousing the vallorn of Sweetglades. They succeeded, but at what cost? The cursed woodlands are scattered with the flower of Dawnish nobility, cut down amidst the [seeping miasma](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md#vallorn-miasma). As the [corrupting rot](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_Run_Red.md) of [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) creeps and pools across a territory already [scarred](../winds-of-war/Ashes.md#objective-prevent-the-use-of-the-sephals-cauldron) by the malice of the [Buruk Tepel](../barbarians/Druj_lore.md#buruk-tepel), Navarr armies and their Varushkan and Dawnish allies make a desperate strike. The stakes could not be higher; the orcs of the Mallum are without virtue or conscience and have already shown they are prepared to unleash the vallorn against the Empire. If their presence in Therunin goes unchecked, who knows what else they may have in store?

And in [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md), the [Gryphon's Pride](../../mixed/government/military/Gryphon-s_Pride.md) slip the bonds of law and march to attack the Rahvin. The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) declared the orcs of the Barrens to be foreigners promising them the full protection of the law; but that same law gives General Archavion Wolfborne sole command of their army, and he has ordered the 'Pride to seize the mines and kill anyone who fights back. The Rahvin have sworn to defend the mine, so left unchecked, the sept will be destroyed, and show that the Senate's will is naught but a paper shield. There is a chance for Imperials to [intercede](../winds-of-fortune/386YE_Winter_Solstice_interlude.md#in-the-barrens) so that some of the Rahvin are spared. The general and any captains who fight with them, will face the full force of the law but that will be too late for the Rahvin, and too late for any hope of peace with the other slaves of the Druj for a generation to come. Who will trust an Empire that speaks of liberation after this?

Of course, all this is the present, the moment between breathing in and breathing out. Where the Imperial Military Council has put their strategies into effect, so their enemies have made their own decisions. Neither side knows with any certainty what the other is planning. Will the Druj stay in Therunin and fight or will they withdraw and strike like the assassins' blade elsewhere? Do the Children of Wrecks really threaten Redoubt; how reliable is rumour? Will the Jotun stand and fight in Mournwold or sweep away into Bregasland where their [enchanted castle](../winds-of-war/Only_forward.md#object-halt-the-burhfæst) rises over Gravenrock, and only the Strong Reeds stand against them? Or perhaps rampage east toward the rich city of [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md), and the three armies recuperating there? Where else might the flames of war kindle, unlooked for, in the months to come?

The winds of war shift and twist, and nobody can speak with certainty of what the winter may bring, but what is certain is that the choices made by Imperial captains will help to shape the outcome.
