# 388YE Autumn Equinox Military Council orders

## Overview

The Military Council is the gathering of Imperial [generals](../../mixed/titles/General.md) and [admirals](../../mixed/titles/General.md#admiral). Individually, the generals have responsibility for the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) given to the [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) under their command, while the admirals provide the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy_orders.md) for the [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md). A general commands a single [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md), drawn from volunteers from their nation. Once a general is given their command, they have sole authority to [order](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) the army to [move](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#movement) or [attack](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#issuing-orders) as they choose. After each summit, the Civil Service quickly circulate the orders each general has given among Imperial citizens, allowing [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) captains to decide if they wish to commit their own force to [support an army](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#support-army), and if so which army to support.

During the Autumn Equinox 388YE, the following orders were submitted by Imperial generals.
