# 387YE Winter Solstice Military Council orders

## Overview

The Military Council is the gathering of Imperial [generals](../../mixed/titles/General.md) and [admirals](../../mixed/titles/General.md#admiral). Individually, the generals have responsibility for the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) given to the [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) under their command, while the admirals provide the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy_orders.md) for the [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md). A general commands a single [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md), drawn from volunteers from their nation. Once a general is given their command, they have sole authority to [order](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) the army to [move](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#movement) or [attack](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#issuing-orders) as they choose. After each summit, the Civil Service quickly circulate the orders each general has given among Imperial citizens, allowing [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) captains to decide if they wish to commit their own force to [support an army](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#support-army), and if so which army to support.

During the Winter Solstice 387YE, the following orders were submitted by Imperial generals.

### Green Shield

*[Image: GreenShields Colour.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:GreenShields_Colour.png)*

- **General:** Iron Osric
- **Orders:** Grey go the days with their grinding strain,

Small are their victories, shadowed with pain;
But bright is the battle-line that banishes fear,
And hearts are made whole when the war-cry is near.
There stand the shield-lines, steadfast and strong,
Helms all a-glimmer, spears set in throng;
Blade-song rises where bold battlers stand,
And the field is made fair by their fate-forged hand.
We shield the folk, spare the untried and weak,
Let no sly scavenger plunder or sneak;
No hand shall dishonour the dead where they lie,
Nor trouble their rest ’neath the wide watching sky.
These are our war-ways, our word and our will;
Beautiful the battle-days, fierce and yet fair still-
Not for the scarecrow that shrinks from the fray,
But the bold who break forth where the war-banners sway.

The Jotun then came with Stephen of Sarcombe, letting slaughter follow in their wake. Let the Ambitious and Courageous heroes we create here be the foundation of the talks due in the newly built meeting place.
