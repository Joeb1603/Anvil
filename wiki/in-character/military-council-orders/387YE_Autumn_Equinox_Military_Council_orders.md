# 387YE Autumn Equinox Military Council orders

## Overview

The Military Council is the gathering of Imperial [generals](../../mixed/titles/General.md) and [admirals](../../mixed/titles/General.md#admiral). Individually, the generals have responsibility for the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) given to the [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) under their command, while the admirals provide the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy_orders.md) for the [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md). A general commands a single [Imperial army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md), drawn from volunteers from their nation. Once a general is given their command, they have sole authority to [order](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) the army to [move](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#movement) or [attack](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#issuing-orders) as they choose. After each summit, the Civil Service quickly circulate the orders each general has given among Imperial citizens, allowing [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) captains to decide if they wish to commit their own force to [support an army](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#support-army), and if so which army to support.

After the Autumn Equinox, there are several flashpoints where the Empire is engaged or where barbarians threaten Imperial citizens, summarised in an in-character way in the mini-wind of war **[Questions of war](#questions-of-war)**. It's important to note that it *only* takes into account the orders of Imperial generals, and the situation at the end of the Autumn Equinox. It doesn't predict what the barbarian armies will do - it's simply a summary intended to help lend context to the orders submitted by the generals.

Following that, there is a list of the orders that were submitted by Imperial generals, as well as where they are deployed, and whether the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has decided that military units supporting them will receive the guerdon or not.

## Questions of war

As the equinox dawns, chaos swirled like ink in water around the borders of the Empire on three sides. To the west, the banners of the Jotun. To the east, the malice of the Druj. To the south, the brine-whipped sails of the Grendel and the Children of Wrecks. It may seem, sometimes, as if the world stands still at the four cardinal points of the year, but such is an illusion. The winds continue to blow, the dice continue to bounce, the skein of years continues to weave and wend.

*[Image: In Bregasland and Skallahn, the Empire takes the fight to the Jotun.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Fielding_Jotun.jpg)*

*[Image: In Mareave and Redoubt, the Empire fights against the Grendel.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Readied_Grendel.jpg)*

*[Image: In Sarangrave, the Empire advances on the Tower of the Skink.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Druj_in_the_woods.jpg)*

The [Mourn](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) is once again fully Imperial, but the Jotun remain in complete control of [Ashvale](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md) and [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md). The [Strong Reeds](../../mixed/government/military/Strong_Reeds.md) have lain there in wait for a year or more, and now it seems the time has come to throw them back, as they were thrown back in the south. Three more armies join the fray, hoping perhaps to take the Jotun by surprise while their attention is elsewhere. From Mitwold to Gravenmarch, they come - an eerie mirror to the Jotun strategy that began this most recent invasion. Will they be sufficient to break the Jotun stranglehold on the Dour Fens?

Further north, six whole armies brave the strengthening winds of winter as they launch a courageous assault into the heart of [Skallahn](../barbarians/Skallahn.md), into the domain of the late Jarl of Kierheim. Perhaps, with Jotun forces focused in the south, there is a hope to catch the orcs of Kallsea off guard, to draw their forces away to defend their lands. It is a strategy that has worked several times before; will it work this time?

In the south, Imperial forces move to face the Grendel assault in [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md) and in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md). Yet the lords of the Broken Shore are canny, and their [ships](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) give them unmatched manoeuvrability around the [Bay of Catazar](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md). The Salt Lords seem set on reclaiming the arid land claimed by the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md), and [raiding the libraries of Urizen](../winds-of-war/To_ruin.md#objective-preserve-the-shrouds), but is that their only goal?

And what of the Children of Wrecks? The Red Wind Corsairs sail west from Sarvos toward Feroz, to challenge piratical control of the eastern islands. Who knows what precautions the chosen of [the storm](../eternals/Siakha.md) may have in store? Who can predict the intent of the rampaging *Unbound Storm*?

Then, finally, to [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md). During the Equinox, the efforts of Imperial heroes captured [Sarangrave](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md) from the Druj and through the [will](../senate-sessions/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#assign-sarangrave) of the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) made it part of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md). While there may have been victorious battles, the war continues. The massive [Tower of the Skink](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#tower-of-the-skink) still holds in the south, and scouts report that at least some of the Druj armies have moved to reinforce it. How will the tides of conflict turn, with the Empire in the ascendant and the Druj scrabbling to keep their iron grip on their lands?

Or perhaps not finally. It is dangerous to deal in absolutes when one talks of war. The enemies of the Empire have their own strategies, their own ambitions; their generals and admirals have issued their own orders. Imperial planning can only predict the movements of the barbarians so far - there is always be space for the unexpected. Even the wisest seer or most clear-sighted prognosticator cannot say with certainty what is going to happen. Yet whatever the question, when it comes to war the answer is always ultimately the same. Someone will win, and someone will lose, but death alone will be the true victor...
