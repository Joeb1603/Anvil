# 388YE Spring Equinox winds of war

*[Image: Sometimes, even in the tumult of battle, there are moments of silence.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Alone_in_the_battle.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the last three months, Imperial forces have engaged in military campaigns. Sometimes those engagements have been wide-ranging and dramatic, other times they have been more sedate. Where there has been dramatic action, or where there are [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md), we've given a Wind of War its own page; other military actions are discussed here.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of War and the [Winds of Fortune](../winds-of-fortune/388YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_fortune.md) is to make players aware of things their characters *should* know, based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing, and to maintain the Empire as a living, breathing place where all kinds of events take place beyond the scope of [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md).

It should go without saying that we ask everyone to abide by the rules about [online roleplaying](../../out-of-character/rules/Online_roleplaying.md) with regard to the military campaign. It's important that the key in-character activity of the Empire world takes place at events. The game is busier and better if everything that can happen in the field, does happen in the field. Military planning and political discussions should happen at events. For this reason, we generally discourage too much downtime roleplaying between events and put strict limits on what can be done online, particularly using our forums and Facebook groups.

All that said, we now present a summary of the key military activity in the Empire - both with links to the expanded Wind of War pages and notes about other important things that have happened that didn't need a full battle campaign write-up.

## Wide Carnivorous Skies

Spring is the cruellest season, forcing life out of ground left barren by Winter frost. The inevitable wheel does not change, any more than war changes. Snow gives way to rain, and the rain falls on the wicked and the virtuous alike. It falls across the [Bay of Catazar](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md), across the battlefields of [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) and onto the churning salt-expanse that is the bounded sea. The waves of the **[Deep blue sea](Deep_blue_sea.md)** murmur of [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) and rocky shores, and the machinations of the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md).

From the mountains of Redoubt, let the mind's eye wander with the wings of the gulls. South to the arid coast of [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md), now remarked and reshaped by the churning of the hungry sea. The sandy soil is thirsty, and there is neither blood nor rain enough in the world to quench that thirst it seems. The salt-winds blow from the sea and the storm rides on them. The horns blow, the drums beat, and armies clash beneath the **[Wide azure skies](Wide_azure_skies.md)**.

On the other side of the Empire, the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) press their claim to the farmlands of [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). The [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) has slipped through their grasp, but they have not abandoned their ambitions here. [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md) has been invaded for the first time in centuries, the city of [Meade](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#meade) conquered and looted. The call goes out to the oathsworn [Drakes](../../mixed/government/military/Drakes.md) and their allies to drive the barbarians out, and battle rages **[Where the slow river flows](Where_the_slow_river_flows.md)**. Across the border to the west, it is the Empire who are the invaders, striving to take back the Dour Fens of [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) from the Jotun who now occupy it. The war is fought over the only piece of dry land in the whole of the marshes, [Gravenmarch](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#gravenmarch), and the old stone plateau that dominates its skyline. As the winds of the new year blow, they set the **[Old blue banners dancing](Old_blue_banners_dancing.md)**.

And then at last we come to the eastern border, to the lands where chaos reigns between the water and the peaks. To [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md), where the [Pilgrim's Defiance](../../mixed/government/military/Pilgrim-s_Defiance.md) marches against the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) of Tharunind. They know they will not win, but the effort must be made to slow the vallorn, to let it rage against them, to answer its abominable furore with virtue and faith. They fight the unspeakable from the darkest past, to secure a future for [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md), burning with **[A bright cerulean flame](A_bright_cerulean_flame.md)**.

Finally let our eye drift further east, to what was once and still is [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md). To the marshes of the [Sarangrave](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md), where the Empire advances on the last stronghold of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) in that benighted territory. The [Tower of the Skink](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#tower-of-the-skink) looms above the woods and wetlands, controlling the pass that leads to allied [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md). The eastern barbarians are beaten but not yet broken; their forces in Sarangrave are depleted but they still fight. As the Imperial armies move, the air is thick with uncertainty; if we have learned anything at all from the campaign to take the territory it is that there are **[Other monstrous geographies](Other_monstrous_geographies.md)** at play in the eastern marshes

### Battle participation

If you have your own [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) mask please do bring it along as we're keen to ensure we can field forces predominantly comprised of barbarian orcs. If you are unable to wear a mask, then all battle opportunities have limited roles for human barbarian combatants and low-combat roles such as healers, support mages, artisans, messengers and attendants. Player [elite monster units](../../out-of-character/events/Monstering_a_battle.md#elite-monster-units) (EMUs) are expected to monster as their barbarian orc units regardless of the battle opportunity, and should not include human combatants.

- **The Sarangrave opportunities both involve forces composed primarily of Druj orc fighters**

We ask the majority of player volunteers to take on the roles of Druj orcs as normal. If you have your own [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) mask, please do bring it along as we're keen to ensure we can field a force predominantly comprised of Druj orcs. You can help by wearing leather or plate armour you have, as well as any iconic Druj weaponry, particularly swords, spears and great weapons, as well as bows of all types. You can find out more about the iconic Druj look [here](../../mixed/look-and-feel/Druj_look_and_feel.md), but the key element for portraying the Druj is a mantle and ragged material layers that echo natural greens, browns, greys, and black, with occasional bright accent colours as their iconic look.

- **The Mareave opportunity involves forces composed primarily of Grendel orc soldiers**

We ask the majority of player volunteers to take on the roles of Grendel orcs as normal. You can help by wearing leather or plate armour you have, as well as any iconic Grendel weaponry, particularly swords, spears, and bows of all types. You can find out more about the iconic Grendel look [here](../../mixed/look-and-feel/Grendel_look_and_feel.md), and the key elements for portraying the Grendel are a light coloured base layer, with mantles, shoulder capes, wraps and other purple clothing and other bright colours as their iconic look.

- **The Mitwold and Bregasland opportunities both involves forces of composed primarily of Jotun or Lasambrian orc warriors**

We ask the majority of player volunteers to take on the roles of Jotun orcs as normal. You can help by wearing any Jotun, Marcher, or Wintermark-style kit or armour you have, as well as any iconic Jotun weaponry, particularly axes, maces, and circular shields. You can find out more about the iconic Jotun look [here](../../mixed/look-and-feel/Jotun_look_and_feel.md), but the key element for portraying the northern Jotun is leather and fur over mail as their iconic look. For the Lasambrian Jotun orc forces elements of Brass Coast kit, armour and weapons can be used emphasise their distinct visual nature.

**IMPORTANT** - we will **not** be reusing any orc masks to players for these battle opportunities. Our orc masks will only be used once this event and then washed ready for the next event. We are continuing the provision of battle opportunities with non-orc enemies until such a time as we have sufficient masks to run two fully orc-masked battles.

## Conjunctions

The [Sentinel Gate](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md) also allows smaller groups of Imperial citizens to reach locations all over the Empire where their intervention might change the course of events. Many of these *[conjunctions](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#conjunction)* involve combat, but there are also a variety of other opportunities presented by the magic of the Gate.

For most conjunctions, the Civil Service has identified the Imperial titles that are the most appropriate to take overall responsibility for ensuring that a response is organised. Whilst the individual has been named by the Civil Service, there is no legal requirement for them to coordinate a response or travel on the conjunction. They may delegate oversight to another individual of their choosing. As always, Imperial war scouts will be on hand at the Sentinel Gate to provide further information to any citizens wanting to learn more. They can provide updates on the barbarians' goals, guidance on how to engage the enemy troops or advice on how to avoid casualties and return back to Anvil in safety.

Sometimes it is necessary for us to make small adjustments to the timing or size of encounters at the time of this publication and when an encounter runs at an event. Details for the conjunctions on Friday will be finalised on Friday afternoon of the event before time-in (18:00) whilst details for the conjunctions on Saturday will be finalised on Saturday before time-in (10:00). Please check the details of any conjunction you are interested in as soon as possible to ensure that you have the full and up-to-date information; it might also be sensible to check again shortly before the planned time of the encounter. You can check the details of a conjunction by casting [Detect Magic (Discover Conjunction)](../../out-of-character/rules/Detect_magic.md#discover-conjunction) on the Sentinel Gate in the presence of a referee.

It's very important to pay attention to the [accessibility](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md) note for each conjunction, which covers the likelihood of combat. Further details about the accessibility of a conjunction are [available both before and during the event](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#who-and-how-to-ask3f). You can learn more about this on the [accessibility](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md) page.

### Legends (Hahnmark)

*[Image: Morgan Drifting Bough](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:SpeakerMagpies.jpg)*

- **The hunt for Avalanche has led to a warband of Lasambrians**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- ***Morgan Drifting Bough* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for recovering the letter from one of the Lasambrians**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

The trail of Vanya Heartsworn's attacker has led to a curiously overstretched Lasambrian warband. Camped around [Gulnar's Deeps](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#gulnars-deeps), the Lasambrians, with some Jotun among them, have been spotted keeping uncharacteristically covert as they search for something.

There is no doubt, however, that the attackers' trail leads to them. It is clear that it could not have been the whole warband; Vanyar Heartsworn survived after all. Still, *Delphysius*, herald of [Zakalwe](../eternals/Zakalwe.md), has been working alongside Magpies over the past tenday, and believes that one of the Lasambrians has in their possession a letter which will have some kind of lead on where Avalanche might be found.

### Ways of Honour (Sermersuaq)

*[Image: Ceolmund Dreth , Banner-bearer of Atkonaroq](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Ceolmund_Dreth.jpg)*

- **A warband of yegarra and Jotun are on a raid into the Suaq Wastes**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- **The *Banner-bearer of Atkonaroq* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for killing Hallr Winterborn**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

The monsters of Sermersuaq have been quieter as of late. The defeat of the Falconer and its cult, along with years of hunting, has brought a sense of tranquillity to the Suaq Wastes not experienced in years. It is noticeable then, when a warband of yegarra, accompanied by some Jotun, ventures into the tundra in search of a fight.

Led by *Hallr Winterborn*, of Fisk, they bring with them the banner once flown by Eisa Winterborn as the leader of one of the large warbands that support the Jotun armies on campaign. It is clear that Hallr is looking to take Eisa's place, in position if not in legacy, and the first step is leading a successful raid into the Suaq Wastes. If the [Banner-bearer of Atkonaroq](../../mixed/titles/Banner-bearer_of_Atkonaroq.md), **Ceolmund Dreth**, can arrange for Hallr to be killed or defeated and the banner claimed, then that will put a stop to any hopes of a new Winterborn coming into prominence for at least a year.

### A Place to Begin (Hercynia)

*[Image: Earl Percival Summerstrong , Speaker of the Vallornguard](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Percival_Summerstrong.jpg)*

- **Ettercaps under the influence of the Phoenix appear to be attempting to build something**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- **The *Speaker of the Vallornguard* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for taking out the ettercaps and destroying their structure**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

Since the events of the last few months, Imperial prognosticators have been getting more and more used to the presence of constellations in odd configurations and conjunctions all over the Empire. Usually this has done little more than making their jobs even more hectic and confusing than usual, but sometimes something truly *interesting* crops up. The presence of [The Phoenix](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Phoenix.md) high about deep vallorn territory in Hercynia is one such case. What could the volatile Spring magic of the vallorn and the constellation tied to knowledge have to do with each other. Upon discovery of a sentinel gate conjunction, the answer, unfortunately, becomes clear; ettercaps.

All signs seem to point to them building...something, in the heart of Hercynia. What exactly it is, who knows, but allowing them to continue unmolested seems like a bad idea for the Empire. Given this is a new development for the vallorn, the duty of stopping them falls to the [Speaker of the Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md), **Earl Percival Summerstrong**. It's recommended that hammers and axes are brought in order to smash up whatever it is that is found in Hercynia.

**OOC Note:** Destructible objectives will be marked with a ribbon and can be destroyed with 1 minute of uninterrupted appropriate roleplay with a larp safe axe or hammer.
