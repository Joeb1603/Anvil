Here are the [Grey Fens](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#grey-fens) of [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md). The Jotun rule here, at least in theory. Yet what is this emerging from the marshes, from the dead pools? The [shades of Marchers past](Old_blue_banners_dancing.md#objective-dismiss-the-grey-walkers) walk the Grey Fens, now that [Ullsmere Rock](Old_blue_banners_dancing.md#objective-recover-the-ullsmere-rock) is gone. They do not tolerate trespassers, and unlike many ghosts they are capable of enforcing their will at least to a degree. They have no time for the self-styled Jarls of Bregasland, but they also care not for the folk of the Marches it seems. The Jotun defend their thralls, which includes those Bregas folk who have taken the Choice, but their efforts go only so far. Slowly, methodically, the Grey Walkers [drive our](../winds-of-fortune/Pride_in_the_Marches.md#get-off-my-fen) everyone living in "their" fens, human and orc alike.

Here are the [North Fens](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#north-fens), where stands [Fisher's Rock](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#fishers-rock). The interloper queen of the Fishers is dead now, interred far to the north by her erstwhile Jotun allies. She is gone but the shadow of her touch remains; the bitter anger of [House Brawness](Old_blue_banners_dancing.md#battle-opportunity-open-relations-with-house-brawness) festers here, steeped in daydreams of [rebellion](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#rebellious). Yet it is not House Brawness that the people of North Fens fear at night. It is not rebels who come creeping out of the Gullet to steal away the unwary. It is not the bitter anger of Matilda Fisher's former friends that has seen villages and farms deserted. The Jotun try to defend their thralls here, too, but the monsters that come out of the sea strike without warning and often as not the yeofolk are dragged kicking into the sea, shouting for aid that arrives too late.

Here then is [Ottermire](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#ottermire), and the town of Ottermire where the Jotun have planted their standards, from where they rule over Bregasland. They control the hub from which roads run like spokes - the Odd's Way east into [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md), and Sallow's Walk south toward the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md), and innumerable paths and byways stretching out across the fens. These lesser roads quickly give way to streams and lakes and rivers, and the flat-bottomed boats of the Bregasfolk. Especially since that season of rains which left all the Empire's marshes deeper, and stranger. And the fens of Bregasland were strange enough to begin with...

And here are [the Rushes](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#the-rushes), and the town of the Sallowfolk, which rises in time toward the dry land of [Graven Rock](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#graven-rock) and its [march](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#gravenmarch). There is a [new woodland](Old_blue_banners_dancing.md#the-forest-endures) here, where the marshwaters give way to the stone, where Jotun and Imperial once fought in the shadow of that great, flat rock (that rock that echoes still with the fading notes of that [eerie song](Old_blue_banners_dancing.md#the-mountain-rings) that once rang out across the battlefield). This is the border that the barbarians watch, between their lands and that fraction of Bregasland wrested free from their grasp.

It is from that rock, right down there in the south of Bregasland, that the ripples of [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) still spread across Bregasland bringing with them unnatural life, and the [chaotic touch](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md#chaos) of the [Primal Forest](../eternals/Realm.md#spring). It runs through the waters of the marshes and the swamps, bringing [indiscriminate healing](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md#healing-and-vitality) to all who need it, whether they want it or not. Since the flat-topped mountain sang, all save the most mortal of injuries heals in a matter of hours, provided one bathes in or drinks from the waters of Bregasland.

Spring rarely, if ever, brings unalloyed benefit to those it touches, and as Summer fades it is clear that Bregasland is no different. The vitality that infuses the humans, and the orcs, and their beasts, infuses also the vermin of the marsh. Great clouds of mosquitos and biting flies rise from the waters of the fens, spreading fear, and painful bites, and the threat of pestilence. The Jotun fight to defend their thralls, but against clouds of insects, what use can axes be?

The Jotun also cannot forget that out there, hidden in the fens and the marshes, are the [Strong Reeds](../../mixed/government/military/Strong_Reeds.md). They have not forsaken their oath to the Bregas, not for one day. They fought to take back Gravenmarch, and then they slipped back into the rushes and the water without a trace, and every day the Jotun jarls must remember that they are *still here*, and oh how that must rankle, how that must tug at their pride. They cannot root out the Strong Reeds, and every raid, every battered baggage train, every prisoner spirited away from beneath their noses, is a reminder that the spirit of Bregasland will never submit to them.

This is Bregasland, then, in the heartbeats between the height of Summer and the slow descent toward Winter. And none of these problems besetting the Jotun - not Grey Walkers, not coral-tainted husks, not bitter rebels, not bloodthirsty mosquitos, not even the lurking threat of the Strong Reeds - is anything compared to the storm of war that breaks over Bregasland as the sun rides high in the sky and the grain darkens toward gold in the fields.

## A Storm of Summer's Passing

Eleven Imperial armies explode into Bregasland with hurricane force, while the Strong Reeds rise from the rushes to meet them, to lead their assault against the Jotun conquerors. Across the territory, those the Reeds have inspired throw their strength behind the Empire, launching long-rehearsed strikes against barbarian strongholds across the fens. They strike supply caches in Ottery, fire the palisade at Sallow, and wreck bridges across the North Fens. Then, with the skill of the Bregas, they melt back into the marshes before the Jotun can retaliate.

Into the Rushes, the Empire comes in force. A dozen armies, half of them marked with the power of Imperial [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A further five thousand strength of [warbands](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md), led by bold captains eager to see the Jotun brought down, driven out of the Marches entirely.

The Strong Reeds rise, flanked by the [Tusks](../../mixed/government/military/Tusks.md) and the [Bounders](../../mixed/government/military/Bounders.md). This is a war for the Marches, but not solely *by* the Marches. Three Marcher armies are matched by three armies of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) - by the [Bloodcloaks](../../mixed/government/military/Bloodcloaks.md), the [Green Shield](../../mixed/government/military/Green_Shield.md), and the [Narwhal's Spear](../../mixed/government/military/Narwhal-s_Spear.md). The first guards the lives of those who fight, bringing succour to the fallen. The second [fights with honour](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#army-orders), reminding their allies that there are some tactics that will not be borne - and showing the Jotun that the heroes of Wintermark at least remember how to glory in battle without letting it taint their spirit. The hunters of the third army, [quick witted](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#quick-witted) and fleet footed and no stranger to fighting in marshes, test the mettle of their foes and seek that understanding which is key to any successful hunt.

Six armies, and only halfway through their accounting!

Next are two Freeborn armies, bright and burning despite the mud and the damp Summer. The [Burning Falcon](../../mixed/government/military/Burning_Falcon.md) blaze with passionate ardour, a deadly bonfire of [true conviction](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#true-conviction) that will not settle for anything less than absolute victory over the foe. The Jotun cannot help but respect this incomparable enthusiasm for battle. The [Red Wind Corsairs](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md) match the bold courage of their cousins with daring and audacity of their own, making the Jotun pay not in blood but in treasure to recover the prisoners they claim on the battlefield. The war-price is a sacred tradition, after all, and the orcs can hardly complain when the Freeborn take advantage of it.

Then the virtuous [Valiant Pegasus](../../mixed/government/military/Valiant_Pegasus.md) and the untested soldiers of the League fresh mustered to the [Sparrows Reckoning](../../mixed/government/military/Sparrows_Reckoning.md). They eschew glory and heroism in the pursuit of preserving lives, a [steady conquest](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#steady-conquest) that sees them ensure their soldiers are never far from the healing touch of the [physick](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) or the [healer](../../mixed/magic/spells/Heal.md). The soldiers of one of the oldest nations of the Empire fight beside those belonging to one of the youngest. The one shares their experience with the other, who in turn share their pride at their chance to serve their nation, their Empire, and their [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md).

Last, then, but by no means least, come the [Autumn Hammers](../../mixed/government/military/Autumn_Hammers.md). The [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of the [Unshackled](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) have the strangest of allies; with them marches a cohort of figures, features concealed in cowled hoods and metal masks. They fight not with sword or spears but with chains of green iron or pale glass or stranger materials. They are hunters bound by contract with the [Sovereign Lords of the City of Bar and Bond](../eternals/Lictors.md), and they bring with them the power of the [Iron Labyrinth](../eternals/Realm.md#autumn) itself. Its power is subtle, but as the Empire engages the armies of the Jotun, it begins to spread wherever the orc army treads.

Twelve armies. Four - the Hammers, the Reeds, the Tusks, and the Bounders - are at the heart of the Imperial strategy. Their soldiers are granted supernatural [clarity of thought](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clarity_of_the_Master_Strategist.md) by the Empire's masters of [Day magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md). They can see how best to wield the strength of Imperial soldiers against the defenders, the straight road from where they are to the future where Bregasland is freed.

Two are enchanted with the [Knights of Glory](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Knights_of_Glory.md). With the Green Shield is *Ollavan the Reeve*, sharp-eared archer and master of the bow. He brings his warband of hunters and rust-red hounds, and their arrows sing as they seek out the Jotun foe. When not fighting, they are eager to test their skill against that of the finest archers among the Imperial troops, congratulating winner and loser with equal enthusiasm. Their thirst for glory is contagious, spreading easily through the bold warriors of Wintermark.

The Burning Falcon are joined by *Kore the Lion-maned*, resplendent in crimson armour, leading their leonine lancers at the forefront of every charge. Kore's knights tower above the Freeborn beside them; while their torso, head, and arms are humanlike, their lower bodies are those of deadly predatory cats. (And if there are also rumours of laughing, dancing [heralds](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#heralds) of the [Shadowed Fire](../eternals/Janon.md) with the Burning Falcon, they are surely just that - rumours. Surely such sightings are simply kohan inspired by the passion of [Eleonaris'](../eternals/Eleonaris.md) captain, and not the servants of any [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) has placed under [enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity). Surely.)

The storm of swords breaks over Bregasland, and the Jotun are in its path. But what of the Jotun?

## An Answering Gale

The Jotun are on the back foot in Bregasland. The challenges to their rule, the threats to the thralls they are sworn to protect, are only the half of it. Their forces withdrew [fell back](../winds-of-fortune/Pride_in_the_Marches.md#mitwold-triumph) from [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md) last season, surrendering the land they had taken without a fight. The Lasambrian armies continued southward, down towards their home in [Reinos](../barbarians/Reinos.md), beset by the armies of the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md). The Imperial attack on [Skallahn](../barbarians/Skallahn.md) threatened the unity of the north and the south, and drew the attention of both the king and the queen away from Bregasland.

This season, it seems, the Jotun planned to tighten their grip on Bregasland once more. Perhaps they expected the Empire to be busy elsewhere, perhaps they simply didn't predict the sheer *might* that the Empire would bring to bear. Either way, when the Strong Reeds rise and the Imperial armies attack, the Jotun are in the midst of mobilizing for an attack of their own. Led by the *Skjaldirborn*, the Jotun forces in Bregasland sought to make a [cautious foray](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#cautious-advance) into Gravenmarch, to take back the only dry land in the marshes from the Empire.

Likely this was a calculated gamble - if the Empire were to wait another season before committing forces to Bregasland, they would be able to take back the March and remove the Imperial beachhead. If the Empire chose to defend, they could probe those defences without risking too many casualties. And if, as has happened, the Imperials launched an attack of their own they would at least be able to face it without wholesale bloodshed. Unfortunately for the Jotun, it is nowhere near as good as a carefully planned defence for holding in the face of the hurricane of war that has descended upon them.

There are some eight armies remaining in Bregasland, and the warbands of many of the Jotun champions. There are warriors of the *Fields of Glory* with them also; the warbands of the hound-knight *Ser Grumbar* and the stag-knight *Ser Helvennan* battle alongside the Jotun. The households of the rival twins glower and glare at each other, but seem united in their disappointment at how conservative the orcs' strategy is this season. It does not stop them fighting, however, and as always the court of the *Lady of the Pennants* seek every opportunity to test their skill against one another.

With the disruption spread by the Strong Reeds and their Bregas allies, the Jotun are barely able to respond to the awesome force of the Empire before they have already lost control of Sallow and most of the Rushes. They rally as best they can; the Roaring Thunder, the Southern Bear and the Shield of the Mountain try to hold the southern end of *Sallow Walk* while the skirmishers of the Howling Night and the vanguard of the Bear who Swims seek to flank the Imperial forces. Their rearguard action slows the Empire only briefly; the Jotun are forced to retreat in disarray. In their wake they leave the would-be Jarl Kiergmunt of Sallow. He and his household warriors are found floating in the eel ponds below the town he thought to make his hall, cut to pieces with the distinctive long knives favoured by the reed-cutters of the Rushes. The people here may resent the Empire, but they resent the Jotun even more; and when the Strong Reeds rise, they rise with them.

## The Battle of Sallow Walk

As the Empire quells barbarian resistance in the Rushes, the Jotun regroup and prepare a counter-attack from their stronghold at Ottery. While the siege-engineers of the Tower of the North feverishly reinforce the defences along the northern part of Sallow Walk and the town at its head, the relentless Fell Hammers and the Southern Bear lead the bulk of the Jotun forces against the Empire. As they march south along Sallow Walk, they meet the Burning Falcon marching north, eagerly pulling ahead of the main Imperial line. Perhaps ten thousand Freeborn and allied captains, along with the heralds of Eleonaris, clash with maybe twelve thousand Jotun, and their own supernatural allies. Neither Imperial nor barbarian are prepared to step aside, and furious battle is joined with control of the Walk being the ultimate prize.

Battle rages on the raised road, and the sections of dry land to either side. The four Imperial armies most familiar with fighting in open marsh - the Bounders and the Drakes to the east and the Narwhal's Spear and the Green Shield to the west - sweep round to attack the Jotun from the side. Before they can strike, the rest of the Jotun forces arrive to support their vanguard. It's clear that while many of the barbarians are at a disadvantage when forced to leave the dry ground of the Walk, others have made the most of their time in Bregasland to adapt their tactics a little to the realities of shifting ground and open water.

At first it seems the furious charge of the Burning Falcon will give them the advantage, but the Fell Hammers and the Southern Bear have their own momentum. The enthusiasm of the Freeborn proves to be both an advantage and a disadvantage when tested against the more cautious strategy of the orcs. The front lines press forward too far, and while at first it seems the Jotun line is breaking, they are actually falling back and to the side to allow the Falcon to over-extend itself. The barbarians close around the vanguard of the Burning Falcon like a fist, and exact a terrible price from the bright burning warriors of the south before the tides of battle allow them to retreat to their own lines.

In the end though it is the unstoppable force of the Falcon and their allies that wins the day. The Jotun are forced to sound the retreat - great hollowed horns braying mournful notes across the sodden battlefield. The Empire continues to press them all the way back to Ottery, with the pitched battle of Sallows Walk becoming a ragged mess of smaller fights and skirmishes. The barbarians are still more interested in the fight than they are in defending their hold of Bregasland - at least for the moment.

A butchers' trail lies along the top of Sallow Walk as the Empire presses the Jotun north. The unheard rippling song of Graven Rock still washes across Bregasland, but even Spring magic has its limits. The Jotun take their wounded with them where they can but must leave the bodies of the dead behind; at the same time they make no effort to execute the fallen Imperial soldiers who have fought valiantly against them. The Empire may be their enemies, but to slaughter the fallen would be to surrender the heart and soul of what it means to be Jotun. For their part, the Empire is also restrained in its treatment of barbarians that fall behind the retreating armies - the Green Shield in particular will not allow the mistreatment of prisoners. Some Freeborn joke that the Red Wind Corsairs are spending almost as much time negotiating the exchange of prisoners and the ransom of captured Jotun as they do fighting the barbarians!

## The Battle of Ottery

The Tower of the North have been busy, wringing every ounce of opportunity from every hour of time the rest of the Jotun have bought for them. Wood is at a premium in Bregasland, but it seems the Tower has brought wagons of lumber out of Liathaven to prepare their defences. A stout wood-and-stone palisade surrounds Ottery, reinforced with archers-towers. Spiked barricades have been thrown like the toys of a frustrated giant across the road, and the marshy land surrounding the town. The Bregas themselves are trapped in their houses, a curfew imposed by savvy Jotun who will not risk Strong Reeds or their sympathisers infiltrating Ottery.

Arrayed before Ottery to meet the Empire is the full might of the Skjaldirborn, banners flapping fitfully in the sour wind blowing from the north. The mighty Jorgen Hylasson, one of the most respected of the northern Jotun warlords, stands a little ahead of the army. As the Imperial forces begin to move forward, he raises his hand in a salute to the attackers, and then sounds the massive horn he hefts easily in one hand. The Jotun are not content to cower behind makeshift walls - and it seems they are not prepared to risk harm coming to the thralls of Ottery. With the Skjaldirborn in the lead, they attack the Empire head-on.

Yet even so, they are not reckless. They make good use of the barricades the Tower of the North have erected; archers are positioned in key locations across the battlefield and in the newly constructed towers of Ottery. Rather than volleying indiscriminately, these skilled marksmen pick their targets with care. More than one Imperial battlemage is brought down without any hint of a warning by an arrow to the back of the head. These deadly warriors support the [Ulfur](../barbarians/Elite_Jotun_troops.md#ulfur) of the Howling Night in their efforts to counter the impact of Imperial battlefield magic.

Many of the Jotun warriors here bear [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md), or the boons of the [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md). Summerbound [Skjaldir](../barbarians/Elite_Jotun_troops.md#skjaldir) with truly supernatural levels of [endurance](../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance) battle relentlessly alongside [Ulvenwar](../barbarians/Elite_Jotun_troops.md#ulvenwar) with the strength to [rend](../../mixed/magic/spells/Shatter.md) the wargear of their enemies. These magically empowered warbands especially seek to test themselves against the knights of Dawn and the Marcher armies, and before the real threat is made clear are able to splinter many Marcher polearms and break the bright heraldry of Dawnish shields.

While the battle rages south of Ottery, the Bounders, Strong Reeds, and the Sparrows Reckoning avoid the thick of the fighting and flank round to the east. They launch a coordinated raid against the less-well defended gates of the town and quickly gain purchase within the town itself. The League army is untested, but the [bravos](../nations/the-league/Bravo.md) that fight with it are well versed in fighting in the confined spaces of city streets - skills that serve them well inside Ottery. Once the folk here realise the Reeds and the Bounders are here, that the Empire is here, many of them take up weapons and join the effort to reclaim their town. The struggle within Ottery itself culminates in a trio of Marcher beaters and a League crossbow marksman capturing one of the archer towers, and firing on the Jotun from behind. When Jorgen Hylasson takes a Marcher arrow to the shoulder, piercing through his mandowla-hide cloak and finding a chink in his armour, it is the turning point of the battle.

Ottery lost, and the Empire pressing forward, the second retreat is sounded. Again the Jotun fall back toward [North Fens](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#north-fens). Not one Jotun Army tries to retreat west into Grey Fens - they would always rather fight living Imperials than dead phantoms.

The Bloodcloaks quickly take charge of Ottery, establishing field hospitals wherever they can. Public buildings are used, but there are also sickrooms set up in the homes of grateful Bregas who open their doors to the injured and to the Winterfolk healers. There is a significant Jotun supply cache here, and its not clear who gives the order to break it open, but the night after the Battle of Ottery an impromptu celebration begins in the streets of the town. At first it is muted - this liberation has been bought at a terrible cost after all - but perhaps it is that very cost, that spectre of death and war, that adds kindling to the feasting and the dancing and the singing in the streets of Ottery that night. A weight has been lifted from the Bregas - hope replacing fear and bitterness. Perhaps there are some who think the celebration premature, but over the next two weeks the Imperial forces win out against the Jotun again and again and eventually Ottermire is Imperial - and with it the territory of Bregasland as a whole.

## A Long Slog Through Thick Mud

After Ottermire, the writing seems to be on the wall for the Jotun. They continue to launch attacks against the Imperial armies, but they are short-lived, probing stabs rather than committed efforts to bring the Empire to battle again. The Jotun could dig-in at Fisher's Rock, but for the most part they focus on trying to bog the Imperial forces down wherever they can. It's clear messengers have been sent west and north, to call for aid against the massive Imperial host, to warn King Gudmundr and Queen Yrsa of the situation in Bregasland.

The fighting continues as the Autumn Equinox approaches, and as it does so too the Empire's front line approach Fisher's Rock where the Jotun have their fallback position. One last push, and the Jotun will have no choice but to retreat again - either into the Grey Fens to the south and thence to Liathaven, or west into Hordalant. It seems inevitable that the Empire will win - if they keep up this relentless pressure the remaining Jotun forces will not be able to stand against it.

The cost has been high. Even with the magic of Graven Rock and the efforts of the Bloodcloaks, the butchers' road northward has claimed so many lives. The dead are counted in the thousands, and the Empire's forceful strategy has seen them pay the lion's share. Many of the fallen are recovered, but just as many are not, will likely never be found, taken by the mud and the water. The marshes give but they also take, say some of the Bregas dourly.

## The Manacle Snaps Shut

Autumn is in the wind, in the water, in the sun setting behind the western peaks. But that is not the *only* place the Autumn lies. All through this long campaign, the Autumn Hammers have been busy. While their warriors have fought no less valiantly against the Jotun than anyone else, that is not *all* they have been doing. As the battle presses the Jotun north, bands of thief-takers and oathwrights have made forays into the marshes, or led swift raids against key positions that seem to have little strategic importance. With them have gone the iron-masked chainfighters of the City of Bar and Bond.

The whole time the Jotun have been fighting over the marshes of Bregasland, the Unshackled have been fixing unseen manacles about their ankles. The [lictors](../eternals/Lictors.md) are the unparalleled masters of [binding](../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md#binding) and this season they are contracted to the service of the Imperial Orcs. None of the Jotun armies escape their touch. While binding is often dramatic and obvious - the spells of the war magician that hold folk in place - there are also more subtle methods of holding in place, of impeding travel. As the last light of Summer drains from the sky, and Winter's herald delicately touches the leaves on the scrubby marsh trees, the jaws of the final trap snap shut.

## Battle Opportunity : Big Talk

The Empire has pushed back the Jotun armies in Bregasland this season, securing the regions of The Rushes and Ottermire, and restoring Imperial control over the territory. The presence of the Strong Reeds in the territory throughout the campaign is fast becoming something of a modern legend with people swapping stories of their involvement. Some of the stories assign quite incredible feats to the army's Proud general and their Loyal soldiers, yet most people are not fool enough to believe those tales, but it cheers the spirits to swap the stories.

The victories thus far have not yet countered the new threats of coral-infested dead emerging from The Gullet or the shades of the dead laying claim to the desolate expanses of the Grey Fens. As summer turns to autumn, as harvests are gathered, and preparations made for winter, threats abound on all sides. Some joke that at least the Jotun will stand and fight on equal terms, instead of menacing good honest folk at night or lurching out of the dunes and sea gorse. Still, most people are in good cheer - Bregasland will soon be free and the Jotun gone - again.

Well, most people. Not everyone in Bregasland is cheering and waving flags for the Reeds. Memories of Mathilda Fisher are still fresh and those who bore the brunt of that when the would-be queen was slain are still nursing their grudges. Few people can hold a grudge like a Marcher after all. The leaders of [House Brawness were executed](../winds-of-fortune/Despise_treachery.md#house-brawness-of-the-north-fens) and the remainder of the [household](../nations/the-marches/Marcher_household.md) and their allies have been a simmering brew that [threatens rebellion](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#rebellious) ever since. Talk is cheap, however, and the House have done nothing but nurse their grievances up to this point when the return of the Empire forces them to finally pick a side.

Six months ago there was a chance to strike against the Jotun, to save Friar Bloughton from capture, and seek to open the door to the wayward Brawness. A way to bring the sheep back into the fold so to speak. The Military Council favoured other courses of action at the time, and that window closed. Fate doesn't repeat itself, but it does rhyme, so they say. The chance to make peace with the rebellious house is gone, all that remains is to cauterise the wound.

It appears that Bloughton has been negotiating a deal with the Jotun, a way for the most aggrieved members of the household to pack up their things and march west. Ironically, the Empire's victory has forced their hand - either they make good on their dreams of getting out from under the Empire or they shut up about it. The Empire has one last opportunity to lance this boil; whatever happens after this is in the wood for good.

Imperial prognosticators have detected a conjunction to the *Whelksquay* in the North Fens that will enable heroes to reach the area on the Saturday of the Autumn Equinox.

Apparently Mott, wily one that she is, has come up with an idea to give the Jotun a taste of Navarri potions. Taking advantage of the work that [beaters](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#beaters) have done to identify a supply route used by Jotun. Apparently, it is traversed almost daily by small groups of yegarra and reinforcements who move - amongst other things - [potions](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md).

The plan is simple: take out one of the supply convoys, steal the potions they carry, replace them with something with a bit of bite, and leave them for the next group to "*recover*". The apprentice of Mott suggested that the [Tincture of the Whipped Cur](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_War.md#tincture-of-the-whipped-cur) would be most effective. They estimate that the Yegarra are carrying a total of around 50 philtres and elixirs; for each ten replaced with an equivalent number of tinctures, 80 casualties would be inflicted on the Southern Bear, up to a maximum of four hundred. If the yegarra survive and deliver the supplies, they would instead represent a reinforcement equivalent to 200 strength to the Southern Bear. Of course, if the yegarra are killed and the supplies looted, but *not* replaced by tinctures, then the Southern Bear would neither gain nor lose strength.

The [Blackroot Broker](../../mixed/titles/Blackroot_Broker.md), **Egan Dusk Crow**, is responsible for intercepting the supplies, though replacing them is optional. The yegarra are unlikely to simply stop and fight; their task is getting the supplies to where they need to go; they will need to be forced to engage by the Imperials.

## A Field of Glass (Conjunction)

*[Image: Joaquim i Erigo , General of the Red Wind Corsairs](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Joaquim_i_Erigo.jpg)*

- **A warband of yegarra are moving to pay House Brawness for food they have provided**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- **The *General of the Red Wind Corsairs* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for intercepting the pay**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

*Yanya Uranduln*, general of the *Bear Who Swims* is of the opinion that the best way to feed an army on campaign is to pay farmers for their food rather than just taking it. If you just take it, you might find that the well you've been using has a corpse in it or that someone's fired your stores in the night. Not to mention if the plan is to fully conquer a land and rule it, then simply commandeering crops is a terrible way to ensure you'll be able to feed yourself in the winter. All that to say: "*plant a grudge in the soil, it will last a lifetime. Set it in stone and it will last forever."'*

House Brawness have done well at helping to provide food for the Bear Who Swims when they've been in the territory over the past eighteen months since the Jotun reconquered the Dour Fens. *Yanya Uranduln* has dispatched a warband of yegarra to deliver pay for the last season of food. Pay that is up for grabs. The daring raids of the [Red Wind Corsairs](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md) have brought wealth to the army and its general. Captives are taken, and wealth is returned; the western orcs continue to recognise the changes made to the Freeborn army and applaud their worth. The [Jackals](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md#composition) are always keen for a new target, and this [haul](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md#traditions) may prove to be the juiciest in a while.

The Jackals ask that **Joaquim i Erigo**, as [General of the Red Wind Corsairs](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md), intercept the yegarra and claim the pay to be used to better the Brass Coast. They suggest that the yegarra appear unlikely to simply stand and fight; they will need to be intercepted and brought to heel.
