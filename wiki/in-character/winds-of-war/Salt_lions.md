# Salt lions

*[Image: The Empire has opened the Vaults of the Chainbound, but victory in Mareave is not so easily unlocked.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Strategy_of_salt_and_stone.jpg)*

## The Strategy of Salt and Stone

During the Summer Solstice, Imperial heroes clashed with Grendel raiders over the [Saltbound Stone](Mithril_and_rope.md#objective-secure-the-saltbound-stone), a [Skouran](../foreign-nations/Skoura.md) relic of the distant past. A partial [Autumn magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md) ritual text concealed within a hidden [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) that promised a potent strategic advantage to whichever side managed to retrieve it. The Stone was denied to the barbarians, secured by the Empire, its secrets awaiting the careful attention of Imperial magicians. Custody of the relic brings with it political headaches of course - the [treaty](../senate-sessions/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#ratify-treaty-with-skoura) between the Empire and the Skouran Consolidation requires that the Empire "*return, at reasonable costs, any cultural and historical artefacts of the Skouran people*". Yet that is a matter for [ambassadors](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) and diplomats; more important is that the Grendel were denied their prize and the Empire profited.

In the days following the battle at the *Ruins of Sinat*, as the [Rivers of Life](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_of_Life.md) continued to flow sluggishly over the hot, dry territory, the Empire withdrew all the [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) previously stationed in Mareave save the [Wolves of War](../../mixed/government/military/Wolves_of_War.md). They did not abandon the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md); far from it. Five fresh armies took the place of those returning to the heartlands of the Empire. The [Winter Sun](../../mixed/government/military/Winter_Sun.md) and the [Seventh Wave](../../mixed/government/military/Seventh_Wave.md), the [Granite Pillar](../../mixed/government/military/Granite_Pillar.md) and the [Eastern Sky](../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md) took [Curstag's Way](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#curstags-way) down the pass from [Apulian](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#apulian), swiftly reinforcing the Wolves in [Clisearn](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#clisearn). With the Eastern Sky come a great host of Imperial [warband](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) led by captains from every nation save [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md).

The generals of the Empire continue to favour a strategy that served them well enough last season. While the Eastern Sky and the Wolves of War go on the offensive, the Sun, and the Pillar, and the Wave take defensive positions, setting themselves as a wall of steel and virtue against Grendel attempts to complete their conquest of Clisearn. Even as they are moving into position, another army arrives down the [Apulian Way](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#the-apulian-way). The [Fire of the South](../../mixed/government/military/Fire_of_the_South.md) do nothing by halves; previously garrisoned in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) to [defend their homeland](Phantasms_and_foam.md#take-your-prize) they have torn a burning path all along the northern coast of the [Bay of Catazar](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md) to join the Imperial assault in Mareave. They arrive tired and dusty... and immediately throw themselves into the fray in southern Mareave barely pausing to quench their thirst and set their camps before battle is joined.

Every Imperial army in Mareave bears the mark of [Day magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md). Each has been [enchanted](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) with the [Clarity of the Master Strategist](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clarity_of_the_Master_Strategist.md), allowing them not only to coordinate their efforts with expert skill but also ensuring every soldier knows their role. Every flaw in the Grendel strategy is identified and exploited - every overreach, every failure to effectively defend a supply cache, every inconsistency created by squabbling commanders. For those on the offensive, it means they are ideally positioned to probe and test the defences of the Grendel; for those protecting the Unshackled from the orcs of the Broken Shore it means they are able to predict where the barbarians will strike with an uncanny exactitude bordering on prescience.

The Imperials don't fight alone, either. [Potent sigils](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#ramparts-of-ashlar) still burn all along the walls of the [Bastion](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#bastion) of [Rucraic's Redoubt](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#rucraics-redoubt). Bronze giants drawn from the [City of Fire and Stone](../eternals/Estavus.md) continue to fight alongside the orc [garrison](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#garrison), who continue to support the [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in the south. Three [magical citadels](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Frozen_Citadel_of_Cathan_Canae.md) loom over Mareave. From [Icarion](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#icarion) and in [Nadir](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#nadir), mighty warriors from the court of the *[Queen of Ice and Darkness](../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md)* march to join the Empire's armies. In Clisearn itself, the grey stone fortification rises on the borderland between Imperial-held territory and that claimed by the barbarians, serving both as a base camp for the Empire's soldiers to strike against Grendel positions and for the defenders to hold the line against their assault.

There is also aid unlooked for. A dozen Polemarchs in service to the [Master Strategist](../eternals/Zakalwe.md) of the [Endless Sea and the Fathomless Sky](../eternals/Realm.md#day) arrive shortly after the Fire of the South joins the battle. They observe, offer support, help smooth lines of communication between attacking forces and those defending Mareave. They are adept at supporting those drawing on the supernatural *Clarity* created with [Day magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md) - and looking for opportunities to counter Grendel plots. When their presence is questioned, they seem surprised. Their master promised the [Archmage of Day](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Day.md) that he would offer aid to the Empire, and so they are here. Plus, the decision to embrace the power of clear strategy on such a scale, in such a place, cannot help but attract the attention of the *Tidehunter*. The Polemarchs do not go where they are not wanted, and they do not push their opinions or observations, but their ability to communicate with one another over great distances in particular proves valuable in the days following their appearance. They noticeably avoid both the warriors of the [Summer realm](../eternals/Realm.md#summer) and the [Iron Labyrinth](../eternals/Realm.md#autumn), however.

## Solid Stand on Shifting Sand

The Empire's successfully executed two-pronged strategy falters slightly when it rubs up against the plans of the Grendel however. Unlike previous seasons, the orcs of the Broken Shore are not on the offensive. They have dug in, securing the land they have claimed, focusing their efforts purely on the defensive. For the Imperial Orcs and Highborn armies, this means three months of relatively excitement-free patrols. With the Grendel remaining resolutely on "their side" of the border that splits Clisearn - without even light raiding - they have nothing to do but bolster the Imperial positions in the northern quarter of the region.

The Wolves, the Sun, and the Fire however have a *much* more perilous time of it. Three armies - albeit three armies supported by innumerable supernatural allies - face the entire Grendel force in Mareave. Fortunately, the supernatural allies and courageous warbands help to even the odds but those three armies are engaging a force of more than forty thousand barbarians, with their magic, and their own allies. Both [Asavean](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) battleships and mercenary forces are active in Mareave this season, although the impact of the former is thankfully reduced by the fact the bulk of the fighting is taking place inland rather than along the coast.

(And at least some of the mercenaries are fighting elsewhere this season. Their arrangement with the Grendel *Maistir Brothaig* resolved, the [Ilium Jackals](Mithril_and_rope.md#opportunity-pay-off-the-ilium-jackals) of [Sarcophan](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) received a surprising offer on the battlefields of Sinat and are now under contract to the *Boyar's Hasta*. Can the professional treachery of the dedicated mercenary be made any clearer than this? How the City of Locks must smile to see these fair-weather warriors skip from one side of the line to the other!)

There is nothing especially subtle about the Grendel strategy this season. The [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) simply [hold their ground](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#solid-defence) with the [support](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy_orders.md#naval-support) of their [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md). At the forefront of the defence are the bitter Brine Turtles and the youthful Hamsin, along with the siege-engineers of the Iron Gulls and the barely-leashed Storm Sharks. The latter are the only ones who seem to actively relish the chance to fight the Imperial opposition. The others demonstrate the discipline the Grendel are known for, refusing to be drawn out, resisting any attempt to pull them into northern Mareave where the rest of the Empire's forces would make short work of them. It becomes clear early on that these four armies are leading the defence of Grendel-held Mareave in part because they are each enchanted with the same kind of magic as the Empire's armies. They react to threats with supernatural speed and acuity, the insight granted by Day magic guiding them to counter attempts to circumvent their positions or catch them in a pincer movement.

There is no single, key conflict during the months between the Summer Solstice and the Autumn Equinox. Rather there are a series of skirmishes and probing strikes from the Empire which are met with a wall of barbarian iron. The border between Imperial and barbarian held territory frays a little - Imperial troops press forward in one place and make temporary gains before the Grendel regroup and reclaim the land they have lost. The Empire is largely able to dictate *where* each battle takes place, but the commanders of the Broken Shore - bolstered as they are with supernatural clarity - are quick to respond to every attempt to gain ground.

When the four armies at the forefront respond to an Imperial incursion, their manoeuvres are given a great deal of additional weight by the rest of the Grendel forces who have for the most part made camp in Fleisardh. The fighting is bitter, brutal work. If not for the Spring curse laid on the territory, then thousands would have died on both sides - as it is, the Empire loses just over a thousand soldiers between the three armies and the garrison of Rucraic's Redoubt. The Grendel pay a higher price - primarily due to the presence of so many independent warbands on the Imperial side. Their defensive position, however, and the fact that they simply need to hold against the Empire means that even there their casualties are reduced noticeably from what they might have been. The Empire struggles to recover its wounded, while the Grendel can ensure that as long as they do not retreat their injured soldiers can return to the fray the next day.

The fact that a large portion of the Grendel forces are at sea, rather than in the shape of boots on the ground, is a blessing for the Empire. It also helps that the *Golden Winds* seem to have entirely withdrawn from Mareave - likely due to the [grim boon](../winds-of-fortune/The_keys_of_blood.md#do-some-harm-arhallogen) offered to the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md) by the [Centipede General](../eternals/Arhallogen.md). Plentiful fresh water is rare in Mareave, and it seems that poisoning the wells the Grendel rely on has caused enough death and suffering that the admiral felt the need to flee back to the safety of the western Broken Shore to recuperate.

Even so the support of the remaining Grendel navies strengthens their armies. They transport large amounts of supplies from elsewhere along the Shore, avoiding the desiccating trek through the [Petrified Forest](../barbarians/Ayereed.md#petrified-forest), and removing the threat of having their food and drink poisoned by opportunistic wizards. For the most part though the sailors themselves, even those trained in war, do not engage the Empire directly. This also reduces the impact of the Asavean warships sent by the Plenum. While there are a few mercenaries fighting alongside the armies - especially the Hamsin - they seem unenthused by the defensive strategy the Salt Lords have decreed.

If these sea-borne forces *had* been involved - if the fighting had taken place in Fleisardh for example - then there's no doubt that a great many more League, Dawnish, and Freeborn lives would have been lost. Perhaps something to bear in mind in the future? The Grendel strength has always lain with their navies, rather than their armies, after all.

Their navies... and their [cunning](../barbarians/Grendel_religion.md#cunning).

## The Crab Has Two Hands

At the same time that the League, Dawn, and the Brass Coast are pressing the defences of the Grendel, the Highborn are about a different task. The Granite Pillar lead a disciplined defence, and that leaves parts of the Seventh Wave free to [employ different tactics](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#scouting). With some support from the [canny guerillas](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#freedom-fighters) of the Winter Sun, and a little assistance from two of the Polemarchs, they send out scouts and spies to seek out both any sign of a Grendel [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md), and any threat of espionage from the Broken Shore. With no enemy forces attacking the Empire, they are free to focus their efforts on Imperial-held territory (and to a slightly lesser degree, on the city-state of [Beoraidh](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#beoraidh)).

Just as well they do. While everyone's attention is in the south, only the most [vigilant](../religion/Vigilance.md) eyes are watching in the north, and that almost leads to disaster. While the *Katabat* and *Shamal* are busy supporting the armies, the *Tempest* and *Simoom* have split their attention. Along with what seems to be a significant number of [independent vessels](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) controlled by Grendel captains, they are busy below the cliffs of [Icarion](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#icarion) in a location not too far from where the old Urizen lighthouse once stood.

They are not attacking - they are doing their best not to attract attention at all - and it is initially not clear what they are up to. It seems that the ships are transporting large amounts of rubble and sand from somewhere in the Broken Shore and simply dumping it over the side at several key locations along the cliffs of Icarion. At first the Highborn are at a loss to explain what is going on - partly because the crumbling cliffs present a treacherous obstacle to getting close enough to see what they are doing. Yet with the help of [spyglass](../../out-of-character/game-design/Technology.md#spectacles-spyglasses-and-lenses) and not a little luck the shape of the Grendel scheme becomes horribly apparent.

It seems that in some parts of Icarion there are tunnels in the rock that reach up to sites previously occupied by some Urizeni and Grendel ruins. Smugglers' tunnels perhaps - or more likely the remnants of an Urizeni strategy to allow vessels from Spiral to dock at the base of the cliffs and transport travellers and goods up to Mareave proper. The hunger of the Bay has washed away any sign of such docks... but the Grendel are looking to recreate them albeit temporarily. If they are successful, it will mean that Icarion is accessible from the sea, at least for a time. The precise locations of the tunnels is not clear - thanks to the [recent chaos](../winds-of-fortune/A_wizard_did_it.md#rains-in-mareave) caused by [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) gone awry the ruins themselves are collapsed and overgrown with scrubby grass and orange flowers.

The Grendel however clearly know where the lower levels of the tunnels come out. They are seeking to create a literal "beach head" at the base of the cliffs, one that will likely not be permanent but represents a significant short-term threat to Mareave. With access from the sea to the clifftops, they would be able to bring their full force to bear on Icarion, both the navies themselves and the armies they transport. And if they can use that force to capture Icarion, they would control the southern entrance to the Apulian Way, Disrupting the narrow umbilical that connects Mareave to the rest of the Empire. Such a strategy would not only cut Mareave off from the rest of the Empire, but there is a serious risk it would see Imperial armies caught in the jaws of the Grendel, unable to retreat...

### Mareave

- **No land has changed hands in Mareave this season**
- **Beoraidh remains neutral in the conflict between Grendel and Imperial forces**

The Empire continues to hold Clisearn, but three-quarters of the region remains in Grendel hands. The Grendel have defended their conquered land, and no headway has been made in pushing them back. On the other hand, there have been no successful attacks into Imperial parts of the region this season, and no repeat of the daring raid against [Fundindelve](../../mixed/titles/Fundindelve.md) last season.

The Seventh Wave have undertaken a [scouting](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#scouting) order in Mareave this season, and in addition to uncovering the schemes of the Salt Lords in Icarion they will receive a report at the Muster on Friday night detailing any foreign [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) operating in the territory, and including any strategic or tactical opportunities related to defending the territory from outside forces. They won't receive any details of enemy campaign armies however because none were taking attacking orders. One thing of note is that the Polemarchs of Zakalwe have been active in supporting the Highborn army, and are believed to have an opportunity for the General to pursue involving gaining up-to-date information on Grendel military capabilities.

Finally, the city state of [Beoraidh](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#beoraidh) remains neutral in the fighting between the Grendel and the Empire. The Palace of Chains - the fortification in Beoraidh city - has aided neither side.

### Participation : Unshackled in Mareave

- **Any Imperial Orc whose personal resource is in Mareave may have received a boon from Cathan Canae**
- **The boon is treated as an enchantment on the character and is removed in the usual way**

The heralds of Cathan Canae are once again active in Mareave. While many of them fight the Grendel alongside the Empire's attacking forces, they also protect every part of Mareave. Massive castles drawn from the Summer Realm tower over Icarion, Nadir, and Clisearn and there are plenty of warriors and giants who look to the defence of the orcs who live there. The influence of Cathan Canae is felt in every part of the territory controlled by the Unshackled, not only at the warfront in the south.

Any Imperial Orc character whose [personal resource](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource) is in Mareave may choose to have had a significant encounter with one of the servants of the *Lady of the Frost* and received a boon from them. You gain one additional [hit](../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance), as if you were under the effect of the [Strength of the Bull](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Strength_of_the_Bull.md) [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). You also experience a potent roleplaying effect: *You find it hard to back down, even if you might be in the wrong. Your natural stubbornness is greatly enhanced, and it feels satisfying to refuse to change your position. It is easy to see anyone who tries to convince or force you to modify your beliefs as a rival or enemy, and it is hard not to respond to such perceptions with force.*

In addition, while under the influence of this roleplaying effect, it is easier for an orc to hear the voices of ancestors who encourage them to stick to their beliefs, to resist anyone who tries to tell them what to do, or who encourages direct action against rivals or enemies. This particularly includes the bold [Rucraic](../barbarians/Grendel_religion.md#rucraic---the-storm-lord) and ruthless [Dubhtraig](../barbarians/Grendel_religion.md#dubhtraig---the-salt-lord) if you feel a connection to those two Grendel ancestors.

All of these effects are lost if you receive a different personal enchantment during the summit - as with any enchantment you can only have one such effect at a time. Otherwise it persists until shortly before the start of the Winter Solstice 388YE.

## Battle Opportunity : Jaws of the Leomarid

- **An opportunity exists to disrupt a Grendel plan to establish naval access to the region of Icarion**

Nine months ago a secretive cult emerged from Attar and offered the power of the *Hawser of the Drowned* to the Salt Lords allowing them to collapse the cliffs along the coast of Fleisardh. As the campaign has increasingly focused on the interior of Mareave, the Grendel desperately need the support of their navies to counter the Imperial presence in the territory. The greatest barrier is the stubborn neutrality of the port city of Beoraidh, and the towering coastal cliffs of Icarion.

The *Tempest* and *Simoom* have [appraised](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to overcome the natural sea defences of Icarion and create a way to make the region coastal. Through repeated dumping of rocks, sand, and rubble from ancient buildings, a flotilla of small [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) of independent captains have spent the last month building up basic quays beneath the cliffs in what are normally treacherous waters surrounding the *Syagrian Haven*. The headland above contains several old ruined settlements, beneath which the limestone cliffs are riddled with cracks, fissures, and tunnels. The tunnels extend inland, spreading out in all directions. Some of these are large enough to move people and supplies through, passageways of a former minor Urizeni port and more recent enlargements by Grendel smugglers. A whole tunnel network exists, capable of hiding away bands of smugglers or spies for months, but only four of the tunnels are large enough to be of use to military forces.

If the Grendel's work is left uninterrupted then their navies - four of which are currently in Mareave - would be able to land marines onto the makeshift docks and then quickly bring elite moridun soldiers up into Icarion. With no coastal defences to speak of it would be hard for the Empire to deter their ships, and the steep drop would offer the orcs making landfall ample protection whilst they entered the tunnels. From there, Wave Riders could quickly spread out into the region, allowing the Stone Born to consolidate their gains. If this cunning innovation by the Grendel is not stopped, the Grendel will be able to treat Icarion as a coastal region for the next year.

The [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) has identified a conjunction that will enable heroes to reach the *Syagrian Haven* in Icarion on the Saturday of the Autumn Equinox.

### Objective: Locate and Collapse the Tunnel Network

- **The tunnels have a system of shafts to ventilate the briney caverns**
- **There are six vents that lead down to the tunnels below, but only four connect to active tunnels**
- ***Clear Lens of the Eternal River* can be used to divine which vents link with the tunnels used by the Grendel**
- **Once an active tunnel has been located, actions can be taken to collapse it**
- **Zakalwe offers the *Spheres of Tarachi* to assist in this endeavour**
- **The Grendel plan will allow them to treat Icarion as if it were coastal for the next year**
- **Each destroyed tunnel will reduce the time they can maintain this by one season**

The tunnels that extend inland from the *Syagrian Haven* are the focus of the Grendel's scheme, and they already have an exploratory force in place on the headland above. The first hurdle to thwarting the Grendel in the area is to actually determine where to focus efforts. There are six shaft vents that connect down to the tunnels below, bringing fresh air into the caverns and storehouses, regulating temperature and evacuating the often stale brine-filled atmosphere. Not all of them are useful, two connect to dead-end chambers or old passageways; simply hoping for the best risks collapsing an unused tunnel or empty cavern, wasting resources with little impact on the Grendel.

To determine if a vent connects to an actively used tunnel, a day mage can cast the ritual [Clear Lens of the Eternal River](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clear_Lens_of_the_Eternal_River.md) to learn factual information about the immediate area connected to the shaft. This will allow Imperial forces to decide if the tunnel is worth destroying. The tunnels themselves are deep underground and protected from direct assault or magical destruction that could be achieved through spring or winter rituals. This means the tunnels must be destroyed from inside.

Zakalwe has offered the *Spheres of Tarachi* to assist the Empire in this matter. Each sphere is a crystalline cage that can bind and deliver powerful magical forces in battle. Normally they can be lofted into enemy ranks to burst open, [hurling soldiers aside](../../mixed/magic/spells/Repel.md), and [pinning advancing troops](../../mixed/magic/spells/Paralysis.md). The *Master Strategist* has adapted these alembics so that each orb can contain the magical power from rituals of the other realms. This allows ritualists to imbue the object in a manner similar to enchanting an implement, via the [Rising Roots that Rend Stone](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rising_Roots_that_Rend_Stone.md) or the [Inevitable Collapse into Ruin](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Inevitable_Collapse_into_Ruin.md) rituals. If dropped down one of the tunnel shafts, the crystalline globe will shatter when it reaches the bottom, loosing the destructive energies and collapsing the tunnel.

The Grendel opportunity will allow them to treat Icarion as coastal for one year if they are not stopped. Over time the coastal forces of the Bay will slowly eat away at the makeshift docks, wearing down the quays and washing out the tunnel entrances. After a year the temporary landings will be lost, unless a significant [engineering project](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) is undertaken to secure the docks against the action of the tides. Each of the four active tunnels that is destroyed will impede the Grendel efforts, reducing the time the barbarians can maintain their coastal access by a single season. If all four tunnels can be located and dealt with, then the threat to Icarion from the sea will be completely stopped.

### Objective: Deflect the *Quintian Spears*

- **Zarides is a representative of The Plenum supporting the Grendel in Mareave**
- **Mercenaries under her command are ready to assist the *Simoon* navy in a coastal assault**
- **The prefectoi can be convinced, or most likely coerced, to call off support for the navy this season**

Trichas Manouelites Zarides is a representative of the ruling Plenum council in Asavea, and one of many prefectoi assigned to assist efforts in furthering the war against the Empire. Zarides is in frequent contact with her seniors back in Asavea, and is involved in securing contracts for the Guardians of the Black Bull and Adherents of the Red God mercenaries. With the flick of a quill, exchange of coin, and [magical missive](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md) she has the authority to arrange contracts, make key tactical decisions, and determine how best to further Asavea's ultimate aims in its long-range proxy war through their Grendel allies. Even though she has considerable influence with the Asavean mercenaries she can order and contract, there are limits to what she can do, and indeed what she is bound to do by law or oaths to the Plenum.

Targeting the prefectoi during this opportunity offers a chance to make a small intervention and change the direction of the next season's campaigning. While Zarides is a loyal subject of the Plenum, she is no hero. A career of sending mercenaries to their deaths to achieve greater goals doesn't always steel one's own mind against the horrors of battle and bloodshed. If she can be located on the field, separated from her bodyguards, and then in some way threatened, she could be forced to change the orders she has given to the *Quintian Spears*, a mercenary force operating at her behest, to get them to stand down.

If the Empire can locate Zarides and convince her that her life is on the line, they could extract a promise to stand down the Quintian Spears. She might not be brave, but her word is good. If the Empire can secure a promise from her to call her mercenaries off, then it will reduce the strength of the Simoon by one thousand in the coming season until the Grendel can replace the missing mercenaries. If Zarides is killed, then it will not be possible to stop the Quintian Spears from supporting the Grendel this season.
