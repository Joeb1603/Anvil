# Willow and rain

*[Image: The willows weep in Therunin, for the cost that has been paid and the cost that is yet to come.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:TheruninLake.jpg)*

## Beauty Once

There was beauty in [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md), once. Spring would bring delicate marsh flowers, see blossoms kindle and spread like wildfire through the trees, waken the raucous beasts of the fens and the forests to full-throated life. Summer would herald long, warm days when jewelled dragonflies darted here-and-there across the waters, fruits and berries ripening, the great beetles of the deep woods scuttling about their inscrutable business under blue skies. The [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) have changed that, perhaps forever.

The Tarn valley is marked by [poison](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#poisoned) that taints the waters and threatens to kill anyone who drinks, or is unlucky enough to let too much of it sink into their skin. They have ravaged [Eastring](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#eastring) and [East Ashes](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#east-ashes) with war, slaughter, and terror. They have come to [Peakedge Stead](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#peakedge-stead) with burning brands and a thirst for destruction that has barely been seen since they swept through Zenith as a burning plague devouring everything wholesome or beautiful in their path.

And then, their dominion secured, they chose to destroy what they had taken. Spite, it might seem, motivated by little more than a desire to deny Therunin to the Navarr and drown it in the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) of Tharunind. Or perhaps not just spite? There are strategic reasons why rendering Therunin impassable would help the Druj in their war with the Empire, just as the Empire has benefited from closing passes into Jotun lands along the western Empire. There are the [heralds](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#heralds) of the [Green Mother](../eternals/Yaw-nagrah.md) who walk alongside the [Tainted Basilisk](../barbarians/Druj_armies.md#tainted-basilisk) - that wicked [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) of the [Spring realm](../eternals/Realm.md#spring) has long desired to see the vallorn unleashed across the world. She offered the Empire aid in return for unleashing the vallorn of [Béantal Dol](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#béantal-dol), it stands to reason that the Druj might also gain her boons by releasing the vallorn into Therunin.

Yet spite is often enough, with the Druj. They break what is precious not to hear the sound it makes but to cause others to fear what they might do to their own precious things. In Therunin, the vallorn rouses and as Spring fades toward twilight the first signs of that awakening are seen across the territory. The [miasma](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md#vallorn-miasma) rolls out of the southern woodlands into the rest of Therunin - for there is no part of this territory that does not edge along the forest claimed by the Doom of Terunael. With the miasma comes the [vallornspawn husks](../world/Vallornspawn_husk.md) but there are also significant numbers of [ettercaps](../world/Ettercap.md) - more than have been seen before in one place. The insect-creatures are especially worrisome, for they evidence a cunning hitherto unseen among them. They practice caution, where the husks simply charge anyone who intrudes upon their domain. They use crude tools and salvaged armour. There is speculation that they are actively raiding the [steadings](../nations/navarr/Steading.md) the Druj have conquered, ignoring the slaughtered Navarr in favour of looting their weapons of war.

The miasma is no respecter of borders, however. The whisper of the vallorn’s verdant fog pooling in northern [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md) are now confirmed, along with a few clashes between [sentinels](../nations/urizen/Sentinel.md) and vallornspawn in the foothills of [Peregro](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md#peregro) and [Altis](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md#altis). The miasma settles in the lowlands, mind, meaning that many spires are simply too elevated to be threatened by it. Not yet, at any rate. But this is little consolation to those in the lower valleys of Morrow, or who need to travel at ground level for whatever reason. The problem is contained for the moment, but what bulwark can hope to hold back poisoned air?

The miasma, terrible though it is, is only the tip of the spear. Abominations have risen in [Sweetglades](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#sweetglades) and [Greenheart](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#greenheart), and begun to press out into regions long since claimed from the vallorn. The rising power of the vallorn begins to flow through the roots, the waters, the beasts and insects, of the forested marshes. Things begin to shift, near the border with the Bane of Tharunind. It’s slow and fragile at first, but it grows in power as it rises. It spreads most slowly in the Lower Tarn Valley, but even the poison there is not enough to turn back its tendrils. There is little news from the east of the territory, but in Peakedge Song and the Upper Tarn Valley the vallorn is certainly advancing, seemingly inexorably.

## Raincallers Song

There is an oasis of safety, however. The gentle power of *[Lord Rain](../eternals/Ossegrahn.md)* continues to ward [Peakedge Stead](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#peakedge-stead). A wall of [impenetrable briars](To_hold_death-s_hand_in_mine.md#rainfall) surrounds the hospital and the [House of Healing](../../mixed/titles/Hand_of_the_Healers.md#houses-of-healing). The [guardians](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#guardians) of *Beckburn’s* realm - quadrupedal creatures of living wood that spread flowers wherever they walk - continue to sing their bubbling song of life and protection. The people seeking sanctuary there are safe, for now. Yet that safety comes with a price.

During the Spring Equinox, the [Archmage of Spring](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Spring.md) met with *Father Tarn* in [parley](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#parley). During that meeting, the eternal agreed to expend his might in preserving Peakedge Stead for a second season, to keep alive those who sheltered there and prevent both the Druj and the vallorn from devouring it. Yet *Coomarta of the Rains* believes he was [very clear](../winds-of-fortune/Speak_to_me.md#regarding-peakedge-stead) what his aid would cost. That the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) would grant the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) the declaration of [Remorse](../../mixed/government/conclave/Interdiction.md#remorse) and they would use it to remove the rituals [Rivers Run Red](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_Run_Red.md) and [Mountain Remembers Its Youth](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mountain_Remembers_Its_Youth.md) from [Imperial lore](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md).

On the understanding this could not be achieved in Spring, but could be done by the end of the Summer Solstice, *Willowbraid* exerted himself to cause the wall of life around Peakedge Stead to continue for another three months. If it were achieved, then the eternal would do as he promised and not only protect Peakedge Stead from the Druj and the vallorn, but enfold [Thornsong House](../../mixed/titles/Thornsinger.md#thornsong-house) as well so it would be safe once its wards failed. He would not only create a place of sanctuary, but a place that might be used as a stronghold to fight the vallorn when the time comes.

Yet he was also clear what the consequences would be if his trust were not repaid. If the Archmage cannot follow through, if the Senate and the Conclave do not fulfil their end of this bargain, then Ossegrahn will withdraw from the Empire. Not just from Therunin - from the *entire* Empire. He will step away; he will not agree to future [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) until the Empire can repair their fundamental breach of trust. Peakedge Stead will be destroyed by the Druj who remain in Therunin, or subsumed by the vallorn. Those sheltering there will die, and the Houses of Healing will be lost. When the wards protecting Thornsong House fall, the college of the apothecary arts will doubtless meet the same fate. The healers and peace-makers of the Empire both will lose a potent ally, and there are few other eternals whose interests coincide with those who seek to preserve life.

Given there are no Imperial armies in Therunin, it is unlikely any force could get into position in time to save Peakedge Stead and the Houses of Healing, and the people sheltering there. Ossegrahn will do what he can to ease their passing, but he is already at the limits of his power and without the show of faith from the Empire he will have no choice but to walk away.

The mood in Therunin is beyond bleak. There are no Imperial armies in Therunin, none to reclaim the territory from the Druj and none to fight the vallorn. Full two-thirds of the [stridings](../nations/navarr/Striding.md) returned home, believing that they were coming back to begin the great war against the vallorn. But those who returned to Therunin endured defeat at the hands of the Druj and now they face destruction at the hands of the vallorn.

Barely a year ago, Gaelen Embercast judged the vallorn to be the greatest spiritual threat to the Empire, [a statement the Assembly of Nine backed with a greater majority](../synod-judgements/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-173). Only last season, Estella Lucati's sermon called on the Ambitious to dare do all and imagine a world in which the vallorn were defeated. The General Assembly [overwhelmingly rejected that vision](../synod-judgements/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-9) and said that the Druj must be defeated first. But there is none here to do that either...

Now the Navarr Assembly have called on the people of Therunin to do everything possible to harry the Druj, to resist the enemy in any way they can. There are still a handful of steadings in the territory - the people that came home to fight the vallorn - but there simply aren't enough people left to make a difference. They [sacrificed everything](To_hold_death-s_hand_in_mine.md) to help their people escape the wave of orcs breaking across the Tarn Valley and Peakedge Stead. They have precious little left to give.

The vallorn is on the move, and unless a major military force moves into Therunin after the Summer Solstice, then it will expand to claim at least one region and maybe more. There may be a chance to stop it becoming another [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md) but decades of striving and sacrifice are set to be undone in months.

Nothing stands between the folk still in Therunin and [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md) orcs. Nothing stands between them and the vallorn. Only *Lord Rain*, and only in Peakedge Song, and only for a little while longer.

It is enough to make anyone despair.

## Game Information

### Therunin and Lord Rain

- **The Druj completely control Therunin, except for the two regions of vallorn, Greenheart and Sweetglades**
- **Peakedge Song still stands but if a way is not found to save it this season, then it will be destroyed**
- **The Empire has comparatively little information about Therunin as there are no Imperial forces there**

The Druj still control Therunin, but it is being contested by the vallorn. It may be worth reviewing the information presented last season about the situation in Therunin, as recounted in the game information section of the **[To hold death's hand in mine](To_hold_death-s_hand_in_mine.md#regarding-therunin)** wind of war.

[Peakedge Stead](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#peakedge-song) and the [Houses of Healing](../../mixed/titles/Hand_of_the_Healers.md#houses-of-healing) are still being protected by [Ossegrahn](../eternals/Ossegrahn.md) but by the end of the Summer Solstice his patience and power alike will have run out. If the Conclave has not removed [Rivers Run Red](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_Run_Red.md) and [Mountain Remembers Its Youth](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mountain_Remembers_Its_Youth.md) from Imperial lore by the end of the summit, Ossegrahn will be forced to withdraw his protection and Peakedge Stead (and [Thornsong House](../../mixed/titles/Thornsinger.md)) will be destroyed, and the people taking refuge there killed. Furthermore, Ossegrahn will turn away from the Empire, meaning he will no longer respond positively to plenipotentiary nor offer new boons to Imperial citizens.

The only other way to save the steading would be to move enough Imperial armies into Therunin to take the region. But that would bring its own cost, as the advancing Imperial force would meet the expanding vallorn head-on.

The Empire’s actual awareness of what is going on in the territory is limited by its lack of presence there. What reliable information is coming out of the [conquered territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#conquered-territories) is confused and unreliable and mostly focuses on what is happening with the vallorn.

### Vallorn

- **The vallorn is on the march, advancing on the Upper Tarn Valley, with a force strength of 25,000**
- **Its focus will shift to attack any army that comes into the territory**

What is certain is that the vallorn is expanding in Therunin. By the start of the Autumn Equinox, if nothing else changes, it will have claimed the Upper Tarn Valley. Expanding in that way will dissipate some of the vallorn's strength, so it will be weaker the following season when it attacks the next region.

There is some speculation that the Druj forces are, or have been, up to something in Eastring and East Ashes designed to hold the vallorn back, or at least preserve some sort of presence in the east. They have had uncontested control of the [Docks at Feverwater](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#the-docks-at-feverwater) and [Mudlark’s Haven](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#mudlarks-haven) for nearly a year and if they *are* doing something it is probably there.

## Conjunctions

### Scent of Madness

- **A group of Druj are trying to escape Greenheart**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- **The *Spear of the Seven* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for defeating the Druj**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

Last season, the Druj managed to unleash the vallorn in Therunin. Now, several groups of the Druj are moving within areas controlled by the vallorn, on the hunt for [vital honey](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#vital-honey), chaos fruit, and other strange products created from the influence of the [Primal Forest](../eternals/Realm.md#spring). One such group has been tracked by the thorns of the Firstblade steading, who remain in the territory, and who have called on the [Spear of the Seven](../../mixed/titles/Spear_of_the_Seven.md) to help.

Unfortunately, there are vallornspawn between where the Sentinel Gate will open and the Druj. If the Druj, and the vallornspawn before them, can be defeated, then the resources they carry can be claimed by the Spear of the Seven, **Corwyn Heartsbane**, for whatever they wish to achieve.

### A Veil Hides

- **A group of Druj are attempting to cede the region of Eastring to Arhallogen**
- **The Druj are accompanied by several heralds of Arhallogen**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- **The *Champion of Wisdom* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for defeating the Druj and the heralds of Arhallogen**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

This season, the Druj armies withdrew from Therunin, leaving the raw, unleashed force of the vallorn to run wild in the territory. There are pockets of Druj remaining in the territory; administrators and guards for the most part, but also treasure seekers searching for Spring treasures in the vallorn miasma, while others desperately seeking out any scrap that might show them where Thornsong House might be hiding.

A few groups have even grander plans. Deep within the forests of Eastring, a group of Druj have been working alongside children of the [Queen-with-a-thousand-young](../eternals/Arhallogen.md) to create a place dedicated to the eternal, something akin to the Druj equivalent of a [fane](../eternals/Fane.md) - a place of power that the eternal's servants can use to come and go freely from the world.

If the Druj and the heralds of Arhallogen are not stopped, then they will create a profane site. Over the coming season, Arhallogan will spread its influence and lay claim to the region. By the start of the Autumn Equinox 387YE, the region of [Eastring](../eternals/Arhallogen.md#eastring) will belong to Arhallogen, and it is expected that the Druj will have full access to it – including the [Thimble](../../mixed/titles/Thimble.md). It is the responsibility of the [Champion of Wisdom](../../mixed/titles/Champion_of_Wisdom.md), **Valens Leos**, to prevent this from happening.
