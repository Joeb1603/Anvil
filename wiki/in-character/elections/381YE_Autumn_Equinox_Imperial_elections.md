The [Singing Caves](../../mixed/titles/Singing_Caves.md) in [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) has been [conquered by the Empire](../winds-of-war/381YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#times-like-these-28mournwold29). If it is made Imperial, it will be auctioned during the Winter Solstice for a three-season term; if it is made National it will likewise be appointed by the appropriate nation for a short term. As with all other mithril seats, the Singing Caves will be auctioned or appointed again in Autumn.
