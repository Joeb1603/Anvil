# 381YE Spring Equinox Imperial elections

This page was updated throughout the event as elections and vacant positions occurred (i.e. opportunities!).
With the event over the last few position title holders were tidied up and then we transferred this information over to the rolling historical record that can be found  [here](../../mixed/titles/Current_Imperial_titles.md)

Please note the following titles have either lapsed or been vacated:

- The Legacy (Currently in Grendel hands)
- Senator for Spiral (The Territory of Spiral is no longer in Imperial hands.)
- Sheriff of Overton (Sinecure destroyed during the fall of Overton)
- Civilian Commissioners (Motion to create the titles [abrogated by Senate.](../senate-motions/Abrogate_Civilian_Commissioners.md))
- [Custodian of the Imperial War Memorial](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Imperial_War_Memorial.md) (Title resigned by the holder)
- Dean of the Cathedral of Courage (Title resigned by the holder)
