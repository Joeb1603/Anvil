# Appoint Jarmish ambassador

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint an ambassador to Jarm  
>  
> *Proposed by Kallavesa, seconded by Morrow.*

### Overview

- This motion was to choose a citizen to appoint to the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Ambassador to Jarm](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Jarm.md).

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Outcome

- The incumbent, Oswi Twice-burned, was elected.
