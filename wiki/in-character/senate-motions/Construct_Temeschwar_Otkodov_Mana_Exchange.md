# Construct Temeschwar Otkodov Mana Exchange

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Temeschwar Otkodov Mana Exchange in Hanuri, Temeschwar at a cost of 10 white granite, 10 mithril, and 40 crowns in labour costs. The ministry will be overseen by the Keeper of the Tome appointed by tally of the votes of business owners of the League. They will serve with tenure and can not be revoked as a position in the bourse.
> >
> > Elsha Bertolf, Chair of the Wolf

## Overview

- Creates a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), as detailed in the **[Don't blame me](../winds-of-fortune/Don-t_blame_me.md#exploit-the-thule)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Chair of the Wolf](../../mixed/titles/Chair_of_the_Wolf.md)

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per wind of fortune
- 10 wains of white granite
- 10 wains of mithril
- 5 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril, 5 thrones and 10 wains of white granite provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- As a position appointed by Tally of the Votes the Keeper of the Tome [cannot serve with tenure](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md#selection). The Speaker for the Senate will raise an administrative motion at the first session to amend the position to serve for a year.
- Now that construction has been completed the position of [Keeper of the Tome](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Tome.md) will be appointed by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of the business owners in the League for the first time at the Summer Solstice 385YE assuming the administrative motion passes.
