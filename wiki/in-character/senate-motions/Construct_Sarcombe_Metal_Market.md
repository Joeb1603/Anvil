# Construct Sarcombe Metal Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission a ministry in Sarcombe, Mournwold, to allow the purchase of metals, called the Sarcombe Metal Market. A ministry to create the Marcher position of Keeper of Metal, to be appointed annually by agreement of the Marcher Senators, revocable by the Marcher assembly. Self-funded.  
> *Proposed by Mournwold, seconded by Bregasland*

## Overview

- Commission a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md).
- Passed in principle.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md#costs).

## Progress

- 20 wains of mithril and 5 Thrones provided by Landon of Longridge after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- Created an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), *[Master of Sarcombe Metal Market](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Sarcombe_Metal_Market.md)*.
- The ministry will help to secure the economic stability of the [market town](../nations/the-marches/Market_town.md) of Sarcombe.
