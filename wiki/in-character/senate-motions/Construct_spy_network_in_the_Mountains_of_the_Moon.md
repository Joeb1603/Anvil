# Construct spy network in the Mountains of the Moon

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a spy network in the area known as the Valley of the Moon, known as the Valley of Silence in Axos.  
>  
> *Proposed by Casinea, seconded by Redoubt. Passed.*

### Overview

- To construct a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) to the east of [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) known as the "[Mountains of the Moon](../foreign-nations/Mountains_of_the_Moon.md)".
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 381YE

### Cost

- [Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md#costs) apply.
- Materials: 10 weirwood.
- Time: 3 months to construct.
- Labour: 2 crowns per wain
- Upkeep: 2 Thrones per season.

### Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood and 20 crowns provided by Nira of Cantiarch's Hold after Winter Solstice 381YE.
- Due to the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) and [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) occupation of [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) and [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md) no progress was made on this [commission](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) after the Winter Solstice 381YE.
- With the conquest of [Ossuary](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#ossuary) in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md), the Empire now has access to the target territory again and if nothing changes work will be completed shortly before the Summer Solstice.

### Campaign Outcome

- A spy network will be constructed in the territory known as the Mountains of the Moon.
- Work cannot begin on this commission until the Empire [controls a region adjacent to the territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md#limitations). These regions include [Lustri](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md#lustri), [Occursion](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md#occursion), [Ossuary](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#ossuary), or [Apstrus](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#apstrus).
- The spy network was completed shortly before the beginning of the Summer Solstice 382YE.
- Following Summer 382YE, a significant force of scouts supported the spy network, and uncovered extensive details of the [Mountains of the Moon](../foreign-nations/Mountains_of_the_Moon.md) and some information about the [Tsark](../foreign-nations/Tsark.md) folk who live there.

## Constitution

- The Mountains of the Moon are an independent territory - they are not part of [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md).
- There may be problems with this construction given the Empire currently has no regions or indeed territories bordering the Mountains of the Moon.
