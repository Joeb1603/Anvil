# Appoint Throne I

> *In-character text:*
>
> That the Senate wishes to elect a citizen onto the Throne.  
>  
> *Proposed by Sermersuaq, Seconded by Hercynia.*

### Overview

- This motion is to choose a new [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md).
- The motion passed in principle, but no candidate received sufficient votes to take the Throne.

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Nominees

- The [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) Syn Nighthaven
- The [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [Earl](../nations/dawn/Earl.md) Beaumont de Rondell
- The [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) Vanhe Korppi
- The [Imperial orc](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) Irontide Bruk
- The [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [icewalker](../../mixed/magic/lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#icewalkers) Atte Arrow-Tongue Metsastajason

### Campaign Outcome
