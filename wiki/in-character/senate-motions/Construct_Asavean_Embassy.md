# Construct Asavean Embassy

> *In-character text:*
>
> Construct an embassy for the Asavean Empire in Sarvos.  
>  
> Proposed by Sarvos, seconded by Temeschwar.

### Overview

- Construct an [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md) for the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md).
- Passed at 35 Thrones

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Cost

- 20 White Granite
- 20 Weirwood
- Special: Note that this is not the [standard cost](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md#costs) for an embassy commission.

### Progress

- 20 wains of white granite and weirwood supplied by Mirislav, Senator for Sarvos after Spring Equinox 378.

### Campaign Outcome

- The [embassy](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#asavean-embassy) will be a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) that allows the Imperial citizen appointed to operate it to purchase [Cerulean Mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), [Orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), [Iridescent Gloaming](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), [Tempest Jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and [True Vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md).
- The title of [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) to the Asavean Archipelago was automatically created.

### Voting

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| At 35 Thrones | 18 | 6 | Passed |
| At 40 Thrones | 9 | 15 | Failed |
