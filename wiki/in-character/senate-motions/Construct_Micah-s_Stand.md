# Construct Micah's Stand

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct Micah's Stand wayhouse in High Chalcis, Reikos as described in the civil service records. Title to be appointed by unanimous decision of Navarr senators. Cost: 12ww, 12wg, 72cr. Self funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Hercynia, Seconded: Miaren

## Overview

- Constructs a [wayhouse](../nations/navarr/Wayhouse.md) in High Chalcis, Reikos as per the **[Wasp nest](../winds-of-fortune/Wasp_nest.md#stand-for-a-dream)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 weirwood
- 12 white granite
- 72 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 8 thrones 5 crowns and 12 wains of white granite provided by Sister Micah (who was Earna) of the Navigators of Virtue after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 12 wains of weirwood provided by Sorcha Embracing Fold after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Fintan Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete, Micah's Stand has had the following effects:

- Increases the production of the [Benefactor of High Chalcis](../../mixed/titles/Benefactor_of_High_Chalcis.md) to eight of each herb.
- Increases the great work production of the Gardens of High Chalcis by one Imperial roseweald
- The [Keeper of the Stand](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Stand.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of Navarr senators for the first time at the Spring Equinox 387YE
