# Repair Blood Red River Museum

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > That the senate authorise the repair and rebuilding of the Blood Red River Museum in Tassato. This will also create the title "Curator of the Blood Red River." This will be a tenured position, a League citizen, selected by unanimous vote of the League senators. Self-funded.
> >
> > Proposed Tassato, Seconded Temeschwar

## Overview

- Commissions repairs of the Blood Red River Museum in [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md)
- Further details on the Blood Red River Museum, and what the repairs mean, can be found [here](../winds-of-fortune/All_in_red.md#fire-in-the-museum)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 15 wains of white granite
- 15 wains of weirwood
- 150 crowns in labour and art costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 2 crowns provided by Ergot Di Cevia after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Cpt. Hendrick van Hoven after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 throne provided by Rafael Barossa d'Apulian after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Harwyn d'Apulian after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 2 rings in small donations provided after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 17 thrones 1 crown 18 rings, 15 wains of weirwood and 15 wains of white granite provided by Astrid Fjellrevening Rezia di Tassato after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- The Blood Red River Museum will remain a great work of Pride.
- The Curator of the Blood Red River cannot be appointed by unanimous vote of the League senators, as it [provides votes in the Synod](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#synod-votes). The Constitutional Court prepared an [administrative motion to clarify this](../senate-sessions/384YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-curator-of-the-blood-red-river) which was passed by the Imperial Senate.
- Work has been completed on the repairs and the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Curator of the Blood Red River](../../mixed/titles/Curator_of_the_Blood_Red_River.md) will be appointed at the Summer Solstice 385YE
