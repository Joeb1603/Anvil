# Construct extension to the Imperial Menagerie

> *In-character text:*
>
> To expand the Imperial menagerie at the behest of the Young Heroes of the Empire, to have enclosures for more exotic creatures.  
> *Proposed by Volodmartz, seconded by Karov.*

## Overview

- Increases the size of the [Imperial Menagerie](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Imperial_Menagerie.md#imperial-menagerie) as part of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented [during the Spring Equinox](../winds-of-fortune/Fly_high.md#opportunity-imperial-menagerie).
- Passed

## Date

Summer 382YE

## Cost

- This commission is part of an [opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/Fly_high.md#opportunity-imperial-menagerie).
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 5 Thrones

## Progress

- 20 wains of weirwood and 5 Thrones provided by Kay Griffinsbain after the Summer Solstice 382YE.

## Campaign

- The [Keeper of the Imperial Menagerie](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Imperial_Menagerie.md) receives an additional income of 16 crowns each season.
- Opportunities to expand the collection of the menagerie are now available.
