# Invest in Beoraidh Docks

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To invest in the docks of Beoraidh (commission an edifice) so that the fleet owners of the Empire can engage in Prosperous trade with the port and raise 10 thrones a season in tariffs and tax. Seeking a disbursement of funds for 75 thrones
> >
> > Proposed: Bloodcrow Rasp, Senator for Skarsind, Seconded: Rane Shardbearer, Senator for Kallavesa

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Beoraidh, Mareave
- Passed at 75 thrones
- 75 thrones disbursed to Senator for Skarsind
- More information can be found in the **[Each ages a lens](../winds-of-fortune/Each_age_a_lens.md#investment-of-sea)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 30 wains of weirwood
- 90 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 6 thrones 1 crown provided by Pathfinder Gaddak after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 5 thrones provided by Zaccaria di Sarvos after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 20 wains of weirwood provided by Bloodcrow Rasp after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 10 wains of weirwood provided by Karrak after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the docks will have the following effects:

- Opens the port of Beoraidh to Imperial fleet captains
- Raises taxation in Mareave by three thrones
- Raises taxes from tariffs by seven thrones
