# Create title of Conclave Liaison

> *In-character text:*
>
> Create an liaison from the Conclave with the power to address the Senate

### Overview

- Passed unanimously

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Campaign Outcome

A new [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), the [Conclave Liaison to the Senate](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md) has been created. The title will be appointed by declaration of the Conclave and has the power of address in the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).

- The title was [amended](Amend_powers_of_the_Conclave_Liaison.md) at the Autumn Equinox 378YE summit, and the title changed to be [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md).
