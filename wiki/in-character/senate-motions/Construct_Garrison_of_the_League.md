# Construct Garrison of the League

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission a business sinecure, The Garrison of the League, in Hanuri in Temeschwar. Appointed by League Generals annually.
> >
> > Announced by Elsha Bertolf, Chair of the Wolf

## Overview

- Constructs a sinecure in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md)
- Announcement by the [Chair of the Wolf](../../mixed/titles/Chair_of_the_Wolf.md)

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply (minimum)
- 10 wains of white granite
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 4 wains of white granite provided by Erikas Van Temeshwar after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 6 wains of white granite provided by Erikas Van Temeshwar after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Now completed, the position of [Quartermaster of the Hanuri Garrison](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_of_the_Hanuri_Garrison.md) will be elected for the first time at the Spring Equinox 386YE.
