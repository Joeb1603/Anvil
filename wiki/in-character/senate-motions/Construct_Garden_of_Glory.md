# Construct Garden of Glory

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build the Garden of Glory a liao sinecure in Therunin and the title of Keeper of the Garden. This is taking advantage of an opportunity from Spring 382YE to build at reduced cost of 8 weirwood and 16 crowns labour. This will produce 6 liao and no Synod votes. To be assigned by Navarr National Assembly, revoked by Navarr National Assembly. This is a self funded motion.
> >
> > Proposed: Tassato, Seconded: Astolat

## Overview

- Creates a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md), as detailed in the **[Long walk home](../winds-of-fortune/Long_walk_home.md#liao-garden)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per wind of fortune
- 8 wains of weirwood
- 2 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones and 8 wains of weirwood provided by Eadric Fjellrevening de Rondell Rezia di Tassato after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the position of [Keeper of the Garden](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Garden.md) will be available to appoint by the National Assembly of Navarr.
