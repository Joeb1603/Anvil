# Construct Challenger in Waiting

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*

> > Challenger in Waiting liao sinecure without Tenure 10 white granite. Votes in Synod, congregation based in Weirwater. Appointed by National Assembly, with expectation that they will appoint tourneys. Position to be holdable once per lifetime (as per High Bard). Regalia to be provided proudly.
> >
> > Announced by Valentin Ivarovich Orzel, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

- Constructs a sinecure in [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of white granite
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Valentin Ivarovich Orzel after Summer Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- With construction complete the [Challenger in Waiting](../../mixed/titles/Challenger_in_Waiting.md) was first elected during the Autumn Equinox 385YE
- Further [scrutiny](Scrutiny.md) will take place on this announcement
