# Ratify Liberty Pact

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion to ratify the international Liberty Pact agreement.  
> *Proposed by Necropolis, seconded by Conscience of the Senate.*

## Overview

- A [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) was prepared by Ianthe Ravenswatch and Eska Crowspeaker with reference to the [trade summit to oppose slavery](../winds-of-fortune/Rope_and_iron.md).
- The treaty required a [constitutional vote](Senate_motion.md#constitutional-vote) due to expansion of the definition of a person in Imperial law.
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Campaign Outcome

- The treaty has been ratified.
- A copy of the text is required for the Constitutional Court to complete scrutiny.
- The treaty applies [trade sanctions](../../mixed/government/senate/Tariffs.md#sanctions) to several nations.

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The empress is moved. The world is moving. And we will move to a vote.
> >
> > Speaker John of Meade

- The definition of a person has been updated to include hylje and daeva.
- The definition of "slavery" in Imperial law is already at least as strong as that defined in the Liberty Pact and so has not been changed.
- The [Liberty Pact](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Liberty.md#liberty-pact) was [amended](../senate-sessions/384YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-liberty-pact) during the Summer Solstice 384YE
