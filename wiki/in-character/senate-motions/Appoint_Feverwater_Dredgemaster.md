# Appoint Feverwater Dredgemaster

> *In-character text:*
>
> Assign a person to the position of Dredgemaster of Lake Feverwater, to be nominated by Navarr senators.  
>  
> *Procedural Motion.*

### Overview

- This motion is a reference to an earlier Senate motion to [permit the dredging of Feverwater](Permit_dredging_of_Feverwater.md).
- The earlier motion created a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Feverwater](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#the-docks-at-feverwater) lake in [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md).

### Date

- Spring 378YE

### Nominees

- There was only one nominee,

### Campaign Outcome

Corey Brackensong was appointed to the position of [Dredgemaster of Feverwater](../../mixed/titles/Dredgemaster_of_Feverwater.md) by unanimous decision of the [Navarri](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) senators.

### Constitution

The formal process for electing a national position is for Merlot, the master of elections, to hold an election involving the relevant nation's senators.
