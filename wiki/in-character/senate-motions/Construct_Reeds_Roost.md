# Construct Reeds Roost

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a sinecure 'Reed's Roost' in Bildewold Bregasland, Self-funded. Sinecure to produce mana per season. Sinecure to create the national position 'Roost's Reeve' to be appointed by the marcher senators annually.
> >
> > Proposed: Bregasland, Seconded: Upwold

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) that produces mana crystals in North Fens, [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 mithril
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 3 thrones 6 crowns provided by Willem Tarne after Summer Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the position of [Roost's Reeve](../../mixed/titles/Roost-s_Reeve.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the senators of the Marches.
