# Construct Temeschwar Superlative Citizen Sinecure

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I announce that I am constructing the Temeschwar Superlative Citizen Sinecure in Hanuri, Temeschwar. This distillery is a money sinecure costing 10 wains of weirwood and 3 thrones 6 crowns in labour. This will create the title *the Jewel of Anvil*, auctioned annually on the Imperial Bourse. The title-holder will receive 1 throne, 2 crowns, and 16 rings a season from the sale of its goods. They are responsible for using their income to perform and promote acts of Pride so brazen as to inspire even the eternal Barien to admit the Way achieves his ends better than any tools of the realms. Paid for by Giuseppe's ever-increasing debts
> >
> > Announced by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar, Master of Rings

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Hanuri, Temeschwar
- Announcement by the [Master of Rings](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Rings.md)

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns provided by Giuseppe Sanguineo von Temeschwar after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 10 wains of weirwood provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Jewel of Anvil](../../mixed/titles/Jewel_of_Anvil.md) will be available for auction for the first time at the Spring Equinox 388YE
