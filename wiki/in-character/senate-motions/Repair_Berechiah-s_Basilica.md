# Repair Berechiah's Basilica

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission repairs to Berechiah's Basilica in Bastion to act as a rallying point for the vigilant against foreign threats as per the civil service report from before Winter Solstice 385YE. The construction costs will be covered by the Custodes Clavium and the wains (at least 10 white granite and 5 weirwood) will be sought from virtuous benefactors.
> >
> > Proposed: Bastion, Seconded: Karsk

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Bastion](../nations/highguard/Bastion.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Knocking on every door](../winds-of-fortune/Knocking_on_every_door.md#the-basilica-in-bastion)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 10 wains of white granite
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 45 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 5 wains of white granite provided by Cyrus of Athaliah's Handiwork after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 5 thrones 5 crowns provided by Custodes Clavium after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 5 wains of white granite provided by Cyrus of Athaliah's Handiwork after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 wain of weirwood provided by Merari Navigators of Virtue after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 4 wains of weirwood provided by Merari Navigators of Virtue after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, a title will be created to be appointed by the Vigilance Assembly
