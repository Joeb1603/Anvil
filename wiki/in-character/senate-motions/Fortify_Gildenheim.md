# Fortify Gildenheim

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To fortify Gildenheim in Skarsind with a rank one fortification and the runic wards offered by the Tamazi.
> >
> > Announced by Groza, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Constructs a [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in Gildermark, [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) as per the **[Standing in the sun](../winds-of-fortune/Standing_in_the_sun.md#warded-walls)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)
- This motion benefited from the [mandate](../synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-5) enacted by **Viviane Barossa de Coeurdefer**

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 48 white granite
- 16 mithril
- 240 crowns (paid in full by the [Tamazi](../nations/imperial-orcs/Tamazi.md))
- One year to complete

## Progress

- 16 wains of mithril and 48 wains of white granite provided by Irontide Flax after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, Gildenheim will be protected with a rank one fortification of 3000 strength that takes only half the normal damage from magical attacks such as [Thunderous Tread of the Trees](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Thunderous_Tread_of_the_Trees.md).
