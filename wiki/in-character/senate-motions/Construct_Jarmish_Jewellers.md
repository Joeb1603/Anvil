# Construct Jarmish Jewellers

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct the Jarmish Star Jewellery exchange in Seren, Miaren, the liaison to be appointed by open auction on the Imperial Bourse. Costs are 24 crowns, 8 white granite, 4 mithril. To be self-funded.  
> *Proposed by Temeschwar, seconded by Kahraman.*

## Overview

- Commission a fortified premesis for the Kobarid family of Jarm to sell *glazamy neba* to Imperial customers.
- This [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) is the result of [an offer](../winds-of-fortune/Civil_tongue.md#jarmish-jewels) by the [Jarmish](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) Magician-Prince Matvei Kobarid.
- Passed in principle.
- This motion was [abrogated](Abrogate_Jarmish_jewelers.md) during the Winter Solstice 382YE.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/Civil_tongue.md#jarmish-jewels).
- 8 wains of White Granite.
- 4 wains of Mithril.
- 24 Crowns in labour costs.
- Takes three months to complete.

## Progress

- None

## Campaign Outcome

- This motion was [abrogated](Abrogate_Jarmish_jewelers.md) during the Winter Solstice 382YE.
- When complete, the [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) would have created an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) auctioned through the [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md).
- While the Jarmish would sell their jewellery directly to those who could afford it, the liaison would have the ability to purchase up to three pieces of their rare magical jewellery each season at a bargain price.
