# Fortify Miekarova

> *In-character text:*
>
> Permission to raise a fortress in the territory of Miekarova to defend against possible threats from the Northern Barbarians  
>  
> *Proposed by Volodmartz, seconded by Zenith*

### Overview

- The creation of a new [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), currently unnamed, in [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md).
- This motion was defeated.

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Costs

- Materials: 80 [white granite](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#white-granite) and 20 Thrones. ([Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#costs)).
- Time: 1 year to construct
- Upkeep: 10 Thrones per season.

### Progress

None

### Campaign Outcome

This would have allowed the construction of a rank one fortification in Miekarova.
