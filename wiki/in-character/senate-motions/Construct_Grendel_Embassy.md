# Construct Grendel Embassy

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To amend the title of Imperial Fleetmaster by constructing an embassy to the Grendel in Cargo. It costs 25 weirwood and 25 white granite.
> >
> > Proposed Redoubt, Seconded Kahraman

## Overview

- Commissions the construction of an [embassy](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#embassy) for the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) at [Cargo](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#cargo) in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md).
- Related to an opportunity presented in the **[Everything has a price](../winds-of-fortune/Everything_has_a_price.md#a-grand-edifice)** wind of fortune, but the Senate declined to build the large embassy outlined in that proposal.
- Passed at 0 Thrones

## Date

- Spring 384YE

## Cost

- Standard costs for an embassy apply
- 25 wains of [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md)
- 25 wains of [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md)
- 100 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 10 thrones, 25 wains of weirwood and 25 wains of white granite provided by Sejanus of the Scarlet Guard after Summer Solstice 384YE
- 2 thrones 4 crowns provided by Ehud after Summer Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the [Imperial Fleet Master](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Fleet_Master.md) will have access to the standard benefits of an [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md).
