# Construct extension to House of Barbs

*[Image: A bust of Bloody Trees Rhain Winters Vigil, who commissioned the House of Barbs while serving as senator for Hercynia .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Bust.jpeg)*

> *In-character text:*
>
> To expand and upgrade the Navarr national sinecure ‘the House of Barbs’. Self-funded.  
> *Proposed by Therunin, seconded by Holberg.*

## Overview

- Expands the [House of Barbs](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Barbs.md#the-house-of-barbs)
- An [earlier attempt](Improve_House_of_Barbs.md) in Spring 382YE failed to be commissioned as the Senate had already reached its limit of new construction.
- Passed.

## Date

- Summer 382YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).

## Progress

- 6 wains of white granite and 12 crowns provided by Meredith Nighthaven after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.

## Outcome

- When complete, the [Voice of Barbs](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Barbs.md) will receive additional [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) and votes in the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md).
