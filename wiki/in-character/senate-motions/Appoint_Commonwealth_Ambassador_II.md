# Appoint Commonwealth Ambassador II

> *In-character text:*
>
> Appointment of Cael of Splitroot as Ambassador to the Commonwealth*Proposed by Reikos, Seconded by Casinea*

### Overview

- This motion is to appoint a citizen to the position of [Ambassador to the Commonwealth](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Commonwealth.md) and to have the power to deal with the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) on behalf of the Empire.
- This election was announced at the last senate session of the Spring summit. Following Senatorial procedure on elections the election will occur at the first senate session of the Summer Solstice 381YE

### Date

- Spring 381YE
