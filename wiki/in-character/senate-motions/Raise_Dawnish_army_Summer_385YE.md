> > Proposed: Astolat, Seconded: Kallavesa

## Overview

- Raises a fourth Dawnish [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md)
- Passed at 250 thrones
- An administrative motion was raised by the [Speaker for the Senate](../../mixed/titles/Speaker_for_the_Senate.md) to amend this motion to also raise a Dawnish army which passed

## Date

- Summer 385YE

- 50 thrones
- One year to complete
