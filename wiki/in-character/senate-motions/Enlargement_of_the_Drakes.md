# Enlargement of the Drakes

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission the enlargement of the Imperial Marcher army the Drakes.  
> *Proposed by Mitwold, seconded by Mournwold.*

## Overview

- To enlarge the Drakes army, in line with the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented by the [recent proposal](../winds-of-fortune/Bills_or_bows.md#the-drakes).
- Passed at 0 Thrones

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- This enlargement uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/Bills_or_bows.md#the-drakes).
- 125 wains mithril or weirwood
- No money is required
- nine months to complete

## Progress

- 24 wains of mithril and 6 wains of weirwood provided by Abbott Thomas Arch after Winter Solstice 382YE
- 18 wains of weirwood provided by Abbott Thomas Arch after Summer Solstice 383YE
- 10 wains of mithril provided by Abbott Thomas Arch after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 14 wains of mithril provided by Abbott Thomas Arch after Winter Solstice 383YE
- 6 wains of mithril and 7 wains of weirwood provided by William Lowther after Winter Solstice 383YE
- 5 wains of mithril and 21 wains of weirwood provided by William Lowther after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 14 wains of mithril provided by Abbott Thomas Arch after Spring Equinox 384YE

## Campaign Outcome

- When completed the Drakes will become a [large](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#enlargement) army.
