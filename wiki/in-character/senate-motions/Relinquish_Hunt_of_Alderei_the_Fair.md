# Relinquish Hunt of Alderei the Fair

> *In-character text:*
>
> To relinquish the Imperial Bourse Seat ‘The Hunt of Alderei the Fair” that it may be later reassigned as a Varushkan National Position in a separate motion, at the request of the Varushkan people.
>  
> *Proposed by Miekarova, seconded by Karov.*

## Outcome

- This motion was to [relinquish](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#relinquishment) the [Hunt of Alderei the Fair](../../mixed/titles/Hunt_of_Alderei_the_Fair.md) which is currently an [Imperial Bourse Seat](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#imperial-weirwood-seats) following an [offer](../winds-of-fortune/The_honey_offering.md#proposal-buying-the-hunt) from an affiliation of Varushkans
- Failed

- Autumn 383YE
