# Appoint Quartermaster General

> *In-character text:*
>
> To elect a Quartermaster  
>  
> *Administrative Motion.*

### Overview

- This motion was to choose a citizen to appoint as [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md).
- The candidates were drawn from a short list voted on by the Military Council

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- Brendan Brackensong was elected.
