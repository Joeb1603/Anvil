# Expand Pride of Ikka's Tears

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To expand the mithril mine the Pride of Ikka's Tears, including additional investment in transport infrastructure to increase the outpost to 22 wains per season.
> >
> > Proposed: Sermersuaq, Seconded: Hahnmark

## Overview

- Expands the [Pride of Ikka's Tears](../../mixed/titles/Pride_of_Ikka-s_Tears.md) as detailed in the **[Write the lines](../winds-of-fortune/Write_the_lines.md#deep-pride)** wind of fortune.
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 384YE

## Cost

- As per the wind of fortune
- 210 thrones
- Three months to complete
- Increases upkeep by 6 thrones a season

## Progress

- 210 thrones provided by Atte Arrow-Tongue Metsastajason after Winter Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete the [Pride of Ikka's Tears](../../mixed/titles/Pride_of_Ikka-s_Tears.md) will produce 22 wains of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) each season
- This Senate Motion authorises both the expansion of the mine itself and the [infrastructure](../winds-of-fortune/Write_the_lines.md#bringing-it-home) required to bring the mithril safely south to Atalaq, which will increase the upkeep of the mine by 6 thrones.
