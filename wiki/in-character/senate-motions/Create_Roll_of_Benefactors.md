# Create Roll of Benefactors

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create a permanent roll of honour to list Imperial citizens who have made significant, prosperous contributions to the implementation of approved senate motions. Named “Benefactors of the Empire”, decided by the Assembly of Prosperity through statements of Principle passed with a greater majority. Roll to be posted each season.  
>  
> *Proposed by Weirwater, seconded by Hahnmark.*

## Overview

- Provides the Prosperity assembly with the ability to create a "[Roll of Benefactors](../../mixed/government/synod/Roll_of_Benefactors.md)" using [Statement of Principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md).
- Passed.

## Date

- Spring 382YE

## Cost

- None

## Campaign Outcome

- The Roll of Benefactors will be made public.
- The civil service does not anticipate an upkeep for this service. The expectation is that only a few names are likely to be added to the roll each season at most.
