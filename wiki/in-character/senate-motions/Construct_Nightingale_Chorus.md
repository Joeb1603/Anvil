# Construct Nightingale Chorus

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Nightingale Chorus wayhouse in Astolat. The wayhouse acts as a sinecure and increases tax in Astolat of one throne per season, and acts as a great work for Astolat, Weirwater, and Semmerholm. It boosts military units against the Vallorn. Election will be by Cat's Graces with the title of Conductor of the Nightingale Chorus.
> >
> > Announced by Mharen Splitroot, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [wayhouse](../../mixed/commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) in Brightway, Grovesyard, Astolat
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)
- More information can be found in the **[Ripple of echoes](../winds-of-fortune/Ripple_of_echoes.md#cant-stop-the-music)** wind of fortune.

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 25 wains of white granite
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 105 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Outcome

Once complete, the Nightingale Chorus will have the following effects:

- Increases investment in Astolat for military units engaged in the [Quest Against The Vallorn](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#quest-against-the-vallorn)
- Increases the taxation of Astolat by 1 throne per season
- Allows Navarr to take a new personal resource in [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md) for free the season it is completed
- Creates a new [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), the *Conductor of the Nightingale Chorus*
- Also increases investment in Weirwater and Semmerholm for the [Quest Against The Vallorn](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#quest-against-the-vallorn), if the Dawnish Assembly names the fight against the vallorn to be their highest calling.
