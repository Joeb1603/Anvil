# Construct the Lakesedge Highway

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Lakesedge Highway connecting the Holmauer Gate to Misericorde through Rebeshof in Holberg. This creates the Imperial title Rebeshof Roadwarden, to be elected annually by the senators of the League.
> >
> > Proposed: Holberg, Seconded: Segura

## Overview

- Constructs a sinecure in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) as per the **[Schools and sawmills](../winds-of-fortune/Schools_and_sawmills.md#the-lakesedge-highway)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 30 wains of white granite
- 54 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 25 wains of white granite provided by Gideon of the suns of Couros after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 5 crowns and 4 wains of white granite provided by Otmar van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 wain of white granite provided by Lia Adelaar after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 thrones 2 crowns provided by Jonah Yakovitch after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 thrones 2 crowns provided by Bernhard Kleist von Ennerlund after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 6 crowns provided by Claus Friedrich von Holberg after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 6 crowns provided by Cees Van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now completed, the position of [Rebeshof Roadwarden](../../mixed/titles/Rebeshof_Roadwarden.md) will be available for the senators of the League to appoint for the first time at the Winter Solstice 385YE for a limited term.
