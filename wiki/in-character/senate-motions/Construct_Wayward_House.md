# Construct Wayward House

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the WAYWARD HOUSE in the city of Apulian, Apulus, Spiral. A block of apartments constructed with twelve wains of white granite, to provide hospitality to Jarmish pilgrims of the Way visiting the Empire. Constructed by the Edificer's Guild of Apulian, with funding from Damian i Martan i Erigo.
> >
> > Announced by Paris of Apulus, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Apulus, Spiral
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)
- More information can be found in the **[Full circles](../winds-of-fortune/Full_circles.md#path-of-the-spirits)** wind of fortune.

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 wains of white granite
- 36 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 4 thrones 4 crowns and 12 wains of white granite provided by Paris of Apulus after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the Wayward House will provide hospitality to Jarmish pilgrims of the Way visiting the Empire
