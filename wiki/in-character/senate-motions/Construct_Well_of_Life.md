## Outcome

Once complete, the Well of Life will have the following effects:

- Adds 15 mana crystals to the Conclave font each season
- Creates a large well-furnished hospital to treat the sick and the wounded
- Reduces the cost of mana purchases by the Grandmaster
- Allows the Grandmaster to purchase vital honey from Crucible
- Raises the level of investment of herb gardens (2 true vervain and 1 cerulean mazzarine) in the territory by one
- Provides every member of the order with a single dose of true vervain for a year
- Provides 2 rings of ilium to the order's vaults each season
- All casualties suffered by Imperial armies fighting in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md), [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md), [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md), or [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) are reduced by a tenth
- Creates the Freeborn position *Hakima of the Rippling Marshes*
