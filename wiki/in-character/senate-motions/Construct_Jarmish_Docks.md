# Construct Jarmish Docks

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build the Jarmish Weirwood docks in Cargo, to be designed by the Asavean Architect*Proposed by Kallavesa, Seconded by Redoubt*

### Overview

- A [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) built to purchase weirwood from [Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) in Cargo, [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Cost

- 15 wains of weirwood
- 15 wains of white granite
- 60 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 15 wains of weirwood, 15 wains of white granite, and 60 crowns provided by Olle Markusson after Winter Solstice 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- There are [necessary details](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#necessary-details) missing from this motion, Senator Kallavesa may decide on these.
- once completed created the title [Prime Factor of the Pallas Docks](../../mixed/titles/Prime_Factor_of_the_deBruin_Docks.md).
