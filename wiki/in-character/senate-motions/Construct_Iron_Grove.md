# Construct Iron Grove

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission "The Iron Grove", a herb garden sinecure in Kallavesa with the title "Keeper of the Iron Grove". It will be appointed by the Wintermark senators with tenure.
> >
> > Announced by Wren Ironclaw, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Creates a sinecure in Skymark, [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md) that produces eighteen herbs each season
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 weirwood
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Wren Ironclaw after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Keeper of the Iron Grove](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Iron_Grove.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the senators of Wintermark at the Summer Solstice 386YE.
