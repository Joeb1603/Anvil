# Construct Ethengraw Armoury

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a folly - smithy and armoury in Gildermark. 60 wains of mithril. 120 crowns. Folly to be ceded to the Ethengraw.
> >
> > Proposed: Skarsind, Seconded: Redoubt

## Overview

- Creates a armoury-smithy for the [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) by the [Ethengraw](../winds-of-fortune/All_too_well.md#metal2c-bone2c-and-soil) sept as detailed in the **[All too well](../winds-of-fortune/All_too_well.md)** wind of fortune.
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 384YE

## Cost

- As per the wind of fortune
- 60 wains of mithril
- 15 thrones
- Six months to complete

## Outcome

- Once complete the Ethengraw will join the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). They will form the core of any new Imperial Orc army, provide opportunities to muster such an army and present the option of a unique quality reflecting their fighting arts.
