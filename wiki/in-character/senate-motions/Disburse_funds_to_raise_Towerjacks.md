# Disburse funds to raise Towerjacks

> *In-character text:*
>
> The dispersal of 400 Thrones for the purchase of mithril sufficient to raise the army known as the Towerjacks and allow them to operate as an Imperial field army.  
>  
> *Proposed by Holberg, seconded by Temeschwar*

## Overview

- A request for funds with which to raise the [Towerjacks](../../mixed/government/military/Towerjacks.md) army in [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md).
- The motion was withdrawn as worded, since this does not actually grant permission to *raise* the army, just to disburse funds for it.

## Date

- Withdrawn Summer 379YE
