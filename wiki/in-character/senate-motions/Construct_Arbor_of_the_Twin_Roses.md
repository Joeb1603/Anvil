# Construct Arbor of the Twin Roses

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a sinecure "The Arbor of the Twin Roses" in Semmerholm to honour the exemplar Elayne Silverlark.
> >
> > Creating the position "The Voice of the Twin Roses" elected annually by the Dawnish National Assembly. Revocable by the Dawnish National Assembly.
> >
> > Self-fundedProposed by Weirwater, Seconded by Reikos

## Overview

- Commissions a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Ferrond, [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md) as detailed in the **[Draw strength from vulnerability](../winds-of-fortune/Draw_strength_from_vulnerability.md#roses)** Wind of Fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 8 wains of white granite
- 8 wains of weirwood
- 32 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 4 thrones, 8 wains of weirwood and 8 wains of white granite provided by Claude Deeds after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the [Voice of the Twin Roses](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_the_Twin_Roses.md) will be available to appoint by the Dawnish National Assembly
