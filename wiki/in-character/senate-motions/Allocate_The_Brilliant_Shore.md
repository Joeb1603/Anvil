# Allocate The Brilliant Shore

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Brilliant Shore, a source of White Granite in Skarsind, to become a National Bourse position assigned to [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md).  
>  
> *Procedural vote*

## Autumn 377YE

### Overview

- [The Brilliant Shore](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#the-brilliant-shore) to become a National [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) position.
- Passed by 21 votes to 4.

### Synod Veto

- This motion was vetoed by the [General Assembly of the Imperial Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-general-assembly)

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) used the power of the [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).
- This [administrative motion](Senate_motion.md#administrative-motions) will automatically be raised again during a sitting at the next Summit.
- The Brilliant Shore is currently unassigned. Production from Autumn 377YE has been stockpiled and will be made available to the first Imperial citizen elected to operate The Brilliant Shore.

### Constitution

Assignation of The Brilliant Shore *to [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md)* is legally extraneous. A national Bourse position can only be eligible to Imperial citizens of the nation that controls the territory the resource is in.

## Winter 377YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Brilliant Shore to become a National Bourse position.  
>  
> *Procedural vote*

### Overview

- The motion during the Autumn Equinox 377YE was vetoed by the Imperial Synod.
- The question of the assignment of the Brilliant Shore was automatically raised again at the Winter Solstice 377YE.

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The [The Brilliant Shore](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#the-brilliant-shore) is now a National [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) position.
- Production from Autumn 377 has been stockpiled and will be made available to the first Imperial citizen elected to operate The Brilliant Shore.

### Constitution

Assignment as a National resource means that the Brilliant Shore automatically comes under the auspices of the nation that controls the territory wherein it is located - in this case [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) in [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md).

### Voting

| State | Votes For | Votes Against | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| In Principle | 21 | 3 | Passed |
