# Construct Parador of Dust

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To refurbish the Parador of Dust in the Jade Range, Kahraman as a mithril ministry.
> >
> > A commission to create the title Mistress of the Dust Parador with the power to purchase mithril and the responsibility to use it for the benefit of the Freeborn people.
> >
> > The title will be appointed through the bourse.
> >
> > Self funded.Proposed by Kahraman, Seconded by Holberg

## Overview

- Commission a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in Jade Range, [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) that draws on an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) [presented](../winds-of-fortune/Four_strong_winds.md#the-parador-of-dust) by the merchants of the [Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 16 wains of white granite
- 32 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 4 thrones and 16 wains of white granite provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the [Mistress of the Dust Parador](../../mixed/titles/Mistress_of_the_Dust_Parador.md) will be available to appoint by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md)
