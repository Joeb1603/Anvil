# Create title Patron of the Pavilion

> *In-character text:*
>
> Create Imperial Title ‘Patron of the Pavilion’, a sinecure with an income of 10 liao and 20 votes in the Synod.
>
> Elected by appointment of the League National Assembly. Privately funded by the benefactors of Highguard.
>
> No civilian commission required.
>
> *Proposed by Necropolis, seconded by Holberg.*

## Outcome

- Create a new Imperial title following the [opportunity presented](../winds-of-fortune/In_the_garden_of_defiance.md#pavilion-on-the-lake) by the curse on Holmauer Park.
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs as described [here](../winds-of-fortune/In_the_garden_of_defiance.md#pavilion-on-the-lake).
- 60 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 7 thrones 4 crowns provided by Shiloh after Autumn Equinox 383YE

## Campaign Outcome

- Once completed this commission will be overseen by the title [Patron of the Pavilion](../../mixed/titles/Patron_of_the_Pavilion.md) appointed by the League National Assembly.
