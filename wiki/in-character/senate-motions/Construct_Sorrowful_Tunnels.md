## Overview

- Constructs a [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) mine in Gildermark, [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Long shadows](../winds-of-fortune/Long_shadows.md#the-sorrowful-tunnels)** wind of fortune and the **[Sorrowful Tunnels](../winds-of-fortune/387YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_fortune.md#sorrowful-tunnels-skarsind)** entry in the 387YE Summer Solstice winds of fortune

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 60 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 7 thrones 2 crowns and 20 wains of weirwood provided by Irontide Flax after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the *Sorrowful Tunnels* bourse resource is available to allocate by the Imperial Senate
