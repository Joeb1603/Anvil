# Construct college of magic in Morrow

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a magical College aligned with the realm of Summer within the territory of Morrow. The Dean of the College to be appointed by vote of the Conclave  
>  
> *Proposed by Redoubt, seconded by Kallavesa*

### Overview

- Permission to create a [College of Magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) in [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md), specialising in [Summer magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md).
- Corvus, Archmage of Summer, was called as an expert witness.
- No funding was sought

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Cost

- Materials: 90 wains of White Granite, 30 wains of Mithril, 20 wains of Weirwood
- Time: 1 year
- Labour: 35 Thrones
- Upkeep: 25 Thrones (this number is dependent on the number of other colleges in the Empire)
- Special: This college of Summer magic is being built to designs provided by the [Eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Meraud](../eternals/Meraud.md); as such it differs from the [standard cost](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) to build a [College of Magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md).

### Progress

- This commission has lapsed

### Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the college will automatically create the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of "Dean".
- Dean of the college will be appointed through the [Declaration of Candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy).
