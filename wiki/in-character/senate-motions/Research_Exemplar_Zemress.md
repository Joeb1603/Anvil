# Research Exemplar Zemress

> *In-character text:*
>
> Request for access to archives for research of Exemplar Zemress past lives.  
>  
> *Proposed by Madruga, Seconded by Bregasland.*

### Overview

- [Research project](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md).
- Commission research into the life of the [Zemress](../religion/Zemress.md), [Exemplar](../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md).
- Passed by 27 votes to 0, funded at 1 Throne.

### Date

- Passed Summer 377YE (Event Three 2013).

### Cost

- 5 Thrones;
- 1 Throne paid from Senate budget; remainder paid by Darius i Ezmara i Erigo.

### Progress

- Complete

### Campaign Outcome

- A report on the life of Zemress was compiled and presented to Darius i Ezmara i Erigo once funding was received.
