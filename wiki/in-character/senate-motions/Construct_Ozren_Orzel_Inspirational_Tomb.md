# Construct Ozren Orzel Inspirational Tomb

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission the inspirational tomb of the exemplar of Pride Ozren Orzel in Sandling, Weirwater. Self funded. True liao is ready for this great work.
> >
> > Proposed: Weirwater, Seconded: Astolat

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Sandling, Weirwater.
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[An eagle in song](../winds-of-fortune/An_eagle_in_song.md)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 30 wains of white granite
- 90 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 9 thrones 4 crowns and 30 wains of white granite provided by Starac Sijed Orzel after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 throne 1 crown provided by Ser Geoffrey de Lorilam after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Ser Gareth Haywain after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Lady Nineve Orzel after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the inspiration of Orzen Orzel will have the following effects:

- [Congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) in [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md) will receive a [Philtre of Strength](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_War.md#philtre-of-strength) each season
- The eighty-one individuals named by **Tarik**, former [High Bard of the Empire](../../mixed/titles/High_Bard_of_the_Empire.md), will receive some form of reward from the Witches of Culwich
