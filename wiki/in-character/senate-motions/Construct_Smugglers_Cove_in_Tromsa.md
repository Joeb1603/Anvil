# Construct Smugglers Cove in Tromsa

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a Smuggler's Cove in Tromsa. And a disbursement of funds.
> >
> > Proposed: Sermersuaq, Seconded: Hahnmark

## Overview

- Constructs a [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) in [Tromsa](../barbarians/Tromsa.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Fedelmidd Sydanjaa's Heart Floer after Winter Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, fleets are able to support the cove in the same manner as military units can support [spy networks](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md)
