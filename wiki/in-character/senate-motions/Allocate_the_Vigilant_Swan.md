# Allocate the Vigilant Swan

> *In-character text:*
>
> To assign the Vigilent Swan bourse position  
>  
> *Propsed by Necropolis, Seconded by Casinea*

### Overview

- The [Vigilant Swan](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#the-vigilant-swan) in [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md) was recently liberated by Imperial troops.
- Passed as a National position.
  - Nations proposed were [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) and [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md).
  - Assigned to the Imperial Orcs.
- The motion was subject to the [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md).

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Synod Veto

- This motion was vetoed by the [General Assembly of the Imperial Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-general-assembly)

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) used the power of the [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).
- The motion to assign the Vigilant Swan cannot be raised again during the current summit.
- The Vigilant Swan is currently unassigned. Production from Spring 379YE will be stockpiled and made available to the first Imperial citizen elected to operate this [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) resource.

### Constitution

- If the motion had passed, the National status of the Bourse Seat would have followed the nation the territory of Reikos was eventually assigned to.
