# Construct Municipal Docks

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Raise a commission to build the Municipal Docks in Madruga (inc Quaymaster). Self-funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Madruga, Seconded: Hahnmark

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Calvos Sound, Madruga
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Germinating a city](../winds-of-fortune/Germinating_a_city.md#a-spiritual-successor)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 80 wains of white granite
- 40 wains of weirwood
- 45 thrones in labour costs
- 5 thrones per season upkeep
- Twelve months to complete

## Progress

- 45 thrones, 40 wains of weirwood and 80 wains of white granite provided by Gisli i Phovas i Guerra after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the Municipal Docks will have the following effects:

- Boosts taxation from the territory by 12 thrones a season
- Protects Calvos Sounds as a level one fortification
- Acts as a [shipyard](../../mixed/commissions/Shipyard.md) allowing the construction or refuge of up to two navies at a time
- Creates the Freeborn position [Quaymaster of the Municipal Docks](../../mixed/titles/Quaymaster_of_the_Municipal_Docks.md), appointed for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 388YE for a shortened term of two seasons

The following will take effect following the Autumn Equinox 388YE:

- Raises the level of investment in fleets in Madruga which engage in trading, questing or raiding
- Raises the level of investment in businesses in Madruga
- Raises the income of all Freeborn characters by 7 rings for a year after completion
- Reduces the cost for Freeborn characters to upgrade fleets by 1 weirwood for a year after completion
- Creates a new questing Voyage for Freeborn fleets, **Hunt the Free Landing Kraken**
