# Construct Dancer's Rest

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Build a money sinecure in Seren, Miaren with 10 wains of weirwood. The Dancer's Rest creates the title of Hand to the Dance to be appointed by the Navarr National Assembly with tenure. This is a centre of education and rehabilitation for refugees and ex-slaves, part of the Proffered Hand Network. Fully funded by the Network.
> >
> > Announced by Firesong Arachas, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Serenael, [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md)
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood provided by Sister Rosa after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 3 thrones 6 crowns provided by Sufyan i Zuhri i Guerra after Autumn Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Hand to the Dance](../../mixed/titles/Hand_to_the_Dance.md) will be available to appoint by the Navarr Assembly for the first time at the Winter Solstice 387YE
