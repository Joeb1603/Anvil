# Amend powers of Dredgemaster of Feverwater Winter 381YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> to amend the title ‘Dredgemaster of Feverwater’ to remove the power of Historical Research.  
>  
> *Proposed by Morrow, seconded by Volodmartz.*

### Overview

- To remove the ability to perform [historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) from the [Dredgemaster of Feverwater](../../mixed/titles/Dredgemaster_of_Feverwater.md).
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Dredgemaster of Feverwater has lost the ability to commission historical research.
