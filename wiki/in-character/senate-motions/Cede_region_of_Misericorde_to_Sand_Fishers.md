# Cede region of Misericorde to Sand Fishers

> *In-character text:*
>
> To cede the region of Misericorde to the Sand Fishers.  
>
> *Proposed by Holberg, seconded by Karsk*

## Overview

- This motion cedes the region of [Misericorde](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#misericorde), in Holberg to the orc nation in [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) known as the Sand Fishers
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 383YE

## Campaign Outcome

- The region has been ceded to the Sand Fishers
- Further details of the Sand Fisher settlement can be found [here](../winds-of-fortune/A_dream_that_can_last.md).
- The overall tax revenue for the territory of [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) will increase by 8 thrones by the end of 383YE.
