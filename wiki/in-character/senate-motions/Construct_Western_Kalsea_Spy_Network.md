# Construct Western Kalsea Spy Network

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct a spy network to the west of Skallahn to super charge the offensive against the Jotun
> >
> > Announced by Bernard Dugdale, Imperial Spymaster

## Overview

- Commissions a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the territory west of [Skallahn](../barbarians/Skallahn.md).
- Builds on the opportunity presented by the success of the [Skallahn raid](../winds-of-war/Under_heaven.md#wealth) to build a spy network in the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) west of [Skallahn](../barbarians/Skallahn.md). This opportunity was presented in the **[Take it further](../winds-of-fortune/Take_it_further.md#on-land)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md)

## Date

- Autumn 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 10 crowns
- Three months
- 5 thrones each season upkeep

## Progress

- 2 crowns 10 rings provided by Cpt. Hendrick van Hoven after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 2 crowns 10 rings provided by Tevoro von Ghurayb von Holberg after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Ergot Di Cevia after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Fintan Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Æðelric Dagursson after Autumn Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- The spy network was destroyed by the forces of Jarl Ustigar of Kierheim using [information gathered](../winds-of-war/In_each_line_lies_another_line.md#the-freezing-north-28battle29) from destroying the Skallahn spy network.
