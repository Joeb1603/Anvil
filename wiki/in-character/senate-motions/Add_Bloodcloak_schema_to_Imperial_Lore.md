# Add Bloodcloak schema to Imperial Lore

> *In-character text:*
>
> To add the Bloodcloak schema to Imperial Lore.  
>  
> Raised by Semmerholm, seconded by Karsk

## Overview

- To make the [schema](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) for the Bloodcloak [magic item](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) available to all Imperial citizens.
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 381YE

## Cost

- 10 Thrones
- The schema itself must be handed over to the Civil Service. In the process of disseminating the knowledge the schema will be destroyed.

## Progress

- 10 thrones and the schema provided by Lofyn Blood-cloak after Winter Solstice 381YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The Bloodcloak has been Imperial lore, allowing any Imperial citizen to learn how to make it.
