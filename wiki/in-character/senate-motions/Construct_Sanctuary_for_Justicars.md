## Overview

- Creates a sanctuary for the [justicars](../winds-of-fortune/Bones_in_the_ocean.md#game-information-justicars) on the [Isle of the Osseini](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#isle-of-the-osseini) in [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md).
- Details of this opportunity are provided in the **[Bones in the ocean](../winds-of-fortune/Bones_in_the_ocean.md#the-sanctuary-of-the-justicars)** wind of fortune.
- Passed at 70 Thrones

## Date

- Spring 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 15 wains of [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md)
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones 5 crowns and 15 wains of white granite provided by Delara of the House of the Wanderer after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Spring Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete the sanctuary would allow the [title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) [Justicar of Osseini](../../mixed/titles/Justicar_of_Osseini.md) to be appointed.
