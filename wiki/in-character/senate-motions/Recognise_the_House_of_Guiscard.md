# Recognise the House of Guiscard

> *In-character text:*
>
> To recognise the House of Guiscard as rulers of Kalino.  
>  
> *Proposed by Segura, Seconded by Skarsind.*

### Overview

- This motion announces formal support to the Iron Confederacy house of Guiscard in their bid to become rulers of Kalino, following on from a [similar motion](Recognise_ducal_quality.md) during the Summer Solstice 381YE.
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- This statement by the Senate has no legal implications but may have diplomatic consequences.
