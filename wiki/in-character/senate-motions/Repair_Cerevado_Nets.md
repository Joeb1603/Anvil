# Repair Cerevado Nets

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the repair of the Cerevado Nets, self funded by the Dhomiros Council for 50 thrones. This gathers 6 rings of ilium per season, and is a nationally elected position
> >
> > Proposed: Kahraman, Seconded: Feroz

## Overview

- Repairs the [Cerevado Nets](../../mixed/titles/Cerevado_Nets.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Where the river rises](../winds-of-fortune/Where_the_river_rises.md#glitter)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 50 thrones
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 49 thrones 7 crowns provided by Ferran i Rincón i Erigo after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Khalil i Carno i Guerra after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now completed, the Cerevado Nets will provide six rings of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) each season.
