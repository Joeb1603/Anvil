# Construct Bladeroot Sinecure

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a sinecure for the production of bladeroot. To be constructed in Old Ranging, Hercynia, creating the title of "Steward of the Nabarr" appointed by the senators of Navarr. Self funded. Virtue demands ambition.
> >
> > Proposed Hercynia, Seconded Mitwold

## Overview

- Commissions a herb garden [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Hercynia](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 384YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs) apply
- The costs for the sinecure have not been defined in the motion so the civil service assumes minimum size (10 wains)

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Radic Splinterovich Schaslyvyy after Summer Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the sinecure will create the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Steward of the Nabarr](../../mixed/titles/Steward_of_the_Nabarr.md).
