# Construct Iron Qanat II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission the great work ‘The Iron Qanat’ in Yellow Chase, Segura, to allow the capacity for the Freeborn to support an additional army and guarantee supply for the existing ones. Costs: 50 weirwood, 100 crowns with zero upkeep cost. This project is self-funded.  
> *Proposed by Segura, seconded by Sarvos*

## Outcome

- Construct the Iron Qanat, taking advantage of the [opportunity presented by](../winds-of-fortune/Free_to_live.md#yellow-grain) the settlers from the Isle of Zemress.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply as detailed [here](../winds-of-fortune/Free_to_live.md#yellow-grain).
- 50 wains weirwood
- 100 crowns labour cost
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 50 wains of weirwood and 100 crowns provided by Alexios i Zemress i Riqueza after Summer Solstice 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The basic Iron Qanat would allow the Brass Coast to support an additional army or navy. In addition the Red Plains Farm and the Golden Terraces would immediately be upgraded by a level (assuming they were in Imperial hands)
- The Iron Qanat has been built in the [Sobral Grasses](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md#sobral-grasses) as [proposed](../winds-of-fortune/Free_to_live.md#yellow-grain) by the [Zemress islanders](../nations/the-brass-coast/Zemress_islander.md) who provided the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md).
