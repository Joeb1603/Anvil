# Statement of Intent regarding the Stalker

> *In-character text:*
>
> As part of our ongoing peace treaty with the Thule and to accept their gesture of goodwill.
>  
> We agree to use the Thule ritual to divert the Stalker comet to the Thule territory of Sküld  
>  
> *Proposed by Volodmartz, Seconded by Hercynia*

### Overview

- This motion was to address the red star which is [currently](../winds-of-fortune/Blackest_night.md) believed to be set to fall in [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md)
- Passed in principle

### Date

- Summer 380YE
