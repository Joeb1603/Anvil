# Change to Imperial Breadbasket

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Keeper of the Imperial Breadbasket, Rowan Merrick, announced that the Imperial Breadbasket is to be distributed to the Citadel Guard Army.,br>  
> *Approved by the Keeper of the Imperial Breadbasket*

### Overview

- The Imperial Breadbasket ceases to support the Mournwold and will begin to contribute to the upkeep of Imperial armies again
- Passed

### Date

- WInter 381YE

### Outcome

- As detailed [here](Amend_the_use_of_the_Imperial_breadbasket.md#campaign-outcome), it will take three months for the Breadbasket to begin supporting the [Citadel Guard](../mandates/Citadel_Guard.md).
- The money received by farms in the Marches territories of [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md),[Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md), and [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) is restored immediately.
