# Disburse funds to Grendel raiders

> *In-character text:*
>
> Payment of funds to the Grendel, in return for which they will not invade of sack any Imperial territories on the Bay of Catazar this season.  
> *Proposed by Holberg, seconded by Morrow.*

## Overview

- This motion relates to the [demands made by the Grendel](../winds-of-war/382YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#castle-of-glass-28sarvos29) prior to the Autumn Equinox.
- Passed at 300 thrones.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Outcome

- 300 Thrones were disbursed to the Senator for Holberg.
