# Fortify Miekarova II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To raise a fortress in Miekarova (Self Funded)  
>  
> *Proposed by Miekarova, seconded by Karsk*

*[Image: Regions of Miekarova](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RegionsofMiekarova.png)*

### Overview

- The creation of a new [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), in the region of [Perumaki](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#perumaki).
- The fortification will be named Grimhold.
- The fortress is being built in the Vale of Strascovia, home of the Vor'Azi people, using the crumbling ruins of their ancestral castle as the foundation.
- A previous [motion](Fortify_Miekarova.md) to raise a fortification in this [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) territory was defeated in Winter 378YE.

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Costs

- Materials: 80 [white granite](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#white-granite) and 20 thrones. ([Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#costs)).
- Time: 1 year to construct
- Upkeep: 10 thrones per season.

### Progress

- 40 wains of white granite and 20 thrones provided by Dušan Otecovna Zlata after Spring Equinox 379YE.
- 40 wains of white granite provided by Dušan Otecovna Zlata after Autumn Equinox 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- When complete, this will create a rank one fortification in Miekarova.
