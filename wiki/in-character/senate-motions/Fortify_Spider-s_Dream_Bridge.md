# Fortify Spider's Dream Bridge

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To fortify Spider's Dream Bridge. Request disbursement of funds (not much).
> >
> > Proposed: Necropolis, Seconded: Segura

## Overview

- Fortifies the [Spider's Dream Bridge](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#the-spiders-dream) as per **[Dreaming in strands](../winds-of-fortune/Dreaming_in_strands.md#old-precautions)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 20 white granite
- 60 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 7 thrones 4 crowns and 20 wains of white granite provided by Eli of the Cenotaph after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete, it is no longer possible for a warband to sabotage the bridge. Given sufficient time, an enemy army could still destroy the bridge if they controlled the regions on both sides of the river and a season to spare. But short of that, attacking the bridge is now practically impossible. In addition, if one of [Torres](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#torres) or [Bocche](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#bocche) were lost then the Empire would be able to maintain military control of the Spiders Dream. This means the Empire can use the bridge to overcome the limit of claiming a [beachhead](../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#beachheads) when invading Torres from Bocche, and vice-versa.
