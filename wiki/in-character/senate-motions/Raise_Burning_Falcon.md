# Raise Burning Falcon

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the raise of a Freeborn army, the Burning Falcon. The General of the Burning Falcon shall hold the Flame Rampant banner as their regalia. We request a disbursement of funds from the Senate for this commission.
> >
> > Proposed: Madruga. Seconded: Miaren

### Overview

- This motion was to [raise](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#recruitment) the [Burning Falcon](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#the-burning-falcon).
- Passed at 250 Thrones

### Date

- Spring 384YE

### Cost

- Standard costs for raising an Imperial army apply
- 200 wains of [Mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md)
- 50 Thrones
- 1 year to complete

### Progress

- 33 wains of mithril and 13 thrones provided by Chiara Zayden Riqueza after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 10 wains of mithril provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 7 wains of mithril provided by Vrael i Sol-Devorador i Erigo after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 4 wains of mithril provided by Aracelis I Erigo after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 35 wains of mithril and 12 thrones provided by Chiara Zayden Riqueza after Summer Solstice 384YE
- 15 wains of mithril provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Summer Solstice 384YE
- 75 wains of mithril and 25 thrones provided by Chiara Zayden Riqueza after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 15 wains of mithril provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 6 wains of mithril provided by Vrael i Sol-Devorador i Erigo after Autumn Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, a third Freeborn army will be raised.
- In Autumn 382YE, a number of [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were [presented](../winds-of-fortune/Put_on_your_war_paint.md) to allow the Freeborn National Assembly to raise [mandates](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md) to support the raising of the army. Unfortunately, those mandates were [only available](../winds-of-fortune/Put_on_your_war_paint.md#special-opportunities) until the end of the Spring Equinox 383YE.
