# Accept Druj peace treaty

> *In-character text:*
>
> To accept the treaty between the people of the Druj and the people of the Empire.  
>  
> *Proposed by the Conscience of the Senate, Seconded by Zenith.*

*[Image: The Druj treaty](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:DrujTreaty.png)*

### Overview

- A delegation from the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) negotiated a treaty.
- The treaty was presented to the Senate.

### Date

- Defeated Summer 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The terms of the treaty were not accepted.
- The Druj delegates were present during the discussion of the treaty and are aware it has been rejected.
