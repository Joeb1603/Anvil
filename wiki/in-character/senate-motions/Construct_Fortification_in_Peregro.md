# Construct Fortification in Peregro

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build a citadel in the region of Peregro, in the territory of Morrow, to stand watch over the border of Therunin where the Druj and vallorn hold sway. Also a disbursement of funds.
> >
> > Proposed: Morrow, Seconded: Skarsind

## Overview

[Image: TowerJacks Colour.png](../military-council-orders/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Military_Council_orders.md#towerjacks)

- Constructs a [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in Peregro, Morrow
- Passed
- This motion received support from the [Towerjacks](../../mixed/government/military/Towerjacks.md) following the Spring Equinox 387YE

## Date

- Winter 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 80 white granite
- 240 crowns
- Twelve months to complete

## Progress

- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 10 rings in small donations provided after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 15 thrones and 40 wains of white granite provided by Sejanus of the Scarlet Guard after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 14 thrones 2 crowns and 20 wains of white granite provided by Sejanus of the Scarlet Guard after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 20 wains of white granite provided by Pontus Figures after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 4 crowns 10 rings provided by Corinthia of the Wise Citadel after Summer Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Creates a [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), [Quicksilver's Flame](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md#quicksilvers-flame), in Peregro
- This Senate motion has been updated to create a fortification inline with the [rules update for 2026](../../out-of-character/rules/Rules_update_2026.md#fortifications).
