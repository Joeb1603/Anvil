# Construct Statue of that Cockscroft Lad

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I announce the construction of a statue celebrating the Vigilant hue and cry of Johnathan Piper of the Cockscroft. The "Statue of that Cockscroft Lad" will mark and memorialise the site of the massacre of the Wayford Wanderers and their supporters in Mitwold. 5 white granite, 15 crowns. Funded by the Cockscroft Household.
> >
> > Announced by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov, Mistress of Monuments

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Maiden Downs, Mitwold
- Announcement by the [Mistress of Monuments](../../mixed/titles/Mistress_of_Monuments.md)

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 5 white granite
- 15 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 throne 7 crowns and 5 wains of white granite provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the statue of **Jonathan Piper of Cockscroft** is in pride of place near [the site](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#statue-of-cockscroft-lad) of the senseless loss of life
