# Construct Library of Worth

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Motion to construct the Library of Worth in Guildenheim, Skarsind. This is a folly and will cost at least 12 white granite and 42 crowns. Funded by the Imperial Orc nation. It will allow them to add a word and symbol to their collection each season, to preserve history.
> >
> > Proposed: Reikos, Seconded: Skarsind

## Overview

- Constructs a [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Gildermark, Skarsind as per the **[The Deer in the Sun](../winds-of-fortune/The_Deer_in_the_Sun.md#the-library-of-worth)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- This motion received the support of the Imperial Orcs Assembly through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-122) enacted by **Skywise Gralka**

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 14 white granite
- 42 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Outcome

Now complete, the Library of Worth serves to establish a collection of words and symbols. The Imperial Orcs, through the [Weigher of Worth](../../mixed/titles/Weigher_of_Worth.md), can add one word and the accompanying symbol each season to the collection.
