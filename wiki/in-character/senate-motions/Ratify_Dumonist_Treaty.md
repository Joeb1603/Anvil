# Ratify Dumonist Treaty

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To ratify treaty with the Iron Confederacy. That the Iron Confederacy agrees to step down trade embargo to sanction. In return for the Empire instructing the magistrates to co-operate with the Suranni templars in tracking down members of the Hand of Dumon.
> >
> > Proposed Miekarova, Seconded Miaren

## Overview

- The motion presents a [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) negotiated by the [Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Iron_Confederacy.md).
- The treaty has two provisions: the Iron Confederacy will remove the [embargo](../../mixed/government/senate/Tariffs.md#embargo) on trade with the Empire, replacing it with [sanctions](../../mixed/government/senate/Tariffs.md#sanctions).
- The Imperial magistrates will cooperate with [Suranni](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) lawkeepers in tracking down members of the illegal Hand of Dumon.
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 384YE.

## Outcome

- The treaty has been enacted by the Civil Service; new trade status is dependent on the Iron Confederacy also ratifying the treaty
- The treaty is subject to some additional [scrutiny](Scrutiny.md) as the Imperial magistrates determine the best way to cooperate with the Suranni templars.
