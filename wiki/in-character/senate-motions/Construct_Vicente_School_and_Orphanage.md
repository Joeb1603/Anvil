# Construct Vicente School and Orphanage

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Vicente School and Orphanage, a mana ministry in Apulian, Apulus, Spiral. Cost: 12 mithril + 36 crowns (plus Black Plateau costs pending result of mandate regarding the Block). Nature: An orphanage with a focus on training orphans as reckoners and traders with the aid of reckoners and traders invited from across the city, and acting as a mana trading house. Title: Custodian of the Vicente School and Orphange. Appointed by the Senators of the League. Tenured League position. Revoked by the League National Assembly. Responsibilities: To shelter safe the broken and lost, and teach them how to reckon each cost.
> >
> > Announced by Heliodoro Vicente, Bearer of the First Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) to purchase mana crystals in Apulus, [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of the First Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)
