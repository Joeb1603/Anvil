# Construct Bleak Tower

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build the Bleak Tower, a great work in Morrow. To provide a home to the refugees of the herb garden of Pallas and its herbalist researchers. Self-funded.  
> *Proposed by Spiral, seconded by Reikos.*

## Outcome

- Create a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Ravion](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md#ravion), [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md), as part of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) [presented](../winds-of-fortune/You-ll_recognise_yourself.md#poise) during the Spring Equinox.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 382YE

## Costs

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/You-ll_recognise_yourself.md#poise).
- 30 wains of weirwood
- 30 wains of white granite
- 120 crowns labour cost
- Six months to complete.

## Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood, 22 wains of white granite, and 8 Thrones provided by Edmundo of Damakan's Forge after the Summer Solstice 382YE.
- 20 wains of weirwood, 8 wains of white granite, and 7 thrones provided by Thalia of Netherwatch after the Spring Equinox 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) will distribute a share of 80 random herbs through the [herb gardens](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) of [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md).
- It creates an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) - [Custodian of the Bleak Tower](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Bleak_Tower.md) appointed by Urizen senators.
- In addition to other effects the Custodian will receive six doses of [heart's blood](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#hearts-blood) each season.
