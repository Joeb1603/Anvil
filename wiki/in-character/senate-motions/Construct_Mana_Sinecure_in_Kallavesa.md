# Construct Mana Sinecure in Kallavesa

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > This house should remain mindful of its duty to support the prosperity of hard-working citizens of this Empire. Small halls who have banded together to gather resources, yet tried and failed to afford a wayleaf should not be abandoned.
> >
> > Proposed: Hahnmark, Seconded: Bregasland

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) that produces mana crystals in [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 mithril
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 crown 10 rings provided by Amund en Craite after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 1 throne 5 crowns provided by Acwyn after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Amund en Craite after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 5 rings in small donations provided after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 10 wains of mithril and 1 throne 6 crowns 5 rings provided by Hodwyn Aevardson after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the title of [Remembrancer of Blood and Salt](../../mixed/titles/Remembrancer_of_Blood_and_Salt.md) will be appointed by unanimous decision of the senators of Wintermark for the first time at the Summer Solstice 387YE
