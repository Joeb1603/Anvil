# Declare war on the Hierro Clan

> *In-character text:*
>
> Declaration of War on the Hierro Clan for the loyalty to the Empire*Proposed by Skarsind, Seconded by Sermersuaq*

### Overview

- Passed

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Veto

- Senator Hahnmark has appraised the Senate that the Imperial Synod seek the veto for this motion.

### Campaign Outcome

- The Hierro Clan are no longer foreigners protected by Imperial Law.
- A delegation from the Hierro Clan would still be protected, as if foreigners, under the [law](Create_law_of_delegation_protection.md) of delegate protection.
