# Construct Ossuary Temple

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct a folly, the Ossuary Temple, in Southpine, Skarsind. 60 wains of white granite and 120 crowns. To be ceded to the Illarawm sept.
> >
> > Proposed: Redoubt, Seconded: Skarsind

## Overview

- Creates a temple for the bones recovered from [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) by the [Illarawm](../winds-of-fortune/All_too_well.md#a-sound-of-silence-28illarawm29) sept as detailed in the **[All too well](../winds-of-fortune/All_too_well.md)** wind of fortune.
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 384YE

## Cost

- As per the wind of fortune
- 60 wains of white granite
- 15 thrones
- Six months to complete

- Once complete the Illarawm will join the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). They will share knowledge of their ancestors with the Imperial Orcs, and offer their expertise to the rest of the nation helping them to better hear the voices of their own ancestors.
