# Improve Walls of Holberg

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > A motion to commission fortification of the walls of Holberg. Funded by citizens of Holberg.
> >
> > Proposed Holberg, Seconded Volodmartz

## Overview

- Commissions the expansion of the Walls of [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md)
- Further details on the walls, and what the expansion means, can be found [here](../winds-of-fortune/Watches_from_the_Wall.md#an-impregnable-fortress)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 100 wains of white granite
- 200 crowns in labour costs,
- Six months to construct
