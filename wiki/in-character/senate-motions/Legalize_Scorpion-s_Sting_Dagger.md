# Legalize Scorpion's Sting Dagger

> *In-character text:*
>
> We wish to repeal the ban on scorpion's sting.  
>  
> *Proposed by Hercynia, seconded by Therunin*

## Overview

- Remove prohibitions against creation and possession of [Scorpion's Sting](../../mixed/magic/items/Scorpion-s_Sting.md) dagger.

## Date

- Winter 378YE

## Campaign Outcome

- It is no longer illegal to create or possess a [Scorpion's Sting](../../mixed/magic/items/Scorpion-s_Sting.md) dagger.
