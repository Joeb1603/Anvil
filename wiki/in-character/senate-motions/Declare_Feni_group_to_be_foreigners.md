# Declare Feni group to be foreigners

> *In-character text:*
>
> To declare the Feni tribe “The Woods that fell” as foreigners.  
> *Proposed by Bregasland, seconded by Kallavesa.*

## Overview

- Declares the Feni known as "The Woods That Fell"
- Passed.

## Date

- Summer 382YE

## Outcome

- The Woods-that-fell Feni are those Feni who fled when the sinkhole opened. Currently there is one large group in [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) and one large group in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md). At preset both groups are still engaged in raiding from local settlements in the area.
