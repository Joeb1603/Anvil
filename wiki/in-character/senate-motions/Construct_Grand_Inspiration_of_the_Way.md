# Construct Grand Inspiration of the Way

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission the Grand Inspiration of the Way in Bastion. 1000 white granite, 250 thrones, 3 years.
> >
> > Proposed: Casinea, Seconded: Temeschwar

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in [Bastion](../nations/highguard/Bastion.md) as per the **[Blood will thicken](../winds-of-fortune/Blood_will_thicken.md#a-question-of-scale)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- This motion received support from citizens of the Marches through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-56) enacted by **Friar John of the Mourn** during the Summer Solstice 385YE
- This motion received support from citizens of the Brass Coast through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-71) enacted by **Sufyan i Zuhri i Erigo** during the Summer Solstice 385YE
- This motion received support from citizens of Highguard through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-125) enacted by **Eli of the Cenotaph** during the Summer Solstice 385YE
- This motion received support from citizens of Wintermark through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/385YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-113) enacted by **Eldrid Vestardottir** during the Autumn Equinox 385YE
- This motion received support from citizens of Navarr through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/385YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-134) enacted by **Fintan Nighthaven** during the Autumn Equinox 385YE
- This motion received support from citizens of the League through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-30) enacted by **Rafael Barossa d'Apulian** during the Winter Solstice 385YE
- This motion received support from citizens of Dawn through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-216) enacted by **Darian Numbers** during the Winter Solstice 385YE
- This motion is receiving support from citizens of Highguard due to the decision of the Benefactors Council
- This motion was [amended](../senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-grand-inspiration-of-the-way) during the Spring Equinox 388YE to incorporate seven great bells into the original design, based on [designs](../winds-of-fortune/Lay_the_foundations.md#let-the-bells-ring-out) presented as a result of the Highborn Assembly's highest calling
- This motion was amended twice during the Summer Solstice 388YE; first to [incorporate](../senate-sessions/388YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-grand-inspiration-of-the-way-i) gardens for the [Voices of Virtue](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Virtue.md), and second to [incorporate](../senate-sessions/388YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-grand-inspiration-of-the-way-ii) a choristers balcony. Both of these amendments were based on [designs](../winds-of-fortune/A_lasting_commitment.md) presented as a result of the Highborn Assembly's highest calling

## Outcome

- Once completed, this edifice will ensure that the Empire is recognised as the undisputed source of spiritual guidance for all followers of the Way in the known world
- Citizens of Highguard will provide 12 wains of white granite and 36 crowns every season until it is completed or until the Benefactors Council direct the funds elsewhere
- Nations can no longer [commit themselves to supporting the project](../winds-of-fortune/Pride_and_paragons.md#the-grand-inspiration).
- Citizens of the Marches provided 16 wains of white granite and 32 crowns every season until the start of the Summer Solstice 386YE
- Citizens of the Brass Coast provided 12 wains of white granite and 24 crowns every season until the start of the Summer Solstice 386YE (this amount was reduced to 8 wains of white granite and 16 crowns as the Brass Coast did not control Madruga)
- Citizens of Wintermark provided 12 wains of white granite and 24 crowns every season until the start of the Autumn Equinox 386YE
- Citizens of Navarr provided 12 wains of white granite and 24 crowns every season until the start of the Autumn Equinox 386YE (this amount was reduced to 8 wains of white granite and 16 crowns as Navarr did not control Therunin)
- Citizens of the League provided 20 wains of white granite and 40 crowns every season until the start of the Winter Solstice 386YE
- Citizens of Dawn provided 16 wains of white granite and 32 crowns every season until the start of the Winter Solstice 386YE

## Notes

Work to actually build the grand inspiration could not begin until a suitably inspiring design was chosen to serve as the basis of the construction. This was achieved at the Summer Solstice 385YE, and work actually constructing the temple-city began in the following months.
