# Construct Gardens of Morrow

> *In-character text:*
>
> Construct the great work, Gardens of Morrow, to produce herbs in Morrow.
>
> Cost of 40 wains of weirwood, 10 thrones.
>
> *Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of Urizen*

### Overview

- A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md)
- This motion was approved by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate

### Date

- Summer 380YE

### Cost

- 40 wains of weirwood
- 10 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 40 wains of weirwood and 10 thrones provided by Juliana after Spring Equinox 381YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- This [Gardens of Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md#gardens-of-morrow) provide 75 herbs to the citizens who own herb gardens in Morrow, split equally.
