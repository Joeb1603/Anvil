# Construct Smugglers Cove in Ayereed

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a smugglers cove in Ayereed and decommission the spy network in Sarangrave
> >
> > Announced by Rahmah i Darwisj i Erigo, Imperial Spymaster

## Overview

- Constructs a [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) in Ayereed
- Announcement by the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md)
- This follows an earlier announcement [below](#summer-386ye-announcement)

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Karrak after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, fleets will be able to support the smugglers cove in the same manner as military units can support spy networks.

>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > We are the true masters of the Bay of Catazar. We will wrestle it from the Grendel yoke. We see you, we know you. As all things will be Imperial, the Way is clear. The time of the Salt Lord is over. Small waves belie strong, irresistible tides, I announce a Smugglers Cove to be constructed in Ayereed.
> >
> > Announced by Bernard Dugdale, Imperial Spymaster

## Overview

- Constructs a [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) in the Grendel territory of [Ayereed](../barbarians/Ayereed.md)
- Announcement by the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md)

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 crown provided by Llandun Waystone after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 4 crowns provided by Triska Markova after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Protectorate Kardak after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Work on the smugglers cove had to be abandoned shortly after the Winter Solstice 386YE, when mercenaries under the pay of *[Maistir Varden](../winds-of-war/On_southern_sands.md#hill-of-the-golden-dawn-ayereed)* overran the area.
