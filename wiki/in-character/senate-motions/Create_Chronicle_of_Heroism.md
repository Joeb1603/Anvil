# Create Chronicle of Heroism

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create the “Chronicle of Heroism” to remind every citizen of the Mark the importance and nature of heroism in our culture. In a similar manner to the “Roll of Benefactors”, the Wintermark national assembly may add a name to the Chronicle with a greater majority, along with a clear record of their deeds.  
> *Proposed by Hahnmark, seconded by Kallavesa*

## Overview

- To create a Chronicle of Heroism similar to the Roll of Benefactors.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Outcome

- The Wintermark National Assembly may add a name to the Chronicle of Heroism using a [statement of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) recording their heroic deeds, provided it reaches a Greater Majority.
- The Chronicle of Heroism is recorded [here](../../mixed/government/synod/Heroism.md#chronicle-of-heroism).
