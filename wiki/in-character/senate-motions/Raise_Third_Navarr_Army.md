# Raise Third Navarr Army

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission an Imperial Navarr army to muster in Treji, Glen of Shadows, Hercynia. Cost 200 wains of mithril (or equivalent resources as outlined by the civil service) and 75 thrones. Self funding in progress
> >
> > Proposed: Hercynia, Seconded: Weirwater

## Overview

- Raises an [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in Hercynia
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[The first shot](../winds-of-fortune/The_first_shot.md#hercynia)** wind of fortune
- This motion received support from citizens of Dawn through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-61) enacted by **The Fool of Glory** during the Summer Solstice 387YE
- This motion received support from citizens of the Marches through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-119) enacted by **Basil Goodbeard** during the Summer Solstice 387YE
- This motion received support from citizens of Varushka through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-175) enacted by **Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov** during the Summer Solstice 387YE
- This motion received support from citizens of the Imperial Orcs through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-244) enacted by **Skywise Gralka** during the Summer Solstice 387YE

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 200 mithril
- 75 thrones
- Twelve months to complete
