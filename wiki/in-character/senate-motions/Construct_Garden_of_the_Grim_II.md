# Construct Garden of the Grim II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To raise a herb sinecure in Miekarova to be called the "Gardens of the Grim", with the title of the sinecure to be "The Grim Gardener".
>
> Appointment of the title holder to be done by the Senators of Varushka
>
> Self Funded
>
> *Proposed by Miekarova, Seconded by Volodmartz*

### Overview

- A [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md)
- Passed
- This followed a [previous](Construct_the_Gardens_of_the_Grim.md), failed motion to construct the sinecure
- This commission was announced after the maximum number of commissions had been authorised, but was announced in case one of the previous commissions had been subject to the synod's [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md). The veto was not employed and so this commission was not authorised

### Date

- Winter 380YE
