# Disburse funds for Empress Lisabetta's Celebrations

> *In-character text:*
>
> That there be a disbursement of funds to hold a celebration of the crowning of the twentieth Empress.
>
> This will allow the empress to send a message to her people and will act as a symbol of the Empire's loyalty and pride.
>
> *Proposed by Tassato, Seconded by Temeschwar*

### Overview

- This motion was to authorise the celebrations, the details of which can be found [here](../winds-of-fortune/Raise_a_glass.md)
- Passed in Principle
- Passed at 40 thrones 4 crowns

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Cost

- [Grand](../winds-of-fortune/Raise_a_glass.md#grand-celebrations) celebrations, similar to those accompanying [Empress Britta](../history/Empress_Britta.md) and [Emperor Walter](../history/Emperor_Walter.md), would cost 80 thrones
- [Breath-taking](../winds-of-fortune/Raise_a_glass.md#breath-taking-celebrations) celebrations, similar to those accompanying [Empress Giselle](../history/Empress_Giselle.md) and [Emperor Nicovar](../history/Emperor_Nicovar.md), would cost 250 thrones
- [Wondrous](../winds-of-fortune/Raise_a_glass.md#wondrous-celebrations) celebrations, similar to those accompanying [Empress Richilde](../religion/Empress_Richilde.md) and [Emperor Giovanni](../history/Emperor_Giovanni.md), would cost 500 thrones

### Progress

- 40 thrones and 4 crowns disbursed to Senator Tassato
