# Construct First Voice Glade

> *In-character text:*
>
> An announcement by bearer of an Imperial Wayleave. To announce the construction of a herb garden sinecure called:
> ‘Gerallt the bard was First Voice of the Navarr nation who died at Return. The bards of Navarr gather at his Glade.’ (To be called First Voice Glade.) In Miaren, appointed by the Navarr Senators, revocable by National Assembly..  
> *Imperial Wayleave by Idwyn Splitroot, proxy to Matt Twisted Tree*

## Overview

- Commission a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md)
- Announcement.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).
- The size of the sinecure has not been specified.

## Progress

- 20 weirwood and 40 crowns provided by Nemae after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) creates an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), [Dean of the First Voice Glade](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_First_Voice_Glade.md)
