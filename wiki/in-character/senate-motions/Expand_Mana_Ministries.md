# Expand Mana Ministries

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > There there be a disbursal of funds to upgrade mana ministries in Tassato, Sarvos and Temeschwar, taking advantage of the opportunity to upgrade the grandmasters of the conclave orders respective ministry by so doing.
> >
> > Proposed Tassato, Seconded Astolat

## Overview

- This project expands the ability of the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) [grandmasters](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) to purchase crystal mana.
- More details on the opportunity to upgrade of the mana ministries can be found in the **[Honey and Vinegar](../winds-of-fortune/Honey_and_vinegar.md#old-stones)** wind of fortune.
- Passed at 200 Thrones

## Date

- Spring 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 60 wains of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md)
- 120 crowns in labour costs
- Six months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones provided by Oscuro Kira di Tassato after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 1 throne provided by Richardo Glostari di Sarvos after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 1 throne provided by Catellus di Tassato-Regario after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 60 wains of mithril and 11 thrones provided by Cesare Sanguineo Rezia Di Tassato after Summer Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the amount of mana available to each [grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) will be increased.
