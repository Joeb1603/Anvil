# Construct spy network in Ossium

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a spy network in the territory of Ossium. To be called the Ossium Watchers.  
> *Proposed by Karov, seconded by Ossium*

### Overview

- To construct a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) of [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Spring 383YE

### Cost

- [Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md#costs) apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct.
- 2 thrones per season upkeep

### Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood and 20 crowns provided by Vuk the Wolfeater after the Spring Equinox 383YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- A spy network will be constructed in the territory of [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md)
