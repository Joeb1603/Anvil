# Reconstruct Gardens of Pallas

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To reconstruct the Gardens of Pallas in Proceris, Zenith, taking advantage of the appraisal last season, as per the withdrawn motion
> >
> > Announced by Nikolai, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

- Constructs a sinecure in [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) as per the **[Restoration and renewal](../winds-of-fortune/Restoration_and_renewal.md#restoring-the-gardens)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per the wind of fortune
- 30 wains of weirwood
- 12 wains of mithril
- 116 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 1 throne and 2 wains of weirwood provided by Anastasia after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown 1 ring provided by Bartimaeus after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Fintan Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Richardo Glostari di Sarvos after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 4 rings in small donations provided after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 3 wains of mithril, 3 thrones 3 crowns 15 rings and 7 wains of weirwood provided by Esteban Ezmara of Sarangrave Reforged after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Juno of Lapis Heights after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Serenus Starsong after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 9 wains of mithril, 9 thrones 2 crowns and 21 wains of weirwood provided by Esteban Ezmara of Sarangrave Reforged after Summer Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Gardener of Pallas](../../mixed/titles/Gardener_of_Pallas.md) will be available to appoint by the senators of Urizen for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 386YE
