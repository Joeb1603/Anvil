# Construct spy network in the Sarangrave

> *In-character text:*
>
> A motion to commission a spy network in the Sarangrave. Costing 10 weirwood, 2 thrones 4 crowns to build, funded by Cyrus of Felix’s Watch. Upkeep costs will be 2 thrones per season.  
> Proposed by Temeschwar, seconded by Karsk

## Overview

- Commissions a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the [Sarangrave](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md#the-sarangrave) in [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md)
- Passed at 40 thrones.

## Date

- Passed Summer Solstice 383YE.

## Costs

- [Standard costs apply](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md#costs)
- 10 weirwood
- 20 crowns
- Upkeep: 2 crowns

## Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood and 20 crowns provided by Giuseppe Sanguineo von Temeschwar after Summer Solstice 383YE.

## Outcome

- Once complete, Imperial [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) will be able to support the spy network.
