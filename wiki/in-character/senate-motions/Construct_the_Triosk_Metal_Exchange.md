>

> > Proposed: Ossium, Seconded: Karsk

## Overview

- Constructs a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) seat in [Volodmartz](../nations/varushka/Volodmartz.md) as per the **[Deep forests and dark hills](../winds-of-fortune/Deep_forests_and_dark_hills.md#the-metal-of-triosk)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per wind of fortune
- 16 wains of mithril
- 6 thrones
- Three months to complete
