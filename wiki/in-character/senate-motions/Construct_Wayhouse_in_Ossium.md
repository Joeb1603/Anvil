# Construct Wayhouse in Ossium

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build a wayhouse in Ossium. To be attached to the Navarr strategos title. This will house refugees from Therunin and assist in funding the Navarr army.
> >
> > Proposed: Miaren, Seconded: Mitwold

## Overview

- Constructs a [Navarr wayhouse](../../mixed/commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) in Nearweald, Ossium.
- Passed
- More information on this motion can be found in the **[The way home](../winds-of-fortune/The_way_home.md)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 mithril
- 6 thrones
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 16 wains of mithril and 5 thrones 7 crowns provided by Gryiff Embercast after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the [Strategos of Navarr](../../mixed/titles/Strategos.md) will gain custodianship of a sinecure and Navarr will be able to take a personal resource in Ossium without suffering the foreign nation penalty.
