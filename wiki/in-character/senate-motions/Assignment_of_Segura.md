# Assignment of Segura

> *In-character text:*
>
> To assign conquered territory of Segura to the Brass Coast.  
>  
> *Proposed Segura, Seconded Kahraman*

*[Image: A battlefield, now a centre of trade again](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Regionsofsegura.png)*

### Overview

- The territory of [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md) was conquered following an extended military campaign

### Date

- Summer 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The territory of Segura is now part of the nation of the Brass Coast.
- It was noted that the territory is not secure; orcs of the Lasambrian orcs still control three of the seven regions. The loss of a single region would result in the loss of this Imperial territory.
