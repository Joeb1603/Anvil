# Construct Rose's Respite

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Announce the building of the "Rose's Respite" a herb sinecure in Astolat costing 16 wains of weirwood. To be elected by senators. This is self funded.
> >
> > Announced by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar, Master of Rings

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in The Chase, Astolat
- Announcement by the **Laughlan Vi Temeschwar**, [Master of Rings](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Rings.md)

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 wains of weirwood
- 6 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 6 thrones and 16 wains of weirwood provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the *Keeper of Rose's Respite* will be available to be appointed by the Senators of Dawn
