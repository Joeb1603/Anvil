# Construct Imperial Consulate

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > That there be a dispersal of funds to commission and fund the creation of the Imperial Consulate building in Tassato
> >
> > Proposed by Tassato, Seconded by Hercynia

## Overview

- Motion to construct an Imperial Consulate as detailed in the **[All the news from Twin Town](../winds-of-fortune/All_the_news_from_Twin_Town.md#establishing-the-consulate)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- Passed at 250 thrones

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 25 wains of weirwood
- 15 wains of white granite
- 80 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months

## Progress

- 10 thrones, 25 wains of weirwood and 15 wains of white granite provided by Cesare Sanguineo Rezia Di Tassato after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- 250 thrones disbursed to the Senator for Tassato
- Once the Consulate is completed the [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md) will begin receiving regular diplomatic briefings and have access to mercantile support similar to that enjoyed by [ambassadors](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md).
