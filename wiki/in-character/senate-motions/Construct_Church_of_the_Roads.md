# Construct Church of the Roads

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a liao sinecure in Bastion, to be appointed by the Highguard National Assembly, the Chaplain of the Roads, to be responsible for the protection of souls travelling on the roads of Highguard. This position should have tenure and is revocable by the Highguard National Assembly. Fully funded.
> >
> > Announced by Diego i Del Toro i Erigo, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Woodbury, [Bastion](../nations/highguard/Bastion.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Winter 386YE

## Cost

- Standard cost apply
- 10 white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Asael after Winter Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Chaplain of the Roads](../../mixed/titles/Chaplain_of_the_Roads.md) will be appointed by the Highborn Assembly for the first time at the Spring Equinox 387YE
