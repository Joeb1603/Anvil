# Construct Floerstrand

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build Floerstrand, a "Hot Spring Hall", in Tanikipari as detailed in last season's appraisal of Sermersuaq's prosperity
> >
> > Proposed: Sermersuaq, Seconded: Hercynia

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Tanikipari, Sermersuaq as per the **[Farm, fur, and fish](../winds-of-fortune/Farm-_fur-_and_fish.md#hostel-and-hot-water)** wind of fortune
- Passed at 15 thrones
- 15 thrones disbursed to Senator for Sermersuaq.

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 20 white granite
- 60 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 7 thrones 3 crowns and 20 wains of white granite provided by Fedelmidd Sydanjaa's Heart Floer after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

Now the Floerstrand is complete, the position of [Thane of Floerstrand](../../mixed/titles/Thane_of_Floerstrand.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous vote of the senators of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE
