# Construct Dima's Light of Ossium

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission Dima's Light of Ossium, a great work on the Bittershore, costing 40 white granite, 10 mithril, 5 ilium, and 150 crowns. Fully self-funded. Many thanks to King-under-the-Mountain and his dependable forekoboldi Drageer for this boon.
> >
> > Announced by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Bittershore, Ossium
- Announcement by **Dima Vasilyevna Novosad**, [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)
- The [plans](../winds-of-fortune/Is_there_anybody_out_there.md#beacon-in-ossium) for the grand beacon were first presented by one of the *koboldi* shortly before the Spring Equinox 387YE

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 40 wains of white granite
- 10 wains of mithril
- 5 rings of ilium
- 150 crowns in labour costs
- Six months to construct

- 9 wains of mithril and 7 crowns 12 rings provided by Kostjya after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 2 wains of white granite provided by Velko Perunovich Razoradze after Autumn Equinox 388YE

- 1 throne provided by Triska Markova after Autumn Equinox 388YE

- 1 crown 15 rings provided by Katya Vasillova Reznikov after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, Dima's Light of Ossium will have the following effects:

- Warns of threats against Kostjyas Respite
- Establishes a beacon in Bittershore, increasing investment of fleets in Ossium by one
  - Any fleet in Ossium undertaking a [trading](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#trading) action does so with a bonus rank
