# Construct Crimson Drummers of Madruga

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > A sinecure to produce liao and synod votes. Title: Crimson Sutannir. Elected by Brass Coast National Assembly. Has tenure. To be built in the territory of Torres in Madruga. To be funded in full by Ahraz i Guerra of the Celesti.
> >
> > Announced by Ahraz i Guerra, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Commissions a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Summer 384YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs) apply
- 12 wains of white granite
- 24 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 12 wains of white granite provided by Ahraz i Guerra after Summer Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the sinecure would create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), the [Crimson Sutannir](../../mixed/titles/Crimson_Sutannir.md) which would be appointed by judgement of the Freeborn National Assembly, and have [tenure](../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure).
