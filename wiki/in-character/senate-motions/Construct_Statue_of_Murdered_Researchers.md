# Construct Statue of Murdered Researchers

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > As Mistress of Monuments, I announce the building of an edifice in Apulian to honour the researchers killed by the Whisper Gallery in the search for the truth. This will be fully funded by Ignacio i Zuhri i Erigo. The Senator for Apulian has consented
> >
> > Announced by Ada Droon Drifting Bough, Mistress of Monuments

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Apulus, Spiral
- Announcement by the [Mistress of Monuments](../../mixed/titles/Mistress_of_Monuments.md)

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 3 wains of mithril
- 1 wain of weirwood
- 1 wain of white granite
- 15 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 wains of mithril, 1 wain of weirwood and 1 wain of white granite provided by Ignacio i Zuhri i Erigo after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 throne 1 crown provided by Firesong Arachas after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 5 crowns provided by Fiorente Vicente d'Apulian after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, a statue will be raised in Apulus, Spiral
