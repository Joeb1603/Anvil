# Fortify Lustri

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a fortification in Lustri, Zenith. Requires Senate funds over the year.
> >
> > Proposed: Semmerholm, Seconded: Weirwater

## Overview

- Passed

## Date

- Winter 384YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#costs) apply
- 80 wains of white granite
- 20 thrones
- One year to complete
- Upkeep is 10 thrones a season

- Now complete, Lustri is protected by *The Stork's Gaze* a 3000 strength [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md).
- While the people of the [Mountains of the Moon](../winds-of-fortune/Write_your_own_ending.md#a-border-27tween-the-mountains-and-the-moon) offered to assist with this project, the Senate has not asked them to participate.
