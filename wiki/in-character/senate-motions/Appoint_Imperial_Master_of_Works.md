# Appoint Imperial Master of Works

> *In-character text:*
>
> Approve Richard Tunstall of the Marches as the Imperial Master of Works.  
>  
> *Procedural Motion.*

### Overview

- First appointment of the [Imperial Master of Works](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Master_of_Works.md).

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Constitution

The Imperial Master of Works is normally appointed during the Autumn Equinox, but the position was vacant. It will be appointed again at the Autumn Equinox 377YE.

### Voting

| State | Votes Against | Votes For | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Appoint Richard Tunstall. | 0 | 25 | Passed |
