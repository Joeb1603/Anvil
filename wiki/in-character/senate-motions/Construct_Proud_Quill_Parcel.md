# Construct Proud Quill Parcel

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Proud Quill Parcel in Kalpaheim, Hahnmark. 16 wains of weirwood. A herb sinecure to be planted in open land behind the Proud Quill Ale Hall by unanimous agreement of Wintermark senators, title to have tenure
> >
> > Announced by Cormack Dun, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a sinecure in Kalpamark, [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 weirwood
- 48 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 6 thrones and 16 wains of weirwood provided by Cormack Dun after Winter Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

Now construction is complete, the [Guard of the Proud Quill Parcel](../../mixed/titles/Guard_of_the_Proud_Quill_Parcel.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the senators of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) for the first time at the Spring Equinox 387YE
