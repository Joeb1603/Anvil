# Amend powers of the Conclave Liaison

> *In-character text:*
>
> That in order that the Conclave Liaison to the Senate is better able to serve the Empire:  
>
> - The Liaison may have the power Advising the Senate, allowing them to attend and speak in Senate sessions, with the intention that this be used for Conclave related discussions
> - That the Liaison may address the Conclave once per summit without having to pay the initial mana cost.
> - That the post of Conclave Liaison to the Senate henceforth be renamed Imperial Magus.  
>  
>   *Proposed by Feroz, Seconded by Morrow*

### Overview

- The abilities of the Conclave Liaison to the Senate were modified.
- The position was renamed [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md)
- Passed in principle

### Date

- Passed Autumn 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The powers of the Conclave Liaison to the Senate were amended, and the name of the title changed.
