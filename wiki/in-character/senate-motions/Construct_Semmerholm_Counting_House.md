# Construct Semmerholm Counting House

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the construction of a Prosperous Counting House in Semmerholm.
> >
> > Proposed: Semmerholm, Seconded: Temeschwar

## Overview

- Constructs a [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md) that increases the Virtue Fund as per the **[Forwards and Rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 5 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 throne provided by Amicia of the Semmerlak after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Gianluca Di Zobbras after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Kostjya after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Berith Relieur after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 2 crowns provided by Triska Markova after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 4 crowns 10 rings and 4 wains of white granite provided by Amicia of the Semmerlak after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 1 ring in small donations provided after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 4 crowns 5 rings, 5 wains of weirwood and 1 wain of white granite provided by Amicia of the Semmerlak after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 7 crowns 4 rings provided by Bertrand Gardener after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete the [Virtue Fund](../../mixed/government/synod/Rewarding.md) receives 11 crowns each summit in donations from citizens of Semmerholm
