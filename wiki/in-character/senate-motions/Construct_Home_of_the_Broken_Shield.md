# Construct Home of the Broken Shield

> *In-character text:*
>
> I, the Civilian Commissioner of the Imperial Orcs commission the construction of the Home of the Broken Shield, a communal home for those Imperial Orcs of the Broken Shield.  
> *Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of the Imperial Orcs*

### Overview

- A [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) that produces herbs in Skogei's Glen, [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- This motion was announced by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [The Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Cost

- 10 wains of weirwood
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood and 20 crowns provided by Bloodcrow Yargol after Winter Solstice 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- Once completed, the construction will automatically create a new national title within the Imperial Orcs, [Broken Shield Guardian](../../mixed/titles/Broken_Shield_Guardian.md).
- Certain necessary details are required before construction can be started.
