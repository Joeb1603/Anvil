# Raise Fourth Highborn Army

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To raise a fourth Highborn army in Casinea
> >
> > Proposed: Casinea, Seconded: Bastion

## Overview

- Raises an [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in Casinea
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[The war of thorns and flowers](../winds-of-fortune/The_war_of_thorns_and_flowers.md#highguard)** wind of fortune
- The cost has been reduced by each of the Generals of Highguard receiving an artefact, hallowed banner as [regalia](../senate-sessions/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#assign-regalia-to-generals-of-highguard)
- The general of the new army will have the artefact, hallowed banner, *Ever Forward*, as regalia

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 80 mithril (reduced from 200 mithril)
- 240 crowns (reduced from 600 crowns)
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 40 wains of mithril and 15 thrones provided by Asael after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 3 crowns 6 rings provided by Soběslava Soběslavna Sovová after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Olyvar of Esther's Sanctum after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Vardas Sentinels Tower after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 31 wains of mithril and 14 thrones 14 rings provided by Asael after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 9 wains of mithril provided by Naomi, Felix Watch after Summer Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now completed, the [General of the Pilgrim's Defiance](../../mixed/government/military/Pilgrim-s_Defiance.md) will be available for election by unanimous decision of the Highborn senators for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 387YE for a shortened term of one season
