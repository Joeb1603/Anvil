# Construct Vallornguard Mustering Ground

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Vallorn Mustering Ground at a cost of 10 weirwood, 10 mithril, and 60 crowns. It will take 3 months to construct, with no additional upkeep costs required. Self-funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Ossium, Seconded: Hercynia

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Holtford, Miaren, as per the **[Every path you know](../winds-of-fortune/Every_path_you_know.md#green-eyed-monster)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 10 wains of mithril
- 60 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 5 wains of mithril, 5 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Kevan Heartsbane after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 5 wains of mithril provided by Corwyn Heartsbane after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 throne provided by Drysfa Broken Bough after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 2 crowns provided by Eleri Bronwen's Rest after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 2 crowns provided by Nathair Autumngale after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [First Thorn of the Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/First_Thorn_of_the_Vallornguard.md) will be available to appoint by the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md) at the Summer Solstice 388YE for a shortened term of nine months
