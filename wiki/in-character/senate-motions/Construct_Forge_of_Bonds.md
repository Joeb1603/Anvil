# Construct Forge of Bonds

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct the Forge of Bonds in the Fingers, Hahnmark. This will create the position of Keeper of Oaths, to be elected by the Loyalty Assembly with tenure. This is a time sensitive opportunity to create an edifice which would allow the Keeper of Oaths to carve one oath per season into the Fingers via a statement of principle. This has been fully funded and there are no upkeep costs
> >
> > Proposed: Kallavesa, Seconded: Temeschwar

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Wood Heath, [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Carved in stone](../winds-of-fortune/Carved_in_stone.md)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 11 wains of white granite
- 33 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 4 thrones 1 crown and 11 wains of white granite provided by Veikko Bondforger after Autumn Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Keeper of Oaths](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_Oaths.md) will be available to appoint for the first time at the Winter Solstice 387YE by the Loyalty Assembly
