# Construct Giritane's Gaze

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I Sagua i'Ezmara, Master of the Koboldi, am hereby announcing the construction of Giritane's Gaze, a great work supporting miners in Weirwater. It will take the form of a large white mandowla statue with glowing eyes. Enchanter Baldwin, senator of Weirwater, has agreed to provide the funding.
> >
> > Announced by Sagua i'Ezmara, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Commissions a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md) as detailed in the **[Stone and stone](../winds-of-fortune/Stone_and_stone.md#the-alabaster-owlbeast)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)

## Date

- Autumn 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the Wind of Fortune
- 40 wains of white granite
- 80 crowns in gold and jewels
- Three months to construct

## Outcome

- Builds an immense white stone statue of a [Mandowla](../world/Legendary_beasts.md#mandowla) to harness the power of a Summer [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md), in [Wickmoor](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md#wickmoor) on top a hill to the northwest of the [Weirwater Vales](../../mixed/titles/Weirwater_Vales.md).
- Plans include the use of two massive [lightstones](../../out-of-character/game-design/Technology.md#lightstones) which will make the colossus especially visible at night
- Now completed, this *Alabaster Owlbeast* provides every [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) in Weirwater with a share in a bounty of 900 rings, representing precious metals and gemstones hewn from the earth alongside the magical materials the mines also produce.
