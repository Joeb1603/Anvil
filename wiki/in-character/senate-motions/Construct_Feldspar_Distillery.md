# Construct Feldspar Distillery

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build Herbsheart/Feldspar Distillery in Skarsind. Creates the title 'Wise Arborist'. This is a type of herb garden sinecure. The title holder must be of the Yerende sept.
> >
> > Announced by Groza, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Creates a sinecure in Solvihill, [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) that produces 3 realmsroot and 3 of each [herb](../../mixed/magic/potions/Herb.md) every season.
- More information can be found in the **[Standing in the sun](../winds-of-fortune/Standing_in_the_sun.md#herbsheart)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 weirwood
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 2 crowns provided by Frith after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 4 thrones and 12 wains of weirwood provided by Irontide Flax after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Wise Arborist](../../mixed/titles/Wise_Arborist.md) will be available to be appointed by the senators of the Imperial Orcs for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE. Imperial Orcs with herb gardens will be able to choose to produce realmsroot when they upgrade their herb gardens. Although there is no legal requirement that the title be held by a member of the [Yerende](../nations/imperial-orcs/Yerende.md) sept, it will not function if it is held by a member of another sept.
