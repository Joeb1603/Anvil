# Construct Sumaah embassy

> *In-character text:*
>
> Permission to build an Embassy for the Sumar Republic plus funding towards the cost of the building.  
>  
> *Proposed by Bastion, seconded by Upwold.*

### Overview

- This motion was defeated

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Costs

- Materials: 25 wains of Weirwood and 25 wains of White Granite ([Standard cost](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md#costs))
- Labour: 12 Thrones
- Time: 1 season
- Upkeep: 5 Thrones

### Campaign Outcome

- This would have allowed the construction of an [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md) with the [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md).
