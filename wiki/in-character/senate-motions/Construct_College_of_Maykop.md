# Construct College of Maykop

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build the College of Maykop in Casinea
> >
> > Proposed by Volodmartz, Seconded by Ossium

## Overview

- Motion to construct a campus for the Halls of Maykop in [Casinea](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md) as detailed in the **[A mountain of vipers](../winds-of-fortune/A_mountain_of_vipers.md#a-scholarly-overture)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 20 wains of white granite
- 10 wains of mithril
- 60 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril, 6 thrones 4 crowns and 20 wains of white granite provided by Dagon Barossa after Winter Solstice 383YE
- 1 throne provided by Richardo Glostari di Sarvos after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the title of [Bursar of the House of Spirits](../../mixed/titles/Bursar_of_the_House_of_Spirits.md) will be created. This will be a national Highborn Bourse position appointed annually through the [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md).
