# Construct Swords of Nearweald

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission and authorise the disbursement of funds for the Swords of Nearweald in Ossium
> >
> > Proposed: Ossium, Seconded: Mournwold

## Overview

- Commissions a fortified barracks in [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md).
- The [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) is detailed in the **[Thousands or more](../winds-of-fortune/Thousands_or_more.md#the-swords-of-nearwald)** Wind of Fortune
- Passed at 150 Thrones

## Date

- Autumn 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 30 wains of mithril
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 120 crowns in labour costs (increased from 100 Crowns due to the [under-developed](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#under-developed) quality of Ossium)
- Three months to construct
- 5 Thrones each season upkeep

## Progress

- 30 wains of mithril provided by Pavuk Vid Strascovich after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 14 thrones 7 crowns and 20 wains of weirwood provided by Vesna Borkovna Prochnost after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, this has removed the [underpopulated](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#underpopulated) quality from [Nearweald](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#nearweald) in [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md)
- While the upkeep continues to be paid, it provides an additional defending order to Varushkan armies in Ossium' *Hospitality of the Boyars*
- While the upkeep continues to be paid, it allows Varushkan armies to be raised in Ossium in nine months rather than a year
