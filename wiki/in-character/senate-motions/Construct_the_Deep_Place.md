# Construct the Deep Place

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct mine workings in the mountains of Brez, to aid the cabalists of Void and maintain vigilance over their doings. A time limited opportunity, creating the title 'Bredavoi Emissary' to be elected by the senators of Varushka.
> >
> > Proposed: Volodmartz, Seconded: Miekarova

## Overview

- Constructs a ministry in [Volodmartz](../nations/varushka/Volodmartz.md) as per the **[The horror and the wild](../winds-of-fortune/The_horror_and_the_wild.md#speakers-of-the-void)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of white granite
- 5 wains of mithril
- 45 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of white granite provided by Marzanna Verchernyaya Zorya after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 5 wains of mithril and 4 thrones 4 crowns provided by Victor Gryllian after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 throne provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Iskra Marzenanova Navratil after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now completed, the position of [Bredavoi Emissary](../../mixed/titles/Bredavoi_Emissary.md) will be available for the senators of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) to appoint for the first time at the Winter Solstice 385YE
