# Construct Tassato Counting House

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Motion to commission and that there be a disbursal of funds to construct a Counting House in Tassato.
> >
> > Proposed: Conscience of the Senate, Seconded: Tassato

## Overview

- Constructs a [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md) that increases the Virtue Fund as per the **[Forwards and Rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 5 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 crown provided by Gianluca Di Zobbras after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Richardo Glostari di Sarvos after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 3 thrones 4 crowns, 5 wains of weirwood and 5 wains of white granite provided by Virgil Sanguineo Rezia di Tassato after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Once the work is complete, the [Virtue Fund](../../mixed/government/synod/Rewarding.md) will receive 16 crowns each summit in donations from citizens of the territory
