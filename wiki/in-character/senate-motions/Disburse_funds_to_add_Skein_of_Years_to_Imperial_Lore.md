# Disburse funds to add Skein of Years to Imperial Lore

> *In-character text:*
>
> Disbursal of funds to place a ritual developed by the halls of knowledge into Imperial Law: Skeins of Years.  
>  
> *Raised by Morrow, seconded by Bregasland*

### Overview

- Request for funds to pay for adding [Skein of Years](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Skein_of_Years.md) to [Imperial Lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list).
- Ritual developed at the [Halls of Knowledge](../../mixed/titles/Provost_of_the_Halls_of_Knowledge.md).

### Date

- Summer 378YE

### Progress

- 10 Thrones disbursed to Senator Morrow

### Outcome

Adding a ritual to Imperial Lore requires a [Declaration of Imperial Lore](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-imperial-lore) from the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md). These funds are intended to pay for the ritual to be added, pending the decision of the Conclave.
