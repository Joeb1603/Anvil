# Amend Aurelie's Garden

> *In-character text:*
>
> To amend the Imperial title of Keeper of Aurelie’s Garden to change the method of appointment. Fixing the nature of the contest in law has made it extremely difficult to elect the title. After extended discussions with Llewellyn Leafstalker, the Constitutional Court asks the Senate to change the title such that henceforth the title be appointed by unanimous decision of the Dawnish and Navarr Senators. The intention is that the senators will ensure that a suitable contest to identify the appropriate candidate is arranged and overseen.  
> *Raised by the Speaker for the Senate*

## Overview

- This administrative motion proposed a change to the method of appointment of the [Keeper of Aurelie's Garden](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_Aurelie-s_Garden.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Outcome

- The Keeper of Aurelie's Garden is now appointed by unanimous decision of the senators of Dawn and Navarr.
