# Raise Freeborn navy

> *In-character text:*
>
> Propose that the Empire construct a navy for the Brass Coast at the shipyards in Madruga.  
>  
> *Proposed by Madruga, seconded by Kahraman*

## Overview

- Raise a new [navy](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) at the [Atalaya shipyards](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#atalaya) in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md)
- Passed at 500 thrones.

## Date

- Winter 378YE

## Cost

- Materials: 200 [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#weirwood)
- Time: 1 year to construct
- Upkeep: 50 thrones per season.
- Special: Thanks to the recent [victories in Segura](../winds-of-fortune/The_Glorious_Advance.md) and a [judgement](../synod-judgements/378YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#statements-of-principle) of the Assembly of [pride](../religion/Pride.md), this navy will require only 200 wains of weirwood rather than the [standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md#costs).

## Progress

- 500 Thrones disbursed to Senator Madruga
- 50 wains of weirwood supplied by Anwar i Del Toro i Riquezza after Winter Solstice 378.
- 50 wains of weirwood supplied by Anwar i Del Toro i Riquezza after Spring Equinox 379.
- 50 wains of weirwood supplied by Anwar i Del Toro i Riquezza after Summer Solstice 379.
- 50 wains of weirwood supplied by Anwar i Del Toro i Riquezza after Autumn Equinox 379.

## Campaign Outcome

- Will create an Imperial navy
- This will automatically create the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of admiral.
