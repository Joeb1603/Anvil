# Construct Beoraidh Trading Post

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > A commission to build a trading post in Mareave in the region of Icarion. To establish a trade of salt for beef with Beoraidh. Fully funded!
> >
> > Proposed: Mareave, Seconded: The Barrens

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Icarion, Mareave, as per the **[A knock at the door](../winds-of-fortune/A_knock_at_the_door.md#salt-and-beef)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 5 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Sandviper Nid after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the Beoraidh Trading Post has had the following effects:

- Increases the income of the [Shepherd of the Southern Herd](../../mixed/titles/Shepherd_of_the_Southern_Herd.md) by 10 crowns each season
- Increases the income of the [Shepherd of the Great Herd](../../mixed/titles/Shepherd_of_the_Great_Herd.md) in Skarsind by 5 crowns each season
- Secures trade between the Unshackled and Beoraidh
