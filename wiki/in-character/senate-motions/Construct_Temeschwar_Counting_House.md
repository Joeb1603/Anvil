# Construct Temeschwar Counting House

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a prosperous counting house in Temeschwar. This will add 14 crowns to the Virtue Fund per summit. Self funded
> >
> > Proposed: Temeschwar, Seconded: Kallavesa

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Forwards and Rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 5 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 14 rings, 5 wains of weirwood and 1 wain of white granite provided by Sebastian Adolphus von Temeschwar after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 4 crowns 6 rings provided by Askien Alanovich Vesimerov after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Giuseppe Sanguineo von Temeschwar after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 4 wains of white granite provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Virtue Fund](../../mixed/government/synod/Rewarding.md) will gain an additional 14 crowns each season from donations to [the Bloodied Rose](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md#the-bloodied-rose) in Temeschwar, starting at the Summer Solstice 388YE
