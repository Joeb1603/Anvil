# Allocate the Singing Caves III

> *In-character text:*
>
> To allocate the Singing Caves Bourse seat.  
>  
> *Proposed by Holberg, seconded by Bregasland*

### Overview

- The [Singing Caves](../../mixed/titles/Singing_Caves.md) in [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) were recovered by the Empire
- During this discussion a motion of [curtailment](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_session.md#curtailment) was passed, but subsequently [vetoed](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#power-of-veto) by [The Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md).
- The motion was withdrawn by Senator Holberg

### Date

- Autumn 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Singing Caves have not been allocated.
