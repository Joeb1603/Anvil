# Construct Eastern Spring Farm

> *In-character text:*
>
> Announcement by bearer of an Imperial Orc Wayleave, Palerictus Krunk. To build a sinecure in the east of Skarsind for the betterment of the spring ritualists within the nation, to create the position Farmer of Eastern Spring Farm.  
> *Imperial Wayleave by Palerictus Krunk*

## Overview

- Commission a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Announcement.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).
- The size of the commission has not been specified.

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 20 crowns provided by Palerictus Krunk after Autumn Equinox 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) will create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), [Farmer of Eastern Spring Farm](../../mixed/titles/Farmer_of_Eastern_Spring_Farm.md)
