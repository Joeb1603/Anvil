# Construct Red Wind Wharfs

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To add the Red Wind Wharfs to the Municipal Docks at Calvos
> >
> > Proposed: Eulàlia i Phovas i Riqueza, Senator for the Madruga, Seconded: Garviel i Abrira i Erigo, Senator for Feroz

## Overview

- Adds an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) to the [Municipal Docks](Construct_Municipal_Docks.md) in Calvos Sound, Madruga
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Mermaids in the basement](../winds-of-fortune/Mermaids_in_the_basement.md#the-red-wind-wharfs)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 5 wains of mithril
- 15 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 throne 7 crowns and 5 wains of weirwood provided by Gisli i Phovas i Guerra after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the wharfs will have the following effects:

- Allows any army with the Forayer quality to move normally between Free Landing and the mainland, ignoring the island quality
- Increases the upkeep of the wharfs by one throne
