# Construct Misericorde Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> An Announcement by bearer of an Imperial Wayleave. Tristan Exile’s End announces the construction of a covered market in the territory of Holberg, to facilitate trade between Holberg citizens and the Sand Fishers. The commission would be managed by the Merchant Prince of the Misericorde Market, a title with lifetime tenure, appointed by the well worn paths of the Senate by unanimous consent of the League Senators. This is self-funded, revocable by the League Assembly and the General Assembly.  
> *Announcement by Tristan Exile’s End, bearer of an Imperial Wayleave*

## Overview

- This takes advantage of the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to [build a market for use by the Sand Fishers orcs](../winds-of-fortune/A_dream_that_can_last.md#green-leaves) at [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md).
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Costs

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/A_dream_that_can_last.md#green-leaves).
- 16 wains of weirwood
- 32 crowns
- Construction takes three months

## Progress

- 2 crowns provided by Tom Bull after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 16 wains of weirwood provided by Beatrix Amalia De Rondell zu Holberg after Winter Solstice 383YE
- 3 thrones 6 crowns provided by Lula Kleist von Ennerlund after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- When complete, the market would create an Imperial title - the [Merchant Prince of Misericorde Market](../../mixed/titles/Merchant_Prince_of_Misericorde_Market.md) - appointed by unanimous decision of the League senators.
- During the Winter Solstice 383YE [an opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/Across_the_Abyss.md#food-that-goes-uneaten) was presented that added a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) to the market allowing [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) and [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md) to be sold to the orcs in return for large amounts of herbs
- It will also create an opportunity for [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) owners in Holberg to diversify their resources to acquire herbs.
