# Construct Lyceum Schloss

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To permit the Commonwealth to build a schloss on the island east of Siroc - Madruga and to cede the schloss to the Commonwealth
> >
> > Proposed by Madruga, Seconded by Karov

## Overview

- Authorises the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) to construct a [schloss](../winds-of-fortune/Large_but_far_away.md#commonwealth) for their citizens who are visiting the Lyceum and cedes the structure to the Commonwealth.
- This construction does not count towards the [limit](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#maximum-commissions-available) on commissions.
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 40 wains of white granite
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 100 crowns in labour costs

## Progress

- 12 thrones 4 crowns, 40 wains of white granite, and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Militärattache Ludkhannah Schöningen after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- Now complete the Lyceum receives a permanent improvement in the speed with which it can [codify](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md#guiding-the-magicians-of-the-lyceum) rituals designed to enchant an army.
