# Create title of Minister of Historical Research

> *In-character text:*
>
> Create a titled position with the authority to perform historical research.  
>  
> Proposed by Morrow, Seconded by Astolat

### Overview

- Create a minister responsible for historical research
- Passed by 19 votes to 6

### Date

- Autumn 377YE

### Cost

- Imperial research requests cost 5 Thrones each.
- The civil service are able to process a maximum five research requests each season. Appointment of an Imperial citizen as minister allows them to authorise any of those five requests - it does not increase the number of requests possible.

### Campaign Outcome

- [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) [Minister of Historical Research](../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) created
- Civil service have requested clarification by the Imperial Senate on how they intended the costs of research requests to be met. A procedural motion has been raised to clarify this issue at the next Senate session.
