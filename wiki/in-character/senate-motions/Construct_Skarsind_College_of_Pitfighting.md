# Construct Skarsind College of Pitfighting

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission a business sinecure, the Skarsind College of Pitfighting, creating the title Pitmaster. 10 wains of white granite, in Skogi, Skarsind.
>  
> *Announced by Palerictus Dura, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave*

## Outcome

- Construct a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Announcement

## Date

- Autumn 383YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply as detailed [here](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).
- 10 wains of [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md)
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- three months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 5 wains of white granite provided by Dura after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 5 wains of white granite provided by Bloodcrow Yargol after Autumn Equinox 383YE

## Campaign Outcome

- Once completed this commission will be overseen by the title [Pitmaster](../../mixed/titles/Pitmaster.md)
