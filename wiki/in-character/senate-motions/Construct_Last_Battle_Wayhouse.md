# Construct Last Battle Wayhouse

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct Last Battle Wayhouse in the Bleaks, the Barrens. The Wayhouse will be a vital strategic tool in our fight against the Druj, providing crucial reconnaissance into Sarangrave. Appointed annually by Generals of Navarr. Self funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Hercynia, Seconded: Semmerholm

## Overview

- Constructs a wayhouse in the Bleaks, the Barrens as per the **[The way home](../winds-of-fortune/The_way_home.md#the-last-battle)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 18 weirwood
- 54 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 13 wains of weirwood provided by Dax after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 3 thrones 7 crowns and 4 wains of weirwood provided by Corwyn Heartsbane after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown and 1 wain of weirwood provided by Aneira Exiles End after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 throne provided by Rowyn of the Waystone Path after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 throne provided by Llandun Waystone after Autumn Equinox 386YE

- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Nathair Autumngale after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the [Keeper of the Last Battle](../nations/navarr/Keeper_of_the_Last_Battle.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the generals of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE. Nesustak Forest and Turan Flats in [Sarangrave](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md) have both gained the [under threat](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#under-threat) quality. As a wayhouse it also allows Navarri characters to have personal resources in the Barrens, benefiting from any [great works](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in the territory and will increase seasonal taxation from the Barrens by one throne.
