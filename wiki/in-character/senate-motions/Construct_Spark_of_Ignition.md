# Construct Spark of Ignition

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Build mana sinecure in Urizen in Whisperwood, Sarangrave. Building - The Spark of Ignition. Title - Lighter of the Forge. Memorial to those who fell defending Sarangrave. Costs 16 wains of mithril and 48 crowns - self funded by Sarangrave Reforged
> >
> > Announced by Berrak of The Phoenix Reach, Crystal Architect of the Spires

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Whisperwood, Sarangrave
- Announcement by **Berrak of The Phoenix Reach**, [Crystal Architect of the Spires](../../mixed/titles/Crystal_Architect_of_the_Spires.md)
- The labour cost is increased by the presence of [outlaw Druj](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#outlaw-druj) in the territory

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 wains of mithril
- 76 crowns and 16 rings in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 6 wains of mithril and 3 thrones 4 crowns 16 rings provided by Able the Taller of Sarangrave Reforged after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 3 wains of mithril and 1 throne 4 crowns 16 rings provided by Esteban Ezmara of Sarangrave Reforged after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 2 wains of mithril and 2 thrones provided by Bartimaeus after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 2 wains of mithril and 1 throne 1 crown 12 rings provided by Abel the Rounder of Sarangrave Reforged after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 wain of mithril and 4 crowns 16 rings provided by Hector of Sarangrave Reforged after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 wain of mithril and 4 crowns 16 rings provided by Terentia of Sarangrave Reforged after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 wain of mithril provided by Vossk of Sarangrave Reforged after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the *Lighter of the Forge* will be available to be appointed by the Senators of Urizen
