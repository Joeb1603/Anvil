# Construct Temple of the Way in Nemoria

> *In-character text:*
>
> To begin construction in Nemoria, capital of Asavea, of a Temple of the Way
>  
>  
> *Proposed by Feroz, seconded by Madruga*

### Overview

- This is relating to the [Temple of Balo and the Black Bull](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#the-temple-of-balo-and-the-black-bull) and the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented [here](../winds-of-fortune/Those_who_worship.md#the-temple-of-the-lords-of-virtue)
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Cost

- 20 wains of white granite
- 10 thrones in emancipated labour costs
- Six months to construct

### Progress

- 20 wains of white granite and 10 thrones provided by Oria i Shartha i Riqueza after Winter Solstice 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- Having been completed, the Temple of the Way has created an Imperial Title of liaison, to ensure the temple receives the valuable [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) required, effectively creating a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) that allows the trade of liao for Asavean trade goods.
- Following the Autumn Equinox 381YE, the method of appointing the liaison was determined as being by [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Appointment.md) of the Freeborn [assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies). It will be available for appointment during the Winter Solstice 381YE
