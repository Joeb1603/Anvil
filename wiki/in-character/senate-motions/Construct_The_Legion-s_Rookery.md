# Construct The Legion's Rookery

> *In-character text:*
>
> A motion to transform Cardinal Atla’s congregation, Hall of White Ravens, into a great work ‘The Legion’s Rookery’ that provides 25 votes and 12 liao distributed amongst congregations in Skarsind.*Proposed by Sermersuaq, Seconded by Hahnmark*

### Overview

- A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) to take advantage of the opportunity that [presented](../winds-of-fortune/Seize_the_fire.md#a-home-for-the-faithful-28opportunity29) itself following the recent changes in [Doctrine](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Spring 381YE

### Cost

- 15 wains of white granite
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 5 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 15 wains of white granite, 5 wains of weirwood, and 5 thrones provided by Atte Arrow-Tongue Metsastajason after Spring Equinox 381YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- Work on this [commission](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) has been completed now that explicit permission from the current owner of the [Hall of White Ravens](../../mixed/government/military/Skarsind_spoils_of_war.md#the-hall-of-white-ravens) has been received by the civil service.
- The [Legion's Rookery](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#legion27s-rookery) is now complete. It provides a share 12 [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) and 25 votes in the Imperial Synod spread amongst those citizens of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) who own [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) each season, following attendance at the Anvil Summit
