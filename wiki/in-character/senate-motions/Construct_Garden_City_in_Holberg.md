# Construct Garden City in Holberg

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the work to transform Holberg into the City of Flowers.
> >
> > Proposed: Holberg, Seconded: Redoubt

## Overview

- Constructs a [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) as per **[The greatest city in the world](../winds-of-fortune/The_greatest_city_in_the_world.md#flower-gardens)** wind of fortune
- Given that this commission requires a total of four commissions over a year the civil service, working with the Senator for Holberg, opted to run the motion as if it were a disbursement of funds with increasing numbers of commissions
- Passed at one commission
- This construction was supported by announcement of **Dima Vasilyevna Novosad**, as [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md), at the [Spring Equinox 386YE](../senate-sessions/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#construct-city-of-flowers)
- This construction was supported by announcement of **Gustavus Tolski van Temeschwar**, as [Chair of the Wolf](../../mixed/titles/Chair_of_the_Wolf.md), at the [Summer Solstice 386YE](../senate-sessions/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#construct-garden-city-of-holberg) and [Autumn Equinox 386YE](../senate-sessions/386YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#construct-garden-city-of-holberg)
