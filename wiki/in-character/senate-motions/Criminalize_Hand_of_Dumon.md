# Criminalize Hand of Dumon

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To make membership and aiding of the Hand of Dumon illegal, and to denounce this cabal of heretics.
> >
> > Proposed Miaren, Seconded Hercynia

## Overview

- The motion creates a law [making it illegal](../../mixed/government/senate/List_of_Criminal_Offences.md#crimes-of-position) to be a member of, or offer aid to, the Hand of Dumon.
- The Hand of Dumon is an [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) conspiracy of magicians dedicated to the so-called dark god of the [Suranni pantheon](../religion/Suranni_pantheon.md). They are not believed to be active within the Empire.
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 384YE.

## Outcome

- The Hand of Dumon is outlawed, as is offering aid to the organisation.
- Magistrate Karkovich advises that the magistrates will treat this crime as being on par with membership of the [vyig](../nations/varushka/Vyig.md) rather than a more serious crime such as treason. Someone found guilty of being a member of the group, or offering aid to it, is likely to receive a fine rather than be executed (unless of course other more serious crimes come to light while investigating the case). Furthermore, [heresy](../../mixed/government/synod/Religious_crime.md#heresy) is a matter for the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) to determine, and so without a [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) of [condemnation](../../mixed/government/synod/Sanction.md#condemnation) from the Synod that element will not be considered during criminal trials.
