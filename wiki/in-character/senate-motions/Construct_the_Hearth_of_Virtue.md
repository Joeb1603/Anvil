# Construct the Hearth of Virtue

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build a Seminary School known as the Hearth of Virtue that none may doubt Varushkas dedication to The Way. This will be built in Perumaki, Miekarova and will be appointed by ?, revocable by the Varsuhkan National Assembly.  
> *Imperial Wayleave by Nikolovich Drakov*

## Overview

- Commission a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md)
- Announcement.

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- This [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).

## Progress

- 20 wains of granite and 8 thrones received from Nikolovich Drakov after the Winter Solstice 382YE.
- 3 thrones returned to Nikolovich Drakov after Summer Solstice 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) will create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), [Tender of the Hearth](../../mixed/titles/Tender_of_the_Hearth.md)
- Appointment is by the Varushkan national assembly, and the title has tenure.
