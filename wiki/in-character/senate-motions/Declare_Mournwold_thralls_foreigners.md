# Declare Mournwold thralls foreigners

> *In-character text:*
>
> To designate the orc thralls in the Imperial territory of Mournwold as foreigners.  
>  
> *Proposed by Upwold, seconded by Segura.*

### Overview

- Decides legal status of ex-Jotun thralls in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The orc thralls in the Mournwold are now foreigners
- It is legal to trade with them, and illegal to force them off their land, kill them, engage them with Imperial armies, or steal their holdings.
