# Construct Kragg's Hollow

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct Kragg's Hollow, a mana sinecure in Skarsind costing 10 wains. Funded with the prosperity of Ashborn Kragg, with thanks to the Imperial Spymaster. Will create the Imperial title Scholar of Kragg's Hollow, to be elected through Conclave.
> >
> > Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad, Arcane Architect

## Overview

- Constructs a mana [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Announcement by the [Arcane Architect](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Architect.md)

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of mithril
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril provided by Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 2 thrones 4 crowns provided by Kostjya after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now that construction is completed the position of [Scholar of Kragg's Hollow](../../mixed/titles/Scholar_of_Kragg-s_Hollow.md) will be available to appoint by the Imperial Conclave using a [Declaration](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Candidacy.md).
