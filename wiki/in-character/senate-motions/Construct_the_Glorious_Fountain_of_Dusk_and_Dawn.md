# Construct the Glorious Fountain of Dusk and Dawn

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create the Sinecure "The Glorious Fountain of Dusk and Dawn" to produce Mana in Morrow. Assigned by Urizen senators. This is self funded.  
>  
> *Proposed by Zenith, seconded by Karsk*

### Overview

- **More details forthcoming**
- The [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) will produce 9 [crystal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) each season

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Costs

- Materials: 10 wains of mithril ([Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs))
- Time: 3 months
- Labour: 20 crowns
- Upkeep: None

### Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 2 Thrones 4 crowns supplied by Cato, senator for Zenith after Autumn Equinox 379.

### Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the sinecure will automatically create a new [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) to oversee it; [Keeper of the Glorious Fountain](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Glorious_Fountain.md).

### Constitution

- Sinecures are life-time appointments.
- The title will be a National position, appointed by unanimous decision of the Senators for [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md).
- The title will be able to be revoked by the Urizen assembly, the General assembly and the Assembly of nine.
