# Establish Imperial Offices

> *In-character text:*
>
> That the Imperial Offices be established in the centre of Anvil
>  
>  
> *Proposed by Hercynia, seconded by Temeschwar*

### Overview

- This motion has authorised the setting up of the [Imperial Offices](../world/Anvil.md#the-imperial-offices) in the centre of Anvil, expected to be used by The [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md)
- Passed
