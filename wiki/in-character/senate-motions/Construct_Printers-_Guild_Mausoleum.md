# Construct Printers' Guild Mausoleum

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I announce the commission of the Printers' Guild Mausoleum, to be constructed in Tassato. This commission will take 20 wains of mithril to construct. All provided in Pride by the Printers' Guild to create a legacy for its members. This commission will create the title of Custodian of the Printers' Guild Mausoleum. The title is to be appointed by League senators. The title is to be a tenured position.
> >
> > Announced by Gustavus Tolski van Temeschwar, Chair of the Wolf

## Overview

- Creates a sinecure in [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md) that produces thirteen mana crystals each season
- Announcement by the [Chair of the Wolf](../../mixed/titles/Chair_of_the_Wolf.md)

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 20 mithril
- 40 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

## Outcome

- Once complete the *[Custodian of the Printers' Guild Mausoleum](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Printers-_Guild_Mausoleum.md)* will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the League senators.
