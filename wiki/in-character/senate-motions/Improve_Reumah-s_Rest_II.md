# Improve Reumah's Rest II

> *In-character text:*
>
> Upgrade of Reumah’s Rest, the fort in Necropolis.
>
> *Announced by the Imperial Master of Works, Marley of Treen*

## Outcome

- This commission increases the strength of the [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) [Reumah's Rest](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#reumah27s-rest) in [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md)
- Announcement

## Date

- Autumn 383YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#improvement)
- 100 wains of [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md)
- 200 crowns of labour
- One year to complete
