# Construct Path to the Lyceum

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Build that Path to the Lyceum. Costs 15 weirwood, 5 rings of ilium, 45 crowns in labour costs. Self-funded by Dean of the Lyceum
> >
> > Announced by Amalia Vicente, Arcane Architect

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Oranseri, Feroz, as per the **[Sound and vision](../winds-of-fortune/Sound_and_vision.md#a-gate-of-mists)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Arcane Architect](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Architect.md)

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 15 wains of weirwood
- 5 rings of ilum
- 15 crowns in labour costs (reduced by the [impoverished](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#impoverished) nature of Fontargenta)
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 5 rings of ilium and 15 wains of weirwood provided by Syl after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 5 crowns 10 rings provided by Lula Kleist von Ennerlund after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 5 crowns 10 rings provided by Bernhard Kleist von Ennerlund after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 4 crowns provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the Path to the Lyceum has restored the connection between Oranseri and the [Lyceum](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md#the-lyceum)
