# Appoint Sarcophan Delves Ambassador

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint an Imperial Citizen to the position of Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves*Administrative Motion*

### Overview

- First appointment of the Ambassador to the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md)
- This motion appoints someone to be responsible for the [Sarcophan Embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md), and to represent the Empire in dealings with the Sarcophan Delves

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Thanmir Hrafnar was appointed as the first [Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sarcophan_Delves.md)
