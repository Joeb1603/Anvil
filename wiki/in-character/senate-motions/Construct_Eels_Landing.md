# Construct Eels Landing

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To develop the docks at Eels Landing in the North Fens, Bregasland. Ceding these docks to House Greywater in recognition of their service during the occupation of Bregasland. To grant the town of Eels Landing a market charter as funded by House Bolholt and the Black Vigil.
> >
> > Proposed: Bregasland, Seconded: Therunin

## Overview

- Establishes the market town of Eel's Landing in the North Fens, [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) as per **[New port, old wine](../winds-of-fortune/New_port-_old_wine.md#eels-landing)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- Charter ratified by Emperor Vesna Borkovna Prochnost

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 6 weirwood
- 6 white granite
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 wains of weirwood and 3 wains of white granite provided by Orrick after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 3 wains of weirwood and 3 wains of white granite provided by Lizzie Stamp of Bildewold after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 4 thrones provided by Willem Tarne after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 3 crowns provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete Eel's Landing will start expanding into a small town. Taxation drawn from Bregasland has been reduced by two thrones a season.
