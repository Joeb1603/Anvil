# Authorise targets of the Imperial Guerdon I

> *In-character text:*
>
> I, Dan Brackensong, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies announce that the Imperial Guerdon will apply to the following armies
>
> The Wolves of War
>
> The Fist of the Mountain
>
> The Winter Sun
>
> The Gryphon's Pride
>
> *Approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies*

### Overview

- This motion was approved by the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) using the legal power to authorise targets of the [Imperial Guerdon](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#imperial-guerdon) granted to them by the Senate

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Owners of [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) who support one of announced armies will receive a share of the Imperial Guerdon
