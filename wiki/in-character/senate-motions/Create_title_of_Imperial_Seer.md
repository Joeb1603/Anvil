# Create title of Imperial Seer

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create the Imperial Position of Imperial Seer to coordinate the use of divination magics.
>
> The position will have the power to address Conclave, The Senate and the Military Council once each per season
>
>  
> *Proposed by Madruga, seconded by Miaren.*

### Overview

- Elected by Declaration of Candidacy in Conclave
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Imperial Seer](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Seer.md) was created.
- The title will be appointed by [declaration of candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy) in the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md).
