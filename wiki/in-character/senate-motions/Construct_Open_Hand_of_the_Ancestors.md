# Construct Open Hand of the Ancestors

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*

> > Build sinecure (congregation) north Solvihill, Skarsind for 10 wains of white granite. The Open Hand of the Ancestors creates title Guide to the Lost. To be appointed by the Imperial Orc National Assembly.
> >

## Overview

- Constructs a sinecure in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Summer 385YE

- 20 crowns in labour costs
