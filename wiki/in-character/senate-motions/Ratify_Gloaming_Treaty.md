# Ratify Gloaming Treaty

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > That the Senate ratifies a treaty with the Faraden. That the Empire removes trade sanctions with the Faraden and moves to a mercantilist trade policy. That "the Gloaming Road" would become a Imperial title, appointed by auction of the seat in line with the well worn path. This will require the Faraden to cede the facility to the Empire and will incur a cost to the Empire of 5 thrones each season to maintain roads and bridges.
> >
> > Proposed Karsk, Seconded Miekarova

## Overview

- The motion presents a [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) negotiated by the [Ambassador to Faraden](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Faraden.md).
- The treaty has two provisions: the Empire will remove [sanctions](../../mixed/government/senate/Tariffs.md#sanctions) on trade with [Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md), moving them to [mercantilist](../../mixed/government/senate/Tariffs.md#mercantilist) status.
- The [gloaming road](../../mixed/economy/Overseer_of_the_Gloaming_Road.md) becomes an Imperial title appointed by [Auction of the Seats](../../mixed/economy/Auction_of_the_Seats.md).
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 384YE.

## Outcome

- The treaty has been enacted by the Civil Service and new trade status has been assured.
- The [Overseer of the Gloaming Road](../../mixed/economy/Overseer_of_the_Gloaming_Road.md) title is now an Imperial title, appointed by [Auction of the Seats](../../mixed/economy/Auction_of_the_Seats.md), and will be appointed for the first time at Winter 384YE. Therafter it will follow the usual appointment cycle for a seat or ministry that provides [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md), being [auctioned](../../mixed/economy/Auction_of_the_Seats.md) during the Autumn Equinox.
- The Senate has undertaken to upkeep roads and bridges that support the trade route, with costs of 5 Thrones per season
