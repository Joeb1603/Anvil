# Fortify Ruicraic's Redoubt

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a fortification at Ruicraic's Redoubt, Eoradal as per the opportunity identified by the Tamazi sept in Autumn 386YE. This is fully funded by the Imperial Orc nation with help from Reumah's Redoubt. This is in Mareave.
> >
> > Proposed: Skarsind, Seconded: Mareave

## Overview

- Constructs a [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in Eoradal, Mareave
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[We all stand](../winds-of-fortune/We_all_stand.md#storm-lords-sentinel)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 50 white granite
- 30 weirwood
- 36 thrones (increased by the [under-developed](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#under-developed) nature of Icarion)
- 8 thrones upkeep
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 18 thrones, 15 wains of weirwood and 25 wains of white granite provided by Ehud after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Karrak after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 17 thrones 5 crowns, 15 wains of weirwood and 25 wains of white granite provided by Karrak after Autumn Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, Rucraic's Redoubt stands in Eoradal, Mareave, as a rank one fortification
- This fortification could also serve as an [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md) to Skoura if agreed with that foreign nation
