# Construct Master Apothecary's Garden

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create a herb garden sinecure in Wintermark for the purpose of potion manufacture to benefit the troops of the Empire.  
>  
> *Proposed by Hahnmark, seconded by Astolat*

### Overview

- **More details forthcoming**
- Passed at 36 Thrones
- The [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) will produce 21 [herbs](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md#herbs) each season

### Date

- WInter 378YE

### Costs

- Materials: 12 wains of weirwood ([Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs)).
- Time: 3 months to construct per 50 wains of materials used
- Labour: 2 crowns per wain
- Upkeep: None

### Progress

- 12 wains of weirwood supplied by Hengest Dun of Wintermark after Winter Solstice 378
- 24 crowns supplied by Hengest Dun of Wintermark after Winter Solstice 378

### Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the sinecure will automatically create the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Master Apothecary](../../mixed/titles/Master_Apothecary.md).

### Constitution

- Sinecures are life-time appointments.
