# Construct Ratibor's Gate Shrine

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Consecrate Ratibor's Gate and construct an inspirational tomb in Temeschwar, costing 20 wains of white granite and 5 thrones, plus one dose of true liao. This will create the title "Watcher of Ratibor's Torch", to be appointed by the League National Assembly.
> >
> > Proposed Temeschwar, Seconded Casinea

## Overview

- Commissions the rededication of Ratibor's Gate in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) as an [inspirational memorial](../../mixed/government/synod/Inspirational_location.md) dedicated to the exemplar [Ratibor of Temeschwar](../religion/Ratibor_of_Temeschwar.md)
- Discussion of Ratibor's inspirational tomb, and the significance of Ratibor's Gate, can be found [here](../religion/Ratibor_of_Temeschwar.md#inspirational-memorial).
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 20 wains of white granite
- 40 crowns in labour costs,
- A dose of [True Liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md#true-liao)
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 5 thrones and 20 wains of white granite provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Summer Solstice 384YE

- A dose of true liao was provided by Laughlan of the Crimson Reaper Cartel

## Outcome

- Once completed created an inspirational tomb, and the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Watcher of Ratibor's Torch](../../mixed/titles/Watcher_of_Ratibor-s_Torch.md).
