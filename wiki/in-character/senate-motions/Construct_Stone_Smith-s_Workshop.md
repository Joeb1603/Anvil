# Construct Stone Smith's Workshop

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a sinecure - Stone Smith's Workshop - in the Woldstone, Ashbrook, Upwold. Sinecure is to be a place where smiths can congregate, discuss, act as a repository of lore, and be a place to train new smiths. Sinecure to create the Stone Smith position, to be appointed by tally of the votes. This sinecure is self funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Mitwold, Seconded: Upwold

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Ashbrook, Upwold
- Passed
- More information on the smiths can be found on the [smith](../nations/the-marches/Smith.md) page and the **[A pinch of salt](../winds-of-fortune/A_pinch_of_salt.md)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 8 white granite
- 8 mithril
- 8 ilium
- 6 thrones
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 8 rings of ilium and 8 wains of white granite provided by Gemma Brightsmith after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 6 thrones provided by Orrick after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 4 wains of mithril provided by Orrick after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 2 wains of mithril provided by Watkin after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 wain of mithril provided by Annie Webster after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 wain of mithril provided by Willum of Goatsbridge after Summer Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Stone Smith](../../mixed/titles/Stone_Smith.md) will be available for election by Tally of the Votes of Marcher [farm](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) owners for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 387YE
