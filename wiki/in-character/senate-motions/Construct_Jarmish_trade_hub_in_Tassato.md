# Construct Jarmish trade hub in Tassato

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build a Jarmish trade hub in Tassato.
> *Proposed by Tassato, Seconded by Holberg*

### Overview

- A trade hub for traders from the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) who wish to use the [mana exchange](../../mixed/titles/Steward_of_the_Tassato_Mana_Exchange.md#tassato-mana-exchange) in the city of Enterio, [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Cost

- Unknown; met by Jarmish traders.

### Progress

- Completed shortly before Spring Equinox 381YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The Jarmish traders have constructed a [lodge](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md#the-jarmish-lodge) in [Enterio](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md#enterio), Tassato.
