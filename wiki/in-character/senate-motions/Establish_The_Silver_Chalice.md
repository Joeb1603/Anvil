# Establish The Silver Chalice

> *In-character text:*
>
> To found a new Conclave order called ‘The Silver Chalice’. The Order will focus on working with and expanding healing magic and other projects which can be used to aid the bodies and minds of Imperial Citizens, the Nations, and the Empire.  
>  
> *Proposed by Therunin, Seconded by Segura.*

### Overview

- This motion was to create a new [Conclave order](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave_order.md), the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md).
- Passed

### Date

- Summer 381YE

### Progress

- 30 wains of mithril provided by Raewynn Farkas after Spring Equinox 382YE.
- 60 crowns provided by Lisabetta Giacomi von Holberg after Spring Equinox 382YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) of the Silver Chalice will now receive the ability to [fund the order](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md#funding-the-order). The ability to create the [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) requires 30 wains of mithril and 60 crowns. As the senate had a commission free, it does not require an additional motion to begin construction of the ministry.
- The Silver Chalice requires both a manifesto and a symbol suitable for use with [Arcane Mark](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Arcane_Mark.md).
