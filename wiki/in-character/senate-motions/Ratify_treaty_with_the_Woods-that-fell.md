# Ratify treaty with the Woods-that-fell

> *In-character text:*
>
> To ratify the treaty with the southern Woods-That-Fell Feni tribe.  
> *Proposed by Upwold, seconded by Temeschwar.*

*[Image: Treaty composed by the Imperial Consul.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Feni_Treaty_A382_YE.jpeg)*

## Overview

- The [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) was composed by the [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md).
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This treaty incurs a cost of 10 Thrones to the [Imperial treasury](../../mixed/economy/Imperial_treasury.md)

## Progress

- Landon of Longridge donated 10 Thrones to the Imperial treasury to cover the costs involved in this treaty.

## Campaign Outcome

- In the absence of an [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md), it is the responsibility of the [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md) to inform the [Feni](../nations/the-marches/Feni.md) of military action. (**OOC Note:** The Imperial Consul can email Profound Decisions at plot@profounddecisions.co.uk to inform them that the bag of food is being left at the crossroads. No other information can be included in this instruction).
