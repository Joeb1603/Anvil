# Construct Spy Network in Forest of Ulnak

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct a spy network in Forest of Ulnak. To speed destruction of the vile fear cult formerly known as the Druj. In the interest of Imperial security, stability and victory. Through virtue's illumination comes a prosperous future.
> >
> > Announced by Bernard Dugdale, Imperial Spymaster

- Constructs a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the [Forest of Ulnak](../barbarians/Forest_of_Ulnak.md)
- Announcement by the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md)

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 2 crowns and 1 wain of weirwood provided by Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 throne provided by Triska Markova after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Nathair Autumngale after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 9 wains of weirwood provided by Bernard Dugdale after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 7 crowns provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Ivana Petara Vodjavic after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Once completed military units will be able to support this spy network to uncover information about the Forest of Ulnak in the Mallum
