# Construct the Stones from Blood

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build Stones from Blood, a mana ministry for the Navarr vates council. This is to support the Navarr vates in organisation and in providing mana for national rituals such as Dance of Navarr and Thorn. It is expected the holder will take an oath.
> >
> > Proposed: Miaren, Seconded: Conscience of the Senate

## Overview

- Creates a ministry in [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md) for the purchase of mana crystals
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 12 wains of mithril
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 5 wains of mithril and 4 thrones 4 crowns provided by Bledri Eternal after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 5 wains of mithril provided by Eleri Bronwen's Rest after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 wains of mithril provided by Cara after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now complete the [Factor of Stones from Blood](../../mixed/titles/Factor_of_Stones_from_Blood.md) will be available to be elected by the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) of Navarr for the first time at the Winter Solstice 385YE
