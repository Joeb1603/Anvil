# Construct Cathedral of the Navigators

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct the Cathedral of the Navigators, a Temple of the Way, in the Sarcophan Delves, creating the title "Bishop of the Navigators".
> > Appointment to be through the General Assembly of the Synod
> >
> > Proposed by Temeschwar, Seconded by Casinea

*[Image: Final impression of the planned cathedral of the Navigators.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Starcathedral.jpg)*

## Overview

- Motion to construct a Temple of the Way in the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) as detailed in the **[All at sea](../winds-of-fortune/All_at_sea.md#the-temple-of-the-way-in-sarcophan)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- Passed at 50 thrones

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 40 wains of white granite
- 30 wains of mithril
- 140 crowns in labour costs
- 6 months to construct

## Progress

- 30 wains of mithril, 17 thrones 4 crowns and 40 wains of white granite provided by 'Shacklebreaker' Thanmir Hrafnar after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- 50 thrones disbursed to the Senator for Temeschwar
- Once the Cathedral of the Navigators is completed the [Bishop of the Navigators](../../mixed/titles/Bishop_of_the_Navigators.md) will be able to be appointed by the [General Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly)
