# Construct Spider's Dream Bridge

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct Spider's Dream Bridge with the additional 20 white granite fortification. Requesting funds.
> >
> > Proposed: Necropolis, Seconded: Segura

## Overview

- Repairs the damage done to the Spider's Dream Bridge as per the **[Dreaming in strands](../winds-of-fortune/Dreaming_in_strands.md)** wind of fortune
- Passed at 240 thrones
- This motion was [amended later](../senate-sessions/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-spiders-dream-construction) in the season to remove the fortification
- 240 thrones disbursed to Senator for Necropolis.

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 30 wains of mithril
- 10 wains of white granite
- 15 thrones in labour costs
- three months to construct

## Progress

- 15 wains of mithril, 15 thrones and 10 wains of white granite provided by Eli of the Cenotaph after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 15 wains of mithril provided by Giacomo Corvinoscuro after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete, Imperial armies can again march quickly from Sarvos and Tassato to Madruga and vice versa. In addition, the [Spider's Tollkeep](../../mixed/titles/Spider-s_Tollkeep.md) once again receives their seasonal income of two thrones.
