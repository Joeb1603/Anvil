# Grant permission to contact Jotun

> *In-character text:*
>
> Grant permission to the Military Council to contact the Jotun, to agree Terms of Engagement for the resumption of conflict after the expiry of the ceasefire.  
>  
> *Proposed by Semmerholm, seconded by Segura*

### Overview

- The Senate has chosen to [abrogate](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#abrogation) the [Jotun ceasefire](Accept_Jotun_ceasefire.md).
- The ritual [Call Winged Messenger](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md) can be used to send a letter to anyone whose name and physical location is known to the caster
- Passed in principle

### Date

- Summer 379YE

### Cost

- Call Winged Messenger is an Autumn ritual of sixth magnitude; a coven who has mastered the ritual can perform it for 3 crystal mana per casting
