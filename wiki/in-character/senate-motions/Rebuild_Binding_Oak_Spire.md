# Rebuild Binding Oak Spire

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission to rebuild the Binding Oak Spire, an edifice in Proceris, Zenith. This will increase production of the Arch of the Sky and allow purchase of additional white granite per opportunity identified by the Architect of Hammers. This will include the removal of investment for businesses in Zenith from the Bridge of the Air to halve the cost of additional wains. Fully self-funded by Sarangrave Reforged
> >
> > Proposed: Velasco i Guerra, Senator for Kahraman, Seconded: Bartimaeus, Senator for Sarangrave

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Proceris, Urizen
- The design for the spire was [drawn up](../winds-of-fortune/A_friend_like_you.md#arch-of-the-sky-estavus) by *Architect Noether*, following the [Architect of Hammers](../../mixed/titles/Architect_of_Hammers.md) announcement last season.
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 70 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 70 thrones provided by Able the Taller of Sarangrave Reforged after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the [Arch of the Sky](../../mixed/titles/Arch_of_the_Sky.md) will produce an additional two wains of white granite and the custodian will be able to purchase additional wains of white granite
- The investment of [businesses](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in Zenith has been removed as the wealth has been redirected to support the workers of the quarry
