> *In-character text:*
>

## Overview

- Constructs a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in Tsirku
- Announcement by the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md)

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Aengel Dunn after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) will be able to support the spy network to investigate Tsirku
