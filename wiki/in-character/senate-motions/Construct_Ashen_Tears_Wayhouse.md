# Construct Ashen Tears Wayhouse

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Ashen Tears wayhouse to be built in Skogei, Skarsind for 16 wains of white granite and 6 thrones. Keeper of the Ashen Tears Wayhouse to be appointed by Navarr senators.
> >
> > Announced by Firesong Arachas, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Constructs a [wayhouse](../../mixed/commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) in Skogei Glens, [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 white granite
- 48 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 6 thrones and 16 wains of white granite provided by "Grim" Grimmeth Ashen-Tear after Summer Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Keeper of the Ashen Tears](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Ashen_Tears.md) will be available to be appointed by unanimous vote of the senators of Navarr for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 387YE
