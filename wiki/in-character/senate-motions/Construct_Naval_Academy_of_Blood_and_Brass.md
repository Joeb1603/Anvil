# Construct Naval Academy of Blood and Brass

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct the Naval Academy of Blood and Brass as a school of shipbuilding and international trade in the city of Siroc using the designs of Frederick di Sarvos. This Great Work will be half funded as a Butchers Benevolence by Lorenzo La Volpe von Temeschwar and half funded by the New Promise Cartel of the Brass Coast as a gift to the Empire to celebrate the victory over the Grendel of Dubhtraig.  
> *Proposed by Feroz, seconded by unknown.*

## Overview

- Commission a school of shipbuilding in [Siroc](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc) in line with [plans proposed by Frederick di Sarvos](../winds-of-fortune/Focused_and_methodical.md#siroc-school-of-shipbuilding-and-international-trade).
- Failed

- Winter 382YE
