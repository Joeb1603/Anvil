# Appraisal of Bite legalisation

> *In-character text:*
>
> To use the power of appraisal to assess the economic impact of legal bite production.  
> *Proposed by Sarvos, seconded by Temeschwar.*

## Overview

- Commissions an [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) from the civil service, relating to the [illegal](Create_law_prohibiting_Bite.md) drug known as "bite."
- Only one appraisal may be carried out each season.
- Failed
