# Construct Golden Bee Assayer's Office

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The people of Karov wish to self-fund and build the Golden Bee Assayer's Office. This will increase tax from Karov by 2 thrones per season. This also creates a national sinecure gaining 5 ingots of orichalcum, weltsilver, and green iron per season. This is time-sensitive to this season only!
> >
> > Proposed: Miekarova, Seconded: Karov

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Duzekani, Karov, as per the **[Outside the wall](../winds-of-fortune/Outside_the_wall.md#golden-bee-assayers-office)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 14 wains of mithril
- 42 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the Golden Bee Assayer's Office will have the following effects:

- The Golden Bee Assayer will be available to appoint by Tally of the Votes of Varushkan [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) owners.
- Taxation from Karov will increase by two thrones
