# Restore the Throne Room

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > For the restoration of the throne room in the Castle of Thorns within Withy, Astolat, which is a fortification. The cost of the restoration will be 15 white granite and 45 crowns. This has been secured with thanks to the Castellan of Spiral Castle and houses of Dawn. When consecrated with true liao, the work will generate historical research into the early monarchs of Dawn. This work will take three months to complete
> >
> > Proposed: Astolat, Seconded: Mitwold

## Overview

- Amends [the Castle of Thorns](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md#the-castle-of-thorns) in Withy, Astolat, to restore the throne room
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Dog days are over](../winds-of-fortune/Dog_days_are_over.md#the-throne-of-glory)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 15 white granite
- 45 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 crown and 5 wains of white granite provided by Tallis Hart after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 3 thrones and 4 wains of white granite provided by Berith Relieur after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 4 wains of white granite provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 2 wains of white granite provided by Morien de Carsenere after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 2 thrones provided by Katerina Walker after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Thalen Elric after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Summer Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, it will reduce the cost to upgrade [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) by one white granite for a year (to a minimum of one wain)
- *Hugh de Veran* will complete and publish [historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) concerning the pre-Imperial [monarchs of Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn_monarchy.md)
