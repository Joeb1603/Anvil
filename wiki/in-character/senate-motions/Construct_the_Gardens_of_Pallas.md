# Construct the Gardens of Pallas

> *In-character text:*
>
> I, Juliana of Phoenix Reach, the Civilian Commissioner of Urizen have commissioned.
>
> A herb Sinecure, the Garden of Pallas.
>
> Located in Zenith.
>
> Creates the Imperial Title of Head Gardener of Urizen.
>
> *Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of Urizen*

### Overview

- A [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md)
- This motion was approved by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Cost

- 20 wains of weirwood
- 5 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 20 wains of weirwood and 5 thrones provided by Juliana after 380YE Winter Solstice

### Campaign Outcome

- This sinecure created the [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) national title of [Head Gardener of Urizen](../../mixed/titles/Head_Gardener_of_Urizen.md).
- In Autumn 381YE, the title was [amended](Amend_Head_Gardener_of_Urizen.md) by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
