# Create title of Bursar

> *In-character text:*
>
> Create a position of Bursar within the Conclave.  
>  
> Proposed by Kahraman, seconded by Redoubt.

### Overview

- Create a title within the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) in support of prior [declaration](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-concord)
- Passed by 27 votes to 0

### Date

- Summer 2013

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Bursar of the Conclave](../../mixed/titles/Bursar_of_the_Conclave.md) was created.

### Constitution

Members of the Conclave were advised that any attempt to limit the remit of the Bursar's powers - or to make them answerable to the Archmage would be unconstitutional. Likewise, attempts to grant the Bursar access to the [Bourse private auction](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#access-to-the-private-auction) are not legal.
