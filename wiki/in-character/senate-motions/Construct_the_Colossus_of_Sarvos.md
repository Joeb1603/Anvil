# Construct the Colossus of Sarvos

> *In-character text:*
>
> Senate Announcement on behalf of Lorenzo 'La Volpe', Civilian Commissioner of The League:
>
> To commission construction in the City of Sarvos of a Great Work to be known as
> “The Colossus of Sarvos”  
> To celebrate the liberation of Sarvos and commemorate those heroic citizens who fell defending their city. To be funded by the Bloody Butchers Guild and other loyal citizens of The League.
>  
> Construction to be overseen by Lorenzo 'La Volpe' Overseer-General of the Blood Red Roads, Patron of Imperial Art and The Empire's First 'Beacon of Pride'.
>
> *Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of The League*

*[Image: Colossus.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Colossus.jpg)*

### Overview

- A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md)
- This motion was approved by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [The League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Cost

- [Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs) apply

### Progress

- 40 wains of white granite and 10 thrones provided by Lorenzo “La Volpe” Macelliao von Temeschwar after Autumn Equinox 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- Work was completed on the [Colossus](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#the-colossus-of-sarvos) shortly before the Winter Solstice 380YE
- This great work provides additional income to all citizens who own a [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) and who attended the previous Anvil summit.
