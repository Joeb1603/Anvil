# Construct Bounders Hall of Meade

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the building of a Bounders Hall in Meade. This will create a Marcher National title of the "Bailiff of Meade" appointed by Marcher senators. This was funded by the prosperous house of Talbot and the House Mudd, both of Mitwold.
> >
> > Announced by Protectorate Thenrik, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Constructs a Bounder's Hall in [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md) as per the **[Kith and kin](../winds-of-fortune/Kith_and_kin.md#hard-work)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 white granite
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 12 wains of white granite provided by Matthias Talbot after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 4 thrones 4 crowns provided by Thomas Oak after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the position of [Bailiff of Meade](../../mixed/titles/Bailiff_of_Meade.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous vote of the senators of [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE. Marcher [beaters](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#beaters) can now send their [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to defend Mitwold.
