# Raise Fourth Navarr Army

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To raise the fourth Navarr army mustering in Oakways, Miaren. This will be army built to fight the vallorn with the support of an eternal such as Llofir, Kaela, or other options being pursued.
> >
> > Proposed: Rowyn of the Waystone Path, Senator for Hercynia, Seconded: Thalia of Netherwatch, Senator for Redoubt

## Overview

- Raises an [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in Miaren
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[The first shot](../winds-of-fortune/The_first_shot.md#hercynia)** wind of fortune
