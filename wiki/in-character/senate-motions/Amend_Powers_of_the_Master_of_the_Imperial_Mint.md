# Amend Powers of the Master of the Imperial Mint

> *In-character text:*
>
> Grant a the Master of the Mint the following additional power: Emergency Deficit Powers. In the event of a predicted deficit, the Master of the Mint is able to choose in which order specific Armies, Navies, Fortresses and Imperial Titles lapse sufficient to balance the budget.  
>  
> Proposed by Sarvos, seconded by Feroz.

### Overview

- Amend the powers of the [Master of the Imperial Mint](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md) to allow them to decide on the order assets are disbanded in the event of a deficit.
- Defeated

### Date

- Spring 378YE

### Voting

| State | Votes For | Votes Against | Result |

| In Principle | 0 | U | Failed |

### Constitution

As the Senate have indicated that there is some interest in ensuring good oversight of this process, the civil service are looking to find a practical way to transfer this ability directly to the Senate.
