# Appoint Sarcophan Delves Ambassador II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint an Imperial Citizen to the position of Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves  
> *Proposed by Necropolis. seconded by Reikos*

### Overview

- This motion appoints someone to be responsible for the [Sarcophan Embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md), and to represent the Empire in dealings with the Sarcophan Delves

### Date

- Autumn 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Thanmir Hrafnar was appointed as [Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sarcophan_Delves.md)
