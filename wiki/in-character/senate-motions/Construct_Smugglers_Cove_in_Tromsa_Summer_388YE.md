# Construct Smugglers Cove in Tromsa Summer 388YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a smugglers' cove in Tromsa
> >
> > Proposed: Mel Bleak, Senator for Mournwold, Seconded: Ramos I Inigo I Raqueza, Senator for Kahraman

## Overview

- Constructs a [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) in [Tromsa](../barbarians/Tromsa.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Beneath a moonless sky](../winds-of-fortune/Beneath_a_moonless_sky.md)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 14 wains of white granite
- 42 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 5 crowns provided by Rahmah i Darwisj i Erigo after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 10 wains of white granite provided by Rahmah i Darwisj i Erigo after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the smugglers' cove will have the following effects:

- [Fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) from across the Empire will be able to take the **Scout Smugglers Cove in Tromsa** voyage
- [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) fleets will be able to take the **Sail into the Cold** voyage
