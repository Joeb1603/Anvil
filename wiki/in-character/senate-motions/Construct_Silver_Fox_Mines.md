# Construct Silver Fox Mines

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Opportunity to build the Silver Fox Mines, to support businesses and mines in Temeschwar. It is a great work and costs 50 weirwood, 50 white granite, and 25 thrones. It increases taxation by 5 thrones. Named the Silver Fox Mines and sponsored by the Crimson Reapers.
> >
> > Announced by Alexnadro von Temeschwar, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Locul, [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)
- More information can be found in the **[Don't blame me](../winds-of-fortune/Don-t_blame_me.md#tear-out-the-earths-heart)** wind of fortune
- This commission has lapsed as of the start of the Summer Solstice 388YE

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 50 weirwood
- 50 white granite
- 300 crowns
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 12 thrones 4 crowns provided by Descent Guild of Ore Extraction after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Casha di Apulian after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the mines and businesses of the territory will provide 36 rings and two random ingots, respectively
- The [Custodian of the Assayer's Guild](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Assayer-s_Guild.md) will gain two additional ranks to their ministry
- Taxation from Temeschwar will increase by five thrones a season
