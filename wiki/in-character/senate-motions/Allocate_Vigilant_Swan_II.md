# Allocate Vigilant Swan II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To allocate the Vigilant Swan bourse seat  
> *Proposed by Bastion, Seconded by Necropolis*

### Overview

- Passed as a National position.

### Date

- Winter 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Bourse seat connected to the [Vigilant Swan](../../mixed/titles/Vigilant_Swan.md) has been appointed. The seat will be up for re-election at the Summer Solstice 380YE.

### Constitution

- This motion should have been to allocate the Vigilant Swan as either a national or an Imperial seat.
