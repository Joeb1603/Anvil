# Appoint Axos Ambassador

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint an Imperial Citizen to the position of Ambassador to Axos*Administrative Motion*

### Overview

- First appointment of the [Ambassador to Axos](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Axos.md)
- This motion appoints someone to be responsible for the Axos [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md), and to represent the Empire in dealings with [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md).

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Tarquinius of Ankarien was appointed as the first [Ambassador to Axos](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Axos.md)
