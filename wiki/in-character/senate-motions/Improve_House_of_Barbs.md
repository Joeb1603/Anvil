# Improve House of Barbs

> *In-character text:*
>
> To upgrade a sinecure (House of Barbs) in Hercynia, Navarr, to increase the production of Liao and votes. Sinecure to produce 7 Liao and 14 votes per season. Cost: 2 wains of white granite, 4 crowns. (Self-funded.)  
>  
> *Proposed by Semmerholm, seconded by Madruga.*

## Overview

- To improve the [House of Barbs](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Barbs.md#the-house-of-barbs) such that the [Voice of Barbs](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Barbs.md) would gain an additional 2 liao and 4 votes in the Imperial Synod.
- While the motion passed, there were insufficient opportunities for the Senate to commission work available, so the motion failed.
