# Construct Atalaq Tradehall

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Atalaq Tradehall, a great work in Sealtoq to help the investment of businesses in Sermersuaq. 50 weirwood, 150 crowns. Lapses this season. Seeking funds.
> >
> > Proposed: Fedelmidd Sydanjaa's Heart Floer, Senator for Sermersuaq, Seconded: Mel Bleak, Senator for Mournwold

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Sealtoq, Sermersuaq
- The tradehall was most recently suggested as part of the [appraisal](../winds-of-fortune/The_circle_game.md#fish-and-fur-in-sermersuaq) to identify alternate trades that ministries could make that would not involve paying coin. The appraisal diverted slightly into previous opportunities to increase the funds flowing into the Anvil economy.
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 50 wains of weirwood
- 150 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 4 thrones 2 crowns and 50 wains of weirwood provided by Jambert Wulfgar after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 14 thrones provided by Kanute Adelaar van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Otmar van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Kitty Adelaar van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the Atalaq Tradehall will have the following effects:

- Increases investment in businesses in Sermersuaq by one.
- Increase taxation from Sermersuaq by 4 thrones.
- Increases the income of the [Custodian of the Bloody Great Sausage](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Bloody_Great_Sausage.md) by 7 crowns
- Boosts income of Wintermark fleet owners by 36 rings for one year whenever they engage in trade with foreign nations
