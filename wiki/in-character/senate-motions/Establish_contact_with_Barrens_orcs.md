# Establish contact with Barrens orcs

> *In-character text:*
>
> To send Winged Messengers to the unaligned Orcs of the Barrens, inviting them to send a delegation to Anvil  
>  
> *Proposed by Skarsind, seconded by Sermersuaq*

### Overview

- There are believed to be independent orcs in [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) engaged in conflict with the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md)
- The ritual [Call Winged Messenger](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md) can be used to send a letter to anyone whose name and physical location is known to the caster
- Passed in principle; no funding requested

### Date

- Summer 378YE

### Cost

- Call Winged Messenger is an Autumn ritual of magnitude 6; a coven who has mastered the ritual can perform it for 3 crystal mana per casting
