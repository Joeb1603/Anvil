# Provide aid to the Hierro Clan

> *In-character text:*
>
> Authorise the civil service to provide aid to the Hierro clan refugees in Segura.
>
> Costed at 75 thrones per season.
>
> *Proposed by Feroz, seconded by Segura.*

### Overview

- This motion was to provide [vital supplies](../winds-of-fortune/The_dead_tree_gives_no_shelter.md#supplies) to the Hierro Clan currently camped in [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md).
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Cost

- Upkeep: 75 thrones per season

### Campaign Outcome

- The Hierro Clan have begun to receive supplies and have not, as of yet, had to resort to banditry.
