# Construct mana repository in Morrow

> *In-character text:*
>
> To develop the mana crystal capacity of Morrow.

> The owner of the Sinecure, the Augur of the Phoenix Caverns, whose duties will be to manage the caverns, will be appointed by Urizen Senators in unanimity, falling back to a greater majority nomination by the senate.
