# Construct the expansion of Spiral Castle

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission the expansion of Spiral Castle, Weirwater such that the area possible to implement resupply includes the Territories adjacent to Weirwater.*Proposed by Astolat, Seconded by Tassato*

### Overview

- The [Castellan of Spiral Castle](../../mixed/titles/Castellan_of_Spiral_Castle.md) is able to utilise their power of [resupply](../../mixed/titles/Castellan_of_Spiral_Castle.md#resupply) on any army that spends the entire season in either [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md), [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md), [Karov](../nations/varushka/Karov.md), [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md) or [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md).
- Passed

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Cost

- The upkeep of Spiral Castle has increased from 7 Thrones to 10 Thrones per season.

### Campaign Outcome

- The Castellan of Spiral Castle now has he power to resupply an army in a territory adjacent to Weirwater.
