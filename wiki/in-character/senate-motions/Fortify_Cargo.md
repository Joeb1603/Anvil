# Fortify Cargo

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a fortification around the city of Cargo, utilising the aid offered by the Sarcophan Delves, Axos, and Jarmish in its construction.
>  
>  
> *Proposed by Morrow, Seconded by Holberg*

## Overview

- This motion takes advantage of the [opportunities](../winds-of-fortune/The_whole_world.md#an-opportunity-in-cargo) offered by the diplomatic envoys from the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md), [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md), and the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md).
- Passed at 200 Thrones.

## Date

- Spring 382YE

## Cost

- This fortification uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/The_whole_world.md#an-opportunity-in-cargo)
- 80 wains of white granite, 40 wains of mithril
- 240 crowns labour costs
- 6 months to construct.

## Progress

- 40 wains of White Granite received from Thanmir Hrafnar after the Spring Equinox 382YE.
- 20 wains of Mithril and 13 Thrones received from Ariadne after the Spring Equinox 382YE.
- 24 white granite, 20 mithril, and 13 Thrones received from Edmundo of Damakan's Forge after the Summer Solstice 382YE.
- 16 wains of White Granite and 32 crowns effectively provided due to the [Silent Sentinel](../winds-of-fortune/Sing_your_name.md#securing-the-borders) opportunity.

## Campaign Outcome

- The time to construct this [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) has been significantly reduced by the aid of Jarmish magicians; no further reduction in time to construct is possible.
- When complete, the Axos-designed fortification will inflict half again as many casualties presuming that the Axou are free to offer their assistance to the garrison.
