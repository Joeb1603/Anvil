# Relinquish Brilliant Shore

> *In-character text:*
>
> To relinquish the Brilliant Shore, the National Bourse position located in Skarsind.  
> *Proposed by Skarsind, seconded by Sermersuaq.*

### Overview

- Uses the Senate power of [relinquishment](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#relinquishment) to propose that the [Brilliant Shore](../../mixed/titles/Brilliant_Shore.md) become available for [allocation](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#allocation).
- Passed with a [constitutional majority](Senate_motion.md#constitutional-vote); [ratified](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#ratification) by [the Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md).

### Date

- Spring 382YE

### Cost

- None

### Campaign Outcome

- The Brilliant Shore ceases to be a National position, and is available for allocation.
