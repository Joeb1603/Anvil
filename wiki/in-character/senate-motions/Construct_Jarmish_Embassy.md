# Construct Jarmish Embassy

> *In-character text:*
>
> Construct an embassy for the Principalities of Jarm in Redoubt.  
>  
> *Proposed by Sermersuaq, seconded by Upwold.*

### Overview

- Construct an [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md) for the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) in Cargo, [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md).
- Passed at 40 Thrones

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Cost

- 20 White Granite
- 20 Weirwood
- Special: Note that this is not the [standard cost](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md#costs) for an embassy commission.

### Progress

- Materials provided by Atte, Senator for Sermersuaq after Summer Solstice 378.
- While the Jarmish Embassy has been built in [Cargo](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#cargo), the [House of Princes](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md#poltics) is refusing to name an ambassador until the Senate chooses which of their [ports](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#principalities-of-jarm) the reciprocal embassy will be established in.

### Campaign Outcome

- The embassy will be a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) that allows the Imperial citizen appointed to operate it to purchase [crystal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals), [Dragonbone](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), [Ambergelt](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), [Green Iron](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), [iridescent Gloaming](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), [Cerulean Mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md) and [Marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md).
- Oswi Twice-Burned was appointed [Ambassador to Jarm](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Jarm.md) at the Autumn Equinox 378YE
