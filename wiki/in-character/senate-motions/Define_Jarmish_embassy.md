# Define Jarmish embassy

> *In-character text:*
>
> To assign the Jarmish Embassy a remote principality to work with.  
>  
> *Proposed by the Imperial Civil Service.*

### Overview

- The Senate requests that the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) construct their Imperial embassy in the port city of [Rigia](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#principalities-of-jarm) in the eastern Principalities.

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- This decision should cause the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) to recognise the [Imperial embassy](Construct_Jarmish_Embassy.md) in [Cargo](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#cargo).
- Oswi Twice-Burned was appointed [Ambassador to Jarm](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Jarm.md) at the Autumn Equinox 378YE
