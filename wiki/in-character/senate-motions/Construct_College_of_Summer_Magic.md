# Construct College of Summer Magic

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build a Summer College dedicated to Cathan Canae  
>  
> *Proposed by Miaren, seconded by UNKNOWN*

### Overview

- Permission to create a [College of Magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) specialising in [Summer magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md).
- The college will be constructed in [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md).
- No funding was sought.

### Date

- Summer 379YE

### Cost

- Materials: 90 wains of White Granite, 30 wains of Mithril, 30 wains of Weirwood
- Time: 1 year
- Labour: 35 Thrones
- Upkeep: 25 Thrones (this number is dependent on the number of other colleges in the Empire)
- Special: This college of Summer magic is being built to designs provided by the [Eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Cathan Canae](../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md); as such it differs from the [standard cost](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) to build a [College of Magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md).

### Progress

- 50 wains of white granite, 10 wains of mithril, 30 wains of weirwood, and 35 Thrones provided by Llewellyn Leafstalker after Autumn Equinox 379YE
- 16 wains of mithril provided by Llewellyn Leafstalker after Winter Solstice 379YE
- 40 wains of white granite and 4 wains of mithril provided by Llewellyn Leafstalker after Spring Equinox 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The college is to be built in [Volchitrava](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#srodkoja) in [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md).
- The college will be called [The Icy Crag of the Eternal Sun](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#the-icy-crag-of-the-eternal-sun).
- The college is now complete creating the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Master of Ice and Darkness](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Ice_and_Darkness.md).
- The senator has decided that the Master of Ice and Darkness will be an Imperial Position assigned by declaration of the Conclave.
