# Construct Scholars of the Sword Academy

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Self-funded to build a white granite sinecure in the city of Holberg known as the Holberg Scholars of the Sword Academy, the custodian of which will be known as the Master of Defence of the Scholars of the Sword Academy. The Master of Defence will receive financial benefit whilst in governance. Appointment as a tenure position (until death, resignation, or reallocation) by the Senators of the League
> >
> > Announced by Achille di Sarvos, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Commissions a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Autumn 384YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs) apply
- This announcement is missing [necessary details](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#necessary-details) regarding the cost to create the sinecure, so the Civil Service have assumed the minimum size (10 wains).

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Giacomo Corvinoscuro after Autumn Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the sinecure would create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), the *Master of Defence of the Scholars of the Sword Academy* which would be appointed by unanimous decision of the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md), and have [tenure](../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure).
- The *Master of Defence (etc)* would receive the benefits of a money sinecure (taking the form of a regular stipend from the college).
- The construction is complete and the [Master of Defence of the Scholars of the Sword Academy](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Defence_of_the_Scholars_of_the_Sword_Academy.md) is due to be elected at the Winter Solstice 384YE.
