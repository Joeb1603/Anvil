# Abrogate Jarmish jewelers

> *In-character text:*
>
> Abrogate the Jarmish jewelers passed in Autumn 382YE.  
> *Proposed by Mournwold, seconded by Upwold.*

### Overview

- The motion referred to that passed during the Autumn Equinox 382YE, commissioning the [construction of a jewelers premises](Construct_Jarmish_Jewellers.md) for the Kobarid family of Jarm.
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 382YE

### Outcome

- Construction of the jewelers premises has been halted. A [later motion](Construct_Kobarid_Jewelers.md) was raised before the Senate to resume construction but was defeated.
