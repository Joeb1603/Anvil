# Declare peace with Lasambrian orcs

> *In-character text:*
>
> A declaration of peace with the Lasambrian orcs and promotion to foreign nation status, upon the condition of the return of the remaining occupied regions of Segura, in exchange for 62 wains of either Weirwood, Mithril or White Granite  
>  
> *Proposed by Segura, seconded by Astolat.*

### Overview

- Passed in principle
- Passed at 110 Thrones

## Date

- Passed Autumn 378YE

### Cost

- 62 wains of Weirwood, Mithril or White Granite

### Progress

- Funds disbursed to Avisena i Ezmara i Guerra, Senator for Segura

### Campaign Outcome

- The declaration means that the orcs of the Lasambrian hills are [foreigners](../../mixed/government/senate/Imperial_Law_Overview.md#individuals-and-the-law) under [Imperial Law](../../mixed/government/senate/Imperial_law.md).
- Magistrate's interpretation: A declaration of peace (or war) can only be absolute because a senate motion cannot compel the actions of a future senate. The conditions referred to above are therefore provided for information purposes only. In the event that the conditions are not fulfilled (or for any other reason) a future senate may raise a motion to declare war on the Lasambrian Orcs.
- After the Autumn Equinox, the Lasambrian orcs ceded the regions of [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md) they controlled back to the Empire and withdrew into the Lasambrian hills.
- The [Eastern Sky](../nations/dawn/Dawn_military_concerns.md#eastern-sky) [Imperial army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) claimed the vacated regions for the Empire; there was some token resistance from a few orcs who refused to withdraw from the Empire, and from bandits who had prospered without Imperial law.
