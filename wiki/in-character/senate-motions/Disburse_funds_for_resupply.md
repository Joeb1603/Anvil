# Disburse funds for resupply

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion for the dispersement of funds with the intention to resupply the Imperial Armies  
>  
> *Proposed by Bregasland, seconded by Sermersuaq.*

### Overview

- This did not give Senator Bregasland power to perform [emergency resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) on the armies.
- Passed in principle
- Passed at 150 Thrones

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Progress

- 150 Thrones disbursed to Senator Bregasland
