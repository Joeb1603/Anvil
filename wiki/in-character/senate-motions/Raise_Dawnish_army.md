# Raise Dawnish army

> *In-character text:*
>
> To raise a fourth Dawnish Army in Astolat.  
>  
> *Proposed by Semmerholm, seconded by Weirwater*

### Overview

- The motion was defeated

### Date

- Autumn 378YE

### Costs

- Materials: 250 [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#mithril). ([Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#costs)).
- Time: 1 year to construct
- Upkeep: 50 Thrones per season.

### Campaign Outcome

- This would have begun the creation of a [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md).
