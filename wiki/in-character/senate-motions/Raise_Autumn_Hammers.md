# Raise Autumn Hammers

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To raise an army in Skarsind from the Ethengraw sept. The Legion will be called the “Autumn Hammers” in deference to their heritage.
> >
> > Proposed: Skarsind, Seconded: The Barrens

## Overview

- Raises a third Imperial Orcs army from the [Ethengraw](../winds-of-fortune/A_lost_king.md#second-to-none) sept in the territory of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 200 wains of mithril
- 75 thrones
- One year to complete

## Progress

- 65 wains of mithril and 25 thrones provided by Bloodcrow Rasp after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 65 wains of mithril provided by Irontide Flax after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 35 wains of mithril and 37 thrones 4 crowns provided by Irontide Flax after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 35 wains of mithril and 12 thrones 3 crowns provided by Bloodcrow Rasp after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Kostjya after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Now that all necessary funds have been provided, and thanks to [Hammers of Fury](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#hammers-of-fury), the third army of the Imperial Orcs will finish raising in time for the [General of the Autumn Hammers](../../mixed/government/military/Autumn_Hammers.md) to be elected for the first time at the Spring Equinox 386YE. Due to it being raised early it will not immediately have an [army quality](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md) and will only be able to take the standard [army orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md).
