# Amend powers of Imperial Master of Works

> *In-character text:*
>
> To change the powers of the Master of Works, such that they have the power to repair existing fortifications, but not to build new ones. Additionally, a new stipend to be voted on.  
>  
> Proposed by Hahnmark, Seconded by Bastion

### Overview

- The ability of the [Imperial Master of Works](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Master_of_Works.md) to raise new [fortifications](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) was removed
- The 10 Throne stipend per season was removed.

### Date

- Spring 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The powers of the Imperial Master of Works were amended.
