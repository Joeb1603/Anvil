# Abrogate fund disbursement

> *In-character text:*
>
> Revoke the law disbursing unused Senate funds to the military council and conclave at the end of each quarter.  
>  
> Proposed Temeschwar, seconded by Tassato.

### Overview

- End the distribution of unused funds by [abrogration](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#abrogration) of [the motion to distribute funds of Winter 376YE](Disburse_Imperial_budget.md).
- Passed by 27 votes to 0

### Date

- Passed Summer 377YE (Event Three 2013).

### Cost

- None

## Campaign Outcome

No more funds will be disbursed to the [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md) or [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md).
