# Rebuild the Storm Vault

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Rebuild the Storm Vault, a mana sinecure sacked by the Druj into the infested vault. Location Proceris, Zenith
> >
> > Announced by Tiberius Echostorm, Crystal Architect of the Spires

## Overview

- Repairs the Storm Vault in Zenith as per the **[Write your own ending](../winds-of-fortune/Write_your_own_ending.md#the-lost-sinecures)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Crystal Architect of the Spires](../../mixed/titles/Crystal_Architect_of_the_Spires.md)

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 8 mithril
- 10 thrones
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 8 wains of mithril and 10 thrones provided by Xanthius Storm's End after Summer Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Custodian of the Storm Vault](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Storm_Vault.md) once again receives 10 mana crystals each season, and is able to fund the gathering of precious [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) in the form of a ministry
