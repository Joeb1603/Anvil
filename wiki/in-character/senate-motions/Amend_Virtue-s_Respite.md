# Amend Virtue's Respite

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Amend Virtue's Respite to become the Bloody Massive Pride of Temeschwar. This will cost 30 crowns and amend the associated title to be called the "Bloody Custodian of Pride". This will be appointed annually by the League National Assembly. It provides four liao and seven crowns each season
> >
> > Proposed: Temeschwar, Seconded: Tassato

## Overview

- Amends a [sinecure](../../mixed/titles/Bloody_Custodian_of_Pride.md#virtues-respite) in Hanuri, Temeschwar, as per the **[The concisest tenant](../winds-of-fortune/The_concisest_tenant.md#pride-of-temeschwar)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Costs per opportunity
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 2 thrones 6 crowns provided by Giuseppe Sanguineo von Temeschwar after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 5 crowns provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Casha di Apulian after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Baldritch Lace von Temeshwar after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Guardian of Giselle's Tomb](../../mixed/titles/Bloody_Custodian_of_Pride.md) has been renamed the [Bloody Custodian of Pride](../../mixed/titles/Bloody_Custodian_of_Pride.md)
- Citizens of Temeschwar with [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) can take the **Preach Pride - Pride of Temeschwar** lecture until the start of the Winter Solstice 388YE, producing no liao but increasing the number of Synod votes they receive by two.
