# Expand College of Warcasting

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To amend the title of Preceptor of the Warcaster College of Skarsind to include a ministry and to change how it is appointed to the Bourse voting style of the Imperial Orcs
> >
> > Proposed Skarsind, Seconded Weirwater

## Overview

- Commissions changes to the [College of Warcasting](../../mixed/titles/Preceptor_of_the_Skarsind_College_of_Warcasting.md#skarsind-college-of-warcasting) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md).
- The [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) is detailed in the **[Tired of sleeping](../winds-of-fortune/Tired_of_sleeping.md#the-college-of-warcasting)** Wind of Fortune.
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 12 wains of weirwood
- 24 crowns in labour costs,
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 4 wains of weirwood provided by Bloodcrow Yargol after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 2 wains of weirwood provided by Ashborn Vana after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 2 wains of weirwood provided by Ghillihg after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 2 wains of weirwood provided by Zerk after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 2 wains of weirwood provided by Ringforge Kerath after Spring Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the [Preceptor of the Skarsind College of Warcasting](../../mixed/titles/Preceptor_of_the_Skarsind_College_of_Warcasting.md) will have custody of a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) provided by the Tamazi orcs
- The method for appointing the Preceptor has been changed to [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md), and the next Preceptor will be appointed by [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) owners who have supported an Imperial army in the previous year.
