# Construct Good Leaf Gardens

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a sinecure, Good Leaf Gardens, to be stocked by Navarri herbs, given in thanks for the continuing support of the Marches in fighting the vallorn.  
> *Proposed by Miaren, seconded by Mitwold.*

## Overview

- Commission a wayhouse in Upwold, a [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) that, with [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) support will function as a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) intended to share Navarr herbs with [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md).
- This [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) is the result of [a suggestion](../winds-of-fortune/Blood_and_gold.md#a-fitting-testimony) by Morgan of Brock Bottom Steading following a [statement of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) by Corey Brackensong.
- The accompanying [mandate](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md) was [raised in](../synod-judgements/382YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-52) the Navarr [National Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#national-assemblies). Brat Umbral Path has provided the [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) to enact the mandate, so Good Leaf Gardens will function as a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md).
- Passed in principle.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/Blood_and_gold.md#upwold-wayhouse).
- 15 wains of Weirwood
- 30 crowns
- Takes three months to complete.

## Progress

- 15 wains of weirwood and 30 crowns provided by William Talbot after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The commission creates an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), a National position in the Marches, called *[Steward of Good Leaf Gardens](../../mixed/titles/Steward_of_Good_Leaf_Gardens.md)*.
