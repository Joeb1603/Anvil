# Construct Cinderpath Exchange III

> *In-character text:*
>
> Issachar of the Unbound, proxy to Caleb of Reumah's Redoubt, the Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave. Building the Cinderpath Exchange. Materials: 10 white granite, 20 crowns. Title: The Cinderpath Custodian. Self-funded. A White Granite ministry in Highguard. Appointment by the Highguard Bourse voting method on advice from the Constitutional Court.  
> *Announced by bearer of an Imperial Wayleave*

## Overview

- Commission a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in [Mareholm](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#mareholm), [Casinea](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md) that draws on an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) [presented](../winds-of-fortune/Leaves_and_fruit.md#the-cinderpath-exchange) by the benefactors of [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md)
- This announcement follows on from [two](Construct_Cinderpath_Exchange.md), [previous](Construct_Cinderpath_Exchange_II.md), failed motions
- Announcement

## Date

- Spring 383YE

## Cost

- This [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/Leaves_and_fruit.md#the-cinderpath-exchange)
- 10 wains of white granite
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 10 wains of white granite and 20 crowns provided by Rhesa after the Spring Equinox 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) will create a Highguard [national position](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md#national-position), [Cinderpath Custodian](../../mixed/titles/Cinderpath_Custodian.md), elected by Highborn owners of [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) as a bourse position.
