# Construct City of Wonders

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Legion Engineer will follow the example of Dust, Glass, and Flame and construct the city of wonders in Anduzjasse, Segura. This is an edifice that costs 50 white granite, 35 weirwood, 5 mithril, 30 ilium, 270 crowns and takes six months. It requires a foundational piece of artwork and will provide wealth, adventure and the novelty of new ideas
> >
> > Announced by Firesong Arachas, Legion Engineer

## Outcome

Once complete, the edifice have the following effects:

- Creates the Burning Agora, a torchlit public space dedicated to the passionate exchange of ideas
- Erects a new propylaeum on the south side of the city
- Restores the Market of a Thousand Sails to its former glory
- Enchants the Fountain of Wonders at the centre of the city
- Creates a new Freeborn archetype, the Firebrand
- Creates a new Freeborn archetype, the Hawker
- Creates a new Freeborn archetype, the Trailblazer
- Changes the fate of Anduz, to become the City of Wonders
