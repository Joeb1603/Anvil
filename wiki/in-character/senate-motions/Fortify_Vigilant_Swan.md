# Fortify Vigilant Swan

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To improve the Vigilant Swan, a White Granite bourse seat in Riverwatch, Reikos, with the aid of Manse of the Lionsmith. This will create a 3000 strong fort to defend the region, with no upkeep, and does not take a commission. Costing 20 mithril, 20 ilium and 120 crowns.
> >
> > Proposed: Reikos, Seconded: Hercynia

## Overview

- Constructs a fortification at the [Vigilant Swan](../../mixed/titles/Vigilant_Swan.md) as per the **[Metal and stone](../winds-of-fortune/Metal_and_stone.md#alabaster-and-onyx-swans)** wind of fortune.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 20 mithril
- 20 ilium
- 120 crowns
- six months to complete

## Progress

- 20 rings of ilium, 20 wains of mithril and 14 thrones 7 crowns provided by Shiloh after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the region of [Riverwatch](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md#riverwatch) is defended by the *Swans of Alabaster and Onyx* fortification.
