# Disburse Funds to the Quartermaster General IV

> *In-character text:*
>
> A disbursement with the intention of funding emergency resupply by the quartermaster.  
>  
> *Proposed by Bregasland, seconded by Hercynia*

### Overview

- This motion is to disburse funds with the intention supplying the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) with sufficient funds to purchase materials to perform [emergency resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) of as-yet unnamed Imperial armies.
- Passed at 100 Thrones

## Date

- Summer 379YE

## Progress

- 100 Thrones disbursed to senator Bregasland
