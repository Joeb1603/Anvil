# Expand the Kruidenkenner Magazjin

> *In-character text:*
>
> To expand the Kruidenkenner Magazjin at Crown’s Quay.
>
> This will create no new imperial titles but will significantly enhance the existing ministries of the High Herbalist of Sybella in Highguard, the Master of the Clearing House in the League, the Vizier of the Incarnadine Satchel in the Brass Coast, and the Kruidenkenner Trademaster.
>
> This will require a civilian commission. This is the last season that this opportunity will be available.
>
> *Proposed by Segura, seconded by Madruga.*

## Outcome

- Expand the [Kruidenkenner Magazjin](../../mixed/titles/Kruidenkenner_Trademaster.md#the-kruidenkenner-magazijn) in [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md) in line with the [opportunity presented](../winds-of-fortune/The_mathematics_of_tears.md#investment) by the Kruidenkenner guild of herbalists
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply as detailed [here](../winds-of-fortune/The_mathematics_of_tears.md#investment)
- 24 wains of [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md)
- 48 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 6 thrones and 24 wains of weirwood provided by Dino i Riqueza after Autumn Equinox 383YE

## Campaign Outcome

- Completed shortly before the WInter Solstice 383YE, the expansion has granted the [High Herbalist of Sybella](../../mixed/titles/High_Herbalist_of_Sybella.md), [Master of the Clearing House](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Clearing_House.md), and [Kruidenkenner Trademaster](../../mixed/titles/Kruidenkenner_Trademaster.md) access to a ministry to buy herbs from the Sarcophans.
- The [Vizier of the Incarnadine Satchel](../../mixed/titles/Vizier_of_the_Incarnadine_Satchel.md) also has access to a new ministry, but before they can take advantage of it the Senate would need to establish new offices for them as detailed in the [To curb those raging appetites](../winds-of-fortune/To_curb_those_raging_appetites.md#lost-opportunities) Wind of Fortune.
