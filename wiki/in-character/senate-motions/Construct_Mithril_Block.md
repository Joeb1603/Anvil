# Construct Mithril Block

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct the mithril Block to ward the spires of Spiral.  
> *Proposed by Spiral, seconded by Bastion.*

## Overview

- Commission The Block in [Cinion](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#cinion) as laid out by recent [proposals](../winds-of-fortune/The_path_that_winds.md#the-block)
- Passed at 0 Thrones

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/The_path_that_winds.md#the-block).
- 40 wains of mithril
- 80 crowns
- Three months to complete.

## Progress

- 22 wains of mithril provided by Edmundo of Damakhan's Forge after the Winter Solstice 382YE.
- 18 wains of mithril and 10 thrones provided by Maximilian after the Winter Solstice 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the current penalties suffered to the production of [mana sites](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md), [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md), [herb gardens](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md), [mines](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md), and [forests](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) owned those living in Spiral would be removed.
- Furthermore, the protection offered by the block would mean that people would be feasibly able to live in Spiral despite the curse of the Black Plateau, allowing for a slight increase in the rate of recovery for the territory and its contributions to Imperial coffers.
