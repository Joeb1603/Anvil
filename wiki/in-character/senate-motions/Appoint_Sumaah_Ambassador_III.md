# Appoint Sumaah Ambassador III

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion to appoint an ambassador to the Sumaah Republic*Proposed by Karov, Seconded by Kallavesa*

### Overview

- This motion appoints a citizen to the position of Ambassador to the Sumaah Republic and to have the power to deal with the [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md) on behalf of the Empire.

### Date

- Spring 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Vera Runova Helgrod was re-appointed [Ambassador to the Sumaah Republic](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sumaah_Republic.md)
