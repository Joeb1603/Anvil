# Construct Kauppahall

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct Kauppahall and the title "The Talon of Kauppahall" to be appointed annually unanimously by Wintermark senators.
> >
> > This will allow the purchase of white granite and a small amount of ilium.
> >
> > Costs 16 wains weirwood and 32 crownsProposed by Hahnmark, Seconded by Skarsind

## Overview

- Motion to construct a trading hall in [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) as detailed in the **[Apples from a tree](../winds-of-fortune/Apples_from_a_tree.md#a-troll27s-shadow)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- Passed at 84 thrones

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 16 wains of weirwood
- 32 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 4 thrones and 16 wains of weirwood provided by Hengest Dun after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- 84 thrones disbursed to the Senator for Hahnmark

## Constitution

- As detailed in the opportunity, the constitutional method to appoint this title is through the Imperial Bourse. It is not constitutional to appoint a title via the Imperial Senate if that position is primarily concerned with the acquisition of bourse resources.
- Construction of Kauppahall is well underway and will be completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 384YE.
- The Constitutional Court will raise an administrative motion; that the Senate amend the title of [Talon of Kauppahall](../../mixed/titles/Talon_of_Kauppahall.md) to be appointed via [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md). if they do so the title can be appointed during the summit
- The [administrative motion](../senate-sessions/384YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-talon-of-kaupphall) was raised and passed.
