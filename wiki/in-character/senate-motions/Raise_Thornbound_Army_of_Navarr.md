# Raise Thornbound Army of Navarr

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission the creation of the third Navarr army to be mustered in Miaren. The army to have the Thornbound quality. Self funded, to be created by the opportunity existing. Built by the will of the Navarr and the friendship and solidarity of the Empire. Virtue demands we turn words to action.  
> *Proposed by Hercynia, seconded by Bastion*

## Overview

- This motion takes advantage of the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) [presented by the Navarr vates](../winds-of-fortune/Comes_a_time.md#without-fear-or-favour).
- Passed in principle

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Costs

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/Comes_a_time.md#without-fear-or-favour)
- 200 wains of mithril
- 50 wains of weirwood
- 500 crowns
- The commission takes a year to complete

## Progress

- 103 wains of mithril, 57 thrones 4 crowns and 11 wains of weirwood provided by Corey Wayfarer after Summer Solstice 383YE
- 12 wains of mithril provided by Gwendolyn Splinterspear after Summer Solstice 383YE
- 10 wains of mithril provided by Rhisiart Heartsbane after Summer Solstice 383YE
- 1 wain of mithril provided by Lleu Winter's Reach after Summer Solstice 383YE
- 41 wains of mithril and 24 wains of weirwood provided by Gwendolyn Splinterspear after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 5 thrones provided by Corey Wayfarer after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 15 wains of mithril and 15 wains of weirwood provided by Rhisiart Heartsbane after Winter Solstice 383YE
- 18 wains of mithril provided by Gwendolyn Splinterspear after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- When complete, a third Navarr army with the *[Thornbound](../winds-of-fortune/Comes_a_time.md#thornbound)* quality will be raised.
