# Construct Drusila's Obelus

> *In-character text:*
>
> An announcement by bearer of an Imperial Wayleave, Lukash, to construct the sinecure Drusilla’s Obelus in the Morass, Holberg. Producing 18 drams of bladeroot per season. Cost 10 weirwood and 20 crowns. To be appointed for life by Bourse auction.  
> *Imperial Wayleave by Lukash*

## Overview

- Commission a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [the Morass](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#the-morass), [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md)
- Announcement.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 20 crowns labour costs
- three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood and 20 crowns provided by Lukash Biessek von Temeschwar after the Autumn equinox 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) creates an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), [Drusilla's Obelus](../../mixed/titles/Drusilla-s_Obelus.md), that will be auctioned through the [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md).
