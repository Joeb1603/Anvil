# Recognise Sand Fishers as foreigners

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Senate declares those of the Sand Fishers in Ossium that wish to remain part of their tribe to be foreigners.  
> *Proposed by Miekarova, seconded by Therunin.*

## Overview

- The [Sand Fishers](../winds-of-fortune/Chilled_to_the_bone.md#the-sand-fishers) tribe of [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) have until recently been subjects of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md).
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

## Campaign Outcome

- The Sand Fishers are now foreigners; they receive the protection of Imperial law as long as they abide by it.
- Further implications are uncertain.
