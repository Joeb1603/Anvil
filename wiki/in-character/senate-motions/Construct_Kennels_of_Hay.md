# Construct Kennels of Hay

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct a money sinecure in Hay, Mitwold. Costs 10 wains of white granite and 30 crowns. Self-funded by Carefoot and Shaw Trading Company. Appointed as the 'Kennelmaster of Hay's Home for Wayward Dogs' with tenure by senators of the Marches.
> >
> > Proposed: Mitwold, Seconded: Upwold

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Golden Downs, [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Coal Carefoot after Autumn Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Kennelmaster of Hay](../../mixed/titles/Kennelmaster_of_Hay.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the senators of [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 387YE
