# Appoint Iron Confederacy Ambassador

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint an ambassador to the Iron Confederacy
>  
>  
> *Proposed by Temeschwar, seconded by Holberg*

### Overview

- This motion was to elect a citizen to the position of [Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Iron_Confederacy.md)

### Date

- Summer 380YE

### Nominees

- Lukash Biessik von Temeschwar
- Starac Sijed Orzel
- Gellan de Rondell

### Campaign Outcome

- Starac Sijed Orzel was appointed [ambassador](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md#ambassador-to-a-foreign-nation) to the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md)
