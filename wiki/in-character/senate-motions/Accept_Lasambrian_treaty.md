# Accept Lasambrian treaty

> *In-character text:*
>
> To accept the treaty negotiated with the Lasambrian hills.  
>  
> *Proposed by the Skarsind, Seconded by Madruga.*

*[Image: The Lasambrian treaty](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:LasambrianTreaty.png)*

### Overview

- A delegation from the Hierro clan of the Lasambrian hills orcs negotiated a treaty.
- The treaty was presented to the Senate.
- Acceptance of this treaty [required](../winds-of-fortune/Strange_allies.md#significance) the [abrogation](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#abrogation) of the Jotun ceasefire.

### Date

- Summer 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The terms of the treaty were accepted.
- Imperial captains can deploy their [forces](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to assist the general of the Hierro army.
