# Appoint Faraden ambassador

> *In-character text:*
>
> Appoint an Ambassador to Faraden  
>  
> *Administrative Motion*

### Overview

- First appointment of the [Ambassador to Faraden](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Faraden.md).
- This motion appoints someone to be responsible for the [Faraden Embassy](Construct_Faraden_embassy.md), and to represent the Empire in dealings with the Faraden.

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- Ricardo di Tassato was appointed ambassador to [Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md).
