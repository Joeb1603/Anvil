# Create title of Captain of the Senate Guard

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create the position of "Captain of the Senate Guard", voted by simple majority by the senate of nominated citizens, for a term of one year. Revocable by the Assembly of Loyalty.  
> Responsibilities are to protect the Senate and Public Gallery whenever the senate is in session.  
> Granted the ability to use the defence of Unwelcome Visitor within the Senate building.  
>  
> *Proposed by Weirwater, Seconded by Bastion*

*[Image: Irontide Isk, first Captain of the Senate Guard](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Irontide_Isk.jpg)*

### Overview

- Create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) within the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md)
- The Captain is given leave to treat the Imperial Senate and public gallery as if they were their camp for purposes of the [unwelcome visitor](../../mixed/government/senate/List_of_Criminal_Offences.md#crimes-against-the-person) defence while the Senate is in session.

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The title of [Captain of the Senate Guard](../../mixed/titles/Captain_of_the_Senate_Guard.md) was created.
- Irontide Isk was nominated; there were no other contenders.
- Irontide Isk was appointed to the title

### Constitution

The Imperial Senate has given the Assembly of Loyalty the ability to [revoke](../../mixed/government/synod/Revocation.md) the Captain of the Senate Guard. The Constitutional Scholars point out that this is in *addition* to the ability of the Assembly of Nine and the General Assembly to revoke the title. The Senate can give additional assemblies the power to revoke a title, but they cannot create a title in such a way that they remove the ability of an assembly to revoke it.
