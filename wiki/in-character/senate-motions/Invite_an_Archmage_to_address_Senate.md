# Invite an Archmage to address Senate

> *In-character text:*
>
> To invite an archmage of the conclave to address us on the activities of the conclave and how they can assist the empire.  
>  
> Proposed by Kallavesa, seconded by Sermersuaq.

### Overview

- Passed 25 votes to 3.

### Date

- Passed Autumn 377YE.

### Progress

- Savik Summerstorm, Archmage of Winter, addressed the Senate Summit of 377YE.

### Constitution

It is very rare for it to be deemed constitutional for the Senate to have the power to compel an Imperial citizen to appear before them, that power more normally resides with the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md), but they can grant the power of address to an Imperial citizen, either on an ongoing basis or to be used once.
