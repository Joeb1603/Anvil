# Amend the use of the Gardens of High Chalcis

> *In-character text:*
>
> To redistribute the bounty of herbs granted by the Gardens of High Chalcis to the Great Forest Orcs.  
>  
> *Proposed by Reikos, Seconded by Necropolis.*

### Overview

- This motion was to use the [Gardens of High Chalcis](../../mixed/titles/Benefactor_of_High_Chalcis.md#gardens-of-high-chalcis) to provide support to the orcs of the Great Forest currently resident in [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md)
- It concerns an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented [here](../winds-of-fortune/The_place_you_called_home.md#the-bounty-of-reikos).
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Gardens of High Chalcis [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) provide no benefit to the [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) owners of [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md) for the next year (until after the Autumn Equinox 382YE).
- The [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of the Great Forest will receive a generous supply of [herbs](../../mixed/magic/potions/Herb.md).
- This exceptional act of generosity will also create an unrivalled opportunity for a closer relationship between the people of Reikos and the orcs of the Great Forest.
