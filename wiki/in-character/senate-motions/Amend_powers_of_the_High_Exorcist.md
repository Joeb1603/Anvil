# Amend powers of the High Exorcist

> *In-character text:*
>
> To increase the stipend of the High Exorsist.  
>  
> Proposed by Astolat, Seconded by Segura

### Overview

- Passed at a 4 Throne increase to the existing stipend.
- The [High Exorcist](../../mixed/titles/High_Exorcist.md) previously received a stiped of 3 Thrones each season.

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The stipend of the High Exorcist was amended to 7 Thrones each season.
