# Research Fire from the Heavens

> *In-character text:*
>
> To research “Fire from the Heavens”, an enchantment from an old coven in Willstone, Redoubt.  
>  
> *Announced by Ioseph, Minister of Historical Research.*

### Overview

- [Historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) concerning this topic has been commissioned through the civil service.
- Historical Research request announced by the [Minister of Historical Research](../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) using the legal powers to announce historical research delegated to them by the Senate.

### Date

- Winter 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- A historical research report will be delivered to the Minister at the Spring Equinox.
- The report will be published publicly after the Spring Equinox.
