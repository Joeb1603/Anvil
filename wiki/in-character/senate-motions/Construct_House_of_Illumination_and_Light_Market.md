# Construct House of Illumination and Light Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the House of Illumination and Light Market in Cargo in Redoubt and Essk in Miekarova respectively, creating two Imperial titles: the Architect of Illumination and Merchant Boyar of the Light Market. In the spirit of partnerships and prosperity this commission was designed for, I profusely thank Bledri and Siân of the Eternal Family in Navarr, who have pledged 10 wains to this construction.
> >
> > Announced by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Creates two sinecures in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) and [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md) as per the **[Out of the abyss](../winds-of-fortune/Out_of_the_abyss.md#illumination)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 75 wains of white granite
- 225 crowns
- Six months to complete
