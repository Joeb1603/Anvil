# Fortify Ateri in Spiral

> *In-character text:*
>
> To complete the partial fortification in Ateri in Spiral.  
>  
> *Approved by the Imperial Master of Works.*

### Overview

- The completion of the fortification, Fort Mezudan, at Ateri in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md).
- Fortification approved by the [Imperial Master of Works](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Master_of_Works.md).

### Date

- Approved Winter 377YE

### Cost

- 25 wains of white granite ([Standard cost](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#creating-a-new-imperial-army))
- Time: 1 season
- Upkeep: [10 Thrones](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#upkeep)

### Progress

- 25 wains of White Granite provided by Marcus of Endsmeet Autumn Coven, after Winter Solstice 377YE

### Campaign Outcome

The partially complete fortification, Fort Mezudan, in Spiral has now been completed.
In Autumn Equinox 379YE, a powerful magical storm struck the fortress, and it was completely destroyed.

### Voting

This motion was approved by the [Imperial Master of Works](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Master_of_Works.md) using the legal power to raise fortifications granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
