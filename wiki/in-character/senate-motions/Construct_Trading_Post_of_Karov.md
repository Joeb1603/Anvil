# Construct Trading Post of Karov

> *In-character text:*
>
> Construction of Trading Post in Karov.
>
> Funding and materials provided.
>
>  
> *Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of Varushka*

### Overview

- This motion was to take advantage of the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented to [improve the wealth](../winds-of-fortune/The_worth_of_a_life.md#crossroads-of-the-north) of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- This motion was approved by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate.

### Date

- Summer 380YE

### Cost

- 8 wains of white granite
- 2 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 8 wains of white granite and 2 thrones provided by Nadezhda Nadyovna Gremani after Summer Solstice 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- On completion, this ministry created the title of [Overseer of the Westward Road](../../mixed/titles/Overseer_of_the_Westward_Road.md), a [National](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md#national-title) [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md).
