# Construct Archives of the Advisor

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Archives of the Advisor, to be built in Solvihill, Skarsind. This sinecure will produce 12 iridescent gloaming a season. This sinecure will be attached to the already existing title of Advisor on Orc Affairs
> >
> > Proposed: Merari Navigators of Virtue, Senator for the Bastion, Seconded: Pathfinder Gaddak, Senator for Mareave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Solvihill, Skarsind and amends the title of [Advisor on Orc Affairs](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_Orc_Affairs.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Frith after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Advisor on Orc Affairs](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_Orc_Affairs.md) will receive 12 measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) each season starting at the Winter Solstice 388YE
