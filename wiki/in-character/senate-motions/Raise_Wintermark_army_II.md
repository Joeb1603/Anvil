# Raise Wintermark army II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To levy a Wintermark army.  
>  
> *Proposed by Hahnmark, seconded by Skarsind*

### Overview

- Permits the [Winterfolk](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) to begin raising a new [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md)
- Passed in Principle
- This motion follows on from a [previous](Raise_Wintermark_army.md) motion that was withdrawn.

### Date

- Summer 380YE

### Cost

- 200 wains of mithril and 50 thrones
- 1 year to raise
- Upkeep of 50 thrones per season from the Senate’s budget

### Progress

- 50 wains of mithril provided by Hengest Dun after Winter Solstice 381YE.
- 50 wains of mithril provided by Hengest Dun after Summer Solstice 381YE.
- 49 wains of mithril and 40 thrones provided by Hengest Dun after Autumn Equinox 381YE.
- 51 wains of mithril and 10 thrones provided by Hengest Dun after Winter Solstice 382YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- A new Wintermark army will muster in Kallavesa.
- Once completed, this army will automatically create a new [national title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md#national-title) of [general](../../mixed/titles/General.md)

### Scrutiny

- The senator who raised this motion must indicate what region in Wintermark the new army will be raised in, before work can begin.
