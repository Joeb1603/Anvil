# Construct Statue Commemorating Conquest of Ossium

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I, Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov as the first Mistress of Monuments announce the construction of a statue to commemorate the liberation of Ossium and the bringing of the Way to the first new Imperial territory in 300 years. Location: Lomaa, Galath Fields, Ossium. Costs: 3 white granite, 1 weirwood, 1 mithril, 15 crowns. Fully funded by prosperous Ossian citizens
> >
> > Announced by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov, Mistress of Monuments

- Constructs a [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Galath Fields, [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md)

- Summer 386YE
