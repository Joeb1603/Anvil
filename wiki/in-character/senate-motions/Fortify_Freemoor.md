# Fortify Freemoor

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build a fort in Freemoor in the Mourn.  
>  
> *Proposed by Miekarova, seconded by Temeschwar*

## Overview

- The creation of a new fortification, in the [Freemoor](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#freemoor) in [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md).
- Passed in principle

## Date

- Autumn 381YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#senate-commissions) for a [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) apply.
- 80 wains of white granite
- 20 thrones
- Time: 1 year
- Upkeep: [10 Thrones](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#upkeep)

## Progress

- 29 wains of white granite provided by Nikolovich Drakov after Winter Solstice 381YE.
- 16 thrones provided by Nikolovich Drakov after the Spring Equinox 382YE.
- 16 wains white granite and 4 thrones effectively provided due to the [Silent Sentinel](../winds-of-fortune/Sing_your_name.md#securing-the-borders) opportunity.
- 15 wains of white granite provided by Landon of Longridge after the Spring Equinox 383YE.
- 5 wains of white granite provided by Orrick of House Bolholt after the Spring Equinox 383YE.
- 15 wains of white granite provided by Orrick of House Bolholt after the Summer Solstice 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) of [Landskeepers Bulwark](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#landskeepers-bulwark) is now complete.
