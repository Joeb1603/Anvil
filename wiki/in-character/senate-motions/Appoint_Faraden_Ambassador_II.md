# Appoint Faraden Ambassador II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint an Ambassador to Faraden*Proposed by Necropolis, Seconded by Reikos*

### Overview

- This motion appoints someone to be responsible for the [Faraden Embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md), and to represent the Empire in dealings with Faraden

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Tal'Shar of House Zabala appointed as the second [Ambassador to Faraden](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Faraden.md)
