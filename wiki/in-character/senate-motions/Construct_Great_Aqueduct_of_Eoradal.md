# Construct Great Aqueduct of Eoradal

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the construction of the Great Aqueduct of Eoradal in Mareave to bring water to that region.
> >
> > Announced by Protectorate Thenrik, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Clisearn, [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md) as per the **[Million miles of water](../winds-of-fortune/Million_miles_of_water.md#the-eoradal-tunnels)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Winter 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 white granite
- 4 weirwood
- 4 mithril
- 15 crowns (caused by the underdeveloped nature of Icarion)
- 12 thrones upkeep
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 throne 2 crowns 6 rings provided by Pathfinder Gaddak after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown 14 rings provided by Fedelmidd Sydanjaa's Heart Floer after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Karrak after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 4 wains of mithril, 4 wains of weirwood and 12 wains of white granite provided by Bloodcrow Rasp after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the aqueduct is using water purchased from the Skourans of Jadefall:

- Supplies fresh water to the main settlement in Eoradal.
- Enables the region to support a large town once Curstag's Way is also completed, increasing taxation here by 4 thrones a season.
- Removes the arid quality from the region while it is operational
