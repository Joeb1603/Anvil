# Fortify Coursmouth

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion: to construct a fortification along the coast in Coursemouth, Necropolis.  
> *Proposed by Casinea, seconded by Necropolis.*

### Overview

- The creation of a new [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) at [Coursmouth](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#coursmouth) in [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md).
- Passed in principle.

### Date

- Autumn 382YE

### Cost

- This commission uses [standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#costs).
- 80 wains of white granite
- 160 crowns labour costs
- Time: 1 year
- Upkeep: [10 Thrones](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#upkeep)

### Progress

- 24 wains of white granite and 48 crowns provided by Lieselotte van Holberg after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.
- Targeted by *The Immaculate Architect* after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.
- 30 wains of white granite and 10 Thrones provided by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) after the Winter Solstice 382YE.
- 16 wains white granite and 32 crowns effectively provided due to the [Silent Sentinel](../winds-of-fortune/Sing_your_name.md#securing-the-borders) opportunity.
- 10 white granite provided by Ehud of Reumah's Redoubt after the Spring Equinox 383YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- A new fortification, [Reumah's Rest](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#reumahs-rest) was built at Coursmouth in Necropolis.
