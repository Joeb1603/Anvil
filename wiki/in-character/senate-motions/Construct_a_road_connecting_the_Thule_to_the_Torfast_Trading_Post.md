# Construct a road connecting the Thule to the Torfast Trading Post

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a road connecting the Thule to the Torfast Trading Post in Skarsind. Self-funded.  
>  
> *Proposed by Skarsind, Seconded by Temeschwar.*

### Overview

- Passed

### Date

- Summer 381YE

### Cost

- As detailed in this [opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/Spear_of_the_stars.md#the-northern-trade-network).
- 30 wains of white granite
- 60 crowns in labour costs
- Six months to construct

## Progress

- 30 wains of white granite and 60 crowns provided by Irontide Vio

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Bonesetter of Torfast Trading Post](../../mixed/titles/Bonesetter_of_Torfast_Trading_Post.md), [Broker of Treji Wayhouse](../../mixed/titles/Broker_of_Treji_Wayhouse.md), [Overseer of the Westward Road](../../mixed/titles/Overseer_of_the_Westward_Road.md), and [Northbound Trademaster](../../mixed/titles/Northbound_Trademaster.md) are now able to trade materials for crystal mana.
- This trade remains viable only as long as the Empire remains at peace with the Thule.
