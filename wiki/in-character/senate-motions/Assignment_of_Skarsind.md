# Assignment of Skarsind

> *In-character text:*
>
> Assignment of conquered territory of Skarsind to an Imperial nation.  
>  
> Procedural vote.

*[Image: Regions of Skarsind](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Skarsindregions2.png)*

### Overview

- Assigned to [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md).
- Passed unanimously.

### Date

- Autumn 377YE (Event Four 2013).

### Campaign Outcome

- The territory of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) is now part of the nation of Wintermark.
