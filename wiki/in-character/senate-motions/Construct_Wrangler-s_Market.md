# Construct Wrangler's Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission the Wrangler's Market in Arbonne. Seeks disbursement of 14 weirwood or equivalent (64 thrones)
> >
> > Proposed: Ana i Taziel i Riqueza, Senator for Segura, Seconded: Eulàlia i Phovas i Riqueza, Senator for Madruga

## Overview

- Constructs a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in Arbonne
- Passed at 54 thrones
- 54 thrones disbursed to Senator for Segura
- More information can be found in the **[No matter what](../winds-of-fortune/No_Matter_What.md#break-the-chains)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 14 wains of weirwood
- 42 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 5 thrones and 14 wains of weirwood provided by Tancred Enrico i Taziel i Riqueza after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the market will have the following effects:

- The *Chief Wrangler* will be available to appoint by Freeborn tally of the votes of fleet owners
- Imperial Orc [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) will be able to use the market to [spy](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) on [Arbonne](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#kalino-or-arbonne)
