# Construct Imperial Cord Brokerage II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a weirwood ministry in Seren, Miaren, the Invisible Cord Brokerage, to provide guaranteed prices as a hedge for independent weirwood traders.  
> *Proposed by Miaren, seconded by Karov.*

## Overview

- Commission a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in Seren, [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md) that draws on an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) [presented](../winds-of-fortune/What_will_be-_was_not.md#the-invisible-cord-brokerage) by the broker of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Costs

- This motion uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/What_will_be-_was_not.md#the-invisible-cord-brokerage)
- 16 wains of weirwood
- 32 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 7 crowns 19 rings and 16 wains of weirwood provided by Llewellyn Leafstalker after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 1 ring in small donations provided after Autumn Equinox 383YE

## Outcome

- When complete the Invisible Cord Brokerage will create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), the [Broker of the Invisible Cord](../../mixed/titles/Broker_of_the_Invisible_Cord.md). The constitutional path for appointment would be through the Bourse, by Navarr owners of herb gardens.
