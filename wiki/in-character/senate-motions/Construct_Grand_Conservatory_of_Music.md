# Construct Grand Conservatory of Music

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a Grand Conservatory of Music in the territory of Redoubt to match the Pride of Urizen in creating the enchantment of Art and Culture upon the Empire and to continue to foster our Pride in both our music and our magic.*Proposed by Morrow, Seconded by Hercynia*

### Overview

- A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) to take advantage of the [opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/In_brightest_day.md#the-power-to-exalt) that presented itself during the [period](../winds-of-fortune/In_brightest_day.md) of inspiration.
- Passed

### Date

- Spring 381YE

### Cost

- 15 wains of white granite
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 5 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 15 wains of white granite, 5 wains of weirwood and 5 thrones provided by Ariadne after Spring Equinox 381YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- This great work, the [Grand Conservatory of Music](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#grand-conservatory-ruined), provides 15 [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) and 30 votes in the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) spread amongst the citizens of [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) dedicated to [Pride](../religion/Pride.md) who own [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) each season, following attendance at the Anvil Summit.
