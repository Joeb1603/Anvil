# Authorise retraining of The Valiant Pegasus

> *In-character text:*
>
> That an Imperial army should have its quality changed to that of 'physick'  
> *Proposed by Bastion, Seconded by Therunin*

### Overview

- This motion was to take advantage of the opportunity presented following the [celebrations](../winds-of-fortune/Iron_and_wine.md) of Empress Lisabetta's coronation which granted the Senate the [chance](../winds-of-fortune/Iron_and_wine.md#opportunity-the-quality-of-mercy) to retrain an Imperial army to have the [quality](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md) of [physick](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#physick)
- The [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) army, [The Valiant Pegasus](../nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md#the-valiant-pegasus) was nominated
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Valiant Pegasus has lost its [siege](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#siege) training and has received an influx of trained herb masters who are in the process of changing the army's quality
