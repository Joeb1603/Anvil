# Raise Fifth Marcher Army

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To raise the fifth Marcher army, to be raised in Upwold
> >
> > Proposed: Henry Birchsmith, Senator for Upwold, Seconded: Mary Birchsmith, Senator for Mitwold

## Overview

[Image: Estavus001a.png](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#hammers-of-fury)

- Raises an [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in Upwold
- Passed
- This army benefited from [Hammers of Fury](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#hammers-of-fury) performed by the Cabal Lyktan during the Summer Solstice 388YE.

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 200 wains of mithril
- 75 thrones in labour costs
- Twelve months to complete

## Progress

- 200 wains of mithril and 74 thrones 7 crowns provided by Henry Birchsmith after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Tom Keeper, of Copperhill after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Once completed, the fifth general of the Marches will be available for election by unanimous decision of the Marcher senators
