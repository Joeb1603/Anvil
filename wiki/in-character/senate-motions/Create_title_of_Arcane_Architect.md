# Create title of Arcane Architect

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create the Imperial Title of Arcane Architect with responsibilities to support ritualists of Empire in virtuous application of mana.
>
> Powers as follows
>
> Ability to freely create mana producing Sinecures, funding for these Sinecures to be arranged by title holder.
>
> Right of Address to Conclave once per summit.
>
> Title appointed by Declaration of Candidacy within Conclave  
>  
> *Proposed by Volodmartz, seconded by Madruga.*

### Overview

- Create a title within the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) to [commission](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) mana [sinecures](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md).
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) [Arcane Architect](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Architect.md) was created.
- The title was [amended](Amend_powers_of_the_Arcane_Architect.md) by the Senate in Spring 380YE to restrict the use of the power to once per season.

### Constitution

- It is the decision of the Constitutional Court that any mana sinecures constructed by this Imperial Title will be appointed a [Declaration of Candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy) within the Conclave.
