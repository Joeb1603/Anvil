# Construct Cascade of Living Flame

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission a 10 wain of mithril mana sinecure. Location; Peregro, Morrow. Name: Cascade of Living Fire. Self funded. Appointed by Urizen senators
> >
> > Announced by Tiberius Echostorm, Crystal Architect of the Spires

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) that produces mana crystals in [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md)
- Announcement by the [Crystal Architect of the Spires](../../mixed/titles/Crystal_Architect_of_the_Spires.md)

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 mithril
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 3 thrones 6 crowns provided by Rhode after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the position of [Custodian of Living Flame](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_Living_Flame.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous vote of the senators of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE.
