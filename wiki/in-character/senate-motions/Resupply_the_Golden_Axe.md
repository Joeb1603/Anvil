# Resupply the Golden Axe

> *In-character text:*
>
> Resupply army of Golden Axe  
>  
> *Proposed by Volodmartz, seconded by Sermersuaq.*

### Overview

- Provide [emergency resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) to the army of the [Golden Axe](../nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md#the-army-of-the-golden-axe).
- Passed 27 votes to 0.
- Funded at 50 Thrones.

### Date

- Summer 377YE (Event Three 2013)

### Cost

- [Standard resupply costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#resupply) apply.

### Progress

- None.

### Campaign Outcome

- Unlimited resupply of the Golden Axe is authorised by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) until end of Spring 378.
