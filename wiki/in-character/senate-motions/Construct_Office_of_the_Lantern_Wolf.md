# Construct Office of the Lantern Wolf

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Office of the Lantern Wolf within the walls of Tassato as fully described in the opportunity.
> >
> > Announced by Gustavus Tolski van Temeschwar, Chair of the Wolf

## Overview

- Creates a sinecure in Tassato Regario, [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md) as per the **[Battle cries](../winds-of-fortune/Battle_cries.md#a-lantern-for-wolves)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Chair of the Wolf](../../mixed/titles/Chair_of_the_Wolf.md)

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 10 wains of white granite
- 60 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 wain of weirwood and 9 wains of white granite provided by Ikelos Moretti after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 throne 2 crowns provided by Gianluca Di Zobbras after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Fintan Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 2 rings in small donations provided after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 7 wains of weirwood provided by Ikelos Moretti after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 5 thrones 5 crowns 18 rings, 2 wains of weirwood and 1 wain of white granite provided by Katarina van Palantíri after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete, the [Lantern Wolf](../../mixed/titles/Lantern_Wolf.md) is available to elect by the National Assembly of the League as a yearly appointment for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE.
