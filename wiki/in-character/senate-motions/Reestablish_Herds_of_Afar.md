# Reestablish Herds of Afar

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission to reestablish the herds of Afar in Feroz at a cost of 300 crowns and establish the Dhomiro of Afar to oversee them, outlined by the civil service. Fully self funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Feroz, Seconded: Kahraman

## Overview

- Creates a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Afarjasse, Feroz as per the **[Send for me](../winds-of-fortune/Send_for_me.md#herds-of-afar)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 300 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 37 thrones 2 crowns provided by Garviel i Abrira i Erigo after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Karrak after Winter Solstice 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Dhomiro of Afar](../../mixed/titles/Dhomiro_of_Afar.md) will be available to appoint by Tally of the Votes of Freeborn [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) owners for the first time at the Spring Equinox 387YE
