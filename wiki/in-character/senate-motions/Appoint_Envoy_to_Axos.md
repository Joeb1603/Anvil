# Appoint Envoy to Axos

> *In-character text:*
>
> That one of the nominees should be given the imperial title Envoy to Axos.  
>  
> *Procedural Motion.*

### Overview

- This motion is a reference to an earlier Senate motion to [send an envoy to Axos](Send_envoy_to_Axos.md).

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Nominees

- Valimar Clarev Parakgoosier Bashnya ([League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md))
- Marissa Shatterspire ([Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md))

### Campaign Outcome

Valimar Clarev Parakgoosier Bashnya was appointed envoy to [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md).
