# Construct City of Dreams

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Navarr marches to war with the vallorn. What will we be when it is destroyed? Here is an answer. To commission the Seren City of Dreams project. Self funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Miaren, Seconded: Bastion

## Overview

- Constructs the City of Dreams in Serenael, [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md) as per the **[Comes a time](../winds-of-fortune/Comes_a_time.md#the-city-of-dreams)** wind of fortune.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 90 weirwood
- 30 white granite
- 360 crowns
- Nine months to complete

## Outcome

Once complete the City of Dreams will increase [investment](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in the congregations of Miaren by one, providing a vial of liao and a dose of [Goosewhisper Infusion](../../mixed/magic/potions/Infusions_of_Feathers.md#goosewhisper-infusion) each season; the archetype of Dreamspeaker will be established in Navarr; and the title of *Dreamer's Guide* will be available to appoint by the National Assembly of Navarr.
