# Reconstruct the Tower of the Fall

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To reconstruct the Tower of the Fall in Occursion, Zenith, and make it a part of the Proffered Hand network. This is an existing opportunity and is a mana sinecure for the cost of 6 mithril and 9 thrones. This will produce ten mana and 6 heart's blood a season to the Keeper of the Tower of the Fall, an existing tenured title in Urizen. Self-funded by Herminius of the Temple of the Wanderer. Appointed by Urizen Senators.
> >
> > Proposed: Zenith, Seconded: Kahraman

## Overview

- Repairs a mana sinecure in [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) as per the **[Write your own ending](../winds-of-fortune/Write_your_own_ending.md#the-lost-sinecures)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 6 wains of mithril
- 78 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 6 wains of mithril and 9 thrones 6 crowns provided by Herminius of the House of the Wanderer after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now completed, the position of [Keeper of the Tower of the Fall](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Tower_of_the_Fall.md) will be available for the senators of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) to appoint once again at the Winter Solstice 385YE.
