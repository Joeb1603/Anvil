# Construct Delev Grand Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The people of Karov wish to self fund and build a great market in Delev. This will provide the Senate four additional thrones per season in tax (and assorted local benefits!). This is time sensitive to this summit only!
> >
> > Announced by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Duzekani, Karov, as per the **[Outside the wall](../winds-of-fortune/Outside_the_wall.md#a-great-market)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 50 wains of white granite
- 150 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Outcome

Once complete, the grand market will have the following effects:

- Raises the level of investment in businesses in Karov by one
- Allows citizens to take a business in Karov for free for one year after completion
- Adds an additional purchase rank to the [Iron Tower](../../mixed/titles/Bargainer_of_the_Iron_Tower.md), [Korotny Docks](../../mixed/titles/Harbourmaster_of_the_Semmerlak.md) and the [Delev Market](../../mixed/titles/Overseer_of_the_Westward_Road.md)
- Increases taxation of Karov by an additional 4 thrones a season if the Little Boyar's Welcome is also upgraded
- Would allow Senate to use a motion to permit the Boyar of the Iron Roads to create a sinecure instead of a ministry each season without using a Senate commission
