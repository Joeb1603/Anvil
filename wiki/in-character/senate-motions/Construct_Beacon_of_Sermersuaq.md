# Construct Beacon of Sermersuaq

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Beacons of Wintermark must be completed. We shall construct the final set within Sermersuaq. As part of this commission we call upon a boon provided by Cathan Canae in recognition of our resilience against two armies of Oblivion.
> >
> > Proposed: Sermersuaq, Seconded: Madruga

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Sealtoq, Sermersuaq
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Farm, fur, and fish](../winds-of-fortune/Farm-_fur-_and_fish.md#extraneous-castles)** and **[Tales of imagination](../winds-of-fortune/Tales_of_imagination.md#wolves-and-bears-cathan-canae)** winds of fortunes

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 30 wains of white granite
- 25 wains of weirwood
- 165 crowns in labour costs
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 1 crown, 4 wains of weirwood and 29 wains of white granite provided by Garth after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 10 thrones provided by Otmar van Holberg after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 21 wains of weirwood and 1 wain of white granite provided by Garth after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 10 thrones 1 crown provided by Fedelmidd Sydanjaa's Heart Floer after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Matias Sigeing after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the Beacons of Sermersuaq have the following effects:

- [Military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) are able to [guard](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#guarding) the beacons of [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md)
- [Cathan Canae](../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md) will send the ritual text of *Drums of the Iron Harpoon* to the [General of the Narwhal's Spear](../../mixed/government/military/Narwhal-s_Spear.md) at the Summer Solstice 388YE
