# Raise Towerjacks army

> *In-character text:*
>
> To raise the Towerjacks as an Imperial League army. We will request funds from the Senate for this motion.  
>  
> *Proposed by Sarvos, seconded by Holberg*

*[Image: Towerjacks.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Towerjacks.jpg)*

### Overview

- Permits [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) to restore the [Towerjacks](../nations/the-league/The_League_military_concerns.md#the-towerjacks) to full fighting strength.
- Passed in principle
- Passed at 200 Thrones

### Date

- Summer 379YE

### Costs

- Materials: 125 [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#mithril).
- Time: 6 months to construct
- Upkeep: 50 Thrones per season
- Special: This [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) is being raised under [special circumstances](../../mixed/government/military/Holberg_spoils_of_war.md#the-towerjacks); as such it differs from the [standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#costs) to raise an Imperial army.

### Progress

- 200 Thrones disbursed to Senator Holberg
- 65 wains of mithril supplied by Ros Jordan after the Summer Solstice 379YE.
- 60 wains of mithril supplied by Ros Jordan after the Autumn Equinox 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The Towerjacks, a League army incorporating many [siege](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#siege) experts, will muster in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md).
- When the army is raised, it will automatically create the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [General](../../mixed/titles/General.md).
