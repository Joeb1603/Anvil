# Construct Madruga shipyard

> *In-character text:*
>
> Build a new Imperial shipyard in Madruga  
>  
> *Proposed by Sarvos, seconded by Madruga.*

### Overview

- Construct a [shipyard](../../mixed/commissions/Shipyard.md) at Madruga.
- Provides additional wealth to [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) operating from [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md).

### Date

- Passed Winter 376YE (Easter 2013).

### Cost

- 35 Wains of White Granite
- 5 Wains of Weirwood
- Upkeep: 5 Thrones

### Progress

- Costs paid by Mirislav, Senator for [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) in the Spring Equinox 2013 downtime

### Production

- Increased wealth for Madrugan shipping

This [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) means that all [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) operating out of [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) will produce more wealth for their owners. The total benefit will be 720 rings per season - divided between all the fleets which are active in Madruga that season.
