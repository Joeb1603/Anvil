# Construct Garden of Peaceful Quietitude

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Build a sinecure in Hahnmark, built in Kalpamark. Appointed by Wintermark National Assembly, but with tenure. Called Garden of Peaceful Quietitude
> >
> > Announced by Hilda Sirens Scream Ursan, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Kalpamark, Hahnmark, as per the **[Poet lights but lamps](../winds-of-fortune/Poets_light_but_lamps.md#zaboravi)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 wains of weirwood
- 36 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 4 thrones 4 crowns and 12 wains of weirwood provided by Hilda Sirens Scream Ursan after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the title of [Seeker of Quietitude](../../mixed/titles/Seeker_of_Quietitude.md) will be available to appoint by the Wintermark Assembly for the first time at the Summer Solstice 388YE
