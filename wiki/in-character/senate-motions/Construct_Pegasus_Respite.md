# Construct Pegasus Respite

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build the Pegasus Respite as a sinecure. The title ‘Medical Officer of the Pegasus Respite’ to be appointed by the Highborn national assembly.
>
> It will cost 14 wains of weirwood and 28 crowns, fully self funded. Herbs produced will go to the battle physicks of Highguard to serve the Empire.
>
> The House of the Proffered Hand will have a former slave rehabilitation wing on the premises, to aid the convalescent hospital’s work.
>
>  
> *Announced by Nathaniel of Balthazar’s Vineyard, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave*

## Outcome

- Construct a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Chalcis Mount, [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md)
- Announcement

## Date

- Autumn 383YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply as detailed [here](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).
- 14 wains of [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md)
- 28 crowns in labour costs
- three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 12 wains of weirwood provided by Tolame after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 1 crown provided by Septus Sentinels Tower after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 1 ring in small donations provided after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 2 crowns 19 rings and 2 wains of weirwood provided by Tolame after Spring Equinox 384YE

## Campaign Outcome

- Once completed this commission will be overseen by the title [Medical Officer of the Pegasus Respite](../../mixed/titles/Medical_Officer_of_the_Pegasus_Respite.md).
