# Construct Rose Towers

> *In-character text:*
>
> An announcement by bearer of a Wayleave, to build a sinecure in Senmer’s Rose, Semmerholm. Currently known as the Rose Towers, this sinecure shall provide 12 Liao and 24 Synod votes. To be appointed annually by the Throne.  
>  
> *Announced by Sandro Visconti di Sarvos, bearer of an Imperial Wayleave*

## Overview

- This motion create the Rose Towers in [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md) as detailed [here](../winds-of-fortune/Budded_roses.md#the-rose-towers).
- Replaces the minor benefits provided by Lacre's defences
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 381YE

## Cost

- An [opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/Budded_roses.md#the-rose-towers) applies
- 15 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns
- 3 months to complete

## Progress

- 15 wains of white granite and 30 crowns provided by Sandro Visconti di Sarvos after the Spring Equinox 382YE.

## Outcome

- When complete, creates the [imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) *The [Queen's Rose](../../mixed/titles/Queen-s_Rose.md)* with custodianship of a place of pilgrimage, providing 12 doses of liao and 24 votes in the Imperial Synod.
- While the wayleave references the fortification at Semmer's Rose, the planned commission in fact makes use of the minor fortifications at [Lacre](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md#lacuve) in the region of Lacuve.

## Constitution

- The well-worn path for a sinecure such as this would be appointment by an [assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md) of the Imperial Synod.
- In recognition of the purpose of the commission, and with the support of [statement of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) from the [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) national assembly1, appointment by [the Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md) in its role as [Monarch of Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn_monarchy.md) has been approved. In the absence of a Throne, this title could not be appointed.

1 "*The Dawnish National Assembly recognises the Empress Lisabetta as Queen of Dawn. She is the ultimate authority in this life and her words should be followed above all others.*" ([Spring 381YE](../synod-judgements/381YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md)) and "*The Queen of Dawn holds authority over the Dawnish people.*"([Summer 381YE](../synod-judgements/381YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#statements-of-principle)).
