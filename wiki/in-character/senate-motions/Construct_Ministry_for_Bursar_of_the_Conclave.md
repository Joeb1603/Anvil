# Construct Ministry for Bursar of the Conclave

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To improve the Bursar of the Conclave's ministry to allow them to purchase crystal mana at standard costs. It will cost 12 wains of mithril and 36 crowns and will be constructed in Moresvah, Karsk.
> >
> > Announced by Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad, Arcane Architect

## Overview

- Improves the [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) of the [Bursar of the Conclave](../../mixed/titles/Bursar_of_the_Conclave.md)
- Announcement by the [Arcane Architect](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Architect.md)

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 12 wains of mithril
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 7 wains of mithril and 4 thrones 4 crowns provided by Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 5 wains of mithril provided by Otmar van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now that work on the ministry is complete, the Bursar of the Conclave is able to purchase mana crystals at the [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md#20-wains)
