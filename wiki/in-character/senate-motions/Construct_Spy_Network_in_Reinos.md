# Construct Spy Network in Reinos

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I, Bernard Dugdale, Imperial Spymaster announce that out of Loyalty to the General of the Red Wind Corsairs, honouring a previous agreement, that a new spy network will be constructed in Reinos, so to speed closure of the Jotun war. May the information finally be the first step in hamstringing Lasambrians and ending the western front.
> >
> > Announced by Bernard Dugdale, Imperial Spymaster

## Overview

- Creates a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in Reinos
- Announcement by the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md)

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Bernard Dugdale after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Once the work is complete, [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) will be able to support this spy network to uncover information about Reinos
