# Construct Healing House of Wittal Grove

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To permit the build of a great work, Healing House of Wittal Grove in Wittal, Kallavesa, to benefit herb garden owners.
> >
> > Costs: 56 wains of weirwood and 14 thrones in labourProposed by Kallavesa, Seconded by Mitwold

## Overview

- Motion to construct a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) as detailed in the **[All life together](../winds-of-fortune/All_lift_together.md#healing-house-of-wittal-grove)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- Passed at 328 thrones

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 56 wains of weirwood
- 14 thrones in labour costs

## Progress

- 7 thrones and 28 wains of weirwood provided by Vauraus Korppitkotka after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 7 thrones and 28 wains of weirwood provided by Rane Shardbearer after Spring Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- 328 thrones disbursed to the Senator for Kallavesa
- Once completed the great work will provide a shared bounty to all [herb gardens](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) in Kallavesa
- There are constitutional issues with this commission

## Constitution

- The [opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/All_lift_together.md#healing-house-of-wittal-grove) presented an option to create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) to oversee the Healing House. This was not presented as part of the Senate Motion.
- Discussion with Senator Kallavesa confirmed the intent to create the title [Grimnir of Wittal Grove](../../mixed/titles/Grimnir_of_Wittal_Grove.md) with the intent that "the title ... is intended specifically to be held by a grimnir".
- The commission will go ahead but an administrative motion will be raised to allow the Senate to abrogate the title if they wish. Should they do so, this will create significant problems with the grimnir of Wintermark who have committed their time and resources to supporting the House.
- The constitutional court notes that this convoluted approach is being put forward as a consequence of the [problems caused by memory altering mists](../winds-of-fortune/Cloud_waves_break.md), and urges all Senators to ensure they have included vital details on their Senate motion in future.
- The [administrative motion](../senate-sessions/384YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#abrogate-grimnir-of-wittal-grove) was raised and failed.
