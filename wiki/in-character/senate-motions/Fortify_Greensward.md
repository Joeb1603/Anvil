# Fortify Greensward

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build a fort in Greensward named "Farstriders Watch". To be funded by the Imperatrix.  
>  
> *Proposed by Conscience of the Senate, seconded by Holberg*

### Overview

- The creation of a new fortification, Farstrider's Watch, on the [Greensward](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#greensward) in [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md).
- Passed in principle

### Date

- Autumn 381YE

### Cost

- [Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#senate-commissions) for a [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) apply.
- 80 wains of white granite
- 20 Thrones
- Time: 1 year
- Upkeep: [10 Thrones](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#upkeep)

### Progress

- 40 wains of white granite provided by Orrick after Winter Solstice 381YE.
- 16 wains white granite and 32 crowns effectively provided due to the [Silent Sentinel](../winds-of-fortune/Sing_your_name.md#securing-the-borders) opportunity.
- 24 wains of white granite and 16 thrones provided by Orrick after Spring Equinox 382YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- This will build a new rank one fortification in the Greensward.
- The new fortification will be completed shortly before the Winter Solstice 382YE.
