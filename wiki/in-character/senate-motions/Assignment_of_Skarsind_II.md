# Assignment of Skarsind II

> *In-character text:*
>
> Allocation of the territory of Skarsind  
>  
> *Proposed by Hahnmark, seconded by Temeschwar.*

### Overview

- Assigned to the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md).

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) has been assigned to the Imperial Orcs, and can elect a [Senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md).
- Skarsind is no longer a [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) territory, which has [significant implications](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#resources-in-other-nations) for any Winterfolk still living there.
- Many of the implications of this move are covered [here](../winds-of-fortune/Land_fit_for_heroes.md).

### Constitution

- A method whereby the Imperial Orcs appoint a senator has been determined.
- This has directly affected the method whereby the Imperial Orcs appoint their [Generals](../../mixed/titles/General.md) and similar titles.
