# Construct Runesmith's Caution

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I commission a money sinecure, Runesmith's Caution in Kalpaheim, Hahnmark in memory of Wayland Runesmith's Caution Thane of the Proud Quill, who died in Summer 386YE fighting the Druj. This white granite school for artisans from the Mark will teach rune lore and tell the tale of Wayland, who broke his oath and shared Runesmith's Law with people from other nations. The school will be established in his name, self-funded, by his friend The Oathwright editor, Cormack Dun Farstrider, and the people of Wintermark. This creates the Wintermark national title: Dean of Runesmith's Caution to be elected by tally of the votes.
> >
> > Announced by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar, Master of Rings

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Kalpamark, Hahnmark
- Announcement by the [Master of Rings](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Rings.md)

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones 5 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Cormack Dun after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 6 crowns and 2 wains of white granite provided by Cormack Dun after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Dean of Runesmith's Caution](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_Runesmith-s_Caution.md) will be appointed by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of Wintermark [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) captains for the first time at the Summer Solstice 388YE
