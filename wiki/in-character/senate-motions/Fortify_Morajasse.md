# Fortify Morajasse

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission a fortification in Morajasse, Feroz.  
> *Proposed by Feroz, seconded by Mournwold.*

### Overview

- The creation of a new [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) at [Morajasse](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#morajasse) in [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md).
- Passed at 90 thrones.

### Date

- Autumn 382YE

### Cost

- This commission uses [standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#costs).
- 80 wains of white granite
- 20 thrones labour costs
- Time: 1 year
- Upkeep: [10 thrones](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#upkeep)

### Progress

- 24 wains of white granite and 6 thrones received from Chiara i Zayden i Riqueza after the Autumn Equinox 382YE
- 16 wains white granite and 4 thrones effectively provided due to the [Silent Sentinel](../winds-of-fortune/Sing_your_name.md#securing-the-borders) opportunity.
- 20 wains of white granite and 5 thrones provided by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) after the Winter Solstice 382YE
- Targeted by *The Immaculate Architect* after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.
- 20 wains of white granite provided by Landon of Longridge after the Spring Equinox 383YE
- A further 5 thrones provided by Imperatrix Lisabetta after the Spring Equinox 383YE

### Campaign Outcome

- A new fortification has been built at Morajasse
