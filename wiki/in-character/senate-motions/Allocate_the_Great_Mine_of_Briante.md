# Allocate the Great Mine of Briante

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion to Allocate the Great Mine of Briante as an Imperial Bourse position*Proposed by Kahraman, Seconded by Feroz*

### Overview

- This motion was to allocate the [Great Mine of Briante](../../mixed/titles/Great_Mine_of_Briante.md) bourse seat in [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) as an Imperial position
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Great Mine of Briante remains Imperial and is due for auction, for a reduced term, at the Winter Solstice 380YE
