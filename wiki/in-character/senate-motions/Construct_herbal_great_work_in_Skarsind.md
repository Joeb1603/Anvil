# Construct herbal great work in Skarsind

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission a great work in Skarsind to those orcs that never got to see a homeland  
>  
> *Proposed by Skarsind, Seconded by Sermersuaq*

### Overview

- A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) that will produce [True Vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md)
- This motion was to take advantage of the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) [presented](../winds-of-fortune/The_worth_of_a_life.md#one-life-to-live) following the [assignment](Assignment_of_Skarsind_II.md) of Skarsind to the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Cost

- 45 wains of weirwood
- 90 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 45 wains of weirwood and 90 crowns provided by Bloodcrow Yargol after Winter Solstice 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- This great work, once complete, will provide 90 doses of [True Vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) split between the owners of [herb gardens](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) in Skarsind who attended the previous Anvil summit
