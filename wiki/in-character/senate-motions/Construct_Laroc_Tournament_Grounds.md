# Construct Laroc Tournament Grounds

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build the tournament grounds of Laroc in Astolat.
> >
> > Proposed: Astolat, Seconded: Tassato

## Overview

- Constructs the Laroc tournament grounds as detailed in the **[Good old smooth red wine](../winds-of-fortune/Good_old_smooth_red_wine.md#renewing-the-tourney-grounds)** wind of fortune.
- Passed at 50 thrones

## Date

- Winter 384YE

## Cost

- As per the wind of fortune
- 40 wains of weirwood
- 10 thrones
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 thrones and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Ren Tanner after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 10 wains of weirwood provided by Guy Grimbold-Antenucci after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 10 wains of weirwood provided by Ren Tanner after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 10 wains of weirwood provided by Guy Grimbold-Antenucci after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- 50 thrones disbursed to Senator Astolat
- Once complete the Tournament Grounds will function as a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) that will provide a share of 743 rings to every [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md). The season it is completed the [Castellan of Spiral Castle](../../mixed/titles/Castellan_of_Spiral_Castle.md) will be able to select up to a dozen [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) owned by a citizen of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) to be upgraded to rank 2. For the next year after it is completed any Dawnish military unit can be upgraded for one less wain of mithril less than the normal costs.
- The opportunity allows for the colour scheme and decorations for the tourney grounds to be specified as a [necessary detail](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#necessary-details); this decision lies with the Senator for Astolat who proposed the motion.
