## Overview

- Commissions the repair of the Basilica of the Little Mother as detailed [here](../winds-of-fortune/Three_celebrations_and_a_funeral.md#repairing-the-basilica)

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- at least 40 wains of white granite
- at least 80 crowns in labour costs
- at least 3 months to construct
