# Construct Kurstrozi's Lumbermill

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > In times of great hardship, we must be vigilant of unused wealth and unnecessary spending. With this in mind, I commission Kurstrozi's Lumbermill in Drownbark, Ossium. A great work costing 40 white granite which will increase the prosperity of Ossium's forests. This is fully funded by Krasivit Buhkova.
> >
> > Announced by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md)
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 40 wains of white granite
- 15 thrones
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 13 thrones 6 crowns and 40 wains of white granite provided by Oleinsky Kasprzak after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 7 crowns provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 crowns provided by Kostjya after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- The [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) will distribute a share of 20 random materials to the Varushkan [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) owners of [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md)
