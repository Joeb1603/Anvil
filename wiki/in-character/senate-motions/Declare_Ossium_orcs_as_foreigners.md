# Declare Ossium orcs as foreigners

> *In-character text:*
>
> To declare the former orc tribes subjugated by the Druj in Ossium as being foreigners in Imperial Lands  
> *Proposed by Ossium, seconded by Volodmartz*

## Overview

- To declare the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) who are not [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) to be foreigners.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Outcome

- The orcs of Ossium are now foreigners
- Has implications for the Varushkan settlers as indicated [here](../winds-of-fortune/Weight_of_the_world.md#here-to-stay).
