# Construct Bakers' Bowl

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a sinecure, the BAKERS' BOWL, a stadium in Holmauer, Holberg as part of the newly formed Casinean League. Sinecure to create the position SPONSOR OF THE HOLBERG HAMMERS, a lifetime League National position, to be appointed by League Senators. REVOCABLE by the Assembly of Nine, General Assembly and League National Assembly. POWERS: receives an income of at least 216 rings per season. RESPONSIBILITIES: Responsible for the well-being of the staduim and the team. SELF-FUNDED with 20 wains weirwood and coin, by Lula and Bernhard Kleist von Ennerlund. We invite Calcio and Foot-the-Ball teams for other cities and towns unfortunate enough not to yet have their own stadiums to use the Bakers' Bowl for train and to play fixtures.
> >
> > Announced by Lula Kleist von Ennerlund, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Holmauer, Holberg
- Announcement by **Lula Kleist von Ennerlund**, [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)
