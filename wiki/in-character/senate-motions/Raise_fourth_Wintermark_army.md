# Raise fourth Wintermark army

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To raise a Wintermark army supported by the consecration of the Kallavesa marshes. Wintermark's ambition is to field a fourth army. Every single one of you, my beloved and esteemed colleagues' great nations, have benefited from my unbridled support to levy and improve your armies and military situations.
> >
> > Proposed: Hahnmark, Seconded: Sermersuaq

## Overview

- Raises a fourth Wintermark army in the territory of [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md)
- Passed at 400 thrones

## Date

- Winter 384YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 200 wains of mithril
- 50 thrones
- One year to complete

## Progress

- 50 wains of mithril and 12 thrones 4 crowns provided by Hengest Dun after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 40 wains of mithril and 12 thrones 4 crowns provided by Hengest Dun after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 10 wains of mithril provided by Lumi Warmthbringer after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Corwyn Heartsbane after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 50 wains of mithril and 12 thrones 4 crowns provided by Hengest Dun after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 50 wains of mithril and 11 thrones 6 crowns 15 rings provided by Hengest Dun after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown 5 rings provided by Arlen Frostmire after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, a fourth Wintermark army will be raised
- If the [smokehouse of Atalaq](../winds-of-fortune/Write_the_lines.md#hunt-of-the-north) was constructed at least six months before the mustering of the army was complete then the army would be offered [army qualities](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md) in line with the [Suaq](../nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) tradition without further opportunities being required
