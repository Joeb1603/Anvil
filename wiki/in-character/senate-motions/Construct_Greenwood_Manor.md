# Construct Greenwood Manor

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct Greenwood Manor in Semmerholm and to renovate the Greenwood Market. A unique opportunity that creates two titles: Knight of the Greenwood - elected annually by Dawnish National Assembly. Reeve of the Greenwood Fair - elected annually by Dawnish National Assembly. Both subject to revocation by General and Dawnish National Assembly.
> >
> > Announced by Bertrand, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Commissions Greenwood Manor in Elvette, [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md)
- Further details on Greenwood Manor and the Greenwood Market can be found [here](../winds-of-fortune/Between_wind_and_water.md#the-manor-of-the-greenwood).
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Summer 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 10 wains of white granite
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 40 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 5 thrones, 10 wains of weirwood and 10 wains of white granite provided by Bertrand after Summer Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete the commission would create two [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md); the [Knight of the Greenwood](../../mixed/titles/Knight_of_the_Greenwood.md), and the [Reeve of Greenforest Fair](../../mixed/titles/Reeve_of_Greenforest_Fair.md) both of which would be appointed annually by judgement of the Dawnish National Assembly.
