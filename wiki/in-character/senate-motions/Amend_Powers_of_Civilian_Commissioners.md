# Amend Powers of Civilian Commissioners

> *In-character text:*
>
> To amend the role of Civil Commissioner to remove the power to build Follies*Proposed by Kahraman, Seconded by Feroz*

### Overview

- This motion was to amend the previous [motion](Create_titles_of_Civilian_Commissioners.md) to create the title of [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) to remove the power to [announce](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) the construction of [follies](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Summer 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The power of the Civilian Commissioners to announce the construction of Follies has been removed.
