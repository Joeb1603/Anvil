# Amend powers of the Bursar of the Imperial Academy

> *In-character text:*
>
> To amend the sinecure of Bursar of the Academy to be a lifetime position, and to grant the position a stipend of 6 thrones a year.  
>  
> *Proposed by Tassato, Seconded by Casinea.*

### Overview

- Amending the title [Bursar of the Imperial Academy](../../out-of-character/events/Bursar_of_the_Imperial_Academy.md) to include an annual, rather than seasonal, stipend.
- The amendment also makes the this [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) a life-long appointment rather than one that needs to be regularly re-elected; this amendment was not necessary as the title was already a life-time appointment.

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Cost

- Stipend: 6 Thrones annually.

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Bursar of the Imperial Academy](../../out-of-character/events/Bursar_of_the_Imperial_Academy.md) was amended.
