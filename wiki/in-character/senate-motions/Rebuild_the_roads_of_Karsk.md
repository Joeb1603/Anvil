# Rebuild the roads of Karsk

> *In-character text:*
>
> To Commission a Great Work The Rebuilding of the Roads of Karsk in Karsk.
>
> To benefit the mines and forests of region with 100 resources.
>
> Will also allow Varushka to support a third Army.
>
> Self Funded.  
>  
> *Proposed by Karsk, seconded by Holberg*.

### Overview

- This is to restore the [devastated](../winds-of-fortune/Crow_on_the_cradle.md#supply) road network of [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md).
- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Cost

- 80 wains of White Granite
- 20 Thrones in labour costs
- Time: 6 months to construct

### Progress

- 40 wains of white granite and 20 thrones supplied by Radomir of Varushka after the Autumn Equinox 379YE.
- 40 wains of white granite supplied by Radomir of Varushka after the Winter Solstice 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- This [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md), when completed, will increase production for all [mines](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forests](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) in the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) of Karsk.
- Once the road network of Karsk is restored to its former state, [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) will be able to support a third [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md).
