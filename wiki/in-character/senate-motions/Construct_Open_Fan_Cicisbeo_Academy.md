# Construct Open Fan Cicisbeo Academy

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the construction of the Open Fan Cicisbeo Academy. A self funded money sinecure costing 10 white granite & 3 thrones 6 crowns in labour. It will provide income and create a title of "Headmistress of the Open Fan Academy" as appointed by senators of the League. This title has tenure and will be built in Cigno, Sarvos.
> >
> > Proposed: Sarvos, Seconded: Spiral

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Cigno, [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md).
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Giacomo Corvinoscuro after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the position of [Headmistress of the Open Fan Academy](../../mixed/titles/Headmistress_of_the_Open_Fan_Academy.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous vote of the senators of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE
