# Resupply Imperial Armies

> *In-character text:*
>
> I, Dan Brackensong, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies announce resupply of the following armies:
>
> The Bounders
>
> The Golden Axe
>
> The Quiet Step
>
> The Red Wind Corsairs
>
> *Approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies*

### Overview

- Resupply [The Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) army, [The Bounders](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#bounders)
- Resupply the [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) army, [The Golden Axe](../nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md#the-army-of-the-golden-axe)
- Resupply the [Navarri](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) army, [The Quiet Step](../nations/navarr/Navarr_military_concerns.md#the-quiet-step)
- Resupply the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) army, [The Red Wind Corsairs](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#the-red-wind-corsairs)
- This motion was approved by the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) using the legal power to [resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) armies granted to them by the Senate
