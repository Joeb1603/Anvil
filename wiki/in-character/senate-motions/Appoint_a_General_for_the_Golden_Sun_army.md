# Appoint a General for the Golden Sun army

> *In-character text:*
>
> Elect a new general from available Dawnish candidates.  
>  
> *Procedural Motion.*

### Overview

- One of the Senators for [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) was forced to retire early from the Summit, leaving them unable to appoint a new general.

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Nominees

- Clarence Oswald of House Arwood was the sole nominee

### Campaign Outcome

- Clarence Oswald of House Arwood was appointed General of the Golden Sun army.

### Voting

| State | Votes For | Votes Against | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nominee | 23 | 0 | Passed |
