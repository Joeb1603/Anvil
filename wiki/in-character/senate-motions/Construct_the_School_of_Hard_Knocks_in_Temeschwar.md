# Construct the School of Hard Knocks in Temeschwar

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build the School of Hard Knocks in Temeschwar, using 20 wains of white granite. Creating the title 'Maestro of Wolves' appointed by unanimous decision of League senators.
> >
> > Announced by D'ardante, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) as per the opportunity presented in the **[Schools and sawmills](../winds-of-fortune/Schools_and_sawmills.md#school-of-hard-knocks)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 20 wains of white granite
- 60 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 7 thrones 4 crowns and 20 wains of white granite provided by Valentina von Temeshwar after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now complete the [Master of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Sword_Academy_of_Temeschwar.md) will be available to be elected by the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 385YE
