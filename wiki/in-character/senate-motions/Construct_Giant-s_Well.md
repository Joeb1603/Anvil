# Construct Giant's Well

> *In-character text:*
>
> That we construct a folly in Mitwold, in the Marches, self-funded to "cap the giants well".  
> *Proposed by Mitwold, Seconded by Bastion*

### Overview

- A [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) consisting of a white granite menhir - a standing stone - placed over the "Giant's Well" in [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md).
- Passed in principle

### Date

- Autumn 381YE

### Cost

- [Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md#costs) apply; this is only the minimum required to construct the folly there is no upper limit.
- 5 wains of white granite
- 10 Crowns labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 5 wains of white granite and 10 crowns provided by William Guildenstern after Autumn Equinox 381YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- A menhir, known as [the Pale Chain](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#the-pale-chain), now covers the Giant's Well.
