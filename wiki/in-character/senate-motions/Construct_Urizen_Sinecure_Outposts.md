# Construct Urizen Sinecure Outposts

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct outposts and wards upon Urizen Mana Sinecures to resolve the escalating manavore problem currently affecting production, and preventing spreading to the rest of the Empire.
>
> To be self funded at a cost of 2 White Granite for the outposts and an additional 4 crowns per mana sinecure.
>
> *Proposed by Morrow, Seconded by Kallavesa*

### Overview

- The commission of a series of preventative measures to protect mana [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) production within Urizen.
- Passed in Principle.

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Cost

- 10 wains of White Granite
- 20 Crowns in labour costs
- Time: 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 10 wains of White Granite and 20 crowns supplied.

### Campaign Outcome

The wards are now in place around, and the worst of the vermin have been cleared from the vicinity of the [Caves of the Claw](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Claw.md#the-caves-of-the-claw), the [Tower of the Fall](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Tower_of_the_Fall.md#the-tower-of-the-fall), the [Storm Vault](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Storm_Vault.md#the-storm-vault), the [Glorious Fountain of Dawn and Dusk](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Glorious_Fountain.md#the-gorious-fountain-of-dawn-and-dusk), and the [Starlight Drift](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Starlight_Drift.md).

The new construction has been entirely successful in repelling the crystal-eaters - meaning that no mana is now lost to the creatures. Such creatures are normally a problem (albeit small) so as a consequence of eliminating them, each sinecure now provides an additional bounty of 1 crystal mana per season.
