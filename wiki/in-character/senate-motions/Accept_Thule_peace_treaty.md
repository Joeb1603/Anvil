# Accept Thule peace treaty

> *In-character text:*
>
> To accept the peace treaty negotiated with the Thule.  
>  
> *Proposed by Volodmartz, Seconded by Morrow.*

*[Image: The Thule peace treaty](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:ThuleTreaty.png)*

### Overview

- A delegation from the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) negotiated a treaty.
- The treaty was presented to the Senate.

### Date

- Summer 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

The Thule will withdraw from two regions - Branoc and Crowslook - and will not harm any Varushkan, and Varushkans will not harm the Thule.

The Thule will receive the Silver Peaks and the Empire will withdraw all forces, and the Thule will leave the rest of Sermersuaq.

All hostilities between the Thule and the Empire will cease for three years. After one year of the treaty holding, information aid will be negotiated to the Empire about the Enemies of the Empire and other trade opportunities will be explored.

The Thule will not invade any other controlled region of the Empire and the Empire will not invade any other controlled region of the Thule.

The Thule will receive a ritual they have chosen from an approved list of rituals. The Thule will receive Crystalline Focus of Aesh from the approved list and shall be provided in a usable ritual script.

There will be no raids between the Empire and the Thule. Any breach against the Thule will be treated as an act against the Empire and will be dealt accordingly, and any act against the Empire will be treated as an act against the Thule and will be dealt with accordingly.
