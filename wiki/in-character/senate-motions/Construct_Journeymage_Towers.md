# Construct Journeymage Towers

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Journeymage Towers in Lacre, Malimorzi and Bittershore. Call for a dispersal of funds
> >
> > Proposed: Semmerholm, Seconded: Karov

## Overview

- Constructs a series of [follies](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Semmerholm, Ossium, and Karov to remove the threat to ships posed by [Dho'uala](../../mixed/titles/Dho-uala.md) as per the **[Less welcome tenants](../winds-of-fortune/Less_welcome_tenants.md#wards-and-bargains)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- 60 Thrones disbursed to the [Senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md)
- This motion received the support of the Wisdom Assembly through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-58) enacted by **Kalina Jadwigowna Piosnkowa Prochnost**

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 24 white granite
- 72 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Outcome

Now complete, the threat to the ships on the Semmerlak has been resolved. The *Wavewatchers* sodality has been firmly established. The Imperial Senate passed a [motion](../senate-sessions/386YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#create-title-of-watcher-of-the-semmerlak) to create the title of [Watcher of the Semmerlak](../../mixed/titles/Watcher_of_the_Semmerlak.md) during the Autumn Equinox 386YE.
