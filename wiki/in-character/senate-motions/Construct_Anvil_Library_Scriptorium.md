# Construct Anvil Library Scriptorium

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Anvil Library Scriptorium is a self funded sinecure (12 wains of white granite and three thrones in labour using standard costings). The sinecure will provide liao and votes in the Imperial Synod. The sinecure of the Scriptorium and the sodality of the Anvil Library will be governed by the Armarius of the Anvil Library, an Imperial title appointed by judgement of the Wisdom Assembly, and revoked by the Wisdom Assembly. The Armarius has tenure serving until they die, resign or are revoked. The sinecure will be built in Rodez, Foracci, Sarvos
> >
> > Claus Friedrich von Holberg, Bearer of the Second Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md)
- Announcement by the [Bearer of the Second Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 12 wains of white granite
- 3 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 12 wains of white granite provided by Giacomo Corvinoscuro after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the position of [Armarius of the Anvil Library](../../mixed/titles/Armarius_of_the_Anvil_Library.md) will be available to appoint by the Virtue Assembly of Wisdom.
