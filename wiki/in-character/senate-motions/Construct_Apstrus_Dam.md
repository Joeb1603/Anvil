# Construct Apstrus Dam

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Legion Engineer is to build the way less taken and construct the dam in Apstrus to direct water into Icarion. This will cost 30 white granite and the labour of an Imperial army. As part of this, we will relocate the books of Solen's Doubt which will cost 5 thrones. This is self-funded.
> >
> > Announced by Firesong Arachas, Legion Engineer

## Overview

[Image: TowerJacks Colour.png](../military-council-orders/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Military_Council_orders.md#towerjacks)

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Apstrus, Spiral
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)
- More information can be found in the **[Million miles of water](../winds-of-fortune/Million_miles_of_water.md#the-way-less-taken)** and **[Damming the Doubt](../winds-of-fortune/Damming_the_Doubt.md)** winds of fortune
- This motion received support from the [Towerjacks](../../mixed/government/military/Towerjacks.md) following the Winter Solstice 387YE

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 30 wains of white granite
- 5 thrones to carve the names of the dead into the stones
- 5 thrones to move the books of Solen's Doubt

## Progress

- 30 wains of white granite provided by Bloodcrow Rasp after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 4 thrones 7 crowns provided by Raine Tealeaf after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 3 thrones 6 crowns 4 rings provided by Halkyon of the Myriad Stream after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 throne 1 crown 16 rings provided by Callista after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the dam will have the following effects:

- [Icarion](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#icarion) will become an oasis
- Taxation produced by the territory will increase by 6 thrones a season
