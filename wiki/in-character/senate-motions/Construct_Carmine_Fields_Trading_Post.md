# Construct Carmine Fields Trading Post

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Wordes, Numbers, Figures and Knotes Carmine Fields Trading Post and create the position of Envoy to the Karass
> >
> > Proposed: The Barrens, Seconded: Semmerholm

## Overview

- Constructs a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in Carmine Fields, [The Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Little mercy](../winds-of-fortune/Little_mercy.md#trading-at-carmine-field)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 weirwood
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 4 thrones 2 crowns and 12 wains of weirwood provided by Reynard of De Gauvain after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the title of [Envoy to the Karass](../../mixed/titles/Envoy_to_the_Karass.md) will be available to appoint by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of Dawnish [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) captains for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 387YE for a shortened term of three seasons.
