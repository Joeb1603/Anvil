# Decommission Verthandi spy network

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion: to decommission the Otkodov spy network.  
>  
> *Proposed by Kallavesi, seconded by Karsk.*

### Overview

- The [Verthandi](../barbarians/Verthandi.md) [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md) will be [decommissioned](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md#decommissioning-a-spy-network).
- Passed at 5 Thrones

### Date

- Winter 381YE

### Cost

- 5 Thrones, directly from treasury
- 3 months to complete
- 2 Throne upkeep is still paid for this season

### Campaign Outcome

- The [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in [Verthandi](../barbarians/Verthandi.md) will be rolled up discreetly.
- [Military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) can no longer be assigned to the spy network
