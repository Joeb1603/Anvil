# Construct Sweetwater Springs

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To disburse funds for and to construct the Sweetwater Springs, a series of wells and fountains in Sarvos for protection against fire.
> >
> > This is a great work and does not create an Imperial positionProposed by Sarvos, Seconded by Bastion

## Overview

- Commissions a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) in line with the plans presented in the **[The DOOM that came to Sarvos](../winds-of-fortune/The_DOOM_that_came_to_Sarvos.md#rising-flames)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- Passed at 150 thrones

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 20 wains of white granite
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 10 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 10 thrones, 20 wains of weirwood and 20 wains of white granite provided by Gancio di Catazar after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- 150 thrones disbursed to the Senator for Sarvos
- Once completed the Sweetwater Springs will provide a shared bounty to all [herb gardens](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) in Sarvos
