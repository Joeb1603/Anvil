# Construct Bears Rest

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Legion Engineer will construct Bears Rest, a mana sinecure in Northspires, Hahnmark for ten wains of mithril and 30 crowns of labour. The title of Steward of Bears Rest will be chosen by unanimous decision of the Wintermark senators and be responsible for collecting the mana that flows down the river from the melting glacier upstream.
> >
> > Announced by Firesong Arachas, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Northspires, Hahnmark
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of mithril
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 3 thrones 5 crowns provided by Bjorn after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the title of *Steward of Bear's Rest* will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the Wintermark senators
