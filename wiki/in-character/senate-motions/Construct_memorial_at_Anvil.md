# Construct memorial at Anvil

> *In-character text:*
>
> Create a sinecure of a Memorial to the fallen on Anvil.  
>  
> Proposed by Conscience of the Senate, seconded by Bastion

### Overview

- Construct a memorial in [Anvil](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#anvil)
- Passed in principle; no funding requested

### Date

- Summer 378YE

### Cost

- 3 Wains of White Granite to create the monument.
- 10 White Granite to create the monument as a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md).

### Progress

- 10 wains of White Granite provided by Ira of Felix's Watch Banner of Highguard after Winter Solstice 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- When complete and the title [clarified](Amend_title_of_Custodian_of_the_Imperial_War_Memorial.md), the sinecure will automatically create the title of [Custodian of the Imperial War Memorial](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Imperial_War_Memorial.md).
