# Enlargement of the Golden Sun

> *In-character text:*
>
> Dispersal of funds for the enlargement of the Golden Sun. This is a limited time opportunity to commission with a saving of 50 wains of mithril and 100 crowns compared to standard enlargement costs.  
> *Proposed by Weirwater, seconded by Tassato.*

## Overview

- To enlarge the Golden Sun army, in line with the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented by the [recent proposal](../winds-of-fortune/Swords_and_sorcery.md#golden-sun).
- Passed at 0 Thrones

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- This enlargement uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/Swords_and_sorcery.md#golden-sun).
- 100 wains of mithril
- 200 crowns
- Nine months to complete

## Progress

- 25 wains of mithril provided by Bohemond De Rondell after Spring Equinox 383YE
- 6 thrones 2 crowns provided by Eadric Fjellrevening de Rondell Rezia di Tassato after Summer Solstice 383YE
- 2 crowns provided by Richardo Glostari di Sarvos after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 18 rings in small donations provided after Autumn Equinox 383YE
- 50 wains of mithril and 18 thrones 3 crowns 2 rings provided by Branwyn i Leons del Mar i Coeurdefer i Riqueza after Winter Solstice 383YE
- 25 wains of mithril provided by Branwyn i Leons del Mar i Coeurdefer i Riqueza after Spring Equinox 384YE

## Campaign Outcome

- When completed the Golden Sun will become a [large](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#enlargement) army.
