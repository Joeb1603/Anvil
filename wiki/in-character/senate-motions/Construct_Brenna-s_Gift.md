# Construct Brenna’s Gift

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a great work in Hahnmark that increases farming revenue.  
>  
> *Imperial Wayleave by Jorgen Brenna*

## Overview

- Passed.

## Date

- Spring 382YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md#costs) apply.
- 40 wains of weirwood
- 80 crowns in labour costs.

## Progress

- 40 wains of weirwood and 80 crowns provided by Jorgen Brenna after Spring Equinox 382YE.

## Outcome

- When completed this will provide cash split between the farm owners of [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md).
- The necessary detail of which [region](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md#region) the great work is built in remains.
