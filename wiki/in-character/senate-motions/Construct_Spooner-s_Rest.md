# Construct Spooner's Rest

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the liao sinecure "Spooner's Rest", to be located in Hahnmark at Wood Heath within Dunhearth Hall. To be held by the "Conscience of the Dunnings". Appointed annually by the Wintermark National Assembly and to be revoked by the Wintermark National Assembly. Built to celebrate the life and passions of the fabled Tobrytan Dunning, the church has many depictions of golden eagles and yellow suns. The holder is responsible for providing advice and guidance for the Dunning family
> >
> > Announced by Eldrid 'Phoenix Flame' Vestardottir, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Wood Heath, Hahnmark
- Announcement by **Eldrid 'Phoenix Flame' Vestardottir**, as the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Spring 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones 5 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Eldrid 'Phoenix Flame' Vestardottir after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Conscience of the Dunnings](../../mixed/titles/Conscience_of_the_Dunnings.md) will be available to appoint by the Wintermark Assembly for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 388YE
