# Appoint General of the Wolves of War

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint a General to the Wolves of War  
>  
> *Proposed by Sarvos, seconded by Karsk.*

### Overview

- This motion was to choose a citizen to appoint as [General](../../mixed/titles/General.md) to [League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) the [Wolves of War](../nations/the-league/The_League_military_concerns.md#the-wolves-of-war).
- This motion was raised in the absence of a unanimous agreement among the four League [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md).

### Date

- Autumn 379YE.

### Outcome

- Gabriel Barossa was elected.
