# Construct Barossa College of Imperial Strategy

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Senate give permission to build the Barossa College of Imperial Strategy. That the prismatic ink produced by this College be divided between the League Generals.  
> *Proposed by Tassato, seconded by Redoubt*

## Overview

- This [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) arose from a [suggestion](../winds-of-fortune/An_affirming_flame.md#the-business-of-war) by the [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Zakalwe](../eternals/Zakalwe.md).
- Passed.

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Costs

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/An_affirming_flame.md#the-quill-and-the-sword)
- 20 wains of white granite
- 20 wains of mithril
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 120 crowns labour
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of white granite, 10 wains of mithril, and 15 thrones provided by Severin Teyhard von Holberg of the House of Seven Mirrors after Summer Solstice 383YE.
- 2 wains of white granite and 3 wains of mithril provided by Gabriel Barossa after Summer Solstice 383YE.
- 8 wains of white granite, 7 wains of mithril, and 20 wains of weirwood provided by Rodrigo Barossa de Tassato Regario after Summer Solstice 383YE.

## Outcome

- Once complete, the Barossa College of Imperial Strategy would provide 8 doses of [prismatic ink](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#prismatic-ink) each season, to the General of the Wolves of War and 8 doses to the general of the Towerjacks.
