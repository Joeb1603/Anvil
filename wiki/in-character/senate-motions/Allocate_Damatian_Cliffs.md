# Allocate Damatian Cliffs

> *In-character text:*
>
> To allocate the Damatian Cliffs as an Imperial Position.  
> *Proposed by Therunin, seconded by Upwold.*

## Overview

- To [allocate](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#allocation) the [Damatian Cliffs](../../mixed/titles/Damatian_Cliffs.md) as an Imperial position.
- Passed.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Outcome

- The Damatian Cliffs will continue to be an Imperial position, and any Imperial citizen is eligible to hold the associated title.
