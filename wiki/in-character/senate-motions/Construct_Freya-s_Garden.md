# Construct Freya's Garden

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a Great Work in Hahnmark, that provides herbs to heal the Empire, to be named Freya's Garden.  
> *Imperial Wayleave by Tyr Brennan*

## Overview

- Commission a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md)
- Announcement.

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- This [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md#costs).

## Progress

- 50 wains of weirwood and 100 crowns provided by Tyr Brenna after Winter Solstice 382YE

## Campaign Outcome

- The [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) will provide a bounty of 84 [herbs](../../mixed/magic/potions/Herb.md) divided between the [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) owners of [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md).
