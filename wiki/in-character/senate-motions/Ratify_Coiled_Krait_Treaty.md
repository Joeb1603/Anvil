# Ratify Coiled Krait Treaty

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Ratify treaty with the Druj sept known as Coiled Krait
> >
> > Proposed: Karov, Seconded: Karsk

## Overview

- Ratifies a [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) with a group of the Druj
- Failed

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## The Treaty

Treaty with the Coiled Krait formerly of the Banded Snake clan, Summer 387YE

We the Empire:  
Declare the Coiled Krait to be foreigners with all the protections of Imperial law  
Cede a town. There, they will be allowed to establish homes and live freely as foreigners, safe from the ravages of their kinfolk

The Coiled Krait:  
Will offer information of military significance to the Empire to assist in their campaign in the [Sarangrave](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md).  
They will not take up arms against the Druj, but will offer whatever other help they are able to in this season before leaving for their new home.  
The Coiled Krait agree to be bound by all Imperial laws and will end the practice of slavery amongst their people when they cross the border into Imperial lands.
