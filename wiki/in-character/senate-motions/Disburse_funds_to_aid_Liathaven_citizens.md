# Disburse funds to aid Liathaven citizens

> *In-character text:*
>
> Send an aid package to Liathaven  
>  
> *Proposed by Kallavesa, seconded by Therunin.*

### Overview

- A request for funds
- Passed at 10 thrones

### Date

- Passed Autumn 378YE

### Progress

- Funds disbursed to Uskolli, Senator for Kallavesa.
- Additional funds for this project were contributed to the civil service by Geraint Broad-Backed, consisting of 4 thrones, 7 crowns, 16 rings and 5 measures of [ambergelt](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt).
