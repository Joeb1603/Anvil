# Construct Garden of Falling Snow

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create at no cost to the senate the Garden of Falling Snow, a herb garden in Wintermark - Hahnmark, Position of "Gatekeeper of falling snow" to be appointed by unanimous vote of Wintermark's Senators, Herb gardens are great. Can we move to a vote please?  
>  
> *Proposed by Hahnmark, seconded by Sermersuaq*

### Overview

- A [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) which provides additional [herbs](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md#herbs).
- The holder of the sinecure will be appointed by unanimous decision of the [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md).

### Date

- Summer 379YE

### Cost

- The motion does not specify the size of the sinecure, but [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs) apply.

### Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood supplied by Hengest Dun of Wintermark after Summer Solstice 379
- 20 crowns supplied by Hengest Dun of Wintermark after Summer Solstice 379

### Campaign Outcome

- Construction is now complete creating the Wintermark national [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Gatekeeper of Falling Snow](../../mixed/titles/Gatekeeper_of_Falling_Snow.md).

### Constitution

- No information is provided as to how large this sinecure is. The senator may decide this.
- No information is provided as to where in Hahnmark the sinecure will be constructed. The senator may decide this.
