# Construct House of Guerra

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission a great work in Madruga, Brass Coast, to take advantage of a special opportunity to construct a ministry to allow purchase of liao. To create the position of Hand of Guerra, a national title in the Brass Coast. Appointed yearly by the Brass Coast National Assembly. Revocable by National and General Assembly. Cost 20 white granite, 40 Crowns. Self-funded.  
> *Proposed by Madruga, seconded by Kahraman.*

## Overview

- This commission is the result of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to [support refugees](../winds-of-fortune/What_will_be-_was_not.md#there-is-pride-in-sanctuary).
- Passed.

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Costs

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/What_will_be-_was_not.md#the-house-of-guerra)
- 20 wains of white granite
- 40 crowns labour
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 5 thrones and 20 wains of white granite provided by Constanza i Kalamar i Guerra after Autumn Equinox 383YE

## Outcome

- When complete the commission will create the title of [Hand of Guerra](../../mixed/titles/Hand_of_Guerra.md). The title would most likely be appointed by the Freeborn national assembly, as a Freeborn national position.
