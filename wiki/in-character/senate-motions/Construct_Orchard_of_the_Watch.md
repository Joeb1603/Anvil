# Construct Orchard of the Watch

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Orchard of the Watch in Greensward, Mournwold. Fully funded by Friar John of the Mourn.
> >
> > Proposed: Mournwold, Seconded: Necropolis

## Overview

- Constructs a [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Greensward, [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) as per the **[Let the day die](../winds-of-fortune/Let_the_day_die.md#the-morning-also)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- This motion received the support of the Pride Assembly through a [statement of principle](../synod-judgements/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-162) raised by **Friar John of the Mourn**

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 10 white granite
- 20 weirwood
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns, 20 wains of weirwood and 10 wains of white granite provided by Friar John of the Mourn after Summer Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete the [haunted](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#haunted) nature of Greensward has changed. The region has gained the *memorial* quality - as long as the trees stand the angry ghosts still left on the Greensward will not trouble the living. If the Orchard of the Watch is ever destroyed, however, then not only will the ghosts rise from their graves once again there is every chance they will be absolutely furious. Their wrath will likely fall on whoever destroyed the orchard and there's no easy way to predict what form it would take.
