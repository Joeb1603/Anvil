# Construct High Path to Otkodov

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a new high path through the mountains to Otkodov, enabling ilium to be exchanged for good as a ministry, and enhancing northern trade.
> >
> > Proposed: Volodmartz, Seconded: Karsk

- Constructs a high path as per **[Pride and prejudice](../winds-of-fortune/Pride_and_prejudice.md#gift-of-the-dwindling-star)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per the wind of fortune
- 8 wains of white granite
- 2 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 8 wains of white granite provided by Kostjya after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 throne 7 crowns provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Summer Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Now completed this ministry allows the custodian of the [Gift of the Dwindling Star](../../mixed/titles/Gift_of_the_Dwindling_Star.md) to trade food for additional rings of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) from the Thule
