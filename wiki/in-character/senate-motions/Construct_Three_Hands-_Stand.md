# Construct Three Hands' Stand

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the weirwood bourse seat sinecure in Holberg at the request of the Sand Fishers and to create the Imperial title of the Red-Eyed Reaper of Three Hands' Stand to be appointed by League National Auction
> >
> > Announced by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar, Master of Rings

## Overview

- Creates a bourse seat in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) producing [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md) as per **[The greatest city in the world](../winds-of-fortune/The_greatest_city_in_the_world.md#flaxen-pillars)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Master of Rings](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Rings.md)

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Cost as per opportunity
- 20 wains of mithril
- 60 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 4 wains of mithril and 1 throne 4 crowns provided by Claus Friedrich von Holberg after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 4 wains of mithril and 1 throne 4 crowns provided by Gancio di Catazar after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 4 wains of mithril and 1 throne 4 crowns provided by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 4 wains of mithril and 1 throne 4 crowns provided by Lula Kleist von Ennerlund after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete, the [Red-Eyed Reaper](../../mixed/titles/Flaxen_Pillars.md) will be [auctioned](../../mixed/economy/Auction_of_the_Seats.md#additional-titles) as a national Bourse seat of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) for the first time at the Spring Equinox 386YE for a reduced term
