# Expand the Docks at Korotny

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To enlarge and expand the docks at Korotny and create the title of Harbourmaster of the Semmerlak
> >
> > Announced by Pavel Novak, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Expands the docks at Korotny.
- More information on the opportunity to expand the docks at Korotny can be found in the **[Apples from a tree](../winds-of-fortune/Apples_from_a_tree.md#a-ripple-on-the-lake)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by [bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md).

## Date

- Spring 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in Wind of Fortune
- 18 wains of [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md)
- 36 crowns labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 18 wains of white granite provided by Vitaly Grigorovich Dukov after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 4 thrones 4 crowns provided by Triska Markova after Spring Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

Once work on the docks are completed the title of [Harbourmaster of the Semmerlak](../../mixed/titles/Harbourmaster_of_the_Semmerlak.md) will be available to be appointed
