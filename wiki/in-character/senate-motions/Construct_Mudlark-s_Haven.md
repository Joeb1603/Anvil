# Construct Mudlark's Haven

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct a fortified steading in the Feverwater, Therunin, called "Mudlark's Haven." This is a limited opportunity and fully funded.
> >
> > Proposed Therunin, Seconded Hercynia

## Overview

- Commissions an expansion of the Feverwater Docks, [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md).
- Further details on the Feverwater Docks, and what their construction means, can be found [here](../winds-of-fortune/Make_a_deal.md#expanding-the-docks)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 6 wains of white granite
- 12 wains of weirwood
- 36 crowns in labour costs,
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 4 thrones 4 crowns, 12 wains of weirwood and 6 wains of white granite provided by Travid Longest Path after Summer Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete the [Dredgemaster of Feverwater](../../mixed/titles/Dredgemaster_of_Feverwater.md) will see their seasonal income of ingots increase as well as gaining access to a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md)
- The expanded docks will also allow [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to take the *Adventure in the Sarangrave* action.
