# Construct Grand Lodge of Sevenfold Path

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I announce the building of the Grand Lodge of the Sevenfold Path in Necropolis, including the extended basilica. We built it with the aid of Adamant, the Shaper of Mountains, via a personal boon.
> >
> > Announced by Galene Netherwatch

## Overview

- Constructs a grand lodge in Sanctuary Sands, Necropolis
- Announcement by **Galene Netherwatch**
- The grand lodge of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md) was first [presented](../winds-of-fortune/In_harmony_and_unison.md) shortly before the Winter Solstice 387YE.

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 65 wains of weirwood
- 45 wains of white granite
- 15 wains of mithril
- 375 crowns in labour costs
- 5 thrones in upkeep
- Nine months to construct

## Progress

- 4 crowns provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Elagabala after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 18 rings in small donations provided after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the Grand Lodge of the Sevenfold Path will have the following effects:

- Adds 15 mana crystals to the Conclave font each season
- Incorporates a [grand basilica](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md#grandeur) dedicated to the Way
- Reduces the cost of mana purchases by the Grandmaster
- Raises the level of investment of congregations in the territory by one
- Allows Highguard to raise and support one additional navy over the current limits
- Provides a dose of true liao to the order's vaults at the Summer and Winter Solstice
- Provides every member of the order with a dose of liao each season for a year
- Adds the *Voice of the Paragons* to Imperial lore for as long as [Net of Gossamer Chains](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Net_of_Gossamer_Chains.md) is interdicted
- The Grandmaster of the Sevenfold Path will be able to use a Declaration of Concord to request a single relic linked to an important historical figure for the Winter Solstice after the grand lodge is complete
