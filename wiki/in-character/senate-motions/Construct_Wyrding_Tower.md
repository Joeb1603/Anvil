# Construct Wyrding Tower

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I commission the Wyrding Tower, a volhov tower proposed in an appraisal by Graciana i Lopez i Guerra. The Vale of the Verdant Promise will bring prosperity to the ruins left by the Cold Sun and will fund this ministry
> >
> > Announced by Borys Vardovich Straskovich, Boyar of the Iron Roads

## Overview

- Constructs an [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in Livardz, Volodmartz
- Announcement by the [Boyar of the Iron Roads](../../mixed/titles/Boyar_of_the_Iron_Roads.md)
- More information can be found in the **[Sunrise over Livardz](../winds-of-fortune/Sunrise_over_Livardz.md#the-wyrding-pond)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 wains of mithril
- 36 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 6 wains of mithril and 3 thrones 5 crowns provided by Emil Artemov Lenka after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 6 wains of mithril provided by Yaroslav Goraschenko Vernost after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 4 crowns provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the Wyrding Tower has had the following effects:

- The title [Volhov of the Wyrding Tower](../../mixed/titles/Volhov_of_the_Wyrding_Tower.md) will be available to appoint by tally of the votes of Varushkan mine and forest owners for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 388YE
- The threat of the [ashen wind](../winds-of-fortune/Sunrise_over_Livardz.md#the-ashen-wind) has been removed
