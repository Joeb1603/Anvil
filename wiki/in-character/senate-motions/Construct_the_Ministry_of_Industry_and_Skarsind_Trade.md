# Construct the Ministry of Industry and Skarsind Trade

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build the Ministry of Industry and Skarsind Trade, in the territory of Temeschwar. This will form part of the greater Torfast ministries.
>  
>  
> *Proposed by Kahraman, seconded by Madruga*

### Overview

- Passed
- This motion was to take advantage of the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented to [improve the wealth](../winds-of-fortune/The_worth_of_a_life.md#crossroads-of-the-north) of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md).

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Cost

- 8 wains of White Granite
- 2 Thrones in labour costs
- Time : 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 8 wains of white granite and 2 thrones provided by Gant Archama after Spring Equinox 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Northern Traders Guild](../../mixed/titles/Northbound_Trademaster.md) has been built in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) city proper, in the region of [Hanuri](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md#hanrui).
