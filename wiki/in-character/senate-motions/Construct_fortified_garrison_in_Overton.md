# Construct fortified garrison in Overton

> *In-character text:*
>
> Seek Disbursement of funds with the intent of purchasing wains for the building of a Blockhouse in the Mourn, with a view to future upgrades.  
>  
> Proposed by Upwold, seconded by Feroz

### Overview

- Construct a fortified garrison in the town of [Overton](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#overton) in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md).
- Passed at 15 Thrones

### Date

- Summer 378YE

### Cost

- 5 wains of white granite for a fortified manor house to provide a place of refuge for local citizens.
- 5 wains of weirwood for a staging house, which would allow [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to base themselves in Overton to protect the citizenry.
- 2 Thrones Upkeep

### Progress

- 5 wains of white granite provided by Silas Merrick of the Marches in Summer 378YE.
- 5 wains of weirwood provided by Silas Merrick of the Marches in Autumn 378YE.
- During the Spring Equinox 380YE, the garrison was disbanded as part of constructing the [Sheriff of Overton](../../mixed/titles/Sheriff_of_Overton.md) title.

### Campaign Outcome

The senate [voted to disband](Conversion_of_Overton_Garrison.md) the garrison in 380YE following the completion of [Orchard's Watch](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#orchards-watch).
