# Construct Hall of the Soturi

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Hall of the Soturi, an edifice in the Kallavesa Marsh, Kallavesa. This will enable a new Wintermark army to be created with the fearless quality, a unique quality. 20 white granite, 20 weirwood, 120 crowns. Self-funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Rane Shardbearer, Senator for Kallavesa, Seconded: Delara of the House of the Wanderer, Senator for Zenith

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Kallavesa Marsh, Kallavesa
- The Hall of the Soturi was first [proposed](../winds-of-fortune/Flight_of_birds.md#no-half-measures) by the Mystics of Ishal in response to a call to action from the [Raven Seer](../../mixed/titles/Raven_Seer.md).
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 20 wains of white granite
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 15 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 wain of white granite provided by Lumi Warmthbringer after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 wain of white granite provided by Vauraus Korppitkotka after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, Wintermark will be able to raise a single new army based on the Kallavesi tradition with the fearless quality
