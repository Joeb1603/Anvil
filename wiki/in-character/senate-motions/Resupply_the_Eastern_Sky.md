# Resupply the Eastern Sky

> *In-character text:*
>
> Resupply the army of the Eastern Sky.  
>  
> *Approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies.*

### Overview

- Resupply the Dawnish army of the [Eastern Sky](../nations/dawn/Dawn_military_concerns.md#the-eastern-sky).
- Action approved by the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md).

### Date

- Approved Winter 377YE

### Progress

- 27 wains of Weirwood provided by Janusz Dalibor von Temeschwar, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies, after Winter Solstice 377YE

### Campaign Outcome

Resupply an army using 27 wains of weirwood restores fighting strength equal to 540 soldiers to the army.

### Voting

This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General using the legal power to resupply armies granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
