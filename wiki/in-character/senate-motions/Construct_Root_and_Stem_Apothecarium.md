# Construct Root and Stem Apothecarium

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion to construct a Kruidenkenner warehouse in Meade to trade specifically in Bladeroot. To be called the ‘Root and Stem Apothecarium.’ To create the title ‘Master of the Root and Stem’, to be appointed Nationally by the Bourse. Limited opportunity, self funded.  
>
> *Proposed by Mitwold, seconded by Mournwold*

## Overview

- Relates to the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to construct a [warehouse](../winds-of-fortune/A_force_to_be_reckoned_with.md#kruidenkenner-warehouses) in the town of Meade, [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#meade-march)
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 383YE

## Cost

- This opportunity uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/The_mathematics_of_tears.md#warehouses)
- 8 wains of weirwood
- 16 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 4 wains of weirwood provided by William Lowther after the Spring Equinox 383YE.
- 4 wains of weirwood and 2 Thrones provided by Will Talbot after the Spring Equinox 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) will create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), [Master of the Root and Stem](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Root_and_Stem.md)
