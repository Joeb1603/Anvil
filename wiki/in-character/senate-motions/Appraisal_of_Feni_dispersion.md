# Appraisal of Feni dispersion

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appraise the cost of using the militia or private citizens to disperse or arrest the northern Woods-that-Fell Feni in Hercynia.  
> *Proposed by Hercynia, seconded by Hahnmark.*

## Overview

- Commissions an [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) from the civil service, relating to the [feni](../nations/the-marches/Feni.md) currently [causing disruption](../winds-of-fortune/Lethal_and_elusive.md#the-feni) in [Hercynia](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md).
- Only one appraisal may be made each season.
- Failed

## Date

- Winter 382YE
