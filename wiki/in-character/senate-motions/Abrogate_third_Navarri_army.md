# Abrogate third Navarri army

> *In-character text:*
>
> Abrogate the motion raising the third Navarr army
>  
>  
> *Proposed by Therunin, seconded by Sermersuaq*

### Overview

- This motion [abrogates](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#abrogation) the [motion](Raise_Navarr_Army.md) to [raise](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#senate-commissions) the third [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) army
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

It is no longer legally possible to raise a third Navarr army without a new motion to approve it.
