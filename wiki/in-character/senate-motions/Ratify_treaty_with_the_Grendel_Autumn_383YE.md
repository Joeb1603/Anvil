# Ratify treaty with the Grendel Autumn 383YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> To accept the peace treaty with the Grendel.
>
> *Proposed by Madruga, seconded by the Conscience of the Senate*

## Outcome

- This motion was presented as a [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) by the [Imperial Fleet Master](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Fleet_Master.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 383YE

## Treaty

Civil service records indicate the following points were agreed in the treaty with the Grendel.

- There will be peace between the Empire and the Grendel for two years, to be renegotiated in eighteen months during the Spring Equinox 385YE
- The Grendel will not trade with the Druj, nor offer them any other material assistance in their military engagement against the Empire,
- The Grendel will release Imperial slaves, one for each day that the treaty is upheld, with the first lot two weeks after the treaty then each month after that.
- The Empire [cede](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) the regions of [Afarjasse](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#afarjasse) and [Morajasse](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#morajasse) in [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md), and [Ankra](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#ankra), [Cinion](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#cinion) and [Ateri](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#ateri) in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md) to the Grendel
- The Empire will not build any [shipyards](../../mixed/commissions/Shipyard.md) or [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) in the [Bay of Catazar](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md) for the duration of the treaty
- The citizens currently in the regions to be ceded will be allowed two season's grace to relocate to Imperial territory with all of their possessions
- No raiding will take place from either side against the other
- Any altercation that involves citizens of the Empire or the Grendel in the others territories should be dealt with between the [ambassadors](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) in the first instance.

## Campaign Outcome

- The Grendel [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) are now foreigners
- It is illegal for Imperial ships to raid Grendel vessels, or to attack the Broken Shore.
