# Construct Prosperous Counting House in Karsk

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the construction of a Prosperous Counting House in Karsk. Self funded by Wolfs Hearth and Embers of Iversgaard. Costing 5 weirwood, 5 white granite, and 30 crowns.
> >
> > Announced by Laughlan, Master of Rings

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md) that increases the Virtue Fund as per the **[Forwards and Rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Master of Rings](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Rings.md)

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 5 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 throne 1 crown provided by Triska Markova after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 2 crowns provided by Amris Johan Merikovich after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Tom Keeper, of Copperhill after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Ivana Petara Vodjavic after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Vladimir Vladirovich Reznikov after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 1 ring in small donations provided after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 1 throne 6 crowns 19 rings provided by Askien Alanovich Vesimerov after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 5 wains of weirwood and 5 wains of white granite provided by Katya Vasillova Reznikov after Winter Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete, the [Virtue Fund](../../mixed/government/synod/Rewarding.md) receives 12 crowns each summit in donations from citizens of the territory to [the Iron Hearth](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#the-iron-hearth)
