# Fortify Golden Downs in Mitwold

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create a fortification in Mitwold.  
>  
> *Approved by the Imperial Master of Works.*

### Overview

- The creation of a new fortification, Forte Fidelis, at Golden Downs in [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md).
- Fortification approved by the [Imperial Master of Works](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Master_of_Works.md).

### Date

- Approved Winter 377YE

### Cost

- 100 wains of white granite ([Standard cost](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#creating-a-new-imperial-army))
- Time: 1 year
- Upkeep: [10 Thrones](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#upkeep)

### Progress

- 25 wains of White Granite provided by Richard Tunstall, Imperial Master of Works, after Winter Solstice 377YE
- 25 wains of White Granite provided by Richard Tunstall, Imperial Master of Works, after Spring Equinox 378YE
- 25 wains of White Granite provided by Richard Tunstall, Imperial Master of Works, after Summer Solstice 378YE
- 25 wains of White Granite provided by Richard Tunstall, Imperial Master of Works, after Autumn Equinox 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

Construction of the fortification is now complete.

### Voting

This motion was approved by the [Imperial Master of Works](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Master_of_Works.md) using the legal power to raise fortifications granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
