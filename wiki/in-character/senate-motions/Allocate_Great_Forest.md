# Allocate Great Forest

> *In-character text:*
>
> For the allocation of the Great Forest as a national bourse position for Highguard, and is proposed on the virtue of Prosperity*Proposed by Reikos, Seconded by Astolat*

### Overview

- This motion would have allocated the [Great Forest at Reikos](../../mixed/titles/Great_Forest_at_Reikos.md) bourse seat in [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md) as a national position.
- Failed
- A subsequent [motion](Allocate_Great_Forest_II.md) to allocate the Great Forest as Imperial was passed

### Date

- Spring 380YE
