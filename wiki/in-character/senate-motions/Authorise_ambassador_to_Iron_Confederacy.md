# Authorise ambassador to Iron Confederacy

> *In-character text:*
>
> To authorise the Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy to invite the Duke Guiscard of the Iron Confederacy to send his forces into Feroz to deal with the Iron Confederacy rebels, and then immediately withdraw.  
>  
> *Proposed by Feroz, seconded by Upwold.*

### Overview

- The [Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Iron_Confederacy.md) has been authorised by the Imperial Senate to invite Duke Guiscard of Kalino to send troops into [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md).
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 381YE

### Constitution

- The ambassador will still need to invite the Duke to bring his troops to the Empire.
