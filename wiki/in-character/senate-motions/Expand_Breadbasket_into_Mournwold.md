# Expand Breadbasket into Mournwold

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Mournwold expansion to the Breadbasket, along with the Keeper's Cottage in Mitwold and to ask for a disbursement of funds.
> >
> > Proposed: Mournwold, Seconded: Weirwater

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Mournwold and a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Maiden Downs, Mitwold, as per the **[Search high and low](../winds-of-fortune/Search_high_and_low.md)** wind of fortune.
- Passed

- Spring 388YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the expansion of the Breadbasket will have the following effects:

- Grant every farm in the Mournwold an additional 36 rings each season, starting at the Winter Solstice 388YE
- Increases the Marcher ability to support armies by one, once Bregasland is back in Marcher hands

Now complete, the Keeper's Cottage provides the [Keeper of the Breadbasket](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Breadbasket.md) with an income of 12 crowns a season, starting at the Winter Solstice 388YE
