# Disburse funds to citizens of Skarsind

> *In-character text:*
>
> That the Senate recognises the Loyalty, Pride and Prosperity of the Wintermarkers affected by the gifting of Skarsind, and that it allocate funds to ensure their continued Prosperity.*Proposed by Sermersuaq, Seconded by Karov*

### Overview

- This motion followed on from a [previous](Relinquish_Skarsind.md) motion to relinquish [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Passed in Principle
- Passed at 10 Thrones

- Spring 380YE

- 10 Thrones disbursed to Senator Sermersuaq
