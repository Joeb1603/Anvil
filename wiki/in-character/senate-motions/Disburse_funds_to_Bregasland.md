# Disburse funds to Bregasland

## Overview

- Request for funds to be used to [resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#resupply) the [Imperial armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md).

## Progress

- Autumn 378YE, 400 Thrones disbursed to Orrick, Senator Bregasland
- Summer 378YE, 200 Thrones disbursed to Orrick, Senator Bregasland

## Autumn 378YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion to provide funds with the intention of resupplying the Imperial Armies, either though future motions or the Imperial Quartermaster.  
>  
> *Proposed by Bregasland, seconded by Hahnmark*

## Summer 378YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> To distribute funds to Bregasland with the intention of resupplying the armies. The Quartermaster General will be called as an expert witness.  
>  
> *Proposed by Bregasland, seconded by Mitwold*
