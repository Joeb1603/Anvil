# Create title of Imperial Censor

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create a Conclave title of Imperial Censor
>
> With the power to submit one free Declaration of Interdiction per Conclave session
>
> Term of office – Declaration of Candidacy
>
> Responsibilities – To ensure the Empire is safe from malign magical items, especially those of unknown or unacceptable provenance.
>
>  
> *Proposed by Miekarova, Seconded by Volodmartz*

### Overview

- Created an [Imperial Title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) within the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Summer 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Imperial title of [Imperial Censor](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Censor.md)
- The title is appointed by [Declaration of Candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy) in the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md)
