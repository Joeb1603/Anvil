# Construct Hall of the Hunters

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct sinecure. Hall of the Hunters. Sermersuaq, Suaq Wastes, Lake Atkonaroq. 12 white granite, 3 thrones, 3 months. Creates title "Banner-bearer of Atkonaroq"
> >
> > Groza, Legion Engineer

## Overview

- Creates a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md), as detailed in the **[Watching my mistakes](../winds-of-fortune/Watching_my_mistakes.md#a-beacon-in-the-north)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Legion Engineer](../../mixed/titles/Legion_Engineer.md)

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 12 wains of white granite
- 3 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 12 wains of white granite provided by Liv Tidgaring after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Once complete the position of [Banner-bearer of Atkonaroq](../../mixed/titles/Banner-bearer_of_Atkonaroq.md) will be available to appoint by judgement of the Wintermark National Assembly. It will also create a new [adventure](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#adventure) option, *Hunt the Suaq Wastes*, available to military units from Dawn and Wintermark.
