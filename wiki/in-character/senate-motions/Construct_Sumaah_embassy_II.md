# Construct Sumaah embassy II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To grant permission to construct an embassy for the Sumaah Republic.  
>  
> *Proposed by Upwold, seconded by Necropolis.*

### Overview

- Construct a [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md) [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md).
- The embassy will be constructed in [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md).
- Passed at 0 Thrones.

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Costs

- Materials: 25 wains of Weirwood and 25 wains of White Granite ([Standard cost](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md#costs))
- Labour: 12 Thrones
- Time: 1 season
- Upkeep: 5 Thrones

### Progress

- 20 wains of weirwood and 20 wains of white granite supplied by Lucifer Son of Cyrus after the Summer Solstice 379YE.
- 5 wains of weirwood, 5 wains of white granite and 10 thrones supplied by Cyrus, son of Felix after the Winter Solstice 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- Follows the defeat of [a similar vote](Construct_Sumaah_embassy.md) in Winter 378YE.
- When the embassy is complete it will automatically create the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Ambassador to the Sumaah Republic](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sumaah_Republic.md).
