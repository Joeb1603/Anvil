# Construct Palace of Flowers

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct a folly. Palace of Flowers. Solvihill, Skarsind. 60 wains weirwood, 120 crowns. Folly to be ceded to the Yerende sept.
> >
> > Proposed: Skarsind, Seconded: Morrow

## Overview

- Constructs a [garden](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) for the Yerende sept, as detailed in the **[All too well](../winds-of-fortune/All_too_well.md#metal2c-bone2c-and-soil)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per wind of fortune
- 60 wains of weirwood
- 120 crowns in labour costs
- Six months to construct

## Progress

- 11 thrones 2 crowns 12 rings and 33 wains of weirwood provided by Irontide Flax after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 3 thrones and 15 wains of weirwood provided by Bloodcrow Rasp after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 4 crowns 8 rings and 7 wains of weirwood provided by Abel the Rounder of Sarangrave Reforged after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 5 wains of weirwood provided by Redhand Gezzar after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the Yerende will join the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). They will share details of their herbal lore, the key part of which is a set of potion recipes using [realmsroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Realmsroot.md) to create potions useful to orc magicians.
