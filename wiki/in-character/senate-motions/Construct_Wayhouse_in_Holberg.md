# Construct Wayhouse in Holberg

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Self-funded construction of a wayhouse in Utterlund, Holberg. We are providing 16 wains of white granite and 48 crowns. It will be named The Splintered House
> >
> > Announced by Ciapek Splintered Oak, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [wayhouse](../../mixed/commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) in Utterlund, Holberg
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 wains of white granite
- 1152 rings in labour costs (increased by being built in a marsh)
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 6 thrones and 16 wains of white granite provided by Ciapek SplinteredOak after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 6 crowns 10 rings provided by Kaspar Yakovich von Holberg after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown 12 rings provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown 10 rings provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

Now completed, the wayhouse has had the following effects:

- Navarr will be able to take a personal resource in Holberg without suffering the foreign nation penalty.
- The [Keeper of the Splintered House](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Splintered_House.md) will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the senators of Navarr for the first time at the Spring Equinox 388YE
