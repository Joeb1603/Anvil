# Construct Ritual School of the White Shaman

> *In-character text:*
>
> Construct a mana sinecure in Pakaanan's Pass, Skarsind*Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of the Imperial Orcs*

### Overview

- A [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Pakaanan's Pass, [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- This motion was approved by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Cost

- Unknown, but [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs) apply

### Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 20 crowns provided by Yargol after Autumn Equinox 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- This sinecure, once complete, will create the title of [Storm Shaman](../../mixed/titles/Storm_Shaman.md), a national title for the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md)
