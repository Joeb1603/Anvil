# Excavate Gildenheim Runeforge

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To confirm that the runesmiths of Wintermark are the rightful heirs of the Gildermark Runeforge.
> > This should allow the ruins to be carefully transported, preserved and removed to Runegrott in Hahnmark
> >
> > Proposed by Bastion, Seconded by Kallavesa

## Overview

- Motion to excavate and transport the ruins of the [Gildenheim runeforge](../history/Gildenheim_runeforge.md) to Runegrott, [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) as detailed in the **[Conduit to destiny](../winds-of-fortune/Conduit_to_destiny.md#the-runed-ruins)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- 12 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months

## Progress

- 11 thrones provided by Baal after Winter Solstice 383YE
- 1 throne provided by Richardo Glostari di Sarvos after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- Once the excavation of the ruins is complete the cost to construct a [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md) in Runegrott will be reduced by 30 wains of white granite and 60 crowns.
