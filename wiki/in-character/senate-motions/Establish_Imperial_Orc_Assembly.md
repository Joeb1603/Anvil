# Establish Imperial Orc Assembly

> *In-character text:*
>
> Establish a national assembly for the Orc people as for other nations' assemblies in the Synod.  
>  
> Proposed by Conscience, seconded by Hercynia.

*[Image: Gralka, Conscience of the Senate 376YE-377YE](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Gralka.jpg)*

### Overview

- Establish an assembly for the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) in the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md).
- Recognises the [judgement](../synod-judgements/377YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#synod-judgments) of the [Assembly of Pride](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies)

### Date

- Winter 377YE

### Campaign Outcome

- A [national assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies) for the Imperial Orcs has now been established in the Imperial Synod.
- The national assembly will have all the powers and responsibilities of other national assemblies.
