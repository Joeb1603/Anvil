# Amend powers of the High Exorcist II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To amend the powers of the High Exorcist via the following changes:
>
> - Remove the stipend of 7 Thrones.
> - Add the power of rewarding from the Virtue fund up to the value of 7 Thrones.
>
> *Proposed by Segura, Seconded by Kahraman*

### Overview

- This motion was to amend the powers of the [High Exorcist](../../mixed/titles/High_Exorcist.md) to remove their [stipend](../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md#stipend) and grant them an additional judgement each summit to be used on [rewarding](../../mixed/government/synod/Rewarding.md) up to 7 thrones each season.
- Passed

### Date

- Spring 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- A member of the Synod has raised an intent to veto
