# Construct Iron Roundtower

> *In-character text:*
>
> An Announcement by bearer of an Imperial Wayleave, to build the Iron Round tower, a Mithril Ministry. In Delev, near Temeschwar, to create the title "Bargainer of the Iron Tower”  
> *Announced by Dragomir Dragovich*

## Overview

- This commission takes advantage of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to create a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) that deals in mithril called the [Iron Roundtower](../winds-of-fortune/The_honey_offering.md#proposal-iron-roundtower)
- Announcement

## Date

- Summer 383YE

## Costs

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/The_honey_offering.md#proposal-iron-roundtower).
- 10 wains of mithril
- 20 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 20 crowns provided by Dragomir Dragovich after Summer Solstice 383YE.

## Outcome

- Will create the title [Bargainer of the Iron Tower](../../mixed/titles/Bargainer_of_the_Iron_Tower.md), a Varushkan national position.
- Will impact the amounts of mithril going to the public bourse auction at Anvil
