# Amend conditions of Brock's Buffet

> *In-character text:*
>
> The National Bourse position of Brock's Toll's ownership decided by 'Brock's Buffet' an annual competition of arms between the yeoman who were so eager to fight of Dawn and the Marches. This proposal is to allow Navarr a place in this tradition.  
>  
> Proposed by Miaren, seconded by Therunin.

### Overview

- Custodianship of [Brock's Toll](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md#brock27s-toll), a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md), in [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md), is determined by a contest between [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) yeomen.
- The proposal was that the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) would be able to field contestants and potentially gain custodianship of the toll bridge.
- Defeated

- Spring Equinox 378YE
