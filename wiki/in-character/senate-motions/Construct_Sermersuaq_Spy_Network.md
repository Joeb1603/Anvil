# Construct Sermersuaq Spy Network

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The threat of the claw lingers and grows. I commission the means to spy the oncoming wave. Commission a spy network in Sermersuaq
> >
> > Announced by Rahmah i Darwisj i Erigo, Imperial Spymaster

## Overview

- Constructs a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md)
- Announcement by the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md)

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 weirwood
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Aengel Dunn after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 2 crowns in small donations provided after Summer Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) will be able to support the spy network in the territory
