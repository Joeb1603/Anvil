# Construct The Gloaming Forum

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a self-funded ministry called the Gloaming Forum in Operus, Morrow, to be assigned by Tally of the Votes as an Urizen national position. The Gloaming Merchant to be able to purchase weirwood as per the opportunity presented by the Basilisk of the Bourse and the Spire of Golden Wood.
> >
> > Announced by Zorya (Zosia) Swietoslawa Przewalski, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Creates a bourse seat in [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md) as per the **[Bright yellow blooms](../winds-of-fortune/Bright_yellow_blooms.md#the-tide-still-rises)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 wains of white granite
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 4 thrones 4 crowns and 6 wains of white granite provided by Edmundo of Damakan's forge after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 6 wains of white granite provided by Spyridonakes of the Lighthouse-That-Was after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Once the work is complete, the *Gloaming Merchant* will be available to elect by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of the [mana site](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md) owners of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md)
