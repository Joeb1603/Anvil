# Construct Tolvoli Slaughterhouse

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Tolvoli Slaughterhouse in Miekarova. A ministry to sate the dubik of Miekarova. Limited time opportunity, fully self-funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Miekarova, Seconded: Volodmartz

- Constructs a slaughterhouse in Miekarova as per the **[The horror and the wild](../winds-of-fortune/The_horror_and_the_wild.md#heart-of-the-tempest)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per the wind of fortune
- 12 wains of weirwood
- 3 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones 3 crowns and 12 wains of weirwood provided by Misha Klubnika Alexandrovich Petrov after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 4 crowns provided by Kostjya after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Summer Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Now completed the Varushkan national title of [Tolvoli Butcher](../../mixed/titles/Tolvoli_Butcher.md) will be appointed annually each Autumn Equinox by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md)
