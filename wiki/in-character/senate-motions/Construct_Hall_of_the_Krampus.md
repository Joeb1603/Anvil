# Construct Hall of the Krampus

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a hall for the Krampus in Skarsind.
> *Proposed by Skarsind, Seconded by Karsk*

### Overview

- A [folly](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) built for the Krampus, following recent [events](../winds-of-fortune/Goatfoot.md), in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Cost

- 13 wains of white granite
- 26 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 13 wains of white granite and 26 crowns provided by Irontide Vio after Winter Solstice 380YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Krampushall](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#krampushall) has been constructed in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md).
