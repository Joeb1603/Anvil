# Safe passage for the Jotun

> *In-character text:*
>
> That safe passage be granted for Jotun representatives to visit Anvil at an appropriate time to discuss a potential peace treaty.  
>  
> Proposed by Sermersuaq, seconded by Weirwater

### Overview

- For the duration of the visit, the Jotun delegation would have the status of foreigners.
- Passed unanimously

### Date

- Passed Spring 377YE (Event Two 2013).

### Cost

- None

### Progress

- Complete

### Campaign Outcome

A Jotun delegation attended the Summer solstice and [agreed a treaty](Accept_Jotun_ceasefire.md).
