# Construct spy network in Feroz

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a spy network in Feroz
> >
> > Announced by Ka'bal, Bearer of an Imperial Orc Wayleave

## Overview

- Commissions a special [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md), as outlined in the **[Bones in the ocean](../winds-of-fortune/Bones_in_the_ocean.md#a-cuckoo27s-egg)** Wind of Fortune.
- Announcement
- The Senate had previously [amended the power](../senate-sessions/383YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-imperial-orc-wayleave) of the Imperial Orc Wayleave specifically to allow it to commission a spy network.

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non standard costs apply
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 10 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct
- 2 thrones per season upkeep

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 5 wains of weirwood provided by Tog after Winter Solstice 383YE
