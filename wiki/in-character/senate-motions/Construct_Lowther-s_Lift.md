# Construct Lowther's Lift

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the folly "Lowther's Lift", within the Singing Caves, near Overton in Mournwold. This folly would enable the extraction of greater quantities of mithril in the form of a ministry. This will cost 10 wains of weirwood and 20 crowns. This is self funded.
> >
> > Proposed: Mitwold, Seconded: Kallavesa

## Overview

- Constructs a [lift](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) that allows access to rich new veins of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md), as detailed in the **[Wildest dreams](../winds-of-fortune/Wildest_dreams.md#the-deep-mine)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per wind of fortune
- 15 wains of weirwood
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- Six months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by William Lowther after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 5 wains of weirwood provided by Fern after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 5 wains of weirwood **taken by** Jotun Raiders after Spring Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Once completed it will grant the [Custodian of the Singing Caves](../../mixed/titles/Singing_Caves.md) access to a new ministry to purchase wains of mithril at set prices.
- Following the [actions of Yanya Uranduln](../winds-of-war/Shallows_and_miseries.md#meanwhile-in-mournwold) the lifts require another five wains of [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md) and will take another three months
