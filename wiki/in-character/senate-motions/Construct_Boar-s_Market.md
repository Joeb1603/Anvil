# Construct Boar's Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To fund and construct the Boars Market in Astolat, a market which will allow Dawn to purchase bourse resources. This will create the title, Reeve of the Boars Market, which will be elected by well worn path.
> >
> > Proposed: Weirwater, Seconded: Casinea

## Overview

- Commissions a market-fayre at [Boar's Hollow](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md#boars-hollow) in [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md) allowing purchase of [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md), [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md), and [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md).
- This [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) is detailed in the **[Good old smooth red wine](../winds-of-fortune/Good_old_smooth_red_wine.md#boar27s-market)** wind of fortune.
- Passed at 50 Thrones

## Date

- Autumn 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the wind of fortune
- 12 wains of weirwood
- 24 Crowns
- Three months

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 12 wains of weirwood provided by Bertrand after Autumn Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the market-fayre would be overseen by an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) in [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md), the [Reeve of Boar's Market](../../mixed/titles/Reeve_of_Boar-s_Market.md), intended for a [yeofolk](../nations/dawn/Yeofolk_of_Dawn.md) and appointed by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md).
