# Rebuild Roads of Kahraman

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission to rebuild the roads in Kahraman and to build a series of Kamkrag across the territory to allow improved troop movement and defence. This will require 100 white granite, 50 weirwood, and 300 crowns. if the Empire ever cuts trading ties with the Faraden, the Senate will reimburse the Faraden families for the materials they contribute.
> >
> > Proposed: Kahraman, Seconded: Volodmartz

## Overview

- Rebuilds the roads in [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md), as detailed in the **[Time and time again](../winds-of-fortune/Time_and_time_again.md#restoring-the-roads)** wind of fortune
- Passed at 200 thrones

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per wind of fortune
- 130 wains of white granite
- 50 wains of weirwood
- 45 thrones in labour costs
- Nine months to construct; the project cannot progress while the Lasambrian Jotun have free access to Kahraman (see the [Against the Jotun](../winds-of-war/Against_the_Jotun.md#hill) Wind of War).

## Progress

- 37 thrones 4 crowns, 22 wains of weirwood and 30 wains of white granite provided by Delara of the House of the Wanderer after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 7 thrones 4 crowns and 30 wains of white granite provided by Bataguin Family after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 13 wains of weirwood and 38 wains of white granite provided by Delara of the House of the Wanderer after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 15 wains of weirwood and 32 wains of white granite provided by Delara of the House of the Wanderer after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 10 wains of white granite provided by Vesna Borkovna Prochnost after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 10 wains of white granite **taken by** Jotun Raiders after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Due to the [disruption caused by the Lasambrian Orcs](../winds-of-war/Against_the_Jotun.md#hill) after the Autumn Equinox, completion on this project has been delayed.
- The Lasambrian Jotun also seized 10 wains of white granite which will need to be replaced
- Now complete, the roads of Kahraman have been repaired and a series of Kamkrag have been built throughout the territory that allow Freeborn citizens better access to abundant [mines](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md).
