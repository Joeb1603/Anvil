# Construct night mana sinecure in Redoubt

> *In-character text:*
>
> I, Vitore van Temeschwar, the Arcane Architect announce the construction of a night mana sinecure in the territory of Redoubt
> *Announced by the Arcane Architect*

### Overview

- A [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in Naris, Redoubt.
- This motion was announced by the [Arcane Architect](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Architect.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate.

### Date

- Spring 381YE

### Cost

- Unknown

### Progress

- 20 ingots iridescent gloaming, 10 wains of mithril, 10 wains of weirwood, 5 mana crystals, and 30 crowns supplied by Vitore van Temeschwar, the Arcane Architect.

### Campaign Outcome

- This sinecure, [Penumbral Watcher](../../mixed/titles/Penumbral_Watcher.md) will create a new Imperial title appointed by [Declaration of Candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy) in the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md)
