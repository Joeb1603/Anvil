# Construct Meeting Place

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build a feasting hall on Gull Island, Kallavesa. This would act as a new meeting place in matters of diplomacy between the Empire and the Jotun. The Empire will contribute 55 wains of white granite to this edifice, with ~~167~~ 198 crowns of labour costs. This construction will be a joint effort with the Jotun. They are also contributing to this build.
> >
> > Proposed: Sermersuaq, Seconded: Conscience of the Senate

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in West Marsh, Kallavesa
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[A stone for a stone](../winds-of-fortune/A_stone_for_a_stone.md#create-a-new-meeting-place)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 110 white granite
- 396 crowns (increased by being built in a [marsh](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#marshes))
- Nine months to complete
