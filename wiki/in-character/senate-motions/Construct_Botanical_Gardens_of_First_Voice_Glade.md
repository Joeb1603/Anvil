# Construct Botanical Gardens of First Voice Glade

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the restoration of the Botanical Gardens at the First Voice Glade School in Miaren. Self-funded, raised to the benefit of all who seek to study with the Navarr as masters of herb-lore. The restoration of the gardens will empower the Dean of the First Voice Glade to upgrade a member of the Vallornguard's herb garden without cost each summit, strengthening prosperity and the health of the Empire
> >
> > Proposed: Rhisiart Heartsbane, Senator for Miaren, Seconded: Bronimir Ranedzkah Pinewheel, Senator for Ossium

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Oakways, Miaren
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 30 wains of weirwood
- 90 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 8 wains of weirwood provided by Vick Dagger after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 2 wains of weirwood provided by Mharen Splitroot after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 5 thrones provided by Drysfa Broken Bough after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 5 thrones provided by Merewen Splitroot after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 7 crowns provided by Lady Mercy Goldvein after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Halwyn Splitroot after Autumn Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the botanical gardens will have the following effects:

- The [Dean of the First Voice Glade](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_First_Voice_Glade.md) will once again receive 27 drams of herbs each season.
- The Dean of the First Voice Glade will be able to upgrade a single basic [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) belonging to a member of the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md) each season
