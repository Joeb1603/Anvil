# Construct Wayhouse in the Barrens

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a wayhouse for Navarr in Murderdale, the Barrens
> >
> > Proposed: Lady Gwendoloena de Ordraig, Senator for the Barrens, Seconded: Mel Bleak, Senator for Mournwold

## Overview

- Constructs a [wayhouse](../../mixed/commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) in Murderdale, the Barrens
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 wains of white granite
- 6 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 12 wains of white granite provided by Taren Summergleam after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 4 wains of white granite provided by Paulk Thornweaver after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 2 thrones 7 crowns provided by Myrddin Ravensong after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 4 crowns provided by Eleri Bronwen's Rest after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Keeper of Caedwynn's Hold](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_Caedwynn-s_Hold.md) will be available to appoint for a term of three seasons by the senators of Navarr for the first time at the Autumn Equinox 388YE
