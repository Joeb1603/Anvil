# Construct Great Hall of Fabric Arts

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Great Hall of Fabric Arts, to celebrate in Varushka's industry, in Delev, Karov.
> >
> > Announced by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Constructs a great hall of fabric arts in Delev, [Karov](../nations/varushka/Karov.md) as per the **[The Deer in the Sun](../winds-of-fortune/The_Deer_in_the_Sun.md#constructing-the-folly-2)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)
- This motion received the support of the Varushkan Assembly through a [mandate](../synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-151) enacted by **Anatolij Paukov Zorkin**

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 80 white granite
- 240 crowns
- Six months to complete

## Outcome

- Once complete, this commission will start a renaissance in the fabric arts, reminding all Varushkan of their traditional heritage and strength in these skills and bringing them to the fore. It will change the nation, restoring the prominence and emphasis that such skills should, arguably, have always had in discussions of traditional Varushka culture.
