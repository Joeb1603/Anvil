- Commissions a unique [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md), created with assistance of the [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Sung](../eternals/Sung.md).
- The opportunity to create this network is detailed in the **[The Net of the Realms](../winds-of-fortune/The_Net_of_the_Realms.md#an-aerie-for-the-watchers)** wind of fortune.
- During [scrutiny](Scrutiny.md) the Constitutional Court spotted a potential problem with this commission, and have raised an [administrative motion](Senate_motion.md#administrative-motions) to be deliberated by the Senate during the Summer Solstice.
