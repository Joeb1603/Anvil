# Allocate the Singing Caves

> *In-character text:*
>
> Allocate the Singing Caves*Proposed by Mitwold, Seconded by Hahnmark*

### Overview

- The [Singing Caves](../../mixed/titles/Singing_Caves.md) in [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) were recently liberated by the heroes of the Empire.
- Passed as a National position
  - Nations proposed were [The Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and [The League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md)
  - Assigned to the Marches
- The motion was subject to the [veto](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#power-of-veto-2) of [The Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md)

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Constitution

- If the motion had passed, the National status of the Bourse Seat would have followed Mournwold to whichever the nation the territory was eventually assigned to
