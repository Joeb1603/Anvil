# Construct Bulgakov Memorial

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I announce the construction of a memorial in Bulgakov to commemorate those Varushkan citizens who were killed in the attacks by the Cold Sun, and those brave knights-errant who gave their lives against the scions to allow many citizens of the vale to escape. The monument will be designed by Triska Markova of the Druzhba Karova. Bulgakov; Duzekani; Karov
> >
> > Announced by Ada Droon, Mistress of Monuments

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Duzekani, [Karov](../nations/varushka/Karov.md)
- Announcement by the [Mistress of Monuments](../../mixed/titles/Mistress_of_Monuments.md)

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 4 wains of weirwood
- 1 wain of white granite
- 15 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 throne 5 crowns, 4 wains of weirwood and 1 wain of white granite provided by Triska Markova after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the warding stakes stand around Bulgakov, in [Duzekani](../nations/varushka/Karov.md#duzekani), Karov
