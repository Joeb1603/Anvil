# Construct the Broken Shore Mithril Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > I, Octavius of Damakan's Forge, request the commission of the ministry the Broken Shore Mithril Market to be built in Cargo for the purpose of exchanging weirwood for mithril.
> >
> > The position of Broker of the Broken Shore is to be appointed by the Tally of Votes by Urizen national mana site holders, to be appointed on a yearly basis.
> >
> > The commission is self fundedAnnounced by Octavius of Damakan's Forge, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Commissions an expansion of the docks in Cargo to allow trade with the Grendel for mithril in exchange for weirwood as detailed in the **[Everything has a price](../winds-of-fortune/Everything_has_a_price.md#metal-for-wood)** Wind of Fortune
- Announcement

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply
- 30 wains of white granite
- 60 crowns in labour costs

## Progress

- 7 thrones 4 crowns and 30 wains of white granite provided by Osbhert Kinarlson after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- Once completed the [Broker of the Broken Shore](../../mixed/titles/Broker_of_the_Broken_Shore.md) will be available for appointment by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md)
