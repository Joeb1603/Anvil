# Auras illegal in the Civil Service hub

> *In-character text:*
>
> To expand the existing law regarding auras on the Senate to include the Civil Service Hub. The Citizens granted a vote would be the Magistrates and Civil Servants  
>  
> *Proposed by Astolat, seconded by Bastion.*

### Overview

- Extend the law prohibiting [auras in the senate](Auras_illegal_in_the_Senate.md).

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- At the time, a member of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) indicated an intention to discuss raising a [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md). In the end they did not do so, and the law came into effect after the first Senate session at the Spring Equinox event.
