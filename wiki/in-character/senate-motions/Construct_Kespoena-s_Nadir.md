# Construct Kespoena's Nadir

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct Kespoena's Nadir in Zenith
> >
> > Proposed: Aurianna du Meravon, Senator for Semmerholm, Seconded: Ismene of the Scarlet Guard, Senator for Morrow

## Overview

- Constructs a new [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) seat in Occursion, Zenith
- Kespoena's Nadir is a new [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) vein, [uncovered](../winds-of-fortune/Value_beneath_the_mountain.md) by the [Argent Sword](../../mixed/government/military/Argent_Sword.md)
- Passed
- Construction time was reduced from six months due to [exploits under Kespoena's Reach](../winds-of-fortune/Value_beneath_the_mountain.md#under-kespoenas-reach)

## Date

- Autumn 388YE

## Cost

- Costs per opportunity
- 300 thrones in labour costs
- 10 thrones a season upkeep
- Three months to complete

## Outcome

- Once complete, an administrative motion will be raised to [allocate](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#allocation) the Kespoena's Reach bourse resource
