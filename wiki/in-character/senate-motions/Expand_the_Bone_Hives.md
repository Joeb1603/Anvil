# Expand the Bone Hives

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To upgrade the Bone Hives. To both improve the structures of the hives and to provide the Amber Scorpion, who work the hives, with housing. Total cost is 26 weirwood and ~~52~~ 78 crowns.
> >
> > Proposed: Ossium, Seconded: Volodmartz

## Overview

- Expands the [Bone Hives](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Bonehives.md#the-bone-hives) as per the **[Thousands or more](../winds-of-fortune/Thousands_or_more.md#secrets-of-the-bonehives)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 26 weirwood
- 78 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 17 wains of weirwood provided by Druhain Olodniky after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 6 thrones 5 crowns and 9 wains of weirwood provided by Sketchemi Justicast Mentyevik after Autumn Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete the [Keeper of the Bonehives](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Bonehives.md) receives an additional dose each season of *Flickerbright*, *Stormspark*, and *Fireheart*. They have also gained access to a ministry to allow them to purchase additional doses of the substances, and give them access to the rare *Slumbersting*.
