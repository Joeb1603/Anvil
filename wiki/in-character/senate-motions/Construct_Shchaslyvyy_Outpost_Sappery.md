# Construct Shchaslyvyy Outpost Sappery

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Borys Vardovich Strascovich, Boyar of the Iron Roads, announces the building of the Shchaslyvyy Outpost Sappery a (12 mithril) mana ministry in Varushka to be assigned by Tally of the Votes.
> >
> > Announced by Borys Vardovich Strascovich, Boyar of the Iron Roads

## Overview

- Creates a ministry in [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) that allows the purchase of mana crystals
- Announcement by the [Boyar of the Iron Roads](../../mixed/titles/Boyar_of_the_Iron_Roads.md)

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 12 mithril
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 12 wains of mithril and 4 thrones 3 crowns 19 rings provided by Radic Splinterovich Schaslyvyy after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 ring in small donations provided after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Custodian of the Shchaslyvyy Spile](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Shchaslyvyy_Spile.md) will be appointed by Tally of the Votes of the Varushkan mine and forest owners for the first time at the Summer Solstice 386YE.
