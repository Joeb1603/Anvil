# Construct Peak Edge trading enclave

> *In-character text:*
>
> A large trading enclave in Therunin arranged with the Axou nation and Imperial servants. Allows trade with Axou, for exotic herbs and resources. Counts as a single commission. Self funded.  
>  
> *Imperial Wayleave by Selenius*

## Overview

- Passed.

## Date

- Spring 382YE

## Cost

- Non-standard costs apply.
- 12 wains of weirwood, 12 wains of white granite,
- 48 crowns in labour costs.

## Progress

- 12 wains of weirwood, 12 wains of white granite, and 24 crowns received from Selenius after Spring Equinox 382YE.
- This commission has been completed, creating the position of [Eastern Broker](../../mixed/titles/Eastern_Broker.md)

## Outcome

- Will create a trading post allowing trade with the Khiklotu Khrysoú of Axos for both mana and valuable materials.
- When completed this will create an Imperial title, [Eastern Broker](../../mixed/titles/Eastern_Broker.md), to be appointed by unanimous agreement of the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md).
