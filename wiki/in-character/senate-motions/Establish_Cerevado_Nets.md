# Establish Cerevado Nets

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To establish a new Brass Coast Bourse seat at Cerevado in Segura using nets and traps to collect ilium from the Scorrero river.
> >
> > Proposed: Semmerholm, Seconded: Weirwater

## Overview

- Establishes a new bourse seat as detailed in the **[Drums beat in my head](../winds-of-fortune/Drums_beat_in_my_head.md#stealing-the-scorrero)** wind of fortune.
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 384YE

## Cost

- As per the wind of fortune
- 200 thrones
- Three months to complete
- Upkeep is 5 thrones a season

## Progress

- 127 thrones provided by Gancio di Catazar after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 70 thrones 3 crowns 10 rings provided by Delara of the House of the Wanderer after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 2 thrones provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 4 crowns provided by Maria i Espiri i Guerra after Winter Solstice 384YE
- 10 rings in small donations provided after Winter Solstice 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete there will be a new [Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) seat - the *Cerevado Nets* - that produces 4 rings of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) each season.
