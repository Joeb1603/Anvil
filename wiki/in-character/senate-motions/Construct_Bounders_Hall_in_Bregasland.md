# Construct Bounders Hall in Bregasland

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the building of a Bounders hall in Ottery, Bregasland. This will create a Marcher national title 'Bailiff of the Fens' appointed annually by unanimous decision of Marcher senators. Sponsored by the Prosperity of Morgan Townsend and Thomas Oak. Costs as per the stated opportunity
> >
> > Announced by James Forrester, Bearer of the Second Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a Bounders Hall in [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) as per the **[Kith and kin](../winds-of-fortune/Kith_and_kin.md#hard-work)** wind of fortune.
- Announcement by the [Bearer of the Second Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)
- This construction has now lapsed as a result of insufficient funds and Bregasland having been conquered by the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md)

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 white granite
- 36 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 4 thrones 4 crowns provided by Thomas Oak after Summer Solstice 386YE
- 3 thrones 3 crowns returned to Thomas Oak after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the *Bailiff of the Fens* will be available to appoint by unanimous decision of the Marcher senators. Will also extend the protection of the **Beating the Bounds** [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) action to Bregasland once Sarcombe is reclaimed from the Jotun.
