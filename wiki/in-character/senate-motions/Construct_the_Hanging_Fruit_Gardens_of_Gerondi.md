# Construct the Hanging Fruit Gardens of Gerondi

> *In-character text:*
>
> Construction of the Hanging Fruit Gardens of Gerondi in Serenael, Miaren to provide herbs  
>  
> *Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of Navarr*

### Overview

- A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Serenael, [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md)
- This motion was approved by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Cost

- 40 wains of weirwood
- 10 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

#### Autumn Equinox 381

- This commission has [lapsed](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#lapsed-commissions)

### Campaign Outcome

- This great work, once complete, will provide an additional 75 herbs spread between the owners of herb gardens in the territory of Miaren who attended the previous Anvil summit
