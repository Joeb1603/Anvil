# Relinquish Skarsind

> *In-character text:*
>
> With true pride the Winterfolk have resolved to relinquish our claim on the territory of Skarsind.
>
> We take this courageous step out of loyalty to the Empire, and in particular the citizens of the Imperial Orc nation.
>
> It is our ambition that Skarsind should become the first home for the Imperial Orc nation, such that they may find Prosperity.
>
> This may be vetoed by the Wintermark National Assembly in addition to the normal processes  
>  
> *Proposed by Sermersuaq, seconded by Skarsind.*

*[Image: The Wintermark senators lead the move to make Skarsind a home for the Imperial Orcs.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Held_High.jpg)*

### Overview

- This motion would renounce the [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) claim to the territory of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md)
- As Skarsind is still part of the Empire, it would allow for a new motion of [assignment](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#assignment) to be called.
- This motion has *significant* constitutional ramifications.
- It motion requires [ratification](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#ratification) by [The Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md), and has no legal basis until then.
- This motion was passed unanimously by the Senate.

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- If the motion is ratified, the territory of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) would no longer be part of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md).
- Once relinquished, the territory will be assigned to a new Imperial nation following a standard vote of [assignment](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#assignment) by the Imperial Senate.
- The [Brilliant Shore](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#the-brilliant-shore) is a national Bourse position, control will remain with Wintermark until the territory is assigned to a new Imperial nation.

*[Image: The motion was particularly welcomed by the Imperial Orcs.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Triumphant.jpg)*

- This motion would later lead directly to the creation of the Senate power of [relinquishment](Create_Senate_power_of_relinquishment_II.md), allowing the Senate to relinquish any [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) or Bourse seat so that they could be [assigned](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#assignment) or [allocated](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#allocation) again.

## Constitution

- The Constitutional Court acknowledge that this motion represents the true will of the Wintermark people due to the unanimous support of the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) of that nation and the right of the Wintermark Assembly to veto it.
- This motion may be [abrogated](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#abrogration) by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) at any time before its ratification
- This motion may be [vetoed](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) by the Assembly of the Nine, the General Assembly or - as specified - the Wintermark National Assembly; the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) has until the start of the first senate session at the Summer Solstice to apply this veto.
- As this is a constitutional matter, the motion would not count against the one veto allowed each summit.
- During the Autumn Equinox 379YE, the use of the [Imperial Favour](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#using-an-imperial-favour) of [Empress Britta](../history/Empress_Britta.md) allowed the ratification of this motion in the absence of a Throne. The senate then chose to [assign Skarsind](Assignment_of_Skarsind_II.md) to the Imperial orcs.
