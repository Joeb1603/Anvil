# Allocate the Singing Caves II

> *In-character text:*
>
> Allocate the Singing Caves
>  
>  
> *Proposed by Upwold, seconded by Mitwold*

### Overview

- The [Singing Caves](../../mixed/titles/Singing_Caves.md) in [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) were recovered by the Empire
- This motion follows a [previous](Allocate_the_Singing_Caves.md) motion that was subject to the [veto](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#power-of-veto) of [Empress Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md)
- Passed as Imperial

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Singing Caves are now an Imperial bourse position and were auctioned for a reduced term at the Winter Solstice 380YE
