# Disburse funds for Farstrider's Watch Spring 382YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> Motion to disburse senate funds intended for the purpose of building the fort in Greensward named ‘Farstrider’s Watch.’  
>  
> *Proposed by Mournwold, seconded by Upwold.*

## Overview

- Funds to purchase materials to construct the [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) of *[Farstrider's Watch](Fortify_Greensward.md)* on the [Greensward](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#greensward) in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md)
- Passed at 80 Thrones.

## Date

- Spring 382YE

## Outcome

- Funds disbursed to Senator Mournwold.
