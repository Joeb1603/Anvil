# Construct the Red Depths

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Red Depths in Kahraman as a Brass Coast National Bourse seat, self funded by the virtuous citizens of the Brass Coast.
> >
> > Proposed: Segura, Seconded: Weirwater

## Overview

- Creates a [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) seat in [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) as per the **[Landscape](../winds-of-fortune/Landscape.md#the-red-depths)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per the wind of fortune
- 50 thrones
- Three months to complete; due to the [disruption caused by the Lasambrian Orcs](../winds-of-war/Against_the_Jotun.md#hill) after the Autumn Equinox, completion on the Red Depths has been delayed.
- 10 thrones seasonal upkeep

## Progress

- 49 thrones 6 crowns provided by Bakar i Riqueza after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Due to the [disruption caused by the Lasambrian Orcs](../winds-of-war/Against_the_Jotun.md#hill) after the Autumn Equinox, completion on the Red Depths has been delayed until at least the start of the Spring Equinox 385YE at the earliest.
- Once complete [the Red Depths](../../mixed/titles/Red_Depths.md) will be elected by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) by the [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) of the Brass Coast.
