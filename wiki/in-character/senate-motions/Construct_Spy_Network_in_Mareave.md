# Construct Spy Network in Mareave

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a spy network in Mareave funded by Ematius, Archmage of Winter.
> >
> > Proposed: Zenith, Seconded: Upwold

- Constructs a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the Grendel territory of the [Mareave](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#mareave)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Ematius 'Icebreaker' Ankarien after Summer Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Once completed military units will be able to support this spy network to uncover information about the territory south of [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md).
