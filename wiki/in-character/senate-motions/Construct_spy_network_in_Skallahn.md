# Construct spy network in Skallahn

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission a spy network in Skallahn — the Jotun territory west of Sermersuaq and Kallavesa.
>
> *Announced by the Imperial Spymaster, Cayleb of Felix’s Watch*

## Overview

- Commissions a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) territory of [Skallahn](../barbarians/Skallahn.md)
- Announcement

## Date

- Autumn 383YE

## Costs

- Standard costs apply as detailed [here](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md#costs)
- 10 wains of [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md)
- 20 crowns
- 2 thrones in upkeep

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns and 10 wains of weirwood provided by Cayleb after Autumn Equinox 383YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, Imperial [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) will be able to support the spy network.
