# Allocate the Legacy Spring 382YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> The reallocation of “The Legacy” mithril mine, in accordance with its historical position as an Urizen National Bourse position.  
>  
> *Proposed by Spiral, seconded by Sarvos.*

## Overview

- To [allocation](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#allocation) the [Legacy](../../mixed/titles/Legacy.md).
- The motion was raised twice; the first time it was withdrawn by Spiral’s absence from Senate.
- Passed as a National Bourse position.

## Date

- Spring 382YE

## Campaign Outcome

- The Legacy is a nation Urizen position once more.
