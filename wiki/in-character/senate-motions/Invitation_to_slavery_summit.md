# Invitation to slavery summit

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Empire formally sends invitation to the Commonwealth, the Sarcophan Delves, the Sumaah Republic, and the Axou, to send a delegation to a summit against slavery to be held in the Empire at the Winter Solstice 382YE.  
> *Proposed by Bastion, seconded by Spiral.*

## Overview

- A formal statement declaring a summit against slavery to be held at the Winter Solstice 382YE.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 382YE

## Outcome

- Formal notification has been sent to the Commonwealth, Sarcophan Delves, Sumaah Republic, and Axos regarding this invitation.
- The [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) to each nation will deal with the details of the invitation.
