# Construct Hall of Rund

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Hall of Rund in Rundhal, Kallavesa, a sinecure giving liao. To be held by the Speaker of the Crows who will lead and guide the National Assembly, appointed by the Wintermark National Assembly.
> >
> > Proposed: Kallavesa, Seconded: Kahraman

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md) as per the **[Old ways](../winds-of-fortune/Old_ways.md#the-hall-of-rund)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 10 wains of weirwood
- 6 wains of white granite
- 48 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of weirwood and 6 wains of white granite provided by Veikko Bondforger after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 6 thrones provided by Gisli i Phovas i Guerra after Winter Solstice 385YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete, the [Speaker of the Crows](../../mixed/titles/Speaker_of_the_Crows.md) will be able to be appointed by the National Assembly of Wintermark for the first time at the Spring Equinox 386YE
