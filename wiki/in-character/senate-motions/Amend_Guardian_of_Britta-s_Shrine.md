# Amend Guardian of Britta's Shrine

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To amend the title of "Guardian of Britta's Shrine" to "Guardian of Britta's Memorial".
> >
> > This will be elected by the Assembly of Courage, revocable by the Assembly of Courage and the General Assembly.
> >
> > This will repurpose the tomb into a site of pilgrimage for pilgrims of Courage.
> >
> > Title holder responsible for safeguarding the siteProposed by Skarsind, Seconded by Astolat

## Overview

- Motion to amend the title of [Guardian of Britta's Shrine](../../mixed/titles/Guardian_of_Britta-s_Memorial.md) in line with the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) detailed in the **[Daughters of the Empire](../winds-of-fortune/Daughters_of_the_Empire.md#mistress-of-the-empire)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 383YE

## Cost

- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

## Progress

- 2 thrones 4 crowns provided by Rurik after Winter Solstice 383YE

## Outcome

- Once work on the memorial is completed the title of [Guardian of Britta's Memorial](../../mixed/titles/Guardian_of_Britta-s_Memorial.md) will be available to be appointed by the Assembly of Courage
