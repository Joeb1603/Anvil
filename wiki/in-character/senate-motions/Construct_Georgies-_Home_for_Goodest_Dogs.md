# Construct Georgies' Home for Goodest Dogs

> *In-character text:*
>
> That the Senate commission the construction of a business Sinecure in Holberg to be known as "Georgies Home for Goodest Dogs". This shall be assigned annually by the League Senators.  
> *Proposed by Tassato, Seconded by Morrow*

## Overview

- Commission a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- This [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) uses [standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs).
- The size of the commission has not been specified.

## Progress

- 10 wains of white granite and 20 crowns provided by Victor van Holberg after the Winter Solstice 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) will create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), [Custodian of the Kennels](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Kennels.md).
