# Allocate Crawling Depths

> *In-character text:*
>
> That the Mithril mine ‘The Crawling Depths’ in Ossium be assigned as an Imperial Bourse Seat.  
> *Proposed by Tassato, seconded by Holberg.*

## Overview

- To [allocate](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#allocation) the [Crawling Depths](../../mixed/titles/Vale_of_the_Sunless_Depths.md) as an Imperial position.
- Passed.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Outcome

- The Crawling Depths will be an Imperial position, and any Imperial citizen is eligible to hold the associated title.

## Constitution

It is worth noting that once Ossium is conquered and assigned to an Imperial nation, the Crawling Depths will remain an Imperial position; there will be no further opportunity to allocate it.
