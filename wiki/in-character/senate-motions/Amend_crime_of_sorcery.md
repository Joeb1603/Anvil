# Amend crime of sorcery

> *In-character text:*
>
> Add the following to the declaration of Sorcery: A declared Sorcerer is barred from knowingly interacting with eternals and their heralds.  
>  
> Proposed by Zenith, seconded by Kahraman.

### Overview

- Amend the law to prohibit interaction with a herald or eternal by any individual declared a sorcerer by the Conclave.

### Date

- Spring Equinox 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Declaration of Sorcery](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-sorcery) was amended.
