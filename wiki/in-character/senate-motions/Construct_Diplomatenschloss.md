# Construct Diplomatenschloss

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build the Diplomatenschloss in Necropolis.
> >
> > Proposed: Casinea, Seconded: Reikos

## Overview

- Rebuild a new, [fortified](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md) for the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) in Hedrossan, [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md) as per the **[For the Common Good](../winds-of-fortune/For_the_Common_Good.md#fighting-talk)** wind of fortune
- Passed
- 50 Thrones disbursed to the [Senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for [Casinea](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md)

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 70 white granite
- 30 weirwood
- 300 crowns
- Six months to complete
- 5 thrones a season upkeep

Now complete, the [Ambassador to the Commonwealth](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Commonwealth.md) again has access to [mercantile support](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#mercantile-support) and the Diplomatenschloss serves as a rank one fortification in Hedrossan, Necropolis.
