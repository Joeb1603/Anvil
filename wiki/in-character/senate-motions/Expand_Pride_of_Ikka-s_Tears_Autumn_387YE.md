# Expand Pride of Ikka's Tears Autumn 387YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To expand the Pride of Ikka's Tears, via deep shaft and galleries. Effect: production increase of 5 wains per season of mithril; upkeep increase of 2 thrones per season. The 250 throne cost to do this is self-funded, no commission necessary
> >
> > Proposed: Sermersuaq, Seconded: Mournwold

## Overview

- Expands the [Pride of Ikka's Tears](../../mixed/titles/Pride_of_Ikka-s_Tears.md) in Suaq Fount, [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md)
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Metal and stone](../winds-of-fortune/Metal_and_stone.md#pride-of-ikkas-tears)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 250 thrones
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 124 thrones 7 crowns provided by Lumi Warmthbringer after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 124 thrones 7 crowns provided by Vauraus Korppitkotka after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the Pride of Ikka's Tears will produce an additional five wains of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) per season, and its upkeep will increase by two thrones per season
