# Construct Runeforge in Hahnmark

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build a runeforge in Hahnmark
> >
> > Proposed: Hahnmark, Seconded: Sermersuaq

## Overview

- Constructs a [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md) in Hahnmark, as detailed in the **[Snow might fall](../winds-of-fortune/Snow_might_fall.md#five-tasks)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per wind of fortune
- 45 wains of weirwood
- 30 wains of white granite
- 30 wains of mithril
- One year to construct

## Progress

- 11 wains of mithril, 6 wains of weirwood and 30 wains of white granite provided by Derowen after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 1 wain of weirwood provided by Lumi Warmthbringer after Spring Equinox 385YE
- 13 wains of mithril and 16 wains of weirwood provided by Derowen after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 wain of mithril provided by Æthwin Aldwynson after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 22 wains of weirwood provided by Derowen after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 3 wains of mithril provided by Kaapo Kingfishering after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 wain of mithril provided by Arsenio Sanguineo Rezia di Tassato after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 1 wain of mithril provided by Erasmo di Tassato after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now completed, the [Keeper of the Runegrott](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Runegrott.md) will be first appointed by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of the [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) captains of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) at the Spring Equinox 386YE
