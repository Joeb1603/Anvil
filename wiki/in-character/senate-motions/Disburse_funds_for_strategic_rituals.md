# Disburse funds for strategic rituals

## Overview

- Request for funds to provide mana towards the performance of strategic rituals.

## Progress

- 95 Thrones disbursed to Ariadne of the Auric Horizon, Proxy Senator for [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md) during the Autumn Equinox 378YE
- 25 Thrones disbursed to Probus, Senator for [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) during Spring Equinox 378YE

## Campaign Outcome

- The intention of these motions is that the funds be used to purchase [crystal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) to allow the performance of rituals that benefit the Empire and the [Imperial armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) on a strategic level.

## Autumn 378YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> Disbursal of funds for the intention of funding of the purchase of Mana to be used for strategic military purposes.  
>  
> Proposed by Morrow, seconded by Sarvos.

### Overview

- Passed in principle
- Passed at 95 Thrones
- Money to be distributed to Senator Morrow

## Spring 378YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> Disbursal of funds with the intent of use to be casting strategic rituals on armies.  
>  
> *Raised by Redoubt, seconded by Madruga.*

### Progress

- 25 Thrones disbursed to Senator Redoubt
