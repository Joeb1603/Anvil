# Map the Bay of Catazar

> *In-character text:*
>
> To map the Bay of Catazar to provide Imperial fleets with the resources to explore the Bay and create an Imperial map.  
>  
> *Proposed by Kahraman, seconded by Madruga.*

### Overview

- The [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to map the Bay of Catazar is detailed [here](../winds-of-fortune/Talking_sailor.md#map-the-bay).
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 381YE

### Cost

- Upkeep: 50 Thrones

### Campaign Outcome

- This created an opportunity for [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) captains to contribute to a [special project](../winds-of-fortune/Talking_sailor.md#participation).
- The new map of the Bay of Catazar was [completed](../winds-of-fortune/How_sweet_is_the_sea.md) after the Winter Solstice.
- Details of the map and the territories surveyed can be found [here](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md).
