# Construct Office of Fire and Stone

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Self funded construction of "Office of Fire and Stone", an edifice in Iteri, Zenith. Urizen national position elected by Tally of the Vote.
> >
> > Announced by Edmundo of Damakan's Forge, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Iteri, Zenith
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)
- More information can be found in the **[The thing with feathers](../winds-of-fortune/The_thing_with_feathers.md#fire-and-stone-estavus)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 20 wains of mithril
- 10 wains of white granite
- 10 rings of ilium
- 90 crowns in labour costs
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 rings of ilium, 20 wains of mithril, 11 thrones and 10 wains of white granite provided by Edmundo of Damakan's forge after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Architect of Hammers](../../mixed/titles/Architect_of_Hammers.md) will be available to appoint by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of Urizen owners of [mana sites](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md) for the first time at the Spring Equinox 388YE for a shortened term until the Autumn Equinox 388YE.
