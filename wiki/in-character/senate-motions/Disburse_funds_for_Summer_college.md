# Disburse funds for Summer college

> *In-character text:*
>
> 100 Thrones for the continued contruction of a Summe college (in Urizen or Navarr, which one matters not).  
>  
> *Proposed by Miekarova, seconded by Karsk*

## Overview

- A request for funds with which to provide materials for the construction of a [College of Magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md), focused on Summer rituals.
- The senate has [comissioned](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) two such colleges; one in [Morrow](Construct_college_of_magic_in_Morrow.md) and one in [Miekarova](Construct_College_of_Summer_Magic.md).

## Date

- Summer 379YE

## Cost

- Passed at 100 Thrones

## Progress

- 100 Thrones disbursed to senator Miekarova
