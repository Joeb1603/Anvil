# Claim Sungold Pass

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Senate reasserts that the Sungold Pass and the mountain known as the Traveller are part of the region of Opascari, in Volodmartz, and that the exploitation of resources in Imperial Land by foreigners without the direct invitation of the Empire is not acceptable.  
> *Proposed by Karov, seconded by Sarvos.*

## Overview

- The Senate asserts that the [Sungold Pass](../nations/varushka/Sungold_Pass.md) is in the Empire, rather than in [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md).
- Passed.

## Date

- Summer 382YE

## Outcome

- This has implications for the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) who may also have laid claim to the mine.
- During the Summer Solstice 383YE, the Senate chose to [cede Sungold Pass to the Thule](Ratify_Sungold_Treaty.md), effectively abrogating this motion.
