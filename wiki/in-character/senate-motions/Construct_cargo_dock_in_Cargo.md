# Construct cargo dock in Cargo

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct a cargo dock in Cargo in order to purchase white granite from the Sarcophan Delves.  
>  
> *Proposed by Madruga, Seconded by Segura*

### Overview

- A [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) built to purchase white granite from the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) in [Cargo](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#cargo), [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Cost

- 10 White Granite and 40 Weirwood
- Labour: 12 Thrones 4 Crowns
- Time: 3 months

### Progress

- 40 Weirwood, 10 White Granite and 12 Thrones, 4 crowns provided by Thanmir Hrafn after the Summer Solstice 381YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- There are [necessary details](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#necessary-details) missing from this motion, Senator Madruga may decide on these.
- Completion of the docks creates the title [Bewaarder van het Vandersaar dok](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Concordium_Dock.md)
- When complete the docks will create an Imperial title that will function as a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md), but allow the purchase of white granite from the Sarcophan Delves. The agreed rate is:
  - 32 crowns each wain for the first 8 wains
  - 29 crowns each wain for the second 8 wains
  - 26 crowns each wain for the last 8 wains
- The [well worn path](../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md#well-worn-paths) for appointing the title associated with these docks you be as an Imperial or National title [appointed](../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md#the-imperial-bourse) through the [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md)
- As a ministry, these docks do not have an upkeep - the suggestion that they did was in error.
