# Assignment of Holberg

> *In-character text:*
>
> That Holberg be allocated to the League  
>  
> *Proposed by Semmerholm, seconded by Astolat*

*[Image: The territory of Holberg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:HolbergRegions.png)*

### Overview

- An alternative was suggested; that Holberg be assigned to the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md).
- The motion passed by 19 to 11 in favour of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md).

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) has been assigned to the League, and can elect a [Senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md).
