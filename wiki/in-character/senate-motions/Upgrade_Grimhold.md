## Overview

- Upgrades the [Grimhold](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#grimhold), a [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in Perumaki, Miekarova
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)

## Date

- Summer 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 70 white granite
- 210 crowns
- Twelve months to complete

## Progress

- 1 wain of white granite provided by Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 3 crowns provided by Triska Markova after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 387YE
- 25 wains of white granite provided by Keir Amourd after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 1 throne provided by Pjesmina Zbirka after Autumn Equinox 387YE
- 25 wains of white granite provided by Keir Amourd after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 24 thrones 2 crowns and 19 wains of white granite provided by Blazh Zbignev after Spring Equinox 388YE
- 4 crowns provided by Pjesmina Zbirka after Spring Equinox 388YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, Grimhold will be a rank 2 fortification
