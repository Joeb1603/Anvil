# Construct Kobarid Jewelers

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build the Jarmish Jewellery Exchange Ministry in Miaren, Navarr. This will create the title of the "Star Jeweler" to be appointed by an Imperial Bourse Auction. Costing 8 White Granite, 4 Mithril, and 24 Crowns, to be self funded.  
> *Proposed by Temeschwar, seconded by Madruga.*

## Overview

- Commission a fortified premesis for the Kobarid family of Jarm to sell *glazamy neba* to Imperial customers.
- This [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) is the result of [an offer](../winds-of-fortune/Civil_tongue.md#jarmish-jewels) by the [Jarmish](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) Magician-Prince Matvei Kobarid.
- It was raised following the [abrogation](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#abrogation) of the [earlier motion](Construct_Jarmish_Jewellers.md) to build the jewelers.
- Failed

- Winter 382YE
