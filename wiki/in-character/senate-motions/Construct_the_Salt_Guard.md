# Construct the Salt Guard

> *In-character text:*
>
> We ask permission to build the Salt Guard, Menno van Ritsjhof’s planned fortification in Siroc, and for a disbursement of funds for the construction.  
> *Proposed by Madruga, seconded by Feroz.*

## Overview

- Commission a unique [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) as part of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented by the [famed architect Menno van Ritsjhof](../winds-of-fortune/The_same_bed.md#the-salt-guard).
- Passed at 80 Thrones

## Date

- Summer 382YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/The_same_bed.md#the-salt-guard).
- 100 wains of white granite
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 30 Thrones labour costs
- **Upkeep:** 10 Thrones each season
- A year to complete

## Progress

- 25 wains of White granite, 5 wains of Weirwood, and 10 thrones provided by Adan i DelToro i Riqueza after the Summer Solstice 382YE.
- 30 wains of white granite, 14 wains of weirwood, and 11 thrones provided by Lieselotte van Holberg after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.
- Targeted by *The Immaculate Architect* ritual after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.
- 25 wains of White Granite, 1 wain of weirwood, and 4 Thrones provided by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) after the Winter Solstice 382YE.
- 20 wains of white granite, and 5 Thrones, will be provided by the [vigilant citizens](../winds-of-fortune/Sing_your_name.md#securing-the-borders) inspired by the shrine of the [Sentinel](../nations/urizen/Sentinel.md) in [Casinea](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md)

## Campaign Outcome

- When complete, the resulting fortification will have an effective strength of 3,000 against any conventional land forces invading anywhere in Madruga.
- Against any [Shore Offensive](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy_orders.md#shore-offensive) aimed at capturing [Siroc](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc) or [the Lyceum](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#the-lyceum) (essentially any Shore Offensive aimed at the [Siroc Plains](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc-plains)), the fortification would resist conquest with an effective force of 6,000 (OOC Note: bearing in mind that the benefit of a fortification in a region directly being attacked will further increase this force, this is a significant advantage).
