# Resupply Announcement Winter 382YE

> *In-character text:*
>
> To resupply the Valiant Pegasus and the Seventh Wave.  
> *Announcement by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies.*

## Overview

- The Valiant Pegasus and the Seventh Wave will receive [emergency resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply).

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) apply.
- Three months

## Progress

- None.

## Campaign Outcome

- Any Imperial citizen may contribute mithril or weirwood and money towards the resupply of the named armies.
