# Construct Lyceum Annexe

> *In-character text:*
>
> To construct an annexe to the Lyceum. Opportunity from Sadogua via the Dean. Allows the Dean to speed up research with Iridescent Gloaming. Self-Funded. No White granite involved.  
> *Proposed by Redoubt, seconded by Miekarova*

## Overview

- Commission an annexe at the Lyceum to allow faster codification of rituals.
- This [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) is the result of [an offer](../winds-of-fortune/Mean_what_you_say.md#the-black-sloth-and-the-inclination-of-gratitude) from the [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Sadogua](../eternals/Sadogua.md) made during Summer 382YE to the [Dean of the Lyceum](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md).
- Passed in principle, no funds requested.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This is not a standard commission.
- Requires a *minimum* of 6 wains of weirwood.
- Requires a *minimum* of 4 wains of mithril.
- Requires a *minimum* of 20 crowns in labour costs.
- Takes three months to complete.

## Progress

- 6 wains of Weirwood, 4 wains of Mithril, and 20 Crowns provided by The Empty One after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.

## Campaign Outcome

With the completion of the [Quiet Annexe](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md#the-quiet-annexe) the Dean of the Lyceum is now able to use [iridescent gloaming](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) to [speed the codification of rituals](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md#the-boon-of-the-black-sloth) as long as the eternal [Sadogua](../eternals/Sadogua.md) has not been judged an [enemy](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) of the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md).
