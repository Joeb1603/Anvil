# Construct House of Barbs

> *In-character text:*
>
> Civil Service Summary: To create a Sinecure in Hercynia - The "House of Barbs", with the associated title "Voice of Barbs" to be elected by the Navarr national assembly, and provide 12 votes in the Synod.  
>  
> *Proposed by Hercynia, seconded by Miaren*

*[Image: Full text of the original motion](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RhainMotion.JPG)*

### Overview

- Passed at 1 ring
- To be constructed in [Hercynia](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md).
- The [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) will provide 12 [votes](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#synod-votes) in the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) to the *Voice of Barbs*.

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Costs

- Materials: 10 wains of white granite ([Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs)).
- Time: 3 months to construct per 50 wains of materials used
- Labour: 2 crowns per wain
- Upkeep: None

### Progress

- 10 wains of white granite and 20 crowns supplied by Bloody Trees Rhain Winters Vigil after Autumn Equinox 379YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The House of Barbs is now complete
- The [Voice of Barbs](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Barbs.md) must now be appointed
- After a false start earlier in the year, the Senate commissioned an [extension](Construct_extension_to_House_of_Barbs.md) to the House of Barbs in Summer 382YE that increase the amount of liao and votes provided by the sinecure.

### Constitution

- Sinecures are normally life-time appointments.
- The title "[Voice of Barbs](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Barbs.md)" provides votes in the Imperial Synod, and so grants the holder of that title the legal powers and responsibilities of membership of the Synod. If the holder of the title is not already a member of the Synod, it automatically includes membership of all appropriate assemblies (usually the General Assembly, the Assembly of the Way, and the appropriate National Assembly).
- In future, any Senator submitting a motion of equivalent length or verbosity will be advised to revise it before the motion will be accepted by a speaker.
