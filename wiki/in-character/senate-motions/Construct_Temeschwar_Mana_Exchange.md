# Construct Temeschwar Mana Exchange

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build a mana ministry in Temeschwar as part of the opportunity offered by the Jarmish, self funded. Position holder to be appointed by the Senators of the League.  
>  
> *Proposed by Temeschwar, seconded by Miaren.*

## Overview

- This motion was to build a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) to oversee purchase and sale of crystal mana.
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 381YE

## Cost

- Taking advantage of this [opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/Fly_high.md#opportunity-tassato-mana-exchange)
- 12 mithril
- 24 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 12 wains of mithril and 24 crowns provided by Lorenzo “La Volpe” Macelliao von Temeschwar after Winter Solstice 381YE.

### Outcome

- Will create mana exchange ministry in Temeschwar and an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) - the [Magic Butcher](../../mixed/titles/Magic_Butcher.md) - to oversee it.

### Constitution

- The Constitutional Court points out that this construction is not related to any offers from the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) but rather is a response to the completion of the [great works](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) known as [Lorenzo's Legacies of Virtue](../nations/the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#the-legacies-of-virtue) in [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md).
