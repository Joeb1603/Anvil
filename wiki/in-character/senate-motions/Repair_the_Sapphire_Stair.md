# Repair the Sapphire Stair

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > That the Senate authorise the Repair of the Sapphire Stair in Tassato.
> >
> > Proposed: Tassato, Seconded: Karsk

## Overview

- Repairs the damage done by *Eisa Winterborn* as part of her raid detailed in the **[Shallows and miseries](../winds-of-war/Shallows_and_miseries.md#meanwhile-in-tassato)** wind of war.
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per [opportunity](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Sapphire_Stair.md#overview)
- 10 white granite
- 10 weirwood
- 10 mithril
- 90 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril, 11 thrones 2 crowns, 10 wains of weirwood and 10 wains of white granite provided by Raphael Sanguineo Rezia di Tassato after Summer Solstice 386YE

## Outcome

Now complete, the [Keeper of the Sapphire Stair](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Sapphire_Stair.md) has started to once again receive their previous income.
