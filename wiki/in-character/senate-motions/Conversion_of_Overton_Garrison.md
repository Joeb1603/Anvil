# Conversion of Overton Garrison

> *In-character text:*
>
> To commission the conversion of the Overton Garrison in Overton, Mournwold into a sinecure, The Sheriff's House in Overton, Mournwold to stem the banditry and raiding on the Mournwold mines and reclaim ore.
>
> Sinecure to create the annual national title Sheriff of Overton to be appointed by a vote of Marcher farmers revocable by the Marcher national assembly.
>
> *Proposed by Upwold, Seconded by Bregasland*

### Overview

- A sinecure in Overton, Greensward, Mournwold.
- Passed

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Cost

- None

### Progress

- The conversion work will be completed by the Summer Solstice 380YE, ready to be elected at the Anvil Summit.

### Campaign Outcome

- Following the completion of [Orchard's Watch](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#orchads-watch) the [Overton garrison](Construct_fortified_garrison_in_Overton.md) was obselete and disbanded.
- When the conversion work is completed, this sinecure will automatically create the Marcher [national title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md#national-title) of [Sheriff of Overton](../../mixed/titles/Sheriff_of_Overton.md).
- It is currently unclear which of the [ores](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#metals) will be recovered.
