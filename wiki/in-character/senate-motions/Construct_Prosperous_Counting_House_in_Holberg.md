# Construct Prosperous Counting House in Holberg

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Commission to construct a Prosperous Counting House in Holberg. This is an edifice costing 5 weirwood and 5 white granite. It will increase the Virtue Fund by 15 crowns a season
> >
> > Proposed: Reikos, Seconded: Segura

## Overview

- Constructs an [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in Holberg
- Passed
- More information can be found in the **[Forwards and Rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md#prosperity-and-posterity)** wind of fortune

## Cost

- 5 white granite

- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete
