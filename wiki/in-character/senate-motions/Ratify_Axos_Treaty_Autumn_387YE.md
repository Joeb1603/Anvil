## Overview

- Ratifies a [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) with [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 387YE

## The Treaty

1. Axos shall commit the army, the Shield of Ipotavo, to aid the military conquest of the Sarangrave in concert with the strategy of the Military Council of the Empire

2. The Empire shall accept the Axos claim over the region of Thornfen in the Sarangrave, and shall cede any claim over it.

3. Axos shall not impede the movement of Imperial forces through the Sarangrave, including passage through Thornfen.

4. Axos shall commit to the safety of the Bloodwater Spears, Waterwalkers, and the Free People of Sarangrave following the Empire's lead as to the diplomatic status of these people, including those residing in Sephals in Thornfen.

5. In the event of a dispute between Axos and the Bloodwater Spears, Waterwalkers, or the Free People of Sarangrave, then the Empire shall be called on as Mediator.

6. The Empire retains the right to hire additional military aid from Axos at a price to be mutually agreed.

7. Axos shall be approached regarding further cooperation when the Empire looks to make incursion into the Salt Flats of Sanath.
