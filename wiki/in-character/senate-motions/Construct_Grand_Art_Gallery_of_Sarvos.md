# Construct Grand Art Gallery of Sarvos

> *In-character text:*
>
> To build a Great Work in the form of a grand art gallery in Sarvos, dedicated to Pride in accordance with the recent Virtuous Interest in our cultural renaissance.*Proposed by Sarvos, Seconded by Tassato*

### Overview

- A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) to take advantage of the [opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/In_brightest_day.md#the-power-to-exalt) that presented itself during the recent [period](../winds-of-fortune/In_brightest_day.md) of inspiration
- Passed

### Date

- Spring 381YE

### Cost

- 15 wains of white granite
- 5 wains of weirwood
- 5 thrones in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 5 wains of weirwood, 15 wains of white granite, and 5 thrones provided by Lorenzo “La Volpe” Macelliao von Temeschwar after Spring Equinox 381YE.

### Campaign Outcome

- The great work, the [Blood Red Quays Art Gallery](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#the-blood-red-quays-art-gallery), provides 15 [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) and 30 votes in the Imperial Synod spread amongst those citizens of [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) dedicated to [Pride](../religion/Pride.md) who own [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) each season, following attendance at the Anvil Summit
