# Construct Trencher's Shining Ambition

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct a great lighthouse, Trencher's Shining Ambition, in West Marsh, Kallavesa; a great work benefitting fleets. Let us restore Westerhal as a hub of commerce in the west!
> >
> > Proposed: Kallavesa, Seconded: Reikos

## Overview

- Constructs a [great lighthouse](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md#great-lighthouses) in West Marsh, Kallavesa
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 387YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 20 wains of white granite
- 20 wains of weirwood
- 10 wains of mithril
- 180 crowns in labour costs (increased by being built in a marsh)
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 10 wains of mithril, 21 thrones 2 crowns, 20 wains of weirwood and 20 wains of white granite provided by Trencher Smoulder in The Cold after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 throne provided by Triska Markova after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Winter Solstice 387YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, all [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) in [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md) will gain an additional rank when engaging in [trade](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#trading)
