# Construct the Northern Way

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To construct the Northern Way, a great work building a safe passage between the city of Apulian and the territory of Zenith. This is funded by the Crimson Reapers.
> >
> > Announced by Laughlan Vi Temeschwar, Master of Rings

## Overview

- Constructs a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Apulian, Apulus, Spiral
- Announcement by the [Master of Rings](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Rings.md)
- More information can be found in the **[My friends are my estate](../winds-of-fortune/My_friends_are_my_estate.md#northern-way)** wind of fortune
- This commission received the support of League citizens as it was announced by the Master of the Rings

## Date

- Summer 388YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 50 wains of white granite
- 30 wains of mithril
- 30 thrones in labour costs
- Six months to construct

## Progress

- 15 wains of mithril, 26 thrones and 31 wains of white granite provided by Bella di Sarvos after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 6 wains of white granite provided by League citizens after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 5 wains of mithril provided by Leofric “Leo” d’Apulian after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Casha di Apulian after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Farren Silver Streams after Summer Solstice 388YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Summer Solstice 388YE

## Outcome

Once complete, the Northern Way will have the following effects:

- Raises the level of investment in [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) by one, providing 36 rings for each [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in the territory
- Removes the penalty to businesses in Spiral from the Black Plateau
- Enables the Crucible of Fate to boost the output of the [Legacy](../../mixed/titles/Legacy.md) and upgrade personal resources
