# Construct Hall of the Unbroken

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Providing Sanctuary and healing, both physical and emotional, to those that have endures slavery and to rehabilitate them into the Way and the Empire. Part of the Proffered Hand Network. A mana sinecure to be built in East Floes, Sermersuaq. Appointed by the Wintermark National Assembly
> >
> > Proposed: Sermersuaq, Seconded: Astolat

## Overview

- Commissions a mana [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 384YE

## Cost

- [Standard costs](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md#costs) apply
- 12 wains of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md)
- 3 thrones in labour costs
- Three months to construct

## Progress

- 12 wains of mithril and 2 thrones 6 crowns provided by Lady Eleanor Novarion after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Ergot Di Cevia after Autumn Equinox 384YE
- 1 crown provided by Fintan Nighthaven after Autumn Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- Once complete, the sinecure is intended to provide mana to the [Hearthtender of the Unbroken](../../mixed/titles/Hearthtender_of_the_Unbroken.md) appointed by unanimous decision of the senators of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md)
