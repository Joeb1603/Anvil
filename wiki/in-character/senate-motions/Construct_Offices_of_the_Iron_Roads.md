# Construct Offices of the Iron Roads

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Construct the Offices of the Iron Roads in Ivarsgard, Karsk; Delev, Karov; Lomaa, Ossium; Essk, Miekarova; and Ruin, Volodmartz for 30 white granite, 30 mithril, and 15 thrones, creating a Boyar of the Iron Roads elected by Tally of the Votes
> >
> > Announced by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Constructs a folly in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) as per the **[Deep forests and dark hills](../winds-of-fortune/Deep_forests_and_dark_hills.md#boyar-of-the-iron-roads)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)

## Date

- Summer 385YE

## Cost

- As per the wind of fortune
- 30 wains of white granite
- 30 wains of mithril
- 15 thrones in labour costs
- 6 months to construct

## Progress

- 20 wains of mithril, 13 thrones 6 crowns and 15 wains of white granite provided by Borys Vardovich Straskovich after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 throne provided by Triska Markova after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Kostjya after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Summer Solstice 385YE
- 10 wains of mithril and 15 wains of white granite provided by Borys Vardovich Straskovich after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now complete the title of [Boyar of the Iron Roads](../../mixed/titles/Boyar_of_the_Iron_Roads.md) will be appointed by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) for the first time at the Winter Solstice 385YE for a limited term
