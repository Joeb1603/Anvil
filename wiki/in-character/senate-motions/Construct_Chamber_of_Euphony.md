# Construct Chamber of Euphony

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To build and cede the Chamber of Euphony, a fane to Sung in Apulus, Spiral. The associated Imperial title, the Map to the City Below, to be appointed on an annual basis by the League National Assembly, and revocable by the League or General Assemblies, or the Assembly of Nine, with the responsibility to oversee the Chamber of Euphony and encourage the spread of magic, music, and mystery in Apulian.
> >
> > Proposed: Spiral, Seconded: Tassato
