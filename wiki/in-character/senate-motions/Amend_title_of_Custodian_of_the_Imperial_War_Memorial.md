# Amend title of Custodian of the Imperial War Memorial

> *In-character text:*
>
> To confirm that the title of Custodian of the Imperial War Memorial is a sinecure appointed by the Highguard National Assembly on an Annual basis.  
>  
> *Proposed by Bastion, Seconded by Kahraman.*

### Overview

- This motion was to clarify the appointment method of the [Custodian of the Imperial War Memorial](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Imperial_War_Memorial.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Summer 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The appointment and removal methods for the title have been amended.
