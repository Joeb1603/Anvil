# Appoint Thule Ambassador

> *In-character text:*
>
> To elect an Imperial Citizen to the position of Ambassador to the Thule*Administrative Motion*

### Overview

- This motion appoints a citizen to the position of ambassador to the Thule and to have the power to deal with them on behalf of the Empire.

### Date

- Spring 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Juha, the Cave Spider was appointed as the [Ambassador to Otkodov](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Otkodov.md)
