# Authorise Ambassador to Axos

> *In-character text:*
>
> To allow the Ambassador to Axos to offer Axos access to the spy network in the Mountains of the Moon.  
> *Proposed by Weirwater, seconded by Necropolis.*

## Overview

- To authorise the [Ambassador to Axos](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Axos.md) to issue an invitation for [Axou](../foreign-nations/Axos.md) troops to [support](../winds-of-fortune/Civil_tongue.md#citadels-of-axos) the [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) in the [Mountains of the Moon](Construct_spy_network_in_the_Mountains_of_the_Moon.md)
- Passed

## Date

- Summer 382YE

## Outcome

- The Ambassador has the support of the Senate in issuing an invitation to Axos.
