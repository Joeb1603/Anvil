# Alter manifesto of Sevenfold Path

> *In-character text:*
>
> Change the wording of the manifesto of the Sevenfold Path from “Human Destiny” to “The Destiny of Imperial Citizens”  
>  
> *Proposed by Sermersuaq, seconded by Skarsind*

### Overview

- The manifesto of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md) contained a point that "*Magic should work in harmony with, and to promote, the fulfilment of human destiny.*"
- The motion follows on from a [Declaration of Concord](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-concprd) in the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md)

### Date

- Summer 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The official [manifesto](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md#manifesto) of the Sevenfold Path has been altered to "*Magic should work in harmony with, and to promote, the fulfilment of the destiny of Imperial citizens.*"
