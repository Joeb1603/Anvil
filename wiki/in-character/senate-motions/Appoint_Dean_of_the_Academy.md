# Appoint Dean of the Academy

> *In-character text:*
>
> To appoint an Imperial Citizen to the position of Dean of the Academy*Administrative Motion*

### Overview

- First appointment of the [Dean of the Academy](../../out-of-character/events/Dean_of_the_Academy.md)
- This motion was raised by the civil service as an administrative motion, as it was the first time the position was up for [appointment](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#appointments). Any future appointment must be raised as a [motion](Senate_motion.md) by a member of the Senate.

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Faustina Notturno di Sarvos was appointed as the first Dean of the Academy
