# Disburse funds for resupply III

> *In-character text:*
>
> To disburse Senate funds intended to resupply the Imperial Armies  
>  
> *Proposed by Bregasland, seconded by Mitwold.*

### Overview

- This motion does not give Senator Bregasland power to perform [emergency resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) on the armies.
- Passed in principle
- Passed at 250 thrones

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Progress

- 250 thrones disbursed to Senator [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md)
