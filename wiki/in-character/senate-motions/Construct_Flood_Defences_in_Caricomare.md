# Construct Flood Defences in Caricomare

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > A motion to develop flood defences in Sarvos with the aid of the Vandersar. Does not require a commission. Costs are 60 thrones. The defences are to be decorated with an elegant letter B and a rose in honour of those who supported their creation.
> >
> > Proposed: Sarvos, Seconded: Spiral

## Overview

- Develops flood defences in Caricomare, [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) as per **[Sound the bells](../winds-of-fortune/Sound_the_bells.md#flooding-of-caricomare)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 12 white granite
- 72 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 8 thrones 7 crowns and 12 wains of white granite provided by Rosalina Gulia Viola di Sarvos after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 1 crown provided by Aloysius di Sarvos after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the defences ensure that Sarvos will not suffer unduly from flooding in the future if the territory is cursed with [Foam and Spittle of the Furious Sea](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Foam_and_Spittle_of_the_Furious_Sea.md)
