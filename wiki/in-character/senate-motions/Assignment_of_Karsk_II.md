# Assignment of Karsk II

> *In-character text:*
>
> That the territory of Karsk be allocated to an Imperial Nation.  
>  
> Proposed by Mitwold, Seconded by Karov.

*[Image: Regions of Karsk](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Karskregions.png)*

### Overview

- The territory of [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md) was reconquered by Imperial forces during the Autumn of 378YE.

### Date

- Winter 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The territory of Karsk is now part of the nation of Varushka.
- It is noted that the territory is still not secure - the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) still control three of the seven regions.
