# Construct the Eternal Flame of Miaren

> *In-character text:*
>
> I, the Civilian Commissioner of Navarr commission the construction of the Eternal Flame of Miaren.  
> *Approved by the Civilian Commissioner of Navarr*

### Overview

- A [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) that produces mana crystals in Seren, [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md)
- This motion was approved by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Cost

- 10 wains of mithril
- 20 crowns in labour costs
- 3 months to construct

### Progress

- 10 wains of mithril and 20 crowns provided by Bronwyn Kane

### Campaign Outcome

- Once completed, the construction created a new national title within Navarr, [Eternal Flame of Miaren](../../mixed/titles/Eternal_Flame_of_Miaren.md).
- Certain necessary details were required before construction could be started.
