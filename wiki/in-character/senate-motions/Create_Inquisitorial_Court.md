# Create Inquisitorial Court

> *In-character text:*
>
> To create the Inquisitorial Court which shall be a body in the Synod to oversee Inquisitions and ensure their accountability and transparency.  
>  
> *Proposed by Hercynia, seconded by Bregasland.*

*[Image: The motion to create the inquisitorial council](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:InquisitorialCouncil.png)*

### Overview

- Eight [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Virtue Inquisitor](../../mixed/titles/Virtue_inquisitor.md) would be created, one in each Virtue assembly.
  - The Virtue Inquisitors would be able to escalate any [inquisition](../../mixed/government/synod/Inquisition.md) to a statement of principle without it counting as their single judgement per season.
  - The Inquisitors will be appointed by the appropriate cardinal and serve until they die, step down, are revoked, or a new cardinal is appointed who chooses to replace them.
  - Virtue Inquisitors would be [revoked](../../mixed/government/synod/Revocation.md) by the appropriate Virtue assembly or by the General assembly.
  - Virtue Inquisitors are responsible for overseeing and coordinating the use of inquisition in service of the Empire.

### Date

- Summer 379YE

### Cost

- None

### Campaign Outcome

- One virtue inquisitor Imperial title was created in each virtue assembly and the assembly of the Way.

### Constitutional Court

- The constitutional court notes that it would also have been appropriate to give the virtue inquisitors the [inquisitor](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Inquisitor.md#inquisitor) power of the [Imperial Inquisitor](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Inquisitor.md) - to perform an [inquisition](../../mixed/government/synod/Inquisition.md) each season without the need for a vote in the Synod, and in addition to any existing judgements they have raised normally.
- The constitutional court further notes that it would be appropriate to extend the power of [inquisitor's judgement](../../mixed/titles/Virtue_inquisitor.md#inquisitor27s-judgement) to the Imperial Inquisitor as well.
