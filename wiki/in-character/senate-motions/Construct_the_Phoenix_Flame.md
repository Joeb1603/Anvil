# Construct the Phoenix Flame

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Phoenix Flame in Astolat, Dawn, and create the title of Tender of the Phoenix Flame. The commission costs 30 wains of mithril, 30 wains of white granite, and 180 crowns. Once constructed the Phoenix Flame will be ceded to Meraud.
> >
> > Proposed: Weirwater, Seconded: Astolat

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md) as per the **[Back in town](../winds-of-fortune/Back_in_town.md#meraud)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 30 wains of mithril
- 30 wains of white granite
- 180 crowns
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 15 wains of mithril, 11 thrones 2 crowns and 15 wains of white granite provided by Enchantress Areloe Larmallevés after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Serenus Starsong after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Rhys Nighthaven after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Ser Lucan Mortére after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 15 wains of mithril, 10 thrones 5 crowns and 15 wains of white granite provided by Enchantress Areloe Larmallevés after Spring Equinox 386YE
- 2 crowns provided by Aramis du Froste after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete, the [Tender of the Phoenix Flame](../../mixed/titles/Tender_of_the_Phoenix_Flame.md) can be appointed by [Declaration](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Candidacy.md).
