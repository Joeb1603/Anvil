# Declare peace with Orcs of the Great Forest of Peytaht

> *In-character text:*
>
> To grant the Orcs of the Great Forest of Peytaht the status of foreigners*Proposed by Casinea, Seconded by Necropolis*

### Overview

- Under Imperial law, this motion grants all the legal protections and responsiblities of a foreigner to the orcs of the [Great Forest of Peytaht](../world/Great_Forest_of_Peytaht.md).
- This follows on from two previous motions, the [first](Declare_peace_with_Barrens_orcs.md) to declare peace, the [second](Declare_war_on_the_Barrens_orcs.md) to declare war on the collective orcs of [The Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md)

### Date

- Summer 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The declaration means that the orcs of the Great Forest of Peytaht are [foreigners](../../mixed/government/senate/Imperial_Law_Overview.md#individuals-and-the-law) under [Imperial Law](../../mixed/government/senate/Imperial_law.md)
