>
> > *In-character text:*
> >

> >
> > Proposed: Spiral, Seconded: Astolat

## Overview

- Establishes skyways across Urizen as per the **[Road out of nowhere](../winds-of-fortune/Road_out_of_nowhere.md#southern-skyways)** wind of fortune
- Passed

## Date

- 225 mithril
- 675 crowns
- Fifteen months to complete
