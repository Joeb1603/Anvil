# Construct Kalpaheim Tradehall

> *In-character text:*
>
> Build the Kalpaheim Tradehall ministry connected to the Northern Trade Network. A very productive tradehall requested by artisans and traders of Wintermark.  
> *Proposed by Hahnmark, seconded by Kallavesa.*

## Overview

- Commission a trade hall in [Kalpaheim](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#kalpaheim) in line with the recent [proposal](../winds-of-fortune/Trade_all_my_tomorrows.md#noonday-sword).
- Passed

## Date

- Winter 382YE

## Cost

- This commission uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/Trade_all_my_tomorrows.md#noonday-sword).
- 16 wains of weirwood
- 32 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 16 wains of weirwood and 32 crowns provided by Hengest Dunn after the Spring Equinox 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The hall is a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) that creates the title of [Kalpaheim Tradeholder](../../mixed/titles/Kalpaheim_Tradeholder.md).
