# Improve Towerjacks

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To disburse funds to improve the Towerjacks providing the Engineer quality.
> >
> > Proposed Astolat. Seconded Conscience of the Senate

## Overview

- The motion comes from an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to improve the [Towerjacks](../../mixed/government/military/Towerjacks.md) presented in the **[Watches from the Wall](../winds-of-fortune/Watches_from_the_Wall.md#apply-what-you-have-learned)** wind of fortune.
- Passed at 140 Thrones. The full amount was [disbursed](Motion_wording.md#disbursement) to the Senator for Astolat.

## Date

- Spring 384YE

## Cost

- As detailed in the opportunity.
- 50 wains of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md)
- 100 crowns
- Requires the Towerjacks to spend a season in Holberg (see [below](#outcome))

## Progress

- 1 throne 1 ring provided by Magdalena Teyhard von Holberg after Spring Equinox 384YE
- 38 wains of mithril and 11 thrones 3 crowns 19 rings provided by Natalia 'The Falcon' Barossa after Summer Solstice 384YE
- 12 wains of mithril provided by Natalia 'The Falcon' Barossa after Autumn Equinox 384YE

## Outcome

- [As outlined in the opportunity](../winds-of-fortune/Watches_from_the_Wall.md#apply-what-you-have-learned), completing the improvement to the Towerjacks requires them to return to Holberg for a season once the [materials](#cost) are provided. The [general](../../mixed/titles/General.md) must order them to enter the city in triumph through the Holmauer Gate and make a grand procession through the streets of the city they fought for so many years to save.
- This can be done while the army is resting and recuperating.
- The army has until the start of the Spring Equinox 385YE to return to Holberg.
- Once complete, the Towerjacks gain the [Engineer](../winds-of-fortune/Watches_from_the_Wall.md#engineer) quality in place of their existing Siege quality.
