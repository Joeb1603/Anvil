# Construct Townsend Treasury

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a money sinecure in Sarcombe in the Mournwold named the Townsend Treasury. This will create a most glorious Marcher national title the 'Town Clerk of Sarcombe' to be appointed annually by unanimous vote of Marcher senators at the Winter Solstice. All costs to be provided (10 white granite and building costs) by Morgan Townsend
> >
> > Announced by Bertrand, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Constructs a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md).
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 10 wains of white granite
- 30 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones 6 crowns and 10 wains of white granite provided by Morgan Townsend von Holberg after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the position of [Town Clerk of Sarcombe](../../mixed/titles/Town_Clerk_of_Sarcombe.md) was first appointed by the senators of [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) at the Winter Solstice 385YE
