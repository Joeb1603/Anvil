# Construct Leshun Market

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Boyar of the Iron Roads, Borys, announces the building of the Leshun Market
> >
> > Announced by Borys Vardovich Straskovich, Boyar of the Iron Roads

## Overview

- Creates a ministry in [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md) as per the **[Pride and prejudice](../winds-of-fortune/Pride_and_prejudice.md#the-orcs-of-branoc)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Boyar of the Iron Roads](../../mixed/titles/Boyar_of_the_Iron_Roads.md)

## Date

- Winter 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 16 wains of weirwood
- 3 thrones
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 1 wain of weirwood provided by Druhain Olodniky after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 2 thrones 6 crowns 17 rings provided by Fretek Sargava Slavomiryn after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 1 crown provided by Konstantin Rabovich Bolotnikov after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 3 rings in small donations provided after Winter Solstice 385YE
- 15 wains of weirwood provided by Pavel Novak after Autumn Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now that work is complete, the [Speaker to the Leshun](../../mixed/titles/Speaker_to_the_Leshun.md) will be available to appoint by [Tally of the Votes](../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of Varushkan [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) owners for the first time at the Winter Solstice 386YE.
