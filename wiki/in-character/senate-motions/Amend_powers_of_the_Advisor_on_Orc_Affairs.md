# Amend powers of the Advisor on Orc Affairs

> *In-character text:*
>
> To modify the position of Advisor on Orc Affairs to change the term to one year.  
>  
> Proposed by Redoubt, Seconded by Conscience

### Overview

- The [Advisor on Orc Affairs](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_Orc_Affairs.md) will now have an annual term, rather than a life-time tenure.

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The tenure of the Advisor was amended.
