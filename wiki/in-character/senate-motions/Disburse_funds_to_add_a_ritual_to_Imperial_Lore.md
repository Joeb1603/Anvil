# Disburse funds to add a ritual to Imperial Lore

> *In-character text:*
>
> To request funding to place a ritual, approved by the conclave, into imperial lore.  
>  
> *Proposed by Karov, seconded by Miekarova.*

### Overview

- A request for funds to be used to add a ritual to the body of [Imperial Lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list).
- Passed at 7 thrones

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Progress

- Funds disbursed to Simargl, proxy Senator for Karov

### Campaign Outcome

- The cost to place a ritual in Imperial lore is 10 thrones, and requires a [declaration](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md) of the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md).
