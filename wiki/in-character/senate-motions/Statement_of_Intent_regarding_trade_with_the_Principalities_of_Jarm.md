# Statement of Intent regarding trade with the Principalities of Jarm

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Empire recognises the importance of extant and future trade in strategic materials with our allies in Jarm and welcomes any overtures to strengthen existing agreements or form new ones.*Proposed by Upwold, Seconded by Morrow*

### Overview

- Passed

### Date

- Autumn 380YE

### Outcome

- The motion secured favoured trading status for the Principalities of Jarm, resulting in an increased amount of crystal mana available on the docks.
- The motion was [later abrogated](Abrogate_Jarmish_favoured_nation_status.md) during the Autumn Equinox 382YE.
