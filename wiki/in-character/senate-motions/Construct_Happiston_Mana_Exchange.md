# Construct Happiston Mana Exchange

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > As bearer of the First Imperial Wayleave, I hereby announce the commission of a 16 wain mana ministry in Happiston in the Marches. This will create a Marcher National title called "The Reeve of the Happiston Mana Exchange" to be annually elected by a Tally of the Votes. Costs to be covered in entirety by "Morgan Townsend" through the Prosperity of Happiston Bakes.
> >
> > Announced by Vivane Knotes, Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

- Creates a ministry in Chalkdowns, [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) that allows the purchase of mana crystals
- Announcement by the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)

## Date

- Spring 386YE

## Cost

- Standard costs apply
- 16 mithril
- 48 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 16 wains of mithril and 6 thrones provided by Morgan Townsend von Holberg after Spring Equinox 386YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Reeve of the Happiston Mana Exchange](../../mixed/titles/Reeve_of_the_Happiston_Mana_Exchange.md) will be available to elect by Tally of the Votes of Marcher [farm](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) owners for the first time at the Summer Solstice 386YE for a short term until the Winter Solstice 386YE.
