# Raise Wintermark army

> *In-character text:*
>
> To reform the Bloodcloaks Wintermark Army, a Grimnir (Physick) laden army.  
>  
> *Proposed by Hahnmark, seconded by Bregasland*

*[Image: Full text of the original motion](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:HengistMotion.JPG)*

### Overview

- Permits the [Winterfolk](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) to begin raising a new [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md).
- Withdrawn after passage of a motion to allow the [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) to [raise an army](Raise_Dawnish_army_II.md).

### Date

- Spring 379YE

### Costs

- The motion would have had all the [standard costs](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#costs) of raising a new army.

### Campaign Outcome

- The Bloodcloaks were originally a Wintermark army, lost to the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) during the reign of [Empress Brannan](../history/Empress_Brannan.md). Known for their crimson cloaks, the army was composed of many [grimnir](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#grimnir) and [mystics](../nations/wintermark/Mystic.md); all save a few were slaughtered during a disastrous retreat from [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md).
