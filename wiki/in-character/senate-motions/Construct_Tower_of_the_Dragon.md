# Construct Tower of the Dragon

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission the Tower of the Dragon in Nearweald, Ossium, an official residence of the Archmage of Summer which functions as a ministry. Fully funded by myself and the Archmage of Summer, Mirella of the Twisted Rose. 15 white granite and 45 crowns
> >
> > Announced by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, Master of the Koboldi

## Overview

- Constructs a [ministry](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) in Nearweald, Ossium
- Announcement by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md)
- More information can be found in the **[Stone and stone](../winds-of-fortune/Stone_and_stone.md#tower-of-the-dragon)** wind of fortune

## Date

- Spring 387YE

## Cost

- Costs as per opportunity
- 15 white granite
- 45 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 3 thrones and 15 wains of white granite provided by Dima Vasilyevna Novosad after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 throne provided by Mirella of the Twisted Rose after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 1 throne provided by Kostjya after Spring Equinox 387YE
- 5 crowns provided by Vesna Borkovna Prochnost after Spring Equinox 387YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Archmage of Summer](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Summer.md) has gained a ministry allowing them to trade [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) for [Jewels from the Deep Mountain](../winds-of-fortune/Stone_and_stone.md#jewels-from-the-deep-mountain).
