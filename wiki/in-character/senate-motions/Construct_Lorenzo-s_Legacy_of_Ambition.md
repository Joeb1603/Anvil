# Construct Lorenzo's Legacy of Ambition

> *In-character text:*
>
> That the Senate use the skills of the Asavean Architect to construct a Great Work in Tassato. This great work will be for the production of Mana. To be known as "Lorenzo's Legacy of Ambition." To cost 40 wains of mithril and 10 thrones to be funded by the Bloody Butchers Bank.  
>  
> *Proposed by Karov, seconded by Tassato.*

## Overview

- This motion was to build a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in Tassato to improve the production of [mana sites](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md)
- This commission will be overseen by [Almodin Oktístis, Priest of the Builder](../winds-of-fortune/Those_who_worship.md#almodin-oktc3adstis2c-priest-of-the-builder).
- Passed

## Date

- Autumn 381YE

## Cost

- 40 wains of mithril
- 10 thrones
- 3 months to complete

## Progress

- 40 wains of mithril and 10 thrones provided by Lorenzo “La Volpe” Macelliao von Temeschwar

### Outcome

- [Lorenzo's Legacy of Ambition](../nations/the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#the-legacy-of-ambition) was completed in [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md) shortly before the Winter Solstice 381YE.
- The great work will provide 37 crystal mana each season, spread amongst those citizens of Tassato who own a mana site.
