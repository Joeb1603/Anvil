# Appraisal of integration of Mournwold Orcs

> *In-character text:*
>
> To conduct an appraisal regarding the integration of the Mournwold Orcs into the Marcher Nation.  
>
> *Proposed by Mournwold, seconded by Sarvos*

## Overview

- Commissions an [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) from the civil service, relating to the integration of the orc thralls of the Mournwold.
- Only one appraisal may be carried out each season.
- Passed

## Date

- Spring 383YE

## Campaign Outcome

- The civil service will prepare a report for the Empire delivered before the Summer Solstice 383YE

## Speaker's Note

- This was the second motion seconded by the Senator for Sarvos at the Spring Equinox 383YE
- The matter will be passed on to the magistrates as a matter of [subverting the agencies of state](../../mixed/government/senate/List_of_Criminal_Offences.md#crimes-against-the-processes-of-the-state).
