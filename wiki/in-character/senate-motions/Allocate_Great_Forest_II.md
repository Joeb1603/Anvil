# Allocate Great Forest II

> *In-character text:*
>
> To assign the Great Forest of Peythat as an Imperial Bourse Seat  
>  
> *Proposed by Bastion, Seconded by Necropolis*

### Overview

- This motion was to allocate the [Great Forest at Reikos](../../mixed/titles/Great_Forest_at_Reikos.md) bourse seat in [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md) as an Imperial position.
- Passed
- This motion followed on from a previous, [failed motion](Allocate_Great_Forest.md) to allocate the seat as a national seat.

### Date

- Spring 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The Great Forest of Reikos is now Imperial and is due for auction, for a reduced term, at the Summer Solstice 380YE
