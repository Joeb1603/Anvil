# Create law of delegation protection

> *In-character text:*
>
> Any delegations from barbarian nations who arrive on the field at Anvil under a flag of peace should have protection under the law and shall be protected as citizens for the duration of their visit to the Empire.  
>  
> *Proposed by Miaren, seconded by Segura.*

### Overview

- Creating a law to offer protection to barbarian delegations

### Date

- Passed Autumn 378YE

### Cost

- None

### Campaign Outcome

- This law appears to extend the status of [foreigner](../../mixed/government/senate/Imperial_Law_Overview.md#individuals-and-the-law) to any barbarian delegation that reaches the field at [Anvil](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#anvil).
- The law was [amended](Amend_law_of_delegation_protection.md) during the Winter Solstice 378YE.
