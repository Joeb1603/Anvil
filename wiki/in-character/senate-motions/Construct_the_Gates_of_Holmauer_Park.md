# Construct the Gates of Holmauer Park

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > To commission a great work “The Gates of Holmauer Park” in Holmauer. Holberg, to secure the Park's eight gates with mithril, and providing security to the Park's visitors in the form of a small garrison supplemented with local magicians and priests. The influx of paying tourists would provide coin that will benefit business owners of Holberg. Inspired by the judgement of the Cardinal of Wisdom, Hembeneth i Guerra, in Summer 384YE. Self-funded by Lula and Bernhard Kleist von Ennerlund together with other citizens of the League. Costs are 20 wains of mithril and 60 crowns.
> >
> > Announced by Elsha Bertolf, Chair of the Wolf

## Overview

- Creates a great work in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) as per the **[Dear green space](../winds-of-fortune/Dear_green_space.md#closing-the-gates)** wind of fortune
- Announcement by the [Chair of the Wolf](../../mixed/titles/Chair_of_the_Wolf.md)

## Date

- Autumn 385YE

## Cost

- Costs as per the wind of fortune
- 20 wains of mithril
- 60 crowns
- Three months to complete

## Progress

- 6 wains of mithril and 7 thrones 4 crowns provided by Lula Kleist von Ennerlund after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 6 wains of mithril provided by Bernhard Kleist von Ennerlund after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 wains of mithril provided by Magdalena Teyhard von Holberg after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 wains of mithril provided by Otmar van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 wains of mithril provided by Victor van Holberg after Autumn Equinox 385YE
- 2 wains of mithril provided by Taskel Di Apulian after Autumn Equinox 385YE

## Outcome

- Now complete, the [Gates of Holmauer Park](../../mixed/commissions/Gates_of_Holmauer_Park.md) increase the seasonal production of the [businesses](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) of Holberg by 36 rings
