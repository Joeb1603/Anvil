# Enlargement of the Seventh Wave

> *In-character text:*
>
> Enlarge the Highborne army the Seventh Wave.  
> *Proposed by Reikos, seconded by Miaren.*

## Overview

- To enlarge the Seventh Wave army, in line with the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented by the [gathering of soldiers](../winds-of-fortune/A_blessing_in_disguise.md#enlargement) in [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md).
- Passed in principle.

## Date

- Autumn 382YE

## Cost

- This enlargement uses [non-standard costs](../winds-of-fortune/A_blessing_in_disguise.md#enlargement).
- 100 wains mithril
- 200 crowns
- Six months to complete

## Progress

- 50 wains of mithril and 100 Crowns provided by Cadeus of the Shattered Tower after the Autumn Equinox 382YE.
- 50 wains of mithril, 12 Thrones, and 4 Crowns provided by Cadeus after the Spring Equinox 383YE.

## Campaign Outcome

- The Seventh Wave is now a [large](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#enlargement) army.
