# Allocate Gift of the Dwindling Star

> *In-character text:*
>
> To accept the gift of red star ilium from the Thule which will create a national Bourse position seat for Varushka and be a stepping stone towards a lasting peace with the Thule.
>
> It will be called 'Gift of the Dwindling Star'
>
> This offer is only available this season and in this form. Any attempt to force this into an Imperial position will result in the Thule withdrawing the offer.
>
> *Proposed by Volodmartz, Seconded by Karov*

### Overview

- This motion was in relation to the offer presented by the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) regarding the [Ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) acquired from the red star detailed [here](../winds-of-fortune/Lantern-s_light.md)
- Passed

### Date

- Winter 380YE

### Campaign Outcome

- The [Gift of the Dwindling Star](../../mixed/titles/Gift_of_the_Dwindling_Star.md) is now a [national](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#imperial-and-national-bourse-positions) Bourse position of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md)
- The seat will be elected through the [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) by secret vote of the owners of the [mines](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forests](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) of Varushka
- The seat will be elected for the first time at the Spring Equinox 381YE
