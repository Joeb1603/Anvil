# Imperial Roll of Honour

## Overview

The Imperial Roll of Honour is a list of [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) that have supported the Empire's [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) each season. The lists are compiled by civil servants following [a motion](../senate-motions/Commission_roll_of_honour.md) passed by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
