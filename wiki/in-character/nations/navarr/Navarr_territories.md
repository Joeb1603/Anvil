# Navarr territories

The Navarr territories are scattered throughout the Empire. Most of the land they claim is heavily forested, surrounding the ruins of one or more ancient Navarr settlements.

### Territories

- [Brocéliande](Brocéliande.md) - Never conquered
- [Hercynia](Hercynia.md)
- [Liathaven](Liathaven.md) - Lost 349YE
- [Miaren](Miaren.md)
- [Therunin](Therunin.md) - Lost 386YE
