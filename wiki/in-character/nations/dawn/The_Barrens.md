# The Barrens

## Overview

The Barrens describes an area to the east of Semmerholm that remained unconquered by the Empire until Spring 385YE. Dawn maintains a series of long-established outposts on the western edge that reinforce the border against the barbarians. In general the Barrens is rich and fertile but its natural resources are almost entirely unexploited - enough to make the most jaded [Varushkan](../varushka/Varushka.md) merchant or wagon-raider salivate at the thought of all the untapped potential. There have been several attempts to settle it more fully, but the presence of several barbarian orcs makes establishing permanent settlements impossible. Nonetheless, the Dawnish refuse to abandon their [foothold](#dawnguard) in the Barrens and it serves as a common proving ground for [knights-errant](Knight-errant.md).

The Barrens has always been a magnet for the exiled, the disenfranchised and the disgruntled. Between 325YE and 328YE it was also the scene of a short-lived rogue kingdom called Montane, founded by renegades during the events of the [Freedom Heresy](../../religion/Freedom_Heresy.md). The lonely territory is scattered with ruins, old stone circles, solitary towers and hidden [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md), any one of which could prove to be the home of some lost treasure ... or unspeakable horror.

Girdling the Barrens is the Nameless River, a broad, slow-moving waterway that connects [the Semmerlak](../varushka/The_Semmerlak.md), the Barren Sea, and the [Feverwater](../navarr/Therunin.md#feverwater) with the sea to the east.

## Recent History

By the time [Empress Britta](../../history/Empress_Britta.md) ascended [the Throne](../../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md), the [Druj](../../barbarians/Druj.md) barbarians had dominated the eastern Barrens for a little over a century. They ruthlessly conquered the disparate barbarian nations who had lived there previously, and installed themselves as overlords. They used the central position of the Barrens to co-ordinate attacks on [Holberg](../the-league/Holberg.md) and [Reikos](../highguard/Reikos.md); depleted armies would withdraw to the Barrens, and be replaced by fresh armies. There is also evidence that the Druj used the Barrens to move materials (weirwood, mithril and white granite) between the two conquered territories.

Further information about the long history of the Barrens, and detailed information about its recent history, can be found in **[Conflict in the Barrens](../../history/Conflict_in_the_Barrens.md)**

## Major Features

### Houndsgate

The Druj called this fortress *The Jaws of Suffering*; the Dawnish used to refer to it as the *Spires of Dusk* or occasionally *Hounds Gate*. Built by the [Druj](../../barbarians/Druj.md), twin spires of [white granite](../../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md) and [weirwood](../../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md) rise along the eastern borders of the Barrens. This is at least the second castle to stand on the site; an earlier iteration was destroyed in 379YE by a combination of an assault by the combined septs of the Barrens and powerful [Spring magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md). When the Imperial forces struck into the Barrens at the beginning of 384YE, however, it quickly became clear that the Druj had not only rebuilt the Towers of Dusk but they had significantly reinforced them and the surrounding lands. They glared across the Barrens toward the Towers of Dawn until the resurgent Druj destroyed that fortification. They became a powerful symbol of the Druj domination of the Barrens.

In Spring 385YE, however, the Empire launched a major offensive into the Barrens that reached the Spires of Dusk. Even forced back to defensive positions, the Druj might have managed to hold the territory if not for the [glorious sacrifice of the Hounds of Glory](../../winds-of-war/Cost.md#the-battle-of-dusk). The army spent its last iota of strength to bring the Spires into Imperial hands, and has now become something of a place of pilgrimage for both Dawnish folk who wish to reflect on their great deed, and Imperial pilgrims dedicated to [Courage](../../religion/Courage.md), [Pride](../../religion/Pride.md), and [Ambition](../../religion/Ambition.md).

During the Spring Equinox 386YE the Imperial Senate voted to [remodel the fortification](../../senate-motions/Amend_Spires_of_Dusk.md). Now, pennants of the armies who fought the Barrens campaign fly from the battlements. Intermingled with the pennants are favours that represent other contributions toward the campaign, and above it all stands statues of winged hounds crowned in flowers.

Gone are the fire-hardened spines and gibbets, replaced instead with heraldries of families who lost members to the charge which breached the gates. Proudly fluttering from a long scrolling pennant are the names [recognised](../../synod-judgements/385YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-148) by the National Assembly of Dawn.

Within lies a tomb for **Garravaine De Rondell**, general of the Gryphon’s Pride, his resting place facing westwards towards [Holberg](../the-league/Holberg.md). Within the grounds a small tree memorialises **Brennos Brackensong**, general of the Quiet Step who was fatally wounded at the Battle of Dusk. Finally, a memorial stands to **Mera De Couerdefer**, the war witch who was mortally wounded on the Carmine Fields saving soldiers of the Wolves of War

Everywhere painted flowers spill across walls, columns and doors, the grounds are dotted all over with blooms of black roses, and resplendent over the repaired gate are carved the words that carried much of the campaign “*Where they have sown fear, we will make flowers grow*".

The Spires of Dusk are gone, and now the Houndsgate protects the borders of the Barrens against the malice of [the Mallum](../../barbarians/The_Mallum.md). The consecration of Houndsgate with [true liao](../../../mixed/religion/Liao.md#true-liao) has had a significant effect in the Barrens, as detailed in the **[Poetry and prose](../../winds-of-fortune/Poetry_and_prose.md#the-spires-of-dusk)** wind of fortune.

### Eaves of Peytaht

On the northern borders of the [Heart of Peytaht](#heart-of-peytaht), a massive grove of weirwood trees that has been somewhat cultivated by the inhabitants of the Barrens. Part of the [Great Forest](../../world/Great_Forest_of_Peytaht.md), the Eaves of Peytaht are controlled by the [Great Forest Orcs](../../barbarians/Great_Forest_Orcs.md); it is estimated that it might produce 25 wains of weirwood each season in Imperial hands.

### The Fangs

Along the [eastern coast](#bitter-strand) of the Barren sea, the Fangs are a series of perhaps a dozen massive rocky columns that rise out of the water which are mined for mithril. Several similar columns protrude from the headlands around this area. There are obvious signs of mine workings, and observation indicates that there is mithril ore to recover. It appears that the mines are quite primitive – the constant threat of flooding and the natural difficulty of mining at sea mean the Rahvin are exploiting only a fraction of the potential of this resource.

Controlled by the Rahvin, it is estimated that the Fangs would produce 16 wains of mithril each season in Imperial hands – or more if work were done to alleviate the problems left by the cruelty of Druj rule. This would require an initial investment of time and resources, as laid out in the **[Start a war](../../winds-of-fortune/Start_a_war.md#the-disposal-of-the-fangs)** wind of fortune

In Winter 386YE, after a long period of debate and negotiation and an attack by the [Gryphon's Pride](../../../mixed/government/military/Gryphon-s_Pride.md), the bourse resource was ceded to the Rahvin as part of a [greater treaty](../../senate-motions/Ratify_Rahvin_Treaty_Spring_387YE.md).

### Last Battle Wayhouse

*[Image: NBNavarr.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBNavarr.jpg)*

Standing in [the Bleaks](#the-bleaks), Last Battle was the first of the grand [Navarr wayhouses](../../../mixed/commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) built in the Empire. The opportunity to build it came about in part due to the efforts of *Last Battle*, a relatively young [striding](../navarr/Striding.md) made up almost exclusively of [thorns](../navarr/Thorn.md) united in the belief that the final battle against the [vallorn](../navarr/Vallorn.md) will surely happen in their lifetime. Their desire to confront the vallorn is tempered with the knowledge that it will require linking up all the hearts with [trods](../navarr/Trod.md), which requires the Empire to fulfil its destiny and conquer the intervening lands.

The wayhouse they inspired is built in the eastern [Bleaks](The_Barrens.md#the-bleaks), right on the edge of [the Mallum](../../barbarians/The_Mallum.md). The striding visit there regularly, and it is rare for there to be no thorns relaxing or training in the vicinity of the little settlement. From there, they engage in reconnaissance and patrols into the lands of the [Druj](../../barbarians/Druj.md).

Of course the wayhouse also serves as a hub to keep other Navarr steadings and stridings in contact with one another, and it's common for members of the nation to stop off in the Bleaks when they are travelling the eastern Empire. This has lead to some conflict with the local [orc](../../../mixed/world/Orc.md) sept, the [Karass](../../winds-of-fortune/Not_to_conquer.md#karass). The orcs were apparently [furious](../../winds-of-fortune/Hard_to_find.md#who-would-dare-to-go) that the wayhouse was built without consulting them, but there has been extensive negotiation with the sept to try and reduce tensions.

### White Bear

A mighty statue of a rearing ice bear stands in the midst of the ruined [Towers of the Dawn](#towers-of-the-dawn-ruined), in [Dawnguard](#dawnguard). Easily thirty feet tall, its fierce [grandeur](../../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md#grandeur) is undeniable. [Built](../../senate-motions/Construct_Statue_of_the_White_Bear.md) in 387YE by then [Master of the Koboldi](../../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md) Dima Vasilyevna Novosad, it was commissioned as part of a bid to win the [favour](../../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md#favour-of-cathan-canae) of [Cathan Canae](../../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md) for the [army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) of the [Eastern Sky](../../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md). The location was [specifically chosen](../../winds-of-fortune/Down_the_cavalier_road.md#cathan-canaes-favour) by the eternal herself. Apparently she regards the Barrens as part of the rightful dominion of Dawn, and in the past had been vexed by their reluctance to retake the land. Today the statue stands in part as a commitment to ensure that the great wild territory remains part of the nation regardless of the challenges that lie ahead.

### Towers of the Dawn (ruined)

The four massively built white granite tower-keeps collectively known as the Towers of the Dawn once stood along the western borders of the Barrens in [Dawnguard](#dawnguard). This great [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) served as a base of operation for Dawnish knights exploring the Barrens, and a reminder to the orcs that while they may dominate the Barrens, they did not do so without opposition. During the [Freedom Heresy](../../religion/Freedom_Heresy.md), the towers were the site of an atrocity as rebellious [briars](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Briar.md) murdered the yeomen and knights assigned to the First and Second towers. Ever after, the garrisons of the Towers of Dawn were unfriendly towards briars - and woe betide anyone who supported the 'virtues' of [freedom](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#anarchy) within their earshot! After several seasons of being attacked by Barrens orcs, the Towers of the Dawn were the subject of a major offensive by the Druj after the Spring Equinox 381YE. The fortification fell, smashed by overwhelming Druj force, and Dawnguard fell with it.

## Regions

Most of this information was gathered by the actions of brave Imperial [forces](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) working with a [spy network](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) [constructed](../../senate-motions/Construct_Barrens_Spy_Network.md) by order of the [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). It is correct as of Autumn 378YE.

### Dawnguard

Prior to the [Druj invasion](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#occupation-28dawnguard29) of Summer 381YE Dawnguard was the location of a number of Imperial settlements, the most prominent of which was **Drycastle**. It held for centuries against the barbarians of the Barrens, and the Druj armies, protecting Semmerholm and northern Brocéliande alike from the depredations of the orcs. Many of the so-called "Barrens houses" can trace their origin back to pre-Imperial Dawn, and several were founded by those fleeing the vicious purges of [King Penni the White](The_life_of_Penni_the_White.md). Others were founded by political exiles, and consequently a strong streak of independence runs through the Dawnish of the Barrens.

Often under attack, sometimes defeated, but never truly conquered, the [houses](Noble_house.md) of Dawn tended to be more martial than average, and much more prepared for violence than Dawnishfolk in other territories. Much glory awaited the brave knights who volunteered here for dangerous adventure, and it was a popular base of operation for both [questing knights](Questing_knight.md) and [knights-errant](Knight-errant.md). Before the Druj invasion, the [Towers of the Dawn](#towers-of-the-dawn) watched over the western borders of the Barrens; when Dawnguard fell, the fortification fell with it. This ushered in a period of unmatched tyranny and oppression by the cruel masters of [the Mallum](../../barbarians/The_Mallum.md). Those unwilling or unable to flee suffered greatly at the hands of the Druj, but in time were liberated by a major Imperial push to claim the entire territory once and for all.

### Murderdale

Battle between orc forces and Imperial forces are common here, giving the region its name. The soil here is particularly fertile, and there are a number of ruined villages that represent previous attempts by Imperials to settle the Barrens.

Through much of 378YE, a steady stream of orcs from [Brocéliande](../navarr/Brocéliande.md) were marked moving from west to east. The presence of the Druj forces in the [Carmine Fields](#carmine-fields), and their superstition of the [forest of Peytaht](#heart-of-peytaht) to the south, meant that many of these recent arrivals took up residence in this region – although there has been little conflict between the indigenous orcs and the Brocéliande refugees.

### Heart of Peytaht

- **Qualities:** Forest, Watchful, Great Forest Orcs

The [Great Forest of Peytaht](../../world/Great_Forest_of_Peytaht.md) stretches across the southern Barrens, and into [Reikos](../highguard/Reikos.md) to the south west. Stories from [Highguard](../highguard/Highguard.md) that describe the forest as being possessed of some dark spirit - something that partakes of the nature of a [Varushkan](../varushka/Varushka.md) [sovereign](../varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#sovereigns) - appear to be based on fact. The deeper a force presses into the forest the more uneasy they feel – the more they report being watched, and the more certain they become that they are unwelcome. Imperial forces have not pressed into the deeper forest. There is, however, no hint of discernible [vallorn](../navarr/Vallorn.md) presence here.

In the past, the forest was said to be equally hostile to human and orc, and attempts by either Imperial or barbarian forces to explore the interior invariably met with tragedy - assuming they were ever heard of again. This seems to have changed in 367YE when the Druj launched a crippling surprise attack through the forest into Reikos; it is unclear what the situation is today, however. .

There appear to be no permanent orc forces in this forest - especially now the Great Forest orcs have left the Barrens. The Druj forces that passed through the forest from Reikos, on their way to Holberg, reportedly suffered significant losses for doing so. Even today, corpses are occasionally found huddled against the boles of the massive trees, or hung by their entrails from thick branches. The orcs appear to have died in agony and great terror.

On the north-eastern borders of the forest is a massive strand of weirwood trees, one that has clearly been cultivated for some time. The [Eaves of Peytaht](#eaves-of-peytaht) is the equivalent of a [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) seat that produces a bounty of weirwood each season.

### The Carmine Fields

The soil on these wide plains has a red colour which the fanciful might say is from blood but is more likely due to a high clay quotient. This region is covered with isolated rice fields, and other crops that prosper on this kind of soil.

The region has clearly been settled by orc farmers and hunters for a very long time. However, as of 378YE there are signs of recent invasion; several settlements have been burnt to the ground and the populace enslaved.

### Farweald

- **Quality:** Forest

On the northern side of the Barren Sea is a damp forest that rises slowly to the north-east. As of 378YE, it is occupied by what seems to be several large groups of indigenous orcs who have built makeshift fortified camps along the rivers and the eastern borders. It would provide a strategic location from which to strike across the river into [Holberg](../the-league/Holberg.md).

### Hope's Rest

- **Quality:** Hills, [Rimebound](../../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#rimebound)

The weather in Hope's Rest is invariably worse than elsewhere in the Barrens. When it rains here, it buckets it down and the wind blows it's strong enough to knock you off your feet. Gales are common, turning to blizzards in winter that drive the snow into deep drifts and embankments. The vast lake that forms a border where the Barrens meets [Therunin](../navarr/Therunin.md) regularly freezes over in winter, that reveal strange figures with long gangly limbs and azure skin swimming beneath the thick ice.

In depth exploration of this region has proved tricky due to the rough terrain. Several times, spies encountered well-hidden traps designed to kill – spiked pits, spring-loaded impaling arms and the like. The high hills surround a well-fortified keep that appears to have only recently been completed. The southern slopes drop towards a large fresh-lake of brackish water on the other side of which are the marshy forests of [Therunin](../navarr/Therunin.md).

At one point, there was a small [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) high in the hills here, named **Dourfell Keep**. It was not garrisoned by orcs but by humans. Imperial spies were unable to get a very close look at them but they appeared to show signs of very strong [briar](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Briar.md) lineage, and the castle itself appeared to surround a strong Spring [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md). Scouts reported the remains of dozens of rotted and skeletonised Druj bodies nailed to trees, impaled on spikes and crucified on makeshift “x”s. Popular belief suggested this was a community of briars descended from those who entered the Barrens as part of the [Freedom Heresy](../../religion/Freedom_Heresy.md), and that they were allied with the indigenous orcs - especially those of the Great Forest.

Some time before the Spring Equinox 381YE, however, Dourfell Keep was destroyed. Rumour suggests that the community of briars themselves destroyed the fortification with [Spring magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md), to prevent it falling into Druj hands, Some exiled survivors of the community crossed into [Therunin](../navarr/Therunin.md) along with the Great Forest orcs in later 380YE. In recent months heralds of the [Thane of the Rimebound Shore](../../winds-of-fortune/Golden_magicians_fayre.md#frozen-strength) have been observed sheltering in the ruins of the keep whenever a gale or a blizzard is blowing.

A grand statue of *Hugh Farrier*, some thirty feet tall, stands in the region and faces east toward the Mallum. Built by the *koboldi* of [Adamant](../../eternals/Adamant.md) following the Summer Solstice of 387YE it serves as a grim reminder of the cost of war against the Druj.

### The Untrod Groves

- **Quality:** Forest

A deep, tangled forest that stretches from the hills of [Hopes Rest](#hopes-rest) to the very edge of the [Plain of Teeth](#the-plain-of-teeth). The woods seem largely uninhabited by the orcs, and the only trails appear to be game trails, trackless, it is easy to become lost in the groves – but reports also indicate that the woods are rich with untapped reserves of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), [beggarwood](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone).

### Bitter Strand

- **Quality:** Coastal, Rahvin

The Barren sea is technically a great lake, but the waters are not drinkable – they are not as salty as the Bay of Catazar but it is obvious that some mineral gives the waters a foul taste. They are home to a vicious species of predatory fish the size of an ox with armoured plates on the head. The northern coast is relatively smooth, but the southern coast is scattered with islands, many of them inhabited. Conquering this region would be made considerably easier by the addition of forces skilled at fighting amphibious assaults, such as the Red Wind Corsairs. Along the eastern coast of the Barren sea, where it narrows as it feeds into the [Saltmarsh](#saltmarsh), are the [Fangs](#the-fangs) a series of perhaps a dozen massive rocky columns that rise out of the water which are mined for mithril.

In Winter 386YE, after a long period of debate and negotiation, this region was ceded to the Rahvin sept.

### The Bleaks

- **Quality:** Forest, Foreigners (Karass)

The southern forests become danker and damper as Therunin gets closer. The woods are home to a number of orc clans, many of whom live in the branches of ancient trees. There are heavily wooded marshes in the south; there are occasional sightings of both [hydra](../../world/Legendary_beasts.md#hydra) and small [marshwalkers](../../world/Marshwalkers.md). The [Last Battle](../navarr/Keeper_of_the_Last_Battle.md#last-battle-wayhouse) wayhouse is in the east of the region, from which Navarri thorns engage in reconnaissance and patrols across the border into Therunin and the Sarangrave. There are scattered settlements all across the Bleaks belonging to the Karass sept: it is hard to be sure exactly of their numbers, as their locations often change without warning. While the Karass can also be found in the Plains of Teeth and in the Untrod Groves, it is in this region that they are found in the greatest number.

### Saltmarsh

- **Qualities:** Coastal, ~~Marsh~~

The eastern Barren Sea floods out across a great flat basin that contains the Saltmarsh. It is covered with treacherous rivers and streams that shift their banks regularly and without warning. The place is thick with flies and mosquitoes that leave nasty stinging welts on exposed flesh. The orcs who lived here maintain huts on stilts, and also moved about using long wooden stilts that occasionally gave them the appearance of walking on water. There appeared to be a large number of [herb gardens](../../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) maintained here by the indigenous orcs, no doubt captured from the Druj during the rebellion. With the Druj having largely recaptured the Barrens, it is not clear what the situation of the orcs here might be.

In Summer 384YE, then [Archmage of Spring](../../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Spring.md) **Ser Fabienne De Miel** unleashed a [devastating storm](../../winds-of-war/Into_the_valley.md#a-sudden-flood-28conjunction29) in the northern Barrens that battered [Bitter Strand](#bitter-strand) and flooded Saltmarsh.

**OOC Note:** The region lost the *Marsh* quality and gained the *Coastal* quality, a situation predicted to continue until such a time as an equally volatile [astronomantic conjunction](../../winds-of-fortune/Every_time_it_rains.md) occurs and someone takes advantage of it.

### The Plains of Teeth

The Druj oversaw their domination of the Barrens from this broken plain, and signs suggest that now they have returned, they are starting to do so again. This area may once have been heavily forested but it was ruthlessly cleared to provide building materials for the [Spires of Dusk](#spires-of-dusk). This [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) guarded access to the Druj lands to the east. After the Spring Equinox 381YE, it was finally confirmed (after a year or more of rumour) that the towers had been destroyed - allegedly by indigenous orcs working in conjunction with Imperial citizens - although the details are not common knowledge.

## OOC Notes

- As of the Spring Equinox 387YE the Great Forest Orcs control the Heart of Peytaht and the Rahvin control Bitter Strand. Dawn, and by extension the Empire, controls the rest of the territory.
- [Houndsgate](#houndsgate) is a rank two [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in the Plains of Teeth.
- [Last Battle Wayhouse](../navarr/Keeper_of_the_Last_Battle.md) in the Bleaks allows Navarri characters to establish a [personal resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource) in the Barrens and gain the benefit of any [great work](../../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in the territory without suffering the penalty for having a [resource in another nation](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#resources-in-other-nations).
- Prior to the [Druj invasion](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#occupation-28dawnguard29) of Summer 381YE, there were between ten and twenty thousand Dawnish citizens living the Dawnguard. With the chaos following the invasion it is impossible to say how many are still alive. There was no substantial Dawnish population outside of Dawnguard.
