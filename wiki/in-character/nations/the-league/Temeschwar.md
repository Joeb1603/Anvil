# Temeschwar

*[Image: Harsh northern weather and a pragmatic spirit](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RegionsofTemeschwar.png)*

## Overview

Temeschwar was [once part](The_League_history.md#origins-of-the-league) of wild [Varushka](../varushka/Varushka.md), but it joined the Empire alongside [Sarvos](Sarvos.md) and [Tassato](Tassato.md), when the League was formed. Citizens of Temeschwar commonly make an effort to be as flamboyant as their southern fellows, but their dress is inevitably more practical, to accommodate the harsh northern weather. They are traditionally more brutally pragmatic than those who live on the bay. It is a foothold of trade to Varushka, Wintermark and nearby Dawn. Trading in raw materials and crafted goods, especially weapons and armour, it is seen by many as the merchant capital of the northern lands.

Despite its good relations with Varushka, Temeschwar has always suffered somewhat from being distant from Sarvos and Tassato whose close proximity made them the natural heart of the League. Temeschwar may well be the richest of all the cities of the League, but it can never quite shake the fear that it is not as good as the others. This anxiety is just part of what drives the Temeschwari to be so competitive and so ruthless. Temeschwari strive to work that bit longer and that bit harder than anyone else in the League. If a Sarvossian closes their shop at sunset, their Temeschwari neighbour will light a pair of lanterns and keep trading for another hour.

Not all relations with Varushka are good, however. There is still some bad blood between those of old Varushkan stock in Temeschwar and the citizens of [Delev](../varushka/Karov.md#delev) in [Karov](../varushka/Karov.md). When Temeschwar joined the Empire it attempted to prove that it had legitimate ownership of the [Eternal Shafts of Time](../varushka/Karov.md#the-eternal-shafts-of-time), a rich mithril mine on the border between the two territories. There are still plays and theatrical adaptations that revolve around the underhand, ruthless way the boyar of Delev "stole" the mine from under the noses of the Temeschwari, and relations between some Delev traders and some citizens of Temeschwar are chilly to this day - especially in the rare cases where there is some distant blood connection between the two.

The Temeschwari are not just the most competitive people in the League, they also have a well deserved reputation as the most ruthless. In the years before the
creation of the Empire, the city was plagued by gangs of thieves and assassins who operated with open impunity despite the best efforts of the boyar, [Ratibor](../../religion/Ratibor_of_Temeschwar.md). Thousands of inhabitants were linked with the different gangs who used tattoos to mark membership and enforce loyalty.

*[Image: They wear their reputation for cold, hard dealing with pride.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:TemeschwariDraugir2.jpg)*

In the end Ratibor hired a force of Varushkan mercenaries and brought them in secret into the city during the depths of winter. In the notorious night of a thousand torches (named for the torches the soldiers carried to light their way) every inhabitant of the city was turned out of their beds, stripped and searched for marks. Anyone with a tattoo on their body was given a few minutes to collect their belongings before being forced from the city into the frozen snows beyond the walls. A few managed to bribe their way past the search but most were given over to the winter and were never seen again. The act was one of monstrous brutality, but it established the power of Ratibor as boyar and broke the power of the criminal gangs forever.

The Temeschwari wear their reputation for cold, hard dealing with pride. They view themselves as the strongest people in the League, combining the ambition and drive of the south with the strength and determination of the north. Physical as well as mental strength is prized here - Temeschwari bravos are as likely to favour the axe as the rapier - and her free companies have a reputation for toughness, as soldiers and as people. Most Temeschwari free companies are careful not to break the rules for they know the magistrates watch them carefully - but they will do almost anything required to win short of this. You can find many things in Temeschwar, it is said, but you will not find a scruple though you search the city from sunrise to sunset.

## Recent History

Rumours suggest that the [vyig](../varushka/Vyig.md) criminal organisation has become active again in the northern city. This Varushkan criminal fraternity is united by an absolute hatred of Imperial law, and once threatened to overtake both Temeschwar and the League. Becoming increasingly bold, their activities led to a major operation supported by the [Synod](../../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) to once again [drive these criminals](../../winds-of-fortune/A_big_sleep.md) out of the northernmost League city - and indeed the League as a whole.

Shortly before the Winter Solstice 381YE, the [Blood Red Roads](Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#blood-red-roads) were completed, with Temeschwar as a major hub. They providing merchants and traders from across the Empire with easy access to the markets of Temeschwar, and vice versa. For more than half a decade the roads provided a major boon to the [farms](../../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) and [businesses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) of the territory. In the Winter of 386YE, however, the Imperial Senate determined to [levy tolls](../../senate-sessions/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#levy-tolls-on-blood-red-roads) on traffic using the road, meaning they no longer increase the income of individual citizens but provide a significant boost to the Imperial Treasury, in part to pay for the war against the [vallorn](../navarr/Vallorn.md).

### Rat Plague

Beginning in the Spring of 386YE, Temeschwar suffered a [plague of rats](../../winds-of-fortune/They_bit_the_cats.md). Reports of fat sewer rats attacking cats and dogs [soon escalated](../../winds-of-fortune/Pitter_patter.md). [Businesses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) were targeted by the rats, and the [Unfettered Mind](../../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md) [Conclave order](../../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave_order.md) were brought in to analyse the obviously magical situation. They discovered it was more complex than it at first appeared, with reports of [creatures stranger than rats](../../winds-of-fortune/Pitter_patter.md#rodents-of-assorted-sizes) being involved. The [Temeschwari Chamber of Commerce](../../winds-of-fortune/Pitter_patter.md#the-chamber-of-commerce) received several pitches for plans to deal with the rat plague, and in the end chose to accept a tender from the [Rat Kings](../../winds-of-fortune/Pitter_patter.md#the-oldest-guild), one of the oldest guilds in the city. As [the year wore on](../../winds-of-fortune/Graveyard_rats.md) there were concerns about sickness, addressed in part by the [bishops](../../religion/Bishop.md) of Temeschwar but the Rat Kings guild [appeared to have contained the problem](../../winds-of-fortune/Graveyard_rats.md#guild-of-ratcatchers). There was some talk of parley, and of agreements reached with an unknown *thing* in the sewers beneath the city, but at the time of writing the rat problem is broadly [contained, if not entirely ended](../../winds-of-fortune/Graveyard_rats.md#contained-not-ended). Rumours suggest the [relocation](../../winds-of-fortune/Golden_light.md#the-womans-work) of the body of [Empress Giselle](../../history/Empress_Giselle.md) to [Capodomus Cathedral](../../../mixed/titles/Archbishop_of_Capodomus.md#capodomus-cathedral) in [Sarvos](Sarvos.md) may have played some role in the relative cessation of rat attacks but specifics are not commonly known.

## Major Features

### Temeschwar

The city of Temeschwar occupies most of [Hanuri](#hanuri). It is also the site of the [Northern Traders Guild](../../../mixed/titles/Northbound_Trademaster.md) (which forms the southern arm of the [Northern trade network](../navarr/Northern_trade_network.md). The [Tomb of Empress Giselle](../../../mixed/titles/Bloody_Custodian_of_Pride.md#virtue27s-respite) was built here in Winter 380YE, and Temeschwar is also the site of the [Bloody Great Theatre](Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#the-bloody-great-theatre), a theatrical venue that is also a site of pilgrimage for League citizens interested in exploring their [Proud](../../religion/Pride.md) history of the dour city. In Winter 381YE, a branch of the [Tassato Mana Exchange](../../../mixed/titles/Steward_of_the_Tassato_Mana_Exchange.md) - the [Temeschwar Mana Exchange](../../../mixed/titles/Magic_Butcher.md#the-temeschwar-mana-exchange) - was built on the eastern side of one of the cities' largest and oldest meat markets. Until Spring 383YE, it was the site of a [statue](../../senate-motions/Construct_statue_of_Janon.md) dedicated to the eternal [Janon](../../eternals/Janon.md). The statue was [reduced to expensive rubble](../../winds-of-fortune/Smash_it_up.md) by a band of Varushkan wardens apparently inspired to action by the Council of Nine.

Northernmost of the four great cities of [the League](The_League.md), Temeschwar was one of the three founding cities. It is very different in style to [Sarvos](Sarvos.md) and [Tassato](Tassato.md), and its origins as a [Varushkan](../varushka/Varushka.md) trade town are obvious in the architecture of its many old buildings. Structures are built to last in Temeschwar, with stone being a much more popular building material than wood, and with slate roofs more common than thatch. The city is extremely cold during the Winter (and during late Autumn and early Spring most years), and snowfall is a regular problem not faced by the inhabitants of more southerly cities of the League.

The people have a well deserved reputation for competitiveness, and for ruthlessness. Temeschwar may well be the richest of all the cities of the League, but it can never quite shake the fear that it is not as good as the others. This anxiety is just part of what drives the Temeschwari to be so competitive and so ruthless. Temeschwari strive to work that bit longer and that bit harder than anyone else in the League. If a Sarvossian closes their shop at sunset, their Temeschwari neighbour will light a pair of lanterns and keep trading for another hour.

### The Walls of Temeschwar

The city proper is surrounded by [great white granite walls](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md). While not on the scale of the walls of [Holberg](Holberg.md) in the east, they have proved more than sufficient to protect the city from its enemies. Nine great towers are placed roughly equidistant around the walls, seven named for each of the Virtues and two (those that incorporate the gates through which the Gancio enters and leaves the city) called the Northern and Southern Rivergates. Each is equipped with a great iron bell, used to toll the hours, and to warn of potential dangers. In cases of fire, for example, the bell in the tower nearest the blaze is sounded repeatedly until the flames are extinguished.

Temeschwar has grown since the walls were built, of course, and there are nearly as many buildings outside the walls as there are within them. In times of emergency, however, the population can take refuge within the walls - although how long Temeschwar could survive an actual siege remains to be seen.

### Beneath the City

Under Temeschwar are a maze of sewer tunnels, forgotten basements, dry well shafts, occasional cisterns, and tunnels deployed by criminals (such as the now-departed [vyig](../varushka/Vyig.md)) to move unseen around the city. There are also known to be tunnels down there that predate the earliest Varushkan settlement here, and nobody knows their full extent. This subterranean labyrinth is widely believed to be the domain only of rats and other vermin - and of the [Rat King of Temeschwar](../../history/Rat_King_of_Temeschwar.md), should such a creature actually exist.

### The Bloody Great Theatre

The [Bloody Great Theatre](Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#boody-great-threatre) is one of the most prominent theatres in the Empire. It was [commissioned](../../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) by Gant Archama and approved by the [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) in Winter 380YE, in the wake of a [powerful surge of interest](../../winds-of-fortune/In_brightest_day.md#the-power-to-exalt) in the arts. A small movement to have the building officially named "Grand Temeschwari Theatre" gained very little traction - the amusement of calling such a large and impressive structure by such a common name has meant it has largely stuck. It serves as an inspiration, and a source of [Pride](../../religion/Pride.md), for the citizens of the city.

### The Great Game

[Commissioned](../../senate-motions/Construct_Lorenzo-s_Great_Game.md) by the [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) in Spring 382YE, and completed a little over a month later, the [Great Game](Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#lorenzo27s-great-game) was paid for by Lorenzo "la Volpe" Macelliao von Temeschwar and built by [Almodin Oktístis](../../winds-of-fortune/Those_who_worship.md#almodin-oktc3adstis2c-priest-of-the-builder), the [Asavean](../../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) architect. It is a grand casino that also contains numerous places of refreshment and hostelries that stands beside the [Blood Red Road](Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#blood-red-roads) outside the southern walls of the city proper.

### The [Fortress of Salt](../../../mixed/titles/Fortress_of_Salt.md)

The Fortress of Salt is a Bourse resource located in [Metri](#metri). Custodianship of the Fortress is an [Imperial Title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that brings with it a Seat on the [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). It produces 25 Imperial wains of mithril every season. Control is allocated to any Imperial citizen by open auction during the Autumn Equinox.

### Lorenzo's Legacy of Loyalty

Spread across the entire territory of Temeschwar are a number of red stone [stelae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stele). A central stone, the keystone for the entire [great work](../../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md), stands in front of the Butcher's Guild in the city itself. The [legacy](Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#the-legacy-of-loyalty) guides and focuses the flows of mana through the territory, increasing the amount of crystal mana produced by [sites](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md) across Temeschwar.

### Mellfort

In [Locul](#locul), on the higher slopes of Mellfort Peak, stands old **Mellfort**. This ancient hillfort consists of a ruined set of concentric rings of black stone, believed to have been of Terunael origin (although some scholars claim the Terun simply adapted a much older structure, perhaps one built by [The Sentinel](../../religion/The_Sentinel.md)). The fort was in ruins when the first [Varushkan](../varushka/Varushka.md) settlers came here, believed to be a casualty of the wars between the people of Terunael and the orcs. Human settlers lost no time in incorporating the sturdy stones of the ruin into their own fortification.

The fortification here was decommissioned during the reign of [Empress Mariika](../../history/Empress_Mariika.md), during a compromise which saw funds channelled to the fortification of Temeschwar itself. Following the fall of Skarsind there was discussion of rebuilding it as a watchpost and beacon. In early 377YE the fortress was partially refitted to serve as a base of operation for groups of [bravos](Bravo.md) hunting bandits and brigands preying on Temeschwari trade. Overseen by Bridget Eastville (senator for [Mitwold](../the-marches/Mitwold.md)) as part of a larger plan to provide protection to towns throughout the Empire, the defences were not sufficient to qualify Mellfort as a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md). Still, they helped reduce outlawry throughout the territory and led to further discussion about completing the work and restoring Mellfort to its former glory as a strategically placed fortification.

Throughout its history, people who have spent long periods of time here have reported strange dreams - dreams of sadness, loss and bittersweet remembrance. Unlike many such 'accursed places' it gained a reputation for strangeness, rather than terror or fear, and in the early years of the Empire became something of a place of pilgrimage for those suffering grief or regret. Interest in the strange aura here culminated in Winter 383YE with [proposals](../../winds-of-fortune/A_health_to_the_company.md#restful-isolation) as to how to make the best use of it. The [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) ultimately approved the plan of the Emerald Magpie Guild to turn Mellfort into a [place to rest and recuperate](../../winds-of-fortune/A_health_to_the_company.md#restful-isolation), establishing the [Reaper's Repose](../../../mixed/titles/Chatelaine_of_the_Reapers-_Repose.md#reapers-repose). The existing structures - the palisades and the watchpost - were incorporated into the new structure, and consequently Mellfort is no longer even lightly fortified.

### Lorenzo's Deep Pockets

*[Image: The politics of Temeschwar can be robust even for the League.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Butchers.jpg)*

Just over a month after the Spring Equinox 381YE a large section of north-western [Goldglades](../navarr/Miaren.md#goldglades) in [Miaren](../navarr/Miaren.md) [collapsed with an almighty roar](../../winds-of-fortune/Rocks_and_gravel.md). After the initial collapse, the hole began to spread. Warnings by the Navarr [vates](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Navarr_magical_traditions.md#vates) reached the already-worried people of the Vardstein Vale not a moment too soon. Parts of that region soon followed north-western Goldglades into the spreading hole. Thanks to the Navarr, the few settlements here were all evacuated in time to avoid major loss of life although the disruption caused significant losses to those invested in the forests of the south-western territory.

Where Temeschwar once bordered Upwold, there is now a massive [sinkhole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinkhole). Estimated to be seven hundred feet deep, with a lake at the bottom that may well be bottomless. The cliff walls are nearly vertical and still prone to collapse in some places, but there are also signs of caves and tunnels opening out in several places. Initial explorers have excitedly reported what look to be ancient mine workings - some reaching back to the time of the Terunael, and some reaching back even further. The full extent of the network of caverns here has yet to be determined. More worryingly, some of the early daredevils who have made a preliminary investigation of some of the more accessible caves and tunnels have failed to return - and all those who have made it back in one piece have described encounters with particularly angry [trogoni](../../world/Trogoni.md) - creatures rarely encountered outside of the mountains of [Urizen](../urizen/Urizen.md), and the northern peaks of [Wintermark](../wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [Varushka](../varushka/Varushka.md).

The sinkhole was officially named "[Lorenzo's Deep Pockets](../../../mixed/titles/Delver_of_the_Depths.md#lorenzo27s-deep-pockets)" by Lorenzo Macelliao von Temeschwar, but is sometimes known smirkingly as "The Foxhole" or (rather more lewdly) as "La Volpe's Arse".

### The Bloodied Rose

An [edifice](../../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md), this [counting house](../../../mixed/government/synod/Rewarding.md#counting-houses) collects donations for the Virtue fund from citizens of Temeschwar. This imposing stone building stands across the road from civil service offices. Just above the door is a bas-relief bloodied rose, from which the counting house takes its name. Just inside the door, the walls are decorated with scenes of virtuous Temeschwari citizens past, including Ratibor and Lorenzo. In the central room, seven [runeboxes](../../../out-of-character/game-design/Technology.md#runeboxes-and-runebound-chains) sit at the end of orderly queue lines: one for each virtue. Each one has a visitor's log where people are encouraged to write their name, the date, and the amount donated. Each month, the name of the largest donor is displayed on the wall.

## Regions

### Hanuri

- **Qualities:** City, Fortification

The main feature of Hanuri is the city of [Temeschwar](#temeschwar) itself.

### Locul

- **Quality:** Hills

Much of the territory of Temeschwar is rugged and hilly, but in Locul the foothills rise steeply into the heights of **Mellfort Peak** which took its name from the [hill fort](#mellfort) built on the upper slopes. These ruins were ultimately repurposed to create the prestigious [Reaper's Repose](../../../mixed/titles/Chatelaine_of_the_Reapers-_Repose.md#reapers-repose), a premiere location for exhausted merchant-princes to rest and recuperate away from the pressures of city life.

Before the forerunners of the League restored the ruins there, the peak was called *Suyan*. The mountain dwarfs the surrounding territory, for all that [Urizen](../urizen/Urizen.md) visitors like to claim (somewhat smugly) that it is not a *real* mountain. The peaks and foothills of Locul are the site of many prosperous mines, especially those working [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). One of the best known of these mines is **Descent** - a mining village built around a large open sinkhole at the foot of Mellfort Peak. Many years ago a stairs and ramp were built spiralling down into the depths of the Descent, and green iron is mined from an extensive cavern complex beneath. The [Custodian of the Assayer's Guild](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Assayer-s_Guild.md) often spends a lot of time surveying the mines of Locul, when they are not at their offices in Temeschwar proper.

### Metri

The [Fortress of Salt](#fortress-of-salt) is found in Metri, very close to the border with [Varushkan](../varushka/Varushka.md) [Karov](../varushka/Karov.md). During Winter 380YE, something unnatural settled [into the woods](../../winds-of-fortune/Into_the_woods.md) here, that brought with it both a peculiar magical effect and a strange sense of safety. Gaunt spirits, ghosts, and a general sense of mild unease permeated the place but the roads were unmolested by bandits and beast alike. Anything or anyone that tried to bring violence against another person in Metri suffers bloody, unnatural judgement at the claws and teeth of alien creatures that haunted the region. Just before the Winter Solstice 384YE it was uncovered that the place was home to a number of heralds of the [Hag Queen](../../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#skathe). During the Winter Solstice 384YE the Order of the Rod and Shield were instructed to go and clear out these heralds - removing the [haunted](../../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#haunted) nature of the region - which led to a [showdown](../../winds-of-fortune/Fade_out_again.md#a-disturbing-encounter) between the *Grand Enchantrix* and the Grandmaster of the Rod and Shield, **Sakari Nightscale**.

### Vargat

A somewhat hilly region, dotted with villages and scattered areas of cultivated [woodlands](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md). Vargat is perhaps best known for the brewing of strong spirits, the good quality, thick-crusted dark rye-bread made from [locally sourced flour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) known colloquially a *Vargat Bianch*, and for extremely strong-tasting pickled cabbage that is considered a local delicacy by those from Vargat, and a challenge to the digestive system and sense of taste by everyone else. Increased trade with southern nations, especially the [Freeborn](../the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), has seen a recent fashion in Vargat for vegetable dishes prepared by [fermenting with a variety of seasonings](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimchi). It remains to be seen if this culinary fashion will become popular in the League.

### Vardstein Vale

- **Quality:** Forest

The southern Vardstein Vale that slopes down toward [Miaren](../navarr/Miaren.md) has a reputation for wildness. While the periphery has been cultivated with a number of small [woodland preserves](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md), most of the woodland is left "wild". The forest is known to be home to many wolves, and to at least one pack of wily [dire wolves](../../world/Mundane_beasts.md#dire-beasts). The decision to leave the Vale as a hunting preserve has caused occasional friction with the [Navarr](../navarr/Navarr.md) and [Wintermark](../wintermark/Wintermark.md) who point out that wild beasts do not respect borders. More artistically minded individuals point to the decision to leave the Vardstein Vale "wild" as reflecting a key element of the Temeschwari (and indeed League) soul. They are civilised, but they are not tame ...

## OOC Notes

- All the regions of Temeschwar are in the hands of the League (and by extension the Empire) making it a staunchly Imperial territory.
- The [city walls](#the-walls-of-temeschwar) are a rank two [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md).
- [Businesses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in the territory benefit from both the [Great Game](#the-great-game) and the [Drovers' Guild Timbermill and Sorting Yard](../../../mixed/titles/Vardstein_Vandal.md#great-wealth) and produce two random [measures](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#natural-materials) and an extra 36 rings each season.
- [Congregations](../../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) in the territory no longer benefit from the [Bloody Great Theatre](#the-bloody-great-theatre) following a [mandate](../../synod-judgements/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-18) to encourage patrons to donate to the [Vallornguard](../../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md)
- [Mana sites](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md) in the territory no longer benefit from the [Lorenzo's Legacy of Loyalty](#lorenzos-legacy-of-loyalty) following a senate motion to [divert production to Spiral](../../senate-sessions/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#league-crystal-mana-to-spiral).
- Due to the presence of the [sinkhole](Temeschwar.md#lorenzo27s-deep-pockets) in the [Vardstein Vale](#vardstein-vale) it is not possible for a [campaign army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) to move directly between Temeschwar and Upwold despite their apparent proximity on the map.
