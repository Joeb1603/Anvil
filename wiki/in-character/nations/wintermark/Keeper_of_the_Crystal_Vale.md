# Keeper of the Crystal Vale

```
This title has been abrogated as the sinecure attached was added to the Strategos of Wintermark
```

## Overview

The Keeper of the Crystal Vale was a [Wintermark](Wintermark.md) [Imperial title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) created following an [announcement](../../senate-motions/Construct_Vales_Might.md) in Winter 380YE by the [Civilian Commissioner](../../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md). The Vale was completed shortly before the 381YE Spring Equinox. The position of Keeper was a [sinecure](../../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of crystal mana.

## Responsibilities

The Keeper had no particular responsibilities; they simply received a bounty of [crystal mana](../../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) from the [Vale's Might](#vales-might) in [Hahnmark](Hahnmark.md). If the vale were to be threatened in any way, it would have been the responsibility of the Keeper to deal with those threats.

## Appointment

The Keeper of the Crystal Vale was appointed by unanimous decision of the [Wintermark](Wintermark.md) [senators](../../../mixed/titles/Senator.md). If a unanimous decision could not be reached, the title [could have been appointed](../../../mixed/government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title could only be held by a Wintermark citizen. The Keeper had [tenure](../../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and served until they died or stepped down. They could be [revoked](../../../mixed/government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Wintermark [National Assembly](../../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
