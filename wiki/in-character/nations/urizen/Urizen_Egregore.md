# Urizen Egregore

## Menos

Menos, the Urizen [Egregore](../general/Egregores.md), almost always appears to have lineage. It's often that of a golden skinned individual with a calm and polite demeanour. It plays the role of observer, commenting on the events around it and loving the interplay of conversation with intelligent and educated individuals from all walks of life.

The Urizen egregore has taken one host

### Previous Hosts

#### Kassiani

*[Image: Kassiani](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:KassianiEgregore.png)*

Kassiani was raised in Saliscloud Spire, a small library focused community in Redoubt that primarily collects memoirs and biographies. Her interests, however, lay more in practical pursuits, and she trained to become an artisan. When away from Anvil, she can often be found tending a small hive of bees that help to pollinate a small, but surprisingly successful, fruit garden on the slopes below Saliscloud, or practising practical crafts.

#### Philostrate

Philostrate is an aged Draughir sentinel who had his first taste of war when the Grendel invaded Spiral and Hadrian shattered the lighthouse at Apulian.

*[Image: Philostrate](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Egregore_Urizen_Liam.jpg)*

Spending his years in Spiral coordinating resistance against the Grendel, he maintains a keen interest in strategy and tactics, in politics as well as in war and particularly the interconnectedness of people and events in the Net of the Heavens.
