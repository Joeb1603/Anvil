# Morrow

*[Image: The spires here are the oldest in Urizen](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RegionsofMorrow.png)*

## Overview

The spires of Morrow are amongst the oldest in Urizen, dating back to the nation's birth. The heart of the Heliopticon is here, making it a centre of communication for the Nation. The miracle of the Heliopticon is administered from this territory, and most spires maintain a series of prisms, lenses and mirrors atop their tallest roof in order to pass on the flickering messages that keep the nation in constant communication.

Many spires in Morrow inhabit some of the original buildings discovered on the mountaintops by the first Urizen to ascend the mountains. Large open buildings, with doorways and halls just larger than a human might need, they create a sense of open space that has set the pattern for all Urizen architecture.

Despite its great age Morrow moves inexorably forward, and many of its spires are undergoing constant reconstruction and improvement. It is said that “no two days find the same Morrow” as the Urizen constantly strive to perfect their land even further. A project may take a decade or even more to come to fruition, but the Urizen pride themselves on vision and their desire to perfect their world.

## Recent History

Following the Summer Solstice 382YE, the [Druj](../../barbarians/Druj.md) armies [poured across the southern borders](../../winds-of-war/382YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#lost-in-the-echo-morrow) of Morrow from [Zenith](Zenith.md). Despite the best efforts of the [Highborn](../highguard/Highguard.md) armies of the Granite Pillar, Valiant Pegasus, and Seventh Wave the orcs conquered [Peregro](#peregro) and [Caeli](#caeli). In the process they destroyed not only the [Halls of Knowledge](#halls-of-knowledge) but also the central [Heliopticon](Urizen_culture_and_customs.md#the-heliopticon) tower, the [Glorious Fountain of Dawn and Dusk](../../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Glorious_Fountain.md#the-glorious-fountain-of-dawn-and-dusk), and the [Gardens of Morrow](#caeli). They also destroyed the Temple of the Winds, slaughtering nearly two thirds of the [sword scholars](Sword_scholar.md) in the process. Their advance was finally turned back at the borders of [Operus](#operus).

## Major Features

*[Image: The mountains of Morrow are cold.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Ariadne_in_the_Snow.jpg)*

### Grand Library of Canterspire

Over time, a number of spires built around the same high peak have merged to form one whole, the Canterspire. In any other country, it would be called a town, and would sprawl like a nest of termites. As this is Urizen, the merge was carefully planned and executed over centuries. Each Spire specialises in one of the pillars of Urizen philosophy, and they are defended by a network of citadels sitting on the lower peaks. Canterspire overlooks the preternaturally still lake known to the Urizen as the [Eye of Morrow](#eye-of-morrow). During the Druj invasion of Morrow, Canterspire served as a sanctuary for those fleeing the orcs of the Mallum. With the aid of the [eternal](../../eternals/Eternal.md) [Phaleron](../../eternals/Phaleron.md), the spires were sheathed in magical crystal transforming them temporarily into a mighty fortress protected by bizarre guardians drawn from the Day realm.

During the Druj invasion, a large number of books and libraries from across Morrow were transported to the safety of the crystal sanctuary. With the orc armies pushed back into [Zenith](Zenith.md), many who had made the desperate trip to the spires here chose to stay. They have since been joined by a large number of refugees from Zenith [rescued during the daring raid of Spring 383YE](../../winds-of-war/383YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#zenith). Together with the original inhabitants, and with several exiles from [Spiral](../the-league/Spiral.md) who refuse to live under the oppressive aura there, Canterspire has unsurprisingly become a central "node" for the [new magical research initiative](../../../mixed/titles/Doyen_of_the_Spires.md) spreading across Urizen.

In Summer 383YE, in response to a proposal by the Celestial Library, the Imperial Senate [commissioned](../../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) a [major expansion of Canterspire](../../senate-motions/Construct_Celestial_Library_at_Canterspire.md) employing two-score wains of mithril and the work of countless Urizen artisans to transform Canterspire into the Grand Library of Canterspire - which was then [ceded](../../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) to the eternal. This cooperation with Phaleron provides several benefits - not only will Phaleron "enfold" the library should it ever be threatened, drawing it into a grand chamber between the mortal realm and the realm of Day where neither the residents nor the books may be threatened, but it will also serve as the lynchpin of the new body of [Urizen Lore](Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore)

Until Autumn 382YE, Canterspire was home to the **Canterspire Academy**, an august educational institution which oversaw the [Imperial Academy for Young Heroes](../../../out-of-character/events/The_Academy.md) in Anvil. Following the imminent threat of Druj invasion, with a heavy heart the Chancellor [announced the offices of the Academy would be moving](../../winds-of-fortune/The_arch_of_the_door.md) to the [Castle of Thorns](../dawn/Astolat.md#the-castle-of-thorns) in [Astolat](../dawn/Astolat.md).

### The Three Watchers

One of the main passes from Morrow to Zenith lies in the shadow of the mountains known as the Three Sorrows, but following the spring Equinox 383YE their countenance was [irrevocably altered](../../winds-of-fortune/An_affirming_flame.md#the-three-watchers). Little *koboldi*, servants of the [eternal](../../eternals/Eternal.md) [Adamant](../../eternals/Adamant.md), carved their lower slopes under the guidance of an Urizen illuminate named Furia Severia (a famous sculptor in her time). The koboldi, summoned by the *Ashen Flame* coven using a specially prepared [arcane projection](../../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) fashioned three great statues of Highborn soldiers gazing south into [Zenith](Zenith.md). Each soldier is nearly two hundred foot tall, clothed in armour, weapons at the ready to turn back any who attempt to invade. Each represents one of the Highborn armies who fought to defend Urizen from the encroaching tide of Druj - the Valiant Pegasus, the Seventh Wave, and the Granite Pillar. They tower over the pass and the surrounding countryside, a permanent tribute memorialising the Highborn sacrifice for all time.

Between the feet of the middle statue lies an austere stone shrine; a place of pilgrimage for those who wish to gaze upon the wondrous sculpture and meditate on the virtues that inspired its creation. The shrine was [consecrated](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md) with [true liao](../../../mixed/religion/Liao.md#true-liao) by Aurum of Virtue's Shield shortly before the Summer Solstice 383YE.

### The [Canterspire Circle](../../../mixed/titles/Canterspire_Circle.md)

The Canterspire Circle (or more correctly, Circles) is a Bourse resource located in [Operus](#operus). Custodianship of the Circle is an [Imperial Title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that brings with it a Seat on the [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). It produces 22 Imperial wains of weirwood every season. Control is allocated to any Imperial citizen by open auction during the Winter Solstice.

### Bridge of the Air

In winter 385YE an [appraisal](../../appraisals/Appraisal.md) by the [Imperial Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) led to a proposal to create a series of marvelous cables connecting the Arch of the Sky to the rest of Urizen. Following a throw-away comment from one of the Urizen about it being a shame that [white granite](../../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md) cannot be sent through the air the way the Heliopticon sends words, [Graciana](../../appraisals/Appraisal.md#graciana-i-lòpez-i-guerra) of the [Prognosticator's Office](../../appraisals/Appraisal.md#the-prognosticators-office) had a brainwave. Inspired in part by the washing lines that stretch between houses in [Tassato](../the-league/Tassato.md), and in part by the great cranes of the city of [Cargo](Redoubt.md#cargo), she proposed the construction of a unique “bridge” connecting the Starstair to the lower peaks of Caeli.

By stretching [mithril](../../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) cables across the valley between the two peaks, and using ushabti to provide motile force, shipments of stone are wheeled through the air from one mountain top to the next. By picking the right peaks and using the occasional lift where needed, the "skyway" has been extended all the way through [Operus](Morrow.md#operus), past [Canterspire Circle](../../../mixed/titles/Canterspire_Circle.md), to [Cargo](Redoubt.md#cargo). [Extensive additional investment](../../winds-of-fortune/Road_out_of_nowhere.md#a-road-in-the-sky) saw the skyway expanded into parts of [Zenith](Zenith.md), [Spiral](../the-league/Spiral.md), and [Redoubt](Redoubt.md). The “Bridge of the Air” is a true wonder, transporting people and trade goods between spires. Lifts help transport travellers and cargo from the lowlands to the highlands spires, and conventional roads and bridges have been employed where they are most useful.

Since completion of the Bridge of the Air, it has become easier than ever before to traverse the often difficult terrain of Urizen, and of Spiral.

### Quicksilver's Flame

High on the scorched slopes of Morrow’s hills, the [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) of Quicksilver’s Flame rises from a jagged basalt outcrop above a steaming hot-spring lagoon. Overlooking the misty valleys of Peregro and the wild border of [Therunin](../navarr/Therunin.md), it stands sentinel where volcanic stone meets enchanted forest. Its white granite battlements, etched with [The Mountain](../../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Mountain.md) and [The Oak](../../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Oak.md) in obsidian, house a garrison of sentinels and battlemages.

A deep central chamber, known colloquially as the Flameheart, serves as a strategic command centre and a place of meditation, symbolising the fusion of martial prowess and enlightened thought. Its halls and galleries are adorned with murals depicting the history and philosophy of Urizen, serving as a constant reminder of the nation's enduring spirit. Crowning the fortress is the Areté Spire, engraved with the motto: “*Where the mountain burns, reason shines*.”

Quicksilver’s Flame’s elevated position offers a strategic vantage point over the border with Therunin and allows for swift communication via the Heliopticon network, ensuring rapid response to threats to Peregro.

### The Caves of the Claw

The Caves of the Claw are a series of deep caves recently excavated by ushabti associated with the citadel of Phoenix Reach. While local records mentioned "bloody crystals" the exact location of the Caves was a secret lost to modern Urizen. Their position was uncovered by the [Minister of Historical Research](../../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) in 378YE, and initial explorations determined that they were rich in subterranean mana flows and [crystal mana](../../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals).

In Spring 379YE, the newly appointed [Crystal Architect of the Spires](../../../mixed/titles/Crystal_Architect_of_the_Spires.md) announced that work would begin on developing and expanding the magical auras here, and the [Imperial title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) of [Custodian of the Claw](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Claw.md) was created to oversee the resultant [sinecure](../../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md). More details about the history and nature of the caves can be found [here](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Claw.md#the-caves-of-the-claw).

### Highwatch

A citadel that focuses on military science and leadership rather than the combat skills of individuals. It is where the Urizen met Giovanni the Peacemaker to negotiate their incorporation into the Empire. It was [expanded](../../senate-motions/Construct_Highwatch_Citadel.md) shortly after the Summer Solstice 384YE following negotiations with *Agon, Polemarch of Clarity*, a herald of [Zakalwe](../../eternals/Zakalwe.md). Now, the citadel serves as a base of operations for several heralds of the *Master Strategist* who spend their time debating matters of strategy and warfare with visitors to the citadel. Games of strategy and tactics are common, both those played in the abstract on boards and those involving dozens of participants on both sides in exercises and actual (albeit nonlethal) fighting. Defence of the citadel falls under the purview of the [Polemarch of Highwatch](../../../mixed/titles/Polemarch_of_Highwatch.md), appointed each Autumn Equinox by unanimous decision of the Urizen senators.

### The Weave

A spire dedicated to the study of the Net of the Heavens, with particular importance to the [illuminate](Urizen_religious_beliefs.md#illuminates) philosophy. The renowned writer Arria wrote her influential treatise on illumination *A Light In Dark Places* here, a text that is still studied by her successors three centuries later. The spire overlooks [Three Watchers Pass](Morrow.md#the-three-watchers) to [Clypion](Zenith.md#clypion), whose creation was [facilitated by Furia Severia](../../winds-of-fortune/An_affirming_flame.md#the-three-watchers), herself an outspoken supporter of the illuminate approach to virtue.

### The Eye of Morrow

A small lake at the foot of the mountain that holds the Canterspire. Famed as a place of meditation. The air in this valley is remarkably still, and sound carries a vast distance. A perhaps misleading message is carved into a rock that stands next to the lake - it is in the Imperial tongue, and simply states "The Eye is Closed". That message has been there as long as any can remember.

### The Halls of Knowledge (ruined)

When Zenith was added to the Empire, the Urizen proposed that the Empire should have a second seat of magical study, in addition to the [Lyceum](../the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#lyceum). Rather than threaten the Lyceum, the new academy would help unite the magicians of Urizen and allow them to pool their magical lore for the benefit of the Empire. Instead of building their academy of magic around an existing spire, they created an entirely new one and called it the Halls of Knowledge. A number of respected theorists and seers travelled to the new academy, and a Provost was appointed to help co-ordinate their research - and to encourage other Urizen magicians to offer their assistance. It soon became known as a centre for prognostication and the study of Day magic - to this day some of the best diviners in the civil service spend time studying here to hone their abilities.

The college stood in the hills of [Clypion](Zenith.md#clypion) until shortly after the Autumn Equinox 381YE when it [vanished overnight](../../winds-of-fortune/Foggy_mountain_top.md#the-halls-of-knowledge) in an eerie fog bank. The majority of the faculty, students, and researchers had evacuated but a small number of them were left unaccounted for including Provost Octavius of the spire of the Auric Horizon. Two months later, on the last night of the full moon before the Winter Solstice, the Halls of Knowledge reappeared in Morrow, on the shores of one of the largest hot springs of [Peregro](Morrow.md#peregro). None of the buildings appeared to be any the worse for wear for the two months they had spent... elsewhere. The unaccounted for scholars, however, remained unaccounted for.

Shortly after its singular disappearance and reappearance, the college was destroyed by the invading Druj, its libraries burnt and many of its scholars killed or enslaved by the rampaging orcs.

### Sunspire (ruined)

This spire watched the forests of Therunin as much as it did the skies and heavens. If any outside Navarr understood the vallorn, it was the Urizen of Sunspire. Navarri vates were known to visit here to exchange news and knowledge. This all changed shortly after the Summer Solstice 386YE when a [force of Druj](../../winds-of-war/Ashes.md#twilight-morrow) from Therunin looted what books and scrolls they could before burning the rest of the spire.

### The Heliopticon

A network of communication devices using bronze mirrors, which have been maintained and used by Urizen since time out of mind.

## Regions

### Altis

- **Quality:** Hills

At the northernmost border of Urizen is the region of Altis, home to the citadel of [Highwatch](#highwatch) and known for particularly rich [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) [mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md). Many of the caves within the mountains here possess unique, resonant acoustics and several spires are positioned specifically to study or make use of these properties.  
  
The **Spire of the Canticle** is perhaps the most famous of these [spires](Spire.md). Known for producing some of the most beautiful, haunting, and eerie music in the modern Empire, their finest works must be enjoyed in situ as they incorporate the esoteric acoustic effects provided by the echoing caves. Over many years the Spire of the Canticle has "fine tuned" the properties of the caves, creating the *Cathedral Cavern* deep within the cold stone of the mountains. A premier performing venue in the eastern Empire, those who know how to exploit the unique acoustics can produce truly remarkable musical performances. Unsurprisingly, Altis is a common destination for lovers of music and poetry from across the Empire.

Numerous stories speak of crystals brought out from the deeper tempest jade mines that possess remarkable properties for storing and reproducing music created in their presence. These *songstones* are much sought after, but rarely mined. Experiments have shown that their magic is much stronger if they are left where they are found, and several chambers have been hollowed out around them to exploit their properties. Many of these caverns have (perhaps coincidentally) become strong [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) connected to the realms of Day, Night, and occasionally Summer.

### Caeli

- **Quality:** Hills

The towering peaks of Caeli are not the tallest in Urizen - those are in Zenith - but their beauty is unparalleled. On a clear day, an observer can gaze across the entire territory. The **Seat of the Arbiter** is a unique magical location that takes advantage of this property; a carved stone throne at the heart of a powerful regio tied to the realm of Day. Any magician who sits in the chair can gain a bird's eye view of any location in Morrow. The **Spire of the Arbiter's Seat** protected and oversaw the regio and its divinatory treasure until it was captured by the Druj invaders shortly before the Autumn Equinox 382YE. The magicians here were well-known for being excellent hosts and inveterate gossips.

On the tallest mountain in Caeli once stood the tower heart of the entire Heliopticon network and the spires and citadels that were devoted to its study, function, maintenance and protection. The attendants at the tower chose to destroy it in an invoked devastation, probably with the aid of the eternal [Kimus](../../eternals/Kimus.md), rather than let it fall into the hands of the Druj.  
  
The **Gardens of Morrow** once stood on the lower slopes of Caeli; like the [Gardens of Pallas](../../../mixed/titles/Head_Gardener_of_Urizen.md#the-gardens-of-pallas) before them, they were looted and then burnt to the ground by the Druj.

Between the Heliopticon heart and the Seat of the Arbiter, it is no surprise that Caeli had more than its fair share of magicians with have an interest in communication, magical and mundane. As such, these spires are not for those whose study needs demand quiet contemplation or solitude. Sadly these magicians were forced to flee ahead of the Druj advance leaving their precious library of divination and magical theory behind to the merciless orcs.

### Operus

- **Quality:** Hills

This region boasts some of the oldest spires in Urizen, including **The Weave**. Like its newer sister spire the tragic **Arch of the Sky** that once stood in Zenith, this spire is dedicated to the study of the [Net of the Heavens](Net_of_the_Heavens.md). The [seers](Urizen_magical_traditions.md#seers) and [stargazers](Urizen_magical_traditions.md#stargazers) of the Arch have a reputation for radical interpretations of this key Urizen philosophy. They are credited as being the source of the school of thought that believes that places as well as people and events act as nodes in the net, and that particularly significant locations could be identified through [study of the stars](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md).  
  
Operus is also the site of the **Auric Horizon**, an influential spire known for its expertise with the divinations of both Day and Night. Politically active, the spire is occasionally linked with [heretical philosophies](../../../mixed/government/synod/Religious_crime.md#heresy) that have caused friction with the [Synod](../../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) in the past. The alleged "prophet" (or charlatan, depending on who one asks) [Abraxus Whitespire](../../history/Abraxus.md) spent his last years here, and it is said to have been where he wrote the Abraxian prophecies. In the centuries since, successive firebrands and especially [ambitious](../../religion/Ambition.md) mages have secured Auric Horizon's reputation for involvement (and occasionally interference) in Imperial affairs.

The [Canterspire Circle](../../../mixed/titles/Canterspire_Circle.md), a rich weirwood forest, grows on the lower slopes of Operus. One of the richest spires in Urizen, the **Spire of Golden Wood** overlooks the forest, and owns some of the richest [farms](../../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) and beggarwood [forests](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) in Urizen. They are widely known both for their fine quality mutton, and for the delicate yellow dyes they prepare using the heather that grows in great profusion around their homes. Indeed, many of the spires of Operus are known for a signature dye, or type of leather.

### Peregro

Peregro is one of the most volatile regions of Morrow, known for several hot springs and even a few superheated geysers. Several spires harness the waters to provide heating or make a study of the complex currents that run beneath the surface of the earth. Magicians theorise that there is a great deal of subterranean activity in this region, and predict that one day - probably far in the future - they might lead to a serious disaster - perhaps even a volcanic eruption.

After the Summer Solstice 382YE, the Druj invaded across the southern border from Zenith crushing and enslaving everything in their path. They destroyed the [Glorious Fountain of Dawn and Dusk](../../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Glorious_Fountain.md), tearing it apart and permanently damaging the pool above which it stood. At the same time, they also destroyed the [Halls of Knowledge](#the-halls-of-knowledge-ruined), which briefly stood on the shores of the largest hot lake here after its [mysterious translocation](../../winds-of-fortune/Foggy_mountain_top.md#the-halls-of-knowledge) from [Zenith](Zenith.md) shortly before the Winter Solstice 381YE.

### Ravion

A remarkably flat region of western Urizen, Morrow is home to a number of spires and citadels including the citadel of Phoenix Reach. It is also the location of the [Caves of the Claw](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Claw.md#the-caves-of-the-claw). The [Bleak Tower](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Bleak_Tower.md#the-bleak-tower) stands in north-eastern Ravion, a spire composed of displaced herbalists previously associated with the Gardens of Pallas in [Zenith](Zenith.md), and joined by herbalists fleeing the devastated Gardens of Morrow. The [Custodian](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Bleak_Tower.md) liaises with the spire and protects the unique [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) that stands at its heart.
