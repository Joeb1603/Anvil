# Zenith

*[Image: Regions of Zenith](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RegionsofZenith.png)*

## Overview

The mountains of Zenith are the tallest in the nation, and perhaps the Empire, and the air here is still and cold. The spires tend towards the contemplative, the night sky is rarely concealed by clouds making the territory perfect for [stargazers](Stargazer.md). Many religious thinkers and philosophers also reside in Zenith, where they claim that the rarefied air helps them maintain a sense of detachment and clarity.

Zenith is well known for its abundant magical auras and many spires have experimented with arrays of metal mirrors that focus the light of the sun into internal chambers to try to enhance the production of magical crystals. Zenith also has some of the most beautiful waterfalls in the Nation, with apparently bottomless mountain lakes providing succulent fish to supplement the diet of the inhabitants.

Somewhere to the east of Zenith lies the nation of [Axos](../../foreign-nations/Axos.md). Between the two nations is a mountainous area that the Urizen call the [Mountains of the Moon](../../foreign-nations/Mountains_of_the_Moon.md). Explorers claimed that the area was impassable, but there were always stories of hidden or hard to find passes that allowed access to an unknown [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) that lay within. While the war with the [Druj](../../barbarians/Druj.md) still raged, several bands of scouts risked the high passes from [Spiral](../the-league/Spiral.md) to scout the Mountains of the Moon - and following the Summer Solstice 382YE were the first Imperials to become aware of the people of [Tsark](../../foreign-nations/Tsark.md).

## Recent History

A month after the Spring Equinox 381YE, the [Druj](../../barbarians/Druj.md) barbarians [invaded](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#valley-of-darkness-28zenith29) Zenith from [the Mallum](../../barbarians/The_Mallum.md). The initial attack swept the unprepared defenders away, and saw the destruction of the powerful spire of Endsmeet. [Lustri](#lustri) and [Occursion](#occcursion) fell to the orcs in short order.

Only a [brave defence](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#resolve-28zenith29) by the three Highborn armies - the Seventh Wave, the Granite Pillar, and the Valiant Pegasus - stopped the Druj from conquering the entire territory. The Druj responded with [blackest malice](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#hunting-by-the-rivers), unleashing a [marshwalker](../../world/Marshwalkers.md) against the high mountain dam supplying water to the Golden Cascade. The resulting catastrophic flood ruined the fertile plains of [Proceris](#proceris) and forced the defenders back west to a thin sliver of higher ground. At the same time, the Druj destroyed numerous [sinecures](../../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md), and burnt several prominent spires including the [Arch of the Sky](#arch-of-the-sky-ruins).

After the Winter Solstice 381YE, the Druj [launched a major assault](../../winds-of-war/382YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#cascade-28zenith29) against the defenders of Zenith. They pushed the Highborn westward inexorably, eventually driving the three armies out of the territory entirely and claiming it for the Mallum. Only the fact the [Halls of Knowledge](#halls-of-knowledge) had been moved under mysterious circumstances a few months previously prevented them from capturing (and presumably destroying) it. The [Gardens of Pallas](../../../mixed/titles/Head_Gardener_of_Urizen.md#the-gardens-of-pallas) however were not so fortunate; the Druj looted and then burnt them to the ground.

Apart from a few isolated spires hidden beneath veils of [Night magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md), much of the population of Zenith has been displaced. Refugees fled to Redoubt and Morrow, while those who remained behind were enslaved by the Druj. The loss of life in Zenith was much smaller than it could have been - the courageous defence of the territory by the Highborn armies allowed many who might otherwise have fallen into the hands of the eastern orcs to escape that dread fate.

Following the Winter Solstice 382YE, a daring raid was launched into Zenith by a combination of Urizen sentinels, Highborn soldiers, heralds, and Imperial heroes from several nations. Its aim was to free as many prisoners from the Druj as possible. The raid was a [resounding success](../../winds-of-war/383YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#zenith) and almost two-thirds of those trapped in Zenith were liberated including over seven hundred Highborn soldiers and more than two hundred sentinels who later went on to join the Citadel Guard.

## Major Features

*[Image: The Halls of Knowledge expand the Empire's understanding of magical lore.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Medea_Ruth.jpg)*

### Crucible of Fate

The vast foundry in [Iteri](#iteri) allows the artisans and architects of Urizen to work together to forge a new fate for Zenith, for Urizen, and for the Empire. The construction was overseen by the [Master of the Koboldi](../../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md), and the Concordium of Pallas and their allies invoked powerful Autumn magic to ensure the work was completed more quickly than anyone had predicted. Now it is overseen by the [Architect of the Crucible](../../../mixed/titles/Architect_of_the_Crucible.md) who guides the construction of bespoke ushabti to [support](../../../mixed/titles/Architect_of_the_Crucible.md#miracle-and-wonder) Urizen holdings.

The most recent addition to the Crucible of Fate is the famous **Damakan’s Forge**. Damakan was renowned as the greatest Urizen swordsmith of antiquity, and even today her blades change hands for small fortunes. Although many of her secrets went with her to her grave, a small lodge of smiths maintained her forge and traditions in its original site in Spiral before it was sacked by the Grendel. A small number artisans returned to the forge when Spiral was reclaimed in 380YE and came with it when it [was moved](../../winds-of-fortune/The_fate_of_the_many.md#the-wyrm27s-bottle) by [Sadogua](../../eternals/Sadogua.md) after the Summer Solstice 385YE.

### Bridge of the Air

The [Bridge of the Air](Morrow.md#bridge-of-the-air) was constructed in [Morrow](Morrow.md) to help transport [white granite](../../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md), but the mithril cables and lifts also spread through the adjoining territories to create a transport network that has greatly improved travel particularly in northern Redoubt. A true wonder, the Bridge transports people and trade goods between spires. Lifts help transport travellers and cargo from the lowlands to the highlands spires, and conventional roads and bridges have been employed where they are most useful. The *skyways* are concentrated in the mountainous regions, but the roads included as part of their construction have spread through eastern and southern Zenith as well greatly improving the ability of travellers and traders to move across the territory.

### [Arch of the Sky](../../../mixed/titles/Arch_of_the_Sky.md)

Before the Druj invasion of 381YE, the Arch of the Sky was a collection of spires on the tallest mountain in Zenith. Stargazers and [astronomancers](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md) from all over the Empire gathered here to observe the night skies, and debate the [Net of the Heavens](Net_of_the_Heavens.md). One of the most prestigious schools of astronomancy, it was also one of the finest observatories in Urizen, if not the Empire.

The flood waters unleashed by the [destruction of the Golden Cascade](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#hunting-by-the-rivers) did not reach the spire itself of course, but even as the dam was being smashed apart Druj guerrillas were moving into position. Many of the astronomancers and star-watchers had already retreated, but a handful of magicians and sentinels remained behind in the hope of holding the [orc](../../../mixed/world/Orc.md) invaders at bay. Such hope was in vain. Less than a week after the Autumn Equinox, the eastern orcs captured and looted the Arch of the Sky. What they could not carry away was reduced to ashes and broken ruins.

This all changed when the Senate voted to [construct](../../senate-motions/Construct_Roads_of_the_Sky.md) the [Bridge of the Air](#bridge-of-the-air). Part of the work involved removing the ruins scattered across the formerly tallest mountains in Zenith and delving deep to quarry precious [white granite](../../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md) from the mountain.

The Arch of the Sky is now a national [Bourse seat](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) which produces white granite. Control of the Arch of the Sky is assigned by [Tally of the Votes](../../../mixed/economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) of Urizen owners of [mana sites](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md) at the Summer Solstice each year. This Bourse position produces 18 wains of white granite every season.

### Endsmeet

This spire in south-western [Occursion](#occursion) studied interaction between people, and had particular interest in times of political turmoil in the Empire. It was home to refugees from Spiral, particularly those who brought with them the great library of [Ankarien](../the-league/Spiral.md#ankarien). Sadly, when the Druj invaded they quickly overwhelmed the spire. Many of the Urizen living there managed to escape, but the library was burned and those who did not flee were either killed or enslaved by the ruthless eastern orcs.

### Bountiful Autumn

The lakes of Zenith are deep, but of particular interest are the two bodies of water known together as "Bountiful Autumn". Found in [Occursion](#occursion), their shores were once regarded as a particularly fortuitous place to marry for the success of the new family. Although modern Urizen have found little support for these claims, it is worth noting that the lakes lie within a strong [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) tied to the Autumn realm. To this day, it is still traditional to eat fish from the lakes at a wedding, or to present them (fresh or cooked) as gifts to particularly close friends. Ritual magic used to create bonds between individuals is said to be especially potent when performed at these lakes, or using pure water gathered from their depths at certain specific times of year (the exact times are closely guarded secrets shared only by the spires that stand on the mountainsides above the lakes).

### The Stork's Gaze (Ruined)

Work on this small [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in the forests of [Lustri](#lustri) was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 387YE. Commissioned by the Imperial Senate [two years earlier](../../senate-motions/Fortify_Lustri.md), there was initially an [offer](../../winds-of-fortune/Write_your_own_ending.md#a-border-27tween-the-mountains-and-the-moon) from the folk of [Tsark](../../foreign-nations/Tsark.md) to help complete it, but the Senate chose not to take advantage of it. The fortification guarded the routes into and out of Zenith from [the Mallum](../../barbarians/The_Mallum.md), keeping a careful watch on the border with the [Sarangrave](Sarangrave.md) to the north. A single high tower was staffed at all times by keen-eyed soldiers equipped with a large spyglass built with remarkable lenses imported from [the Brass Coast](../the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) referred to as "the Kyerglass" in reference to [one of the stars](../../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Stork.md#stars-of-the-stork) of the [constellation](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md#constellations) that gave the fortification [its name](../../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Stork.md).

The [sentinels](Sentinel.md) who garrisoned the towers quickly established a reputation for being a little odd - anyone who spent more than a few nights in the fort began to experience especially vivid dreams. They were not threatening and didn't appear to be dangerous, but they were memorable. Even folk who do not usually remember their dreams could recall with clarity strange phantasmagorical visions that came to them when they sleep. It was assumed to likely be a side effect of the [Night magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) that spreads quietly under the trees of Lustri, a region known for its [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) aligned with the [Forests of the Night](../../eternals/Realm.md#night).

The fortification was overrun and destroyed by the Druj during their [invasion](../../winds-of-war/Root_and_branch.md#dark-beneath-the-boughs) of Lustri following the Spring Equinox 387YE.

### Spire of Twisting Shadows (Ruined)

The [spire](Spire.md) was an ancient structure dedicated to the treatment (and, functionally, incarceration) of those suffering long term spiritual or mental damage due to exposure to magic. Most of the inmates were there voluntarily, but a few were restrained for their own safety. The spire had a grim reputation, unsurprisingly. A significant portion of the patients here had been hurt due to overuse or misuse of [astronomancy](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md) - permanently twisted by exposure to a tulpa. The [physicks](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) of the Spire of Twisting Shadows had a great deal of practical experience dealing with magical [curses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) as well as more esoteric maladicaments, and were always interested in reports of new malign enchantments - or the opportunity to study them.

In Summer 380YE, during the ascent of the [red star](../../winds-of-fortune/Red_star.md), a riot erupted among the patients that led to the destruction of the spire. The ruins are widely believed to be haunted by the ghosts of the staff and inmates who died in its fall.

The herb conservatory of [Origen's Rest](../../senate-motions/Construct_conservatory_for_the_Anvil_Hospital.md) stood near the Spire, and was largely untouched by the destruction inspired by the Red Star. Sadly, it was not untouched by the Druj invasion and in Autumn 381YE confirmation came that the Druj had looted and destroyed it, robbing the [Anvil Hospital & Imperial School of Medicine](../../world/Anvil.md#the-anvil-hospital) of a valuable source of medicinal herbs.

## Regions

*[Image: A very rough region map of Zenith](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Regions_of_Zenith.png)*

### Clypion

- **Quality:** Hills

The passes between Clypion and [Proceris](#proceris) house a cluster of spires keen to take advantage of the relative seclusion of their foothills. The spires are all "related" - formed initially by settlers originating in the nearby [Arch of the Sky](#arch-of-the-sky), and later by successive generations gathered from among these daughter-spires as their populations grew. The spires of the "Clypion Alliance" maintain cordial relations, and each tends to specialise in study of impractical esoterica. Critics suggest that the [mages](Urizen_magical_traditions.md#mages) of Clypion see practical applications of theory or magic as somehow beneath them. There have been spires devoted to the study of boundaries, of ageing, of ceremonies, of symbols, and perhaps most recursively, to the study of the studies of other spires...

Several of the older spires are starting to feel a little crowded, and it is likely that a new spire will be formed within the next few years - there is likely to be fierce competition between the Clypion spires to determine the location and focus of this new spire. The [Halls of Knowledge](../../../mixed/titles/Provost_of_the_Halls_of_Knowledge.md) once stood high in the mountains of Clypion. One of the greatest Imperial [colleges](../../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) dedicated to the study of magic, it attracted students, lecturers, and magical theoreticians from across the Empire. Shortly after the Autumn Equinox 381YE, however, [the college vanished](../../winds-of-fortune/Foggy_mountain_top.md#the-halls-of-knowledge). It reappeared shortly before the Winter Solstice, relocated to the volcanic pools of [Peregro](Morrow.md#peregro) in [Morrow](Morrow.md). The Provost at the time, Octavius of the Auric Horizon, disappeared along with the college - and did not reappear when the buildings were relocated. He returned several months later, none the worse for his experience, with no memory of the intervening time.

### Iteri

The plains of Iteri in western Zenith are home to the river Utique, a tributary of the Couros which flows from [Redoubt](Redoubt.md) in the south. The land here is rich in fine clay and valuable [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) deposits, both of which contribute to a reputation for producing beautiful ceramics. Iteri also boasted the ancient **Spire Calator** near the foot of **Mount Lutum**, where the secrets of [ushabti](Urizen_economic_interests.md#ushabti) production are believed to have first been codified. The [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) of Urizen once gathered here to study and perfect the craft of these valuable magical constructs. The spire was known as much for its art as for the practical application of the [artificer's craft](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md). Some [mages](Urizen_magical_traditions.md#mages) sent their students to Spire Calator specifically to study the arts of formal debate at this famously argumentative spire. An ongoing debate between those who favoured artistic form over practical function that had been going on for nearly 150 years without any clear victor ended abruptly when the Druj conquered the spire toward the end of 381YE. Most of the inhabitants were able to escape, thanks to the valiant action of Highborn soldiers.

Along the banks of the Couros stood the [Gardens of Pallas](../../../mixed/titles/Head_Gardener_of_Urizen.md) [sinecure](../../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md). The Druj looted the gardens of their herbs, smashed the ushabti gardeners, and then burnt everything in a grand bonfire the smoke from which was visible in both Morrow and Redoubt.

### Lustri

- **Quality:** Forest

Lustri is heavily forested, and was once home to many [carefully cultivated preserves](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md). Known both for the quality of its [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) and the [beggarwood](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) trees that provide wood, bark, and lye to artisans across Urizen, the deeper parts of the woods have a bad reputation. Following the [magical storms](../../winds-of-fortune/Light_upon_the_water.md#sodden-ground) of late 384YE, the northern forests in the region are significantly more marshy than they used to be.

There are many stories of encounters between travellers and dangerous creatures such as the [tulpas](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md#tulpas) said to have escaped from the [Spire of Twisting Shadows](#spire-of-twisting-shadows-ruined), and peculiar [visitors](../../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md) from the [Forests of the Night](../../eternals/Realm.md#night). There are known to be a large number of [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) aligned with the realm of Night, that give the woodlands here a reputation for mystery and strangeness. As such a number of spires dedicated to the study of Night lore, or of mysteries and secrets, stand among the foothills and on the peaks to the east and west of the forest proper.

### Occursion

- **Quality:** Hills

More than any other region in Zenith, Occursion was home to spires dedicated to religious philosophy and many [illuminates](Urizen_religious_beliefs.md#illuminates) and [questors](Urizen_religious_beliefs.md#illuminates) had their homes here. The northernmost slopes of these mountains enjoyed a particularly still, cold, dry air which was believed to aid contemplation, and focus the mind. Priests here practiced meditative arts and subtle magics that allowed them to explore their own thoughts, and often receive unique insights into the natures of the virtues and the philosophies of [the Way](../../world/The_Way.md). The [Tower of the Fall](../../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Tower_of_the_Fall.md#the-tower-of-the-fall) was believed to have been built as a retreat for just such an exploration of the inner magic of the spirit. Sadly, the Tower was a casualty of the Druj invasion. Along with the beautiful [Starlight Drift of Shimmering Snow](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Starlight_Drift.md#the-starlight-drift-of-shimmering-snow), it was [torn apart by the rapacious orcs](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#hunting-by-the-rivers).

Shortly after the Spring Equinox 387YE, tremors began to be felt across the region, as Mount Polus [began to rise](../../winds-of-fortune/Sound_and_vision.md#a-mountain-rises-zenith). The tremors continued for much of the season, stopping only shortly before the Summer Solstice, and leaving a crevasse near the base of the mountain that tempts the adventurous sentinels nearby. The [Narwhal's Spear](../../../mixed/government/military/Narwhal-s_Spear.md), the [Seventh Wave](../../../mixed/government/military/Seventh_Wave.md), or the [Wolves of War](../../../mixed/government/military/Wolves_of_War.md) (hiring the appropriate mercenaries), or an Imperial army enchanted with [Bound by Common Cause](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Bound_by_Common_Cause.md) could take a defensive order to fully explore the crevasse. The general would likely receive a sample of what might be found in the crevasse, and the civil service would publish a report of what was uncovered and how best it might be exploited for the Empire. This opportunity is available until the situation changes. During the Winter Solstice 387YE, the Senate voted to [rename](../../senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#rename-mount-polus) Mount Polus to *Kespoena's Reach*, after the astronomancer for whom that mountain was raised. Most of the citizens of Occursion have taken up the new name, but there are still those who steadfastly refuse to give up the old name.

Of particular note were the famous spire of [Endsmeet](#endsmeet), and the **Hall of the Risen Sun**, a beautiful edifice of white granite, polished marble, and [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) mirrors which serves as a great cathedral for the faithful of Zenith (and Urizen as a whole). Dedicated to the virtues of [Pride](../../religion/Pride.md) and [Ambition](../../religion/Ambition.md), the Illuminates combined study of the Way with mastery of the [Net of the Heavens](Net_of_the_Heavens.md) and were known to be quite active in political affairs of the [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md), nation, and (from time to time) the Empire itself. Since the reign of [Emperor Walter](../../history/Emperor_Walter.md) they had been somewhat reticent about involving themselves in wider affairs - but during the reign of the late [Empress Britta](../../history/Empress_Britta.md) there were signs this spire was beginning to pay close attention to affairs of the [Synod](../../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) and [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) once more. Both spires were captured by the Druj during the 381YE invasion, and those Urizen who did not manage to flee are presumed either dead or enslaved.

### Proceris

- **Quality:** Marsh, Ruined, [Nightwoven](../../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#nightwoven)

Much of the region of Proceris lies in the shadows of the lofty peaks that surround it. While there were few spires here, there were settlements devoted to what little agriculture the Urizen and their [ushabti](Urizen_economic_interests.md#ushabti) could muster in this chilly climate. Proceris actually had a fascinating microclimate for those that are interested in such matters, with many frost hollows and sun traps that provided unique opportunities to the spires that lived here. The region was sparsely populated, even for Urizen, and with the exception of the [Arch of the Sky](Zenith.md#arch-of-the-sky) what spires there were tended to explore somewhat mundane, practical matters such as farming and economy. [Architects](Urizen_economic_interests.md#architects) in particular often found reasons to visit or live in Proceris, and many of the magicians here focused their studies on the secrets of Spring and Autumn magic.

Perhaps the richest farmers in Urizen lived in Proceris, three spires that between them maintained and worked the **Golden Cascade** - a series of [terraces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrace_%28agriculture%29) carved into the foothills and supported both with powerful ritual magic, expertly constructed aqueducts, and great polished mirrors that ensured that the fields here received as much sunlight as possible all year round. The Golden Cascade was seen by many as a wonder of the Empire, and while it had been copied by a few other spires, there was no match for the size and fertility of this great farm anywhere in Urizen. Sadly, when the Druj came in 381YE they saw the Golden Cascade not as a wonder but as an opportunity. In the autumn of that year they unleashed a [marshwalker](../../world/Marshwalkers.md) against the dam that channelled water into the aqueducts. The [catastrophic flood](../../winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#hunting-by-the-rivers) that followed devastated the Golden Cascade, ruined the agricultural spires, destroyed hundreds of ushabti, and drowned scores of farmers. The tragic loss of the Golden Cascade had dire significance for the territory; most of its agriculture lost in one fell swoop. During the Winter Solstice 384YE the Imperial Senate commissioned the [reconstruction](../../senate-motions/Reconstruct_Golden_Cascade.md) of the terraces and the repairs were completed shortly before the Summer Solstice 385YE.

The [Storm Vault](../../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Storm_Vault.md#the-storm-vault), high in the mountains above Echostorm Spire, was another casualty of the Druj invasion, torn apart for its mana and valuable mithril.

During the Winter Solstice 381YE, the Druj undertook esoteric action including the creation of several [miasma pillars](../../barbarians/Druj_miasma.md#miasma-pillars) that began transforming the entire region into a stinking morass of marsh and swampland.

In southern Proceris stands one of the [two mithril serpent statues](../../winds-of-fortune/The_fate_of_the_many.md#the-serpents-edge) that connect the Urizen living in Spiral to their siblings in the rest of their nation. Using the power of [Sung](../../eternals/Sung.md) they allow the [spires](Spire.md) to the south to maintain their metaphysical connection to the rest of Urizen provided they draw on the power of the [Vale of Shadows](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Vale_of_Shadows.md). If the statue were damaged or destroyed, that connection might well be permanently severed.

## OOC Notes

- As of the start of the Autumn Equinox 387YE, the Urizen (and by extension the Empire) control the entire territory. Zenith is an Urizen territory.
- Any attempt to build a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in flooded [Proceris](#proceris) are likely to encounter additional costs and difficulties.
- [Farms](../../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) in the territory benefit from the Golden Cascade and produce three drams of random [herbs](../../../mixed/magic/potions/Herb.md) each season.
- [Herb gardens](../../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) in the territory benefit from the Golden Cascade and the Gardens of Pallas producing 36 rings and three drams of random herbs each season.
- [Mana sites](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md) in the territory benefit from the Bridge of the Air and produce an extra mana crystal each season.
- The [Stork's Gaze](#the-storks-gaze-ruined) was a rank one [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in [Lustri](#lustri)
- As of the start of the Summer Solstice 385YE, there are no more miasma pillars in Zenith.
