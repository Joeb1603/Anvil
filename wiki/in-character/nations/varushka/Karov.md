# Karov

*[Image: Rich heartland of the nation](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RegionsofKarov.png)*

## Overview

The rich heartland of the nation, and the wealthiest territory in terms of timber and amber, this is where most outsiders think of when Varushka is mentioned. The vales here exist among rich forests, where they have not been cleared to make way for small farms. Karov has the largest towns in Varushka, and the majority of its boyars come from this relatively civilised territory. A great deal of trade happens in Karov, with wagon trains and river barges carrying raw materials south to [Temeschwar](../the-league/Temeschwar.md) or [Weirwater](../dawn/Weirwater.md) and north to more isolated valleys who are in need.

Despite its settled nature, it would be a mistake to think the land is entirely tamed and great tracts of wilderness still remain, often home to particularly malignant – if quiescent – [sovereigns](Varushkan_Monster.md). These creatures awaken or emerge from time to time with often tragic consequences - so the [wise ones](Varushka_religious_beliefs.md) take care to ensure each generation remembers the warning stories about creatures whose last depredations may have been in the times of their grandparents or great-grandparents.

Karov was one of the first places settled by the Vard during their early conflict with the Ushka. They ruthlessly cleared areas of forests to build their settlements and some of the oldest towns show clear influence of their shared heritage with the [Steinr](../wintermark/Steinr.md) of Wintermark, especially in the west. Indeed, there are several old battlefields in western Karov where Vard and Steinr, and later Varushkan and Wintermark, clashed in the distant past long before the foundation of the Empire. A number of very old tales from Karov include two siblings who romance a stranger; the former represent Vard and Steinr, the latter the Ushka. Invariably the elder sibling wins the affection of the stranger, and the younger angrily retreats to the west. This is a highly stylised and historically dubious version of the founding of Varushka, but it is popular nonetheless.

On the whole, the folk of Karov enjoy many of the finer things in life - good food, good drink and regular festivals. Many towns and villages in Karov employ [stzena](../../../mixed/music/Stzena.md), local musicians who perform at local civic events and celebrations; there are several vales here dedicated specifically to the training of stzena, and recording and preserving the music and songs of the Varushkan people.

## Recent History

Karov is perhaps the most peaceful of the Varushkan territories. Until [Karsk](Karsk.md) fell to the [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md), they had enjoyed three and a half centuries of peace. In 372YE the Thule attempted to push through [Malimorzi](#malimorzi) and briefly occupied the settlement of Korotny, but they were driven back within a season by the combined forces of the [Northern Eagle](Varushka_military_concerns.md#the-army-of-the-northern-eagle) and [Golden Axe](Varushka_military_concerns.md#the-army-of-the-golden-axe). Raiding parties still creep across the border from time to time, but for the most part Karov has been unmolested.

Shortly before the Winter Solstice 381YE, the [Blood Red Roads](../the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#blood-red-roads) were completed, connecting [Delev](#delev) to the rest of the Empire and allowing easier access to Varushkan goods for Imperial merchants and traders. It also provided a means for Varushkan travellers to reach some of the wealthiest cities across the Empire. For more than half a decade the roads provided a major boon to the [farms](../../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) and [businesses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) of the territory. In the Winter of 386YE, however, the Imperial Senate determined to [levy tolls](../../senate-sessions/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#levy-tolls-on-blood-red-roads) on traffic using the road, meaning they no longer increase the income of individual citizens but provide a significant boost to the Imperial Treasury, in part to pay for the war against the [vallorn](../navarr/Vallorn.md).

In late 385YE, the *scions* of the [eternal](../../eternals/Eternal.md) known as [Cold Sun](../../eternals/Cold_Sun.md) [attacked Karov](../../winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-blade-of-skies). The *Blade of Skies* emerged from a [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) in the village of Bulgakov, and began a series of destructive raids across the southern territory, razing the entire vale of Olvanshka in the process. They left [Delev](#delev) suspiciously untouched, and fears grew that they were intentionally allowing refugees to flee to the small city with the intent of murdering them all once they were gathered in one place. Indeed in the Spring of 386YE, the scions and Varushkan defenders clashed before the settlement in the *[Battle of the Weirwater Gate](../../winds-of-war/Eclipse.md#blade-of-skies)*. The [Northern Eagle](../../../mixed/government/military/Northern_Eagle.md), [Quiet Step](../../../mixed/government/military/Quiet_Step.md), and [Iron Helms](../../../mixed/government/military/Iron_Helms.md) fought to defend Delev and in the end were victorious. A devastating explosion of uncontrolled [force](../../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md#force) that would likely have obliterated Delev and everyone behind its walls had the Imperial heroes not disrupted their scheme.

Parts of southern Karov remain marked by the assault of the *scions*, scattered with areas of dangerous black glass where nothing grows. Even more troubling is the matter of the bees - they fled Duzekani *en masse*. While these "heralds of Prosperity" buzz happily around the blooms of Skoremujac and Kamienczka, and across the rest of Varushka, the meadows and woodlands of Duzekani remain ominously silent.

## Major Features

### Delev

The oldest and proudest of the vales that guard the road from [the Semmerlak](The_Semmerlak.md) to [Seren](../navarr/Miaren.md#seren) and the south, Delev is a hub for the many smaller vales dotted around southern Varushka. It has a complex rivalry with Temeschwar, which outgrew its parent and then left the family to join the [League](../the-league/The_League.md). Delev is again the foremost trading town in Karov, which suits it well. Despite the intervening centuries, there is still some bad blood between the citizens of Delev and those of Varushkan descent in Temeschwar. A particular bone of contention are the [Eternal Shafts of Time](#the-eternal-shafts-of-time), which the Temeschwari attempted to "take with them" when they seceded. Several Karov families at least keep alive tales of treachery, ingratitude and attempted theft.

In early 377YE a sequence of fortified watchtowers and walled camp sites was erected around Delev, and in various strategic points along the road to [Seren](../navarr/Miaren.md), serving as secure bases of operation for bounty-hunters and [wardens](Warden.md) concerned with both bandit and [wolves](Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves) alike. Overseen by **Henry Ward**, as Senator for [Upwold](../the-marches/Upwold.md), as part of a larger [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) initiative to [provide protection](../../senate-motions/Fortify_multiple_towns.md) to towns throughout the Empire, the defences were not sufficient to qualify Delev as a fortification but have already helped reduce casual outlawry throughout the territory. These were later expanded and given the name [the Little Boyar's Welcome](#the-little-boyars-welcome)

Shortly before the Autumn equinox 380YE, work was completed on the offices of the [Overseer of the Westward Road](../../../mixed/titles/Overseer_of_the_Westward_Road.md), a [ministry](../../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) dedicated to coordinating trade between western Varushka and the orcs of [Skarsind](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md). It forms the eastern arm of the [Northern trade network](../navarr/Northern_trade_network.md).

Delev was also the site of the climactic battle against the *Blade of Skies*, an army composed of the *scions* of the [eternal](../../eternals/Eternal.md) known as [Cold Sun](../../eternals/Cold_Sun.md). The  *[Battle of the Weirwater Gate](../../winds-of-war/Eclipse.md#blade-of-skies)* saw the [Northern Eagle](../../../mixed/government/military/Northern_Eagle.md), [Quiet Step](../../../mixed/government/military/Quiet_Step.md), and [Iron Helms](../../../mixed/government/military/Iron_Helms.md) defended Delev, leading to the destruction of the servants of Oblivion. The battle ended in a devastating explosion of uncontrolled [force](../../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md#force) that would likely have obliterated Delev and everyone behind its walls had the Imperial heroes not disrupted their scheme.

### The Little Boyar's Welcome

Following the almost ruinous attack of *scions* on Delev, a timely opportunity to fortify the region was taken advantage of. The senator of Karov, **Myshki Marishka Letuchaya** (also known to some as the Little Boyar of Levski), [raised a motion](../../senate-motions/Fortify_Delev.md) to complete a long-forgotten building project to fortify the area. The fortification is a testament not just to its benefactor and patron, nor to the Imperial armies who saved Delev from certain destruction, but also to the Karovian people’s love for their foremost trading town. Strong walls now surround the trade hub, protecting it from external threats.

A team of inspired crafters have adorned the outer wall of the fortification in a sprawling mural, inlaid with [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It depicts the Little Boyar herself; one hand holding aloft a lantern to outshine the false light of Cold Sun, the other hand sitting on the head of a loyal, mighty warhound at her heels. Above her soars a noble, protective eagle with its wings spread wide. The homage to the armies of the Iron Helms and the Northern Eagle is clear, representing the Courage and Loyalty of Varushka’s fighting force. A more subtle nod is given to the Quiet Step; the keen-eyed notice the Boyar leaves not one set of footprints behind her, but two. This mural was later supplemented with [mithril wards](../../senate-sessions/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#ward-the-little-boyars-welcome) designed to protect the town from the wolves of the forest, and other dark powers. This cemented Delev as a safe haven for those who had lost their homes during the attack of Cold Sun, or through the concedence of northern regions to the [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md).

The walls incorporate much of the former defence of the town. For example, great care was taken in salvaging the ruined Weirwater Gate. Pieces of twisted iron, charred wood, and stone gouged by weapons make up the new gate and entrance to the fortified town, serving as a stark warning to anyone who enters of the devastation endured and the lives lost. A plaque at the gate explains the story of the battle to visitors and lists as many names of the fallen as could be collected.

### The Weeping Stone

This is a great standing stone atop a mound in [Kamienczka](#kamienczka) near the border with Skarsind. The stone appears melted, like the wax of a candle, as if it once stood in a limestone cave. No grass will grow on the mound, and there are several stories of unnatural disappearances in the vicinity. According to legend, roads built in the vicinity of the Weeping Stone fall into ruin with preternatural speed, and any building that can be seen from the stone is likewise doomed to fall apart. The tales say that within three years no two stones will still lie side by side, and there are enough stories of collapsed bridges, unexpected raids and disastrous fires to support this belief.

In legends this locale is sometimes associated with the Sovereign known as the *Tallowman*, although this connection might simply be based on the way the "melted" appearance of the stone.

### The [Eternal Shafts of Time](../../../mixed/titles/Eternal_Shafts_of_Time.md)

The Eternal Shafts of Time are a Bourse resource located in the mountains near [Vorota](#wieliczka). Custodianship of the Shafts is an [Imperial Title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that brings with it a Seat on the [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). It produces 27 Imperial wains of mithril every season. The seat is allocated during the Autumn Equinox, to whichever Varushkan candidate receives the most support from Varushkan owners of [mine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forest](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) resources.

### The Iron Roads

A spur of the [Blood Red Roads](../the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#blood-red-roads) connects the town of Delev to the wider Empire, but the majority of the roads in Karov are the result of a grand building project completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 385YE. The Iron Roads were first proposed by the civil service [several years](../../winds-of-fortune/Starting_at_the_end.md#the-iron-roads-of-varushka) earlier. Much of the project involved maintaining and expanding the existing [Varushkan roads](Varushkan_Road.md), especially between Delev, Olvanshka, and Kolvy. The roads were laid out with a special emphasis on providing improved access to several of the less well-known vales. At the same time, a new road was constructed along the border with [Weirwater](../dawn/Weirwater.md), connecting Kolvy to the bustling port of Korotny. Korotny was already touched by the Crow Roads of Karsk, and the new roads allow significantly easier movement for Varushkan traders from the eastern territories into the rest of the Empire.

## Regions

### Duzekani

- **Quality:** Sun Burned

This lightly wooded region is where [Delev](#delev) - one of the major Varushkan trade hubs - can be found. Duzekani is well-known for the fine quality of its bees, as well as for some of the best [forests](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) for finding [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) in Varushka (the land is often too cold for the butterflies that produce the rare material to truly prosper). For the most part, however, the woods of Duzekani have been cleared or tamed - this is the civilised heart of the most civilised territory of Varushka.

Duzekani bore much of the brunt of the attack of [Cold Sun](../../eternals/Cold_Sun.md) in late 385YE and early 386YE. It remains, scattered with areas of dangerous black glass where nothing grows. Even more troubling is the matter of the bees - they fled Duzekani en masse. By the start of Spring 387YE, there were reports that these "heralds of Prosperity" that used to buzz happily around the blooms of Skoremujac and Kamienczka, and across the rest of Varushka, were [also disappearing](../../winds-of-fortune/Outside_the_wall.md#the-bee-question). The meadows fall silent, and it is a matter of great concern.

Around the ruined vale of Bulgakov are weirwood [warding stakes](../../senate-motions/Construct_Bulgakov_Memorial.md). Inlaid in white granite are scenes of the courageous resistance against the Cold Sun attack by the villagers and the heroic Dawnish knights-errant who came to their defence.

### Kamienczka

The least settled region of Karov, Kamienczka has a reputation for being an unfortunate place to be born. Most of the blame is aimed squarely at the [Weeping Stone](#the-weeping-stone) which stands in the foothills to the west. The only vale of any great size was **Olvanshka**, which was home to several [Warden](Warden.md) fellowships who stood guard on the Sovereigns believed to sleep in the hills to its north and west - especially keeping watch over the [Weeping Stone](#weeping-stone). In Winter 385YE, Olvanshka was [destroyed](../../winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-blade-of-skies) by the *scions* of [Cold Sun](../../eternals/Cold_Sun.md), burnt to ruins along with many of the wardens who fought in its defence.

### Malimorzi

The easternmost of the regions of Karov, and the one most in danger of invasion when Karsk was in the hands of the Thule. A great deal of trade comes through Malimorzi from other settlements along the shore of the Semmerlak. Much of that trade comes through the bustling port of **Korotny**, overseen by the [Harbourmaster of the Semmerlak](../../../mixed/titles/Harbourmaster_of_the_Semmerlak.md), built on the north-western shores of the great freshwater lake that allows trade with [Dawn](../dawn/Dawn.md) and [Holberg](../the-league/Holberg.md). It is also known for one of the largest fishing fleets in Varushka. Korotny is also known to be home to *Oksana Lyava Pinavora*, the spokesperson for the affiliation of wealthy Varushkans keep to [purchase](../../winds-of-fortune/Daylight_fading.md#the-honey-offer) the Imperial Bourse resources that are in Varushka, especially the [Hunt of Alderei the Fair](../../../mixed/titles/Hunt_of_Alderei_the_Fair.md).

Some of the more entrepreneurial minded inhabitants of Korotny dream of how their fortunes might rise if the [Barrens](../dawn/The_Barrens.md) was brought into the Empire and their vessels could trade the length of the southern rivers to [Therunin](../navarr/Therunin.md). From time to time, exploratory expeditions set forth to the eastern shore, scouting lands believed to be under the thrall of the [Druj](../../barbarians/Druj.md) or perhaps exotic foreigners. Some return with goods, tales, and a measure of pride. Most do not.

### Skoremujac

- **Quality:** Forest

Forested Skoremujac is a centre for fur-trapping and the production of both [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). Much of this industry revolves around **Kolvy**. The locals know well that wealth attracts [wolves](Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves) and the hunting is done subject to great care, attempting to leave as little disturbance to any slumbering horrors as can be. Today, Kolvy is primarily known outside of Karov as the site of the "last" major engagement between the Varushkan armies and the ex-slave [orc](../../../mixed/world/Orc.md) forces during [the Orc Rebellion](../imperial-orcs/The_Orc_Rebellion.md), and is said to be where the agitator who started the revolt lost his life.

Northern Skoremujac is also the site of the **[vale of Zoloto](../../winds-of-fortune/Saint_of_Bees.md#kvetmir-of-the-bees)**, a historical location for the study of bee keeping in Varushka and the birthplace (and home) of the [exemplar](../../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) *[Kvetmir of the Bees](../../religion/The_bees_of_Kvetmir_Kvetmirovic.md)*. The settlements here recently returned to prominence concerning [work done](../../winds-of-fortune/Saint_of_Bees.md) by the [Highborn](../highguard/Highguard.md) to investigate the disappearance of bees in Varushka after the invasion of [Cold Sun](../../eternals/Cold_Sun.md).

### Wieliczka

- **Quality:** Hills

The people of Wieliczka have the closest ties of any Varushkan to the people of the League. The fortified settlement of **Vorota** was once a vital guard against the people of the south, and its folk consider themselves the last vale of true Varushka on the road south. It is home to some who chose to leave Temeschwar rather than leave Varushka, and many don’t have the warmest of feelings towards the prodigal city. Indeed, when the Temeschwari ejected their criminals during the notorious Night of a Thousand Torches some of the most organised made it as far as Vorota and laid siege to the town. The settlement has been fortified of late due to the [Jotun](../../barbarians/Jotun.md) incursions in Wintermark, and the guards are even more suspicious of travellers than usual.

This title is currently held by Morana Vesnayevich; it will be reelected at Summer Solstice 389YE.
