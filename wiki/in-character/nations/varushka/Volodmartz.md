# Volodmartz

*[Image: Rich in timber and bottomless mines](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RegionsofVolodmartz.png)*

## Overview

Mountainous Volodmartz is rich in timber and deep mines. There are comparatively few vales here, and the boyars have a reputation for being particularly iron-fisted. Much more common are [outposts](Varushka_economic_interests.md#outposts), scattered across the brooding hills and growing rich from the mineral wealth they exploit. Both tend to be very well fortified, and not without reason. There are dark things in the Volodmartz mountains, and plenty of folk tales tell of malign horrors that burrow under them, emerging without warning to overwhelm the foolish, especially on Winter nights when the [northern lights](../wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#northern-lights) dance in the sky. An equally worrying threat is that posed by the northern [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md) barbarians who sometimes strike down through the north-eastern forests. As recently as 376YE a major offensive was launched through the northern mountains in an attempt to secure a beachhead near [Void](#brez); it is very unlikely to be the last.

The [Volodny](Volodny.md) had their old centre of power in Volodmartz, and it was here that [Alderei the tyrant-boyar](Alderei_the_Fair.md) was defeated by the Imperial Army. The ruins of several strongholds still lie beneath the forest canopy. One of the most vicious battles took place in the foothills of the [Opascari](#opascari) mountains at what is now a valuable [weirwood forest](#the-hunt-of-alderei-the-fair). This vital strategic asset, the largest source of weirwood in Varushka, was essential to the support of the tyrant-boyar's armies. The penultimate, savage battle of the Imperial campaign against Alderei took place here; after the forest was lost, his forces were forced to fall back towards [Alderei's Run](#murupa) in Murupa, where they finally met their end.

## Recent History

Following the [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md) invasion of [Karsk](Karsk.md) in 368YE, probing assaults were made against [Livardz](#livardz) and [Suvretz](#suvretz). These were turned back by Imperial forces, but the threat of further attacks has never been ruled out. The northern barbarians certainly covet the valuable resources of Volodmartz; in the Summer of 377YE an attempt was made to create a [beachhead](../../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#creating-a-beachhead) in the north near the vale of [Void](#void) - almost certainly a strike not only against the mana sites and libraries of the [cabalists](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#cabalists) but against the [Granites of Veltsgorsk](#the-granites-of-veltsgorsk). The expeditionary force was scattered by a force of Imperial heroes, and a follow-up campaign by Imperial forces wiped out the survivors, stymieing the attempted invasion.

## Major Features

### Triosk

A riverside town in [Livardz](#livardz), with a fish-dam and low bridge that has always been the main land route into Karsk. Once a hotbed of support for Alderei the Fair on the edge of what was then Varushka, and the scene of savage fighting in the war against his forces. Empire armies laid siege to it and burnt the original town to the ground. The tyrant-boyar sallied forth before the final assault, and gathered those still loyal from across Volodmartz and the wilds to his final battle to the north. Today, the town serves as something of a crossroads; a trade hub that serves travellers from [Karsk](Karsk.md), [Miekarova](Miekarova.md), and [Karov](Karov.md).

### Void

The cabalists of the mountains of [Brez](#brez) tend to be somewhat isolationist; the best known settlement is the vale of Void in the western foothills, home to a number of allied cabals who study and perform those rituals best kept away from centres of population. Rumour has it that heralds from the realm of Winter come here to treat and bargain with the inhabitants, while others speak of a *shadow library* containing great stores of information about curses, malign magics and the nature of the Winter [eternals](../../eternals/Eternal.md).

The [Bredavoi Emissary](../../../mixed/titles/Bredavoi_Emissary.md) liaises with - and keeps a watch on - the cabalists, especially since they opened the [Deep Chamber](../../../mixed/titles/Bredavoi_Emissary.md#the-deep-chamber), a mine that delves into the very roots of the mountains here.

Void has also become a place of contact for the orcs of [Otkodov](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md) - the offices of the [Gift of the Dwindling Star](../../../mixed/titles/Gift_of_the_Dwindling_Star.md) are here, and the warlocks of the north have begun to visit the settlement more regularly in the wake of this arrangement.

### Sungold Pass

High in the mountains of [Opascari](#opascari), above the treeline on the easternmost peak known as the Traveller, lies [Sungold Pass](Sungold_Pass.md). Extensive mineworkings and ruins that date back to Terunael times were revealed during a [terrible storm](../../winds-of-fortune/Sowing_on_the_mountains.md) that wracked the northern mountains of Varushka after the Autumn Equinox 381YE. Sungold Pass was a source of contention between the Empire and Otkodov - both claiming that the ruins lay within their own nation - until it was resolved in favour of the Thule as part of the [Sungold Treaty](../../senate-motions/Ratify_Sungold_Treaty.md) of Summer 382YE. It is not entirely clear where the pass lies with regard to the border between Volodmartz and Otkodov - the Empire claims it is in Volodmartz while the Thule claim it is in [Kógur](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md#skc3bcld) in Sküld.

### The [Hunt of Alderei the Fair](../../../mixed/titles/Hunt_of_Alderei_the_Fair.md)

The Hunt of Alderei the Fair is a Bourse resource located at the foot of the [Opascari](#opascari) mountains. Custodianship of the Hunt is an [Imperial Title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that brings with it a Seat on the [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). It produces 26 Imperial wains of weirwood every season and control is allocated to any Imperial citizen by open auction during the Winter Solstice. As with the [Night Quarry](../../../mixed/titles/Night_Quarry.md), there is some resentment that the Hunt is Imperial, rather than a Varushkan national seat.

### The [Granites of Veltsgorsk](../../../mixed/titles/Granites_of_Veltsgorsk.md)

The Granites of Veltsgorks are a Bourse resource located deep in the mountains of [Brez](#brez). Custodianship of the Granites is an [Imperial Title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that brings with it a Seat on the [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). It produces 26 Imperial wains of white granite every season. The seat is allocated during the Summer Solstice, to whichever Varushkan candidate receives the most support from Varushkan owners of [mine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forest](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) resources.

### The [Gift of the Dwindling Star](../../../mixed/titles/Gift_of_the_Dwindling_Star.md)

Following the impact of the [falling star](../../winds-of-fortune/Starfall.md) known as the Stalker or the Miscreant during the Autumn Equinox 380YE, the [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md) [orcs](../../../mixed/world/Orc.md) arranged to [provide some of the bounty](../../winds-of-fortune/Lantern-s_light.md) of [ilium](../../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) the star provides to the [Varushkan](Varushka.md) people. The offices for overseeing this tribute are located at Void in the mountainous foothills of [Brez](#brez). Custodianship of the [Gift of the Dwindling Star](../../../mixed/titles/Gift_of_the_Dwindling_Star.md) is an [Imperial Title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that brings with it a Seat on the [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). It produces 4 rings of ilium every season. The seat is allocated during the Spring Equinox to whichever Varushkan candidate receives the most support from Varushkan owners of [forest](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) and [mine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) resources.

### The Iron Roads

The majority of the [roads](Varushkan_Road.md) in Volodmartz are the result of a grand building project completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 385YE. The Iron Roads were first proposed by the civil service [several years](../../winds-of-fortune/Starting_at_the_end.md#the-iron-roads-of-varushka) earlier. In Volodmartz, the project was somewhat more ambitious. Rather than just expanding on existing roads, the plan involved building a major road along the edges of the foothills north of [Triosk](#triosk), passing by the [Hunt of Alderei the Fair](../../../mixed/titles/Hunt_of_Alderei_the_Fair.md) and then striking north-west through the dark woods of central Volodmartz to [Void](#brez), and ultimately to [Veltsgorsk](../../../mixed/titles/Granites_of_Veltsgorsk.md) in the shadow of the northern mountains. The new road linking to the Hunt of Alderei and the Fair and the Granites of Veltsgorsk required careful planning, and protection from the wolves of the forests, but once it was complete it greatly increased the prosperity of the territory.

## Regions

### Brez

- **Quality:** Hills

The mountains of Brez have a shadowy reputation among Varushkans. The peaks are high enough that they are perpetually shrouded in snow, even at the height of summer, and even the Iron Roads have not made the foothills much more accessible. The [Granites of Veltsgorsk](#the-granites-of-veltsgorsk) lie high in the eastern peaks of the region, and it is said that without the presence of this valuable Bourse resource, no outsider would ever visit the peaks here despite the rich seams of copper, iron, silver and [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) the mountains conceal.

The mountains are known to hold several strong [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md), associated primarily with the realm of Winter which have attracted [cabalists](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#cabalists) and magical scholars since before there was a Varushka. The cabalists of the mountains of Brez tend to be somewhat isolationist; the best known settlement is the vale of **[Void](#void)** in the western foothills.

The southern peaks are the site of **Ruin**. Once a key mining settlement called *Vikram's Haven*, its destruction in 349YE is generally believed to have been at the hands of a group of miners who delved too far into rocks under the broken peak above Alderei’s Run in search of rare ores. The vale did not survive their return. Precisely what led to the doom of Ruin is unclear, but scattered rumours suggest that the miners came upon a trove of ancient pre-Imperial artefacts, and fell prey either to a maddening curse or possession by malign occult forces. Ruin is now the occasional home of [Warden](Warden.md)-bands who seek to ensure no Vale makes the same mistake again, and is closely watched by the inhabitants of the nearby **Vale of Gorazima** who are keen to avoid a similar fate.

### Livardz

- **Quality:** Ruins

The southernmost region of Volodmartz, and once the most densely settled, was for centuries a prosperous crossroads where trade flowed freely. The vales - the best known among which was certainly the riverport of [Triosk](#triosk) - maintained close ties west to [Miekarova](Miekarova.md) and south to [Karov](Karov.md) - especially to the bustling port-town of [Korotny](Karov.md#malimorzi) - and [Kosti](Karsk.md#kopevnost) in [Karsk](Karsk.md). In addition to the permanent population, there are a number of vales who maintain their prosperity by catering to the [wagon raiders](Varushka_military_concerns.md) - either as a base of operations or serving as middle-men purchasing the raw materials they rip from the northern mountains and forests.

This changed in late 385YE when [Cold Sun](../../eternals/Cold_Sun.md) [attacked the Empire](../../winds-of-war/To_the_last_syllable.md#in-varushka). An army of *scions* called the [Shepherds of Ash](../../winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-shepherds-of-ash) ravaged Volodmartz, and much of the damage was focused here. The scions themselves [mysteriously vanished](../../winds-of-war/Eclipse.md#shepherds-of-ash) shortly after, lured into the *[Star Caverns](#murupa)* in pursuit of some hidden [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) and never returning. The legacy of their attacks, however, left many of the vales [in ruins](../../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#ruined). Nearly a dozen vales and settlements across Livardz were shattered or razed. Several hundred people were slain, many more forced to abandon their homes. Ever since, when the wind blows north through the woods where the *Sumisev Peegel* lies (the regio from which the Shepherds of Ash emerged), it brings with it the ashes of those the heralds obliterated. Some of the vales have been rebuilt, but Livardz has yet to reclaim the prosperity it once enjoyed.

### Murupa

- **Quality:** Forest

Central Volodmartz is home to several vales and outposts drawn by the rich bounty of [Dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) that the forests promise. Several of the vales here maintain close ties with [Razorwatch](Miekarova.md#perumaki) in [Miekarova](Miekarova.md) - and keep a close eye on the [Razors](Miekarova.md#the-razors) and the [dark power](Varushkan_Monster.md#sovereigns) that lurks beneath them. There is also a brisk trade in items scoured from the old battlefields, where Varushkan and Imperial forces fought Alderei's armies. The valley known as **Alderei’s Run** is the largest of these battlefields, the Tyrant-Boyar’s forces were finally destroyed by the Imperial Armies. Rumour says that Alderei can still be heard there, ordering his forces back into the fray.

To the north of the run are a series of small caves that Alderei’s army used for stores. They were generally referred to as **The Star Caverns**, due to a prevalence of eroded carvings of star patterns attributed to the Ushka mystics. Some scholars suggested that some of the patterns were much older, probably pre-Terunael in origin. The caverns themselves contained a [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) aligned with the realm of Day, whose power waxed and waned from season to season and year to year - studies suggested the strength of the regio was related to the position of certain [constellations](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md) especially the [Key](../../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md#the-key) and [Lock](../../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md#the-lock). In 385YE, allegedly following an agreement with the [Night Below](../../../mixed/titles/Night_Below.md) to destroy invading *scions* of [Cold Sun](../../eternals/Cold_Sun.md), the caverns were scoured by that dangerous sovereign. The carvings and the regio are both gone.

### Opascari

- **Quality:** Hills

The mountains of Opascari are rich in gold, [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and precious stones. Opascari is known in particular for its sapphires and emeralds, which are often of uncommon size and lustre. The range is also rich in [mana sites](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md), often jealously guarded by and requiring regular protection from packs of hungry [trogoni](../../world/Trogoni.md) (among other things). While much of Opascari's wealth is mineral in nature, at the feet of the southern peaks lies the rich weirwood forest known as [The Hunt of Alderei the Fair](#the-hunt-of-alderei-the-fair). High in the easternmost peaks, atop the mountain known as the Traveller, lies [Sungold Pass](Sungold_Pass.md), although the mines there were ceded to the Thule as part of the [Sungold Treaty](../../senate-motions/Ratify_Sungold_Treaty.md). The cavern known as [Zoria's Cave](../../winds-of-fortune/Birds.md#zorias-cave) is found at the foot of the Black Hill, part of the Opascari mountains.

### Suvretz

- **Quality:** Forest

A sprawling region of forests that extends to the eastern borders of Volodmartz and, by extension, the Empire. Many of the vales here are especially fortified, even for Volodmartz - fortifications designed to offer protection not only from wolves, but also from barbarian raiders coming down from the cold forests of barbarian [Otkodov](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md). There are few permanent settlements in the eastern woods - but many crow-haunted battlefields.

Of special interest in Suvretz is a place of legend named **Runa's Loss**. Runa was a [draughir](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Draughir.md) [warden](Warden.md) who watched the [monsters](Varushkan_Monster.md) of the hills with Toki, her [changeling](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Changeling.md) lover, an apprentice [volhov](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#volhov). Toki's master was seduced by an eternal of Winter (generally held to be [Agramant](../../eternals/Agramant.md)), and Toki chose loyalty to master over love. Runa was one of the wardens who hunted and killed the volhov and all loyal to him. The Vale reminds all wardens that their calling is more important than lesser [loyalties](../../religion/Loyalty.md), and is considered a site of pilgrimage for devotees of Loyalty and [Vigilance](../../religion/Vigilance.md) - albeit a particularly perilous one. The vale maintains a careful watch not only over the eastern forests but also over the borders with [Karsk](Karsk.md).

As an Imperial territory, Volodmartz is represented by a [senator](../../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) elected in the Autumn. This title is currently held by Desilav Sergeyevich Jesen; it will be reelected at Autumn Equinox 389YE. ## OOC Notes

- All the regions of Volodmartz are under Imperial control.
- [Mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) in the territory benefit from the [Iron Roads](#the-iron-roads) and produce an extra 2 ingots each season.
- [Mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) in the territory benefit from the [Light Market](../../../mixed/titles/Merchant_Boyar_of_the_Light_Market.md) and produce either an extra 2 ingots or an extra 36 rings each season, depending on the announcement of the [Merchant Boyar of the Light Market](../../../mixed/titles/Merchant_Boyar_of_the_Light_Market.md).
- [Forests](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) in the territory benefit from the [Iron Roads](#the-iron-roads) and produce an extra 2 measures each season.
