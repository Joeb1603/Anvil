# Miekarova

*[Image: The woods are lovely, dark and deep](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RegionsofMiekarova.png)*

## Overview

Miekarova is more heavily forested than the rest of Varushka. It is known for its furs, for its hunting, and for its particularly harsh winters. Snow regularly falls here for up to six months in the year, and it is common for vales to be snowed-in during the darkest months - months when [eerie lights](../wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#northern-lights) often dance in the sky. The [wolves](Varushkan_Monster.md) of Miekarova are especially savage, and every few years or so gather in great hordes that sweep down out of the north, threatening the unwary and causing widespread trouble until they are eventually dealt with. [Plaguewulfs](../../world/Plaguewulf.md) are more common here than anywhere else in Varushka, and the [sovereign](Varushkan_Monster.md#sovereigns) believed to be their progenitor, *The Howling Queen*, is said to slumber somewhere beneath the hills of north-eastern Miekarova.

Noted for beautiful painted caves beneath several of the larger ranges of hills, it was also the heart of Ushkan mysticism in the dim days long before the foundation of the Empire. It was in Miekarova that the [Imperial Faith](../../world/The_Way.md) found its deepest roots in Varushka, and there are a number of vale here that surround [churches and shrines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) to the Way. The priests here are especially vigilant against the dangers of the dark winters and the monsters that come with it, and several [warden](Warden.md) fellowships make a practice of spending their winters here to offer additional assistance.

## Major Features

### Mormyk

In [Mierchernya](#miechernya), Mormyk was the furthest western vale and closest to the territory of [Skarsind](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md). It enjoyed a long history of good relations with the people of [Krysse](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#estermark). Varushkan merchants regularly travelled east to the great market there, while adventurous Winterfolk traders and pedlars were a common feature along the road linking the two. This came to an end with the fall of [Skarsind](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) to the [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md) in 373YE. Amid concerns that the barbarians would push east into Miekarova, the people of Mormyk fortified their vale and cast expectant eyes towards the barbarian bastion that the orcs had made amid the halls of Estermark. Now Miechernya has been ceded to the Thule, the town serves as the seat of power for the warlock Vatnafjöll who is busy overseeing settlement of the area by the orcs of Otkodov.

### The Painted Caves

This is a placeholder page for content that PD are actively working on.

Several clusters of beautifully painted caves lie under the hills of Miekarova, thought to be of Ushkan origin. The paintings in the cave are incredibly vibrant, and [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) has clearly been added to the pigments. They are often quite abstract, but occasional enigmatic images of people and animals are common features. Several of the caves are known to have potent wards laid over them that still maintain their power today, and more than one contains a strong [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md), usually connected to the realm of Night.

The most famous are those in western [Mieriada](#mieriada); they have been extensively explored and the galleries extend for at least a mile beneath the hills. Many of the paintings here are damaged, either by weapons or by a deliberate attempt to deface them, and historians believe the damage may be the result of an early conflict between Vard and Ushka. Others mutter darkly that the damage here has nothing to do with the Vard, but talk about an internal conflict between the ancient Ushka - they generally claim to have come by this information through visionary dreams, and can provide little proof. Some [cabalists](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#cabalists) have claimed that the caves enhance night rituals intended to provide visions, while the famous [volhov](../../../mixed/magic/lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#volhov) Pakaanan claimed that the caves were somehow connected to the enigmatic dreams created with [Sift the Dreamscape's Sands](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Sift_the_Dreamscape-s_Sands.md). Other caves exist under several other peaks.

### The Razors

These seven spines of black obsidian stand atop a mountain that shadows the road through [Perumaki](#perumaki). An outpost at the foot of the mountain observes the razors each morning - they are usually shrouded in cloud as the day dawns, which is a good omen. If the razors are "unsheathed" (clearly visible at sunrise), it often signifies that a band of [plaguewulfs](../../world/Plaguewulf.md) will attack somewhere along the road before the next sunrise. Warning bells are rung, and runners dispatched to nearby vales to warn them of the danger. The razors themselves have never been closely examined - the approach is sheer and the presence of wolves makes it effectively impossible to approach - but study through spyglass has reinforced the belief that several of the ravening beasts that accompany their *unsheathing* dwell in caves and gulleys around the top of the mountain. Stories say that the *Howling Queen* rests under the mountain peak, and sometimes gives audience to brave or foolhardy explorers and volhov. The stories further suggest that the mists that generally shroud the razors are nothing other than a massive cloud of corpse-raising venom similar to that produced by the plaguewulfs. They fear what might happen if that malignant fog were to flow down the mountain and into the vales of Perumaki.

### The [Night Quarry](../../../mixed/titles/Night_Quarry.md)

The Night Quarry is a Bourse resource located in [Mieriada](#mieriada). Custodianship of the Quarry is an [Imperial Title](../../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that brings with it a Seat on the [Bourse](../../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). Since the Thule took control of Mieriada, the seat only receives 14 Imperial wains of [white granite](../../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md) every season. Control is allocated to any Imperial citizen by open auction during the Summer Solstice - the fact that the Night Quarry is not a national Bourse seat is a bone of contention for some Miekarovans.

### The Icy Crag of the Eternal Sun

This [college of magic](../../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) focused on the study of the [summer realm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md) was built in Volchitrava shortly before the Summer Solstice 380YE. It stands in the forest south-west of Olodny, near the Imperial Orc border. Said to have been built with the assistance of the Summer [eternal](../../eternals/Eternal.md) [Cathan Canae](../../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md), the towers of the white granite castle that houses the college glisten as if carved from glacial ice, and are visible for leagues around. The studies of the magicians associated with the Icy Crag are directed by the [Master of Ice and Darkness](../../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Ice_and_Darkness.md), an Imperial title appointed by [Declaration](../../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy) of the [Conclave](../../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md).

### The Iron Roads

The majority of the roads in Miekarova are the result of a grand building project completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 385YE. The Iron Roads were first proposed by the civil service [several years](../../winds-of-fortune/Starting_at_the_end.md#the-iron-roads-of-varushka) earlier. Much of the project involved maintaining and expanding the existing [Varushkan roads](Varushkan_Road.md). While Miekarova is heavily forested, a central road already linked the rich mines of [Essk](#dupadealuri), the prosperous trade-hub of [Olodny](#srodkoja), and the fortified trade-town of [Mormyk](#mormyk). These roads have always seen regular travel by Varushkan, [Wintermark](../wintermark/Wintermark.md), [Navarr](../navarr/Navarr.md), and [Imperial Orcs](../imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) traders. The existing roads were reinforced and carefully expanded to allow access to several of the more isolated vales, forests, and mines. The plan drawn up by the roadbuilders and the civil servants included a new road that cut north-east from Mormyk to the [Night Quarry](#the-night-quarry), then skirts the southern foothills to [Wendell's Hope](Miekarova.md#mieriada) and the [Embassy to Otkodov](#embassy-to-otkodov).

### Grimhold

In Spring 379YE, the Imperial Senate [commissioned](../../senate-motions/Fortify_Miekarova_II.md) the construction of a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in [Perumaki](Miekarova.md#perumaki). Built in the Vale of Strascovia, home of the Vor'Azi people, it used the crumbling ruins of their ancestral castle, notable for the peculiarity of its inward facing fortification, as the foundation. The castle was completed in early 380YE and now broods over the surrounding forest. Following the Winter Solstice 382YE, the fortress was enshrouded in an eerie mist for the better part of a month. The mist disappeared as inexplicably as it appeared, and if the Vor'Azi know what it was about, they have not shared that knowledge with outsiders.

### Embassy to Otkodov

In [Mieriada](Miekarova.md#mieriada), just across the Coldmere from Wendell's Hope, stands the [Thule Embassy](../../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Otkodov.md), a cold and forbidding structure in the foothills of the northern mountains. Under snow for half the year, it is already seen as a poor posting by civil servants associated with the diplomatic corps. The Thule ambassador to the Empire, Runa Who-Speaks-For-The-Dragon's-Undivided, has been comfortably established in the old apartment of Rak. For their part, the Thule have repurposed a watchpost in [Kógur](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md#skuld) as their Imperial embassy. The draughty tower stands just on the other side of the border, overlooking one of the few passes through the northern mountains. Imperial civil servants posted to the Thule embassy to the Empire report that they are forbidden to venture into Sküld without an escort, and describe the embassy as cold, gloomy, and oppressive.

### The Mountain Passes

The mountains to the north and west of Miekarova present a significant obstacle to [military forces](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) travelling to and from [Skarsind](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) and [Otkodov](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md). They form an impassable wall between [Srodkoja](#srodkoja) and [Estermark](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#estermark) for example, and the only accessible route through to [Skogei Glens](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#skogei-glens) is via **Ustyma's Gate** in western [Miechernya](#miechernya). It's likewise not possible to reach Otkodov from Srodkoja; there are passes north into [Urdur](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md#urdur) from Miechernya, and one can follow the river past [Wendell's Hope](#wendells-hope) into [Erskjuvaten](../../barbarians/Verthandi.md#erskjuvaten) from [Mieriada](#mieriada), but otherwise the mountains are simply too hostile to massive groups of soldiers. These strategic considerations are only a matter for armies; [smaller groups of soldiers](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) can still use the less-accessible paths across the borders of Miekarova.

### Heart of the Tempest

In late 379YE, an [edifice](../../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) was constructed in [Mieriada](#mieriada) by [order](../../senate-motions/Construct_the_Hearth_of_the_Tempest.md) of the [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). Swiftly completed, over the course of the next season, a powerful Spring regio formed around the edifice. It was believed that the edifice - then called the *Heart**h** of the Tempest* - would serve in some fashion as a focus for the power of the [eternal](../../eternals/Eternal.md) known as [Papa Otec](../../eternals/Irra_Harah.md).

Whether it did so or not is unclear; but during the Spring Equinox 380YE there was a significant and unexpected development. The structure disappeared within a spreading thicket of powerful trees that grew from nothing to tower over the area in exactly one day. Now renamed the Heart of the Tempest, the woodlands are extremely unwelcoming. In addition to thorny bushes, there are several plant- or plant-like creatures now in residence. Similar to *dubik* (the blood-hungry plant-like [wolves](Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves) that inhabit certain isolated forests), these guardians prevent trespassers from getting too close to whatever waits in the heart of the woodlands, allowing only certain powerful masters of [Spring magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) to enter and leave unmolested.

A little less than a year later, the forest around the Heart of the Tempest [again started to expand](../../winds-of-fortune/The_flood_and_the_storm.md#heart-of-the-tempest) and there were rumours that the *dubik*-like creatures were pressing beyond its bounds. During the Solstice, however, the forest abruptly ceased expanding. Over the next several weeks, sightings of strange creatures tailed off and then ceased entirely. By the Spring Equinox 382YE, adventurous [warden](Warden.md) fellowships venturing into the area reported that not only were the woodlands deserted but the structure at the heart of the forest had completely collapsed and lay in ruins entirely overgrown with vines and briars. If there was ever a [regio](../../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) here, they say, it has also disappeared.

Three years later an enterprising group of wagon raiders, inspired by the [mandate of the Varushkan Assembly](../../synod-judgements/385YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-146), set out from the nearby vale of Tolvoli to see if they could gain anything from the haunted groves around the Heart. What they discovered was a host of [dubik](Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves) that had made their home amongst the forest who were [thirsty for blood](../../winds-of-fortune/The_horror_and_the_wild.md#heart-of-the-tempest) and able to offer dark red liquor in return. The wagon raiders asked for the support of the Imperial Senate and a [slaughterhouse](../../../mixed/titles/Tolvoli_Butcher.md#tolvoli-slaughterhouse-and-the-heart-of-the-tempest) was [commissioned](../../senate-motions/Construct_Tolvoli_Slaughterhouse.md) to help support the burgeoning trade.

## Regions

### Dupadealuri

The hills of Dupadealuri are scattered with vales that mine rich veins of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It is watched over by **Essk**, a hilltop town behind a great wooden wall. The broad [road](Varushkan_Road.md) that runs through Essk links Miekarova to the rest of Varushka. It is famed for the Bell of Essk, a great Winterfolk-forged monstrosity that is high in the wooden watchtower at the junction of the roads from Miekarova, [Karsk](Karsk.md) and [Karov](Karov.md). This bell can be heard for miles, and is sounded when a known Sovereign of the area has been spied. The tower itself serves as a place of pilgrimage for those dedicated to [Vigilance](../../religion/Vigilance.md).

### Miechernya

- **Quality:** Hills, [Otkodov](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md)

At one point, the trading vale of [Mormyk](#mormyk) protected the road from Varushka to Skarsind. Since the region was ceded to the Thule as part of the [Sermersuaq treaty](../../senate-motions/Ratify_treaty_with_Thule_Winter_383YE.md) in 383YE, the majority of the Varushkan inhabitants here have moved out, either establishing new vales elsewhere or joining existing settlements.

Much of the region is wild, and the hills and mountains are home to both [mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and well-defended [forests](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) that endure the cold winters and occasional attacks by wolves. A former danger in Miechernya were [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md) raiders who would come down out of the northern mountains, from [Otkodov](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md), to steal the bounty of the miners and the foresters. There are several ancient battlefields in Miechernya where human and orc have clashed - and on occasion a conflict between barbarian and Imperial warriors near one of these battlefields stirs up old ghosts, or remnants of soldiers who attack both sides indiscriminately, angry that their sleep has been disturbed.

Construction on *Eldfell*, a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) built supernaturally quickly thanks to the Thule's [Autumn magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md) enchantment, was finished [shortly before](../../winds-of-fortune/The_sweetest_of_songs.md#voice-of-the-dragons) the 384YE Summer Solstice. The fortification is positioned to overlook the passes through to [Otkodov](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md), and also secures the eastern end of **[Ustyma's Gate](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#the-mountain-passes)** (what the people of Wintermark called **Wulfric's Fall**).

### Mieriada

- **Quality:** Forest, Otkodov

This region is known for rich veins of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) in the mountains, and for savage raids by the [Thule](../../foreign-nations/Thule.md) who used to follow the rivers down out of the mountains to attack the vales or the [mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md). Both the [Painted Caves](#the-painted-caves) and the [Night Quarry](../../../mixed/titles/Night_Quarry.md) are found here. The remains of the [Hearth of the Tempest](#heart-of-the-tempest) are found here not far from the town of **Tolvi**, where is found the [Tolvoli Slaughterhouse](../../../mixed/titles/Tolvoli_Butcher.md#tolvoli-slaughterhouse-and-the-heart-of-the-tempest). The area is known to be inhabited by large numbers of *dubik*, but the [Tolvoli Butcher](../../../mixed/titles/Tolvoli_Butcher.md) reduces the threat they pose. In theory.

The most famous vale in Mieriada lies on shores of the **Coldmere** (which often freezes solid in the depths of winter). **Wendell’s Hope** is a mining vale that grew up around a tavern and trading post. It is unusual in that its [boyar](Varushka_leadership.md) is traditionally the owner of the largest tavern in the vale, which is a place of pilgrimage for the dedicates of [Courage](../../religion/Courage.md). In the past groups of warriors who were planning to raid into Otkodov would often gather here on their outward journey, and on their return. It is especially welcoming to [stzena](../../../mixed/music/Stzena.md); the nights are long, and both the miners and the warriors are wealthy. It is sometimes called the Last Warm Hearth in the North.

Across the Coldmere stands the [Thule Embassy](../../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Otkodov.md), a cold and forbidding structure in the foothills of the northern mountains. Under snow for half the year, it is already seen as a poor posting by civil servants associated with the diplomatic corps. The Thule ambassador to the Empire, Rak Who-Speaks-For-The-Dragon's-Undivided, is likewise said to be a little regretful that he has had to leave the relative welcome of the *Red Lantern* inn in Void for these comparatively less hospitable quarters.

### Perumaki

- **Quality:** Hills

In the mountains of Perumaki are the [Razors](#the-razors), and at the foot of their mountain lies the vale of **Razorwatch** which keeps an eye on them. As with most regions of Miekarova, Perumaki contains a number of prosperous [mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md), and there are numerous rich veins of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron). The richest material in Perumaki is probably silver, however, and a number of [businesses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) are scattered through the vales here that deal in precious metals or fine jewellery.

Perumaki is also the ancestral home of the Vor'Azi, who have lived in the *Vale of Strascovia* for generations. At the Spring Equinox 379YE, the [Senate](../../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) [approved construction](../../senate-motions/Fortify_Miekarova_II.md) of a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) at the Vale of Strascovia, building on the crumbling remains of the Vor'Azi estates. In the shadow of Grimhold stand the [Gardens of the Grim](../../../mixed/titles/Grim_Gardener.md), a [herb garden](../../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) [sinecure](../../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) that also serves as a memorial to the respected Varushkan hero Chernobog.

### Srodkoja

- **Quality:** Forest

The centre of the northern fur trade; pelts from here adorn many a [Dawnish](../dawn/Dawn.md) [Earl](../dawn/Earl.md) or [Merchant Prince](../the-league/The_League_leadership.md) of the [League](../the-league/The_League.md) cities. The largest vale in Srodkoja is **Olodny**, and has made much of its wealth from trapping and skinning. It is built to defend against wolf packs - the ditches are deep and the walls thick with spikes - and it serves as a base of operations for both hunters and [wagon raiders](Varushka_military_concerns.md). Not far from Olodny stands the [Butterfly Holt](../../../mixed/titles/Gloaming_Sentinel.md#butterfly-holt), an area of forest girdled with wooden palisades that serves as a breeding ground for a hardy strain of [iridescent butterfly](../../world/Mundane_beasts.md#iridescent-butterfly).

In the forest south-west of Olodny, near the Imperial Orc border, is the vale of **Volchitrava**. Home to the Gremani family, this vale is also the site of the [Icy Crag of the Eternal Sun](#the-icy-crag-of-the-eternal-sun), a [college](../../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) of [Summer magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md).

In the forests to the north of Olodny lies the vale of Sciany. On the perimeter of this vale lies the tower of Gorowac, which is believed by some to be home to a powerful Volodny.

Srodkoja is also the location of **Olga’s Ashes**, a place of virtuous study, part-built into caverns above the road. Olga was an early Varushkan convert to the Way, a warden by calling, and her ashes are said to have miraculous powers to reveal possessing spirits. Some Varushkans claimed her as an [exemplar](../../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md#exemplars) of [Vigilance](../../religion/Vigilance.md), and she was finally recognised as such by the [Synod](../../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) in  [Autumn 377YE](../../synod-judgements/377YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md).

## Strategic Considerations

In addition to the qualities of the individual regions, there are certain strategic consideration that affect [military campaigns](../../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#campaign) in Miekarova. [The passes](#the-mountain-passes) through the mountains to the west and north restrict free movement of Imperial armies. Armies cannot move between [Srodkoja](#srodkoja) and [Estermark](../imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#estermark), nor between Srodkoja and [Otkodov](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md) (and vice versa). There are passes into Skarsind and [Urdur](../../barbarians/Otkodov.md#urdur) from Miechernya, and into [Erskjuvaten](../../barbarians/Verthandi.md#erskjuvaten) from [Mieriada](#mieriada) in [Verthandi](../../barbarians/Verthandi.md) from [Mieriada](#mieriada), but otherwise the mountains are simply too hostile to massive groups of soldiers. These strategic considerations are only a matter for armies; [smaller groups of soldiers](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) can still use the less-accessible paths across the borders of Miekarova.

## OOC Notes

- As of the Autumn Equinox 387YE, the Thule control Miechernya and Mieriada. Varushka, and by extension the Empire, controls the rest of the territory.
- [Grimhold](#grimhold) in [Perumaki](Miekarova.md#perumaki) is a rank one [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md).
- Eldfell in [Miechernya](#miechernya) is a rank one fortification controlled by the Thule
- [Mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) in the territory benefit from the [Iron Roads](#the-iron-roads) and produce an extra 2 ingots each season.
- [Mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) in the territory benefit from the [Light Market](../../../mixed/titles/Merchant_Boyar_of_the_Light_Market.md) and produce either an extra 2 ingots or an extra 36 rings each season, depending on the announcement of the [Merchant Boyar of the Light Market](../../../mixed/titles/Merchant_Boyar_of_the_Light_Market.md).
- [Forests](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) in the territory benefit from the [Iron Roads](#the-iron-roads) and produce an extra 2 measures each season.

> **The businesses of Miekarova**  
> Several vales of Miekarova are described as dealing in valuable minerals or furs. While you could represent this kind of endeavour with a [mine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) or a [farm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md), you could also represent it with a [business](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md). A trading post that deals in ostensibly mundane goods would produce money rather than game materials, making business ideal - and it would be easy to imagine [diversifying](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md#diversification) it to provide small amounts of appropriate raw materials. Perhaps the miners who provide your silversmith with silver occasionally bring ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) as well.  
>  
> Likewise, while you might represent a gang of wagon raiders with a [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md), an inn or tavern like [Wendell's Hope](#wendells-hope) that serves as their base of operations would make an interesting business - perhaps again with a little diversification to reflect the spoils of war they might trade you to pay off their bar tab.
