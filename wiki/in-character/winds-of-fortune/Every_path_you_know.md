## Overview

Initially, the creation of the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md) [did not receive unanimous praise](Eight_for_a_wish.md#bound-by-thorn-navarr) in all corners of Navarr. People were ambivalent, unsure of how to respond to the idea that those who had sworn themselves completely to the eradication of Terunael's folly should now share that responsibility with others.

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The people of Navarr welcome and embrace the multi-nation commitment to the fight against the vallorn as represented by the Vallornguard. We must all work to ensure that our skills, knowledge, and experience, all hard-won over a millennium of conflict are shared with this new ally. This is the highest calling of our assembly.
> >
> > Xandor Trodbroker, Navarr National Assembly, Autumn 387YE, Vote: Upheld with a **Greater Majority** 263-10

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > We the Navarr Assembly support the formation and operation of the Vallornguard in the mission to eradicate the vallorn. Our oaths are clear and we act as one nation and an Empire in our ambition to see this threat destroyed.
> >
> > Haco, Navarr National Assembly, Autumn 387YE, Vote: 201-0

The guides of the nation came together last summit to answer that question. Speaking with a clear voice they have committed the nation to aiding this nascent body as the newest ally in the fight. Surely, this was the impetus for Navarr joining the Empire in the first place? To galvanise the other nations to work with them to destroy the vallorn... Even with [the recent revelations](A_thousand_years_of_longing.md), the plain truth is that the Navarr cannot defeat the vallorn acting alone. With the clock ticking now that the trods are no longer being walked and maintained as they once were, the Vallornguard may be the best way to unite the nations in common cause.

The national assembly have not merely thrown their moral weight behind the Vallornguard. They declare supporting the [sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) to be the nation's [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md#the-highest-calling), urging everyone to share an epoch of skills, knowledge, and experience with this new ally. Excited by the prospects of taking action, the Navarr have responded with fierce urgency.

## Thanks for Sharing

- **During the coming year the Navarr Assembly could select a single different nation each season to support**
- **Whichever nations the Navarr choose will get an opportunity to create a new title that helps the Vallornguard fight the vallorn**

The Assembly called on all Navarr to share their skills, knowledge and experience with others. People are enthusiastic to heed the call - but uncertain of where to start. It won't work to just spread out across the Empire, it's clear the impact of that would be limited. Better, most people think, to concentrate efforts on a single ally at a time - that way they can achieve palpable results.

In 388YE, the Navarr National Assembly will be able to pick a single different Imperial nation each season (in a similar manner to [Highguard's](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [Peerless Kinship](A_peerless_sacrifice.md#a-renewed-kinship)) and share their skills, knowledge, and experience with them. Navarr will stride across the chosen nation looking to to share all that they have learned over the centuries. They will seek out any [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) where their abilities might help build support for the [sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) and provide it with the tools and resources it will need.

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Virtuous raise up their fellows. Any nation willing to raise arms and join the Vallornguard shall have our full support. We send {named priest} with 50 doses of liao to lead our people in sharing everything we have with {nation} to prepare to finish the fight.
> >
> > Synod Mandate, Navarr

If this mandate is enacted, Navarr from all walks of life will travel to the named nation looking for ways to help. This will generate at least one [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) for a [new Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md) to help the Vallornguard prepare for the fight with the enemy.

The new sodality could benefit if other nations are able to help supply and provision the nascent Vallornguard, but what can be done closer to home?

## Home on the Remains

- **Restoring the botanical gardens at the First Voice Glade School would allow the Navarr to share their skills with members of the Vallornguard**
- **The Dean would gain 27 herbs each season**
- **The Dean could upgrade a basic herb garden owned by a member of the Vallornguard each season**

The [First Voice Glade School](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_First_Voice_Glade.md#first-voice-glade-ruined) has [just been rebuilt](../senate-motions/Rebuild_First_Voice_Glade.md) after it was destroyed by the [Empty Hands](../winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-empty-hands) two years ago. The school itself has now been rebuilt on a much grander scale, and has once again become a scholastic haven, promoting the musical and cultural traditions of the Navarr, sharing them with students from near and far. The school has encouraged emerging talent, ensuring Navarr musical traditions remain vital.

Sadly the associated botanical garden were overlooked in the proposal presented by the civil service, which focused on other benefits. But with scores of visitors coming to the shrine, there is now a chance to recreate the gardens and take advantage of that with the school itself. Whisper it quietly, but the Navarr recognise no equal in herblore in the Empire - no other nation has so many skilled apothecaries and herbalists.

The work proposed is not insignificant, but if the gardens were restored to their former glory and a new classroom built, the Dean would resume receiving a bounty of herbs, but they would also be able to select a member of the Vallornguard to train and study with Navarr masters of herb lore. Provided the student was not already highly proficient, the skills they gained would provide them with permanent benefits, allowing them to grow more herbs each season.

Any member of the Vallornguard would be free to roleplay spending time at the School to learn the history of the Empire's longest war, and to advance their skills in botany. The Dean could select a single member of the Vallornguard with a [basic herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) each season - their herb garden would be upgraded by one rank without needing any wains or money. A starting herb garden is one that does not have any existing [upgrades](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md#upgrades).

- **A mustering ground could be built in Miaren to help Imperial armies fight the vallorn**
- **The First Thorn of the Vallornguard would receive blade oils effective against vallornspawn and a set of regalia**

One of the challenges facing members of the Vallornguard is that the Empire as a whole has far less experience fighting the vallorn, than the barbarians they have been at war with for decades. There have been a number of notable engagements in the last few years, but few soldiers have been a part of an extended campaign, fighting for seasons at a time against the host of the vallorn. If that isn't corrected, the Empire's soldiers are in for a rude awakening when the the trods are connected and the vallorn is fully roused.

The idea is mooted for a mustering ground to drill new members of the Vallornguard until slaying [husks](../world/Vallornspawn_husk.md), [ettercaps](../world/Ettercap.md), and all the other horrors the vallorn can produce becomes second nature. [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md) is the most logical choice - the area is firmly under Imperial control, but is central to all the vallorn hearts in the Empire. The Navarr could train warriors in the techniques needed to defeat the vallorn, showing them how to use potions like [Oil of Blackthorn](../../mixed/magic/potions/Legacy_of_Thorns.md#oil-of-blackthorn) that are invaluable for destroying the enemy.

Having the mustering ground there would mean that any Imperial [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) that receives [natural resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#natural-resupply) in the territory could recruit from the training grounds as well as secure useful anti-vallorn potions and blade-oils. As a result the army could take the attacking order, **Uproot the Aberration**, the following season. (If multiple armies resupply in Miaren, the First Thorn should email [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) in order to choose which army will gain the benefit).

> **Uproot the Aberration**
>
> - **The ability of this army to conquer vallorn-held territory is increased by one tenth**
> - **Casualties inflicted by this army against the vallorn are increased by one tenth**
> - **Casualties suffered by this army from vallorn are reduced by one tenth**
>
> Bolstered by the fresh recruits trained by the Vallornguard, the army can employ a supply of potions useful for fighting the vallorn provided by the Mustering Ground against the enemy. These potions are quickly consumed, but can prove vital when used at the decisive moment, to turn the tide against *Terunael's Folly*.

The Vallornguard would need to appoint the First Thorn to oversee the training. Once that was done, local artisans would contribute time and materials to fashion them with potions and magical items to fight the vallorn. They would receive two doses of Oil of Blackthorn each season and the season after they were elected they would receive a [Barbed Spear](../../mixed/magic/items/Barbed_Spear.md), a [Death's Door](../../mixed/magic/items/Death-s_Door.md) and one [Bloodfire Periapt](../../mixed/magic/items/Bloodfire_Periapt.md).

| **Responsibilities:** To tend the deep steps of the Grim Pyramid, protecting the edifice from damage. To ensure the boons provided by Kaela are used to end the vallorn threat forever. To deliver Kaela's will to the Empire should she call on them to do so. |

- **The vates of The Final Note steading have asked Kaela to offer her aid to the Vallornguard**
- **The *Queen-of-Silence* has agreed to patronise a grand edifice in Hercynia**
- **The Grim Pyramid empowers an Imperial title - Quartermaster of the Grim Legion**

The [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Kaela](../eternals/Kaela.md) has long watched the Navarr's quest to end the vallorn with great interest, offering her aid from time to time as suits her whims. Most recently she permitted the extension of [her gift](../eternals/Kaela.md#the-gift-of-kaela) to not just ease the suffering of Imperials facing the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md), but to those who fight the vallorn so that those who carry on do not have to fight an enemy wearing the face of a fallen loved one. The vates of *The Final Note* [steading](../nations/navarr/Steading.md) near [Old Ranging](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md#old-ranging) have long experience dealing with *Dark-Between-the-Stars*, and sought out one of her [heralds](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#heralds) to discuss ways of harnessing the power of [ending](../../out-of-character/rules/Winter_magic.md#endings) against the vallorn.

*[Image: Wolf Cloak.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Wolf_Cloak.png)*

The *End-of-Strength* has consented to offer her aid. She will support the building of a grand [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) in the Starless Vale, a powerful Winter [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) located in a narrow cleft in Old Ranging near to Last Note steading. The steading believe it was the site where the old [Winterfolk](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) Queen Ilsin [met her end](../nations/wintermark/Crown_of_Three_Tears.md#queen-ilsin-and-the-vallorn), but historians dispute this claim. Regardless, the *Lady of Oblivion* is prepared to support a site dedicated to her name that will offer aid against the vallorn.

Before she will do so, however, she asks the Navarr of Hercynia for an oath. One day, the vallorn will end. It may be that the Navarr and the Vallornguard bring about that ending. She wishes an oath that when that day comes, when Hercynia is once again free of corruption, the ruins of the Terunael city of Hacynian will be left to succumb to the passage of time. They will not be rebuilt, no new city raised on their foundations, no new Hacynian. They may raise a new city, if they wish, but not in [Deer's Folly](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md#deers-folly), and not named Hacynian. It must be something new, not an attempt to cling to something that has ended. With that in mind, The Last Note has proposed a [mandate](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md).

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Terunael city of Hacynian has passed into history, ruined by the vallorn. We send (named priest) with 25 liao to urge the folk of Hercynia to swear an oath that neither they nor their descendants will seek to rebuild Hacynian. Let us cherish its memory, but accept its passing as a sacrifice for a future free from the folly of Terunael.
> >
> > Synod Mandate, Navarr National Assembly

If this mandate is upheld and enacted, Kaela will happily provide her support to the edifice in the Starless Vale. If not, the structure cannot be commissioned. Obviously, though, if the National Assembly enacts this mandate, and urges the folk of Hercynia to make an oath to let Hacynian pass into history, any future efforts that break that oath will likely have severe consequences for them and their descendants.

Assuming the Navarr meet her requirement, the *Queen of Silence* proposes a grand structure be carved into the ground itself; an inverted stone pyramid thirty floors deep. Those who come in search of the aid of the *Black Dog* will have to descend into the bones of the earth to meet with her representatives. The Grim Pyramid would be watched over by the *Quartermaster of the Grim Legion*, an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) appointed annually by the Vallornguard. While she might otherwise have considered the Quartermaster being appointed by the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md), her only interest here is simply to end the vallorn and the vates of The Final Note steading have been clear in their desire to empower the sodality. Whoever is appointed, they will be expected to spend time at the Grim Pyramid, liaising with the servants of Kaela. If the *Black Dog* has reason to need something of the Empire, especially if it has to do with the vallorn, she may charge the Quartermaster to convey her wishes to the Empire.

In return for their service, the *Queen-who-sits-on-the-Black-Throne* will gift the *Cold-One's Cestus* to be part of the regalia of the title. The vates of The Final Note have examined this [artefact](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#artefacts), and declared that it is a single gauntlet that [helps the wearer cast](../../indexes/by-category/Foci.md) the rituals [Surcease of Sorrow](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Surcease_of_Sorrow.md), [Ravenous Tongue of Entropy](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Ravenous_Tongue_of_Entropy.md) and [Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clarion_Call_of_Ivory_and_Dust.md).

The artefact is only a sign of her regard, however. She will station one of her *Forgotten Generals* at the Grim Pyramid, along with a complement of warriors (equivalent to 3,000 force strength). These grim legionaries will attack any force that tries to conquer the [region](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md#region). As long as the titleholder is a member of the Vallornguard, then they may order the Forgotten General to march forth alongside an Imperial [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) to battle the vallorn, provided the force were not under an existing [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). Obviously the legionaries would not be able to defend the region if it were attacked during a season when the legionaries are fighting the vallorn alongside an army. (**OOC Note:** To use this power the Quartermaster should email [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) and let them know the army they have selected, and the general of that army should reference the presence of the grim legionnaires in their [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md).)

The Final Note caution that if the Grim Pyramids garrison were called forth to fight with an army and did *not* end up fighting vallornspawn, then the title-holder will suffer her *extreme displeasure*. Which sounds bad, but hopefully everyone involved will be wise enough to elect someone who can ensure that doesn't happen? The Hercynian vates also point out that all things will eventually come to an end; when the last vallornheart is destroyed the *Queen of Silence* will withdraw from that place, leaving it to be reclaimed by whomever remains, or fall to the ravages of time. Either is fine with the *Ending One* after all.

One note of caution is raised by the [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md). Kaela's heralds have been clear that she *will not* commit to send heralds to fight the vallorn unless control of the Grim Pyramid has been [ceded](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) to her by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) (like a [fane](../eternals/Fane.md)). [Amity](../eternals/Alignment.md#amity) is not enough - the eternal will not guarantee something unless she is assured of her ability to carry though on her promise. Of course, if the Senate does cede the edifice to Kaela, then even [enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) would not prevent the [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) from using the Grim Pyramid, and given it already has a significant force of her grim legion associated with it, they would potentially be in a position to cause significant trouble for the people of Hercynia and [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md).
