## Overview

[Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) has been part of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) for six years now, and what was once a wretched place clenched in the iron fist of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) is rapidly becoming one of the most prosperous territories in the Empire. Years of investment have paid off, with the vales here becoming stronger and richer with each season. Some of the finest Imperial [forests](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) are found here, and it is also home to places like the [Palace of Orieb](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#the-palace-of-orieb), the [Bone Hives](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Bonehives.md#the-bone-hives), and [Marshstand Skerry](../../mixed/titles/Cabalist_of_the_Hollow_Stone.md#marshstand-skerry) that show what can be achieved when resources are taken from the masters of [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md). There is even some cautious trade with the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md) who control the northern forests, although there is also a little friction from those who resent the presence of interlopers who are not Imperial in *their* territories. Then, a couple of years ago, another "wave of settlement" took place when [Awstin's Rest](../../mixed/titles/Strategos_of_Navarr.md#awstins-rest) wayhouse was built in [Nearweald](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#nearweald), bringing with it the families of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) keen to share their own culture and customs with their Varushkan neighbours and learn from them in turn.

All of this preamble sets the scene for what happens after the Summer Solstice. Magic of the [Wondrous Forest](../eternals/Realm.md#night) rises in isolated places across the territory, seeping out from beneath the trees, pooling in their shadows. It spreads quickly across the entire territory - in Imperial and Thule lands alike - bringing with it the unmistakable blessings of [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) - blessings that have a regrettable tendency to be somewhat *mixed*.

## Dark Dreams

- **People in Ossium this season experience vivid dreams full of strangeness and wonder**
- **Some of those dreams are dangerous and folk are injured or even disappear**
- **Characters in Ossium may choose to experience the last of these dreams in Anvil**

Within a few nights of the Summer Solstice, [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) creeps out from under the trees of Ossium. It brings strange dreams, and the influence of both the [Wondrous Forests](../eternals/Realm.md#night) and the [creatures that live there](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md). Each dream is as different as each dreamer, but as the season lengthens the commonalities between the night-time visions become apparent.

Not every dream is influenced by enchantment, but those that are, are very distinctive. They involve dark forests, and while they may feel familiar at first they quickly turn dark. Exploring them finds strange foliage - and in some cases trees give way entirely to stone halls supported by marble columns carved to resemble trees or great forests of titanic fungus. Sometimes northern pines are replaced with trees more suited to the jungles of [Sumaah](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md) or [Asavea](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md), or waterloged willows and mangroves reminiscent of the [Siroc Bayou](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc-bayou). Some even dream of exploring vibrant forests of coral and anemone that are clearly deep under the sea despite the absence of anything approaching water around them.

Even stranger than the trees are the creatures that live there. Moths of all colours, ranging in size from the naturally familiar to creatures so immense one could imagine riding on their backs. Fabulous squirrels the size of dogs; scuttling little star-nosed monkey moles who shout mockery from the high boughs or try to engage visitors in unwise trades; talkative caterpillars longer than an arm; stealthy cats with fur the colour of night and polychromatic eyes; snakes that swim effortlessly through the air, shedding scales behind them, of music not skin.

Both fauna and flora might shift size, shape, or colour without warning but almost without fail they are beautiful and strange. While some fear sleep and seek to avoid the unknown mysteries of the enchantment, others embrace their nightly sojourn, eager to see what new splendours they will encounter.

There are clearly dangers here and everyone who experiences one of these dreams knows it. Yet the sleepers are not without protection. Every Varushkan knows that there are certain [virtuous animals](../nations/varushka/Varushka_religious_beliefs.md#the-virtuous-animals) that look after those under threat, or that come to the aid of the lost and the frightened. Unlike the peculiar, phantasmagorical creatures of the dreams, these guides and sentinels look entirely mundane, entirely normal, for all that some of them possess the power of speech. There are a number that are familiar - the spider and the squirrel; the goose, the bear, the boar and the hound; deer and eagle; hare, fox, cat, and rabbit; and of course the bees. There are a few that are less familiar - snakes that offer good advice, doughty oxen who face down too-wide smiling tricksters, rebellious otters, industrious beavers, placid [dodo birds](../world/Mundane_beasts.md#dodo-birds) that caution against rushing off into the unknown half-cocked, and many more. Helpmates and companions, these creatures appear in dreams to guide the sleeper and keep them safe.

One danger seems omnipresent; those who experience many of these dreams speak of a stalking creature that they never quite catch a glimpse of but that they know is shadowing their dream quest. The signs of its presence and passage, and the sensation of its unseen presence, haunt many of the most sensitive dreamers. Some say it is clearly an unnaturally massive wolf, others describe it as a constricting serpent with a naked-bone skull for a head, others as a sleek black shark that glides silently through the coral, or a great brindled [lion](../world/Mundane_beasts.md#big-cats) larger than anything found in the wild forests of [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md). Some sleepers awaken with wounds that cannot be easily explained - raked by claws, bitten, gored - and while the injuries can be treated easily enough they are still a cause for alarm. There are also about two dozen stories of actual disappearances as well. Such things are by no means unknown in Varushka at the best of times, but there is a different tenor to these particular nocturnal vanishments. Those who disappear always seem to be [magicians](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician), and they always vanish unnoticed while everyone is asleep. At first there is worry that this is the work of [Night Below](../../mixed/titles/Night_Below.md) but it lacks many of the telltale marks of such an event. Some [cabalists](../../mixed/magic/lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#cabalists) point to the disappearances that very occasionally accompany the [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) *[Sift the Dreamscape's Sands](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Sift_the_Dreamscape-s_Sands.md)* and mutter to themselves.

But for all the mystery and apparent danger, the dreams are also sometimes helpful. Sleepers awaken from particularly vivid nocturnal experiences with their minds ringing with symbols and metaphors. A rare few manage to untangle the visions to get some sense of them - intentional or coincidental is hard to say - and again the cabalists suck their teeth and point to a known magic - *[Signs and Portents](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Signs_and_Portents.md)* - and speculate on what might be gleaned when an entire territory of people experience it over the course of a season.

### Participation : Dreams

- **Any character living in or visiting Ossium this season will have experienced odd magical dreams**
- **Any character whose personal resource is in Ossium might gain additional benefits**

Anyone who lives in Ossium or spends time there this season is encouraged to make up some dream experiences by using the above narrative as a guide, or perhaps looking at the *Sift the Dreamscape's Sands* ritual. As well as phantasmagorical nighttime adventures, and fairytale encounters with virtuous animals, you might have experienced the sensation of being hunted, or even woken with injuries reflective of a bestial attack. You may have had a particularly symbolic dream that forewarned you of a coming challenge, or helped you untangle a thorny problem, or even just nudged you into finding your glasses after they had been missing for several days.

Any character whose [personal resource](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource) is based in Ossium - most likely either a Varushkan or a Navarr character - may choose to have had a deeper, more magical experience and find that the dreams linger a little into their time at Anvil. Provided they are either targetted by [Signs and Portents](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Signs_and_Portents.md), or consume a dose of [Goosewhisper Infusion](../../mixed/magic/potions/Infusions_of_Feathers.md#goosewhisper-infusion) at some point during the day before going to sleep, they will again experience vivid dreams. This cannot be done by anyone who for whatever reason cannot dream, or for anyone using a different method to get magical dreams whether it is [Dreamscape of the Endless Hunt](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Dreamscape_of_the_Endless_Hunt.md), *Somnolent Wanderer*, or the dreams created with [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) using the [dedication](../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) skill. The next morning when they awaken they will enjoy a short-lived benefit:

- If they have the [magician](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill, they can have two additional personal mana over their usual maximum. These points are the first spent, and once gone cannot be recovered.
- If they have the [hero](../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) skill, they can have one additional hero point. This is also temporary and is the first one spent and cannot be recovered.
- If they have both skills they must pick one benefit.
- If they have neither skill, they instead awaken with a source of [spiritual strength](../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md#overcoming-roleplaying-effects) that will allow them to overcome any roleplaying effect that would cause them to become afraid or filled with despair (including the druj misamsa).

In each case, the benefit lasts until time out and can only be gained once. It *cannot* be used if you have benefitted from other downtime option related to your activities before the event, such as those provided by supporting an Imperial army.

While you benefit from this boon, you also experience a roleplaying effect: *You occasionally catch sight of things that are clearly not real out of the corner of your eye; weird trees, strange animals. You may also experience visions of a particular virtuous animal accompanying you that nobody else can see or hear that responds when you speak to it and offers appropriate advice.*

There is one final option - if you choose to take this boon, the next time you sleep you may choose to vanish entirely. If you do this, simply don't go back into character after time-in the next day and when you have a moment go to GOD and mark your character "dead" on the system. In character, you have slipped away into dreams in the manner mentioned in *Sift the Dreamscape's Sands*.

## Twisted Paths

- **Travellers in Ossium who leave the path find it all too easy to get lost**
- **Following the Autumn Equinox there will be opportunities to rescue or avenge people lost in the forests**
- **The *Protect the Vales of Varushka* venture provides different rewards in the coming season**

There is however a darker side to this enchantment, and it is a serious concern in a place like Ossium. While dreamers wander phantasmal woodlands, waking folk have to contend with woodlands turned malignantly mysterious. Every area of trees, from a small copse to the great expanses of places such as [Echofell](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#echofell) becomes hard to navigate. Travellers easily find themselves becoming lost or turned around even in areas that would otherwise be familiar. Odd creatures, some similar to those seen in dreams but more threatening, are occasionally glimpsed. As long as everyone stays on the [roads](../nations/varushka/Varushkan_Road.md) everything is fine. Anyone who tries to cut through the wilderness however...

The obvious problem here is that people who get lost in the woods of Varushka are often never seen again and this certainly appears to be the case. For every band of hunters that recounts the seemingly miraculous assistance of a chirping sparrow who helped them find their way back to their vale, there is another that disappears into the woods and never returns. Or worse, sometimes they do return, shambling in the dead of night and trying to get back into the houses they once lived in, hungry to share their pitiful accursed state with their loved ones. The woods of Varushka are much more dangerous for the lost and the unwary than any amount of magical dream forests.

As the Autumn Equinox draws near, some of the missing parties or travellers are accounted for but most are not. With Winter approaching, those left behind are frantic to see their loved ones, friends, business partners, and neighbours brought back to safety or - if they have already fallen prey to the wolves - freed into death and/or avenged. Even at the best of times this would need a certain type of person to step up.

Following the Autumn Equinox any Varushkan or [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) that participates in the [Protect the Vales of Varushka](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#protect-the-vales-of-varushka) [venture](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md) will receive additional rewards from the frightened folk of the vales. As well as the standard production (90 rings, and five random measures or ingots for a standard military unit), each such unit will receive a consumable item from their adventures in the deep woods. These will include sanguine potions related to the [dubik](../../mixed/titles/Tolvoli_Butcher.md) such as *Bloodvine Liquor*, *Blackblood Thorn*, or *Darkwine*, as well as the *Elder's Gift* created by the now-scattered [trudniki](Lean_and_athirst.md), and even [Heart's Blood](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#hearts-blood).

### Participation : Twisted Paths

- **Anyone living in or visiting Ossium may have gotten lost in even familiar woodlands**

Anyone who lives in or visits Ossium will be aware of the dangers of the woodlands, and if appropriate to their characterisation they are encouraged to create stories of getting lost in familiar woods, encountering dangerous [wolves](../nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves), or knowing people who have been lost in the wilderness possibly forever. It is only woodlands that are rendered dangerous and people traversing [Bittershore](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#bittershore) or [Galath Fields](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#galath-fields) remain oblivious. The effects are also limited in scope - the larger the group of travellers the less likely it is anyone will get lost. Armies will have lost a few scouts or unwise sentries but it has no effect on their ability to pass through the territory for example.

The effects are particularly pronounced for people visiting the more out-of-the-way parts of the woodlands, such as those tending the [Bone Hives](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Bonehives.md#the-bone-hives) or anyone who takes care of a [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) resource given they are often off the beaten path.

## Night Walkers

- **At least three Night eternals have focused their attention on Ossium with mixed results**
- **Another eternal, who was *not* invited, has nonetheless expressed approval for the enchantment**

The enchantment on Ossium has other effects. Night regio are empowered, and in some cases regio connected to that realm that are normally too weak to be noticeable may open like late-blooming flowers able to affect the world. Encounters with heralds of the Night eternals, and the interests of those [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) themselves, become more common. Three in particular have focused their attention on Ossium with mixed results.

### Shadow Singer

- **Heralds of Sung have walked the roads of Ossium singing and asking questions**
- **One of the heralds has presented a challenge to the Varushkan assembly**
- **They have also put forward an offer relating to the Field of Glory**

The *[Rainbow Serpent](../eternals/Sung.md)* can appear whimsical sometimes, even for an eternal of the Night realm. When the magic takes hold in Ossium, she sends her heralds to wander the Iron Roads, singing songs and collecting stories. They also ask two questions; *what is grief?* and *what does it mean to be forgotten?* Her servants visit [Awstin's Rest](../../mixed/titles/Strategos_of_Navarr.md) to spend time with the Navarr, explore [Lomaa](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#lomaa), [Ketsov](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#ketsov), and [Kostjyas Respite](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#kostjyas-respite), and gather finally on the [Galath Fields](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#galath-fiends). At the [Field of Glory](../../mixed/titles/Grieftender_of_the_Forgotten.md#field-of-glory) to be precise.

Once thirteen of the heralds have gathered at the Field of Glory, they spend an entire day and a night singing a *threnody* that weaves everything they have been told in the past few months into one intricate, unending ribbon of sorrow and remembrance. All those who hear it are filled with profound awareness of the bittersweet nature of life, and the sad truth that in time everything will be forgotten. The song ends on a hopeful note - that bad things are forgotten along with good things and that the things that matter last the longest. That grief gives way in time to acceptance and that the idea that everything passes eventually is a blessing as well as a curse.

The heralds then pack up and return whence they came - all save one. *Threnody* is also the name of the herald, a quiet creature with the aspect of a naga but who, when not singing, communicates in sign language. She communicates two offers from her mistress.

The first is for the Varushkan National Assembly and also the Navarr National Assembly. If they are prepared to tackle her questions - *what is grief?* and *what does it mean to be forgotten?* - then her mistress will consider their answers and if she finds that they contain truth or wisdom she will grant boons in recognition of their understanding of these mysteries. All they need to do is raise a [statement of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) in their national assembly and answer one of the questions - but only one of them for each statement. Whichever statements get the highest margin in an assembly, she will take as the answer of the entire assembly.

The second matter concerns the Field of Glory itself. Grief, like joy and love, is a mystery. When the Fields were first recognised as a place for the forgotten to be remembered, there was [talk of a chapel](../../mixed/titles/Grieftender_of_the_Forgotten.md#exploiting-the-field-of-glory). It would please the *Lord of Illusions* if that chapel were built. Provided the work was completed within a year - by the start of the Autumn Equinox 389YE - Sung would send one of her heralds each season to provide the Grieftender with two *feather tokens* that can each be used to smooth the way toward a performance of [Secrets of the Soul's Desire](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Secrets_of_the_Soul-s_Desire.md), [Shadowed Glass of Sung](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Shadowed_Glass_of_Sung.md), and [Clear Counsel of the Everflowing River](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clear_Counsel_of_the_Everflowing_River.md). As long as Sung was not placed under the [enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) of the Conclave, and as long as Varushka continued to use the chapel to honour the lost and the forgotten, the gift would continue. The civil service have confirmed that the opportunity to build the chapel still exists, and will remain available until the end of the Summer Solstice 389YE.

In a seperate matter, Sung has asked that **Sofonisba Amilcara of the City Below**, the [Map to the City Below](../../mixed/titles/Map_to_the_City_Below.md), **Nathair Autumngale**, the [Singer of the Silence](../../mixed/titles/Singer_of_the_Silence.md), and **Ceslev of the Circle of Zulgan Tash** (or one of their covenmates) help the assemblies consider her questions, and determine if her offer regarding the chapel is acceptable.

Finally the herald has a peculiar personal message for the **Martiza of the Circle of Zulgan Tash**. The *Moon Moth* has no knowledge of the ones she seeks, but names are like colours, and everyone sees them differently. If the Martiza asks of this matter of the *Night Singer* when next they meet, then she must throw her history into the river and together they will see what floats. Then she will know the truth of what she fears.

The herald treats all three messages with equal weight, and having delivered them, departs after her fellows.

### *Not* Father of Bats

- **The Towers of Zulgan Tash are labouring under the effects of a curse of some kind**
- **The soldiers of the garrison appear to have forgotten they work there**

The [Towers of Zulgan Tash](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#towers-of-zulgan-tash) were drawn from the Night realm in an act of consummate magic performed by the eponymous coven of Varushkan cabalists. As many magicians know, they "stole" the fortification from the realm of the [Brother of Wizards](../eternals/Sadogua.md), binding them to the marshes of the Drownbark Forest using a great stake of pure star metal (and anchoring it with the star metal found in the waters of the old marsh). The towers are [garrisoned](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#garrison) by Varushkan soldiers, who keep a [vigilant](../religion/Vigilance.md) eye on both the borders with the Mallum and with the Thule-claimed forests to the north.

About a month after the Summer Solstice, the vales of Ossium realise that it has been some time since anyone saw anyone from the Towers of Zulgan Tash. Given the way the enchantment interferes with travel, which have already become apparent, the assumption is that something like that has happened. But another three weeks pass and people begin to worry. Especially when soldiers known to be part of the garrison start turning up at their homes, often homes they haven't lived in in years, across the territory with no recollection of the Towers of Zulgan Tash or their duties there. So a party of schlacta and wardens sets off to the site of the Towers to make sure nothing has gone wrong.

Something has gone wrong.

The towers are wrapped in silken cobwebs of impossible quantity, and the entire tower appears to have been overrun by little multi-legged creatures similar to many-eyed crustaceans or armoured spiders. They run away from what is now clearly a rescue party, but wickedly launch surprise attacks and ambushes whenever they think they can get someone alone. They don't kill people though; rather they attack their memories. The garrison of the Towers of Zulgan Tash isn't dead. It isn't even missing. It's just that the soldiers have mostly forgotten what they are meant to be doing. Some have forgotten the outside world even exists; others have apparently deserted simply because they failed to remember they had a duty to perform.

The critters aren't particularly dangerous in small numbers, but when a dozen or more get together they can cause nasty bites. Worse, if they can take someone unawares they can start poisoning their memories. In the end, it takes a month or more to clear them out of the Towers of Zulgan Tash. Fortunately, the wardens and schlacta receive some unexpected assistance as the Autumn Equinox gets closer. A cadre of battle-wizards with iridescent mage armour and staffs of twisted beggarwood appear one evening to support the Varushkans, and to deliver some helpful advice. They deny it but they are clearly servants of the Brother-of-Wizards; as one points out they *can't* be friends of the Black Wyrm because he is still amazingly pissed off with the Varushkans and so would never send any of his little wizards to help them out, would he?

They turn out to be moderately useful, and seem to take a particularly grim joy in stamping on the spider creatures whenever they get the chance. They also confirm the suspicions of the wardens - this is the work of a creature of the Night realm but not the Globbersnotch himself. Rather this is a symptom of something happening in the Night realm, in the demesne of the Father of Bats. The creatures were able to slip through into the mortal world here - in defiance of the [enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) of the Conclave - because of the connection that still exists between the Towers (which the heralds call the *Whistling Strand*), the nearby [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md), and the Wondrous Forests. The other side of the regio is safe now, so no more should come through, but the wardens reckon that the main factor allowing them to reach Ossium was probably the enchantment.

#### Forgotten Garrison

- **The garrison of the Towers of Zulgan Tash has dropped to 0**
- **It will not start recovering naturally until the curse lying over it is removed**
- **Removing the curse requires Piercing Light of Revelation cast at magnitude 60**
- **Once the curse is removed the garrison will begin to recover naturally**

The Towers are clearly under a curse, one that is showing no signs of going away on its own. The wardens on the ground assessing the situation say they think a [Piercing Light of Revelation](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Piercing_Light_of_Revelation.md), cast at magnitude sixty and targeting the towers, will destroy the webs and kill most if not all of the weird critters - which they think might be particularly vicious [boggarts](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#boggarts). Fortunately, the fortification is in an Imperial territory and should be reachable from the Anvil regio.

They don't *think* it will damage the Towers themselves - they are physically present after all - but it's not impossible there may be some damage given their origin. But without this kind of dramatic action the only thing to do will be to wait for the cobwebs to fade and there's no indication they are going to do so by themselves.

The garrison of the Towers of Zulgan Tash is currently zero, and it will not start recovering until the curse is removed. At that point it will receive natural resupply, which can be sped up by any of the usual means ([mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) or an enchantment such as [Bells of Silver and Brass](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Bells_of_Silver_and_Brass.md)). ### Shadow Garden

- **Dubik have become more active in Ossium this season**
- **Forests in Ossium will suffer a one rank penalty in the coming season due to the Dubik**
- **Such forests will instead receive additional resources harvested from the bloodthirsty monsters**
- **There is an opportunity to gather dubik for *harvest* but it may go unpleasantly wrong**

As the enchantment takes root, it becomes apparent that it is not the only thing blooming in Ossium during the late Summer and early Autumn. Travellers in Drownbark Forest, Echofell, and [Nearweald](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#nearweald) report multiple encounters with the vampiric *dubik*. They appear in wooded areas in small numbers, and there are sightings of more of them lurking along the edges of less-travelled paths and the outskirts of isolated vales. A type of [wolf](../nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves), they take the form of animate plant-like creatures that thirst for human blood. They have an ability to mimic human speech, and seek to draw unwary travellers off the road so they may feast. The enchantment plays right into their hands. While they are not appearing in sufficient numbers to try and overwhelm a vale, there are still enough of them to make people nervous. Unsubstantiated reports say they may also be gathering in Bonewood and Webwood as well.

The main threat they represent is to those who control [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) resources in the territory. Whether composed of [beggarwood](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggar27s-lye), harvested for [ambergelt](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), host to the beautiful winged [butterflies](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), or with roots dug deep into soil rich in [dragonbone](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) the enchantment seems to be drawing the dubik to them like bees to flowers. There have been a few incidents, and there are likely to be more as Winter draws closer and the nights get longer. Monsters are not a new threat for the people of Varushka, and wardens, sellswords, and questing-knights aplenty to [protect](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#protect-the-vales-of-varushka) the more isolated forests but it will not be an easy winter.

Following the Autumn Equinox, every [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) in [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) (whether Varushkan or Navarr) will produce 2 fewer measures of base material due to the depredations of dubik. It is not all bad news; fighting off the dubik mean that every such forest will instead receive a *blackblood thorn* or a dose of *bloodvine liquor*.

#### Bloody Harvest

- **The ritual *Verdant Bounty of the Twilight Bayou* will attract larger numbers of dubik to a forest**
- **This would cause more disruption to the forest but provide more opportunity to harvest the dubik**
- **If too many forests are enchanted in this way it would damage Kurstrozi's Lumbermill**

Some forest owners may want to go further though. Any forest in Ossium enchanted with [Verdant Bounty of the Twilight Bayou](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Verdant_Bounty_of_the_Twilight_Bayou.md) will be absolutely irresistible to the dubik in the territory. They will gather in numbers and attack the forest. Instead of the usual increase to production that the ritual applies, it will instead produce *four* fewer measures but receive *five* *blackblood thorn* or *bloodvine liquor* to its production. There is a potential side effect though - if more than half the forests currently in Ossium are enchanted in this way it will focus the dubik on those forests. Any forest that *isn't* enchanted with the ritual will not be attacked, receiving its usual production with neither losses nor increases.

Unfortunately, focusing the dubik in the territory into areas of naturally high industry, will also result in damage to [Kurstrozi's Lumbermill](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#kurstrozis-lumbermill), the [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) that provides a significant boost to the production of Ossium's forests. Until the start of the Autumn Equinox 389YE the lumbermill will not provide any measures to forests - instead each one will receive either a *blackblood thorn* or some *bloodvine liquor* as they continue to experience problems with the dubik. Causing trouble for the Lumbermill could have other impacts as well - [there are plans to make use of the Lumbermill](Come_together.md#where-it-comes) which could *not* be pursued while it was plagued with dubik.

The civil service cannot provide accurate numbers easily but they estimate that between Varushka and Navarr, there are at least a hundred individuals attending Anvil this summit who operate a forest. If fifty individuals receive *Verdant Bounty of the Twilight Bayou* it will be enough to drive the dubik into a frenzy, draw them away from forests that haven't been enchanted, and damage Kurstozi's Lumbermill.

## Dreams of Wisdom

- **Dedicates of Wisdom who have visited the Maze of Zoria may have experienced revelatory dreams**
- **The dreams come wherever they are in the Empire or beyond - not just to those in Ossium**

Part of the power of the [Guardian of the Maze of Zoria](../../mixed/titles/Guardian_of_the_Maze_of_Zoria.md) is to empower [The Naming of the Beasts](Mime_each_lonely_word.md#the-naming-of-the-beasts). Precisely what that is and how it works is a spiritual mystery, but it often takes the form of dreams - sleeping or sometimes even waking - in which animals representing the virtues bring knowledge to the [Wise](../religion/Wisdom.md).

As the enchantment is rippling over Ossium, a very different ripple is spreading out across the Empire. [Dedicates](../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) of [Wisdom](../religion/Wisdom.md) who have made a pilgrimage to the [Maze of Zoria](../../mixed/titles/Guardian_of_the_Maze_of_Zoria.md#the-maze-of-zoria) experience strange dreams of their own that don't seem to be caused by the Night magic in the north-east but nonetheless are clearly connected to it. These dreams contain revelations - warnings - of dark powers. Of sovereigns. Of the source of the dubik, and of the false-friend who even now spreads poison on the roads of Varushka.

### *The* Dubik

- ***The* Dubik is apparently loose in Ossium**
- **The sovereign is the font of the lesser creatures that share one of its names**
- **It is a horrible spirit that corrupts and poisons woodland and vegetation**

Those [dedicated](../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) to Wisdom, especially those who have made a pilgrimage to the [Maze of Zoria](../../mixed/titles/Guardian_of_the_Maze_of_Zoria.md#the-maze-of-zoria), report dream encounters with white-pelted squirrels and small pale-feathered birds crowned with crimson that provide an unpleasant explanation as to why [dubik are spreading across Ossium](#shadow-garden).

The magical creatures whisper a secret to those able to listen - *the* Dubik, which is also called *Odech the Wanderer*, *That Which Walks Among the Boughs*, the *Death of Grass*, *Thorn-that-thirsts*, *Venomroot*, *Ghost-of-the-Dead-Forest*, *Thornroots*, *Bloody Rose*, *Death-that-waits*, *Moontree*, and *Summer's Bane* among others - has returned to Varushka. The Dubik, the progenitor of the creatures that share its name, always appears as a creature of wood and leaf, but it is actually a bodiless spirit that can move as a wind. Sometimes it slumbers, like a tree in Winter, but when it wakes it follows a pattern. It finds a plant - usually a tree - one that has stood proud and strong for many years - and it leeches the life from it, possessing it, transforming it into a vessel for its vile consciousness. It cannot move - not without outside assistance - but it squats somewhere in some out of the way place and it waits. It births dubik, who go out into the world to seek the blood of the living. Sometimes it draws to it those poisoned by hate, or jealousy, or bitterness. It stings them with its thorns, and a little of its malice takes root within their spirits and slowly spreads through them, granting them boons but ultimately turning them into living tools of its spiteful design. It also creates other servants - it can use a fraction of its essence to craft servants from plants that died of poison and send them out to bring ruin and misery.

Finding *Odech the Wanderer* is a daunting task. The sparrows sing that it is *one tree in a forest*, that there is something in its essential nature that allows it to hide as long as it is surrounded by living plants. Magic will not find it - and the squirrels whisper that [Zoria](../religion/Zoria.md) herself could not find its location with her [divinations](../../out-of-character/rules/Divination.md), no matter how long she looked. She wondered if perhaps the true secret of *Thorn-that-thirsts* is that it is the ghost of a dead [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md) tree, and that it enjoys all the protections that a living tree might have.

Even if it is found, like any sovereign it probably cannot be destroyed while Varushka persists. Its body can be destroyed, but that frees it to move as a spirit and find a new host to poison and inhabit, and the only thing one can say with certainty is that host will be far, far away from where the last one died. The only good news is that after it moves as spirit, it inevitably slips into slumber. Indeed, say the little birds and the ivory squirrels in the dreams of the wise, it has slept away the last few years in the [Heart of the Tempest](../../mixed/titles/Tolvoli_Butcher.md#tolvoli-slaughterhouse-and-the-heart-of-the-tempest), and called its children around it. Now though, it has awoken again and the enchantment on Ossium has drawn it to that place to take up its new body and begin the new season of its cycle of misery.

There is more of course - the Dubik is said to have been the creator of the Crown of the Dark Forest, or at least arranged for it to come into Varushkan hands - but those stories are for another time. For now it is enough to know that the Dubik is awake, and it is somewhere in Ossium.

### Stranger on the Road

*[Image: Take care when fighting monsters, lest you become a monster yourself.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Mora.jpeg)*

- **The Stranger apparently walks the roads of Varushka**
- **It is a source of creatures that appear as mortals but are actually monsters**
- **It corrodes bonds of trust and corrupts people into performing terrible deeds**
- **Unlike the vast majority of monsters it can travel along the roads of Varushka - albeit with certain caveats**

At the Spring Equinox, the Guardian of the Maze of Zoria **Illya Nikovitch Volkov**, proposed an odd [statement of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) to the Wisdom assembly. It was supported with a [greater majority](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority). At first there was a lot of discussion and very little happened, but following the Summer Solstice something blossoms in the hearts of the Wise and not just in Varushka. Dedicates of Wisdom who have ever made a pilgrimage to the Maze of Zoria dream of hounds with red eyebrows and of imperious geese - and sometimes these dreams come when they are wide awake. Both the dogs and the birds bear a warning to anyone prepared to listen.

The Stranger is a dark power of Varushka, and it is *Pleasing Face*. It is *You Are Not Who You Are*, and the *Wicked Storyteller*, and *Do-not-take-my-hand*, the *False Friend*, the *Smiling Mask*. It is *Wolf-on-the-inside*, and *Fellow Traveller*, and *Uncanny Companion*. It rarely appears in the stories of Varushka in a recognisable form because it is always, always in disguise. The [mora](../nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#mora) are either its children, or creatures it has twisted to its purpose. The trudnik were its creations - warped and twisted by boons they should not have asked for into the horrors they became, empowered with glamours that let them pass as human. That uncanny child that turned out to be a twisted stump full of spiders that came out of Ossium with the freed slaves all those years ago? A pawn of the Stranger. It is fundamentally the danger that looks like a friend, the false sanctuary, the trap that closes when one feels safe.

>
> > *In-character text:*

The Maze of Zoria has proven in the past to be a conduit of knowledge regarding magical beings such as the sovereigns of Varushka. It is the only dark power of Varushka that can travel by the roads, but when it does so it is bound to harm none and present only a pleasing face. It cannot hurt anyone who travels with it - and they often do not realise the horror that walks alongside them - unless they somehow invite it to do so. It is an echo of this essence that causes the mora to act as if they are simple travellers until someone breaches the bonds of hospitality, that let the trudnik masquerade as innocent draughir until their hosts or guests gave them the opening to attack. What it can do, however, is use words as weapons. It can poison those who listen to it tell its tales or offer its friendly advice, and if they accept its twisted words and act on them, it lures them into its service. The worst of its servitors are those that look to all eyes as if they are just normal folk, but whose spirits and minds are rotten and twisted. The wolves who are "hairy on the inside" - who stab their friends in the back, hack their parents with axes without warning, poison a meal for no reason. Not all random acts of inexplicable cruelty and violence are the doing of the Stranger, but many are.

*Uncanny Companion* can be bargained with, and as long as it is in its fair seeming its boons may be dangerous but they will be true. If it is *not* in its fair seeming form, then the boons it offers are curses that will drag those who accept them to their doom. The problem is that it is often not possible to tell simply by looking at it whether it is truly fair or just pretending to be. One of its strangest elements is that it tells the truth. When it is wearing its friendly seeming it never lies, but it allows people to deceive themselves by interpreting its words the way they want to. Always and always, it works to corrode trust. To make people suspicious of strangers who could be their friends, to drive wedges between loyal companions, to sow distrust of honest merchants and virtuous folk.

The hounds and the geese whisper one last secret - it is the roads of Varushka that are its domain even though paradoxically it cannot directly harm anyone who uses them. The roads promise safety, but that safety is constrained by rules, and it is this tension that grants the Stranger and its servants their power.

Why is this relevant? Because like the Dubik, the Stranger is in Ossium as the Autumn Equinox begins, walking the roads in its favoured guise of a storyteller who spins yarns that encourage people to monstrous action. And, say the magical beasts, its current intent is to tear apart the tenuous alliance between the Thule and the Varushkans. Not the treaty - it doesn't care about that - but to find any bit of common ground and poison it with words and bloodshed.

## The Dark Power Problem

- **Large scale magic used in Varushka will invariably affect the monsters that live there as well as the mortals**

Rituals that create territory-wide effects - enchantments and curses alike - always do certain things. They might also do additional things depending on the circumstances in the territory where they are cast, but the effects are unpredictable. There are [monsters](../nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md) in Varushka, and while they are not an omnipresent threat by any means they are certainly not tamed. Any large-scale magic that targets a Varushkan territory will impact not only the Thule - who can at least be reasoned with - but also the wolves, sovereigns and other entities that lurk in those territories, who cannot.

Imperial enchantments by contrast are rarely focused enough to have a significant impact on the sovereigns of Varushka or their creatures. Yet even here there is a little bit of risk. An Imperial curse that made the dead walk for example could easily create significantly more problems in Varushka.

Before anyone asks, it isn't possible to make an arcane projection that targets a territory but circumvents this problem. The only way to avoid or mitigate the problem is to create the arcane projection *carefully* and give thought to what its effects, and especially roleplaying effects, might do to a monster.

## Ten Shadows

- **The Thule are not happy**
- **They have not yet demanded any reparations for losses in their part of Ossium but may still plan to do so**
- **If the Conclave endorses the enchantment it will make it difficult for Imperial law to become involved**

The Thule, through their ambassador *Runa, Who-Speaks-for-the-Dragons-Undivided*, have expressed significant dissatisfaction with the enchantment that has also shrouded the [Bonewood](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#bonewood) and [Webwood](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#webwood) - especially as they are in the middle of negotiations with the [Ambassador to Otkodov](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Otkodov.md) to determine how to [proceed in the future](The_little_things.md#outstretched-hands). They have [let it be known](The_little_things.md#the-parting-shot) that they expect the Empire to identify and deal with these reckless individuals responsible. Whether they do or not, the orcs of Otkodov will certainly be considering whether to draw on the [treaty](../senate-motions/Ratify_Thule_Treaty_Spring_387YE.md) that says either side can *resolve any breaches of the law by the citizens of one nation in the lands of the other through diplomatic channels and in mutual respect.*

Given that the Thule-controlled parts of Ossium are two areas of wild forest, and that if there are any roads there they will be perfunctory at best and used for the transport primarily of weirwood and armies, there is little doubt they will have experienced some significant difficulties with the effect of this enchantment - even if they will also have had all the benefits of incredible dreams and occasional flashes of prophecy.

There is no suggestion that the enchantment is in any way illegal, it's not a crime to annoy the Thule. The [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) could choose to [endorse](../../mixed/government/conclave/Endorsement.md) the casting of the enchantment, which would ensure that the coven involved was shielded from any legal consequences. If that happens, the Thule would instead demand that the Conclave itself make amends for their losses.

## Conjunctions

### The Place That Was Not (Karsk)

- **A band of Mirk Lizards are attempting to establish a camp in the Semmerlak**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- ***Romand Reinetova Remislav* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for killing the Mirk Lizards**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

It is the wettest night of the summer when a thing with the shape of a person slides out of the Semmerlak and makes itself known to a cabalist in Moresvah. Its fellow servants of [Undertow](../../mixed/titles/Dho-uala.md) have apparently been conversing with a band from the Mallum who have sailed up the Nameless River, across the Barren Sea, and now make their way upriver towards the Semmerlak. Later reports from the river patrols identify the band as Mirk Lizards, and that they have landed on an island in the Semmerlak and are preparing to establish a camp there.

**Romand Reinetova Remislav**, as the incumbent [Watcher of the Semmerlak](../../mixed/titles/Watcher_of_the_Semmerlak.md), has been tasked with killing the Mirk Lizards before they can pose a threat to Imperial citizens. If unsuccessful, the Mirk Lizards will no doubt become a thorn in the side of those who make their homes on the shores of the Semmerlak.

*[Image: KaelaArt.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:KaelaArt.jpg)*

**Kaela's Gift**  
Secured through a bargain with the Winter Archmage, the gift allows any Imperial citizen in the hands of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) to choose to die painlessly. No corpse remains for the Druj to defile, to impale, or to use to taunt the living - the body turns to dust and ashes. This gift extends across the entire eastern Empire, into the Mallum, [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md), and [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md).

The gift allows any Imperial citizens fighting in the [vallorn miasma](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md#vallorn-miasma) to choose death and *dissolution* before their bodies could be infested with the vallorn's sick version of immortality.

**OOC Note:** Using the gift requires thirty seconds of appropriate roleplaying. This ability may be used by player characters captured by the Druj during an event or who is [dying](../../out-of-character/rules/Combat.md#dying) in an area of vallorn miasma; if you do so you should ensure that you mark your character as dead as soon as you can so that your egregore is aware of your death. There is, however, one drawback to this, as Imperial magicians may soon discover: anyone who takes advantage of this ability cannot be contacted with [Whispers through the Black Gate](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Whispers_through_the_Black_Gate.md).

This ability is an exception to the normal rules on using abilities while [terminal](../../out-of-character/rules/Combat.md#terminal); a terminal character *can* use this ability if they are captured by the Druj or fallen in vallorn miasma.

### The Itch (Ossium)

- **A group of bandits are attempting to escape justice after they robbed travellers on the road**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- **The *Merchant Boyar of the Bittershore* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for dealing justice**
- **There are [no innocents present](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law) on this conjunction; magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

Roads are important. Every child of Varushka knows the importance of staying on the path and making sure to show hospitality to those you pass. So when news that a trader bound for the docks at Kostjyas Respite was attacked **by people**, it sent a shock through the settlement. Calls for the militia went up, but the bandits were unable to be found. Until a prognosticator noticed the conjunction. It is the responsibility of the [Merchant Boyar of the Bittershore](../../mixed/titles/Merchant_Boyar_of_the_Bittershore.md). **Kostjya**, to deal justice .

The trader, *Adna Kostjyavic*, will recover from their losses, and their family has pledged twelve crowns to the Merchant Boyar of the Bittershore if they can deal justice to the bandits.

### Keeping Custom (Ossium)

- **An Imperial patrol is injured and needs assistance**
- **This skirmish is a [combat suitable for cadets](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-suitable-for-cadets) encounter**
- **The *Academy* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for aiding the injured patrol**
- **There may be [innocent people present](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law) on this conjunction; magistrates advise caution must be taken to avoid innocent casualties**

The call has gone out to the Academy for aid in treating an injured patrol in [Bonewood](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#bonewood). Nobody can quite remember the patrol setting off, but that is quickly disregarded as a consequence of the Night magic that has pooled in the territory.

The cadets of the Academy are tasked with travelling through the Sentinel Gate and aiding the injured patrol.

### Practice (Ossium)

- **A gang of bandits has been tricking people into ambushes**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- ***Arkady Morazovich* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for springing the trap and killing the bandits**
- **There are [no innocents present](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law) on this conjunction; magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

There have been several reports of a gang of cunning bandits making use of some strange tactics over the past season. Apparently, a few of them lie on the ground, pretending to be injured, whilst the others beg passersby for help. Then, when their targets are nearby with their guard down, the bandits strike. Gutting the nearest and causing chaos. They've not *killed* anyone... yet. But at one point this ploy is going to go wrong. They have stolen, and apparently spent, a lot of wealth over the last three months; mostly targeting the workers of the [Vale of the Sunless Depths](../../mixed/titles/Vale_of_the_Sunless_Depths.md). The magistrates have asked the incumbent Custodian of the Sunless Depths, **Arkady Morazovich**, to spring the trap and kill the bandits.

If successful, the grateful workers will provide two wains of mithril to **Arkady Morazovich** after the Autumn Equinox.
