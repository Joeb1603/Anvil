## Overview

[Pride](../religion/Pride.md) in small things, [Loyalty](../religion/Loyalty.md) to great ones. That's what they always say when people talk about [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). But Pride in what? And Loyalty to what? That's the sort of thing [people go to war over](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_history.md#the-cousins-war) if you're not careful. There's a lot going on in the Marches on the way to the Summer Solstice, a lot to be proud of but also a lot to worry about. The reward for a good job is more work, and if you want a job done give it to someone who's busy, as they say.

It's time to take stock. Of the situation in [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md); worrisome developments in the Gullet; questions of folk heroes new and especially old; hauntings in [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md); and the overwhelming question of the large number of [heretics](../../mixed/government/synod/Religious_crime.md#heresy) in the Marches (also know as the Marcher [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) population); and the scions of Red Walder who have apparently failed to get the message that *now is not the time*, who are engaging in peculiarly targeted rowdiness that needs nipping in the bud before it spirals out of hand.

So let's start at the beginning, then, with the war in Mitwold...

## Mitwold Triumph

- **The Jotun have abandoned Mitwold in the face of the concerted counter-attack by the Empire**

At the end of last year the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) barbarians [broke into](../winds-of-war/Rose_grow_the_rushes_o.md) the territory of [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md), seizing both [Oddmire](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#oddmire) and [Meade March](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#meade-march) and pressing into [The Meadows](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#the-meadows). At the end of last year, five Imperial armies fought to take back what had been conquered, culminating in the [Battle of Wayford](../winds-of-war/Where_the_slow_river_flows.md#the-battle-of-wayford) and the magical chaos that accompanied it. They reclaimed the Meadows, and drove the Lasambrian armies back across Meade March, but it was clear that the orcs intended to fight to keep what they had taken. Only the absence of the Jotun armies that had withdrawn north to [Skallahn](../barbarians/Skallahn.md) allowed the Empire their hard-fought victory. It was clear that if the Jotun contributed more of their forces to defend Mitwold - or to press east - Imperial armies would have a major fight on their hands.

The [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md) doubled down, with the [Drakes](../../mixed/government/military/Drakes.md) (of course) at the forefront, supported by the [Autumn Hammers](../../mixed/government/military/Autumn_Hammers.md), the [Bloodcloaks](../../mixed/government/military/Bloodcloaks.md), the [Green Shield](../../mixed/government/military/Green_Shield.md), the [Valiant Pegasus](../../mixed/government/military/Valiant_Pegasus.md), and the [Golden Sun](../../mixed/government/military/Golden_Sun.md). More than ten thousand force of strength joined the Drakes, who also marched with the sungold banners of the [Knights of Glory](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Knights_of_Glory.md) at their side, as did the Green Shield. With the [garrison](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#garrison) of [Forte Fidelis](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#forte-fidelis) and the [beaters](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#beaters) of [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) beside them, they enacted a split strategy. While the Autumn Hammers and the Bloodcloaks took a defensive stance, the remaining four armies thrust into the heart of the Jotun held territory, seeking to force them back and reclaim the city of Meade and Odd's End.

They discovered the Lasambrian Jotun in full retreat. They had already abandoned their defence of Meade, and forced-marched westward back into Bregasland. From there, they swiftly withdrew south into [Liathaven](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md) and there Imperial scouts lost track of them. The folk of the Marches might characterise their withdrawal as a cowardly rout, claiming they fled with their tails between their legs, but this is far from the truth. *Abott Matilda* of [Becktunn Monastery](Old_ways.md#the-ambition-of-margaret-of-meade) told a different story. It was, she said, not as if the Lasambrians were running *from* something, but as if they were desperately running *toward* something. The most logical assumption is that news of the Iron Confederacy invasion of Reinos had reached them and they were returning to protect their homes.

Not every Jotun fled; some warbands stayed behind intentionally to seek out "a ruck" as the Marchers put it. Others were a little slow to get back across the borders with their ill-gotten loot. Yet the victory of the Empire was bought with a few skirmishes, rather than anything approaching a full-scale battle. On the one hand, few lives lost. A victory is a victory for the Marchers, land is land after-all - but some of their allies grumble that a triumph because the other side ran away is hardly heroic or glorious.

### Tom Drake's Pride

|  |
| --- |
| **Walls and Statue** |
| **Commission Type:** Edifice |
| **Location:** Meade March, Mitwold |
| **Cost:** 25 white granite, and 75 crowns in labour |
| **Time:** 3 months |
| **Effect:** • Repairs the walls of Meade and creates a statue of Tom Drake in the heart of the city <br> • Improves the *Drum Up Support* order available to the Drakes |
| **Availability:** One year. Until the end of the Spring Equinox 389YE, or until the general of the Drakes breaks their oath |
| **Special:** This commission can be undertaken by the Mistress of Monuments despite the amount of white granite needed |

- **The people of Meade are Proud that their army and their allies have defeated the Jotun and liberated Meade**
- **An opportunity exists for the people of Meade to show their appreciation for the Drakes**
- **Investing in swift repair of the walls, and a statue of Tom Drake, will improve the *Drum up Support* order available to the Drakes**

The *Corazón* and the Jotun champions between them have looted the heart and soul of the Marches, the city of Meade. They haven't gone out of their way to harm the city, or the people, because that isn't the Jotun way. But they haven't been shy about taking anything of value they can get their hands on. They raided the markets and the warehouses, tore open vaults, took any jewels they could find. They didn't go out of their way to steal old relics or documents - again not the Jotun way - and they've been scrupulous about avoiding anything that might be a burial ground. But the wealth of the city has been decimated. But not ruined.

The people of Meade are angry because they've been robbed and burgled, at swordpoint. But they are overjoyed to see the Imperial armies, with the Drakes marching before them. Tom Drake's beloved army, marching to free Meade from occupation, causes folk to put aside their cares and come out to line the walls and cheer, an inverse rough music. The soldiers that come to Meade, Marcher and otherwise, are showered not with rose petals but with food and drink and comfortable beds. They've lost almost everything valuable, but they've kept what is most precious to them - the Marcher spirit that no bandit can ever steal and no thief ever take from them.

The walls of Meade are broken in places, the gates destroyed, and the scars of the fight to defend the city from the Jotun are still visible. But walls, and gates, can be rebuilt, just as Prosperity can. Because the free people of the Marches can recover from any setback - especially when they have an example like Tom Drake's army to fill them with Pride. As in the Mournwold, as in Bregasland, the people of Mitwold take great Pride in the Loyalty shown them by the general of "their" army, and they are not shy about showing it.

The [alders](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_economic_interests.md) of Meade don't come out and say it, but this is a great opportunity for the general of the Drakes to make a mark in history. Assessing the damage to the walls, it will require 16 wains of white granite to repair them; yet the alders also suggest that it is about time that someone built a proper statue commemorating the Drakes in Meade, or more specifically its founder, Tom Drake. Nothing too extravagant - 9 wains ought to be enough to make a nice sculpture you could take the kiddies to see. The repairs and the statue represent a single [commission](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) that costs 25 white granite and 75 crowns in labour, and would take three months to complete.

Despite the amount of white granite, the commission *could* be overseen by the [Mistress of Monuments](../../mixed/titles/Mistress_of_Monuments.md), **Ada Droon Drifting Bough**. The actual statue of Tom Drake is the heart of the commission, the repairs to the city are vital but easily done once the materials are on hand. If the Mistress were to commission the repairs and the statue, the grateful folk of Meade would provide all the labour needed for the entire project - removing the requirement to provide any crowns.

Once the statue and repairs were complete, it would also stand as a monument to the Pride and Loyalty the people of the Marches feel towards their armies. As long as the statue stood and remained in Imperial hands, the *Drum Up Support* order available to the Drakes would be significantly enhanced, providing a total of 500 natural resupply whenever they took the order in Marcher territory.

| **Availability:** One year, until the start of the Summer Solstice 389YE |

- **The Empire could appraise the creation of a foot-the-ball league to restore the sport's prestige**

Meade was looted, but the Jotun had a purpose for that wealth beyond mere theft. Last season there was a report of a [treasure train](../winds-of-war/Where_the_slow_river_flows.md#battle-opportunity-plundered-gold) full of stolen treasures from Meade and Wayford including [Beater trophy](../winds-of-war/Where_the_slow_river_flows.md#objective-recover-the-trophy), a symbol of Marcher pride. It's doubtful the Lasambrians realised what they had - it was just a shiny cup to them - but it's struck to the heart of what foot-the-ball means to people of Mitwold (and Upwold, truth be told). The foot-the-ball season has been cut short; even with the Jotun gone there's less enthusiasm for the game than there has been in a while. "*We were robbed,*" as one of the Wayford [alders](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_economic_interests.md) put it, "*and we can make more coin. But we can't make another Beater's cup.*"

Which is fine as a sentiment if it were *true*. The Marchers certainly can't replace the history and legacy of the Beater's cup, but they surely could order a new trophy. Truth be told the old one was widely regarded as somewhat cheap and tawdry in appearance. But what can be done about the enthusiasm for the foot-the-ball? Would some kind of grand league help? The League have their great game after all, but the problem with that, as one Marcher points out, is that all that messing around doing politics is neither *great* nor a *game*. Maybe an appraisal is the answer.

## Worrisome Gullet

- **The Jotun gave the stolen loot as tribute to the Fjorknae orcs**
- **Orc warships have turned up in the Gullet**
- **They are interfering with Marcher and Wintermark fleets**

While sporting matters are a concern for the Marches, a bigger concern are the reports of exactly what the Corazón *did* with the treasure. There weren't many eyewitnesses, but it seems that the reason they were [dragging it on a sled](../winds-of-war/Where_the_slow_river_flows.md#objective-return-the-bounty-of-meade) to make some formal gift to the folk of [Fjorknae](../world/Sea_of_Snow.md#fjorknae). They met an honest-to-goodness fleet of ships at [Steward's Landing](By_fountains-_by_fishes.md#stewards-landing) and handed over the [runebox](../../out-of-character/game-design/Technology.md#runeboxes-and-runebound-chains) full of stolen loot to what are described as "very nasty looking orcs in scale and chain". Marchers aren't great at judging such things in general, but the witnesses are confident that these orcs had *ships* and not *boats*. Jotun with ships? Is such a thing even possible?

Not long before the Summer Solstice, it becomes clear that such a thing is indeed possible. Marcher captains engaged in [raiding Kalsea's coast](../../mixed/economy/Voyage.md#raid-kalseas-coastline), and Winterfolk providing an [escort to the fisherfolk of Westerhal](../../mixed/economy/Voyage.md#escort-the-fisherfolk-of-westerhal) report fast moving ships with uniformly blood-red sails, crewed by armed and armoured orcs. Orcs who are definitely *not* Jotun thralls. The assumption is that they are Jotun, but the mere fact they seem to be at home on the sea raises questions as to whether they are true Jotun or represent some other sept the orcs have drawn on. They attack Marcher raiders, and engage with the Winterfolk escort, with clear enthusiasm for the fray that suggests a Jotun connection but they are utterly fearless of the sea of the Gullet. They even give the crews of captured or surrendered ships a version of "the Choice" - they can either lay down their weapons and become thralls, to be carried back to wherever the ships came from, or they can "fight the sea" - they're given a weapon and thrown overboard. These ships at least seem disinterested in making warriors of their defeated human foes, and there are disturbing reports of *akhlut* in the Gullet trailing behind the Fjorknae vessels that give the Winterfolk in particular pause.

Following the Summer Solstice, every Wintermark or Marcher fleet suffers a -2 rank penalty to *Raid Kalsea's Coastline*, *Escort the Fisherfolk of Westerhal*, or to [trade](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#trading) with the [Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md) port of [Caitun](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#caitun). At the moment the presence of these ships is focused in the Gullet and the northern Sea of Snow - Marcher and Wintermark ships who sail fast and don't stop for anything are able to outrun the raiders.

These orcs on ships are also an unknown quality. A little over a year ago, the Senate commissioned [Graciana i Lòpez i Guerra](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#graciana-i-lòpez-i-guerra) to look into ways that the [Kongegőr](../barbarians/Skallahn.md#the-kongegőr) could [be neutralized](A_bridge_too_far.md). The [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) she identified had a limited shelf-life anyway, but the addition of this new factor renders them moot. The attack on Skallahn brought the Empire within a hair's breadth of claiming [Bjarkey](../barbarians/Skallahn.md#bjarkey), and the northern end of the Kongegőr. The Jotun are now awake to the very real threat of Imperial armies permanently separating their northern and southern Jarldoms.

Controlling either [Bjarkey](../barbarians/Skallahn.md#bjarkey) or [Alsterbo](../barbarians/Hordalant.md#alsterbo) is no longer a guarantee that the Kongegőr can be severed. It would certainly be possible to damage it, or to create a bottle-neck where Jotun forces trying to cross it would need to recover Imperial controlled region at either end, but to be sure of destroying the bridge permanently the Empire would need to control *both* ends. And if the Fjorknae are as comfortable fighting at sea as they appear to be, even that might not be enough.

The biggest obstacle to assessing the threat posed by the Fjorknae is that the Empire knows next to nothing about them. During their efforts to map the Sea of Snow, Imperial fleets did not cross the Blood Straits between Bryadvik and Fjorknae. The Empire knows there is what looks to be a huge island on the other side, and the assumption is that it is Jotun held. No landfall was made, although a handful of small settlements were visible from aboard ship, but they looked rough and primitive - crude buildings with slate walls and turf rooves. There were signs of industry, though, and vivid red flags that matched the colour of the ships' sails. Of course the banners also bore the image of a monstrous sea-beast of some kind, and so far that hasn't been seen on the ships in the Gullet.

There's also the worry raised by some of the gloomier sorts about the *other* problem the Gullet is having at the moment. Weird coral husks, that rise in the depths and come ashore to drag helpless victims into the surf. The Fjorknae are churning up the waters with violence and blood in a way that it has not been disturbed for centuries, if ever. Even the [Asavean](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) attempt to [destroy Meade](../winds-of-war/Harvest_of_infamy.md) was mostly fought on land or in the immediate vicinity of the city. Is it possible that the Fjorknae are going to rile up the coral horrors even more? They have been conspicuous by their absence for a little while now, and nobody is so addled by [hope](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#hope) that they can imagine the problem has just... gone away.

At the moment, the Empire seems to have limited ability to find out more about what is going on in the Gullet and the threat the new orcs represent. A [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) or [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) needs a certain proximity to the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) where it will be built that means Fjorknae is out of reach of the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md). The [Regario Dossier](../../mixed/titles/Regario_Dossier.md) has no agents that could be assigned there - it's hard to imagine what they might look like - and the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md) would have no idea where to start.

The best suggestion as to what *could* be done is an appraisal. Either one to find out more about the Fjorknae, or to deal with the ships in the Gullet. These are two very different appraisals, in the opinion of the [Prognosticator's Office](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#the-prognosticators-office), and to do *both* would take two appraisals. The hylje of the north have made [their own proposals](Beneath_a_moonless_sky.md#with-sermersuaqs-cunning), but these would be complementary. The hylje's proposal would require the re-establishment of the [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) at Tromsa before it could be fully realised. If the spy network was completed in the same season the appraisal took place, it would put the Prognosticator's Office in a perfect position to work with the sealfolk - with the right choice of prognosticator, that is.

The other possibility lies with [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotenatiry) either from an [archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) or given Autumn is coming up, from the [Stone Smith](../../mixed/titles/Stone_Smith.md). The challenge would be working out which [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) to ask about the orcs of Fjorknae and the ships in the Gullet. In the past, the [Archmage of Night](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Night.md) might have been able to invoke the [Caucus Forum](../eternals/Lashonar.md#caucus-forum) to arrange a meeting with someone from the island, but that option is [no longer available](Monarchs_and_outcasts.md#closed-caucus-lashonar). Any other eternal will want something of value in exchange for their aid, assuming they have any to offer in the first place.

## Bregasland

- **The Jotun remain in Bregasland**
- **The Strong Reeds are lying low in the territory, building their strength for a counterattack**
- **The Grey Walkers are spreading through Grey Fens to the chagrin of the invading orcs**

Last season saw serious fighting in [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md), with the Empire ultimately victorious and [Graven Rock](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#graven-rock) recovered from the Jotun. In the wake of their victory, the Empire pulled their troops out. All save the [Strong Reeds](../../mixed/government/military/Strong_Reeds.md), who have slipped back into the fens and the rushes, and now [lie low](../military-council-orders/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Military_Council_orders.md#strong-reeds) waiting for another opportunity to strike. The assumption must have been that the Jotun would immediately move to recover Gravenmarch, but in the end... they don't.

There are still Jotun armies here, but they are braced for further Imperial efforts to reclaim Bregasland. In the absence of such attacks, the barbarians simply continue to consolidate their hold over the marshy territory. Although in the process they encounter problems they themselves have created. At the Spring Equinox, news reached the [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md) of *Sunne Astar* and a [quixotic plan](../winds-of-war/Old_blue_banners_dancing.md#battle-opportunity-a-broken-rock) to drag one of the stones that make up the Ullsmere Rock [dolmen](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Marches_hearth_magic.md#dolmens) back to Hordalant to inspire the Howling Night. The Jotun achieved their plan, and as near as the Strong Reeds can tell, the left leg of the dolmen has already left Bregasland and is presumably being installed in the *kirkje* of the Howling Night.

### Horror at Ullsmere Rock

- **The Grey Walkers haunt Grey Fens and seek to drive out all trespassers**
- **They are treating the Jotun as trespassers**
- **Unfortunately they are also treating everyone *else* as trespassers**

What's unfortunate is that what it has left in its wake are a large number of [Grey Walkers](../winds-of-war/Old_blue_banners_dancing.md#objective-dismiss-the-grey-walkers). Shadow figures, clearly Marchers, wearing garb common in the fens hundreds of year ago, are draped in the tatters of funeral bindings. They slowly and silently seek out "trespassers" and drive them away. Hitting them with weapons causes them to dissipate... but a few minutes later they simple coalesce again a short distance away. They can be [exorcised](../../out-of-character/rules/Exorcism.md) individually, but there are a *lot* of them. Worse, some of them are clad for battle, deliver potent [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md), and are much harder to deal with than the "lesser" spirits.

The good news is that right now, they are causing serious issues for the Jotun in [Grey Fen](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#grey-fen). The entire region has become [haunted](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#haunted) as the Grey Walkers have spread. The Jotun are no stranger to ghosts, but the ability of their warriors and *ghodi* to deal with them is significantly lower than that of the Empire. The Grey Walkers are treating the orcs as intruders and trespassers, and slowly eroding the barbarian's control over the entire region. They don't seem to be extending beyond the Grey Fens themselves, at least, but that covers a very large area of marsh. There are Marcher households there, and they are likewise reporting harassment by these spectral creatures. True to form, the Jotun are actively *protecting* the households - just as they would any other thralls - but it's clear that they're struggling to keep a firm grasp on the Fens.

### Get Off My Fen

- **The Jotun are slowly losing their grip on Grey Fen**
- **While this is causing problems for the Jotun, it will also be a problem for the Empire should they retake Bregasland**

Something clearly needs to be done, but Grey Fens is currently a Jotun problem. If the Empire retakes Bregasland, however, it's going to become a Marcher problem pretty quickly. While Grey Fens remains under Jotun control, the Grey Walkers are a tenth of the way towards taking the region themselves. What this will mean is impossible to say - but if the region does fall under control of these spectral walkers the one thing it *won't* be is under Imperial control, making it that much harder for anyone to control the territory.

There is an effective force of 1,000 strength in Grey Fens - each season it will attempt to take control of the region. *Any* army that issues an appropriate [defending](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#defending) order in the territory can prevent the ghosts gaining any headway, and an attacking order will drive the ghosts back and reassert control over the region (as long as it achieves sufficient victory points). But while the *haunted* quality remains, the ghosts will simply come back next season, and the season after, and keep trying to drive everyone out of Grey Fens. They don't take casualties (although they do inflict them), and anyone actively trying to stop them claiming Grey Fens will not be able to resupply that season.

Notably, the presence of these Grey Walkers is even messing with the magical elements of the territory. For some reason, any magical ward created in Grey Fens - [Dripping Echoes of the Fen](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Dripping_Echoes_of_the_Fen.md) for example - will not impede the ghosts at all. They will continue to slowly but inexorably try to usurp control of the region, even as the enchantment has its normal effects for everyone else.

## Pride and Politics

*[Image: William Guildenstern , General of the Drakes](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:William_Guildenstern.jpg)*

- **Two judgements of the Marcher assembly related to Pride in a way that resonates with the Marchers**

Recently the Marcher Assembly passed two big judgements. The first was about Pride, celebrating a folk hero. It's received a broad welcome across the nation, a bit of good news in dark times. But people can't be Proud of someone unless they know what they're Proud of them *for*. So they want to know what this folk hero has done to be singled out like that.

The second judgement is also a matter of Pride. Nearly two years ago now the [Pride](../religion/Pride.md) Assembly [recognised](../../mixed/government/synod/Recognition.md) the leaders of [the Ore Hills Rebellion](../nations/the-marches/The_Ore_Hills_Rebellion.md) and then the [General Assembly narrowly agreed](../synod-judgements/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-5). Now it's down to the Pride Assembly to see if they want to consecrate Rebellion's Ridge the new monument they've just built to remember folks by.

If that happens, it will be a lit match in a dry field. All the arguments about just who should be appointing senators will come boiling back to the surface.

### Holding Out For a Hero

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > William Guildenstern was recognised as a Marcher Folk Hero in 384YE. As my cousin Pipit Boon called out to our people in 385YE; "Let us sing his story for generations to come, across our territories." There is no better time than these - our homes under siege and our territories dividing - to draw Marchers to great Marcher deeds. As a Folk Hero walks among us as General of the Drakes, remember: Jonah Gold made weapons so fierce it turned a war's tide. Odd of Odd's End drained the Oddmire against the Marshwalker threat. Tom Drake struck down Alderei the Fair. William Guildenstern caught a falling star in his chest. All are Marchers, all fought our battles, all did deeds of great renown, all had rivals who would see them fail, and all are remembered in song.
> >
> > Jedediah Boon, Marcher National Assembly, Spring 388YE, Vote: Upheld with a **Greater Majority** 152-20

- **William Guildenstern has been recognised as a folk hero by the Marcher Assembly**
- **The Marches are eager to hear what act or acts he has done to be inspired by**
- **It is unclear what being recognised as a folk hero will actually *do*, it will depend on what the act is**

Most Marchers hold three things close to them: a favourite [proverb](../nations/the-marches/Proverbs.md), a patch of land they call home, and someone they look up to. Proverbs are important and an apt demonstration of [Wisdom](../religion/Wisdom.md); they allow an important idea to be communicated with as few words as possible. A patch of land to call home is the foundation of every [Marcher household](../nations/the-marches/Marcher_household.md), the starting place for [Loyalty](../religion/Loyalty.md). But someone to look up to can be important too; it lets people take [Pride](../religion/Pride.md) in the actions of their fellow Marchers, and inspires them to do great deeds themselves.

Last season, the Marcher Assembly upheld a [judgement](../synod-judgements/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-81) raised by **Jedediah Boon** which echoed the [call](../synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-115) from **Pipit Boon** that **William Guildenstern**, General of the [Drakes](../../mixed/government/military/Drakes.md) and former Senator for [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md), was a folk hero. The statement of principle called out several other folk heroes from the history of the Marches and the inspirational acts they had done. People are keen to hear more about this folk hero - and Friar Alyn asks that the Marcher Assembly use the coming summit to recognise the thing William Guildenstern has done that is most inspiring. Something that has been done that the Marchers can be inspired by and rally behind. When tales of Jonah Gold were spread of making weapons, it inspired the blacksmiths to focus on making blades for soldiers; when stories of Odd of Odd's End were spread, it led to beaters tracking marshwalkers and protecting settlements from them. If Guildenstern has done something that people be Proud of and rally behind it just [do some good](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md).

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Inspirations are a lot like wheat. You need to harvest them when you can, and be aware that they might turn rotten.
> >
> > Friar Alyn of Hay

Some nations have annals or chronicles to record their inspirations. Some assemblies have rolls or walls to mark theirs. *Friar Alyn of Hay* pours scorn on that idea. Marchers don't need to keep records of their folk heroes - what's the bloody point? If you have to write their name down to know who they are, then they're not much of a bloody hero are they? In the opinion of the friar, the best inspirations are fleeting. They represent the zeitgeist of the Marches at the height of summer, when the sun is high and there is time to have the proper chats without worrying about the weather.

### Rebellion's Ridge

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Three years ago, Meri stated on my behalf that we knew the importance of all Marchers working the land, that the Nation must consider the plight simmering over Mourn hillsides. The Pride Assembly agreed. The miners of the Mourn have worked their land for us. They have battled the Jotun, fought off the Howling Bear. They raised rebellions against tyranny twice, were the last ones standing against recent invaders. They work tirelessly to supply our armies, to build our Great Works, braving the Singing Caves and tearing its bounty from enemy hands. They have stood with our Nation since Marcher boots first met Mourn soil. So we too stand with them - we will not turn our backs another year not take their Loyalty to the Marchers, the Pride in their work for granted. We recognise their right to a voice in the leadership of the Mournwold, where the ore runs deep and so many of us have fought together, shoulder to shoulder.
> >
> > Jedediah Boon, Marcher National Assembly, Autumn 387YE, Vote: Upheld with a **Greater Majority** 208-22

- **Rebellion's Ridge has been completed in the Mournwold**
- **A writ of consecration for the Leaders of the Ore Hills Rebellion on the great work would have consequences**
- **It would breathe new life into old arguments, long since buried, about who runs the Mournwold**

During the Autumn Equinox 387YE, **Alfred Aewulfson-Boon**, as the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), commissioned the [construction](../senate-motions/Construct_Rebellion-s_Ridge.md) of Rebellion's Ridge. A [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) in [Ore Hills](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#ore-hills) dedicated to the [leaders of the Ore Hills Rebellion](../../mixed/government/synod/Known_exemplars.md#leaders-of-the-ore-hills-rebellion). These exemplars of [Pride](../religion/Pride.md) were [recognised](../synod-judgements/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-5) during the Winter Solstice 386YE as a group, in much the same way as [the Marked](../religion/The_Marked.md). The exemplars were instrumental in [the Ore Hills Uprising](../nations/the-marches/The_Ore_Hills_Rebellion.md), and were identified as ringleaders and executed by the magistrates for their role in inciting, and in most cases participating in, the violence that marred Mournwold.

Now, [Rebellion's Ridge](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#rebellions-ridge) stands completed. The finishing touches have been made, and the [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) of the Mourn will receive their first doses of [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) at the Autumn Equinox. But what next? During the Autumn Equinox 387YE, the Marcher Assembly voted to uphold a [judgement](../synod-judgements/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-98) raised by **Jedediah Boon** which drew attention to the plight of the miners of the Mourn.

*[Image: Resentment between miners and farmers in Mournwold had been simmering for almost three centuries, until it finally kicked off in 326YE.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:The_Versatile_Pickaxe.jpg)*

If the Assembly chooses to pass a [writ of consecration](../../mixed/government/synod/Writ_of_consecration.md) for Rebellion's Ridge, then it would inevitably reopen those old wounds. Miners work every bit as hard as farmers, nobody denies that, but they have no say in who speaks for them. That's what the Ore Hills Rebellion was all about. Power, who has it, and who doesn't. The miners demanded that the yeofolk share their power. When the yeofolk refused to let their power be diluted and their traditions lost, it ultimately resulted in bloodshed.

Folks are full of fond memories for the Rebellion now, but it was the most bitter fight imaginable at the time. Any move to consecrate Rebellion's Ridge will force the Mournwold to reckon with that struggle. You simply cannot draw inspiration from a bunch of Marchers who died fighting for something they believed in, without making everyone ask *what were they fighting for?*. It'll fire up every single mine owner in the Mournwold with fresh demands for a change in how the the territory appoints its senator. And it won't just be the yeofolk who are worried - it'll make every Marcher who thinks traditions are important, worried about where all this is going.

Seriously contemplating a change in how the Mournwold elects its senator is *absolutely guaranteed* to open a sack full of angry kittens.

## Loyalty and Heresy

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The scars of Whittle run deep. A people massacred due to zealotry. The Marchers know too well the risks of following your traditions. While the Marchers have no intent of undermining the Synod’s spiritual authority, the fact of the matter remains; A Marcher, A Marcher. Should the Synod proscribe the beliefs of the Marcher Orcs as heretical, the Marches as a whole will defend their neighbours as they would any other Marcher unduly persecuted. To echo the words of the First Empress: “You will take all of us, or none of us.” The Synod may make its choice.
> >
> > Statement of Principle, Brother Luke, Marcher Assembly, Winter Solstice 388YE, Vote: Greater Majority 146-50

- **The priests of the Marches preach of Pride, inspiring both human and orc**
- **The folk of the Marches are mostly united - the Marcher orcs are Marchers**

And talking of lit matches... At the Spring Equinox, the Marcher Assembly voted to uphold a [judgement](../synod-judgements/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-13) raised by **Brother Luke** with a [greater majority](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority). It called on the Marches to defend the [Marcher orcs](../world/Mournwold_Orcs.md) if things came to a head with the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md). After the Summer Solstice, inspired by the statement, more than half the [congregations](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) in the Marches preached the importance of supporting the Marcher Orcs, and that any attack on them was the same as an attack on anyone else in the nation. "*Pride in small things,*" as the saying goes. The congregations who took this action used their liao to create suitable [auras](../../mixed/magic/lore/Aura.md), but in return received gifts of recognition and support from Marcher Orcs and their allies.

It's been less direct than a mandate, but it's had the desired effect. If there was any doubt as to whether the Marcher Orcs are Marchers or not, it's been dispelled. If the Empire sends anyone to attack the Marcher Orcs for their beliefs, whether that be armies or magistrates, the folk of the Marches will resist. The argument that their beliefs mark them as heretics butters no mustard in Mournwold or elsewhere. The nation *knew* they believed in reincarnation when they embraced them; the [egregore](../../out-of-character/crew/Egregores.md) *knew* their beliefs were at odds with doctrine when they recognised them as Marchers. They are Marcher folk, and the general consensus in the Marches is that their belief in reincarnation is not a problem in a nation that firmly believes humans can be reincarnated as trees, or vermin.

When the Generals of the Marches swore their oaths to protect their territories, there was no equivocation about whether they would protect some Marchers and not others. If the Synod moves against the Marcher Orcs, the Marches will resist. With weapons to protect those who won't take them up themselves, if need be. And they will expect the generals to honour their oaths, and if need be fight the armies of the Empire to defend an attack against their own.

Not everyone is on board, but those who are not have the good sense to keep quiet about it. When challenged to explain how the beliefs of the Marcher Orcs can be reconciled with doctrine, one pugnacious Abbott turns the question around, and demands to know why nobody ever asks how Doctrine can be reconciled with the beliefs of the Marcher orcs? The *only* thing that brands the Marcher Orcs as heretics is the [Doctrine of the Howling Abyss](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md#the-doctrine-of-the-howling-abyss) that was created less than a decade ago based solely on the spiritual beliefs of the [Unshackled](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) - and a belief that is challenged by septs of orcs all over the world.

### The Tom Drake Revelation

*[Image: Hazelelponi of the Shattered Tower is the controversial Lepidean Librarian who commissioned the investigation into the life of Tom Drake.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Hazelelponi.jpg)*

- **Recent revelations regarding Tom Drake incline the folk of the Marches to view the state bodies of the Empire with scepticism**
- **Inspired by Pride and Loyalty, the human Marchers will defend the orc Marchers**

The Marchers as a whole have no patience for people who give themselves airs and graces, but they take a quiet Pride in a number of folk from [history](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_history.md). Among those they are proudest of is [Tom Drake](../nations/varushka/Life_of_Tom_Drake.md), the original general of the Drakes who helped put the Military Council together. Recently, the [Lepidean Librarian](../../mixed/titles/Lepidean_Librarian.md) undertook an examination of [Tom's life](../nations/varushka/Life_of_Tom_Drake.md), and the truths contained within were [at odds](../nations/varushka/Life_of_Tom_Drake.md#unacceptable-history) with the popular historical version. Tom Drake fought *against* Brigit of Dourfell, and led the rebels. He was rightly recognised as an exemplar of [Loyalty](../religion/Loyalty.md), always willing to show it to those who deserved it, to the Marches, and to its people.

It's been quiet, but these historical revelations have spread quietly across the Marches and increased Marcher scepticism of what some see as "Imperial excesses". It's nothing like as dramatic as the problems in [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) in the wake of the Fishers - but there is a quiet, grass-roots belief that the Marcher dislike of people putting themselves above their peers extends to *everyone*. Including, arguably, the Senate and the Synod. When push comes to shove, they ask themselves what Tom Drake would do, and the answer is usually some variation of "stand up and be counted", "be Proud and Loyal", and "tell the Empire to go... take itself away... if it comes to it."

### What This Means

- **The Empire risks a second Cousin's War if they persecute the Marcher Orcs for their beliefs**

With these two key factors in mind, the only predictable outcome is that Marchers will defend their orc citizens against the Synod, the magistrates, or Imperial armies. What form this will take is not clear, it will depend on exactly what is tried. There are tens of thousands of Marcher orcs. Their willingness to pitch in, the fact they are never slow to contribute when there's a big project on, and their sheer *enthusiasm* for the Marches has won them a lot of friends even before the priests of the Marches spoke of the Pride of having them among their number. Then there's the lessons of Tom Drake, exemplar of Loyalty, who would *never* stand by and let the Empire declare war on his neighbours (or at least that's the current feeling).

This is obviously a problem. For an Imperial nation to reject the rule of law in this fashion - the Synod has the power to declare something heresy, which is a crime. The magistrates are expected to deal with criminals under the law.

What seems clear is that any move to condemn the Marcher Orcs as heretics is likely to trigger a conflict along the lines of the [Cousin's War](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_history.md#the-cousins-war), albeit with most of the Marches lining up against the rest of the Empire, and with those Marchers who regularly visit [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md) to represent their nation potentially caught in the middle.

### Pragmatism

- **Some Mournwold priests have proposed a "solution" to the problem**

So far only one positive proposal has come out, and that's from the Marcher Orc spiritual leader and a couple of his old friends among the priests of the Mournwold. He proposes that while the Synod determines what is a crime, it's the Senate and the magistrates who decide what the punishment is. There are basically two punishments - death and fines. "So fine us," say the Mournwold priests. "Fine the orcs for their heretical beliefs. How about 5 thrones a season that the Marcher Orcs continue to believe in reincarnation, and then maybe we can all get back on with sorting the harvest?" Obviously this is an outrage and the magistrates will do their nut. But a Senate motion to this effect would see 5 thrones a season into the Imperial treasury, and its likely the Marcher Orcs might go higher if they had to especially if their human neighbours pitched in.

While this proposal seem ludicrous, it does highlight at least one potential avenue to try and deal with the problem represented by tens of thousands of Marchers that the Synod seem to deem to be heretics. Ultimately, the Synod does not operate in isolation within the Empire. Nobody is suggesting that the Senate alone can solve this problem, but at the end of the day it is the Senate that has the power to define the laws, and guide the magistrates as to the punishments that arise when those laws are broken. Perhaps some compromise can be reached?
