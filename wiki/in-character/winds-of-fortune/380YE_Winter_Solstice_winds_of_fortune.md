# 380YE Winter Solstice winds of fortune

*[Image: Jigterlude.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Jigterlude.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/380YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md).

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters 'should' know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months.

Many winds of fortune open with a short section of in-character text. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip. They're mostly just a bit of fun and they tend not to contain anything vital to the understanding of the wind of Fortune.

## Winds of Fortune

### A Matter of Faith

- **Everybody**

The Imperial Synod stands on the brink of crisis. There is the very real threat of a catastrophic schism within the Way, as supporters of the vision supported by the Congregation of the First Empress clash with those who believe it to be heresy. You can learn more about this matter of faith [here](Matter_of_faith.md).

### Beware My Power (Winds of Magic)

- **Conclave, assorted magicians, dramaturgists, and astronomancers, citizens of Reikos, Spiral, and the shores of the Semmerlak**

Magic ebbs and flows. A round-up of significant magical effects, some with wider implications, some without, can be found [here](Beware_my_power.md).

### A Business Like Any Other (Trade Winds)

- **Fleet captains, Congregation leaders, Ambassadors**

International trade, like international relations, can be fraught with pitfalls - and rich with opportunities. You can find a quick round up of things related to the current state of affairs, with an emphasis on trading, [here](A_business_like_any_other.md).

### City Folk and Country Folk

- **The Marches, the League (especially Tassato)**

The people of [Whittle](Up_on_Whittle_Hill.md) in the [Mourn](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) are on the move. Following [recent developments](Will_to_survive.md) and the renewed domination of the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) they have left their homes en-masse and travelled south through the [Freemoor hills](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#freemoor) with all their possessions, seeking sanctuary in [Tassato Mestra](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md#tassato-mestra). You can learn more about this situation [here](City_folk_and_country_folk.md).

### Darkest Night

- **Naga, Imperial Conclave especially astronomancers, poets**

After a year of piecemeal disappearances, the stars have finally disappeared from the skies across the *entire* Empire. They've even gone from some non-Imperial territories adjacent to the Empire. Perhaps more importantly, every naga in the Empire is experiencing a powerful urge to keep their cards close to their chests. You can learn more about these eerie magical occurrences [here](Darkest_night.md).

### Death of a Salesman

- **Imperial Bourse, Wintermark, the Brass Coast, Imperial Orcs, Highguard (especially [benefactors](../nations/highguard/Benefactor.md))**

The [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) [private auction](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#access-to-the-private-auction) has been suffering [for a little while now](Disloyal-_unprosperous-_unvigilant-_unwise.md), and at last it has shuffled off this mortal coil. A number of alternatives are already springing up. You can learn about them [here](Death_of_a_salesman.md).

### Forge of Champions

- **Anyone who has challenged the Iron Duke**

The eternal [Barien](../eternals/Barien.md) appears preoccupied, but his lieutenant [Castellan](../eternals/Barien.md#castellan) has let it be known that she plans to visit Anvil during the next Summit to talk to various people about their [challenges](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Challenge_the_Iron_Duke.md). Apparently, the paperwork is in a dreadful state, even for the Summer realm. You can learn more about this [here](Forge_of_champions.md).

### In Brightest Day

- **Everyone, but especially artists, priests of Pride, and artisans**

Powerful magic inspires artists and culture across the Empire. In addition to its purely artistic effect, it also has some implications for priests of [Pride](../religion/Pride.md), and there is a delicious rumour of some sort of announcement from *The Looking Glass* relating to a new schema. You can learn more about the fallout of this magic [here](In_brightest_day.md).

### Into The Woods

- **Temeschwar, the Fortress of Salt**

Something cold and dark has settled over the [Metri](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md#metri) in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md). Gaunt figures appear without warning, slaughtering anyone who tries to bring violence to another citizen. The roads have never been safer, but the woods have never been darker. You can learn more about these events [here](Into_the_woods.md).

### Lantern's Light

- **Imperial Senate, Varushka, Imperial Orcs**

The Stalker has [fallen to earth](Starfall.md). Diverted at the last moment from [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md) to smash into eastern [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md), its supernatural influence has faded. As per their agreement, the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) are offering an arrangement whereby the Empire can share in the bounty of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) it contains... but nothing is every quite that straightforward where the Dragons are concerned. You can learn more about these events [here](Lantern-s_light.md).

### Missionary Work

- **Imperial Synod**

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has [offered an opportunity](../senate-motions/Statement_of_Intent_regarding_providing_missionaries_to_the_Iron_Confederacy.md) to the people of the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) to receive missionaries, to learn of the virtues and the practice of the Way. Some Suranni have asked to learn more, and now it is up to the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) how they wish to respond. You can learn more about this opportunity [here](Missionary_work.md).

### No Evil Shall Escape Our Sight

- **Imperial Synod, Vigilance Assembly**

The Silent Bell are an [Imperial Sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) associated with the [Vigilance](../religion/Vigilance.md) [Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md) who investigate internal threats to the Empire. They are looking to formalise their arrangement with the Synod, which you can learn about [here](No_evil_shall_escape_our_sight.md).

### Serpent's Tooth

- **Military Council, Imperial Senate, Dawn**

The [Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md), the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), and the situation in the east. An offer to negotiate peace from the orcs of the Mallum. You can learn more about this diplomatic opportunity [here](Serpent-s_tooth.md).

### Symposium of Revelation

- **Imperial Synod**

A [Symposium](Symposium.md) is due to be held at the Winter Solstice. The Imperial Synod has been planning the event for six months, which will bring priests and theologians from all over the world to Anvil to discuss pressing matters of doctrine, faith, and the future path of the Way. You can learn more about the Symposium [here](Symposium_of_Revelation.md).

### Tale of the Golden Hare

- **Varushka**

The [Virtuous Animals](../nations/varushka/Varushka_religious_beliefs.md#the-virtuous-animals) are a significant element of the spiritual life of the [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) people. Thanks to the [National Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md), and the actions of a certain Fellowship, a new candidate for inclusion in the lexicon of virtue has presented itself - the golden hare. You can learn about this opportunity [here](Tale_of_the_Golden_Hare.md).

### The Widening Gyre

- **Wintermark, Imperial Senate**

Dogri Thulebane, renegade leader of the [Winterfolk](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) of [Crow's Ridge](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#crows-ridge) is preparing something, the impact of which may be felt across the northern Empire and beyond. You can learn more about it and the opportunities it presents [here](The_widening_gyre.md).

### Those Who Worship

- **The Brass Coast, Imperial Synod, Imperial Senate**

The [Asavean](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) [temple](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#the-temple-of-balo-and-the-black-bull) in [Oran](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oranseri) is nearing completion despite some opposition from the Imperial Synod. As the structure is now largely complete, it may be time to consider the opportunities presented by the Asavean counter-offer to build a temple in Nemoria. At the same time, the idolatrous priest responsible for overseeing the structure, and his team of architects and builders, are looking for something challenging to occupy their time. You can learn more about these developments [here](Those_who_worship.md).

### The Witches of Applefell

- **Dawn, Weirwater Vales**

In [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md), bandits have attacked a caravan of weirwood near the town of [Applefell](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md#applefell), apparently in alliance with a band of renegade witches. This is the latest in a line of escalating problems with witches in southern Weirwater. You can learn more about this problem [here](The_Witches_of_Applefell.md).

## Ongoing Events and Resolutions

### The Other Empress

- **The League, Highguard, Imperial Senate**

Following a [Senate motion](../senate-motions/Construct_tomb_for_Empress_Giselle.md) proposed by Senator Gant Archama, the sarcophagus of [Empress Giselle](../history/Empress_Giselle.md) has been moved to [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) where it is awaiting the completion of the new tomb. It is being stored in one of the disused dungeons beneath Ratibor's Gate, the old prison now used by the civil service.

### The Crumbling Court of the Fountain

- **Urizen**

The [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) that has been degrading the [Court of the White Fountain](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#the-court-of-the-white-fountain) in [Optarion](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#optarion) has finally run its course, but not before one final season of attacking the [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md). Shortly before the Winter Solstice, the fire that gives the fortification its name suddenly flares up, as bright and powerful as ever - although the white stone that comprises the tower still has several cracks that will need to be repaired. Now that the curse is over, the Court will begin to recover normally - assuming no more restorative magic is brought to bear by magicians from the Spire of the Auric Horizon, or that no Grendel attack it in the coming months.

### The Creeping Morass

- **Holberg, Imperial Senate**

[Ennerlund](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#ennerlund) has completed its slow transformation into a marsh. It joins [Rebeshof](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#rebeshof) and the [Morass](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#the-morass) to form one of the largest marshes outside of [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md) and [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) in the west. The production of all farms in the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) has been permanently reduced by 36 rings, and the [Great Pits of Ennerlund](../../mixed/titles/Great_Pits_of_Ennerlund.md) will now need additional pumps and precautions, permanently increasing the upkeep of this important Bourse resource by 2 Thrones per season.

### Song of the Lakefolk

- **Wintermark**

Raids by [strange hunters](Song_of_the_lakefolk.md) continue to disrupt [businesses](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) and [farms](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md). At the Autumn Equinox the [Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md) of [Wisdom](../religion/Wisdom.md), with some support from the Wintermark National Assembly, resolved to take the cautious route of gathering more information. This means that Savik Summerstorm is coordinating efforts in the area to learn more about this situation, and is the person to talk to.

### Lashonar and the Song of Peace

- **Dawn**

The [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Lashonar](../eternals/Lashonar.md) has been [interfering musically](Peace_song.md) with the armies of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md). The [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) decided to strip Lashonar of its [amity](../eternals/Alignment.md) - although they did not declare enmity. At the same time, the National Assembly of Dawn stridently opposed the message of peace being spread by the agents of the eternal. Lady Tamain Sepulchre was named by the Assembly to directly oppose the influence of the Night eternal, and was supplied with a hundred [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) to support their efforts. Lady Tamain composed and popularised [The Heralds of Lashonar](../../mixed/music/The_Heralds_of_Lashonar.md) in order to combat the insidious threat posed by the eternal. As a consequence, while the effects of Lashonar's heralds continued to be felt during the last few months, they have been entirely countered by the start of the Winter Solstice.

### Castle of Wind and Sky

- **Varushka, Imperial Conclave**

The [disruptive weather](Castle_of_wind_and_sky.md) plaguing the [Icy Crag of the Eternal Sun](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Ice_and_Darkness.md#the-icy-crag-of-the-eternal-sun) shows no sign of dissipating. The upkeep of the [College of Magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) continues to be artificially high.

### Blood Red Roads

- **The League**

Work on the [Blood Red Roads](../senate-motions/Construct_the_Blood_Red_Roads.md) continues. The project still has many months to go before the four cities of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) are linked by wide stone roads.

### The Orcs of Therunin

- **Navarr**

The [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of the Great Forest, seeking sanctuary from [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md), continue to occupy the [Lower Tarn Valley](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#lower-tarn-valley) in [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md). The situation has not changed very much from where it was [last season](When_all_this_was_trees.md). The [Senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for Therunin, Caerwyn Summer Crow, provided the orcs with significant support in the form of money and supplies, but the matter of land is still uppermost in the thoughts of many of the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) of the south-east.

### Krampus Tales

- **Wintermark**

The Krampus continues to [act erratically](Goatfoot.md). There has been no movement on constructing a home for the supernatural creature in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md). As it is winter, the spirit is due to appear at Anvil to hear the sins of the [Winterfolk](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) - but it is not clear whether its "allegiance" is still with Wintermark, or the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md), or the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md).
