# 381YE Autumn Equinox winds of fortune

*[Image: Tables of War.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Tables_of_War.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/381YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md).

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters 'should' know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months.

Many winds of fortune open with a short section of in-character text. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip. They're mostly just a bit of fun and they tend not to contain anything vital to the understanding of the wind of Fortune.

## Wind of Fortune

### An Apple That Falls

- **The Mournwold**

The situation in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) remains complicated. There are any number of things that *could* be done, but what things *will* be done? What *should* be done, for that matter? You can find out more about this double-length opportunity [here](An_apple_that_falls.md).

### The Art of Restraining Power (Trade Winds)

- **Fleet captains; [congregation](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) leaders; ambassadors and diplomats**

The Empire is not the only power in the world. Trade is a big part of it - but there is much more to trade than simply moving goods from one place to another. You can find a quick round up of things related to the current state of affairs, with an emphasis on trading - and a digression into the state of international relations - [here](The_art_of_restraining_power.md).

### Birds and Bats

- **Imperial Conclave; writers of love letters; tea houses**

Two eternals of the realm of Night have sent messengers to speak with the civil service. [Lashonar](../eternals/Lashonar.md) has sent a sonnet that outlines an invitation to participate in a competition. [Sadogua](../eternals/Sadogua.md) has sent a little girl to politely request a formal parley with the Conclave. You can learn about both events [here](Birds_and_bats.md).

### Desperate Affairs, Desperate Measures

- **The Brass Coast; [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) captains of all nations**

Over the last several months, the [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) pirates of the Broken Shore have shown more than usual organisation. They launched two vicious raids against the people of the Bay of Catazar - first to try and [attack the fisherfolk](Down_along_the_cove.md), the second to try and [enslave the people](Shelter_from_the_storm.md) of the Isle of Zemress. The captains of the Imperial fleets have not stood idly by and instead launched counter-raids of their own against the dastardly southern orcs. You can learn about the outcomes, and a few new developments [here](Desperate_affairs-_desperate_measures.md).

### Empty Handed

- **Wealthy folks, especially those with an interest in peculiar rarities.**

Since the demise of the Bourse Private Auction, entrepreneurs and the desperate alike have made their way to Anvil seeking to tap the rich markets that may be found there. Markets such as the traditional [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [Winter Market](../nations/varushka/Varushka_culture_and_customs.md#the-winter-market), which takes place during the Autumn Equinox. This season, there is news of a fallen star in the Marches; an antique artefact belonging to a famous Dawnish enchanter; a rare tea recipe and a herald associated with the Night realm; and some peculiar potions belonging to the Great Forest Orcs. You can learn about these opportunities [here](Empty_handed.md).

### Hunters and wolves

- **Imperial citizens; Marchers; and magicians**

Three Winter eternals are involved in activity within the Empire. The grim eternal [Sorin](../eternals/Sorin.md) seeks participants in a trial of hard choices and suffering, The Bound-King [Tharim](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#tharim) of [the Thrice-cursed Court](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md) has sent his heralds to ... well nobody knows. And a crazed follower of [Agramant](../eternals/Agramant.md) has been attempting to spread chaos and discord through the Empire. You can learn more about these eternal-related opportunities [here](Hunters_and_wolves.md).

### More Love, Less Paperwork

- **The League assembly; the General Assembly; the Virtue Assemblies of Ambition, Loyalty, Pride, and Prosperity**

Lorenzo “La Volpe” Macelliao von Temeschwar, Master of the Butchers Bank, Treasurer of the Bloody Butchers of [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), former custodian of the [Fortress of Salt](../../mixed/titles/Fortress_of_Salt.md), former overseer of the [Regario Dossier](../../mixed/titles/Regario_Dossier.md), former [Civilian Commissioner](../../mixed/government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) for [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md), and architect - or at least financier - for a significant number of interesting things made out of stone. An inspiration to many, his name associated with a number of [great works](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md). Philanthropist, merchant-prince, visionary. You can learn a little about why his name is on everyone's lips [here](More_love-_less_paperwork.md).

### The Mother of Morality

- **Wintermark, Brass Coast, and League**
- **This wind of fortune is a counterpart to [praise and blame](Praise_and_blame.md)**

The infamously cruel [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) army continue to be divisive. The [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) assemblies have unambiguously rejected them. The priests of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) have spoken out against the use of [fear](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#fear) as a weapon - although to be fair their assembly was far from united in that condemnation. Several significant opportunities have arisen as a result of the Synod's ongoing discussion of what is, or is not, too high a price to pay for victory. You can learn more about some of them [here](The_mother_of_morality.md).

### Out of Void, Out of Chaos (Conjunction)

- **Primarily magicians versed in the lore of [night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md)**

In the night sky, [the Wanderer](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Wanderer.md) hangs in a peculiar conjunction with [the Great Wyrm](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Great_Wyrm.md). The stars often reflect events happening in the world, but this time it seems that they may also be reflecting events in the realm of Night. Two powerful rituals are acting strangely, and two inhabitants of the night realm are acting *significantly* more oddly than usual. You can learn more about this strange conjunction, and these odd events [here](Out_of_void-_out_of_chaos.md).

### The Parents of Security

- **Citizens of the League, especially Sarvosans**

The League national assembly has made several [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) regarding the rebuilding and security of the city of [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md), as well as a statement condemning the army of the Iron Helms ... who have just spent three months in Sarvos. Some of the repercussions of these two related events, as well as a quick update on the fate of the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) studying at the [College of the Liberated](../../mixed/titles/College_of_the_Liberated.md) can be found [here](The_parents_of_security.md).

### The Place You Called Home

- **The Navarr of Therunin; Imperial Senate; Imperial Conclave**

The orcs of the Great Forest came to [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md) as [exiles](When_all_this_was_trees.md) fleeing the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md). Their presence presented [several challenges](Price_of_experience.md) to the Empire. During the Summer Solstice, the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) ratified a [treaty](../senate-motions/Accept_Orcs_of_the_Great_Forest_treaty.md) designed to define the relationship between the displaced orcs and the Empire. You can learn about the repercussions of this treaty [here](The_place_you_called_home.md).

### Praise and Blame

- **Dawn, Highguard, Navarr, Varushka, Urizen**
- **This wind of fortune is a counterpart to [the mother of morality](The_mother_of_morality.md)**

The Iron Helms army continues to promote debate.The assemblies of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md), [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md), and [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) have joined their voices with [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) and [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) in sanctioning the use of cruelty and fear on the battlefield. Several significant opportunities have arisen as a result of the Synod's ongoing discussion of what is, or is not, too high a price to pay for victory. You can learn more about these opportunities [here](Praise_and_blame.md).

### Proud to Serve (Imperial Address)

- **All Imperial citizens**

The [Imperatrix](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) has used her power to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) to allow Heilyn Bronwen's Rest, the [Warmage](../../mixed/titles/Warmage.md), to dispatch a message to all Imperial citizens. You can read it [here](Proud_to_serve.md).

### Queens and Kings

- **Imperial Conclave; [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) owners**

The ongoing saga of the [Icy Crag of the Eternal Sun](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#the-icy-crag-of-the-eternal-sun) takes another twist as the champions of the Summer eternals gather to compete for the right to offer patronage to the [college of magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md). At the same time, [Eleonaris](../eternals/Eleonaris.md) makes her displeasure with the people of Dawn clear, and the King Under The Mountain sends his *koboldi* to make an unexpected offer to the mine-owners of the Empire. You can learn all about these events [here](Queens_and_kings.md).

### Rabbits and Snares (Plenipotentiary)

- **Imperial Conclave**

Three [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) have requested formal [parley](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#parley) with an [archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md), apparently as a consequence of [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) messages received during the Summer Solstice. You can learn which eternals, and which matters they are keen to discuss, [here](Rabbits_and_snares.md).

### Scholars and Mandarins

- **Imperial Conclave**

Four [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) of the Day realm have offers and requests to make of the Empire. The eternal library of [Phaleron](../eternals/Phaleron.md) requests an audience with Imperial magicians and librarians. Meanwhile, a peculiar trio of heralds have travelled the Empire offering certain boons on behalf of their masters. You can learn about these opportunities [here](Scholars_and_mandarins.md).

### A Silver Chalice

- **Magicians; Imperial Conclave**

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has chosen to [establish a new](../senate-motions/Establish_The_Silver_Chalice.md) [Conclave order](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave_order.md) - the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md). The Order will focus on working with and expanding healing magic and other projects which can be used to aid the bodies and minds of Imperial Citizens, the Nations, and the Empire. You can learn about the last few steps needed to establish the order [here](A_silver_chalice.md).

### Seals and Stars (Winds of Magic)

- **Everyone, but especially the people of Sermersuaq and the Imperial Conclave**

A roundup of magical effects covering: the state of the night skies; the status of the [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) of the Spring realm; the raising of castles with magic; the seal-folk of [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md) and their blessing; and a grateful [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) architect with an offer for the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md). You can learn about these disparate matters [here](Seals_and_stars.md).

### Unable to See

- **Highguard; Imperial Synod**

At the Summer Solstice, the General Assembly of the Imperial Synod condemned Yael, who claimed to be the First Empress reborn, for heresy. Reusing to renounce her vision, she was duly executed. But one nation did not reject her. The Highborn stood by their own, just as the nation once stood by the First Empress when she set forth with on a journey to unite all humanity. Now everyone is faced with the fact that their founding nation of The Way has taken a doctrinal position at odds with the rest of the Empire. You can learn more about the repercussions [here](Unable_to_see.md).

### Workers in struggle

- **Imperial Bourse; Dredgemaster of Feverwater**

Three situations related to the [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) have arisen that offer either [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or potential threats. You can learn more about the great sinkhole, a theft from [Serra Briante](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#serra-briante), and a threat to the [Dredgemaster of Feverwater](../../mixed/titles/Dredgemaster_of_Feverwater.md) [here](Workers_in_struggle.md).

### Conquered Territories

Resources in Liathaven are receiving the [Conquered Territories](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#conquered-territories) penalty to their production.

## Imperial Lore

The Imperial Conclave has declared that two new rituals "Combing\_the\_Beach" and "[Golden Voyage](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Golden_Voyage.md)" be entered into Imperial lore. With regard to Unknown Unknown, however, at this time we have no confirmation that the ten thrones has been provided, nor that the ritual text has been handed over.

### Bittersweet Legacy

Every Highborn character who owns a resource in Highguard will see a small boost to their production this event, a side effect of the recent focus on religious matters. The bonus will continue as long as they do not formally reject the belief in the Land Without Tears.
