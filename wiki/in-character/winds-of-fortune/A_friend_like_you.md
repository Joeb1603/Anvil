# A friend like you

*[Image: Then a strange creature appeared with a head of man and a body of a fish.webp](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Then_a_strange_creature_appeared_with_a_head_of_man_and_a_body_of_a_fish.webp)*

## Overview

Six [realms of magic](../eternals/Realm.md) align with the mortal world, whose [inhabitants](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md) are fundamentally different to the people of the mortal world and yet, to a degree, share some of their concerns. They are both complex and simple at the same time, seeking to change the world to match their perception of how it should work. They bargain and negotiate, threaten and cajole, and while it can be difficult to predict exactly where their gifts or curses might lead, still they have much to offer.

The Empire can reach out to the [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) through the powers of the [Archmages](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) and the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md), but the creatures of the realms may sometimes take the initiative themselves. The power of [alignment](../eternals/Alignment.md) allows the Conclave to make it easier or harder for them to touch the world, relationships shaped by the tension between true friends and dire enemies. Some desire that state of [amity](../eternals/Alignment.md#amity), some revel in the [enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) they have earned, but none can deny the power of the Conclave to guard the borders of the Empire.

It is a rare season that passes without an eternal, or a handful, seeking to influence the Empire. There are other winds of fortune that speak of different situations. The responses to the [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) missives of the Archmages from this season is covered in the **[Here you come again](Here_you_come_again.md)** wind of fortune, while **[A habit I can't break](A_habit_I_can-t_break.md)** deals with magic of a mortal or mysterious provenance, and **[There is hope](There_is_hope.md)** deals with astronomantic shiftings.

## Summer

### An Immovable Challenge (Cathan Canae)

[Image: CathCane001b.webp](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:CathCane001b.webp)

- **The Immovable Warhost of Cathan Canae heralds are travelling to the Arena at 20:40 on Friday evening**
- **The Thane of the Enduring Bastion has laid down a challenge to four title holders to withstand the Immovable Warhost**
- **Four titles have been called on to recruit warbands of 20 people to compete against the heralds**
- **This is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**

The Thane of the Enduring Bastion has called on each of **Lord Roland de Lagriffe**, the [Knight-protector of Summer](../../mixed/titles/Knight-protector.md); **Florian von Temeschwar**, the [Captain of the Throne Guard](../../mixed/titles/Captain_of_the_Throne_Guard.md); **Quint**, the [Dauntless Warrior](../../mixed/titles/Dauntless_Warrior.md), and **William Guildenstern** to gather warbands of allies to attempt to withstand the Immovable Warhost.

Each warband must number no more than twenty people, though only ten will be able to fight at any one time. The contest will comprise four bouts, each lasting up to five minutes, with a five-minute break before the next is scheduled to take place. The active warband will be able to field up to ten of its members to start against fifteen heralds of the warhost, with the rest held in reserve. Any herald which is defeated - that is, unable to fight - will score that warband one point, and be replaced by another herald so that there are always fifteen heralds to fight. Any member of a warband who is defeated - again, unable to fight or who yields - will be allowed to be escorted from the arena, but may not return or be replaced by a reserve. At any point during the bout, a member of the warband may step outside the arena to be replaced by one of their reserves, up to a total of ten times. Within the arena, only those who are part of a warband may heal or repair their allies, whilst outside the arena there are no restrictions on receiving aid and support.

If multiple warbands are able to last the full five minutes, then it will instead be decided by the number of heralds each warband was able to defeat. Each member of the winning warband will receive a boon, similar to [that](../winds-of-war/Salt_lions.md#participation-unshackled-in-mareave) offered to Unshackled in [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md), whilst the leader of the winning warband will receive a suitable reward in time for the Winter Solstice 388YE.

During the Winter Solstice 387YE, the team captained by **Enchanter Vesper Lucentia** of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) won the [Drottningbjotha](Opening_ceremonies.md) at the invitation of [Barien](../eternals/Barien.md) . The next season, four of the Monarchs of Summer laid down their rewards for the nation: a [favour](Monarchs_and_outcasts.md#a-boon-for-all-seasons-various) to each of the [Knights-protector](../../mixed/titles/Knight-protector.md) that allowed them to call on their patron for a boon. **Artreus D'Fauntine**, former Knight-protector of Summer, asked that [Meraud](../eternals/Meraud.md) help with a [great work](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) for the mana sites of [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md). Such an act is beyond the means of an eternal - even the Prince of the High Tower has his limits - but he is determined to do what he can to help.

The *King of the Twilight Sun* has sent *Assoni the Bellower* to [Axmure](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md#axmure), walking the trails and poking at various trees. They concur with Artreus that a set of statues of [heraldic beasts](../../mixed/magic/lore/Aspect_magic.md#heraldic-magic) could be built in the forests of the region, leading up to [Highgrove](../../out-of-character/groups/Dawn_groups.md#house-dfauntine). Of course, which beasts are built will be *crucial* - the trail *must* have at least five beasts to be effective. It could have more, of course, up to thirteen (one of each of the heraldic beasts), but the extra beasts won't help - well, they won't help it produce mana crystals. Assoni recommends that the questing knight pick the Unicorn, the Hydra, the Mandowla, the Dragon, the Wyvern and the Wyrm, but they will have to study the beasts and pick the most appropriate ones. Assoni has chosen ones with great strength and ferocity (apart from the Gryphon - which Meraud would take as an *enormous* insult if Artreus chose). The designs incorporate a simple hunting lodge for the Knight-protector of Summer, a place for them to rest and relax and enjoy the hunt when not hard at work.

Once the beasts are carved and put in place, then Meraud will weave the magic of the mountains and bind it to the statues with the power of star metal. A powerful enchantment will settle over the statues, allowing them to enhance the flows of magic through the territory - effectively raising the level of investment in mana sites in the territory by one. The questing knight will need to get the permission of the Senate to commission the great work, but once that is complete, all that would be needed is to provide the white granite and ilium and money for the masons, and then Meraud will do the rest.

[Image: Meraud001a.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Meraud001a.png)

Of course, that is still a lot of white granite and ilium to secure - but Meraud has the most *wonderful* friends among the mortals of the Empire, and he implores them to do whatever they can to help him reward the questing knight. The Prince of the High Tower considers himself a friend or patron of **Catrina Words The Unbelievably Magical**, **Leo the Rainbow Sage**, the [Tender of the Phoenix Flame](../../mixed/titles/Tender_of_the_Phoenix_Flame.md), the [Churnspire Adjutor](../../mixed/titles/Churnspire_Adjutor.md) and the League guild, the **Order of the Golden Chalice**. The *Chatelaine of Sunrise and Sunset* hopes that his mortal friends will help him make good on his promise - and will look with favour on those who work to make this wonderful magic possible.

Once the statues are complete, Meraud will work the promised enchantment over the statues. In addition, he will grant House D'Fauntine the power to command the statues. Should Axmure ever be threatened, the heraldic beasts would come alive at the house's command and move to attack the invaders. The beasts can't easily leave Axmure, but a giant beast fashioned from white granite is incredibly difficult to overcome. Each beast would have an effective military strength of 300 in battle. Of course, any that were destroyed would be gone for good, but that would surely be a small price to pay for House D'Faunine to protect their lands.

However, there is always room for improvement! *Architect Noether* has spent much of the last season on the fabled [Bridge of the Air](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md#bridge-of-the-air) speaking with the residents and merchants of the territory who make use of the mithril cables. The skyways have clearly created much good for the citizens of Zenith, and further afield, but *Noether* has a plan that uses one of the stopping points and potentially makes better use of the Bridge.

[Image: Estavus001a.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Estavus001a.png)

The Binding Oak Spire was once home to a vibrant and bustling community of artisans, Summer magicians, and sentinels. The true potential in the plan comes from an unlikely source. *Crown*, a herald of [Ephisis](../eternals/Ephisis.md) who has spent some time in Zenith this season, suggests making better use of the Bridge of the Air. It would mean permanently reducing investment in businesses in the territory by one, but the excitable herald points seems confident the nation will welcome the proposal.

Last season, [Prospero](../eternals/Prospero.md) met with several influential magicians of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) at the request of former [Archmage of Autumn](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Autumn.md), **Vossk of Sarangrave Reforged**. One of the many things discussed was the potential patronage of the *Sovereign Lord of the City of Bridges* for a [college of magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) in Dawn. Some of the requests were unfortunately impossible for even the *Lord of the Loom*, but eventually an agreement was reached that the *Weaver* would be willing to serve as patron of a college in Dawn, provided that it was made a national position.

[Image: Prospero001a.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Prospero001a.png)

Patronage would allow whoever was appointed to oversee codification at the college, the Golden Enchanter, to call on the aid of the eternal's golden spiders - each around the size of a small sheep - in much the same way as the [Doyen of the Spires](../../mixed/titles/Doyen_of_the_Spires.md#patron-of-the-spires). Rather than [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), the Golden Enchanter would need to provide [green iron](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) in exchange for the expertise and suggestions of the heralds. Of course, there would be an expectation that any rituals codified with the assistance of the golden spiders would serve to further Dawn's interests. Of course, it could be shared to allow individuals of other nations to master the ritual, but there would be an expectation that the ritual text would be used for Dawn first and foremost. If any ritual that was codified at the Tower of the Loom with the aid of Prospero's heralds was entered into Imperial lore or Urizen lore, there would be serious consequences

Whilst there was some discussion about the title being elected by war-witches, weavers, and enchanters, the eternal has made clear that his appreciation for the practice of Tally of the Votes - of individuals having to encourage, cajole, and owe favours to those with votes is something that he is in favour of.

Of course, what would a Tower be without its protector? The motion, or announcement, to construct the Tower of the Loom could state that a set of luxurious rooms be set aside for the [Knight-protector of Autumn](../../mixed/titles/Knight-protector.md).

After looking into the matter of where the Tower of the Loom might be built, the *Subtle Tailor* has one additional *request* for his friends in Dawn to consider. The obvious place for the Tower is Weaving in Astolat. This busy town is already one of the foremost centres of learning for ritual magic in Dawn. However, Prospero would like the Tower to be built just outside Weaving, next to Silver Lake. This large, shallow lake near the River of Sighs is renowned for its beautiful waters that are so perfectly still that they resemble a mirror. With the Tower built on the banks of the lake, its image will be *perfectly* reflected in the still waters.

And *that* is what Prospero would like. The Golden Prince *cordially* invites the magicians of Dawn to grant him [dominion](../eternals/Eternal.md#dominion-in-the-empire) over the *reflection* of the Tower of the Loom. He assures them that this will have **no** impact on the Tower itself - Prospero's power will only ever be over the *other* Tower - the one in the pool. Prospero is at pains to stress that this part of the arrangements is simply a request - the magicians of Dawn are free to decline to extend this favour if they choose.

### One Hand Washes the Other (Ephisis)

[Image: Ephisis001.webp](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Ephisis001.webp)

- **The City of Gold and Lead has updated its guidance for merchants to reduce the price if offers for herbs and liao**
- **Crown warns merchants that the City will be increasingly focused on using contracts for large volumes of goods going forwards**
- **Merchants are welcome to come and go during the Balancing of the Scales**

The City of Gold and Lead has updated [the guidance](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Ephisis-_Scale.md#guidance) they provide to Imperial merchants. The aim of the guidance is to help Imperial traders understand what the City might pay for their goods. To that end, Crown has let citizens know that the City will only offer "low" prices for herbs and liao going forwards. While these things are useful in the City, and will be bought if offered, it is now painfully clear to the *High Merchant*, that they are not as valuable as Imperial herbalists were hoping. As such, they have lowered the guidance to make that clear.

After the success with the contracts in the previous season, the City intends to redouble its focus on this new approach. They are more keen than ever to discourage large volumes of goods being sent to them on spec - they will still make an offer, but citizens who send dozens of wains, or scores of herbs will find that the prices drop to reflect the lower demand. Individuals trading small volumes of goods shouldn't be affected, but merchants looking to move on large volumes should look for the opportunities presented by the contracts.

And finally, Crown wishes to remind all merchants that the Balancing of the Scales is long and takes time. Merchants are welcome to come and go throughout the meeting - if a box is called while a merchant is away, it will be put on the shelves behind the counters. If you return and spot your box on the shelf, then you point it out to a herald, and they will call it in for balancing at the next available opportunity.

As part of the response to the plenipotentiary, Roshanwe made [an offer](Ambassadors_of_death.md#shining-ink) to support the construction of a site in Operus, Redoubt to supply the Empire with more valuable [prismatic ink](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#prismatic-ink). The Archmage pointed out that Urizen already have a number of such commissions, and that it might be good for something to be built elsewhere. When pressed, the Archmage suggested Wintermark, and so *She-Who-Walks-Beside* has spent the season reviewing the vast tracks of [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md), [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) and [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md) for an appropriate regio.

Fortuitously, the Winterfolk are currently partaking in a grand quest to [scout Tsirku](Roses_in_the_snow.md), and so *Beacon* is keen to make an offer that will *also* support that wise and prudent endeavour. *Beachcomber* proposes the construction of a great beacon in Suaq Fount that will peer into the storm of Sydanjaa, both to render some small aid in revealing its secrets and to act as a lighthouse to help those returning home from their journey to find their way. This will create an Imperial title elected by the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) called the **Keeper of the Northern Light** who will receive five vials of prismatic ink each season, which Roshanwe hopes can initially be used on rituals supporting that endeavour.

### A Symposium of Thought (Zakalwe)

[Image: Zakalwe001.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Zakalwe001.png)

- **Polemarchs of Zakalwe will host a meeting with interested Imperials in the Hall of Worlds**

The [General of Day](../eternals/Zakalwe.md) has announced their intention to host a symposium to discuss the Empire's conflicts, both ongoing and future, and to discuss how Imperial citizens wish the Empire to conduct itself in its wars. Several Polemarchs will be in attendance to discuss a range of topics with the citizens who attend.

- The *Polemarch of Sagacity* is keen to discuss the ongoing war with the Druj, especially about what analysis the Empire made about the various conflicts that occurred since the fall of Empress Britta, the historical importance that this war will have, and how people might discuss it in the future.

- The *Polemarch of Wisdom* is eager to discuss the Empire's future conflicts and conquests. With the Druj defeat imminent, where does the Empire go next, and what changes will be necessary in order to overcome these threats?

- The *Polemarch of the Revelation* wishes to discuss a worrying trend in the Imperial armies and the Empire's magicians in both the "overuse" of Summer magic and the decision to turn back to the use of savage rituals.

- The *Polemarch of the Pinnacle* would like to speak with those who have an interest in naval warfare and potential assistance that the *Hunter of Tides* could provide once the Empire built a navy.

As well as discussion on the above topics, the Polemarchs will also bring gifts from Zakalwe, honouring the Pact of Amity between the *Master Strategist* and the Empire. However, Zakalwe notes that he is concerned about what recent decisions mean for how the Empire will fight its wars and openly suggests that perhaps the time has come to discuss the future of the pact.

### Sweet Memories (Apeiron)

The Conclave's decision to declare enmity against *Apeiron* has raised a few eyebrows among those who know something of the denizens of the Night realm. The *Porcelain Spider* is not an eternal known to have been recently active within the Empire, and the declaration was made before anyone involved in the decision had spoken to it or learned what it might want. Quite what the enmity is intended to achieve, or what Apeiron might make of it, remains to be seen.

[Lashonar](../eternals/Lashonar.md) is one of those denizens of the Night realm who has taken an interest. The Eater-of-Silence has a tenuous connection to this mysterious eternal, but its curiosity is less about the *Lady of Quiet Succour* itself and more about the Empire's apparent willingness to make an enemy of the entity without first attempting a conversation. Lashonar is, after all, a great believer in the power of communication. It may take the opportunity to discuss some of the eternals who are not currently active in the Empire, and the difficulty of knowing what such beings are up to when nobody is speaking to them. The [Archmage of Night](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Night.md) might be encouraged to send a plenipotentiary to Apeiron, if only to find out what it has to say - for better or worse.

[Sadogua](../eternals/Sadogua.md) has rather more personal reasons to be interested. The Toad King has recently been visited by entities supposedly associated with Apeiron, and the experience has not been entirely to his benefit. Rather understandably, Globbersnotch is being a little guarded about exactly who and what they are, what they want, and whether this eternal has anything to do with their meddlesome activities. A plenipotentiary might provide some answers, although whether Apeiron would be inclined to give them is another matter.

For the moment, neither Lashonar nor Sadogua can offer much certainty about Apeiron's intentions. The declaration has, however, brought attention to an eternal about whom the Empire knows relatively little, and there may be some value in finding out more before deciding what to do next.

### Call to Arms (Tharim)

[Image: Tharim 001.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Tharim_001.jpg)

- **Tharim's legion of Ashen Knights remains camped outside of Vorota**
- **The legion has begun to fortify their encampment**
- **The Bound King offers to provide a similar ritual that could replace the broken Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust**
- **Conclave would need a suitable Declaration of Concord recognising that *Claudia Varkulova Remislav* deserves to be the Chosen of Tharim**
- ***Eevi of the Gadrai* would need to provide the Imperial Necromancer with an acceptable apology**

The roiling tempest of [Tharim](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#tharim) has calmed somewhat over the past season. He is still furious that both Surut and Skathe continue to be welcomed by the Conclave, but at least it is no longer a crime for a mortal to be chosen by the *Bound King*. His legion of Ashen Knights remains camped outside of [Vorota](../nations/varushka/Karov.md#wieliczka), indeed, they have begun to fortify their camp with palisades and black-iron chains. The Ashen Knights still refuse to answer why they are here, of all places, maintaining their stoic, monosyllabic, utterly unhelpful answers.

*Chainwright*, one of the *King-in-Chains* heralds, presents an offer from Tharim on the loss of [Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clarion_Call_of_Ivory_and_Dust.md). He would be more than happy to aid his friends in the Empire and can provide an arcane projection to **Claudia Varkulova Remislav** if the cost is acceptable to the Conclave and the Synod. First, the Conclave would need to pass a Declaration of [Concord](../../mixed/government/conclave/Concord.md) recognising Claudia Varkulova Remislav as the Hand of the Bound King, the Keeper of Tharim's Throne, and the Chosen of Stoneheart. It is by no means an Imperial title; Claudia Varkulova Remislav can continue to serve as the [Imperial Necromancer](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Necromancer.md) for as long as she chooses. Tharim's only wish is that the Conclave publicly acknowledge that Claudia deserves to sit at the right hand of the King-in-Chains. There must be no more sordid accusations by pen-pushers and those who might grow envious of Claudia's status.

Second, the priest who raised the original proscription that saw the status of chosen come under scrutiny, **Eevi of the Gadrai**, would need to issue some kind of apology that was acceptable to Claudia. The Bound King leaves it to Claudia to decide what form the apology must take, but he will not provide the projection until Claudia assures him that she has granted the priest forgiveness for the foolishness.

Finally, the assurance on the ritual would be broken if the Empire ever moved to make it illegal to be the Chosen of Tharim (or indeed any other regent of the realms for that matter). A King must have courtiers; it is beyond acceptable that any mortal might think to limit who might serve the Bound King. Provided none of the political bodies of the Empire tried to make it illegal to be chosen by an eternal, then the ritual would continue to function. The resulting ritual would work in a similar way to Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust: a magnitude 20 Winter ritual that would call Tharim's Ashen Knights to support the military unit whilst on campaign.
