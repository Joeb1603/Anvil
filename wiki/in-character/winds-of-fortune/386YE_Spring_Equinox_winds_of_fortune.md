# 386YE Spring Equinox winds of fortune

*[Image: Anvil, a sea of tent, sprawls across the fields around the Forge and the Senate building.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Torches_and_smoke.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, events have taken place which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. These are the winds of fortune, counterparts to the [Winds of War](../winds-of-war/386YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md) which detail the Empire's military campaigns. They lay out situations, and usually include things that characters can do to take advantage of [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or to resolve problems. In almost every case, they start with a piece of flavour text. This fiction helps introduce the wind of fortune, or draws out a particular theme or element. Often, it represents a viewpoint that one or more NPCs might have in-character. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip if they want to use it in character.

The body of the wind of fortune describes situations and lays out ways players can engage with them. Except where we explicitly talk about something being "rumoured" or use a phrase like "some might say..." the details of a wind of fortune are factual. They represent briefing material presented by the civil service or similar authoritative sources and represents something the average educated citizen would acknowledge as a reliable truth. Unless we specify otherwise, we're also being as thorough as possible. This information doesn't need further checking in-character. It's not opinion, and we're not trying to trick you.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters could know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months. It's perfectly acceptable to turn up to Empire having read only a few Winds of Fortune that directly interest your character; part of the fun on the field can come from learning about situations and opportunities in other parts of the game.

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

## Winds of Fortune

### Promising Light

- **Imperial citizens, National Assemblies**

[The Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md) has the unique power to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire), to communicate with every Imperial citizen. The first such address, the inaugural address traditionally made immediately after their [coronation](A_Crown_in_Winter.md), is seen as setting the tenor for their reign and has a particular impact on the people of the Empire. Especially as it is usually accompanied by the [festivities](A_Sparrow_in_Spring.md#a-new-era) that surround the ascension of a new Throne. In this case, the Empress has sent out a powerful call for unity, but also laid out a vision of Imperial destiny that many find particularly appealing - especially given it coincides with an Empire-wide party.

You can learn about the address, and the festivities that accompany it, in the **[Promising light](Promising_light.md)** wind of fortune.

### New Port, Old Wine

- **The Marches, especially Bregas, Imperial Senate, the Throne**

The overwhelming majority of [Bregas](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) are delighted that the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) have been banished from the territory - although you'd probably not know it just by looking at them. The [pretender Mathilda Fisher](Despise_treachery.md#homecoming-queen) is no more, and her body has been sent away to rest with whatever honour the orcs are prepared to offer. Yet there's still people in the western fenland with a grudge against the Empire... but [Empress Vesna's inaugural address](Promising_light.md) reaches out to them just as much as it does to any other Imperial citizens.

You can learn about the situation in Bregasland, and the impact both of the address and other recent actions, in the **[New port, old wine](New_port-_old_wine.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Three Pillars

- **Urizen and the League, especially those from Spiral, Imperial Senate**

There is open rebellion in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md) among those [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) who struggle with the decision to [allocate the territory](A_question_of_loyalty.md) to [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md). Keen to avoid the rebellion spreading, the Empire has made concerted efforts to meet the rebels demands where possible. The [words of the Empress](Promising_light.md) have come at an opportune moment, just as the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) were wrestling with a way to satisfy the demands of one of the Urizeni factions.

You can learn about the situation with the Urizeni rebels, and the opportunity to reach an understanding with at least one of them, in the **[The three pillars](The_three_pillars.md)** wind of fortune.

### Dream to Me (Plenipotentiary)

- **Archmages; Imperial Magicians; House deGauvin and Rhydian de Rondell; Emperor Vesna; Cabalist of the Hollow Stone, the Custodian of the Bleak Tower, and the Keeper of the Tower of the Fal; Witness of Conclave; musicians and artists**

Half-a-dozen [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) have responded to the [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) messages sent by the six [Archmages](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) during the Winter Solstice. Four have agreed to a meeting of some kind, while two have decided to offer boons directly to Imperial magicians in response to the missives they have been sent. At least one set of offers is likely to prove... problematic.

You can learn about the responses of the eternals, and the messages they have sent, in the **[Dream to me](Dream_to_me.md)** Wind of Fortune.

### Hurricane Party

- **Imperial magicians; militia and magistrates**

The eternal [Siakha](../eternals/Siakha.md) offers her boons indiscriminately to Imperial magicians, should they be prepared to reach out and take them. Given the *Mother of Wrecks* has been declared an [enemy of the Empire](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) by the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md), it is quite illegal to actually take advantage of these offers, or to be the beneficiaries of them. Still, the offers are there... as is the spectre of the chaos and destruction that they might bring if anyone chooses to accept them.

You can learn about the boons, both with regard to storms and savagery, in the **[Hurricane party](Hurricane_party.md)** wind of fortune.

### An Angry Blade (Appraisal)

- **Imperial Senate; Quaymaster of the Elosian Docks; General of the Red Wind Corsairs**

During the Winter Solstice the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) commissioned [Graciana i Lòpez i Guerra](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#graciana-i-lòpez-i-guerra) to [appraise](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) options to deploy [fleet captains](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) to disrupt enemy naval and logistical operations in the [Bay of Catazar](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md). Graciana is an excellent choice for the appraisal, more than her reputation for novel and imaginative solutions, she was once a successful corsair herself. Surely if anyone can find a way for Imperial fleets to take the fight to the Grendel, it is Graciana?

You can learn what her appraisal has concluded, and the opportunities to deal with the Grendel navies, in the **[An angry blade](An_angry_blade.md)** wind of fortune.

### Parched Earth (Appraisal)

- **Imperial Senate; Unfettered Mind and Silver Chalice; Mistress of the Dust Parador and Hakima of Salt and Sand; Brass Coast**

The second [Appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) commissioned during the Winter Solstice was to send [Lutomysla Niegoslava](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#lutomysla-niegoslava) to find a solution to the Dry Patrician problem in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md). At the same time the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) agreed that the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md) would focus their arcane acumen on the problem, and the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) asked the [Silent Bell](../../mixed/government/synod/Silent_Bell_-Sodality.md) to investigate the provenance of the problem. Not before time - it seems that the Dry Patricians are engaged in an attempt to spread the blight into neighbouring territories, and it will take the might of Imperial heroes to stop them.

You can learn about the findings of the appraisal and the Conclave investigation, as well as some proposals regarding dealing with future threats on a similar scale, in the **[Parched earth](Parched_earth.md)** wind of fortune.

### Long Shadows

- **Imperial Orcs, especially the Steward of the Mines of Gulhule; Golden Pyramid order; Imperial Senate; Minister of Historical Research**

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has paid [a hundred thrones](../senate-sessions/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#prospecting-in-skarsind) to encourage miners, prospectors and skilled labourers to travel to [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) and search the mountains for valuable deposits of metal that can feed the hungry forges of the [Ethengraw](../nations/imperial-orcs/Ethengraw.md). They have been aided in their quest by diviners, recruiters, investors, and entrepreneurs from the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md) who have come at the [request of the Grandmaster](../conclave-sessions/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Conclave_sessions.md#concord-golden-pyramid-and-skarsind) to lend whatever support they can. Together, they seek out opportunities to enrich the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) - and the last three seasons have proven potentially quite lucrative.

You can learn about the opportunities they have uncovered, and an unexpected number of mysteries, in the **[Long shadows](Long_shadows.md)** wind of fortune.

### Cities of the Iron Labyrinth

- **Imperial magicians (except Varushka); Imperial Conclave; Ashborn Trosk**

The [Archmage of Autumn](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Autumn.md) has [requested](Dream_to_me.md) that the [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Estavus](../eternals/Estavus.md) provide aid in the Empire's wars. The City of Shikal has responded by offering opportunities directly to Imperial magicians. As always, the *Forgemistress* has a limitless desire for [ingots](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#metals) and [measures](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#natural-materials) to feed her innumerable forges and workshops. Her emissaries have asked the civil service to distribute information on three offers that may be of interest. Where the City of Flame and Stone leads, other cities are not far behind; emissaries of [Gold and Lead](../eternals/Ephisis.md) have leaked details of certain deals that are especially appealing to their brokers this season, and the [Lictors](../eternals/Lictors.md) of the Iron Oubliette have announced their intention to offer boons to the newly completed Autumn Hammers [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md).

You can find details of the war-trade of Estavus, and the opportunities from the other two cities, in the **[Cities of the Iron Labyrinth](Cities_of_the_Iron_Labyrinth.md)** Wind of Fortune.

### Sound the Bells

- **Urizen, the Marches; people of Sarvos, Necropolis, Redoubt, and Spiral; Grandmasters of the Conclave; Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies, Steward of the Sarvos Mana Exchange, Steward of the Tassato Mana Exchange; Arthur Payne, Bella Flowerfields, Brother Harvest, Robyn Of Bassingham, Saura Penny, and Eli Scrump; Ambassador to Axos, Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy, Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves, Ambassador to the Sumaah Republic; Crystal Architect of the Spires, Arcane Architect; Champion of Prosperity,Imperial Spymaster, High Exorcist**

As Winter turns to Spring, the Empire is plagued by bad weather; terrible storms wrack the [southern coast](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md). Some blame [the Empress of Sharks](../eternals/Siakha.md) for these tempests, but the wise know that the cause lies closer to home. Curses hang heavy on the territories that reach the coast - an attempt to drown the arrogant [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) - but [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) will not be governed and the pirates of the Broken Shore are not the only ones to suffer. If that were all, it might be bad enough, but the malevolent magic does not stop at the shore. The territories of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) are pummelled with thick drifts of snow and hail. Magic is the source of this foul weather, but politics is the cause. The febrile conflict between Urizen and the Marches started this curse war - can anything be done to halt its course before the effects spread further and [more Marchers are declared sorcerers](../conclave-sessions/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Conclave_sessions.md#sorcery-ruined-hills-and-scorched-lands)?

You can learn about the magical storms and foul weather, and the wide-reaching effects on Imperial citizens, in the **[Sound the bells](Sound_the_bells.md)** wind of fortune.

### It's Only Me

- **Sociable astronomancers; people with an interest in ghosts, especially stewards of the dead; Holberg meat and victual entrepreneurs; League doctors and experts in magical maladies; Jaromira Orlov and Jereni Hinda Bogdanov, the Burgher of Ketsov; Aniera Exiles End, and the Navarr guides especially those who preach Ambition; Morwenna Withered Rose, Brat Umbral Path, the Grandmaster of the Sevenfold Path, the Bursar of the Conclave, and the Archmage of Night, among others; Urizen with an interest in rebels; people who know Theodosia of Ipotavo**

People come to Anvil from all over the Empire and beyond. Some travel discreetly, others make a point of ensuring people know they are coming. As often as not the latter group do this to ensure that the people they want to meet with are expecting them. This season there is news of an astronomantic symposium attracting magicians eager to come together with their peers; stewards of the dead shepherding spirits; serious sausage business; a doctor and a patient with a peculiar malady; two groups of Ketsov who appear to be unaware of each other; some Navarr guides who are not exactly coming to Anvil so much as arranging for Anvil to come to them; the Globbersnotch; and some last-minute rebels.

### Momento Mori

- **Dawn and Varushka, especially people in Weirwater and Karov; Overseer of the Westward Road, Castellan of Spiral Castle, Boyar of the Iron Roads**

During the Summer Solstice, an undying horror roused from slumber on the borders between [Karov](../nations/varushka/Karov.md) (where it is known as the Schlacta of Rot) and [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md) (where it is called the Deadwood Knight). It issued its traditional challenge, but something went awry and rather than returning to decades long slumber the beast has instead begun a rampage on either side of the border between the two nations. Its attacks are becoming bolder as the strength of its unliving retinue increases. At the moment it is believed to have a force of perhaps two hundred or more warriors, but as the numbers increase it becomes capable of attacking larger targets. The speed with which the cadaver-commander's legion is growing can only increase.

You can find accounts of the situation in the north-eastern Empire, along with notes about visitors, opportunities, and skirmishes, in the **[Momento mori](Momento_mori.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Pride of Gerallt Brackensong

- **General Assembly; the Proud; people of Navarr**

During the Winter Solstice, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) passed the first ever [writs of consecration](../../mixed/government/synod/Writ_of_consecration.md). This new [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) governs the use of [true liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md#true-liao) to create [inspirational locations](../../mixed/government/synod/Inspirational_location.md) to guide and inspire the virtuous. The [virtue assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) of [Pride](../religion/Pride.md) enacted one of these judgements calling for the creation of a memorial dedicated to the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [guide](../nations/navarr/Navarr_religious_beliefs.md) and exemplar [Gerallt Brackensong](../../mixed/government/synod/Known_exemplars.md#gerallt-brackensong). The civil service have worked to provide [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md), identifying key locations where such a memorial might have profound effects, and provide true inspiration to pilgrims of the Way.

You can read about the locations they have identified, and the opportunities they have presented, in the **[The Pride of Gerallt Brackensong](The_Pride_of_Gerallt_Brackensong.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Ambition of Thrace

- **General Assembly; the Ambitious; people of the Imperial Orcs**

The second writ of consecration was that raised by the [Ambition](../religion/Ambition.md) assembly, seeking the creation of an inspirational location dedicated to the memory of the leader of [the Orc Rebellion](../nations/imperial-orcs/The_Orc_Rebellion.md), the [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) exemplar [Thrace](../../mixed/government/synod/Known_exemplars.md#thrace). Despite assessing a number of locations, including several in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) and [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md), the Civil Service are forced to concede that the only reasonable location to build such a memorial is just where everyone already knew it should go - in chilly [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md).

You can read about the inspiration such a location would offer to Imperial Orcs across the Empire, and the challenge of finding the right person to perform the consecration, in the **[The Ambition of Thrace](The_Ambition_of_Thrace.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Prosperity of Good Walder

- **General Assembly; the Prosperous especially the Prosperity assembly**

The final writ of consecration upheld at the Winter Solstice concerned the beloved paragon of [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md) [Good Walder](../religion/Good_Walder.md). Jolly and judgemental by turns, the lessons of Good Walder remind people that the virtuous should be rewarded, and the wicked should be punished. Most loved in [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), it's no surprise that the locations the civil service have identified are all found in that nation, in [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md) and [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md). But the inspiration of Good Walder is truly Imperial in scope - people in every nation know his name and tell his (or perhaps her) stories. The big question facing the General Assembly is which of the many stories of Good Walder to pick to best inspire Imperial pilgrims?

You can learn about the three locations, and the costs of providing a suitable focus for the inspirational location, in the **[The Prosperity of Good Walder](The_Prosperity_of_Good_Walder.md)** wind of fortune.

The Marches is wealthy but rarely settled. There's always something going on, and it's usually quite loud. The Empire is one of magic, woven both by mortal magicians and eternal inhabitants of the realms. ### Bury the Lede

- **Imperial Ambassadors; Imperial Senate**

International diplomacy is hard. Every nation, whether grand or not, has its own unique culture, ambitions, goals, and attitudes. All too often, these agendas are at odds with those of Imperial citizens. There are six great powers in the Known World, and the Empire is only one of them. It has a close alliance - the [Liberty Pact](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Liberty.md#liberty-pact) - with the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) and the [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md). It is at war with one of them - the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) - and has terrible relations with another - the magocratic [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md). The last nation is the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md), that holds the freedom to trade with whoever it wishes as the only one worth seeking.

You can read about any amount of snippets of foreign diplomacy, and the occasional threat, in the **[Burying the lede](Burying_the_lede.md)** wind of fortune.

## Other Events

There are a number of other events and situations which are noteworthy, but which for whatever reason have not led to a full Wind of Fortune in their own right.

### Broken Sword

The mandate encouraging the sword scholars to scatter to the four winds, spreading Sulemaine's words as they go was [raised by Herminius of the House of the Wanderer](../synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-5) and received the backing of the greater majority of the Urizen Assembly. It was enacted by Clytemnestra of the House of the Wanderer who spread word of the decision of the Synod and the sword scholars throughout Urizen. A number of [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) were raised at the same time. The consequences of these landmark judgements are detailed in the **[Resolution](Broken_sword.md#resolution)** section of last season's [Broken sword](Broken_sword.md) wind of fortune.

Their departure means there will be no new sword scholars in Urizen. Those who attend Anvil now will be the last of that tradition. (**OOC Note:** we have removed the sword scholar archetype from the character creation options to reflect this change).

### The Greatest City in the World

The matter of what commission the city of [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) should build to celebrate its status as [The greatest city in the world](The_greatest_city_in_the_world.md) was [settled by judgement of Hildebrand von Holberg](../synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-51) who called on the citizens of Holberg to build a giant temple-orphanage. The cities of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) have a fine tradition of such efforts, with the [Church of the Little Mother](../religion/The_Little_Mother.md) and rival institutions providing shelter and sustenance to orphans. Such are their efforts that [the prosperity and wellbeing of the Empire's orphans is being largely dealt with by the existing orphanages](../religion/The_Little_Mother.md#ooc-note-regarding-orphans).

None-the-less, some people are eager to create a grand new building to support the orphans in Holberg. Details of the potential commission are given in the **[Resolution](The_greatest_city_in_the_world.md#resolution)** section of last season's [The greatest city in the world](The_greatest_city_in_the_world.md) wind of fortune.

### Temple of Waking Dream

The plans for turning Seren into the City of Dreams have been redesigned following the changes to how [great works](../../mixed/commissions/Great_work.md) function. You can find the updated plans and effects on the [Comes a time](Comes_a_time.md#the-temple-of-waking-dream) wind of fortune.

### Soothing Rains

[Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) has had unexpectedly soothing weather this season; soft rainfall and verdant growth of plants - in the regions that suit it at least. Anyone living in the territory will be able to discern that it is under the effect of a magnitude 40 Spring ritual; most likely to be [Regrow the Land's Heart](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regrow_the_Land-s_Heart.md).

## Trade Summary

Rather than spread this information out around the various foreign nations, we've collected it here for ease of reference.

- **Asavean Archipelago:** The port of [Nemoria](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#nemoria) is closed to Imperial trading vessels.
- **Axos:** The Axos continue to be annoyed about revelations relating to Imperial involvement in the failure of their [joint venture with the Grendel](Roll_and_fall.md) into the Mountains of the Moon. Consequently, Imperial fleets suffer penalties to trade with Axos. A fleet captain trading at the [Towers of Kantor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#towers-of-kantor) suffers a -2 rank penalty.
- **Commonwealth:** The ports of [Leerdam](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#leerdam-28eastern-commonwealth29) and [Volkavaar](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#volkavaar-28western-commonwealth29) welcome Imperial traders.
- **Principalities of Jarm:** The ports of [Kavor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#kavor-28northern-principalities-of-jarm29) and [Vezak](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#vezak-28southern-principalities-of-jarm29) remain closed to Imperial fleets.
- **Sarcophan Delves:** Imperial captains continue to be welcomed in the Delves. Fleets trading with the great port-city of [Sarcophan](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#sarcophan) receive an additional dose of [Marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md) and an additional dose of [Imperial Roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) on top of their normal production. The port of [Betovering](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#betovering) is open to Imperial fleets.
- **Sumaah Republic:** There is no penalty to trade with Zemeh and Imperial fleets are welcomed. Be aware that the amount of liao and Imperial roseweald offered has switched as per the [Parts of speech](Parts_of_speech.md#trade-in-liao) wind of fortune.
- **Grendel:** The port of [Oran](Everything_has_a_price.md#a-new-destination) is closed to Imperial fleets.
- **Iron Confederacy:** The port of [Robec](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#robec) is available for Imperial fleets but suffers a -1 rank penalty due to sanctions from the Suranni.
- **Faraden:** The port of [Caitun](Butterfly_in_reverse.md#trade-with-the-faraden) is available for Imperial fleets. Fleets from the Marches and Wintermark benefit from a +1 rank bonus due to close proximity.
