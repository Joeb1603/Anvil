# 383YE Winter Solstice winds of fortune

*[Image: The fires are banked but every night breaks at dawn.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:A_Field_Lies_Waiting.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/383YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md).

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters 'should' know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months.

Many winds of fortune open with a short section of in-character text. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip. They're mostly just a bit of fun and they tend not to contain anything vital to the understanding of the wind of Fortune.

## Winds of Fortune

### Some Heavenly Music

- **Urizen**

The inclusion of half-a-dozen rituals in [Urizen lore](../nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) - the newly created body of magical knowledge only available to [Urizeni](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) magicians - has caused great excitement in the nation of the high peaks. As awareness of its potential spreads, a number of groups within the nation have come forward to propose their own magical texts for conclusion in the corpus of Urizen lore. Adding a ritual to Urizen lore requires the [ritual text](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) be sent to the [celestial library](../eternals/Phaleron.md) via the [Gift of Knowledge](The_way_that_the_sunshine_gleams.md#a-new-archives) ritual - a ritual that is not itself part of Urizen Lore. As a consequence these custodians of ritual grimoires must come to [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md) and find someone (ideally a fellow Urizeni) capable of working the magic required. Enthusiasm for the novel only goes so far however. A respect for [Ambition](../religion/Ambition.md) and [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md) mean that most of those who have proposed that their tomes become part of Urizen lore want something in return for their largesse...

You can learn about the groups who have rituals to add to the new body of Urizeni magical knowledge - and what they want in return - in **[Some heavenly music](Some_heavenly_music.md)**.

### Cloud Waves Break

- **Of special note for all Imperial citizens, but especially: storytellers, bards, and musicians; priests; members of [Conclave orders](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave_order.md); adepts of [Night](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) and [Autumn](../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md) lore; those seeking a new start in life.**

A peculiar fog with memory-eroding qualities is seeping across the Empire. Many of those exposed to it find their recollection of the smaller details of their life have been lost, or altered slightly. Confusion spreads, but a few find the loss of memory inspires them to profoundly change their path through life.

Solutions to the eerie fog's effects have been found, and some of them suggest actions that can be taken during the Solstice to help mitigate its impact.

As is common with such magical events, there is also news of perturbations to the effects of certain rituals - especially those dealing with memory and identity - but more peculiarly there is also talk of odd figures in the mists - of tulpas with an unsettling taste for human and orc memories.

You can learn about this strange fog, the steps to counter its effects, and the perturbation of certain rituals in **[Cloud waves break](Cloud_waves_break.md)**.

### A Horn Resounding

- **Young citizens; parents; [Gatekeepers](../../mixed/titles/Gatekeeper.md)**

In the wake of the Autumn Equinox, young people all across the Empire have undergone uncanny growth spurts. It starts with a burst of energy and that lasts for a month or so, and when it ends they have visibly aged by between one and three years! Stories filtering out of Wintermark suggest that the change has been sparked by a combination of a mysterious magical horn and possibly ill-advised Summer magic intended to call forth *heroes*.

Whatever the cause, this unwarranted event offers its own opportunities. One [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) chapter for example, eager to prove an esoteric theory about reborn heroes, offers a dose of [True Liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md#true-liao) to the most virtuous of these "Summer Children", with the intention they use it to receive a vision of their past-life that might explain their sudden maturiation.

At the same time, a whole bevy of [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) offer boons to any of these children prepared to commit themselves to their "heroic destiny" - whatever that means. Indeed, the eternals have very different ideas of what counts as a "heroic destiny" - and anyone tempted to accept their boons might give thought to what that might entail.

You can learn about the peculiar growth spurt, the Highborn offer, and the opportunities provided in **[A horn resounding](A_horn_resounding.md)**.

Five of the Imperial [Archmages](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) have invited [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) to attend a [parley](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#parely) with them, as representatives of the Empire and the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md). Replies have been received, and arrangements have been made.

During the Winter Solstice, [Lord Rain](../eternals/Ossegrahn.md) will hold court for the first time in over three centuries; favoured emissaries of the [Walker on the Littoral](../eternals/Roshanwe.md) and the [Spiral Dancer](../eternals/Sung.md) will meet with very different groups of truth-seekers; and the [City of Gold and Lead](../eternals/Ephisis.md) will arrange a meeting with emissaries of other cities to see what can be done about the Sanguine Market.

The [King Beneath the Mountain](../eternals/Adamant.md) has also responded enthusiastically to an invitation from the Summer Archmage, but has apparently made different arrangements, which do not include parley. Very public arrangements, in fact, which we will learn more about... [very shortly](Stone_and_stone.md).

As for the plenipotentiaries for the Winter Solstice, you can learn all about them in **[Impossible landscapes](Impossible_landscapes.md)**.

### Stone and Stone

- **[Master of the Koboldi](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Koboldi.md); [Archmage of Summer](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Summer.md); [Master of Ice and Darkness](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Ice_and_Darkness.md); [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md); [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md); people of [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md), [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md), [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md), [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md), [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md), [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md), and [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md)**

The eternal [Adamant](../eternals/Adamant.md), He Who Sits on the Granite Throne, King Beneath the Mountain, the Deep Dragon with the Glinting Heart, has responded to a plenipotentiary from the Summer archmage by dispatching dozens of his *koboldi* servants to all corners of the Empire. Looking to share their enthusiasm for building impressive structures with Imperial citizens, they have identified a number of special opportunities, just in time for the appointment of the Master of the Koboldi by the Imperial Conclave.

In addition to opportunities to create, however, they've also identified an opportunity for terrible destruction. Namely, a great horde of monstrous [trogoni](../world/Trogoni.md) gathering in the depths of [Lorenzo's Deep Pockets](../../mixed/titles/Delver_of_the_Depths.md#lorenzo27s-deep-pockets) in the north-western Empire. Don't worry though; they have a solution! There's not a hole so large that the *koboldi* can't plug it with a bit of mithril, a lot of hard work, and the help of a few hundred bold adventurers!

You can learn all about the opportunities for building presented by the *koboldi*, and about the threat in the north-west, in **[Stone and stone](Stone_and_stone.md)**.

### Apples from a Tree

- **[Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md), [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md); the [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md) Assembly**

The Empire has been wrestling with the contentious question of how the Empire's most precious resources - [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md), [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md), [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md), and [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) - should be allocated for some time now. Details have been prepared outlining an opportunity to construct a trading hall in Wintermark, and a market in Varushka. The outstanding opportunities to take advantage of similar situations in the Marches and the Brass Coast have likewise been updated. The problem is, there's only so much to go around. Someone is going to lose out...

At the same time, all of these opportunities arguably began with the Prosperity Assembly. In recognition of this, a unique opportunity has been presented for the Cardinal of Prosperity to lead the way in blazing a trail away from the Bourse and toward a new, more Prosperous, Empire.

You can learn about these opportunities in **[Apples from a tree](Apples_from_a_tree.md)**.

### The Price of Wisdom

- **[Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md); the [General](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), [Wisdom](../religion/Wisdom.md), [Loyalty](../religion/Loyalty.md), [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md), [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md), and [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) assemblies; the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md); [sword scholars](../nations/urizen/Sword_scholar.md)**

Between them, the Assembly of Wisdom and the sword scholars of Urizen are obstructing anyone trying to enact a Synod mandate that has not been upheld with a greater majority. [Several have failed as a result](383YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_fortune.md#mandates). A number of [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) and one [mandate](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md) were upheld during the Autumn Equinox relating to the [heirs](../nations/urizen/Sword_scholar.md) of [Sulemaine](../nations/urizen/Sulemaine.md). There have been [incendiary calls for action](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-4), and calmer voices [urging debate and discussion](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-127) rather than open opposition to the Synod. Decisions need to be made, some of which may have implications that go far beyond the questioning of the Synod authority by the Wise.

There are other considerations as well; the Urizen Assembly has expressed [unequivocal support](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-100) for the swords scholars, and the opportunity exists to solidify that support in Morrow. At the same time, the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) Assembly has made its own [statement of solidarity](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-21) with the sword scholars, reminding the faithful of the Brass Coast of their own strained relationship with the Synod.

You can learn about this developing situation and the many ways it might be addressed in **[the price of wisdom](The_price_of_wisdom.md)**.

### From the Sieve of Your Hands

- **[Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md), especially those in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md); Urizen Assembly; [General](../../mixed/titles/General.md) of the [Citadel Guard](../mandates/Citadel_Guard.md); [Doyen of the Spires](../../mixed/titles/Doyen_of_the_Spires.md); [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md), [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md), [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md)**

During the Autumn Equinox, the Imperial Senate ceded the regions of [Ankra](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#ankra), [Cinion](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#cinion), and [Ateri](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#ateri) in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md) as part of the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) peace [treaty](../senate-motions/Ratify_treaty_with_the_Grendel_Autumn_383YE.md#treaty). Provisions were made to allow citizens currently in those regions two seasons' grace to relocate to Imperial territory with all of their possessions. The new governor of Spiral, the Salt Lord Mahiri Kaliact, has a different proposition. Stay, and stay out of her affairs, and both sides might still find Prosperity.

This might include the sentinels of the Citadel Guard. With so much of their nation conquered by orcs, the only Urizen army has been on the verge of collapse for some time. The Grendel governor has a proposal to make, one that could secure its short-term survival... and perhaps lead to a longer lasting solution.

You can learn all about events in Spiral under the Grendel in **[From the sieve of your hands](From_the_sieve_of_your_hands.md)**.

### To Curb Those Raging Appetites

- **The Brass Coast; Freeborn national assembly; [Imperial Fleet Master](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Fleet_Master.md); [Keeper of the Spice Gardens](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Spice_Gardens.md);[Celesti Lighthouse Keeper](../../mixed/titles/Celesti_Lighthouse_Keeper.md); [Vizier of the Incarnadine Satchel](../../mixed/titles/Vizier_of_the_Incarnadine_Satchel.md); [Imperial Delegate to the Temple in Feroz](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Delegate_to_the_Temple_in_Feroz.md)**

During the Autumn Equinox the Imperial Senate ratified a peace treaty with the Grendel orcs, ceding the regions of [Morajasse](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#morajasse) and [Afarjasse](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#afarjasse) to the orcs of the Broken Shore. The rapacious governor Rahab already controls the eastern half of the territory, and the provisions for free movement only apply to those in the western regions.

The orcs of the Broken Shore have spent the last three months consolidating their hold on the territory and their control of Feroz is now uncontested. The remaining Freeborn populace - at least in Afarjasse and Morajasse - look to the Synod for guidance. Should they stay and cooperate with the Grendel? Remain behind but resist their new orc overlords? Or should as many people as possibly can flee the yoke of Grendel domination? Which course of action is most in keeping with Freeborn tradition - and what will the implications be for the Brass Coast, and for the Empire itself?

You can learn all about the situation in Feroz under the Grendel - and the implications for several Imperial titles - in **[To curb those raging appetites](To_curb_those_raging_appetites.md)**.

### Everything Has A Price

- **Imperial Senate; [Imperial Fleet Master](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Fleet_Master.md); [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) captains**

The peace treaty with the southern orcs has had a major impact on Feroz and Spiral, but it also has implications for the entire Empire. The Empire now considers the orcs of the Broken Shore to be foreigners, able to trade with and visit each others lands. The Grendel pride themselves on their mercantile expertise, and claim that they view war as something not to be pursued until all other avenues for profit are exhausted. They are unsurprisingly eager to take advantage of the new treaty.

Since the Grendel are slavers, the Empire imposed the sanctions required under the Liberty Pact, and the Grendel swiftly responded in kind, but this move doesn't seem to have dented their interest in trade in the slightest. They appear to regard the inevitable taxes as just a regular part of the costs of doing business with the Empire. Merchants and messengers have all arrived at the borders of the Empire with various different proposals for trade. Now it remains to be seen if any part of the Empire wants to trade with the Grendel...

You can learn about the general situation with regards to the Grendel, and the opportunities the peace treaty presents, in **[Everything has a price](Everything_has_a_price.md)**.

### Bones in the Ocean

- **Brass Coast, Freeborn Assembly, Imperial Senate**

As part of the Grendel treaty, a contract was signed between the Grendel ambassador and a Freeborn priest. Intended as a codicil to the main treaty, it secured certain provisions relating to the Isle of the Osseini in Feroz. A contingent of priests from the Brass Coast has visited the island - deep in Grendel territory - and reported on the situation there. Opportunities are presented, especially around the agreement that a small enclave might be established here for the use of priests interested in the arts of exorcism. While there, they made contact with a representative of the corsair families of nearby Shantarim - who offered an opportunity all their own that may have special appeal to the privateers of the Brass Coast.

You can learn about the situation with the Isle of the Osseini, and the offer of the corsairs of the Cazar Straits, in **[Bones in the ocean](Bones_in_the_ocean.md)**.

### All Lift Together

- **Wintermark; Imperial Senate**

The Wintermark Nation Assembly has sent out a call for more healing herbs, desperately needed to support the heroes of the 'Mark. Some elements have caused confusion, but the virtuous in Wintermark understand that there is a grievous need, and propose ways that need can be met. Much of the conversation takes place among the grimnir, who combine the study of the healing arts with practical experience of the battlefield. The solutions they suggest are equally practical and include redefining unfocused sinecures; rebuilding the healing house of Wittal's Grove; helping the warrriors to help themselves; and raising the profile of Asny of Stormspire whose name inspires the opportunity.

You can learn about the situation, and the practical solutions put forward by the healers of Wintermark, in **[All lift together](All_lift_together.md)**.

### An Unexpected Alignment

- **Wintermark; potentially Varushka, the Imperial Orcs, the Military Council, and the Imperial Senate**

The situation in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md) remains dire, and attempts to retake the [Suaq](../nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) homeland have been rebuffed by the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md). Diplomatic overtures have failed. The Empire clings to a foothold in [Sealtoq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#sealtoq), but the sands run swiftly through the hourglass and soon [the arrangement](../winds-of-war/383YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#sermersuaq) that keeps the Jotun from retaking that ancient town will have run its course.

The Jotun are not the only orcs in Sermersuaq of course. The [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) control the [Silver Peaks](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#silver-peaks) in the north-east of the territory, and seem to have made their own accord with their western cousins. Now, though, something has changed. The Thule are no longer content merely to watch. A small delegation has approached Sealtoq, under a white flag of truce - it seems the Thule have a proposition for the people of Wintermark...

You can learn about the Thule offer, and its potential repercussions, in **[An unexpected alignment](An_unexpected_alignment.md)**.

### Seize the Moments

- **Imperial Orcs, Dawn, potentially Navarr; Ambition, Vigilance, and General assemblies**

During the Autumn Equinox, Bloodcrow Udoo announced the ambition of the Imperial Orcs to claim the Barrens for their nation, a call to arms that received the backing of the Imperial Orc Assembly. The Druj hold the territory, and show no signs of letting it slip through their talons, but with the orcs of the Mallum defeated in Ossium and on the back foot in the Forest of Ulnak, now might be the moment to take action and drive them out of the Barrens as well.

One matter still to be resolved is what form an assault against the Barrens should take; a direct assault to destroy the Druj, or something more focused around uniting the remaining orcs that live under the thumb of the cruel tyrants. There are also allies to consider; perhaps they could be found among the virtue assemblies? Perhaps the orcs of the Great Forest, or even the briarborn who live among them? This is no simple undertaking after all.

And of course there is also the matter of Dawn. The glorious nation has a long history of conflict in that sprawling wilderness, and has attempted to conquer the territory many times, but implacable opposition from the Druj has always stymied them. How will they react to this ambitious claim by the Imperial Orcs?

You can learn about the opportunities for the Imperial Orcs to press their claim to the Barrens in **[Seize the moments](Seize_the_moments.md)**.

### Between Wind and Water

- **Dawn, especially the national assembly, the [troubadours](../religion/Troubadour.md), and the knights both [questing](../nations/dawn/Questing_knight.md) and [errant](../nations/dawn/Knight-errant.md); [Lady of the Proffered Hand](../../mixed/titles/Lady_of_the_Proffered_Hand.md)**

The role of the troubadour in Dawn is to use poetry, storytelling, and song to recount the deeds of glorious heroes, both the living and the dead. There are plenty of priets in Dawn, but it is often the troubadours that lead the way. During the Autumn Equinox, three statements of principle in particular resonate with their calling. Three opportunities have arisen as a consequence.
In Weirwater and Semmerholm, the legacy of storytellers and musicians is discussed, and a chapel for the troubadours is proposed. On the borders of Brocéliande, the threat of the vallorn is on everyone's tongues, and ways to support the knights who quest against it are discussed. And in the far west, in the Freeborn lands, the plight of ancient cousins is made clear and new homes amidst the splendor of Astolat are offered.

You can learn all about these opportunities in **[Between wind and water](Between_wind_and_water.md)**.

### Beyond the Light

- **Varushka, Imperial Orcs, Dawn, the League, especially their national assembly and generals; military units**

It has been almost exactly a year since [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) became part of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md), and there are ample opportunities for ambitious settlers to carve out new vales.

[Three challenges](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#special-qualities) remain however, preventing the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) reaching its full potential. The territory is [Underdeveloped](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#underdeveloped) - lacking all but the most basic infrastructure. It is [underpopulated](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#underpopulated) - there are few Imperial citizens here. It is [under threat](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#under-threat) by the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) - it would be an easy matter for the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md) to return.

Still, the Varushkans have a great deal of experience dealing with rich, perilous lands, and for many of them Ossium is just another challenge to be understood and overcome - one that promises rich rewards. Now the national assembly calls for the [wagon raiders, sellswords](../nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md#wagon-raiders-and-sell-swords), and [wardens](../nations/varushka/Warden.md) of Varushka to head east, and with axe and torch bring these wild, dark lands to heel.

You can learn about the opportunities presented in Ossium in **[Beyond the light](Beyond_the_light.md)**.

### Thousands or More

- **Varushka; Imperial Senate, Synod, Bourse, Conclave; potentially any Imperial citizen, especially from Dawn or League territories bordering the Semmerlak**

It's taken a while but the Civil Service have finally completed their survey of the wealth of Ossium. They confirm the wealth of the forests, and the paucity of minerals in territory, but they also catalogue the spoils of war seized from the Druj. These include valuable opportunities; the Palace at Orieb; the Stinking Market; Marshstand Skerry; the Bonehives; the Fields of Glory. It will fall to the Imperial Senate to determine who will made custodians of these unique sinecures and ministries.

They've also presented a report on ways to make Ossium more hospitable. On the wider benefits offered by a new port on the Semmerlak; a barracks-armoury in Nearweald to defend the new vales; and an extension to the Iron Roads already in progress. It seems the future may be bright for Varushkan settlers in the Empire's newest territory - if they're able to capitalise on these opportunities!

You can read about these opportunities in Ossium - to assign new titles and build new things - in **[Thousands or more](Thousands_or_more.md)**.

### Across the Abyss

- **The League; Prosperity Assembly; Imperial Senate; Imperial Conclave**

The Sand Fishers were one of the first groups of orcs that the Imperial armies encountered when they invaded Ossium. At first they seemed a peaceful people; later it became clear that they had been enslaved by the Druj and brutalized for decades. Forced to gather food under soul-crushing horror of the Druj miasma, when the Empire gave them an opportunity to claim a dank forest in Holberg, they jumped at the opportunity to put the Semmerlak between them and their former home.

The Druj followed them, but the Empire kept them at bay. They brought them the Virtues, and showed them how a different path than the one the Druj had forced on them. They're grateful, but there's more the Empire and their friends in the League can do to help them get away from their legacy of fear. Yet they've also embraced the virtue of Prosperity, which proposes harsh punishment for those who take without giving. What can the Sand Fishers give to the Empire that they will value?

You can learn about these questions, and some of their answers, in **[Across the Abyss](Across_the_Abyss.md)**.

### Daughters of the Empire

- **Highguard, especially the National Assembly; Archivists; the Virtue Assemblies; the House of Seven Mirrors and the Cenotaph**

Since the mandate of Summer 383YE, the congregations of Highguard have been experiencing a period of introspection. It is perhaps unsurprising that few of the statements raised during the previous summit have attracted much attention in Highguard. With one meaningful exception. The archivist Elkiah proposed that the shrine of Empress Britta be dedicated to a new purpose, and the Assembly agreed. Now two proposals have been submitted as a response to that judgement. One deals with the memorial itself, one speaks of a different kind of memorial.

The other statement that has received some attention also deals with memorials, and Thrones. Ruth of the Cenotaph made a personal appeal to the tempestuous architect Frederick di Sarvos, inviting him to discuss the tomb of Empress Lisabetta. It has apparently been well received - what ambitious artist could refuse such a once-in-a-career opportunity? Will he be convinced to add his expertise to the memorial for the Empress of Flowers?

Three memorials then, as the Day of the Dead approaches. A tomb, and obelisk, and a flame to light the way.

You can learn about all three of these memorial opportunities in **[Daughters of the Empire](Daughters_of_the_Empire.md)**.

### The Net of the Realms

- **Urizen; Imperial Conclave; Imperial Senate; Imperial Spymaster; Cato of Scarlet Guard**

### Briar Prejudice

- **Synod judgements have rebuked citizens for their prejudice against briars and encouraged greater acceptance**

Over 383YE, the growing threat posed by the Heirs of Terunael and cultists of Yaw'nagrah caused long-standing prejudice regarding the place of briars in the Empire to bloom. These questions were answered with emphatic responses by the Imperial Synod. The greater majority supported a statement by Josephine of Adina's Charge openly rebuking those citizens who judge briars for what they are rather than what they do, and citing the lack of Pride of those who act that way. There were related statements of Principle passed in the General, the Highborn and Marcher assemblies, by Friar Jack Reed, Tabris, Son of Armisael, and Mel Bleak of the Marches.

The clarity and forcefulness of the Synod's statements have decisively settled the questions that were being asked about briars. There's been no requirement for mandates; as the strong leadership the Synod has shown has been sufficient to address concerns. The prejudice that was openly growing has now abated, and in some cases the response has gone further. Some citizens have taken the words of the Synod to heart and are now openly questioning long-standing prejudice, not just against briars but also against Imperial Orcs. There is no conflict over the issue at this time, that has been settled by the Synod's statements, but more than a few citizens have personally resolved to treat briars (and Imperial Orcs) with more Pride.

**Game Information:** If you want your character to have been influenced by the statements of the Synod, then you are free to do so. Any character who wishes to put aside any prejudice that is outlined in the brief for their nation in favour of treating the lineaged and the orcs on the basis of what they do rather than who they are, is free to do so. This includes, but isn't limited to, the burial traditions of the Marches, and the Navarr concerns about briar susceptibility to the vallorn miasma. We are going to update the lineage and species pages to reflect these changing attitudes once we have had an opportunity to consult more widely on the potential impact of the evolving world.

### Flowering Briars

- **Flowers are now an additional trapping for briars**
- **Some further information, relating to peculiar dreams, can be found in [Faded from the winter](Faded_from_the_winter.md#dreams) wind of fortune**

During the Autumn Equinox, several briar lineaged citizens exposed to the vallorn and the followers of [Yaw'nagrah](../eternals/Yaw-nagrah.md) began to develop an additional trapping - blooms and flowers growing from their bark. There was some concern that these flowers represented some kind of invasive "mind control" targeting briars, but further studies have shown that this was predicated on a misunderstanding. It appears the flowers were related to some kind of curse inflicted by Yaw'nagrah, but fears of briars falling under the control of the Spring eternal seem to have resulted from a rumour mill run amok.

That curse now appears to have run its course. Briars are no longer experiencing the strange, peculiar dreams associated with the flowers, nor experiencing the burst of vitality and health that accompanied them. However, more than a few briars have found that the flowers continue to grow, and while the flowers may have begun in Brocéliande, they've also begun to appear on briars who've never been there. Examination shows them to be entirely natural, and not a cause for concern, divination repeatedly proves that any lingering curse or connection the flowers once had with Yaw'nagrah has now ended.

**Game Information:** We've added the option to include flowers and blossoms as part of the trappings of briars. There's nothing sinister about this; the worry that the flowers were somehow controlling briars was based on a miscommunication. There's no particular roleplaying effects associated with the flowers.

We'd initially offered [additional briefing](../winds-of-war/383YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#participation-briars) to player-character briars who might have been receptive to the approaches of the Heirs of Terunael, but we've taken that plot in a different direction. A number of [mandates](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md) were upheld at the Autumn Equinox Solstice and enacted in the months that followed. You can find a complete list [here](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#mandate).

Some mandates were upheld by their respective assemblies but failed due to the [intervention](Mercy_is_weakness.md) of the Wisdom assembly and the [sword scholars](../nations/urizen/Sword_scholar.md). [Judgement 7](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-7) (encourage Imperial citizens to find opportunities for cooperation with the [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md)), [Judgement 17](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-17) (encourage citizens to travel to [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) and offer aid there), [Judgement 52](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-53) (encourage Marcher pilgrims to make a pilgrimage to Holberg), and [Judgement 82](../synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-82) (encourage the virtuous to undertake a pilgrimage of dreams) were all ineffective as a consequence.

## Imperial Lore

No new rituals were added to [Imperial lore](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md) during the Autumn Equinox.

## Urizen Lore

Five new rituals became part of [Urizen lore](../nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) during the Autumn Equinox: [Swords in the Noonday Sun](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Swords_in_the_Noonday_Sun.md), [Good Green Oak](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Good_Green_Oak.md), [Eyes of the Hills](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Eyes_of_the_Hills.md), [Hook of the Hoarfrost Guardian](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Hook_of_the_Hoarfrost_Guardian.md), and [Ethereal Courier](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Ethereal_Courier.md).
