# Green and pleasant lands

## Overview

In distant [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md), in the [Boar's Dell](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md#boars-dell), a band of [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) has emerged without warning from [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md). They have come under a flag of truce, and claim to represent the two great clans of the independent orc nation to the east of the Empire. A combination of factors have encouraged them to send envoys to Anvil - the main one apparently being the decision taken by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) at the Spring Equinox to [recognise them](../senate-motions/Declare_peace_with_Barrens_orcs.md) as having the status of foreigners, rather than barbarians.

They are not a united force - the delegation is led by a shaman of the Great Forest clan, and a warrior of the Black Wind clan and there appear to be some internal tensions. They are accompanied also by a human with strong briar lineage who appears to be either a Navarr or one of the mysterious denizens of [Dourfell Keep](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#hope27s-rest).

This is believed to be the first time the people of the Empire have had any peaceful contact with the orcs of the Barrens. Reports claim that they threw off the oppressive chains of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) over Winter 377YE. Information about what they want - if anything - is in short supply.

## Significance

Initially led into Greenstead by a herald of [Lashonar](../eternals/Lashonar.md), they have been entrusted to the guidance of the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md). After much discussion, several members of the Splitroot [Striding](../nations/navarr/Striding.md) have agreed to escort them to Anvil. Using the [trods](../nations/navarr/Trod.md), it is expected that they will arrive some time on Saturday afternoon. As they are escorted by Navarr, it is likely the Navarr will be the first people to hear when the delegation gets close.

It is worth noticing that they are *not* here under the auspices of the law of delegation protection - the Imperial senate has formally recognised them as foreigners rather than barbarians. They are protected by the law and expected to abide by it in the same way as any [Asavean](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md), [Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md), or indeed any Imperial citizens.

## Resolution

The delegation from the Barrens came to Anvil and negotiated a treaty which was [discussed in the Senate](../senate-motions/Accept_Barrens_peace_treaty.md). The independent orcs returned safely to the Barrens.
