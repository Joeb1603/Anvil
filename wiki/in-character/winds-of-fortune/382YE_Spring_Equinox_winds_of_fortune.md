# 382YE Spring Equinox winds of fortune

*[Image: Anvil Darkness.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Anvil_Darkness.png)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/382YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md).

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters 'should' know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months.

Many winds of fortune open with a short section of in-character text. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip. They're mostly just a bit of fun and they tend not to contain anything vital to the understanding of the wind of Fortune.

## Winds of Fortune

### Before the Sunrise (Winds of Magic)

- **Navarr, especially in Therunin; Winterfolk; Dawnish; Artisans; people who like cats**

For the first time in ages, the state of the stars in the night skies are not the main event. Among other things, this round up of magic events includes mention of the new [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md) in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md); a very problematic offer from a powerful [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md), and an even more problematic [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) settling over [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md)-haunted [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md). Oh, and something about the cats of [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md). You can read the round-up [here](Before_the_sunrise.md).

### Beg, Steal, or Borrow

- **Freeborn; Marchers; Wintermark maggots; relic owners; monster hunters**

While everyone expects politics on the Anvil field, it's easy to forget that the Summits are also a time to engage in trade. There are mysterious Freeborn manuscripts and strange Feni letters to be sold, whose true value no doubt lies in the secrets they contain; a maggot selling loot; an agent purchasing religious relics; a refugee with a haunted jar; and a pair of Navarr who want to meet with monster hunters. They're all described [here](Beg-_steal-_or_borrow.md).

### Benevolence, Inspiration, and Legacy

- **Imperial Synod, Imperial Senate, all Imperial followers of the Way**

In [resolving a dispute](381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_fortune.md#all-work-together) between several [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) factions, the [General Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) has passed a [mandate](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md) that opposes further spiritual or religious conflict in the Empire. As part of the mandate, Severin von Holberg was empowered to present a sermon which was distributed throughout the Empire. The sermon examines what it means to be a virtuous citizen, and lays out a series of questions that can be used to help choose the correct course of action when faced with almost any dilemma. You can read his words - and learn about some of the sometimes radical responses - [here](Benevolence-_inspiration-_and_legacy.md).

### A Bit of Peace (Orc Affairs)

- **Imperial Senate, Navarr, Highguard, and Dawn**

Three nations of [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) - [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md), [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md), and the [Great Forest](../barbarians/Great_Forest_Orcs.md). Two-and-a-half delegations. Talk of peace treaties. And the sudden unexpected arrival of a number of people in northern Highguard out of a [forest](../world/Great_Forest_of_Peytaht.md) humans have no business coming out of... You can learn more about these developments [here](A_bit_of_peace.md).

### Change

- **[Mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) owners; magicians especially practitioners of night magic**

The [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) known as [Murit](../eternals/Murit.md) and [Soghter](../eternals/Soghter.md) have apparently undergone some sort of all-consuming, fundamental change. In their stead a new eternal rises. This has had some peculiar side-effects on the Empire - especially if you own a mine or a forest - which you can learn about [here](Change.md).

### City Lights

- **The League (especially bravos and mountebanks); Hahnmark; Madruga**

Money. You either have it, or you want it, or so the saying goes. In the League, the bite trade is contributing to a bit of a crisis that threatens to further complicate the rich street life of the four cities. In Madruga, the Grendel threat to the city of sails - to Siroc - is a topic of great concern. In Kalpaheim, the "city" of Wintermark, the opportunities presented by the easy access to the whole Empire provided by the Blood Red Roads are being extensively examined. You can learn about these three monetarily-driven situations [here](City_lights.md).

### Come Back to Stay

- **People attending Anvil**

Zenith has fallen to the Druj. Rumours are rife that this means the imminent return of a [certain](../history/Empress_Varkula.md) long-dead [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md). It's probably nonsense. But just in case it isn't, you can read about the eerie unexplained events [here](Come_back_to_stay.md).

### Coming Home

- **Wintermark**

An exceptionally rare conjunction of the [Sentinel Gate](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md) leads to the marshes of [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md), signalling an opportunity to do... something. Talk turns to [heroism](../../mixed/government/synod/Heroism.md), and the right way for the Winterfolk to live. You can learn more about this situation [here](Coming_home.md).

### Everything Has Rhythm (Conjunctions)

- **Imperial Heroes**

The [Sentinel Gate](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md) is a true wonder of the world, allowing Imperial heroes to visit places all around the Empire and deal with threats to the safety of their people. Some of those conjunctions can be determined well in advance, and the fact of their existence made public knowledge. You can learn bout some of these conjunctions [here](Everything_has_rhythm.md)

### Fight

- **Marchers, Imperial Orcs; Priests**

In the wake of the Imperial Senate declaring them to be foreigners, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) has sent not one but two priests - a Marcher and an Imperial Orc - to speak to the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) thralls. There have been some results, which you can learn more about [here](Fight.md).

### Free to Live

- **The Brass Coast**

The exiles from the island of [Zemress](../religion/Zemress.md) have settled in [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md). [Ambitious](../religion/Ambition.md) and [hard working](../religion/Prosperity.md), they are full of enthusiasm to reshape their new home, to bring about its full potential. Yet, unsurprisingly, integrating a people who have been separate to the Empire for centuries is not an entirely straightforward proposition. You can learn more about these developments [here](Free_to_live.md).

### Heroes

- **Senate; the Brass Coast**

The [Suranni](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) Duke of Kalino has sent five thousand troops across the Imperial border into [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) at the invitation of the [Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Iron_Confederacy.md). The rebel slaves that had established camps along the border have been decisively smashed, and almost all have been executed or returned to the Iron Confederacy in chains. You can learn more about these developments [here](Heroes.md).

### How Sweet is the Sea

- **Fleet captains; cartographers**

The Imperial Senate decided to go ahead and [map the Bay of Catazar](Talking_sailor.md#map-the-bay). You can find out more about how this went - as well as learning about the challenges facing sailors on a [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) dominated Bay - [here](How_sweet_is_the_sea.md).

### Hunter of Stars (Plenipotentiary)

- **Imperial Conclave**

For the first time, there are six responses to [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) messages sent during the Winter Solstice. Five [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) have agreed to a formal [parley](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#parley) with an [archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md), but one has refused outright. You can learn which eternals are keen to talk to the Empire, and which one is not, [here](Hunter_of_stars.md).

### Our Choice

- **Varushka; Brass Coast and Wintermark**

During the Winter Solstice, Bogdan Bogdanovich encouraged the Varushkans to embrace their heritage and stand with the Iron Helms. The people have listened, and now the generals of the northern nation have some choices to make. You can learn about those choices - and consequences - [here](Our_choice.md).

### Start a Fire

- **Navarr; Imperial Synod**

The Navarr have stymied the expansion of the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) in [Liathaven](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md). The forces of that coil in the depths of [Liath's Heart](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md#liath27s-heart) are overextended - weakened. A once in a generation opportunity presents itself - to strike against the vallorn and drive its infestation back. You can learn about these opportunities [here](Start_a_fire.md).

### United in Law (Imperial Address)

- **All Imperial citizens**

The [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md), [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md), has used her power to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) to present a personal message to all Imperial citizens. You can read her message [here](United_in_law.md).

### The Whole World (Trade Winds)

- **Fleet captains; Imperial Senate; Urizen**

International politics and trade are an unpredictable ocean of conflicting currents and unpleasantly spiky rocks. The Imperial Ambassadors do their best to navigate the Empire through those dangerous waters, but there is always something new to worry about. You can learn about current developments - including an opportunity to gain more commissions for the Empire, and some interesting suggestions about what to do about the imminent threat of Druj invasion - [here](The_whole_world.md).

### You'll Recognise Yourself

- **Lorenzo "la Volpe" Macelliao von Temeschwar; Practically everyone but especially the Imperial Senate**

As the Blood Red Roads spread across the Empire, and various constructions are created in the League cities, the benevolence of the Lorenzo "la Volpe" Macelliao von Temeschwar is slowly growing to become a legend. Some people like that. Some people don't. You can learn about both factions - along with the plans for quite a large number of possible [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) - [here](You-ll_recognise_yourself.md).

## Ongoing Situations and Outcomes

The [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) situation is currently stable - work has begun on the fortifications in [Greensward](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#greensward) and [Freemoor](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#freemoor). Sarcombe - should it be rebuilt - has been given a [charter](../senate-motions/Charter_Sarcombe_market_town.md). The Empire is [engaging in some dialogue](#fight) with the Jotun thralls. For the time being, at least, things are broadly positive... and everyone is waiting with bated breath to see what happens with the [Singing Caves](../../mixed/titles/Singing_Caves.md).

The Blood Cloaks army has been completed, bringing Wintermark to three armies, and the Imperial Orcs have chosen to permanently change the quality of the Winter Sun to Freedom Fighters.

## Roundup

### Arcane Projections

As of this event, the changes to the way [arcane projections](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) work that we announced before the first event are now in full effect. Magicians contribute arcane projections while downtime is open, rather than being done at events.

### Limited Opportunities

Last event there were a number of time-limited opportunities to [commission](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) unique structures. In each case we've updated the *resolution* section of the Winds of Fortune to clarify which are still available and which are not. The key commissions that remain available to the Empire at this time are the opportunities in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) following the [epic storm](Sowing_on_the_mountains.md), and the magical structures designed by [Richard of Holberg](Foggy_mountain_top.md#richard-of-holberg) for the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md).

## Imperial Lore

The ritual [Infant Starts with a Blank Slate](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Infant_Starts_with_a_Blank_Slate.md) has been added to [Imperial lore](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md) by the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) and any Imperial magician may master them following the normal rules.

During the Autumn Equinox, the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) announced that the [schema](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) for the [Bloodcloak](../../mixed/magic/items/Bloodcloak.md) would be [added to Imperial lore](../senate-motions/Add_Bloodcloak_schema_to_Imperial_Lore.md), allowing any [artisan](../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) to master the creation of these items. The knowledge of this [magic item](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) has now been distributed and any Imperial artisan can choose it as one of the items they know how to create.
