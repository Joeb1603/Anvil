## Overview

A long shadow has fallen over the west of the Empire. Isolated [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) raids have long been a feature of the border regions, but there were rumours that they were growing in intensity at the Spring Equinox and now it is clear that the threat is all too real. Bands of Jotun raiders have attacked farmsteads in [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md), [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md) and [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md) before retreating back over the border to the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md). The cities of [Meade](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#meade) and [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md) remain safe but the lands around them both are plagued by raids, while in the north there are reports of Jotun attacks against halls in western [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md) and [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md).

The news is not all grim. Two distinct Jotun warbands raiding Sermersuaq are corned and wiped out by the Imperial armies stationed there.

Better yet, the two military units assigned to [Fort Braydon](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#the-gilded-peak) in [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) - their ranks swelled by a regimented array of crystal warriors commanded by Anwar i Del Toro i Riqueza - are able to intercept a Jotun scout, carrying information back to the Jarls. It is clear from the scout's interrogation that in the coming season the Jotun intend to continue the probing attacks on Mitwold and Kahraman, but to launch major raids into Kallavesa, Bregasland, Upwold, and Tassato.

## Significance

The [fortifications](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) of [Forte Fidelis](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#golden-downs) in Mitwold and Fort Braydon, as well as the [Imperial armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) in Sermersuaq, are sufficient to protect these territories from the raids this season, provided the raids do not grow worse.

If the Empire does not find a way to blunt the impact of the other raids however, then every [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md), [farm](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md), [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md), [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) in Kallavesa, Bregasland, Upwold, and Tassato will suffer a significant loss of production. The Eastern Guard in Upwold is too far from the Marcher border to protect against the raids as they currently stand, it would need further reinforcements to provide assistance.

At present there is definitely no sign of any Jotun army moving over the border, but this is a grim omen. Historians claim that when the Jotun raids begin in force, it is usually a prelude to war.

## Resolution

The response has been swift and co-ordinated; the generals of the [Red Wind Corsairs](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#the-red-wind-corsairs), [Winter Sun](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_territories.md#the-winter-sun), [Fist of the Mountains](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#the-fist-of-the-mountains), [Hounds of Glory](../nations/dawn/Dawn_military_concerns.md#the-hounds-of-glory) in Kallavesa, Bregasland, Upwold, and Tassato respectively. This has led to several skirmishes, but the presence of the Imperial armies blunts the impact of these raids quite effectively.
