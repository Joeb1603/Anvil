## The Waters of Therunin

- **A powerful Spring curse has settled over the territory of Therunin**

Therunin is a marshy forest with plenty of streams and pools of standing water. Rain is common, and the climate alternates between warm and moist, and cold and damp, depending on the time of year. A handful of weeks before the Spring Equinox, however, all the waters of Therunin become infused with supernatural vitality. Dewdrops glisten like diamonds, rain is refreshing and fills those exposed to it with energy and vigour, and every cup drawn from every stream brims with healing power. The [Rivers of Life](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_of_Life.md) have settled over the Navarr territory; any wound that is less than fatal heals quickly and completely without leaving a scar. The Empire is familiar with this ritual and a simple [detect magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Detect_magic.md) seems to confirm it is simply the exercise of [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) (a magnitude fifty [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) from the Spring realm).

The ritual works by indiscriminately infusing all the water in the territory with Spring magic. And this is Therunin... where there is a [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md).

For the most part, the Navarr avoid using rituals such as Rivers of Life and its counterpart [Rivers Run Red](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_Run_Red.md) on territories with vallorn in them because the results can be very unpredictable. When the Jotun came into Liathaven thirty years ago, the Warmage at the time cautioned against using Rivers of Life to resist their advance, and was supported by the Archmage of Spring and several prominent Navarr vates.

There are already stories of the [miasma](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md#vallorn-miasma) thickening in [Greenheart](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#greenheart) and [Sweetglades](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#sweetglades). The giant insects of Therunin are becoming restive - and reports of attacks on some of the more isolated [steadings](../nations/navarr/Steading.md) are beginning to circulate. For now, the situation appears to be manageable but it is impossible to predict what might happen if the curse is allowed to run its full course over the next three months - especially with all the questions still hanging over the vallorn of [Liathaven](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md)'s currently active status.

One major fly in the ointment is that if this is Rivers of Life, then the best-known tool for countering it is beyond the reach of Imperial magicians - Rivers Run Red is [interdicted](../../mixed/government/conclave/Interdiction.md) by the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md), and casting it is a serious criminal offense.

## The Krampus Incident

- **The pair who assaulted the Krampus are available for Inquisition should a priest raise the judgement.**

During the Winter Solstice, the peculiar Krampus-beast associated with both the [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [egregores](../nations/general/Egregores.md) and the [Imperial Orc](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) was apparently attacked and slain. It is not certain that the creature has remained dead however - there are scattered reports that the spirit of the mysterious being was banished to the [Krampushall](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#krampushall) in [Pakaanan's Pass](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#pakaanan27s-pass) and was able to reform there, much as a ghost or a spectre will sometimes do. It remains to be seen if it will ever be able to return to full strength.

Regardless, many Winterfolk are concerned about this assault on such a prominent symbol of their traditions. The perpetrators of the attack have been identified as the [Suaq](../nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) **Qimmiq Swiftblade** and **Nukilik Adamantine**. They have made no attempt to deny their actions, but have thus far refused to explain the motivation for doing so. The venerable [stormcrow](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_religious_beliefs.md#stormcrow) Baena Stormsdottir publicly challenged the pair, and announced that she would be traveling to Anvil explicitly to bring them to [Inquisition](../../mixed/government/synod/Inquisition.md). Unfortunately, a fortnight before the Spring Equinox she suffered an unfortunate fall leaving her with two broken hips and unable to travel. As far as anyone has been able to discover there was no foul play or curse... but still the timing was a little unsettling.

Baena has sent several messengers to respected priests and stormcrows in Wintermark, urging them to complete the task she is unable to discharge. She has, she says, agreed with Qimmiq and Nukilik that they will be present at Anvil between six and seven on Saturday evening, should she have been successful at passing the judgement of inquisition. They are expecting to be inquisited by Beana but any priest might raise the [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) and challenge the pair to explain their actions

The legal situation with the two perpetrators is uncertain - given that nobody is entirely sure what the Krampus actually is, and given that it does not appear to have been killed so much as badly inconvenienced, there is more than a little confusion as to whether a crime has taken place or not. No doubt the magistrates may be able to shed some light on this situation should any Winterfolk wish to consult with them.

(**OOC Note:** Any stormcrow attending Anvil is free to roleplay they have received a letter from Baena Stormsdottir urging them to raise an inquisition against Qimmiq and Nukilik if they wish).

## The Spiral Sword

- **The runeforge being built in Redoubt is now complete.**

Work has finally been completed on the *[Spiral Sword](../../mixed/titles/Seer_of_the_Spiral_Sword.md#the-spiral-sword)* - the first [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md) successfully constructed in the Empire since its foundation. It was [commissioned by the Senate](../senate-motions/Construct_runeforge_in_Redoubt.md) during the Spring Equinox 381YE, and paid for by a quartet of wealthy benefactors - Edmundo, Achillies of Damakan's forge, Thomas of Upwold, and Asael of Cantiarch's Hold. Construction has been overseen by the [Asavean](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) architect [Almodin Oktístis](Those_who_worship.md#almodin-oktc3adstis2c-priest-of-the-builder), meaning that it has much of his trademark style about it - mosaics of divine smiths and weavers, statues of badgers and six-armed magicians, and the like.

The [Seer of the Spiral Sword](../../mixed/titles/Seer_of_the_Spiral_Sword.md) is a new [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that bestows custodianship of the runeforge for a year, and is appointed through open auction via the [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md). Any Imperial citizen may hold the title, although only an [artisan](../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) will gain the ability to [create brand new](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md#unique-crafting) [magic items](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) or unique [schema](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md#forge-schema). The auction is predicted to be hotly contested, given the singular abilities the custodian of the runeforge will gain access to.

The runeforge stands on the road between [Cargo](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#cargo) in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) and the [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) trading-port of [Hedra](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#hedrossan). Ironically, this puts it dangerously close to the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) occupied territory of [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) - if the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) were to move in force against Redoubt it may well prove to be quite a short-lived structure. Perhaps the rumours that the Empire is cursed, and any runeforge will bring disaster, are not so far-fetched.

## Thorn of the Ancestors

- **The eternal Irra Harah has placed a bounty on Imperial citizens**

Three weeks before the Spring Equinox, a briar child brings a message to the civil servants associated with the Imperial Conclave. The Spring eternal [Irra Harah](../eternals/Irra_Harah.md) has placed a bounty on the head - or rather the heart - of the [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [naga](../../mixed/magic/lore/Naga.md) **Zadkiel de Coeurdefer**. If he is killed, and his heart cut out, Irra Harah will grant a boon to anyone who brings him the heart. Likewise, he will offer boons for the hearts of **Chiara i Zayden i Riqueza** of [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and **Henry Ward** of [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md). During the Spring Equinox, anyone who brings one of these hearts to the [Imperial Regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) and [opens the portal there](../../out-of-character/rules/Operate_portal.md) can cast the heart through and it will be received by the eternal.

The eternal also offers a lesser boon to anyone who lays the [Curse of Gangrenous Flesh](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Curse_of_Gangrenous_Flesh.md) or the [Curse of Decrepitude](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Curse_of_Decrepitude.md) on one of these three will receive a lesser boon, to be agreed with an agent of the herald once the curse is placed.

If all three are dead by the end of the Spring Equinox, Irra Harah will meet with the Spring [archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) to discuss a boon to be granted to the Imperial Conclave.

## The Unwoven Stars

- **The stars are missing in Semmerholm, the Barrens, and Zenith**
- **The [penumbral veil](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Drawing_the_Penumbral_Veil.md) has been drawn over Otkodov, and over the Imperial territories of [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md) and [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md)**

For most of 381, the [stars were absent](Series_of_dreams.md) from the night skies over most of the Empire, and over [Liathaven](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md) and [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md). They returned - for the most part - shortly before the Winter Solstice 381YE. They are still absent however over the Barrens and [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md), and shortly before the Spring Equinox, the stars over [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md) likewise disappear from the night sky.

Rituals such as [Eyes of the Sun and Moon](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Eyes_of_the_Sun_and_Moon.md), [Eye of the High Places](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Eye_of_the_High_Places.md), and [Dreams in the Witch House](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Dreams_in_the_Witch_House.md) performed on one of these territories are impeded. A [detect magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Detect_magic.md) incantation in Semmerholm confirms that the shroud there is a Night effect of magnitude fifty, meaning any attempt to scry the territory must at least equal that magnitude or no information can be discovered. The strength of the shroud over the Barrens, and the new shroud over Zenith, are *assumed* to be comparable, but there is no widespread information about whether that is the case.

Magical scholars suggest that if these shrouds are similar to those that cloaked the entire Empire, and to those created by [Drawing the Penumbral Veil](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Drawing_the_Penumbral_Veil.md) the first two will last until shortly before the Winter Solstice 382YE, and the third over Zenith until the start of the Spring Equinox 382YE, unless they are removed prematurely - or replaced, or reinforced, by whatever created them.

#### Naga influence

As is expected by now, any [naga](../../mixed/magic/lore/Naga.md) who has spent significant time in any of the three territories where the stars are still hidden experiences the following roleplaying effect; *your [natural urge](../../mixed/magic/lore/Naga.md#nagaroleplaying-naga) toward subtlety and secretiveness is greatly strengthened. Naga in general relish being mysterious and secretive, but while experiencing this potent roleplaying effect you may need to make a significant effort of will to answer direct questions or be honest about your intentions.*

The roleplaying effect is significantly lessened at Anvil - indeed the longer one stays out of a territory where it applies the less pressing the urge to secrecy and subtlety becomes - but a naga with a strong lineage who has spent time in the Barrens, Zenith, or Semmerholm will still experience the roleplaying effect at least until Saturday morning.

### The Stars are Wrong

A more traditional magic appears to have been drawn over [Urdur](../barbarians/Otkodov.md#urdur) in [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md). Merchants visiting the Thule trading posts in the territory report that at night the sky is full of peculiar phenomena, and while the constellations are familiar they are in peculiar positions. The moon seems to shift its phase at random, and eerie lights or clouds of colour are commonplace. Magical scholars believe that this means the Thule have [drawn the penumbral veil](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Drawing_the_Penumbral_Veil.md) over the territory to discourage observation. It is not clear at this time if the same veil lies over [Nithoggir](../barbarians/Otkodov.md#nithoggir), [Sküld](../barbarians/Otkodov.md#skc3bcld), or [Verthandi](../barbarians/Verthandi.md) and difficult to determine without using scrying magic to examine the territories in question.

Likewise, it appears that ritual shrouds have been drawn over [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md) and [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md), warping their stars and protecting these territories from magical scrying for the coming year. These shrouds have a particular resonance to them that means both territories are also prone to heavy, dark fog, and the altered stars are often hidden behind inky black clouds.

## The Citadel of Fog

- **A dripping castle of mist and mud rises again in the Morass**

A magical fortification appears in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md) [again this season](Foggy_mountain_top.md#castles). The dripping edifice of mud, woven reeds, and crooked roots is wreathed in ephemeral mists that none-the-less reduce visibility to a scant dozen yards wherever they drift. The [enchanted fortification](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Dripping_Echoes_of_the_Fen.md) rises from the muddy waters of [the Morass](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#the-morass), again at the behest of the Sussivari Frost Coven. The mists that settle across the Morass stretch into all parts of the territory, emerging without warning between the trees and across the marshes. Where they appear, they are sometimes accompanied by mysterious figures - masked humanoids in dark blue leather, shrouded in hooded cloaks of multicoloured feathers. Several hunters and travellers in the dark woods of the north speak of encountering them - and on occasion being aided by them against [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) ambushes, or when lost. Shortly before the Spring Equinox, the fogs dissipate and take the eerie strangers with them.

## The Cats of Spiral

Over the last three months, every cat in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md) has been subject to a potent [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) curse that has given them terrifying nightmares. They sleep poorly and awaken ill-tempered and unhappy. This peculiar curse seems unconnected to the thing in [Screed](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#screed). Those few residents of the devastated territory who keep cats report that many of their pets have unaccountably gone missing - they assume the animals to have fled the territory.
