# 384YE Autumn Equinox winds of fortune

*[Image: The Sentinel Gate is the gateway to adventure.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Ribbon_at_the_Gate.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns in the [Winds of War](../winds-of-war/384YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md).

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters 'should' know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months.

Many winds of fortune open with a short section of in-character text. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip. They're mostly just a bit of fun and they tend not to contain anything vital to the understanding of the wind of Fortune.

## Winds of Fortune

The Winds of Fortune will be presented prior to the Autumn Equinox.

### Hopeless as a Feather (Plenipotentiary)

- **Archmages and the Imperial Conclave; specifically invited guests including Brat Umbral Path, Morwenna Winterbuilt, Ahzrukhal of Highguard, Spyridonakes of Urizen, Clèrabela Vicente de Metri of the League, Baerwynn of the Sussivari, and anyone carrying a boon of Sadogua that has lost its power; Grandmaster of the Golden Pyramid, Catellus di Tassato Regario of the Cat's Curiosities, Izaac von Temeschwar, and anyone with an interest in religious curiosities; Elienne of Dawn; Leo the Rainbow Sage and Catrina the Unbelievably Magical**

The six archmages can each dispatch a single [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) message to an [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) of the magical realm they represent every season. The eternal is bound to respond - perhaps with a formal [parley](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#parely), perhaps with something unexpected. During the solstice passed, each of the archmages sent such a message, and replies have been received. During the Autumn Equinox parleys will take place with the [Globbersnotch](../eternals/Sadogua.md), the [Summer Mage](../eternals/Meraud.md), and the [Prince with a Thousand Foes](../eternals/Irra_Harah.md); the [Sovereign Lord of the City of Chains](../eternals/Callidus.md) will provide a peculiar market-auction for items of religious and spiritual significance; and the [Wise Crow](../eternals/Wise_Rangara.md) will provide an opportunity for several people to do something that is apparently ill-advised and bound to end badly.

You can find the details of this season's plenipotentiaries in **[Hopeless as a feather](Hopeless_as_a_feather.md)**.

### Back in the Same Place (Appraisal)

- **Imperial Senate; the League National Assembly; the Assembly of Ambition or Pride; citizens of the League especially those of Temeschwar**

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has instructed [Graciana i Lòpez i Guerra](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#graciana-i-lc3b2pez-i-guerra) to perform an [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) into the potential to construct a university in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), that will increase the [number of commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md#maximum-commissions-available) the Empire can initiate each season. This has proved to be a far from easy task, given the restrictions placed on her by the Senate's motion. She has done her best however and presents two extravagant possibilities for achieving the Senate's goals.

You can read about those two possibilities in **[Back in the same place](Back_in_the_same_place.md)**

### Into Tired Shadow

- **Highborn Assembly; Highborn generals; Urizen**

For three seasons the [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) armies have committed themselves to a [crusading storm](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#highborn), straining every sinew to wipe the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) from the face of [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md). It seems they will stop at nothing to ensure the salvation of those [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) people who still suffer under the cruel tyranny of the pernicious barbarians. Even now, with the Druj [trapped by the cunning of the Imperial generals](../winds-of-war/The_darkest_road.md) victory in Zenith is not assured. For there is another threat, one less martial but as every bit as terrible. The Druj have unleashed their unspeakable evil on Zenith, and if it is not dealt with it threatens to taint the territory for years to come.

You can read about the choices facing the Highborn Assembly in **[Into tired shadow](Into_tired_shadow.md)**.

### Dear Green Space

- **Wisdom Assembly; Imperial Senate; The League, especially the people of Holberg; Patron of the Pavilion; the Imperial Conclave**

During the Summer Solstice, Hembeneth i Guerra of the Brass Coast, [Cardinal](../../mixed/titles/Cardinal.md) of [Wisdom](../religion/Wisdom.md), called on the virtuous to find a practical solution to the [terrible corruption](In_the_garden_of_defiance.md) of [Holmauer Park](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#holmauer-park) in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md). The beautiful park was cursed at the same time the mirrors of [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) were shattered, apparently as part of some attack against [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) by the [Hag Queen](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#skathe) of [the Thrice-cursed Court](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md). Now, the Wisest minds in Holberg and the Empire have turned their attention to the situation, considered the problem of the cursed park from every angle, and come up with a practical solution to the problem. Not that *everyone* in Holberg views the cursed Park as a problem to be solved...

You can learn about the proposed solution, and the alternative suggestion, in **[Dear green space](Dear_green_space.md)**.

### Good Old Smooth Red Wine

- **Imperial Senate; people of Dawn, especially Astolat; Prosperity assembly**

The Summer Solstice saw the [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [Cardinal of Prosperity](../../mixed/titles/Cardinal_of_Prosperity.md), Darian Numbers, turn the eyes of their assembly on [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md). Over the last three months, the pilgrims of Prosperity have been speaking with [yeofolk](../nations/dawn/Yeofolk_of_Dawn.md) across the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) coming up with proposals for ways to make Astolat even more prosperous. Thanks to the [Blood Red Roads](../nations/the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#blood-red-roads), the fayres of Dawn are seeing many more merchants and traders from other nations - both Imperial and foreign - and now there is a chance to build on that growing tide of prosperity.

You can learn about the proposals for increasing the wealth of Astolat in **[Good old smooth red wine](Good_old_smooth_red_wine.md)**.

- **Dawn, especially the Dawnish assembly; Imperial Military Council; Imperial Senate; [Dean of Laroc Cathedral](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_Laroc_Cathedral.md); [Voice of the Twin Roses](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_the_Twin_Roses.md), [Lady of the Proffered Hand](../../mixed/titles/Lady_of_the_Proffered_Hand.md), [Keeper of Aurelie's Garden](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_Aurelie-s_Garden.md), Claudia Varkulova of House Remys**

The dream of conquering [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) has been a [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) aspiration for longer than anyone can remember. For centuries they have [contested the territory](../history/Conflict_in_the_Barrens.md) with the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), but its great size has prevented them from ever managing to seize complete control. Even with the [evacuation](One_more_day-s_light.md) of 383YE, there are a few [houses](../nations/dawn/Noble_house.md) that still cling on to their estates, suffering the brutal rule of the Druj than give up their ancient homes. In recent years that dream has begun to seem distant, and now, as the tide of war with the Druj is beginning to swing decisively in the Empire's favour, they must endure the censure of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md).

You can learn about the situation in Dawn with regard to [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) in **[Throw back the echoes](Throw_back_the_echoes.md)**.

### Down the Cavalier Road

- **Imperial Conclave; Archmage of Summer; Master of Ice and Darkness; Enchanters of Dawn; General of the Eastern Sky**

The [king and queens](../eternals/Eternal.md) of the Summer realm are notoriously fickle with their affections. Quick to anger, their rage is usually mercifully brief but it can be intense and all consuming. At the recent summit, the Empire took steps to change the nature of their relationship with several of them and has reaped a number of rewards as a result, some of which have in turn lead to further [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md). The [enchantresses](../nations/dawn/Earl.md#enchanters) of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) have been busy getting the lay of the land in the Summer realm over the last several months for their own reasons - which relate to the future of the [Eastern Sky](../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md). It appears most of the Summer eternals are currently well disposed to Imperial citizens - with one glaring exception.

You can learn about relations with the Summer realm, and a new incarnation of an old opportunity for the people of Dawn, in **[Down the cavalier road](Down_the_cavalier_road.md)**.

### The Bones of What you Believe

- **Navarr; Advisor on the Vallorn; League and Highborn Assemblies; General Assembly; [Cinderpath Custodian](../../mixed/titles/Cinderpath_Custodian.md), [Talon of Kauppahall](../../mixed/titles/Talon_of_Kauppahall.md), [Mistress of the Dust Parador](../../mixed/titles/Mistress_of_the_Dust_Parador.md), [Broker of the Invisible Cord](../../mixed/titles/Broker_of_the_Invisible_Cord.md), [Bargainer of the Iron Tower](../../mixed/titles/Bargainer_of_the_Iron_Tower.md), [Harbourmaster of the Semmerlak](../../mixed/titles/Harbourmaster_of_the_Semmerlak.md); Imperial Senate; People who hate the vallorn**

At the Summer Solstice, the Navarr assembly sent Iniska Ashwood to enact a mandate urging their people to take Pride in what has been achieved so far - that one day the vallorn will be eradicated and that the Navarr must plan for that future. Perhaps that day is closer than it has ever been? Should the Empire be prepared to build up the Great Library of Hacynian, it may even be imminent... And even if it is not, there are opportunities here for Navarr captains to adventure in vallorn-tainted areas, and for the Highborn and Navarr National Assemblies to pool their resources in pursuit of their shared cause.

You can learn about these several opportunities related to Navarr and to the vallorn in **[The bones of what you believe](The_bones_of_what_you_believe.md)**.

### The City Asleep

- **Navarr, Highguard, Dawn; Advisor on the Vallorn; Military Unit captains; Imperial Conclave; people who like adventure or hate the vallorn**

The Empire has recently undertaken a number of daring endeavours - the [raid on Skallahn](../winds-of-war/Under_heaven.md), the [attack on Chalonsio](../winds-of-war/Mosaic.md), and the [installation of the Red Iron Door](../winds-of-war/Brightness_long_ago.md) to name but a few. This demonstration of the Empire's adventurous spirit has attracted the attention of [Queen Rhianos](../eternals/Rhianos.md), the *Regent of the Eternal Sea*. Signs of Rhianos' increased interest in the Empire have been apparent for a while - their team very nearly [won the patronage](Our_thumping_hearts.md) of the [Icy Crag](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Ice_and_Darkness.md#the-icy-crag-of-the-eternal-sun), and they are offering to [sponsor Dawnish champions](Good_old_smooth_red_wine.md#field-of-silver-fishes) on dangerous quests. But a fortnight before the Autumn Equinox, their heralds turn up simultaneously across Highguard, Dawn, and Navarr bringing news from *Foamfollower* of a most ambitious proposal: offering their aid to anyone prepared to try and find out what lies at the heart of the fallen city of Terunael in [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md).

You can learn all about this dangerous adventure into the depths of vallorn-infested Brocéliande in **[The city asleep](The_city_asleep.md)**.

### Never Be Found At Home

- **Imperial Senate; Fleet captains especially Navarr and Wintermark; Imperial Quartermaster; perhaps Ambassadors to Faraden and Thule, and the Imperial Consul**

The [raid on Skallahn](../winds-of-war/Under_heaven.md) was an epic triumph on land and at sea. Thanks to the courage of Imperial [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) captains supporting the raid, a window of opportunity has been opened up to allow the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) to commission a project to map the [Sea of Snows](../world/The_seven_seas.md#sea-of-snow) - or at least the part of it that abut [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md), [Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md), and perhaps even [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) lands. Much like the successful [mapping](../senate-motions/Map_the_Bay_of_Catazar.md) of the [Bay of Catazar](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md), this project requires Senate investment and will produce large-scale maps (rather than the kind of detailed maps that a [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) might produce). However, it also presents a real opportunity to explore some of the mysteries of the waters west of the Empire and maybe provide some insight into a question that has troubled Imperial mariners from time to time: why *do* the Faraden and the Jotun both fear the sea so much?

You can read about the opportunity to map the Sea of Snow in **[Never be found at home](Never_be_found_at_home.md)**.

### Let That Be Your Last Battlefield

- **Imperial Synod; Lady of the Proffered Hand; Anyone with an interest in the Free Folk**

At the Summer Solstice, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) upheld several [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) relating to the "*[Free Folk](Two_steps_on_the_water.md#strangers-in-a-strange-land)*". The former Asavean slaves have sought to share their understanding of the Way with the Empire, but have received a mixed reception. As crops all across the Empire have ripened and the days have begun to grow shorter once again, the Free Folk have returned to their diligent work wherever they are welcomed to apply their labour. But they have no stopped speaking about the [paragons or exemplars](../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) they term the *Divine Family*. By enthusiastically sharing their stories with anyone who asks, they risk incurring the wrath of the Synod - but that seems to do little to deter them. Indeed, it seems some of them would welcome some kind of definitive response from the Empire, so that they can decide what to do next.

You can read about the Free Folk situation, and how it is coming to a head, in **[Let that be your last battlefield](Let_that_be_your_last_battlefield.md)**.

### Grown from the Revolution Seed

- **Imperial fleet and military unit captains; Quartermaster of the Imperial Armies**

Last season, the Empire joined forces with their allies in the [Liberty Pact](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Liberty.md#liberty-pact), to launch a [surprise attack](../winds-of-war/Mosaic.md) on the city of [Chalonsio](There_is_thunder.md#chalonsio) in the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) - and the raid was a triumph. Perhaps the greatest victory of all was the capture of vital information about the *Noble Lady of the Sea*... which it turns out is not a person at all but a ship belonging to a group called the *Slave Lords of Rachensgrab*. Their red-sailed raiding ships have attacked every coastline in the world at some point, and then shown up weeks later to sell human misery in the slave markets of Asavea and [Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md). But now the Empire knows where the Slave Lords' aerie is to be found... and can strike a potentially fatal blow to the international slave trade...

You can learn about the potential to bring down the Slave Lords of Rachensgrab in **[Grown from the revolution seed](Grown_from_the_revolution_seed.md)**.

### Light Upon the Water

- **Farm, business, mine, and herb garden owners; Imperial Senate; residents of Tassato; Master of the Imperial Mint; Dawnish and Varushkan residents of Semmerholm, Weirwater, Karsk, Karov, and Ossium; the Marches; the Silver Chalice; holder of the Syrene's Wisdom Bourse Seat; Gatekeepers; citizens of Madruga**

The Great Drought of 384YE has instead become the Great Storm of 384YE. During the Summer Solstice, Imperial magicians cast both [Thunderous Deluge](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Thunderous_Deluge.md) and [Regrow the Land's Heart](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regrow_the_Land-s_Heart.md) using the special resonance imparted by a magical [conjunction](Every_time_it_rains.md). This caused the [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) and the [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) to spread unchecked across every territory that was Imperial at that time. Now, with the storms abated, the rebuilding can begin. Some places have weathered the tempest better than others - the city of [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md) has been badly damaged by the surging waters of the Vassa, but the rain has brought new opportunities to [the Semmerlak](../nations/varushka/The_Semmerlak.md) and [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). All over the Empire, the [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) has left things changed - and the full impact of those changes has not yet been assessed.

You can learn about the lingering effects of the storms, and what they have stirred up, in **[Light upon the water](Light_upon_the_water.md)**.

## Other Events

There are a number of other events and situations which are noteworthy, but which for whatever reason have not led to a full Wind of Fortune in their own right.

### Hylje

- **The hylje have provided a place that they might be contacted by the Mediator of Hyljehal**
- **The current [Mediator of Hyljehal](../../mixed/titles/Mediator_of_Hyljehal.md) is *Raal Dunn* of Wintermark**

The *hylje* - the shapeshifting seal-people of [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md) - continue to strengthen their friendship with their [Suaq](../nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) cousins. Following the [rescue of Koskinen](../winds-of-war/In_good_company.md#second-skin-28conjunction29) in Summer 384YE, the *hylje* have realised they cannot live apart from the people of Sermersuaq. As such, they have provided the Mediator with an address where they may send a [Call Winged Messenger](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md) should they have matters to discuss with the sealfolk. They are clear that they are unlikely to be interested in making regular trips to Anvil - it is a very long way and there is little there they are interested in - so any request for them to do so is likely to be denied. The ability to send a winged messenger is intended for the mediator to raise matters of mutual interest, request assistance, or let the hylje know things happening in the wider world that might effect them. At this time, the hylje are not especially interested in hearing from anyone else.

### Axou Disgruntlement

- **The Axou are signatories of the Liberty Pact**
- **There is lingering dissatisfaction due to the removal of the Tamazi from Axos**
- ***Tarquinius Ankarien* of Urizen is the ambassador to Axos**

As part of the Liberty Pact, the Grand Ilarchs have ratified the same amendments to the alliance as the Empire and the other signatory nations. However, while as a group they have agreed to remove impediments to Imperial trade, not all the citadels are in favour of the move, and nor can they simply instruct individual citizens to favour the Empire. On the whole, though, Imperial merchants to Axos are receiving the benefits of free trade mentioned above.

Anger over the [Tamazi incident](Tired_of_sleeping.md) has largely faded, but there are still Axou merchants reticent to deal with Imperial citizens. [Fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) trading with Axos will experience a 1 rank penalty after the Autumn Equinox, and it is believed that the penalty will have vanished entirely by the start of the Winter Solstice. This still makes trade with the Chambers of Issyk somewhat unappealing for most Imperial captains, where it combines with the parlous state of the docks there to inflict a 3 rank penalty on all such endeavours.

## Trade Summary

Rather than spread this information out around the various foreign nations, we've collected it here for ease of reference.

- **Free Trade:** Imperial ships trading with Commonwealth, Axos, or Sumaah receive a +1 rank bonus until the start of the Autumn Equinox 385YE.
- **Asavean Archipelago:** With sanctions imposed by both the Empire and the Asavean plenum, any fleet captain trading with [Nemoria](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#nemoria) receives a -2 rank penalty to their production.
- **Axos:** The impact of [recent events](Tired_of_sleeping.md#diplomatic-incident) continues to fade, but the [dreadful docks at Issyk](A_mountain_of_vipers.md#trading-with-issyk) remain a problem. Consequently Imperial fleets suffer significant penalties to trade with Axos. A fleet captain trading at the [Towers of Kantor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#towers-of-kantor) suffers a -1 rank penalty, while trading at the [Chambers of Issyk](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#chambers-of-issyk) suffers an incredible -3 rank penalty.
- **The Commonwealth:** Commonwealth citizens remain enthusiastic about trading the Imperial fleet captains. Fleets trading with [Leerdam](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#leerdam-28eastern-commonwealth29) receive an additional dram of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), while fleets trading with [Volkavaar](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#volkavaar-28western-commonwealth29) receive an additional vial of [Artisan's Oil](../../out-of-character/rules/Artisan-s_Oil.md) on top of their normal production.
- **Principalities of Jarm:** The ports of [Kavor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#kavor-28northern-principalities-of-jarm29) and [Vezak](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#vezak-28southern-principalities-of-jarm29) remain closed to Imperial fleets.
- **Sarcophan Delves:** Imperial captains continue to be welcomed in the Delves. Fleets trading with the great port-city of [Sarcophan](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#sarcophan) receive an additional dose of [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md) and an additional dose of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) on top of their normal production.
- **Sumaah Republic:** Imperial captains are free to trade with the Sumaah Republic. Fleets visiting [Zemeh](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#zemeh) receive standard production.
- **Grendel:** The port of [Oran](Everything_has_a_price.md#a-new-destination) is closed to Imperial fleets.
- **Iron Confederacy:** The port of [Robec](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#robec) remains closed to Imperial fleets.
