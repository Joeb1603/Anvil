# 388YE Winter Solstice interlude

*[Image: All the news from Anvil town.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:All_the_news_from_Anvil_town.jpg)*

## Overview

Downtime following the Autumn Equinox is now open. It allows players to process their character's downtime choices. Not all resources allow a player to make a choice about how they operate between events, but we've provided information about new opportunities for [congregation](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md), [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md), and [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) owners in particular, as well as a reminder about existing opportunities created at earlier events.

On this page, we've outlined and offered some context for various effects and events. As always, these include quick summaries, mostly out of character, to explain effects such as large-scale [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) and [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md).

In some cases, there may be further developments around the events listed here in the Winter Solstice winds of fortune. They may also be handled with a *Resolution* entry on the appropriate Wind, or a note in the *Other Events* section of the next events Winds of Fortune page.

**The deadline for Downtime submissions is midnight on *Sunday the 18th of October***

## Choices

You can find details of these many decisions here:

Not everything is widely distributed across the Empire, but any character who attends [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md) can easily find out details of what went on in the houses of power during the event.

## Imperial Guerdon

The [Imperial Guerdon](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#guerdon) is a bursary set up by the Imperial Senate primarily to reward military units that support Imperial armies, spy networks, and fortifications. The value of the Imperial Guerdon is divided between each military unit and fleet that supports an army in proportion to their effective strength. The [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has the power to exclude any or all Imperial armies and navies, fortifications, or special projects (such as spy networks) from the Guerdon. If they do so, then a character who supports such an army, fortification, or special project will receive nothing for doing so.

The Guerdon remains at **350 Thrones**.

This season, the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has [announced](../senate-sessions/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#assign-the-guerdon) that shares of the Guerdon will be shared between military units supporting the following armies:

- [Golden Axe](../../mixed/government/military/Golden_Axe.md) in [Salt Flats of Sanath](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md)
- [Seventh Wave](../../mixed/government/military/Seventh_Wave.md) in [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md)

And by military units taking the following actions:

- **Find the Drakes** and **Take Your Time Looking For Lost Marchers**, both [searching for the missing Drakes](#the-missing-legion-drakes) in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md)

No other armies, or actions by military units or fleets, will receive shares of the Guerdon.

## Military Units

### Ward Kroll's Lair

- **Urizen can send their military units to the *Ward Kroll's Lair* guarding venture**
- **A total of seventy-five hundred strength is needed to secure the lair and allow ilium to be used**
- **If successful, an arcane projection could be created to call on Kroll to defend the region**

The danger of Kroll was [investigated](Metamorphosis.md) last season by the sentinels of Urizen. Of course, the creature could have been defeated; its sacs destroyed and its tendrils hunted down. But a different route was chosen: one of divination and wards that allow the magicians of Urizen to shackle the creature and see it defend the nation.

If the venture succeeds, then the nation will be able to put wards in place using seven rings of ilium to create a seven-pointed [heptagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heptagram), a "lock" that will keep Kroll submerged until called by the magicians of Urizen using a ritual.

There are no rewards for military units taking this action.

### Reconquer Therunin

- **Navarr can send their military units to support efforts to reconquer Therunin**
- **The efforts are led by *Dochas Weeping Glade* and heralds of Agramant**
- **Heralds of Agramant will help military units claim heart's blood as spoils of war from those slain**

The [Golden Axe](../../mixed/government/military/Golden_Axe.md) have moved from Therunin, and in its place, the heralds of the [Abominable One](../eternals/Agramant.md) stand ready to offer [their aid](Accomplices.md#does-he-ever-shut-up) to the people of Navarr. Led by *Dochas Weeping Glade*, some of the Navarr who remain in the territory look to take back the nominally Druj-controlled regions with Agramant's aid.

Only Navarri can take this campaigning venture. A starting military unit will receive two pawns of [heart's blood](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#hearts-blood) as spoils of war; an upgraded military unit will gain proportionately more.

A list of everyone whose military unit - or enchanted resource - supported this action will be widely publicised; though anyone who takes part can give a pseudonym (by emailing [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) before downtime closes). They will be committing the crime of [treason](../../mixed/government/senate/List_of_Criminal_Offences.md#crimes-of-position), which will likely end in execution if they are found out.

Any who take up the offer of the *Bloody Ally* will be allowed to invite a small portion of the magic of its domain in the wasteland to their military unit (also by emailing [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) before downtime closes), marking them as true believers in the wisdom the *Horned Manticore* has to teach. He may use this to offer them ventures in the future that might interest them or send aid to support them.

### Protect the Vales of Varushka

- **Military units that take the *Protect the Vales of Varushka* guarding venture will receive a potion of the deep wood**
- **A potion of the deep wood is one of *Bloodvine Liquor*, *Blackblood Thorn*, *Darkwine*, *Elder's Gift,* or *Heart's Blood***

The frightened folks of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) have provided additional rewards for the Dawnish and Varushkans who [protect the vales of Varushka](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#protect-the-vales-of-varushka) this season. Any military unit that travels the roads and defends the vales will receive a potion of the deep wood; a consumable from the forests of Varushka. A potion of the deep wood will either be a *Bloodvine Liquor*, *Blackblood Thorn*, or *Darkwine* - the sanguine potions related to the [dubik](../../mixed/titles/Tolvoli_Butcher.md) - an *Elder's Gift* - created by the now-scattered [trudniki](Lean_and_athirst.md) - or a vial of [heart's blood](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#hearts-blood).

### Commonwealth Against Cold Sun

- **Military units can travel to the Commonwealth to aid them against the *scions* of Cold Sun**
- **A starting military unit that takes this action will receive a War Amulet, three doses of Soldier's Booze, and 36 rings**
- **A military unit from Highguard or the League will receive a one-rank bonus**
- **If at least 10,000 strength supports this action, then the Commonwealth will secure additional success for the armies fighting there**

In previous seasons, the Commonwealth has carefully not solicited Imperial military aid in fighting in the east. In part, former diplomat *Ludkhannah Schöningen* did so out of recognition that the Empire was embroiled in its own fight against its enemies, and would not draw away Imperial soldiers without the express approval of the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). It appears that [her replacement](If_we_never_meet_again.md#resignation) has no such qualms. An [invitation](If_we_never_meet_again.md#against-the-end-of-all) has been presented to the Empire's warriors to join the battle against Cold Sun in the Commonwealth if they wish to accept it.

Following the Autumn Equinox, any [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) commander can commit to support the **Commonwealth against Cold Sun** [campaigning](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning) [venture](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md). This venture involves travelling to the Commonwealth and fighting the *scions* of Cold Sun alongside the armies of that nation. A standard military unit that takes this venture will be well compensated - the Commonwealth will provide three doses of [Soldier's Booze](../../mixed/economy/Trade_goods.md#soldiers-booze), a [War Amulet](../../mixed/economy/Trade_goods.md#war-amulets), and 36 rings. A military unit that is [enchanted](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) or [upgraded](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#upgrades) will receive more rewards. In particular, the Commonwealth are interested in securing professionals from [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) and disciplined soldiers from [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md), and anyone of that nation who answers the call will receive a one-rank bonus to the rewards they receive.

If at least 10,000 effective strength of military units is sent to the Commonwealth this season, it will secure additional success for the armies fighting there. It won't end the war with Cold Sun outright, but it will help the Commonwealth achieve a strategic win against the incursion into their lands.

### Jarm Against Oblivion

- **Military units can travel to the Principalities of Jarm to aid them against the *scions* of Cold Sun**
- **A starting military unit that takes this action will receive four mana crystals and a bag of golden apples**
- **A military unit from League, Dawn, or Urizen will receive a one-rank bonus**
- **If at least 10,000 strength supports this action, then Jarm will secure additional success for the armies fighting there**

With a ceasefire in place, and following several months of support from [Conclave orders](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave_order.md), the House of Princes [extends an offer](If_we_never_meet_again.md#aid-against-oblivion) to the Empire's mercenaries. Given that some of them no doubt have experience fighting [Cold Sun](../eternals/Cold_Sun.md), their experiences would prove valuable to the Principalities in their efforts to deal with the incursion of the *scions*.

Following the Autumn Equinox, any [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) commander can commit to support the **Jarm against Oblivion** [campaigning](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning) [venture](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md). This venture involves travelling to the Principalities of Jarm and fighting the *scions* of Cold Sun alongside Jarmish soldiers in a concerted effort to destroy the supernatural armies. A standard military unit that takes this venture will be well compensated - the Jarmish will pay four crystal mana and a [golden apple](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#golden-apple). Warbands that are [upgraded](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#upgrades) or [enchanted](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) will receive commensurately more reward. They are particularly keen to employ professionals from [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md), and warrior-wizards from [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) and [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md). A military unit belonging to one of these three nations receives a one-rank bonus to its effective rank if it takes this venture.

If at least 10,000 effective strength of military units is sent to Jarm this season, it will secure additional success for the armies fighting there. It won't end the war with Cold Sun outright, but it will help the Jarmish achieve a strategic win against the incursion into their lands.

### Support Black Eagle Banner

- **Imperial military units can support the Black Eagle Banner**
- **The Suranni army is part of a larger effort to conquer Reinos**
- **A standard military unit that takes this action will receive three ingots of green iron, valuables worth 108 rings, and 6 random resources**
- **A military unit from Dawn will receive an additional two ingots of green iron**
- **A military unit of 200 strength or more receives an extra 72 rings, and a military unit of more than 300 strength receives an additional 72 rings**

Any Imperial military unit can take a [campaigning](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning) action to support the *Black Eagle Banner* under General Hendrick. A starting military unit will receive a payment of three ingots of [green iron](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and valuables worth 108 rings, along with six [random resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#loot) worth of loot. An [upgraded](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#upgrades) or suitably [enchanted](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) military unit will receive more loot. The Iron Confederacy values elite warriors and veterans more than less experienced soldiers, and as such any military unit that contributes 200 strength to the campaign receives an extra 72 rings, and any unit contribution of 300 strength or more will receive another 72 rings on top of that.

General Hendrick has made clear a personal desire to fight alongside the knights of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md); any Dawnish military unit that fights with the Black Eagle Banner this season will receive another two ingots of green iron (for a total of 5) no matter how experienced they are.

There is one potential problem; purely magical troops will be greeted with horror by the Suranni. If anyone sends soldiers created with rituals such as [Conclave of Trees and Shadow](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Conclave_of_Trees_and_Shadow.md), [Carve the Crystal Guardian](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Carve_the_Crystal_Guardian.md), or [Raise the Dragonsworn Cohort](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Raise_the_Dragonsworn_Cohort.md) to fight alongside the army in Reinos it will trigger a diplomatic incident.

### Scout the Tsirku Scouting Network

- **Tsirku is so large and inhospitable that only a single region can be explored each season**
- **The venture requires at least twenty-four hundred strength to gain any useful information**
- **Military units assigned to *Conceal the Northern Lights* will reduce the effective strength of this venture**
- **Military units from nations other than Wintermark and Varushka suffer a one-rank penalty due to the cold**
- **There are no rewards for taking this option**

The call of the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md), **Rahmah i Darwisj i Erigo**, is for scouts to aim westwards, towards [Tromsa](../barbarians/Tromsa.md) and the region described as "*barren, windswept, with wide and unsettling skies*". Lake Nutjuitoq likely extends into the area, and there may be a connection to the [Sea of Snow](../world/Sea_of_Snow.md) given that there must be *some* way for the great sea beasts to reach the [Grave of the Giants](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#the-grave-of-the-giants). The *hylje* have [offered aid](Beneath_a_moonless_sky.md#as-semersuaq-strode) in exploring this region, and during the Autumn Equinox, **Raal Dunn**, the [Mediator of Hyljehal](../../mixed/titles/Mediator_of_Hyljehal.md) was able to provide three measures of stardust to the hylje to secure their aid.

The vast territory of Tsirku is so inhospitable that it [represents a huge challenge to scouts](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#scouts). Rather than exploring the entire territory in one go, the scouting network will explore a single region each season. If the scouts are successful, the Imperial Spymaster will be able to choose where to explore next. Only once the entire territory has been scouted will a map suitable for scrying be created. There are no rewards publicly available for anyone taking this option.

The Empire has seen that *[Masked Courtiers of Secrets](../eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md)* have tried to hire military units to thwart efforts to scout the wastes. Every military unit that takes the venture to **Conceal the Northern Lights** will count its military strength *against* the total strength of those trying to explore Tsirku.

### Conceal the Northern Lights

- **The Whisper Gallery is eager to prevent the secrets of Tsirku from being spoiled**
- **Characters that have had contact with the eternal can take this action to reduce the effective strength of efforts to Scout Tsirku**
- **Military units from nations other than Wintermark and Varushka suffer a one-rank penalty due to the cold**
- **Heralds of the eternal will provide crystal fire and luxury goods to anyone who helps to thwart the efforts to scout the territory**

The [Whisper Gallery](../eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md) is determined to prevent Imperial scouts from [destroying the secrets of Tsirku by exposing them to the sterile light of day](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#tsirku-is-secret). The *Court of the Shadowed Oracle* [have made an offer](Monarchs_and_outcasts.md#tyrannical-discipline-whisper-gallery) to those Steinr poets, offering [crystal fire](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#crystal-fire) and luxury goods to anyone who will help them try to thwart the efforts of the scouts by creating false trails, spoiling supplies and otherwise sabotaging the efforts to discover more. Every military unit working to **Conceal the Northern Lights** subtracts their military strength from those attempting to scout Tsirku.

Only characters with a military unit who have had contact with the Whisper Gallery can take this action. A starting military unit will receive two pieces of crystal fire and luxury goods worth 72 rings for their help; an upgraded military unit will gain proportionately more.

### Swords Against Darkness

- **Urizen, League, and Dawnish military units can take a questing action to attack the threats in Spiral**
- **Sufficient strength has been put towards the venture that it will remain indefinitely**
- **A standard military unit that takes this action will receive three mana crystals and two doses of liao**

After a lot of work persuading some of the wealthier Apulian merchants and key [arbiters](../nations/urizen/Arbiter.md), Naomi has been able to persuade them to back an attempt to [solve the problem](My_friends_are_my_estate.md#swords-against-darkness). The solution is simple enough, and for once, Naomi's idea requires relatively little upfront investment. If the Empire can muster enough military units to directly attack the threats in Spiral, then the inhabitants of the territory would be able to get on top of the problem. If that could be achieved, it would only need a little support on an ongoing basis to keep the threats under control. Naomi likens it to weeding an overgrown garden - it requires a lot more effort to get the weeds under control than it does to keep it that way.

With the backing of wealthy patrons, the venture provides three mana crystals and two liao to any standard military unit taking part. A military unit with at least 200 effective strength (from upgrades or rituals) also receives a [Circlet of Falling Snow](../../mixed/magic/items/Circlet_of_Falling_Snow.md) as part of their rewards.

The efforts of Urizen, League, and Dawnish military units were effective over the past three seasons. The horrors of the Black Plateau have been beaten back, and the penalty to military units from the malignancy in [Screed](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#screed) has been removed.

## Fleets

### Hunt the Free Landing Kraken

- **Freeborn fleets can take the *Hunt the Free Landing Kraken* questing voyage**
- **A starting fleet will receive 180 rings, with upgraded fleets receiving more money and doses of ambrellic**
- **Freeborn who are members of the Magpies sodality benefit from the knowledge of the Wraith King and receive an additional 36 rings**

With the completion of the Municipal Docks, the Freeborn have the infrastructure in place to begin hunting the kraken of [Free Landing](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#free-landing). Freeborn [Fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) can take the opportunity to hunt the juvenile kraken, claiming treasure - in the form of money - and even doses of ambrellic. Whether the [City of Gold and Lead](../eternals/Ephisis.md) will offer the same amount as previously is still unknown, but [apothecaries](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary) will still be able to use it for brewing certain potions. Specifically, anyone who knows how to brew [Philtres of the High Peaks](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_the_High_Peaks.md) or [Philtres of War](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_War.md) can use ambrellic in this fashion. The resulting potions are not quite as potent as those that require [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md), but the techniques for creating them are the same.

- The *Elixir of Sunlit Salt* can be brewed by any apothecary who knows how to brew the Philtres of War. It requires three drams of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), two drams each of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) and [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), a dram of [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), and three doses of *ambrellic*. It will restore all the [hero points](../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) of someone who [consumes it](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md#consuming-a-potion).

- The *Elixir of Abyssal Depths* can be brewed by any apothecary who knows how to brew the Philtres of the High Peaks. It takes three drams of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), two drams each of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) and [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), one dram of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), and three doses of *ambrellic*. It restores all [personal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#additional-mana) to someone who drinks it.

The Freeborn members of the [Magpies](../../mixed/titles/Magpies.md) are supported by the crew of the [Wraith King](Wayfaring_strangers.md#hail-to-the-wraith-king), receiving an additional 36 rings in treasure when they hunt the kraken.

### Jakhalsmarkt

- **The Sarcophan port of Jakhalsmarkt is no longer available to Imperial fleets**
- **The House of the Jakhal wanted 70 thrones to allow fleets to continue to access the Jakhalsmarkt**

For the better part of a year Imperial [ship captains](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) were able to trade with the [Jakhalsmarkt](All_the_night-tide.md#jakhalsmarkt). The opportunity was arranged by *Annabel de Gouden* of the [House of the Jackal](../foreign-nations/Founders_of_the_Delves.md#the-jackal), responding to the Imperial need for luxury goods and substances of use to ritual magicians. It seemed that de Gouden and her House have made a canny investment; the number of traders from the Empire visiting the Jakhalsmarkt only increased over the last four seasons.

*Mistress de Gouden* assessed the situation and put [forward an offer](Those_memories_of_you.md#jakhalsmarkt). She and her people will continue to keep Jakhalsmarkt open to Imperial traders, and commit to ensuring there are valuable goods for them to buy - [vis](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md) and *star jewels*. Nothing needs to change in that regard. However, in order to secure sources for these goods for the next year, it would have required an up-front investment of 70 thrones from the Empire. *Annabel de Gouden* would have preferred to receive the investment from the [Imperial Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md), but was equally happy to do business with the [Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sarcophan_Delves.md), **Dino i Riqueza**, (although she made clear she would *really* prefer to work with a group like the [Court of Far Horizons](In_the_Court_of_Far_Horizons.md), with whom she imagined it would be possible to make a much better deal, but she understood politics does not always give one what one wants).

As the money was not provided to the House of the Jakhal, the port of Jakhalsmarkt has been closed to Imperial traders for the foreseeable future.

## Arcane Projections

- **Arcane projections must be submitted before the close of downtime**
- **It is not possible to submit arcane projections that create personal curses**

The deadline for submitting an [arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) is now the close of downtime. This is a significantly shorter window than previously. We've made this change to improve our ability to process arcane projections - an earlier deadline means that the game team have more time to consider each arcane projection, which will allow us to be more detailed and consistent in our responses.

### Colleges and Forges

- **We've changed the email address for college of magic and runeforge submissions**

It is important for characters responsible for a [college of magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) or [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md) to remember that the deadline for submitting rituals for codification, or instructions to the runeforge, is also the point that downtime closes. You *must* use let us know how you plan to use your college or forge by this point, otherwise it will not be possible to submit anything and the season will be lost.

The e-mail address to which such instructions should be sent is now [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) rather than rules. The rules decisions about an arcane projection that is being codified, or a new item, have already been made, and it is the plot team who deal with the creation of ritual texts, unique items, or schema.

### Artefacts

- **If you are creating an artefact you will need to email plot@profounddecisions.co.uk with the requested name and roleplaying effect**

Artisans can use [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) to make a permanent artefact version of any magic item they can create. These powerful items retain their properties indefinitely, are readily identifiable as unique named items, and can exert powerful [roleplaying effects](../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) over anyone bonded to them.

Anyone wanting to create an artefact should email [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) before downtime closes with their CID and what magic item they want to be an artefact. They should also include what name they want the artefact to have and the desired roleplaying effect. With roleplaying effects, it is best to avoid anything with religious themes; that is the purview of the [hallow](../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md) ceremony. It is best to avoid naming specific concepts: a roleplaying effect of "*You feel an urge to protect Urizen lore*" would be returned as "*You feel an urge to preserve the magical secrets of your people*". It can be interpreted as the same for someone in [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md), but it would encourage someone in [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) to protect [Runesmith's Law](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law), or a magician-prince of [Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) to protect their own collection of ritual texts.

As with [hallow](../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md#effects), comedic names or names which are *obviously* taken from existing works of fiction are not appropriate for item names in Empire and will be rejected when identified.

## Magical Lore

- **Every summit it is possible that new rituals, magic items, or potions may become available to Imperial citizens**
- **Some of these options are only available to characters from certain nations, such as those entered into Urizen Lore or Runesmith's Law**

During the Autumn Equinox, three rituals were put forward for inclusion in Imperial Lore:

- [Chance Blossoms, Chaos Thrives](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Chance_Blossoms-_Chaos_Thrives.md) was [raised](../conclave-sessions/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_Conclave_sessions.md#imperial-lore-chance-blossoms-chaos-thrives) by **Yarvi Swiftwind**, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md) and funded by the *[Wise Flower chapter](Apparently_with_no_surprise.md#a-decrepit-flower)*
- [Shadows Hide the Passing Spear](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Shadows_Hide_the_Passing_Spear.md) was [raised](../conclave-sessions/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_Conclave_sessions.md#imperial-lore-shadows-hide-the-passing-spear) by **Syl**, as [Dean of the Lyceum](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md) and funding was provided by **Seren Embercast** on behalf of the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md)
- [Arc of Sküld](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Arc_of_Sküld.md) was [raised](../conclave-sessions/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_Conclave_sessions.md#imperial-lore-the-arc-of-sküld), and funded, by **Yarvi Swiftwind**, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)
