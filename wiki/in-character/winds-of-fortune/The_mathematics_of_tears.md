## Overview

During the Winter Solstice, the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) sent a delegate named **Johann Van Basten** to participate in the [Liberty Pact](../senate-motions/Ratify_Liberty_Pact.md) summit. While they nation committed to end slavery and were an active participant in the discussions, the Delves chose not to sign the pact once it had been drawn up. Over the last several months, however, representatives of various *bedelaar husbaas* have sent emissaries to the Empire with proposals intended to forge stronger trading ties between the people of the Delves and their Imperial "neighbours" to the north.

## The *Banket van Vrijheid*

- **The [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) have made the slave trade illegal in their nation, and for their citizens.**

The Sarcophan do not use slaves themselves. For a society dedicated to the pursuit of wealth, they are uncharacteristically squeamish when it comes to buying and selling thinking beings. A handful of families dealt in slaves, but they were barely tolerated by their peers. Shortly after the Winter Solstice, that toleration comes to an abrupt end.

The *bedelaar huisbaas* of Sarcophan meet at the great table in the banquet hall atop the palace of beggars each month. In between courses, they make decisions that affect the entire nation. The priests who oversee the feast and serve as an unofficial civil service announce their infrequent decrees. After the Solstice, they announce the decree that the trading of slaves is an unacceptable act for any Sarcophan, and furthermore that the practice will be a crime in the Delves.

*[Image: Bedelaar Huisbaas Annike advises her peers on dealings with the Empire. A Winged Messenger can be sent to her at The Tower of Crows, Sarcophan, the Sarcophan Delves.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:WingedMessenger.png)*

It is very rare for the *bedelaar huisbaas* to make such a decree - but it carries with it the force of law. Anyone found to be breaking this law will forfeit all their assets and be cast down, given over to the soulweavers to be made into one of the *ongelukkig*. Even the *bedelaar huisbaas* are not immune; a beggar-landlord caught supporting the slave trade will forfeit their seat at the great table with potentially ruinous consequences for their entire family. The rulers of Sarcophan do not make such decrees lightly.

The immediate response is subdued. With a single turning of the moon to divest themselves of any connection to the slave trade, a number of Sarcophan quit the Delves, heading for [Asavea](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) and [Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md). Others - many already in the process of transitioning to new markets - simply unload their slaves at a loss. The remainder take the hit to their finances and free their former property at whichever dock they were moored when news of the decree reached them.

There is surprisingly little disruption to the day-to-day lives of the people of the Delves. For a time the labour market is glutted with former slaves. Those few families so mired in slavery that they quit the Delves rather than face ruin are replaced by ambitious successors. While they are not signatories to the Liberty Pact, within a season the Sarcophan Delves stand beside the Empire, the [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md), the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) and even [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md) as a nation where the trade of slaves is not permitted.

## The Kruidenkenner Propositions

*[Image: Ockje Duisenberg currently resides at Gezondheid Huis , Crown's Quay , Necropolis for anyone who wants to talk about Kruidenkenner business via Call Winged Messenger .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:WingedMessenger.png)*

The Kruidenkenner are a powerful guild of herbalists and apothecaries from the Delves. They sponsored the [Kruidenkenner Magazjin](../../mixed/titles/Kruidenkenner_Trademaster.md#the-kruidenkenner-magazijn) in [Crown's Quay](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#longbeach), overseen by the [Kruidenkenner Trademaster](../../mixed/titles/Kruidenkenner_Trademaster.md). **Ockje Duisenberg** is the nominal head of the *Kruidenkenner* presence in the Empire. A skilled apothecary and wily businesswoman, she has drawn up two proposals for the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) to consider. The first is an ongoing opportunity to build warehouses in various nations that would allow the opportunity to purchase large amounts of a individual [herbs](../../mixed/magic/potions/Herb.md). The second involves an extension to the existing Kruidenkenner Magazjin that would support the trade of herbs with certain key [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) holders.

### Warehouses

- **The *Kruidenkenner* herbalists guild remains keen to construct additional warehouses in the Empire as detailed [here](A_force_to_be_reckoned_with.md#kruidenkenner-warehouses).**
- **The opportunity will be lost at the end of the Spring equinox.**

The Kruidenkenner Magazijn serves as a base of operations for the guild of Sarcophan herbalists. While the Trademaster claims their bounty of [Cerulean Mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), the herbalists themselves pursue their own trades within Imperial borders - buying and selling herbs, potions, and drugs in [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) and across much of the southern Empire.

For several seasons the *Kruidenkenner* are keen to expand their operation to other parts of the Empire, but they are prepared to wait on the Empire's interest only for so long. The [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to build one or more of their warehouse-enclaves will expire at the end of the Spring Equinox.

You can review the details of the Kruidenkenner warehouses [here](A_force_to_be_reckoned_with.md#kruidenkenner-warehouses).

With the creation of the Liberty Pact, the Gjullit family of Sarcophan predict that the [Cavabianca Dock](../../mixed/titles/Overseer_of_the_Cavabianca_Dock.md#cavabianca-dock) in [Caricomare](../nations/the-league/Caricomare.md) and the [Pallas Docks](../../mixed/titles/Prime_Factor_of_the_deBruin_Docks.md#pallas-docks) in [Cargo](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#cargo) may become unusable by the Empire due to the increase in prices associated with the new [tariffs](../../mixed/government/senate/Tariffs.md). They cannot match the quantities of material provided by the Asavean Heraclien and Ragnabe families, or by the Jarmish Magician-Prince of Parnau Principality, but they surmise that the Empire may be interested in securing white granite and weirwood at reasonable prices from Sarcophan merchants.

With that in mind, they propose an arrangement in which Gjullit ships bring both white granite and weirwood to the Empire for sale. This would, as usual, require reinforced docks and secure warehouses, and with an eye towards safety they propose [Siroc](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc) as the location of their new enterprise which they have rather prosaically christened the *Gjullit Docks*.

> **The Gjullit**  
> The Gjullit are newcomers to the great table of Sarcophan politics. While their traders have made regular visits to the Empire for several years, they have recently increased in prestige following a vicious take-over of assets left behind by one of the slaving families that quit the Delves for Asavea. They are enthusiastic entrepreneurs, seeking an opportunity for further profits.  
>  
> The Gjullit are busy establishing their position in Sarcophan politics, but are working with Bedelaar Huisbaas Annike, the [trade advisor](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md#known-correspondents) to the Delves. Any communication relating to this opportunity should go through her.

Constructing the docks would require a [senate motion](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) (or the use of an [Imperial wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md)), a [commission](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md), 30 wains of weirwood, 20 wains of white granite, and 100 Crowns in labour. It would take three months to complete, and create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) - *Trademaster of the Gjullit Docks* most likely. The well worn path would be to appoint this title through the [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) but it could be either Imperial or national in nature.

The title holder would be able to purchase *both* white granite and weirwood from the Gjullit. While the amounts of both would be initially small, if the docks proved profitable to the Sarcophan merchants there may be an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to negotiate an increase.

The Gjullit are keen to establish themselves; if they are to survive in the shark-pool that is the great feast they will need to begin showing a profit very soon. As such this opportunity is available only until the end of the Summer Solstice 383YE - and if work on the docks is not completed by the start of Winter 383YE the Gjullit will have to look elsewhere.

## The Van Basten Trade

- **Johann Van Basten has arranged a one-off opportunity to purchase 100 White Granite from the Sarcophan Delves for 500 Thrones.**

Johann Van Basten journeyed to Anvil as the Sarcophan Delves' delegate to the Liberty Pact summit. Following discussions with the [Master of the Imperial Mint](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md), **Lieselotte van Holberg**, and the [Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sarcophan_Delves.md), **Thanmir Hrafn**, he has arranged the sale of one hundred wains of white granite to the Empire as a bulk, one off deal. The price to purchase this would be 500 Thrones.

The opportunity is a limited one; even the wealthy Van Basten family cannot afford to sit on such a stockpile of white granite for too long. If the Empire has not taken advantage of the opportunity by the start of the Autumn Equinox, then regretfully the Van Basten will need to find other markets for their white granite.

(**OOC Note:** To take advantage of this opportunity, either the Master of the Imperial Mint or the Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves should ensure they have the 500 Thrones in their inventory and e-mail plot@ProfoundDecisions.co.uk after the event. The white granite will be available by the start of the next event).

## Sarcophan Enclaves

Two potent *bedelaar huisbaas* - the leaders of the Vandersaar and the Diepenbeek - have laid proposals before the Imperial Senate that would involve transforming certain ruined locations into trading enclaves. Both require that the Senate [concede](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) control of those locations to the Sarcophan Delves, but each provides very different benefits. The [Vandersaar proposal](#the-vandersaar-proposal) involves building an enclave in the southern Empire on the ruined Alivetti estates of [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md). The [Diepenbeek plan](#the-diepenbeek-plan) by contrast involves rejuvenating the ruins of Isember in [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md).

These opportunities are not exclusive; both could be approved by the Senate to allow the Sarcophan an enclave in the north and the south.

### The Vandersaar Proposal

- **The Vandersaar propose the Senate [concede](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) the ruined Alivetti guild estates in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) to their consortium so that they may build a Sarcophan enclave on it.**
- **Benefits include additional [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) for the [Steel Fist](../../mixed/titles/Steel_Fist.md); the permanent opportunity for [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) owners to gain [herbs](../../mixed/magic/potions/Herb.md) through [diversification](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md#diversification); and improved profit for Imperial [traders](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) visiting the [Delves](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#sarcophan).**

The **Alivetti guild estates** lie along the coast of the city of [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md), on the outskirts of the district of [Caricomare](../nations/the-league/Caricomare.md). They were once the home of the Alivetti - a powerful [League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) guild whose most famous recent scion was most likely [Empress Giselle](../history/Empress_Giselle.md). The estates were ruined by the storm that devastated Caricomare; during the rebuilding of the district in 378YE the waterlogged ruins were largely left to their own devices. Much of the old estate is now swampy marsh, haunted by mosquitos and vicious frogs.

This makes them ideal for the purposes of Bedelaar Huisbaas Edwin of the Vandersaar family. He represents a loose alliance of some of the richest families of the Sarcophan Delves. They are keen to establish closer trading ties with their nearest neighbours and the recent resolution of the Liberty Pact has convince them that now is the time to strike. They want the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) to [concede](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) control of the Alivetti estates to the Sarcophan Delves. The Sarcophan are masters at reclaiming and building in marshes and swamps; they will clear the ruins, reclaim as much of the estates as possible, and construct a dock, warehouses, and residences in the Sarcophan style, transforming it from marshy ruins into a home-away-from-home for Sarcophan captains and merchants. It will serve as a stopping off point for traders from the south, as well as a place Imperial merchants can visit to speak with Sarcophan captains.  
  
**Benefits**  
Edwin does not expect the Empire to simply *give* the estates to the Delves, however. As soon as the Imperial Senate passes the motion of concedence, the consortium he represents will increase the amount of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) provided to the [Steel Fist](../../mixed/titles/Steel_Fist.md) by half again - another four rings each season bringing the total bounty to twelve. This increase will continue as long as the Empire remains on good terms with the Sarcophan Delves, or until the Imperial Senate abrogates their concedence. In the event that the Empire wishes to end the agreement, however, Vandersaar and his fellows will expect a suitable payment in recognition of their investment in Alivetti.

> The Vandersaar  
> The Vandersaar are a well established and powerful family of the Delves who trace their roots back to the first settlers in the ruined city of Sarcophan. They already have extensive trade interests in the Empire, including the sale of weirwood at the [Concordium Dock](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_the_Concordium_Dock.md#concordium-dock).  
>  
> Edwin Vandersaar requests that any correspondence relating to his plan go through Bedelaar Huisbaas Annike, the [trade advisor](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md#known-correspondents) to the Delves.

The presence of a Sarcophan enclave of this size on the southern coast of the Empire will also offer an advantage to Imperial [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) owners. They will gain the ability to [diversify](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md#diversification) their resource to gain an income of herbs as well as money following the normal rules (an investment of 1 Throne, which will reduce the production of the resource by 36 rings and replace the money with two herbs of the character's choice).

Finally, the enclave will significantly improve the confidence of Sarcophan merchants - both those visiting the Empire and those at home in the Delves - that Imperial citizens are people with whom they can do business. Every Imperial [ship captain](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) visiting [Sarcophan](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#sarcophan) will gain an additional dose of [Marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md) and an additional dose of [Imperial Roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) each season.  
  
**Limitations**  
The Vandersaar are prepared to give the Empire some time to consider their proposal, but if the Senate has not acted by the end of the Summer Solstice 383YE the offer will be withdrawn and they will look elsewhere. Bedelaar Huisbaas Edwin requests that any official correspondence regarding the proposition go via **Bedelaar Huisbaas Annike**.

There is only one significant structure still present in the Alivetti estates - the broken tomb of Empress Giselle. Broken open by the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) during the [sack of Sarvos](The_sack_of_Sarvos.md), the body of Empress Giselle was recovered and now rests in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md). The initial intention of the Vandersaar is to clear the tomb along with the other ruins, but they have suggested that if the concedence motion requests them to leave the tomb intact they are prepared to work around it rather then destroy this landmark.

### The Diepenbeek Plan

- **The Diepenbeek propose the Senate [concede](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) the [ruins of Isember](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#the-ruins-of-isember) in [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md) to them so that they may build a Sarcophan trading enclave on it.**
- **Benefits include additional options for the members of the [Northern trade network](../nations/navarr/Northern_trade_network.md); and a new option for [independent captains](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) prepared to protect Sarcophan caravans in the dangerous northern Empire.**

Merchants from Sarcophan already have a presence in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md). Traders from the Delves who are prepared to risk the perilous journey find a ready welcome at the [Sarcophan Mooring](../../mixed/titles/Custodian_of_Ivarsgard_Docks.md#the-sarcophan-mooring) at [Ivarsgard](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#ivarsgard). Here they trade herbs - and occasionally more exotic substances - for Varushkan lumber, fur, and [ambergelt](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). The Diepenbeek family are keen to build on the opportunity presented by Ivarsgard, and have an ambitious proposal for the Empire; they wish to lease the [ruins of Isember](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#the-ruins-of-isember) from the Empire.

> The Diepenbeek  
> Unlike the Gjullit, the Diepenbeek have a place at the great table but they are not quite as wealthy and influential as the Vandersar. They are known in the Delves for the diversity of their business interests, prepared to take on any job that promises a clear profit. Their employees clear ruined tombs for valuable relics; sell fine [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) and [Imperial Roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md); and have a reputation for skill in the arts of [Autumn magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md).  
>  
> The Diepenbeek have requested that any correspondence relating to their proposals regarding Isember are sent via Bedelaar Huisbaas Annike, the [trade advisor](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md#known-correspondents) to the Delves.

Isember was destroyed during the war with the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md). Even before its destruction it had a reputation for lawlessness. The folk of the vale betrayed their people and sided with the orc invaders; their treachery attracted criminals, exiles, and heretics from across the north, drawing the ire of the heroes of Anvil and ultimately leading to their downfall. Nobody lives there now. Since the death of **Vesna Kovac** at the beginning of last year, even the [Sovereign's Heart](../../mixed/government/military/Karsk_spoils_of_war.md#the-sovereigns-heart) inn has closed its doors. The ruins have quickly been reclaimed by the forest, and there is talk of [wolves](../nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves) lairing in the cellars of the broken buildings. Traders prefer to go to Ivarsgard rather than risk the haunted woods.

The Diepenbeek plan is straightforward enough. They wish the Imperial Senate to [concede](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) Isember with a suitably worded [Senate motion](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md). They will take custody of the land, and build a trading enclave there. Their ships will continue to use the docks at Ivarsgard - Isember has limited access to the Semmerlak - but for as long as they maintain the lease of the land it will be considered part of the Sarcophan Delves.

In return, they will clear the ruins - and any monsters that may have taken up residence - and rebuild the town. Once the area is cleared, the Diepenbeek will ensure the immediate area is kept clear of wolves and bandits, in cooperation with the boyars of the local vales. Indeed they anticipate seeking the assistance of the people of Lestasny to ensure that they do not inadvertently transgress any of the unwritten laws that allow mortals to prosper in the dark hills and forests of Varushka.

The building will take a little time - they anticipate six months once permission is given - but it will cost the Empire nothing and will not require a commission. Once complete, Isember will be reborn as a Sarcophan settlement in the far north overseen by the Diepenbeek. This will allow them to trade with the northern nations - the rest of Varushka, with the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md), with [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), with the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) in [Hercynia](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md), with the [Winterfolk](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) at [Kalpaheim](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#kalpaheim), and with the Thule at [Krevsaty](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#krevsaty) and [Urdur](../barbarians/Otkodov.md#urdur).
  
  
**Benefits**  
First, the Sarcophan will need to clear the runs of wolves and bandits. They anticipate that this will be a significant undertaking, but are prepared to pay Imperial mercenaries to help them deal with the wolves and bandits. The immediate effect will be the creation of a one-of opportunity in the following season for Imperial captains to [help secure Isember](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#paid-work). The Diepenbeek will pay well in money, herbs, and metal.

The option to **Guard Sarcophan Caravans** would be added to the [paid work](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#take-independent-action) dropdown for Imperial [military commanders](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). The landlord would also serve as a point of contact for the Diepenbeek if there were any local problems that were not worth bothering the [Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sarcophan_Delves.md) with. This would create an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md), with tenure (serving until they died or stepped down) appointed by the [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) owners of Varushka, that would initially receive an income of 11 crowns each season but might be able to gain other benefits depending on how the settlement of Isember proceeds. Alternatively, the Senate may choose to state in their motion of concedence that they do not want the Sarcophan to do this, in which case the inn will be demolished along with the rest of the ruins.

If the Senate has not acted by the end of the Autumn Equinox 383YE, however, they will make other arrangements. They request that any official correspondence regarding the plan for Isember go via **Bedelaar Huisbaas Annike**.

### A Note About Concedence

[Conceding](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) either the Alivetti estates or the ruins of Isember to the Sarcophan Delves would function in a similar manner to conceding an Imperial commission. The estates and the ruins could be seen as representing [follies](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md), in a legal sense. The Sarcophan will claim the land, and begin to build their enclaves on it. Neither Isember nor Alivetti have any current Imperial population; the Sovereigns Heart Inn in Isember is effectively defunct since the death of its owner and has fallen into disrepair.

One of the advantages from the Sarcophan point of view to having land conceded to them is that they will not need to concern themselves with the Empire's laws regarding construction - they will be free to build without requiring Senate motions for every significant structure. This does mean that the Empire will have no oversight on what they construct in the land that has been ceded to them - while they maintain that they wish to build warehouses and docks the Empire would no legal recourse in the event that they chose to build (for example) a heretical temple.

it is important to understand however that concedence asserts the legal will of the Empire to give up *all* control over the affected area - it is categorically not possible to cede control of something but retain any element of control over it under any circumstances. Attempts to restrict what the Sarcophan can do with the land in question would meet neither the requirements of the concedence power itself, nor the terms of these [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md).

The Senate *could* abrogate their concedence at a later date - but doing so could lead to significant diplomatic repercussions if it were done without the cooperation of the Sarcophan Delves.

## Limited Opportunities

The opportunity to [commission Kruidenkenner warehouses](#warehouses) expires at the end of the Spring Equinox. The opportunity to [expand the existing warehouse](#investment) expires at the end of the Autumn Equinox; work on the extension must have begun by the start of the Winter Solstice.

The [Gjullit opportunity](#the-gjullit-opportunity) to build a dock at Siroc is available only until the end of the Summer Solstice 383YE. If work on the docks is not completed by the start of Winter 383YE the Gjullit will have to look elsewhere.

The [Van Basten Trade](#the-van-basten-trade), offering the purchase of 100 wains of white granite, is available only until the start of the Autumn Equinox summit.

The [proposal regarding the Alivetti estates](#the-vanderesar-proposal) expires at the end of the Summer Solstice. The [plan regarding Isember](#the-diepenbeek-plan) expires at the end of the Autumn Equinox.

## Resolution

Following the Summer Solstice 383YE, the Empire availed itself of the [Van Basten](The_mathematics_of_tears.md#the-van-basten-trade) offer, purchasing 100 wains of white granite for 500 Thrones.

During the Autumn Equinox 383YE, the Senate voted to [expand the Kruidenkenner Magazjin](../senate-motions/Expand_the_Kruidenkenner_Magazjin.md). Work was completed shortly before the Winter Solstice, and the opportunities for the four Imperial titles were adjusted appropriately.
