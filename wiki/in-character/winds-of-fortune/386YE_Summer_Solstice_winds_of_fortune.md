# 386YE Summer Solstice winds of fortune

*[Image: News spreads through word of mouth as fast as it does through broadsheets and posters, and is sometimes more reliable. Or at least, unreliable in more amusing ways...](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:News_in_the_Round.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, events have taken place which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. These are the winds of fortune, counterparts to the [Winds of War](../winds-of-war/386YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md) which detail the Empire's military campaigns. They lay out situations, and usually include things that characters can do to take advantage of [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or to resolve problems. In almost every case, they start with a piece of flavour text. This fiction helps introduce the wind of fortune, or draws out a particular theme or element. Often, it represents a viewpoint that one or more NPCs might have in-character. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip if they want to use it in character.

The body of the wind of fortune describes situations and lays out ways players can engage with them. Except where we explicitly talk about something being "rumoured" or use a phrase like "some might say..." the details of a wind of fortune are factual. They represent briefing material presented by the civil service or similar authoritative sources and represents something the average educated citizen would acknowledge as a reliable truth. Unless we specify otherwise, we're also being as thorough as possible. This information doesn't need further checking in-character. It's not opinion, and we're not trying to trick you.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters could know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months. It's perfectly acceptable to turn up to Empire having read only a few Winds of Fortune that directly interest your character; part of the fun on the field can come from learning about situations and opportunities in other parts of the game.

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

## Winds of Fortune

### Inextricable, indefatigable, and inevitable

- **All Imperial citizens**

The [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md), [Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md), has used her power to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) to present a personal message to all Imperial citizens. The message has been distributed across the Empire by the civil service, and you can read it in the **[Inextricable, indefatigable, and inevitable](Inextricable-_indefatigable-_and_inevitable.md)** wind of fortune.

### For the Common Good

- **Ambassador to the Commonwealth; Imperial Senate; military captains and warbands; Gatekeepers; citizens interested in relations with the Commonwealth**

Among the six [great powers](../foreign-nations/Foreign_nations.md#great-powers) of the known world, the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) are perhaps the closest allies the Empire has. Their militant society, committed to the idea of ensuring the greatest good to the greatest number of people, does not see eye-to-eye with the Empire on all matters but they clearly respect them. The Empire shared their mastery of magic with the magicians of the Commonwealth, and Commonwealth soldiers fought and died alongside the Freeborn in the [fall of Siroc](../winds-of-war/A_beacon_of_smoke.md) against the Grendel. Both nations know they must eventually come to blows – Empire and Commonwealth alike are dedicated to extending their influence over the whole world – but for now they can be friendly, at least.

You can learn about diplomatic relations and political complications relating to the Commonwealth, as well as several opportunities, in the **[For the Common Good](For_the_Common_Good.md)** wind of fortune.

### Thrones and Principalities

- **Ambassador to Jarm; Imperial Senate; Imperial Conclave; Champion of Wisdom; Guildmaster of the Artisans and the Artisans Guild**

Among the great powers, the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) are no friends to the Empire. The *magocracy*, ruled by the House of Princes, has repeatedly denounced Imperial citizens for interfere with its interests. The Empire is also allied with their great rivals, the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md), and open war has recently erupted between the two great nations. While they are not at war with the Empire, communication in recent years has tended to be brusque and unfriendly.

You can learn about the state of diplomatic relations with the Principalities, as well as some opportunities for trade and commerce, in the **[Thrones and principalities](Thrones_and_principalities.md)** wind of fortune.

### Towers and Crows

- **Ambassador to the Sarcophan Delves; Imperial Senate; Weirwood seats; Regario Dossier**

Among the six great powers, the *Bedelaar Huisbaas* merchant-princes of the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) seek to maintain a level of neutrality. They trade enthusiastically with the Empire, and welcome Imperial merchants in their turn. While they have outlawed slavery, and the trade in slaves, they refuse to pick a side between the nations that make up the [Liberty Pact](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Liberty.md#liberty-pact) and the so-called "Freedom Accord". More than anything else, they want to ensure that their trade vessels have free access to the wealth of the world, all the better to funnel as much of it as possible into their own pockets.

You can learn about the state of diplomatic relations with the Delves, as well as a couple of opportunities to trade with them, in the **[Towers and crows](Towers_and_crows.md)**.

### Guide the Spirit

- **Ambassador to the Sumaah Republic; Imperial Synod; Imperial Conclave**

Among the six great powers, the [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md) are simultaneously rivals to, and uneasy allies of, the Empire. Their shared practice of the Way both unites them through virtue, and divides them through disagreements about how the faith should be practiced and spread. Allies in the Liberty Pact, the priests of the two nations have recently agreed to an amicable split - each Synod going their own way but united by a shared commitment to spreading the Way as far and wide as possible. They just don't agree on what the best way to actually *do* that is, and while things are stable at the moment there's no guarantee they'll stay that way.

You can read about relations between the Republic and the Casinean Empire, as well as some opportunities to offer aid to the Sumaah, in the **[Guide the spirit](Guide_the_spirit.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Price of Peace

- **Ambassadors to Otkodov, the Grendel, the Iron Confederacy; Imperial Consul; Imperial Senate; Avisena i Kharizmi i Guerra**

The Empire shares a continent with a number of adjacent powers, some stronger than others. Four powerful orc nations surround the Empire, the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md), [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) and [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md). The nations of [Skoura](../foreign-nations/Skoura.md) and [Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md) are small, each only a few territories at most. The [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) is much bigger however and claims to be the Empire's equal in size and prominence. It may even be true. The Empire maintains careful diplomatic relations with most of these nations, including the Thule and the Grendel.

You can read about diplomatic matters relating to various [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) and human nations, as well as learn about opportunities to interact with them in person, in the **[The price of peace](The_price_of_peace.md)** wind of fortune.

### Winds and Flames

- **Ambassador to Faraden; Imperial Senate; people with weirwood, orichalcum, green iron, and iridescent gloaming; Military Council**

[Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md) is one of the smaller nations that neighbour the Empire, a country of merchants and wizards, proud warriors and heretical priests who *almost* follow the Way but not quite. There is no shared border - the lands of the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) lie between them - yet there is still a fair amount of trade and has been for centuries. Relations between the two nations vary from year to year, warm one moment, cool the next, but there has never been open war between the two nations. The Faraden would rather trade.

You can read about the current state of diplomacy between the Empire and Faraden, and learn about a couple of opportunities to trade, in the **[Winds and flames](Winds_and_flames.md)** wind of fortune.

### In the Hills, the Cities

- **Ambassador to Axos; Imperial Synod and the Throne; Imperial Senate; citizens with white granite to sell**

For centuries, the Empire and [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md) had little communication, but recently relations thawed, primarily as a result of the Empire's successful intervention in liberating the [Gates of Ipotavo](../foreign-nations/Axos.md#gates-of-ipotavo) from its siege by the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md). In the years since, the Empire and Axos enjoyed relatively positive but often volatile relations, with the Empire successfully strong arming the nation into abandoning slavery and then inviting them to become signatories to the Liberty Pact. In the last year or two, however, things have gone from bad to worse, every perceived slight by the Empire making the Axou angrier and angrier.

You can learn about the current unstable situation, and a few opportunities that shouldn't make it any *worse*, in the **[In the hills, the cities](In_the_hills-_the_cities.md)** wind of fortune.

### Drumbeat of War

- **Ambassador to Asavea; Imperial Synod, including the Way, Highborn, Freeborn, Ambition, and Loyalty assemblies; Imperial Senate; Prelate of Adelmar's Shrine**

Among the six great powers, the [Asavean Plenum](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) claims to be the oldest. They were a major power before the [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) came to what is now the Empire. *[Old Asavean](../world/Languages.md#latin)* was once the language of international diplomacy, reflecting the influence the Asaveans had across the known world. Today their power is much reduced, their society fallen into decadence and introspection, but they are still known for both their political acumen, and their ruthlessness in the face of any attempt to challenge their power. And right now they are at war with the Empire.

You can read not only about the diplomatic tensions with the Asaveans, but also about opportunities related to the missionaries and wayfarers bolstering the Way in the Archipelago, in the **[Drumbeat of war](Drumbeat_of_war.md)** wind of fortune.

### Peaks and Planes

- **Marcher and Urizen Assemblies, as well as priests of both nations; Marcher Landskeepers and Threshers**

The [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) Assemblies have continued to try to build rapprochement between the two nations following the conflict that developed over the emergence of [Cold Sun](../eternals/Cold_Sun.md). At the same time, though, a growing rift between the Marcher Assembly and the people of the Marches bodes ill for all concerned. While one crisis may be brought to a close, another is rearing its ugly head.

You can learn about the opportunity for an exchange of ideas between Urizen and the Marches, and about the challenges facing the Marcher Assembly, in the **[Peaks and planes](Peaks_and_planes.md)** wind of fortune.

Six [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) received [missives](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) from the [archmages](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) during the Spring Equinox; six eternals have responded to those messages in the months leading up to the Summer Solstice. Four have arranged meetings - both parley and not - for the coming summit, and while two decline to attend they have nonetheless provided boons to Imperial citizens. At the Spring Equinox, an [expedition was proposed](Say_her_name.md#far-from-these-shores), sending [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) and [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to the [Sea of Steel](../world/The_seven_seas.md#sea-of-steel) with the intention of bringing [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md) to the former slaves liberated during the fall of [Rachensgrab](../winds-of-war/Burning.md). ### Rock and a Hard Place

- **Marches; Marcher artisans; Marcher assembly**

Last season, the Empire [completed the dredging of Westmere](I_already_know_you.md#block-and-dredge). Mixed in with the mud and the silt dragged from the bottom was a huge block of rune-carved stone. Landskeepers who examined the rock confirmed it was hideously cursed, and boded certain doom for anyone who attempted to use it. The question was what could be done with it? It couldn't stay near Meade, that was the one thing everyone could agree on...

You can learn about what has happened with the *Anvil of Silence*, and what that means for [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), in the **[Rock and a hard place](Rock_and_a_hard_place.md)** wind of fortune.

### Kith and Kin

- **Marches and Highguard; Imperial Senate; Champion of Prosperity**

At the Winter Solstice, the [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) Assembly declared their [peerless kinship](Full_of_sacred_sound.md#a-peerless-kinship) with the Marches. The desire was there to help strengthen their kin and do what they could to support their fellow nation. However they were held back by a lack of direction from the Marches and a nagging fear that the current situation in Bregasland might mean that instead of helping they could just make things worse. The Marcher Assembly has allayed those fears, by using a [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) to identify what would help most to their fellow priests.

You can find out about what opportunities this has revealed, and how peerless kinship might continue, in the **[Kith and kin](Kith_and_kin.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Way Home

- **Navarr; Imperial Senate**

At the Winter Solstice, the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) Assembly supported a [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) that emphasised the importance of travelling the roads and [trods](../nations/navarr/Trod.md) of the Empire, of being the Empire's strength and blood and soul. Most importantly thought, it spoke of the [wayhouses](../nations/navarr/Wayhouse.md) - those vital buildings that the Navarr raise along the trods to support the [stridings](../nations/navarr/Striding.md), that give them a place to rest and resupply. There are innumerable small wayhouses across the Empire, but few of their operators attend the great summits at Anvil, such is the burden of keeping them running. While some larger establishments such as Treji Wayhouse have been built in recent years, they have generally been in the heartland of Navarri territories - not, in a sense, the truest expression of the wayhouse tradition. Now there might be a chance to change that.

You can learn about the intriguing opportunity to explore the purpose and power of wayhouses, and some specific examples of what might be done, in the **[The way home](The_way_home.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Final Countdown

The final answer to the threat of the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) has [been discovered](A_thousand_years_of_longing.md). ### The City Below

- **The League, especially citizens of Apulian; Urizeni; Grandmaster of the Shuttered Lantern, Imperial Spymaster, Assembly of Nine, Assembly of Vigilance, Oblivion Sentinel; Estella Lucati and Donato Zanella di Riposi di Sarvos**

The [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) rebellion in the mountains of [Ankra](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#ankra) and [Cinion](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#cinion) is largely resolved, or at least stabilised, by the decision of the [treaty with the Silver Flame](../senate-sessions/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#ratify-treaty-with-urizen-of-the-silver-flame). It's still hard to see what would ever reconcile some of the rebels with the Empire, but the situation should not deteriorate any further. With that achieved, eyes return to [Apulian](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#apulian), the City of Nets, or the City Below as some of the mountain dwells have nicknamed it. More people arrive every day, League citizens looking to make their fortune. They are not the only ones though. There have always been Urizeni living in Apulian, even when it the city was occupied by the Grendel, but now their numbers are slowly rising, encouraged by the mandate enacted by the Urizen Assembly to share the best of Urizen culture with the League. The fusion of League ambitions and Urizeni poise is proving fertile, and a welcome antidote for some, for the more brash self-promotion of the former Grendel. No-one can know what this union might yet yield, but the League will need to make the decision now whether this is the destination they choose.

You can learn about the situation in Spiral, the opportunities and challenges, in the **[The city below](The_city_below.md)** wind of fortune.

## Other Events

There are a number of other events and situations which are noteworthy, but which for whatever reason have not led to a full Wind of Fortune in their own right.

### Flavia's Will Enacted

- **Flavia's Will has been discharged**

Shortly before the Winter Solstice 384YE a prolific [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) [seer](../nations/urizen/Seer.md) named *Flavia of Crystal Hall* peacefully passed away. Her will [instructed](Gorgeous.md#the-will-of-flavia) that a certain notable item from her collection of curiosities should be granted to the first person who could complete several puzzles of her own devising.

At the Spring Equinox the executor of her will, a man named *Vincenzo di Sarvos*, made the journey to [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md) to present the prize to the contest's joint winners: **Eleri Bronwen's Rest** and **Melchior of Adina's Charge**. Vincenzo has since now announced that he now considers the matter to be concluded, his obligation discharged. He suggests anyone wishing to learn the puzzle's solutions - including those in the [File:Flavia's Will.pdf](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Flavia%27s_Will.pdf) - seek out one of the winners to discuss the answers.

### Loyalty to Great Things

- **The Marcher Synod passed mandates to confirm the important link between each one of their armies and the territory from where most of the soldiers are drawn**

Inspired by the actions of the [general](../../mixed/titles/General.md) of the [Strong Reeds](../../mixed/government/military/Strong_Reeds.md), the [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) synod chose to pass the three [mandates](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md), asking [Larkin Otterways](../synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-156), [Sister Meredith](../synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-165) and [Friar John of the Mourn](../synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-119) to spread word of the oaths across the Marches. Everyone is aware that a solemn oath has been sworn that the army will return to defend the territory if it is ever attacked. General Amberlain has already sworn a similar oath that the Strong Reeds would defend Bregasland. If that doesn't happen, if one of the Marcher territories is attacked, and the oath-sworn army doesn't immediately return to defend the land in the season that follows, then the oath will have been broken.

More details of the circumstances leading to these oaths can be found in last seasons **[I already know you](I_already_know_you.md#reeds-drakes-tusks-bounders)** wind of fortune.

## Trade Summary

Rather than spread this information out around the various foreign nations, we've collected it here for ease of reference.

- **Asavean Archipelago:** The port of [Nemoria](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#nemoria) is closed to Imperial trading vessels.
- **Axos:** The Axos continue to be annoyed about revelations relating to Imperial involvement in the failure of their [joint venture with the Grendel](Roll_and_fall.md) into the Mountains of the Moon. Consequently, Imperial fleets suffer penalties to trade with Axos. A fleet captain trading at the [Towers of Kantor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#towers-of-kantor) suffers a -2 rank penalty.
- **Commonwealth:** The ports of [Leerdam](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#leerdam-28eastern-commonwealth29) and [Volkavaar](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#volkavaar-28western-commonwealth29) welcome Imperial traders.
- **Principalities of Jarm:** The ports of [Kavor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#kavor-28northern-principalities-of-jarm29) and [Vezak](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#vezak-28southern-principalities-of-jarm29) remain closed to Imperial fleets.
- **Sarcophan Delves:** Imperial captains continue to be welcomed in the Delves. Fleets trading with the great port-city of [Sarcophan](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#sarcophan) receive an additional dose of [Marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md) and an additional dose of [Imperial Roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) on top of their normal production. The port of [Betovering](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#betovering) is open to Imperial fleets.
- **Sumaah Republic:** There is no penalty to trade with Zemeh and Imperial fleets are welcomed. Be aware that the amount of liao offered has been reduced as per the **[Guide the spirit](Guide_the_spirit.md#the-liao-trade)** wind of fortune.
- **Grendel:** The port of [Oran](Everything_has_a_price.md#a-new-destination) is closed to Imperial fleets.
- **Iron Confederacy:** The port of [Robec](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#robec) is available for Imperial fleets but suffers a -1 rank penalty due to sanctions from the Suranni.
- **Faraden:** The port of [Caitun](Butterfly_in_reverse.md#trade-with-the-faraden) is available for Imperial fleets. Fleets from the Marches and Wintermark benefit from a +1 rank bonus due to close proximity.
