## Overview

The heroes of Wintermark and their allies have pressed north, exploring the mysterious and uncharted wilderness of Tsirku. [Last season](Warriors-_Gate.md) they made several alarming discoveries, the worst of which was the presence of at least one of Agramant's followers in this frozen land. Long suspected, now confirmed, Wemba the Bloody is loose in Tsirku... but not for long.

However, just as everyone is finding their feet, they discover that these lands hold more surprises than just *the Howler* and his followers.

## The North

- **The explorers of Tsirku have pressed forwards, but have fallen short of being able to fully explore the new region**

North of Warriors' Gate lies a region described as "icy, covered in snow, with vicious looking chasms". The eternal storm itself does not extend into this region, but it is one that those walking that way would pass through. Imperial explorers pressed into the unknown, but unfortunately even with the support offered by the [Smokehouse of Atalaq](../senate-motions/Construct_Smokehouse_of_Atalaq.md), the worsening weather and all the [many](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#scouts), [many](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#mystics) problems of exploring Tsirku took their toll. While some headway was made, the explorers have not managed to get a thorough overview of the region.

A first in Imperial history, the region does not appear to have a name as such. Presumably it has a name in the language of the dire penguins and snow hares that call the place home, but it seems that no sapient beings have given the place a name that has stuck. Even use of [Gralka's Gift to the Lost Seeker](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Gralka-s_Gift_to_the_Lost_Seeker.md) proves less than helpful, either providing eerie sounds of blowing winds and croaking birds, or simply confirming that someone is in "the wastes of Tsirku north of Warriors' Gate". It seems that even Day magic has its limitations when it comes to a place like this.

There are two significant features here, or at least two that have been found by scouts.

The first is a massive hole, perhaps a mile across. Plates of thick ice extend out around the edges and make exploration particularly dangerous. There is a long drop down into the depths of this hole, and a spider-work of cracks and chasms extends for several miles in all directions around it. The area itself is risky because some of the narrower crevices have been covered over with a thick crust of snow - one that is by no means strong enough to support the weight of a human or orc. Explorers who sleep near the hole report unsettling dreams - a great fire plunging down out of the clear night sky and filling them with terror. This has lead to some speculation that the hole might actually be a crater - one caused by the kind of starfall that struck [Sküld](../barbarians/Otkodov.md#sküld) in recent years and brought with it a lot of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md).

The other feature is a stone settlement about a day and a half's hike east of the crater. Settlement is perhaps too strong a word. A squat three-storey hexagonal tower surrounded by a low white granite wall that also contains what seems to be a warehouse and some kind of ox stable. It takes a bit of effort to explore because the heavy doors and shuttered windows are frozen solid. Breaking through, explorers find the place eerily empty of all life, and everything coated with a thin layer of ice that glitters unpleasantly. Investigation quickly leads to the surmise that this is a [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) outpost - three are images of the five-headed dragon often used in decoration by the orcs of [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md). Unfortunately, the ice covering everything means that its impossible to say exactly how *old* this place is. It might have been build a century ago or it might have been finished last month. The question of where the inhabitants have gone is eventually answered and it is not pleasant. One of the big dogs that accompany some of the explorers dig at the snow in the open area before the tower, whining. Clearing the snow, the remains of a score of Otkodov orcs are found buried. [An Echo of Life Remains](../../mixed/magic/rituals/An_Echo_of_Life_Remains.md) discovers the name of one of the warlocks - *Deyadloth of the High Pass*. Some bodies show signs of wounds, others no indication what caused their death. Some of them are fully armoured in leather and fur, others are wearing little more than sleeping clothes. Their discovery leads to a decision to place some markers around the outpost and retreat, and perhaps leave further investigation to specialists who are not worried about how haunted that place might be. It also seems a good idea to avoid any kind of diplomatic incident - or at least to make sure it is someone of the appropriate authority who causes that incident.

Some explorers talk of meeting a figure that a little surreptitious [insight](../../out-of-character/rules/Insight.md) indicates is clearly a [creature of the realms](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md). It appears as an older orc lady, garbed in thick wool and fur and using skis to travel. Her face is hidden by a veil formed of knotted leather thongs, each weighted with white bone beads. Her presence is unsettling because she remains at a distance, often atop a snowdrift, and merely observes explorers. Those who hail her receive no response, and anyone approaching soon finds themselves being bogged down in deep drifts of snow, or falling prone thanks to hidden ice, during which she simply disappears again. Comparing tales though it seems that either she has several sisters, or she has made a point of taking a look at most of the explorers in the region. At least one eternal has an interest in this nameless snowscape, but it is too soon to say if they are hostile to those who trespass.

### Names Matter

- **The region north of Warriors' Gate has no name as far as Imperials are concerned**
- **If the Jotun or Thule have a name for it it has not "stuck"**
- **The scops of Wintermark could provide a name to the region and the big hole**

[Names](../../mixed/magic/lore/Wintermark_hearth_magic.md#names), as folk never tire of pointing out, are important. In Wintermark, the solemn duty of granting a name to a thing more often than not lies with the [scops](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_culture_and_customs.md#scops). It is risky to name something before you fully understand it, but it can be useful to give something a "use name" so that people know what you are talking about rather than relying on "the snowy region north of Warriors' Gate". During the Autumn Equinox, the [egregores](../../out-of-character/crew/Egregores.md) of Wintermark will meet with the scops attending Anvil to discuss the results of the recent exploration. If they wish, the scops can decide on a name for the region itself, and for the large notable feature - the hole, crater, or whatever that gouge-mark in the snow actually is. Alternatively, if they agree that they need more information, they can wait until the place is more thoroughly explored. The egregores will inform the civil service of the scops' decision.

As more of Tsirku is uncovered, the task of naming things that do not have names will fall to the scops. They may choose to consult other explorers, but the final decision (at least for the moment) falls to the namers of Wintermark.

## Winter in Tsirku

- **After the Autumn Equinox, Winter will set in *quickly* in Tsirku**
- **The strength needed to map another region is *doubled* until the start of the Spring Equinox 389YE**
- **The Imperial Spymaster directs the exploration of Tsirku**

After the Autumn Equinox, the weather in Tsirku deteriorates dramatically and with incredible speed. While the wastelands are hostile in Summer, it is nothing to [how bad they become](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#tsirku-is-barren) once Winter sets it and the seasons shift here without warning. Blizzards become commonplace, and the temperature drops cold enough to kill anyone unprepared for the cold. Winter is also when the [Northern Lights](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#major-features) are at their most active, and according to the [Suaq](../nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) it is a particularly perilous time to be abroad in the wastes.

The strength required to gather any information from Tsirku via the scouting network is *doubled* in the period straight after the Autumn Equinox, and remains that way until the start of the Spring Equinox. This will make the challenge of [mapping the territory](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#tsirku-is-big) even more arduous. Either [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to deal with the abysmal weather will need to be found, or a major force of military units will need to be applied.

While the explorers have started with the region north of Warriors' Gate, there's no need for them to continue to focus their efforts there. Some progress has been made, and some key features identified, but it will still require the full total of strength to be applied to actually complete the review of the region. That is, to complete exploring any region enough to be able to establish its borders requires the full 5,000 strength (or 10,000 over winter). As such it may be more appealing to move to one of the other two regions that border Warriors' Gate and at least get a rough overview of them... but of course such things take time.

As before, the decision on where to focus exploration after the Autumn Equinox lies with the [Imperial Spymaster](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Spymaster.md), although they may receive counsel from the people of Wintermark. At the very least, the Imperial Spymaster should let the Wintermark people know which direction to head in, and the best way to do that would be to address their muster during the Equinox. The Wintermark egregores should be able to arrange such an opportunity for them.

As indicated last season, there are three regions that can be explored in Tsirku.

- The region that has been partially explored is north of Warrior's Gate towards [Sydanjaa](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#sydanjaa). Finishing the exploration requires the full amount of strength - there is no benefit for having performed a partial exploration.
- Westward, toward [Tromsa](../barbarians/Tromsa.md), lies a region that preliminary exploration identifies as "*barren, windswept, with wide and unsettling skies*". It's likely that Lake Nutjuitoq extends into this area, and there may be a connection to the [Sea of Snow](../world/Sea_of_Snow.md) given that there must be *some* way for the great sea beasts to reach the [Grave of the Giants](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#the-grave-of-the-giants). The *hylje* have [offered aid](Beneath_a_moonless_sky.md#as-semersuaq-strode) in exploring this region, should Wintermark wish to avail themselves of it.
- Eastward, towards [Urdur](../barbarians/Otkodov.md#urdur) lies a region described as "*rocky, with scattered trees, that feels ominous*". It's possible that the region actually abuts Urdur and if it does that increases the likelihood of some kind of Thule presence there.

### The Varushkan Viewpoint

- **Some Varushkan explorers suggest Winter is the ideal time to focus on treasure, not exploration**
- **These two mandates are considered to be competing**

Winter is a bad time for exploration, but it might still present an opportunity. Some Varushkan explorers argue that rather than worrying about where things are and how things fit together, Winter is the best time to wrap up very warm indeed and go ~~wagon raiding~~ looking for valuable resources. There's one season left to take part in the *[Adventure in Tsirku](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#tsirku-is-enticing)* opportunity, so why not make the most of it? They're looking to their National Assembly to back them up.

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Tsirku is a wasteland full of dangers, but we Varushkans know well that where the danger is greatest the finest treasures can be found. We send (named priest) with 25 does of liao to encourage the explorers of Varushka to make the most of the coming Winter to gather the treasures of Tsirku.
> >
> > Synod Mandate, Varushkan National Assembly

If this mandate is enacted, then following the Autumn Equinox any Varushkan military unit will be able to undertake the *Adventure in Tsirku* venture, without suffering the 2 rank penalty to their effectiveness. In addition, by focusing on gathering the resources of the wasteland, each such military unit will receive an additional "treasures of the wasteland" (a consumable representing unique resources found in Tsirku, most likely harvested from beasts of Agramant or dangerous locations) on top of the two they would normally get. A military unit that has at least 11 ranks thanks to enchantments or upgrades will also receive an additional *wonder of the wasteland*, a much rarer consumable. There is, as so often with these things, a drawback. If the mandate is enacted every Varushkan military unit that takes the expedition will be competing for resources with the explorers of Tsirku; for every one that does so the effective strength contributed to *explore Tsirku* will be reduced by 50 points.

Alternatively, the Varushkans may choose to share their good sense with their friends in Wintermark and among the Imperial Orcs. They could pass this alternative mandate.

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Tsirku is a wasteland full of dangers, but there is enough there for everyone to seize the opportunity and embrace prosperity. We send (named priest) with 75 does of liao to encourage the explorers of the northern nations to make the most of the coming Winter to gather the treasures of Tsirku with the aid of the virtuous.
> >
> > Synod Mandate, Varushkan National Assembly

If this mandate is enacted, in addition to the benefits above it will also remove the 2 rank penalty from Wintermark and Imperial Orcs characters undertaking the *Adventure in Tsirku* venture after the Autumn Equinox, and any such military unit that has at least 11 ranks will receive an extra *treasure of the wasteland* alongside their wonders. The Varushkans will still get the full benefit of the earlier mandate, and everyone taking the Adventure in Tsirku venture will reduce the effectiveness of those exploring the wastes.

These two mandates are [in competition](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md#competing-mandates).

## Wemba the Bloody

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The servants of Agramant gather in Warriors Gate and must be dealt with. We send the Banner-bearer of Atkonaroq with 25 doses of liao to lead heroes and glory-seekers alike to face the dangers of Treachers Dagger and drive them back from whence they came.
> >
> > Hildelith Kaukosdottir, Wintermark Assembly, Summer Solstice 388YE, Vote: 290-0 Upheld with a **Greater Majority**

- **Imperial forces have captured Wemba the Bloody and are dragging the monster back to Anvil in chains to face justice**
- **He is expected to arrive at 21:00 on Friday at the earliest**

> **What No Skirmish?**
>
> Normally we would offer a skirmish to slay Wemba the Bloody, but on this occasion we've taken a different choice. There is no possibility of any kind under which Wemba would agree to come to Anvil - and we wanted to offer the nation a chance to get a look at this villain. We wanted the many, many players who don't play combatants and so rarely get a chance to look in the eyes of Wintermark's enemies to have an opportunity to come face to face with evil.
>
> We realise some players will be disappointed, but we're keen in Empire to have plots play out differently from time to time. Not everything should go the way you expect. There is still one more of Agramant's named creatures running round Tsirku, so those who are looking for a chance to clash swords with the enemy will still get the opportunity in the future!

Urged on by the Wintermark Assembly, many military units diverted from efforts to explore Tsirku to focus on hunting down and defeating Wemba the Bloody. Signs of his presence are everywhere in the far north, once the scouts pick up the trail they discover abandoned camps littering the region on the other side of the great crater. The bodies of various beasts are scattered on the ground, blood spilled, their flesh half-eaten, dire penguins, bears and rabbits. This is what Wemba the Bloody has been living on? The man who swore to eat nothing but the flesh of his enemies? Surviving on a diet of uncooked rabbit - it would be tragic if it wasn't so pathetic.

The confrontation with this enemy of the Mark is more of the same. It should have been an epic tale, one for the scops to speak of in hushed tones. A brutal fight, the heroism of the Mark against the bloody steel of Agramant's cur. But the Wintermark scouts outnumber Wemba and his followers a hundred to one and more. His terrible followers have been granted inhuman powers by Agramant, their flesh turns aside blows that would stop a bear in its tracks, but somehow they keep going...

...and it makes not the slightest bit of difference.

If this was Dawn, brave knights might have made this glorious, by calling Wemba out, fighting him one-on-one, the glory of Dawnish knighthood against the terror of Agramant's brood. But [heroism](../../mixed/government/synod/Heroism.md) has no truck with [glory](../nations/dawn/Glory.md). Heroism is about achieving what has to be done, not how difficult or dangerous it is. Deliberately making an act more difficult than it need be impresses no-one in Wintermark. More than five thousand elite Wintermark soldiers descend on Wemba's camp where he and his thirty followers are resting and they cut them to bloody ribbons.

None of them ask for quarter, they know this is a fight to the death and it is a fight they cannot win. Utterly outnumbered, utterly outmatched, they are surrounded and slaughtered. Wemba himself is one of the first to fall, but the only one to survive. His body lies on the frozen ground, disembowelled by a spear to the belly, bones in both legs shattered, chest pierced in a dozen places, and yet somehow he *still will not die*.

Not even Agramant's power can save a man from execution, Wemba will die when the time comes. But the party take it as a sign. His weapons are taken, his hands bound, his wounds closed, and then he is dragged in chains back to Sermersuaq, from there to be sent to Anvil. Let the mighty and the powerful interrogate him and learn from him what they can - the heroes of Wintermark have done their part. This monster's story is over.

Well, almost over. Wemba the Bloody will be dragged to the Wintermark camp on Friday evening. There will be a chance for those with Courage to question him and learn what they can. He is not going to tell the Empire *anything* about Tsirku - that much is abundantly clear - but those who want to find out what drives a man to swear his soul to Agramant, to consume the flesh of his enemies, can speak with him if they see merit in it. Once the nation is done with him, he can be handed over to the magistrates and receive his very just desserts.

## Silenced Bell

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Assembly of Vigilance directs the Silent Bell to investigate the location of Wemba the Bloody, Kaarina White-Eye, and their followers, last known to be in Sermersuaq. Locate them but do not confront them, that is work for other hands.
> >
> > Brother Geoffrey Orchard, Vigilance Assembly, Summer Solstice 388YE, Vote: Upheld 168-0

- **Following the Summer Solstice the remaining Silent Bell members were assigned to seek out Agramant followers in the north**
- **They have identified that Kaarina White-Eye is also in Tsirku**

During the Summer Solstice, **Brother Geoffrey Orchard** directed the Silent Bell to Tsirku to investigate the location of [Wemba the Bloody](Anyone_but_you.md#wemba-the-bloody), Kaarina White-Eye, and their followers. These two servants of [Agramant](../eternals/Agramant.md) have been a thorn in the Empire's side since [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md) was recovered from the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md). Some of the Silent Bell accompanied those scouts and raiders already heading for [Treachers Dagger](Warriors-_Gate.md#dangers-and-hunters). Others spread out to see if they could track the movements of [Kaarina White-Eye](Anyone_but_you.md#kaarina-white-eye), who was believed to have fled west toward the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) border.

The trail is cold, but it does seem that Kaarina used her superior knowledge of the ice fields of the north to strike out of [Suaq Wastes](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#suaq-wastes) and [Suaq Fount](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#suaw-fount) to harry the hated Jotun. She was also evidencing a keen interest in the Grave of the Giants - the bay where the majestic creatures of the Sea of Snow come to die.

About a year ago however, it looks as if she abandoned her camps in the wild places of Wintermark, and followed the same path Wemba the Bloody took into Tsirku. The Silent Bell follows, relying on the assistance of guides experienced in travelling into the unknown. Everything points to Kaarina currently being based in Tsirku, east of Warrior' Gate - although it's not clear if she is in the region *directly* east of there. She is clearly continuing her efforts to extract her blood-price from the orcs who slew her Thane.

### Finale

- **Every member of the Silent Bell that entered Tsirku has vanished**
- **Remaining members within Imperial borders, and those associated with them, have been attacked by servants of the Whisper Gallery**

Brother Geoffrey Orchard was very clear that the Silent Bell should gather evidence and information and not engage the followers of Agramant. Unfortunately, it turns out that it is not *Blood on the Snow* who is their greatest threat. Between the end of Summer and the start of Autumn, every member of the Silent Bell that leaves the Empire for Tsirku... vanishes. There are wild stories - a half-dead Suaq explorer suffering severe frostbite rants about how the three members of the Silent Bell they were escorting were killed by "their own reflections" that dragged them into the smooth wall of glacial ice north of the Grave of the Giants. Other stories talk of empty tents, with clear signs of struggle that nonetheless failed to attract the attention of a single sentry.

At the same time, there is a rash of attacks across the Empire as well. Here, the attackers are more identifiable. Servants of the [Whisper Gallery](../eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md) have targeted members of the assembly of Vigilance associated with the Bell, retired members, and those very few who [survived](Know_your_enemy.md#feardread) the expedition into [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md) to explore the [malign spiritual presence](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md) of [fear](../../mixed/magic/lore/On_the_nature_of_fear.md). Fortunately for the majority, there is a dedicated cadre of Imperial captains who maintain a [careful watch](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#war-of-whispers) for the malice of these hateful eternals. The majority of the overt attacks are stopped before they start, or interrupted before their target can be slain or kidnapped (for later murder).

The overwhelming evidence is that whatever has happened to the Silent Bell members sent outside of the Empire - and the protection of the [Conclaves](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) [enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) - have been much less fortunate than the few who remained behind. [Tsirku is secret](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#tsirku-is-secret), and it seems the Whisper Gallery want to keep it that way. Or at least, prevent the Silent Bell investigating the place.

### Bell and Bella

- **The Whisper Gallery takes responsibility for the attack on the Silent Bell**
- **They claim it is in vengeance for the deaths of Bella and Heliodoro**

While the Vigilance assembly is still reeling from the attacks, and the disappearances of so many Vigilant souls, a package is delivered by a [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) messenger to the grave orchard in Pickham, near the famous monastery, where Major Joshua Benson rests. A [site of pilgrimage](Pruning_shears.md) for the last three years, it is not unusual for gifts to be delivered. This gift, however, is not a bequest or an offering of support. It is a quartet of [League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) masks, covered in bloodstains, and a bloody knife on which the blood is still, unaccountably, fresh. With it is a gorgeous strip of parchment, decorated with an intricate drawing of dramaturgical masks with all kinds of expressions, and the two words "*FOR BELLA AND HELIODORO*".

### Watch End

- **The Silent Bell sodality is no longer able to launch investigations on behalf of the Vigilance assembly**

The expedition into the Mallum to seek to expand the Empire's understanding of fear cost the Silent Bell a great deal. Many of its bravest, and most committed members paid the ultimate price for the insight they gained there. Important information was discovered, but it [left the Silent Bell weakened and vulnerable](387YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_fortune.md#those-who-are-not-free). It was inevitable that if many more of them were killed then that would have be the end of the sodality.

By rights, the Silent Bell should have been safe enough in Tsirku - this is a dangerous land outside the Empire and filled with enemies, but even so, few expected them to suffer in the way they have. Unfortunately they did not expect to become the victims of a concerted of murder aimed directly at them. In the Empire they would have been safe, protected by the enmity of the conclave and watched over by the Empire's heroes engaged in the War of Whispers. But out here - in the frozen north, beyond the safety of the Conclave's dominion...

The Whisper Gallery saw a chance for revenge for the deaths of it's favourites and seized it. The Empire has taken something from the Gallery that for all it's capering the *Concilium of the Perjured Throne* did not want to lose. Now it has struck back, not with play, but in murderous earnest. It is a brutal reminder that for all the Whisper Gallery is baroque and mysterious, it can also be terrible and deadly when given the opportunity.

The disappearances in Tsirku are the final nail in the coffin of the Vigilant sodality that has served the Empire for years. There are survivors of course - the [War of Whispers](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#war-of-whispers) exists for a reason - but there are nowhere near enough of them to launch any kind of in-depth investigation into the threats that beset the Empire. But the sodality will be unable to continue now and cannot be reconstituted at this time. After a year has passed, the Empire might consider appraising the problem, to see if there was a way to bring the sodality back, but otherwise they have become a turned page in the Empire's long and storied history.

### Participation : Silent Bell

- **Characters associated with the Silent Bell are free to create stories about their experiences**

Any character who has roleplaying an association with the Silent Bell is free to create a story about their encounter with agents of the Whisper Gallery. Obviously, no player character has been killed or abducted by the Whisper Gallery, but you may have had a close call. You may have been saved by characters undertaking the War of Whispers, personally fought off an assassin with your friends, or escaped their clutches with cleverness or dumb luck.

If you were in Tsirku this season, then the threat will be have been considerably worse. No mortal agents were used here, rather terrible creatures formed of billowing chromatic smoke that boiled up out of the ice and attacked.

## Would You Like Some Help?

- **Agramant offers to help the Empire track down Kaarina White-Eye**

At the same time that bloody masks and a knife are being delivered to Pickham Monastary, a very different message is being delivered in Tsirku. The raiders returning from Treacher's Dagger have been victorious. Tired and injured, they are keen to get back to the warmth of Sermersuaq and the embrace of Wintermark friends. Just before they cross the border, a creature turns up in the middle of their camp and asks for parley. It's obviously a monster - a human-shaped monster wearing a coat made of tattooed skin, with a mouthful of shark teeth and lambent red eyes that are nothing like those of a draughir. It brings a message, for the ears of the Conclave, from it's master.

It is *dreadful* what the eternals of Night have done to the Silent Bell. Cowardly, even. Waiting until they were sent outside the protection of the Conclave and then murdering them all? Rude, to be honest. *[Giver of Gifts](../eternals/Agramant.md)* takes affront at the *Secret Seekers* paddling around in *his* part of the world and so he offers a boon for the Assembly of Vigilance. He doesn't expect them to take it but the offer is *genuine* and *sincere*. All they need to do is raise a [statement of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) urging the vigilant to keep watch over the Grave of the Giants. If that statement gets a [greater majority](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority), then Agramant will instruct his heralds to give Kaarina White-Eye bad advice, luring her into making a trip back to the Grave during the Winter Solstice to gather more... resources. The vigilant will surely spot her, allowing the jolly old [Sentinel Gate](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md) to get some Imperial heroes in position to murder her. And, sadly, as she is inside Imperial borders, the ability of her patron to protect her will be limited.

Why is *Blood-on-the-Snow* doing this? The herald says that his master wants to test Kaarina, and just how powerful her hate is. Either she'll prevail over the Imperial heroes, or they'll kill her. Either way, Agramant wins. Plus for that matter he has gotten just a little tired of Kaarina's constant banging on about how awful the Jotun are. And if she's foolish enough to fall for bad advice, and weak enough to be killed by so-called Imperial champions? Well, what can be destroyed *should* be destroyed.
