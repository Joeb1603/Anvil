## Overview

Two nations have chosen to use their [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md) to focus their people on the coming final war against the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md). The [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) are developing the skills and resources to prepare themselves, while [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) are looking for ways to support the nascent [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md) by sharing centuries of accumulated wisdom with other nations.

The Dawnish Assembly have chosen to [throw open the doors of Greenwood Manor](Love_and_monsters.md#unicorns) to all comers who might seek to quest against the Vallorn, rather than focusing Dawnish efforts. If the [administrative motion](../senate-sessions/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-knight-of-the-greenwood) is passed by the Senate, then military units from across the Empire will be able to undertake the **Quest Against the Vallorn** [venture](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#quest-against-the-vallorn).

More remains to be done, however, and thanks to the skilled negotiation of Dawnish enchanters and their enthusiastic patrons, new opportunities have been negotiated with *The Lone Swordsman* while the weavers of Astolat have identified a way to help Dawnish magicians take the fight to the vallorn.

[Meraud](../eternals/Meraud.md), or more specifically his herald *Gwinthav who speaks with Majesty* has come forward with an offer to the Dawnish in order for them to better combat the threat of the Vallorn. *Gwinthav* has an interest in rituals that turn the tide of battle, and knows how to create consumables that boost those that unleash devastating effects on multiple enemies, such as [Thundering Roar of the Lion-bound Horn](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Thundering_Roar_of_the_Lion-bound_Horn.md). The herald is keen to support their friends in Dawn in getting more of these rituals cast and seizes this opportunity to reach out. They note that [Fetid Breath of Teeming Plague](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Fetid_Breath_of_Teeming_Plague.md) and [Grave's Treacherous Edge](../../mixed/magic/rituals/The_Grave-s_Treacherous_Edge.md) are rituals of this kind that are extremely potent when facing the *Green Tide*, and *Gwinthav* is willing to provide vials of the dense mist that rise from the waters of Meraud's lake that can empower magicians to make it easier for them to perform the rituals.

In order to enable the agreement, Whitesmoke Brook would need to be diverted to pass through the regio upon which the [Flame of the Phoenix](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md#flame-of-the-phoenix) is built. The Brook is well known for its natural effervescence, but it is not large. It's much smaller than a river, so diverting it isn't difficult, but it does require some engineering and a touch of magic to bind it all in place. Once done, that would allow *Gwinthav* and their apprentices to help the staff of the [Tender of the Phoenix Flame](../../mixed/titles/Tender_of_the_Phoenix_Flame.md) harvest the effervescent water into a strange potion that would boost a magician's ability to cast rituals that invoked the venomous properties of Spring over a large area.

> **Vial of Whitesmoke**  
> If inhaled, a vial of whitesmoke provides three additional ranks to the next ritual that performed within ten minutes that allows the target to call [MASS VENOM](../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#mass-calls) ([The Grave's Treacherous Edge](../../mixed/magic/rituals/The_Grave-s_Treacherous_Edge.md) or [Fetid Breath of Teeming Plague](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Fetid_Breath_of_Teeming_Plague.md)), subject to the normal rules for effective skill. The caster regains all expended hero points and mana if they perform an appropriate ritual in the time allowed. This is a tonic, so the effect of any other tonic the magician has drunk immediately ends. Anyone who consumes whitesmoke will experience a heady rush of energy and confidence that leaves most eager to test their ability to push the limits of magic to see how much they could accomplish.

The [Eastern Sky](../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md) is [favoured](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#favoured) by five eternals of Summer Realm, but not [Hayaak](../eternals/Hayaak.md), the *Peregrine Knight*. At one time the ruler of the Forest of Arden, the *Outlaw Prince* is now a lone warrior who has made a home of the Badlands. No longer one of the Monarchs of Summer, *Sharptalon* now prefers to focus his attention on those individuals who catch his eye, those with promise and the fire to better themselves in their belly.

The *Hawk Lord* remains defiant and Proud in his new role, and quick to act over any perceived injustice. He resents that he was unable to offer the Eastern Sky a chance to win his favour as a patron, as if that might imply that he is somehow *less* important an ally than those tyrants in their gilded castles. Thus he is eager to take his rightful place as one of the army's patrons, and demonstrate his strength. What's more, while he has changed, his desire to hunt monsters endures, and the great aberrations of the vallorn seem like a worthy challenge for those who would seek his favour.

Thus, he makes an offer to the General of the Eastern Sky. Should they swear an oath to commit themselves to standing against the acts of tyrants wherever they may appear, he will offer them his aid in fighting the Vallorn. This will allow the Eastern Sky to take a new attacking order called **Slay the Monsters**.

> **Slay the Monsters**
>
> - **Casualties inflicted by this army against the vallorn are increased by three tenths**
> - **Casualties suffered by this army is increased by one tenth**
>
> The strength and fury of Hayaak flows through the soldiers of the army, driving them to risk everything to defeat monstrous foes such as the Vallorn. The army's discipline breaks down somewhat, as individuals gloriously engage the largest and most dangerous beasts they can find. The soldiers of the Sky are driven to seek out and test themselves against the greatest fecund creatures that the vallorn's twisted chaos has spawned. Taking down these creatures quickly reduces the strength of any force of the vallorn present, but increases the risk to the soldiers taking on the most dangerous fights.
>
> If a general issues an order to Slay the Monsters while not engaging the Vallorn (the only monstrous threat the Empire faces at this time), then the army will instead make an [overwhelming assault](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#overwhelming-assault).

[Image: EasternSky Colour.png](../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md)

Hayaak demands that this oath be sworn publicly by one whom people will know can speak with the voice of the Sky - either the current general of the Eastern Sky, **Dame Aurum De Castellan**, or their successor. Their most recent [orders](../military-council-orders/388YE_Summer_Solstice_Military_Council_orders.md#eastern-sky) imply that they do not intend to stand again as general, but Hayaak will accept either them as the long-standing general, trusted by the nation to be the voice of the people's army, or their successor, to start a new chapter in the army's story. Should that oath ever be broken in the eyes of the *Hunter of Beasts*, however, not only will this support be withdrawn, but his wrath will fall on the Eastern Sky and whoever leads them.

Hayaak does still wish to be clear that he stands *against* the Tyrants of the Summer Lands, and so makes a further offer. The General of the Eastern Sky can draw on each of their patrons but once a year - that would include the Peregrine Knight, so they can only use an order to slay the monsters once a year. However, if the Eastern Sky *renounces* their patrons by name when they take their vow, the *Masterless Warrior* will grant them an additional annual use of this order for each patron renounced.

It was the Earl of Fools who put forward the judgement to the Dawnish Assembly to make the fight against the vallorn Dawn's highest calling. That focus [produced a slew of opportunities](Love_and_monsters.md) to devote resources to fight the vallorn, which remain available until the end of the Spring Equinox next year. There is more that might be done, potentially, but it's not fully clear that everyone is committed to this cause.

During the conquest of the Barrens, the Dawnish Assembly took a very different view of the struggle than that of some Dawnish generals. It's impossible to know how deep those differences ran, but to many outside Anvil it seemed like the Assembly and their generals were not always on the same page. Perhaps as a result, some nobles and yeofolk, particularly those less inclined to follow the guidance of their Assembly, haven't committed to this cause as strongly as they might.

What would help now would be for the Earl's Council to put their own voice behind that of the Assembly. If they stood shoulder-to-shoulder with the Assembly and officially agreed that the fight against the vallorn was the nation's highest calling, then that would be bound to generate at least one additional [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) for Dawn to prepare for the coming war.

Of course, such a decision implies that Dawn would be making the war with the vallorn a priority over other conflicts, such as the current war with the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), or even the battles in the west and the south against the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) and the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md). If you decide something is your *highest* calling, then by extension, everything else is less important. The Council could ask those who look to their earls for leadership to make a different issue their priority and find some [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to pursue that cause instead.

To help the council with their determinations, the civil service has provided a list of earls and enchanters who have indicated they are attending Anvil this season.

## Vows

It is Highguard's turn to receive visiting Navarr, eager to share everything they know that can help undo *Terunael's Folly* and provide opportunities for the Vallornguard to prosper across the Empire. Many Navarr are well known to the grey pilgrims from their time walking the trods together in recent years, and they receive a warm welcome in chapter houses across the nation.

The grey pilgrims are eager to discuss the Vallornguard and the fight against the enemy, even as they encourage the Navarr to take advantage of the nation's hospitality. Weary Navarr who have been travelling the Empire are encouraged to [unburden](../nations/navarr/Navarr_culture_and_customs.md#unburdening) themselves so that they can put aside old responsibilities and prepare to take on new ones as they lead the Empire to war against the vallorn.

The Highborn have looked to what new support they might offer the Vallornguard, and they note that there is no central headquarters as yet for the sodality. The [Speaker](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md#speaker-of-the-vallornguard) has no offices to occupy, no place to receive delegations, no home to house supporting staff - somewhere those across the Empire can come to offer other aid. The Highborn would be honoured to host the Vallornguard in Highguard, and believe the [Unbound Steel Hall](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md#the-unbound-steel-hall-ruined) in [Tabernacle](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md#riverwatch) would be ideal. It has remained a ruin since the *scions* of the [Cold Sun](../eternals/Cold_Sun.md) razed it some years ago. The foundations remain, however, and it could be rebuilt as a formidable chapter house for **Corwyn Heartsbane** and his successors to situate themselves. The estates in the grounds of the chapter house would provide a valuable income to help the fund of the office of Speaker, helping them to lead the sodality.

The proposal is fine, but they don't go far enough to satisfy everyone. With the backing of the Senate, a much more ambitious structure in Highguard would facilitate a deep well of support for the Vallornguard, which could be transformational for the sodality. *Exarch Solomon of the Epistemological Architects*, who has recently been helping out with the plans for the Sevenfold Path in the Necropolis, points to the example set by the [grand lodge](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md#grand-lodge) of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md) or the [proposal for the Sevenfold Path](In_harmony_and_unison.md) as examples of what can be achieved when the Empire invests the necessary time and resources to create something extraordinary. The Vallornguard is a sodality; in theory, it is as important in its own right as the Conclave - it is every bit as significant as the Golden Pyramid. Why shouldn't support for it match theirs?

Of course that begs the question of what this "chancery" might do and how big it would need to be? The grander the scale, the more impact it will have; such is the way of things. To answer either of those questions, the Empire would need to appraise the possibilities of constructing a chancery for the Vallornguard in Highguard. The appraisal would have to explicitly instruct the prognosticators to look at the others in either Reikos *or* Casinea to see what possibilities exist.

If the appraisal was a success and the Senate adopted the commission, then similar appraisal options would be available for the other sodalities - looking to find a suitable chancery to provide a firm base of support for each extension of the Imperial government and to help them uphold their responsibilities and further their goals.

The Highborn have been committed to the fight against the vallorn for much of the last decade. First, those who felt the calling to be a [grey pilgrim](../nations/highguard/Grey_pilgrim.md) joined the stridings of Navarr to walk the trods. Then, when the call went out for the pilgrimage to end, they came home and formed the core of the [Pilgrim's Defiance](../../mixed/government/military/Pilgrim-s_Defiance.md). An army whose core purpose is to stand against any horrors the vallorn they may face and now be cowed. Surely, say many grey pilgrims, the general of this army should be elected by the Vallornguard?

Virtue is shown by the unity of word and deed, and so if the Senate show their commitment to the cause by changing the election method of the Defiance to be done by vote of the Vallornguard, the army will be able to spend its time while resupplying preaching and encouraging the citizens of the territory they find themselves in to believe that the Empire is all-in and that the centuries old foe can be defeated in this lifetime. As a consequence, the civil service would supply the general with a commensurate amount of liao as they would for any congregation.

Changing the title to be appointed by the Vallornguard wouldn't change the nation of the title - it would still be a Highborn national position. That means it would be elected by a simple vote of members of the sodality, but the title would be open to any Highborn citizen (even if they weren't a member of the sodality).

### Cursed Tool

The Stewards of the [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md) have spent the season with the aid of Navarri historians looking through their collections and the various tombs of figures from shortly after the founding of the Empire. After much effort, they have recovered and analysed a relic that appears to have been the property of Rhonwen, the archmage who famously sacrificed herself as part of the [reconquest of Seren](../nations/navarr/Reclamation_of_Seren.md). Seren was recently reborn, but this piece of history may give the Navarri one last look at where that story began, and the Vallornguard some insight into the killing of the first heart.

After careful recovery (and a little restoration), this wand has been found to be capable as acting as a focus for [Whispers through the Black Gate](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Whispers_through_the_Black_Gate.md) to speak with the shade of Rhonwen herself; however, there are some "small" issues that need to be resolved.

- It's cursed. Really, really cursed. This makes it a truly *awful* item to handle, but also means that using it for a person who died over three centuries ago is an incredibly volatile and frankly, outright dangerous undertaking. The Stewards are *pretty* sure it will call forth the shade of Rhonwen, but cannot vouch for the state of her shade or if anything else might try to come through with her.
- Who should they give it to? The [Imperial Necromancer](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Necromancer.md)? The Senator for [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md)? The Speaker of the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md)? The [Archmage of Winter](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Winter.md)? The Stewards ask that the Highborn Assembly guide them as to who, if anyone, care of this item should be given to, in order to arrange the casting. The judgement with the highest margin will tell them who to deliver it to for the Winter Solstice.

The Stewards advise that there was a rather excellent [piece](../nations/navarr/On_Rhonwen-s_Fall.md) of [historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) done recently that would be a worthwhile read for any who take an interest in the matter.

**Brynmor Rootround** took it upon themselves to action the [request](Survival.md#hercynia) made by the orcs of the Great Forest, offering to help defend Imperial infrastructure from the vallorn with regard to their great foe; the [Ancestor Slayer](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#surut). It did not achieve a greater majority and thus did not circulate far beyond the priests of Anvil, but the orcs have been in discussion with the vates of Navarr to learn how the Imperial powers of state might be used to achieve their aims. The aegis to determine the fit and proper use of magic falls to the Conclave after all, not the Synod.

This season, the orcs have made an appeal to any who have influence in the Conclave to [interdict](../../mixed/government/conclave/Interdiction.md) the act of magical deforestation. If done, they will follow through with their offer of aid to help fortify the heartwood. Instead, if the Conclave are willing to [turn away](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) from the *Silencing Flame* completely, they will offer a bequeathment of three wains of [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md) for the order that raises it, or to the office of the [Archmage of Winter](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Winter.md) if they use their declaration to do so for as long as the declaration stands.
