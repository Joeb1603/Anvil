# 381YE Winter Solstice winds of fortune

*[Image: Milestones.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Milestones.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md).

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters 'should' know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months.

Many winds of fortune open with a short section of in-character text. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip. They're mostly just a bit of fun and they tend not to contain anything vital to the understanding of the wind of Fortune.

## Winds of Fortune

### All Work Together

- **Imperial Synod**

Following a period of turmoil and religious conflict, it may be time for cooler heads to prevail. A [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) by Nina of Cantiarch's Hold leads to several [mandates](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md) that seek to find ways to encourage the Empire to embrace the Way... although there are various different ideas of exactly what that means. You can learn about these mandates [here](All_work_together.md).

### Better World

- **Fleet captains, Imperial Synod, owners of mana sites**

Two major announcements have come out of the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) over the last season. In [Rigia](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#rigia-28eastern-principalities-of-jarm29), the provincial capital of the Eastern Alliance, Magician-Prince Barbara Radz has announced radical reforms to the treatment of slaves in her principality - and made membership of the alliance contingent on adopting these reforms among the other Princes. At the same time, a powerful cabal of princes from Northern Jarm have announced their intention to make a unique substance - *beloye zerno* - available to Imperial magicians which has significant implications for anyone who owns a [mana site](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md). You can learn about these international developments [here](Better_world.md).

### Budded Roses

- **Dawn**

Last season, having completed their conquest of the Barrens, the Druj struck deep into Semmerholm. They conquered Axmure and destroyed the Gate. With the fortification gone, the Druj were certain to press the advantage, and the nation collectively held its breath as the fate of Semmerholm hung in the balance. Dawn has never lost a territory to the barbarians - was this the moment that proud boast would fail? No. It was not. And you can learn about the fervour that has swept across Dawn in the wake of that boast being upheld [here](Budded_roses.md).

### Chain Around My Leg

- **Imperial Orcs. Arguably Varushkans, Wintermark, and Temeschwari should not read this wind of fortune so it comes as a nice surprise.**

The Winter Sun have spent the last season in Casinea, resupplying and seeking a stronger connection with their ancestors via an experimental ritual and the use of a unique relic called *Defiance*. You can learn about the consequences of his action [here](Chain_around_my_leg.md).

### Dusty Old Dust (Trade Winds)

- **Fleet captains, ambassadors, traders**

The Empire does not exist in isolation; with four fractious neighbours and five great powers overseas, international trade and diplomacy is an ever shifting sea of opportunity and obstacle. You can read about current Imperial relations with foreign powers [here](Dusty_old_dust.md).

### The Flood and the Storm

- **Imperial magicians; Wintermark, Urizen, Navarr, Varushka.**

The [difficulties](Pay_in_blood.md) Imperial magicians have been experiencing accessing the power of [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) aligned with Spring have ended as abruptly as they began. Not only can magicians draw on regio to empower [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) again, but the magic seems to be in flood - leading not only to some [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) for new [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) but also to the chance to make risky decisions when performing powerful rituals. You can learn about the situation [here](The_flood_and_the_storm.md).

### Foggy Mountain Top (Winds of Magic)

- **Magicians and Imperial Conclave primarily, but there is something for everyone here**

The stars are back! This news, and a round up of magical effects and eternal-related activities, can be found [here](Foggy_mountain_top.md).

### Fly High

- **The League**

Over the last season, three grand construction projects in [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) have been completed: the [Blood Red Roads](../nations/the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#blood-red-roads), in all their glory; the long-delayed [Grand Park](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#holmauer-park) of [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md); and a series of red stone monoliths that now focus and enhance the flows of mana through each of the four League territories.- [magical monuments](../nations/the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#the-legacies-of-virtue) to the virtue of Lorenzo Macelliao von Temeschwar. These great works have quite inspired the people of the League, and opportunities abound! You can learn more about them - and about a potent new street drug - [here](Fly_high.md).

### Froggy Went A'Courting

- **People interested in orc diplomacy**

The [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) in [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md), the [Great Forest Orcs](../barbarians/Great_Forest_Orcs.md) in [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md), and the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) on the Bay of Catazar. Three nations of [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md), each with their own relationship to the Empire. You can learn about recent developments with these orc nations [here](Froggy_went_a-courting.md).

### Hard Times

- **The Brass Coast, the Imperial Senate, potentially many others**

Some two hundred people have appeared in southern [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md), revolutionaries fleeing from the lands of Duke Guiscard of the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md). Escaped slaves, they have built makeshift camps and are engaging in periodic raids across the border into Kalino. You can learn more about this complex diplomatic and political situation [here](Hard_times.md).

### House of Earth

- **Marchers, especially Mournwolders; Imperial Orcs;; Senate, Synod, Military Council**

Not all the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) have left the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md). There remain significant numbers of [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) former-thralls living throughout the Mourn. Large numbers of them fled west marching with the Jotun armies as they retreated before the Empire's advance, but many remained behind. And now the question is what to be done with them. The problems presented by this population of orcs - and some potential solutions - are outlined [here](House_of_earth.md).

### Poor Boy

- **the Brass Coast, magicians, and astronomancers.**

Anvil is a bustling centre of commerce, as well as politics. Merchants, traders, entrepreneurs, and chancers alike are drawn to the Winter Summit looking for opportunities to buy and sell. A youth with a unique pearl; a seeker after celestial knowledge; a naval treasure; and a foreign jeweller are all described [here](Poor_boy.md).

### Right Now (Conjunctions)

- **Lorenzo de la Lupe; Imperial champions, especially from Wintermark, Dawn, and the Marches**

Late breaking news - a collision in Sarvos, drowned dead in Sermersuaq, and raiding Feni in Hahnmark and Upwold among others. You can learn about these tumultuous events, and the [conjunctions](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#conjunctions) of the [Sentinel Gate](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md) associated with them [here](Right_now.md).

### Root Hog, or Die

- **Navarr, Bregas, Military Council**

The vallorn of [Liathaven](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md) is on the move, flexing and shifting. If nothing is done, it will consume all of [West Ranging](../nations/navarr/Liathaven.md#west-ranging) before its aberrant vitality subsides. At the same time, eerie mists bring the seeds of peculiar short-lived plants to the farmers of [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md), rich with fecund power. You can read more about this situation [here](Root_hog-_or_die.md).

### Sing Your Name

- **Highguard, Imperial Synod, potentially the Military Council**

The [Heirs of Lepidus](../religion/Lepidus.md#heirs-of-lepidus) have completed another significant piece of research that conclusively proves the [Silent Sentinel](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#the-silent-sentinel), the oldest and ugliest [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) in the Empire, is also the likely habitation of the enigmatic paragon known as [the Sentinel](../religion/The_Sentinel.md). If the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) is interested there are opportunities to celebrate this new revelation in a way that aids the entire Empire - and the [assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) of [Vigilance](../religion/Vigilance.md) as well perhaps. You can find more about this opportunity [here](Sing_your_name.md).

### Sowing on the Mountains

- **Varushka primarily, but also some elements of interest to the Navarr, the Courage assembly, and potentially the Military Council**

A once-in-a-century storm has raged across the northern mountains of Varushka, subtly shifting the terrain as torrential flood waters lead to mudslides and rockslides and all sorts of realignment of earth and water. As the storms abate, a quartet of intriguing opportunities to build from destruction present themselves. You can learn about these opportunities [here](Sowing_on_the_mountains.md).

### Talking Sailor

- **Fleet captains, Imperial Senate**

With the [destruction of the Freeborn Storm](../winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#to-fool-the-crowd), the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) have effectively gained military domination of the Bay of Catazar, contested only by a handful of brave Imperial captains. With their efforts to [attack the fishing fleet](Desperate_affairs-_desperate_measures.md#first-gain-the-victory) stymied so effectively the pirates of the Broken Shore are looking for new prey. Urged on by the blood-thirsty [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Siakha](../eternals/Siakha.md), their next target is the trading fleet. At the same time, there has been an upswell of interest in finding out exactly where the Grendel *are* along the Bay, with an eye towards taking the battle to them in some way. You can find out about both situations [here](Talking_sailor.md).

### This Land is Your Land

- **Marchers, especially in Mournwold; sections very relevant to Imperial Orcs, Senate, Military Council, and Synod**

The Jotun have fled the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md), driven out by a combination of Imperial armies and Imperial magic. But lingering resentment remains in the hearts of many who dwell, a bitter legacy of the malevolent magic used to break the Jotun. Determined to seek amends, the people of the Mourn selected three representatives to take their concerns to Anvil in person. They were sent with a list of demands so see if they could get some recompense for the wrongs done. Nobody expected to get everything they had demanded - but "*Things turn up for one who digs*", as they say ... and you can learn about what has turned up [here](This_land_is_your_land.md).

### What Did The Deep Say? (Plenipotentiary)

- **Imperial Conclave**

Four [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md) have requested formal parleys with an archmage, and one has agreed to a meeting but specifically *refused* to do so under its aegis. You can learn which eternals have responded, and the matters they wish to discuss [here](What_did_the_deep_say.md).

### You Know the Night

- **Imperial Orcs, Varushka, General Assembly**

The debate between those who believe cruelty and fear are weapons the Empire should use, and those who mandate a more heroic path, continues. The [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) have chosen the heroic path; the [Varushkans](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) have the opportunity to remind the Empire once again that they are who they are. You can learn about the current stage of the disagreement [here](You_know_the_night.md).

## Roundup

This section contains information that is potentially relevant, but doesn't merit its own Wind of Fortune.

### Zemress Islanders

Mazo i Zabala i Erigo of the Freeborn Assembly raised a [statement of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) that the exiles from the Isle of Zemress should settle in [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md). Since the Autumn Equinox they have been working to establish a home for their extended family in that territory. It is anticipated that they will have settled in by the Spring Equinox.

### Lorenzo the Builder

> *In-character text:*
>

It demonstrates the Benevolence of Lorenzo “La Volpe” Macelliao von Temeschwar, and will stand as a lasting Legacy. We send Jonah Yakovitch with 25 liao to encourage the faithful to aid in this endeavour.
> >
> > Jonah Yakovitch, League Assembly, Autumn 381YE, Upheld 508-165

Thanks to the inspirational preaching of Jonah Yakovitch, supported by the League Assembly, the Butcher's Benevolence - the [Blood Red Roads](../nations/the-league/Lorenzo-s_Legacy.md#blood-red-roads) are complete. The four cities of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) have been connected by a series of broad highways, and the roads extended to [Stockland](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md#stockland), [Kalpaheim](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#kalpaheim), [Delev](../nations/varushka/Karov.md#delev), [Siroc](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc), and [Holt](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md#holtford) in [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md). The roads will bring prosperity to each [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) they pass through, and there is already [a plan afoot](../senate-motions/Construct_south_west_road_network.md) to extend the south-western roads to include the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) trade town of [Anduz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md#anduz).

Not content with the Blood Red Roads, work has also been completed on an extensive series of red stone monoliths in [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md), [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md), and [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) designed to focus and enhance the flows of mana through the League territories. There has been some controversy - the [Asavean](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) architect [Almodin Oktístis](Those_who_worship.md#almodin-oktc3adstis2c-priest-of-the-builder), priest of the Builder, has put his own unique stamp on the monoliths in Temeschwar and Tassato. Lorenzo watchers are waiting with interest to see what project the visionary designer will turn his hand to next.

### Hordalant Spy Network

Over the last three months, the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) orcs have dismantled the [Hordalant](../barbarians/Hordalant.md) [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md). It will no longer be possible to assign a [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to it.
