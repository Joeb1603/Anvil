## Overview

The [Unshackled](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) territory of [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md) continues to prosper despite the best efforts of the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md). Their [attempt to recapture](../winds-of-war/Lines_of_care_and_sorrow.md) the arid lands have faltered and been [driven back](../winds-of-war/Scarlet_sands.md). Imperial armies clash with the warriors of the Broken Shore, and it is clear that the Empire has the upper hand. This victory, along with some egregious mishandling of the former Grendel [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) in the territory, has gone a long way towards reassuring some of the more [uncooperative](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#uncooperative) septs that the Unshackled can deliver on their promises to build Mareave up into a jewel of the Empire.

Mareave is thriving. The grand aqueduct is complete, and Ruicraic's Redoubt proudly protects both it and the ruins of [Eoradahl](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#eoradahl). The enigmatic mountain-folk of [Skoura](../foreign-nations/Skoura.md) seem keen to [secure closer ties](Poets_light_but_lamps.md#skoura) with their western neighbours. The independent city-state of [Beoraidh](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#beoraidh) still prizes its neutrality, but is clearly [facing up to the realities](Each_age_a_lens.md#beoraidh) of living in an Imperial territory under "new management". The magicians of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md) have completed their [Grand Lodge](../senate-sessions/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#construct-grand-lodge-of-the-golden-pyramid), and with it their year of investigations and investments looking for more opportunities to improve the [prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md) of both the Unshackled and their own order.

## Golden Curtain Call

*[Image: The Grand Lodge of the Golden Pyramid stands as an example of Imperial wealth](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Grand_Lodge_of_Golden_Pyramid.jpg)*

- **The Golden Pyramid have completed their Grand Lodge**
- **The Lodge brings prestige to the region of Clisearn**
- **They have also completed their year of searching for opportunities in Mareave**

The Grand Lodge of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md) has been completed. Despite some initial nervousness caused by the approach of Grendel armies, it stands strong in [Clisearn](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#clisearn), bringing [many advantages](Lit_up.md#golden-opportunity) to both the Imperial Orcs and the Golden Pyramid order itself. There is no doubt that this is a prestigious building - the heart of an entire [Conclave order](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave_order.md) has been built in Clisearn - and this has the knock-on effect of granting prestige to the families of former Grendel that live nearby. There has been no open movement yet, but magicians of the Lodge recount informal approaches from all three of the major Clisearn families wishing to discuss opportunities for trade and advancement. Clisearn has consequently lost the [uncooperative](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#uncooperative) quality. The orcs here are not *friends* of the Empire or the Unshackled, but neither are they openly opposing them.

The completion of the Grand Lodge also marks the end of the year its members have spent talking to inhabitants from across Mareave in search of ways that both the prosperity of the territory and the Order could be improved. They have finished their investigations of the rich hills of Mareave and are, for the most part, looking forward to a rest *not* under threat from the Grendel. Assuming that the armies still in [Fleisardh](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#fleisardh) can be dealt with of course.

- **The mine of a local orc could be expanded, and the excess ingots purchased**

The magicians of the Golden Pyramid have been exploring potential business deals with the orcs of the Broken Shore who have remained, and struck upon a potential agreement with a respected orc named *Savurn Marr*. *Savurn* and his family have lived in the hills of northern Mareave for generations, and few can match their understanding of the terrain and its hidden wealth. The *Marr* are a little suspicious of the Imperial Orcs, but they have so far simply accepted that dominion over Mareave has shifted from Grendel to Unshackled, and broadly got on with their lives. Now, though, there is a lucrative opportunity for mutual enrichment and *Savrun* is singing a very different tune.

With the help of Golden Pyramid diviners, a rich untapped vein of metal has been uncovered in Savurn's own mine. If it can be opened up, there is significant potential for profit - there are even small deposits of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) that could be mined here with enough investment. The mine belongs to Savurn, but if the Empire is willing to fund the expansion, they will provide an agreement for the Imperial Orcs to buy the additional output from him at heavily discounted rates. Furthermore, if there is sufficient investment, he will make a bequeathment of one vial of [prismatic ink](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#prismatic-ink) each season to the Golden Pyramid, in thanks to the order for helping him find the lucrative new vein in the first place. Where he is getting the ink from is presumably a question for another time.

- **Members of the order have drawn up a design to take advantage of the natural flows of magic in the territory**

Over the last year, some of the more theoretically minded magicians of the Order have noticed something... decidedly odd about the eddies and currents of magical power that flow across the territory. Whether something ancient or something new, these flows of natural magic appear to correspond - more or less - with the constellation of [the Stallion](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Stallion.md). With enough investment of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) and a steady hand to keep a run of the things, the Golden Pyramid believe these energies could be focused into a confluence in [Clisearn](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#clisearn) that would make it produce mana crystals as normal. However, because of the peculiar shape of the flows, it will also produce an amount of [vis](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md), the kind depending on the season.

The members of the order are happy to support the work necessary, but are insistent that the resulting title be appointed by [Declaration](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Candidacy](../../mixed/government/conclave/Candidacy.md). The good work of the grand lodge in Clisearn has ingratiated the order with the locals; if the title holder is a member of the Golden Pyramid, then they will receive an income of two crowns each season, from the ongoing relations with the orcs in the area.

## Build a Tower for Tomorrow

|  |
| --- |
| **Tower of the Immovable** |
| **Commission Type:** Edifice |
| **Location:** Eoradal, Mareave |
| **Cost:** 40 wains of white granite, 10 rings of ilium, three months |
| **Effect:** • Provides an income of 216 rings to the Strategos of the Imperial Orcs <br> • Will establish a settlement in Mareave over the coming years |

- **Rucraic's Redoubt has been completed in Eoradal**
- **It is not possible to build *another* fortification in Eoradal**
- **Vranitas, herald of Cathan Canae, has suggested building a great tower to show they will not abandon the region**
- **It will be infused with adamant from the Summer realm**

The [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) of [Rucraic's Redoubt](We_all_stand.md#storm-lords-sentinel) has been completed, and a sturdy [garrison](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) recruited and armed. It now stands proudly at the Mareave end of the grand aqueduct, offering a place of sanctuary for the northern citizens of Mareave, and soldiers to protect the people all the way to the southern border. There is some discussion that the new fortification could serve as an excellent site for a [embassy](../../mixed/commissions/Embassy.md) with [Skoura](../foreign-nations/Skoura.md) if they could be persuaded to agree to such a thing.

The Redoubt is impressive, but it is not the same as creating a fortified town. Doing so was [the original plan](Here_to_stay.md#not-going-anywhere) proposed by [Eilian Sweetwater](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#eilian-sweetwater) and the [Tamazi](../nations/imperial-orcs/Tamazi.md) for reassuring the septs of Eoradal that the Imperial Orcs were here to stay. They are surely impressed, but there are still a number who think that the fortress is a good start, but nowhere near sufficient to convince them that the Unshackled are serious about maintaining their dominion. There may, however, still be a way to get the advantages of both opportunities.

>

One way to achieve that would be to upgrade the existing fortification to incorporate the original plans for a walled settlement - but that would clearly be expensive. A different opportunity has presented itself in the form of *Vranitas*, a [herald](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#heralds) of [The Immovable One](../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md). For the last few months, they have been knocking around Mareave, talking to both Imperial and local orcs, standing on hillsides looking moody, and kicking rocks and piles of sand. Their presence has gone unremarked until a trio of Golden Pyramid orcs literally bump into them in the ruins of Eoradal. According to the voluble Vranitas, they are here on the orders of their mistress to get a feel for Mareave and see how she might offer aid to the Imperial Orcs.

After discussion with the Golden Pyramid, Vranitas has suggested building a mighty tower at Ruicraic's Redoubt, something truly majestic that can be seen for miles. They believe this would demonstrate the intent of the Unshackled to remain in a way that was tangible and visible to everyone in Eoradal - and that as a unique landmark it would serve naturally to draw people to the area. Both local orcs and Unshackled would come to the Redoubt, not only for water but also for companionship and the sense of security the castle brings. Vranitas has even offered to remain in the tower, providing his mistress' guidance, sparring with anyone who wishes to visit, and from the point of view of the Golden Pyramid providing a sign that the Immovable One supports the Unshackled intent to remain here.

If the proposed tower is built, then, over time, a new settlement will be established around Rucraic's Redoubt. It won't be a walled town, as originally planned, but rather a sprawling conurbation that might eventually grow to the size of a city. Rucraic's Redoubt could shelter people if the area was attacked and the landmark tower would give people confidence and certainty to settle here. Completing the Tower of the Immovable would get the locals enthusiastic about the Unshackled.

Finally, the people drawn to the Tower and the Redoubt would provide funds to support the Unshackled nation, and especially the [Strategos of the Imperial Orcs](../../mixed/titles/Strategos.md). The Strategos is a figure whose duties plainly involve protecting Imperial Orcs lands - and the people who live on them. The tower would secure an income of 216 rings each season from donations, for as long as it remained standing and the region of Eoradal was controlled by the Imperial Orcs.
