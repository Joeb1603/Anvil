# 388YE Summer Solstice interlude

*[Image: In the stands.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:In_the_stands.png)*

## Overview

Downtime following the Spring Equinox is now open. It allows players to process their character's downtime choices. Not all resources allow a player to make a choice about how they operate between events, but we've provided information about new opportunities for [congregation](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md), [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md), and [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) owners in particular, as well as a reminder about existing opportunities created at earlier events.

On this page, we've outlined and offered some context for various effects and events. As always, these include quick summaries, mostly out of character, to explain effects such as large-scale [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) and [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md).

In some cases, there may be further developments around the events listed here in the Spring Equinox winds of fortune. They may also be handled with a *Resolution* entry on the appropriate Wind, or a note in the *Other Events* section of next events Winds of Fortune page.

**The deadline for Downtime submissions is midnight on Friday the 26th of June**

## Choices

- **During the Spring Equinox, there were a number of Senate motions, Conclave declarations, Synod judgements, Military Council orders, Sodality resolutions, and elections**

Each summit the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) raises and votes on [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md), the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) debates [declarations](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md), the assemblies of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) discuss [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md), the [generals](../../mixed/titles/General.md) of the [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md) issue [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) to their [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md), [Imperial sodalities](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) decide on resolutions, and citizens are elected to Imperial titles. You can find details of these many decisions here:

- [Senate sessions](../senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md)
- [Synod judgements](../synod-judgements/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md)

Not everything is widely distributed across the Empire, but any character who attends [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md) can easily find out details of what went on in the houses of power during the event.

## Imperial Guerdon

The [Imperial Guerdon](../../mixed/government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#guerdon) is a bursary set up by the Imperial Senate primarily to reward military units that support Imperial armies, spy networks, and fortifications. The value of the Imperial Guerdon is divided between each military unit and fleet that supports an army in proportion to their effective strength. The [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has the power to exclude any or all Imperial armies and navies, fortifications, or special projects (such as spy networks) from the Guerdon. If they do so, then a character who supports such an army, fortification, or special project will receive nothing for doing so.

The Guerdon was announced at **350 Thrones**.

This season, the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has [announced](../senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#assign-the-guerdon) that shares of the Guerdon will be shared between military units supporting the following armies and action:

- [Argent Sword](../../mixed/government/military/Argent_Sword.md) in [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md)
- [Drakes](../../mixed/government/military/Drakes.md) in [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md)
- [Red Wind Corsairs](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md) in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md)
- [Summer Storm](../../mixed/government/military/Summer_Storm.md) in [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md)

- [Spy on the Thule](#spy-on-the-thule) in the Forest of Ulnak

No other armies, or actions by military units or fleets, will receive shares of the Guerdon.

## Military Units

- **Players who control military units can choose from a number of downtime options**
- **Not every military unit is eligible to participate in every venture**
- **Left to their own devices, a military unit defaults to the *mercenary work* option**

Any player whose character controls a [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) can choose from several downtime options by logging into the downtime system. They may choose to [support an army](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning), [defend a fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#guarding), or [support a spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#scouting). They can also choose one of the many [ventures](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md). Left to their own devices, a military unit defaults to [mercenary work](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#mercenary-work), guaranteeing an income of money along with a random chance of herbs, more money, mana crystals, ingots or measures.

Players who want to support one of the [Imperial armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) can also read the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) issued by each [general](../../mixed/titles/General.md) before making their decision. These orders are a matter of public record within the Empire and also available on the **[388YE Spring Equinox Military Council orders](../military-council-orders/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Military_Council_orders.md)** page. This new page makes it easier for players to read all the orders before making a decision; previously the only way to do this was to painstakingly click every army in turn. It's also possible for players without military units to see the orders, without waiting for the Winds of War (assuming the orders related to an area that produced a full write-up).

As per the [the guidelines around discussing military matters](../../out-of-character/rules/Online.md#you-may-not-post-military-updates), you must not post statements encouraging people where to send their military units and fleets in downtime.

### Map the City Below

- **Military units from League, Dawn, and Urizen can take a scouting action to attempt to map the city below**
- **If a total of three thousand strength is put to the action then it will succeed**
- **Any military unit that takes this action will receive one piece of crystal fire and a completely mundane rainbow feather**
- **Military units assigned to Rescue the Merchant Prince will reduce the effective strength of this venture**

The eternal, Sung, is [seeking help to map the passageways and sewers beneath the city of Apulian](Our_elaborate_plans.md#the-end-of-laughter). If the total effective military strength of all the military units assigned to **Map the City Below** is 3000 or more, then the operation will be a success. However, the attempt to Rescue the Merchant Prince will undermine efforts - every military unit working to rescue Prince duSesna subtracts their military strength from those attempting to Map the City Below. [Sung](../eternals/Sung.md) is utterly incapable of articulating what that will mean or entail and traditional attempts at divination with day or night magic have proven essentially useless. The rewards are a single brightly coloured rainbow feather with no magical powers taken from Sung's wings and a single piece of [crystal fire](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#crystal-fire).

### Rescue the Merchant Prince

- **Any military unit can take a scouting action to attempt to help the duSesna Guild search for the missing Merchant Prince**
- **If a total of three thousand strength is put to the action then it will find the Prince whether he is alive or dead**
- **Military units assigned to Rescue the Merchant Prince will reduce the effective strength of the attempt to Map the City Below**
- **There are no rewards for taking this option**

The duSesna Guild are [seeking to find their merchant prince](Our_elaborate_plans.md#desperately-in-need), *Teodore duSesna* who they believe has been disappeared for opposing the machinations of Sung. If the total effective military strength of all the military units assigned to **Rescue the Merchant Prince** is 3000 or more, then the merchant prince will be found, either alive or dead, as the case may be. However, the attempt to Rescue the Merchant Prince will undermine efforts - every military unit working to rescue Prince duSesna subtracts their military strength from those attempting to Map the City Below. There are no rewards publicly available for anyone taking this option.

### Scout the Tsirku Scouting Network

- **Tsirku is so large and inhospitable that only a single region can be explored each season**
- **The venture requires at least fifteen hundred strength to gain any useful information**
- **Military units assigned to Conceal the Northern Lights will reduce the effective strength of this venture**
- **Military units from nations other than Wintermark and Varushka suffer a one-rank penalty due to the cold**
- **There are no rewards for taking this option**

The vast territory of Tsirku, is so inhospitable that it [represents a huge challenge to scouts](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#scouts). Rather than exploring the entire territory in one go, each season the scouting network will explore a single region. If the scouts are successful, the Imperial Spymaster will be able to choose where to explore next. Only once the entire territory has been scouted will a map suitable for scrying be created. There are no rewards publicly available for anyone taking this option.

| Strength | Tsirku Scouting Network Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 1500 | An overview of the region including the names of significant features such as lakes, frozen seas, or mountain ranges. |
| 3000 | Details of any significant Jotun or Thule presence in the region, along with any connections to Otkodov, Tromsa, or Skallahn, as well as information about locations with a tie to Agramant or other hostile eternals. |
| 5000 | A thorough overview of the region, showing the locations of any significant outside presences, several points of interest located in the region, and the names and a short overview of the terrain of adjoining regions. |

The Empire has been warned by [Sung](../eternals/Sung.md) and [Sadogua](../eternals/Sadogua.md) that *[Concilium of the Perjured Throne](../eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md)* will try to hire military units to thwart efforts to scout the wastes. Every military unit that takes the venture to **Conceal the Northern Lights** will count its military strength *against* the total strength of those trying to explore Tsirku.

### Conceal the Northern Lights

- **The Whisper Gallery is eager to prevent the secrets of Tsirku from being spoiled**
- **Characters that have had contact with the eternal can take this action to reduce the effective strength of efforts to Scout Tsirku**
- **Military units from nations other than Wintermark and Varushka suffer a one-rank penalty due to the cold**
- **Heralds of the eternal will provide crystal fire and luxury goods to anyone who helps to thwart the efforts to scout the territory**

The [Whisper Gallery](../eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md) is determined to prevent Imperial scouts from [destroying the secrets of Tsirku by exposing them to the sterile light of day](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#tsirku-is-secret). The *Court of the Shadowed Oracle* [have made an offer](Monarchs_and_outcasts.md#tyrannical-discipline-whisper-gallery) to those Steinr poets, offering [crystal fire](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#crystal-fire) and luxury goods to anyone who will help them try to thwart the efforts of the scouts by creating false trails, spoiling supplies and otherwise sabotaging the efforts to discover more. Every military unit working to **Conceal the Northern Lights** subtracts their military strength from those attempting to scout Tsirku.

Only characters with a military unit who have had contact with the Whisper Gallery can take this action. A starting military unit will receive two pieces of crystal fire and luxury goods worth 72 rings for their help; an upgraded military unit will gain proportionately more.

### Adventure in Tsirku

- **Tsirku is exciting and has tempted adventurers from Wintermark and Dawn**
- **Dawnish and Wintermark military units can take the Adventure in Tsirku action to quest into the wasteland and recover treasures**
- **Military units from Dawn suffer a one-rank penalty due to the cold**

In addition to [scouting Tsirku](#scout-the-tsirku-scouting-network), some military units can [quest into the wasteland](Snap_rope_and_pass_foil.md#tsirku-is-enticing) in search of treasures and wonders. A heroic trek into Tsirku focused around exploring not the terrain but the hidden nooks where danger lies... that might be something that appeals to the folk of Wintermark as much as any mapping expedition. For a start, it's known that there are terrible creatures in the wastelands. Some are [supernatural beasts](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#guardians) of Blood-on-the-Snow, that raven down from the wild places into Sermersuaq. Others are dangerous spirits of the wild places, or creatures adapted to live in the terrible frozen wastes.

A standard military unit that takes this action will receive two "treasures of the wasteland" (consumables representing unique resources found in Tsirku, most likely harvested from beasts of Agramant or dangerous locations) and 36 rings representing valuable or novel things recovered from the wasteland that can be sold to the curious. A military unit that has an effective rank of at least 11 (counting [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) and [upgrades](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#upgrades)) will also receive a *wonder of the wasteland*, a much rarer consumable.

### Spy on the Thule

- **The Karass have offered to help the Empire survey Thule attempts to claim a Black Lotus plant in the Forest of Ulnak**
- **Military units can take the Survey Thule attempts to claim a Black Lotus plant action until the spy network in Forest of Ulnak is destroyed**
- **Military units taking this action will receive shares of the guerdon**

Last season, the Karass [put forward an offer](Fear_quickens_the_breath.md#into-the-forest) to help the Empire spy on any attempts by the Thule to claim a black lotus plant. The Imperial Spymaster, **Rahmah i Darwisj i Erigo**, made an announcement [supporting](../senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#authorise-supporting-karass) the action during the Spring Equinox. Military units assigned to the action would collaborate with Karass scouts, who would help cover their tracks while they spied on the Thule. If two thousand points of total strength are assigned to the action, then the Empire will become aware if the Thule managed to claim a Black Lotus plant. If four thousand strength is assigned to the action, then the Empire will have a chance to employ the [Sentinel Gate](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md) to prevent that from happening.

There is no possibility that the Thule will discover the presence of scouts in the Forest of Ulnak. The Thule could only get an inkling of what was going on if the Karass betrayed them, if they were informed by the [Ambassador to Otkodov](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Otkodov.md), or if the Empire badly botched a military raid of some kind.

### Swords Against Darkness

- **Urizen, League, and Dawnish military units can take a questing action to attack the threats in Spiral**
- **If at least two thousand strength is assigned to the action for three seasons over the next year, then the venture will remain**
- **A standard military unit that takes this action will receive three mana crystals and two doses of liao**

After a lot of work persuading some of the wealthier Apulian merchants and key [arbiters](../nations/urizen/Arbiter.md), Naomi has been able to persuade them to back an attempt to [solve the problem](My_friends_are_my_estate.md#swords-against-darkness). The solution is simple enough, and for once, Naomi's idea requires relatively little upfront investment. If the Empire can muster enough military units to directly attack the threats in Spiral, then the inhabitants of the territory would be able to get on top of the problem. If that could be achieved, it would only need a little support on an ongoing basis to keep the threats under control. Naomi likens it to weeding an overgrown garden - it requires a lot more effort to get the weeds under control than it does to keep it that way.

With the backing of wealthy patrons, the venture would provide 3 mana crystals and two liao to any standard military unit taking part. A military unit with at least 200 effective strength (from upgrades or rituals) would receive a [Circlet of Falling Snow](../../mixed/magic/items/Circlet_of_Falling_Snow.md) as part of their rewards. The potential returns aren't great - but of course the Senate could always apply the Guerdon to the expedition.

The venture lasts for a year - but if at least 2000 strength of military units undertakes the venture on three seasons out of the four, then the venture will remain available for the foreseeable future. If that happens, then the taxation of the territory would permanently increase by 6 thrones a season, and the current penalty to military units from the Black Plateau would be permanently removed.

The efforts of Urizen, League, and Dawnish military units were effective last season; at least two more seasons of attacking the threats in Spiral will see the horrors of the Black Plateau beaten back.

## Fleets

- **Players who control fleets can choose from several downtime options**
- **Not every fleet is eligible to participate in every voyage**
- **Left to their own devices, a fleet defaults to the *maritime escort* option**

Any player whose character controls a [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) can choose from several downtime options by logging into the downtime system. They may choose to [trade](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#trading) with a [foreign port](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md), engage in [raiding](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#raiding) barbarians, [quest](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#questing) in search of glory or renown, [scout](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#scouting) either to support a [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) or as part of another stealthy option, or commit to [guarding](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#guarding) a convoy of other ships.

Left to their own devices, a fleet defaults to [maritime escort](../../mixed/economy/Voyage.md#maritime-escort), guaranteeing an income of money along with a random chance of herbs, more money, mana crystals, ingots or measures.

### Raid Leen - Rescue Slaves

- **Freeborn fleets can join a raid against the Druj city of Leen**
- **This action received the support of the Imperial Spymaster**
- **A standard fleet taking this raiding voyage will receive six random herbs and four random resources**

[Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) captains are able to travel to [Leen Rarckan](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md#leen-rarckan) in the [Salt Flats of Sanath](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md) to [take part in a raid on Leen to free slaves](Push_for_victory.md#see-that-we-fight). The total effective strength assigned to the opportunity will determine the outcome. If the effective military strength is less than a thousand, then the raid will be botched, few if any slaves will be freed, and the raiders will be lucky to get away with their lives.

| Strength | Raid Leen Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 1000 | The raid is able to rescue a handful of slaves, but the raiders are able to escape unharmed |
| 5000 | The raid is a success and the corsairs and their allies will free hundreds of slaves, mostly former Imperials, from the clutches of the Druj. |
| 7500 | The raid is a triumph; hundreds will be freed including scores of individuals who have never been part of the Empire. |

A standard fleet taking this raiding voyage will receive six random herbs and four random resources. A fleet that is enchanted or upgraded receives more, and may also acquire some Druj Potions as part of their loot, usually representing a captured potion or two useful to warriors. Whatever happens, the raid won't impact the Druj's ability to fight the Empire.

### Support the Wings of Kobol

- **Freeborn fleets can support the *Wings of Kobol*, a Suranni navy in attacking Free Landing**
- **A standard fleet taking this action will receive 72 rings and six random resources**
- **If at least 4000 strength supports the navy, Freeborn mariners will be able to recover relics of the founding**

After the Spring Equinox, any Freeborn [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) can support the Wings of Kobol navy. The Suranni offer no additional incentive to take part beyond the [chance for retribution](Gossamer_threads.md#spears-of-retribution), and the chance to recover some of the wealth lost to the Grendel and the Children of Wrecks. If at least 4000 military strength supports the Wings of Kobol, then whether the Suranni are victorious or not, the mariners of the Brass Coast will be able to reach the ruins of Atalaya, where the Brass Coast began. This town was at one point a treasure trove of historical relics that reminded the Freeborn of where they came from and why their nation exists. The Suranni will not stop the Freeborn taking back as many of these items as they can find - not after the Spring Equinox at least.

A standard fleet that takes this action will receive 72 rings worth of valuable items and six random resources in [loot](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#loot). This might mean additional money, or even mana crystals, and represents goods recovered from the Children of Wrecks and the ruins of Free Landing.

### Support the Wreckers

- **Wind Lord Moraith has made arrangements for some Imperial fleets to help the Children of Wrecks fight the Wings of Kobol**
- **If enough fleets help the Wreckers, then the Iron Confederacy could be beaten back**
- **There are no rewards for taking this option**

A Grendel Wind Lord who hates the Iron Confederacy, has made arrangements for some Imperial fleets to help the Children of Wrecks try to fight off the Wings of Kobol. This action is highly illegal, but the total chaos of a battle between Iron Confederacy forces and the Wreckers will make it impossible for the Iron Confederacy to know that the Empire played a role in the battle or indeed who. The only information the Empire will receive is which nations were represented among those forces fighting.

Only characters with a fleet that have had contact with the Wind Lord can take this action. There are no rewards publicly available for anyone taking this option.

### Trade Summary

- **Imperial fleets can trade with ports in Axos, the Commonwealth, Faraden, the Iron Confederacy, the Sarcophan Delves, the Sumaah Republic, and Zemelslo**
- **Production depends on the port chosen, and can be increased by upgrades and enchantments**

Imperial fleets can [trade](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#trading) with a number of foreign nations who have chosen to open some of their ports to visitors from the Empire. The assumption is that a ship trading with a foreign nation makes enough from selling their cargo to cover their running costs, crew salaries and the like, but that there is enough profit to provide the character who owns the fleet with valuable goods of interest to the folk who attend [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md). What goods are received for visiting a port is changeable, and heavily influenced by Imperial diplomacy.

- **Axos:** The ports of the **[Towers of Kantor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#towers-of-kantor)** and **[Chambers of Issyk](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#axos)** welcome Imperial fleets. Navarr fleets receive a frosty reception due to the association with the *Scions of Cavan* and suffer a two-rank penalty; more information can be found in the **[Wise and measured](Wise_and_measured.md#treason-and-the-vallorn)** wind of fortune
- **Commonwealth:** The ports of **[Leerdam](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#leerdam)** and **[Volkavaar](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#volkavaar)**, as well as the new port of **[Murolosch](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#murolosch)** welcome Imperial traders. Imperial fleets suffer a one-rank penalty with both ports whilst the Empire continues to trade with Zemelslo whilst embargoing Jarm. More information can be found in the **[Weaving tangled nets](Weaving_tangled_nets.md#future-good)** wind of fortune
- **Faraden:** The port of **[Caitun](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#caitun)** welcomes Imperial fleets. Fleets from the Marches and Wintermark benefit from a one-rank bonus due to close proximity.
- **Iron Confederacy:** The port of **[Robec](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#robec)** is available for Imperial fleets.
- **Sarcophan Delves:** The usual ports of **[Sarcophan](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#sarcophan)** and **[Betovering](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#betovering)** welcome Imperial traders.
- **Sumaah Republic:** The port of **[Zemeh](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#zemeh)** welcomes Imperial traders.
- **Zemelslo:** The port of **[Radosmo](My_passions_from_a_common_spring.md#free-ports-and-free-people)** welcomes Imperial traders following the Imperial Senate's decision to [permit trade](../senate-sessions/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#confirm-trade-with-zemelslo) with the nation very close to the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md)

## Congregations

*[Image: Assembly General.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_General.png)*

- **In addition to the standard preaching options taken by owners of congregations, special *lectures* may be available**

During downtime, the owner of a [congregation](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) can choose to take a [preaching](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md#downtime-options) action. Normally, these actions are used to preach one of the Imperial virtues, or the general teachings of [the Way](../world/The_Way.md), allowing a Synod member to change which of the virtue assemblies they belong to.

After the Spring Equinox, there are three lectures available.

### Preach Pride - Support Marcher Orcs

*[Image: Assembly Marcher.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Marcher.png)*

- **A standard congregation that takes the *Preach Pride - Support Marcher Orcs* action will receive the no liao, seven crowns, and 10 votes in the Synod**
- **A congregation that takes this action will be in the Pride Assembly at the next summit**
- **This lecture is only available following the Spring Equinox 388YE**

At the Winter Solstice, the Marcher Assembly voted to uphold a [judgement](../synod-judgements/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-13) raised by **Brother Luke** with a greater majority. The statement of principle called on the Marches to defend the [Marcher orcs](../world/Mournwold_Orcs.md) if it came to it. This has been met with acceptance across the Marches, though some are still uncertain, and those who agree with the assembly have been looking at ways they can help support the message.

Priests who take this action are assumed to be making use of the [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) they receive to [inspire congregants to travel across the Marches](388YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_fortune.md#pride-in-the-mournwold) and support the [Marcher orcs](../world/Mournwold_Orcs.md). It's small, but "*Pride in small things*" as the famous saying starts. Rather than receiving any [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md), the congregation will receive money from orcs grateful for their work.

*[Image: Labyrinth.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Labyrinth.jpg)*

- **Anyone with a congregation can choose to preach the Way by encouraging pilgrims to support the Grand Inspiration of the Way**

- **Congregations taking this action will contribute at least seven rings to the labour costs of the Grand Inspiration of the Way**
- **A congregation that takes this action will be in the Way Assembly at the next summit**
- **This lecture is available until the start of the Spring Equinox 389YE**

Following on from the [highest calling](Lay_the_foundations.md) of the Highborn Assembly, the congregations of the Empire can preach the good news of the Second Age of Virtue. The more congregations that took part each season, the greater the benefits would be. The individual effects would be tiny, but a thousand raindrops can quickly turn into a flood. Virtuous citizens would make a pilgrimage to [Bastion](../nations/highguard/Bastion.md), to volunteer to help with the work of completing the Grand Inspiration, working to raise the monument to the heavens - doing their part to support the Grand Inspiration and ushering in a new era. They may not be builders, but they believe in the dream to be given form. Children, the elderly, all united by faith to add even a single brick and know they were part of this momentous occasion in the history of mortal ascension.

[After those who are brave enough to make the journey](Sing_a_song.md#find-the-right-question) experience the awe-inspiring power of the true aura in the shrine, they will return home inspired to embrace Wisdom and pursue it in the certain belief that inaction is the only true mistake.

A congregation that preaches this special action will receive three fewer [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) each season (assumed to have been spent on creating auras or used to provide patronage), but four additional votes in the Synod (representing the swelling of their congregations). The priest leading the congregation will become part of the Wisdom Assembly if they were not already.

## Arcane Projections

- **Arcane projections must be submitted before the close of downtime**
- **It is not possible to submit arcane projections that create personal curses**

The deadline for submitting an [arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) is now the close of downtime. This is a significantly shorter window than previously. We've made this change to improve our ability to process arcane projections - an earlier deadline means that the game team have more time to consider each arcane projection, which will allow us to be more detailed and consistent in our responses.

As detailed in the **[The moth for the star](The_moth_for_the_star.md#a-drowning-sailor)** wind of fortune, it is not currently possible to create an arcane projection that involves a [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) directed at a character.

### Colleges and Forges

- **We've changed the email address for college of magic and runeforge submissions**

It is important for characters responsible for a [college of magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) or [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md) to remember that the deadline for submitting rituals for codification, or instructions to the runeforge, is also the point that downtime closes. You *must* use let us know how you plan to use your college or forge by this point, otherwise it will not be possible to submit anything and the season will be lost.

We've also made a small change there; the e-mail address to which such instructions should be sent is now [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) rather than rules. The rules decisions about an arcane projection that is being codified, or a new item, have already been made, and it is the plot team who deal with the creation of ritual texts, unique items, or schema.

### Artefacts

- **If you are creating an artefact you will need to email plot@profounddecisions.co.uk with the requested name and roleplaying effect**

Artisans can use [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) to make a permanent artefact version of any magic item they can create. These powerful items retain their properties indefinitely, are readily identifiable as unique named items, and can exert powerful [roleplaying effects](../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) over anyone bonded to them.

Anyone wanting to create an artefact should email [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) before downtime closes with their CID and what magic item they want to be an artefact. They should also include what name they want the artefact to have and the desired roleplaying effect. With roleplaying effects, it is best to avoid anything with religious themes; that is the purview of the [hallow](../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md) ceremony. It is best to avoid naming specific concepts: a roleplaying effect of "*You feel an urge to protect Urizen lore*" would be returned as "*You feel an urge to preserve the magical secrets of your people*". It can be interpreted as the same for someone in [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md), but it would encourage someone in [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) to protect [Runesmith's Law](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law), or a magician-prince of [Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) to protect their own collection of ritual texts.

As with [hallow](../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md#effects), comedic names or names which are *obviously* taken from existing works of fiction are not appropriate for item names in Empire and will be rejected when identified.
