## Overview

There are six great powers in the known world whose influence and reach rivals that of the Empire. There also innumerable lesser powers who do not have the might to shake the world, but who are nonetheless important to those with whom they share a border. The ambitions of these nearby nations are no less relevant to the Empire. Sometimes diplomatic relations require in-depth analysis; sometimes there is an opportunity to catch one's breath and let the drama move slowly towards the next crisis point.

There are [ambassadors](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) to some of these nations - the [Suranni](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md), [Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md), the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md), and [Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md) in particular. Other nations are dealt with by the [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md). Sometimes a neighbouring nation focuses on diplomacy with the Empire, and requires an in-depth discussion. Sometimes however there is less going on in a given season and diplomacy can be part of a wider, roundup. For example **[Myth makers](Myth_makers.md)** covers nearly half a dozen of the orc nations that lie near the Empire.

Here we can read about several neighbouring nations to the Empire not covered in detail elsewhere - the [Axou](#axos), the [Faraden](#faraden), the [Iron Confederacy](#iron-confederacy), and [Tsark](#tsark), as well as the folk of [Marracossa](#marracossa), the exiles of the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) who live in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#trajadoz).

## Axos

*[Image: The Victorious Axis continue to go from strength to strength.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Alikos_Helio.jpg)*

- **Axos remains allied to the Empire through treaty and through the Liberty Pact**
- **They have taken ownership of the region of Thornfen in Sarangrave**
- **The Imperial ambassador to Axos is Thanmir Hrafnar of Wintermark**

Imperial relations with the [Citadels of Axos](../foreign-nations/Axos.md) remain steadily positive. Partly this is due to the [Synod's](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) work during the [reclamation of Solokha](It_never_happened.md#solokha), partly due to the ongoing alliance between Axos and the Empire in [Sarangrave](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md) which has seen them gain custody of the massive [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) known as the [Tower of the Skink](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#tower-of-the-skink). A new status quo has been established in the tiny nation following the [upheaval](Victory_and_unity.md#times-change) that followed the rise of the ambitious *Grand Ilarch Kyther*. A formal Grand Council guides the nation, and the [Unified Convocation](A_better_future.md#unified-convocation) works to bring Axos' disparate religious [sects](../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects) together in a similar way.

The ambassador cannot become complacent however; Axos is no less touchy about its independence that it has ever been, and relations could sour again very easily. For the moment, though, the Axos Ambassador to the Empire *Ilarch Kario Skopos of Solokha* remains cordial and sometimes even friendly. Skopos is a scion of an influential family from the [Chambers of Issyk](../foreign-nations/Axos.md#chambers-of-ussyk), who has renounced his ties to the citadel of his birth at the instruction of the Grand Council for as long as he holds the post. This is intended to signify neutrality in the politics of the citadel, to make him a worthier intermediary through which the Axou can deal with their allies in the Empire.

### Claiming Thornfen

- **Axos has claimed the region of Thornfen in Sarangrave**
- **They have removed the former Druj subjects who lived there**
- **The Axou are preparing to rebuild the Tower of the Skink**

Ambassador Skopos thanks his opposite number in the Empire for their timely warning relating to [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md), [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), and Imperial [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). They wax lyrical about how this kind of cooperation between allies is a portent of the great things Axos and the Empire can achieve together when they put their minds to it. He even praises Imperial magicians - the [Autumn magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md) laid on the territory made the task of removing the Druj subjects in Thornfen significantly easier. In return, the experts at the [Halls of Maykop](../foreign-nations/Axos.md#halls-of-maykop) have been considering how to address the dangers presented by Druj armies supported by the shambling dead Ambassador Hrafnar has spoken of.

*[Image: Thane Thanmir Hrafnar is Ambassador to Axos.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Thane_Thanmir_Hrafnar.jpg)*

Over the last season however the Ipotavan forces in Sarangrave have been occupied by completing the task of claiming [Thornfen](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#thornfen). Their efforts were hampered a little by fallout from the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) expansion, and the need to eject several thousand people from the marshes around the Tower of the Skink. As the Summer Solstice dawns, the region as a whole is now in Axou hands. Imperial armies are free to move through it, as per their [treaty](../senate-motions/Ratify_Axos_Treaty_Autumn_387YE.md#the-treaty) with the Empire, but the civil service note that the Axou could theoretically remove this privilege at any time.

As they [said they would](Wise_and_measured.md#sephals-and-strategy), the Axou army has exiled all the former subjects of the Druj they can find in the region. They have been channeled primarily into the [Whisperwood](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#whisperwood), with a few stragglers ending up in [Kroll](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#kroll). The vast majority of these exiles are [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) but there are also humans among their number - only those former slaves of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) who could prove a connection to Axos were allowed to remain. The Axou have by all accounts made only minimal efforts to identify any actual Druj among these former inhabitants of Thornfen - they have simply thrown *everyone* out. Or at least, everyone they could find. The army is unable to focus its full attention on the wilder places of the region, and there are a number of organised groups of Druj guerillas in Thornfen who were once part of the garrison of the Tower, who continue to harass Axou soldiers.

This means that there are now a significant number of people whose legal status is very unclear loose in the Sarangrave, without supplies, shelter, or in many cases, any real idea what is going on. That, however, is a problem for Urizen and the Empire, not the Axou.

Most Imperial citizens in the territory are busy engaged with the vallorn, or fighting off the Druj this season, but there are some reports that the Axou are moving large amounts of [white granite](../../mixed/economy/White_granite.md) into the region through the [Path of the Eye](../nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#path-of-the-eye) - the pass that links [the Mallum](../barbarians/The_Mallum.md) with Axos. They've also brought in a large number of labourers, recruited at apparently ruinous expense from all the citadels of the nation. While some are being garrisoned at the Tower, an equally large number have begun establishing camps in the vicinity of the wrecked city of Sephals - and it doesn't look as if they are planning to rebuild it. Indeed, it looks a lot more like they are planning to tear it apart to salvage building materials. Presumably after looting it.

### Armies of the Dead

- **The Axou are struggling to establish a garrison at the Tower of the Skink**
- **Ilarch Skopos asks for the empire to donate up to 80 wains of mithril to help get the new Axou garrison established**
- **Before the garrison can protect Sarangrave it requires a Senate Motion granting it the explicit power to move around and fight threats**

The Axou control the Tower of the Skink, and following negotiations with the [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md) are prepared to help protect Sarangrave from the threat of invasion or rebellion. With that in mind, Ilarch Skopos asks that the Senate make this arrangement clear with a [Senate motion](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md). If they are prepared to explicitly invite the Axou to defend Sarangrave (with the obvious current exceptions of Nesustak Forest and Bendol), then their garrison stands ready. Such a motion would provide the needed legal permission to go anywhere in the Imperial held regions of the territory and deal with threats to its people as they see fit. The freedom would apply to both the garrison of the Tower of the Skink and Axou armies - who would be free to move should they wish to in the same way Imperial armies can currently move through Thornfen. Without such a mandate to act, the garrison and the army will be restricted to defending Thornfen only.

As it stands the garrison would certainly protect Thornfen from the Empire if they ever tried to claim it, or to move armies through it without the permission of the Grand Ilarchs. The [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) advise that as well as the obvious benefits, the declaration requested by Ilarch Skopos will give Axou troops to move around Sarangrave, which could in theory present problems if relations with Axos were to sour. Ultimately the key issue is one of control. With the garrison under the control of the Axou it will ultimately be down to them what it does.

That might not be a problem for a while as the new Axou garrison at Thornfen is taking a while to establish itself. With vallorn and Druj alike in the territory this season, there has been no movement toward establishing the new. Ilarch Skopos informs Ambassador Thanmir that there is a recruitment drive taking place in Axos at the moment but that response has been slow. The Grand Ilarch of Ipotavo is frustrated by this state of affairs - it's not as if the Axou were unaware of this potential problem - and has demanded Ilarch Skopos find a solution.

The civil service estimate that it will take a minimum of two and a half years for the Axou garrison at the Tower of the Skink to reach its full strength, assuming there are no additional setbacks. Any season where there is fighting in Sarangrave will prevent the Axou from resupplying and could even see losses if the garrison is drawn into the fight; opposing forces prevent natural resupply even if the garrison is not directly involved.

The Axou could use either mithril or weirwood to increase the speed with which the garrison would establish itself, but the Grand Ilarchs' Council seems loath to contribute their own comparatively limited wains to do so. Ilarch Skopos does not come straight out and *ask* Ambassador Thanmir to see if the Empire can be convinced to provide such support, but the *implication* is very clear. If the Empire wants the garrison to be ready for battle on a meaningful time-scale they will need to provide the wains to the Axou.

While the Halls of Maykop are considering the matter of Druj armies supported by ghoulish horrors, Ilarch Skopos has also asked them for any guidance they can offer the [High Exorcist](../../mixed/titles/High_Exorcist.md) **Dame Belmarie de Courtenay Wyldrose** in their work to combat one of the *lemuré*. Ambassador Hafnir mentioned former Ambassador **Tarquinius Ankarien**, and Ilarch Skopos has had some of his staff review the archives and found some information that his own predecessor *Maxatios* provided in the past. The libraries of Maykop are extensive, especially when it relates to ghosts and similar entities. Ilarch Skopos is confident that information exists; the challenge will be finding something specifically useful to the High Exorcist in their current endeavour.

After discussion with *Grand Ilarch Keloparia* of Maykop, an agreement has been reached (albeit a little grudgingly). It is the request to study with masters of *necromantia* that has apparently swung Keloparia's opinion in favour of the proposal. The High Exorcist of the Empire wanting aid from the magicians of Maykop, rather than the *Convocation of Faith* apparently bodes well. Ilarch Skopos cannot quite conceal the fact that the popularity of "Theodosia Azesos' experiment" seems to have stalled a little, to the amusement of some of her political opponents.

Regardless, the Halls will make some members of the research staff and one of the *necromantia* librarians available to host an Imperial scholar should they wish to visit after the Summer Solstice to investigate *necromantia* relating to the lemuré. Unfortunately, due to the faculty schedules, the Empire would need to take this offer up during the Summer Solstice - after that it would need to be rearranged and there is no guarantee that anyone would be available to assist such a scholarly endeavour. Ilarch Skopos notes that the faculty at Maykop will be expecting a member of the [Department of Historical Research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md#the-department-of-historical-research); the invitation sadly cannot be extended to other scholars (explicitly this means the [Lepidean Librarian](../../mixed/titles/Lepidean_Librarian.md) cannot commission this research).

As the ambassador proposed, this would be an ideal gift for the Grand Ilarch and the faculty staff to thank them for their time. Not because they intend to use it for any religious purposes, Ilarch Skopos clarifies, but because it is very useful in certain *necromantia* rites. The civil service note that if the commission opportunity is taken up, whoever commissions it could include some guidance as to what kinds of things the High Exorcist would find useful. ### An Empty Bottle (Conjunction)

- **A company of Grendel are moving to attack a grounded Axou ship**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- ***Telemachus of the Lighthouse-That-Was* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for ensuring that less than a dozen Grendel survive**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

Trade between Axos and the Empire is flourishing, in no small part due to the efforts of the [Quaymaster of the Elosian Docks](../../mixed/titles/Quaymaster_of_the_Hesychian_Docks.md). Unfortunately, ships sailing in and out of the docks mean more chance of a mishap leading to disaster. Two weeks before the solstice, an Axou trading vessel runs aground while on the return trip downstream. The crew and cargo are relatively safe and decidedly more sober than they were, and awaiting rescue. Unfortunately, a passing ship of the Grendel has landed close by, and its crew are moving towards the grounded Axou.

It is the responsibility of the incumbent [Quaymaster of the Elosian Docks](../../mixed/titles/Quaymaster_of_the_Hesychian_Docks.md), **Telemachus of the Lighthouse-That-Was**, to ensure that fewer than a dozen Grendel make it to the ship. If fewer than a dozen pirates survive the encounter, the Axou repel the Grendel. If a dozen or more survive, the Axou crew will be forced to abandon their cargo.

If the Axou survive, it will increase confidence in trading at the Elosian Docks, reducing costs for the Quaymaster in dragonbone to purchase white granite by roughly a tenth. If, however, the Grendel claim the cargo of the grounded ship, it will have the opposite effect, increasing costs in dragonbone by roughly a tenth.

## Faraden

- **Faraden is at peace with the Empire, and trades freely with Imperial citizens**
- **The Liege of Five Winds appears to favour the Empire, but there are powerful factions in Faraden that prefer closer ties with Asavea**
- **Faraden is in the process of disentangling itself from Asavean interests**

> **[Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md)**
>
> - **Status: Foreigner**
> - **Ports: [Caitun](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#caitun)**
> - **Ambassador: Mina i Yashum i Riqueza of [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md)**

[Faraden](../foreign-nations/Faraden.md) lies to the west of the Empire, across the Lasambrian Hills of [Reinos](../barbarians/Reinos.md). Trade is its lifeblood, but it is also a country of canny wizards, [Courageous](../religion/Courage.md) warriors, and [Proud](../religion/Pride.md) (albeit heretical) priests. They currently do not share a border with the Empire, lying beyond the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) and the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md), but that does not stop them sending their caravans to Imperial markets despite the high tariffs they must pay to pass through the lands between. Diplomatic relations between the Court of the Five Winds and the Imperial Senate are changeable - warm one moment, cool the next - but it has never come to open warfare. Right now Faraden is squeezed between two competing interests - the Empire on one side and the angry Plenum of the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) on the other - and they must walk a knife-edge between the two powerful nations.

The Faraden ambassador to the Empire is *Mistress Singua Karadragha*. Mistress Singua advises the Court of the Five Winds on all diplomatic matters, and speaks on their behalf to foreign ambassadors. Her attitude to the Empire appears to be cool but professional, and heavily informed by the wishes of the Liege and their court, who seek more opportunities to trade with the Empire.

*[Image: Mina i Yashum i Riqueza , Ambassador to Faraden](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Mina6596.jpg)*

Kistress Karadragha begins by sending greetings and congratulations to the new [Ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Faraden.md) **Mina i Yashum i Riqueza**. As former [Azure Sutannir](../../mixed/titles/Azure_Sutannir.md), their name is already known to the Faraden diplomat. She also sends a note on behalf of the Liege of the Five Winds thanking the former Ambassador, **Ebenezer**, for their work cementing the growing friendship between the two nations, along with wishes for a warm future full of Prosperity and Pride.

It is perhaps a shame that the first message to the new ambassador also contains confirmation that the Faraden will be signing a new peace treaty with the Jotun during the Summer Solstice. As discussed [previously](Five_flames.md#peace-and-the-jotun), without a major commitment from allies to support Faraden against the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) the nation is not prepared to go to war. Reading between the lines, it seems that the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) invasion of [Reinos](../barbarians/Reinos.md) is not going the way the Court of Five Winds would have hoped.

In response to the polite request of the new ambassador, however, Mistress Karadragha explains that the main concern for the Faraden at this moment is that their merchants are able to travel freely to the Empire, and trade with Imperial citizens. Ensuring that they retain the ability to do so is of paramount importance.

### Ashes to Ashes

- **Faraden authorities have executed an Asavean citizen accused of funding the Cinders**
- **The Asaveans have closed their embassy in Faraden, but have not broken off diplomatic relations**
- **The Liege of the Five Winds has made a public speech commending Imperial law enforcement**

For some weeks after the spring equinox Faraden is abuzz over an intersection of international diplomacy and high criminality. Tommasso Triusti, an Asavean citizen caught up in the [purge of the Cinders](Five_flames.md#cinders-extinguished), has been publicly executed for conspiracy to commit treason. It was an undignified affair, so the rumour goes. Unlike the Cinders, who met their deaths with dignity, their foreign enabler wept with fear and alternated between pleading for a reprieve and haranguing both the crowd and the executioner. The Faraden have strong opinions as to how condemned criminals ought to act, and this cowardly display has earned opprobrium from all corners of society.

The Asaveans are, by all accounts, beside themselves with rage. After what was rumoured to be a tense stand-off in the Palace of Five Winds between the Liege and the head of the embassy staff, the entire Asavean embassy was vacated. Those citizens of Faraden who once spoke of a close alliance with the Asaveans now carefully step away from their prior convictions, or at least keep very quiet about them. There are apparently rumblings about trade sanctions, but for now diplomatic relations remain open.

Just before the summer solstice, an address by the Liege of the Five Winds is circulated throughout Faraden. While it mostly speaks to national concerns, the Liege makes a point of commending Imperial legal authorities for their recent cooperation in the destruction of the Cinders. The Imperial Magistracy and militia are praised for their diligence and attentiveness, and much is made of the benefits of cooperation between Faraden and the Empire. Despite all attempts to the contrary, the position of the Liege remains strong and relations between Faraden and the Empire have never been better.

### Faith and Law

*[Image: Brother Geoffrey Orchard is a talented horticulturalist, and vigilant investigator, who now lives at Pickham Monastery in Upwold.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Orchard_A.png)*

- **Mistress Karadragha asks for clarification relating to a new proscription**

Ambassador Mina has raised the matter of faith and religion, and Mistress Karadragha responds by expressing Faraden concerns. According to Faraden merchants the Empire has made it a crime to bear a [dedication](../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) or [anointing](../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md) of [Justice](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#vengeance). This clearly refers to the recent proscription [recent proscription](Stones_in_the_wellspring.md#blasphemy) raised by *Brother Geoffrey Orchard* at the Winter Solstice.

Obviously there are other prescriptions that are likely of concern (such as [Judgement 72](../synod-judgements/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-72) given that the Faraden faith believes that [Ambition](../religion/Ambition.md), [Wisdom](../religion/Wisdom.md), and [Vigilance](../religion/Vigilance.md) are not useful spiritual qualities). Yet it is this matter on which Mistress Karadragha seeks clarification from the Ambassador.

Previously, sensible Faraden travellers have avoided legal issues while visiting the Empire by simply keeping their belief in the [Five Flames](../religion/On_Faraden_faith.md) to themselves. By not preaching the Faraden faith, they have not been held guilty of [blasphemy](../../mixed/government/synod/Religious_crime.md#blasphemy) or [heresy](../../mixed/government/synod/Religious_crime.md#heresy). Now it seems that simply being dedicated, or bearing an anointing, will be a crime in Imperial territory. Is this understanding of the situation correct?

Speaking candidly, Mistress Karadragha expresses the concern that the Liege of the Five Winds and many of the priestly families that support them have spent a lot of time talking about the "new tolerance" of the Empire - that as long as they avoid single-minded pursuit of Justice they will be able to trade freely in the Empire. If this is no longer the case, the Court of Five Winds needs to know so that they can decide how best to proceed when encouraging their people to travel to the Empire.

## Iron Confederacy

- **The Iron Confederacy remains at peace with the Empire**
- **They have recently taken both Cazar Straits in Feroz and Free Landing in Madruga from the Children of Wrecks**

> **The [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md)**
>
> - **Status: Foreigner**
> - **Ports: [Robec](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#robec)**
> - **Ambassador: Tethros i Zuhri i Erigo**

The lands of the Suranni people - the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) - lie directly over the Empire's south-western border. They do not welcome visitors from the Empire except in carefully selected enclaves such as the port of [Robec](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#robec). While they are currently at peace with the Empire, they are by no means allies. Courted by both the Empire and the [Asaveans](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md), the Dukes have been content to to play both interests off against each other. They made some concessions to the Liberty Pact by "redefining" their slaves as "serfs", but those who have actually visited Suranni lands say that the lives of the lowest class of their society have barely improved with this change in terminology.

*[Image: Tethros i Zuhri i Erigo , Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:AmbassadorIronConfederacy.png)*

The Suranni recently moved military forces into the territory of [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md), attacking and defeating the Children of Wrecks and seizing the [Cazar Straits](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#cazar-straits). Most recently, they have [done the same](../winds-of-war/Phantasms_and_foam.md#take-your-prize) in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md), having claimed [Free Landing](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#free-landing) as well. Both regions are made up of islands that are a traditional home of the [corsair](../nations/the-brass-coast/Corsair.md) families for [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). Their ambassador has been clear both that they intend to go further, having dealt with the Children of Wrecks, and that they have no intention of conceding the islands to the Empire.

The Suranni ambassador to the Empire is *Baroness Katerine Lavasse of Meore*, who operates from the Tour Lavasse in Chaubrette, a territory unknown to Imperial citizens that apparently lies to the west of [Veroigne](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#veroigne). A noble [naga](../../mixed/magic/lore/Naga.md), she replaced the significantly more dogmatic Jeanne de Hibernat, a High Priestess of the Suranni god [Evra the Smith, Maiden of the Forge](../religion/Suranni_pantheon.md#evra-the-smith-maiden-of-the-forge). She has already indicated that she is very much more pragmatic than her predecessor and more interested in compromise. She still reports to the Dukes of the Iron Confederacy, however, and so her apparent goodwill toward the Empire cannot be relied upon.

### Diplomatic Considerations

- **The Iron Confederacy propose a meeting at the Autumn Equinox to discuss a potential treaty**
- **They would like the Empire to prohibit attempts to spread the Way to the Suranni**
- **They have no intention to return Free Landing and Cazar Straits, but it is clear they could be bought off, but it would require skilful negotiation**

[Last season](Gossamer_threads.md) saw diplomatic approaches from the Iron Confederacy that included a request to [permit armies](Gossamer_threads.md#rings-and-wains) of the Iron Confederacy involved in their [invasion](Gossamer_threads.md#the-western-hills) of [Reinos](../barbarians/Reinos.md) to resupply via western [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md). This request was ultimately refused. They also sought to purchase resources to protect the northernmost Suranni territory of [Arbonne](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#kalino-or-arbonne), which the Empire knows as Kalino, from an ongoing campaign of baleful [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md). In this, the Ambassador was more able to provide the requested assistance.

*[Image: Kobol the Warrior is the uncompromising Iron Confederacy god of warriors and battle.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Kobol.PNG)*

*[Image: Evra the Smith is the god of artisans and merchants in the Iron Confederacy.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Evra.PNG)*

*[Image: Dumon the Liar is the hated god of traitors, liars, and wizards whose followers strive to overthrow the divine order of the Iron Confederacy.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Dumon.PNG)*

Moving forward Ambassador Tethros has apparently been in negotiation with Baroness Katerine regarding a possible [treaty](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty). Now that the Suranni have taken retribution against the Wreckers, and dealt with the Hand of Dumon hiding among them, they seem more inclined to discuss such matters. The intent is to send a few diplomats to Anvil during the Autumn Equinox, to see if such a treaty can be prepared, and the points raised by the Ambassador so far have proved very useful. Baroness Katerine asks that this discussion continue between them, with the Ambassador continuing to help refine the matters that will be under discussion.

The first proposal presented by the Imperial Ambassador is apparently an offer to outlaw the preaching of the Way in Suranni Lands. Baroness Katerine indicates that this isn't of interest to the Dukes, because any Imperial citizen found preaching the Way in their lands would be arrested and executed as blasphemers. However what the Dukes *are* interested in is for the Senate to make it illegal to preach the Way to Suranni citizens in the *Empire*. Specifically, to make it illegal to target Suranni travellers when proselytising.

The Dukes are pleased to hear that imperial efforts to restore the bridges over the Scorrero [proceed apace](The_Oran_operation.md). They are also open to the idea of establishing a formal trading enclave in the Empire; the town of Bramar in [Fortargenta](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#fontargenta) is well known to Iron Confederacy merchants and some kind of market and a temple for the priests of [Evra the Smith](../religion/Suranni_pantheon.md#evra-the-smith-maiden-of-the-forge) would be appealing. Either that or dedicated markets in [Oran](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oran), [Calvos](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#calvos), and [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#sarvos) for use by ships travelling along the coast through the eastern islands.

Military cooperation against the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) has also been discussed, and Baroness Katerine understands the Ambassador's reticence to commit to anything. Candidly, the fact the Empire is fighting the barbarian orcs in Imperial territories already provides a measure of assistance. The Dukes are however prepared to agree that any Jotun land the Empire takes in Reinos is theirs to keep, without contest - provided the Empire is in turn prepared to offer the same recognition to the Iron Confederacy. Both nations, says the Baroness, would surely benefit from the extermination of the Lasambrian Hills bandits.

As to the matter of Free Landing and Cazar Straits, however, the Baroness is much less forthcoming. As the Dukes have said over and over, they plan to keep what they have taken from the Children of Wrecks. The Empire had every opportunity to destroy the pirates sheltering in those islands before the Suranni took charge of matters. Relinquishment of the islands is unlikely to be on the table during negotiations. In theory at least, it might be possible for the Empire to negotiate the Suranni withdrawing from Free Landing, but the Ambassador must be aware that the price for doing so is likely to be a great deal of money or truly eye-watering amounts of mithril, weirwood, and white granite. The islands of the Brass Coast represent significant economic and strategic benefits to the Suranni. They conquered the islands with the lives of their soldiers, and in the name of the [Maiden of Battle](../religion/Suranni_pantheon.md#kobol-the-warrior-maiden-of-battle), and any concession to the Empire is going to need to reflect that.

### Hand of Dumon

- **Baroness Katerine concedes the Empire had no role in the recent curses on Arbonne**

After due consideration, the Iron Confederacy accepts that the Empire had no role in the cursing of Arbonne, and that they will ensure the organisation puts down no roots within their borders. Even the Duke of Arbonne is apparently [satisfied](Gossamer_threads.md#dumon-the-liar) with the Ambassador's assurances this time, and the Suranni lawkeepers engaged in assisting the magistrates as part of the [Dumonist treaty](../senate-motions/Ratify_Dumonist_Treaty.md) have tempered their zeal a little.

With the provision of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) to help create a warding temple in Arbonne to combat the malignancy of the Hand of Dumon it *seems* the matter is put to rest for the moment. The civil service are at pains to point out that if any Hand of Dumon agents or sympathisers *were* active in Imperial politics, and their identities were exposed, then it could potentially prove quite embarrassing for the Ambassador and the Empire. Having access to the magistracy, even in a limited capacity, means that the Suranni lawkeepers are aware whenever someone is prosecuted for [membership of the Hand of Dumon](../senate-motions/Criminalize_Hand_of_Dumon.md). Obviously there's no evidence of such an individual or conspiracy, but [Vigilance](../religion/Vigilance.md) is clear about the need to [prepare for malice](../religion/Vigilance.md#guidance-on-the-path), and to avoid becoming complacent.

### Marko Siwarsbairn

- **The well-regarded scholar has return to the Empire and is eager to return to work**

Poor [Marko Siwarsbairn](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md#marko-siwarsbairn)! Their visit to the Iron Confederacy to research military history took significantly longer than expected and arguably at various points they were in fact imprisoned by their Suranni hosts. Worse, when they were finally given leave to return to the Empire, their research complete, they were [attacked](Gossamer_threads.md#the-perils-of-marko-siwarsbairn-arbonne) by the Hand of Dumon and narrowly avoided being sacrificed to the Suranni [God of Lies](../religion/Suranni_pantheon.md#dumon-the-liar-the-wicked-one) . Thanks to the intercession of the [Minister of Historical Research](../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) **Watt Lambrook**, and by all accounts the direct aid of Ambassador Thethros, the [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) scholar was safely rescued. They handed over the fruits of nearly a year of research and then... went straight back to work.

There has been some suggestion that Marko may wish to take a leave of absence to recover from their recent adventures, but the plucky scholar is having none of it. Given time to recover, they are of the opinion that if nothing else all this business has been *exciting* - even thought it may have been a waste of a year from a scholastic point of view it was never *dull*. They're furious with the Iron Confederacy about the way their freedom to explore the subject of Suranni military history was so closely monitored, and how their words were edited and reviewed by the Baroness Katerine and her priests. They also want to be clear that when the document said "area of industry in Arbonne" for example, they meant "mithril mine for supplying armies", as just one example.

There has been some suggestion Baroness Katerine may be required to pay some kind of compensation. Instead the Suranni diplomat merely sends her best wishes to Marko, invites them to visit if ever they are in the vicinity of her holdings, and that given they are now back in the Empire she considers the matter closed. After all, it was Hand of Dumon agents *in the Empire* who attacked Marko, and while the Minister of Historical Research may have needed to intercede, her knights still did their job by ensuring the scholar returned to the bosom of the Empire alive. Marko's response involves quite an amount of coarse language.

## Tsark

- **The people of Tsark have sent an adept to Occursion**
- **Adept Camilla invites letters on the subject of Prosperity**
- **Urizeni congregation will be able to take the lecture 'Discuss Prosperity with the Adepts of Tsark' after the Summer Solstice.**

The Seeker of the Perfect World has written to Sage Kristoph, the Tsarkoi sage who has long been the primary diplomat of the Tsarkoi to the Empire, opening a conversation on the subject of Prosperity and the ways in which it might benefit Tsark. After a little deliberation, the Council of Sages appointed Camilla - as a senior adept - to be the primary representative to the Empire on this subject. They are - cautiously - interested in hearing more.

Camilla's parents were Urizeni who found a way to Tsark a generation ago and decided to stay, so she is a little familiar with the Empire and [the Way](../world/The_Way.md) as a result. However, the Tsarkoi do not make use of spiritual forces *other* than that of Peace - quite the contrary to their neighbours, the notoriously blasphemous and heretical Axou who make use of auras of every kind, and so she is not greatly familiar with the Seven Virtues of the Way except through her parent's stories.

*[Image: Those wishing to write to Camilla on the subject of what Prosperity means to them should send a Winged Messenger to Adept Camilla, Garden of the Perfect World, Occursion, Zenith .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:WingedMessenger.png)*

Camilla says that she intends to stay a little while at the Garden of the Perfect World. There are other adepts and sages who intend to visit in the season that follows: she has been asked to prepare the way. The Council of Sages believes, from what it understands of Prosperity, that the Virtue *may* be one that fits in the perfect world that the Tsarkoi seek - in accordance with their esoteric beliefs - to bring about. Camilla says that she will make herself available for any Imperial citizen who wishes to write to her explaining what Prosperity means to them. In addition, she and the other adepts chosen for this initiative would be delighted to meet with Urizeni who wish to share their perspective on Prosperity.

After the Summer Solstice, holders of an Urizeni congregation may take the lecture 'Discuss Prosperity with the Adepts of Tsark'. This will represent the priest taking themselves - and possibly their congregation - to Occursion to meet with Adept Camilla and her fellows, to discuss Prosperity and demonstrate the auras associated with it. As such, the congregation will not produce its normal complement of liao, as it is assumed it will be used in the creation of auras. However, Camilla has promised that in return she offers a portion of zaboravi. Obviously, any priest who takes this lecture will end up in the Prosperity assembly as a result at the subsequent summit.

## Marracossa

*[Image: Leonorada Rousseau currently resides in Trajadoz , Torres , Madruga for anyone who wants to talk about Marracossan business.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:WingedMessenger.png)*

*[Image: Eulàlia i Phovas i Riqueza , Senator for Madruga](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Eul%C3%A0lia_i_Phovas_i_Riqueza.jpg)*

- **The proscription against the Theist Heresy creates major problems for the Marracossans**
- **They are appealing to the Marracossa Sendito for help**

Representatives of the [Trajadozi Marracossans](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#trajadoz), [came to Anvil](Journeys_end_in_lovers_meeting.md#by-roads-both-straight-and-winding) for the Spring Equinox. They planned to meet with both the current [Marracossa Sendito](../../mixed/titles/Marracossa_Sendito.md) **Karg i Estrico i Erigo** and the former holder of the title, **Hugo i Bamesh**. By all accounts the meeting went well - the Marracossans returned to Trajadoz satisfied at least. Or rather... *mostly* satisfied. There is a great deal of concern among the former inhabitants of the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) regarding the blanket criminalisation of their religion.

The [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) of [proscription](../../mixed/government/synod/Proscription.md) raised by **Frater Pontius of Adoniram's Testament** [defines](../synod-judgements/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-114) the "Theist Heresy" as *the claim that there are gods that seek dominance of, control over or communion with sapient spirits*." The Marracossans have never tried to hide their faith in their gods, and given that the town of Trajadoz has been [ceded](../senate-sessions/384YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#cede-trajadoz) to them by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) they are not concerned that they will need to fend off zealous magistrates. What they are very concerned about is their relationship with their neighbours.

The Marracossans have over the last few years formed bonds of friendship and business with Freeborn folk in [Torres](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#torres) in particular. Some of their number fought against the Grendel when they attempted to conquer Madruga, and indeed the Freeborn Assembly [recognised and praised](../synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-160) them for their [Courage](../religion/Courage.md), [Pride](../religion/Pride.md), and [Loyalty](../religion/Loyalty.md). Now they're concerned that anyone who trades with them, visits them, or even speaks to them might face prosecution. Worse, they are concerned that they might face prosecution if they leave the environs of Trajadoz - effectively trapping them in the town for the foreseeable future.

They're *very* keen for the Marracossa Sendito to advise them on the situation. This is in line with the [responsibilities](../../mixed/titles/Marracossa_Sendito.md#responsibilities) of the post - given that this proscription may represent a threat to Trajadoz and involves a request for representation. The civil service indicate that Karg i Estrico i Erigo may wish to seek the advice of the [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md) **Atte Arrow-Tongue Metsastajason** on this matter. While Karg is responsible for dealing with the Marracossans they are legally foreigners with whom the Empire does not have a recognised [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md), and any [treaties](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md#treaty) for example would need to be prepared by the Consuls office. The advice of the magistrates might also be valuable in what exactly this new heresy means for the Marracossans.

The Marracossans are *also* keen for their friends in Madruga to speak on their behalf. Can the Freeborn Assembly offer any reassurance, for example? Or should they begin preparing for a siege? Can they truly be considered heretics when they do not follow the Way, or even pretend to, but likewise have no interest in proselytising their own religion to others?
