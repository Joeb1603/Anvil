# 379YE Autumn Equinox winds of fortune

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/379YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md).

### A Big Sleep

The [vyig](../nations/varushka/Vyig.md) suffer significant set-backs in [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md), and the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) exists to finally dislodge the criminal ticks that have been feeding on the cities of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) for too long. You can learn more about this event [here](A_big_sleep.md).

### Briar Liar

A [briar](../../mixed/magic/lore/Briar.md) demagogue has been spreading damaging lies in [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and whipping up bad feeling wherever he and his sister go. You can learn more about this event [here](Briar_liar.md).

### Crow on the Cradle

With the [cessation of hostilities](../senate-motions/Accept_Thule_peace_treaty.md) in [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md), the [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) inhabitants of the northern vales are seeking closer ties with their estranged cousins. You can learn more about this event [here](Crow_on_the_cradle.md).

### The Rise of the Fellowship of Purple Sails

A [sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) of ship-owners, merchants, captains, and [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [benefactors](../nations/highguard/Benefactor.md) is taking a renewed interest in Imperial politics: also, a few more details regarding foreign trade. You can learn more about this event [here](Rise_of_the_Fellowship_of_Purple_Sails.md).

### Fog on the Brine

Less than a month ago, a bank of supernatural fog has descended along the southern Imperial coast, and seems to stretch along the entire northern half of the Bay of Catazar. You can learn more about this event [here](Fog_on_the_brine.md).

### Forge of Sorrows

A surprise find in [Gildenheim](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#gildenheim) presents an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to the Empire - to refurbish an ancient [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md). The opportunity does not come without complications, however; and the provenance of the ruined forge hints at greater mysteries. You can learn more about this event [here](Forge_of_sorrows.md).

### Good Faith

The [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) Salt Lord Suriad has sent a reasonably open letter to the Empire, concerning [recent treaty negotiations](../senate-motions/Accept_Grendel_peace_treaty.md). You can read the letter [here](Good_faith.md).

### Hearth Tithe

Conflict between the Jotun and the Empire has moved to an active phase, there is talk in [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) of reviving the Hearth Tithe. You can learn more about this event [here](Hearth_Tithe.md).

### Professional Killers

Following the [bloody murder](Bloody_murder.md) of civil servants associated with the department of [historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md), the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has [acted](../senate-motions/Assign_guards_to_historical_researchers.md) ... but the attacks have not stopped, Not precisely. You can learn more about this event [here](Professional_killers.md).

### Reap What You Sow

Troubles in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) continue; miners in particular are being encouraged to get as much metal out of the ground as they can before the territory is handed over to the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) - and dissatisfaction is spreading. You can learn more about this event [here](Reap_what_you_sow.md).

### Red Phoenix Down

The mountains of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) have been struck by a powerful [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) that not only halves the amount of crystal mana coming from the [mana sites](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md), but leads to more than one catastrophic loss of [poise](../nations/urizen/Urizen_culture_and_customs.md#poise). You can learn more about this event [here](Red_phoenix_down.md).

### Reed of Fortune

The herald Reed, the Reckoner of Hours, a favoured servant of [Ephisis](../eternals/Ephisis.md) has been abroad in the Empire for the last three months, and has expressed a desire to purchase a [Declaration of Amity](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-amity) from the Empire. You can learn more about this event [here](Reed_of_fortune.md).

### The Rose of Astolat

Unsettling events at [the Castle of Thorns](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md#the-castle-of-thorns) in [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md); is there something dark at the heart of the Empire? The old seat of the [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) kings and queens, the Empire's première academy for civil servants, appears to be falling down ... You can learn more about this event [here](Rose_of_Astolat.md).

### Strange Harvest

In the months since the Summer Solstice, [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) activity along the borders of [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md) has spilled over into neighbouring [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md), [Casinea](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md), and [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md), leaving a wave of odd transformations in its wake. You can learn more about this event [here](Strange_harvest.md).

### The Dead Tree Gives No Shelter

A significant force of orcs has crossed over the border from the Lasambrian hills into [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md). You can learn more about this event [here](The_dead_tree_gives_no_shelter.md).

### Sitskrieg

The [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) raids in the west of the Empire have been decisively driven back by a combination of [fortifications](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) and [Imperial armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). You can learn more about this event [here](Sitskrieg.md).

## Ongoing Events

### Reikos Flux

The [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md) flux continue to rage unabated in that war-torn [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md). In addition to significant damage to [resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource) in the territory, it has cut a swathe through the armies engaged in fighting the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) at [Urith Barath](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md#urith-barath-high-chalcis). The only significant development comes from the presence of [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) companies in the territory has demonstrated that the disease appears only to affect humans. You can learn about the Reikos flux in [this season's Winds of War](../winds-of-war/379YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#the-death-of-death), or the original information about the disease can be found [here](Reikos_flux.md).

### Unquiet Dead

Following the dual haunting crises revealed [last season](Unquiet_dead.md), the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md), Ozren de Orzel of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) was [named by the Imperial Synod](../synod-judgements/379YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#statements-of-principle) to oversee the exorcism of the [Great Pits at Ennerlund](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#the-great-pits-of-ennerlund). During the Autumn equinox, the Synod named High Exorcist Solas to oversee a similar cleansing of the [Vigilant Swan](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md#the-vigilant-swan) in Reikos.
