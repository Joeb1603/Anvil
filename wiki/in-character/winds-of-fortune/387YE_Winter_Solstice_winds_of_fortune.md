Over the past three months, events have taken place which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. These are the winds of fortune, counterparts to the winds of war that detail the Empire's military campaigns. They lay out situations, and usually include things that characters can do to take advantage of [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or to resolve problems. In almost every case, they start with a piece of flavour text. This fiction helps introduce the wind of fortune, or draws out a particular theme or element. Often, it represents a viewpoint that one or more NPCs might have in-character. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours, the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip if they want to use it in character.

The body of the wind of fortune describes situations and lays out ways players can engage with them. Except where we explicitly talk about something being "rumoured" or use a phrase like "some might say..." the details of a wind of fortune are factual. They represent briefing material presented by the civil service or similar authoritative sources and represent something the average educated citizen would acknowledge as a reliable truth. Unless we specify otherwise, we're also being as thorough as possible. This information doesn't need further checking in-character. It's not an opinion, and we're not trying to trick you.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters could know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months. It's perfectly acceptable to turn up to Empire having read only a few Winds of Fortune that directly interest your character; part of the fun on the field can come from learning about situations and opportunities in other parts of the game.

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations or political bodies to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place, and very little happens in isolation. We are also adding the dates that they were last updated (other than typos and correcting red links) in an attempt to make it easier to keep track of the newest releases.

## Winds of Fortune

### We are the storm (Imperial Address)

- **Imperial citizens**

[Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md) has used their power to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) to present a personal message to all Imperial citizens, celebrating Imperial victories over the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), and calling on the heroes of the Empire to turn their attention to the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) - especially those in [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md).

The message has been distributed across the Empire by the civil service, and you can read it in the **[We are the storm](We_are_the_storm.md)** wind of fortune.

### Opening Ceremonies

- **Imperial citizens; warriors and gladiators; Archmage of Summer; Imperial Apothecaries' Guild; others still to be revealed**
- **Last updated 19/04/2025**

A new structure nestles on the outskirt of Anvil, drawn from the [Summer realm](../eternals/Realm.md#summer) with the aid of *[King Stone's](../eternals/Adamant.md)* industrious *koboldi*. Celebrating the friendship between the monarchs of Summer and the people of the Empire, the *[Lord of the Crossroads](../eternals/Barien.md)* called the *Drottningbjotha* for the Winter Solstice - a challenge of arms between ten nations to delight Imperial citizen and [herald](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md) alike.

You can learn about the new Anvil Arena, and about most of what the *Drottningbjotha* involves, in the **[Opening ceremonies](Opening_ceremonies.md)** wind of fortune.

### Mushroom, Mushroom, Mushroom

- **Navarr; Advisor on the Vallorn; Spring magicians; Imperial Conclave; People who dislike the Druj; Highguard, Urizen, and the League**
- **Last updated 04/03/2026**

The [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) appear to have [declared neutrality](../conclave-sessions/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Conclave_sessions.md#alignment-llofir-neutrality) with the [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Llofir](../eternals/Llofir.md). Relations between the *Rotlord* and the Empire have been strained for several years, and the removal of [enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) represents a significant step by Imperial magicians. Llofir's heralds can now move freely about the Empire, which is a potential matter for concern, but it can't wield its power freely, as it could if it were granted [amity](../eternals/Alignment.md#amity). The compromise this represents has prompted a response from the *End of Empires*, whose servants have made it clear that it has not yet forgiven the Empire for the destruction of its garden, but it seems that there is cautious optimism for the future.

You can learn about boons offered by Llofir to Imperial magicians, and to the Navarr in particular, in the **[Mushroom, mushroom, mushroom](Mushroom-_mushroom-_mushroom.md)** wind of fortune.

### Mermaids in the Basement (Appraisal)

- **Imperial Senate; Brass Coast; Red Wind Corsairs and Burning Falcon; Urizen**

The Imperial [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has [called](../senate-sessions/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#appraise-bridges-in-brass-coast) for an [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) looking into the potential for constructing a bridge between the mainlands and the islands of [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). *[Lutomysla Niegoslava](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#lutomysla-niegoslava)* was called on to investigate the possibilities, and has since delivered her findings which include some discussion of things that seem impossible, and some things that are at least achievable if not necessarily in the way the Senate has proposed.

You can find out about the appraisal, and how the islands may be reconnected to the mainland, in the **[Mermaids in the basement](Mermaids_in_the_basement.md)** wind of fortune.

### Cold War

- **Imperial Senate, Military Council, Conclave orders; Ambassadors to Jarm and the Commonwealth; Military unit captains; Generals of the Burning Falcon, Gryphon's Pride, Granite Pillar, Valiant Pegasus, Summer Storm, Autumn Hammers, Bounders, Drakes, Strong Reeds, Black Thorns, Quiet Step, Northern Eagle, Green Shield, Bloodcloaks**

"*The Cold Sun has risen, and Lantir is condemned to history.*" These were the words of [Leviathan](../eternals/Leviathan.md) spoken to the *Wings of the Skywise* at the end of the Autumn Equinox. Both the [Ambassador to the Commonwealth](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Commonwealth.md), and the [Ambassador to Jarm](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_Jarm.md), reached out to their opposite numbers following this news, offering Imperial aid if it were needed. Exact details are thin on the ground, but it appears that the power of *[Oblivion](../eternals/Cold_Sun.md)* has all but annihilated the capital city of Lantir and is spreading throughout that tiny nation far to the east. Given that Lantir is positioned between the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) and the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md), and is ostensibly the reason for their current ongoing war, the fate of that little kingdom might portend something even more disastrous.

You can read about the rise of Cold Sun in the east, and what it means for international politics, in the **[Cold war](Cold_war.md)** wind of fortune.

### My Friends Are My Estate (Appraisal)

- **Imperial Senate; League and Urizen citizens of Spiral, especially military unit, fleet, and business owners; League and Urizen assemblies; Oblivion Sentinel; Architect of the Crucible; the Legacy Bourse Seat; Dawnish questors**

The second appraisal commissioned by the Senate during the Autumn Equinox concerned [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md). For centuries [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) heartland, now part of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md), there has been significant conflict between the two nations in the recent past. Where the two sides have managed to cooperate, however, the results have been impressive. The [Senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for Spiral, **Genoveva Barossa d'Apulian**, proposed a timely appraisal to build on these successes by looking "for ways to draw on the best of League innovation and Urizen wisdom to bolster Spiral's prosperity." The Senate agreed, and as requested [Naomi of Virtue's Rest](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#naomi-of-virtues-rest) was dispatched to explore [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) in the south-eastern Empire. She has now submitted her findings for the perusal of Imperial citizens.

You can find out what Naomi has proposed, and how that might change the situation in Spiral, in the **[My friends are my estate](My_friends_are_my_estate.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Truth But Tell It Slant (Appraisal)

- **Printers' Guild of Tassato, Imperial publishers, Vigilance assembly, Chair of the Wolf**

During the Equinox, the [Printers' Guild](../../out-of-character/groups/The_League_groups.md#printers-guild) of [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md) secured the right to serve as [patrons of the Beurteilung](../../mixed/titles/Patron_of_the_Beurteilung.md). They asked the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) academics to explore ways to expand the [printing](../../out-of-character/game-design/Technology.md#printing-press) empire of the Guild, especially to find ways to communicate their message to others outside the Empire's borders. After an enjoyable three months in the Twin City, Professor Gelberg and his associates have presented their findings, some of which have come a little from left field.

You can read about the proposals for the Printers' Guild, and some larger scale suggestions with regard to Imperial printing, in the **[The truth but tell it slant](The_truth_but_tell_it_slant.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Circle Game (Appraisal)

- **Imperial Senate; Grandmasters, Ambassadors, Northern Trade Network; Cinderpath Custodian, Bargainer at the Iron Tower, Mistress of the Dust Parador, Reeve of Boar's Market, Broker of the Invisible Cord, Talon of Kauppahall; Bursar of the Conclave, Bursar of the House of Spirits, Kruidenkenner Trademaster, Master of Root and Stem, Apothecary of Orieb, Merchant-Boyar of the Stinking Market; League and Marcher assemblies; and assorted other folk with an interest in the Imperial economy**

For the last six months, [Graciana i Lòpez i Guerra](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#graciana-i-lòpez-i-guerra) of the [Prognosticators Office](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#the-prognosticators-office) has been engaged in an appraisal put forward by **Fedelmidd Sydanjaa's Heart Floer**, the [senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md). Instructed to examine the [ministries](../../mixed/commissions/Ministry.md) of the Empire, and in spite of her expertise, she has been forced to admit that there are precious few options that will fulfil the thrust of the appraisal. Frustrated, the prognosticator has tried to take a broader view and look at what she has *guessed*, is the more fundamental problem - declining revenues for the Imperial treasury, using the time available to study the problem as thoroughly as possible. She has included the findings of that investigation as part of the overall appraisal.

You can learn about Graciana's work to locate alternate ministry opportunities, as well as her wider take on the Imperial economy, in the **[The circle game](The_circle_game.md)** wind of fortune.

### Forever is Composed of Nows

- **Imperial Senate; the Throne; potential sodalities; Imperial Master of Works; Master of the Imperial Mint, Conscience of the Senate, Imperial Magus, Advisor on Orc Affairs, Imperial Consul; Patron of the Beurteilung**

One of the powerful tools available to the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) is the ability to commission an [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md). There are limits to what the [Prognosticators Office](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#the-prognosticators-office) can achieve however, and competition for their resources is often fierce. In recent months these resources have become a little stretched with retirements, in-depth long-term projects, and at least one sabbatical/disappearance. With that in mind, several opportunities have been discussed in those parts of the civil service responsible for establishing, evaluating, and presenting valuable [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to the Empire.

You can read about some of the suggestions that have arisen from these discussions, and a few changes to the civil service, in the **[Forever is composed of nows](Forever_is_composed_of_nows.md)** wind of fortune.

### Ourselves and Immortality (Plenipotentiary)

- **Imperial magicians; Archmages; Conclave; Claudia Varkulova Remislav, william Guildenstern; warriors of all kinds; High Bard of the Empire; Grandmaster Fausta of the Silver Chalice, Senator Genoveva Barossa d'Apulian, Rafael Barossa d'Apulian, Mikael Reskovich (again), Morgan Waystone Path; Imperial Senate; Master of the Koboldi, Legion Engineer, Master of Rings, Chair of the Wolf; Imperial Master of Works; Joseph of the Ashen Flame**

Each of the six [archmages](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) are empowered to send a [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) message to an [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) of their [realm](../eternals/Realm.md) each season. Those eternals are bound to respond, although *how* exactly they respond is broadly up to them. Following the Autumn Equinox, the eternals have responded to what are presumed to be missives from the respective archmages. In a move that is almost unprecedented in recent years, *seven* of them have arranged some kind of parley during the Winter Solstice...

You can find out about the eternals, and the nature of their responses, in the **[Ourselves and immortality](Ourselves_and_immortality.md)** wind of fortune.

### In the Ground

- **Marches, especially smiths, landskeepers, and mummers; Stone Smith and Keeper of the Golden Fields; Marches Assembly; Imperial Senate**

[The Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) are rarely numbered among the great magical powers of the Empire, but they are by no means deficient in that regard. Their [landskeepers](../nations/the-marches/Landskeeper.md) see to the wellbeing of the [farms](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) and the [households](../nations/the-marches/Marcher_household.md), while [mummers](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Marches_magical_traditions.md#mummers) bring a little magic into the lives of villages and labourers, and the newly re-established [smith](../nations/the-marches/Smith.md) tradition settles back into their old role of parley with the [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md). Since the Autumn Equinox, three kings of the [eternal realms](../eternals/Realm.md) have reached out to magicians of the Marches. [King under the Mountain](../eternals/Adamant.md) responds to a message from the [Stone Smith](../../mixed/titles/Stone_Smith.md) by sending one of his servants to speak with the smiths. [King in Chains](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#tharim) sends his [herald](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#heralds) to offer gifts to the landskeepers he believes helped him achieve rough justice for the murder of his lieutenant. And [King of Wyrms](../eternals/Sadogua.md) celebrates his friendship with the Marches by offering particular support to the performing arts.

You can learn about the boons of these three kings, both for the magicians of the Marches and more mundane folks, in the **[In the ground](In_the_ground.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Concisest Tenant

- **Temeschwar and the folk of the League; Dramaturges; League Assembly; Unfettered Mind; Imperial Senate**

Throughout much of 386YE the city of [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) was bedevilled by rats and rat-adjacent creatures. The situation was calmed in large part due to a municipal contract with an old [guild](../nations/the-league/Guild.md) called the *Rat Kings*. This disreputable organisation catches vermin and delves the stinking sewers beneath the city for lost treasures. They are also now responsible for "protecting" the city from the rat plague. Some also point to the relocation of [Empress Giselle](../history/Empress_Giselle.md) back to [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) as having played a role in the quiescence of the rats. Yet there are rumours of something *else* living deep below the sewers of the old city. A creature that dates back long before there was an Empire, before there was a [League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md), when this entire [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) was part of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md). Perhaps these rumours have a little more of the ring of truth to them than many will be comfortable with.

You can find out about matters to do with rats in Temeschwar, and implications for other parts of the guild, in the **[The concisest tenant](The_concisest_tenant.md)** wind of fortune.

### To Fight Aloud

- **Urizeni; Imperial Synod; Grandmaster of the Sevenfold Path, Champion of Loyalty, Polemarch of Highwatch; General of the Argent Sword; Architect of the Crucible**
- **Last updated 18/04/2026**

The re-integration of the [sword scholars](../nations/urizen/Sword_scholar.md) into [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) and the wider Empire has never failed to be eventful. In the most recent development, the sword scholars split into two factions. One group who decided to forgo the teachings of [Sulemaine](../nations/urizen/Sulemaine.md) and stay aligned with the Empire. The other group, under the *nominal* leadership of [Severus of the Blinding Sun](The_deepest_cut.md#interred-with-their-bones), chose to withdraw from the Empire and remained committed to what they believe are the true teachings of the Paragon of Blades. Facing the risk of execution should Imperial law catch up with them, most have gone into hiding in the Urizen mountains. Last summit, a decisive blow was made that has completely unravelled the Disciples.

You can read about the fight against the followers of Sulemaine, and their response, in the **[To fight aloud](To_fight_aloud.md)** wind of fortune.

### When Peace Was Far Away

- **Ambassador to Jarm; Imperial Consul; Fleets visiting Radosmo; Imperial Senate; Imperial Apothecaries Guild**

The Empire has five peers, five other nations that operate on a similar scale. The furthest east of these are the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md), a great magocracy locked in tumultuous rivalry with its neighbour the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md). Imperial relations with Jarm are currently tense; trade between them is subject to a mutual [embargo](../../mixed/government/senate/Tariffs.md#embargo) for example. Yet diplomats from both the Empire and the Principalities still exchange messages, and sometimes common ground can be secured.

You can learn about relations between Jarm and the Empire, including several opportunities, in the **[When peace was far away](When_peace_was_far_away.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Price is Great

- **Ambassador to the Commonwealth; Imperial Senate; Highguard and Urizen; Voice of Liberty; Troubadours and priests of Dawn; Advisor on the Vallorn**

Among the Empire's peers is the [Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md), a nation that seeks to bring the entire world into their philosophy of the Common Good. Allies to the Empire through the [Liberty Pact](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_Liberty.md#liberty-pact), they are at war with their rivals in the [Principalities of Jarm](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md). Relations between Empire and Commonwealth are reasonably good, but there are always potholes in the road to understanding and sometimes they are deep enough to snap an axle...

You can read about relations between the Commonwealth and the Empire, as well as some opportunities related to their presence in the Empire, in the **[The price is great](The_price_is_great.md)** wind of fortune.

### Flight of Birds

At the Autumn Equinox, **Azir i Shartha i Riqueza**, former [Inquisitor of Ambition](../../mixed/titles/Virtue_inquisitor.md), raised a statement of principle in the Freeborn Assembly that called on the nation to reject [Anarchy](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#anarchy). There was a somewhat clear [response](The_tune_without_the_words.md#liberty-for-all) in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md), but there is uncertainty in the rest of the Brass Coast, partly due to the lingering effects of Night magic.

There are ongoing discussions, disagreements, and instances of disavowal across the nation as the Synod has clearly made a statement on something close to the heart of many in the nation. What Freedom means to the Freeborn is an *existential* question - something that cuts right to the soul of the nation. This is dangerous stuff. Some argue that this judgement is a scalpel that will let the Assembly cut out the infection, leaving it Virtuous and pure. Others claim it is a bhuj that will gut the nation, emptying its blood out onto the sand. Will purging the corruption of [Freedom](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#anarchy) make the nation stronger or fatally weaken it at a moment when it is beset by troubles? How does a denial of freedom and liberty and, yes, anarchy fit with the example of the Founders - of [Riqueza](../nations/the-brass-coast/Riqueza.md), [Guerra](../nations/the-brass-coast/Guerra.md), and [Erigo](../nations/the-brass-coast/Erigo.md)?

The judgements raised by [**Sufyan i Zuhri i Guerra**](../synod-judgements/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-183) in the Freeborn Assembly, and [**Nekoda of the Shattered Tower**](../synod-judgements/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-16)  in the Courage Assembly have complicated matters, but as neither judgement was upheld with a greater majority, neither has been received much additional attention. The thing that matters, the one thing that is crucial is the judgement of Azir i Shartha i Riqueza. The Assembly could choose to press forward with a mandate.

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > Beware the seductions of the false virtue of Anarchy! We send (named priest) with 150 doses of liao to rally the people of the Brass Coast to turn away from those who would revel in false freedoms. Reject Anarchy, lest your virtue be lost!
> >
> > Synod Mandate, Freeborn Assembly

If this mandate is enacted, Freeborn priests will encourage their fellows to denounce the [false virtue of Anarchy](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#anarchy). Those who openly profess a reverence for freedom and anarchy will be censured, and in many cases driven out of their families, communities, and even homes. Others will react strongly *against* the words of the Synod. They will look to [Riqueza](../nations/the-brass-coast/Riqueza.md) in particular, who spoke at length about the need for personal responsibility in matters of faith. As in Madruga, there will be backlash against the National Assembly. They will point out that [Ahraz](../religion/Emperor_Ahraz.md) freed the Imperial Orcs, and that while he may have been conflicted the [Lepidean Librarian](../../mixed/titles/Lepidean_Librarian.md) has presented a man who absolutely believed in freedom. They will point to the Liberty Pact that preaches that none should be enslaved... and so on and so on. There are always justifications, and as with any nation there are folk in the Brass Coast who would rather abandon the Way than abandon their personal beliefs.

*[Image: Assembly Freeborn.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Freeborn.png)*

Normally the prognosticators are able to indicate what might happen when a mandate is enacted, but matters of faith are much more complicated than economics or magic and divinations are deeply unreliable when dealing with the impact of [the Way](../world/The_Way.md). In this case, the [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) can offer little other than to say that the impact will be incredibly far reaching and that it will bring matters of faith and freedom to a head in the Brass Coast one way or another.

The safe alternative would actually be to take no action at all. Freedom is rife in the Coast at the moment, but it will likely burn itself out as it has in the past. The Assembly could simply wait for this current fever to pass, allowing calmer heads to prevail. Freedom will remain a recurring problem across the Coast, but this way doesn't risk killing the patient to cure them.

The one thing Night divinations all agree on is that enacting the mandate will *set the entire Brass Coast on fire* - but what *that* will do is impossible to know.

Like the Way itself, it appears that the decision is a matter of Virtue.

### Potency of Ambrellic

*[Image: Juvenile kraken are less dangerous than the adolescents encountered in the Sea of Snow but they are still not creatures to underestimate.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Kraken.JPG)*

- **Ships captains who hunted the kraken have returned with a significant haul of ambrellic**
- **Ambrellic is valuable and can be sold to Ephisis for 42 rings a dose**
- **It can be used by Imperial apothecaries to mix two unique potions**

At the Autumn Equinox, while the [Red Wind Corsairs](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md) were [battling the Gathering Storm](../winds-of-war/Red_skies.md), an armada of smaller fleets descended on the waters around [Free Landing](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#free-landing) to [hunt the juvenile kraken](387YE_Winter_Solstice_interlude.md#hunt-free-landing-kraken) that infest the waters. By the light of magical globes, they struggled with the young beasts - some larger than an ox by this time with long, reaching tentacles many times that length - but also with Wreckers who had not accompanied their ramshackle [navy](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) north. The stories from those who took part speak of desperate fights, nervous hunts, unexpected enemies... and a bounty of *ambrellic*.

This substance is pretty much unknown in the Empire, being found only in the bile tracts of immature krakens. It is highly sought after in the [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md), whose [physicks](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) use tiny amounts to remove scars and other skin blemishes. It is also used as the base for a variety of luxurious perfumes and scents. Even a small amount can command a good price. The [City of Gold and Lead](../eternals/Ephisis.md) in particular are aware of the demand for the substance in the Delves, and anyone wishing to test the waters is free to place as much ambrellic as they wish into an [Ephisis box](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Ephisis-_Scale.md). Despite the recent injunction against [potions and drugs](Volumes_of_forgotten_lore.md#drugs-and-potions) being presented to the City, members of the *Silent Market* have let it be known that they are keen to acquire Imperial ambrellic if anyone wishes to trade it with them, offering 42 rings a piece for as much as the Empire is prepared to offer.

There is another potential use for ambrellic, however. It seems that while physicks can make use of the substance to remove scars, it is also useful to [apothecaries](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary). Specifically useful to those capable of brewing [Philtres of the High Peaks](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_the_High_Peaks.md) and [Philtres of War](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_War.md). Anyone who knows how to brew the basic potions can use ambrellic in this fashion. The resulting potions are not quite as potent as those that require [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md), but the techniques for creating them are the same.

- The *Elixir of Sunlit Salt* can be brewed by any apothecary who knows how to brew the Philtres of War. It requires three drams of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), two drams each of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) and [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), a dram of [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md) and three drams of *ambrellic*. It will restore all the [hero points](../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) of someone who [consumes it](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md#consuming-a-potion).

- The *Elixir of Abyssal Depths* can be brewed by any apothecary who knows how to brew the Philtres of the High Peaks. It takes three drams of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), two drams each of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) and [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), one dram of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) and three drams of *ambrellic*. It restores all [personal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#additional-mana) to someone who drinks it.

These recipes rely on a supply of ambrellic, obviously. One reason they are not widely known in the Empire (or anywhere else for that matter) is the challenge presented by finding and hunting juvenile kraken. It might be feasible for the Empire to secure more persistent sources of the material, given that the Children of Wrecks introduced so many kraken larvae into the Bay of Catazar. How feasible that is and how it might be achieved is a matter for an [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md).

### Rumour Control

- **Some misconceptions have been corrected by the civil service**
- **There are Druj remnants in the Barrens, but they are only a skirmish-level threat**
- **Calvary’s End is a small settlement in the Barrens. It is an outlier, and is not a widespread problem**

The Empire is wide and complicated. It's easy for a rumour to get out of control and become potentially damaging misinformation. If a misunderstanding spreads far and wide enough, there is a chance that it might provoke mistakes at the highest level. To avoid that, the civil service steps in to correct these misconceptions when they become aware of them. Since the Autumn Equinox, there have been two rumours in particular that have begun to circulate in terms that are increasingly misleading.

The first concerns the number of Druj in [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md). There are several small groups of Mallum orcs remaining in the wildest areas of the Barrens. This is also the case in [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md), [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md), [Zenith](../nations/urizen/Zenith.md), and other territories that have had Druj incursions in the last few years. In fact, every territory in the Empire faces threats of this kind, whether it be brigands in the wilds of [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md), Druj partisans in Zenith or remnants of Cold Sun's forces in [Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md). These groups are not capable of capturing and holding territory, but they can threaten settlements and commissions. When that happens, it requires Imperial heroes passing through the Sentinel Gate to deal with them, or a similar force acting between summits.

The other rumour concerns the status of Calvary’s End, a small settlement in the Barrens. In terms of access to food, comfort, and overall competence of nobles, Calvary’s End is an outlier in the Barrens and the wider Empire. Under normal circumstances, almost every settlement in the Empire has access to sufficient food, schooling, and other things necessary to live a life of relative comfort under [Imperial law](../../mixed/government/senate/Imperial_law.md) following the principles laid out in the [Imperial Constitution](../../mixed/government/senate/Imperial_Constitution.md). Where this is not the case, the civil service draws attention to those situations and presents [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) for the Senate, Conclave, and Synod to deal with them. Here, it seems that extraordinary circumstances, including the isolation of the settlement, allowed things to deteriorate to a worrying degree. That situation has now been fully rectified by Imperial heroes, with the inhabitants of the town finding new homes across Dawn.

**OOC Note:** We don't feel the need to step in to correct misconceptions or misinformation very often, and we make a concerted effort to try to do so only when it would be obvious to people in the world that something is incorrect. Here, the presence of Druj in the territory is useful for plot - be that at Profound Decisions events or as part of a sanctioned event - in order to threaten sinecures and other commissions - there is no secret army in the Barrens. Likewise, the assumption should be that every Imperial citizen enjoys “Dignity, Freedom, and Prosperity” in their day-to-day life. Where this is not the case, such as with the [terrible harvests](March_on_their_belly.md) in the Marches, or more recently, the ongoing [support](../winds-of-war/No_Peace.md#stay-alive-conjunction) of the Greywaters in Bregasland, it is an active plot and not a change to the status quo.

### Marcher Maritime Traditions

- **Interest in Marches maritime traditions has been inspired by a short pamphlet**

Fleets are uncommon in the Marches, where people are inextricably connected to their farms and their lands. Marchers are not averse to sailing and both Bregasland and Mitwold have an extensive coast but beyond rumours of smugglers in Bregasland, few yeofolk pay much attention to those Marchers who put to sea. This changed in late 387YE when a short pamphlet entitled "Marcher Maritime Traditions" was penned in Meade and distributed freely in the Marches. It credits two Marchers - the late *Quay Stone* of [House Stone](../../out-of-character/groups/The_Marches_groups.md#house-stone) from the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md), and *Jarvey "Strongoar" Oddsboy* of [Steward's Landing](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#oddmire) in [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md) as having provided impetus for the publication, alongside the Marcher Assembly, with encouraged interest in the customs of the Marches' ships and sailors.

These customs have been practiced for centuries in some cases, though often discreetly when necessary. Now, though, they are becoming much better known in the Marches and beyond. You can read about the maritime customs of many Marcher sailors on the Marches [culture and customs](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_culture_and_customs.md#maritime-traditions) page.
