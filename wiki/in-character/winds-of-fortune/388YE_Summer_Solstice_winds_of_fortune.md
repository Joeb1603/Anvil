Over the past three months, events have taken place which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. These are the winds of fortune, counterparts to the winds of war that detail the Empire's military campaigns. They lay out situations and usually include things that characters can do to take advantage of [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or to resolve problems. In almost every case, they start with a piece of flavour text. This fiction helps introduce the wind of fortune or draws out a particular theme or element. Often, it represents a viewpoint that one or more NPCs might have in-character. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours, the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip if they want to use it in character.

The body of the wind of fortune describes situations and lays out ways players can engage with them. Except where we explicitly talk about something being "rumoured" or use a phrase like "some might say..." the details of a wind of fortune are factual. They represent briefing material presented by the civil service or similar authoritative sources and represent something the average educated citizen would acknowledge as a reliable truth. Unless we specify otherwise, we're also being as thorough as possible. This information doesn't need further checking in-character. It's not an opinion, and we're not trying to trick you.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters could know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months. It's perfectly acceptable to turn up to Empire having read only a few Winds of Fortune that directly interest your character; part of the fun on the field can come from learning about situations and opportunities in other parts of the game.

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations or political bodies to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place, and very little happens in isolation. We are also adding the dates that they were last updated (other than typos and correcting red links) in an attempt to make it easier to keep track of the newest releases.

## Winds of Fortune

### In the Court of Far Horizons

- **Throne; Senate; Fleet owners; Ambassadors; titles involved in international trade**

At the recent summit, [the Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md) requested that the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md#constitutional-court) [appraise the implications](Forever_is_composed_of_nows.md#the-abraham-gambit) of a new [Imperial sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md), the *Court of Far Horizons*. Magistrate Abraham has conducted an analysis of the constitutional implications of the new sodality's provisional manifesto. Abraham notes that the manifesto is "bold and vigorous" in establishing its remit, but that means that some of the implications are far-reaching... which can be both a good and a bad thing depending on where you stand.

You can read about Abraham's findings, as well as some proposals from interested parties, in the **[In the Court of Far Horizons](In_the_Court_of_Far_Horizons.md)** wind of fortune.

### Satraps of the Abyss

- **Conclave; Ambassador to Sumaah; scholars of magic and the eternal realms**

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has taken advantage of an opportunity brokered by the [Ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Sumaah_Republic.md) Accalon to engage in a spot of magical cooperation with the [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md). Freshly-appointed prognosticator *[Lisane de Terresford](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#lisane-de-terresford)* has been sent to *[appraise](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) how the High Houses of Virtue and the Imperial Conclave can find ways to co-operate in interactions with [eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md)*. While Lisane is expected to be engaged for the next several seasons, she has dispatched a preliminary report on her findings; a review of the relation between the magicians and the Präster of the republic - and the dealings they have with the creatures of the [realms](../eternals/Realm.md).

You can read about Lisane's findings, and about the opportunity for the Conclave to assist, in the **[Satraps of the Abyss](Satraps_of_the_Abyss.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Oran Operation

- **The Brass Coast, especially citizens of Feroz and Madruga; Imperial Senate; Military Council**

The river Scorrero broke its banks several years ago, and washed away the dreadful threat of the *dry patricians*. As a consequence of the wild river rising, the bridges connecting Madruga and Feroz were washed away. Last season, the Senate accepted [an offer](An_arrow_nocked_and_in_flight.md#a-garden-proposal) from the *Protectorate of Morajasse* to apply their expertise to the challenge of reconnecting the eastern Brass Coast. Their [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) of the situation has been submitted, with notes added by the [Prognosticators Office](../appraisals/Appraisal.md#the-prognosticators-office), and involves a major feat of engineering in the southern Empire.

You can learn about their proposal, and some changes that might make it a little more feasible, in the **[The Oran operation](The_Oran_operation.md)** wind of fortune.

### Viridian Nemesis

- **The League; Navarr; the Vallornguard; Loyalty assembly; Senate; Military Council**

The [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md) of the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) is to share their long experience fighting the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) with other nations. Last season, they [chose to work](Cup_of_choices.md) with the people of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md); now the Navarr National Assembly has urged their people to embrace the citizens of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) to arm and armour them for the upcoming battle with the Doom of Terunael. Once again, the [Loyalty Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) has supported the cause, calling upon those whose heart drives them towards the destruction of *Terun's Folly*.

You can read about the proposals put forward to help the League fight the vallorn, some of which are somewhat esoteric, in the **[Viridian nemesis](Viridian_nemesis.md)** wind of fortune.

### Love and Monsters

- **Dawn, especially the national assembly; Senate; Vallornguard; Knight of the Greenwood**

The Earl of Fools has convinced the National Assembly that the fight against the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) is the [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md#the-highest-calling) of the [Dawnish](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) people. With [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) won and the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) crumbling beneath the Empire's anvil and the [Thule's](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) hammer, surely it makes sense to focus on the next threat to their prize. The vallorn has not one but *three* hearts adjacent to the sprawling expanse of the Barrens - and so defeating it will be a truly glorious endeavour! Plus, the idea of a great, glorious war against the vallorn is more than enough to stir the blood of the people of the most splendid nation in the Empire!

You can learn about the opportunities presented by this highest calling, both for the assembly and the rest of the nation, in the **[Love and monsters](Love_and_monsters.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Right Answer

- **Assembly of the Way; Imperial Synod**

The Assembly of the way have found the [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md#the-highest-calling) of those priests who commit themselves to the faith as a whole rather than any single virtue. Formally the Assembly of the Way is expected to be responsible for the [integrity of the Way itself](../../mixed/government/synod/Inquisition.md#virtue). Many in the Assembly dedicate themselves to creating a more perfect understanding of the Way, even as they strive to maintain the fidelity of the [Doctrines of the Faith](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md). Such exploration is never without risk, however, and as recent events have shown revelation can shake the foundations of the Way. However, by making the need to test the faith their highest calling, the Assembly of the Way has an opportunity to examine the faith in a way that tries to ensure that nothing disastrous can happen.

You can learn about what this might involve, and the opportunities and costs involved, in the **[The right answer](The_right_answer.md)** wind of fortune.

### A Lasting Commitment

- **Ambition assembly; Highguard; Senate**

The [Ambition](../religion/Ambition.md) assembly has declared their own [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md#the-highest-calling), one that is as challenging as it is aspirational. The [Grand Inspiration of the Way](../senate-motions/Construct_Grand_Inspiration_of_the_Way.md) is well underway, but if it can be completed within the next few seasons it will be a capstone not only to the Imperial faith but for the virtue of Ambition. Just the idea of declaring something to be a highest calling embraces the Virtue of Ambition, but even so this is an extraordinary thing to attempt. There is still a vast amount of work to be done, but there is nothing more Ambitious than achieving the impossible....

You can learn about the Ambition assembly highest calling, and how it applies to the Grand Inspiration of the Way, in the **[A lasting commitment](A_lasting_commitment.md)** wind of fortune.

### Six Sheets to the Wind (Challenge the Iron Duke)

- **Challengers of the Iron Duke**

*[Image: BarienArt.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:BarienArt.jpg)*

Each season, the eternal *[Barien](../eternals/Barien.md)* presents opportunities to those prepared to [Challenge the Iron Duke](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Challenge_the_Iron_Duke.md). For the Summer Solstice, he has offered two challenges: a grand melee in the arena and a game of carefully manoeuvring a boat on a map. As ever, the rules of the ritual apply. Each person who receives the ritual will be *guaranteed* entry to a challenge. Depending on the challenge, they *may* be able to bring a certain number of attendants with them to assist. The exact number will depend on the nature of the challenge and the number of challengers. The [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) will publish exact numbers in [the Hub](../world/Anvil.md#the-hub) on Saturday morning for all to see.

You can find details of the challenges issued by Barien this season in the **[Six sheets to the wind](Six_sheets_to_the_wind.md)** wind of fortune.

### Ambassadors of Death (Plenipotentiary)

- **Imperial Conclave; magicians; General of the Black Thorns, Senator for Miaren; practitioners of Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust; Ambassadors to Jarm and the Commonwealth, Grandmaster of the Celestial Arch; Knight-protector of Autumn, Dawnish enchanters; practitioners of From the Mouths of Babes; Grandmaster of the Sevenfold Path, Cardinal of Wisdom; practitioners of Forge the Wooden Fastness, Marcher landskeepers, magistrates**

Each of the six [archmages](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) are empowered to send a [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) message to an [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) of their [realm](../eternals/Realm.md) each season. Those eternals are bound to respond, although *how* exactly they respond is broadly up to them. Following the Spring Equinox, six - sorry *five* - eternals have responded to what are presumed to be missives from the respective archmages. One of the realms is not represented and, perhaps fittingly given its nature, conspicuous in absence.

You can find out about the eternals, and the nature of their responses, in the **[Ambassadors of death](Ambassadors_of_death.md)** wind of fortune.

### All Laudable Qualities

- **People interested in Symposia; the *Raboviches* of False Hopes; People interested in proscriptions**

At the Winter Solstice 387YE, the [General Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) [supported a call](Stones_in_the_wellspring.md#a-convocation-of-condemnation) for a symposium to discuss [proscriptions](../../mixed/government/synod/Proscription.md). He has apparently sworn an oath, and though the wording of that oath is not common knowledge, it was deemed good enough for the Nine.

You can find out more about the upcoming symposium in the **[All laudable qualities](All_laudable_qualities.md)** wind of fortune.

### Full Circles

*[Image: OrderSevenfoldPath.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:OrderSevenfoldPath.jpg)*

- **Sevenfold Path; Synod**

In addition to fighting the [Cold Sun](../eternals/Cold_Sun.md) in the east, magicians of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md) have been making contacts with a community of [Jarmish](../foreign-nations/Principalities_of_Jarm.md) pilgrims called the *Convocation of Virtue*. The magicians of the Convocation seem friendly and enthused by the idea of discussing their faith with the visiting magicians. With a gift of [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md), with the backing of the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md), and against a backdrop of [political chaos](War_games.md), magicians of the Sevenfold Path have been able to get closer to these foreign pilgrims and a learn a little more about who they are.

You too can discover more about the Jarmish Convocation of Virtue, and opportunities to cultivate closer ties with them, in the **[Full circles](Full_circles.md)** wind of fortune.

### Propensity to Truck, Barter, and Exchange

- **Entrepreneurs and traders; practitioners of Ephisis' Scale; members of the Golden Pyramid conclave order**

[Heralds](../eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#heralds) from the [Sovereign Lady of the City of Gold and Lead](../eternals/Ephisis.md) have become regular attendants of the Hall of Worlds towards the end of each summit. They seek to make deals with Imperial citizens who have submitted their goods for potential sale via the [Ephisis' Scale](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Ephisis-_Scale.md) ritual. For the most part, trades have been largely successful and have brought Prosperity to both parties. Recently, though, the volatile nature of the markets has raised some concern among Imperial magicians. In response, the heralds related to the market have put together a comprehensive list of the kinds of things they are interested in buying - and selling. They're also trialling a new idea, in which merchants who are able to reach the Hall of Worlds can secure contracts to provide specific goods in bulk for known rewards, as well as discussing the matter of patronage for the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md) order in general.

You can learn about the guidance from the City of Gold and Lead, and about new systems being trialled, in the **[Propensity to truck, barter, and exchange](Propensity_to_truck-_barter-_and_exchange.md)** wind of fortune.

## Diplomatic Winds

Each season, the Civil Service prepares a summary of the Empire's relationship with the foreign nations, based on communications received from their ambassador, along with any useful intelligence gathered from other sources. While the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) and the other houses of Imperial power may respond to these offers, it is important to note that it is the appropriate [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) that the foreign power will expect to hear from. Their communication with the foreign power is the primary element that will determine the tenor of any response.

These winds of fortune deal with the Empire's diplomatic relationship with one or more foreign or barbarian nations. Usually, the wind is shaped by communications from the appropriate [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) or the [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md). While these winds revolve around international matters, their focus is still on the Empire and what its citizens can do to influence diplomatic matters, so they're as likely as any other wind to contain something of interest to characters from all walks of life.

It starts with a single, unremarkable ship leaving [Oran](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oran). Those moored at port to take on supplies quickly move to cast off and follow the ship; the single ship slowly gathering others in its wake. As it passes east, it gathers more; the ones at anchor watching to the north for a glimpse of how the Suranni have fared. Out through the Cazar Straits, careful to avoid catching the watchful eye of the Iron Confederacy. As the ships move south, the sails gather until it is an armada almost fifteen thousand strong. Then, almost as one, the sails crack in the wind and the ships pass into the waters of Arbonne.

Through [Arbonne](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#kalino-or-arbonne), some ships sail past the wrecks of the [Golden Fleet](../../mixed/titles/Golden_Fleet_Salvagemaster.md), then into [Shavronne](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#shavronne) and [Veroigne](../world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#veroigne) after it. As the armada sails, and occasionally grows as the last straggling ships arrive, the wary Grendel track the voyage closely. Eventually, though, the ships pass out of the Bay of Catazar and into the [Sea of Sighs](../world/The_seven_seas.md#sea-of-sighs) proper.

#### Sailing

The voyage takes weeks. Some crews resort to racing each other to mark the passage of time. Others are altogether focused on what awaits them, poring over the maps of the [Salt Flats of Sanath](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md) and trying to gauge how long they might have to enact this daring plan. Half the ships rest for a time at the [Towers of Kantor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#towers-of-kantor), others press on ahead further north and prepare at the docks of [Issyk](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#chambers-of-issyk). There, they take on the last of the supplies and pay for the emergencies that spring up on voyages before once again sailing north into the Salt Flats.

#### Fight

Some of these ships have been here before; the [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) in the Salt Flats was one of the first [commissioned](../senate-motions/Construct_Smuggler-s_Cove_in_Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md) by the Senate just over two years ago. Now though, it is not discovery and understanding that drives them. It is a drive to free those who have been chained by the hateful Druj. A chance to give freedom to those who have known no other life but toil under the thrall of the orcs of the Mallum. There is no hope for stealth with an armada this large. Instead, a burning brazier is lit on the prow of a ship to signal the advance, and the first boats touch the shores of [Leen Rarckan](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md#leen-rarckan).

It is a wretched place. Its wealth is evident, and if the goal of this voyage was to gain wealth, the myriad streets would present easy pickings. Instead, the corsairs and the kohan seek out the worst places, where those who have never been able to conceive of their own freedom languish in the filth passed down by their masters. Here they find lost Imperials, lost Axou, and those who have never had a true home. Whose families were born into this life - if it can be called that - for generations. These are the ones who take the most convincing; those who were once Imperials explain it quickly. The ills they have suffered, the horrors heaped upon them, have been blamed on the Empire.

It is perhaps lucky that the garrisons of [the Witness](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md#the-witness), the [Beacon](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md#beacon-of-iskrid), and the [Spires](../barbarians/Salt_Flats_of_Sanath.md#spires-of-rarckan) are occupied with [ensuring](../winds-of-war/Thorn_and_bone.md#an-old-story) that the Poison Crane and the Hidden Snake do not reenter the Salt Flats. The presence of the Freeborn can hardly be hidden, but the extra time does give them the chance to rescue thousands from their fate.

There is fighting, disparate and deadly, in the city. Crooked snickets and alleyways allow for the Druj who remained behind to launch ambush after ambush on the Freeborn. It is the sheer weight of numbers that allows the Imperials to escape with so many. Some unlucky few fall in the retreat, but far fewer than the Druj who would try to stop them.

#### Aftermath

The armada stops once again at Kantor and Issyk, this time to discharge those who were once Axou. Ships take on necessary supplies before casting off and returning again to the Empire. At this point, there is no need for an element of surprise - nor is there much chance of being caught unaware. The Grendel are busy, and enough Imperial ships are travelling together that even the fleeing children of wrecks keep a wide berth.

Eventually, the ships arrive back in western Bay of Catazar and begin to dock at Oran and [Calvos](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#calvos). Some go further afield, where their rescued former Imperials warrant it, to [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md), [Crown's Quay](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#longbeach), [Elos](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#elos), and [Apulian](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#apulian). Now, the work truly begins. Those who were once Imperial will find their way back to their nations or find another. Those who have never known anything else, though, will take time and patience to help.

Each one of them accepted the offer to flee the Druj, to be given the chance to claim their freedom. But if you have lived your whole life underground, the wide sky can be daunting.

### Road to Nowhere (The Barrens)

- **The roads of the Barrens opportunity could be refreshed at any time**
- **The civil service require guidance from the Dawnish Assembly on how to proceed**

Last season, the [Master of the Mint](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md), **Tristan Ramson von Holberg**, announced the [reappraisal](../senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#reappraise-roads-of-the-barrens) of roads in [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md). Originally [suggested](Road_out_of_nowhere.md#the-barrens) shortly before the Winter Solstice 385YE, the roads were part of a larger appraisal into the impact and opportunities to link the unconnected citizens of the southern, northern, and eastern territories of the Empire with new road networks.

When the Master of the Mint examines the proposal, it is clear that there are issues. To be effective, the roads need to fan out across the territory, but thus far the Dawnish have been somewhat reluctant to claim their prize. Mindful of the recent tensions, they have largely avoided settling the Barrens. Do the Dawnish now want to create roads *into* the Forest of Peytaht? They have the right, but would the Great Forest Orcs welcome them?

Do they want to create roads into the Untrod Groves and the Bleaks - regions that the notoriously secretive Karass claim as their own? The Karass loathe the presence of the Navarr wayhouse in "their" lands. Again, the Empire has the legal right to create these roads, but are the benefits worth the aggravation it may bring?

The Empire *don't* have the legal right to build the roads through Bitter Strand - they ceded the entire region to the Rahvin. It is part of the Barrens, so they could physically build there, but they would either need the permission of the Rahvin or find a way to force the issue. Is it worth the risk given the Rahvin are widely rumoured to be raising their own army?

The plans for the roads can be updated quickly and efficiently, but until the civil service know where the Dawnish want the roads to run it is impossible to complete them. What is clear is that the benefits provided will be significantly curtailed, if the only occupants of the Barrens they serve are the Dawnish. Even if the roads avoid just one of the three groups of inhabitants it will have an impact, reducing the benefits of the roads.

As it was the Dawnish Assembly that originally encouraged the Dawnish to refrain from settling the Barrens, they are looking to them for guidance now. They civil service will take notice of any statement of principle in the Assembly that references the Barrens and offers advice on how the appraisal should proceed. If multiple judgements are submitted which appear to conflict, they will pay most heed to the judgements that achieve the greatest majority.

Next season they will strive to present a finalised appraisal of the roads that takes account of the judgements of the Assembly. The work is in progress now, so it can wait if needed. If the Assembly pass a statement of principle calling for a delay until they have had chance to negotiate with the affected groups, then the plan will go on hold. It will then resume automatically once the Assembly provide all the guidance they want and indicate that the roads can now progress.

If the Assembly says nothing - or if the Master of the Mint uses their power to press the issue by raising this matter again this season, then the civil service will present plans for a set of roads that go straight through the Bleaks, the Bitter Strand and the Heart of Paytaht next season, but they advise that that is could generate considerable hostility and the Empire won't know about it until the roads are complete.

### A Sudden Threat (Reikos)

- **A band of Grendel are moving to assassinate Professor Gelberg and his team**
- **This skirmish is a [combat highly likely](../../out-of-character/rules/Accessibility.md#combat-highly-likely) encounter**
- ***Nekoda of the Shattered Tower* is [responsible](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#prognostication) for preventing the assassination**
- **This conjunction is [against barbarians](../world/Sentinel_Gate.md#imperial-law); magistrates advise that lethal force is fully justified**

Days before the Summer Solstice, alarming news reaches Anvil. A small party of [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md) [Wave Riders](../barbarians/Elite_Grendel_troops.md#the-wave-riders) have been spotted in southern Reikos, having apparently travelled undercover along almost the entire length of the Couros. Posing as Unshackled from [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md), they kept weapons and armour out of sight and stayed away from major population centres. Only at the last minute have they abandoned the pretence and taken up arms. If it were not for a Vigilant cataphract catching sight of them while out for a morning run, it is possible that they could have worked their will without the chance of intervention from Imperial heroes. Had the construction of the [Haros Riverpatrol](../senate-motions/Construct_Haros_Riverpatrol.md) been completed then they would doubtless have stopped this from happening, but it is too late for that now.

Fortunately, warning was given and the target quickly identified: Professor Gelberg and his party of researchers. While it is uncertain why the Grendel would wish to target a Commonwealth academic, he is the only target nearby. The professor and his team have fled into a derelict chapterhouse where they are laying low, but the Grendel are combing the countryside with speed and efficiency, and will find him soon if they are not stopped. Oddly, the raiding party seems to be accompanied by either a high-lineage [cambion](../../mixed/magic/lore/Cambion.md) or a herald of the Autumn realm, who is not visibly armed but seems to be playing some sort of advisory role.

If they are not stopped the Grendel will kill Professor Gelberg, damaging relations between the Empire and the Commonwealth. As the last [Patron of the Beurteilung](../../mixed/titles/Patron_of_the_Beurteilung.md), **Nekoda of the Shattered Tower** is responsible for organising Professor Gelberg's defence. A conjunction has been identified that would allow the heroes of the Empire to intercept the Grendel assassins and save Professor Gelberg's life.
