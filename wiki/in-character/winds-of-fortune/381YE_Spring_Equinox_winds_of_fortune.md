# 381YE Spring Equinox winds of fortune

*[Image: Map And Pieces.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Map_And_Pieces.jpg)*

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/381YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md).

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations, or political bodies, to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place and very little happens in isolation.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters 'should' know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months.

Many winds of fortune open with a short section of in-character text. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip. They're mostly just a bit of fun and they tend not to contain anything vital to the understanding of the wind of Fortune.

## Winds of Fortune

### A Spiritual Prism

- **Everyone, but especially the Imperial Synod, the Imperial Military Council, Dawn, and Highguard**

The [crisis](Matter_of_faith.md) that threatens to slowly tear the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) apart continues. The threat of a catastrophic schism within [the Way](../world/The_Way.md) deepens. Five nations have denounced Yael of Highguard and her Congregation of the First Empress; one has endorsed her; one has declared no interest in the matter; and three have kept their own counsel. The situation is increasingly volatile, and there are both [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to seize and prices to pay. You can learn more about the deepening crisis [here](A_spiritual_prism.md).

### Distant Deeps

- **Imperial Synod (especially the Freeborn and Prosperity assemblies); fleet captains; Imperial Senate**

Finally, after the better part of a year, the [Temple of Balo and the Black](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#the-temple-of-balo-and-the-black-bull) bull in [Oran](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oranseri) has opened its doors. The immediate impact has been an influx of merchants and sailors from the Asavean Archipelago, eager to receive the blasphemous blessings of their peculiar idols. The Imperial Senate has provided [material support](../senate-motions/Construct_Temple_of_the_Way_in_Nemoria.md) to build a similar temple, dedicated to [the Way](../world/The_Way.md) in far-distant [Nemoria](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#nemoria) ... but the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies) seems less than thrilled ... You can earn about these developments [here](Distant_deeps.md).

### Fearful Symmetry

- **Urizen, Military Council**

The New year brings with it storms to [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md)- and not just the [furious](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Foam_and_Spittle_of_the_Furious_Sea.md) storms that batter the coast. Powerful storms have struck [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md), raging as far inland as [Cargo](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md#cargo) in the north, accompanied by flooding and high winds and battering rain that seems guided by a malign intelligence. The mountains of Urizen rely on well maintained roads and bridges, and this sinister storm appears to have swept away several key avenues through the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md). You can learn about what implications this has for Redoubt, and for the movement of [armed forces](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) through the territory, [here](Fearful_symmetry.md).

### Forests of the Night

- **[Forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) owners, [Naga](../../mixed/magic/lore/Naga.md), Dreamers, people who like moths**

During the Winter solstice, the Coven of Umbral Reflection and the Spire of the Auric Horizon combined their powers to weave the powerful [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) known as the [Wondrous Forests of the Night](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Wondrous_Forests_of_the_Night.md). Working with consummate magical skill, the [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) magicians laid a delicate network of mystery and imagination across the entire Empire. As with the [Hallow of the Green World](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Hallow_of_the_Green_World.md) performed [last year](The_prime_colour_of_the_world.md), the ritual has had several significant magical effects beyond what might have been expected. You can learn all about them [here](Forests_of_the_night.md).

### Immortal Hand

- **Imperial Conclave**

The six [archmages](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) of the Empire have the power to send a formal message to a single [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) each Summit. During the Winter Solstice, five such [plenipotentiary](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) messages were sent - and over the last three months each of the eternals contacted has made a formal response. One of the five has also agreed to a formal [parley](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md#parley) with the Empire. You can learn about these responses [here](Immortal_hand.md).

### In the Tents of Prosperity

- **Various rich folk and traders including (but not limited to) those of Urizen, Highguard, Wintermark, and Navarr**

The [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) [private auction](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md#access-to-the-private-auction) is no more. Rather, individual citizens with interesting good to sell are encouraged to come to [Anvil](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#anvil) and try to get the best, most [Prosperous](../religion/Prosperity.md) deal they can by dealing face-to-face with the leaders of the Empire. Many of them do everything they can to make sure there is a little bit of buzz around their goods, to help ensure they make a good sale. You can learn about several such opportunities at the coming Summit [here](In_the_tents_of_prosperity.md).

### On Wings Aspire (Winds of Magic)

- **Technically everyone, but especially Imperial Magicians**

The Empire is an Empire of powerful magic, but not all powerful magic is of the Empire. The Winds of Magic collects a grab-bag of interesting or important effects and opportunities, both certain and rumoured, including tales of disruption in the Summer realm; a bounty offered by a [Day eternal](../eternals/Roshanwe.md); a glimmering citadel in the marshes of [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md); the details of the ongoing [darkest night](Darkest_night.md); and the [conjunction of stars](../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md) visible from those parts of the Empire that can still see the stars. You can learn more about these diverse effects [here](On_wings_aspire.md).

### The Price of Experience

- **Navarr, Imperial Orcs; Imperial Senate**

The [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of the Great Forest fled south from [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) with the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) at their heels. Invited to come to [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md) six months or so ago, they did just that - but the temporary situation where they squat in the [Lower Tarn Valley](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md#lower-tarn-valley) is proving unsustainable. With spring in bud upon the trees, the orcs of the Great Forest are bringing things to a head. You can learn about the developing situation in Therunin [here](Price_of_experience.md).

### Seize the Fire

- **Imperial Synod, Imperial Senate, Imperial Orcs**

During the [Symposium of Revelation](Symposium_of_Revelation.md), the Imperial Senate accepted two additional doctrines; the [Doctrine of the Howling Abyss](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md#the-doctrine-of-the-howling-abyss) and the [Doctrine of the Ancestors](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md#the-doctrine-of-the-ancestors). With this [change of doctrine](../../mixed/government/synod/Change_of_doctrine.md), the position of the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) has been cemented in [the Way](../world/The_Way.md) ...but there are still some questions to be answered, and not everyone is receptive to these changes. You can learn about this complex situation [here](Seize_the_fire.md).

### Sinews of the Heart (Trade Winds)

- **Fleet captains; [congregation](../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) leaders; ambassadors**

International trade, like international relations, can be fraught with pitfalls - and rich with opportunities. You can find a quick round up of things related to the current state of affairs, with an emphasis on trading, [here](Sinews_of_the_heart.md).

### Some to Misery are Born

- **Tassato, the Marches**

After the doughty folk of Whittle Hill in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) were forced to leave their land by the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md), they have been [exiled](City_folk_and_country_folk.md) to [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md). Welcomed by the Tassato Chamber of Commerce, they have purchased two streets and created a [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) enclave there. Unfortunately, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) is still very concerned about their involvement with [certain](../../mixed/magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#hatred) [blasphemous powers](../../mixed/government/synod/Religious_crime.md#blasphemy). You can learn about this season's chapter in the saga of the folk of Whittle [here](Some_to_misery_are_born.md).

### Spear of the Stars

- **Imperial Orcs, Varushka, Wintermark; the [Northern trade network](../nations/navarr/Northern_trade_network.md); the Senate and the Synod**

Dogri Thulebane has been removed and the northern passes out of [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) to [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md) are now open to the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md). They appear pleased, and continue to extend the slightly guarded hand of friendship to the Empire. There are [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) that might be explored, but as the détente between the Empire and Otkodov improves, there are ... obstacles ... that must be overcome. You can learn more about the diplomatic situation [here](Spear_of_the_stars.md).

### Watered with Fears

- **The Marches, Wintermark**

The recent [campaign season](../winds-of-war/381YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#let-that-be-your-last-battlefield-28mournwold29) in the [Mournwold](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) has been one of the bloodiest ever, thanks to certain [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) and the strategy of the [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md). Twenty-thousand dead warriors and soldiers and who knows quite how many citizens of the Mourn. Four groups have been particularly affected - [the Marchers](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) of Mournwold themselves; the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md), of course; the [Feni](../nations/the-marches/Feni.md) who live in the forest of [Alderly](../nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#alderly); and those folk in [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) who perhaps expected more. You can learn about these four groups, and how the carnage has affected them, [here](Watered_with_fears.md).

## Miscellaneous

### Détente in Sermersuaq

- **Wintermark**

The [raids](Song_of_the_lakefolk.md) by the odd hunters that have been going on for the last six months or so have petered out. It is not obvious if this is a temporary respite, or whether Savik Summerstorm has secured a permanent resolution.

### Krampushall

- **Wintermark, Imperial Orcs**

During the Winter Solstice, the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) gave permission for the [construction](../senate-motions/Construct_Hall_of_the_Krampus.md) of the [Krampushall](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#krampushall) - a residence for the supernatural creature known as the Krampus in [Pakaanan's Pass](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#pakanaan27s-pass) overlooking the [Clattering Gulley](../../mixed/government/military/Skarsind_spoils_of_war.md#the-clattering-gulley). After nearly a year of [very peculiar behaviour](Goatfoot.md), the beast is apparently well pleased with its new home and "*looking forward to meeting the neighbours*" - presumably it means the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). Given the creature is strongly associated with bad luck, this may well be something of a double-edged sword.
