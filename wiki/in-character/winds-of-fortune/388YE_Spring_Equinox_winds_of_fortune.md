Over the past three months, events have taken place which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. These are the winds of fortune, counterparts to the winds of war that detail the Empire's military campaigns. They lay out situations, and usually include things that characters can do to take advantage of [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or to resolve problems. In almost every case, they start with a piece of flavour text. This fiction helps introduce the wind of fortune, or draws out a particular theme or element. Often, it represents a viewpoint that one or more NPCs might have in-character. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours, the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip if they want to use it in character.

The body of the wind of fortune describes situations and lays out ways players can engage with them. Except where we explicitly talk about something being "rumoured" or use a phrase like "some might say..." the details of a wind of fortune are factual. They represent briefing material presented by the civil service or similar authoritative sources and represent something the average educated citizen would acknowledge as a reliable truth. Unless we specify otherwise, we're also being as thorough as possible. This information doesn't need further checking in-character. It's not an opinion, and we're not trying to trick you.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters could know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months. It's perfectly acceptable to turn up to Empire having read only a few Winds of Fortune that directly interest your character; part of the fun on the field can come from learning about situations and opportunities in other parts of the game.

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations or political bodies to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place, and very little happens in isolation. We are also adding the dates that they were last updated (other than typos and correcting red links) in an attempt to make it easier to keep track of the newest releases.

## Winds of Fortune

### Arranging Grace (Sodality)

- **The Throne; Imperial Senate; artists of all stripes; patrons of the arts; Azure Sutannir, Curator of Memories, Curator of the Blood Red River, Dean of the First Voice Glade, High Bard of the Empire, Voice of the Twin Roses**

[The Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md) has [instructed](../senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#appraise-the-cats-graces) the [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) to [appraise](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) the constitutional implications around the creation of the *Cat's Graces*. This potential [Imperial sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) would be concerned with the promulgation of the arts across the Empire and by all accounts has strong backing across [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md) despite a [motion](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) to [formalise](../senate-sessions/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#establish-the-cats-graces) them as an Imperial sodality narrowly failing to pass the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). Magistrate Abraham has reviewed the proposed manifesto of this new sodality, and delivers their findings first to [Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md) and then to the rest of the Empire.

You can read about the new sodality and its implications, as well as some possibilities related to its manifesto, in the **[Arranging grace](Arranging_grace.md)** wind of fortune.

### Push For Victory (Imperial Address)

- **Imperial citizens; Freeborn fleet owners; Generals; Senator for Holberg; Benefactors; Keeper of the Breadbasket; Urizen mana site owners; Emperor Vesna; People with an opinion on the Rahvin and/or the Karass**

[Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md) has used their power to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) to present a personal message to all Imperial citizens, readying the Empire for the final push to finally bring about the end of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), and drawing attention to the sterling efforts made across the ten nations.

The message has been distributed across the Empire by the civil service, and you can read it in the **[Push for victory](Push_for_victory.md)** wind of fortune.

### On the Road Ahead

- **The Brass Coast; especially the Freeborn National Assembly; and residents of Segura**

For the first time in many seasons, the lands of the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) are not *immediately* threatened. With a chance to draw breath, the inhabitants of the ancient town of [Anduz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md#anduz) are looking for new [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) for their home. The place is steeped in history; it was a city long before the Empire was created, before even the [Three Sisters](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_history.md#three-sisters-of-highguard) came to [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). That city was destroyed long ago, but in Anduz, the ruins of the past could lay the foundations of the future!

You can learn about plans for the future of Anduz, and for Segura, in the **[On the road ahead](On_the_road_ahead.md)** wind of fortune.

### Bricks and Boards (Challenge the Iron Duke)

- **Challengers of the Iron Duke**

Each season the eternal *[Barien](../eternals/Barien.md)* presents opportunities to those prepared to [Challenge the Iron Duke](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Challenge_the_Iron_Duke.md). For the Spring Equinox, he has offered two challenges: a tourney of strategy and a game of skill and dexterity. As ever, the rules of the ritual apply. Each person who receives the ritual will be *guaranteed* entry to a challenge. Depending on the challenge, they *may* be able to bring a certain number of attendants with them to assist. The exact number will depend on the nature of the challenge and the number of challengers. The [Civil Service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) will publish exact numbers in [the Hub](../world/Anvil.md#the-hub) on Saturday morning for all to see.

You can find details of the challenges issued by Barien this season in the **[Bricks and boards](Bricks_and_boards.md)** wind of fortune.

### The Matter Will Be Done

- **Dawn; *Emperor Vesna***

The [Earl of the Groves](Hard_to_find.md#the-earl-of-the-groves), the manticore that squats at the heart of the [Untrod Groves](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#the-untrod-groves) has issued a claim to be [Monarch of Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn_monarchy.md). [Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md), Crowned in Thunder, has responded with a proclamation to the manticore's challenge. The majority of Dawn rejects the idea of a manticore as the monarch of Dawn, but there are some houses who would like nothing better than to see the Earl of the Groves take his place on the Seat of the Sun. The time has come for the nobles of Dawn to take up arms and fight in a tourney to decide who, if anyone, should sit on the Rose Throne.

You can read about the groups who support and oppose the manticore's claim as Monarch of Dawn, and the ramifications of the tourney, in the **[The matter will be done](The_matter_will_be_done.md)** wind of fortune.

## Diplomatic Winds

Each season the Civil Service prepares a summary of the Empire's relationship with the foreign nations, based on communications received from their ambassador, along with any useful intelligence gathered from other sources. While the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) and the other houses of Imperial power may respond to these offers, it is important to note that it is the appropriate [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) that the foreign power will expect to hear from. Their communication with the foreign power is the primary element that will determine the tenor of any response.

These winds of fortune deal with the Empire's diplomatic relationship with one or more foreign or barbarian nations. Usually the wind is shaped by communications from the appropriate [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) or the [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md). While these winds revolve around international matters, their focus is still on the Empire and what its citizens can do to influence diplomatic matters, so they're as likely as any other wind to contain something of interest to characters from all walks of life.

Almost a year ago, the Queen of Ice and Darkness offered to lend her aid to the people of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) to defend the Barrens against attacks. The offer was just for the year, but the Immovable One left a route for Imperial magicians to create a lasting bargain for her aid. Dawnish magicians of the **Phoenix Rising** coven eagerly embraced the opportunity and have performed the necessary magic every season. As a result of their dedication, the region of [Hope's Rest](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#hopes-rest) has become [rimebound](../../mixed/economy/Region_qualities.md#rimebound). The weather has taken a distinct turn for the worse, with heavy storms increasingly common over ice-tipped mountains and deep drifts of snow lingering on the ground.

Servants of the Thane of the Rimebound Shore have been observed living in the ruins of Dourfell Keep. The Keep was destroyed by the Montanians to prevent it falling into the hands of the Druj, and is little more than ruins ever since. It's still ruined even now, but travellers who have sought shelter there from the tumultuous weather report receiving a warm welcome at the Thane's hearth, a fine white stone castle with a roaring fire and a feasting hall groaning under the weight of dishes.

This season there is an opportunity for any Imperial character with a resource in the Barrens to infuse it with a touch of Summer magic. Any character who scatters ice drawn from the waters of Hope's Rest across their property will see the ice take root. It will endure thereafter, waxing and waning with the seasons, but never completely melting no matter how hot summer gets. The resource will become *rimebound*, infused with a touch of the power of the Immovable One. Being rimebound doesn't currently have any effect; it doesn't change the output of the resource in any way, but it is immediately obvious to anyone who examines it. Accepting the gift is being widely seen as a gesture of gratitude, a way to thank the *Mistress of Blizzards* for her aid over the last year. (**OOC Note:** Any character who wishes their resource in the Barrens to become rimebound should email [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) after the forthcoming event; we will update the resource on the system).

### Where *is* Ravadi's Tomb?

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > We have the exemplar, now where is their tomb? This assembly authorises and applauds the creation of an inspirational site to memorialise the EXEMPLAR OF VIGILANCE, RAVADI FOUNDERS’ DANCE (consecrated to vigilance, of course).
> >
> > Martin Orchard, Vigilance Assembly, Autumn 387YE, Vote: 334-20

At the Winter Solstice, the Vigilance Assembly passed with greater majority a call to consecrate an inspirational tomb to recently recognised exemplar of [vigilance](../religion/Vigilance.md), [Ravadi Founders' Dance](../../mixed/government/synod/Known_exemplars.md#ravadi-founders-dance). In order to be a [legally binding writ](../../mixed/government/synod/Writ_of_consecration.md) however, the statement must include the chosen location. In order for the consecration to go ahead, the assembly must choose precisely where this site should be, or commission one via the senate if such a place does not yet exist. It is comparatively easy for an assembly to agree that one of their exemplars or paragons should be exalted with an appropriate site, but becomes much harder when priests must agree on where that is, or what part of their life it relates to. In the case of Ravadi, should it be a specific location in [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md) where they were born and raised, where the call to fight threats to the Empire was instilled in them? Should it be somewhere in [Hercynia](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md) where Ravadi was dedicated to Vigilance and first fought the vallorn, swearing a binding of thorns? Should it be the place where they died in [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md), where they fought the dry patricians as the assembly's champion? Should it be somewhere else entirely? This is the question the assembly needs to answer before an appropriate mandate or set of mandates can be proposed by the civil service. If the Assembly passes a statement with lesser majority this summit agreeing on a place, the civil service will produce one or more mandates as normal. If multiple pass, the one with greatest margin will be used.

### Opportunity Knocks Again

- **The civil service will raise an administrative motion to amend the Master of the Mint to give them the power to review a previous appraisal or opportunity**
- **This motion will be raised at the Friday evening session at the Spring Equinox**
- **The Senate could use a Senate motion to grant this power to a different Senate position**

Last season, the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) voted to [recruit all three prognosticators](../senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#hire-prognosticators) that had become [available to expand the Prognosticators Office](Forever_is_composed_of_nows.md#candidates). As such, the [Speaker for the Senate](../../mixed/titles/Speaker_for_the_Senate.md) will raise an administrative motion to amend the [Master of the Imperial Mint](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md) to give them the power to make an announcement to instruct the civil service to review a previous opportunity that is no longer available because too much time has passed or because circumstances have changed.

The [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md#constitutional-court) have confirmed that the power could instead be given to any [Member of the Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_session.md#member-of-the-senate), including [the Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md), [Conscience of the Senate](../../mixed/titles/Conscience_of_the_Senate.md), [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md), [Advisor on Orc Affairs](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_Orc_Affairs.md), or [Imperial Consul](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Consul.md). However, this would require a [Senate motion](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) to name the title whose powers they wish to amend. Alternatively, rather than delegating the power, the Senate could retain the power itself. This would allow any [senator](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) to raise a [Senate motion](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) to direct the [Civil Service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) accordingly.

Regardless of how the power is employed, it is not possible to review more than one expired opportunity each season at this time.
