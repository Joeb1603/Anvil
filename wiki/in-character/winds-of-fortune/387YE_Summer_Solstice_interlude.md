# 387YE Summer Solstice interlude

*[Image: Throne in the Woods.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Throne_in_the_Woods.jpg)*

## Overview

Downtime following the Spring Equinox will soon be open, allowing players to process their character's downtime choices. Not all resources allow a player to make a choice about how they operate between events, but we've provided information about new opportunities for [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) and [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) owners in particular as well as a reminder about existing opportunities created at earlier events.

On this page we'll be outlining, and offering some context for, various effects and events. As always, these include quick summaries, mostly out of character, to explain effects such as large-scale [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) and [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md). In some cases, there may be further developments around these events in the Summer Solstice winds of fortune, or they may be handled with a *Resolution* entry on the appropriate Wind.

**The deadline for Downtime submissions is Midnight on Thursday the 26th of June.**

## Choices

- **During the Spring Equinox, there were a number of Senate motions, Conclave declarations, Synod judgements, and Military council orders**

Each summit the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) raises and votes on [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md), the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) debates [declarations](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md), the assemblies of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) discuss [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md), and the [generals](../../mixed/titles/General.md) of the [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md) issue [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) to their [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). You can find details of these many decisions here:

- [Senate sessions](../senate-sessions/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md)
- [Synod judgements](../synod-judgements/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md)
- [Conclave sessions](../conclave-sessions/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Conclave_sessions.md)
- [Military Council orders](../military-council-orders/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Military_Council_orders.md)

The Civil Service is responsible for ensuring that these motions, judgements, declarations, and orders are public knowledge. Not everything is widely distributed across the Empire, but any character who attends [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md) can easily find out details of what went on in the houses of power during the event.

## Imperial Guerdon

The Imperial Guerdon is a bursary set up by the Imperial Senate primarily to reward military units that support Imperial armies, spy networks, and fortifications. The value of the Imperial Guerdon is divided between each military unit and fleet that supports an army in proportion to their effective strength. The [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has the power to exclude any or all Imperial armies and navies, fortifications, or special projects (such as spy networks) from the Guerdon. If they do so, then a character who supports such an army, fortification, or special project will receive nothing for doing so.

This season, the value of the Guerdon is 150 thrones.

This season, the [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) has announced that shares of the Guerdon will be shared between military units supporting the following armies:

- [Autumn Hammers](../../mixed/government/military/Autumn_Hammers.md)
- [Northern Eagle](../../mixed/government/military/Northern_Eagle.md)

No other armies, or actions by military units or fleets, will receive shares of the Guerdon.

## Military Units

- **Players who control military units can choose from a number of downtime options**
- **Not every military unit is eligible to participate in every venture**
- **Left to their own devices, a military unit defaults to the *mercenary work* option**

Any player whose character controls a [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) can choose from several downtime options by logging into the downtime system. They may choose to [support an army](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning), [defend a fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#guarding), or [support a spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#scouting). They can also choose one of the many [ventures](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md). Left to their own devices, a military unit defaults to [mercenary work](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#mercenary-work), guaranteeing an income of money along with a random chance of herbs, more money, mana crystals, ingots or measures.

Players who want to support one of the [Imperial armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) can also read the [orders](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md) issued by each [general](../../mixed/titles/General.md) before making their decision. These orders are a matter of public record within the Empire and also available on the **[387YE Spring Equinox Military Council orders](../military-council-orders/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Military_Council_orders.md)** page. This new page makes it easier for players to read all the orders before making a decision; previously the only way to do this was to painstakingly click every army in turn. It's also possible for players without military units to see the orders, without waiting for the Winds of War (assuming the orders related to an area that produced a full write-up).

As always, the guidelines around discussing military matters online can be found [here](../../out-of-character/rules/Online.md#you-may-not-post-military-updates).

### Escort the Drovers

- **Marcher and Imperial Orc military units can take a guarding action to protect the herd of cattle being driven to Mareave**
- **If at least one thousand strength is assigned to the action then the herd will arrive in Highguard next season**
- **Military units that take this action will receive no production**

A grand total of two hundred and seventy-two cattle bonds were gathered for the [Southern Herd](High_hopes.md#the-southern-herd) in Mareave. Now, the difficult task of escorting them across hundreds of miles begins. If at least one thousand strength supports the action, then the first leg of the journey will be completed, and the cattle arrive in Highguard shortly before the Summer Solstice 387YE. For more information see the **[Million miles of water](Million_miles_of_water.md#a-long-drive)** wind of fortune

### Golden Glory

- **A military unit that supports one of the armies that started the season in Astolat and has been enchanted with Knights of Glory will receive an additional reward**
- **Supporting the Eastern Sky, Gryphon's Pride, Granite Pillar, or the Northern Eagle will gain a piece of Summer vis, in addition to any other rewards**

Any military unit that supports an army from [Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md) enchanted with [Knights of Glory](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Knights_of_Glory.md), will gain a boon of a single [golden apple](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#golden-apple) that Eleonaris hopes will help encourage the captain to seek an enchantment for themselves or their soldiers. For more information see the **[Golden magicians fayre](Golden_magicians_fayre.md#golden-glory)** wind of fortune

## Fleets

- **Players who control fleets can choose from several downtime options**
- **Left to their own devices, a fleet defaults to the *maritime escort* option**

Any player whose character controls a [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) can choose from several downtime options by logging into the downtime system. They may choose to [trade](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#trading) with a [foreign port](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md), engage in [raiding](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#raiding) barbarians, [quest](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#questing) in search of glory or renown, [scout](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#scouting) either to support a [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) or as part of another stealthy option, or commit to [guarding](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#guarding) a convoy of other ships.

Left to their own devices, a fleet defaults to [maritime escort](../../mixed/economy/Voyage.md#maritime-escort), guaranteeing an income of money along with a random chance of herbs, more money, mana crystals, ingots or measures.

The Empire does not currently have any [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md), and there are currently no campaigning actions available, so it is not possible to take [campaigning](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#campaigning) action, but if it were, then details would be included on the Military orders page.

### Assault the Kongegőr

- **Fleets in the Marches and Wintermark can support the Red Wind Corsairs in launching on the Gullet**
- **The action is only available in a season when the Red Wind Corsairs have taken the *Take to the Sea* order in a territory adjacent to the Gullet**

During the Spring Equinox the Marcher and Wintermark Assemblies supported judgements raised by **Fredegar Bloodmouth** and **Hadrick Hardin** respectively to encourage fleet owners to put their ships to work supporting the Red Wind Corsairs. The mandates enacted, fleets from both nations are now able to take the **Assault the Kongegőr** action when the [Red Wind Corsairs](../../mixed/government/military/Red_Wind_Corsairs.md) take the *Take to the Sea* order whilst in a territory adjacent to the Gullet. If five thousand strength in fleets take the action that season, then the Red Wind Corsairs will successfully become a navy at the start of the following season.

More information on the **[A bridge too far](A_bridge_too_far.md#brewing-up-a-storm)** wind of fortune.

**OOC Note:** We have decided to only have this action available in the season where it has a chance of having an effect. Fleet owners in Wintermark and the Marches who want to roleplay having raided the Jotun along the Gullet are encouraged to take one of the [Escort the Fisherfolk of Westerhal](../../mixed/economy/Voyage.md#escort-the-fisherfolk-of-westerhal), the [Raid Kalsea's Coastline](../../mixed/economy/Voyage.md#raid-kalseas-coastline), or the [Privateering](../../mixed/economy/Voyage.md#privateering) actions.

### Trade Summary

- **Imperial fleets can trade with ports in Axos, the Commonwealth, Faraden, the Iron Confederacy, the Sarcophan Delves, and the Sumaah Republic**
- **Production depends on the port chosen, and can be increased by upgrades and enchantments**

Imperial fleets can [trade](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#trading) with a number of foreign nations who have chosen to open some of their ports to visitors from the Empire. The assumption is that a ship trading with a foreign nation makes enough from selling their cargo to cover their running costs, crew salaries and the like, but that there is enough profit to provide the character who owns the fleet with valuable goods of interest to the folk who attend [Anvil](../world/Anvil.md). What goods are received for visiting a port is changeable, and heavily influenced by Imperial diplomacy.

- **Axos:** The port of the **[Towers of Kantor](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#towers-of-kantor)** welcomes Imperial fleets, and its production has been altered. You can find out more about the change made in the **[What shall we do now?](What_shall_we_do_now.md#luxurious-trade)** wind of fortune
- **Commonwealth:** The ports of **[Leerdam](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#leerdam)** and **[Volkavaar](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#volkavaar)** welcome Imperial traders and their production has been significantly altered. You can find out more about the new items on the **[According to principle](According_to_principle.md#new-trade)** wind of fortune.
- **Faraden:** The port of **[Caitun](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#caitun)** welcomes Imperial fleets. Fleets from the Marches and Wintermark benefit from a 1 rank bonus due to close proximity. The production of Caitun has shifted to no longer produce ingots of weltsilver. Instead, a starting fleet will produce 54 rings. You can find out more about the situation on the **[A pillow of winds](A_pillow_of_winds.md#faraden-luxuries)** wind of fortune.
- **Iron Confederacy:** The port of **[Robec](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#robec)** is available for Imperial fleets.
- **Sarcophan Delves:** The ports of **[Sarcophan](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#sarcophan)** and **[Betovering](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#betovering)** welcome Imperial traders.
- **Sumaah Republic:** The port of **[Zemeh](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md#zemeh)** welcomes Imperial traders, and its production has been significantly altered. You can find out more about the changes on the **[Friend of mine](Friend_of_mine.md#inspire-others)** wind of fortune.

## Arcane Projections

- **The deadline for submitting an arcane projection has changed**
- **Going forward arcane projections must be submitted before the close of downtime**
- **It is not possible to submit arcane projections that create personal curses**

The deadline for submitting an [arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) has changed from the late booking deadline, to the close of downtime. This is a significantly shorter window than previously. We've made this change to improve our ability to process arcane projections - an earlier deadline means that the game team have more time to consider each arcane projection, which will allow us to be more detailed and consistent in our responses.

As detailed in the **[Patterns of fairytales](Patterns_of_fairytales.md#wyrm-and-mountain)** wind of fortune, it is not currently possible to create an arcane projection that involves a [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) directed at a character.

### Colleges and Forges

- **We've changed the email address for college of magic and runeforge submissions**

It is important for characters responsible for a [college of magic](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md) or [runeforge](../../mixed/commissions/Runeforge.md) to remember that the deadline for submitting rituals for codification, or instructions to the runeforge, is also the point that downtime closes. You *must* use let us know how you plan to use your college or forge by this point, otherwise it will not be possible to submit anything and the season will be lost.

We've also made a small change there; the e-mail address to which such instructions should be sent is now [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) rather than rules. The rules decisions about an arcane projection that is being codified, or a new item, have already been made and it is plot that deals with the creation of ritual texts, unique items, or schema.

### Rivers Run Clear (Mournwold)

- **Rivers of Life has been cast on the Mournwold**

As well as the typical effect of reducing losses to any [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) fighting in the territory, the magic has other more narrative effects. ### A Grand Penumbral Veil (Everyone)

*[Image: The Tower of Shadow and Light.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:The_Tower_of_Shadow_and_Light.png)*

- **The Penumbral Veil has been drawn across most of the Empire**

The potent shroud laid across most of the Empire during the Autumn Equinox remains. The [Drawing the Penumbral Veil](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Drawing_the_Penumbral_Veil.md) ritual creates a potent shroud of [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) that conceals people, places, and events in a territory from the prying eyes of scryers and seers.

The penumbral veil has been drawn over half the Empire. All five [League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) cities ([Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md), [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md), [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md), and [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md)). [The Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) territory of [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md), and most of [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) ([Astolat](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md), [Weirwater](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md), and [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md)). Half of [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) ([Bastion](../nations/highguard/Bastion.md), and [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md)). Both [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) territories ([Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md) and [Hercynia](../nations/navarr/Hercynia.md)). Two territories of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) ([Hahnmark](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) and [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md)). And all of [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) bar [Karov](../nations/varushka/Karov.md) ([Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md), [Miekarova](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md), [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md), and [Volodmartz](../nations/varushka/Volodmartz.md)).

The shrouds do not appear to be particularly difficult to pierce, but the effect is noticeable in every territory once the sun sets and the skies over the Empire appear strange and unfamiliar. The effects are relatively low-key, as the shrouds have not been performed at particularly high magnitude, but occasionally they can be dramatic and exciting. Effects include stars not being where they are expected to be; the moon seeming closer, further away, strangely coloured, absent, or in the wrong phase; odd prismatic lights glittering in or moving across the celestial vault. Very occasionally, even stranger effects might be seen such as vast coloured banks of sparkling light, strange translucent birds and fish of immense size that swoop or skim lazily through the clouds, and similar phantasmagoric images of all kinds.

Obviously, it is beyond the special-effects budget of most [sanctioned events](../../out-of-character/rules/Sanctioned_events.md) to affect the appearance of the night sky, but anyone attending such events should bear the effects in mind. Most of the time being aware that the moon is in the wrong phase, or some of the stars are in odd places, will be sufficient to bring this effect to life. The ritual may sometimes cause consternation in people living in affected territories; any details in this regard will be included in the winds of fortune.

### Lights in the Sky (Feroz)

- **A colourful display is in effect in the night sky of Feroz**
- **It is clear to anyone in the territory that this is the result of Summer magic**

A bag bound in chains, set within a cage formed of magnificent light, emerges from what appears to be a hole in the sky. Two figures follow; the first an Urizen changeling, clad in a robe of falling stars. In his hand, he holds a gilded staff topped with a blue crystal. Soon after, a Navarr man follows, adorned in moonlight. The two figures share a glance before they seem to survey the land beneath them. Linking hands, they produce a gilded key, and they walk towards the locked bag. As they unlock the bag, it explodes into a starburst of multiple colours, which settle into the forms of creatures dancing in the sky. After a few minutes, there is a flash of light, and the apparition repeats itself anew.

Any further effects of this ritual will be in the winds of fortune released before the next event.
