Over the past three months, events have taken place which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. These are the winds of fortune, counterparts to the winds of war that detail the Empire's military campaigns. They lay out situations and usually include things that characters can do to take advantage of [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or to resolve problems. In almost every case, they start with a piece of flavour text. This fiction helps introduce the wind of fortune or draws out a particular theme or element. Often, it represents a viewpoint that one or more NPCs might have in-character. In all cases, these pieces are intended to help create an atmosphere and provide a little entertainment. Where they contain opinions, those are the opinions of the fictional people depicted - and where they contain information or rumours, the assumption is that the reader will create their own context for that information or gossip if they want to use it in character.

The body of the wind of fortune describes situations and lays out ways players can engage with them. Except where we explicitly talk about something being "rumoured" or use a phrase like "some might say..." the details of a wind of fortune are factual. They represent briefing material presented by the civil service or similar authoritative sources and represent something the average educated citizen would acknowledge as a reliable truth. Unless we specify otherwise, we're also being as thorough as possible. This information doesn't need further checking in-character. It's not an opinion, and we're not trying to trick you.

As always, how much or how little of this information you choose to know in character is up to you. Part of the purpose of Winds of Fortune is to maintain the illusion of the Empire as a living, breathing place where things happen - and to make players aware of things their characters could know based on what their roleplaying says they have been doing for the past three months. It's perfectly acceptable to turn up to Empire having read only a few Winds of Fortune that directly interest your character; part of the fun on the field can come from learning about situations and opportunities in other parts of the game.

In each case, we've tried to tag the Winds of Fortune entry with the nations or political bodies to which it is most relevant. We've done this to help people who are interested only in events that are especially relevant to them. In no way are these tags intended to be exhaustive; the Empire is a complex place, and very little happens in isolation. We are also adding the dates that they were last updated (other than typos and correcting red links) in an attempt to make it easier to keep track of the newest releases.

## Winds of Fortune

### The Wolves Tempt You (Imperial Address)

- **Imperial citizens; Navarr;**

[Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md) has used their power to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) to present a personal reminder to all citizens that the road to salvation is hard, but everyone is walking it together.

The message has been distributed across the Empire by the civil service, and you can read it in the **[The wolves tempt you](The_wolves_tempt_you.md)** wind of fortune.

### Dream On

- **Navarr**

In [Miaren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md), [Seren](../nations/navarr/Miaren.md#seren) is now become the *City of Dreams* in truth. People with hopes and aspirations, with the imagination to perceive what is needed to achieve them and the determination to try, have flocked to Miaren. With the completion of the Temple of the Waking Dream, the [dreamspeaker](../nations/navarr/Dreamspeaker.md) calling has coalesced as a distinct [archetype](../../out-of-character/rules/Archetype.md) among the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) people, one dedicated to making dreams become reality. This has perhaps predictably amplified arguments about what exactly that means, and which dreams should be pursued, and how.

You can learn about competing arguments, and the changes to the city of Seren, in the **[Dream on](Dream_on.md)** wind of fortune.

### Come Together

*[Image: NBNavarr.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBNavarr.jpg)*

*[Image: Apothecaries Guild.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Apothecaries_Guild.png)*

- **Imperial Apothecaries Guild; Navarr; Benefactor of High Chalcis; Highguard, the League, Imperial Orcs, and Varushka; Senate**

When [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md) fell to the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) [Thornsong House](../../mixed/titles/Thornsinger.md#thornsong-house) was lost. Despite the valiant efforts of the Navarr and the aid of [Lord Rain](../eternals/Ossegrahn.md), the college of herb mastery could not be saved. While the house itself burned, many of those who studied there were able to escape to the safety of [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md). Some stayed behind on [Chalcis Mount](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md#chalcis-mount), but others chose to put as much distance between themselves and the horrors of the Druj as possible. Drawn to the Temple of the Waking Dream, some of these apothecaries and physicks have taken the path of the dreamspeaker themselves, and inspired by the City of Dreams, they have put their ambition to rebuild what was lost into words.

A proposal for a new college of the apothecary arts, and ways various nations may be involved, can be found in the **[Come together](Come_together.md)** wind of fortune.

### Fair Share of Labour

- **Artisan's Guild; Throne; Senate; Artisans of all kinds; Golden Pyramid; Pride Assembly; Navarr; Guildmaster of the Artisans, Dean of Runesmith's Caution, Keeper of the Runegrott, Seer of the Spiral Sword, and potentially several other titles associated with magic item crafting**

At the recent summit, [Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md), requested that the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md#constitutional-court) [appraise the implications](Forever_is_composed_of_nows.md#the-abraham-gambit) of a new [Imperial sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md), the *Imperial Guild of Artisans*. The Artisans Guild has technically been in existence for some time, with the Senate [ordering the construction of Guildhouse](../senate-motions/Construct_Guildhouse_of_the_Artisans.md) in 385YE, creating the position of [Guildmaster of the Artisans](../../mixed/titles/Guildmaster_of_the_Artisans.md). Now the Senate is in a position to consider the full implications of making the Guild into an official Imperial sodality.

You can learn more about the potential Imperial Sodality, as well as proposals from some people very excited by the opportunity it represents, in the **[Fair share of labour](Fair_share_of_labour.md)** wind of fortune.

### Hidden Inventory

*[Image: Cat's Graces Sodality.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Cat%27s_Graces_Sodality.png)*

*[Image: NBWintermark.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBWintermark.jpg)*

- **Wintermark, especially banner bearers; Cat's Graces; Minister of Historical Research; Mediator of Hyljehal; Senate**

When the tides of fate seem to turn against the Empire, to be able to look and see over the battle lines, the colours of your warband or your nation still [Proudly](../religion/Pride.md) flying can help to rally the [Courage](../religion/Courage.md) and turn the tide. In [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) the [position of banner-bearer](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#banner-bearers) is seen as vital to the success of any battle. [Empress Britta](../history/Empress_Britta.md) was a banner-bearer, and bore [Foe-Slayer](../../mixed/magic/items/Three_Tears.md), the legendary war-banner of [Emperor Guntherm](../history/Emperor_Guntherm.md) onto the field. The great flag was lost when Britta died and it has been near two decades since Wintermark could bear its equal into battle. The artisans and craftspeople of [Runegrott](../nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#runegrott) have a proposal to try to remedy this shortfall, involving the construction of a grand [edifice](../../mixed/commissions/Edifice.md) to house banners and paintings of the great banners of the Empire as well as the creation of a new [artefact](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#artefacts) [banner](../../indexes/by-category/Magic_Standards.md) for Wintermark.

You can read more about this plan, and learn a little of the mighty banner Foe-Slayer, in the **[Hidden inventory](Hidden_inventory.md)** wind of fortune.

### A Fork in the Path

- **Wintermark, especially the Mystics; Raven Seer of Ishal; would-be champions; Keeper of Oaths; friends of Sung**

The [Mystics](../nations/wintermark/Mystic.md) of Ishal sought to work more closely with the wider nation of Wintermark. They have since decided [to approach the Witan](Flight_of_birds.md#the-witan) directly, and do what they can to support the goals of the nation itself. The decision to help with the [exploration of Tsirku](Warriors-_Gate.md) has made it impossible for the Mystics of Ishal to offer their usual council and support to the [Raven Seer](../../mixed/titles/Raven_Seer.md) and to that end they have made several changes to the Aviary, and to the support they offer the Raven Seer and the nation. At the same time, there has been a great deal of discussion of the [Crown of Three Tears](../nations/wintermark/Crown_of_Three_Tears.md) and the role of the *Champion of Wintermark* within the nation - the appointment of who has traditionally been the responsibility of the mystics.

You can read about the changes at Ishal, and the situation of the Crown of Three Tears, in the **[A fork in the path](A_fork_in_the_path.md)** wind of fortune.

### Outreach Across the Marshes

*[Image: Assembly League.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_League.png)*

- **The League, especially the Bishops and Princes; Merchant Prince of Misericorde Market; Senate**

[The League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) Assembly [has declared](../synod-judgements/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-57) that their [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md#the-highest-calling) is to bring new peoples to join the Empire. A Virtuous endeavour to be sure, but even the League cannot move the whole world at once. To be effective the nation needs to [focus their efforts](Any_dream_will_do.md#partners-within) on one group at a time. Diplomacy is a complicated matter, so they also need to decide who among them would be best to take the lead with each such group. This past season the Princes of the League have reached out to the [Sand Fishers](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#misericorde) of [Misericorde](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#misericorde), the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) who once lived on the southern shores of [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) as slaves of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) but who have found a new lease of life on the northern shores of [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md).

Ultimately, the egregore decides who can or cannot join a nation - the nature of the bond is that it can only be formed with someone an egregores recognises as having the potential to be a member of the nation. But the League has already embraced the Apulian Orcs, why not the Holberg Orcs as well?

You can read about how the outreach is going, and what it might take to bring the Sand Fishers into the Empire, in the **[Outreach across the marshes](Outreach_across_the_marshes.md)** wind of fortune.

### Stand Proud, You're Strong

*[Image: Assembly Freeborn.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Freeborn.png)*

- **The Brass Coast, especially the Freeborn assembly;**

After several seasons of deliberation (and funding a [whole new city](../senate-motions/Construct_City_of_Wonders.md)), the Freeborn National Assembly has decided that the [highest calling](The_highest_calling.md) of their people should be on defending their lands. The sentiment resonates with people across [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). It was not long ago that both [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) and [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) fell under barbarian control, [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) has faced constant invasion from the Lasambrians over the past few years and [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md) has only recently enjoyed a prolonged period of quiet after nearly two decades of constant warfare. Is it a surprise that the people of the Brass coast feel a little vulnerable, given recent history?

There is so much work that could be done, people are at a loss to know where to start. The Assembly will have to consider the options carefully - they have a year to take advantage of the nation's highest calling, but that won't be enough time to pursue every possibility raised by a people as inventive as the Freeborn...

You can find out how the nation can begin to defend its lands, and the proposals put forward toward that aim, in the **[Stand proud, you're strong](Stand_proud-_you-re_strong.md)** wind of fortune.

### Arrows and Clay (Challenge)

- **Challengers of the Iron Duke**

Each season, the eternal *[Barien](../eternals/Barien.md)* presents opportunities to those prepared to [Challenge the Iron Duke](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Challenge_the_Iron_Duke.md). For the Autumn Equinox he offers two challenges: a game of speed and grace, and a game of creativity and imagination. As ever, the rules of the ritual apply. Each person who receives the ritual will be *guaranteed* entry to a challenge. Depending on the challenge, they *may* be able to bring a certain number of attendants with them to assist. The exact number will depend on the nature of the challenge and the number of challengers. The [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) will publish exact numbers in [the Hub](../world/Anvil.md#the-hub) on Saturday morning for all to see.

You can find details of the challenges issued by Barien this season in the **[Arrows and clay](Arrows_and_clay.md)** wind of fortune.

## Diplomatic Winds

Each season, the Civil Service prepares a summary of the Empire's relationship with the foreign nations. This is usually based on communications received from their ambassador, along with any useful intelligence gathered from other sources. While the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) and the other houses of Imperial power may respond to these offers, it is important to note that it is the appropriate [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) that the foreign power will expect to hear from. Their communication with the foreign power is the primary element that will determine the tenor of any response. While these winds revolve around international matters, their focus is still on the Empire and what its citizens can do to influence diplomatic matters, so they're as likely as any other wind to contain something of interest to characters from all walks of life.

The city itself and the neighbouring region of [Sinfoyard](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#sinfoyard) are recognised as a neutral state by the Senate; a free port where both merchants of all nations are welcome. They actively seek investment from both Imperial and Grendel sources, and have allied themselves with the powerful [Sarcophan Delves](../foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md). One opportunity that has been under discussion for a while now is the creation of a temple dedicated to the Way, a monument to cooperation welcomed by the former Grendel. After the Summer Solstice it appears that some movement has been made towards making intention into reality.

You can learn about diplomatic relations with Beoraidh, and the opportunity to build a temple there, in the **[All I can do](All_I_can_do.md)** wind of fortune.

## Other Events

### Rumour Control

- **Rumours of heads on pikes in the Upwold have been investigated by the magistrates**
- **The magistrates have found no evidence to suggest that this has happened**

The Empire is wide and complicated. It's easy for a rumour to get out of control and become potentially damaging misinformation. If a misunderstanding spreads far and wide enough, there is a chance that it might provoke mistakes at the highest level. To avoid that, the civil service steps in to correct these misconceptions when they become aware of them.

Since the Winter Solstice, there has been a rumour circulating that caught the eyes of officials when it passed into [judgement](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md) in the Synod. A claim that an Imperial citizen had [put heads on spikes](../synod-judgements/388YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-12) is the sort of claim that warrants investigation. The magistrates duly do their duty, in part because if such a thing had happened, then it could easily be evidence of a crime.

They are pleased to report that it has *not*. Whatever else **Brother Orchard** may or may not be guilty of, one thing he definitely has not done is put heads on spikes along the road - of a consecrated tomb or anywhere else, for that matter. There have been no public displays of such, and such an obvious display of barbarism would have been known about and discussed throughout the territory.

The points discussed during the inquisition remain between those present.

**OOC Note:** We don't feel step in to correct misconceptions or misinformation very often, and we make a concerted effort to try to do so only when it would be obvious to people in the world that something is incorrect. Here, the putting of heads on spikes is something that would be widely known about and reported across the territory and beyond. We want to correct this detail, so we can leave players free to argue about everything else that may or may not have happened in Empire.

We want to be clear that the things discussed during the inquisition still happened, just that this particular point - that heads were left on pikes in Upwold - did not occur.

### Strange Currencies

- **The Silver Flame ask for ritual texts for *Strong Ox, Golden Sun* and *Gathering the Harvest***
- **In return, they pledge their sentinels to defend Spiral as needed**

The [Tribunal of the Silver Flame](A_question_of_loyalty.md#spiral) controls the region of [Cinion](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#cinion), in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md) - and is home to several hundred former Urizeni mages at most. Nearly a [year ago](Mirror_and_the_flame.md), they announced grand plans to make a formal alliance with the [eternal](../eternals/Eternal.md) [Sinokenon](../eternals/Sinokenon.md), declaring their intent to build a [fane](../eternals/Fane.md) in their territory so as to allow the eternal to give them more extensive support. Aware that the risks would be shared by the Empire, they wrote to inform them of their intentions, and tried to sweeten the deal with two powerful rituals in exchange for access to Imperial Lore, assuming that the offer would land favourably.

It did not. The [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) rejected the offer outright, seemingly incensed at the repeated request for access to Lore and having deep misgivings about the nature of *Diamondmind's* offer. The Flame sent a delegation to Anvil to try and understand why their strategy had landed so badly. Eager to demonstrate their worth to their neighbours, they sent their sentinels to aid in the [quixotic but ultimately successful effort to free Apulian](This_is_the_end.md#never-follow-me) from the [Whisper Gallery's](../eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md) grasp. Last season, the Conclave agreed to disseminate [Blessing of New Spring](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Blessing_of_New_Spring.md) to them - a sign that there is diplomatic willing there, yet.

Now the [civil service](../../mixed/government/senate/Civil_service.md) have been sent word from Alexius, who acts as the representative of the Tribunal to the Empire. His missive strikes a somewhat contrite note. The Tribunal have abandoned their plans to build a fane for Sinokenon - for now. It seems that someone made a generous donation of liao to the faction within the Tribunal who were most opposed towards any form of eternal alliance, and they have spent the last three months pressing their point. They have convinced many, but in particular they have convinced Caela, the Grand Arbiter of the Tribunal, who has asked the heralds of Sinokenon to leave.

This has not come without cost, it seems. While they have the stocks and stores to get through winter, it will be hard without outside assistance. Their need is pressing, and so they write to ask if their friends in Anvil could see to the dissemination of the other two rituals in the cycle of farm rituals. This would aid the rebels greatly - especially if this happens in time for the rituals to be cast in the Summer and Autumn of next year. Beyond that, the Flame would benefit from any personal enchantments, especially ones like [Gift of the Wily Broker](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Gift_of_the_Wily_Broker.md) which allow for careful economic stewardship. In return, the Tribunal are prepared to guarantee that their sentinels will continue to support efforts in Apulian or be sent to assist in Spiral's defence in any way they can - whether it is simply to act as swords against the darkness of the wilds or to defend some fortification. They have four groups of sentinels with a combined military strength of a little under five hundred.

### All the Right Friends

*[Image: Assembly Prosperity.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Prosperity.png)*

- **Efforts to share the virtue of Prosperity with the people of Tsark have met with some success.**
- **There is an opportunity to bring word of inspirational figures of Prosperity to Tsark.**
- **Eleven Imperials have been invited at 18:35 on the Saturday of the equinox to visit the Tsarkoi in Zenith.**

The isolated nation of [Tsark](../foreign-nations/Tsark.md) is famously slow to change and resistant to outside influence or visitors of any kind. In the last decade, as the Empire has become aware of Tsark's existence yet remained neutral, even friendly towards the tiny sagocracy, there has been a slow but perceptible thawing of relations on behalf of the Sage's Council. [Last season](Seeds_of_discord.md#tsark) they sent *Camilla*, one of the adepts who play some kind of role in day-to-day spiritual guidance of the lives of the Tsarkoi, to visit the Empire to learn more about the virtue of [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md). It seems that the [Seeker of the Perfect World](../../mixed/titles/Seeker_of_the_Perfect_World.md) made the case that there could be a place for Prosperity within the worldview of the sages, and they were not so horrified at the suggestion as to refuse it out of hand.

Camilla solicited letters on the subject of Prosperity and has made herself and her fellow adepts available this last season for members of the Urizen Assembly to discuss Prosperity with her. As the equinox approaches, the Imperial diplomatic corps, through the office of the Seeker of the Perfect World, receive word that the initiative has been a success: at least from Camilla's point of view. She has learned much about Prosperity from all she has read and heard - yet she feels there is yet more to learn. In particular, she understands that the Empire set great stock by inspirational figures of the past who exemplify their virtues - a practice the people of Tsark understand well. The great sages and adepts of the past all have shrines built into the [Wall of the Luge](../foreign-nations/Mountains_of_the_Moon.md#wall-of-the-luge-and-the-east-and-west-rising), the mighty fortified wall that surrounds the sacred plateau of Visoko. She would like to learn more about the inspirational prosperous figures that the Empire holds dear to its heart. If the Empire's representatives can impressively preach of an inspirational figure of Prosperity in a way that resonates with the spirituality of Tsark, then it could well lead to further such opportunities, as the Tsarkoi believe that inspirational figures are the *most* important way to find spiritual enlightenment.

She has asked Sage Kristoph, the well-established representative of the Sage's Council to the "People of the Horse", to make the trip to Zenith. She would like to host a discussion on the premises of the Garden of the Perfect World, with Sage Kristoph to attend, and an invitation is extended to each person who wrote to her or visited. These are, in no particular order: **Farren Silver Streams, Abel the Rounder, Isaac of the Reaching Vine, Berilo i Erigo, Heilyn Bronwen's Rest, Catena of Tropaion, Galene Netherwatch, Able the Taller, Lord Augur Gryphontale, Eleri Bronwen's Rest**, and **Solas of the First Light**. They are invited to speak about three inspirational figures of Prosperity, with ten minutes (allowing time for questions) allocated for each figure. Who actually presents and on which figures Camilla defers to the visitors - she trusts them to collectively come to an understanding in an efficient manner that respects the time of others, as - she has learnt - the Prosperous are wont to do.

This is clearly a fateful occasion, and on such occasions the Sentinel Gate obliges. There is a conjunction for eleven people at 18:35 on the Saturday of the Equinox, to attend the Gardens of the Perfect World, Occursion, Zenith. The civil service has asked the [Seeker of the Perfect World](../../mixed/titles/Seeker_of_the_Perfect_World.md), **Abel the Rounder of Sarangrave Reforged**, to be responsible for the conjunction and for the other arrangements Camilla has mentioned.

Last season, the [Master of the Mint](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md), **Tristan Ramson von Holberg**, announced the [reappraisal](../senate-sessions/388YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#reappraise-blades-of-the-semmerlak) of the Blades of the Semmerlak in Dawnish and Varushkan territories surrounding the [Semmerlak](../nations/varushka/The_Semmerlak.md). Originally [suggested](Less_welcome_tenants.md#blades-of-the-semmerlak) shortly before the Summer Solstice 386YE, the wardenhouse and knightly estate were part of a larger appraisal into the problem of [Dho'uala](../../mixed/titles/Dho-uala.md) killing Imperial citizens and interfering with matters on and around the Semmerlak.

A motion to construct the [Journeymage Towers](Less_welcome_tenants.md#wards-and-bargains) was [proposed](../senate-sessions/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#construct-journeymage-towers) by **Amicia of the Semmerlak**, as Senator for Semmerholm, and the [Watcher of the Semmerlak](../../mixed/titles/Watcher_of_the_Semmerlak.md) has been active, appointed by the Assembly of Vigilance, and tasked with ensuring the nations surrounding the Semmerlak act with Vigilance and countering threats from the Semmerlak.

Lutomysla's suggestion that the Senate establish a small sodality dedicated to protecting the people around the Semmerlak still holds true, and has the same enthusiastic support from the merchant-boyars, earls, and merchant-princes keen to see people protected from the depredations of the sovereign. A knightly order focused on protecting the communities along the lake, and a warden fellowship to travel on ships crossing the Semmerlak to protect the crew; both would actively seek out the creatures of Dho'uala and fight them. Supported by Imperial heroes, the sodality would remove the threat to the people living along the edge of the lake. Instead, it would fall to questing knights, knights-errant, and wardens to serve as the **Blades in the Semmerlak**,

They will need places to operate from - an estate in Dawn for the knights and a wardenhouse in Varushka for the wardens. Lutomysla identified that the best locations would be either [Sandling](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md#sandling), in the vicinity of [Culwich](../nations/dawn/Weirwater.md#culwich), or [Lacuve](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md#lacuve) in [Semmerholm](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md) near the bustling port of [Lacre](../nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md#lacre) for the knights, and the port of Korotny in [Malimorzi](../nations/varushka/Karov.md#malimorzi) in [Karov](../nations/varushka/Karov.md) and the [Kostjyas Respite](../../mixed/titles/Merchant_Boyar_of_the_Bittershore.md#kostjyas-respite) in [Bittershore](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md#bittershore) in [Ossium](../nations/varushka/Ossium.md) and those still hold true. Although they involve work in two regions, the actual project itself requires only a single commission, and the building of the wardenhouse and the estates will take place in parallel. Once construction is complete, any threat to the people who live along the banks of the lake will be met by enthusiastic warriors. It will also create a new guarding [venture](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md) available to questing knights, knights-errant, and wardens - **Blades in the Semmerlak**. This will allow a [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to spend their time actively defending the Semmerlak and the ships that traverse it. A starting military unit undertaking the venture would receive 120 rings and four [random resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#production).

There would need to be a title to oversee the efforts and support the sodality; the *Knight-Warden of the Semmerlak* would be a [martial title](../../mixed/titles/Martial_title.md) of Dawn and Varushka who was appointed by either unanimous agreement of the senators of those two nations, or a [test of arms](../../mixed/titles/Martial_title.md#creating-martial-titles) between members of those two nations. The title-holder would be responsible for protecting the communities around the Semmerlak from threats mundane and magical, and to stand ready to oppose the depredations of Dho'uala whenever they raise their head. In return, they would receive an income of 10 crowns each season with which to equip allies and maintain their sodality.

They would also be the beneficiaries of several magic items provided by Dawnish, Varushkan, and League donors. A [Duelist's Scales](../../mixed/magic/items/Duelist-s_Scales.md) weapon from the Varushkan merchant-boyars to weaken corrupt horrors; a set of [Goldentide Mail](../../mixed/magic/items/Goldentide_Mail.md) from League merchant-princes to protect them from venom; and a [Troubadour's Ring](../../mixed/magic/items/Troubadour-s_Ring.md) from Dawnish earls to help them support their allies. These items would be received once each year, and while the expectation is that the Knight-Warden of the Semmerlak would use them themselves, there is no reason they could not pass them to other warriors fighting against Dho'uala or similar monstrous threats.

### Dusk

- **For this event we are defining dusk as beginning at 19:00 and ending at 20:00**
- **No bow or crossbow use in Anvil after 20:00 or on quests that start on or after this time**

Our [bow safety rules](../../out-of-character/rules/Bow_safety.md#bow-safety) prohibit the use of bows and crossbows after "dusk". At this event we are defining that point as 20:00 each day. This means you cannot use a bow in Anvil after this time, or on a skirmish or quest that starts on or after this time.

The necromantic ritual [Whispers through the Black Gate](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Whispers_through_the_Black_Gate.md) can only be performed for one hour around dusk - at this event that means the ritual can be performed at any time between 19:00 and 20:00.
