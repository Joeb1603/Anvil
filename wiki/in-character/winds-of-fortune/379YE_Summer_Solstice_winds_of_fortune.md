# 379YE Summer Solstice winds of fortune

## Overview

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/379YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md).

### Bloody Murder

In the months leading up to the Summer Solstice, four civil servants were murdered. You can learn more about this event [here](Bloody_murder.md).

### Blue Seas, White Stone

The powerful Tarquinius family of the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md), in conjunction with the trade advisor to the [Plenum](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md#politics), have sent an emissary to attend anvil during the Summer summit - and their arrival has caused some controversy. You can learn more about this event [here](Blue_seas-_white_stone.md).

### Calling the Drottningbjotha

Hirak the Huntsmaster, herald of [Cathan Canae](../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md), has announced the *Drottningbjotha* at Anvil - a contest of champions not just from the Empire but from lands both nearby and distant. You can learn more about this event [here](Calling_the_Drottningbjotha.md).

### Crossroads

There is a tide in magic that speaks of momentous choices. You can learn about this "conjunction" [here](Crossroads.md).

### Golden Sails

A major Grendel [navy](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) has dropped anchor off the southern coast of [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md). Apparently it is serving purely as the escort for a diplomat intending to negotiate at Anvil ... but the threat of potential violence is palpable. You can learn about this event [here](Golden_Sails.md).

### Green and Pleasant Lands

A group of orcs out of [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) have suddenly appeared in the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) territory of [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md). They appear to be on their way to anvil. You can learn more about this event [here](Green_and_pleasant_lands.md).

### Little Boats

A diplomatic delegation of [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) is on their way to [Anvil](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#anvil) under the protection of the [law](../senate-motions/Amend_law_of_delegation_protection.md). You can learn more about this event [here](Little_boats.md).

### Lonely, Dark and Deep

The [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) of [Brocéliande](../nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md) stirs fitfully, thrashing in response to tighter [trods](../nations/navarr/Trod.md) and [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) meddling. You can learn more about this event [here](Lonely-_dark_and_deep.md).

### News from the North

The people of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) have been dealing with a number of [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) and blessings. You can learn more about these events [here](News_from_the_north.md).

### Promises to Keep

A diplomatic delegation of [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) orcs has come down from frozen [Otkodov](../barbarians/Otkodov.md), to parley with the Empire - and with the [Varushkans](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) in particular. You can learn more about this event [here](Promises_to_keep.md).

### Reikos Flux

Sickness has struck [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md), and has spread through both the armies and the remaining [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) population. You can learn more about this event [here](Reikos_flux.md)

### Shadow in the West

The [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) are about to launch a series of punitive raids across the western border into the Empire focusing on territories in [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md), and [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md). You can learn more about his event [here](Shadow_in_the_west.md).

### Sing a Sad Song

Heralds of the eternal [Lashonar the loquacious](../eternals/Lashonar.md) have mobbed the [Golden Sun](../nations/dawn/Dawn_military_concerns.md#the-golden-sun), bringing with them an infectious and intoxicating ballad that is having unlooked for effect on the soldiers of Dawn. Left unchecked, the situation is likely to escalate. You can learn more about this event [here](Sing_a_sad_song.md).

### Strange Allies

The Lasambrian Hills are invaded - twice. They are sending a delegation to Anvil - but so are the [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md), and the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) have left little doubt that interference with their conquest of the northern hills will end the existing peace. You can learn more about this event [here](Strange_allies.md).

### Trade Winds

Imperial [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) have encountered unexpected problems when travelling to [foreign ports](../../mixed/economy/Foreign_ports.md). You can learn more about these events [here](Trade_winds.md).

### Unmade Trove

A [Torchbearer](../nations/urizen/Urizen_leadership.md#torchbearers) and her companion find a treasure with massive implications under a shattered tower in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md). You can learn more about this event [here](Unmade_trove.md).

### Unquiet Dead

The [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), even in retreat, continue to taunt the Empire. The slaughter of their slaves - first at the [Great Pits of Ennerlund](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#the-great-pits-of-ennerlund) and then at the [Vigilant Swan](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md#the-vigilant-swan) has given rise to scores of angry, terrified, hate-filled ghosts who haunt the mine and the quarry preventing all progress on reopening these [Bourse](../../mixed/economy/Bourse.md) resources. You can learn more about his event [here](Unquiet_dead.md).

### Unwelcome Guest

The [Seventh Wave](../nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md#the-seventh-wave) engage twisted spring horrors in [Casinea](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md). You can learn about this event [here](Unwelcome_guest.md).

### Wolf in Summer

All four of [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) cities have been struck by [curses](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Icy_Maw_Devours_the_Spark_of_Essence.md) that have a deleterious effect on [magicians](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [mana sources](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md). You can learn more about this event [here](Wolf_in_summer.md).
