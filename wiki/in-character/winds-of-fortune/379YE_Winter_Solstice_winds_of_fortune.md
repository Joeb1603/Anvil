# 379YE Winter Solstice winds of fortune

## Overview

*[Image: Change is the law of life. And those who look only to the past or present are certain to miss the future.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Windsandclouds.jpg)*

Over the past three months, several events have occurred which are of significance to the Empire and may require a response from her champions. You can also read about the progress of the Empire's military campaigns [here](../winds-of-war/379YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md).

### Audio File

A tongue-in-cheek summary of these Winds of Fortune, in the form of a news bulletin recorded by Ian Horne, can be found [here](https://soundcloud.com/winds-of-war-fortune/winds-of-fortune-379-winter) with an extra special bulletin [here](https://soundcloud.com/winds-of-war-fortune/winter-379ye-wof-addendum). Listen carefully and you may be able to detect a little editorial bias!

### A Fine and Private Place

There are reports from [Anvil](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#anvil) of irregularities involving [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md), tied in some way to the [bound king](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#tharim) of [the Thrice-cursed Court](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md). You can learn more about this event [here](A_fine_and_private_place.md).

### A Heap of Broken Images

In the south-west, the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) of the Hierro clan camp a stones throw from the walls of [Anduz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md#anduz) in [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md). Tensions rise and fall, and the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) faces difficult choices. You can learn more about this event [here](A_heap_of_broken_images.md).

### A Job Worth Doing

After the [difficulties](Rose_of_Astolat.md) experienced by the [Castle of Thorns](../nations/dawn/Astolat.md#the-castle-of-thorns) in the lead up to the Autumn Equinox, some very peculiar stonemasons indeed have turned up to lend a hand. You can learn more about this event [here](A_job_worth_doing.md).

### A Long Good-Bye

The [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) assembly of [Vigilance](../religion/Vigilance.md) has launched a crusade against the [vyig](../nations/varushka/Vyig.md) criminal organisation in [Temeschwar](../nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), [Tassato](../nations/the-league/Tassato.md), and [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md). You can learn more about the outcome of this event [here](A_long_good-bye.md).

### Black and White

In [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), the [magpie](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_culture_and_customs.md#birds-and-feathers) symbolises [Prosperity](../religion/Prosperity.md). The penguin... not so much. But it is the penguins that have stood dour witness to a discovery that could have far-reaching consequences for the Empire, far out on the [very northern edge](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md) of the Empire, at the roof of the world. You can learn more about this opportunity [here](Black_and_white.md).

### Conviction

In [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md) a firebrand preacher named Golan is calling for the Empire to acknowledge that the virtue of the First Empress - the virtue of [Courage](../religion/Courage.md) - is the most fundamental of all the virtues. Following a vision gained in the slave camps of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), Golan claims that the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) has finally admitted that Courage is HER virtue, and that SHE will return when the Empire has rekindled their faith in their own bravery. You can learn more about this burgeoning religious movement [here](Conviction.md).

### Crusade

A fourth army in [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md), and a major assault against [Dawnguard](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#dawnguard) and the [Towers of the Dawn](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#towers-of-the-dawn). The time is ripe, the [troubadours](../religion/Troubadour.md) say, for Dawn to seize its destiny and conquer [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) once and for all! You can learn more about this opportunity [here](Crusade.md).

### Dying Light

Over the last three months an insidious [curse](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) has lain over the [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) territory of [Bastion](../nations/highguard/Bastion.md). Vicious hail and gloomy sleet combine with a malign aura that encourages people to doubt their faith, striking at the very soul of the Highborn people. You can learn more about this curse [here](Dying_light.md).

### Cuttings

In the vicinity of the [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md) town of [King's Stoke](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md#tower-march) in Tower March there have been half-a-dozen very peculiar births. You can learn more about these events [here](Cuttings.md).

### Falcon in winter

Thane Dogri Thulebane has declared that [Crow's Ridge](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#crowe28099s-ridge) in [Skarsind](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) is part of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), and not part of the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) homeland. His followers have claimed the tower and beacon of Crow's Keep as their base of operation, and are attracting other disgruntled Winterfolk to their banner. You can learn more about this event [here](Falcon_in_winter.md).

### Golden Trumpets

Magical golden trumpets have been sounding across the Empire in locations that are, or have been, associated with Summer magic. The eternal [Meraud](../eternals/Meraud.md) has a challenge for the magicians of the Empire. You can learn more about this event [here](Golden_Trumpets.md).

### Land Fit for Heroes

The decision to [relinquish Skarsind](../senate-motions/Relinquish_Skarsind.md) has been [ratified](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#ratification); the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has chosen to [assign Skarsind](../senate-motions/Assignment_of_Skarsind_II.md) to the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). You can learn more about the implications of this momentous decision [here](Land_fit_for_heroes.md); further significant information about opportunities and consequences for the Imperial Orcs can be found [here](The_worth_of_a_life.md).

### Long Roads to Ruin

Four barbarian [orc](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) delegations are on their way to [Anvil](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#anvil) for the Winter Solstice. Delegates of the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md), the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md), the [Grendel](../barbarians/Grendel.md), and the independent nations of [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) are coming either under flag of truce, or as foreign dignitaries depending on their status. You can learn more about this event [here](Long_roads_to_ruin.md).

### March on their Belly

Serious curses bedevil [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), devastating the Autumn harvest and seriously impacting the [farms](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) of [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md), [Mitwold](../nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md), and [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md). This attack on the breadbasket of the Empire has significant implications for the Empire as a whole, especially with regard to military supplies and the price of food. You can learn all about this event [here](March_on_their_belly.md).

### Price of Prosperity (Trade Winds)

The trade winds blow, bringing with them news of the foreign nations with which Imperial [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) trade. You can learn a little about the war with the [Sumaah Republic](../foreign-nations/Sumaah_Republic.md) - and about the international situation in general - [here](Price_of_prosperity.md).

### Red Star

[The Wanderer](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Wanderer.md) and [the Phoenix](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Phoenix.md) are a dangerous combination. [Astronomancers](../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md) across the Empire agree that there is something awry in the night skies, while an unsettling curtain of mania and aberration descends gently on the Empire. You can learn more about these eerie events [here](Red_star.md).

### Road to Bramar

The [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) have begun repatriating slaves with Imperial backgrounds as apart of an agreement with the [Ambassador to the Iron Confederacy](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador_to_the_Iron_Confederacy.md). They are interested in talking to the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) about a treaty - especially now that they have apparently finished conquering the Lasambrian [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) of Kalino and have a land border with the Empire for the first time. You can learn about these events [here](Road_to_Bramar.md).

### The Stars Aren't Right

Several eerie magical effects appear to be in place across the Empire and its borders, a fair proportion of them drawn from [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md). You can learn a little bit more about them [here](The_stars_aren-t_right.md).

### Summer in Winter

It is shaping up to be a harsh winter across the entire Empire, but nowhere moreso than in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md). A powerful [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) has settled over the entire nation, bringing with it the potent influence of [summer magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md). You can learn more about this event [here](Summer_in_Winter.md).

### Swans will sing

It is one thing to [relinquish Skarsind](../senate-motions/Relinquish_Skarsind.md), but to surrender the [Silver Peaks](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#silver-peaks) to the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) has wounded the [pride](../religion/Pride.md) of [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md). As the winter drags on that wound has continued to fester. You can learn more about this event [here](Swans_will_sing.md).

### Tears in the rain

[Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md) [has been liberated](../winds-of-war/379YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#the-rains-of-reikos), and while some barbarian [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) remain it is now Imperial [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) once again. This brings with it several opportunities which you can learn about [here](Tears_in_the_rain.md).

### To Sleep in Kallavesa

The regalia of [Empress Britta](../history/Empress_Britta.md) has been removed from the [Shrine of Britta](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#the-shrine-of-britta) in the [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#the-necropolis), and interred alongside her body in the marshes of [Kallavesa](../nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md). You can learn more about this event [here](To_sleep_in_Kallavesa.md).

### The Three Refrains

The bardic [sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) of the Three Refrains has re-emerged from obscurity to offer aid to the future heroes of the Empire.

### Trove Remade

The discover of a trove of mage weapons and armour in a chamber beneath the [Unmade Spire](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#tomari) in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md) presented a unique [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to the people of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md). They have made their decision about how to use this windfall, and you can learn more about it [here](Trove_remade.md).

### Vermin

The [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) have been driven out of [Holberg](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md), but their legacy still remains, in the ruins of [Holmauer](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#holmauer), in the [spreading marshes](../nations/the-league/Holberg.md#the-morass) ... and in the [killer trees](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Thunderous_Tread_of_the_Trees.md). You can learn more about the events slowly unfolding in Holberg [here](Vermin.md).

### Whispering Wind

A magical wind has gone out across [Madruga](../nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md), [Feroz](../nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md), [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md), and [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) calling the [hakima](../nations/the-brass-coast/Hakima.md) and the [Kohan](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#kohan) to a council. You can learn more about this [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) event [here](Whispering_wind.md).

### The Worth of a Life

The Imperial Orc nation is together in [one place](../nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) for the first time since the days of their formation. The presence of several groups of ambitious [bonesetters](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_economic_interests.md#bonesetters) in particular present intiguing [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to the orcs. You can learn about these opportunities [here](The_worth_of_a_life.md).

## Ongoing Events

### Hearth Tithe

During the Autumn Equinox, both the [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) assemblies threw their support behind the reintroduction of the [Hearth Tithe](Hearth_Tithe.md). The Wintermark assembly named Ealstan of Stormspire to encourage the reintroduction of the practice, while the Marcher Assembly named James Appleseeder of [Upwold](../nations/the-marches/Upwold.md). As a consequence, for the next year. every farm in [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and Wintermark will set aside a plot of land to grow medicinal herbs to help the war against the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md). Every farm produces a little less money, but provides the owner with useful herbs. You can learn more about the effects of the Hearth Tithe [here](Hearth_Tithe.md#significance).

### Crows and Colleges

As agreed by the [peace treaty](../senate-motions/Accept_Thule_peace_treaty.md) the Empire has negotiated with the [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md), the orcs have withdrawn the forces deployed in [Karsk](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md) to the northern-eastern hills of [Krevstay](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#krevsaty). Over the last three months the [Golden Axe](../nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md#golden-axe) have swept through [Crowslook](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#crowslook) and [Branoc](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#branoc), reassuring the Varushkan people living in these [regions](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md#regions) that the [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) are gone. Despite the severe [winter](Summer_in_Winter.md), there have also been several construction projects [commissioned](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md).

Following the [opportunities](Crow_on_the_cradle.md) last season, the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) has agreed not only to [rebuild the roads of Karsk](../senate-motions/Rebuild_the_roads_of_Karsk.md), but also to [restore the Iron Helm](../senate-motions/Raise_Varushkan_army.md). Work has begun on the new road construction, and if it continues at this rate should be completed by the Spring Equinox. However, so far *no* progress has been made towards mustering the Iron Helm despite the aid offered by the miners of [Moresvah](../nations/varushka/Karsk.md#moresvah) and the ability to pursue both projects at the same time.

Progress has begun on [The Icy Crag of the Eternal Sun](../senate-motions/Construct_College_of_Summer_Magic.md) in [Volchitrava](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#volchitrava). There are reports that with the patronage of [Cathan Canae](../eternals/Cathan_Canae.md) a Summer [regio](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Regio.md) has been somehow raised, or perhaps aligned, which will serve as the heart of the [college](../../mixed/commissions/College_of_magic.md).

Furthermore, construction has also begun in [Mieriada](../nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#mieriada) on [the Hearth of the Tempest](../senate-motions/Construct_the_Hearth_of_the_Tempest.md), which will apparently aid in the exploration of [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md) - although details are obscure at this time.
