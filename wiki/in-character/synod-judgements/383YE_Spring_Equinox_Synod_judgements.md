# 383YE Spring Equinox Synod judgements

*[Image: Seventy-three statements of principle were raised during the Spring Equinox, more than half of which received a Greater Majority .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Principled_Statements.jpg)*

## Overview

During the Spring Equinox 383YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 126 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Change of doctrine

No [change of doctrine](../../mixed/government/synod/Change_of_doctrine.md) was proposed during the Spring Equinox summit.

## Veto

The [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) was not sought during the Spring Equinox 383YE.

## Statement of principle

The following [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) were presented to the assemblies of the Imperial Synod. 37 of these statements achieved a [Greater Majority](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority), meaning they will be distributed to appropriate assemblies. Statements of principle in National assemblies that focused on strengthening or improving the prosperity of that nation that were upheld with a lesser majority also received additional attention as a result of [recent mandates](../winds-of-fortune/What_will_be-_was_not.md#further-guidance).

### Judgement 4

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Maarit Suvidottir

- **Judgement:** The root of Idolatry is restricting a spirit or soul from Virtuous choice or action. To subsume your will to, or worship something puts its ideas or goals above the Way. Veneration or exaltation puts respecting an idea above the Way. Exemplars, Paragons, and Virtuous Ancestors inspire us with deeds, words, legacies, or voices. Pilgrims focus on their stories at their tombs. It would be blasphemy to deny their inspiration - but we don't worship, venerate, or exalt them above the Way. Imperial Law avoids this with Clemency and virtuous consideration of legality of curses. This judgement would clarify this constraint of virtue by replacing Idolatry with "Stricture": "*Restricting the ability of a spirit or soul to choose or act Virtuously. This includes subsuming ones will to, worship, veneration or exaltation of beings, powers, or ideas, the restrictions of malign auras, illegal curses, unjust imprisonment, slavery, or thralldom"*
- **Outcome:** Upheld (901 - 590)

### Judgement 17

- **Assembly:** General Assembly
- **Raised By:** Chauntecleeer Words
- **Judgement:** Further to the successful Judgement 7 last summit, a further 40 Thrones is sought by Chauntecleer to purchase the remaining materials required to construct a brokerage for the purposes of establishing a supply of liao to Suranni followers of the Way. We believe we should be seeing to ai them, reinforce their faith, and bring Salvation to others in the Confederacy. However, we are not blind to the political situation with our neighbours, and have already made a commitment to the Throne not to attempt to commission this work through the Senate without their approval. We would however suggest that were the brokerages to be built now, and the appointment of their overseer be via the Throne then their functioning could still await the Throne's discretion - but allow for their immediate utilisation without the delay from construction; should the situation with our neighbours change; for better or worse. Although not currently illegal to operate these facilities, we acknowledge the spirit of the treaty and the inflammatory possibility of flaunting it. We counsel to be patient, but not to forget.
- **Outcome:** Not Upheld (216 - 485).
