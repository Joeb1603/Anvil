# 385YE Winter Solstice Synod judgements

*[Image: The judgement of rewarding allows the Synod to distribute a single dose of true liao each season.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:True_Liao.jpg)*

## Overview

During the Winter Solstice 385YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 227 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Statement of Principle

### Judgement 6

- **Assembly:** Urizen
- **Raised By:** Athenus of the House of the Wanderer
- **Judgement:** The body is not yet cold but Ludwig Bassilo von Temeschwar is already trying to sell its clothes. He calls for an appraisal of Spiral to exploit its resources before the Reckoning is settled. Should the good people of the League unexpectedly support this proposition we invite the Urizen spires of Ossuary to look to their fellows in Ankra and Cinion as to the best means to apply pressure to ensure the Reckoning is completed faithfully.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (176-38)
- **Notes:** References the call by a former Temeschwari bishop to [appraise](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md), as detailed in the [Blue flowers in the snow](../winds-of-fortune/Blue_flowers_in_the_snow.md#unconstructive-commentary) wind of fortune.

### Judgement 7

- **Assembly:** Urizen
- **Raised By:** Clytemnestra
- **Judgement:** Two years ago I said that Urizen would sacrifice no more. Now I say the Brass Coast should sacrifice no more. I cannot command our armies, but I will command our virtue: no Imperial nation should be lost, it is an affront to our Pride, our Loyalty, and our Prosperity to not help when we can. Our enemies offer no quarter, and so we must shelter our cousins. Urizen celebrates the Net of the Heavens - all of us are connected. Let the Temple of the Four Winds stand as a monument to our connections through the Net - let us offer shelter and education to the refugees of the Brass Coast within the Temple's walls. I call on all of Urizen to be inspired by this act: open your homes to the refugees of the Brass Coast. Ambition, Loyalty, Pride, Prosperity: we will not see your people die when we can offer them safe haven in our rebuilt lands.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (232-8)
- **Notes:** Refers to [recent events](../winds-of-war/A_beacon_of_smoke.md) in [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), and the status of the [status](../winds-of-fortune/Broken_sword.md#shadow-of-the-temple) of the [Temple of the Four Winds](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Four_Winds.md#temple-of-the-four-winds) in [Morrow](../nations/urizen/Morrow.md). At this time few Brass Coast refugees are seeking sanctuary in Urizen; the majority are currently taking shelter in [Kahraman](../nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md), [Segura](../nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md), and [Sarvos](../nations/the-league/Sarvos.md).

### Judgement 9

- **Assembly:** Dawn
- **Raised By:** Belmarie de Courtenay
- **Judgement:** The Cold Sun are arguably the greatest threat the Empire currently faces, but they can be defeated. I call upon the Dawnish National assembly to support and recognise the Girding of a Knight Protector of Day to support our heroes and build upon the national Pride that kept that Cold Sun from taking root with our territories.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (230-0)
- **Notes:** There are four [Knight-protectors](../../mixed/titles/Knight-protector.md) in Dawn (Autumn, Spring, Summer, and Winter). Creating a fifth title equal to the other four would be a major undertaking.

### Judgement 10

- **Assembly:** Dawn
- **Raised By:** Aldwyn Wyldrose
- **Judgement:** Our great nation is steeped in glory. Though we must remember our history. Lady Leysandra de Rondell has become the first person in a decade to seek a rose from gardens of the Castle of Thorns. We, the Dawnish national assembly applaud her ambition and sense of national Pride. As such, we would encourage all those seeking glory to engage with the seat of the kings and queens of Dawn.
- **Outcome:** Withdrawn, not upheld (60-0)

### Judgement 11

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Nikolovich Drakov
- **Judgement:** Citizens of the Empire rejoice, for we are renewed. Vesna "Anointed by Thunder" assumes the Throne, Orcs and Humans are equally welcomed into the Way by the Doctrine of Enlightenment. We should all be inspired by these two great acts. I call on all citizens to recommit themselves to virtue. Let the Way guide and inspire our actions, not the petty politics of convenience. Let us stride, heads held high in Virtue, into the bright new future of our Empire!!!
- **Outcome:** Upheld (971-78)
- **Notes:** Refers to the [coronation of](../winds-of-fortune/A_Crown_in_Winter.md) [Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md) as [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md), and the renewal of faith prompted by the [Doctrine of Enlightenment](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md#the-doctrine-of-enlightenment) as detailed in the [Part of this together](../winds-of-fortune/Part_of_this_together.md#renewal-of-the-faith) wind of fortune.

### Judgement 12

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Doctor Lysimachus
- **Judgement:** The last few summits have seen funds stolen from the Hub, funds raised by the Civil Service to go into the Virtue Fund. The amount taken is irrelevant, because to take even a single ring intended for the rewarding of the Virtuous is frankly an insult to the hard work of the Civil Servants who raised it. It is an act perpetrated by small-minded fools. Whose virtuous character is lacking in the most pitifully trivial of was, and who can't think of nowt better to do with their time in Anvil than pinch pennies from those more virtuous than themselves as opposed to doing something virtuous themselves. Wisdom teaches us to despise folly and chastise the fool that spreads it, so we should denounce and rebuke those who engage in such pathetically perfidious petty acts for what they are, and what they are can be summed up quite simply. UNVIRTUOUS BASTARDS!
- **Outcome:** Upheld (1671-52)

### Judgement 14

- **Assembly:** Loyalty
- **Raised By:** Sufyan i Zuhri i Erigo
- **Judgement:** Even the wicked can be loyal when times are prosperous and judgements are unfailing: the truly virtuous are loyal even through hardship and misfortune. To allow others to assume ones loyalties, to act as if one is loyal to a person or body, and to then in a moment of need proclaim that you are not loyal to that person and never have been, is as great a treachery as to betray your proclaimed loyalties, as great a disunity of word and deed. It is also far more common in the Empire. The only solution for our virtuous society is to proclaim your loyalties loudly, let them never go unspoken. Wear your heraldry, your tattoos, your insignia and rings, let your oaths and contracts be witnessed by all. Do not trust those who refuse to make their loyalties clear, for they sow the seeds for treachery.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (183-60)

### Judgement 22

- **Assembly:** Loyalty
- **Raised By:** Anastasiya Volkov
- **Judgement:** Loyalty cannot be presumed or coerced, but it can be inspired. We, the Empire, must strive to be the spark that ignites the flame of Loyalty in the hearts of its people. When the Empire upholds the oath it made in the constitution to serve and protect its people, providing freedom, prosperity and dignity it promises, then it will inspire the strength of Loyalty that Matilda Fisher inspired in Bushel Sykes. The virtuous build up their fellows, they do not tear them down. I challenge the Empire to find ways to heal the rifts that are turning people's hearts away from us.
- **Outcome:** Not upheld - withdrawn
- **Notes:** References the [controversy around Bushel Sykes](../winds-of-fortune/Despise_treachery.md#not-by-words-or-deeds-alone).

### Judgement 23

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Hazelelponi of the Shattered Tower
- **Judgement:** We failed as a Synod. We failed as an Empire. When the Senate declared the Montanians as barbarians rather than foreigners, they opened the door for any army to attack them. And so they did. We had the opportunity to bring true salvation to lost people, yet the choice by the Military Council was to put them to the sword. We should have been inspired by virtue bringing them salvation through benevolence and bringing them into the Way. Do we as an Empire believe so little in our priests? Now our actions have only encouraged the survivors' belief in Anarchy against the Empire, and it may spread further. We could only boast ourselves Liberators, and not see it through. FOR SHAME. WE MUST BE BETTER.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (1189-721)
- **Notes:** Refers to the complex political situation in [the Barrens](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md), specifically the [destruction of the Montanian faction](../winds-of-war/Ripples_and_shadows.md#hopes-rest). The [All those moments](../winds-of-fortune/All_those_moments.md) wind of fortune contains more information.

### Judgement 25

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Richardo Glostari di Sarvos
- **Judgement:** Coin that goes unspent is a sin against Prosperity. If the Senate agrees that the all fines collected by magistrates should pass to the Virtue Fund, we should reciprocate by agreeing that any coin that remains in the Virtue Fund at then end of each Summit should be returned to the Imperial Treasury.
- **Outcome:** Not upheld - Withdrawn
- **Notes:** Relates to the discussion around legal fines and the Virtue Fund, as laid out in the [Forwards and rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md#fines-and-funds) wind of fortune.

### Judgement 31

- **Assembly:** Urizen
- **Raised By:** Limonos of the Astral Observatory
- **Judgement:** As it is inscribed on the walls of the Academy of Arms: "The object of war is not to die for your country, but to make your enemy die for theirs." The nation of Urizen, with my blessing as the Prelate of Adelmar's Shrine, invites all Imperial refugees to aspire to this vision of war. The doors of the Academy of Arms in Redoubt are open to you. Follow the example of Adelmar, take up the spear, and commit yourself to excellence. When you are threatened by the barbarians, destroy them.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (240-12)
- **Notes:** The [Prelate of Adelmar's Shrine](../../mixed/titles/Prelate_of_Adelmar-s_Shrine.md) oversees the [Academy of Arms](../../mixed/titles/Prelate_of_Adelmar-s_Shrine.md#academy-of-arms) in [Redoubt](../nations/urizen/Redoubt.md), an [inspirational location](../../mixed/government/synod/Inspirational_location.md) dedicated to the [exemplar](../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) [Adelmar the Lion](../religion/Adelmar_the_Lion.md).

### Judgement 32

- **Assembly:** Marches
- **Raised By:** Jedediah Boon
- **Judgement:** Once you burn a bridge down, there ain't no point picking at the ashes. The river is long and winding; there are many other places fit to cross. We invite the Urizeni Scholars of the Sword, led by the House of the Wanderer, to bring their teachings to our nation. In return, we will send our friars of the Marches to Urizen. Where we have disagreed in magic, we will unit in faith. Two schools of Virtue, together in one cause; to share how our same religion manifests in the people of our different nations. Together, the friars of Urizen and the sword scholars of the Marches will build a bridge between our two peoples. Together, we will break new ground with Pride.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (162-40)

### Judgement 33

- **Assembly:** Highborn
- **Raised By:** Azreal, Cohort of the Winter Bear
- **Judgement:** Vigilance teaches us to: Prepare for malice, yet thwart malice before it strikes against you. The vallorn, Druj, Jotun, and Grendel are all malice and we must strike against them with the Wisdom of Atun and the Vigilance of the Sentinel. The Grey Pilgrims should be used to defend the Empire. The trods are being ripped asunder by the heralds of the Cold Sun. Let us not forget the Ashen fields of Bastion, the Unbound Steel Hall, and the White Tower that stands in the way of Virtue. We call the Grey Pilgrims of Reikos to witness the devastation the Cold Sun have caused and prepare for The War on the vallorn. We must form an Army of the virtuous of our nation. We will temper our steel against the Cold Sun then with Ambition we will lead the fight with Isaella's Dance to wipe the vallorn from this world. We call you to Unite under the Grey Banner. Let Loyalty guide us and Vigilance light the way. Let the Grey Guard RISE!

### Judgement 39

### Judgement 42

- **Assembly:** Wintermark
- **Raised By:** Hodwyn Aevardson
- **Judgement:** "Despise the thieving bandit, the lazy wastrel, and the grasping miser and those who take without giving." - Paths of Prosperity, Anvil Library Press, 381YE. Good folk of Wintermark I fear for the sanctity of our nation, for it seems we have stumbled from the virtuous path to slog through the mire of heresy. And saddened as I am to declare it, this is no recent misstep, for indeed I worry that we may have never been on the path of prosperity at all. I speak of course of none other than one of our virtue birds, the magpie. A bird most well known for an obsession with stealing trinkets only to be hoarded away and never used! I question both the sanctity and sanity of any priest who would see the magpie - naught but a thieving bandit with wings - as a standard our nation should hold themselves to. I believe it is paramount for the soul of our nation that we do better, the magpie must be stricken from its idolic station to make way for us to find a true virtuous bird of prosperity.
- **Outcome:** Not upheld (74-194)
- **Notes:** Refers to [Wintermark culture and customs](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_culture_and_customs.md) surrounding [birds](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_culture_and_customs.md#birds-and-feathers).

What hollow hypocrisy is this, that we claim to come as liberators and then begin the slaughter of slaves as soon as we lay eyes on the glitter of mithril? Are we no better than **Druj**? Has this Synod not been repeatedly clear on what is virtuous action in the Barrens? I call on the priests of the Empire to speak out against any such horrifically unvirtuous act. No massacre in the mithril mines! No slaughter in the Fangs!

During the Winter Solstice, the Imperial Senate [declared the Rahvin to be foreigners](../senate-sessions/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#declare-rahvin-foreigners) making any attack against them a crime under Imperial law. The [All those moments](../winds-of-fortune/All_those_moments.md) wind of fortune contains more information.

### Judgement 51

- **Assembly:** League

### Judgement 54

- **Assembly:** Urizen
- **Raised By:** Vespasian
- **Judgement:** Embrace your past, never be ashamed of it. The Sentinels and the Sword Scholars have a long and difficult past of conflict. Those of the Sword Scholars who have repudiated Sulemaine have shown themselves an asset to Urizen's security, rather than a threat like the Disciples of Sulemaine. In light of this, we invite those of the Sword Scholars who think that their future lies in a martial rather than priestly direction to enter into a dialogue and debate at the Academy of Arms with the Sentinels, that they may together avoid complacency and strive for excellence in those martial aspects of Arete of which they are Proud.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (220-0)
- **Notes:** Refers to traditional rivalry between [sword scholars](../nations/urizen/Sword_scholar.md) and [sentinels](../nations/urizen/Sentinel.md), and to the [Academy of Arms](../../mixed/titles/Prelate_of_Adelmar-s_Shrine.md#academy-of-arms) overseen by the [Prelate of Adelmar's Shrine](../../mixed/titles/Prelate_of_Adelmar-s_Shrine.md). See also the [Broken sword](../winds-of-fortune/Broken_sword.md) wind of fortune.

### Judgement 55

- **Assembly:** Urizen
- **Raised By:** Ubarius
- **Judgement:** Courage teaches us to despise cowardice which steers the spirit to weakness and inaction. Pride teaches us to despise the half-hearted, the uncommitted, and the pretender. Ambition teaches us that consequences are the price of Ambition; no price is too high to realise your dreams. We Witnessed Hector of Tropaion Citadel meet death with Poise when it was the price he had to pay to achieve his goals, regardless of how the magistrate Nihm Farkas attempted to demean him. Magic is the first Pillar of Urizen. We call upon the Stargazers and other magicians of Urizen to similarly press on with their Virtuous work on the Omnihedron, regardless of the obstacles the petty minded attempt to place in their way.

- **Notes:** The [Omnihedron](../nations/urizen/Stargazer.md#resonance-and-the-omnihedron) is a popular theory promulgated in Urizen, related to [Imperial enchantments](../../indexes/by-category/Imperial_Enchantment.md).

### Judgement 58

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Warsmith the Iron Way
- **Judgement:** Priests of the Empire, remember that you are priests of the Way first and foremost not just priests of your dedicated virtue. To favour preaching your chosen virtue is expected but to actively disregard any of the seven as though it is worthless is heresy. We may tell the layperson to only focus on one but we are the heroes of the Empire, the beacons of inspiration, the ones who set the example. We can achieve so much more than simply following one and scorning the others. The hammer cannot be used to fell a tree, and an axe cannot be used to repair armour, just as the citizens of your congregation will require guidance on multiple virtues not just your dedicated one. You can do better.

### Judgement 65

- **Assembly:** Marches
- **Raised By:** Rosie of Goatsbridge
- **Judgement:** We, the Marcher Assembly, propose that the Tusks should be thought of as the Mournwold army. We believe acceptance of such an identity would bolster the Pride, Loyalty, and morale of the army, the Mournwold, and the nation as a whole. Firstly, consider that the other Marcher armies have their roots in specific Marcher territories, however the Tusks are not currently tied to any such tradition. Whilst the Tusks did not, indeed could not have been founded out of the Mournwold due to its status of occupation, this army was raised for the express purpose of liberating the Mournwold and its foundational campaign was waged for this ambition. Nedry, the first general of the Tusks went to the wicker man in atonement for the bloody liberation of the Mournwold. With his courageous sacrifice he showed the inextricable bond between the Tusks and the Mournwold, and the commitment to make amends. The Tusks have demonstrated a flexible nature and the Mournwold itself is in flux, with many opportunities and challenges on the horizon. Just as the Mournwold evolves and changes, the Tusks will change with it to rise to the occasion.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (148-68)
