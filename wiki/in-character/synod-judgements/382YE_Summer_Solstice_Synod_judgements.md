# 382YE Summer Solstice Synod judgements

## Overview

During the Summer Solstice 382YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 97 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Change of doctrine

No [change of doctrine](../../mixed/government/synod/Change_of_doctrine.md) was proposed during the Summer Solstice summit.

## Veto

The [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) was not sought during the Summer Solstice 382YE.

## Statement of principle

The following [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) were presented to the assemblies of the Imperial Synod. Fourteen of these statements achieved a [Greater Majority](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority), meaning they will be distributed to appropriate assemblies.

### Judgement 8

- **Assembly:** General Assembly
- **Raised By:** Solas
- **Judgement:** In the confict in Asavea we see echoes of the Patrician War and Thrace's rebellion. We call on all Imperial citizens to aid the peoples of Marracoss and Emphedor in their struggle for liberty in any way possible. We denounce any military captain who would seek to profit from this conflict with the coin of slavers.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (1219 - 165)

- **Assembly:** Navarr National
- **Raised By:** Idanach Listing Beacon
- **Judgement:** We condemn the murder of Achilles Damakan's Forge in the strongest terms. It achives nothing but shows a shocking lack of Pride and Loyalty. Remember Isaella; even the most virtuous assassin is not above the law.

- **Raised by:** Jorma Steelhail
