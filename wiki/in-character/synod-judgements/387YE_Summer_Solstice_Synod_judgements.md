## Veto

One [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) was presented to the assemblies during the Summer Solstice

### Judgement 4

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Tribune
- **Judgement:** Nikolai “The Butcher” Petravich seeks the Veto as follows: In the name of the most virtuous and steadfast Alderai the Fair, Mother of the Empress, I beseech you! Our senators have acted ill in the face of Thule deceit. Support this veto against the Thule treaty. Based on outdated intelligence and lies from the mouth of Rak, we have been cowed! 7 regions they have taken since first our “peace” began. No other Barbarian nation has bled our Empire so. The horde they assure us they possess can not exist! They starve for it was their slaves who kept them fed! We offer too much for too little from this travesty of a one sided treaty. It is foul to bind any from preaching the Way at home or abroad! I implore you, veto this motion!
- **Outcome:** Not Upheld (102-1956)

## Statement of Principle

One hundred and eight [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) were presented to the assemblies during the Summer Solstice

### Judgement 9

- **Assembly:** Dawnish
- **Raised By:** Valentin Ivarovich Orzel
- **Judgement:** It is a Troubadour’s duty to recognise and praise Virtuous Endeavour in the citizens of our Nation. The hardest challenge is often the most glorious, and Virtue attends both the attempt and the victory in those countryfolk that Prove their faith and mettle in the sternest trial. Completing the restoration of once-ruined Drycastle was a mighty trial, for though Dawn is the most glorious Nation in our Empire, it also has the least access to bourse resource and wealthy cartel finding. A deed that might be simply ordinary to the Tax-mongers, Bourse cartels and Monopolists of the wealthy nations is close to astonishing in the Storybook poverty of fiscally-deprived Dawn! Thus I laud Juniper Wainwright House Du Froste for the accomplishment of Restoring Drycastle and name that dauntless Yeo of our Nation an admirable example of Virtue and manifest Prosperity.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (398-0)
- **Notes:** The judgement of [rewarding](../../mixed/government/synod/Rewarding.md) exists specifically to reward virtuous behaviour

### Judgement 12

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Solas of the Spire of the Waxing Sun
- **Judgement:** It is folly to claim that the Synod has no ‘greatest Ambition’.
