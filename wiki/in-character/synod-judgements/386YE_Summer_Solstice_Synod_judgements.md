# 386YE Summer Solstice Synod judgements

*[Image: The judgements of the Imperial Synod guide the spirits of the Empire's people.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:SynodSummer386.jpg)*

## Overview

During the Summer Solstice 386YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 216 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Veto

During the final [Senate session](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_session.md) of the summit, Sabella of the Silent Tide declared their intent to seek the [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) following the decision of the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) to [criminalize preaching the Way in Asavea](../senate-sessions/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#criminalise-preaching-the-way-in-asavea). The outcome of the veto will be determined by the General Assembly during the Autumn Equinox.

## Statement of Principle

### Judgement 7

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Herminius of the House of the Wanderer
- **Judgement:** The Druj have returned! They are active in Therunin, the Barrens, and Morrow. They threaten our Eastern border from Ossium to Zenith. I call on all citizens to be proactive, report suspicious activity, check in on your neighbours, and be prepared to fight. Remember! You do not stand alone, Sword Scholars will be active in your territory, seek their protection, aid and Wisdom where needed.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (2478-74); Margin of 2404
- **Notes:** The Druj have recently [invaded](../winds-of-war/Ashes.md) the territory of [Therunin](../nations/navarr/Therunin.md), and launched attacks into [Morrow](../winds-of-war/Ashes.md#twilight-morrow) and [the Barrens](../winds-of-war/Ashes.md#fanning-the-sparks-the-barrens).

### Judgement 10

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Viviane Barossa de Coeurdefer
- **Judgement:** True Virtues guide the spirit to its highest potential, and build for the future. Earl Zadkiel de Coeurdefer, Cardinal of Pride, dedicates to Ambition so the legacy of the House may be Consecrated at Hounds' Gate, that were once the Spires of Dusk. Virtue drives us to actions greater than any one life.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (1224-198); Margin of 1026
- **Notes:** Refers to the consecration of the [Houndsgate](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#houndsgate) as detailed in the [Poetry and prose](../winds-of-fortune/Poetry_and_prose.md#the-spires-of-dusk) wind of fortune. See also [Judgement 9](#judgement-9).

### Judgement 13

- **Assembly:** Varushkan
- **Raised By:** Amris Johan Merikovich
- **Judgement:** The Iron Helms have been shunned and slandered for years by many of the citizens they have protected. They have shown Courage, Loyalty, Pride and Vigilance in doing whatever their Generals and Military Council ask of them. All things grow and change and it is time for the Iron Helms to change. Based on their history and victories we believe they should now focus on their mastery of the dark magics & curses of Varushka. They have a strong magical heritage and would use such abilities Virtuously to advance the cause of our Empire.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (350-10); Margin of 340
- **Notes:** The [Iron Helms](../../mixed/government/military/Iron_Helms.md) are a [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) whose [tactics](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#cruel) have been a regular source of controversy within the Empire.

### Judgement 41

- **Assembly:** Dawnish
- **Raised By:** Claude Deeds
- **Judgement:** Dawnish senators knelt and begged for the Emperor to veto the treaty with the Rahvin, after Senate had approved the treaty and Military Council had voted wholeheartedly in its support. The Emperor vetoed the treaty because it would have given the Rahvin land where the Dawnish make their homes - in the Carmine Fields, in Bitter Strand. There are no Dawnish homes in the Fangs mithril mine. The Rahvin were kept there as Druj slaves. They worked the mine - they slept, ate, and lived their lives there. Then they seized the mine from their slavers, and now they hold it. Where we toil, where we sleep, where we eat, where we live, where we hold - that is our hearth and homestead. Our Empress and Dawn's Senators tell us to value our homestead. So any decision about the future of the Fangs must take into account the claim of the Rahvin. We must reach out to them, negotiate, and demonstrate our Virtue. We must not force them out of their home. We must not forced them out of their home.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (424-74); Margin of 350
- **Notes:** References the Spring 386YE [treaty](../senate-sessions/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#ratify-treaty-with-the-rahvin) passed by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md), but vetoed by [Emperor Vesna](../history/Emperor_Vesna.md), and the Rahvin sept who currently occupy [the Fangs](../nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#the-fangs) [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) mine there.

### Judgement 44

- **Assembly:** Wisdom
- **Raised By:** Maelfaisance Remys
- **Judgement:** Once again we face the Grendel and their beasts; on the battlefield and in the waters of Catazar. For some of the soldiers and sailors of this empire, this is the first time they have seen such creatures. These monsters may be dreadful, but they are not immortal. Courage is when we face that which we fear - Wisdom allows us to do so equipped to defeat it. The Virtuous apply what they have learned; Wisdom knows all knowledge is incomplete. Therefore I call upon my siblings in Wisdom to seek the veterans of this empire and hear their tales of shark-slaying and kraken-killing. Learn of the soft underbelly, the armour-chink and the most effective tactics. Then, tell those tales at the firesides of our armies and in the inns of our docks. Inspire our citizens - grant them the knowledge they need to win.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (368-0)

### Judgement 70

- **Raised By:** Eurybia of the Wanderers

### Judgement 72

- **Assembly:** Highborn
- **Raised By:** Merari of Navigator's Virtue
- **Judgement:** The Empress calls on us to "Let no corner of our Empire be anything less than a shining example of Prosperity." Yet in Highguard we often focus on completing ambition after ambition, rather than enjoying the fruits of our labours. So, I call upon my siblings to take advantage of the hard work of Asher of Felix's Watch and a band of courageous Highguard heroes in recovering the remains of the exemplar Permion, The Lawmaker, in Winter 378 and returning them to Necropolis. At a time when Highborn patricians had become especially corrupt, he refused to be a figurehead and instead attempted to serve all his people - not just the rich and privileged. He even dared not to unveil and wore his cambion horns with pride. So, when your courage is tested, and you must make the hard choice take a short pilgrimage to Permion's tomb, read his biography and look to his example for guidance.

- **Notes:** [Permion](../religion/Permion.md) is an exemplar of [Courage](../religion/Courage.md), whose remains were allegedly recovered some years ago. This statement appears to confirm speculation that Permion possessed the [cambion](../../mixed/magic/lore/Cambion.md) [lineage](../../mixed/magic/lore/Lineage.md).
