# 385YE Spring Equinox Synod judgements

*[Image: The Synod watches over and represents the concerns of the virtuous citizens of the Empire.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Synod_Spring_385.jpg)*

## Overview

During the Spring Equinox 385YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 186 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Considerations

The following considerations should be borne in mind when bringing judgements before the Imperial Synod during the Spring Equinox.

- **All Assemblies:** Statements that prioritise the greater good over personal loyalties are less effective. This is permanent effect of the [Eyes of Loyalty](../../mixed/titles/Cardinal_of_Loyalty.md#additional-powers---the-eyes-of-loyalty).
- **All Assemblies:** Statements that try to compel Loyalty will fail. This is permanent effect of the [Eyes of Loyalty](../../mixed/titles/Cardinal_of_Loyalty.md#additional-powers---the-eyes-of-loyalty).
- **All Assemblies:** Revocations and Excommunications for disloyalty to the nation or Empire would risk causing dissent. This is permanent effect of the [Eyes of Loyalty](../../mixed/titles/Cardinal_of_Loyalty.md#additional-powers---the-eyes-of-loyalty).
- **All Assemblies:** Any mandate that does not pass with a greater majority will have limited or no effect on Urizen or Varushka. It is not impossible that a mandate might lead to a response in one of the two nations, but it won't lead to any immediate consequences for the people of the nation. This lasts until some new turn of events changes things. [Urizen Link](../winds-of-fortune/Hard_and_suspecting.md#the-urizen-assembly). [Varushka Link](../winds-of-fortune/Hard_and_suspecting.md#the-varushkan-assembly).
- **All Assemblies:** No statement of principle that is about Sulemaine i Taziel will lead to a mandate. Lasts until at least the end of the Winter Solstice 384YE and potentially longer. [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Hard_and_suspecting.md#the-urizen-assembly).
- **Highguard and Navarr Assemblies:** Statements of principle in either assembly that receive a greater majority may create opportunities in the other assembly. Lasts until the end of the Autumn Equinox 385YE [Link](../winds-of-fortune/The_bones_of_what_you_believe.md#we-will-all-be-there-tomorrow).

### Judgement 14

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Genoveva Barossa di Tassato
- **Judgement:** The Empire was created to allow all souls to live and die under the banner of the Way of Virtue. During Empress Teleri's reign, Cardinals were members of both the Synod and the Imperial Senate. They were able to be present in Senate, have full voting rights, and could both raise, and second a Senate motion. This was in recognition of the fact that, just as Senators represent a number of Imperial citizens in a territory, so the Cardinals represent the virtuous citizens of their assembly. This power was removed during Varkula's reign, and the Conscience of the Senate created as a political compromise. Recently, Senators have often stated that Synod and the Senate need to work more closely, and at present Synod can only react to Senate's decisions after the fact. We call on the Senate to restore the power to function as full voting members of the Senate to the Cardinals, giving full voice to Virtue in the primary legislative body of the Empire.
