# 384YE Spring Equinox Synod judgements

## Overview

During the Spring Equinox 384YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 165 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Change of doctrine

No [change of doctrine](../../mixed/government/synod/Change_of_doctrine.md) was proposed during the Spring Equinox summit.

## Veto

The [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) was not sought during the Spring Equinox 384YE.

## Statement of principle

The following [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) were presented to the assemblies of the Imperial Synod. Those statements which achieved a [Greater Majority](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority) will be distributed to appropriate assemblies.

### Judgement 7

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Storyteller Goran
- **Judgement:** As our knowledge of the Way expands and changes, so must the Imperial Synod. A reformation is required to determine how priests attend virtue assemblies. The ability to perform liao ceremonies does not make a priest. The ability to be dedicated to a virtue does not confer knowledge of that virtue. Belief in the Way, and the will to guide others makes the priest. Dedication to a specific Virtue is not, and should not, be required to preach a Virtue to a congregation. We call upon the Synod to support this reformation - that all priests should be allowed to follow the call of their souls and spirits, and attend the virtue assembly that calls to them.
- **Outcome:** Upheld, 1063-610

### Judgement 8

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Aspar
- **Judgement:** The virtuous apply what they have learned; Wisdom knows all knowledge is incomplete. Let every word you speak carry meaning; do not use forty when four will do. Wisdom is not always knowing the answer; Wisdom is finding the right question. Test what you learn; only fools accept hearsay as truth. Despise folly and chastise the fool that spreads it.

### Judgement 9

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Galene Netherwatch

### Judgement 11

- **Assembly:** Navarr
- **Raised By:** Quinn Eternal
- **Judgement:** We, the guides of the Navarr national assembly, encourage increased sharing of knowledge, tools, and resources with our Highborn siblings to further improve Highguard's effectiveness in fighting the vallorn. Only together can we look to vanquish the vallorn. The Highborn elected in loyalty and vigilance to change a part of their national identity over the course of 382YE. Since then, the Grey Pilgrims have taken up the mantle of walking the trods to aid in the weakening of the vallorn, helping combat the greatest spiritual threat to the Empire. ## Considerations

The following considerations should be borne in mind when bringing judgements before the Imperial Synod during the Spring Equinox.

- **All Assemblies:** Statements that prioritise the greater good over personal loyalties are less effective. Lasts until the end of the Summer Solstice 384YE. [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Like_a_hurricane.md#know-your-heart).
- **All Assemblies:** Statements that try to compel Loyalty will fail. Lasts until the end of the Summer Solstice 384YE. [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Like_a_hurricane.md#know-your-heart).
- **All Assemblies:** Revocations and Excommunications for disloyalty to the nation or Empire would risk causing dissent Lasts until the end of the Summer Solstice 384YE. [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Like_a_hurricane.md#know-your-heart).
- **All Assemblies:** Any mandate that does not pass with a greater majority will have limited or no effect on Urizen or Varushka. It is not impossible that a mandate might lead to a response in one of the two nations, but it won't lead to any immediate consequences for the people of the nation. This lasts until some new turn of events changes things. [Urizen Link](../winds-of-fortune/Hard_and_suspecting.md#the-urizen-assembly). [Varushka Link](../winds-of-fortune/Hard_and_suspecting.md#the-varushkan-assembly).
- **All Assemblies:** No statement of principle that is about Sulemaine i Taziel will lead to a mandate. Lasts until at least the end of the Winter Solstice 384YE and potentially longer. [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Hard_and_suspecting.md#the-urizen-assembly).
- **Virtue Assemblies:** Any statement of principle in any virtue assembly will not create a mandate for that virtue assembly. If any mandate does arise, then it will need to be passed by the General Assembly. Lasts until the end of the Winter Solstice 384YE. [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Hard_and_suspecting.md#the-general-assembly).
- **Highguard Assembly:** for the coming year statements of principle in the Highborn Assembly that do not directly address matters of orthodoxy and false beliefs in the Empire will be significantly less likely to result in a mandate. Lasts until the end of the Summer Solstice 384YE. [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Walk_on.md#highguard).
- **Highguard and Dawn Assemblies:** Statements of principle in either assembly that receive a greater majority may create opportunities in the other assembly. Lasts until the end of the Autumn Equinox 384YE [Link](../winds-of-fortune/Love_and_rockets.md#virtue).
