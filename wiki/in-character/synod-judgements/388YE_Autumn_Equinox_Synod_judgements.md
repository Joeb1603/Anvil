## Change of Doctrine

### Judgement 30

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Skywise Rykana
- **Judgement:** Orcs are no longer new to The Way, no longer the children of Virtue we were when the Bonewall first enshrined our beliefs in Doctrine. More now look to the Empire and see the chance for a future, and in turn our understanding of our spiritual destiny has expanded. We build now on all that has come before to replace the Doctrine of the Howling Abyss with the Doctrine of the Final Challenge so that we might welcome more into The Way.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (2733-54)
- **Notes:** This judgement removes the [Doctrine of the Howling Abyss](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md#the-doctrine-of-the-howling-abyss) and replaces it with the Doctrine of the Final Challenge. The full implications of this change to [the Doctrines of the Faith](../religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md) will be felt over the coming months.

**The Doctrine of the Final Challenge**

> *In-character text:*
>
> *After death, an orc can become an Ancestor by overcoming the mighty spiritual challenge that we understand as the Howling Abyss, though it takes many forms. This challenge may only be attempted once. Those who fail are lost, but those who succeed become Ancestors who can guide living orcs.*
