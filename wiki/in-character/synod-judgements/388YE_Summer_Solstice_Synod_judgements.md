## Statement of Principle

### Judgement 6

- **Assembly:** Highborn
- **Raised By:** Ames the Amiable, of the Pilgrims Guard
- **Judgement:** The Grand Inspiration of the Way is our highest calling, and still requires vast resources to complete. The Prosperous know that there are three things that should never be made: food that goes uneaten, coin that goes unspent, and magic that goes unused. We call upon the Highborn citizenry to tell their benefactors and local attendants of Anvil of any unexploited resources in their regions. Whether these take the form of untended forests, mines, herb gardens, or legends and local myths of glittering stone, strange golden woods, or odd craters, nothing is too small. Every lead should be followed. Let us use the full resources of our territories to complete our highest calling, and build prosperity for future virtuous projects.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (638-0)

### Judgement 7

- **Assembly:** Pride
- **Raised By:** Brother Luke
- **Judgement:** Pride is not a solitary flame. It is a torch passed from hand to trembling hand until all may see its light. When we build up our fellows - when we safeguard their health, nourish their wellbeing, cherish their art, and celebrate their culture - we declare before the world that no soul is too small to be worthy, no life too humble to be raised. As such. WE will aid, support and uplift the persecuted, the enslaved, the broken, the downtrodden, the oppressed, the voiceless, and those who cannot yet speak for themselves. We will lift them from vanished silene into the gilded embrace of greatness. This is our Highest Calling.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (214-10)
- **Notes:** An Assembly's call to action requires a greater majority, as per the **[The highest calling](../winds-of-fortune/The_highest_calling.md)** wind of fortune

### Judgement 17

- **Assembly:** Highborn
- **Raised By:** Destin of the Woodwych Harts

### Judgement 46

- **Assembly:** Prosperity
- **Raised By:** Merari
- **Judgement:** The Prosperous have always recognised opportunity wherever it can be found. Throughout history, merchants have traded with those possessing unusual talents, bringing rare skills and wonders to the Empire. Those partners may include supernatural beings - Eternals, Heralds, Tulpas, Sovereigns and others. They possess capabilities beyond those of any mortal, not because they are greater than us, but because they are different. Different gifts do not make them our betters.
