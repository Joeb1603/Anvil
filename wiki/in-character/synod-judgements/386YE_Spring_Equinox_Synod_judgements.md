# 386YE Spring Equinox Synod judgements

*[Image: The statement of principle is a public declaration made with the support of the Synod on a matter relevant to the spiritual well-being of the Empire.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:What_in_the_virtues_is_going_on.jpg)*

## Overview

During the Spring Equinox 386YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 210 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Statement of Principle

### Judgement 4

- **Assembly:** Highborn
- **Raised By:** Doctor Lysimachus
- **Judgement:** While we Highborn are righteously committed to ensuring the Cold Sun is extinguished, we must not forget the other threats we still face and fight. While we focus on the Cold Sun on our doors and must prepare for Grendel on our shores, the vallorn remains the greatest spiritual threat to the Empire and is no dormant threat. We formed the Grey Pilgrims to aid Navarr to end this threat, not just keep it at bay for another 1,000 years for others to deal with. In accordance with Sian Eternals statement to Navarr, once the Cold Sun have been snuffed out, even as we keep an eye upon the Grendel, we return to ensuring the vallorn are eventually given a right permanent kicking in! When the time comes for just to act, whether sooner or later, we will Act with certainty.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (332-0)
- **Notes:** The [grey pilgrims](../nations/highguard/Grey_pilgrim.md) were initially founded to oppose the threat of the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md).

### Judgement 7

- **Assembly:** Dawn
- **Raised By:** Vivaine Knotes
- **Judgement:** The Dawnish gave the Empire the Test of Arms, a minimum acceptable standard all combatant citizens shall be trained to by the Empire at no cost to themselves. But since when have we Dawnish settled for the minimum? Should we not strive for our acceptable standard to be higher than other nations? We would ask the nation of weavers and knights, what measures can we collectively take to ensure our minimum training surpasses that of the Imperial Test of Arms. Can the wisdom of our mentors, the magic of our witches or the establishments we build raise our bar higher for all of those Dawnish who fight for the Empire... In time, the other nations may follow. Let them, and we will raise the bar again. For too long has the Empire complacently accepted the same standard.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (214-58)

### Judgement 8

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Eska Harakkadottir
- **Judgement:** It is better by far to try and to fail than to fail to try. Day mages of the Empire, what is your Ambition? Despite being found guilty of treason, and attracting the attention of Cold Sun to the Empire through a series of unwise decisions, Skywise Gralka remains Arch Mage of Day unchallenged. The Synod calls upon the Conclave and its Grand Masters to uplift and encourage those with Courage, Ambition, and Pride to challenge for the position of Arch Mage of Day.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (1018-532)

### Judgement 9

- **Assembly:** Loyalty
- **Raised By:** Able the Taller
- **Judgement:** Together we forge an unbreakable chain stretching across our Empire. Each pilgrim is a thread in the net, a single link connected to many, strengthening and supporting one another, building up our fellows. Your Loyalty is not a precious gem to be hoarded away but a Bright Burning Brand to drive away the darkness of isolation. It is only through building towards the loyalties where all can hear, seek out those who share your feelings, and reach out across any boundary, be it regional, territorial, or even national. With Virtue we can build WONDERS and work MIRACLES. With Loyalty we will inspire the Empire to be the Storm of Change our Emperor commands and build the future that every citizen deserves.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (496-0)

### Judgement 10

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Creighton Blackewater
- **Judgement:** It will soon be two years since the liberation of Rachensgrab, and yet even with three successful statements of principle there has been no mandate or any indication that the former slaves have had their stolen prosperity returned to them. Let us prove that, we the Synod, stand by our virtue and call upon the Empire to raise a fund to be distributed to all former slaves of Rachensgrab living in the Empire. Prosperity Provides.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (1382-164)
- **Notes:** References the events following the [assault on the aerie of the slave lords](../winds-of-war/Burning.md) of Rachensgrab in Winter 384YE, and the judgements of [Winter 384YE judgement 165](384YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-165) [Autumn 385 judgement 75](385YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-75), and [WInter Solstice 385 judgement 162](385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-162). The majority of the slaves freed in the fall of Rachensgrab were taken in by [the Commonwealth](../foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md), with only a few choosing to make the journey to the Empire. There is no Imperial contact with the nations of the northern [Sea of Steel](../world/The_seven_seas.md#sea-of-steel) where Rachensgrab once lay but recent developments have provided an opportunity for Imperial [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) and [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to visit the area around Rachensgrab, as detailed in the *[Say her name](../winds-of-fortune/Say_her_name.md#far-from-these-shores)* wind of fortune.

### Judgement 12

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Elagabala
- **Judgement:** Wisdom calls for listening to expertise; applying what one knows, and finding practical solutions. The Sevenfold Path are the second-oldest Conclave Order; for centuries they have been responsible for ensuring the powers of Conclave are deployed in line with the Way of Virtue. They have held Conclave to account time and time again, and uplifted souls in Virtue over and over. The Synod should support the work of the Sevenfolk Path by encouraging Virtuous magicians to join the Order, seeking their expertise in the Virtuous application of magical might, and bequeathing them liao and other materials that will support their manifesto. The Sevenfold Path serves as a bridge between Synod and Conclave - we encourage Synod to make use of that bridge.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (918-258)
- **Notes:** Refers to the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md) [Conclave order](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave_order.md).

### Judgement 14

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Ser Sable
- **Judgement:** When Dogri Thulebane refused to give up Skarsind we responded with the Iron Helms. When some of the people of Bregasland sided with Mathilda Fisher and her Jotun Masters we responded with the Magistrates. Why then when certain Urizeni in Spiral cede from the Empire in open rebellion do we respond with the olive branch and concessions. Yes since the fall of Britta the title of Warmage has been held mainly by Urizeni but I remind you that the first Warmage was a Warwitch named Jayne de Severnford it has never been a national position! I call on all nations to look to your Loyalty to those amongst you who may covet the title of Warmage and stand against this attack on their ambition if this title were made a national position under the rebels coercion. NO TO WARMAGE BEING A NATIONAL TITLE!
- **Outcome:** Upheld (774-718)
- **Notes:** Refers to the complex political situation in [Spiral](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md), most recently discussed in the *[The three pillars](../winds-of-fortune/The_three_pillars.md)* wind of fortune. The [Warmage](../../mixed/titles/Warmage.md) is an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) that advises the [Military Council](../../mixed/government/military/Military_Council.md) on magical matters. The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) voted to make the Imperial Warmage an [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) national title [during the summit](../senate-sessions/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-title-of-warmage-i).

### Judgement 16

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Lenarius Ankarien
- **Judgement:** Our conflict with Asavea is not merely political, it is a moral and spiritual conflict as well. Moral, because the Empire stands against slavery, whereas Asavean society stands upon the backs of slaves. Spiritual, because their desperate dependency on imaginary deities holds back from achieving their highest potential through experiencing the virtues. Their priesthood is corrupt, thinking only of preserving their own power, and not of the good of their people. The Synod should do all it can to aid followers of the Way in Asavea, so that its people may be free to live in Virtue.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (1738-12)
- **Notes:** References the war with the [Asavean Archipelago](../foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md), and virtuous sects there as discussed in the *[Parts of speech](../winds-of-fortune/Parts_of_speech.md#the-asavean-archipelago)* wind of fortune.

### Judgement 21

- **Assembly:** Highborn
- **Raised By:** Rinnah of the Shattered Tower
- **Judgement:** A year ago, Kirenna called for every Freeborn citizen to contribute whatever they could to support the raising of the Holy City at Bastion. Unasked, the Freeborn came and named their price. Highguard paid. Since, the Freeborn have faced an unprecedented storm of adversity, but have never wavered in their commitment. To live Virtue is to embrace it, and the virtue of every Freeborn citizen who contributed will echo through history for a thousand years. The Highborn recognise the Prosperity displayed in their labour, sweat and toil and invite the Freeborn to send a delegation of Sutannir to teach in Bastion upon its completion. They have earned their place alongside Highguard and should be welcomed to spread their perspective on the Way to pilgrims. Only though these bonds of kinship can the Grand Inspiration enable the Way to reach every soul in existence.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (457-0)
- **Notes:** Refers to the Grand Inspiration of the Way being raised in [Bastion](../nations/highguard/Bastion.md), introduced in the *[Blood will thicken](../winds-of-fortune/Blood_will_thicken.md#no-price-is-too-high)* wind of fortune.

### Judgement 31

- **Assembly:** Dawn
- **Raised By:** Claude Deeds
- **Judgement:** To convert nonbelievers to the Way requires both words and deeds. It is not enough to say we wish to unite the world under the Way. We must also take courageous actions to do so. Let the Troubadours of Dawn study well how the Free Folk have been brought into the Empire, and into the Way. Dawn has led this charge. We listened to their questions. We defended them in the Synod. At the House of the Proffered Hand, in Astolat, the liberated now receive guidance in our faith. There are lessons here to learn. Let us learn them, and seek opportunities to put them into practice. ### Judgement 34

- **Assembly:** General
- **Raised By:** Lord Severin de Rondell
- **Judgement:** Doctrine says that our spirits and our souls have a voice in the future. This our Empire is our voice, magnified by thousands. It is our responsibility in virtue to strengthen that voice, and not to weaken it. I do not command loyalty to the Empire but I say this, our ambition is too great for any one person to carry alone, our pride is in our fellows, our friends, and, yes, our foes, to work for the highest potential of the Empire is a virtuous thing. We are one Empire and we will strive together towards our shared future. We strive to act as the Exemplars and Paragons do to guide and teach benevolence that the actions marked by great virtue are those that benefit our Empire. For this benefits not just us, not just now but also our children and our future selves. We expect the greatest in virtue to have acted with benevolence and to bring the unvirtuous into the faith (salvation) as well as inspiring others to grow and build on their faith (inspiration). We believe that the way is a betterment for all thinking souls and that we as an Empire have a primary drive and responsibility to spread the way for that to occur the Empire must survive and grow to be made stronger as a vehicle of the Way, in this I ask the Empire to accept this charge. To best serve virtue our actions should benefit the Empire; through benevolence to inspire and bring salvation. All citizens should work to benefit the Empire through their virtuous acts over petty self serving or damaging choices. It is the responsibility of priests to guide those at Anvil in how best to do this and hold the Empire to virtue so that lands that have been lost may be regained and the Empire shall bring the way to the unvirtuous.
- **Outcome:** Not Upheld (530-532)

### Judgement 46

- **Assembly:** Urizen
- **Raised By:** Lochias
- **Judgement:** We are saddened that the Bone Pilgrimage do not extend into Urizen. However it is not our custom to preserve physical remains of the fallen we preserve their words. As the Imperial Orcs strive to build a great library in Skarsind, we call upon the scholars of Urizen to open our own libraries to the Sunstorm sept so that the library of Worth may contain the writings of their ancestors who dwelt among the spires.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a **Greater Majority** (264-0)
- **Notes:** Relates to the [Bone Pilgrimage](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#the-bone-pilgrimage), which encourages priests to support Imperial Orcs seeking out remains of former slaves and bringing them to the grand ossuary in Skarsind. This support is not extended by the majority of the Urizen priests, because the [mandate](../../mixed/government/synod/Mandate.md) enacting it in the General assembly did not receive a greater majority and so was rejected. Urizen [funerary traditions](../nations/urizen/Urizen_culture_and_customs.md#funerals) were not extended to the orc slaves they held prior to the [Orc Rebellion](../nations/imperial-orcs/The_Orc_Rebellion.md). As with the vast majority of orc slaves in the Empire, Urizen slaves were not permitted to read or [write](../../mixed/magic/lore/Imperial_Orcs_hearth_magic.md#writing). There are very few records of anything significant that the orc slaves held in Urizen would have said or done.
