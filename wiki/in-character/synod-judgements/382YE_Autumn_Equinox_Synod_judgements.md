# 382YE Autumn Equinox Synod judgements

## Overview

During the Autumn Equinox 382YE Summit, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) raised 96 [judgements](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md).

## Change of doctrine

No [change of doctrine](../../mixed/government/synod/Change_of_doctrine.md) was proposed during the Autumn Equinox summit.

## Veto

The [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) was not sought during the Autumn Equinox 382YE.

## Statement of principle

The following [statements of principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) were presented to the assemblies of the Imperial Synod. Fourteen of these statements achieved a [Greater Majority](../../mixed/government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority), meaning they will be distributed to appropriate assemblies.

### Judgement 12

- **Assembly:** Urizen National
- **Raised By:** Aspar
- **Judgement:** All Urizeni should follow the example set by Sulemaine and take up arms against the Druj. All who can hold a weapon must fight, all who cannot must help whoever they can.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (60-19)

### Judgement 19

- **Assembly:** Dawn National
- **Raised By:** Ser Thomas Aurelius
- **Judgement:** Acts of Glory do not begin and end on the battlefield, we can serve as an inspiration to the people when we recover our strength: practice music, produce art, tell tales, sing songs, *love deeply*! When we look to our heritage, look beyond the end of the rod or tip of the spear for we are the heart of the Empire - so we must *spread love*.
- **Outcome:** Upheld (114-20)

### Judgement 21

- **Assembly:** Brass Coast National
- **Raised By:** Hembeneth i Guerra
- **Judgement:** The Brass Coast National Assembly supports Sagua i Ezmara in her efforts to retrieve the [Ring of Atun](../../mixed/magic/items/Atun-s_Ring.md) which she hid for safekeeping in a past life. As a relic of the Brass Coast and Highguard history we consider it virtuous to assist Sagua in this endeavour. Learning from our history is an act of Wisdom and valuing our heritage is an act of Pride. Returning the ring to the Empire is an act of Loyalty.
- **Outcome:** Upheld with a Greater Majority (102 - 0)

### Judgement 25
