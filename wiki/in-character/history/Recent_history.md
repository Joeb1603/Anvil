# Recent history

## Overview

The following are summaries of the history of the Empire since the death of Empress Britta in 376YE.

A list of the seasons when each [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) is [elected](../elections/Imperial_elections.md) can be found in the [election schedule](../../mixed/titles/Election_schedule.md). To just see the name of the current character holding a particular title, then please refer to the [current Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Current_Imperial_titles.md) page.

You can see a list of [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) and [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) that have supported [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md), [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md), [fortifications](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), and other Imperial endeavours on the [Imperial Roll of Honour](../roll-of-honour/Imperial_Roll_of_Honour.md) page. This page is updated after the last wind of war is released for each event.
