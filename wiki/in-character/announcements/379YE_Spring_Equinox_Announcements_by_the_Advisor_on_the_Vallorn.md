# 379YE Spring Equinox Announcements by the Advisor on the Vallorn

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Advisor on the Vallorn makes the announcement that he intends to use his power of Historical Research: Origins of the Vallorn
>
> *Approved by the Advisor on the Vallorn.*

### Overview

- [Historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) concerning these topics has been commissioned through the civil service.
- Actions approved by the [Advisor on the Vallorn](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_the_Vallorn.md).
- This action allows no vote, but could be [vetoed](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) by the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).

### Date

- Spring Equinox 379YE

### Progress

- Unknown

### Campaign Outcome

- Research reports will be compiled and presented to the Advisor on the Vallorn
- Many civil servants associated with historical research have been murdered in the last three months. There is some concern about the safety of people gathering the information for this project.
