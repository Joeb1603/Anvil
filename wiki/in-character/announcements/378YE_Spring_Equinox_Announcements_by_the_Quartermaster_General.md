# 378YE Spring Equinox Announcements by the Quartermaster General

> *In-character text:*
>
> Resupply the Seventh Wave with 42 wains  
> Resupply the Wolves of War with 43 wains  
> Resupply the Winter Sun with 43 wains  
> Resupply the Army of the Citadel with 42 wains  
>  
> *Approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies.*

### Overview

- Resupply the [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) army of the [Seventh Wave](../nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md#the-seventh-wave).
- Resupply the [League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) army of the [Wolves of War](../nations/the-league/The_League_military_concerns.md#the-wolves-of-war).
- Resupply the [Imperial orc](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) army of the [Winter Sun](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_territories.md#the-winter-sun).
- Resupply the [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) army of the [Citadel Guard](../mandates/Citadel_Guard.md).

The actions are grouped together, but each would count as a separate announcement for purposes of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).

### Date

- Spring Equinox 378YE

### Progress

- 170 wains of Mithril and Weirwood provided by Janusz Dalibor von Temeschwar, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies, after Spring Equinox 378YE
  - 42 wains for the Seventh Wave
  - 43 wains for the Wolves of War
  - 43 wains for the Winter Sun
  - 42 wains for the Citadel Guard

### Campaign Outcome

- [Resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) an army using 42 wains restores fighting strength equal to 840 soldiers to the army.
- Resupply an army using 43 wains restored fighting strength equal to 860 soldiers to the army.

### Voting

This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General using the legal power to resupply armies granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
