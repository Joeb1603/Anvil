# 378YE Winter Solstice Announcements by the Quartermaster General

### Overview

- Resupply [the Brass Coast](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) army of the [Fire of the South](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#fire-of-the-south) with 38 wains of weirwood.
- This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General using the legal power to resupply armies granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
- This motion would be subject to the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).

### Date

- Winter Solstice 378YE

### Progress

- 38 wains of provided by Irontide Rad, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies, after Winter Solstice 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- [Resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) an army using 38 wains restores fighting strength equal to 760 soldiers to the army.
