# 381YE Autumn Equinox Announcement by the Quartermaster General

> *In-character text:*
>
> Announcement of the Quartermaster’s Guerdon:  
>  Quiet Step  
> Bounders  
> Hounds of Glory  
> Granite Pillar  
> Wolves of War  
> Are entitled to draw from the Imperial Guerdon following the Autumn Equinox 381YE.  
>  
> *Approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies*

### Overview

- This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies using the legal power to authorise targets of the Imperial Guerdon granted to them by the Senate

### Date

- Autumn 381YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Owners of military units who support one of the announced armies will receive a share of the Imperial Guerdon.
