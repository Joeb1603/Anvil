# 379YE Spring Equinox Announcements by the Quartermaster General

> *In-character text:*
>
> The Quartermaster General makes the announcement that he intends to use his power of Emergency Resupply:
>
> - 50 Wains to the Bounders
> - 50 Wains to the Citadel Guard
> - 2 Wains to the Wolves of War

### Overview

- Resupply [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) army of the [Bounders](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#bounders) with 50 wains.
- Resupply the [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) army of [Citadel Guard](../nations/urizen/Urizen_military_concerns.md#the-army-of-the-citadel) with 50 wains.
- Resupply [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) army of [Wolves of War](../nations/the-league/The_League_military_concerns.md#the-wolves-of-war) with 2 wains.
- This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General using the legal power to resupply armies granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
- This motion would be subject to the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).

### Date

- Spring Equinox 379YE

### Progress

- 80 wains of weirwood and wains of 22 mithril provided by Irontide Rad, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies, after Spring Equinox 379YE

### Campaign Outcome

- [Resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) an army using 50 wains restores fighting strength equal to 1000 soldiers to the army.
- Resupply an army using 2 wains restores fighting strength equal to 40 soldiers to the army.
