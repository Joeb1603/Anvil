# 380YE Spring Equinox Announcement by the Quartermaster General

> *In-character text:*
>
> I, Dan Brackensong, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies announce the resupply of  
>
> The Granite Pillar  
> The Golden Axe  
> The Strong Reeds
>
> *Approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies*

### Overview

- Resupply the Highborn army, the [Granite Pillar](../nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md#the-granite-pillar)
- Resupply the Varushkan army, the [Golden Axe](../nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md#the-army-of-the-golden-axe)
- Resupply the Marcher army, the [Strong Reeds](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#the-strong-reeds)
- This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies using the legal power to resupply armies granted by the Imperial Senate.
