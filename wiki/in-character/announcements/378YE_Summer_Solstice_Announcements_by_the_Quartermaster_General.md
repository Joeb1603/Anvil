# 378YE Summer Solstice Announcements by the Quartermaster General

> *In-character text:*
>
> Resupply the Red Wind Corsairs  
> Resupply the Drakes  
> Resupply the Strong Reeds  
> Resupply the Fire of the South  
>  
> *Approved by the Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies.*

### Overview

- Resupply the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) army of the [Red Wind Corsairs](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#the-red-wind-corsairs).
- Resupply the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) army of the [Fire of the South](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#the-fire-of-the-south).
- Resupply the [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) army of the [Strong Reeds](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#the-strong-reeds).
- Resupply the [Marcher](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) army of the [Drakes](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#the-drakes).
- The actions are grouped together, but each would count as a separate announcement for purposes of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).
- This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General using the legal power to resupply armies granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).

### Date

- Summer Solstice 378YE

### Progress

- 112 wains of Mithril and Weirwood provided by Janusz Dalibor von Temeschwar, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies, after Summer Solstice 378YE
  - 49 wains for the Red Wind Corsairs
  - 43 wains for the Strong Reeds
  - 20 wains for the Drakes
  - 0 wains for the Fire of the South (this army is engaged by [Thule](../foreign-nations/Thule.md) forces in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md))

### Campaign Outcome

- [Resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) an army using 49 wains restores fighting strength equal to 980 soldiers to the army.
- Resupply an army using 43 wains restored fighting strength equal to 860 soldiers to the army.
- Resupply an army using 20 wains restored fighting strength equal to 400 soldiers to the army.
