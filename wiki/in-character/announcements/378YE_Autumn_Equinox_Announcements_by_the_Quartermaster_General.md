# 378YE Autumn Equinox Announcements by the Quartermaster General

### Overview

- Resupply the [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) army of [The Fist of the Mountains](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#the-fist-of-the-mountains) with 39 mithril.
- Resupply the Wintermark army of the [The Green Shield](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#the-green-shield-army) with 40 mithriil.
- Resupply the [Varushkan](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) army of the [Northern Eagle](../nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md#the-army-of-the-northern-eagle) with 43 weirwood.
- The actions are grouped together, but each would count as a separate announcement for purposes of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).
- This motion was approved by the Quartermaster General using the legal power to resupply armies granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).

### Date

- Autumn Equinox 378YE

### Progress

- 79 wains of mithril and 43 wains of weirwood provided by Irontide Rad, Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies, after Autumn Equinox 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- [Resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#emergency-resupply) an army using 50 wains restores fighting strength equal to 1000 soldiers to the army.
