# 380YE Spring Equinox Announcement by the Civilian Commissioner of the Marches

> *In-character text:*
>
> The creation of a sinecure, in Graven, Gravenmarch, Bregasland to produce mana crystals.
>
> The sinecure to be held by the Keeper of the Dour Fens.
>
> Appointment by vote of the Marcher farmers.
>
> Lifetime tenure.
>
> *Approved by the Marcher Civilian Commissioner*

### Overview

- This motion was to create a sinecure in [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) to produce mana crystals.
- This motion was approved by the Marcher Civilian Commissioner using the legal power to commission constructions delegated to them by the Senate.
- This commission was announced after the maximum number of commissions had been authorised, but was announced in case one of the previous commissions had been subject to the synod's [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md). The veto was not employed and so this commission was not authorised.

### Date

- Spring 380YE
