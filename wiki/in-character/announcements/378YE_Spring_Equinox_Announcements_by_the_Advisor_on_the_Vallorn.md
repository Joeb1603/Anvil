# 378YE Spring Equinox Announcements by the Advisor on the Vallorn

>

- [Historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) concerning [Megan Black Blood](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Thunderous_Tread_of_the_Trees.md#description) has been commissioned through the civil service.
