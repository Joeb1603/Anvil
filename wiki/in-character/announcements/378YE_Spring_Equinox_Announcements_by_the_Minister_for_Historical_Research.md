# 378YE Spring Equinox Announcements by the Minister for Historical Research

> *In-character text:*
>
> Commission research into Emperor Guntherm’s campaigns against the Jotun  
> Commission research into the location of the Gryphon's Pride banner  
>  
> *Approved by the Minister of Historical Research.*

### Overview

- [Historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) concerning these two topics has been commissioned through the civil service.
- Actions approved by the [Minister of Historical Research](../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md).
- The actions are grouped together, but each would count as a separate announcement for purposes of the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md).

### Date

- Spring Equinox 378YE

### Progress

- 10 Thrones provided by Ioseph of Phoenix Reach, Minister of Historical Research, after Spring Equinox 378YE

### Campaign Outcome

- Research report concerning the Gryphon's Pride banner provided to the Minister during Summer Solstice 378YE
- Research report concerning the [campaigns of Emperor Guntherm](../nations/wintermark/Campaigns_of_Emperor_Guntherm.md) is still being prepared

### Voting

This motion was approved by the Minister for Historical Research using the legal power to commission research granted by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md).
