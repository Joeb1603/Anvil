# 387YE Spring Equinox Senate sessions

## Overview

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) governs the Empire, and its authority extends to all areas of Imperial life not already covered by the other houses of government. Each summit the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) meet [several times](../../out-of-character/events/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_event_schedule.md) to deliberate and vote on the [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) they have raised.

After the summit the civil service enact those motions, and ensure a public record of each is made available to the citizens of the Empire. This page lists all the motions raised during the summit, along with the [announcements](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) made by citizens with [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md). In each case, the motion and its effects are summarised including the wording; who proposed and seconded it; whether it passed or not; whether any funds were disbursed to support it; and what the outcome of the motion will be.

Many motions (especially the [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md)) also have a *Further Details* section that provides a link to a wiki page dealing specifically with that motion in more detail, any commentary from the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md), progress on commissions, or expanded information about the outcome of the motion.

Following the mandate [enacted](../synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-127) by **Luca i Taziel i Riqueza** after the Winter Solstice 385YE the number of commissions available has been reduced as the faithful have been convinced to withdraw their labour. This will continue until either the Imperial Senate uses a motion to assign fines from the magistrates to the Virtue fund or the [Prosperity Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) uses a clearly worded [Statement of Principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) upheld with a greater majority to encourage the faithful to once again take up their tools. More information can be found in the **[Forwards and Rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md#fines-and-funds)** wind of fortune.

## Administrative Motions

### Create Judgement of Exaltation

- **Motion:** To create a new judgement of the Synod: Exaltation. This judgement can be raised in any virtue assembly to raise up an individual whose actions should be praised and serve as a pattern for virtue of others. To be upheld it must be passed with a greater majority, and the civil service will keep a public roll of names.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Passed**
- **Notes:** This motion was initially proposed by the Conscience of the Senate during the [Winter Solstice 386YE](386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-judgement-of-exaltation). The original motion allowed it to be raised as a variant of sanction, rather than as an entirely new judgement. This type of judgement will only be able to raise in one of the eight [Virtue Assemblies](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies).

### Create Singer of the Silence

- **Motion:** To create a new title; Singer of the Silence. This title is to be appointed by the Wisdom Assembly every year. The Singer of the Silence will have the responsibility to protect the Great Library of Hacynian, and to organise parley with Sung and Phaleron to try and unlock the potential of the enigmatic regio at the library. This title has no clear powers, beyond any potentially granted by the regio.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Passed**
- **Notes:** This motion is raised as a result of the mandate [enacted](../synod-judgements/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-34) by **Nathair Autumngale** after the Winter Solstice 386YE. For more information see the **[Little faith](../winds-of-fortune/Little_faith.md)** wind of fortune.

### Amend Imperial Apothecaries Guild

- **Motion:** To amend the Imperial Apothecaries Guild to have the power to add new potions to Imperial lore. This power will be enacted by vote of the members of the sodality and cost ten thrones. This motion will remove the Senate's power to add new potions to Imperial lore.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Passed**
- **Notes:** This motion follows the motion to [establish](386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-imperial-apothecaries-guild) the [Imperial Apothecaries Guild](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Apothecaries_Guild.md). For more information see the **[It's what we do](../winds-of-fortune/It-s_what_we_do.md)** wind of fortune

### Establish Magpies Sodality

- **Motion:** To create the Imperial Sodality of the Magpies. The sodality will be dedicated to the acquisition, documentation and preservation of artefacts for the Empire, and the uncovering of lost Imperial history. Its mission is: to aid and coordinate Imperial efforts to seek out lost artefacts and historical knowledge; to support the interests of historians and artefact hunters of the Empire; and to ensure accountability, protection, work, and suitable reward for the historians and artefact hunters of the Empire. This will also amend the title of Speaker of the Magpies to be appointed by members of the sodality rather than the Vigilance Assembly.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Passed**
- **Notes:** This motion will establish the Magpies as an [Imperial sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md)

### Amend Keeper of the Breadbasket

- **Motion:** To amend the Keeper of the Breadbasket to be appointed by Tally of the Votes of the farm owners of the Marches. As a title elected by the bourse, it will no longer be revocable by the Synod. This amendment will allow the Keeper of the Breadbasket to direct the breadbasket to support a territory in response to any ongoing emergency that involves the devastation of local farming or a hungry population. The Imperial breadbasket will no longer boost the income of farms if it is used to support a territory or a nation's armies.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Passed**
- **Notes:** This motion follows the farm owners of the Marches voting to implement the changes made, as per the **[Send for me](../winds-of-fortune/Send_for_me.md#plants-and-seeds)** wind of fortune
