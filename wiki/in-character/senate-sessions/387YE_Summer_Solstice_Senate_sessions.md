# 387YE Summer Solstice Senate sessions

## Overview

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) governs the Empire, and its authority extends to all areas of Imperial life not already covered by the other houses of government. Each summit the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) meet [several times](../../out-of-character/events/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_event_schedule.md) to deliberate and vote on the [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) they have raised.

After the summit the civil service enact those motions, and ensure a public record of each is made available to the citizens of the Empire. This page lists all the motions raised during the summit, along with the [announcements](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) made by citizens with [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md). In each case, the motion and its effects are summarised including the wording; who proposed and seconded it; whether it passed or not; whether any funds were disbursed to support it; and what the outcome of the motion will be.

Many motions (especially the [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md)) also have a *Further Details* section that provides a link to a wiki page dealing specifically with that motion in more detail, any commentary from the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md), progress on commissions, or expanded information about the outcome of the motion.

Following the mandate [enacted](../synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-127) by **Luca i Taziel i Riqueza** after the Winter Solstice 385YE the number of commissions available has been reduced as the faithful have been convinced to withdraw their labour. This will continue until either the Imperial Senate uses a motion to assign fines from the magistrates to the Virtue fund or the [Prosperity Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) uses a clearly worded [Statement of Principle](../../mixed/government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) upheld with a greater majority to encourage the faithful to once again take up their tools. More information can be found in the [Forwards and Rewards](../winds-of-fortune/Forwards_and_Rewards.md#fines-and-funds) wind of fortune.

### Redefine Advisor on the Vallorn

The following three motions will be brought on Saturday evening session, in an order chosen by the Speaker of the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md). If an earlier motion passes, the others will be withdrawn. If none of the three pass, then the position of [Advisor on the Vallorn](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_the_Vallorn.md) is [no longer able to be appointed](../winds-of-fortune/Eight_for_a_wish.md#role-of-the-advisor).

- **Motion:** To reassign the title of Advisor on the Vallorn, making it an Imperial title appointed by the Vallornguard sodality. The title would no longer be responsible for protecting the Great Library of Hacynian; this duty will instead be transferred to the Singer of the Silence.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Passed**
- **Notes:** The Advisor on the Vallorn has become an Imperial title appointed by the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md), and the [Singer of the Silence](../../mixed/titles/Singer_of_the_Silence.md) is now responsible for protecting the Great Library at Hacynian. The Advisor retains the power to address the Senate and could still be given additional powers, such as address the Military Council.

- **Motion:** To amend the title of Advisor on the Vallorn, to remove the ability to address the Senate and the responsibility to advise the Senate and protect the Empire. The title will instead gain the power to address the Vallornguard, and the responsibility to advise the sodality in their mission.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Withdrawn**
- **Notes:** If passed, the title holder would have gained the power to make a single, short address during a formal meeting of the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md) each summit, even if not themselves a member. They would have remained the custodian of the Great Library of Hacynian and would have continued to be appointed by Navarr Senators.

- **Motion:** To amend the title of Advisor on the Vallorn, removing its power to address the Senate and its responsibility to advise the Senate and protect the Empire. The title will be renamed *Custodian of Hacynian*, and its sole responsibility will be the oversight and protection of the Great Library at Hacynian.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Withdrawn**
- **Notes:** The renamed Custodian of Hacynian would have remained a Navarr Senator appointed title and a respected authority on the vallorn and the history of Terunael, but would have held no legal powers. Their duties would focus entirely on safeguarding the Library and the invaluable lore it contains.

### Establish the Cat's Graces

- **Motion:** Create the Imperial Sodality of The Cat's Graces. The sodality is dedicated to art and music. It holds the following to be true: Arts are for everyone to enjoy, no matter their skill; Virtue demands that we give a space for joy as well as for work; The value of a nation is judged by its culture as much as its might. The sodality therefore exists to: To promote and encourage the arts and creatives in Anvil and across the Empire; To encourage all people and nations, within and outside the Empire, to teach each other through their art; To hold social events to enjoy and promote the arts in Anvil and outside it. The symbol of the sodality is a cat holding a scroll. The Imperial title 'Maestro' will be elected by the sodality, with the responsibility to represent the Cat's Graces views to other parts of the Empire, to recruit new members, and to advance the sodality's goals and the members' interests.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Failed**
- **Notes:** This motion required a [Constitutional Vote](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md#constitutional-vote).

### Establish the Artisans Guild

- **Motion:** Create the Imperial Sodality of the Artisans Guild. The Imperial Artisans Guild holds the following to be true: Artisans and the items they create are valuable strategic resources for the Empire; Artisans deserve to be compensated fairly for their craft; Artisans are the citizens best placed to make decisions that affect artisanry. The Artisans Guild therefore exists to: Provide aid and guidance on artisanry across the Empire; Support and coordinate the efforts of artisans in the creation and innovation of magic items; Represent the Empire's artisans and support opportunities for them within our borders and beyond. The symbol is an anvil with the Xun rune. The title of Guildmaster of the Artisans will be amended to be elected by the sodality, and gain the responsibility to represent the Artisans Guild's views to other parts of the Empire, to recruit new members, and to advance the sodality's goals and the members' interests.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate
- **Failed**
- **Notes:** This motion required a [Constitutional Vote](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md#constitutional-vote).
