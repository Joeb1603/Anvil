# 388YE Spring Equinox Senate sessions

## Overview

*[Image: Art by Fedelmidd Sydanjaa's Heart Floer](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Speakers_of_the_Senate.jpg)*

The [Imperial Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) governs the Empire, and its authority extends to all areas of Imperial life not already covered by the other houses of government. Each summit the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) meet [several times](../../out-of-character/events/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_event_schedule.md) to deliberate and vote on the [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) they have raised.

After the summit, the civil service enacts those motions and ensures a public record of each is made available to the citizens of the Empire. This page lists all the motions raised during the summit, along with the [announcements](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) made by citizens with [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md). In each case, the motion and its effects are summarised, including the wording, who proposed and seconded it, whether it passed or not, whether any funds were disbursed to support it, and what the outcome of the motion will be.

Many motions (especially the [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md)) also have a *Further Details* section that provides a link to a wiki page dealing specifically with that motion in more detail, any commentary from the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md), progress on commissions, or expanded information about the outcome of the motion.

## Administrative Motions

### Acknowledge Axou claim to Thornfen

- **Motion:** The Empire will not contest the claim of the Axou to the region of Thornfen, including the Tower of the Skink, in accordance with the existing treaty between the Empire and the Axou.
- **Raised by:** Speaker for the Senate during the Friday session
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** This follows from civil service advice in the **[Other monstrous geographies](../winds-of-war/Other_monstrous_geographies.md#game-information-dominion)** wind of war and the [current treaty](../senate-motions/Ratify_Axos_Treaty_Autumn_387YE.md) with the Axou. This motion will be subject to the Urizen assembly to [veto](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md), as it concedes a region under their control.

### Amend Master of the Mint

- **Motion:** Amend the Master of the Imperial Mint to give them the power to make an announcement in the Senate once a season to direct the civil service to review a previous appraisal or opportunity.
- **Raised by:** Speaker of the Senate at the Friday evening session
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** This follows on from a previous motion to hire the [three additional prognosticators](387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#hire-prognosticators) raised during Winter 387YE. If this motion is passed, then the [Master of the Imperial Mint](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md) would gain the power to make an announcement in the Senate to review a previous [appraisal](../appraisals/Appraisal.md) or [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md), as per the **[Forever is composed of nows](../winds-of-fortune/Forever_is_composed_of_nows.md#opportunity-knocks-again)** wind of fortune. If this motion is passed, then the Master of the Imperial Mint will be able to make use of the power at one of the following sessions.

### Amend Liberty Pact

- **Motion:** To make the following amendments to the Liberty Pact: 1) The requirement that each signatory to the Liberty Pact enforce free trade with the other signatories should be replaced with a prohibition on embargo between members of the Pact. Members of the Pact should trade with each other, but should be free to set their tariffs to best support themselves. 2) The creation of two degrees of Membership. Members of the Pact will have full rights and voting ability. Signatories will be protected by the Liberty Pact, and have the rights due all members, but will not be permitted to raise amendments or vote.
- **Raised by:** Speaker of the Senate at the Saturday Afternoon session
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Amend Imperial Consul

- **Motion:** To amend the position of Imperial Consul to add the following power: Once per summit, the Imperial Consul may make an announcement ameding the Empire's trading terms with a foreign nation.
- **Proposed:** Lucrezia Corvinascura, as [Citizens' Voice](../../mixed/titles/Citizens-_Voice.md) at the Sunday afternoon session
- **Passed**
- **Outcome:** Passed scrutiny with clarifications.
- **Constitution:** The Imperial Consul has been given the power to amend the Empire's trading terms with a foreign nation once per summit, *where no position of Imerial Ambassdor exists for that nation*. The authorty of the Imperial Consul is restricted to dealing with nations where no position of Imperial Ambassador exist for strict constitutional reasons. The Court will not permit a situation to arise where the Consul is perceived to have authority over the ambassadors. While the power to set trading arrangements is distinct from the legal power to create a treaty, there is too much overlap in the implied responsibilities to allow the Consul to set terms with a foreign power where an ambassador exists

### Establish Cat's Graces

- **Motion:** To establish the Cat's Grace, an Imperial Sodality dedicated to art and music. It holds the following to be true: Arts are for everyone to enjoy, no matter their skill level; Virtue demands that we give space for joy as well as for work; The value of a nation is judged by its culture as much as its might. The Sodality therefore exists to: Support and promote creatives, providing opportunities to exhibit their talents; Share learning through the arts, fostering understanding between peoples and traditions; Ensure creators are valued, and that their work is recognised and fairly rewarded.
