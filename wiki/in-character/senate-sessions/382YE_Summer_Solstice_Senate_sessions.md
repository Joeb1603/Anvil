# 382YE Summer Solstice Senate sessions

## Overview

- Imperial treasury during this season announced by the Civil Service at 806 thrones and 4 crowns.
- The following has been donated to the Imperial treasury:
  - 8 thrones donated to the Senate treasury by **Imperatrix Lisabetta von Holberg** after the Summer Solstice 382YE

## Administrative

An administrative motion was raised by the civil service.

- The Civil Service would like the Senate to confirm whether the [Arcane Architect](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Architect.md) can construct sinecures that provide vis, rather than only crystal mana. Motion Passed.

## Announcements

- [Hand of the Chancellor](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#the-hand-of-the-chancellor) was exercised by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) to the amount of 212 Thrones.
- The Quartermaster General announced that the Imperial Guerdon for Summer 382 is to go to the following armies: Hounds of Glory, Quiet Step, Drakes
- The Quartermaster General announced that [all armies](../senate-motions/Resupply_Imperial_armies_Summer_382YE.md) would receive emergency resupply this season.
- The [Keeper of the Breadbasket](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Breadbasket.md) announced that the breadbasket shall be directed to support Imperial armies.
- The [Advisor on the Vallorn](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_the_Vallorn.md) announced plans to research the life and eventual final fate of the Noble Terran General Bryony. The one who wore the crystal crown, forged in the last runeforge with a schemata granted by Zakalwe, the Weapon-wise. The crown led Bryony to lead a crystal army leading to her epitaph of Commander of the Crystal Hope. The results were published [here](../nations/navarr/General_Bryony_and_the_Crystal_Crown.md).
- The intention to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) was announced by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md), but was later withdrawn.
- An announcement by Vilkas Blake, [bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), to [Construct Prosperity of Afal](../senate-motions/Construct_Prosperity_of_Afal.md)
- An announcement by Silvio di Tassato, bearer of an Imperial Wayleave, to [Construct the Thorned Rose](../senate-motions/Construct_the_Thorned_Rose.md)
- An announcement by Edmundo, bearer of an Imperial Wayleave, to [Construct Legacy of Wisdom](../senate-motions/Construct_Legacy_of_Wisdom.md)
