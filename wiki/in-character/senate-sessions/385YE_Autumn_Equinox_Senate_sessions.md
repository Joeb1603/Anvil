# 385YE Autumn Equinox Senate sessions

## Overview

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) governs the Empire, and its authority extends to all areas of Imperial life not already covered by the other houses of government. Each summit the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) meet [several times](../../out-of-character/events/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_event_schedule.md) to deliberate and vote on the [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) they have raised.

After the summit the civil service enact those motions, and ensure a public record of each is made available to the citizens of the Empire. This page lists all the motions raised during the summit, along with the [announcements](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) made by citizens with [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md). In each case, the motion and its effects are summarised including the wording; who proposed and seconded it; whether it passed or not; whether any funds were disbursed to support it; and what the outcome of the motion will be.

Many motions (especially the [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md)) also have a *Further Details* section that provides a link to a wiki page dealing specifically with that motion in more detail, any commentary from the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md), progress on commissions, or expanded information about the outcome of the motion.

### Create title of Benefactor of Regalia

- **Motion:** To create the title of Benefactor of Regalia who will have the power of announcement to the Senate once per session to amend a title to grant it regalia, to be appointed by the unanimous decision of the Highborn Senators. To have the responsibility to seek out titles which would benefit from regalia and aid citizen in this. Revoked as standard. Proposed: [Necropolis](../nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#recent-senate-elections), Seconded: [Casinea](../nations/highguard/Casinea.md#recent-senate-elections).
- **Passed**
- **Outcome:** The [Benefactor of Regalia](../../mixed/titles/Benefactor_of_Regalia.md) will be available to appoint during the Winter Solstice 385YE.
- **Constitution:** The ability to make an announcement in the Imperial Senate is one of the most significant powers that a title can possess - it circumvents the need for a vote and hands significant power directly to the individual citizen appointed. Even a relatively minor legal change, such as the allocation of regalia to an Imperial position, represents an important and substantial act and one which has ramifications for other Imperial titles with similar powers. As such the court has ruled that the Benefactor of the Regalia can make one announcement once each summit, not one announcement *each session*. This announcement can only amend a single title, but the title can be granted any number of items of regalia in a single announcement. If the Imperial Senate feels that that is insufficient to deal with the need for regalia to be properly dealt with, then the Court advise that the best way to deal with that would be to create additional new Imperial titles with the same power.
