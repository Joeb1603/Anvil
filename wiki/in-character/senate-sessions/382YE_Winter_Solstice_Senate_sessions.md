## Announcements

- [Hand of the Chancellor](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#the-hand-of-the-chancellor) was exercised by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) to the amount of 350 Thrones.
- Announcement by Palerictus Krunk, [bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), of their intent to [Construct Emilio's Herbarium](../senate-motions/Construct_Emilio-s_Herbarium.md).
- Announcement by Nikolovich Drakov, [bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), of their intent to [Construct the Hearth of Virtue](../senate-motions/Construct_the_Hearth_of_Virtue.md).
- Announcement by Tyr Brennan, [bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), of their intent to [Construct Freya's Garden](../senate-motions/Construct_Freya-s_Garden.md).
- The [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) announced that the Imperial Guerdon for Winter 382YE will be shared between the Green Shield, Quiet Step, Strong Reeds, Wolves of War, Hounds of Glory, and the [raid to free Grendel slaves](../winds-of-fortune/Unleashing_the_storm.md#beorath) from Beoraidh.
- The [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) is [resupplying the Valiant Pegasus and the Seventh Wave](../senate-motions/Resupply_Announcement_Winter_382YE.md).
- The [Advisor on the Vallorn](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_the_Vallorn.md) announced plans to [Research the life of Drustan](../senate-motions/Research_the_life_of_Drustan.md).
- The [Minister of Historical Research](../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) announced plans to [Research Sungold Pass](../senate-motions/Research_Sungold_Pass.md).
