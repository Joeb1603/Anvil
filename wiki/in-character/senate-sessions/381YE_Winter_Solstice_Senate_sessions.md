# 381YE Winter Solstice Senate sessions

## Overview

- Imperial treasury during this season announced by the Civil Service at 1433 thrones and 4 crowns.
- The following has been donated to the Imperial treasury: None.

## Administrative

This administrative motion was raised as the first order of business at the first Senate session on Friday.

- [Amend powers of the Advisor on the Vallorn](../senate-motions/Amend_powers_of_the_Advisor_on_the_Vallorn.md) to include [Custodianship](../../mixed/titles/New_Imperial_titles.md#custodian) of the [Great Library of Hacynian](../../mixed/titles/Advisor_on_the_Vallorn.md#the-great-library-of-hacynian) - first order of business, Friday
