# 388YE Autumn Equinox Senate sessions

## Overview

The [Imperial Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) governs the Empire, and its authority extends to all areas of Imperial life not already covered by the other houses of government. Each summit the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) meet [several times](../../out-of-character/events/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_event_schedule.md) to deliberate and vote on the [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) they have raised.

After the summit, the civil service enacts those motions and ensures a public record of each is made available to the citizens of the Empire. This page lists all the motions raised during the summit, along with the [announcements](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) made by citizens with [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md). In each case, the motion and its effects are summarised, including the wording, who proposed and seconded it, whether it passed or not, whether any funds were disbursed to support it, and what the outcome of the motion will be.

Many motions (especially the [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md)) also have a *Further Details* section that provides a link to a wiki page dealing specifically with that motion in more detail, any commentary from the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md), progress on commissions, or expanded information about the outcome of the motion.

## Administrative Motions

### Assign Bregasland

- **Motion:** To assign the territory of Bregasland
- **Raised by:** Speaker of the Senate at the Saturday afternoon session
- **Outcome:** Assigned to [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md)
- **Further Details:** The territory of [Bregasland](../nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) has been assigned to [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). The election for Senator of Bregasland will take place at the Winter Solstice 388YE.

### Abrogate Vicente School and Orphanage

- **Motion:** To abrogate the construction of the Vicente School and Orphanage, abrogating the title Custodian of the Vicente School and Orphanage
- **Raised by:** Speaker of the Senate at the Friday session
- **Passed**
- **Outcome:** The [Custodian of the Vicente School and Orphanage](../../mixed/government/senate/Custodian_of_the_Vicente_School_and_Orphanage.md) has been abrogated and is no longer available to be appointed by the senators of the League
- **Further Details:** Following the revelation of a lair under the dominion of the [Whisper Gallery](../eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md), and its subsequent [disappearance](../winds-of-fortune/This_is_the_end.md), the Vicente School and Orphanage is no longer active. This motion would abrogate the [Custodian of the Vicente School and Orphanage](../../mixed/government/senate/Custodian_of_the_Vicente_School_and_Orphanage.md), preventing it from being appointed again.

### Amend Knight of the Greenwood

- **Motion:** To amend the Knight of the Greenwood to become a Dawnish title appointed by the Vallornguard. This will open Greenwood Manor to citizens of every nation, allowing military units from across the Empire to take the quest against the vallorn venture
- **Raised by:** Speaker of the Senate at the Friday session
- **Passed**
- **Outcome:** The [Knight of the Greenwood](../../mixed/titles/Knight_of_the_Greenwood.md) has been amended to be elected by the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md). [Military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) from across the Empire will be able to undertake the [Quest Against the Vallorn](../../mixed/economy/Venture.md#quest-against-the-vallorn) venture.
- **Further Details:** This motion follows the [mandate](../synod-judgements/388YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-58) enacted by **Lord Reynard Griffinsong**. If the motion is passed, then the [Knight of the Greenwood](../../mixed/titles/Knight_of_the_Greenwood.md) will be appointed by the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md) the next time it falls vacant after the Autumn Equinox 388YE.

### Abrogate Marr's Mine

- **Motion:** Abrogate Marr's Mine, on the advice of the Senator of Kallavesa
- **Raised by:** Speaker of the Senate
- **Passed**
- **Outcome:** The motion to [Expand Marr's Mine](#expand-marrs-mine) was abrogated
- **Notes:** This motion was raised to allow Senate to reconsider its commission options after civil service paperwork error. Any senator could propose one of the existing commission motions to abrogate. The senator of Kallavesa was the only senator to make a proposal.

### Consult Foreigners on Magic

- **Motion:** Instruct the civil service to consult all foreign powers that control land in an Imperial territory whenever there is an opportunity to employ beneficial magic in that territory
- **Raised by:** Speaker of the Senate
- **Failed**
- **Notes:** This motion was raised following a [proposal](../winds-of-fortune/The_little_things.md#outstretched-hands) from the Thule. It would have allowed the civil service to incorporate a full assessment of the consequences and reactions by any foreigners living in Imperial territory to a briefing on Imperial magic used on that territory. It would have made all affected foreign powers fully aware of any opportunity that could have affected them, even if it were not pursued
