# 385YE Summer Solstice Senate sessions

## Overview

The [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) governs the Empire, and its authority extends to all areas of Imperial life not already covered by the other houses of government. Each summit the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) meet several times to deliberate and vote on the [motions](../senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) they have raised.

After the summit the civil service enact those motions, and ensure a public record of each is made available to the citizens of the Empire. This page lists all the motions raised during the summit, along with the [announcements](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) made by citizens with [Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md). In each case, the motion and its effects are summarised including the wording; who proposed and seconded it; whether it passed or not; whether any funds were disbursed to support it; and what the outcome of the motion will be.

Many motions (especially the [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md)) also have a *Further Details* section that provides a link to a wiki page dealing specifically with that motion in more detail, any commentary from the [Constitutional Court](../senate-motions/Scrutiny.md), progress on commissions, or expanded information about the outcome of the motion.
