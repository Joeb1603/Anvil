# 383YE Autumn Equinox Senate sessions

## Overview

- Imperial treasury during this season announced by the Civil Service at 1949 thrones and 2 crowns.
- The following has been donated to the Imperial treasury:

  - 10 thrones donated to the treasury by the Chapter Reumah's Redoubt after Autumn Equinox 383YE
