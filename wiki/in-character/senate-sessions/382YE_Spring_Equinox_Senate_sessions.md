# 382YE Spring Equinox Senate sessions

## Overview

- Imperial treasury during this season announced by the Civil Service at 1514 thrones and 4 crowns.
- The following has been donated to the Imperial treasury:
  - 75 thrones were returned to the treasury by **Hengest Dun** as the [motion](../senate-motions/Construct_Runeforge.md) the funds were received for has expired
  - 10 thrones were contributed to the treasury by **Hengest Dun** to cover the costs to [dispatch prospectors to the Pride of Ikka's Tears](../senate-motions/Dispatch_prospectors_to_the_Pride_of_Ikka-s_Tears.md)
  - 30 thrones donated to the Senate treasury by **Thanmir Hrafnar** after the Spring Equinox 382YE
  - 60 thrones donated to the Senate treasury by **Imperatrix Lisabetta von Holberg** after the Spring Equinox 382YE

## Administrative

This administrative motion was raised as the first order of business at the first Senate session on Friday.

## Announcements

- [Hand of the Chancellor](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#the-hand-of-the-chancellor) was exercised by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) to the amount of 504 Thrones and 4 Crowns.
- The Quartermaster General announced that the Imperial Guerdon for Spring 382 is to go to the following armies: The Wolves of War, The Fire of the South, the Bloodcloaks, and the Tusks.
- The intention to [address the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) was announced by [Imperatrix Lisabetta](../history/Empress_Lisabetta.md), but was later withdrawn.
- Research into anomalous conjunctions, particularly the last conjunction of Empress Britta was announced by the [Minister of Historical Research](../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md).
- Research into the past Life of Adelina Barossa was announced by the Minister of Historical Research.

### Commissions

A total of 14 [commissions](../../mixed/commissions/Commission.md) were announced during the Winter Solstice: 9 standard commissions, 3 wayleaves, and 2 under the auspices of [Almodin Oktístis](../winds-of-fortune/Those_who_worship.md#almodin-oktc3adstis2c-priest-of-the-builder).

- An announcement by the [Arcane Architect](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Architect.md) of intention to [construct a summer vis sinecure](../senate-motions/Construct_summer_sinecure.md) in [Reikos](../nations/highguard/Reikos.md).
- An announcement by Selenius, [bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), to [construct a trading enclave in Peak Edge](../senate-motions/Construct_Peak_Edge_trading_enclave.md).
- An announcement by Jorgen Brenna, bearer of an Imperial Wayleave, to [Construct Brenna’s Gift](../senate-motions/Construct_Brenna-s_Gift.md).
- An announcement by Lorenzo La Volpe, bearer of an Imperial Wayleave, to [Construct the Azure Chorus](../senate-motions/Construct_the_Azure_Chorus.md). .
