# 383YE Summer Solstice Senate sessions

## Overview

- Imperial treasury during this season announced by the Civil Service at 397 thrones and 2 crowns.
- The following has been donated to the Imperial treasury:
  - 28 thrones donated to the treasury by **Severin Teyhard von Holberg**, Acting Prince of the House of Seven Mirrors after the Summer Solstice 383YE
  - 10 thrones donated to the treasury by the Chapter Reumah's Redoubt after the Summer Solstice 383YE

### Announcements

- Hand of the Chancellor: The Throne exercised her power of Hand of the Chancellor to withdraw no (zero) funds from this season’s residual income (Zero Thrones) to the Imperial Treasury.
- The Throne announced through her power of [Voice of the Empire](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#address-the-empire) that [General](../../mixed/titles/General.md) Gabriel Barossa shall address the Empire following the Summer Solstice of 383YE.
- The [Keeper of the Breadbasket](../../mixed/titles/Keeper_of_the_Breadbasket.md) announced that they would spread the bounty of the breadbasket across Mitwold, Upwold, and Bregasland, to strengthen Marcher traditions.
- The [Minister of Historical Research](../../mixed/titles/Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) announced a project to [Research the origins of the Highborn](../senate-motions/Research_the_origins_of_the_Highborn.md)
- The [Quartermaster General of the Imperial Armies](../../mixed/titles/Quartermaster_General_of_the_Imperial_Armies.md) announced that the Guerdon would be applied to the following armies for the coming season: the raid on Ikka’s Tears, the Green Shield, the Red Wind Corsairs, the Tusks, the Granite Pillar, the Strong Reeds, and the Northern Eagle.
- [Imperial Wayleave](../../mixed/economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md): Announcement by Tristan Exile’s End of intent to [Construct Misericorde Market](../senate-motions/Construct_Misericorde_Market.md).
- Imperial Wayleave: Announcement by Dragomir Dragovich of intent to [Construct Iron Roundtower](../senate-motions/Construct_Iron_Roundtower.md)
- Imperial Orc Wayleave: Announcement by Palerictus Krunk of intent to [Construct Smith of Ashes](../senate-motions/Construct_Smith_of_Ashes.md).
