# 379YE Spring Equinox Senate sessions

## Overview

- Imperial treasury during this season announced by the Civil Service at 1333 thrones.
- The following has been donated to the Imperial treasury:
  - 80 thrones donated to the treasury by Senator Weirwater after the Spring Equinox 379YE
  - 11 thrones 4 crowns donated to the treasury by Senator Upwold after the Spring Equinox 379YE
