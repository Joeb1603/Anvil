# 387YE Autumn Equinox Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana sent for distribution to the grandmasters following 387YE Summer was 126 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 4 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 387YE Summer Conclave was 355 mana.**
- **The mana used to purchase the Rogue Declaration at 387YE Summer Conclave was 15 mana.**
- **The mana from the Thule as part of the ongoing treaty was 20 mana.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 387YE Autumn is 394 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 126 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 387YE Summer Solstice. This was distributed to the grandmasters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Precedence as per the table below.

This left 4 mana, which is added to the 355 mana collected at Conclave and the 15 mana from the [rogue declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) at the previous event, along with 20 mana from the Thule as part of the [ongoing treaty](../senate-motions/Ratify_Thule_Treaty_Spring_387YE.md) to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 394 mana at the 387YE Autumn Equinox.

## Friday

### Concord: Grand Lodge for Sevenfold Path

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Elagabala, on behalf of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md)
- **Declaration:** The Conclave asks for plans for a Grand Lodge of the Sevenfold Path in Sanctuary, Sanctuary Sands, Necropolis. It would stand in remembrance of the magician-paragon Atun and their Virtue in voyaging to the continent, and looking to the horizon as a promise that this Empire will bring the light of Virtue to everyone, everywhere.
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** More information on this opportunity can be found in the **[Volumes of forgotten lore](../winds-of-fortune/Volumes_of_forgotten_lore.md#grand-lodges)** wind of fortune

### Candidacy: Archmage of Autumn

- **Outcome:** **Vossk of Zenith Ascendant** was elected as the [Archmage of Autumn](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Autumn.md)
