# 388YE Autumn Equinox Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana sent for distribution to the grandmasters following 388YE Summer was 64 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 3 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 388YE Summer Conclave was 296 mana.**
- **The mana used to purchase the Rogue Declaration at 388YE Summer Conclave was 30 mana.**
- **Twenty mana was provided by Thule warlocks as part of their treaty obligations with the Empire.**
- **Fifteen mana was provided by the grand lodge of the Golden Pyramid.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 388YE Autumn is 364 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 64 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 388YE Summer Solstice. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Precedence as per the table below.

This left 3 mana, which is added to the 296 mana collected at Conclave, the 15 mana from the Grand Lodge of the Golden Pyramid, the 20 mana from the agreement with the Thule, and the 30 mana from the [rogue declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 364 mana at the 388YE Autumn Equinox.

## Friday

### Interdiction: Cast Off The Chain of Memory

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Fiorente Vicente d'Apulian, on behalf of the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
- **Declaration:** Vigilance demands caution in placing memories in the river when Apeiron the Sixth Eye is consuming them
- **Outcome:** Passed. Performing [Cast Off the Chain of Memory](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Cast_Off_The_Chain_of_Memory.md) has been added to the list of interdictions.

### Concord: Oracle Pig and Friends

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Tyburn Natkysset, on behalf of the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md)
- **Declaration:** "Go Oracle Pig and friends, you can beat the Eater of I, we believe in you"
- **Outcome:** Passed.

### Endowment: Vossk of Sarangrave Reforged

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Elagabala, on behalf of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md)
- **Declaration:** All that is worthwhile is shared with those who deserve it. This Conclave endows 60 mana to Vossk of Sarangrave Reforged, to pay the other half of the endorsed projection Fortune Favours the Bold in recognition of its completion and Virtue.
- **Outcome:** Passed. 60 mana crystals delivered to **Vossk of Sarangrave Reforged**

### Concord: Invite High House of Wisdom

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Xanthippe Of the Lighthouse-That-Was, on behalf of the [Celestial Arch](../../mixed/government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md)
- **Declaration:** This Conclave invites magicians from the High House of Wisdom to visit the Empire and work with the Celestial Arch to review rituals in Imperial lore that draw on the assurance of an eternal.
- **Outcome:** Passed.

### Imperial Lore: Chance Blossoms, Chaos Thrives

- **Raised by:** Yarvi Swiftwind, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)
- **Declaration:** To enter into Imperial lore "Chance Blossoms, Chaos Thrives" created by the Grandmaster of the Silver Chalice, with support from the order, with thanks to the Wise Flower chapter of Highguard who have promised to pay the 10 thrones for distribution of the ritual
- **Outcome:** Passed. Funded by the [Wise Flower chapter](../winds-of-fortune/Apparently_with_no_surprise.md#a-decrepit-flower)

### Imperial Lore: Shadows Hide the Passing Spear

- **Raised by:** Syl, as [Dean of the Lyceum](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md)
- **Declaration:** Shadows Hide the Passing Spear
- **Outcome:** Passed. Funding provided by **Seren Embercast** on behalf of the [Vallornguard](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md)

### Acceptance: Unifying Pontifex and the League

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Terentia of Sarangrave Reforged, on behalf of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md)
- **Declaration:** For the whole to prosper we have to look to those within one borders - the Conclave chooses the aid of the Unifying Pontifex in efforts to bring non-citizens in the Empire into the Empire. The League's highest calling.
- **Outcome:** Passed.

### Concord: Chosen of Eternals

- **Raised by:** Ilysses Of the Lighthouse-That-Was, on behalf of the [Rod and Shield](../../mixed/government/conclave/Rod_and_Shield.md)
- **Declaration:** It is the position of the Conclave that it is acceptable for an eternal to have a named, favoured individual. It is the business of Senate to determine whether these delegates are to receive Imperial positions.
- **Outcome:** Passed.

### Neutrality: Meraud

- **Raised by:** Vesper Lucentia, on behalf of the [Archmage of Summer](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Summer.md)
- **Declaration:** Since his victory over Jaheris, the behaviour of the two eternals has shifted. Recent missives from the Silver Fox have given cause for concern in how he views his amity. Until we have gained a greater understanding of this new nature, and his intent, Meraud shall remain at neutrality
- **Outcome:** Passed. [Meraud](../eternals/Meraud.md) has been moved to [neutral](../eternals/Alignment.md#neutality)

### Amity: Roshanwe

- **Raised by:** Gralka, as [Archmage of Day](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Day.md)
- **Declaration:** In a voyage full of questions, why found an answer. But that led only to further questions in this journey. How does the seeker of the star help the world repel the Cold Sun? Tonight we ask the Conclave to change the alignment of Roshanwe to amity to find this out. And perhaps by doing so meet Little Lamp before they assume their position among our civil service.
- **Outcome:** Passed. [Roshanwe](../eternals/Roshanwe.md) has been moved to [amity](../eternals/Alignment.md#amity)

### Enmity: Ungholt
