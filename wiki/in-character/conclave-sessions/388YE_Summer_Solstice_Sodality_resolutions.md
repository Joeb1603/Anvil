# 388YE Summer Solstice Sodality resolutions

## Overview

The [Imperial sodalities](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) of the Empire have potentially far-reaching powers. Each season, the members of Imperial sodalities meet to vote on elections, bequests, and other actions that their sodality might take.

Where possible, the civil service records these votes and presents the results after each summit.

## Apothecaries Guild

## Cat's Graces

### Appointments

- **Lady Ralphine Sepulchre** was appointed [Maestro of the Cat's Graces](../../mixed/titles/Cat-s_Graces.md#maestro)
- **João i Cabral i Riqueza** was appointed [Azure Sutannir](../../mixed/titles/Azure_Sutannir.md)
- **Pjesmina Zbirka** was appointed [High Bard of the Empire](../../mixed/titles/High_Bard_of_the_Empire.md)

### Other business

- **[First Voice Glade](../../mixed/titles/Dean_of_the_First_Voice_Glade.md#first-voice-glade)** was selected as the location for a [repository of art](../winds-of-fortune/Arranging_grace.md#art-for-arts-sake)
- The piece ***[Morning Light](../../mixed/music/Morning_Light.md)*** was selected for [preservation](../../mixed/titles/Cat-s_Graces.md#preserve-art)
- The commission of **[Dancer's Rest](../../mixed/titles/Hand_to_the_Dance.md)** was selected for [refinement](../../mixed/titles/Cat-s_Graces.md#refine-commission)
- No disbursements were made from the Patron's Fund; it ended the summit at 5 thrones
