# 384YE Spring Equinox Conclave sessions

- **The basic font at this summit was 0 mana.**
- **The income from the Principle of Proportions was 295 mana.**
- **The remainder from previous seasons was 2 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). The income of mana following the previous summit is listed above and was distributed as per the table below. This left 4 mana, which will be added to the font of the next summit along with the income from the Principle of Proportions at this summit.

| Order | Precedence | Members | Mana | Ilium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Rod and Shield | 1 | 81 | 66 | 3 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Shuttered Lantern | 2 | 68 | 55 | 3 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Sevenfold Path | 3 | 55 | 45 | 2 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Unfettered Mind | 4 | 47 | 38 | 2 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Golden Pyramid | 5 | 46 | 37 | 1 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Silver Chalice | 6 | 41 | 33 | 1 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Celestial Arch | 7 | 24 | 19 | 1 |

## Saturday Declarations

### Reconciliation: 'Ancient Vallorn Text'

- **Raised by:** Simargl, the Empty One
- **Declaration:** This Conclave removes the interdiction from 'Ancient Vallorn Text'
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Concord: Magicians Offenses

- **Raised by:** Cassia
- **Declaration:** That Conclave petitions the Senate to consider an offense for magicians in Conclave similar to that of abuses of the powers of the priest
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Concord: Outside Observers

- **Raised by:** Zephaniah
- **Declaration:** This Conclave reminds outside observers that it has no say in which questions it votes on and that considering a matter repeatedly does not mean Conclave is wavering or changing its opinions.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Concord: Controlling Regio Use

- **Raised by:** Tiberius Cascade
- **Declaration:** This Conclave requests that the senate create a position with the authority to control the usage of the regio for the last 2 hours of each summit to prioritise the important strategic magic
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Imperial Lore: Leonine Pavilion

- **Raised by:** Sagua i Ezmara
- **Declaration:** To accept [Leonine Pavilion](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Leonine_Pavilion.md) into Imperial Lore.

### Concord: Who should be part of Conclave?

### Interdiction: Almery of Silence

### Concord: Sadogua and the Vallorn

- **Raised by:** Laelius
