# 387YE Autumn Equinox Sodality resolutions

## Overview

The [Imperial sodalities](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) of the Empire have potentially far-reaching powers. Each season, the members of Imperial sodalities meet to vote on elections, bequests, and other actions that their sodality might take.

Where possible, the civil service records these votes and presents the results after each summit.
