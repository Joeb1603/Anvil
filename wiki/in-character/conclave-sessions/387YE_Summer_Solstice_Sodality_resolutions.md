# 387YE Summer Solstice Sodality resolutions

## Overview

The [Imperial sodalities](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) of the Empire have potentially far-reaching powers. Each season, the members of Imperial sodalities meet to vote on elections, bequests, and other actions that their sodality might take.

Where possible, the civil service records these votes and presents the results after each summit.

## Apothecaries Guild

## Magpies

### Appointments

- **Morgan Waystone Path** was appointed as [Speaker of the Magpies](../../mixed/titles/Magpies.md#speaker)

## Sun Chamber

### Appointments

- **Zoria** was appointed as [Counsel of the Sun Chamber](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#counsel-of-the-sun-chamber)

### Bequests

- [The Falling Star](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#the-falling-star) (3 rings of ilium) was awarded to **Ffion Tendfallen** of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md)
- [The Line and the Curve](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#the-line-and-the-curve) (25 mana crystals) was awarded to **Leo the Rainbow Sage** of [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md)
- [The Wyrd Stone](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#the-wyrd-stone) (30 crowns) was awarded to **Balaam of Cantiarch's Hold** of [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md)

### Door of the Chamber

- The [Hidden Possibilities](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#hidden-possibilities) was chosen
  - Members of the sodality were encouraged to journey to [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md), and to scout for the white granite obelisk of the Cult of the Falconer
