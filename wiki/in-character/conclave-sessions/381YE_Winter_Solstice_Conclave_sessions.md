# 381YE Winter Solstice Conclave sessions

## Preperation

### Precedence and the Font

- **The basic font at this summit was 122 mana.**
- **The income from the Principle of Proportions at the previous summit was 130 mana.**
- **The remainder from previous seasons was 3 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). The income of mana following the previous summit is listed above and was distributed as per the table below. This left 3 mana, which will be added to the font to be divided at the end of the next summit along with the income from the Principle of Proportions at this summit.

| Order | Precedence | Members | Mana | Ilium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Golden Pyramid | 1 | 79 | 58 | 3 |
| Shuttered Lantern | 2 | 72 | 52 | 3 |
| Rod and Shield | 3 | 65 | 47 | 2 |
| Sevenfold Path | 4 | 55 | 40 | 2 |
| Unfettered Mind | 5 | 33 | 24 | 1 |
| Celestial Arch | 6 | 25 | 18 | 1 |
| Silver Chalice | 7 | 18 | 13 | 1 |

## Process

### The Imperial Titles of the Conclave

The full list of Imperial Titles held in the Conclave at the end of the summit can be found [here](../../mixed/titles/Archive-381YE_Summer_Solstice_Imperial_titles.md).

The following Imperial Titles were declared by the Conclave during the summit.

- **Emilia** was appointed as the [Archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) of [Winter](../eternals/Realm.md#winter) following the Friday session.
- **Fabienne of Vexille** was appointed [Archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) of [Spring](../eternals/Realm.md#spring) following the Saturday session.

In meetings of the members of the appropriate orders, the following Grandmasters were appointed.

- **Zephyr de Rondell** was appointed [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) of the [Rod and Shield](../../mixed/government/conclave/Rod_and_Shield.md).
- **Reichard Callan Remidos von Holmaur** was appointed [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) of the [Celestial Arch](../../mixed/government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md).

### Changes of Sorcery

- Eudaemon and Aurelia of Halcyon Spire were declared sorcerers.
- Gui of House Vexille was reconciled with the Conclave.

### Changes of Amity and Enmity

- The eternal Zakalwe has been granted amity.
- The eternal Ylenrith has acquired enmity.
- Gilean, Herald of Llofir has ceased to exist and been removed from the roster of amity.

155 mana was collected in accordance with the Principle of Proportions. This means that, including the existing remainder, 158 extra mana will be distributed at the next summit along with the basic font.
