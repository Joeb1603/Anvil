# 386YE Autumn Equinox Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana distributed to the grand masters following 386YE Summer was 109 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 4 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Precedence at 386YE Summer Conclave was 235 mana.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 386YE Autumn is 239 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence).There was 109 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 386YE Summer Solstice. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Proportions as per the table below.

This left 4 mana, which is added to the 235 mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 239 mana at the 386YE Autumn Equinox.

## Friday Session

### Alignment: Sadogua, Amity

- **Raised by:** Eliina, [Archmage of Night](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Night.md)
- **Declaration:** Our Brother in magic Sadogua is currently and will remain under Amity, for now. Firstly we gratefully accept your offer to continue protecting the college of the Lyceum and extend much interest in looking into more permanent wards. Secondly, we thank you for this continued assistance and for the boons provided to the magicians of the Navarr.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Concord: Fane of Sung

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Rafael Barossa d'Apulian, on behalf of the Sevenfold Path
- **Declaration:** Conclave calls on Senate to construct the chamber of euphony, a fane which will welcome Sung's influence into the city of nets, encouraging the spread of Night throughout Apulian. Doing so would promote magic, mystery and music in the city and attract the attention of other night eternals. The related title 'The map to the city below' should be appointed annually by the League national assembly to allow proper scrutiny and defend against idolatry.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Alignment: Asavean God of Magic, Enmity

- **Raised by:** Simargl, The Empty One Submitted under the Veil of Night
- **Declaration:** Conclave declares the Asavean God of Magic known as the 'Cowled One' as an enemy of the Empire
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Concord: Dealing with Lions

- **Raised by:** Scarrow Dawnbreak, on behalf of the Rod and Shield
- **Declaration:** Meraud is absent, and his lions run feral. We and the Archmage of Summer believe rituals involving these lions, including Golden Ramparts, should be avoided until the situation is resolved.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Concord: Guide Celestial Arch

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Xanthippe of the Lighthouse at Nikephoros, on behalf of the [Celestial Arch](../../mixed/government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md).
- **Declaration:** We send the Celestial Arch to investigate the Asavean threat in Faraden.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Candidacy: Astral Arcanist

- **Raised by:** Vesna Borkovna Prochnost, the [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md)
- **Declaration:** To appoint the title of Astral Arcanist
- **Outcome:** Passed. **Serenius Starsong** claimed the title of [Astral Arcanist](../../mixed/magic/lore/Astral_Arcanist.md)

### Endowment: Mana for Arsenio

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Arsenio Sanguineo Rezia di Tassato, on behalf of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave endows 40 Mana to Arsenio Sanguineo Rezia di Tassato
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Concord: Guide Shuttered Lantern

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Tyburn Weaver, on behalf of the Shuttered Lantern
- **Declaration:** Conclave sends the Shuttered Lantern to Axos to investigate the political situation there with particular emphasis on the influence of Kyther Delaneria and Theodosia. In vigilance, for the Empire.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Concord: Guide Silver Chalice

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Fausta Ankarien, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)
- **Declaration:** The Imperial Conclave sends the Silver Chalice to the Brass Coast to offer succour to those in need who have suffered as a result of the river Scorrero flooding.
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Notes:** Guides the Silver Chalice order, asking them to help relieve the suffering of Freeborn along the river Scorrero. A significant number of herbs were also provided to help members and allied physicks do their work. The situation in the Brass Coast is detailed in the **[Where the river rises](../winds-of-fortune/Where_the_river_rises.md#lamentable-loss-of-life)** wind of fortune.

## Saturday Session

- **Raised by:** Æsa Sigeing, [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave, on behalf of Bloodcrow Udoo, accept the deal with the City of Locks to purchase The General Dossier as well as a Dossier on the Salt Lords for 130 Measures of Tempest Jade
- **Outcome:** Passed. The [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md) used [The Magus' Precedence](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md#the-magus-precedence) to move this declaration' position on the agenda to be the first thing discussed.
- **Notes:** Takes advantage of an offer made by the [Basileia](../eternals/Basileia.md) of the City of Locks, presented as part of the **[Seven locks](../winds-of-fortune/Seven_locks.md)** wind of fortune. [Tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) was provided by **Æsa Sigeing**.

- **Raised by:** Skywise Gralka, [Archmage of Day](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Day.md)

Our understanding of what happened is as follows: a pearl was received at a tourney as a prize by Tancred de Courdefer it was gifted to the warcaster who used it in Imperial Strategy. It was not part of the assurance between the Empire and Zakalwe. All pearls issued by the Polemarch of Highwatch are accounted for and ready for use on the Druj front.
