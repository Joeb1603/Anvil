# 380YE Summer Solstice Conclave sessions

The following is a summary of Conclave proceedings during the Spring Equinox 380YE.

## The Imperial Titles of the Conclave

The full list of Imperial Titles held in the Conclave at the end of the Spring Equinox, 380YE can be found under [380YE Spring Equinox Imperial Titles](../../mixed/titles/Archive-380YE_Spring_Equinox_Imperial_titles.md).

The following Imperial Titles were appointed by the Conclave during the Spring Equinox, 380YE:

- **Brother Luke of the Shattered Tower** became the new [Master of Ice and Darkness](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Ice_and_Darkness.md).
- **Finn Finnsson** was appointed [Imperial Seer](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Seer.md).
- **Bartholl de Rondell** was appointed [Warmage](../../mixed/titles/Warmage.md).
- **Solomon** was appointed [Archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) of Summer.
- **Mord** retained his title of [Archmage](../../mixed/titles/Archmage.md) of Day.
