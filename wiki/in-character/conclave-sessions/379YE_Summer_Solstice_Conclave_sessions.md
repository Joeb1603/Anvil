# 379YE Summer Solstice Conclave sessions

## Overview

The following is a summary of Conclave proceedings during the Summer summit of 379YE.

Please note that while the Conclave meetings of the 379YE Summer summit were only scheduled to occur on the Friday and Saturday evenings, an emergency meeting did occur on the Sunday as well. This was due to a disturbance of the Conclave on the Saturday that resulted in the Conclave being evacuated early.

## The Imperial Titles of the Conclave

The full list of Imperial Titles held in the Conclave at the end of the Summer Solstice, 379YE, can be found under [379YE Summer Solstice Imperial elections](../elections/379YE_Summer_Solstice_Imperial_elections.md)

The following Imperial Titles were appointed by the Conclave during the Summer Solstice, 379YE:

- **Kyra** became the new [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) of the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
