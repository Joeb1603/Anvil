# 387YE Winter Solstice Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana sent for distribution to the grandmasters following 387YE Autumn was 178 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 2 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 387YE Autumn Conclave was 292 mana.**
- **The mana used to purchase the Rogue Declaration at 387YE Autumn Conclave was 35 mana.**
- **Twenty mana was provided by Thule warlocks as part of their treaty obligations with the Empire.**
- **Fifteen mana was provided by the Grand Lodge of the Golden Pyramid**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 387YE Winter is 364 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 178 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 387YE Autumn Equinox. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Precedence as per the table below.

This left 2 mana, which is added to the 292 mana collected at Conclave and the 35 mana from the [rogue declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 364 mana at the 387YE Winter Solstice.

- **Outcome:** **Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad** was elected as the [Voice of the Horizons](../../mixed/titles/Voice_of_the_Horizons.md)

### Candidacy: Imperial Magus

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Xanthippe Of the Lighthouse-That-Was, on behalf of the [Celestial Arch](../../mixed/government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md)
- **Declaration:** Imperial Magus

### Candidacy: Archmage of Winter

- **Raised by:** Vesna Borkovna Prochnost, [the Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md)
- **Declaration:** Archmage of Winter
- **Outcome:** **Bloodcrow Arora** was elected as the [Archmage of Winter](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Winter.md)

### Concord: Condemn Imperial Lore to Silver Flame

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Zlata Ruznikova Perenel, The Dread Auntie, on behalf of the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
- **Declaration:** Consequences are the price of ambition. Conclave condemns the notion of disseminating Imperial lore to the Silver Flame.
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** Addresses the request for access to Imperial lore from the Silver Flame, a group recognised as foreigners who control [Cinion](../nations/the-league/Spiral.md#cinion), as per the **[Mirror and the flame](../winds-of-fortune/Mirror_and_the_flame.md)** wind of fortune

### Endorsement: Rufter of the White-Winged Courtier on Sermersuaq

- **Raised by:** Serenus Starsong, on behalf of the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave endorses the casting of the magnitude 60 Day arcane projection "Rufter of the White-Winged Courtier" on the territory of Sermersuaq to help unravel the remaining malign influence of the Falconer
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** More information on the remaining influence of the Falconer can be found in the **[Shadow in the grass](../winds-of-fortune/Shadow_in_the_grass.md)** wind of fortune

### Concord: Rod and Shield to Commonwealth

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Sakari Nightscale, on behalf of the [Rod and Shield](../../mixed/government/conclave/Rod_and_Shield.md)
- **Declaration:** Rod and Shield, to Arms! We lead the charge to Lantir, to the aid of the Commonwealth. The Cold Sun must be stopped
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** Encourages members of the order to support the Commonwealth, as per the **[Cold war](../winds-of-fortune/Cold_war.md#magical-aid)** wind of fortune

### Concord: Thanks to Autumn Heralds

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Arsenio Sanguineo Rezia di Tassato, on behalf of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md)
- **Declaration:** We as Conclave express our thanks to the heralds of the City of Gold and Lead and the City of Bar and Bond. Their interest and intervention is noted and appreciated by the Golden Pyramid. Unlike the Grendel, the Empire has seen the potential in Mareave. Wealth has been revealed, new bonds formed, and new opportunities seized. We have shown our commitment with investment and magical prowess. We will continue our investment, we are happy to discuss the mutual benefits you might see.
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** References the actions of the heralds of [City of Gold and Lead](../eternals/Ephisis.md) and the [City of Bar and Bond](../eternals/Lictors.md) during the recent [campaign](../winds-of-war/Scarlet_sands.md) of [Mareave](../nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md).

### Concord: Silver Chalice to Lantir

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Fausta Ankarien, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)
- **Declaration:** The Silver Chalice offers aid to all those suffering as a result of the Cold Sun in Lantir with a focus on reducing loss of life to save lives.
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** Encourages members of the order to help in Lantir, as per the **[Cold war](../winds-of-fortune/Cold_war.md#magical-aid)** wind of fortune

## Saturday

### Concord: Advisor on the Cold Sun

- **Raised by:** Casimir Pakkanovic Mislova, [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave asks the Senate to establish a new Imperial title of 'Advisor on the Cold Sun'. The Cold Sun is an international issue and we need a specialist. We request that this role be established on the same constitutional powers and responsibilities of the Advisor on the Vallorn. We are very specifically not asking to establish a sodality.
- **Outcome:** Passed
