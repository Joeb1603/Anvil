# 388YE Spring Equinox Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana sent for distribution to the grandmasters following 387YE Winter was 96 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 4 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 387YE Winter Conclave was 222 mana.**
- **The mana used to purchase the Rogue Declaration at 387YE Winter Conclave was 25 mana.**
- **Twenty mana was provided by Thule warlocks as part of their treaty obligations with the Empire.**
- **Fifteen mana was provided by the Grand Lodge of the Golden Pyramid**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 388YE Spring is 286 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 96 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 387YE Winter Solstice. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Precedence as per the table below.

This left 4 mana, which is added to the 222 mana collected at Conclave and the 25 mana from the [rogue declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 286 mana at the 388YE Spring Equinox.

## Saturday

### Concord: Caution Territory and Empire Enchantments

- **Raised by:** Casimir Pakkanovic Mislova, as [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md)
- **Declaration:** Imperial magicians are one of the Empire's crowning glory, but also pose one of the greatest potential threats. Virtue is demonstrated in our actions, and while ambition has consequences, hubris is the enemy of virtue. Conclave reminds Imperial magicians of the consequences of casting territory and Imperial enchantments, consequences that are paid for by the unnamed citizens of the Empire, not by the magicians who cast it. Take pride in your magic, but remember your fellow citizens who will pay for your folly if things go wrong.
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** This declaration was raised first using the [Magus' Precedence](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md#the-magus-precedence)

### Concord: Sevenfold Path and Convocation of Virtue

- **Raised by:** Damiàn i Martàn i Erigo, on behalf of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md)
- **Declaration:** The Sevenfold Path of the Imperial Conclave will remain in Jarm to combat the Cold Sun. During which we will provide liao to the Convocation of Seven to support their exploration of the Way
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Note:** 185 liao provided by **Damiàn i Martàn i Erigo**. Only a grandmaster can [guide an order](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md#guiding-an-order). This matter was brought to the attention of the magistrates during the summit.
