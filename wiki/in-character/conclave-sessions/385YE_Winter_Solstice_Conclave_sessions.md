# 385YE Winter Solstice Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana distributed to the grand masters following 385YE Autumn was 4 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 4 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Precedence at 385YE Autumn Conclave was 336 mana.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 385YE Winter is 340 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence).There was 4 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 385YE Autumn Equinox. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Proportions as per the table below.

This left 4 mana, which is added to the 336 mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 340 mana at the 385YE Winter Solstice.

## Friday Session

### Alignment: Roshanwe

- **Raised by:** Skywise Gralka, as [Archmage of Day](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Day.md)
- **Declaration:** Is a quest for knowledge its own reward? Is this not a journey of shared experience? The beach is vast and still waits to be combed but are we to do this without guidance from the Weaver of Nets? Is there no chance to discuss what lessons we have learned? The question for Conclave here tonight is to ask is should Roshanwe be moved in alignment with the Empire to Neutrality?
- **Outcome:** Passed. The eternal [Roshanwe](../eternals/Roshanwe.md) has been moved to [Neutrality](../eternals/Alignment.md#neutrality)

### Imperial Lore: Silver Tongue of Virtue

- **Raised by:** Rafael Barossa d'Apulian, on behalf of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md)
- **Declaration:** With thanks to Nathair Autumngale, who first projected this ritual, we place Silver Tongue of Virtue into Imperial lore. This version was given as a gift by Janon to Rafael Barossa d'Apulian who then paid for it to be codified at the Loom of Spells and will pay for it to enter Imperial lore.
- **Outcome:** Passed. [Silver Tongue of Virtue](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Silver_Tongue_of_Virtue.md) is now in Imperial lore after funds were provided by **Rafael Barossa d'Apulian**

### Concord: Request Title with Endorsement

- **Raised by:** Arsenio Rezia di Tassato, on behalf of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave request Senate create an Imperial title with the power to raise a Declaration of Endorsement in Conclave
- **Outcome:** Passed.

### Concord: Shuttered Lantern to Spiral

- **Raised by:** Tyburn Weaver, on behalf of the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave sends the Shuttered Lantern to carry out a thorough investigate of the rebel spires in Spiral. They are to establish if there is ongoing, or has been, secret magical communication with the Grendel, why, and what are each party's aims.
- **Outcome:** Passed. This relates to an opportunity presented in the **[Blue flowers in the snow](../winds-of-fortune/Blue_flowers_in_the_snow.md#the-rebels)** wind of fortune

### Concord: Not Endorsing Zulgan-Tash

- **Raised by:** Fausta Ankarien, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)
- **Declaration:** The Conclave does not endorse the actions committed by the Circle of Zulgan-Tash regarding the Whistling Strand. The Circle of Zulgan-Tash state that if Sadogua objects to the magic worked by the Circle, then he should take it up with them.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Endowment: Lyceum to Sadogua

- **Raised by:** Brynn Vision Enduring, as [Bursar of the Conclave](../../mixed/titles/Bursar_of_the_Conclave.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave endows the Lyceum to Sadogua. Conclave is aware that this is not legally binding and requires a Senate motion, but would like Senate to be aware of Conclave's opinion on the potential ceding of the Lyceum to the Brother of Wizards.
- **Outcome:** Passed. As noted in the Declaration this is not legally binding and it would require a Senate motion to cede the Lyceum to [Sadogua](../eternals/Sadogua.md)

### Candidacy: Scholar of Kragg's Hollow

- **Raised by:** Eliza, on behalf of the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
- **Declaration:** To appoint the title of [Scholar of Kragg's Hollow](../../mixed/titles/Scholar_of_Kragg-s_Hollow.md)
- **Outcome:** Passed. Bloodcrow Pip claimed the title.

### Sorcery: Ruined Hills and Scorched Lands
