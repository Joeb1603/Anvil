# 387YE Spring Equinox Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana sent for distribution to the grandmasters following 386YE Winter was 0 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 0 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 386YE Winter Conclave was 252 mana.**
- **The mana used to purchase the Rogue Declaration at 386YE Winter Conclave was 15 mana.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 387YE Spring is 267 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 0 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 386YE Winter Solstice. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Precedence as per the table below.

This left 0 mana, which is added to the 252 mana collected at Conclave and the 15 mana from the [rogue declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 267 mana at the 387YE Spring Equinox.

### Interdiction: Shedding Blood for Claw Rituals

- **Raised by:** Lights Aliss, [Arbiter of the Rogue Declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md)
- **Declaration:** To interdict the magical practice of calling on the power of the Claw and shedding blood instead of mana as part of ritual casting to empower specific rituals. Forest Remains; Call Down Lighting's Wrath; Might of the Myrmidon; Talon of the Gryphon; Swift Leaping Hare; Swan's Cruel Wing; Hammer of Thunder; Hook of the Hoarfrost Guardian; Merciless Wrath of the Reaver; Blood and Salt
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Notes:** As per the **[Stars and sigils](../winds-of-fortune/Stars_and_sigils.md#red-in-tooth-and-claw)** wind of fortune. The civil service has interpreted this declaration to [interdict](../../mixed/government/conclave/Interdiction.md) the magical practice of drawing on the Claw to empower the casting of rituals by shedding blood instead of mana. The rituals listed on the declaration are useful, but the interdiction encompasses the practice for *all* rituals, not just those listed, such as *Heaven's Spear Sunders the Mountain* and *Ironwind's Pride*, and any [arcane projections](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) that might replicate the effects of the rituals.
