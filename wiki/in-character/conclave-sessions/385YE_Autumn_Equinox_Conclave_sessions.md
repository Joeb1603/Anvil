# 385YE Autumn Equinox Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana distributed to the grand masters following 385YE Summer was 161 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 4 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 385YE Summer Conclave was 200 mana.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 385YE Autumn is 204 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 161 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 385YE Summer Solstice. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Proportions as per the table below.

This left 4 mana, which is added to the 200 mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 204 mana at the 385YE Autumn Equinox.

## Friday Session

### Alignment : Cold Sun

- **Raised by:** Skywise Gralka, as [Archmage of Day](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Day.md)
- **Declaration:** The Conclave declares enmity upon the Cold Sun. Citizens of the Empire and the sentient thinking peoples of the world are not a scar upon the face of creation, that virtuous spark within each of us, that part of us that grows and thinks and learns cannot be snuffed out. Ours is a destiny that transcends your flames, a legacy of inspirational and enlightenment. You chose oblivion. You chose war. You chose an endless battle. But if you are so keen to jump onto our blades the Empire will oblige. War it is.
- **Outcome:** Passed. The eternal [Cold Sun](../eternals/Cold_Sun.md) has been moved to [Enmity](../eternals/Alignment.md#enmity)

### Endorsement : Skywise Gralka and Lending of Regalia

- **Raised by:** Tyburn Weaver, on behalf of the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave endorses the actions of Gralka in her capacity as archmage in the lending of her regalia for use in the casting of Bright Eyes Gleam in the Depths on the Empire in Summer 385YE
- **Outcome:** Passed
- **Notes:** The Constitutional Court ruled that this has no legal effect; a declaration of endorsement can [only be used](../../mixed/government/conclave/Endorsement.md#necessary-details) for a pact with an eternal or the casting of a ritual, not the lending of paraphernalia

### Endowment : Arete's Promise

- **Raised by:** Rafael Barossa d'Apulian, on behalf of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md)
- **Declaration:** The bracelet *Arete's Promise* into the regalia of the Prelate of Adelmar's Shrine
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** The bracelet has been added to the regalia of the [Prelate of Adelmar's Shrine](../../mixed/titles/Prelate_of_Adelmar-s_Shrine.md)

### Alignment : Janon

- **Raised by:** Elyssiathain, as [Archmage of Night](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Night.md)
- **Declaration:** That Janon be granted Amity
- **Outcome:** Passed. The eternal [Janon](../eternals/Janon.md) has been moved to [Amity](../eternals/Alignment.md#amity)

### Dissemination : Regrow the Land's Heart

- **Raised by:** Fausta Ankarien, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)
- **Declaration:** Share to the Tchemetzi of Faraden the ritual text of Regrow the Land's Heart and in return the Tchemetzi will endow 2 vital honey to the Silver Chalice
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Candidacy : Master of Ice and Darkness

- **Raised by:** Mercurio Ankarien, on behalf of the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
- **Declaration:** Candidacy for the position of Master of Ice and Darkness
- **Outcome:** The incumbent, **Ceinwen Eternal**, retained their position as [Master of Ice and Darkness](../../mixed/titles/Master_of_Ice_and_Darkness.md)

### Concord : Call to Arms

- **Raised by:** Sakari Nightscale, on behalf of the [Rod and Shield](../../mixed/government/conclave/Rod_and_Shield.md)
- **Declaration:** Magicians of the Empire, to Arms! Conclave calls upon the magicians of the Empire to support the fight against the Cold Sun. Enchant your banners, your military units, your nations. Conclave must recognise its responsibility of consequences. The heralds of the Cold Sun must be destroyed
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Dissemination: Delve Deep Beneath the Mountain

- **Raised by:** Vasilmir Radovanovna Esskivic, on behalf of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md)
- **Declaration:** Delve Deep Beneath the Mountain to be shared with Orathator of the Hourglass (Thule)
- **Outcome:** Passed. 10 thrones provided by Vasilmir Radovanovna Esskivic
- **Notes:** Orathor of the Hourglass has made an [offer](../winds-of-fortune/Pride_and_prejudice.md#masks-for-the-sentinel) to the [Warden of the Trees and Hills](../../mixed/titles/Warden_of_the_Trees_and_Hills.md) and the [Gloaming Sentinel](../../mixed/titles/Gloaming_Sentinel.md) as part of the **[Pride and prejudice](../winds-of-fortune/Pride_and_prejudice.md)** wind of fortune

### Alignment : Ossegrahn

- **Raised by:** Ibiss Briarheart, as [Archmage of Spring](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Spring.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave appreciates Ossegrahn's aid and values in input in shaping a Spring college of magic. With thanks, we grant him amity
- **Outcome:** Passed. The eternal [Ossegrahn](../eternals/Ossegrahn.md) has been moved to [Amity](../eternals/Alignment.md#amity)

### Interdiction : Savagery of Armies

- **Raised by:** Ashborn Trosk, on behalf of the [Celestial Arch](../../mixed/government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md)
- **Declaration:** To interdict the magical practice of enchanting entire armies in such a way that their soldiers become prone to savagery
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** The ritual [Fire on the Water](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Fire_on_the_Water.md) is the only ritual in Imperial lore that is interdicted by this declaration. It also affects two rituals in Urizen lore: [Swords in the Noonday Sun](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Swords_in_the_Noonday_Sun.md) (though since then this ritual has changed such that it is no longer covered by this interdiction) and [Loosen the Shackles](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Loosen_the_Shackles.md). It also covers any ritual texts or arcane projections that create similar effects. This follows on from an offer made by [Zakalwe](../eternals/Zakalwe.md) as part of the **[Hierarchs of sea and sky](../winds-of-fortune/Hierarchs_of_sea_and_sky.md#the-key-of-commitment)** wind of fortune

## Saturday Session

### Concord : Sinokenon and the Omnihedron

- **Raised by:** Aesa Sigeing, as [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave requests the Turning Mirror considers the matter of how the damage and detrimental effects of the Empire wide rituals that have been cast towards the Omnihedron can be fixed, in particular the blood red roads, and speculate on the result of completing the Omnihedron.
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** [The Turning Mirror](../eternals/Sinokenon.md) offered a unique appraisal to the Conclave as part of the **[Hiearchs of sea and sky](../winds-of-fortune/Hierarchs_of_sea_and_sky.md#what-do-you-want-to-do3f)** wind of fortune.

### Sorcery : Anthemius Netherwatch

- **Raised by:** Bryn Netherwatch, as [Witness of Conclave](../../mixed/titles/Witness_of_Conclave.md)
- **Declaration:** To declare Anthemius Netherwatch. This is for his role as the organiser the Urizen covens to cast Bright Eyes Gleam in the Depths for which he takes responsibility
- **Outcome:** Passed. Anthemius Netherwatch has been added to the list of [active sorcerers](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sorcery.md#active-sorcerers)

### Concord : Zakalwe and Military Defences

- **Raised by:** Sakari Nightscale, on behalf of the [Rod and Shield](../../mixed/government/conclave/Rod_and_Shield.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave agrees to the offer from Zakalwe, Lord of Shallows, for the Rod and Shield to work with his heralds to assess potential improvements to the Empire's military defences
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** [Zakalwe](../eternals/Zakalwe.md) offered to send his heralds to assess the Empire's defences as per the **[Hierarchs of sea and sky](../winds-of-fortune/Hierarchs_of_sea_and_sky.md#the-key-of-preparation)** wind of fortune

### Endowment : Mithril from Faraden

- **Raised by:** Kaapo "Autumn's Orator" Kingfishering, as [Arbiter of the Rogue Declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md)
- **Declaration:** Nine wains of mithril, to be put towards the Runegrott runeforge and provided to Kaapo "Autumn's Orator" Kingfishering
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** These nine wains of [mithril](../../mixed/economy/Mithril.md) come from the Arshlan family of Faraden as detailed in the **[Two minutes](../winds-of-fortune/Two_minutes.md#just-rewards)** wind of fortune.

### Interdiction : Empire Wide Enchantments

- **Raised by:** Cassandra i Periera i Erigo, as [Imperial Censor](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Censor.md)
- **Declaration:** A declaration of interdiction against the magical practice of weaving an enchantment across the entire Empire. We cannot risk such chaotic and unpredictable magic without adequate safeguards
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** This interdiction does not cover the casting of empire-wide curses. This interdiction covers the casting of empire-wide enchantments on any empire, such as the Druj or the Iron Confederacy, not just the Empire.

### Concord : Espionage Rings in Sarvos

- **Raised by:** Tyburn Weaver, on behalf of the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md)
- **Declaration:** The Shuttered Lantern asks Conclave to allow them 60 mana from the font and sent them to tear the veil and break the espionage ring in Sarvos, with the intention that this mana be reimbursed back to the font
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** The order are able to deal with the espionage ring in Sarvos as detailed in the **[The cities and the city](../winds-of-fortune/The_cities_and_the_city.md#lanterns)** wind of fortune

- **Notes:** Imperial citizens are now able to accept the [Gift of Kaela](../eternals/Kaela.md#the-gift-of-kaela) as per the **[Various storms and saints](../winds-of-fortune/Various_storms_and_saints.md#kaela)** wind of fortune
