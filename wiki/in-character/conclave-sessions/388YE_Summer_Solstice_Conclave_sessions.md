# 388YE Summer Solstice Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana sent for distribution to the grandmasters following 388YE Spring was 64 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 3 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 388YE Spring Conclave was 265 mana.**
- **The mana used to purchase the Rogue Declaration at 388YE Spring Conclave was 11 mana.**
- **Twenty mana was provided by Thule warlocks as part of their treaty obligations with the Empire.**
- **Fifteen mana was provided by the grand lodge of the Golden Pyramid.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 388YE Summer is 314 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 64 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 388YE Spring Equinox. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Precedence as per the table below.

This left 3 mana, which is added to the 265 mana collected at Conclave, the 15 mana from the Grand Lodge of the Golden Pyramid, the 20 mana from the agreement with the Thule, and the 11 mana from the [rogue declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 314 mana at the 388YE Summer Solstice

## Friday

### Candidacy: Witness of Conclave

- **Raised by:** Dick Pike, on behalf of the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md)
- **Declaration:** We, the Conclave, thank Duncan for his years of service. With the order of the Shuttered Lantern behind me I raise this candidacy
- **Outcome:** **Zlata Ruznikova Perenel, The Dread Auntie** was elected as the [Witness of Conclave](../../mixed/titles/Witness_of_Conclave.md)

### Sorcery: Heliodoro and Bella Vicente

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Fausta Ankarien, on behalf of the [Silver Chalice](../../mixed/government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)
- **Declaration:** Heliodoro Vicente d'Apulian and Bella Vicente d'Apulian : Conclave condemns invites enmitied eternals into our Empire in secret; they are under enmity for a reason.
- **Outcome:** Passed. **Heliodoro Vicente d'Apulian** and **Bella Vicente d'Apulian** have been added to the list of [sorcerers](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sorcery.md#prominent-sorcerers)

### Disseminate: Dreadful Ending to Agoksa of the Waterwalkers

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Terentia of Sarangrave Reforged, on behalf of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md)
- **Declaration:** The people of Urizen know that all that's worth having is worth sharing. We disseminate the magnitude twenty Winter ritual Dreadful Ending to deal with the miasma pillar in Nesustak Forest to Agoska of the Waterwalkers of Sarangrave.
- **Outcome:** Passed. Funding provided by **Terentia of Sarangrave Reforged**

### Endowment: Rashid i Hayim i Riqueza

- **Raised by:** Rashid i Hayim i Riqueza, as [Bursar of the Conclave](../../mixed/titles/Bursar_of_the_Conclave.md)
- **Declaration:** I, Rashid i Hayim i Riqueza, will endow 150 mana to myself, to be used for the purchase of wains for grand lodges and other projects, and for the funding of a fortress to defend the grand lodge of the sevenfold path
- **Outcome:** Passed. 150 mana crystals delivered to **Rashid i Hayim i Riqueza**

### Enmity: Sinokenon

- **Raised by:** Gralka, as [Archmage of Day](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Day.md)
- **Declaration:** As it has been promised to the grandmaster of the sevenfold path, I as archmage bring the question of enmity for the Turning Mirror before the Conclave. As we fetch members of our Conclave here to debate their actions so does does this Conclave debate Sinokenon's promotion of "fidelity" by raising this.
- **Outcome:** Failed. [Sinokenon](../eternals/Sinokenon.md) remains [neutral](../eternals/Alignment.md#neutrality)

### Candidacy: Archmage of Autumn

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Zlata Ruznikova Perenel, The Dread Auntie, on behalf of the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
- **Declaration:** To elect the Archmage of Autumn
- **Outcome:** **Ashborn Trosk** was elected as the [Archmage of Autumn](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Autumn.md)
- **Note:** This declaration was raised under the [veil of night](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md#veil-of-night)

### Concord: Sevenfold Path and the Convocation of Virtue

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Elagabala, on behalf of the [Sevenfold Path](../../mixed/government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md)
- **Declaration:** I send the Sevenfold Path to Jarm to meet once more with the Convocation of Virtue to discuss the following matter: Do you use magic to further your understanding of and connection to virtue? If so, what and how? If not, why not?
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Note:** References the [oppportunity](../winds-of-fortune/Full_circles.md#blossoms-of-virtue) available to the Sevenfold Path to discuss religious matters with the Convocation of Virtue, a Jarmish sect of Wayist priests

### Neutrality: Lashonar

- **Raised by:** [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) Xanthippe, on behalf of the [Celestial Arch](../../mixed/government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md)
- **Declaration:** The Celestial arch and the Conclave apologise for allowing falsehoods about Lashonar to be spread. Let this declaration lay the first stone of the bridge between us
- **Outcome:** Passed. [Lashonar](../eternals/Lashonar.md) has been moved to neutrality

- **Raised by:** Silk Farkas, on behalf of the [Celestial Arch](../../mixed/government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md)
