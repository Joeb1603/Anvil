# 387YE Summer Solstice Conclave sessions

## Precedence and the Font

- **The mana sent for distribution to the grandmasters following 387YE Spring was 0 mana.**
- **The mana remaining after distribution was 0 mana.**
- **The mana collected via the Principle of Proportions at 387YE Spring Conclave was 251 mana.**
- **The mana used to purchase the Rogue Declaration at 387YE Spring Conclave was 20 mana.**
- **The mana available in the font for Conclave at 387YE Summer is 271 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). There was 0 mana in the font after the final Conclave session of the 387YE Spring Equinox. This was distributed to the grand masters of the Conclave according to the Principle of Precedence as per the table below.

This left 0 mana, which is added to the 251 mana collected at Conclave and the 20 mana from the [rogue declaration](../../mixed/titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) at the previous event to form the font for the coming summit. The Conclave has access to a font of 271 mana at the 387YE Summer Solstice.

## Friday

### Concord: Support Power to Remove Rituals

- **Raised by:** Æsa Sigeling, [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave support the creation of a power of declaration to allow the removal of rituals from Imperial lore, currently referred to as the Power of Remorse. It is preferred the name of the power be altered to respect and acknowledge the Pride of citizens' creating rituals.
- **Outcome:** Failed
- **Note:** The Imperial Magus used their power of [The Magus' Precedence](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md#the-magus-precedence) on this declaration

### Interdiction: Yassolta

- **Raised by:** Gaius Starfall i Riqueza, [Imperial Censor](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Censor.md)
- **Declaration:** Interdict the potion named Yassolta
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Endorsement: Terror of the Night Hags

- **Raised by:** Bella Vicente d'Apulian, [Arcane Envoy](../../mixed/titles/Arcane_Envoy.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave endorses the casting of the magnitude 60 Winter ritual "Terror of the Night Hags" on the Sarangrave by the Circle of the Drowned coven at the 387YE Summer Solstice
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Alignment: Accept Sadogua's Offer

- **Raised by:** Fiorente Vicente d'Apulian, on behalf of the [Unfettered Mind](../../mixed/government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
- **Declaration:** Conclave accepts Sadogua's offer of access to the Well of Shadows for one full year. Conclave assures Sadogua that it will not place rituals codified at the Well of Shadows into Imperial lore.
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Candidacy: Archmage of Night

- **Outcome:** **Bella Vicente d'Apulian** was elected as [Archmage of Night](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Night.md)

### Alignment: Accept Skathe's Offer

### Alignment: Support Tharim's Hunt

- **Raised by:** Ematius of the Great Library of Ankarien, [Archmage of Winter](../../mixed/titles/Archmage_of_Winter.md)
- **Declaration:** The Imperial Conclave stands steadfast as always with our members and colleagues in the Marches. We call upon the smiths, landskeepers, and threshers to render all appropriate and lawful aid to the magistrates and representatives of the Bound King in finding the sorceror-murderers responsible for the deaths of Obligatus and many a loyal Marcher, such that they face the consequences of their actions. May they be found swiftly with the unblinking eye of the scarecrow

### Interdiction: Hollowing

- **Raised by:** Skywise Gralka, on behalf of the [Shuttered Lantern](../../mixed/government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md)
- **Declaration:** Interdiction of the magical practice of creating "Hollow Ones". The process of Hollowing is an abhorrence to Virtue and denies the passage of the Soul across the Abyss. This Conclave moves to take a firm stance on this and interdicts the magical practice within the Empire
- **Outcome:** Passed
