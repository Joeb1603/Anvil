# 384YE Winter Solstice Conclave sessions

### Precedence and the Font

- **The income from the Principle of Proportions was 335 mana.**
- **The remainder from previous seasons was 4 mana.**

The members of each order in attendance at the end of the previous summit determine precedence for the following summit according to the [Principle of Precedence](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence). The income of mana following the previous summit is listed above and was distributed as per the table below. This left 2 mana, which will be added to the font of the next summit along with the income from the Principle of Proportions at this summit.

| Order | Precedence | Members | Mana | Ilium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Unfettered Mind | 1 | 91 | 66 | 3 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Sevenfold Path | 2 | 76 | 55 | 3 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Rod and Shield | 3 | 76 | 55 | 2 |
| Grandmaster of the Order of the Shuttered Lantern | 4 | 69 | 50 | 2 |

## Friday Session

### Concord: Request title to identify sorcerers

- **Raised by:** Æsa Sigeling, Imperial Magus
- **Declaration:** Conclave requests the creation of an Imperial title, to be appointed by Conclave, to identify sorcerers within the Empire, those who use magic to the detriment of the Empire, and who treat with eternals to the detriment of fellow Imperial citizens and to investigate those with an Imperial title appointed by Conclave, to ensure they are not becoming corrupt with power.
- **Outcome:** Passed.

### Concord: Imperial law and Urizen lore

- **Raised by:** Rafael Barossa di Tassato, Order of the Sevenfold Path
- **Declaration:** Conclave's instruction to the magistrates is that rituals that are part of Urizen lore but subject to interdiction should be treated as if they were part of Imperial lore: illegal to cast, but not to learn or teach. Ritual texts should not be seized or destroyed, whether from the Grand Library of Canterspire or elsewhere
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** The guidance to the magistrates has been widely disseminated (and notes appended to the Urizen Lore box on the [interdiction](../../mixed/government/conclave/Interdiction.md) page).

### Concord: Silver Chalice and Madruga

- **Raised by:** Fausta Ankarien, Order of the Silver Chalice
- **Declaration:** Conclave asks the magician of Silver Chalice to go to Madruga, explore the phenomenon and try to find a solution.
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** This relates to the [thirsty grasses](../winds-of-fortune/Enchanted.md#the-thirsty-grasses) phenomenon spreading across the Great Grasses in Madruga. Details of the outcome can be found in the [Colourblind](../winds-of-fortune/Colourblind.md) Wind of Fortune.

### Imperial lore: The Flower is Withered on the Stalk

- **Raised by:** Simargl the Empty One, Dean of the Lyceum
- **Declaration:** [The Flower is Withered on the Stalk](../../mixed/magic/rituals/The_Flower_is_Withered_on_the_Stalk.md) to be added to Imperial lore
- **Outcome:** Passed.

### Concord: Winterfolk and the Shuttered Lantern

- **Raised by:** Aethalstan Korping, Order of the Rod and Shield
- **Declaration:** The Winterfolk request that Conclave agrees that the Shuttered Lantern investigate blood on the snow cultists in Sermersuaq
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** This declaration, and the [following declaration](#concord-shuttered-lantern-and-sermersuaq), relate to the [influence of Agramant](../winds-of-fortune/Watching_my_mistakes.md#voice-of-the-pines) in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md).

### Concord: Shuttered Lantern and Sermersuaq

- **Raised by:** Tyburn Weaver, Order of the Shuttered Lantern
- **Declaration:** Tyburn Weaver, Grandmaster of the Shuttered Lantern, seeks to tear the veil on activities of Voice in the Pines within Sermersuaq. I ask for the support of the Conclave
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** This declaration, and the [previous one](#concord-winterfolk-and-the-shuttered-lantern), relate to the [influence of Agramant](../winds-of-fortune/Watching_my_mistakes.md#voice-of-the-pines) in [Sermersuaq](../nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md). Details of the outcome can be found in the [Anyone but you](../winds-of-fortune/Anyone_but_you.md) Wind of Fortune.

### Concord: Tharim's Bond

- **Raised by:** Ser Claudia Lovelorn the Imperial Necromancer, Order of the Unfettered Mind
- **Declaration:** Conclave accepts Tharim's offer to strengthen the bonds on the Hall of Worlds; protecting Conclave, Anvil, and the Empire from heralds of eternals under enmity. His image will be displayed in Conclave, as part of this agreement.
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** This related to an offer from [Tharim](../eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#tharim) to [ward the Hall of Worlds](../winds-of-fortune/Enchanted.md#black-frost-and-bonds) - an offer the Conclave has accepted.

### Endowment: Mana to Sadogua

- **Raised by:** Raficci Vicente Barossa, Order of the Celestial Arch
- **Declaration:** To grant 150 mana to Sadogua, specifically and only to support the evacuation of persecuted mages from the territories of the Iron Confederacy
- **Outcome:** Passed.
- **Notes:** This relates to the [request](../winds-of-fortune/Shake_it_off.md#sanctuary-for-the-suranni) from the Night eternal [Sadogua](../eternals/Sadogua.md) that if the Conclave wishes to help [Iron Confederacy](../foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md) wizards escape persecution and flee to the Empire, they would endow him with crystal mana. The mana was provided to the eternals' allies, and further developments are detailed in the [Possibility days](../winds-of-fortune/Possibility_days.md) wind of fortune.

## Saturday Session

### Concord: Vigilance of the Conclave

- **Raised by:** Heilyn Bronwen's Rest, Order of the Rod and Shield
- **Declaration:** This Conclave shall be vigilant. Those who harm civilians through the use of magic must expect the Conclave to investigate them for improper use of magic
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Concord: Question Claude Videre

- **Raised by:** Claude Videre, Arbiter of the Rogue Declaration
- **Declaration:** That Conclave has had opportunity to question potential Throne Claude Videre and determined that they are a suitable potential candidate for the Throne in the eyes of the Conclave
- **Outcome:** Passed: this declaration is considered to represent the Conclave's [approval](../winds-of-fortune/Concord_and_accord.md#approval) of Claude Videre as a [Throne](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md) candidate
- **Constitutional Note:** Claude Videre was not legally eligible to submit the Rogue Declaration due to them holding another Imperial title at the time. Having investigated the matter, the magistrates have concluded that the issue arose because of incorrect guidance issued to Claude by the Imperial Civil Service. The guidance has been updated, and no further action is warranted.

### Concord: Sevenfold Path's Manifesto

- **Raised by:** Rafael Barossa di Tassato, Order of the Sevenfold Path
- **Declaration:** We ask Senate to amend the first item of the Sevenfold Path manifesto's "This order exists" section to read: *Encourage the use of magic in understanding, and supporting, human destiny and the destiny of orcs and other peoples*
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Interdiction: Potion of Bone Dust

- **Raised by:** Bryn Netherwatch, Imperial Censor
- **Declaration:** Interdiction of the potion of bone dust
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Concord: Rod and Shield and Metri

- **Raised by:** Arsenio Rezia di Tassato, Order of the Golden Pyramid
- **Declaration:** We ask the order of the Rod and Shield to deal with the threat presented by the creatures in Metri, Temeschwar
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Candidacy: Imperial Warmage

- **Raised by:** Fausta Ankarien, Order of the Silver Chalice
- **Declaration:** Open candidacy for the position of Imperial Warmage
- **Outcome:** Andronikos of the Lighthouse of Nikephoros was appointed as [Warmage](../../mixed/titles/Warmage.md)

### Imperial lore: Fire on the Water

- **Raised by:** Tyburn Weaver, Order of the Shuttered Lantern
- **Declaration:** [Fire on the Water](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Fire_on_the_Water.md) to be added to Imperial lore
- **Outcome:** Passed

### Concord: Supporting the Scholars of the Seventh Mist

- **Raised by:** Eliza, Order of the Unfettered Mind
- **Declaration:** Conclave supports the scholars of the Seventh Mist creating a permanent research base in the Brilliant Star in Redoubt
- **Outcome:** Failed

### Concord: Suranni magicians

- **Raised by:** Raficci Vicente Barossa, Order of the Celestial Arch
- **Declaration:** Conclave urges the mages of the Empire to reach out in welcome to those fleeing persecution in the Iron Confederacy, and help them establish new lives as virtuous, law-abiding citizens of the Empire
- **Outcome:** Passed
