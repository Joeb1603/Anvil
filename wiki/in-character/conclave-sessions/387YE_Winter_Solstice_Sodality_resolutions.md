# 387YE Winter Solstice Sodality resolutions

## Overview

The [Imperial sodalities](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) of the Empire have potentially far-reaching powers. Each season, the members of Imperial sodalities meet to vote on elections, bequests, and other actions that their sodality might take.

Where possible, the civil service records these votes and presents the results after each summit.

## Apothecaries Guild

## Magpies

- Responsibility for [a conjunction](../winds-of-fortune/The_thing_with_feathers.md#knife-in-the-snow) at the Spring Equinox 388YE was assigned to **Morgan Drifting Bough**

## Sun Chamber

### Appointments

- **Zoria** was appointed as [Counsel of the Sun Chamber](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#counsel-of-the-sun-chamber)

### Bequests

- [The Falling Star](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#the-falling-star) (3 rings of ilium) was awarded to **Baalam of Cantiarch's Hold** of [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md)
- [The Line and the Curve](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#the-line-and-the-curve) (25 mana crystals) was awarded to **Hazel Talbot** of [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md)
- [The Wyrd Stone](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#the-wyrd-stone) (30 crowns) was awarded to **Ffion Tendfallen** of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md)

### Door of the Chamber

- [Eternal Allies](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md#eternal-allies) was chosen
  - The Sun Chamber called upon the [Lictors](../eternals/Lictors.md) to assist with resolving treachery in the Marches, particularly Steven of Sarcombe

## Vallornguard

### Appointments

- No-one was appointed [Burgher of Ketsov](../../mixed/titles/Burgher_of_Ketsov.md), this title will be available to appoint at the next summit

### Bequests

- [The Bloody Bequest](../../mixed/titles/Vallornguard.md#the-bloody-bequest) (10 thrones) was awarded to **Corwyn Heartsbane** of [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md)
