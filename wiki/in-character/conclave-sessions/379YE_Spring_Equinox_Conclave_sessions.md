# 379YE Spring Equinox Conclave sessions

The following is a summary of Conclave proceedings during the Spring summit of 379YE.

## The Imperial Titles of the Conclave

The full list of Imperial Titles held in the Conclave at the end of the Spring Equinox, 379yE, can be found under [379YE Spring Equinox Imperial titles](../../mixed/titles/Archive-379YE_Spring_Equinox_Imperial_titles.md)

The following Imperial Titles were appointed by the Conclave during the Spring Equinox, 379YE:

- **Faustus** became the new [Grandmaster](../../mixed/titles/Grandmaster.md) of the [Golden Pyramid](../../mixed/government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md)
- **Cyrus of Felix's Watch** became the new [Imperial Magus](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_Magus.md).
