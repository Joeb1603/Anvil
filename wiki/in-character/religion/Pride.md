# Pride

*[Image: From A Children's Primer of the Way](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:ChildPrimer4.jpg)*

## The Path of Pride

Some may believe that Pride is simply senators in fine clothing praising the qualities of their Nation, but this is only a tiny part of the path. National Pride is, quite rightfully, important to many citizens and is the reason for the creation of the [egregores](../nations/general/Nationality.md#egregore). Yet the Virtue of Pride exists to inform the small parts of life as much as the large. From the servant who keeps their mistress’ cups clean, to the craftsman who holds all their work to the same high standard, to the general who remembers the name of every soldier who died under their command – all demonstrate Pride and know what it is that they serve and strive for.

### Guidance on the Path

Through meditation on the [auras of Pride](../../mixed/magic/lore/Auras_of_Pride.md), and close study of the stories of paragons and exemplars, the priests of the Way have gleaned insight into the heart of the virtue and how best to pursue it. There are collections of parables, tales, sayings and teachings that distil this for Imperial citizens to learn from. The following are a handful of teachings and sayings deemed to be essential to understanding the path:

- Pride conveyed by words alone is vain boasting; proclaim it by how you conduct every part of your life.
- Do not be complacent; ever strive for excellence in that of which you are proud.
- Embrace your past; never be ashamed of it.
- The Virtuous inspire others to greatness; they do not demean them.
- Despise the half-hearted, the uncommitted and the pretender.

## Paragons and Exemplars

Across history, the paragons and exemplars of Pride have been amongst the easiest to identify for they are, by nature, rarely reclusive and withdrawn. Recognised [paragons and exemplars](../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of Pride include:

- The legendary paragon [Kethry](Kethry.md) of the Kesh kept her peoples' ways alive and ultimately inspired the conquering nation to embrace them.
- The paragon [Richilde](Empress_Richilde.md) inspired the people of the Empire to celebrate their culture and history, and was the first paragon recognised in the modern era.
- The [Highborn](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) paragon [Lepidus](Lepidus.md) kept the spiritual heart of his nation alive during the dark days of the Patrician Council's corruption.
- the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) musician and bard [Gerallt Brackensong](Gerallt_Brackensong.md) who fell fighting the [vallorn](../nations/navarr/Vallorn.md).

## Symbols of Pride

Priests of Pride teach that the best symbol of the virtue is the open display of whatever it is that the pilgrim is most proud of. The **flag** and the **banner** are ways of displaying such symbols, as is the **livery** of a [Marcher household](../nations/the-marches/Marcher_household.md), or the **heraldry** of a noble or [Noble house](../nations/dawn/Noble_house.md) in [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md).

As a more unified symbol of the virtue itself, rather than of the individual's personal Pride, the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) often favours the **sun**. The sun shines brightly, without shame, open for all the world to see. By so doing, it allows others to see as well - and while it may occasionally be occluded by clouds, when the clouds pass the sun's light is undiminished.

In [Wintermark](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), the **eagle**, the **heron**, and the **swan** all symbolise Pride. The eagle is also a popular symbol of the virtue in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) where it is joined by the **deer**. Some magicians use the [rune](../../mixed/magic/lore/Runes.md) of [Majesty](../../mixed/magic/lore/Feresh.md), **Feresh**, which represents glorious splendour, to symbolise Pride. These magicians point to the way that Feresh only enhances attributes that are already present as a particularly telling parable about the nature of Pride.

During their Spring festival, the [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md) represent the virtue of Pride with pink blossoms.

### The Ten Things

It is obviously down to the individual which qualities make them proud, but each of the Imperial nations can be associated with a single common idea that is often referred to as a source of Pride. The Navarr writer Cerin of Winter Trees referred to them as the Nine Things in the early years of the Empire. They were still in use over three hundred years later when the [Imperial Orc](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) philosopher Jakaj added her own people to the list during a speech given at the funeral of [Emperor Ahraz](Emperor_Ahraz.md). Following a recitation of the Nine Things, she appended her own people, and since then the list has been referred to by its current name.

For the [Freeborn](../nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) it is [honesty](../nations/the-brass-coast/Honesty.md); in [Dawn](../nations/dawn/Dawn.md) it is [glory](../nations/dawn/Glory.md) (of course); in [Highguard](../nations/highguard/Highguard.md) it is certainty; among the [Imperial Orcs](../nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) it is strength; in [the League](../nations/the-league/The_League.md) it is cleverness; in [the Marches](../nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) it is either practicality or stubbornness (depending who you are speaking to); among the [Navarr](../nations/navarr/Navarr.md) it is sacrifice; in [Urizen](../nations/urizen/Urizen.md), [excellence](../nations/urizen/Arete.md); in [Varushka](../nations/varushka/Varushka.md) the idea of wisdom; and among the [Winterfolk](../nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [heroism](../../mixed/government/synod/Heroism.md).
