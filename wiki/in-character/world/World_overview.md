# World overview

The Empire is the primary focus of the game, but it is not the only place that exists in the wider world.

## [Maps](Maps.md)

*[Image: Empire-map-illus.gif](Maps.md)*

A number of different maps of the Empire and its environs exist.

## [Gazetteer](Gazetteer.md)

*[Image: Tactical Master Map.png](Gazetteer.md)*

The Empire is made up of many [territories](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md), each of which has its own page.

## [Barbarians](../barbarians/Barbarian.md)

*[Image: OrcsCharging.jpg](../barbarians/Barbarian.md)*

The enemies of the Empire are classified as [barbarians](../barbarians/Barbarian.md), including the [Jotun](../barbarians/Jotun.md) and the [Druj](../barbarians/Druj.md) [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md).

## [Foreign nations](../foreign-nations/Foreign_nations.md)

*[Image: Bay of Catazar.png](../foreign-nations/Foreign_nations.md)*

Not every nation outside the Empire is an enemy. A number of smaller nations, and a handful of large nations equal in power to the Empire, maintain diplomatic relations.

## [Realms](../eternals/Realm.md)

*[Image: AutumnRune.jpg](../eternals/Realm.md)*

Beyond the mundane world exist six magical [realms](../eternals/Realm.md) infused with the power of magic.

## [Eternals](../eternals/Eternal.md)

*[Image: JoDevine.jpg](../eternals/Eternal.md)*

The eternals are the inhabitants of the [realms](../eternals/Realm.md) - supernatural entities with an interest in the Empire and its citizens.
