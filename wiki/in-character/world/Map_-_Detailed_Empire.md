# Map - Detailed Empire

## Overview

This map is appropriate to the Empire prior to Winter 381YE. A more up-to-date version of this map can be found on the [maps](Maps.md) page.

Click on this map to view it in detail. You may need to click again to zoom in on your browser. Believe me, you need to zoom in...

It may be helpful to browse the [Gazetteer](Gazetteer.md) while reading the map - it gives details of the various settlements and places of interest.

In scale, this map is around 900 miles north to south.
