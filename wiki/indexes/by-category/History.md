# Category: History

*[Image: Leontes the Scribe, Marcher scholar and Imperial Archivist, oversees the Department of Historical Research](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Leontes.jpg)*

## Overview

This category contains two broad classes of page:

- Essays and [historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md) documents detailing with past events. A list of historical research projects commissioned since the start of the campaign can be found in the [recent historical research](../../mixed/government/senate/Historical_research.md#recent-historical-research) list.
- Pages concerned with historical figures such as previous Thrones, or Alderei the Fair. Right now this explicitly does *not* include primarily religious figures who are known mainly as [paragons and exemplars](../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md), except where there has been significant historical research in play.
