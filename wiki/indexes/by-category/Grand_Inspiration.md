# Category: Grand Inspiration

## Overview

*[Image: Inspiration for the grand endeavour has been provided, the ground is being cleared, the first white granite towers begin to rise.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Grand_Endeavours.png)*

Introduced in the [Blood will thicken](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Blood_will_thicken.md#a-question-of-scale) wind of fortune, the Grand Inspiration of the Way is an ambitious endeavour to transform the [White City of Bastion](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Bastion.md#bastion-the-white-city) into a grand sacred city dedicated to the Way. It would serve to cement the Empire as the foundation of the Way, and help to unify pilgrims under their version of the faith.
