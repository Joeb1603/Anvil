# Category: Resource

## Overview

This category collects [rituals](../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) that target various [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource). Most are [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) that are performed on the character who owns the personal resource, using the [Law of Dominion](../../out-of-character/rules/Laws_of_magic.md#the-law-of-dominion) to transfer the magic to the resource without actually enchanting the character themselves. The actual effects vary; some make the resource more productive or change their production while others have more esoteric powers such as hiding a resource from enemies or allowing one resource to act as if it were another.

All these rituals are part of either [Imperial lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list) or [Urizen lore](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore).
