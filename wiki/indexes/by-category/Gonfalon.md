# Category: Gonfalon

## Overview

A gonfalon is a [group magic item](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#group-magic-items) that provides an advantage to a [banner](../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). They follow the same phys-rep rules as magic [magic standards](#magic-itemsmagic-standards). However a gonfalon is bonded to the entire band - rather than rather than an single individual like a magic standard.
