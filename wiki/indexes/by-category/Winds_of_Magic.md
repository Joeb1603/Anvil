# Category: Winds of Magic

## Overview

Winds of Magic is a classification of Wind of Fortune that primarily deals with magical matters - generally [eternals](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md), events significant to Imperial magicians, or the business of the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md). At any given event there may be any number of winds of fortune dealing with magical events or beings, but the Winds of Magic serve as a "roundup" of shorter pieces of information themed around magical effects or eternals.
