## Gifts of Estavus

In Summer 384YE, the eternal Estavus made certain rituals drawing on her power [available to the Empire](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Begin_to_speak.md#estavus). The [mystic scrolls of Estavus](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md) detail three rituals - [Ramparts of Ashlar](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#ramparts-of-ashlar), [Hammers of Fury](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#hammers-of-fury), and [Accoutrements of War](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#accoutrements-of-war). They are not part of Imperial lore, and cannot be mastered by Imperial citizens, but their capabilities are reasonably common knowledge.
