# Category: Highest Calling

## Overview

*[Image: The statement of principle allows an assembly to guide the people of the Empire.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Synod_Summer_385YE.png)*

These pages are all related to [the highest calling](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_highest_calling.md) of the assorted [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) assemblies. Each assembly except the General Assembly, and the national assemblies of Wintermark, Urizen, and Varushka. Each of the other assemblies has an opportunity to declare a highest calling which will guide the efforts of that assembly in the year that follows.
