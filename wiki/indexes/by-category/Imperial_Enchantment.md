# Category: Imperial Enchantment

## Overview

An Imperial enchantment is an [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) created by [ritual](../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) magic that targets the entire Empire. These enchantments are of particular interest to [Urizeni](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) [stargazers](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Stargazer.md) as they have special relevance to the theory of the [omnihedron](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Stargazer.md#resonance-and-the-omnihedron).
