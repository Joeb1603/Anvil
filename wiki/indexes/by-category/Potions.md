# Category: Potions

## Recipe List

There is a list of recipes known to Imperial apothecaries, which includes all of the following.

| Potion | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Apothecary Potions](../../mixed/magic/potions/Apothecary_Potions.md) | Every apothecary knows how to mix these five preparations; they provide valuable medical aid. |
| [Balms of the Fountainhead](../../mixed/magic/potions/Balms_of_the_Fountainhead.md) | Ointments that increase the power of ritualists to wield Spring lore. |
| [Decoctions of Hoarfrost](../../mixed/magic/potions/Decoctions_of_Hoarfrost.md) | Preparations that increase the power of Winter ritualists. |
| [Double-sided Blade](../../mixed/magic/potions/Double-sided_Blade.md) | Preparations that allow a character to survive lethal blows but with a significant cost. |
| [Infusions of Feathers](../../mixed/magic/potions/Infusions_of_Feathers.md) | Mysterious infusions that grant supernatural insight to those who breath their mystic fumes. |
| [Lambent Essences](../../mixed/magic/potions/Lambent_Essences.md) | Methodical elixirs that increase the power of ritualists to wield Day magic. |
| [Legacy of Thorns](../../mixed/magic/potions/Legacy_of_Thorns.md) | Toxic preparations that weaken and envenom, used on weapons or to poison the unwary. |
| [Magnum Opus](../../mixed/magic/potions/Magnum_Opus.md) | Mysterious preparations that increase the power of ritualists to wield Night magic. |
| [Master Medicinal](../../mixed/magic/potions/Master_Medicinal.md) | Specialist medicinal potions that restore groups of people or provide multiple healing benefits in one swallow. |
| [Philtres of the High Peaks](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_the_High_Peaks.md) | Potions that restore the personal mana of magicians, or drain it completely. |
| [Philtres of War](../../mixed/magic/potions/Philtres_of_War.md) | Potions that restore hero points and bolster a character's fighting spirit - or remove it. |
| [Runa's Gift](../../mixed/magic/potions/Runa-s_Gift.md) | Potions that allow [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) to perform magical acts. |
| [The Assassin's Gate](../../mixed/magic/potions/The_Assassin-s_Gate.md) | Lethal ingested poisons that cause swift, painful death - and their antidotes. |
| [The Last Resort](../../mixed/magic/potions/The_Last_Resort.md) | Lethal ingested poisons captured from the Druj that cause sickness and agonising death - and their antidotes. |
| [The Winter Moon](../../mixed/magic/potions/The_Winter_Moon.md) | Lethal ingested poisons that cause madness and lingering death - and their antidotes. |
| [Tisanes of the Labyrinth](../../mixed/magic/potions/Tisanes_of_the_Labyrinth.md) | Liao-based preparations that empower religious ceremonies with extra strength. |
| [Tonics of the Deep Forest](../../mixed/magic/potions/Tonics_of_the_Deep_Forest.md) | Potions that enhance a character's ability to take damage, allowing them to shrug off wounds that might otherwise incapacitate them. |
| [Tonics of the Open Sky](../../mixed/magic/potions/Tonics_of_the_Open_Sky.md) | Potions that give enhanced fortitude; the extra time they grant mortally wounded characters has saved many lives. |
| [Tinctures of True Eminence](../../mixed/magic/potions/Tinctures_of_True_Eminence.md) | Heady elixirs that increase the power of ritualists to wield Summer magic. |
| [Unguents of Falling Leaves](../../mixed/magic/potions/Unguents_of_Falling_Leaves.md) | Exotic preparations that increase the power of ritualists to wield Autumn magic. |
| [Unshackled Gift](../../mixed/magic/potions/Unshackled_Gift.md) | Potent preparations that allow [orcs](../../mixed/world/Orc.md) to perform heroic acts. |
