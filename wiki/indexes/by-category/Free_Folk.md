# Category: Free Folk

## Overview

The Free Folk is the name taken on by a group of refugees from the [Asavean Archipelago](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md). They were evacuated at the tail end of the short-lived civil war from the satrapy of Marracossa. Early on, they were invited to live at [Caer Faucon](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md#caer-faucon) by the [Lady of the Proffered Hand](../../mixed/titles/Lady_of_the_Proffered_Hand.md). They claim to be followers of the Way, but their particular take on the religion, and their peculiar [paragons and exemplars](../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) caused some tension in the Empire.
