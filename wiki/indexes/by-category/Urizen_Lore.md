## Urizen Lore

Urizen lore (named in the same manner as [Imperial lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list)) is a special body of magical knowledge supported with the assistance of the [eternal](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Phaleron](../../in-character/eternals/Phaleron.md). It functions in a very similar manner to Imperial lore, but only citizens of Urizen can master the rituals contained within it. Even if an individual Urizen magician has not mastered one of these rituals, they may still perform it, just as any other Imperial citizen can perform a ritual that is part of [Imperial lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#imperial-lore).

The lynchpin of Urizen lore is the [Grand Library of Canterspire](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md#canterspire), a magical library in [Morrow](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md) that is operated by the [eternals](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) of the Celestial Library in conjunction with the people of Urizen.

Adding a ritual to Urizen lore requires the [ritual text](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) to be the target of the [Gift of Knowledge](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_way_that_the_sunshine_gleams.md#a-new-archives) ritual. Rather than being returned to the caster, the ritual is added to the body of Urizen lore and becomes available to every Urizen magician following the summit where the ritual is added to lore.

It is not possible for the [Conclave](../../mixed/government/conclave/Conclave.md) to use a [declaration](../../mixed/government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [dissemination](../../mixed/government/conclave/Dissemination.md) or [Imperial lore](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md) to affect a ritual that is part of Urizen lore, nor is it possible to either remove a ritual from Urizen lore, nor to recover the ritual text once it has been added to the body of knowledge.

A former Urizen citizen who leaves the nation loses access to rituals that are part of Urizen lore; they can be replaced using the normal process of changing mastery.
