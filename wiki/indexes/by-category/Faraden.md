# Category: Faraden

## Overview

Faraden lies to the west of the Empire. It is a rocky, hilly nation on the far side of the Lasambrian hills. The Faraden are canny traders and fierce warriors. Up until very recently, the Faraden traded frequently with the [Freeborn](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) at [Anduz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md#anduz), but since the fall of [Segura](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md) in 363YE this has become a good deal more difficult. They are known to trade with their neighbours the [Iron Confederacy](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Iron_Confederacy.md), but also with lands to the south and west that are largely unvisited by Imperial traders - their nation serves as a “gateway” to the southwestern parts of the continent.

The people of Faraden have a lot in common with the Empire but are very independent. A major bone of contention is that their religion is closely related to the Imperial faith but includes elements that the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) believes is heretical, which helps make them extra touchy around Imperial citizens. They are often unprepared for what they see as the 'superior' attitude of their Imperial neighbours.
