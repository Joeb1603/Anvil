# Category: Costume

## Overview

These pages contain guides written by players that may be of interest in making costume pieces. The "National Costumes" pages in particular are very early pages intended to supplement the Look and Feel pages for the appropriate nations and may have been superseded. Official costume guidance is found on the [General costume guidelines](../../out-of-character/rules/Costume.md) page.
