# Category: Warfare

## Overview

This category collects [rituals](../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) that are relevant to [war](../../out-of-character/rules/War.md), and the military campaign. Most are [enchantments](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) that target either an [Imperial army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) or a [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) [personal resource](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource). although there are also several [curses](../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) that have implications for armies and [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md).

All these rituals are part of either [Imperial lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list) or [Urizen lore](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore).
