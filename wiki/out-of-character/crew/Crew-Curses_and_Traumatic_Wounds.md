# Crew:Curses and Traumatic Wounds

Standard traumatic wounds are generally held by the ref team, get a ref assigned to your encounter if you expect a lot to be handed out, or vist the ref desk in GOD to ask for a handful..

For curses you will need to get the wording of your curse approved by the game team (if in doubt drop Steve K a message on Discord.) Then, make the curse as a Unique Activated Item with RIBBON TRACKER in the name, and send that to Jon Fry. At some time on site - hopefully before time in - we’ll turn it into a laminated card you can hand to players.

You can see an example of a curse tracker [here](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire/ribbon?15&Id=27672)
