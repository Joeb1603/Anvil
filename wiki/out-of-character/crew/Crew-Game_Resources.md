# Crew:Game Resources

Money, mana, and more.

All the resources that make up the in-field economy are stacked in boxes in GOD. The best way to get them is by filling in the “Resources” tab with what you need. They will be delivered in a pack on Friday of the event.

Try to only request what you need, otherwise we risk undermining the game’s economy. A good rule of thumb is that an average NPC can have up to the seasonal output of a [personal resource](../rules/Resource.md) (9 crowns, 7 mana, etc.) Rich NPCs may have more, but you’ll need to get explicit approval from the game team.

You can also make New Ribbons and Potions (Artisan items and Apothecary potions) by selecting them from the drop-down menu.
These will all be put together in a plot pack at the event and brought up to the plot cabin.

NPCs are grateful to be given a crown or two if they’re going to be spending a while on the field, as an easy way to get a drink or snack, but these can be sourced from a box in the monster tent on the day.
