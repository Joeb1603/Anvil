# Dean of the Academy

*[Image: Faustina Notturno di Sarvos was appointed as first Dean of the Academy in Summer 380YE](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Faustina.jpg)*

## Overview

The Dean of the Academy is an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Dean_of_the_Academy.md) by the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) in Spring 379YE. The title had significant constitutional implications, and was eventually [ratified](../../mixed/titles/The_Throne.md#ratification) by [Empress Lisabetta](../../in-character/history/Empress_Lisabetta.md) during the Spring Equinox 380YE.

## Responsibilities

The Dean of the Academy is expected to use their power of address to the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) on behalf of the [Academy Student Council](Academy_Student_Council.md). It is important to realise, however, that as always the holder of an Imperial title may do whatever they wish with the powers granted to them.

## Powers

### Address

The Dean of the Academy can make an address to the Senate once each summit.

## Appointment

The Dean of the Academy is an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). As a title created by a motion with constitutional implications, the appointment requires a [two-thirds majority](../../in-character/senate-motions/Senate_motion.md#constitutional-vote) vote of the [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) of the Empire, whether or not they are present, or even in position at that time. Only Senators may vote - the [Conscience of the Senate](../../mixed/titles/Conscience_of_the_Senate.md) may not participate, and the appointment may be [vetoed](../../mixed/government/synod/Veto.md) by the [Synod](../../mixed/government/synod/Synod.md) as with any other [constitutional vote](../../in-character/senate-motions/Senate_motion.md#constitutional-vote).

Any Imperial citizen can hold this title - the title is *not* restricted to members of the Academy staff. The Dean serves for a year. They can be [revoked](../../mixed/government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
