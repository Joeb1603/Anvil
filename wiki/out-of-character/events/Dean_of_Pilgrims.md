# Dean of Pilgrims

```
This title has lapsed, as detailed here.
```

*[Image: The Dean of Pilgrims is responsible for overseeing donations of liao to the Imperial Academy](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NinaOfPilgrims.jpg)*

## Overview

The Dean of Pilgrims was an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Academy_Religious_Studies_sinecure.md) by unanimous vote of the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md) in Winter 378YE to help the [Imperial Academy](The_Academy.md) to instill the heroes of tomorrow with an appreciation for the power of [the Virtues](../../in-character/world/The_Way.md).

## Responsibilities

The Dean oversaw donations of [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) to the Imperial Academy. The liao was intended to be used in the education of the future heroes of the Empire by [the Academy](The_Academy.md) at Anvil. The Dean was also likely to be responsible for overseeing the accommodation and support of religious visitors or pilgrims to the Academy.

## Powers

The Dean of Pilgrims was an [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) appointed by the Senate and connected with the Academy. It granted custodianship of a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md).

### Donations and Wayfarers

The Dean of Pilgrims received an income of 6 [liao](../../mixed/religion/Liao.md) each season from voluntary donations, supplemented by the Imperial Civil Service. Prior to the Academy leaving Canterspire (as detailed in [The arch of the door](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_arch_of_the_door.md) donations amounted to 18 liao.

## Appointment

The Dean of Pilgrims was an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen could hold the title. The Dean had [tenure](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md#term-of-office), and served until they die or stepped down. They could be [revoked](../../mixed/government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Pilgrims' Annexe

During the Winter Solstice 378YE, the Senate approved an annexe to the spire of the Imperial Academy. A light, airy, octagonal building was constructed at the base of [the Canterspire](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md#canterspire). Each face is decorated and illustrated with symbols of [the Way](../../in-character/world/The_Way.md), and with images of famous [Paragons and Exemplars](../../mixed/government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md). Illustrated scrolls, books, and other learning materials are stored in a number side areas, waiting patiently to be used by pupils. It also contains a record of donations to the Academy, as well as the stores of additional liao themselves.

As the threat of the [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) against [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) grew, the Academy staff took the [difficult decision](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_arch_of_the_door.md) to move from Canterspire to the [Castle of Thorns](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md#castle-of-thorns) in [Astolat](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md). As a consequence the Annexe was abandoned, and the Dean of Pilgrims lost access to their sinecure. While the title technically still exists, it has no powers, and has not been reappointed.
