## Senator Elections

Each summit, a number of nations will elect [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for one or more of their territories to serve in the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). Each nation has a unique method of electing its senators. At the Summer Solstice 388YE, the following nations will elect Senators on Saturday afternoon at the times listed in the table below, inside each nation's camp.

| Nation | Time | Territories with Senator elections | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_leadership.md) | 1:00pm | [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) | Auction begins |
| [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_leadership.md) | 2:30pm | [Weirwater](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weirwater.md) | Upon return from the [grand tourney](../../mixed/titles/Knight-protector.md#appointment) |
| [Highguard](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_leadership.md) | 2:00pm | [Bastion](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Bastion.md) | Inquisition of candidates begins |
| [League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_leadership.md) | 3:30pm | [Sarvos](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) <br> [Spiral](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md) | Presentation of collected bonds |
| [Marches](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_leadership.md) | 1:30pm | [Mitwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md) | [Landowners](../rules/Farm.md) gather |
