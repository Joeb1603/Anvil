## Senator Elections

Each summit, a number of nations will elect [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for one or more of their territories to serve in the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). Each nation has a unique method of electing its senators. At the Winter Solstice 386YE, the following nations will elect Senators at the times listed in the table below, inside each nation's camp.

| Nation | Time | Territories with Senator elections | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_leadership.md) | 2:00pm | [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md)[(1)](#1) | Auction begins |
| [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_leadership.md) | 2:30pm | [Astolat](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md) <br> [Weirwater](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weirwater.md)[(1)](#1) | Immediately after the tourney |
| [Highguard](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_leadership.md) | 2:00pm | [Necropolis](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Necropolis.md) | Inquisition of candidates begins |
| [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_leadership.md) | 2:15pm | [Skarsind](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) | Candidates begin presenting items of [worth](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Worth.md) |
