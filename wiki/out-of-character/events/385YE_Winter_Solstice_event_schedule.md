# 385YE Winter Solstice event schedule

## New Player Schedule

For a list of all New Player Meetings, please see [this page](New_player_schedule.md).

## Coronation of the Throne

As detailed in the [A Crown in Winter](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_Crown_in_Winter.md) Wind of Fortune, the Coronation of Vesna Borkovna Prochnost will occur on Saturday afternoon. A summary of the timings for this event can be found below. Citizens are advised to ensure they arrive ahead of time for any segment they wish to attend.
