## Senator Elections

Each summit, a number of nations will elect [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for one or more of their territories to serve in the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). Each nation has a unique method of electing its senators. At the Spring Equinox 387YE, the following nations will elect Senators at the times listed in the table below, inside each nation's camp.

| Nation | Time | Territories with Senator elections | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_leadership.md) | 2:00pm | [Segura](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md) | Auction begins |
| [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_leadership.md) | 2:30pm | [The Barrens](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) | Immediately after the tourney |
| [Highguard](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_leadership.md) | 2:00pm | [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md) | Inquisition of candidates begins |
| [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_leadership.md) | 2:15pm | [Mareave](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md) | Candidates begin presenting items of [worth](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Worth.md) |
| [League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_leadership.md) | 3:30pm | [Holberg](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Holberg.md) | Presentation of collected bonds |
| [Marches](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_leadership.md) | 1:30pm | [Mournwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) | [Landowners](../rules/Farm.md) gather |
