## Senator Elections

Each summit, a number of nations will elect [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for one or more of their territories to serve in the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). Each nation has a unique method of electing its senators. At the Winter Solstice 387YE, the following nations will elect Senators at the times listed in the table below, inside each nation's camp.

| Nation | Time | Territories with Senator elections | Notes |

| [Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_leadership.md) | 1:00pm | [Madruga](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) | Auction begins |
