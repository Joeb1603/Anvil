## Senator Elections

Each summit, a number of nations will elect [senators](../../mixed/titles/Senator.md) for one or more of their territories to serve in the [Senate](../../mixed/government/senate/Senate.md). Each nation has a unique method of electing its senators. At the Spring Equinox 386YE, the following nations will elect Senators at the times listed in the table to the right, inside each nation's camp.

1. The next regular election for this senatorial title will take place at the Winter Solstice 386YE
2. The next regular election for this title will take place at the Summer Solstice 387YE
