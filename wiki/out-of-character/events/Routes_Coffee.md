# Routes Coffee

*[Image: Routes Coffee](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Routes.jpg)*

## Overview

Routes Coffee are one of the outfits who we have invited to cater at all Empire events in 2018. They will be setting up in the out-of-character area near GOD and the other OOC hostelries. See the [Routes Coffee website](https://www.routescoffee.co.uk/) for more information.
