# Herb garden

*[Image: Herb gardens provide vital supplies to physicks and apothecaries .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Canny_Businessman.jpg)*

## Definition

A herb garden represents an area of land where [herbs](../../mixed/magic/potions/Herb.md) grow. A herb garden can be of almost any size - it could be a tiny personal garden in an Urizen spire, meticulously tended and cared for, or it could be a vast forest in Wintermark or Varushka that a character scours in search of wild herbs.

## Production

A herb garden produces 6 drams of [true vervain](True_Vervain.md), 2 drams of [cerulean mazzarine](Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), 2 drams of [bladeroot](Bladeroot.md), 2 drams of [imperial roseweald](Imperial_Roseweald.md), and 2 drams of [marrowort](Marrowort.md).

## Downtime Options

It is possible to obtain a higher yield from a herb garden. You may spend 6 crowns to have the herb garden produce an additional 3 true vervain, 1 cerulean mazzarine, 1 Imperial roseweald, 1 bladeroot and 1 marrowort in that season.

## Upgrades

A herb garden can be upgraded using [weirwood](../../mixed/economy/Weirwood.md) obtained from the Bourse. Each time a herb garden is upgraded, the owner chooses a single herb from the list. The herb garden produces 2 additional drams of that herb from then on.

To upgrade a herb garden requires Imperial wains of weirwood equal to the level the herb garden is being upgraded to. So the first upgrade of a starting level 1 herb garden costs 2 Imperial wains of weirwood, from 2 to 3 costs 3 Imperial wains, etc.

The [Feldspar Distillery](../../mixed/titles/Wise_Arborist.md) in [Skarsind](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) allows herb gardens owned by Imperial Orcs to be upgraded to produce a single dram of [realmsroot](Realmsroot.md) each season rather than one of the standard herbs.
