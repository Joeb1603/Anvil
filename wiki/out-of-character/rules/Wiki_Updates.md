# Wiki Updates

## Overview

This is a list of significant updates to the wiki. We try to keep it as up to date as possible, in the hope it will be a more useful reference than the [recent changes](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/Special:RecentChanges) page which lists every spelling correction and formatting change.

By its nature, this page will be regularly updated, so when you visit the wiki try checking here first. However, please keep in mind this is a manually updated page, so it may be a little behind the actual updates. We tend to make an entry on this page once an update is finalised - so if something has changed and hasn't been mentioned here it's possible that the changes are not yet complete.

In early 2022, we moved all entries on this page prior to 2019 to the [previous wiki updates](Archive-Wiki_updates_to_2018.md) page in the Archive namespace as detailed in [Archiving wiki updates](#archiving-wiki-updates). We did this again in 2024, moving updates prior to 2021 to the same page.

## Ambassadors

- **09/10/2026**

We've completed a review of the eleven [ambassador](../../mixed/titles/Ambassador.md) positions currently in the Empire. For the most part this has been a matter of reorganising the pages slightly, and adding a little more information about the foreign nation and its relations to the Empire. There's also a new section on each one called "Ambassadorial Lore" which includes discussion of the embassies involved, the foreign ambassador who deals with the Empire, and a note on Ambassadorial briefings.

## Titles in Play

- **05/10/2026**

We've removed the "Title in Play" section from Imperial titles. This was a hangover from the early years of the game, when players were often uncertain about what titles could and could not do. Instead we've added it to the [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) page, in a section titled [limitations](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md#limitations).

## Serpenttooth

- **01/10/2026**

We have removed the *Serpenttooth* skill; a free skill from 2016 that allowed people to create a single potion as part of a set of recipes after learning it from a herbal. This potion is now part of the standard list of potions mixable and so the skill is irrelevant. The characters who had that skill have had it removed from their list of skills.

## Rivers Run Riot

- **10/09/2026**

As discussed in the **[A habit I can't break](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_habit_I_can-t_break.md#rivers-red-and-lively)** wind of fortune, the relationship between [Rivers Run Red](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_Run_Red.md) and [Rivers of Life](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Rivers_of_Life.md) has permanently shifted. Instead of removing one another, they simply replace one another. It is also possible to create a similar effect with [Winter magic](Winter_magic.md), but in each case those rituals would all replace one another. Once a ritual that changes casualties at the [territory](Territory.md) level is in play, one can only change which version is active with the other rituals not return the territory to a state of neutrality.

## Favoured Quality

- **08/09/2026**

We've made a slight adjustment to the wording of the [Eastern Sky](../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md) special quality, [favoured](../../mixed/government/military/Eastern_Sky.md#army-quality-favoured). The restriction on issuing the same order on two consecutive seasons means that if the general issues *Traverse the Crossroads* at the Summer Solstice, they can't also use it at the *Autumn Equinox* even though their favours have refreshed. As long as they use different orders each time, they can issue a special order every event.

## Eyes of the Hills

- **05/09/2026**

This ritual has been replaced with a new war ritual that is compatible with the rules for [war magic](War_magic.md). Anyone who currently has the ritual mastered who does not wish to have the new version of this enchantment mastered, can email admin@profounddecisions.co.uk to have it removed from their mastered rituals.

## Arcane Consolidation

- **01/09/2026**

We have updated the rules for [arcane consolidation](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#magic) to provide 35 mana crystals for a full strength army that takes the order, with proportionately less for a depleted force. This replaces the previous rule which rounded the army's strength to the nearest thousand to calculate the mana produced.

## Shield Rules Update

- **28/08//2026**

We have changed the rules for kite and heater shields to give a simpler definition of the maximum sizes. The new rules make it much easier for our weapon check team to check a shield is the right size, quickly and easily at the event and should make it easier for players to be certain whether or not their shield pass. The new rules are designed to be more permissive than the old rules to ensure that any regular shaped kite or heater shield that would have passed before will pass under the new rules.

## Clear Counsel of the Everflowing River

- **24/08/2026**

We've added a clause to the [Clear Counsel of the Everflowing River](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clear_Counsel_of_the_Everflowing_River.md) ritual to make clearer what happens when [preparing the question](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clear_Counsel_of_the_Everflowing_River.md#preparing-the-question). If the question does not follow the format laid down by [Sung](../../in-character/eternals/Sung.md) (*"The named coven asks Sung why..."*) then the eternal will still respond, but is under no obligation to provide a useful answer.

## Hooded Misery

- **12/08/2026**

We've removed the potion Hooded Misery from [The Last Resort](../../mixed/magic/potions/The_Last_Resort.md) potion set. It is no longer possible to mix this potion, and existing potions will have no effect beyond making the drinker feel ill for a short time. We've done this for several reasons, one of which is that we're not interested in rekindling contagious disease plot.

## Fortifications

- **03/02/2026**

We've made some significant changes to the way [fortifications](Fortification.md) work in the military campaign. The fortification page has been extensively edited; fortifications are now split into a [bastion](Fortification.md#bastion) that represents the physical fortification, and a [garrison](Fortification.md#garrison) that represents the soldiers who defend it. In the process, we've removed the citadel commission. There is a [rules update](Rules_update_2026.md#fortifications-and-citadels) that discusses the changes in more detail.

At the same time, we've made changes to several rituals that interact with fortifications. This includes:

- [Hammers of the Brilliant Shore](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Hammers_of_the_Brilliant_Shore.md) and [Stalwart Stand on Solid Ground](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Stalwart_Stand_on_Solid_Ground.md) explicitly affects only the bastion of a fortification.
- [Stone's Unyielding Defiance](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Stone-s_Unyielding_Defiance.md) and [Ramparts of Ashlar](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#ramparts-of-ashlar) provide additional force to the garrison of a fortification
- [Thunderous Tread of the Trees](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Thunderous_Tread_of_the_Trees.md) and [Mountain Remembers Its Youth](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mountain_Remembers_Its_Youth.md) affect both the bastion and the garrison, and how their damage is inflicted has been adjusted slightly.
- The *Wolves of Winter*, a ritual not in Imperial Lore, provides additional strength to the garrison of the enchanted fortification, but *only* for purposes of inflicting casualties.
- The four "citadel" rituals - [Frozen Citadel of Cathan Canae](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Frozen_Citadel_of_Cathan_Canae.md), [Forge the Wooden Fastness](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Forge_the_Wooden_Fastness.md), [Dripping Echoes of the Fen](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Dripping_Echoes_of_the_Fen.md), and [The Basalt Citadel](../../mixed/magic/rituals/The_Basalt_Citadel.md) have been changed significantly, and each provides different benefits.

Some of the rituals have also had their magnitude adjusted. We're currently still assessing existing fortifications, and outstanding opportunities and will update them as we go.

## Chimes of Annulment

- **08/09/2025**

We've changed the duration of effect for [Chimes of Annulment](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Chimes_of_Annulment.md) from one minute to ten minutes. The previous short duration left too little margin for error to make it an appealing option on battles. The new time is designed to be difficult - but more achievable. - **16/07/2025**

As referenced in the **[Beat of your drum](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Beat_of_your_drum.md#lyceum)** wind of fortune, it is not necessary when casting [Sum of the Parts](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Sum_of_the_Parts.md) for the three additional participants to take damage. ## Sun Chamber

- **14/07/2025**

We have added a new sodality - the [Sun Chamber](../../mixed/titles/Sun_Chamber.md) to the game. This is a powerful [Imperial sodality](../../mixed/government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) whose membership is restricted to citizens aged 14-18. The aim is to directly address the fact that Empire can be a very inaccessible game for players before they turn twenty. The new sodality exists to help members gain positions of power and influence in the Empire and encourage members to approach existing problems in new ways.

## Secrets of the Empty Heart

- **25/06/2025**

We've updated the ritual [Secrets of the Empty Heart](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Secrets_of_the_Empty_Heart.md) to make it explicit that you can use the special ability granted by the [enchantment](Enchantment.md) even if you are under the effect of [weakness](Calls.md#weakness), [dying](Combat.md#dying), or [terminal](Combat.md#terminal). This is a special case where an active ability can be used when in a state where it would normally not be possible.

## Army Enchantments

- **25/03/2025**

We've made several changes to rituals that [enchant](Enchantment.md) an [Imperial army](Army.md). Almost every army enchantment ritual has changed in some fashion. It is no longer possible to change the [quality](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md) of an army with magic, and the resonant effects for each realm have been much more clearly defined. You can find out more about these changes in the accompanying [rules update](Rules_update_2026.md).

- [Swords in the Noonday Sun](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Swords_in_the_Noonday_Sun.md) ([Urizen lore](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore); Summer 80): now fills soldiers with supernatural prowess, increasing the strength of an army by the equivalent of 1000 extra warriors. It's also gained an option for additional targets. The roleplaying associated with it has also changed, meaning it is no longer considered a ritual that uses savagery to create its effect.
- [Find the Best Path](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Find_the_Best_Path.md) (Autumn 80): Now grants a campaign army the ability to move through an additional territory, rather than granting the [fast](../../mixed/government/military/Army_qualities.md#fast) quality. The magnitude has been lowered to 80, and an option to include additional targets has been added.
- [Quickening Cold Meat](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Quickening_Cold_Meat.md) (Winter 120): We've reduced the magnitude of the ritual and been significantly clearer about the unpredictable dangers associated with the ritual.
- [Machines of Destruction](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#machines-of-destruction) (Autumn 135): No longer offered by [Estavus](../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md) and removed from the [Mystic scrolls of Estavus](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md) page.
- [Loosen the Shackles](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Loosen_the_Shackles.md) ([Urizen lore](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore)) Winter 120): now gives an enchanted army the equivalent of 4,000 extra soldiers but only for purposes of inflicting casualties on an enemy. The magnitude has increased to 120. It still draws on the forbidden power of [Agramant](../../in-character/eternals/Agramant.md).
- [Why Sulemaine Walked Away From The Baker](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Why_Sulemaine_Walked_Away_From_The_Baker.md) (Winter 80): The effects of the ritual have been increased. In addition to making the army more resistant to effects related to poison and venom, it also reduces damage suffered from other means.
- [Bound by Common Cause](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Bound_by_Common_Cause.md) (Autumn 80): has had its effect completely redesigned and now reduces the upkeep of an army while providing potential narrative bonuses.
- [Fire on the Water](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Fire_on_the_Water.md) and [Eyes of the Hills](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Eyes_of_the_Hills.md) cannot currently be cast; we are still deciding how to deal with them.

The change has also affected some non-enchantment rituals.

- [Eyes of the Sun and Moon](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Eyes_of_the_Sun_and_Moon.md) (Day 14): now provides information about enchantments on armies and fortifications in the territory scried.
- [Eye of the High Places](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Eye_of_the_High_Places.md) (Day 14): now provides information about large-scale effects on a territory.
- [Dreams in the Witch House](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Dreams_in_the_Witch_House.md) (Night 14): has been reworked to make it better reflect the way the ritual had evolved in play and to widen the kind of information it provides. The magnitude has increased to 14, bringing it into line with similar divinations.

## Fleet Enchantments

- **24/03/2025**

In conjunction with our [changes to military units](#military-unit-enchantments), we've also updated the way a [fleet](Fleet.md) acts in [downtime](Downtime.md). As with military units, we've streamlined the actions that can be taken by a fleet captain, and how they interact with [enchantment](Enchantment.md) rituals. We've introduced several new actions that any fleet captain can take, and defined others more closely. These changes will take effect in the downtime following the Winter Solstice.

The main change to rituals has been to better define which action each ritual supports. One of our goals has been to ensure that each ritual affects only one kind of action, and where possible to strengthen the themes of the individual realms as part of a wider update. The magnitudes of rituals have not changed, nor has the amount of benefit they provide; we've just defined what action that benefit applies to more clearly.

Rituals affected include [Blood and Salt](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Blood_and_Salt.md) (supports raiding), [Winds of Fortune](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Winds_of_Fortune.md) (supports trading), [The Lure of Distant Shores](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Lure_of_Distant_Shores.md) (supports trading), [Sular's Promise](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Sular-s_Promise.md) (supports trading), [Golden Voyage](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Golden_Voyage.md) (supports questing), [Muffled Oar](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Muffled_Oar.md) (supports scouting), and [Piranhas Devour the Kraken](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Piranhas_Devour_the_Kraken.md) (supports campaigning). The ritual *Shroud of Rolling Fog*, which is not in Imperial lore, has likewise been adjusted to support a single category of fleet action.

We've also added a new ritual to Imperial Lore: [Wolves of the Hungry Sea](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Wolves_of_the_Hungry_Sea.md) (Winter 12) that is used to support guarding actions.

As always, if your character has mastered a ritual that has changed you can email [admin@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:admin@profounddecisions.co.uk) and we'll free up the mastered ritual slot for you.

## Military Unit Enchantments

- **14/03/2025**

We've overhauled the way [military units](Military_unit.md) act in [downtime](Downtime.md). The focus has been on streamlining the actions that can be taken by a player with that resource, and how they interact with [enchantment](Enchantment.md) rituals. We've introduced several new actions that any military unit can take (such as mercenary work or seeking glory) as well as a small number of adventures available to specific nations or archetypes. These changes have also affected a few rituals that are not in either Imperial lore or Urizen lore. Specifically, [Accoutrements of War](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Mystic_scrolls_of_Estavus.md#accoutrements-of-war) (supports campaigning, no longer provides green iron), and *Weaver at the Web* (supports scouting actions).

As always, if your character has mastered a ritual that has changed you can email [admin@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:admin@profounddecisions.co.uk) and we'll free up the mastered ritual slot for you.

## Sung and their Counsel

- **22/06/2024**

We've made a couple of tiny changes to the [Sung](../../in-character/eternals/Sung.md) page, to provide more context for the kind of mysteries that the eternal encourages you to probe and look into and those mysteries she prefers to stay hidden. We've coupled this with a set of updates to the main ritual that draws on Sung's power [Clear Counsel of the Everflowing River](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Clear_Counsel_of_the_Everflowing_River.md).

We've decided to change the way we respond to queries to Sung to try and provide more useful information going forwards. We want to keep some of the enigmatic quality of the responses, which some of those players casting the ritual currently enjoy - but we want to make it easier to get more useful insight, guidance and wisdom from the ritual.

To help us do that we've changed the way we'll be responding to the ritual going forwards. We created a set of guidelines for the style of responses that we'll give to the different kinds of questions we've been getting up to now. Hopefully this means that where we can provide some useful information or clues to players about a plot we'll be able to continue to do that, but where Sung can't or won't give an answer we'll be able to respond in a way that makes clear why that is the case.

The goal with these changes is to make the ritual more useful to players but also make it easier for us to produce the responses. We want to emphasise the roleplaying interaction with Sung - but allow the eternal more freedom to give out more useful information when it exists.

## Sentinel Gate

- **01/07/2023**

We have removed references to getting a traumatic wound if you travel back too late through the sentinel gate. Implementation of this rule was always spotty at best and it violated some of our core design principles in the way it confused in-character and out-of-character objectives. We don't want players to return through the gate late, because it plays havoc with the schedule that everyone, players and ourselves, are relying on. Most participants implicitly appreciate this and make the best effort possible to return to Anvil before the gate begins to close. Traumatic wounds are intended to lead to further roleplaying for the participants who receive them, they are not meant to punish participants for taking the wrong action. Empire tries as hard as possible to avoid mixing in-character and out-of-character consequences in that way.

## FIRST AID

- **01/07/2023**

We've changed the MAN DOWN call to [FIRST AID](Calls.md#first-aid). We've made this change for two reasons, one is that we and many of our players are no longer comfortable with such a clearly gendered call. The other reason is that over the years, a few players have developed a bad habit of using the call in-character when someone gets wounded. We've replaced the call with FIRST AID - a clear call asking for the first aid team to provide assistance.

We understand that many of participants will continue to use MAN DOWN in emergency situations, precisely because it is the instinctive thing to shout in an emergency. That is absolutely fine, and everyone should treat the call *exactly* as if they had called FIRST AID. We expect it to take some years to drop the old call in favour of the new one and we would much rather participants shouted either call quickly than pause to think. The hobby has been using the man down call for decades and we do not want to compromise safety while we transition away from its use at Empire.

## Blood of the Hydra

- **14/03/2023**

[Blood of the Hydra](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Blood_of_the_Hydra.md) has been amended to make it explicit that it is intended for the treatment of limbs ruined by effects such as [CLEAVE](Calls.md#cleave) or the like. It cannot be used to regrow internal or damaged organs, and is only useful for traumatic wounds when explicitly called out. Damaged internal organs are very much intended to be in the purview of the [physick](Surgical_skills.md#physick), and regenerating or replacing them magically is a much more significant use of magic than simply repairing a limb ruined by a sword. **(23/03/2023)** Further discussion of this change can be found [here](Rules_update_2023.md#reasoning-blood-of-the-hydra).

## Cardinals and Assemblies

- **15/09/2022**

We've added a note to the [cardinal](../../mixed/titles/Cardinal.md) page in the [appointment](../../mixed/titles/Cardinal.md#appointment) section reinforcing the idea that a cardinal must be a member of the [assembly](../../mixed/government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) they lead. Changing assembly (perhaps through [preaching](Congregation.md#downtime-options) in downtime) means the cardinal forfeits their title. Likewise, someone without a [congregation](Congregation.md) who gains membership of an assembly through the votes granted by a [sinecure](../../mixed/commissions/Sinecure.md) cannot become cardinal because as soon as they do, they lose their former [Imperial title](../../mixed/titles/Imperial_title.md) and no longer meet the criteria to be cardinal. ## Imperial Enchantments

- **15/09/2022**

We've adjusted the magnitude of the three [Imperial enchantments](../../indexes/by-category/Imperial_Enchantment.md) up from 160 to 200. In-character magical scholars believe this is due to the increasing size of the Empire, and might have been heralded by a [subtle conjunction](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_yellow_star_hung.md#a-light-that-never-goes-out) of the esoteric [constellations](../../mixed/magic/lore/Astronomancy.md#constellations) of [the Key](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md#the-key) and [the Lock](../../mixed/magic/lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md) during the Autumn Equinox. Out-of-character this is because we want to keep these rituals challenging, and the former magnitude is no longer as difficult to achieve as it was when the game began.

We also took the opportunity to change how [Hallow of the Green World](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Hallow_of_the_Green_World.md) works. Previously the coven had to choose which herb the herb gardens would create. This is because when we first created these rituals we didn't have the ability to add random herbs to resources. Now that we do, we've update the ritual to work a little bit more like [Bright Eyes Gleam in the Depths](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Bright_Eyes_Gleam_in_the_Depths.md) and [Wondrous Forests of the Night](../../mixed/magic/rituals/Wondrous_Forests_of_the_Night.md) whose bonus production is set by the base resource rather than being chosen.

## Shroud of Fog and Ravens

- **19/08/2022**

As noted in the **[Of earth and sky](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Of_earth_and_sky.md#game-information)** wind of war, we're removing the ritual *Shroud of Fog and Ravens* from the game. Unfortunately, this ritual is completely incompatible with Empire's magic theory and was a remnant from the very first years of the game before we had nailed down all these elements. As a consequence we are removing this ritual. Any character who has it mastered will now have an empty "slot" for mastering new rituals. The in-character explanation is that the recent conjunction of the Lock has rendered the ritual unworkable, taking away whatever strange power it relied on. We will continue to reject attempts to create similar effects in this realm using arcane projections; it is not possible to enchant armies in this way with [Night magic](Night_magic.md).

- **29/7/2022**

## IC Tents

- **12/07/2022**

We have discovered that the range of tents marketed as Oxford Canvas has grown to include a small number of tents that don't meet our minimum requirements for the in-character field because the colour and thickness of the material they are made from looks too much like plastic. We've clarified the [rules](Costume.md) to make clear that these types of tent are not allowed because of this.

We do understand that the tents are appealing because they are cheap and light, but we have to have a cut-off at some point otherwise we would have to allow any tent in the IC area.

We've created an amnesty to allow these ultra-light sandstone bell tents to be used for the remainder of 2022 to remove any immediate need to change.

## Emphasising Agramant

- **13/01/2022**

We’ve made a change to the setting we want to let you know about. From this point forward, the eternal who has been primarily known by the name Wendigo will be more commonly known as [Agramant](../../in-character/eternals/Agramant.md). Likewise we're changing the name of the Wendigo's Bargain item to [Agramant's Bargain](../../mixed/magic/items/Agramant-s_Bargain.md).

We are not changing anything else about the character or tendencies of this eternal, but we are de-emphasising the name Wendigo. Agramant has always been one of Wendigo's names - so we've chosen to emphasise it as the "preferred" name for the eternal on the wiki from now on.

We've done this for several reasons. We've been aware for a while of some concerns about the [origin of the name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wendigo), and what that implies for our use of the eternal. We’ve reflected on that feedback and noted that while many of the other eternals make references (at variable subtlety levels) to literary, mythological, or gaming sources, Wendigo was the only one we know of with a name that is directly featured in a real-world belief system. There are no eternals called Hades, Hel, or Balor for example.

For the time being, the name Wendigo will remain one of Agramant’s less-widely used sobriquets. We'd like people to adopt this de-emphasising approach over time rather than treating this as a retcon or sudden break - for example, we know that many characters have written a lot of material about this eternal. It isn't suddenly off brief or wrong to use the original name, and there's no need to reprint IC booklets that use the name Wendigo. The name will remain one of Agramant's less widely used soubriquets. From this point forward, the eternal who has been primarily known by the name Wendigo will be more commonly known as Agramant.
