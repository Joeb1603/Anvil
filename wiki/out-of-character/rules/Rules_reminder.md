# Rules reminder

## Overview

A handy archive of [rules](Empire_rules.md) reminders from the left bar.

## List

- If a call is not clearly taken by the target, other than VENOM and WEAKNESS, then it has not been used. See the [Calls](Calls.md) page for more information. (25/08/2026)
- In-character theft is prohibited except for gold-ribboned items or looting fallen enemies. See the [Stealing](Stealing.md) page for more information. (12/08/2026)
- Roleplaying effects are spiritual in origin, they represent a supernatural influence affecting your character's soul. See the [Roleplaying effects](Roleplaying_effects.md) page for more information. (06/07/2026)
- You must not attempt to parry an arrow or bolt with a weapon. See the [Combat safety](Combat_safety.md) page for more information. (27/06/2026)
- You may not film or record other participants in the IC area during time-in. See the [Photography](../site/Photography.md#filming-and-audio-recordings) page for more information. (11/05/2026)
- The use of generative AI is prohibited at Empire and in our online spaces. See the [Playing the game](Playing_the_game.md#artificial-intelligence) and [Online](Online.md#do-not-post-ai-content) pages for more information. (25/03/2026)
- An existing character who wishes to move nation will need to seek out an [egregore](../../in-character/nations/general/Egregores.md) of the nation they wish to join. See the [Nation FAQ](Nation_FAQ.md#can-i-change-nation-in-play) page for more information. (11/02/2026)
- You must avoid hitting the groin, neck, or head. See the [combat safety](Combat_safety.md) page for more information. (22/08/2025)
- When casting [detect magic](Detect_magic.md) it is important to make clear which of the seven [effects](Detect_magic.md#effects) you are invoking. (22/06/2025)
- Having a [ruined leg](Calls.md#cleave) does not prevent you from using [unstoppable](Heroic_skills.md#unstoppable), but it will not fix it. (10/06/2025)
- If you have a [ruined leg](Calls.md#cleave) you must keep your backside on the floor. It is not possible to kneel whilst one or both of your legs is ruined. (11/05/2025)
- Performing an [Anointing](Anointing.md) ceremony requires a willing target, even if it is removing an aura. (27/03/2025)
- When wielding a [polearm](Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms) you must hold the weapon in both hands with at least 18” separating them. (10/03/2025)
- A [MASS](Calls.md#mass-calls) call affects everyone in a ninety-degree arc in front of and up to 20' (6m) away from the caster. (18/02/2025)
- All [arrows and bolts](Combat.md#arrows) automatically cause [Impale](Calls.md#impale), *and* do a point of damage! (16/12/2024)
- [Weakness](Calls.md#weakness) and [Venom](Calls.md#venom) are only removed by effects that remove them, if they are not removed then they will last indefinitely. (04/12/2024)
- You should treat others with respect and assume best faith when online. See our [online](Online.md#treat-each-other-with-respect-and-assume-best-faith) rules for more details (02/08/2024)
- All spells require vocals clearly audible to everyone within a few metres See the [Spellcasting](Spellcasting.md#spellcasting) page for more information. (15/07/2024)
- You can only lose one global hit per second from multiple attacks from one enemy; you should aim to land one blow a second at most. See the [Combat](Combat.md#the-one-second-rule) page for more information. (10/07/2024)
- If you play a battle then you must volunteer to monster one. See the [Battles](../events/Battles.md#play-one-monster-one) page for more information. (01/07/2024)
- You must avoid using gendered nouns when referring to mixed groups. See the [Conduct](Conduct.md#language) page for more information. (12/05/2024)
- In-character theft is prohibited except for gold-ribboned items or looting fallen enemies. See the [Stealing](Stealing.md) page for more information. (09/04/2024)
- You can only send messages to [eternals](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) with rituals that explicitly allow you to do so. (29/02/2024)
- Once you have gained the benefit of a [magic item](Magic_items.md), no-one else can use it for that day. Furthermore, you cannot gain the benefit of a different item of the same form ([Weapon](Magic_items.md#weapons), [Armour](Magic_items.md#armour), [Talisman](Magic_items.md#talismans)) that day. (03/01/2024)
- When using things that grant [additional ranks](Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks) of [realm lore](Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) the highest you can go is triple your original skill (so you can't have more than two extra ranks if you've only bought one rank of realm lore). (04/12/2023)
- You take damage from any [call](Combat.md#heroic-blows) made with a weapon or implement even if the call doesn't affect you. (12/11/2023)
- It takes at least two free hands to [move](Combat.md#moving-a-dying-character) someone who's dead, [dying](Combat.md#dying), [terminal](Combat.md#terminal), or [paralysed](Calls.md#paralyse) (07/08/2023)
- You must have purchased at least one rank of a [realm lore](Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) skill to benefit from anything that grants [additional ranks](Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks) in that realm. (17/07/2023)
- [Using](Potion.md#consuming-a-potion) a [potion](Potion.md) or [philtre](Potion.md#philtres) takes five seconds of appropriate roleplaying, usually involving miming drinking the potion. (10/07/2023)
- When you're [dying](Combat.md#dying) you aren't unconscious unless you want to be - you can still speak, shout, or scream for help. (07/07/2023)
- You can only be under the effect of one [enchantment](Enchantment.md) at a time; the most recent is the [only one](Enchantment.md#removing-enchantments) that applies! (03/07/2023)
- You can't use [Unstoppable](Heroic_skills.md#unstoppable) if you're under the effects of a [WEAKNESS](Calls.md#weakness) or [PARALYSE](Calls.md#paralyse) (26/06/2023)
- You need to [check your arrows](Bow_safety.md#essential-check) both when you pick them up *and* when you put them onto the bow! (22/06/2023)
