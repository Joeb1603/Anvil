# Empire rules

## [Rules Summary](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/mediawiki-public/images/b/b6/Rules_Guide_A5.pdf)

*[Image: Warbeast.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/Rules_Guide_A5.pdf)*

A short, printable [rules summary](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/mediawiki-public/images/b/b6/Rules_Guide_A5.pdf) of the key points is available. If you have any questions then check the [FAQ](General_FAQ.md) first and if that doesn't help then please [contact Profound Decisions](../site/Contact_Profound_Decisions.md) and we will answer your questions.

## [Playing the Game](Playing_the_game.md)

*[Image: Emma+Tom.jpg](Playing_the_game.md)*

This section tells you how to [play the game](Playing_the_game.md) - providing the rules and guidelines for creating your character as well as roleplaying the character at an event.

## [Combat](Combat.md)

*[Image: JotunMarch.jpg](Combat.md)*

All player-characters are able to fight in Empire if they choose. The [combat rules](Combat.md) explain what to do in skirmishes and battles and how to use your skills.

## [Safety](Safety_overview.md)

*[Image: Orc prisoner.jpg](Safety_overview.md)*

[Safety](Safety_overview.md) is important to ensure that events are as safe as possible. Everyone must make sure that they understand these rules even if they do not intend to fight.

## [Groups](Bands.md)

*[Image: FreebornFamily.jpg](Bands.md)*

Players creating characters together can create a concept for a group to link those characters together. There are special rules some types of groups, called bands in Empire.

## [Skills](Skills.md)

*[Image: LeagueCrossbowman.jpg](Skills.md)*

A character is defined by the [skills](Skills.md) that they possess. You can purchase any skill you want for your character, though some skills must be bought first.

## [Magic](Magic.md)

*[Image: MagicStaff.jpg](Magic.md)*

Magicians are able to wield the force of [magic](Magic.md), using it to drive enemies away or mend shattered weapons and bones. Read this section if you are interested in playing a magician.

## [Resource](Resource.md)

*[Image: RingsAndCoins.jpg](Resource.md)*

[Resources](Resource.md) provide a character with wealth and precious resources. Every character in Empire may control [campaign resources](Resource.md#campaign-resource) and a single [personal resource](Resource.md#personal-resource).

## [Potions](Potion.md)

*[Image: Nickpouring.jpg](Potion.md)*

Herbs can be mixed by characters with the apothecary skill to make [potions and poisons](Potion.md). Potions can be consumed by anyone, providing valuable magical benefits.

## [Magical Items](Magic_items.md)

*[Image: DawnFighting2.jpg](Magic_items.md)*

There are scores of different [magical items](Magic_items.md) that exist in Empire. They are created and sold by player-characters and many characters will have access to one or two.
