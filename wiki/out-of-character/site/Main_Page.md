# Main Page

[Image: Empire](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Banner-Image.jpg)

## Welcome

Welcome to the Empire Wiki; here you will be able to find everything you need to know about Empire, the award-winning live roleplaying game from [Profound Decisions](http://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/), which began Easter 2013. We often upload new content to the site, so if you are returning after a break then check the [Wiki Updates](../rules/Wiki_Updates.md) section for a list of what has changed since your last visit. For up to date in character information head to the [recent history](../../in-character/history/Recent_history.md) page.

## [The Game](../game-design/The_game.md)

[Image: Warbeast.jpg](../game-design/The_game.md)
Read our introduction to [live roleplaying](../game-design/Live_roleplaying.md) if you're new to the hobby. Then take a look at the [game overview](../game-design/Game_overview.md) to get an idea of what Empire is all about.

## [The Rules](../rules/Empire_rules.md)

[Image: The Rules](../rules/Empire_rules.md)
Empire uses a simple set of rules that are designed to be fun to play and easy to understand. The rules explain how to [make a character](../rules/Characters.md) and [how the game works](../rules/Playing_the_game.md).

## [Conduct](../rules/Conduct.md)

[Image: Emma+Tom.jpg](../rules/Conduct.md)
All participants at Empire are required to follow our conduct rules at all times. Our [conduct](../rules/Conduct.md) policy lays out clear rules so that everyone who participates in Empire knows what is expected.

## [The Empire](../../in-character/world/Empire_overview.md)

[Image: Aedan Myfanwy.jpg](../../in-character/world/Empire_overview.md)
Find out about the political structures of the Empire that allow players to shape the game world. Also here is information about [the Way of Virtue](../../in-character/world/The_Way.md), the unique Imperial religion.

## [The Nations](../../in-character/nations/general/Nation_overview.md)

[Image: Brasscoast Warriors.jpg](../../in-character/nations/general/Nation_overview.md)
The Empire is composed of ten [nations](../../in-character/nations/general/Nation_overview.md). Each culture is detailed on the wiki including advice on costumes.

## [The World](../../in-character/world/World_overview.md)

[Image: The World](../../in-character/world/World_overview.md)
The Empire is part of a rich and vibrant campaign setting that includes [barbarian](../../in-character/barbarians/Barbarian.md) enemies, [foreign powers](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Foreign_nations.md) and [six magical realms](../../in-character/eternals/Realm.md) as well as their [supernatural inhabitants](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md).
