# How to - Make a Suaq coat

## Paul Bassinder's awesome photo guide

[Image: 300px-Coat1.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat1.jpg)

1. Long fur coat, turned inside out with the lining removed

[Image: 300px-Coat2.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat2.jpg)

2. From the back

[Image: 300px-Coat3.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat3.jpg)

3. Coat cut to length and sleeves shortened

[Image: 300px-Coat4.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat4.jpg)

4. Collar cut down to the neckline

[Image: 300px-Coat5.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat5.jpg)

5. Hood: Rectangle cut out of the base (removed in step 3).

[Image: 300px-Coat6.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat6.jpg)

6. Hood stitched on with waxed thread.

[Image: 300px-Coat7.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat7.jpg)

7. Any holes, including those from pocket removal are roughly over-stitched with fur edges turned out.

[Image: 300px-Coat8.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat8.jpg)

8. Stencils: Images printed out on thin card and only black pieces cut out.

[Image: 300px-Coat9.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat9.jpg)

9. Images drawn on in pen and filled in with black leather dye.

[Image: 300px-Coat10.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat10.jpg)

10. Edging: Leather straps cut to length. Stitching line, zig zag and circles added. Stitching holes marked and punched.

[Image: 300px-Coat11.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat11.jpg)

11. Leather stained.

[Image: 300px-Coat12.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat12.jpg)

12. Edges glued in places to hold whilst stitching. 1" bone tubes added to arms for extra decoration.

[Image: 300px-Coat13.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat13.jpg)

13. Holes punched for thonging to do up front of coat.

[Image: 300px-Coat14.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat14.jpg)

14. Thonging in place on innermost side.

[Image: 300px-Coat15.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat15.jpg)

15. Thonging pulls through outermost side and simple fastening used.

[Image: 300px-Coat16.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Coat16.jpg)

16. Back of coat.
