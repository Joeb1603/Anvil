# Imperial Administrator

## Overview

The Imperial Administrator was an [Imperial title](../../titles/Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Senate](Senate.md). It was [created](../../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-imperial-administrator) by the Senate during the Summer 384YE summit. The title was [abrogated](../../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#abrogate-imperial-administrator) during the Winter 386YE summit.

## Responsibilities

The Imperial Administrator was expected to support the bodies of state in efficient and effective diplomatic, trading, and military efforts.

## Powers

### Right of Address

The Imperial Administrator had the right to make a [Senate address](Senate_address.md) once per summit. This was intended to allow them to present important information for the senators' consideration. To make an address, the Administrator had to inform the [Speaker for the Senate](../../titles/Speaker_for_the_Senate.md) of their intention, so that the Speaker could add the address to the agenda for the next [Senate session](Senate_session.md).

### Stipend

The Imperial Administrator received a stipend of 5 thrones each season from the [Imperial treasury](../../economy/Imperial_treasury.md).

## Appointment

The Imperial Administrator was an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen could hold this title, and the Administrator served for a year. They could be [revoked](../synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
