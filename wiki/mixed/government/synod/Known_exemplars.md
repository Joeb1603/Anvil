# Known exemplars

## Overview

There are countless exemplars who are known to have been [recognised](Recognition.md) by the [Synod](Synod.md), but whose life and deeds are not yet renowned throughout the Empire. The list is updated whenever new exemplars are recognised by the Imperial Synod or records of past exemplars are recovered.

## Recent Exemplars

This is a definitive list of all the exemplars recognised in recent times.

### Mother Od

Recognised as an exemplar of [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) by the Synod in the [376YE Winter Solstice Synod judgements](../../../in-character/synod-judgements/376YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md). Also known as [Odd of Odd's End](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#oddmire).

### Olwyn the Witness

Recognised as an exemplar of [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) by the Synod in the [376YE Winter Solstice Synod judgements](../../../in-character/synod-judgements/376YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md).

### Damaris of Cantiarch’s Hold
