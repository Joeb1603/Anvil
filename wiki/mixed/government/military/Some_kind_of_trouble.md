# Some kind of trouble

## Overview

> **Note:** Unless otherwise specified, all major and large conjunctions listed below have the following accessibility information:
>
> - Level of combat: [Combat Highly Likely](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/Accessibility#Combat_Highly_Likely)
> - Movement required: [Free Ranging](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/Accessibility#Location.2C_Terrain_and_Movement)

Before each summit, the prognosticators produce details of every conjunction that they have been able to identify based on reports compiled by the war scouts and from other sources such as the egregores and information being fed to them from the campaign fronts. The conjunctions presented here are not all the conjunctions of the Sentinel Gate at a gathering, but they are the ones that the civil service has been able to find details of. Because of their focus, these conjunctions are usually very military-orientated; they are focused on the movement of the Empire's armies and the barbarian forces arrayed against them. Smaller conjunctions that allow groups to pursue their own personal agendas are only rarely discovered by the prognosticators. New conjunctions are often found by citizens in Anvil after learning details from concerned citizens, communications with eternals or foreign powers, or through magical divination or religious ceremonies.

## Small conjunctions

> **Note:** Small conjunctions can have varying levels of threat and combat. Use the information presented here as a guide and check with a referee if you have further questions.

Further information relating to the nature of the small conjunctions might be available from the War Scouts on duty at the Sentinel Gate. However, smaller conjunctions often escape the attention of the prognosticators and other civil servants focused on the Empire's wars, meaning that the War Scouts are not always able to help.

These are not the only small conjunctions of the Sentinel Gate at the Spring Equinox. Others will become apparent through events happening in play, via NPCs or through information in your player packs.
