### Overview

The Argent Sword were raised by [Senate motion](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Raise_Second_Urizen_Army.md) proposed by **Herminius of the House of the Wanderer**, then Senator for [Zenith](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md), during the Spring Equinox 387YE as a result of an [offer](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_box_for_a_sword.md#the-blade-of-finest-silver) made by [Callidus](../../../in-character/eternals/Callidus.md). Mustering completed in a single season, shortly before the Summer Solstice 387YE.

The second Urizen general leads the Argent Sword army, and is appointed (or re-appointed) at the Winter Solstice each year.

## Army Quality : [Raiders](Army_qualities.md#foraging)

The soldiers of the [Iron Labyrinth](../../../in-character/eternals/Realm.md#autumn) are rough and ready, with an eye for potential profit. The Argent Sword can be unleashed to focus on coin over conquest to make the general wealthy.

### Bound to the Argent Tontine

The Argent Sword is a part of an ongoing contract with [Callidus](../../../in-character/eternals/Callidus.md), the Sovereign-Lord of the City of Chains. The army can only benefit from [resupply](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#resupply), natural or otherwise, whilst the eternal enjoys the [amity](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#amity) of the Conclave. In addition, due to its nature, it is only possible to [enchant](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#rituals) the army with Autumn magic.

Should Callidus be placed under [enmity](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity), there may be some legal complications, but given the mercenaries are not from any one city, it would only pose a problem if the Conclave were to place a significant number of Autumn eternals under enmity.
