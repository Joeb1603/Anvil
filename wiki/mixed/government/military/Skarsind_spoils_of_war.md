# Skarsind spoils of war

*[Image: Skarsind](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Braunwald_Alps.jpg)*

Following the liberation of Skarsind by Imperial troops in 377YE, these resources were presented to the [Military council](Military_Council.md) for assignment as [spoils of war](Spoils_of_war.md)

### Goeki Expanses

**Dragonbone Forest / Rank 4**  
A wide expanse of forest in the hinterlands of the [Skogei Glens](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#skogei-glens) is particularly rich in [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). A twisting maze of interconnected paths forms a network throughout the area along which collectors pick their way, searching the undergrowth for deposits of the valuable material.

### Solvihill Beggarwood Barrens

**Beggar's Lye Forest / Rank 2**  
Frequented by charcoal burners from nearby [Solvi](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#solvihill), the Solvihill beggarwood barrens were abandoned to the Thule, then partially burnt when the barbarians retreated. They are still a rich source of the raw materials to make [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), thanks to prompt action by citizens of the nearby town.

### Southpine Southbound

**Business / Rank 2**  
Based in [Torfast](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#southpine), caravans operated by Southpine Southbound smuggled valuable supplies to the resistance during the recent occupation. Now the war is over, the trading company can return to the lucrative business of selling raw materials to the [Temeschwari](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), and valuable crafted goods to the people of Skarsind.

### Irontongue Hall

**Military Unit / Rank 3**  
When [General Dunstan Irontongue](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#solvihill) fell alongside Empress Britta, his personal unit of Suaq scouts declared themselves [Frayed](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_religious_beliefs.md#the-frayed). They fought valiantly alongside the Imperial armies dedicated to recovering Skarsind, but only when the Thule were driven from the territory did they consider their shame lifted. They are now seeking a courageous Winterfolk Captain to lead them in future campaigns against the enemies of the Wintermark people.

## Previous Spoils of War

### The Hall of White Ravens

- Previously **Congregation / Rank 5**

Gildenheim - once second largest of the Winterfolk settlements - was ruined by the Thule. The Hall of White Ravens was badly damaged, but unlike many structures in Gildenheim it still had all four walls and a roof, and some of the beautifully carved white granite raven façade still remained. Survivors of the Thule oppression visited the Hall to seek spiritual guidance and to remember the occupation. During the Spring Equinox 381YE, the [Senate](../senate/Senate.md) gave orders for the Hall to be used as the basis for a [new structure](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_The_Legion-s_Rookery.md) - the [Legion's Rookery](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#legions-rookery).

### The Clattering Gulley

**Mana site / Rank 3**  
Located several miles east of [Pakaanan's Pass](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#pakaanan27s-pass), the Clattering Gulley is a winding rock crevasse used for generations by [Steinr](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Steinr.md) [Runesmiths](../../magic/lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths). It takes its name from hundreds of [mammoth](../../../in-character/world/Mundane_beasts.md#mammoths) bones hung along the walls and inscribed with mithril-inlaid runes that twist and move in the occasional winds that roar down the gulley, seemingly from nowhere.

Following the Winter Solstice 383YE, the Clattering Gulley was destroyed when the Jotun [collapsed the western end of Pakaanan's Pass](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/384YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#in-the-west).

### Four-Seasons Market

**Business / Rank 3**  
The market of [Krysse](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#estermark) played host to merchants from across Skarsind and nearby Varushka, as well as traders from as far afield as the Brass Coast. When Skarsind was returned to Imperial hands, the market looked set to become a regular fixture again - and the overseer of the market stood to make a pretty profit. Unfortunately, shortly after the Winter Solstice 380YE, the Four-Seasons Market was destroyed in a catastrophic fire.

### The Wells of Janon

**Green Iron Mine / Rank 4**  
A series of wide shafts punch down to remarkable depths in the northwest of [Gulhule](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#estermark), exploiting a seemingly inexhaustible vein of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) deep beneath the mountains. During the occupation by the Thule, the miners took refuge in galleries and passages concealed with the aid of the Night Eternal Janon who had long served as a patron of the hard-bitten miners.

Contact with the Wells of Janon was eventually [lost](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Long_shadows.md#wells-of-janon). During the assessment of Skarsind by the Golden Pyramid, it was discovered to have been hidden in some way and impossible to find. The miners who were working it apparently still trade occasionally with nearby orc settlements butwhat exactly has happened to it is unknown. (**OOC Note:** This personal resource is no longer available.)

### Crimson Peak Mining

**Orichalcum Mine / Rank 3**  
Crimson Peak takes its name from the extensive [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) mining operation rather than from the colour of the mountain itself (which appears quite normal to outsiders). The owners made a tidy profit selling to the runesmiths of nearby [Gulhule](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#estermark) before they perished valiantly during the Thule attack - they intentional collapsed a number of key passages on themselves, denying the barbarians access to the richest seams.

Crimson Peak Mining, was eventually [overrun by trogoni](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Long_shadows.md#drimson-peak-mining). Fortunately there was a significant bounty of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) available to pay for [reavers](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_economic_interests.md#reavers) and foreign mercenaries to clear the mine. With the trogoni cleared, the [Steward of the Mines of Gulhule](../../titles/Steward_of_the_Mines_of_Gulhule.md) beacme able to buy orichalcum directly from the newly operational mines, and the grateful miners arranged for a bequest to be sent to the [Golden Pyramid](../conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md) Conclave vault each season. (**OOC Note:** This personal resource is no longer available)
