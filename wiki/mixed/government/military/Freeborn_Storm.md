# Freeborn Storm

*[Image: FreebornStorm Colour.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:FreebornStorm_Colour.png)*

## Overview

The Freeborn Storm was the first [navy](../../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) constructed by the Empire since the disaster that ended the reign of [Emperor Barabbas](../../../in-character/history/Emperor_Barabbas.md). Launched triumphantly in the months following the liberation of [Segura](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md), the navy was unfortunately outmatched by the [Grendel](../../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) armada and after only two years of active service was destroyed during the [conquest of Atalaya](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#to-fool-the-crowd) by the southern [orcs](../../world/Orc.md).

Despite the obstacles the navy faced in trying to protect the [Bay of Catazar](../../../in-character/world/Bay_of_Catazar.md) from the Grendel, it consistently performed beyond expectations thanks to the courage of independent [ship captains](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md).

Prior to its destruction, the admiral of the Freeborn Storm was appointed during the Winter Solstice.

## History

This Freeborn Storm was constructed at the [Atalaya Shipyards](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#the-atalaya-shipyards-28ruined29) in [Madruga](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) over the course of 379YE. Enthusiasm for constructing the navy peaked during the [celebrations](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_Glorious_Advance.md) surrounding the Imperial victory over the Lasambrian [orcs](../../world/Orc.md) in [Segura](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md), and the [Synod](../synod/Synod.md) capitalised on that enthusiasm to provide additional support to the project. The navy launched for the first time just before the Winter Solstice 379YE.

The first major engagement against the enemy came during the [Battle of Rebekah's Leap](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/380YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#audacity) in Autumn 380YE. Attempting to catch the Grendel fleets by surprise the Storm faced off against the vastly larger Grendel armada. Despite being forced to fall back to [Necropolis](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Necropolis.md), they nonetheless managed to inflict notable casualties on the orc war fleet.

A year later in Autumn 381YE, after helping to [clear the Grendel presence from southern Redoubt](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/381YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#unfinished-business-28redoubt29), the Freeborn Storm again [clashed with the Grendel armada](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/381YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#another-round-28bay-of-catazar29), this time in the waters south of [Spiral](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md). Despite huge support from Imperial captains, the Freeborn navy was again outmatched. This time it was driven back into Highborn waters after suffering crippling casualties from the orc flotilla.

Following the engagement in the east, the Freeborn Storm retreated to [Atalaya](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#atalaya) to regroup and resupply. Unfortunately, the Grendel ships followed them home. The Grendel [attacked the historic Freeborn settlement](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/381YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#to-fool-the-crowd) with a force five times the size of the remaining ships, and despite a valiant effort, the Storm was burnt to the waterline along with the shipyards where it had been created.

## Legacy

During its brief time in service, it was very much a point of pride for Freeborn [corsairs](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Corsair.md) to serve with and support the navy with ships and sailors, but the navy was too short-lived to lead to any specific traditions. Rather, its influence lies more in the legacy created by its brief service and tragic loss.

The destruction of the Freeborn Storm struck a stinging blow to the pride and ambition of the Brass Coast, which was in turn exacerbated by the ceding of Feroz. For some, the burning of the Atalaya shipyard has become symbolic of the losses the Freeborn have suffered in the war with the Grendel. As a consequence, relics of the burnt vessels destroyed in Atalaya are sought after by those who feel a particular connection to the navy. These mementos of the lost navy vary from a few splinters bound together and kept in a locket to partially burnt wooden badges, to actual rods or staffs made from wood reclaimed from the shores of the Madrugan island.

For the wider Empire, the lessons learned from the Freeborn Storm seem to have made the [Senate](../senate/Senate.md) and [Military Council](Military_Council.md) wary of building another [navy](../../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md), at least in the short term. Despite the widespread support among [independent ship captains](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md), a single navy was demonstrably no match for the Grendel armada, and evidence suggests that the orcs of Attar have expanded their naval forces even further since the destruction of the Freeborn Storm. The [Autumn 383YE treaty](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Ratify_treaty_with_the_Grendel_Autumn_383YE.md) with the southern barbarians, which specifically restricts the Empire's ability to build navies in the [Bay of Catazar](../../../in-character/world/Bay_of_Catazar.md), was arguably only acceptable given the reported opposition to further investment in marine forces. Even without the treaty, there is currently no [shipyard](../../commissions/Shipyard.md) capable of building a navy anywhere in the Empire.

Despite this reported opposition from the [senators](../../titles/Senator.md) and [generals](../../titles/General.md), on the Brass Coast itself there is still a strong voice supporting Freeborn ambitions to drive the Grendel from the Bay of Catazar. This movement has gained additional strength since the treaty ceded [Feroz](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) to the orcs. While few are pushing for another navy yet, it has become customary for those who dream reclaiming the waters of the Bay to wear the symbol of the Freeborn Storm as a badge or patch sewn into their clothing.

Most of these supporters broadly accept the idea that the Brass Coast alone cannot hope to build sufficient naval forces to challenge the Grendel. Even if they were to disband the [Red Wind Corsairs](Red_Wind_Corsairs.md) and [Fire of the South](Fire_of_the_South.md) - something which nobody thinks is a good idea - the Grendel would still outnumber the Imperial armada. Rather, the more realistic supporters of the Imperial naval legacy talk of forming a pan-Catazarri alliance with [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md), [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), and even [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md). If each of the four nations was to float their own navy, the dream of an Imperial armada to rival the strength of the Grendel flotilla could become a reality. These ambitious individuals argue that the Grendel will never truly be beaten until the Empire can defeat them at sea. The Grendel must surely know this they say; that is why they insisted on peace terms that would scupper any opposition to their naval supremacy.

## Composition

The core of the Freeborn Storm was made up of several large warships newly constructed at the shipyards of Atalaya. Most of the smaller fighting vessels and support ships were purchased from families across the Brass Coast who wanted to support the new navy.

As there had been no Imperial navy in almost two hundred years, one of the first challenges faced by the mariners of the Brass Coast was to address the logistical issues presented by bringing together such a force of ships and sailors, especially with regards to communication of strategy and coordinating once battle was joined. In many ways the independent nature of the Freeborn gave the Storm an advantage; once battle was joined the individual captains could be relied upon to engage the enemy in broad support of the declared strategy without needing to constantly update orders.

## Navy Quality : None

The Freeborn Storm did not possess a [unique quality](../../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md#special-quality) - beyond the fact that it was the only [navy](../../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) deployed during its period of service. The Empire lacked the expertise in constructing warships to be able to create navies with special qualities, and this technological shortcoming reduce their ability to face the Grendel navies on equal terms. The support of independent ships captains went some way towards ameliorating the martial disadvantage, but couldn't make up for the strategic advantages the orc armada enjoyed with their swift ships and cavernous holds.
