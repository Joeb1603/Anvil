### Overview

This adaptable army attracts many [beaters](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#beaters) who support a solid core of [Upwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md) soldiers and a small cadre of dedicated battlefield magicians. They are skilled skirmish fighters, able to quickly learn the lay of the land and [relentless in their pursuit](Army_qualities.md#relentless) of their enemies. Some of the best military archers in the Empire are part of the Bounders adept at bringing down their opponents with a combination of clever placement and withering storms of arrows. They are also adept scouts and trackers - unsurprising perhaps given the number of [beaters](#beaters) who serve or have served under their banner. Since the Spring of 383YE, they have focused their efforts on [guarding the borders](Army_qualities.md#watchful) of the Empire.

The third Marcher general leads the Bounders army, and is appointed (or re-appointed) at the Autumn Equinox each year.

## Army Quality : [Watchful](Army_qualities.md#watchful)

The Bounders were once known for their [relentless](Army_qualities.md#relentless) pursuit of their foes. In recent years, however, they have [embraced](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Bills_or_bows.md#bounders) a more [watchful](Army_qualities.md#watchful) approach to their campaigns.

## Oathsworn

- **The Bounders fight with an additional thousand strength when in Upwold**

As a result of a [mighty oath](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/I_already_know_you.md#reeds-drakes-tusks-bounders) sworn in 386YE by [general](../../titles/General.md) Birchsmith, when the Bounders fight in [Upwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md) they operate as if they had an additional thousand strength. However, should there ever be an enemy force in Upwold, and the army *not* be in place defending the territory by the start of the following downtime, the oath will be broken with major consequences for the army.
