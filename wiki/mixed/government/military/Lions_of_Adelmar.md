### Overview

The Lions of Adelmar were raised by [Senate motion](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Raise_Dawnish_army_Summer_385YE.md) proposed by **Bohemond de Rondell**, then Senator for Astolat, during the Summer Solstice 385YE. [Mustering](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Love_and_War.md) completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 387YE, having been funded in large part by Emperor **Vesna Borkovna Prochnost** using [the Hand of the Chancellor](../../titles/The_Throne.md#the-hand-of-the-chancellor). The incumbent senator for Astolat, **Éamon Ashenmourn de Cordraco**, named the army after the exemplar [Adelmar the Lion](../../../in-character/religion/Adelmar_the_Lion.md) and endorsed the army's focus on [love](../../magic/lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#love-and-flowers).

The fourth Dawnish general leads the Lions of Adelmar army, and is appointed (or re-appointed) at the Winter Solstice each year.

## Army Quality : [Guardian](Army_qualities.md#guardian)

The Lions of Adelmar put great stock in protecting and supporting other armies, eschewing risk and reckless behaviour, and look to preserve the lives of companions and fellow soldiers.
