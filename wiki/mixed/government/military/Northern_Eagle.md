### Overview

The soldiers of this army have long experience of fighting along the northern mountains that border the [Thule](../../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md) lands. They contain a number of expert mountaineers, ex-prospectors and other specialists who serve as scouts, and have a significant advantage over less-experienced armies when fighting in [mountains, forests and marshes](Army_qualities.md#elusive). Once the Northern Eagle establishes a presence in a territory, it can prove extremely hard to dislodge. [Orders](../../../in-character/military-council-orders/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Military_Council_orders.md#northern-eagle) given by **Aksel Reznov Razoradze** at the Autumn Equinox 387YE, encouraged the army to take up the [suggestion](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Is_there_anybody_out_there.md#home-to-roost-sorin) made by a herald of [Sorin](../../../in-character/eternals/Sorin.md) to hone the army into an elusive force focused on striking at the heart of their enemies.

The first Varushkan general leads the army of the Northern Eagle, and is appointed (or re-appointed) at the Summer Solstice each year.
