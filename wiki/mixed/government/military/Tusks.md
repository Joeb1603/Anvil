### Overview

The fourth Marcher Army was raised by [Senate motion](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Raise_Marcher_Army.md) proposed by Henry Ward, then Senator for Upwold, during the Winter Solstice of 377YE. Mustering completed shortly before the Summer Solstice 378YE. The army of The Tusks was briefly named after [High Courage](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#green-march), an ancient monument in Mournwold. This highly [disciplined](Army_qualities.md#disciplined) force saw its first major engagement when the Empire [launched its liberation of the Mourn](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/379YE_Winter_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#the-mourning-wind) in late Autumn 379YE.

The fourth Marcher general leads the army of The Tusks, and was initially appointed at the Summer Solstice each year. Starting in 384YE, however, the election was shifted to the Spring Equinox following [the consideration of the Civil Service](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Bills_or_bows.md#resolution).

## Oathsworn

- **The Tusks fight with an additional thousand strength when in Mournwold**

As a result of a [mighty oath](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/I_already_know_you.md#reeds-drakes-tusks-bounders) sworn in 386YE by [General](../../titles/General.md) Stanley, when the Tusks fight in [Mournwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) they operate as if they had an additional thousand strength. However, should there ever be an enemy force in Mournwold, and the army *not* be in place defending the territory by the start of the following downtime, the oath will be broken with major consequences for the army and general.
