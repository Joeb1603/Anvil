### Overview

The army of Isaella's Dance was founded around a solid core of thorns with a reliance on vates adept at manipulating the power of the [Spring realm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md), and supported by some of the finest masters of [herb lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary) in the Empire.

The Isaella's Dance were lost following the Autumn Equinox 386YE during the [campaign](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/Under_eastern_skies.md) in [Therunin](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md) against the [Druj](../../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md).

The third Navarr general led the Isaella's Dance, and was appointed (or re-appointed) at the Spring Equinox each year.

#### Recent History

The army of Isaella's Dance finished mustering just before the Summer Solstice 384YE. The army was able to use their mastery of Spring magic and their expertise with herbs to create powerful blade venoms to apply to their weapons. These poisons were designed to kill quickly and without suffering, ensuring that the enemy did not linger in pain. The [thornbound](Army_qualities.md#thornbound) army was founded following an [opportunity](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Comes_a_time.md#without-fear-or-favour) presented by critics of the Spider King.

Isaella's Dance fought in campaigns across the Empire, especially against the [Jotun](../../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md) in the west. Following the Autumn Equinox 386YE, however, the army was involved in vicious fighting in [Therunin](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md), where the Druj had laid a [carefully crafted trap](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/Under_eastern_skies.md). They were able to lead the other armies fighting beside them out of the magical snare the orcs of the Mallum had created, but in the process they were [cut to pieces](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/Under_eastern_skies.md#now-we-are-ready) and ultimately disbanded.
