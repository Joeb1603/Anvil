# Golden Pyramid

## Motto

*Power begets wealth; wealth begets Power*

## The Manifesto

The Order of the Golden Pyramid holds the following to be true:

- Magic is a commodity like any other, and its practitioners should not be subjected to any undue expectation or requirement
- The talents of the Empire's artisans are no less a valuable magical resource than spellcasting and rituals
- Magic has the power to strengthen by enriching and prospering

The Order of the Golden Pyramid therefore exists to:

- Stand up for those magicians who are pressured to use their skills for free, like a servant or slave
- Support the application of magical resources in matters of economic gain that inevitably bring prosperity to the Empire as a whole
- Stand up for the artisans and promote their interests across the Empire

## Symbol

The most popular symbol of the order is a step pyramid of three levels with bronze at the bottom, silver in the middle, and gold at the top.

## Membership of the Order

This Golden Pyramid appeals to artisan-magicians and magicians versed in economic magic (from [Rampant Growth](../../magic/rituals/Rampant_Growth.md) and [Blood and Salt](../../magic/rituals/Blood_and_Salt.md) through [Rivers of Gold](../../magic/rituals/Rivers_of_Gold.md) all the way to [Verdant Bounty of the Twilight Bayou](../../magic/rituals/Verdant_Bounty_of_the_Twilight_Bayou.md) and [Hallow of the Green World](../../magic/rituals/Hallow_of_the_Green_World.md)). After the [Sevenfold Path](Sevenfold_Path.md) this is the order that has the largest number of magician-priests, albeit usually priests dedicated to [Prosperity](../../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md). Its members work to enrich themselves, but they also safeguard the rights of magicians to expect fair recompense for their magic. They are often at odds with the [Rod and Shield](Rod_and_Shield.md).

The Order of the Golden Pyramid began as a reaction against a perceived culture of entitlement; as the nations increasingly assumed that magicians should donate their skills to the Empire for free. Since their creation many members have supported the right of artisans, as well as magicians, to have a voice in the Conclave; but this attitude is far from universal within the Order. At its most altruistic, the Order tries to represent the interests of artisans as fellow practitioners of esoteric lore, and often approaches them for support and funding.

As befits an order fascinated by wealth, members of the Order often strive to cultivate good relations with the [Bourse](../../economy/Bourse.md) and magicians who also occupy Bourse seats are often actively wooed by the Golden Pyramid. When it works well, this relationship gives the Golden Pyramid access to the wealth of the Bourse on behalf of other members of the Conclave, and the Pyramid has been adroit at using this access for political capital. Even when their influence in the Bourse is limited, the Golden Pyramid has a tradition of encouraging the performance of economic rituals to garner support or influence with Imperial citizens who own [businesses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md), [mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md), [forests](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md), [fleets](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md), and mercenary [warbands](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md).

Members of the Golden Pyramid traditionally consider it completely acceptable, even expected, for their [Grandmaster](../../titles/Grandmaster.md) to gain their position by open bribery. It is also common for the grandmaster to take advantage of their access to wealthy patrons to help [fund the order](../../titles/Grandmaster.md#funding-the-order).

## Conclave vault

Each summit, the [Conclave vault](Conclave_vault.md) of the Golden Pyramid garners a pro rata share of the [Font](Conclave_vault.md#crystal-mana) based on the [precedence](Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence) of the orders at the previous summit; a portion of [ilium](../../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) based on their [position](Conclave_vault.md#ilium) among the orders as determined by precedence; and additional materials due to outstanding bequests.

These [bequests](Conclave_vault.md#bequests) are collected by the Civil Service and provided to the Grandmaster at the start of each summit.

- Three measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) have come from the Carmine Band of [Regario](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md#tassato-regario) since 206YE and will continue for as long as they promote the interests of [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) across the Empire.
- Three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) come from the Yesun family of [Faraden](../../../in-character/foreign-nations/Faraden.md), as part of a deal made in 305YE involving a ritual text of [Revelation of the Jewel's Sparkling Heart](../../magic/rituals/Revelation_of_the_Jewel-s_Sparkling_Heart.md).
- Two ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) have come from the town of [Kosti](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karsk.md#kopevnost) in return for support provided by the Order during the [Thule](../../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md) invasion of [Karsk](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karsk.md) in 368YE.
- Three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggar27s-lye) have come from Little Ribston, a quiet settlement in [Birchland](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md#birchland). The bequest began in 368YE and involves recognition of support offered to [Emperor Walter](../../../in-character/history/Emperor_Walter.md) during his ascent to the Throne.
- Three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) come from the miners at [Crimson Peak Mining](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Long_shadows.md#crimson-peak-mining) since Summer 386YE for as long as mine remains operational.
- Two wains of [mithril](../../economy/Mithril.md) from [Fundindelve](../../titles/Fundindelve.md) with the completion of the [Grand Lodge](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Grand_Lodge_of_the_Golden_Pyramid.md) in [Clisearn](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#clisearn).

## Grand Lodge

In Winter 386YE a [proposal was raised](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Lit_up.md#golden-opportunity) for a [grand lodge](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Lit_up.md#grand-lodges) in [Mareave](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md), where the order had been enthusiastically pursuing [opportunities](../../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to improve the prosperity of both themselves and the [Imperial Orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). [Construction](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Grand_Lodge_of_the_Golden_Pyramid.md) was completed shortly before the Summer Solstice 387YE - just in time for the return of the [Grendel](../../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md). Threats were made, but ultimately the lodge was left [unmolested](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/Scarlet_sands.md#gold-in-the-dust).

The plan for the lodge came about when one of their members went rooting through the Green Keep, a fortified compound whose walls are wreathed in thick green vines in [Clisearn](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#clisearn). This decaying building was once home to a garrison of [Stone Born](../../../in-character/barbarians/Elite_Grendel_troops.md#the-stone-born) who guarded both the mine and the villas of Ehsan and his supporters. The Golden Pyramid lobbied for control of the old garrison compound, and rebuilt it into something big enough to house almost any number of the order's wizards. It was expensive to build and maintain - with an upkeep to the Senate - but also provides a boost to taxation in the territory. More crucially it allows the order to extend economic cooperation right across the territory. The order now provides magicians to support mines, mana sites, and businesses throughout Mareave. Crucially, the order is on hand to provide vital support for the workers in the [Fundindelve](../../titles/Fundindelve.md), boosting production in the mines *and* providing the order with a small income of incredibly valuable [mithril](../../economy/Mithril.md) each season.

## Grandmaster

Each [Conclave order](Conclave_order.md) is led by a [grandmaster](../../titles/Grandmaster.md) who is elected by their members. This title is currently held by Terentia of Sarangrave Reforged; it will be reelected at Spring Equinox 389YE.
