# Region qualities

## Overview

Each region in a [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) may have one or more qualities that describe key aspects or features, which may have important economic, strategic or magical considerations.

Some negative qualities have specific methods that the Empire can use to remove them. Without that, it is not possible to create, remove or change the quality of a region without an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or an [appraisal](../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md) - they reflect the essential nature of the land.

## Geographic Qualities

These qualities occur frequently and represent simple aspects of physical geography.

### Arid

This region has little in the way of fresh water, with wells being valuable commodities. They are often also sparsely settled. Building permanent settlements in these regions is a challenge, as is establishing fortifications. All farms in a territory with at least one region that has the arid quality suffer a 1 rank penalty. The curses [Rivers Run Red](../magic/rituals/Rivers_Run_Red.md) and [Rivers of Life](../magic/rituals/Rivers_of_Life.md) are ineffective in a territory whilst any region is arid. At present, no Imperial regions have this quality

### Coastal

A *coastal* region does not simply border the sea or other large body of water, but has a sufficiently accessible shoreline to allow the construction of a [shipyard](../commissions/Shipyard.md). Such regions are also, by consequence, vulnerable to attacks by [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md). Coastal regions can be found in places such as the sprawling islands of [Free Landing](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#free-landing), the smooth and fertile foothills of [Naris](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#naris), and the storm-riddled clay slurries of [North Fens](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#north-fens).

### Forest

A *forested* region features large amounts of distinctive, often dense woodland. Whether the great stands of ancient oaks in [Goldglades](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Miaren.md#goldglades), the dense pine forests of [Solvihill](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#solvihill) or the once-tainted vegetation of [Westwood](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Liathaven.md#westwood), the presence of large forests allows for casting of rituals such as [Forge the Wooden Fastness](../magic/rituals/Forge_the_Wooden_Fastness.md) and enables an Imperial [guerrilla](../government/military/Army_qualities.md#guerrilla) army to execute a *hit and run* order if the Empire controls the region.

### Hills

A *hills* region is mountainous, home to sharp inclines, rough escarpments or similar forms of difficult terrain. Although this does not limit the ability of armies to traverse the territory, it has important implications for strategic magic such as [Frozen Citadel of Cathan Canae](../magic/rituals/Frozen_Citadel_of_Cathan_Canae.md), and enables an Imperial [guerrilla](../government/military/Army_qualities.md#guerrilla) army to execute a *hit and run* order if the Empire controls the region. Examples include the cave-filled slopes of [Altis](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md#altis), the magnificent peaks of [Kalpamark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#kalpamark), and the rugged valleys of [Braydon's Jasse](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#braydon27s-jasse).

### Islands

An *islands* region is made up of large amounts of water and a number of discrete landmasses. It is commonly also coastal, for obvious reasons. A region of islands cannot be entered, left, moved through, or attacked by an [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md), unless it is being transported by a [navy](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md). The most prominent examples are the coastal islands of [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) - the [Cazar Straits](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#cazar-straits) and [Free Landing](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#free-landing).

### Marsh

A *marshy* region might contain swamps, bogs, fens, or similar wetland, sufficient for the casting of rituals such as [Dripping Echoes of the Fen](../magic/rituals/Dripping_Echoes_of_the_Fen.md), or for an Imperial [guerrilla](../government/military/Army_qualities.md#guerrilla) army to execute a *hit and run* order if the Empire controls it. These marshes can have tremendous diversity - compare the murky mangrove swamps of [East Ashes](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md#east-ashes) to the misty bogs of [Rundhal Marsh](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md#rundhal-marsh) or the eerie grey meres of [Grey Fens](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#grey-fens).

Following the [supernatural storms](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Light_upon_the_water.md) of 384YE which turned [large sections of the Empire's marshes into boggy swamps](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Light_upon_the_water.md#price-of-the-marshes), the labour costs of any commission built in a region with the *marsh* quality increase by one fifth.

## Historical Qualities

These qualities reflect the history of a region; they are the by-product of human, orc or other creatures enacting their will upon the area.

### Abandoned

- **See the *[Send for me](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Send_for_me.md#abandoned)* wind of fortune**

The region has been abandoned by those who once lived there. As a result of the lack of people and the stubborn spirit amongst those who do still reside here, no mandate enacted by the Imperial Synod will affect a territory with an abandoned region. There are still people living in the region, but there are far fewer inhabitants remaining than once called this bustling territory their home. The one positive is that there are countless abandoned farms, mines, businesses and likewise scattered across the territory. As a result, any character of the correct nation who chooses to move their personal resource to a territory with a region that is abandoned does not have to pay the standard fee of two crowns. At present, Afarjasse, Cazar Straits, Morajasse, and Oranseri of [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) have this quality.

### Burnt

Through a boon provided by the eternal [Surut](../../in-character/eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#surut), the forests of [West Ranging](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Liathaven.md#west-ranging) in [Liathaven](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Liathaven.md) were consumed by an irresistible fire that left naught but grey ash in its wake.

### City

The city quality denotes a large, established settlement, but it is more than just having a built-up population area or a large town. [Coursmouth](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#coursmouth) has the city quality due to the dead city of [the Necropolis](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#the-necropolis).

### Foreigners

Some Imperial regions have large populations of foreigners living there.

### Haunted

The sites of massacres and the sacred resting places of myriad heroes can develop a *haunted* atmosphere, where unquiet spirits restlessly stalk the land. These ghosts are not necessarily malign, but they often require specially trained individuals - such as the [stewards of the dead](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_religious_beliefs.md#stewards-of-the-dead) in Highguard - to safely keep them restful.

### Ruined

The cruel reality of war can leave a once-prosperous region with little but *ruins*. Such regions often bear a melancholy air, with the echoes of past glories fading with the further passage of time. Despite this devastation, such regions can be crucial for the casting of rituals such as [The Basalt Citadel](../magic/rituals/The_Basalt_Citadel.md).

## Political Qualities

These qualities reflect current political events in a region; the result of social and economic events in the area over time. They are less durable than other qualities, more likely to come and go as a result of the most recent actions in the area.

### Impoverished

- **See the *[Send for me](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Send_for_me.md#impoverished)* wind of fortune**

An impoverished region has seen much of its wealth and assets stripped and sold; the plundered wealth gone. People are desperate for paid work, anything that might earn them enough to feed themselves and their families. As such any [civilian commission](../commissions/Commission.md#civilian-commissions) that is built solely in a region or regions that have the impoverished quality cost only one crown per wain, rather than the usual three.

The impoverished state is however a major hindrance to the restoration of a territory. Very few people could ever be persuaded to move to a region while hunger and poverty are endemic here. This means that the Empire cannot remove the abandoned quality of any region that remains impoverished. At present, Cazar Straits and Oranseri of [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) have this quality.

### Lawless

- **See the *[Send for me](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Send_for_me.md#lawless)* wind of fortune**

Bandits are a ubiquitous problem across the Empire, but the magistrates deal with them where they can. As a result, they rarely grow to the point where they threaten an entire region. A lawless region is infested with large bandit groups. Lawlessness is a problem for everyone - the threat of banditry is a terrible danger for any law-abiding citizen in the territory who cannot defend themselves from the bandits' depredations. It also impacts the [Imperial treasury](Imperial_treasury.md) - the taxation provided by a territory does not increase naturally while any region remains lawless. That reflects the impact thieves, bandits, and pirates have on local businesses and resources, as well as the tendency of more established criminal groups to attack tax collectors directly. At present, only Morajasse of [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) has this quality.

### Outlaw Druj

- **See the [Other monstrous geographies](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Other_monstrous_geographies.md#game-information-dominion) wind of war**

When the garrison of the [Tower of the Skink](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md#tower-of-the-skink) broke after the Winter Solstice 387YE, they were not destroyed. Instead, they fled across the territory and have gone to ground in the regions, launching attacks at burgeoning settlements. Any commission built in Sarangrave has its labour costs increased by a fifth for each region under Imperial control with the quality. At present, Turan Flats, Kroll, and the Whisperwood of [Sarangrave](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md) have this quality.

### Uncooperative

An *uncooperative* region has some reason to make life difficult for Imperial armies stationed in the territory. Any Imperial army stationed in a territory has its upkeep increased by a tenth for every region that has the quality, and as long as any region has that quality the territory cannot support an Imperial army. At present Fleisardh of [Mareave](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md) have this quality.

### Under-developed

*Under-developed* regions lack critical infrastructure. There are few roads or bridges over important rivers, and those that do exist are in poor condition or completely ruined. While the area may have natural resources, little has been done to develop them and there are few prosperous farms or businesses in the region. The presence of each under-developed region adds one-fifth to the labour costs of any commission in the territory. At present, no Imperial region has this quality.

### Under Threat

Regions that are *under threat* are threatened by insurgent forces that are hostile to the controlling power. These partisans engage in acts of sabotage or carry out attacks designed to undermine whoever rules the area. If the region is attacked by a force aligned with the partisans, then the attackers are considered to have a beachhead - that is they do not suffer the usual penalties for [supply lines](../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#supply-lines).

### Underpopulated

There are relatively few inhabitants in an *underpopulated* region. While hostile geography may limit the number of people who dwell in a mountainous or barren region, this quality reflects a history of depopulation in an area that means it sustains far fewer people than it otherwise might. Any region that is under-populated contributes **nothing** to support the armies of whichever nation controls the territory. At present, Afarjasse, the Cazar Straits, and Morajasse of [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) have this quality

## Magic Qualities

These qualities are the result of unique magical effects afflicting a region. They may be the result of some ongoing curse or enchantment, or else the lingering effects that remain after powerful has run its course somewhere.

### Accursèd

The presence of [Black Plateau](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md#the-black-plateau) in [Screed](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md#screed), [Spiral](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md) has left the region *accursèd*. The Black Plateau appears to feed on negative emotions and amplify them, burgeoning the mind with hallucinations and oppressive, malign thoughts causing people to dwell on past failures, insults, and suspicions. Since the Black Plateau was [fully awakened](../../in-character/winds-of-war/382YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#leviathan-wakes-28spiral29) shortly before the Spring Equinox 382YE, this quality has the following effects on Spiral:

- Every army fighting in the territory takes 25% additional casualties
- It is impossible to receive natural resupply in the territory
- Any army that spends the season in the territory will lose 5% of its maximum fighting strength over the course of that season even if it is not engaged
- Fleets, military units, businesses, and farms have their production reduced by a quarter
- The upkeep of Bourse resources is doubled

### Grey Walkers

*[Image: Some regions of Bregasland are spookier than others.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Haunted_Fen.png)*

The ghostly creatures of [Grey Fen](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#grey-fen) seek to drive all trespassers out of the region and seem to count *everyone* as an intruder. Further details are laid out in the [Pride in the Marches](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Pride_in_the_Marches.md#bregasland) wind of fortune from Summer 388YE. The quality reflects the continuing efforts of these ghostly figures to push the living out of the Grey Fens.

There is an effective force of 1,000 strength in the region that tries to take control of the region each season. *Any* army that issues an appropriate [defending](../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#defending) order in the territory can prevent the ghosts gaining any headway, and an attacking order will drive the ghosts back and reassert control over the region (as long as it achieves sufficient victory points). As long as the *haunted* quality remains in Grey Fens howeer, the Grey Walkers will simply keep coming back. They don't take casualties (although they do inflict them), and anyone actively trying to stop them claiming Grey Fens will not be able to resupply that season.

Notably, the presence of these Grey Walkers also disrupts the function of certain magic. Any magical ward created in Grey Fens - [Dripping Echoes of the Fen](../magic/rituals/Dripping_Echoes_of_the_Fen.md) for example - will not impede the ghosts at all. They will continue to slowly but inexorably try to usurp control of the region, even as the enchantment has its normal effects for everyone else.

### Infested

The [vallorn](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) exists in several states, ranging from full domination to pending threat. The *infested* quality means there is a significant presence of vallornspawn and [vallorn miasma](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md#vallorn-miasma), but that the loathsome entity has not "dug in" the way it has in a region with the vallorn quality. At the moment the only Imperial nation with this quality is [East Ashes](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md#east-ashes) in [Therunin](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md). Miasma hangs over its western and northern reaches, and there are numbers of vallornspawn in the region that will need to be dealt with. Unlike a region fully claimed by the vallorn it is not invulnerable to attack. Any [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) or armies trying to conquer the region will need to deal with the vallornspawn there; these function as a battalion with a strength of 4,000 that both defend the region and inflict casualties, similar to the effect of [Forge the Wooden Fastness](../magic/rituals/Forge_the_Wooden_Fastness.md). Claiming the region will disperse this force and remove the infested quality.

### Nightwoven

The Druj have tainted the marshes of [Proceris](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md#proceris) in [Zenith](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md) with Night magic. As a result, any casting of [Dripping Echoes of the Fen](../magic/rituals/Dripping_Echoes_of_the_Fen.md) will increase the victory points necessary to claim the region by fifteen, up from ten.

### Poisoned

A region might become *poisoned* through the working of foul magics, tainting the land with venomous creatures, treacherous foliage and the like. Each Imperial campaign army that moves through, or fights in, such a region suffers 250 casualties during that season of campaigning. The ritual [Why Sulemaine Walked Away From The Baker](../magic/rituals/Why_Sulemaine_Walked_Away_From_The_Baker.md) protects an army from this effect, but orders have no effect to reduce these casualties.

Historically, the Druj have inflicted this quality on regions of [Holberg](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Holberg.md) and [attempted](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Carnival_of_rust.md#ghostlight-28battle-opportunity29) to do the same to [Proceris](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md#proceris) in [Zenith](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md). The Druj managed to use the [Sephals Cauldron](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Ashes.md#poison-and-ashes-battle-opportunity) in [Therunin](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md) and as of the start of the [Autumn Equinox 386YE](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Blood_and_mire.md#game-information-therunin) Lower Tarn Valley is poisoned. Druj armies are not entirely untouched by this quality, but suffer half the expected casualties.

### Rimebound

Rimebound regions are touched by the influence of the *Queen of Ice and Darkness*, [Cathan Canae](../../in-character/eternals/Cathan_Canae.md). The weather in a rimebound region is always *intense* - when it's hot elsewhere then it's baking hot here; when it rains the downpour is torrential; winds blow more strongly than anywhere else and gales are common. In winter the weather is even worse. Snowfall is heavy and lingers on the ground for weeks; winter blizzards are not unknown; and both lakes and rivers freeze over with ice thick enough to pull a caravan over it.

Any [Summer ritual](../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md) that makes the land harder to take would likely be more effective when cast on a rimebound region; rituals of other realms are unlikely to be affected. A [Frozen Citadel of Cathan Canae](../magic/rituals/Frozen_Citadel_of_Cathan_Canae.md) will have 3000 strength rather than 2000, while [Golden Ramparts](../magic/rituals/Golden_Ramparts.md) would increase the strength of a regular garrison in the region by 2250 rather than 1500. A fortification in the region that was targeted with [Stalwart Stand on Solid Ground](../magic/rituals/Stalwart_Stand_on_Solid_Ground.md) will not collapse until the ritual ends, no matter how much damage it takes.

### Vallorn

The Spring-fuelled *[vallorn](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md)*, spawned from the disaster that ended the empire of Terunael, can infest regions with its rampant fecundity. Any such region falls out of Imperial control - it is not possible for civilisation as the Empire understands it to coexist with the overgrowth, miasma and strange monsters that the vallorn exudes.

### Watchful

The [Heart of Peytaht](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#heart-of-peytaht) in [The Barrens](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) has the *watchful* quality. Anyone who presses into the dense woodland feels uneasy, and reports the sense of being unwelcome and watched by an unseen force. Rumours abound that the forest is possessed by some sort of dark spirit, perhaps akin to the sovereigns of Varushka.

Attempts to explore the inside of the Heart of Peytaht have been met with failure. Any army moving through the region can expect to take significant casualties in doing so - it has been some time since a great force of Druj marched through it in 376YE, but corpses from it are still found hung by their entrails or huddled by the boles of massive trees, having died in great agony and terror.

### Woodward

The Woodward quality empowers any casting of [Forge the Wooden Fastness](../magic/rituals/Forge_the_Wooden_Fastness.md) or similar defensive ritual that draws on the power of [Irra Harah](../../in-character/eternals/Irra_Harah.md) and targets that region. The enchanted wards will call up heralds and mortal friends of the eternal to support it, meaning it would provide 6,000 rather than 4,000 strength to protect the region.

Furthermore, while the quality exists, heralds of Irra Harah will find it easier to enter the mortal world there and support the people in that territory who have reason to fear oppression, tyranny, or violence. The exact effect will vary depending on the situation, but it will always mean that those trying to flee an area of violence, or hide from those who wish them harm, will be helped to do so. [Enmity](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity), or similar restrictions, will curtail this power, but even then it will remain possible for some powerful heralds of Irra Harah to enter and support a region with the Woodward quality, even if they cannot easily move beyond its borders.
