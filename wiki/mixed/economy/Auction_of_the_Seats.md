# Auction of the Seats

> *In-character text:*
>
> > *In-character text:*
> >
> > The Senate will be paupers only so long as honest merchants can grow rich.
> >
> > Emperor Giovanni

## Overview

At each summit, the Bourse selects citizens to fill all Imperial positions that are vacant through an open auction called the Auction of the Seats. The name derives from the tradition of referring to major Bourse titles as "Bourse seats". The Auction applies to the Bourse titles that are Imperial positions as well as specific League national titles; all other seats that are vacant are elected by [Tally of the Votes](Tally_of_the_Votes.md).

Participation in the Auction of the Seats is open to all Imperial citizens. Successful bidders claim their chosen title, while all funds raised go to the [Imperial treasury](Imperial_treasury.md)

## Schedule

Under normal circumstances, the Auction will take place on Saturday afternoon, at 2pm, in the Senate. If circumstances force a change in timing or location, then the civil service will update the [event schedule](../../out-of-character/events/388YE_Autumn_Equinox_event_schedule.md) published before the summit begins.

The major Bourse seats operate according to a schedule dictated by the materials produced. For example, all titles associated with the production of white granite are elected at the same summit each year

- [Weirwood](Weirwood.md) seats are re-elected at the Winter Solstice.
- [Ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) seats and the [Master of the Imperial Mint](../titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md) are re-elected at the Spring Equinox.
- [White granite](White_granite.md) seats are re-elected at the Summer Solstice.
- [Mithril](Mithril.md) seats are re-elected at the Autumn Equinox.

In addition, the two [Bearers of an Imperial Wayleave](Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), [Citizens' Voice](../titles/Citizens-_Voice.md), and the [Arbiter of the Rogue Declaration](../titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) are all auctioned at every summit.

At the end of the Auction of the Seats, a single dose of [true liao](../religion/Liao.md#true-liao) is auctioned, with the winning bid contributed to the [Virtue Fund](../government/synod/Rewarding.md).

A list of seats to be appointed at the upcoming event can be found on the [recent history](../../in-character/history/Recent_history.md) page and on notices around Anvil. Once appointed, the new seat holders are announced via the Bourse noticeboard in the Hub.

## Selection

Any Imperial citizen may hold an Imperial position, provided it is the only Imperial title they control.

All Imperial titles that are auctioned through the bourse are re-elected annually. If the title is elected at the normal summit each year, then the chosen citizen serves for one year until the start of the equivalent summit one year later.

If an Imperial Bourse seat becomes vacant outside the normal election cycle (due to death, resignation, or excommunication of the title holder) then the seat is auctioned at the following summit. In this case, the winning bidder will only serve for the remaining term. The title becomes vacant again at the normal point in the election cycle.

Bidding is in [thrones](../../in-character/world/Currency.md), using [an open ascending price auction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auction#Driven_by_bidders_only), and payment for each seat is required before bidding for the next begins to prevent interested citizens from bidding on seats they cannot afford.

The person bidding on the seat need not be the person who takes the Imperial title if successful, but they must be present during the auction to indicate that they accept the title. It is not possible for a proxy to accept a title on another citizen's behalf, nor to collect the production on a Bourse seat for them.

## Production

For the majority of Imperial Bourse seats that produce large amounts of mithril, white granite, weirwood or ilium, the most recent season's production can be immediately claimed from the Agent of the Bourse by the winning candidate, if they are present. Any production that is not claimed is added to their inventory. Subsequent production in later seasons is added to the title holder's inventory before each summit until the last one. The title holder does not receive any production on the run-up to the summit where the title is due to be re-elected - that is reserved for whoever wins the auction for the title at the summit.

For other titles that do not produce large amounts of mithril, white granite, weirwood or ilium, the winning candidate receives whatever production or access to a ministry the title provides in the following downtime, until the title is elected again.

## Removal

The Bourse is explicitly protected from interference from the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) in the [Imperial Constitution](../government/senate/Imperial_Constitution.md). Because of this, it is not possible to revoke a Bourse seat holder to remove them from their position. They serve until the title is reelected or until they die or step down.

If a Bourse seat holder dies, is excommunicated, or the seat is overrun by barbarians or abrogated by Senate, then the title holder receives back any money they paid for the seat - in proportion to the number of seasons of production remaining. If there are two seasons production left, they get half their money back. The seat is auctioned off again - and the refund is paid to the seat holder or their estate *after* the auction is concluded. Any shortfall is taken from the Imperial treasury, any excess is added to it. Bourse seat holders are well-advised to leave a clear will so that Agent of the Bourse can swiftly and correctly identify who these funds should be paid to.

There is no refund payable if a title is resigned.

## Term

All Imperial Bourse seats must be re-elected at the specified summit each year. It is unconstitutional to create an Imperial position with tenure that is auctioned through the Bourse (the same restriction does not apply to national Bourse positions).

## Additional Titles

There are four national League positions that are elected via Auction of the Seats rather than Tally of the Votes. These seats are auctioned just like any Imperial title, but only League citizens may bid on them. In theory it is possible for the Imperial Senate to create new national League titles elected via auction rather than by votes cast by business owners, but such titles are often subject to extended [scrutiny](../../in-character/senate-motions/Scrutiny.md).

In addition, two identical [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) titles, along with the [Arbiter of the Rogue Declaration](../titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md), the [Patron of the Beurteilung](../titles/Patron_of_the_Beurteilung.md), and the [Citizens' Voice](../titles/Citizens-_Voice.md) are also auctioned. These unusual titles are re-elected each season.

### Seasonal

#### Arbiter of the Rogue Declaration

The [Arbiter of the Rogue Declaration](../titles/Arbiter_of_the_Rogue_Declaration.md) is elected to serve for a single season each summit. The Arbiter is able to raise a single [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) to the Conclave during the Saturday session. The title is open to any Imperial citizen and is auctioned in only [mana crystals](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals).

#### Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

There are two identical titles, each [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) is elected to serve for a single season each summit. Each Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave is able to announce the construction of a single [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), or [great work](../commissions/Great_work.md) in the Senate. The titles are open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Citizens' Voice

The [Citizens' Voice](../titles/Citizens-_Voice.md) is elected to serve for a single season each summit. The Citizens' Voice is able to propose a single [motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) to the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) without the need for a second. The title is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Patron of the Beurteilung

The [Patron of the Beurteilung](../titles/Patron_of_the_Beurteilung.md) is elected to serve for a single season each summit. The Patron of the Beurteilung can instruct Professor Immanuel Gelberg and his colleagues to [appraise](../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md) a specific subject by announcement in the Senate. The title is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

### Winter Solstice

#### The Red-Eyed Reaper

The [Flaxen Pillars](../titles/Flaxen_Pillars.md) provides 22 wains of weirwood to the Red-Eyed Reaper each season. It is open to League citizens and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

### Spring Equinox

#### The Master of The Imperial Mint

The [Master of the Imperial Mint](../titles/Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md) is a Bourse Seat open to League citizens and elected by auction to the highest bidder. The Master does not receive an income of Bourse resources, but is privy to privileged information about the Imperial economy and has powers to speak and address the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md).

#### The Regario Dossier

The [Regario Dossier](../titles/Regario_Dossier.md) provides 4 rings of ilium each season, along with an annual report describing the activities of foreign powers. It is open to League citizens and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Seer of the Spiral Sword

The [Seer of the Spiral Sword](../titles/Seer_of_the_Spiral_Sword.md) oversees a [runeforge](../commissions/Runeforge.md) in [Redoubt](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Redoubt.md). It is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Delver of the Depths

The [Delver of the Depths](../titles/Delver_of_the_Depths.md) oversees the sinkhole dubbed *Lorenzo's Deep Pockets*. They receive an income of 50 ingots of [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), 20 ingots of [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), and 12 [crystal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) each season. It is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Tender of Kleist's Winter Gardens

The [Tender of Kleist's Winter Gardens](../titles/Tender_of_Kleist-s_Winter_Gardens.md) oversees the Winter Gardens in [Holberg](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Holberg.md). They receive an income of 45 drams of herbs, comprised of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md). and [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md). It is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Kruidenkenner Trademaster

The [Kruidenkenner Trademaster](../titles/Kruidenkenner_Trademaster.md) oversees the Kruidenkenner magazijn in [Necropolis](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Necropolis.md), and can purchase quantities of [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md) and random herbs at set prices. It is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Jewel of Anvil

The [Jewel of Anvil](../titles/Jewel_of_Anvil.md) oversees the Superlative Distillery in [Temeschwar](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md). They receive an income of 216 rings each season. It is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

### Autumn Equinox

#### The Legacy

The [Legacy](../titles/Legacy.md) provides 22 wains of mithril each season. It is open to League citizens and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Elosian Architect

The [Elosian Architect](../titles/Elosian_Architect.md) receives access to a ministry, allowing them to purchase [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), and [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) for [green iron](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) respectively. It is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.

#### Quaymaster of the Black Sails

The [Quaymaster of the Black Sails](../titles/Quaymaster_of_the_Black_Sails.md) receives access to a ministry, allowing them to both trade [liao](../religion/Liao.md) for goods from [Axos](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Axos.md) and sell liao to the merchants of that nation. It is open to any Imperial citizen and elected by auction to the highest bidder.
