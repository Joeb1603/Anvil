# Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave

## Overview

The Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave has the right to announce a single [commission](../commissions/Commission.md). There are currently two of these titles, all auctioned through the [Bourse](Bourse.md). Although the bearer is not a member of the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md), they may use a single [Senate announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) to instruct the Empire to approve a commission *provided that they do so at the summit that the title was appointed.*

The titles were [created at the Winter Solstice summit of 380YE.](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Imperial_Wayleaves.md) The titles were used to replace the existing [civilian commissioners](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) who were expected to approve and oversee the [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) of sinecures, ministries, and great works in their nation.

In Summer 382YE, the Imperial Senate voted to [reallocate](../../in-character/senate-motions/Reallocate_an_Imperial_Wayleave_to_the_Imperial_Orcs.md) one of the Imperial Wayleaves as an [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) national position creating the [Imperial Orc Wayleave](../titles/Legion_Engineer.md). This reduced the number of Imperial Wayleaves from three to two. They further [amended the powers of the Imperial Orc Wayleave](../../in-character/senate-sessions/383YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-imperial-orc-wayleave) during the Winter Solstice 383YE, leading to the Constitutional Court to request that the name of the title be changed to reflect the fact it was no longer a wayleave - it is now called [Legion Engineer](../titles/Legion_Engineer.md).

The Senate further [amended the powers](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-titles-commissioning-sinecures) during Summer 387YE to include the ability to commission [wayhouses](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) and [edifices](../commissions/Edifice.md).

## Responsibilities

The Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave has no responsibilities and may use the authority granted by the title to order any commission they choose. These titles replaced the previous system where each nation would appoint a [civilian commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md), and carry *some* of the restrictions and limitations imposed those titles.

## Powers

### Commission

Once during the summit where the title is appointed, each Bearer can [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) the construction of an [edifice](../commissions/Edifice.md), [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), [wayhouse](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) or [great work](../commissions/Great_work.md).

If the Bearer announces the commission of a sinecure or ministry, then an [Imperial title](../titles/Imperial_title.md) to oversee the commission is automatically created. The location and size of the commission and the method of appointment and tenure of any associated title are all part of the [necessary details](../../in-character/senate-motions/Motion_wording.md#necessary-details) and may be included in the announcement made by the Bearer or determined later by them.

A Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave cannot [alter an existing commission](../commissions/Commission.md#altering-an-existing-commission).

### Announcement

To use their power of commission, the bearer must make an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) in the Imperial Senate. No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power had been delegated to the bearer.

The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may choose to exercise their [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the bearer's announcement; there is no refund of the money provided to purchase the title if the commission is vetoed.

## Limitations

The bearer of an Imperial wayleave can only commission a sinecure, ministry, edifice, wayhouse, or great work.

Unlike a [civilian commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md), the bearer is **not** prohibited from using their power to commission anything that provides liao. However, the appointment of such a title is still subject to [the normal constitutional restrictions](../../in-character/senate-motions/Motion_wording.md#necessary-details).

They are not required to build their commission in their own nation and may use any method of appointment (for example, through the Bourse, the Conclave, Military Council, or Synod). The civil service are on hand to offer legal advice or support to a bearer just as they are to a senator, and the bearer may wish to submit their commission for scrutiny before it is announced to ensure there are no problems with their design.

## Appointment

Each title of bearer of an Imperial wayleave is determined by auction by the [Bourse](Bourse.md). As an Imperial seat, any Imperial citizen may participate in the auction and hold the title.

The bearer retains the title *for a single season only* - they must use the power of commission at the summit where they gain the title, otherwise it is lost. As an Imperial Bourse title, the position cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
