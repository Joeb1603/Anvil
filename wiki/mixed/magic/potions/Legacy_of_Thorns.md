# Legacy of Thorns

## Overview

Mastery of these preparations allows the brewing of several toxic substances, which can hamper an enemy's ability to fight. They have been extensively used by the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) since time immemorial, but many societies have their own equivalents. This includes the barbarian orcs of course - the [Druj](../../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) of the Eastern swamps are known to make extensive use of preparations such as these when battling their Imperial enemies.

Navarr apothecaries have spent centuries trying to develop a thicker version of these venoms that can be successfully applied to arrows, but to date they have made no progress.

In Autumn 382YE, the original three preparations governed by the Legacy of Thorns was expanded to include a fourth - [Legionnaire's Stare](#legionnaires-stare) - when the recipe was [distributed](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Distribute_potion_recipes.md) to Imperial apothecaries by order of the [Senate](../../government/senate/Senate.md). This new recipe originally came from a Druj herbal captured during the liberation of [Reikos](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md), although an identical preparation was shared with the [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) and [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) as part of a gift of herb lore given to those two nations by the Great Forest refugees as thanks for their assistance establishing themselves in [Therunin](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md).

### Blade Venoms

Three of these preparations are intended to be applied to melee weapons. They cannot be used with arrows or quarrels, and are ineffective when applied to thrown weapons. They are also ineffective when applied to [implements](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#implements) such as [rods](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods) and [staffs](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staffs). It takes five seconds of [appropriate roleplaying](../../../out-of-character/rules/Appropriate_roleplaying.md) to apply a blade venom to a melee weapon, after which you *must* make the appropriate call the next time you hit a target. If ten seconds pass without making the call, the blade venom is wasted.

You cannot combine the call granted by a blade venom with any other combat or magic call; only one call is made at a time.

## Oil of Blackthorn

Oil of Blackthorn is an effective blade venom, and has seen extensive use by the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) in their battles with barbarians, [vallorn](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md)-spawned horrors and hostile outlaws alike. It is considered very useful for dealing with certain creatures of the vallorn-infested forests; such creature often heal at a preternatural rate, but they die just as easily as anyone else when venom is involved. Indeed, the [vallornspawn husks](../../../in-character/world/Vallornspawn_husk.md) are especially vulnerable to venom, dying very quickly if they are struck with a weapon coated in Oil of Blackthorn. [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [wardens](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Warden.md) made occasional use of this venom for the same reason when fighting certain [monsters](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves) that display similar vivacity.

Among the [Unconquered](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md#unconquered) of [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), this thin, slate-coloured oil is more commonly termed *Basilisk Ichor*. A common tactic is for a close-combat team to use it on their weapons to enhance the effectiveness of a volley of arrows fired by nearby allies - a tactic that some claim they learnt by watching the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) - although coordinating this manoeuvre is quite challenging even for the traditionally organised Highborn. Darker rumours suggest their ancestors first encountered it from the receiving end, either in some past conflict with the Navarr, or perhaps more likely in the interminable border skirmishes with the barbarian orcs.

Technically, Oil of Blackthorn should be termed *Oil of Blackroot* and in parts of [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) and [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), they prefer to use this name for this toxic paste. It is occasionally used as an ingestive poison, but the benefits of doing so are minimal; the victim tends to recover quickly after they have thrown up, and there are no permanent effects from consuming the venom orally.

- **Form:** Oil.
- **Description:** This thin, oily paste is the colour of grey slate. If you rub it between your finger and thumb, it has a slick texture that leaves a faint residue behind on your fingers. The oily taste is offensively and deeply unpalatable.
- **Roleplaying Effects:** If you have drunk this oil, you are subject to nausea and painful stomach cramps for the next half hour, although if you vomit or are treated with the [purify](../spells/Purify.md) spell or by anything else that can end the [weakness](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#weakness) or [venom](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) conditions, you can alleviate these symptoms early.
- **Mechanical Effects:** This oil must be smeared on a melee weapon over 5 seconds. The wielder *must* call [VENOM](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) on the next blow with the weapon within 10 seconds.
- **Recipe:** Two drams of [bladeroot](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md) and one dram each of [true vervain](../../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), [cerulean mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md) and [marrowort](../../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md).

## Redwillow Paste

This sticky, rust-coloured paste is called *Dogman's Slather* in the [Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). While it is occasionally used by [beaters](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_military_concerns.md#beaters), it is more commonly encountered smeared on the spears and clubs of the savage [Feni](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Feni.md) who occasionally plague parts of [Upwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md).

Again, in [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) and [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) it is more commonly called *roseweald paste*, but the semantics of what to call it are generally considered less important than its effectiveness in neutralising magicians and enemy champions. As with Oil of Blackthorn, this paste can be administered orally - or accidentally drunk when mistaken for another substance - but it has minimal effect that soon passes.

- **Form:** Oil.
- **Description:** This rust-coloured paste smells faintly of stagnant water. It has a sticky texture if you rub it between your finger and thumb that is hard to get rid of. It has an incredibly sweet, sickly taste that is unappealing.
- **Roleplaying Effects:** If you have drunk this oil, you are subject to the sudden onset of a feverish temperature accompanied by severe sweating, headache, tiredness and other flu-like symptoms that last for the next half hour, although if you vomit or are treated with the [purify](../spells/Purify.md) spell or by anything else that can end the [weakness](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#weakness) or [venom](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) conditions, you can alleviate these symptoms early.

## Legionnaire's Stare

This sticky, yellow-green paste is known as *Stonespear* by the [Great Forest Orcs](../../../in-character/barbarians/Great_Forest_Orcs.md), and (among other names) as *Serpenttooth* by the [Druj](../../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md). Until very recently, the venom has been largely unknown in the Empire. The common Imperial name comes from the [Imperial Orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) responsible for decoding the original Druj herbal, and making the recipes within available to Imperial herbalists.

The potent blade venom quickly overwhelms a foe, rendering them completely immobile for a short period of time. While they can still shout for help, they cannot work any magic, attack, flee, or even defend themselves allowing them to quickly be dispatched or captured.

- **Form:** Oil.
- **Description:** This odourless yellow-green paste has a sticky texture. If you rub it between your finger and thumb, it leaves a residue that is hard to get rid of. It has a metallic, earthy flavour that is surprisingly tasty.
- **Roleplaying Effects:** If you have drunk this oil, you are subject to the sudden onset of a feverish chill accompanied by severe sweating, significant lower back pain, and a strong need to urinate that last for the next half hour, although if you vomit or are treated with the [purify](../spells/Purify.md) spell or by anything else that can end the [weakness](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#weakness) or [venom](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) conditions, you can alleviate these symptoms early.
- **Mechanical Effects:** This oil must be smeared on a melee weapon over 5 seconds. The wielder must call [PARALYSE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#paralyse) on the next blow with the weapon within 10 seconds.
- **Recipe:** Three drams of [bladeroot](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), two drams each of [true vervain](../../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) and [marrowort](../../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), and a dose of [cerulean mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md).

## Gutwrench

This tangy, translucent russet liquid is a nasty ingested poison that can cripple an enemy, allowing them to be easily dispatched. Every now and again a crazed [apothecary](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary) is caught using this preparation to murder a patient under their care by feeding it to them in the guise of a healing potion. It is normally relatively easy to alleviate, but when fed to an unsuspecting victim makes a potent weapon. There is some evidence that this poison was employed by the [Druj](../../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) in their early dealings with the [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), and several stories of massacres in the [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md)'s history where food or drink was tainted with this substance to render enemies weakened and speed the kill.

It is illegal to carry Gutwrench within the Empire.

- **Form:** Liquid.
- **Description:** This translucent liquid has a red-brown tinge to it and sticks viscously to the inside of the container if you shake it. It smells faintly of over-ripe pears and has a faint, tangy flavour.
- **Roleplaying Effects:** As long as you suffer from either [weakness](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#weakness) or [venom](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom), you feel as if your stomach is on fire; you may experience other symptoms including severe sweating, feverishly elevated temperature, throbbing aches in the head and joints, tiredness, agonising stomach cramps, nausea, dizziness and painfully heightened senses that make bright lights or loud noises almost unbearable.
- **Mechanical Effects:** You are subject to both the weakness and venom conditions. These conditions may be removed normally.
- **Recipe:** Two drams each of [Imperial roseweald](../../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) and [bladeroot](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), and one dram of [cerulean mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md).
