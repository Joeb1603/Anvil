# Vital Strength of the Earth

*[Image: This ritual is part of Urizen lore rather than Imperial lore .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBUrizen.jpg)*

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 28

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

When the enchanted character uses the [unstoppable](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#unstoppable) skill they regain up to 5 hits (rather than the usual three). When they use the [relentless](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#relentless) skill they repair all limbs that have been ruined by the [cleave](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) or [impale](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) call rather than just one. Whenever they use either ability they are also cured of the [VENOM](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) status if they were affected although this effect will not work if the [purify](../spells/Purify.md) spell would fail to remove the VENOM effect for any reason.

The character must be in contact with earth or stone to benefit from these abilities. A thin layer of other material will not prevent the enchantment working, but being in the air, up a tree, or standing on wooden planks are all sufficient to stop the magic taking effect.

While enchanted the character experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *you become keenly aware of the fragility of the world and the enduring nature of earth and stone.*

The ritual does not grant access to unstoppable or relentless; the character must have gained those skills separately through use of character points or magic items.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Every two additional characters increases the magnitude by 24. Additional characters must be present throughout.

## Description

This ritual became part of Urizen lore during Winter 383YE along with [Raise the Dragonsworn Cohort](Raise_the_Dragonsworn_Cohort.md) and [Stone's Unyielding Defiance](Stone-s_Unyielding_Defiance.md). All three rituals were contained in *A Year of Perfect Stillness*, a tome of magic owned by the magicians of [Delving](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Redoubt.md#delving), a spire known for their expertise in working stone, in engineering, and in mining. They are also on good terms with [Adamant](../../../in-character/eternals/Adamant.md), and *Raise the Dragonsworn Cohort* draws on that [eternal's](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) power. While this ritual does not draw directly on the power of the eternal, it was apparently [formulated](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) with his assistance. By all accounts the *King Under the Mountain* is inclined to help the people of Urizen. The creation of the [Three Watchers](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md#the-three-watchers) and the [public thanks](../../../in-character/synod-judgements/383YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-49) issued by the Urizen [assembly](../../government/synod/Assembly.md), as well as the [mandate](../../government/synod/Mandate.md) encouraging Urizeni to [work with eternals](../../../in-character/synod-judgements/383YE_Autumn_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-24) all apparently factored in to the architects of Delving receiving his blessing to share the magic he helped to create.

*[Image: Arbiter Severus resides in Delving , Tomari , Redoubt in the event that anyone who wishes to contact them by Call Winged Messenger following the Winter Solstice.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:WingedMessenger.png)*

Home to a number of respected [architects](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Architect.md), they were among the first to see the potential benefits of sharing some of their personal lore with their fellow Urizeni. [Arbiter](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Arbiter.md) **Severus** presented the [opportunity to gain access to the book's rituals](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Some_heavenly_music.md#the-architects-of-stone) in return for an [appraisal](../../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md) commissioned by the [Senate](../../government/senate/Senate.md) to [identify sites for new fortifications in Urizen](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_place_on_a_map.md). When the Senate did so, representatives of the Spire delivered the scrolls that make up *A Year of Perfect Stillness* to [Anvil](../../../in-character/world/Anvil.md) and it was duly added to Urizen lore.

The [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) allows strength and vitality to be drawn from earth and stone, but only when they are in great need. Unless someone already has some ability to [exert their will](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) over their body, either innately or through the use of a [magic item](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md), the ritual does nothing apart from provide an awareness of the enduring nature of rock and stone. Those who have benefited from the ritual talk of being in touch with an inexhaustible reservoir of power which surges into them and massively enhances their constitution and durability for a few moments. They must be in physical contact with a significant amount of earth or stone - generally being on the ground is sufficient. A thin layer of material such as the soles of one's boots, or even a thick rug, will not interfere with the magic. If one is suspended in the air, swimming, climbing a tree, or stood on a floor made of wooden planks however access to the power this ritual provides is temporarily lost. Only part of the body needs to be in contact with the surface - there is a story recounted in *A Year of Perfect Stillness* of a [sentinel](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sentinel.md) who was able to use the enchantment in a [thane's](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_political_leadership.md) wooden hall by keeping one hand in contact with the stone fireplace at all times.

The stone does not have to be natural - worked stone is more than sufficient. However, it is reported that the energy that floods someone when they call on the power of the enchantment in contact with natural earth and stone is somehow more potent, almost overwhelmingly so, than when standing on worked stone.

## Common Elements

This ritual is a personal [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md), and thus the people who will benefit from its effects are the focus. The original text talks of using [the Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) to channel the magic - trracing it on the chest or forehead of the recipients at the very least. One of the great halls at Delving, where the ritual has been practiced for many years, has a white granite floor inlaid with a mithril tracery depicting the constellation, and is regularly used for performing this ritual. Indeed, it is apparently regularly performed on a group of thirteen sentinels at once, each one standing on one of the stars of the Oak. Other notes suggest casting the ritual barefoot - both the recipients and the magicians being in direct skin-to-skin contact with earth or soil helps to focus the magic.

There are a few side notes that the runes [Verys](../lore/Verys.md) and perhaps also [Bravash](../lore/Bravash.md) resonate with the ritual. Evocation of the names of [Adamant](../../../in-character/eternals/Adamant.md) (obviously) and [Cathan Canae](../../../in-character/eternals/Cathan_Canae.md); [heraldic magicians](../lore/Aspect_magic.md#heraldic-magic) might find the [mandowla](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#mandowla) or the [hydra](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#hydra) support the enchantment.
