# Swan's Cruel Wing

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 3

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target may call [cleave](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) once per day with any [melee weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#weapons) except a [pike](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#pike).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Every two additional characters increases the magnitude by 5. Additional characters must be present throughout.

## Description

This [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) is among one of the best-known Summer rituals. Along with [Swift Leaping Hare](Swift_Leaping_Hare.md), [Hammer of Thunder](Hammer_of_Thunder.md) and [Strength of the Bull](Strength_of_the_Bull.md) it provides a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) with the ability to select the right enchantment to enhance any band of warriors.

*The Swan's Cruel Wing* is also known as *The Hungry Blade* (or axe, or what-have-you), or *The Dolorous Blow*. It has several advantages over other enchantments of Summer because almost anyone can benefit from it; there is no requirement to have mastered a specific weapon to strike a devastating blow.

## Common Elements

This ritual usually involves both the target and their armoury. A ritualist might trace matching runes on weapons and warrior, or might anoint them with a mixture of blood and oil. A toast is often drunk, especially when the ritual is performed on a band of warriors rather than a single target. Legendary warriors, especially those who fought and triumphed against great odds, are often invoked as are [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of warlike mien.

Any of the [Summer runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer) might be evoked, but [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md), the rune of victory, is the most common. An evocation of [The Stallion](../lore/The_Stallion.md) or [The Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) may be appropriate with this ritual, as are evocations of horses, [unicorns](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#unicorns) and [dragons](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#dragons-wyverns-and-wyrms).
