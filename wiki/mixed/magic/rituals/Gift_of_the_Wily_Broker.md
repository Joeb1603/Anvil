# Gift of the Wily Broker

## Rules

### Autumn Magnitude 15

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a [business](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The [business](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) provides additional wealth over the next season, in addition to normal production.

At the start of the next season the target business provides one dose of [liao](../../religion/Liao.md); eight random ingots or measures of [metals](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#metals) and [natural materials](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#natural-materials); and one dram each of the herbs [imperial roseweald](../../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), [marrowort](../../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), [bladeroot](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), [true vervain](../../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) and [cerulean mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event. If the owner of the resource does not attend the next event, then the *additional* production provided by the resource is still added to that character's inventory.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional [businesses](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in the same territory. Each additional business increases the magnitude by 12. The character who controls each business must be present throughout the ritual.

## Description

As with rituals such as [Rivers of Gold](Rivers_of_Gold.md) and [The Lure of Distant Shores](Lure_of_Distant_Shores.md), this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) subtly influences the flow of trade, gently warping the effects of chance and synchronicity to encourage people to visit, and spend time in, the target business. Rather than directly improving profits, however, the ritual manipulates chance to provide the owner with a range of valuable goods that can be used for a variety of purposes or sold-on at a profit.

As the name implies, the ritual is popular with [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [brokers](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_economic_interests.md#brokers) who use it to improve their options when it comes to arranging small trades. It is also popular in [The League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) - but much more with [mountebanks](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Mountebank.md) than members of the [troupes](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Troupe.md). It allows the streetwise mountebanks to accumulate useful materials for their various schemes, and supports their images of themselves as clever traders and experts at acquisition. They tend to call it *Gift of the Cunning Mountebank.*

## Common Elements

This ritual attracts wealth and opportunity, and the performance is often accompanied by coins and other precious items. In [The Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and [The Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), coins taken from the previous season's profit are often used as a way to 'prime' the enchantment and attract more wealth, while in [The League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) the ritual often involves complex exchanges of masks, items or symbols - often combining sleight-of-hand with disingenuous invocations. The rune [Pallas](../lore/Pallas.md) is often evoked, but so is the subtle [rune of plots](../lore/Queros.md) and the serendipitous [rune of chance](../lore/Ull.md). Symbols of [Prosperity](../../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md) are also common, often with an invocation highlighting the rewards of clever trade rather than hard work.
