# Raise the Standard of War

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 20

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The military unit gains a significant bonus to its effectiveness when it takes the [campaigning](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning) action (generally meaning it is assigned to support an [Army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md)). This temporary bonus increases the effective fighting force of the unit by 100, the equivalent of 5 normal upgrades.

The character who controls the military unit experiences a short-lived [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) that persists for at least ten minutes: *you are immediately reminded of any military threats, unresolved grudges, or slights against yourself or the things you hold dear, and feel an urge to immediately set about dealing with those threats, grudges, or slights.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event. If the owner of the resource does not attend the next event, then the *additional* production provided by the resource is still added to that character's inventory.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional military units controlled by members of the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional military unit increases the magnitude by 15. The character who controls each unit must be present throughout the ritual.

## Description

This ritual empowers and enhances the warriors in a military unit, granting them physical strength and building their fighting spirit. The soldiers in that unit feel a rush of confidence in their abilities, as the [Summer magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md) enhances their [prowess](../../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md#prowess) with the arts of war. The ritual can have the occasional downside - highly regimented or ordered troops may experience a marked decline in discipline in the face of a strong urge to take charge of situations and deal with things themselves rather than use the chain of command - but this negative effect is generally transitory. As a counterpoint, affected warriors also tend to feel a swell of pride in themselves and their comrades, which can help reinforce their ability to rely on each other in the fray. Unfortunately it also makes rivalries and even brawls between military units more common, especially if there are several units all under the effect of *Raise the Standard of War.*

On the battlefield, warriors under this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) fight ferociously, revelling in the challenges of war. They usually gain additional [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance), and many receive boons similar to enchantments such as [Swan's Cruel Wing](Swan-s_Cruel_Wing.md), [Swift Leaping Hare](Swift_Leaping_Hare.md), or [Hammer of Thunder](Hammer_of_Thunder.md).

This ritual is also sometimes called *The Field of Glory*, a reference to the Summer [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Eleonaris](../../../in-character/eternals/Eleonaris.md) who is the sovereign of a region of the realm by that name. Her association with banners, flags, standards, heraldry and livery means that these are often used as elements when performing the ritual.

## Common Elements

This ritual enhances a small military force, using a connection to its leader to spread magic through the entire unit. It is exceptionally common to include a banner or standard that will be used by the target force as a focus for the ritual. Other common elements include the invocation of [paragons and exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) and [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md), as well as calling on the name of Summer [eternals](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) such as [Eleonaris](../../../in-character/eternals/Eleonaris.md), the *Sovereign in Scarlet and Gold*, or [Hayaak](../../../in-character/eternals/Hayaak.md), the *Brother of Lions* (the latter is especially appropriate when the military force will be engaging hated foes, or foes that deserve retribution). Other elements include [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding) the military leader, the sound of martial horns or drums, and the presence of warriors from the same nation as the target as witnesses.

The Rune of Victory, [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md), is favoured for this ritual not only for its magical meaning but also because of its common associations with banners and flags.
