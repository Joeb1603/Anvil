# Clarity of the Master Strategist

## Rules

### Day Magnitude 80

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 10 minutes of roleplaying. If the ritual is cast using the [Imperial Regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) it requires at least 5 minutes of roleplaying instead.

This ritual targets an [Imperial army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). The [general](../../government/military/Military_Council.md) responsible for the army (or the [egregore](../../../out-of-character/crew/Egregores.md) if the general is not at Anvil) must be present throughout.

During the ritual the casters must be in a strong Day [regio](Regio.md).

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual places an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) on the soldiers of a [campaign army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) that allows them to make and execute strategic and tactical plans more effectively. The target campaign army gains a boost to its combat ability equivalent to 2,000 additional soldiers but *only* for purposes of determining [victory](../../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#victory) - that is, the army does not inflict additional casualties. As with any enchantment on an army or navy, the additional army strength granted by this enchantment may be reduced by the [orders](../../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#orders) issued by the [general](../../titles/General.md). While the effect is expressed as being equivalent to additional soldiers, this enchantment never reduces [casualties](../../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#calculating-casualties) suffered by the army.

The general used to focus this ritual experiences a short-lived [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *you are immediately made aware of every single soldier and piece of equipment in the army, their status, readiness and prowess. This rush of information may be overpowering - it might knock you off their feet, stun you for a minute or two, or even cause a loss of consciousness.*

Thereafter, as long as the enchantment persists, the general experiences a less overwhelming version of this roleplaying effect: *You are supernaturally aware of the status of the soldiers in your army, and their morale. You can bring to mind their names and origins, as well as gain an awareness of their capabilities and equipment. When talking about military matters it is easy and natural to drop details such as these into your conversation.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional armies from the same Empire. The general responsible for each army must be present throughout the ritual. Each additional target increases the magnitude by 60.

> **Law of Scale**  
> This ritual represents the best Day army [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) ritual that can be created without the involvement of an [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md). Centuries of experimentation have shown that it is not possible to improve on this ritual with the magic of this realm. An [arcane projection](../../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) that creates a more powerful version of this effect, one that creates a lower-magnitude version that grants less enhancement to the army, or one that provides a different bonus to the army is not possible due to the [law of scale](../../../out-of-character/rules/Laws_of_magic.md#the-law-of-scale). It might be possible to create a version of the effect that had differently flavoured roleplaying effects, but the mechanical qualities would remain the same. For further notes about this see [war magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/War_magic.md)

## Description

This ritual empowers and enhances an army, using the Imperial general as focus. Every soldier in the target army gains an increased awareness of their role within the army, and the needs of the army as a whole. Officers are aware of the status and needs of the soldiers who fight under them, and gain a much greater understanding of the purpose and goal of strategic and tactical decisions. They become adept at evaluating their opponents, as well as those fighting alongside their own army, compensating for weaknesses in their allies and exploiting those of their foes. Captains and commanders receive an even more wide-ranging awareness, being able to understand and collate information about enemy movements and strength, the effects of terrain and weather, and the probabilities of success for any given maneuverer. The army does not become infallible, but it gains an unparalleled ability to work as a single unit and to make informed decisions about how best to proceed. This clarity extends to interactions both with other campaign armies and with soldiers who join the army later, helping them to integrate and allowing the commanders to make reasonable decisions about their abilities.

On the battlefield, warriors under this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) fight cohesively, and sometimes display a supernatural awareness of their surroundings, their fellow soldiers, and the overall progress of the battle.

When used to affect multiple generals, it draws power from a larger shared strategy. Even if the generals are not participating in the same campaign, they share an awareness of the situation in the other theatres of war that have a bearing - no matter how tenuous - on their own campaign. Including two generals who despise each other and refuse to co-operate may cause the ritual to fail completely.

This ritual is also sometimes called *Ascent of the Pyramid*, a reference to the Day [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Sinokenon](../../../in-character/eternals/Sinokenon.md) who is sometimes invoked during this ritual for its ability to perceive the many parts that make up a whole, and to assist in unifying those disparate agencies towards a single aim. More often, it is associated with the eternal [Zakalwe](../../../in-character/eternals/Zakalwe.md) whose philosophies of warfare are often expounded while the ritual is performed, and who is known to offer boons that empower its performance.

## Common Elements

This ritual enhances a campaign army, and the general of the target army serves as a vital focus for delivering the magic. It is common to include a banner or standard that will be used by the target force as a focus for the ritual. Other common elements include the invocation of [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md) and [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md), as well as the names of Day [eternals](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) associated with information such as [Kimus](../../../in-character/eternals/Kimus.md) and [Sinokenon](../../../in-character/eternals/Sinokenon.md) but most especially [Zakalwe](../../../in-character/eternals/Zakalwe.md). Other elements include [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding) or crowning the military leader, the rhythmic beat of military drums, the presence of warriors from the same nation as the target as witnesses. Another common element is the use of crystals and mirrors to reflect parts of the ritual, slowly moving them towards a single location to symbolise the coming together of disparate elements.

The rune of thought, [Aesh](../lore/Aesh.md), is favoured for this ritual as are the Autumn runes [Queros](../lore/Queros.md) and [Lann](../lore/Lann.md). The constellations of [The Chain](../lore/The_Chain.md) and [The Three Sisters](../lore/The_Three_Sisters.md) might be invoked, as might the characters of [The Captain](../lore/The_Captain.md) or [The Doctor](../lore/The_Doctor.md).
