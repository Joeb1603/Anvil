# Celestial Library

> While still in Imperial Lore, this ritual's usefulness has been greatly reduced. See [Gift of Knowledge](Gift_of_Knowledge.md) for the active version of this ritual.

> **Interdiction**  
> This ritual was [interdicted](../../government/conclave/Interdiction.md) by the [Imperial Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md) in [Winter 382YE](../../../in-character/conclave-sessions/382YE_Winter_Solstice_Conclave_sessions.md#interdiction-the-celestial-library). It is currently illegal to perform this ritual or any [arcane projection](../../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) which substantially replicates its effects. It is not illegal to master the ritual or to possess a copy of its [ritual text](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts).

## Rules

### Day Magnitude 4

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a single book, pamphlet, bundle of letters, scroll or tablet or similar written work that must be present throughout.

### Effects

When the ritual is complete, the targeted written work disappears and is received by the attendants of the Great Library in the realm of Day. The book is sealed in an unused part of the library, and potentially destroyed.

This ritual will only transfer a scroll, pamphlet, tablet, book, or similar piece of written work.

The eternals are peripherally aware of who sent the item.

## Description

Following from the [events of Autumn 382YE](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_way_that_the_sunshine_gleams.md#a-new-archives), the eternal [Phaleron](../../../in-character/eternals/Phaleron.md) no longer supports this ritual. While it is still in Imperial Lore, its usefulness has greatly deteriorated. Phaleron now seals books received with this ritual away, and has them destroyed, rather than having them read. In theory this would be a possible way to dispose of a dangerous book, but such a use might still attract the attention of Phaleron's attendants - and are unlikely to be seen in a positive light. An alternate ritual, the Gift of Knowledge, was given to Gancius della Notte di Sarvos, the Archmage of Day, at the Autumn Equinox 382YE, apparently containing a new ritual for sending books to the eternal.

## Common Elements

The most important element of *The Celestial Library* is the written work to be transferred. It is often wrapped in fine silk or cotton, and usually forms the focus of the ritual; sometimes it is passed from hand to hand, sometimes placed in the centre of the ritual. Invocations of great scholars, [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md), and the rune [Aesh](../lore/Aesh.md) often accompany the ritual.

Some ritualists wrap the book in a chain, and seal it with a lock during the ritual - the constellation of [The Lock](../lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md) is unsurprisingly a common element of such rituals. other ritualists seal the item with a wax seal, often marked with the [rune of discovery](../lore/Sular.md) or [rune of revelation](../lore/Ophis.md), as appropriate to the nature of the work.

The completion of the ritual is often signalled by sprinkling seawater on the written work.
