# Find the Best Path

## Rules

### Autumn Magnitude 80

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 10 minutes of roleplaying. If the ritual is cast using the [Imperial Regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) it requires at least 5 minutes of roleplaying instead.

During the ritual the casters must be in a strong Autumn [regio](Regio.md). This ritual targets an [Imperial army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). The [general](../../government/military/Military_Council.md) responsible for the army (or the [egregore](../../../out-of-character/crew/Egregores.md) if the general is not at Anvil) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

While under the effect of this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md), the [campaign army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) can [move](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md#movement) through an additional territory. That is, an army in [Bregasland](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md) could move through [Kallavesa](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md), [Hahnmark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md), and [Skarsind](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) to reach [Karov](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md) in a single move. The enchantment does not change any other movement rules - such as the need to stop when entering a hostile territory - and explicitly *does* combine with other effects that improve movement such as the *[Traverse the Crossroads](../../government/military/Eastern_Sky.md#favour-of-barien)* order available to the [Eastern Sky](../../government/military/Eastern_Sky.md).

While the enchantment persists, the general responsible for the army experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *You feel a strong affinity for maps, feeling driven to collect them and to spend your spare moments studying and memorising them*.

> **Law of Scale**  
> This ritual represents the best Autumn army [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) ritual that can be created without the involvement of an [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md). Centuries of experimentation have shown that it is not possible to improve on this ritual with the magic of this realm. An [arcane projection](../../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) that creates a more powerful version of this effect, one that creates a lower-magnitude version that grants less enhancement to the army, or one that provides a different bonus to the army is not possible due to the [law of scale](../../../out-of-character/rules/Laws_of_magic.md#the-law-of-scale). It might be possible to create a version of the effect that had differently flavoured roleplaying effects, but the mechanical qualities would remain the same. For further notes about this see [war magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/War_magic.md)

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Options

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of weltsilver spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional armies from the same Empire. The general responsible for each army must be present throughout the ritual. Each additional target increases the magnitude by 60.

## Description

This ritual was entered into Imperial lore during the Autumn Equinox 382YE by Marcus of Endsmeet, the Autumn [archmage](../../titles/Archmage.md). The text that provided the ritual was part of the book *Meditations*, penned by a former [Dean of the Lyceum](../../titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md) from the reign of [Empress Brannan](../../../in-character/history/Empress_Brannan.md). Callus Strategos, the author of the book, was known to be a particularly cynical critic of the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md) during his life, and made it a point of principle not to allow many of the rituals he codified during his tenure as Dean to be added to Imperial lore.

The ritual enchants an army so that it is able to move quickly over long distances. This is not just a quality of speed - moving thousands of soldiers overland is a daunting logistical prospect. The ritual draws heavily on the [autumn](../../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md) resonance of [travel and communication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md#travel-and-communication). It helps to smooth the obstacles to easy travel through the power of serendipity and synchronicity, as well as granting the scouts and pathfinders of an army a supernatural ability to evaluate the relative benefits of potential routes through difficult terrain. Under the influence of this ritual, a captain can stand at a fork in the road and know with cool certainty whether one road will get them to their destination faster than the others. Any circumstantial problem that might delay or impede the armies progress to its destination is revealed, at least in general terms.

Beyond the power of travel, however, the ritual also enhances communication. Captains are quickly able to pass instructions to their soldiers, but the ritual also allows scouts and guides to share some of their expertise with the warriors they are guiding. Further, the resonance of [bands](../../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md#bands) makes it easier for individual soldiers to obey these orders or take advantage of this added expertise. Even when traveling out of sight of each other, soldiers in the army have a subconscious awareness of the rough location of the main body of the army, the direction and speed of travel, and the distance to any specific objective toward which the soldiers are moving.

Some magicians argue that rituals such as these are of limited usefulness because an army can only benefit from a single enchantment at a time. By granting the ability to move quickly, the magic also prevents them benefiting from the added benefits of rituals such as [Knights of Glory](Knights_of_Glory.md) or [Clarity of the Master Strategist](Clarity_of_the_Master_Strategist.md) - potentially impacting their ability to support their allies. Other magicians claim that this is patently absurd - an army that cannot reach the fight is rarely useful no matter what magical enhancement it might otherwise enjoy.

## Common Elements

This ritual enhances a campaign army, and the [general](../../titles/General.md) of the target army serves as a vital focus for delivering the magic. It is common to include a banner or standard that will be used by the target force as a focus for the ritual. Other common elements include the invocation of [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md) and [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md), the [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding) or crowning the military leader, and the rhythmic beat of military drums maintaining a marching rhythm. The ritualists themselves may remain in motion throughout the ritual performance, circling the general - or the general themselves may march, perhaps carrying the army standard, while the magicians weave threads of confidence, certainty, and awareness around them.

The ritual was initially formulated by a [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) magician, and designed to express [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) synergies. It has obvious resonance with [the Captain](../lore/The_Captain.md) - especially as *Scorrero*. Magicians are urged however not to overlook [the Witch](../lore/The_Witch.md), in her *Gancio* aspect; guiding and divining the best route can easily fall within her purview. [The Lantern](../lore/The_Lantern.md) is an obvious [instrument](../lore/Dramaturgy.md#the-instruments) to use - it literally illuminates a path through the wilderness - especially in conjunction with [The Garden](../lore/The_Garden.md), which is usually cast in the role of a wild forest or dangerous journey.

Other elements resonant with the casting of this ritual include maps of the Empire, especially where the army is being enchanted with the intention of making a specific journey from one part to another; the feathers or evocations of migratory birds or birds of prey; hounds, in the aspect of creatures that lead hunters; and the [Rune of Discovery](../lore/Sular.md).
