# Sharp Eyes of the Corsair

## Rules

### Day Magnitude 10

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a military unit. This ritual targets a [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout. This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The military unit gains a bonus to its effectiveness when it takes a [scouting](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#scouting) action (such as when it is assigned to support a [spy network](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) or engage in [bounty hunting](../../economy/Venture.md#bounty-hunting)). This temporary bonus increases the effective fighting force of the unit by 40, the equivalent of 2 normal upgrades.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event. If the owner of the resource does not attend the next event, then the *additional* production provided by the resource is still added to that character's inventory.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional military units belonging to characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 8. The character who controls each military unit must be present throughout the ritual.

## Description

The ritual was based on research performed during Winter 378YE by **Anwar i Erigo**, a [hakima](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Hakima.md) belonging to the Seers of Oran. It was one of the last rituals Anwar developed before leaving the hakima to serve in the [Senate](../../government/senate/Senate.md). It was codified during Winter 378YE at the [Halls of Knowledge](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md#the-halls-of-knowledge-ruined), at that time still in [Zenith](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md), under the supervision of **Octavius of the Spire of the Auric Horizon**, the [Provost of the Halls of Knowledge](../../titles/Provost_of_the_Halls_of_Knowledge.md). The [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) was added to the body of [Imperial lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list) by [declaration](../../government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md) of the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md) during the Spring 379YE summit.

With an increased interest in lands outside the Empire, the usefulness of a ritual that would help scouts and agents uncover details of a hostile territory was obvious. By the Winter of 378YE, the Senate was operating spy networks in [the Barrens](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md), in [Liathaven](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Liathaven.md) and in [Verthandi](../../../in-character/barbarians/Verthandi.md) (in the [Thule](../../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md) nation of [Otkodov](../../../in-character/barbarians/Otkodov.md)). Furthermore, it seemed likely that similar networks would soon be established in the Mallum (the lands controlled by the [Druj](../../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) to the east of the Empire) and, with the approaching end of the [Jotun](../../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md) ceasefire, with the western barbarians as well.

Traditional enchantments such as those provided by [Raise the Standard of War](Raise_the_Standard_of_War.md) or [Merciless Wrath of the Reaver](Merciless_Wrath_of_the_Reaver.md) proved ineffective at supporting soldiers engaged in information gathering rather than conflict with the Empire's enemies. While [Night magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) had some facility with supporting stealth and scouting, the need for a reliable ritual using the [lore of Day](../../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md) was clear. Day magic lends itself particularly well to uncovering information, and what it lacks in the ability to enhance hiding and sneaking it more than makes up for in the ability to help people acquire and evaluate intelligence. The benefits the enchantment provides are twofold.

Firstly, it grants additional precision to the soldiers themselves. It sharpens their senses, granting additional precision when it comes to measurements or observations. When scouting, they will naturally avoid areas covered by other scouts and receive some additional advantages when it comes to finding routes and outposts from which to observe – but avoid – enemy forces. Secondly, it helps the soldiers to submit reports about what they have seen that are clear and useful to the civil servants responsible for collating the experiences of the various companies involved in gathering information through the spy network.

While initially developed to support Imperial scouts in hostile territory, the enchantment proved equally adept at supporting other investigations such as when taking part in [bounty hunting](../../economy/Venture.md#bounty-hunting), and ongoing efforts against internal threats such as the campaign against the Whisper Gallery colloquially referred to as the "[war of whispers](../../economy/Venture.md#war-of-whispers)". The same skills that help a soldier scout dangerous wilderness prove invaluable on the streets of Imperial cities.

While the original [arcane projection](../../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) was being formulated at the Halls of Knowledge, there were complaints from the notoriously sour [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) mage Lucien of the Halls of Knowledge (enjoying an extended visit to the distant [Lyceum](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#the-lyceum) to take advantage of the excellent climate during a particularly cold winter in the peaks of Zenith). This elderly and outspoken magician pointed out that as a ritual of the tenth magnitude, Sharp Eyes of the Corsair was less “efficient” than it could arguably be – a consequence of the [Law of Scale](../../../out-of-character/rules/Laws_of_magic.md#the-law-of-scale).

By simply doubling the magnitude, he claimed, the effectiveness of the ritual would increase by around two-and-a-half times. A twentieth magnitude iteration of this ritual would allow the most basic military company to effectively double their contribution to espionage activities using a spy network. Doing so would be relatively easy – it would certainly take no more time than codifying the ritual at its established magnitude. Calmer heads prevailed, however. The ritual as defined by the Provost might be slightly less efficient, but it is possible for a mistress of Day magic to perform the ritual without the assistance of a coven which could prove to be a significant advantage that should not be discounted.

## Common Elements

While the ritual involves enchanting a body of soldiers, it also involves elements of divination – as such, common elements appropriate to either or both forms of ritual magic are appropriate to the performance of Sharp Eyes of the Corsair. The ritual is also based on initial research performed by one of the [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) hakima, so elements of Brass Coast magic are also appropriate. Indeed, the soothing and harmonious music that often accompanies Erigo rituals seems a particularly good fit for this ritual of the realm of Day.

As with all rituals designed to enhance a company of soldiers, Sharp Eyes of the Corsair uses the leader or captain of the company as a focus, exploiting the [Law of Dominion](../../../out-of-character/rules/Laws_of_magic.md#the-law-of-dominion) to overcome the [Law of Presence](../../../out-of-character/rules/Laws_of_magic.md#the-law-of-presence). The ritual often involves the targets of the enchantment being anointed with oil or freshwater; a [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) researcher recommended girding the subject with symbols associated with clear sight such as a spyglass or map of the area their soldiers would be expected to explore. Providing the commander with a banner marked with an appropriate symbol of [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md), [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md) or clarity is also quite an appropriate element for this ritual.

The ritual resonates strongly with the [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [Rune of Discovery](../lore/Sular.md) – Sular represents not supernatural revelation but the knowledge that comes from exploration and active investigation. That this rune is commonly referred to as “the Ship” is a happy coincidence that makes the chosen name for the ritual even more appropriate.

[Astronomancy](../lore/Astronomancy.md) would tie the ritual to [the Key](../lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md#the-key), with its strong resonance for unlocking secrets and discovering truth. Some Urizen magicians point out that the ritual also has a resonance with [the Spider](../lore/The_Spider_and_The_Web.md#the-spider) – the hidden eye that observes things, especially things outside the Imperial sphere of influence. Spiders are also a [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) symbol associated with Wisdom – making them doubly appropriate.

At the time of this writing, there has been little opportunity to experiment with [dramaturgy](../lore/Dramaturgy.md). However, a theoretical [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) arcanist at the Lyceum by the name of Jenny Greenteeth suggested that [the Bishop](../lore/The_Bishop.md) would be an excellent [persona](../lore/Dramaturgy.md#the-personae) to use with the ritual, combining the concepts of strengthening and revealing with the vital virtue of Vigilance.
