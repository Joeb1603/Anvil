# Heaven's Spear Sunders the Mountain

*[Image: This ritual is part of Urizen lore rather than Imperial lore .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBUrizen.jpg)*

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 8

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout. The target character must possess the [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skill.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains the ability to expand a point of personal mana to call [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) when wielding a staff [implement](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#implements). The target must follow all the rules for casting an [offensive spell](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#offensive-spells), and must recite an appropriate incantation before using their staff this way.

While under the effect of the enchantment the target experiences a roleplaying effect: *you find it difficult to control you temper, and any irritation or anger you experience is significantly stronger to the point it may overpower your good sense if only for a few moments.*

This effect is even more pronounced if you have the [changeling](../lore/Changeling.md) [lineage](../lore/Lineage.md).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 6. Additional characters must be present throughout.
