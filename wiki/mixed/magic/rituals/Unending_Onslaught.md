# Unending Onslaught

## Rules

### Winter Magnitude 12

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains the ability to use the [relentless](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#relentless) ability twice each day as if they knew it without spending any hero points.

They also experience a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *Being struck fills you with rage and an urge to obliterate the one who struck you. Long after the enchantment has faded you will still occasionally feel agonising pain in any limb you have restored through use of this enchantment*.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banner). Every additional character increases the magnitude by 9. Additional characters must be present throughout.

## Description

This ritual was codified in 384YE by the [spires](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Spire.md) of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), under the guidance of **Spyridonakes**, the [Doyen of the Spires](../../titles/Doyen_of_the_Spires.md). It was based on work originally performed by fellow Urizen **Ematius**. Among those who helped to codify the ritual were several scholars in newly-[Grendel](../../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) occupied [Spiral](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md). While their aid helped to speed the formulation of the ritual, it also means that Salt Lord Kaliact and her court are aware of the existence of this ritual – although it is unlikely to cause them any particular concern.

This ritual grants the target the ability to overcome and endure significant injuries. As with enchantments such as [Unyielding Constitution](Unyielding_Constitution.md) and especially [Fight Tooth and Nail](Fight_Tooth_and_Nail.md), it draws on the [Winter resonance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Winter_magic.md#resonance) of *[survival](../../../out-of-character/rules/Winter_magic.md#survival)* – the drive to stay alive. With a few moments to recuperate, even the most savage blow to a limb can be overcome.

The enchantment does not heal so much as repair. By all accounts it can be quite a disconcerting effect to observe; a dislocated arm snapping back into position makes a certain sound that some find distinctly unsettling. Actually experiencing the effect can also be a little unsettling; those who have used it describe a flowering numbness that encompasses the ruined limb as blood flow slows and fractured bone fuses together.

The enchantment also encourages the recipient to ensure that whatever has harmed them is never able to do so again. Being struck even by regular blows can fill them with a near uncontrollable rage and desire to obliterate the one who assaulted them. It also has some lingering effects. The first magicians to experience the enchantment spoke of peculiar incidents where the repaired limb blossomed with agonising pain, reminiscent of the original injury.

## Common Elements

The ritual is a personal enchantment, and is resonant with [Verys](../lore/Verys.md) (the Rune of Strength) – a rune not often used with Winter magic. The first performance involved actually cutting the rune into the flesh of the recipient with a knife, and [blood magic](../lore/Blood_magic.md) is particularly effective with this ritual. The blood of [draughir](../lore/Draughir.md) appears to be unsurprisingly effective in this regard.

A [goeticist](../lore/Aspect_magic.md#goetia) might evoke the names of [Sorin](../../../in-character/eternals/Sorin.md) or [Surut](../../../in-character/eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#surut) – both Winter [eternals](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) are associated with enduring or overcoming injury, and the rage that the enchantment can create in its recipient is particularly associated with the Ashen Knight.

[Astronomancy](../lore/Astronomancy.md) links this ritual to the [constellation](../lore/Astronomancy.md#constellations) of [the Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) (things endure), while a [dramaturge](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) might use [the Doctor](../lore/The_Doctor.md), [the Blade](../lore/The_Blade.md), and [the Battlefield](../lore/The_Battlefield.md) to create a play resonant with imagery of survival and overcoming injury.

Finally, this ritual certainly resonates with the aspect of the [Hydra](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#hydra) – which represents regeneration and personal healing. One [Navarri](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) magician involved in the formulation insisted on calling it *Hydra's Onslaught* but the name never caught on with their fellow researchers.
