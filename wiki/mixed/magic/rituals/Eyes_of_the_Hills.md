# Eyes of the Hills

## Rules

*[Image: This ritual is part of Urizen lore rather than Imperial lore .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBUrizen.jpg)*

### Autumn Magnitude 100

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 10 minutes of roleplaying. If the ritual is cast using the [Imperial Regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) it requires at least 5 minutes of roleplaying instead.

During the ritual the casters must be in a strong Autumn [regio](Regio.md). This ritual targets an [Imperial army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). The [general](../../government/military/Military_Council.md) responsible for the army (or the [egregore](../../../in-character/nations/general/Egregores.md) if the general is not at Anvil) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual places an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) on the soldiers of a [campaign army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) that allows them to make and execute strategic and tactical plans more effectively. The target campaign army gains a boost to its combat ability equivalent to 4,000 additional soldiers but *only* for purposes of calculating the economic benefits of any plunder, or similar, order - that is, the army does not inflict additional casualties or gain additional territory, but it does generate additional wealth. As with any enchantment on an army or navy, the additional army strength granted by this enchantment may be reduced by the [orders](../../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#orders) issued by the [general](../../titles/General.md). While the effect is expressed as being equivalent to additional soldiers, this enchantment never reduces [casualties](../../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#calculating-casualties) suffered by the army.

As long as the enchantment persists, the general experiences the following roleplaying effect: *you are acutely aware of any opportunity to take advantage of every situation that surrounds you, for your benefit or the benefit of allies. It feels easy to ignore any drawbacks or compromises that might be involved in seizing any opportunity for gain.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Options

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of green iron spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

## Description

This ritual was one of the very first to become part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore), following the Autumn Equinox 383YE. Contributed by Mord Thorntarry of the [spire](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Spire.md) of Auric Horizon, the ritual was one of those contained in the book *Meditations*. Written by Callus Strategos, [Dean of the Lyceum](../../titles/Dean_of_the_Lyceum.md) during the reign of [Empress Britta](../../../in-character/history/Empress_Britta.md), the book also contained [Swords in the Noonday Sun](Swords_in_the_Noonday_Sun.md), [Good Green Oak](Good_Green_Oak.md), [Hook of the Hoarfrost Guardian](Hook_of_the_Hoarfrost_Guardian.md), and two rituals that had already become part of [Imperial lore](../../government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md) - [Find the Best Path](Find_the_Best_Path.md) and [Hammers of the Brilliant Shore](Hammers_of_the_Brilliant_Shore.md).

A powerful [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) of the Autumn realm, the Eyes of the Hills infuses soldiers with a degree of cunning and awareness that allows them to seize every opportunity for profit. While war is usually destructive, it invariably creates opportunities to seize the wealth of an enemy and tear it from them. But in the clash of steel, such opportunities all too often go unnoticed. The enchantment ensures that no chance to is missed, as those affected become alert to what is going on around them. The army becomes adept at lightning strikes designed to loot wagon trains, raid Bourse resources, or plunder mines and forests.

Autumn magic is ill-suited for use in war, if a general desires to improve their chances of defeating the enemy. The realm is [well suited](../../../out-of-character/rules/War_magic.md#autumn-magic) to enchantments that improve logistics, making armies move and resupply faster, but the realm is [dissonant with destruction](../../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md#destruction) and that makes it impossible to use to create rituals that increase casualties or take territory.

However, prosperity and wealth is one of the strongest resonances of the realm and that does make it possible to enchant an army to aid them when foraging or looting. This ritual makes every soldier in the army alert to any opportunity to steal wealth or resources from their enemy while on campaign. Any army that can issue an order to plunder - or otherwise loot wealth while on campaign, can benefit from this enchantment.

## Common Elements

As an enchantment that enhances a campaign army, the general of the target army serves as a vital focus for delivering the magic. A banner or standard that will be used by the target force as a focus for the ritual is an obvious component, as is the presence of soldiers who have fought beneath that banner in the recent past. Other elements include girding or crowning the military leader, the slow and steady beat of drums, and the use of symbols of Prosperity like cups or coins are common.

[The Key](../lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md#the-key) has a strong resonance for this enchantment - "Things are revealed" reflects the way the magic imparts potency to scouts, observers, and pathfinders moving stealthily to observe enemy positions. [Pallas](../lore/Pallas.md), the rune of wealth, can always be employed when creating this enchantment which concerns itself so intimately with the value of things.
