# Hammer of Thunder

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 3

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target may call [IMPALE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) once per day with a [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Every two additional characters increases the magnitude by 5. Additional characters must be present throughout.

## Description

This [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) is among one of the best-known Summer rituals. Along with [Swift Leaping Hare](Swift_Leaping_Hare.md), [Swan's Cruel Wing](Swan-s_Cruel_Wing.md) and [Strength of the Bull](Strength_of_the_Bull.md) it provides a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) with the ability to select the right enchantment to enhance any band of warriors. In this case, it allows those who wield [great weapons](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons) to strike a single devastating blow that will potentially maim or completely incapacitate an opponent. In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), in keeping with the naming strategy of the other similar rituals, it is known as *Mammoth's Thunder*; the rest of the Empire tends to use the more common name.

## Common Elements

This ritual usually involves both the target and their great weapon. A ritualist might trace matching runes on weapons and warrior, or might anoint them with a mixture of blood and oil. A toast is often drunk, especially when the ritual is performed on a band of warriors rather than a single target. Legendary warriors, especially those known for their strength or uncompromising nature, are often invoked as are [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of warlike mien.

Any of the [Summer runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer) might be evoked, but [Verys](../lore/Verys.md), the rune of strength, is the most common (although the ill-favoured [rune of storms](../lore/Mawrig.md) may be used by especially war-like magicians). An evocation of [The Stallion](../lore/The_Stallion.md) or [The Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) may be appropriate with this ritual, as are evocations of bears, bulls and other powerful creatures such as lions and mammoths.
