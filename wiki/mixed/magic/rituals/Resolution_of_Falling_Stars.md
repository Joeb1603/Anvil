# Resolution of Falling Stars

This is a placeholder page for content that PD are actively working on.

## Rules

### Day Magnitude 6

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. The ritual targets the caster only; it cannot be performed by a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#coven).

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) provided by the ritual grants the caster the ability to spend a point of personal mana at any time to justify remaining calm and collected when exposed to a roleplaying effect (as if they had spent a hero point). This expenditure of mana has the same restrictions as casting a spell.

While under the effect of the enchantment the character experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *you are centred and focussed and feel empowered to master your emotions rather than be mastered by them regardless of their source.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.
