# Mystic scrolls of Estavus

*[Image: Estavus is known to lend her servitors - both those drawn to her service and those she crafts herself - to magicians who gain her favour.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:EstavusConstruct.jpg)*

## Overview

During the Summer Solstice 384YE, in response to a [plenipotentiary](../../titles/Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) from the [Archmage of Autumn](../../titles/Archmage_of_Autumn.md), Edmundo of Damakan's forge, [Estavus](../../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md) made a quartet of *mystic scrolls* available to Imperial magicians - assuming they were [prepared to pay for them](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Begin_to_speak.md#estavus). These scrolls were temporary texts, similar to a ritual text, that allowed up to a dozen magicians to master a specific ritual drawing on Estavus' power. They were only potent for the Summer Solstice, but any magician who used one to master the magic they contained retained that knowledge just as they would any other mastered ritual.

The scrolls made available included [Ramparts of Ashlar](#ramparts-of-ashlar), [Hammers of Fury](#hammers-of-fury), and [Accoutrements of War](#accoutrements-of-war).

These rituals are *not* part of [Imperial lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list). However as long as one of the [contributors](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#contributors) has mastered one of these rituals, their coven may attempt to perform that ritual as if it were part of Imperial lore (bearing in mind that only those who have mastered the ritual would be able to [double the mana they contribute](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#basic-rules)).

There is no practical way for the Conclave to [add one of these rituals to Imperial lore](../../government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md), and no way to get them into [Imperial lore](../../government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md). Either or both would only be possible as a consequence of a further bargain with Estavus. It is also not possible to use an [arcane projection](../../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md) to [share ritual lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Limits_of_magic.md#sharing-ritual-lore) - new magicians will only be able to master these rituals as a result of some kind of deal with the Forgemistress.

Each of these rituals draws directly on the power of the *Prince of Shikal*. Should the eternal ever be subject to [Enmity](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) by the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md) the rituals would still function, but would be illegal to perform. Each also comes with an *[assurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Eternals_and_rituals.md#communication-and-gifts)* - a restriction that Estavus has placed on the ritual. If this restriction is transgressed, the eternal will withdraw her power and the ritual will become useless from that point onwards.

## Accoutrements of War

### Rules

#### Autumn Magnitude 20

#### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

#### Effects

Calls forth heralds from the Towers of Calx and Coom bearing gleaming brass arms and armour fashioned in the heart of a volcano. The potent armour and weaponsmithing tools they carry, and their supernatural ability to empower the armaments of others, allow them to provide a powerful boost to any military unit. The magical properties they impart lose their inner fire after a season of fighting, but during that time the military unit gains a significant bonus to its effectiveness when it takes the [campaigning](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning) action (generally meaning it is assigned to support an [Army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md)). This temporary bonus increases the effective fighting force of the unit by 100, the equivalent of 5 normal upgrades.

The character who controls the military unit experiences a short-lived [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) that lasts at least ten minutes: *you are encouraged to seek out the best possible equipment for yourself and your followers.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

#### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional military units controlled by members of the same banner. Each additional military unit increases the magnitude by 15. The character who controls each unit must be present throughout the ritual.

#### Assurance

The ritual loses all power and becomes useless if the Empire ever breaches the terms of a [parley](../../titles/Archmage.md#parley) with an Autumn eternal.

### Description

Calls forth several heralds from the Towers of Calx and Coom bearing arms and armour fashioned in the heart of a volcano, as well as potent tools for crafting and repairing arms and armour. These heralds take the form of humanoid figures with skin the colour of old iron, and blazing crimson hair, who effortlessly lift great weights that would cause a mortal to bend under the strain. They use their skills to provide short-lived enchantments to the accoutrements of the soldiers they fight beside, and swiftly repair and restore damaged weapons and armour. The boons they grant cause metal items to gleam with an inner fire, providing a potent boost to any soldiers who wield them. An earlier version of this enchantment called forth a force of warriors that fought alongside a warband; changes in the management of the Towers of Calx and Coom has somewhat altered the focus of the ritual.

### Common Elements

The ritual should include an evocation of Estavus, the great fire mountain of Shikal, and the Towers of Calx and Coom which are believed to be home to some of Estavus' finest warsmiths. Other elements that seem to resonate with the ritual include the rune [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md), [Lann](../lore/Lann.md), and [Pallas](../lore/Pallas.md), the constellation of [the Great Wyrm](../lore/The_Great_Wyrm.md) (which represents the transformation that imbues these accoutrements with warlike potence), the [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) elements of [the Captain](../lore/The_Captain.md), [the Battlefield](../lore/The_Battlefield.md),and [the Blade](../lore/The_Blade.md), and the heraldic magic figure of the [fire dragon](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#dragons2c-wyverns-and-wyrms) that seems to have a sympathy for any of Estavus' rituals that deal with creation and destruction.

## Hammers of Fury

### Rules

#### Autumn Magnitude 80

#### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 10 minutes of roleplaying. If the ritual is cast using the [Imperial Regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) it requires at least 5 minutes of roleplaying instead.

During the ritual the casters must be in a strong Autumn [regio](Regio.md). This ritual targets a [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md), and must be performed at a regio in that territory. If the ritual is used to target an Imperial territory, it may instead be performed at the [Imperial regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) at Anvil.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

#### Effects

Enchants the forges, foundries, and smithies throughout a territory. These sites are infused with the heat of Shikal's forges, and able to forge the arms and armour needed to equip an army with supernatural speed. This reduces the time needed to [muster an army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md#recruitment) in the [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) by one season.

[Mines](../../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) throughout the territory will see their production of metals reduced by two ingots, but will each gain one pouch of [Warm Ashes](../../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#warm-ashes) in their place.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

#### Assurance

The ritual loses all power and becomes useless if the Empire ever does not have a working [runeforge](../../commissions/Runeforge.md).

### Description

This ritual draws power from the volcanic forges of the mountain of Shikal to empower the foundries and smithies throughout a territory. These sites are infused with the power to create great numbers of arms and armour, allowing a new army to be equipped with supernatural speed. The materials needed to craft these fine accoutrements is drawn from mines throughout the enchanted area, but the power of Estavus pays for whatever the ritual takes. As such, mines throughout that territory see some of the metals drawn from their depths replaced with pouches of ash, warm from the forge, infused with the power of [Autumn magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md).

### Common Elements

The ritual should include an evocation of Estavus, the great fire mountain of Shikal, and the endless forges, smithies, and foundries that turn the great volcanic city into such a hive of productivity. Other elements that seem to resonate with the ritual include the rune [Pallas](../lore/Pallas.md) (which has a strong affinity for crafting items), the constellation of [the Mountain](../lore/The_Mountain.md) (both as an image of Shikal, and in the context of overcoming it perhaps with the aid of [the Chain](../lore/The_Chain.md)), the [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) elements of [the Captain](../lore/The_Captain.md), [the Prince](../lore/The_Prince.md),and [the Coin](../lore/The_Coin.md), and the heraldic magic figure of the [fire dragon](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#dragons2c-wyverns-and-wyrms) that seems to have a sympathy for any of Estavus' rituals that deal with creation and destruction.

## Ramparts of Ashlar

### Rules

#### Autumn Magnitude 120

#### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 10 minutes of roleplaying. If the ritual is cast using the [Imperial Regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) it requires at least 5 minutes of roleplaying instead.

During the ritual the casters must be in a strong Autumn [regio](Regio.md). This ritual targets a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), and must be performed in a strong Autumn [regio](Regio.md) in the [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) containing the fortification. If the territory is part of the Empire, then it may instead be performed from the [Imperial regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio).

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

#### Effects

This ritual calls on the power of [Estavus](../../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md) to defend a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md). The garrison is supplemented with bronze constructs drawn from the Autumn realm, while the walls themselves are bound with peculiar brass sigils evoking concepts of stone, strength, and fire. The fortification is supported by a defensive force of 3,000, similar to that provided by [military units](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) taking a [guarding](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#guarding) action to support it. This means that if the fortification has an intact [garrison](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#garrison), its fighting strength is increased. If there is no active garrison, the force will instead defend the walls of the [bastion](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#bastion) associated with the fortification providing 3,000 defensive force in that [region](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md#regions) which helps defend it and will inflict casualties against a force attacking the region containing the bastion.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

#### Assurance

The magic of this ritual is sufficient to summon heralds of Estavus even if the Imperial Conclave has declared enmity with the eternal but doing so not only has legal implications but is likely to anger the Forgemistress.

The ritual loses all power and becomes useless if the Empire ever refuses a lawful demand of the Brass Magistrates (the [Lictors](../../../in-character/eternals/Lictors.md)). The ritual's power might be restored by renegotiation with Estavus but it is by no means certain it would function the same way.

### Description

This enchantment draws power from the great bastion of Ashlar which dominates the eastern face of the mountain of Shikal. The walls of the [bastion](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#bastion) reflect the enchantment, becoming bound with brass sigils that embody the mystic powers of stone, strength, and fire. These sigils glow faintly throughout, as if they had just been taken from the heat of a forge, and can sear the flesh of those who accidentally touch one causing serious burns. These are not [runes](../lore/Runes.md) as mortal magicians understand them - rather they are complex curling shapes that nonetheless seem to communicate the concept they represent directly to anyone who gazes on them. At the same time the walls are being reinforced, the [garrison](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#garrison) is supplemented with bronze constructs drawn from Estavus' realm. They sally force alongside the mortal defenders, protecting the territory where the fortification stands. They are especially eager to engage anyone foolish enough to lay siege to the fortress.

Even while the constructs are on campaign alongside the garrison, the interior of the enchanted [bastion](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#bastion) is always warm - even swelteringly hot in places - and the air is sharp with the tang of smoke and molten metal. The garrison, and any [warband](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) assigned to [defend the fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#defend-fortification) will often report hearing the sounds of industry somewhere in the depths of the castle - great hammers striking metal and the hiss of hot blades being quenched.

### Common Elements

The ritual should include an evocation of Estavus, the great fire mountain of Shikal, and the bronze bastion of Ashlar. Other elements that seem to resonate with the ritual include the rune [Verys](../lore/Verys.md), the constellation of [the Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) (albeit in a more metallic aspect than magicians are traditionally familiar with), [the Captain](../lore/The_Captain.md) and [the Blade](../lore/The_Blade.md), and the heraldic magic figure of the [fire dragon](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#dragons2c-wyverns-and-wyrms).
