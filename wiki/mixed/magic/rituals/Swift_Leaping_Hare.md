# Swift Leaping Hare

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 3

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target may call [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown) once per day with a [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Every two additional characters increase the magnitude by 5. Additional characters must be present throughout.

## Description

Also known as *Cunning Puissance of the Leaping Hare* in some circles, this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) is among one of the best-known Summer rituals. Along with [Swan's Cruel Wing](Swan-s_Cruel_Wing.md), [Hammer of Thunder](Hammer_of_Thunder.md) and [Strength of the Bull](Strength_of_the_Bull.md) it provides a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) with the ability to select the right enchantment to enhance any band of warriors.

The ritual is usually referred to by its popular [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) name. The swift movement of the hare and the way it weaves back and forth underfoot are an image associated with the quick and deft use of a bill to hook a target's legs out from under them. Some magicians use the name of their own favoured totem animal in the ritual - for example it is called *The Dancing Fox* by some [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md), while several cabals of [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) war-witches call it *Glittering Salmon Leap*, and a few [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) refer to it as the *Charge of the Mountain Goat*.

## Common Elements

This ritual usually involves both the target and their polearm. A ritualist might trace matching runes on weapons and warrior, or might anoint them with a mixture of blood and oil. The target is often persuaded to perform a series of quick, deft manoeuvres; sweeping, jabbing and hooking as the ritualist weaves magic over the warrior and the polearm. Legendary warriors, especially those who displayed cleverness or guile, are often invoked as are [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of warlike mien.

Any of the [Summer runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer) might be evoked, but [Jotra](../lore/Jotra.md), the Rune of Battle, is the most common. An evocation of [The Stallion](../lore/The_Stallion.md) or [The Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) may be appropriate with this ritual, as are evocations of horses and [unicorns](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#unicorns).
