# Circle of Gold

## Rules

### Autumn Magnitude 16

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets up to five characters from the same [band](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#band). Each character must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target characters gain the ability to use [stay with me](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#stay-with-me) once each day without needing to know the skill or spend any [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero); however, they can only use the ability on another character who was also a target of this ritual[\*](#ooc-elements).

As long as they are [enchanted](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md), the targets experience a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md); *they feel a desire to stick together, whether on the battlefield or off it. They feel a strong urge to defend the other targets of the ritual, whether from physical harm or from insults.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [band](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#band). Every two additional characters increases the magnitude by 3. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Options

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of weltsilver spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

### OOC Elements

Note that the targeting restriction of this ritual does not mean "*people who are enchanted with Circle of Gold*" it is "*people who were targets of the same Circle of Gold ritual*". The targets of a given ritual can only use the stay with me skill on other targets of the same ritual performance.

## Description

Battlefields are dangerous places, and this ritual grants an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) that allows members of a tight-knit band to prevent their comrades succumbing to mortal wounds easily. The dynamic of the group it is used on often influences how the enchantment is perceived - some groups use this healing as soon as one of their number is incapacitated, while others focus their attention on keeping a certain key member of the group (such as a healer, officer, magician or reckless warrior) on their feet.

Participants feel a strong urge to stick together, and tend to be very defensive of each other. It has an unfortunate side effect of encouraging groups of warriors to become embroiled in brawls and honour-duels; on more than one occasion a [Free Company](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md) captain has been known to restrict his [bravos](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Bravo.md) to camp after the ritual is performed to prevent them becoming engaged in a fight with other bravos and wasting the enchantment's power.

## Common Elements

As with many similar rituals, it is common for the targets to share a drink or consume food together, often with a toast or rousing cheer to mark the beginning and end of the ritual. Tokens are often given to the targets, especially tokens of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) or those woven or painted with a group insignia. Some ritualists have any weltsilver they plan to use towards the performance of the ritual twisted into rings, one for each target of the ritual.

Many ritualists, especially [Imperial Orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md), [Marchers](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and [Varushkans](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), encourage the participants to recite some sort of oath of fraternity or mutual protection while joined in a literal circle, arm to arm. Images and invocations of camaraderie, [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md) and mutual respect are common, as is the rune [Lann](../lore/Lann.md) and the constellation of [The Chain](../lore/The_Chain.md).
