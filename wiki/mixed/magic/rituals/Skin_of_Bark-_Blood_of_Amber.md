# Skin of Bark, Blood of Amber

## Rules

### Spring Magnitude 15

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target character gains three additional ranks of [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance).

The target is also under a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *you feel physically massive, as if you are much larger than you actually are. It takes a significant effort of will to move quickly or to change your mind once it is made up.*

If the target is a [Briar](../lore/Briar.md), they may choose to temporarily increase the amount of [bark on their skin](../lore/Briar.md#briars-bleed-bark), and may include [thorns](../lore/Briar.md#thorns) as well if they wish although these are also temporary and generally do not last longer than a few weeks.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 11. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Option

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 measures of ambergelt spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

## Description

This old ritual is believed to be a remnant of [Ushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_history.md) magic, although the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) and the [Suaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) claim that their people have used similar rituals since time immemorial. It fills a target with the unyielding vigour of a massive tree - an oak, ancient pine or similar long-lived plant.

Some suspicious people see the effect that the ritual has on some briars and assume that they run the risk of becoming briars themselves when exposed to this ritual, but there are no recorded cases of this happening.

## Common Elements

An obvious component commonly part of the ritual is [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), although amber and pine resin are equally common. As with many rituals that create an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md), the casters may symbolically give gifts to the target and branches from old trees, amber jewellery and [favours](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#favours) or tokens that bear images of oak-leaves, acorns or similar plants. Some [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [Weaver cabals](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weaver_cabal.md) [gird](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding) the targets in light or medium armour made of leather or hide, while [Landskeepers](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Landskeeper.md) often crown their targets with leaves to show their connection to and mastery over natural forces.

The rune [Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md) and [Verys](../lore/Verys.md) are often evoked with this ritual, as is [The Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md). Some ritualists also call on [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md), [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) and [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md) - especially [The Sentinel](../../../in-character/religion/The_Sentinel.md) - when they perform *Skin of Bark, Blood of Amber*.
