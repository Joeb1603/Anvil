# Pallid Flesh of the Dead King

## Rules

### Winter Magnitude 13

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains three additional ranks of [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance). At the same time, they are affected by [venom](../spells/Venom.md). The supernatural venom caused by this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) cannot be removed by the [purify](../spells/Purify.md) spell (although attempting to do so will not cost the caster any mana) unless the enchantment is removed first.

If the venom is removed by other means (such as [Ascetic Star of Atun](Ascetic_Star_of_Atun.md) or the use of [Imperial roseweald](../../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) for example) the enchantment immediately ends and the target loses three hits from their current total.

While under this enchantment, the target also experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *You occasionally feel as if your body is wrapped in heavy chains, and suffer instants where an arm or leg will not move. You regularly suffer dreams of being buried alive, or entombed unmoving in a great empty chamber for eternity.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 10. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Options

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), and [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots spent in this way count as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual. However, a magician may *only* use this option if they make it clear through their roleplaying that these are gifts for the eternals of the Thrice-cursed Court, generally by invoking the name of Tharim. It should be obvious to any observer that these ingots are a tribute.

### Assurance

This ritual calls on the [might](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md#munificence-and-might) of an eternal, in this case [Tharim](../../../in-character/eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#tharim), and it has an [assurance](../lore/Eternals_and_rituals.md#assurance-and-stipulation). The assurance connected with the ritual was lost for several centuries but was unearthed by members of the [Unfettered Mind](../../government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md) shortly before the Winter Solstice 386YE. The ritual loses all power and ceases to function if the Conclave ever [interdicts](../../government/conclave/Interdiction.md) the practice of personal curses.

*Greta Salpietra de Holberg*, one of the researchers of the order, highlights that "simply" interdicting certain curses would not be enough to break this assurance. Greta instead notes that it is the magical practice of a personal curse that would need to be interdicted to break this assurance with the Bound King.

## Description

This [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) lets a target shrug off the effects of sword-blows but renders them vulnerable both to arrows and to sustained injury. It provides short-term protection at a price, and a short-sighted, incautious target is likely to die under the influence of this ritual. Only a warrior who understands the limitations the enchantment places on them can use it safely.

The power of this enchantment is drawn from the [Bound King](../../../in-character/eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#tharim) of [the Thrice-cursed Court](../../../in-character/eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md). It demonstrates their obsession with power that comes at a price. Some scholars of the [eternals](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) believe that this ritual alleviates a portion of the Bound King's own curse as long as a mortal 'benefits' from the enchantment, and this explains why the heralds of the court often bear items that make it easier to perform this ritual. Suspicious minds suggest that *in theory* if enough people were subject to this ritual at one time, the Bound King would be permanently freed from his chains.

## Common Elements

Performance of this ritual often involves feeding poisonous or noxious liquid to the target, or binding their wrists or in some cases even going to far as to wrap them in chains while simultaneously crowning them in a symbolic way. The target is often sitting when this enchantment is performed. Some ritualists require that the target verbally confirms that they understand the price for the strength the ritual grants, and agree to seek no vengeance should they die as a consequence of its performance; some [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) ritualists go so far as to demand an oath to that effect.

The ritual often involves the runes [Verys](../lore/Verys.md) and [Kyrop](../lore/Kyrop.md) together; whatever its ultimate source, the enchantment grants strength through weakness, or perhaps demonstrates the idea of offering weakness in one area as a price for strength in another.
