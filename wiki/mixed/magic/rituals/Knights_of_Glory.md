# Knights of Glory

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 120

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 10 minutes of roleplaying. If the ritual is cast using the [Imperial Regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) it requires at least 5 minutes of roleplaying instead. This ritual targets an [Imperial army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). The [general](../../government/military/Military_Council.md) responsible for the army (or the [egregore](../../../out-of-character/crew/Egregores.md) if the general is not at Anvil) must be present throughout.

During the ritual the casters must be in a strong Summer [regio](Regio.md).

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual summons a number of warriors from the [Summer realm](../../../in-character/eternals/Realm.md#summer) to support a [campaign army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). The presence of these supernatural warriors raises the strength of the campaign army by the equivalent of 2,000 additional soldiers. As with any enchantment on an army or navy, the additional army strength granted by this enchantment may be reduced by the [orders](../../../out-of-character/rules/War.md#orders) issued by the [general](../../titles/General.md). While the effect is expressed as being equivalent to additional soldiers, this enchantment never reduces [casualties](../../../out-of-character/rules/Casualties.md#calculating-casualties) suffered by the army.

While the enchantment is in place the general of the army experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *You are confident you can face and overcome any challenge, no matter how daunting. When someone advises caution or restraint, it is difficult not to view that as a challenge and double-down on your glorious course of action.*

The ritual draws warriors loyal to the court of [Eleonaris](../../../in-character/eternals/Eleonaris.md). These heralds have been known to exert unpredictable influence those fighting alongside them, especially those with the [changeling](../lore/Changeling.md) [lineage](../lore/Lineage.md). Past effects have included lingering roleplaying effects or temporary [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) for the captains of [military units](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) [supporting](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#support-army) the enchanted army. Such effects are unpredictable; when they occur they are described in the appropriate Winds of War.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Assurance

The ritual loses all power and becomes useless if the Empire ever finds itself in a position where it is not at war with any neighbouring great power.

### Options

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of orichalcum spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional armies from the same Empire. The general responsible for each army must be present throughout the ritual. Each additional target increases the magnitude by 90.

> OOC Note
>
> The knights that are summoned by *Knights of Glory* will not appear at the event as a result of this ritual being cast. They will only appear in response to plot created by a plot writer. Whatever happens, such knights cannot pass through the [Sentinel Gate](../../../in-character/world/Sentinel_Gate.md), so they can never assist the heroes of [Anvil](../../../in-character/world/Anvil.md) on battles or skirmishes that take place during the summit.

## Description

This ritual draws on a pact with [Eleonaris](../../../in-character/eternals/Eleonaris.md), the [Eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) Queen of the Fields of Glory in the [Summer realm](../../../in-character/eternals/Realm.md#summer), calling one of her innumerable hosts to fight alongside an [army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) for a season. These troops come from Eleonaris' realm and may include elfin knights, rambunctious goblin-folk, and even monstrous beasts. They are usually attired in gold-and-crimson surcotes and fight under the lion banner of their mistress, although some of their heralds will also fly pennants appropriate to the main army if they are asked to do so. They are almost invariably attired in heavy armour, often of burnished bronze, sungold or (in the case of champions) orichalcum-steel. They will die as easily as mortal troops, but they seem to treat death as a minor inconvenience or even a surprise - it is as if they consider themselves immortal. If challenged, they are likely to shrug and comment enigmatically that their slain comrades will "*Return to the Fields of Glory, by and by.*"

The summoned soldiers fight with the appearance of great bravery and civility. They are cordial to mortal soldiers, although they can be a little distant. They engage mercilessly, with great ferocity, and seem to take an unholy joy in challenging and defeating any enemies. They will happily engage in tourneys or honour duels with mortal champions, but they lack a sense of consequences. This means that duels often end in death, or maiming, unless their opponents are very clear on the rules. They are also disinclined to stealth, ambush, or other guerrilla tactics preferring to face their opponents head-on. They love to fight other forces summoned from the realms - they will single out groups of [shadow warriors](Conclave_of_Trees_and_Shadow.md), [crystal guardians](Carve_the_Crystal_Guardian.md), or especially [grim legionnaires](Clarion_Call_of_Ivory_and_Dust.md) unless explicitly ordered otherwise. Even then they chafe under such restrictions.

*[Image: Regal, noble, and terrible in anger; Eleonaris is the Lion of Summer and her soldiers bear many of her traits.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:EleonarisArt.jpg)*

Knights summoned with this ritual are unable to pass through the [Sentinel Gate](../../../in-character/world/Sentinel_Gate.md). As with creatures such as the shadow warriors bound by the [Conclave of Trees and Shadow](Conclave_of_Trees_and_Shadow.md) or the prismatic soldiers raised by [Carve the Crystal Guardian](Carve_the_Crystal_Guardian.md) it seems that the magic of the gate cannot transport them. Attempts to do so result in the knights disappearing - apparently returned to the Summer realm prematurely.

When the [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) ends, the knights travel to the nearest Summer [regio](Regio.md) and depart the mortal realm. They carry with them any of their number who have fallen in battle. The accoutrements of any soldier left behind tend to disappear at around this time - never overtly, but when unobserved the items vanish. The same applies to any remains of dead knights not retrieved by their comrades.

Eleonaris receives a full report from her troops when they return to her, and if they are mistreated, betrayed, or misused in some fashion, she is likely to take vengeance against the perpetrators. On the few occasions in the past when this has happened, she has called a truce with both her perennial rival [Jaheris](../../../in-character/eternals/Jaheris.md) and her more occasional sparring partner [Hayaak](../../../in-character/eternals/Hayaak.md) the gryphon-king and enlisted both of them in seeking revenge on those who have dared to betray her trust. Even her perennial rival [Cathan Canae](../../../in-character/eternals/Cathan_Canae.md) takes a dim view of such behaviour and will side against anyone responsible for mistreating the summoned knights.

## Common Elements

It is exceptionally common to include a flag, pennant, banner or standard as part of this ritual, especially one that bears the device of [Eleonaris](../../../in-character/eternals/Eleonaris.md) - a golden lion on the red field - or the army the summoned knights will fight alongside. Other common elements include the invocation of brave warriors who have fought for the Empire in the past, and symbols of [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) and [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md). [Girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding) the military leader in the [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) manner, or having a scop or [troubadour](../../../in-character/religion/Troubadour.md) boldly announce tales of the general's [name](../lore/Wintermark_hearth_magic.md#names), resonate with the ritual as does the sound of martial horns or drums, and the presence of warriors from the same nation as the general as [witnesses](../../government/synod/Member_of_the_Synod.md#witness).

The rune of victory, [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md), is favoured for this ritual both for its association with banners and flags and for its connection to the Eternal Lady of the Crimson Host.

## History

The Knights of Glory represents very old magic, one of the first rituals codified after the formation of the Empire and entered into [Imperial lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list).

During the Summer Solstice 383YE, Eleonaris [placed restrictions](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Power-_corruption-_and_lies.md#eleonaris) on the performance of Knights of Glory. Her herald delivered a proclamation to the members of the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md), laying down her concerns and ultimately ending with a promise that further castings of the ritual by the Empire may incur serious consequences. Eventually, the angry eternal was mollified by the [construction of a monument](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Statue_of_Eleonaris.md) honouring Eleonaris and her knights in Semmerholm. She made it clear at this time that she would no longer countenance the performance of Knights of Glory by *any* caster and would instead be presenting a new ritual to Brother Luke, in his role as [Archmage of Summer](../../titles/Archmage_of_Summer.md).

The replacement ritual was meant to be created by [the Golden Magician](../../../in-character/eternals/Meraud.md) on Eleonaris' behalf. However, before the Lord of the Lake could undertake the work, the subtle influence of the ongoing conjunction of [the Lock](../lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md) and [the Key](../lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md#the-key) exerted small but significant changes to the ritual. Eleonaris has declared herself to be content with the ritual in its new form. Rather than require the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md) to go through the rigmarole of placing a new ritual into [Imperial lore](../../government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md), she has sent word to the [Archmage](../../titles/Archmage.md) that she is now happy for Imperial magicians to call upon her aid by performing Knights of Glory - provided of course that her [assurance](#assurance) remains intact.

### Eternal Rivals

Some [Enchanters](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Earl.md#enchanter) of [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) say that in the past [Jaheris](../../../in-character/eternals/Jaheris.md), the consort-rival of Eleonaris, offered gifts that allowed the ritual to summon troops loyal to his silver-and-blue Unicorn standard. Similar gifts were apparently offered by [Hayaak](../../../in-character/eternals/Hayaak.md) the Lord of Wrath to summon his hosts of vengeance; by [Cathan Canae](../../../in-character/eternals/Cathan_Canae.md) to call forth her cold-hearted giant warriors; and occasionally by [Barien](../../../in-character/eternals/Barien.md), the arbiter of worth, as a reward for those who have bested one of his [challenges](Challenge_the_Iron_Duke.md) Regardless of what may have once been possible, the *Commander of the Golden Armies* has put a stop to her ritual being used in such a way. Shortly before the Autumn Equinox 383YE, Eleonaris claimed she had "[taken back control](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Power-_corruption-_and_lies.md#eleonaris)" of the ritual. Knights of Glory was crafted with her power, she claimed, and it had only been through her grace that other eternals had been able to make use of it. That grace was now withdrawn, as was her right. The ritual will now only bring forth knights from the Fields of Glory. "*Let other eternals empower their own magical rituals if they wish to aid the Empire*," she concluded.

### Enmity and Neutrality

At the Winter Solstice 379YE, the eternal was given [Enmity](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) by the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md). It was thus a crime to perform this ritual. At the Spring Equinox 380YE Conclave voted to return Eleonaris to the state of [Neutrality](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#neutrality), which, among other things, made this ritual once again legal to perform.

### Relentless Legacy

Knights of Glory is a powerful enchantment. Along with [Clarity of the Master Strategist](Clarity_of_the_Master_Strategist.md) it is one of the most popular rituals used to support the Empire's armies. In 387YE, however, some of the risks associated with reliance on the ritual began to make themselves known. As in the time of [Empress Brannan](../../../in-character/history/Empress_Brannan.md), the regular presence of Eleonaris' glorious warriors alongside Imperial soldiers began to exert unforeseen, subtle influences on the mortal fighters. As described in the *[Even darkness must pass](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/Even_darkness_must_pass.md#the-weight-of-glory)* wind of war, Imperial armies fighting in [Sarangrave](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sarangrave.md) with the aid of the Summer heralds lost the ability to take certain [orders](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army_orders.md). The effect was projected to last until the army in question had spent an *entire year* without experiencing the potent enchantment.

Magicians working with the armies pointed out that this was not something the heralds, or their eternal mistress, were actively *doing*. It was, rather, a side effect of extended exposure to the enchantment, and to the magical warriors it summons, possibly compounded by the terrible aura of fear that hangs over [the Mallum](../../../in-character/barbarians/The_Mallum.md). Eleonaris is not able to "stop doing this" because she and her soldiers are not actually *doing* anything to make this happen.

There are obvious concerns that this situation will spread to other Imperial armies, or intensify to the point that armies reliant on Knights of Glory will become so infused with the [resonance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md#resonance) of Summer that they lose the capacity to undertake any save the most glorious strategies when they fight the Empire's enemies.
