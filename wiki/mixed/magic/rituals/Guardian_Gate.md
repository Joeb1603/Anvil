## Rules

### Day Magnitude 80

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 10 minutes of roleplaying. If the ritual is cast using the [Imperial Regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) it requires at least 5 minutes of roleplaying instead.

This ritual targets a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), and must be performed in a strong Day [regio](Regio.md) in the [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) containing the fortification. If the territory is part of the Empire, then it may instead be performed from the [Imperial regio](Regio.md#the-imperial-regio).

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

Over the coming season, the target [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) becomes a beacon for defenders in a [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md). The garrison and any assigned [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) become focused on identifying threats to the territory and its people, while empowering them with clarity and focus that allows them to swiftly identify hidden threats.

If there is a [spy network](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) operating in that territory, it takes significantly more effort for it to gain useful intelligence - the thresholds for information are increased by two-fifths. Any efforts to infiltrate the territory by a hidden force may be laid bare to the Empire, depending on the situation (may trigger a wind of fortune if such forces are present).

At the next event, the captain of any military unit assigned to defend the fortification will receive a report in their pack detailing the information their soldiers have uncovered. The report contains information about any foreign spy network operating in the territory along with details of any enemy [campaign army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) that undertakes an attacking order in the territory, including their allegiance; an estimation of their strength; information about their special qualities; and possibly the name of generals.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Option

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of weltsilver spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.
