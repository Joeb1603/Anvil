# Champions Shining Resolve

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 16

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains two additional [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero).

The target also experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *You feel an urge to demonstrate your prowess to everyone, and have a sense that you can achieve anything you set your mind to.*

An [Imperial Orc](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) target experiences an additional roleplaying effect: you are more likely to hear your [ancestors](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_religious_beliefs.md#imperial-orcs-ancestors) while under this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 12. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Option

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of green iron spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

## Description

Great deeds - if not necessarily *heroic* deeds - are at the heart of the Summer realm. This ritual functions a little like the [empower](../spells/Empower.md) incantation, but is even more versatile. As with crafted items such as the [Triumphant Blade](../items/Triumphant_Blade.md) or a suit of [Runeplate](../items/Runeplate.md) it is most effective when it is used to [enchant](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) someone who either already possesses a reserve of passion and heroic drive, or who is armed with a [Butcher's Cleaver](../items/Butcher-s_Cleaver.md) or similar item that can be 'powered' by the ritual.

Even without other uses, the ability to steel the will and resist outside influences is sometimes useful in its own right; anyone can benefit from the ability to overcome malign spiritual forces, even if it is only for a short time.

The name of the ritual is the subject of some controversy. Some magicians claim it is *Champion's Shining Resolve*, intending it to be used on one or two glorious and heroic warriors. Other magicians claim it is *Champions' Shining Resolve*, meaning it is most appropriate to use it on multiple warriors so that all can benefit. The [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) and [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) magicians who make a study of such things suggest that one can learn something about one's peers by asking them what they think the ritual is called.

## Common Elements

This ritual often involves anointing the target in some way, often with wine or blood. It may also include a symbolic crowning with green leaves or (especially in [The Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md)) a circlet of woven corn or grass. The names of heroic individuals (especially powerful [ancestors](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_religious_beliefs.md#imperial-orc-ancestors), even for targets who are not themselves [Imperial Orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md)) or particularly inspirational [paragons and exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) are another common element, as are symbols of [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md).

When [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) is used during the ritual, especially by a [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) coven, the ingots are often pressed against and into the weapons and armour of the target, often as part of a formal act of [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding) the target.

Any of the [Summer runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer) might be evoked, but [Feresh](../lore/Feresh.md), the rune of majesty, is the most common. An evocation of [The Stallion](../lore/The_Stallion.md) or [The Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) may be appropriate with this ritual, as may evocations of horses; animals that represent pride or majesty such as eagles or lions; and legendary or heraldic beasts such as [gryphons](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#gryphons), [chimerae](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#chimera) and [unicorns](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#unicorns).
