# Irresistible Stance of Force and Focus

This is a placeholder page for content that PD are actively working on.

*[Image: This ritual is part of Urizen lore rather than Imperial lore .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBUrizen.jpg)*

## Rules

### Day Magnitude 14

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout. The target character must possess the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) can only be performed on a [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and grants them the ability to cast the [repel](../spells/Repel.md) spell four times each day as if they know it without spending any mana.

They also experience a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *you feel a strong desire to respond to people who annoy, threaten, or insult you by hurling them away from your presence using the power granted by this ritual.*

If the target regains all spent personal mana by resting in a magical aura such as that provided by [the Chamber of Delights](Chamber_of_Delights.md), or drinks an [Elixir of Empyrean Art](../potions/Philtres_of_the_High_Peaks.md#the-elixir-of-empyrean-art), they *also* regain all spent uses of this ability at the same time. Other methods of restoring personal mana that are not listed as restoring it all in one go do not restore uses of this ability (for example drinking multiple [Mageblood](../potions/Philtres_of_the_High_Peaks.md#mageblood) or [Philtre of Heavenly Lore](../potions/Philtres_of_the_High_Peaks.md#the-philtre-of-heavenly-lore)).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 11. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Option

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of tempest jade spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.
