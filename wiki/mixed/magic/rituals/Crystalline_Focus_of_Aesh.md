# Crystalline Focus of Aesh

## Rules

*[Image: Spheres of glass and crystal are commonly associated with the power of divination.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Crystal_Balls.jpg)*

### Day Magnitude 20

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout. The target character must possess the [Day Lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) skill.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains one effective rank of the [Day lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) skill for purposes of performing rituals, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).

They gain two effective ranks (total) to the appropriate ritual lore if they are contributing to the performance of any ritual that divines information. The ritual does not need to be part of Day lore, but the normal rules for effective skill still apply.

While the enchantment lasts the target experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *You feel incredibly focused, able to bring all your faculties to bear on a single topic. Distractions become irritating, however, and it can often prove difficult for you to shift your focus to another subject or think about more than one thing at a time.*

If the target is a [merrow](../lore/Merrow.md), the roleplaying effect is especially pronounced. A merrow may also choose to temporarily add to or increase physical trappings of their lineage. These changes are temporary and do not last more than a few hours after the enchantment ends.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 15. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Divination Rituals

The page on [divination](../../../out-of-character/rules/Divination.md) magic lists all the rituals currently in Imperial lore that are considered to be divinations.

### Assurance

This ritual grants an intuitive understanding of magical lores, and as with other such rituals, draws on the power of an [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md), in this case [Kimus](../../../in-character/eternals/Kimus.md). The [assurance](../lore/Eternals_and_rituals.md#assurance-and-stipulation) connected with the ritual was lost for several centuries but was unearthed by members of the [Unfettered Mind](../../government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md) shortly before the Winter Solstice 386YE. The ritual loses all power and ceases to function if either [Eyes of the Sun and Moon](Eyes_of_the_Sun_and_Moon.md) or [Eye of the High Places](Eye_of_the_High_Places.md) are [interdicted](../../government/conclave/Interdiction.md) by the Imperial Conclave.

## Description

This ritual creates a pact between the target and the [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Kimus](../../../in-character/eternals/Kimus.md).

This potent [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) strengthens the recipient's ability to wield [Day magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md).

While enchanted, the magician finds themselves strengthened - they are able to employ [Day magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Day_magic.md) more effectively than normal. It may share a little power with a novice, or allow a master of [Day lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) to wield significant personal power. Those experiencing the enchantment find it easy to concentrate on a single task, applying their entire focus to the act of weaving magical energies. Some magicians using this enchantment speak of a greatly increased awareness of the flows of mana. Others report a form of synaesthesia, the act of performing Day magic being experienced as eerie colours, sounds, smells, and tastes.

The magician gains further prowess when working magic designed to divine information, and the enchantment provides its benefits regardless of the realm involved. While this allows a magician to perform more complex rituals than they might otherwise manage, it also provides them with additional strength to draw on should they need to penetrate a shroud of concealment or deception to gain the information they seek.

This ritual draws on the power of the [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Kimus](../../../in-character/eternals/Kimus.md), and her well-known fascination with observing as many events as possible. According to some theoretical scholars, Kimus is automatically aware of any ritual performance that includes an individual bearing this enchantment - indeed some speculate that the eternal might be able to locate or perceive enchanted magicians more easily. A few paranoid scholars have suggested that the enchantment transforms every magician empowered into a metaphorical "eye" for Kimus - that the eternal can see experience whatever the bearer sees during the entire duration. There is absolutely no evidence to support this supposition, but that does not prevent (sometimes hysterical) conspiracy theories.

Kimus seems to have offered their power to several rituals currently lost to Imperial magicians. Scrolls at [the Lyceum](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#the-lyceum) mention a more potent version of *Crystalline Focus of Aesh* called *Mastery of the Unblinking Eye*. The ritual allows a magician to further increase their ability to wield the lore of Day. According to some fragmentary notes, the ritual either requires or gains additional power from the inclusion of a specially formed sphere of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) which becomes the focus for the magic. Such spheres are also mentioned as being a common form of boon offered by Kimus to empower ritual magicians. The crumbling parchment scrolls apparently formed part of the collection of magical papers donated by the [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [hakima](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Hakima.md) of the line of [Guerra](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Guerra.md) and strongly imply that the founder of that tribe possessed a complete ritual text detailing this ritual.

Two other rituals are referenced in a partial tome held at the Halls of Magic, the journal of a second century [stargazer](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#stargazers) named Mattia of Ankarien. In return for certain significant services performed for Kimus, she received access to something called *The Book of Silver Leaves* - a collection of ritual texts apparently collected in pre-Imperial times by a cabal of diviners and seers in a distant land dedicated to Kimus. The tome references two rituals that Mattia and her cabal apparently mastered, and used to great effect during the reign of [Empress Brannan](../../../in-character/history/Empress_Brannan.md). The first is called *Eternity in an Hour*, which seems to combine elements of the Crystalline Focus with the effect of [All the World in a Grain of Sand](All_the_World_in_a_Grain_of_Sand.md) in that it grants a magician with the ability to focus completely on rote action and familiar magics. The enchantment allows the magician to greatly increasing their ability to perform rituals they have mastered at the expense of seriously curtailing their ability to contribute to any other ritual - even to one that is in Imperial lore. The other is referred to as *Mirror of the Heavens*, and apparently represents an even more profound connection to She Who Watches. While it does not provide much additional focus with magic beyond that granted by *Crystaline Focus*, it apparently greatly increases the ability of the one who bears the enchantment to perform divinations. There are few specifics in the text - which is believed to be a copy of an original tome kept at the Library of Ankarien. If the journal still exists, and should it contain any further information about these two rituals, it is most likely in the possession of the Grendel.

## Common Elements

The ritual usually involves an invocation of the eternal [Kimus](../../../in-character/eternals/Kimus.md), although different covens will phrase this invocation in different ways. As a ritual of the Day realm, and one that draws power from the Unblinking Eye, magicians may paint the symbol of an eye on the target, often on the forehead, as the ritual is performed. A [blood magician](../lore/Blood_magic.md) will often use a little [merrow](../lore/Merrow.md) blood to create this symbol. Spheres of crystal, glass, or [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) provide useful foci for the ritual, as do mirrors of polished silver or [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) - magicians of [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) in particular favour using mirrors for this ritual, drawing on their well known [hearth magic](../lore/The_League_hearth_magic.md#mirrors) property for revealing the truth. Mystics, specially in [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) tend prefer to eschew such showy props, and use a bowl of pure water as the focus, into which they will drop blood, ink, or candle wax and observe the shapes the substance forms on contact with the water.

The runes of [discovery](../lore/Sular.md), [revelation](../lore/Ophis.md), [Wisdom](../lore/Irremais.md), and [thought](../lore/Aesh.md) are all resonant with this ritual and often painted or traced as part of its performance. A [theurge](../lore/Aspect_magic.md#theurgy) might evoke [paragons and exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md), but some such magicians prefer to call on inspirational figures associated with [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md) or [Ambition](../../../in-character/religion/Ambition.md) depending on the ends the additional power will be turned.
