# A Light That Moves

This is a placeholder page for content that PD are actively working on.

## Rules

### Day Magnitude 18

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a [congregation](../../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) creates auras that encourage clear communication and a focus on spiritual and philosophical concerns. The target [congregation](../../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) swells over the next season and is assessed by the civil service to provide an additional 6 doses of [liao](../../religion/Liao.md) and an additional 12 votes in the [Synod](../../government/synod/Synod.md) at the next event, in addition to normal production.

In addition, while the congregation remains enchanted the character who owns it experiences a roleplaying effect: *you feel an urge to communicate as clearly as possible. The more passionately you care about something the stronger the urge to avoid metaphor or circumlocution and speak directly from the heart.*

If the owner of the resource does not attend the next event, then the *additional* production provided by the resource is still added to that character's inventory.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional [congregations](../../../out-of-character/rules/Congregation.md) provided the congregations are all controlled by characters from the same [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects). Each additional congregation increases the magnitude by 14. The character who controls each congregation must be present throughout the ritual.
