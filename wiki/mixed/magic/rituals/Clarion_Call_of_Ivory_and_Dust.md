# Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust

> The assurance on this ritual has been broken, and it can no longer be cast. See the **[Ambassador of death](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Ambassadors_of_death.md#a-silenced-call)** wind of fortune for more details.

## Rules

### Winter Magnitude 20

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual summons a contingent of around a dozen undying soldiers from the grim legion of the [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Kaela](../../../in-character/eternals/Kaela.md) and binds them to fight alongside a band of mortal warriors. The [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) gains a significant bonus to its effectiveness when it takes the [campaigning](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#campaigning) action (generally meaning it is assigned to support an [Army](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md)). This temporary bonus increases the effective fighting force of the unit by 100, the equivalent of 5 normal upgrades.

The character who controls the military unit experiences a short-lived [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) that persists for at least ten minutes: *you are suddenly reminded of every time in the past that you, or someone you care about, has been close to death (or actually died), and receive a hard-to-articulate premonition of your own eventual demise.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event. If the owner of the resource does not attend the next event, then the *additional* production provided by the resource is still added to that character's inventory.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional military units controlled by members of the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional military unit increases the magnitude by 15. The character who controls each unit must be present throughout the ritual.

### Assurance

Given that the magic calls members of the Grim Legion from the Winter realm, it should come as no surprise that the ritual draws on the power of the [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Kaela](../../../in-character/eternals/Kaela.md). The [assurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Eternals_and_rituals.md#assurance-and-stipulation) connected with the ritual was lost for several centuries but was unearthed by members of the [Unfettered Mind](../../government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md) shortly before the Winter Solstice 386YE. The ritual loses all power and ceases to function if the act of joining the Grim Legion is ever interdicted by the Conclave.

*Greta Salpietra de Holberg*, one of the researchers of the order, highlights that a Declaration of [Enmity](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) would not be sufficient to break this assurance - indeed, just as with most other eternals, Kaela has been under such restrictions at various points during the history of the Empire. Laws passed by the [Senate](../../government/senate/Senate.md) would also be insufficient to break the assurance; only a Declaration of [Interdiction](../../government/conclave/Interdiction.md) supported by the Conclave would suffice.

## Description

The grim legion of the [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Kaela](../../../in-character/eternals/Kaela.md) is made up of mortal warriors who have accepted her boon of endless existence. This ritual conjures perhaps a dozen of her troops, often an experienced unit of soldiers who have fought together before - often for centuries. They are armed and usually heavily armoured, and they fight with implacable, hopeless determination. More importantly, perhaps, they cannot be killed. They shrug off wounds that would kill a mortal, quickly recovering from their injuries. It is common to see one of these terrifying, silent soldiers fighting with the broken remains of arrows protruding from chest or belly.

The undying warriors do not socialise with the other soldiers in the [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). They require neither food nor drink, and minimal sleep. They have little conversation, and efforts to strike up friendships are coldly rebuffed. At the end of the season, the grim legionary knows they will be returning to the realm of Winter. By the time they are sent forth again, it is likely that every soldier they fight alongside will have been dead for decades. Furthermore, many of those warriors who took Kaela's gift were already embittered by decades of war and death. They take little joy in life, because transitory pleasures are nothing compared to life's assured continuance.

At the end of the season, or if one of the warriors is injured so badly that they can no longer continue to fight, they simply march away. Nobody has successfully followed the departing warriors, but it is believed they pass through or into a [regio](Regio.md) of Winter and return to the domain of their mistress.

This ritual is not popular in the Empire, but its practical application means that it sees some use. This is especially true since [Winter magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Winter_magic.md), without the aid of an eternal, has little [facility](../../../out-of-character/rules/Winter_magic.md#resonance) for empowering soldiers to fight alongside [armies](../../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md). Few [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) nobles are prepared to fight along the grim legion for long - they are a reminder that ultimately war is a grim business and that there are many for whom glory is fleeting. Likewise, the [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) champions find the undying soldier distasteful - and more than a few priests claim that they are not, cannot be, mortal warriors but rather are eternal spirits in the guise of human warriors. The ritual sees the most use in [The Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), and among the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md), simply because the folk of these nations need little reminder that war is a dark, bloody, muddy, and often ultimately thankless business.

This ritual is also sometimes called *Raise the Tattered Banner*, a reference to the Summer ritual [Raise the Standard of War](Raise_the_Standard_of_War.md) - and to the torn and battered pennants some of the undying warriors fly...

## History

*[Image: The Suns of Couros](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Suns_of_Couros.jpg)*

As with any ritual that draws allies from an eternal realm, Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust is carefully guarded by the one who empowers it. While [Kaela](../../../in-character/eternals/Kaela.md) is generally neutral in her attitude to the Empire, she is still capable of making demands of its citizens. For example, in recent years she has [stipulated](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Out_of_the_realm.md#the-setting-suns-of-couros) that if this ritual is performed by members of the [Suns of Couros](../../../out-of-character/groups/Highguard_groups.md#the-suns-of-couros) [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens), three times as many cadres of her grim legions as are called by the Highborn will assist the enemies of the Empire. It's not clear what led to the respected [magisters](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Magister.md) incurring the ire of the *Lady of the Grim Hosts*, and an attempt to [reach a rapprochement](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Have_you_seen_me_lately.md#kaela) during Spring 385YE withered on the vine. The Suns by all accounts proudly declined to bend their heads to the *Dark-Between-The-Stars*, and Kaela herself has no interest in backing down, and so the simmering enmity between the eternal and the Highborn magicians remains ongoing.

In the lead-up to the Summer Solstice 388YE, Kaela informed the Empire that the [assurance](#assurance) of this ritual have been broken by a recent proscription of the Imperial Synod. The ritual consequently lost all power. More details are discussed in the **[Ambassadors of death](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Ambassadors_of_death.md#a-silenced-call)** wind of fortune.

## Common Elements

This ritual issues a summons to beings from the realm of Winter. They do not appear immediately, but arrive to fight alongside the target [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) within a few days. The ritual usually involves an invocation to [Kaela](../../../in-character/eternals/Kaela.md), who commands the grim legion. Common elements include the naming of dead or legendary warriors, especially any who are known to serve in the grim legion. The trappings of death and war are often intermingled - a [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) captain might be wrapped in a black robe, while any target might wear a shroud or other funeral mantle, over their armour. Other elements may include [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding) the military leader; crowning them symbolically, often with ivory or bone; the sound of martial horns or drums, and the presence of warriors from the same nation as the target as silent witnesses. Like many Winter rituals, music is often brooding and unsettling. In many rituals, the leader of the target legion offers a symbolic payment to Kaela and her warriors, often in the form of spilt wine or blood, or the scattering of coins which are left in the mud where they fall. Tattered or torn banners are often raised, especially those which have seen a great deal of use on a battlefield.

The [Rune of Ending](../lore/Yoorn.md) is often evoked with this ritual. The rune [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md) is *never* used with this ritual; the ritual will apparently fail outright if this rune is visible on the person of any of the ritualists or their target.
