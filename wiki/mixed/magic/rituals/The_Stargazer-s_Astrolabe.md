# The Stargazer's Astrolabe

## Rules

### Night Magnitude 8

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout. When this ritual is performed by a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#coven), the target must be [contributing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#contributors) to the ritual.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains the ability to cast the [detect magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Detect_magic.md) spell four times each day as if they know it without spending any mana.

They also experience a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *you find magic fascinating; it's enjoyable to talk about it and even more enjoyable to observe it being cast or experience it first hand. You also may experience odd sensory effects; any spell that you hear being cast causes you to perceive dramatic displays of colourful light that can be quite distracting. When you are in a location with a magical aura such as a [Chamber of Delights](Chamber_of_Delights.md) or a [regio](Regio.md) you will experience peculiar visions of colours and shapes, as well as peculiar snatches of sound. Coming into contact with a magic item can likewise cause you to experience mild visual, tactile, and auditory hallucinations.*

If the target regains all spent personal mana by resting in a magical aura such as that provided by [the Chamber of Delights](Chamber_of_Delights.md), or drinks an [Elixir of Empyrean Art](../potions/Philtres_of_the_High_Peaks.md#the-elixir-of-empyrean-art), they *also* regain all spent uses of this ability at the same time. Other methods of restoring personal mana do not restore uses of [detect magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Detect_magic.md).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Option

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of tempest jade spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

### Additional Targets

The ritual can target additional characters provided each is also a contributor to the performance of the ritual. Each additional target increases the magnitude of the ritual by 8.
