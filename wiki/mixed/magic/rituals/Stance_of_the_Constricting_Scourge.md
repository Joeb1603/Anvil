# Stance of the Constricting Scourge

## Rules

### Autumn Magnitude 8

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains the ability to call [entangle](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#entangle) once each day following the rules for a [heroic call](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md). The call may be made with any [implement](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#implements) or melee weapon with which they are proficient, with the exception of a [pike](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#pike).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banner). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 6. Additional characters must be present throughout.

## Description

This [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) allows a target to pin an opponent in place for a short time, making them especially vulnerable to capture, or to defeat if they are wielding short weapons. It is sometimes called *The Thief-taker's Net*, and used to stop fleeing fugitives in their tracks. Unlike the ability granted by the [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) spell, the power of the *Constricting Scourge* allows the target to restrain an opponent with a simple strike from a weapon or implement.

## Common Elements

When performing this ritual, a coven often invokes symbols of binding, stagnation and hesitation. Some magicians weave invocations of doubt and uncertainty, while others evoke forces of capture or law enforcement. Skirling pipe-music may often be involved, as might interweaving flutes. Sinuous dances are common, but so are things that bind - chains, threads, ropes, nets and even ribbons.

Other common elements in this ritual might include runes, especially [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md). Beasts such as spiders and great serpents that crush their prey may be mentioned, and some Freeborn use images of octopi, squid or even [kraken](../../../in-character/world/Mundane_beasts.md#kraken) in their magic. The constellation of [The Chain](../lore/The_Chain.md) is sometimes invoked, as are the names of the Autumn Eternals known as the [Lictors](../../../in-character/eternals/Lictors.md).
