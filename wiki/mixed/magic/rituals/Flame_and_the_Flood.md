## Rules

### Night Magnitude 30

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a [forest](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout.

Performing this ritual requires a number of rings of [ilium](../../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) equal to the level the forest will be upgraded to.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual [enchants](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) a forest. It requires a number rings of ilium equal to the level the forest will be upgraded to (one more than the current rank of the forest). When the ritual is complete the ilium is consumed and over the next few weeks the power of the forest is increased - it is permanently [upgraded](../../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md#upgrades) one level.

The enchantment does not prevent a character from upgrading their forest during downtime using the normal rules.

The owner of the forest experiences a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) while their forest is enchanted: *you feel an urge to challenge traditional understanding and hearsay, and to pick apart the "accepted truths" that people take for granted.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.
