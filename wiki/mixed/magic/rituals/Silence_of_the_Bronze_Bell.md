# Silence of the Bronze Bell

*[Image: This ritual is part of Urizen lore rather than Imperial lore .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBUrizen.jpg)*

This is a placeholder page for content that PD are actively working on.

## Rules

### Winter Magnitude 12

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout. The character must possess the [Dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target gains the ability to use [liao](../../religion/Liao.md) to remove an [aura](../lore/Aura.md) on a [person](../lore/Aura.md#personal-aura), [location](../lore/Aura.md#location-aura), or [item](../lore/Aura.md#item-aura) exactly as if they were using the [anointing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md), [consecration](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md), or [hallowing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md) skill. The character can [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with other characters using the appropriate skill or the benefits of this enchantment to help destroy a more powerful aura following all the normal rules.

When removing a durable aura, the enchantment allows the character to spend up to two additional doses of [liao](../../religion/Liao.md) to increase the [strength](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#ceremony-strength) of the ceremony by the same amount.

The ritual paradoxically creates its own [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md); *you become keenly aware of the importance of exercising free will without external influence.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 10. Additional characters must be present throughout.
