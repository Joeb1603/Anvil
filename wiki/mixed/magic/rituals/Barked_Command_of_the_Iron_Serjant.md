# Barked Command of the Iron Serjant

## Rules

### Autumn Magnitude 14

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets up to three characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each character must be present throughout. A character with the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill must also be present throughout (although they are not enchanted by the ritual).

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual targets up to three characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). It requires the participation of a character who is part of that banner who has the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill. The target characters gain the ability to fight with one weapon type chosen from the list: [one-handed spear](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-spears), [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons), [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms), or [pike](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#pike), chosen by the character who has the weapon master skill. Each target receives the ability to use the same weapon.

As long as they are [enchanted](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md), the targets experience a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md); *they feel a desire to stick together, fighting alongside each other and with the character who 'trained' them in their new combat skill.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 4. Additional characters must be present throughout.

## Description

While this [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) does not precisely turn a group of raw recruits into an elite fighting force, it is considered valuable for its ability to grant soldiers the ability to quickly master a weapon favoured by their military unit so they can fight alongside them. It is especially favoured by [Imperial orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) whose legions prefer to fight with a recognisable combination of weapons and armour, and in [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) for allowing everyone the opportunity to take their place in a bill-block or pike-wall.

Some ritualists employ the effect of *Barked Command of the Iron Serjant* on themselves, forming a temporary banner with a skilled combatant and working the performance around their instructions. In [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) where this ritual is often called *Lesson of Shared Excellence* the transfer of skills tends to be accompanied with more careful attention to stance and achieving the right feeling of centred discipline than on barking instructions at magicians as if they were green recruits on a parade ground.

## Common Elements

The focus of this ritual is the character who acts in the role of the *iron serjant*. That person may be one of the ritualists, but is more commonly someone selected for both their martial ability and their capacity to convey their expertise to others. In [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) or a [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) [Free Company](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md) this is as often as not a boisterous drill sergeant or similar character, who runs the targets of the ritual through the use and care of the weapon while correcting their mistakes. The ritualists sometimes join this drill or mark the targets and their weapons with runes or tokens. At the climax, it is common for the targets and the *iron serjant* to share a drink or a toast to their eventual victory.

Oaths, toasts and rousing speeches are also common, as are recitations of songs favoured by soldiers while the weapon drill takes place. The ritual often focuses less on rousing the fighting spirit of the targets and more on encouraging them to fight together and obey orders; invocation of [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md) are especially common, as is the use of the rune [Lann](../lore/Lann.md).
