# Shared Mastery of the Magician's Guild

## Rules

### Autumn Magnitude 4

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout. The target character must possess the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill. and must be a member of the same [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) as the characters performing the ritual.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

A single spell is chosen during the ritual which must be known to at least one character who [contributes](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#contributors) to the performance. The target, who must be a member of the same [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) gains the ability to cast that spell as if they knew it.

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 3. Additional characters must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill and be present throughout; each target receives the ability to cast the same spell.

## Description

This ritual allows a [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) to share their spell casting knowledge with other members of their [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens). It differs from Day rituals such as [Illuminate the Higher Mind](Illuminate_the_Higher_Mind.md) because it builds on the bonds between members of a coven to share skills and knowledge, rather than allowing that knowledge to be accessed by any targets.

While it is less versatile than Illuminate the Higher Mind, it is still useful for allowing a coven to expand their versatility; a common use is to allow members to cast spells such as [mend](../spells/Mend.md) or [repel](../spells/Repel.md) that might be useful in a coming battle. While it is often used by a coven, it is a simple enough ritual that a single scholar of Autumn magic might perform it; even though it draws on the bonds between coven-mates, it does not automatically require the use of the coven bond.

## Common Elements

The ritual usually involves a symbolic transfer of knowledge between the character who knows the chosen spell, and the recipients. This often mimics the master/apprentice or student/teacher relationship, but it may just as often build on the idea of 'sharing guild secrets' or using 'understudies'. Tokens marked with the runes [Queros](../lore/Queros.md) or [Lann](../lore/Lann.md) may change hands. [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) and [Winterfolk](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) ritualists often enact a merging or exchange of blood when they use this ritual, cutting the palm and then clasping hands as the ritual is performed.

A repetition of the coven [oath](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#oaths) is sometimes used with this ritual, especially in the [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [cabals](../lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#cabalists) and between [Imperial orc](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [oathwrights](../lore/Imperial_Orcs_magical_traditions.md#oathwrights).
