# Mark of Agramant

*[Image: This ritual is part of Urizen lore rather than Imperial lore.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBUrizen.jpg)*

> **Interdiction**  
> While [Agramant](../../../in-character/eternals/Agramant.md) is subject to the [enmity](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) of the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md), it is illegal to perform this ritual.

## Rules

### Winter Magnitude 4

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout. The ritual must involve the invocation of Agramant by at least three of his commonly-known names.

*[Image: Those who bear the mark of Agramant cannot be trusted.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MarkofAgramant.png)*

### Effects

This ritual places an enduring magical mark on the target character which can be perceived only with magical detection. The mark created by this ritual takes the form of a skull bleeding from the eye sockets.

A mark of Agramant can be detected by a [detect magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Detect_magic.md) spell cast on the character for that *specific purpose* by a character with the mark of Agramant (a detect magic spell cast for any other purpose does not reveal the mark). If the caster of the detect magic also bears a mark of Agramant, then they discover that their marks are the same; otherwise they discover nothing.

The mark of Agramant is not restricted to any part of the target's body; it cannot be hidden from detection by covering it up.

The mark of Agramant remains; the only two known ways of removing it are the touch of [Agramant](../../../in-character/eternals/Agramant.md) or the use of [true stormlye](Emulsion_of_Lye_and_Lightning.md). It may even persist through death.

### Assurance

This ritual loses all power and stops working if an Imperial [Throne](../../titles/The_Throne.md) bears the Mark of Agramant. While the ritual may lose power, the marks it has created remain as indelible as ever.

*[Image: Those marked by Blood-on-the-Snow rarely find it to be an entirely pleasant experience.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Agramant.jpg)*

## Description

This ritual clearly functions in a similar manner to the Autumn ritual [Arcane Mark](Arcane_Mark.md), and is most likely intentionally developed as a reflection of it. The ritual "*marks*" a person with a symbol which can only be recognised by somebody else bearing the same mark. There are notes in the original text that this was the first ritual that the *Children of the Black Drop* learnt in their attempts to understand the nature of Agramant, and the danger he possessed. By bearing the mark, they were able not only to allay the suspicion of his followers, but use [detect magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Detect_magic.md) to determine if someone had bound themselves to Agramant. While their intentions may have been simply to study Agramant, it is clear from later developments that they quickly succumbed to the temptation to try and use the boons Agramant offered, and then in turn to their own ambitions and cruelties: this was by no means the *last* ritual of Agramant's lore that they mastered.

Some magicians are very concerned that this ritual somehow attracts the attention of the eternal in some fashion. Worse, there are Urizen illuminates who fear the ritual allows Agramant to circumvent the restriction of [enmity](../../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) but *only* for purposes of communicating with the person with the mark. In some fashion it functions as an invocation, inviting Agramant into one's life.

The *Mark of Agramant* [entered Urizen lore](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Shake_it_off.md#bad-lore-and-black-drops) shortly before the Winter Solstice 384YE, as part of a book entitled *Ceremonies of the Black Drop Society*. The attendants of the [Great Library](../../../in-character/eternals/Phaleron.md) were deeply uncomfortable with the rituals in that book and their presence among Urizen lore, more due to their illegal nature than any sentimental concerns about their origin. They were apparently all submitted at the same time as [Treacher's Quill](Treacher-s_Quill.md), but Phaleron itself intervened to delay their appearance in lore while it pondered how best to respond. In the end it decided to do the only reasonable thing; present the rituals to the Urizen as part of their lore as agreed when the lore was established. The Celestial Library does not enjoy causing trouble for its Urizeni allies, but the [Gift of Knowledge](Gift_of_Knowledge.md) is available to every Imperial citizen; it cannot pick and choose who can contribute rituals via the ritual.

## Common Elements

The most common tradition used in performances of this ritual is [goetia](../lore/Aspect_magic.md#goetia) calling on [Agramant](../../../in-character/eternals/Agramant.md) to mark the target, given the requirement to include three of his names in the performance. Ritualists who practice [astronomancy](../lore/Astronomancy.md) will often call on the constellations of [the Three Sisters](../lore/The_Three_Sisters.md), representing ties of blood and consequences, or [the Spider](../lore/The_Spider_and_The_Web.md#the-spider), representing eternals. [Dramaturgy](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) lends itself to [the Prince](../lore/The_Prince.md), with a ritualist taking on that role representing Agramant himself while marking the target physically with [the Ring](../lore/The_Ring.md) specially prepared for that purpose, often in the context of the characters swearing an oath together.
