# Aspect of the Mountain

*[Image: This ritual is part of Urizen lore rather than Imperial lore .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NBUrizen.jpg)*

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 20

### Urizen Lore

This ritual is part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) rather than Imperial lore. Any Urizen character with the appropriate lore can master or [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md#performing-a-ritual) this ritual. A character from another nation who mastered the ritual before it became part of Urizen lore may still perform it, but does so under the [usual rules](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) for performing a ritual learned from a ritual text.

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). The character who controls the target personal [resource](../../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual places an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) on the target [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) that enhances their ability to fight defensively and take advantage of fortified positions. The military unit gains a significant bonus to its effectiveness when it takes a [guarding](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#guarding) action (such as supporting a [fortification](../../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md), or engaging in [mercenary work](../../economy/Venture.md#mercenary-work)). This temporary bonus increases the effective fighting force of the unit by 100, the equivalent of 5 normal upgrades.

The character who controls the military unit experiences a short-lived [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) that persists for at least ten minutes: *you feel an immediate urge to find a location that is significant to you, and to defend it against intruders or trespassers. Your definition of intruders and trespassers may reveal any prejudices you feel against other nations, species, or lineages or may expose a rivalry you feel for another person. You feel a strong urge to challenge such intruders or trespassers, and confronting them feels good and empowering.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event. If the owner of the resource does not attend the next event, then the *additional* production provided by the resource is still added to that character's inventory.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional military units controlled by members of the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each additional military unit increases the magnitude by 15. The character who controls each unit must be present throughout the ritual.

## Description

This ritual was codified at the Summer 383YE by the people of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) under the guidance of the Ioseph of Phoenix Reach, [Doyen of the Spires](../../titles/Doyen_of_the_Spires.md). It was the first ritual ever codified under the guidance of the Doyen of the Spires following the restoration of the Heliopticon network in the wake of the Druj invasion of [Zenith](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md) and [Morrow](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md), and the liberation of [Spiral](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md). It was codified in a single season, primarily due to the additional support provided by refugees from Zenith who had begun new lives in Morrow and Redoubt. In keeping with the unique appointment method of the Doyen of the Spires, the original arcane projection was presented by Ioseph of Phoenix Reach to the magicians of Urizen at Anvil during the Spring Equinox 383YE and judged the magical effect the nation should focus its attention on.

This ritual is closely modelled on the more familiar [Raise the Standard of War](Raise_the_Standard_of_War.md). It empowers and enhances a warband, bolstering strength of will and providing a surge in confidence in their abilities. Unlike the Standard of War however, the magic empowers them to take up a more defensive stand, and provides reservoirs of [supernatural durability](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance). They also gain a new respect for, and greater understanding of, the defensive accoutrements of war – especially shields and heavy armour.

One odd effect of the ritual is that the commander of the warband invariably experiences a powerful urge to find a location and defend it against “trespassers”. This effect is also experienced by the warband – in some cases it is theorised that performing the ritual on soldiers with deep seated prejudices could lead to problems in some circumstances. For example, a Marcher warband already suspicious of briars might find that suspicion enhanced to the point where it promotes undisciplined behaviour.

The usefulness of the ritual in helping to garrison a fortification is clear, but the magic provides valuable benefits to efforts to guard caravans, protect roads, and defend settlements against bandits and monsters. Indeed, the presence of a band of champions empowered by the ritual setting their shields and preparing to face all that comes can discourage sensible assailants without the need for blows to be exchanged - and when the enemy are too foolish to know when to retreat the enchanted warriors make short work of beasts, brigands, and [Varushkan wolves](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md) alike.

## Common Elements

This ritual enhances a small military force, using a connection to its leader to spread magic through the entire unit. It is exceptionally common to include a banner or standard that will be used by the target force as a focus for the ritual. A symbol or banner representing the fortification the commander intends their warband to support might also be included.

Unsurprisingly, from the point of view of an [astronomancer](../lore/Astronomancy.md), [the Mountain](../lore/The_Mountain.md) resonates strongly with this ritual – a fortification is a fine symbol of an obstacle that must be overcome by others or that impedes their progress. The ritual also resonates with the imagery and power of [the Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) which teaches that “things endure”. A [runesmith](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths) will find the [Rune of Strength](../lore/Verys.md) resonates well with this ritual, while despite the name [Theurgists](../lore/Aspect_magic.md#theurgy) might include the invocation of paragons or exemplars associated with defending others – [Inga Tarn](../../../in-character/religion/Inga_Tarn.md) or [Bolstering Bill](../items/Bolstering_Bill.md) are particularly resonant. A [goeticist](../lore/Aspect_magic.md#goetia) may call on the name of Summer eternals such as [Cathan Canae](../../../in-character/eternals/Cathan_Canae.md) and [Adamant](../../../in-character/eternals/Adamant.md), both powers associated with unyielding defence.

A strong element to include would be the girding of the military leader in the style of the people of Dawn; the sound of martial horns or drums; and the presence of warriors from the same nation as the warband or the fortification as witnesses.
