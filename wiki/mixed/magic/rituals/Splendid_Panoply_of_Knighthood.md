# Splendid Panoply of Knighthood

*[Image: This enchantment can quickly empower an entire band of warriors.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Splendid_knights.png)*

## Rules

### Summer Magnitude 14

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets up to five characters. Each character must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

This ritual targets up to five characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Each target gains one additional rank of [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance), and experiences the [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md): *you have a feeling of personal prowess and self confidence.*

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners). Every additional character increases the magnitude by 2. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Options

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 ingots of orichalcum spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

## Description

This [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) is one of the best-known Summer rituals and is used to bolster bands of warriors across the Empire. When dealing with small groups, [Strength of the Bull](Strength_of_the_Bull.md) is a little more efficient, but *Splendid Panoply of Knighthood* is more effective at enchanting a band of soldiers. Indeed, the more soldiers that are part of a single enchantment, the more efficient it becomes. The rituals are otherwise quite similar, although the fact that the *Splendid Panoply* is invariably used on a band rather than an individual means that its performances are usually a much larger-scale affair. It is often performed shortly before battle, often within an hour or so of dawn.

The ability to use [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) to provide power to the ritual - something which is not possible with Strength of the Bull - also makes it appealing to magicians whose access to crystal mana is more restricted than their access to raw orichalcum.

## Common Elements

As with many [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) rituals, common elements include the consumption of wholesome food and drink (especially mead or beer) and the use of tokens and gifts such as [favours](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#favours). Oaths, toasts and rousing speeches are also common, as are choruses of songs favoured by soldiers. The ritual often focuses on rousing the fighting spirit of the targets, and the invocation of [Paragons and Exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) and [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md) are especially common.

Any of the [Summer runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer) might be evoked, depending entirely on the nature of the warriors and the ritualists. Runes are often painted onto the skin, especially the face and arms. Navarr [vates](../lore/Navarr_magical_traditions.md#vates) and [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [runesmiths](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths) alike will trace the scars, brands and tattoos of their targets as they perform the ritual, connecting the strength gained today with oaths or wounds from the past. An evocation of [The Stallion](../lore/The_Stallion.md) or [The Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) may be appropriate with this ritual, as are evocations of horses, bulls, narwhal, [unicorns](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#unicorns) and [dragons](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#dragons-wyverns-and-wyrms).

The [Captain's Mask](../items/Captain-s_Mask.md) is a [ritual focus](../../../indexes/by-category/Foci.md) that helps empower the performance of this ritual.
