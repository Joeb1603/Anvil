# Chirurgeon's Healing Touch

## Rules

### Spring Magnitude 8

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a character, who must be present throughout.

This ritual is an [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). A target may only be under one enchantment effect at a time.

### Effects

The target can use [stay with me](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#stay-with-me) twice a day as if they know the skill without spending any [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points).

The effect lasts until the start of the next Profound Decisions Empire event.

### Additional Targets

This ritual can affect additional characters from the same [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banner). Each additional character increases the magnitude by 6. Additional characters must be present throughout.

### Options

Any caster who has mastered the ritual may choose to substitute [true vervain](../../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) for crystal mana when contributing to it. Every 2 drams of true vervain spent counts as 1 crystal mana when contributing to the ritual.

## Description

Sometimes a [chirurgeon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#chirurgeon) finds that time is against them, and that life hangs in the balance. Sometimes a warrior needs to get a comrade to safety *now*, before the enemy can kill them both. The *Chirurgeon's Healing Touch* ritual gives immediate options to the target, allowing them to quickly get an ally on their feet and moving under their own steam.

To use the healing power provided by this ritual, the recipient usually breathes deeply onto their cupped hands, or rubs them swiftly together to make them warm, then places them on the forehead of their injured companion. Warmth and vitality then flows from the lungs and heart, through the hands, into the patient and jolts them back to life.

## Common Elements

As with many healing rituals, blood and water are common elements, but so is the concept of breath and breathing. Washing the hands with water or wine is common, as is the inhalation of steam especially steam from a bowl where healing herbs are brewed. The [true vervain](../../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) herb is especially valuable as a symbol for this ritual, and the pungent juice of crushed leaves is often used as a way to boost the power of the ritual - while it is rare to see a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) use large amounts of true vervain (it is rarely effective to consume the healing power of the herb to grant healing powers), it is sometimes used to make the ritual more accessible to smaller groups.

Invocations using the names of great healers, legendary beasts such as the [firebird](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#firebird), and the constellation of [The Fountain](../lore/The_Fountain.md) are all popular images to use with this ritual. Runes such as [Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md) may be used, but the healing might also be evoked through the [rune of thought](../lore/Aesh.md) or [the rune of majesty](../lore/Feresh.md).
