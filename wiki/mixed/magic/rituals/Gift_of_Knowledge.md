# Gift of Knowledge

## Rules

### Day Magnitude 4

### Performing the Ritual

Performing this ritual takes at least 2 minutes of roleplaying. This ritual targets a single opaque container in which are placed a book, pamphlet, scroll, or similar written work, and five ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) that must be present throughout.

### Effects

When the ritual is complete, the targeted written work and the weltsilver disappear and are received by the attendants of the [Great Library](../../../in-character/eternals/Phaleron.md) in the realm of Day, who then use the weltsilver to make a copy of the written work.

*[Image: DayVis.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:DayVis.jpg)*

Once the copying is complete - even the longest tome will take only an hour - the book will be returned to the container along with a single vial of [prismatic ink](../../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#prismatic-ink) in thanks for the gift of knowledge provided. The exception is that a book containing a [ritual text](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) will *not* be returned, or will be returned with the pages detailing the ritual excised. Those rituals will then become part of [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore).

If the book is considered particularly fine - especially if it contains new verifiable information that either is not already included in the library, or that confirms such information - the eternal may dispatch an additional token of its appreciation.

### Assurance

This ritual has a guaranteed [response](../../../out-of-character/rules/Eternals_and_rituals.md#communication-and-gifts) from an eternal, in this case [Phaleron](../../../in-character/eternals/Phaleron.md), and has an [assurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Eternals_and_rituals.md#assurance-and-stipulation) attached. It was at first believed that there was no assurance connected to this ritual, but a core requirement was unearthed by members of the [Unfettered Mind](../../government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md) shortly before the Winter Solstice 386YE. The ritual loses all power and ceases to function if support for Urizen lore ever ends or if the nation of Urizen were to leave the Empire.

## Description

This ritual was received as a gift from the [eternal](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Phaleron](../../../in-character/eternals/Phaleron.md), also known as the Celestial Library. It was [proposed for inclusion](../../../in-character/conclave-sessions/383YE_Winter_Solstice_Conclave_sessions.md#imperial-lore-gift-of-knowledge) in [Imperial lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list) during the Winter Solstice 383YE by the [Archmage of Day](../../titles/Archmage_of_Day.md), **Skywise Gralka** (following a [failed proposal](../../../in-character/conclave-sessions/382YE_Winter_Solstice_Conclave_sessions.md#imperial-lore-gift-of-knowledge) a year earlier).

Prior to the creation of *Gift of Knowledge*, [magicians](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magicians) were able to send books and scrolls to the Celestial Library using a [very different ritual](Celestial_Library.md). Following from the [events of Autumn 382YE](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_way_that_the_sunshine_gleams.md#a-new-archives) (after an apparent "attack" on the Library using the earlier ritual was discovered), [Phaleron](../../../in-character/eternals/Phaleron.md) withdrew support for that ritual. Instead, it provided *Gift of Knowledge* to then-Archmage **Gancius della Notte di Sarvos** in its place. Phaleron now seals books sent by the former ritual away, and has them destroyed, while offering appropriate payment for tomes delivered with the correct ritual.

There is some concern about this ritual's inclusion in Imperial lore. During the Spring Equinox 383YE, in the lead up to a [parley](../../titles/Archmage.md#parley) between Imperial magicians and the magicians of Urizen, Phaleron specifically requested that this ritual [not enter Imperial lore](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Who_benefits.md#phaleron). The parley included only magicians from [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), and the Archmage of Day, and during the meeting the foundations of what would later become [Urizen lore](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) were laid, culminating in the [enfolding](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Something_must_break.md#the-grand-library-of-canterspire) of the [Grand Library of Canterspire](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md#grand-library-of-canterspire). The ritual has a particular significance for this unique magical technique, allowing texts to be submitted to the Library that are then available only to Urizen magicians. As of the Winter Solstice 383YE, it remains to be seen how the eternal responds to the ritual entering the main body of Imperial lore.

## Common Elements

As with the earlier version of this ritual, the most important element of the magic is the written work to be transferred. It is often wrapped in fine silk or cotton and usually forms the focus of the ritual; sometimes it is passed from hand to hand before being placed in the container, sometimes it is placed prominently in the centre of the ritual. Invocations of great scholars, [paragons and exemplars](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md), and the rune [Aesh](../lore/Aesh.md) often accompany the ritual, as do [Sular](../lore/Sular.md) and [Ophis](../lore/Ophis.md).

Likewise, the opaque container can be an important focus for the magic; some magicians or covens might have a specially prepared box or chest that is specifically designed for use with this ritual, decorated with runes or [astronomantic](../lore/Astronomancy.md) constellations, and often inlaid with [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver).

Some ritualists wrap the book in a chain and seal it with a lock during the ritual - the constellation of [the Lock](../lore/The_Key_and_The_Lock.md) is unsurprisingly a common element of such rituals. Other ritualists seal the item with a wax seal, often marked with the [Rune of Discovery](../lore/Sular.md) or [Rune of Revelation](../lore/Ophis.md), as appropriate to the nature of the work.

The completion of the ritual is often signalled by sprinkling seawater on the container.
