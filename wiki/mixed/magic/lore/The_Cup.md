# The Cup

The cup is one of the [instruments](Dramaturgy.md#the-instruments) or [dramaturgy](Dramaturgy.md). As such it often represents the method used in a magical ritual. A troupe will often have a specific prop that they use for the cup whenever it appears, and may in time come to treat the inanimate object a little like a member of the cast.

**Appearance:** A troupe's chalice may be represented by anything from a gem-studded goblet made of precious metals to a simple wooden mazer; the significance is that it holds liquid. It is also commonly presented as a [potion](../../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md), or as a magical liquid that causes a change.

**Meanings:** On its own, it represents prosperity and fulfilment. It can also represent transformation and change, especially of the self. As it is passed between the Personae however, its meaning changes.

When held by [The Captain](The_Captain.md), the cup represents celebration and triumph; in the hands of [The Prince](The_Prince.md), it represents prosperity on a national or Imperial scale. When held by [The Bishop](The_Bishop.md) it represents purity and cleansing, especially in rituals such as [Ascetic Star of Atun](../rituals/Ascetic_Star_of_Atun.md). In the hands of [The Mountebank](The_Mountebank.md) it signifies unleashed emotions, confusion and drunkenness. For [The Doctor](The_Doctor.md) it represents assistance or aid - [enchantments](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) may be created by the doctor, or it might be a bitter medicine that inflicts a curse. Finally, in the hands of [The Witch](The_Witch.md) it represents magical potions, and the transformations they can bring or wine that encourages others to speak the truth.
