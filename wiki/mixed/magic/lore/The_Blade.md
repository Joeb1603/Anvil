# The Blade

*[Image: "I hate you.... you brazen hussy."](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:TheBlade.jpg)*

The blade is one of the [instruments](Dramaturgy.md#the-instruments) or [dramaturgy](Dramaturgy.md). As such it often represents the method used in a magical ritual. A troupe will often have a specific prop that they use for the blade whenever it appears, and may in time come to treat the inanimate object a little like a member of the cast.

**Appearance:** A real weapon or a painted wooden prop, the blade represents conflict, war and suffering. Some very theatrical troupes construct hinged or spring-loaded props which can transform rapidly from knife to sword and back again as it is handed back and forth. Some especially self-satisfied troupes have taken to replacing the blade with a letter, pamphlet or quill pen. This is more common in [Sarvos](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) than anywhere else and is sometimes called *The Sarvosan Conceit* when it is observed by troupes from other cities.

**Meanings:** The blade deals with conflict, war, suffering and combat but it also deals with healing and removal of obstacles. It has an element of sacrifice in it, and is a key to rituals such as [Sorin's Rite of Agony](../rituals/Sorin-s_Rite_of_Agony.md) that specifically involve a sacrifice,

When carried by [The Captain](The_Captain.md), it represents individual combat, and powers that enhance combat skills such as [The Swan's Cruel Wing](../rituals/Swan-s_Cruel_Wing.md). With [The Prince](The_Prince.md) it represents armies and nations at war, and so has a role in rituals such as [Clarity of the Master Strategist](../rituals/Clarity_of_the_Master_Strategist.md). With [The Bishop](The_Bishop.md), it is often used to represent truth or righteousness, but also things that deal with alliances - [Ambassadorial Gatekeeper](../rituals/Ambassadorial_Gatekeeper.md) for example, especially when performed on members of a [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners) with a strong connection to the Way. In the hands of a Mountebank it is military subterfuge, hidden knives and the like and may be used in a ritual such as [Shroud of Mist and Shadow](../rituals/Shroud_of_Mist_and_Shadow.md). In hands of [The Doctor](The_Doctor.md) it becomes the Scalpel and is associated with the process of cutting away something bad to allow necessary and painful healing; it might be used with [Black Iron Blade](../rituals/Black_Iron_Blade.md), [Renewed Strength of the New Day](../rituals/Renewed_Strength_of_the_New_Day.md) or [Vitality of Rushing Water](../rituals/Vitality_of_Rushing_Water.md) with equal facility. When carried by [The Witch](The_Witch.md) it is called the Aetheme, and signifies magical authority and mastery over foreign spirits - such as those evoked with [Clarion Call of Ivory and Dust](../rituals/Clarion_Call_of_Ivory_and_Dust.md) or [Conclave of Trees and Shadow](../rituals/Conclave_of_Trees_and_Shadow.md).
