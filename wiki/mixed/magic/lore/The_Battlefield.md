# The Battlefield

The battlefield is a [throne](Dramaturgy.md#the-thrones), a location used in [dramaturgy](Dramaturgy.md) that represents both a setting and a realm of magic.

**Realm:** Summer.

**Setting:** The battlefield is most commonly presented as either a military camp, or as a castle or fortification. Themes of war, conflict, strength and the threat of death are all strengthened on the battlefield. The story might involve preparations for a battle, the arming of a mighty warrior or the fight itself (often a duel) and its aftermath.

**Characters:** [The Captain](The_Captain.md) and [The Bishop](The_Bishop.md) are strong characters in this setting; they are often larger-than-life, taking on iconic roles as figureheads or representations of patriotic, political or religious themes. The Captain leads, and the Bishop inspires; it is here that they are at their most majestic.

**Magical Style:** This is often a location where someone is strengthened, or commits themselves to action.

Rituals that create [enchantments](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) of strength, [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance) or that grant martial abilities such as [Hammer of Thunder](../rituals/Hammer_of_Thunder.md) or [Alignment of Mind and Blade](../rituals/Alignment_of_Mind_and_Blade.md) are made stronger here. Any ritual that targets a [military unit](../../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md), fortification, campaign army or [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners) may be performed in this setting.
