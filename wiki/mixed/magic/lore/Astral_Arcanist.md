# Astral Arcanist

*[Image: Serenus Starsong , first and last Astral Arcanist](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Serenus_Starsong.jpg)*

## Overview

The Astral Arcanist is an [Imperial title](../../titles/Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md). The [motion](../../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-title-of-astral-arcanist) to create the title was brought by **Thalia of Netherwatch**, as Senator for [Redoubt](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Redoubt.md), to the Imperial Senate during the Summer Solstice 386YE. The Astral Arcanist is a position in the Conclave with an expectation to support the magicians of the Empire with information about [constellations](Astronomancy.md#constellations) and tulpas.

The title was [abrogated](../../../in-character/senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#abrogate-astral-arcanist) by the Senate during the Spring Equinox 388YE. This decision followed the apparent death of the Arcanist themselves, **Serenus Starsong**, and the [formal request](../../../in-character/conclave-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Conclave_sessions.md#concord-abrogate-astral-arcanist) of the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md) to have the title abrogated. There is speculation that this may in part have been motivated by the [developing crisis](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/What_regulates_the_wheel.md) relating to the [Imperial regio](../rituals/Regio.md#the-imperial-regio).

*[Image: RelativePositionslores.JPG](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:RelativePositionslores.JPG)*

## Responsibilities

The Astral Arcanist had a responsibility to disperse knowledge about the constellations to Imperial magicians. They are also expected to guide and advise [astronomancers](Astronomancy.md) through the Astronomancy Symposium. The Astral Arcanist did not have the legal power to prevent any interaction between citizens and constellations or tulpas.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Astral Arcanist was made by the [declaration of candidacy](../../government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy) as part of a [Conclave session](../../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). Any Imperial citizen could hold the title.

The Astral Arcanist served until they were replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, they could not be [revoked](../../government/synod/Revocation.md).
