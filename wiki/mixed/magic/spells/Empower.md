# Empower

## Description

This spell allows a magician to support heroic soldiers on the battlefield, helping them to use their martial skills at key moments. It is equally effective when used to grant strength to a neophyte warrior as to a veteran [hero](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero). Anyone who can wield a weapon can benefit from the magical enhancement that empower provides.

It is often associated with the [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md); there are many stories of [enchanters](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Earl.md#enchanter) using it to direct their fellow nobles. It is also popular with the [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) both for supporting their [sentinels](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sentinel.md), and for directing a warrior to unleash a specific valuable attack at a pivotal moment in an engagement.

Some magicians view this spell as strengthening or inspiring their target. More cynical or arrogant magicians think of it in terms of using a warrior as a living weapon to achieve their aims on the battlefield. Magical scholars point to it as being similar to a short-lived [enchantment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) - they draw parallels with magical rituals such as [Swan's Cruel Wing](../rituals/Swan-s_Cruel_Wing.md) or [Horizon's Razor Edge](../rituals/Horizon-s_Razor_Edge.md). The magical energy provided by the spell is very short-lived, however, and does not interfere with a magical enchantment - meaning it can even be used to empower warriors who are already benefiting from more powerful magic such as [Barked Command of the Iron Serjant](../rituals/Barked_Command_of_the_Iron_Serjant.md).

Several [magic items](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) exist that allow a magician to make more use of the empower incantation, such as the [Roaring Chimera Rod](../items/Roaring_Chimera_Rod.md) and [Glamourweave Robe](../items/Glamourweave_Robe.md) which allow a magician to cast the spell many more times each day. The potent ritual [Glorious Crown of Enchantment](../rituals/Glorious_Crown_of_Enchantment.md) likewise increases the number of times the spell can be cast, with the additional advantage that its power can be replenished with the correct [potion](../../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md) or access to a chamber enchanted to restore reserves of magical power. There is also a ritual - [Stag Knight's Glory](../rituals/Stag_Knight-s_Glory.md) - that creates a very similar effect, potentially on a large group of combatants.

## Casting

- **Type:** [Offensive spell](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#offensive-spells)
- **Cost:** 1 Personal mana

## Effects

You may allow the target to make a single heroic call based on the weapon that they are using within 10 seconds. They may call [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown) if they are wielding a [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms), [IMPALE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) if they are wielding a [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons) or [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) if they are wielding a [one handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons), or a [one handed spear](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-spear). The target *cannot* use an [implement](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#implements) to deliver an empowered blow.

## Common Elements

- **Rune Magic:** Any of the [Gryphon Runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer) will serve caster and target well when invoked, though some prefer to invoke [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md) or [Cavul](../lore/Cavul.md) to lend expertise - granting the target "dominion" over their weapon or gifting them with "purity" to strike truly, rather than simply granting them strength. [Mawrig](../lore/Mawrig.md) may be invoked, particularly when the subject will use their new strength to cripple an enemy with a single blow.
- **Astronomancy:** [The Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md) is by far the most common of the constellations called upon when casting this spell, though as with invoking [Cavul](../lore/Cavul.md), [The Stork](../lore/The_Stork.md) may also be used to imbue the subject with the skill needed to make a decisive strike. [The Claw](../lore/The_Claw.md) can be called upon, particularly when the subject will go on to [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) an enemy with their new strength, or more rarely, [The Mountain](../lore/The_Mountain.md) may be used to lend monstrous strength to a [polearm's](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms) [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown).
- **Dramaturgy:** [The Captain](../lore/The_Captain.md) is strongly associated with this spell, both for the caster - one who gives strength to others - and for the subject, who strikes truly and Virtuously. [The Bishop](../lore/The_Bishop.md) may also be invoked, lending strength to another.
- **Blood Magic:** A blood magician who is also a warrior may use their own blood, but more often the magician makes a small cut on the arm or shoulder of the target - enough to spill a little blood. Depending on the situation, the warrior may already be bleeding - and tracing a quick sigil in blood a warrior has already spilled while fighting has a strong resonance with this spell.
- **Other traditions:** A [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) witch might evoke the spirit of the mythical [unicorn](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#unicorn) or the martial prowess of the [chimera](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#chimera) when casting this spell. A theurgist might invoke the name of a warrior-paragon such as [Adelmar the Lion](../../../in-character/religion/Adelmar_the_Lion.md), or the name of an inspirational figure known for wielding a specific weapon (for example, [Inga Tarn](../../../in-character/religion/Inga_Tarn.md) might be evoked to aid someone using a two-handed weapon). A goeticist might call on [Zakalwe](../../../in-character/eternals/Zakalwe.md), [Janon](../../../in-character/eternals/Janon.md), [Estavus](../../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md), or almost any Summer eternal.
- **Realms:** This spell is strongly associated with the lore of the Summer realm. Some magicians - especially those who see themselves as directing or commanding the warriors around them - see a resonance with Autumn lore
