# Weakness

## Description

This spell is a potent malediction that can neutralise the most powerful enemy champion or render an [archmage](../../titles/Archmage.md) as powerless as a raw recruit. The debilitation imposed by this spell is more than mere lack of strength - it is a potentially lethal curse. The victim cannot concentrate on even the simplest [spell](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md), cannot inspire themselves to [heroic action](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md) and cannot use the powers of any [magic items](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) they are bonded to.

It is particularly insidious in that it does not go away naturally; it can be removed with [herbs](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md) by a skilled [physick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick), or purged by a magician using the [purify](Purify.md) spell or a [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) such as [Renewed Strength of the New Day](../rituals/Renewed_Strength_of_the_New_Day.md). Otherwise, it lasts indefinitely.

## Casting

- **Type:** [Offensive spell](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#offensive-spells)
- **Cost:** 1 Personal mana

## Effects

You may call [WEAKNESS](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#weakness) against the target struck by your [implement](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#implements).

WEAKNESS prevents the target from making any [calls](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md) for any reason. In addition, they cannot use any [heroic skills](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md), [religious skills](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md), cast any spells or contribute to any rituals. Only skills, [magic items](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md), or [enchantments](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) that are "always on" will work (such as those that give you extra hits, wield a polearm, or fire a crossbow). They may otherwise move and fight normally.

You must assume that the target has taken the effect.

The effect lasts until cured by the [purify](Purify.md) spell, or a similar potion or magic.

## Common Elements

- **Rune Magic:** The rune of Weakness, [Kyrop](../lore/Kyrop.md) is unsurprisingly invoked in conjunction with this invocation.
- **Astronomancy:** The [Drowned Shepherd](../lore/The_Drowned_Shepherd.md), the astronomantic constellation associated with endings and with curses, is commonly invoked with the weakness spell.
- **Dramaturgy:** A [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) witch might echo the terrifying roar of the [chimera](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#chimera), or the dire spirit of the [manticore](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#sphinxes-and-manticores) when casting this spell.
- **Other traditions:** This spell has a strong resonance for the realm of Winter.
