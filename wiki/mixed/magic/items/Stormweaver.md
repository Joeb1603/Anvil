# Stormweaver

## Description

These powerful weapons are favoured by battle magicians across the Empire. Whether being used by a [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [Magister](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Magister.md) to smash through a defensive battle line alongside the [cataphracts](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md), or hurling a chosen target or employed by a [thresher](../lore/The_Marches_magical_traditions.md#threshers) to separate an enemy sorcerer from her allies, a Stormweaver is a clear sign of a magician who appreciates the potent control offered by the [repel](../spells/Repel.md) spell.

The only drawback to a Stormweaver - and it is very slight - is that the rod seems to attract storms. On land this generally leads to slightly heavier rainfall, or the occasional unpleasant gale. At sea, it can be life-threatening. Very few ship's captains will allow a Stormweaver aboard, especially on a ship sailing out of sight of land. The exception are the most adventurous [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [corsairs](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Corsair.md) or [Steinr](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Steinr.md) merchant-traders, who often welcome the opportunity for excitement an unexpected, magically enhanced, storm can offer.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Three times per day you can cast the [repel](../spells/Repel.md) spell as if you knew it and without expending any mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Stormweaver requires eight ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), seven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). It takes one month to make one of these items.
