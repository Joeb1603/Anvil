# Lowther's Gaze

## Description

Lowther's Gaze is a talisman, similar to an [Alder's Edge](Alder-s_Edge.md), which allows the wearer to use a bow or crossbow with consummate skill. The item takes the form of a brooch or bracelet of smooth [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), reinforced with green iron and polished with [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). The jewelled item should usually be marked with two eyes, most likely made from two faceted pieces of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade).

The talisman will often bear the image of a keen-eyed hero, or a beast known for its excellent vision, especially a bird. The rune [Feresh](../lore/Feresh.md) is often inscribed on the rear of the item, capturing both the perceptive abilities of the hawk and the concept of majestic prowess with arrows and bolts (both of which are of course feathered).

The [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) for the talisman was developed by *Gemma Brightsmith*, the [Seer of the Spiral Sword](../../titles/Seer_of_the_Spiral_Sword.md) using the [runeforge](../../commissions/Runeforge.md) of the [same name](../../titles/Seer_of_the_Spiral_Sword.md#the-spiral-sword) in [Redoubt](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Redoubt.md). It was shared with the [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisans) of the Empire following the Spring Equinox 384YE [by order](../../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#disseminate-lowther27s-gage) of the Imperial Senate.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a piece of [jewellery](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain the [marksman](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#marksman) skill.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Lowther's Gaze requires twelve measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), nine ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), six measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), and three ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron). It takes one month to make one of these items.
