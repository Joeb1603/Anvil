# Scrivener's Guard

*[Image: Cambion Corsair Chic.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Cambion_Corsair_Chic.jpg)*

## Description

This leather cuirass is favoured by [Winterfolk](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) senators, diplomats, negotiators and merchants who go to dangerous places in the pursuit of their duties. It is also popular with [banner-bearers](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#banner-bearers) and [scops](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_culture_and_customs.md#scop). In these professions, many lack the boisterous spirit found among those who thrive on the battlefield, so they often seek out inspiring weapons to accompany the armour such as a [Triumphant Blade](Triumphant_Blade.md).

In [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) each piece of leather has a strand of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) thread running through in an attempt to achieve the greatest possible shine when exposed [light](../lore/Urizen_hearth_magic.md#light), while an [Orcish](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) smith might gather portions of leather from her hardiest comrades before combining them to produce a patchwork armour of great [Worth](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Worth.md) drawing on the resilience of all the past owners. In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and most other lands, tiny runes are engraved into the leather, usually [Evrom](../lore/Evrom.md), the rune of beginnings, to give the wearer a fresh start or occasionally [Naeve](../lore/Naeve.md), the rune of hunger, to drive the wearer through hardship and on to greater things.

The Freeborn Emperor Ahraz is known to have worn a set of Scrivener's Guard during most of his career, including his negotiations with the rebellious orc slaves. When queried about why he did not don a more powerful suit of armour he is said to have replied:

> *In-character text:*
>
> "I am a diplomat, not a warrior. My armour is more to stop me being a burden on my bodyguards than to aid me in battle. If a fight breaks out, I will do my best but I am no soldier and no amount of armour will make me one. Better I save my money and spend it somewhere it will do good - on weapons and armour for those to whom I entrust my life."

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [medium armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#medium-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) to use the [unstoppable](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#unstoppable) skill as if you know it.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Scrivener's Guard requires seven ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), and five measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
