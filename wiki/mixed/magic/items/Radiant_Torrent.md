# Radiant Torrent

## Description

These solid rods are often polished to a mirror-smooth sheen. They allow a battlefield healer to restore health to their allies with a few powerful words. In the right circumstances are capable of preventing a bloody engagement turning into a rout.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Three times per day you can [swift cast](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#swift-casting) the [heal](../spells/Heal.md) spell as if you knew it without spending any mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Radiant Torrent requires fifteen ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), twelve measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and four ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
