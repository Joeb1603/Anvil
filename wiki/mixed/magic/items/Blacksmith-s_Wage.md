# Blacksmith's Wage

## Description

*[Image: In a Marcher bill block, the ability to quickly mend your weapon can mean the difference between life and death not just for yourself but for your comrades in arms.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Field_of_Arms.jpg)*

The haft of this useful weapon is polished with ambergelt, and coloured with iridescent gloaming. Often it is reinforced with rings of orichalcum alloy, that occasionally seem oddly warm to the touch. The weapon has no particular puissance in combat, but "heals" itself when it is damaged. No matter how old, the weapon tends to look much as it did when it was first forged. Casual wear and tear from use vanishes overnight, and if the weapon sustains more catastrophic damage - if it is broken apart by a magician or rendered into pieces by a savage [ogre](../../../in-character/world/Ogre.md) - it can restore itself completely provided the wielder *wants* it enough. The ability of the weapon to repair itself gives it its common name, as it *takes a blacksmith's wage*, because a warrior need not buy a new weapon when this one is damaged, nor pay anyone else to repair it for them.

Some artisans craft the head of a Blacksmith's Wage to resemble the rune [Pallas](../lore/Pallas.md), but more often the rune is simply marked on the haft near the base. Likewise, the rune [Xun](../lore/Xun.md) is sometimes used to form the head of a pole-axe, or inscribed on the metal, and traced when the magic inherent in the weapon is evoked.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may repair this polearm by expending a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) and spending at least five seconds of uninterrupted [appropriate roleplaying](../../../out-of-character/rules/Appropriate_roleplaying.md) involving handling the weapon. This ability fails if you are hit or attack another character, or if the weapon is hit or used to make an attack.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Blacksmith's Wage requires nine ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and six ingots of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
