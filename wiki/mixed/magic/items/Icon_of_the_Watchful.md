# Icon of the Watchful

## Description

An Icon of the Watchful allows a priest to perform [ceremonies](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md) as if they were dedicated to the virtue of [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md). It generally takes the form of a piece of religious art, usually depicting a paragon or exemplar of Wisdom such as [Vardas](../../../in-character/religion/Vardas.md) or [Berechiah](../../../in-character/religion/Berechiah.md). The icon is not always a portrait, however - in [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) it often takes the form of a statuette of a goose, and is known as a *Vigilant Goose* - or occasionally a Vigilant Swan; when a visitor queries the name, pointing out the statue is obviously a goose, they are congratulated on their keen perception. In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) it is sometimes crafted in the form of a medallion or short rod decorated with the bones and feathers of the same bird, generally decorated with the [Rune of Revelation](../lore/Ophis.md), which is known as an *Icon of the Goose* or a *Bright Icon*. Other less common variants are certainly possible - from the colourful illuminated scrolls recounting the lives of the exemplars popular in [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) to the delicate green crystal spindles wrapped in [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) the [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) call an *Emerald Beacon*.

The icon is equally valuable to a priest who is dedicated to one of the other six virtues as it is to a priest who is dedicated to none of them. It allows them to [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) ceremonies such as [anointing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md), [consecration](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md), and [hallow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md), and create [auras](../lore/Aura.md) of Vigilance in place of the auras they would otherwise have access to. It also allows a priest to [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with priests dedicated to Vigilance who are not part of their [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may perform ceremonial skills other than [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) as if you were dedicated to the virtue of [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md).
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Watchful requires 5 measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), 7 ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), and 9 measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone); It takes 1 month to make one of these items.
