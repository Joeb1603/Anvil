# Gryphonsoul Aegis

*[Image: A warrior in Gryphonsoul Aegis is very hard to kill.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:HeavyChangeling.jpg)*

## Description

Crafting a suit of this armour is a serious undertaking for even a skilled armoursmith, and it is certainly one of the most expensive [magic items](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) known to Imperial [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisans). It requires a large amount of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), carefully alloyed with steel and [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron). The metal repeatedly quenched in great baths of [beggrar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). All this effort focuses and reinforces the irresistible force of the human (or [orc](../../world/Orc.md)) spirit.

The armour is fairly popular in [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), and many suits are of Dawnish manufacture. They are often build with the four *Gryphon Runes* ([Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md), [Verys](../lore/Verys.md), [Feresh](../lore/Feresh.md) and [Jotra](../lore/Jotra.md)), or etched with an image of a gryphon. Some rare suits are constructed with an eagle-head helm and clawed gauntlets and boots, visually capturing the image of the heraldic beast.

In other parts of the Empire, the armour has a variety of other names. In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) for example, [Schlacta](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md) wear heavy *Hearthfire Chain* and rely the power to see them through the dark winter nights. Both the [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and the [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) follow a tradition of delicately engraving each scale of the *Lionsong Paean* with a word, phrase or poetic line invoking strength, [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) or the name of a Paragon or Exemplar.

Crafting a Gryphonsoul Aegis requires eleven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), twenty-one ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and nine measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). It takes one month to make one of these items.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [heavy armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#heavy-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** While wearing this armour, you gain 2 additional ranks of [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance) and an additional [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points).
- **Roleplaying Effect:** You feel inspired to attempt great deeds and to pit yourself against the hardest challenges.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Gryphonsoul Aegis requires eleven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), twenty-one ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and nine measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). It takes one month to make one of these items.
