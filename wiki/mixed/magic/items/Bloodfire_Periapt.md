# Bloodfire Periapt

*[Image: EternalVigilance.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:EternalVigilance.jpg)*

## Description

A Bloodfire Periapt most commonly is made as a green or red stone worn on a chain, although they are also worn as rings or bracelets. They are often marked with the rune [Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md) and [Verys](../lore/Verys.md). Some [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) and [Winterfolk](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) artisans mark these items with [Irremais](../lore/Irremais.md), a rune associated with sacrifice - or shape the pendant stone in the form of a hanging blade.

These amulets grant the wearer a feverish, unnatural strength when they are stuck by envenomed attacks. Some fanatics have been known to intentionally envenom themselves to draw on the sick vitality these talismans offer, but doing so is an extremely risky proposal that has led to these items being christened *Fool's Bargains*.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a piece of [jewellery](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** .While under the effect of [VENOM](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom), you gain three additional ranks of [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance). You lose three hits from your current total if the VENOM is cured, or you lose this item.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Bloodfire Periapt requires eleven ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), seven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), three ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), and seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). It takes one month to make one of these items.
