# Thorn's Mark

## Description

The Thorn's Mark is potentially deadly in the hands of both [Kallavesi](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesi.md) and [Steinr](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Steinr.md) warriors. The [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) was purchased from the eternal [Estavus](../../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md) as part of [her offer](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Inkpot_gods.md#the-mediators-bargain) to the mediators of Wintermark. Originally known as the *Eagles Talon* the spear was renamed by the [Steinr](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Steinr.md) runesmith Derowen Voice of the Forge when she passed the schema to [Sulkavaris](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_Egregore.md#sulkavaris) to be added to Runesmith's Law.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed spear](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-spear). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Twice per day you may call STRIKEDOWN with this one-handed spear.
- **Materials:** Crafting the Thorn's Mark requires twelve ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), eight measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), and three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
