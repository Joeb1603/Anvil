# Reaving Mattock

*[Image: A Balston.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:A_Balston.jpg)*

## Description

A re-purposed farming tool, the Reaving Mattock has a heavy steel head and a stout oak handle inlaid with tempest jade. What a mundane mattock does to hard or frozen soil, the Reaving Mattock does to enemy soldiers, leaving them bleeding to death or crippled. They are unsurprisingly popular with the agriculturally-minded [yeomen of the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Yeoman_of_the_Marches.md) and with the [Varushkans](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), and much less so with the citizens of the [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md), [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) and [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) due to their associations with farming.

These weapons were among the first weapons crafted by the [Imperial Orc](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) after they joined the Empire, many of whom were familiar with similar tools from their time in the mines, or working the farms. They have remained a popular choice, especially with those Orcs who prefer to spend their time [slaughtering their barbarian cousins](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_economic_interests.md#reavers) rather than embracing the strict discipline of the Imperial armies.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [Weapon Master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may call [IMPALE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) with this great weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Reaving Mattock requires seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade). It takes one month to make one of these items.
