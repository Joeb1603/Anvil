# Captain's Command

*[Image: Bobert11730.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Bobert11730.jpg)*

## Description

The [sentinels](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sentinel.md) of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) and the [cataphracts](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md) of [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) alike appreciate the tactical versatility granted by these powerful weapons. Whether sweeping a foe's legs out from under them, or driving an enemy back with a mighty blow and an intimidating shout, the Captain's Command offers a greatly enhanced range of options.

The [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) often call these weapons *Tempest Blades* because the [kohan](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#kohan) who wield them use them to devastating effect when moving quickly through the massed ranks of opponents, spreading confusion and scattering their enemies in disarray like powerful winds.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [Weapon Master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) to call [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown) or [REPEL](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#repel) with this great weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Captain's Command requires twelve ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), eleven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron). seven measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and four ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
