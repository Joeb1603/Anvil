# Stalwart's Sword

## Description

A Stalwart's Sword is a powerful weapon for any [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [hero](../../government/synod/Heroism.md) to wield. The ancient creed of the Wintermark people is "The storm can never break us" - the Sword helps an aspiring warrior live up to that heroic ideal, granting them [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) to stand firm in the face of fear as well as the inner strength needed to face down the most powerful enemies.

The artisan who crafts the Sword should take care when binding it to the warrior who will wield it. The choice to take up the Stalwart's Sword can be a key moment in a person's skein, representing a moment when they commit themselves to the path of the hero. Not every warrior is ready to make this choice, not every warrior is ready to carry a Stalwart's Sword. The sword gives the wielder the ability to resist fear, but it can't make a bannerbearer out of a maggot, no matter what magic you grant them. Those who fall short of their Sword's challenge will find their skein is [frayed](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_religious_beliefs.md#the-frayed).

The smiths at [Runegrott](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#runegrott) teach smiths to inscribe the Wintermark rune [Sular](../lore/Sular.md) onto the flat of the blade, three fingers width from the pommel. If that cannot be done then it can be added to the scabbard that holds the weapon. They claim that the Stalwart's Sword can help the bearer discover the best choices to make as their pursue their skein in the face of danger.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain one additional [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points). The weapon counts as a [source of spiritual strength](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md#overcoming-roleplaying-effects) for overcoming roleplaying effects that cause fear.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Stalwart's Sword requires ten ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), four ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and four measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
