# Biting Blade

## Description

A Biting Blade is a respectable weapon that grants additional power to a skilled soldier, as well as offering the ability to strike a significant blow to an individual who is not a professional warrior. The weapon is sometimes simply referred to as an *Orichalcum Blade* or (in the poetry of Wintermark) a *Dawnfire Blade* or *Duskfire Blade* in reference to the slight reddish tinge to the metal produced by alloying orichalcum with steel.

The Biting Blade is the weapon many common folk think of then they think of a traditional 'magic sword.' Artisans in [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) especially like to inscribe the rune [Jotra](../lore/Jotra.md) on a Biting Blade, and [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) crafters often shape the pommel into the head of a [gryphon](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#gryphon) or engrave one onto the blade directly.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day, you may call [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) with this one-handed weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Biting Blade requires seven units of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
