# Ring of Adversity

## Description

The most common form taken by a Ring of Adversity is either a single band marked with one each of the [runes](../lore/Runes.md) of [Spring](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-spring), [Winter](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-winter) and [Night](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-night) or a single ring formed of three interwoven bands, each of a different precious metal.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this ring to gain one additional [rank](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) of either Spring lore, Winter lore or Night lore for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Ring of Adversity requires nine measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), nine measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), six ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), and nine measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
