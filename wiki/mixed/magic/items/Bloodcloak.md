# Bloodcloak

*This page contains details of an item that allows you to use blood in place of herbs; for the Wintermark army see [Bloodcloaks](../../government/military/Bloodcloaks.md)*

## Description

The most common appearance of a Bloodcloak is a dark red mantle or hooded cloak, usually embroidered with the [rune of blood](../lore/Rhyv.md). They are rarely heavily decorated, and tend to have a practical aesthetic.

The first Bloodcloaks were made centuries ago to be worn by [grimnir](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#grimnir) marching alongside the original [Bloodcloaks](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#the-bloodcloaks) army. Indeed, the [general](../../titles/General.md) of the Bloodcloaks was the custodian of the "original" Bloodcloak - an [artefact](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#artefacts) version of the more common cloak. When the army fell to the [Thule](../../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md) during the name of [Empress Brannan](../../../in-character/history/Empress_Brannan.md), the Bloodcloak was lost - and with it the knowledge of how to make this useful [physicks'](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#physick) tool.

During the last months of 380YE, however, an escaped slave from the now [Thule](../../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md) controlled [Silver Peaks](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Sermersuaq.md#silver-peaks) made it to Anvil and was granted hospitality at the [Stormspire](../../../out-of-character/groups/Wintermark_groups.md#stormspire) over the winter. During his stay information was given that the original Bloodcloak was now in possession of a warlock known as Skogr the Red. In Spring 381YE a [conjunction](../../../in-character/world/Sentinel_Gate.md#conjunctions) was identified through the [Sentinel Gate](../../../in-character/world/Sentinel_Gate.md) allowing negotiations between the Winterfolk and the warlock. With the aid of Juha the Cave Spider - the newly recognised [Ambassador to Otkodov](../../titles/Ambassador_to_Otkodov.md) - Raknar Ravenstorm was able to secure not only the return of the cloak itself, but also the release of fifty Wintermark slaves incarcerated in the mines of Skogr the Red. The Bloodcloak itself was awarded to Lofyn Blood-cloak who resided as the general of the newly formed Bloodcloak army of Wintermark.

During examination, it was discovered that concealed within the lining of the cloak - unbeknownst to the Thule - was the original [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema)! In Autumn 381YE, the Senate agreed to [add the schema to Imperial Lore](../../../in-character/senate-motions/Add_Bloodcloak_schema_to_Imperial_Lore.md), allowing any Imperial [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) to weave and enchant it.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [tool](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirements:** You must have the [Physick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) skill to bond to this item.
- **Roleplaying Effect:** While wearing this item, when you see a living creature in pain, you feel an urge to alleviate that pain - regardless of how you feel about them personally.
- **Effect:** Three times per day when you use the [physick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) skill you can draw a little of your own blood and use it as if it were single dose of either [bladeroot](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), [cerulean mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), [Imperial roseweald](../../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), [marrowort](../../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), or [true vervain](../../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md). An [apothecary](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary) cannot use this blood when creating a potion.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Bloodcloak requires twelve ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), twenty measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), eight measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and four measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
