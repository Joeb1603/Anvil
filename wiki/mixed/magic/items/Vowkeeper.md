# Vowkeeper

*[Image: Vowkeeper.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Vowkeeper.jpg)*

## Description

These magical bows have been used for centuries by battlefield archers, skirmishers and scouts. It is traditional to whisper promises and secrets to the bow, and some mystically inclined archers swear that their arrows fly truer when they speak a vow or remind the bow of a past oath just before they loose their arrow. The [Imperial Orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) in particular like to carve or paint the words of key oaths onto the wood of the bow. Each such vow increases the [worth](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Worth.md) of the item, as well as celebrating the [literacy](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_culture_and_customs.md#writing) of the wielders - something very important to a people who are descended from slaves.

In the [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) a Vowkeeper is sometimes called a *Final Word*, but often the crossbow is given an ironic or fanciful name such as *Diplomacy* or *Compelling Argument* referencing the ability of the crossbow to bring a conflict to a speedy conclusion. While a crossbow is often seen as a more pragmatic weapon than a longbow, many of the master archers of the League continue the practice of whispering oaths before firing it.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [bow or crossbow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#bows). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [marksman](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#marksman) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:**You gain one additional [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Vowkeeper requires nine ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and five measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
