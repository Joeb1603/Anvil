# Baersark's Rage

*[Image: Some artisans include images of the Stallion , taming the raw power of the magic](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:WintermarkMedium.jpg)*

## Description

A Baersark's Rage lets the wearer recover quickly from crippling wounds. It is a popular piece of armour with many [Kallavesi](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesi.md) warriors, and they have introduced it to the [Steinr](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Steinr.md) who fight alongside them. It allows a hero to rise up from incapacitating wounds and then fight for a short time even in the face of active opposition. Kallavesi-made suits often have boar-hide as the base and may incorporate a [boar mask](../lore/Wintermark_hearth_magic.md#animals-and-masks), calling upon the beast's almost boundless tenacity. Indeed, Baersark was the name of a particularly [huge and vicious boar](../../../in-character/world/Mundane_beasts.md#dire-beasts) which roamed [Kallavesa](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md) centuries ago, slaying many hunters who sought to make their names by killing it. Eventually a team of [Suaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) slew it, but it killed several of their number before it fell, charging down the spears embedded in its flesh to gore its killers with its massive tusks. Its hide was used to make the first examples of this armour.

[Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) skirmishers also value this armour when spread out in forests and far from medical attention. Navarri archers sometimes call this armour *Arrow Catcher* when being polite and a string of more colourful words in the heat of battle, for a barbarian chieftain dressed in such armour can take several arrows to keep on the ground.

There is debate over which [runes](../lore/Runes.md) are best to work into this armour. [Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md) is used in its healing aspect, but some hold that those suits encourage their wearers to shed their blood freely in the knowledge that they can recover and that such a cavalier attitude is unhealthy. [Verys](../lore/Verys.md), the rune of might, is a popular alternative, multiplying the wearer's natural reserves of strength.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [medium armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#medium-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you use the [unstoppable](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#unstoppable) skill you regain up to 5 lost hits.
- **Materials:** Crafting a suit of Baersarks' Rage requires fifteen ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), eleven measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), five ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and five measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). It takes one month to make one of these items.
