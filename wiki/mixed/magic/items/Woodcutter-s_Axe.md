# Woodcutter's Axe

*[Image: Retainer's Axe.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Retainer%27s_Axe.jpg)*

## Description

Setting out to create one of these great [steel or mithril](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#common-metals) axes can be a daunting proposition given the sheer amount of materials required to create them. Also known as a *Sundering Axe*, their most common form is that of a great daneaxe wielded by a [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) or [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) warrior, although they are also popular in some parts of [Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). Woodcutter’s Axes are often engraved with [Yoorn](../lore/Yoorn.md), the Rune of Ending or [Kyrop](../lore/Kyrop.md), the Rune of Weakness.

In [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md), there is a tradition of making these weapons in the form of heavy, thick bladed greatswords colloquially called *Butterknives* - a waggish reference to the way the weapons cut through steel as if it was butter. For a similar reason, some [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) or [Navarri](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) warriors refer to them as *Hotknives*. The greatsword incarnation of this weapon is often marked with the rune [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md), representing its dominance over other tools of war.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [Weapon Master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) to call [SHATTER](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#shatter) with this great weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Woodcutter's Axe requires nine ingots of [orichalum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), fifteen ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), nine ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), three measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
