# Labyrinthine Vestments

*[Image: Quite apart from the spiritual power it grants, a Labyrinthine Vestment can also be a visible sign of wealth and influence.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Bishop_Severin.jpg)*

## Description

A Labyrinthine Vestment is a sturdy robe or tunic, often with a hood, that allows a priest to use significantly greater amounts of [liao](../../religion/Liao.md) when performing a [spiritual ceremony](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md). Through use of one of these robes, a lone priest can greatly increase the [strength](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#ceremony-strength) of their ceremonies, either to create a powerful and durable [aura](../lore/Aura.md), a lasting [excommunication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Excommunication.md) or [testimony](../../../out-of-character/rules/Testimony.md), or to overcome or [exorcise](../../../out-of-character/rules/Exorcism.md) a potent [malignant](../lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md) spiritual aura or curse.

Quite apart from the spiritual power it grants, a Labyrinthine Vestment also represents the investment of dozens of ingots and measures. A set of Labyrinthine Vestments are significantly more expensive that a [Wayfarer's Robe](Wayfarer-s_Robe.md), and thus more likely to be worn by a powerful and influential priest of the [Synod](../../government/synod/Synod.md) than a mendicant adventurer.

The traditional design for one of these garments is a simple robe of a single colour covered with intricate [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver)-and-[orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) embroidery in the form of swirling or angular maze. This design is by no means universal however. In [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) it is commonly called a *cardinal's robe* or *bishop's robe* - and rather than subtle embroidery the [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisans) who weave them often go out of their way to stress how valuable the robe is and how prestigious the wearer is. Some priests of [Prosperity](../../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md) and [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) see ownership of one of these glorious garments as a sign of their virtue, taking inspiration from the item itself; others view it as a powerful adjunct to their ceremonial abilities. Both schools of thought can appreciate the beautiful dyes, sumptuous fabrics and precious metals that augment and ornament the splendid vestment.

As with many [magic items](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) that are of use to a priest, a set of Labyrinthine Vestments is often [hallowed](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md). Common hallows include [Hallowing of the Masquerade](../lore/Auras_of_Ambition.md#hallowing-of-the-masquerade) or [Aldones](../lore/Auras_of_Ambition.md#hallowing-of-aldones), the [Hallowing of the Throne](../lore/Auras_of_Pride.md#hallowing-of-the-throne), or the [Hallowing of Brass](../lore/Auras_of_Courage.md#hallowing-of-brass).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [vestment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing these vestments to use their magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) or [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) in the performance of a [religious skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md), you may spend up to three additional doses of [liao](../../religion/Liao.md) to increase the [strength](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#ceremony-strength) of the ceremony by the same amount.
- **Roleplaying Effect:** While wearing this robe bodily discomforts like hunger, thirst, or exhaustion feel muted and distant
- **Materials:** Crafting a Labyrinthine Vestment requires five ingots of [Weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), five ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), eleven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), nine measures of [Iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and fourteen measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
