# Icon of the Righteous

## Description

An Icon of the Righteous allows a priest to perform [ceremonies](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md) as if they were [dedicated](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) to the virtue of [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md). It generally takes the form of a piece of religious art, usually depicting a [paragon or exemplar](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of Courage such as [Inga Tarn](../../../in-character/religion/Inga_Tarn.md) or [Isaella](../../../in-character/religion/Isaella.md). The icon is not always a portrait - in [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) it is often crafted in the form of a medallion or short rod decorated with the feathers and bones of jackdaws, painted with the [Rune of Battle](../lore/Jotra.md), and called either a *Jackdaw Icon* or a *Thunderhead Blade*. In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), it is commonly crafted as a statue called variously a *Brave Bear* or a *Courageous Boar.* In some parts of [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), the Varushkan style of icon has proved surprisingly popular - a statuette of a boar called a *Brave Pig* is used by many friars.

The icon is equally valuable to a priest who is dedicated to one of the other six virtues as it is to a priest who is dedicated to none of them. It allows them to [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) ceremonies such as [anointing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md), [consecration](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md), and [hallow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md), and create [auras](../lore/Aura.md) of Courage in place of the auras they would otherwise have access to. It also allows a priest to [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with priests dedicated to Courage who are not part of their [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may perform ceremonial skills other than [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) as if you were dedicated to the virtue of [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md).
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Righteous requires 7 ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), 5 measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and 9 measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone); It takes 1 month to make one of these items.
