# Ring of Triumph

## Description

The most common form taken by a Ring of Triumph is either a single band marked with one each of the [runes](../lore/Runes.md) of [Summer](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer), [Autumn](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-autumn) and [Day](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-day) or a single ring formed of three interwoven bands, each of a different precious metal.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this ring to gain one additional rank of either Summer [Lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore), Autumn Lore or Day Lore for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Ring of Triumph requires nine ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), nine ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), six ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), and nine measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
