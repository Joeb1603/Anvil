# Sceptre of the Necropolis

*[Image: With a touch of the Sceptre of the Necropolis a foe can be crippled or even killed.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Sceptre.jpg)*

## Description

The earliest record of these deadly items has them constructed by the [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [magisters](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Magister.md). They saw some use in the [early battles](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_history.md) with the orcs, and were occasionally used as tools of assassination during the worst periods of patrician rule over the pre-Imperial nation. Today they are generally considered to be deadly but effective implements for pinpoint removal of enemies. If their deathly magic is released properly, the target is potentially removed permanently from the battlefield. If the magic goes awry, however, the wielder may be left dangerously overextended.

Of some interest to historians is that these rods are termed *Sceptres of the Necropolis* in early works that predate the destruction of the Highborn city of [Pharos](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Necropolis.md). No adequate explanation has been found to date.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you cast the [paralysis](../spells/Paralysis.md) spell, you may call [IMPALE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) rather than [PARALYSE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#paralyse).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Sceptre of the Necropolis requires twenty measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), twelve measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) and five ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade). It takes one month to make one of these items.
