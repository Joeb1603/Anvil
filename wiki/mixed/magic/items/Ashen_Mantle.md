# Ashen Mantle

*[Image: Costume by Costume Mercenary](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Urizen-0250.JPG)*

## Description

These robes are sometimes considered the pinnacle of the weaver's art. The process of creating them consists of carefully treating the fabrics involved with beggar's lye and colouring them with dyes infused with iridescent gloaming. The [magisters](../lore/Highguard_magical_traditions.md) of [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) are particularly fond of these beautiful vestments, and use only the richest dyes to combine with the finest and most subtle decoration. In [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) the robes are delicately shaded and sometimes simply called *Robes of the Magi*, although even there the traditional name is used more commonly.

In the [Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) by contrast these robes are usually bright, clashing colours and fine fabrics with beautiful dragonbone decorations, while those [volhov](../lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md) lucky enough to own an Ashen Mantle prefer one made from fine waterproofed lamb's wool.

For many magicians an Ashen Mantle and a [Staff of the Magi](Staff_of_the_Magi.md) are an obvious combination, greatly increasing their [spellcasting](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md) abilities.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain four additional points of personal mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Ashen Mantle requires fifteen measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), seven measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), five measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and four ingots of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
