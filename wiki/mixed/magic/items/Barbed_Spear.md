# Barbed Spear

## Description

The first Barbed Spears were crafted and wielded in combat by the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) - they are especially effective against [vallornspawn husks](../../../in-character/world/Vallornspawn_husk.md) - but they are not the only people to make use of these weapons. In the [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) the weapon is sometimes called a *Biting Glaive*, and valued for its ability to inspire terror in opponents.

In some quarters these weapons are also known as *Teeth of Vengeance*, because they are often used to ensure the death of an opponent who has harmed the wielder or one of her companions. This specific usage of the Barbed Spear has caused concern in the [Synod](../../government/synod/Synod.md) because it can help to encourage [unvirtuous behaviour](../lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#vengeance).

While it is common for a Barbed Spear to bear the rune [Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md), they are often also marked with the rune [Irremais](../lore/Irremais.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:**You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) to call [VENOM](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) with this polearm.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Barbed Spear requires thirteen measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), three ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), and three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
