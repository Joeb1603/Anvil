# Oathsworn Spine

## Description

These weapons are often decorated with, or in some cases carved from, bone or horn. In parts of [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) they are made from the ivory of the great narwhal, while in other areas it is common to decorate them with the bones of [dire beasts](../../../in-character/world/Mundane_beasts.md#dire-beasts), especially great wolves. Hunters, bounty hunters and [thief-takers](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_economic_interests.md#thief-takers) find their properties especially useful - able to prevent prey from fleeing long enough for them to be apprehended or brought down.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed spear](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-spear). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may call [ENTANGLE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#entangle) with this one-handed spear.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Oathsworn Spine requires ten measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and seven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
