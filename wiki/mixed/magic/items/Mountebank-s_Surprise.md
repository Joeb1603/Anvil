# Mountebank's Surprise

## Description

A wand and a one-handed sword are a common combination for a [mountebank](../lore/The_League_magical_traditions.md#mountebanks) in [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). This particular pair of enchantments is favoured by those who specialise in two person confidence tricks. When both of the swindlers are armed with these, they can often take on a surprising number of angry marks by [empowering](../spells/Empower.md) each other. A particularly notorious [Tassatan](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md) pair named Tullio and Miguelito were known to employ such tactics in the latter part of their career, most notably to escape the guards of the de Guerra [Carta](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Guild.md) after conning their [Merchant Prince](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_leadership.md) out of his signet [ring](../lore/The_League_hearth_magic.md#rings), a tale immortalised in the popular opera *The Golden Trod*.

In [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), these items are known as *Mirrors of Glory*, for by strengthening an ally, the [war witch](../lore/Dawn_magical_traditions.md#war-witches) is also strengthened. Those among the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) who have taken the oaths of both the [vate](../lore/Navarr_magical_traditions.md#vates) and the [thorn](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Thorn.md) sometimes wield these in the form of a [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) axe and a stout [worldmarrow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) rod, calling them *Star-Sworn Legacies* in remembrance of [Star](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/The_magician_Star.md), one of the first vates, who provided Navarr and Thorn with magical aid in their battles against the [Vallorn](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md). A number of Navarr [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) make these items only at night, under visible stars, while reciting the [oaths](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_culture_and_customs.md#tattoos-andvows) of vates, thorns and [brands](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Brand.md) to teach the two items to support each other and to assist their wielder in aiding others.

In the League, both items often bear [Lann](../lore/Lann.md), the [rune](../lore/Runes.md) of bargains, representing the benefit of the spell to both parties, while Dawnish versions use the [Gryphon runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer), placing [Feresh](../lore/Feresh.md) and [Verys](../lore/Verys.md) on the rod and [Jotra](../lore/Jotra.md) and [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md) on the blade.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. A pair consisting of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons) and either a [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods) or a [wand](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#wands). You must be wielding both the weapon and the implement to use the set's magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [ambidexterity](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#ambidexterity) and [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skills to bond to these items.
- **Effect:** Once per day after casting the [empower](../spells/Empower.md) spell on a target you also gain the benefit of the empower spell.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Mountebank's Surprise requires no special materials. It takes two months to make a pair of these items.
