# Runesmith's Glare

## Description

The Runesmith's Glare takes its name from the device that is normally painted on the shield, usually a raptor, often an owl, but sometimes an osprey, a hawk or a falcon. Occasionally the artisan will use a raven or another corvidae or sometimes just a pair of eyes. The tradition dates back to the very first Runesmith's Glare which was said to have been brought to [Isenbrad](../../../in-character/religion/Isenbrad.md) by a bird sent by an ice giant. Isenbrad was so transfixed by the bird's glare that he painted the image of it (or the eyes in some version of the tale) on the shield as a token of his respect. Despite the obviously apocryphal nature of the tale (there are no such things as ice giants and there are no birds large enough to carry a shield in Wintermark), the legend persists and most artisans who teach a student how to make the Glare pass on the story.

In addition, or sometimes instead, the artisan will often paint or emblazon runes on the edge of the shield. Runes such as [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md) and [Zorech](../lore/Zorech.md) are both favoured - and often used together. The first invokes the power of the Summer realm, helping to bring strength and prowess in battle. The latter is more commonly associated with the [Night realm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md). It is used here to invigorate the wielder's passions and strengthen their will.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [full-sized shield](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#shields). You cannot make a magic buckler. You must be wielding this shield to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [shield](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#shield) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain one additional rank of endurance and an additional hero point.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Runesmith's Glare requires eight ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), seven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and three measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
