# Kingfisher's Promise

## Description

The Kingfisher's Promise is a useful talisman useful to the ritualists who specialise in rituals of the Autumn. The [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) was purchased from the eternal [Estavus](../../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md) as part of [her offer](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Inkpot_gods.md#the-mediators-bargain) to the mediators of Wintermark. Originally known as the *Ring of Flame* the focus was renamed when the schema was passed to [Sulkavaris](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_Egregore.md#sulkavaris) to be added to Runesmith's Law.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this ring to gain one additional rank of Autumn lore for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting the Kingfisher's Promise requires seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), five ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), three ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), and three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
