# Glamourweave Robe

*[Image: An Urizen magician in Glamourweave Robe](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Urizen-Shopped-2838.JPG)*

## Description

These fine robes are made of the finest cloth woven with threads of gold, silver, mithril and orichalcum. The robes are most often dyed with a vibrant and delicate combination of green iron ore and iridescent gloaming, leaving the cloth with a viridian, cerulean or deep umber tone, decorated with copper-and-green-iron alloy wire. The intent is usually to impress onlookers, and to grant a confidence and sense of pride to the wearer. They are very popular in [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) and [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), places where magicians are likely to fight in close proximity to warriors. On a battlefield the robes are often worn over or beneath a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour).

The robes are often embroidered with runes such as [Zorech](../lore/Zorech.md), [Feresh](../lore/Feresh.md) or [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md), although robes intended for an [enchanter](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Earl.md#enchanters) or a general are likely to include the runes [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md). In Urizen, the rune [Lann](../lore/Lann.md) is most often used on these robes, representing a notional bargain between the magician and the sentinels who work with him - they will protect and support the magician and in return the magician uses his magic to protect and support the sentinels.

Symbols of [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) and [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) are often included on these robes, especially when they are worn by [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [magisters](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Magister.md) or priests with a mastery of magical lore.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Four times per day when you cast the [empower](../spells/Empower.md) spell, you may do so without spending any mana. You must be able to cast the empower spell to use this power.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Glamourweave Robe requires twelve ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), seven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and eight measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
