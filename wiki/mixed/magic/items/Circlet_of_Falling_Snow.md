# Circlet of Falling Snow

## Description

*[Image: The Circlet of Falling Snow allows a magician to take control of their own tumultuous emotions.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Armoured_Kai.jpg)*

A Circlet of Falling Snow is an intricate piece of work, a pinnacle of the jewelcrafter's arts. Usually made of silver or platinum, and designed with an eye to simplicity of form. They are often decorated with regular geometric designs such as spirals or mazes, intended to serve as meditative foci to soothe the mind and clarify the thoughts of the wearer. While often fashioned as a circlet, it is just as common to see one in the form of a ring or a pendant. Emissaries who deal with the [eternals](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) find these items to be useful tools when keeping a clear head, or when cultivating the correct mindset to deal with the complex inhabitants of the realm of [Day](../../../in-character/eternals/Realm.md#day). They are very popular in [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) where they are considered useful in helping to maintain [poise](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_culture_and_customs.md#poise); remaining calm allows a magician to channel their emotions into constructive action, rather than being overwhelmed by them.

Though unlikely, questing [enchanters](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Earl.md#enchanters) of [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) can be often found in possession of these items which they call *Pennants of Pure Heart*. They are commonly given as [favours](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#favours) to a [lover or a true friend](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#love) so that if they come across [malign spiritual presences](../lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md) they remain [Loyal](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md) to their heart. They often take the form of richly embroidered strips of cloth interwoven with threads of precious metals and mithril depicting a [chain](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md#symbols-icons-and-trappings) linking [heraldic symbols](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_culture_and_customs.md#heraldry).

They are also useful to magician-[exorcists](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#exorcism), particularly [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [magisters](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Magister.md) and some [stewards of the dead](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_religious_beliefs.md#stewards-of-the-dead). The Highborn [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) call them *Way Bells* and craft them in the form of delicately engraved [bells or chimes](../lore/Highguard_hearth_magic.md#bells-and-chimes), worn as pendants.

[Isenbrad](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md#the-path-of-wisdom), Paragon of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md), is a legendary smith said to have been so skilful he was able to craft from ice and snow. Stories suggest that he crafted the first Circlet of Falling Snow to allow him to find his way in the eternal ice-storm [Sydanjaa](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_territories.md#sermersuaq).

These items are often marked with [runes](../lore/Runes.md) such as [Irremais](../lore/Irremais.md), Rune of Wisdom, [Cavul](../lore/Cavul.md), Rune of Purity, or even [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md), Rune of Dominion (especially if combined with [Zorech](../lore/Zorech.md), Rune of Passion).

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a piece of [jewellery](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may expend a point of personal mana at any time to justify remaining calm and collected when exposed to a [roleplaying effect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) (as if you had spent a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points)). This expenditure of mana has the same restrictions as casting a spell.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Circlet of Falling Snow requires no special materials. It takes two months to make one of these items.
