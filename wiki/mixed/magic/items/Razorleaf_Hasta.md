# Razorleaf Hasta

## Description

This six-foot spear is usually made of blackened ash-wood, with an orichalcum-iron alloy head, generally shaped like a leaf, and kept at razor sharpness. They were popular with the [Vard](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_history.md), and used extensively during their conquest of the land that is now Varushka. The spear has largely fallen out of use, but it is still popular in some of the more isolated vales where warriors armed with a Razorleaf Hasta and a round shield support heavily armoured [schlacta](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md) in defending their homes against certain [monstrous threats](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves), especially husks.

Beyond Varushka, the spear is used by many [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [brands](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Brand.md) who are not also warriors, and by some [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [cataphracts](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md) who still practice an adapted form of the [one-handed spear](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-spear) and [shield](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#shields) fighting style their ancestors used from [horseback](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Horse.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed spear](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-spear). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) to call [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) with this weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Razorleaf Hasta requires no special materials. It takes two months to make one of these items.
