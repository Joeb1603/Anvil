# Landsknecht's Zweihänder

## Description

A Landsknecht's Zweihänder is a famous greatsword, popular among the [mercenaries](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md) of [Tassato](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_territories.md#tassato-mestra-and-tassato-regario) and [Temeschwar](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_territories.md#temeschwar). There is occasional light-hearted debate on whether its power is magical in nature or simply down to the weight of the large quantities of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) which go into their production.

The [unconquered](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Unconquered.md) of [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) also employ these swords as part of their guerrilla tactics, disarming and disabling their opponents as often as they simply run them through or chop them in half. Merab of Tobias' Patrol was famed for her skill in deploying her sword and was responsible for the slaughter of every [orc](../../../in-character/barbarians/Barbarian.md) shaman in the [Great Forest of Peytaht](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_territories.md#reikos). There have been mutterings that Merab may have been an [Exemplar of Vigilance](../../government/synod/Signs_of_the_paragon_and_exemplar.md), but insufficient evidence has been presented to [the Synod](../../government/synod/Synod.md) on the matter.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [Weapon Master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Twice per day you may call [IMPALE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) with this great weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Landsknecht's Zweihänder requires thirteen ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade). and three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
