# Limbsetter's Point

## Description

The Limbsetter's Point is a tool useful to [physicks](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) who often deal with broken limbs. The [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) was given as a reward from the eternal [Barien](../../../in-character/eternals/Barien.md) as part of [his offer](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Conduit_to_destiny.md#the-half-handed-lord) to support the construction of the Runeforge. Originally known as the *Grimnir's Bistoury* the focus was renamed when the schema was given to [Sulkavaris](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_Egregore.md#sulkavaris) by **Derowen** to be added to Runesmith's Law.

### Limbsetter's Point

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [tool](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirements:** You need the Physick skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may use this tool twice per day to restore the use of a limb as if you were treating it with a dose of [cerulean mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md). This takes 30 seconds.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Limbsetter's Point requires four measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and two ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
