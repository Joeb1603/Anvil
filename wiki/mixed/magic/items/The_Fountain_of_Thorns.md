# The Fountain of Thorns

## Description

The Fountain of Thorns is a very powerful ritual tool attuned to the swirling magic of the Spring realm. While many are crafted in the shape of a wooden or bone spheres or orbs, it is much more likely that the item takes the form of a bowl, cauldron or chalice. As with many items associated with the Spring realm, it tends to have an organic look, as if it were grown rather than shaped by craft. Many items are irregular, and most have small thorn- or hook-like protrusions scattered at random across their surface.

During rituals the Fountain of Thorns often plays a central role, a powerful reminder of the magical power a coven wields. As with the less-powerful [Green World](The_Green_World.md) when in the form of a bowl or chalice, it is often filled with liquid at the beginning of a ritual and passed around, each member of the coven drinking from it or adding a drop of blood to it in turn. Even when it is crafted in another form, it is common for ritualists to anoint it with their own blood during a ritual, pricking their finger on its thorns or cutting their palms with a ritual knife. Especially among the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md), a Fountain of Thorns is likely to be stained with blood after only a handful of uses. By contrast the magicians of [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) and [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) are often careful to keep the item clean, washing it in fresh water after every use to prevent it darkening and (if some stories are to be believed) malignant.

A Fountain of Thorns is usually only marked with [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) runes if it is crafted for the use of a Winterfolk coven. More traditionally, they are inscribed with representations of the constellations of [The Fountain](../lore/The_Fountain.md) or [The Claw](../lore/The_Claw.md).

Covenstones can be crafted in a great variety of different forms but are usually large enough to form a decent centre-piece for a ritual. Regardless of the precise form it takes, a coven that uses one is careful to ensure that it plays a central role in their ceremonies. A covenstone is a potent representation of the bonds that tie a coven together and the power that their oaths allow them to wield. They are usually bespoke and thus depict [runes](../lore/Runes.md) and motifs that are relevant to the coven for which they were made.

## Rules

- **Form:** Paraphernalia. Takes the form of a piece of ritual [paraphernalia](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#paraphernalia). The item must be present in the ritual to use its magical properties.
- **Effect:** Twice per day the coven may perform a Spring ritual that does not count towards their daily limit of rituals performed.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Fountain of Thorns requires twelve measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), nine ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), seven ingots of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and two measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
