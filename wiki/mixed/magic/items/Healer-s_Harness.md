# Healer's Harness

## Description

This delicate suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour) is well suited to protecting a [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) healer who must operate on or near the front lines. Dedicated healers study [physick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) as well as magic and so have their mage armour made with many pockets and ties for their equipment, giving the armour its name. The application of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) can sometimes give the armour a shimmering surface and many dying soldiers have been overjoyed it see that oil-green colouring appear above them, giving it the name *Saviour's Sheen*. The harness also sees some popularity among the [mountebanks](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Mountebank.md) of [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md), giving a better chance of healing themselves if their audience turns nasty. They follow a similar design to battlefield Healer's harnesses, but the pockets are more likely to be filled with cards and other people's purses.

The rune [Cavul](../lore/Cavul.md) is often embossed on these suits of mage armour to help the wearer maintain concentration despite the pain of their wounds.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you cast, or [swift cast](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#swift-casting), [heal](../spells/Heal.md) or [purify](../spells/Purify.md) spell you regain one lost hit.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Healer's Harness requires seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
