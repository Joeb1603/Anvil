# Redwall's Wrath

## Description

The Redwall's Wrath is a dangerous weapon when used by a [hero](../../government/synod/Heroism.md) of [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md). The [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) was recovered during a conjunction through the Sentinel Gate to [rescue](../../../in-character/winds-of-war/A_wandering_fire.md#borderline-28conjunction29) Yantl the Storm Runner. Originally known as the *Linebreaker's Axe* the weapon was renamed in honour of the fallen warrior **Sven Red Wall**, who fell in Liathaven during the Winter Solstice 384YE, by Kali Keen-Eye who passed the schema to [Sulkavaris](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_Egregore.md#sulkavaris) to be added to Runesmith's Law.

The original schema suggested use of the runes of [Storms](../lore/Mawrig.md), [Might](../lore/Verys.md), [Battle](../lore/Jotra.md), and [Victory](../lore/Tykonus.md). The inclusion of Redwall's Wrath has seen a stir of enthusiasm amongst the [Suaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) who have shown a great deal of interest in what could prove to be a very cunning weapon.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) to call [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown) with this one-handed weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Redwall's Wrath requires 18 ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), 11 ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), 5 ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and 5 measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
