# Warmage's Belt

## Description

A sturdy leather belt is a common component of a suit of mage armour, using raw magic to protect the guts and groin. When the magic of the belt is invoked, healing power flows up from the base of the spine and the stomach and spreads through the body, not only fixing damaged skin, bone and blood vessels, but invigorating the wearer. Many wearers feel permanently vigorous and potent. [Briars](../lore/Briar.md) find this effect particularly pronounced, bestowing upon them a restlessness and drive extreme for even the twisted. This leads many briars, ashamed of their heritage, to avoid this mage armour. Those few who embrace their lineage in spite of the suspicions of others wear these belts proudly, relishing their exaggerated liveliness and putting their energies to good use on the battlefield

A Warmage's Belt is usually polished with ambergelt resin, usually inlaid or embossed with weltsilver. The rune [Bravash](../lore/Bravash.md) is often worked into the front of the belt, helping to generate and channel vital energies when the belt is invoked. For this reason, some waggish [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) once named it the *cocksure cummerbund*, a name which appears in more than one bawdy ballad. A Marcher landskeeper who wears a Warmage's Belt may expect some good-natured ribbing after the battle is won.

As might be expected, the Warmage's Belt is common among the [sentinels](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sentinel.md) of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), where it is often made with elegant simplicity. In contrast, a number of extremely decorative examples exist from [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md), made around the turn of the fourth century when the [Merchant Prince](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_leadership.md) Julio de [Tassato](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md) ran a contest to make him a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour) that exemplified his [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) in his city - the prize being a hefty sum of steel and exclusive patronage for the winner. In an ironic turn lost on none of Tassato's salon wits, blind judging led to the prize going to his greatest rival, who purchased *all* of the runners-up with the prize money and marched his [Free Company](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_military_concerns.md) through the streets to display them.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use the [unstoppable](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#unstoppable) skill as if you know it without spending [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Warmage's Belt requires seven ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), and four measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
