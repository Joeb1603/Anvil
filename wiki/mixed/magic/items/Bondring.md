# Bondring

*[Image: The power of the bondring allows the wearer to offer succour to their partner.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Forest_Friends.jpg)*

## Description

Sometimes called a *Dragonbone Ring*, a Bondring is a piece of magical jewellery favoured by anyone who links their life to that of another. They are used extensively by [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) [cicisbeo](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_culture_and_customs.md#relationships), by trusted [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [retainers](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Retainer.md), by bodyguards across the Empire, and by sworn comrades-in-arms and battle partners from all nations. They are also a popular gift between lovers, and it is not uncommon for a wedding to include an exchange of Bondrings and the ceremonial [bonding](../../../out-of-character/rules/Create_bond.md) of the partners together.

The magic of the Bondring works through the power of the bond. It allows the wearer to offer aid and succour to their partner - the person they are bonded to. In some cases, two such wearers are bound together with mutual bonds but this is not a requirement (for example, while a bodyguard or cicisbeo is likely to be bonded to a patron or client, the reverse is rarely the case). While a Bondring does not offer dramatic healing power, it cannot be denied that these talismans save lives across the Empire every year.

A Bondring is almost always a ring, or less commonly a bracer, either made from dragonbone or decorated with dragonbone inlays. They are frequently decorated with symbols of love, commitment or [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md) (such as hounds, hawks or falcons); runes such as [Zorech](../lore/Zorech.md), or [Lann](../lore/Lann.md); or stones that evoke the image of [the Three Sisters](../lore/The_Three_Sisters.md) or [the Chain](../lore/The_Chain.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a piece of [jewellery](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** This item allows the [create bond](../../../out-of-character/rules/Create_bond.md) spell to be cast to connect you to a single willing target; you can be bonded to only one person at a time, and if you stop being bonded to the bond ring, this additional bond breaks. Once per day you may use either [stay with me](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#stay-with-me) or [get it together](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#get-it-together) on the other person that you have bonded the ring to, without spending a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Bondring requires seven measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
