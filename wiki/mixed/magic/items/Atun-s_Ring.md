# Atun's Ring

## Description

The most common form taken by Atun's Ring is either a single band marked with one [rune](../lore/Runes.md) associated with each of the six realms or a single ring formed of six interwoven bands, each of a different precious metal.

These rings are named for the paragon [Atun](../../../in-character/religion/Atun.md), the sister of [Atuman](../../../in-character/religion/Atuman.md), exemplar of Ambition. Stories indicate that Atun was a powerful magician, prone to visionary experiences even without the use of magic, and a mistress of multiple realms. When she departed the nascent [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) nation she left the artefact magical ring that bore her name with her brother as a keepsake. Her followers crafted lesser rings in its image, and when the [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) began their exodus it is believed that all three founding sisters bore versions. The use of the rings was also widespread among the magicians of [Tassato](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md) and [Sarvos](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md), and three were given as gifts by Highborn magisters to the magicians of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) during early diplomatic exchanges. Of these three rings, one resurfaced during the Summer Solstice 385YE in possession of the Hall of the Newborn Sun in [Morrow](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md). It was [presented](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Rings_and_crowns.md#glare-of-the-noonday-sun) to the [Conclave](../../government/conclave/Conclave.md) by the Urizen [spire](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Spire.md), to be [endowed](../../government/conclave/Endowment.md) on the person most responsible for the destruction of [Druj](../../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) [miasma pillars](../../../in-character/barbarians/Druj_miasma.md#miasma-pillars) in [Zenith](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md). The [Bursar of the Conclave](../../titles/Bursar_of_the_Conclave.md) had the ring endowed on Caleb of the Cenotaph, [exarch](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_leadership.md) of the [chapter](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Chapter.md) of the [same name](../../../out-of-character/groups/Highguard_groups.md#the-cenotaph) and a renowned master of the [Chimes of Annulment](../rituals/Chimes_of_Annulment.md) ritual. The whereabouts of the other two rings are currently unknown.

The original ring would be a treasure beyond price, were it to be found, but it vanished during the tumultuous years of the Highborn civil war. Its last known bearer is believed to have been [Permion the Lawmaker](../../../in-character/religion/Permion.md), and it was not seen again after his assassination.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this ring to gain one additional rank of any one [ritual lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting Atun's Ring requires seven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), seven ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), seven measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and seven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
