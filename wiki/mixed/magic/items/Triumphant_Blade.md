# Triumphant Blade

## Description

A Triumphant Blade is a popular enchanted weapon, used throughout the Empire in various forms. A heroic individual may enhance their reservoir of inner strength directly, but with a little effort anyone can benefit from the fire the weapon kindles in their soul. In addition to allowing even the meekest senator to stand firm in the face of malign spiritual presences, it is sometimes coupled with other enchanted items such as [Scrivener's Guard](Scrivener-s_Guard.md) to serve as a mystical 'power source' for the second item.

A [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) Triumphant Blade often bears the rune [Tykonus](../lore/Tykonus.md), while a [Winterfolk](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md)-forged axe or sword is just as likely to bear [Gralm](../lore/Gralm.md) or [Ull](../lore/Ull.md), the runes associated with fate and chance. In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), a *Schlacta's Axe* is a popular weapon bought by rich boyars for their favoured captains, while the [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and officers of the [Free Companies](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md) of the [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) alike are known to favour beautifully crafted and decorated Triumphant Blades as marks of office.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain one additional [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Triumphant Blade requires ten units of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and four ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
