# Pugilist's Shillelagh

*[Image: A battlemage with a Pugilist's Shillelagh can control the battlefield](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Watt_did_you_say%3F.jpg)*

## Description

The Pugilist's Shillelagh, or *Stormcudgel* as it is also known, is usually a rough-hewn slab of wood reinforced with iron at either end. It is intended as much as a weapon for beating sense into the ignorant as it is for channelling magic, and is often favoured by rough-and-ready magicians from [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) and the [Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). In Varushka it is sometimes known as a *Volhov's Cane*, and disingenuous itinerant magicians make a great deal of how important it is for them to keep their staff with them at all times, given how much they rely on it to get around.

In some eyes, the greater length of the Stormcudgel makes it preferable to its close cousin, the [Storm Sceptre](Storm_Sceptre.md), although its use requires greater skill. Mages enlisted into a [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) pike-block find this staff useful for driving back foes who get inside the guard of their comrades’ long weapons. Occasionally a [Landskeeper](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Landskeeper.md) will fight alongside a household for their own peculiar reasons and acquire one of these staves to make up for their ignorance of battle magic. It is said that [Brigit of Dourfen](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_history.md#joining-the-empire), a Landskeeper who served the First Empress as her ambassador to the Marches, took up one of these staves for the first time upon heading out to meet with the Marcher [stewards](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Steward.md), ready to drive back any who might strike her down before hearing the Empress’ proposal.

These staves are also popular among Freeborn magicians who take to a life at sea on one of the [Brass Coast’s many ships](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_economic_interests.md). Most are employed for their abilities as healers and menders and so find that, if a sea battle breaks out, this staff provides them with a means to repel boarders in a very direct fashion. These are sometimes known as “Sweeping Booms”.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staffs). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may cast the [repel](../spells/Repel.md) spell as if you know it.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Pugilist's Shillelagh requires no special materials. It takes two months to make one of these items.
