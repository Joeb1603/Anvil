# Witches' Hammer

## Description

A Witches' Hammer channels and twists the energy from each movement, each strike, and each spell cast through it, storing a little of the energy so that it may be called upon in times of need to allow the magician to unleash the [shatter](../spells/Shatter.md) incantation. With a single crushing burst of power, the battle mage tears asunder a weapon or shield. Their common name comes from their popularity with the war witches of [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md).

These rods are usually plated with tempest jade, and often marked with the rune [Mawrig](../lore/Mawrig.md) (often in conjunction with [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md) to channel and control the release of destructive energy), or the Rune of Transformation [Xun](../lore/Xun.md). The Dawnish war witches who make excellent use of these rods to disarm and then defeat their enemies favour marking them with [Verys](../lore/Verys.md), the Rune of Might, or with the aspect representation of a powerful magical beast such as a dragon or chimera.

In parts of the [Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) this item is called a *Rod of the Ram* and often constructed with a ram's head or the horns of a ram at one end.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Twice per day you can cast the [shatter](../spells/Shatter.md) spell as if you knew it without spending any mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Witches' Hammer requires seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), six measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). It takes one month to make one of these items.
