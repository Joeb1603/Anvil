# Semblance of the Witch

*[Image: The Semblance of the Witch in the style of the League](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Raiment_Witch.png)*

## Description

The Semblance of the Witch is a suit of magical clothing infused with a little of the power of the [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) [persona](../lore/Dramaturgy.md#the-personae) of [the Witch](../lore/The_Witch.md). A [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) who wears this clothing can draw on the power it contains to empower their ability to perform [Night magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md). In addition to providing additional magical power, the robes also infuse the wearer with some of the personality of the Witch.

The robe contains the essence of the Witch, allowing the wearer to embrace that role in their magic and in their day-to-day dealings with others. It enhances the subtlety and confidence of the wearer, potentially giving them an advantage in social and political situations.

The raiment itself is cut from expensive, luxurious fabric - silks and satins are most common. Extra effort is made to ensure the robe is comfortable to wear - [naga](../lore/Naga.md) in particular will find the touch of the material on their skin to be particularly pleasurable. It is [dyed](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) deep, powerful colours and extensively embellished with [moonsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), seed pearls, and pieces of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and dark [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade). The cut is reminiscent of the robes favoured by some [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [weavers](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weaver_cabal.md) and [enchanters](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Earl.md#enchanter) as well as by some [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [magisters](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Magister.md) - a full sleeved robe with a voluminous hood. Several designs include soft fur lining and lavish edging.

Other popular designs include *Fortune's Attire*, which is patterned after [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) designs combining rainbow silks worn in layers of draped material, and the delicate *Nightwarden's Shadow* a layered garment in striking yet subtle shades of blue patterned after traditional Urizen dress and perfect for those who wish to use their magic to aid others without drawing attention to themselves.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this robe to gain two additional [ranks](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#ritual-magic) of Night Lore for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Roleplaying Effect:** While wearing this robe you feel an urge to find out secrets, and use them to gain influence over other people.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Semblance of the Witch requires seven ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), five ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and sixteen measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.

> **OOC Note**  
>
> This is one of six sets of ritual robes designed by *House Bourné*, a [Sarvosan](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) guild formed by the union of [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) master-artisans and a [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [weaver cabal](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weaver_cabal.md). Their unique designs combine the power of [dramaturgy](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) with a deep understanding of the hearth magic of [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding). During Winter 380YE, in conjunction with popular publication The Looking Glass, the Merchant-Prince *Genia Bourné di Sarvos* announced that the guild would allow any Imperial [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) to visit their estate in Sarvos and study the [schemata](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) detailing the six raiments, free of charge. House Bourné is an NPC group and is not represented at Anvil.
>
> Any character can learn how to create this raiment following the normal rules - either by selecting the robe as one of their starting items, or learning it as an [extra item pick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#extra-item). The only restriction is that anyone who knows how to make one of these robes *must* have spent a week or two studying at House Bourné in Sarvos - there is currently no other way to master the patterns which remain the proprietary knowledge of the Bourné guild.
