# Robe of Blood and Bone

*[Image: Costume by Jo Perridge](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:VarushkanRobes.jpg)*

## Description

These robes are commonly red or white (or a combination of both). They are generally hard-wearing, intended to survive the rigours of the battlefield as well as the hospital. The [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) woven and [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) stiffened cloth the robs are made from is called *trollsweave* by the Winterfolk, leading to a common name for these items being *Trollsweave Robes*.

Being able to provide one of these robes is a sign of great skill among the [Imperial orc](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [bonesetters](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_economic_interests.md#bonesetters); it is not only a valuable tool for keeping the legion healthy, but it demonstrates their ability to acquire the necessary materials and commission such a powerful item. Imperial Orc magicians mark the robe with a thumbprint of their patient's blood every time its power is used, displaying its history and adding to its [Worth](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Worth.md). Even after the enchantment wears off, these robes are kept and marked with fingerprints to denote healing fuelled by the wearer's own mana and skill, and it is common for the robe to have the enchantment renewed several times if a legion can afford it. For instance, the Throatgouger Legion's robe is thought to have been made shortly after the Orc Rebellion and is now on its third layer of blood-prints.

When combined with a [Woundbinder](Woundbinder.md) wand, the two items together are sometimes called the *Warhealer's Regalia*. They are occasionally produced as matching sets by artisans who specialise in healing equipment.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Three times per day you can cast [heal](../spells/Heal.md), [purify](../spells/Purify.md) or [restore limb](../spells/Restore_limb.md) on another character without expending any mana. You must be able to cast the appropriate spell to use this power.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Robe of Blood and Bone requires nine ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), six measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and three measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
