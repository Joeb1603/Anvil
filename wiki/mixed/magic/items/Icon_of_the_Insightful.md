# Icon of the Insightful

*[Image: An Icon of the Shrewd depicting Avigliana di Sarvos](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Avigliana.png)*

## Description

An Icon of the Insightful is also known as an *Icon of the Measured*, *Icon of the Shrewd*, *Icon of the Astute*, and of course an *Icon of the Wise*. It allows a priest to perform [ceremonies](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md) as if they were dedicated to the virtue of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md). It generally takes the form of a piece of religious art, usually depicting a paragon or exemplar of Wisdom such as [Zoria](../../../in-character/religion/Zoria.md) or [Avigliana](../../../in-character/religion/Avigliana_di_Sarvos.md). The Icon is not always a portrait, however - in [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) it often takes the form of a statuette of a squirrel, and is known as a *Wise Gatherer*. In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) it is sometimes crafted in the form of a medallion or short rod decorated with the bones and feathers of ravens, generally decorated with the [Rune of Wisdom](../lore/Irremais.md) (or the [Rune of Thought](../lore/Aesh.md)), which is known as an *Icon of the Raven* or an *Icon of the Blade (Staff)*. Other less common variants are certainly possible - from the finely written scrolls of [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) to the clear crystal trapezohedron the [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) call a *White Star*.

The icon is equally valuable to a priest who is dedicated to one of the other six virtues as it is to a priest who is dedicated to none of them. It allows them to [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) ceremonies such as [anointing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md), [consecration](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md), and [hallow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md), and create [auras](../lore/Aura.md) of Wisdom in place of the auras they would otherwise have access to. It also allows a priest to [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with priests dedicated to Wisdom who are not part of their [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may perform ceremonial skills other than [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) as if you were dedicated to the virtue of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md).
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Insightful requires 5 measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), 7 measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and 9 measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone); It takes 1 month to make one of these items.
