# Rake's Progress

*[Image: A skilled warrior equipped with Rake's Progress can carve through lightly armoured barbarians.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Rake%27s_Progress.jpg)*

## Description

Commonly crafted in the form of two wicked blades, these cruel weapons allow a skilled warrior to move quickly through a battle-line dispatching lightly armoured opponents. They have a bloody reputation thanks to their use by the more distasteful [Free Companies](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md) of [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) as a quick means to dispatch unarmed [barbarians](../../../in-character/barbarians/Barbarian.md) in large numbers, as well as their rivals at home, according to some rumours. Though many assume the name refers to these scoundrels, it actually derives from a [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [saying](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Proverbs.md) that soldiers with these blades progressed through the enemies *like a rake through fallen leaves*.

The [Kallavesi](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesi.md) nickname these pairs *Wing-Beats*, likening the striking of two fierce blows to the spreading of a [bird's](../lore/Wintermark_hearth_magic.md#birds) wings. During forging, these blades are commonly quenched in blood, usually animal. A few have been cooled in human blood and these examples are believed to burn with a terrible bloodlust which slowly poisons any soul [bonded](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) to them. Some owners will only bond to these weapons for the day of the battle to prevent any ill-effects, while a few maintain a continuous bond and revel in the blades' subtle influence.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. A pair consisting of two [one-handed weapons](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding both weapons to use the set's magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [ambidexterity](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#ambidexterity) skill to bond to these items.
- **Effect:** Twice per day, when you use [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave), you may use it on two targets instead of one. You have only 10 seconds to deliver the second CLEAVE.
- **Materials:** Crafting a set of Rake's Progress weapons requires eight ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and three ingots of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make a pair of these items.
