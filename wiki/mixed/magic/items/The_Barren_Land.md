# The Barren Land

## Description

The covenstone known as The Barren Land draws upon the realm of Winter to empower a coven with additional capacity for performing rituals, drawing upon the bonds created between members of the coven.

Covenstones can be crafted in a great variety of different forms but are usually large enough to form a decent centre-piece for a ritual. Regardless of the precise form it takes, a coven that uses one is careful to ensure that it plays a central role in their ceremonies. A covenstone is a potent representation of the bonds that tie a coven together and the power that their oaths allow them to wield. They are usually bespoke and thus depict [runes](../lore/Runes.md) and motifs that are relevant to the coven for which they were made.

## Rules

- **Form:** Paraphernalia. Takes the form of a piece of ritual [paraphernalia](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#paraphernalia). The item must be present in the ritual to use its magical properties.
- **Effect:** Once per day the coven may perform a Winter ritual that does not count towards their daily limit of rituals performed.
- **Materials:** Crafting The Barren Land requires five ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), five measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), three ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and six measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
