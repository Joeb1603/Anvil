# Opaline Coat

*[Image: An Opaline Coat increases the magical potential of the wearer.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Highborn_Coat.jpg)*

## Description

An Opaline Coat takes its name from the opal gemstone, favoured by jewellers throughout the Empire for the incredible range of colour in which it is found. Each coat incorporates a small amount of dragonbone, often used to provide extra shape to key areas, or worked into the fabric as delicate beading. [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) tailors make Opaline Coats that shimmer in the sunlight, weaving threads of silver, gold and mithril into the fabric and choosing beautiful pieces of semi-precious stone to create fastenings and buttons. The magicians of [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) often compete with one another as to who can acquire the most splendid Opaline Coat, looking down on rivals who favour more mundane jackets, and being subtly enraged by those few [Mountebanks](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Mountebank.md) capable of acquiring one for themselves. The street magicians of the League tend to favour a shorter cut that they call an *iridescent jacket*, modelled after the Freeborn style.

The [Suaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) and [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) magicians who make use of the Opaline Coat prefer a more tasteful arrangement that has become increasingly popular even in the south - a coat of hard-wearing wool, dyed a single vibrant colour, with a lining of shimmering, iridescent shades. With the coat closed, it is difficult to tell it is anything other than a finely made, warm garment; when it is opened, its true beauty becomes apparent.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain two points of personal mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Opaline Coat requires nine measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
