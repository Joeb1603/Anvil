# Staff of Command

## Description

*[Image: A Staff of Command in the wrong hands is a dangerous thing](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:FreebornStaff.jpg)*

A Staff of Command is often made of metal, or at least shod and inset with pieces of metal and gemstones. Also called a *Staff of the Law*, it provides the wielder with the knowledge needed to control the movement of their enemies. Several prominent wielders of staffs such as these have allegedly fallen into dictatorial or even tyrannical ways - according to common folk tales these staves whisper into the ears of their owners of the joys of control and command, domination and subversion. A magician who relies on a Staff of Command can often receive a cold welcome among the [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md).

It is common to see a Staff of Command engraved with the incantations used to cast the spells it provides. It is also common to see a Staff of Command marked with runes of Autumn, most commonly [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md) although [Feresh](../lore/Feresh.md) is also common.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staffs). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may cast the spells [repel](../spells/Repel.md), [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) and [paralysis](../spells/Paralysis.md) as if you know them.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Staff of Command requires five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), seven measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), and ten ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade). It takes one month to make one of these items.
