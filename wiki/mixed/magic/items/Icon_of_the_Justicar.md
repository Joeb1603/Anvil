# Icon of the Justicar

## Description

*[Image: The Labyrinth might serve as a symbol of authority over spiritual forces.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Highborn_Icon.jpg)*

As with other [icons](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md), this ceremonial tool allows a priest to perform a [Liao](../../religion/Liao.md) ceremony. These icons often bear images of paragons and exemplars who defended people against false virtues, corrupt individuals or supernatural threats. They take their name from a title given to some exorcists in the pre-Imperial history of the Brass Coast, who specialised in driving out malign spirits or cleansing unpleasant auras from people, places and objects.

An Icon of the Justicar is the fruit of months of work for a skilled [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan). The work often includes small amounts of the magical [materials](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md), and it is very common for a crafter to use tools inlaid with [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) when working on an icon. In many parts of the Empire they are crafted to resemble torches or braziers, or may actually be censers used to burn strong-smelling materials. In some parts of [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) they are commonly created in the shape of sturdy metal candlesticks, in which beeswax candles are burnt during exorcism ceremonies.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** While you are wielding this icon you can perform the [exorcism](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#exorcism) ceremony as if you knew it.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Justicar requires no special materials. It takes two months to make one of these items.
