# Choleric Staff

*[Image: Corvus of the Spire of Auric Horizon](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Corvus_and_the_Choleric_Staff.jpg)*

## Description

The Choleric Staff taps upon the power of the [realm of Summer](../../../in-character/eternals/Realm.md#summer) to maximise the potential of the bearer's ritual power. These arcane implements are employed throughout the Empire to fortify the abilities of Summer ritualists.

A Choleric Staff is often slightly shorter and heavier than other [ritual staves](../../../indexes/by-category/Ritual_Staves.md). In [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), a staff of this kind is often referred to as a *Soldier's Shillelagh* and wielded by magicians and [covens](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) that specialise in providing warlike [enchantments](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md). Such staves are carved from gnarled oak branches inlaid with tempest jade and dragonbone sigils and shod in alloys of orichalcum and iron. Marcher [battle magicians](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) or those with an [Alder's Edge](Alder-s_Edge.md) wield these staves to good effect on the battlefield supporting their [yeoman](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Yeoman_of_the_Marches.md) allies.

It is common to see such an implement engraved with the [runes of Summer](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-summer) or with depictions of constellations such as [the Oak](../lore/The_Oak.md), [the Mountain](../lore/The_Mountain.md) or [the Stallion](../lore/The_Stallion.md). A *Staff of Summer Stars*, as it is also known, is a favourite with [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) and [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) ritual magicians who specialise in the higher magnitude rituals such as [Mantle of Lordly Might](../rituals/Mantle_of_Lordly_Might.md) or [Glory to the Sovereign](../rituals/Glory_to_the_Sovereign.md).

Making an [artefact](../../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md#creating-artefacts) version of the Choleric Staff is a costly endeavour, but there are a handful of such items in the Empire. They are usually passed down from magician to magician within a single coven. One of the best known is the *Staff of the Noonday Sun*, crafted in 172YE by Karina Matrinova Vos, a [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [volhov](../lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#volhov) from [Karov](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md) who used it to allow herself to perform [Chasuble of Majesty](../rituals/Chasuble_of_Majesty.md) by herself in the wilderness, as part of her self-appointed role as a defender and pilgrim of [the Way](../../../in-character/world/The_Way.md). The staff has been passed from magician to magician ever since, usually within the [Conclave order](../../government/conclave/Conclave_order.md) of the [Sevenfold Path](../../government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md), and is said to strongly influence its wielder toward hunting and destroying cults dedicated to heretical virtues.

### Archmage Staff

The [staff](../../titles/Archmage.md#staff-of-the-archmage) of the [Archmage](../../titles/Archmage.md) of Summer is a version of the Choleric Staff crafted to a secret pattern, and grants twice as much power to the wielder. Unlike most such staves, the same physical staves are reforged each year; some of the staves are believed to have developed spontaneous auras as a consequence, and blamed for a certain “gung ho” attitude among the archmages of Summer.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [ritual staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#weapons). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain one rank of Summer Lore, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Choleric Staff requires the eleven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), fourteen ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), twenty measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
