# Bloodfeather Harness

## Description

*[Image: A Bloodfeather Harness is a popular item with some who fight in the front rank.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Watt_did_you_say.jpg)*

The Bloodfeather Harness is popular with those [Kallavesa](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md) [mystics](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#mystics) who integrate a bird-mask into the armour along with feathered vambraces and greaves. Some [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisans) hunt [mandowla](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#mandowla), making leather from their ursine hide and working their feathers into the decoration of the mage armour.

When the wearer of the harness is critically wounded, its magic slows their heartbeat and grants them precious minutes before they are finally claimed by death. Time itself seems to slow down from the point of view of the wearer, and many of those who have been saved by the magic of this mage armour have described experiences in which they could not help but dwell on the significant events of their entire life, and the consequences of their deaths - some returned to their comrades profoundly changed. In some parts of [Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) it is called a *nervous guest* or occasionally the more waggish *lurker on the threshold* to laughingly poke fun at a reluctance to step through the door of death and enter the labyrinth.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain three additional ranks of [fortitude](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#fortitude).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Bloodfeather Harness requires three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), five measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), and ten ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.
