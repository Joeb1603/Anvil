# Phlegmatic Staff

## Description

*[Image: The staff of the Winter archmage is a powerful phlegmatic staff.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:An_Archmage_of_Winter.jpg)*

The Phlegmatic Staff taps upon the power of the [realm of Winter](../../../in-character/eternals/Realm.md#winter) to guide the bearer to a greater understanding of the leaching of power that is involved in ritual magic. These arcane implements are employed throughout the Empire to fortify the abilities of Winter ritualists.

It is usual to see such an implement engraved with [Naeve](../lore/Naeve.md), the Rune of Hunger or [Irremais](../lore/Irremais.md), the Rune of Wisdom.

The [staff](../../titles/Archmage.md#staff-of-the-archmage) of the [Archmage](../../titles/Archmage.md) of Winter is a version of the Phlegmatic Staff crafted to a secret pattern, and grants twice as much power to the wielder.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [ritual staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#weapons). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain 1 rank of Winter Lore, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Phlegmatic Staff requires the eleven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), fourteen measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and twenty measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
