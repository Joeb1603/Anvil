# Furrowed Wake

*[Image: A battlemage with a Furrowed Wake is a fearsome sight](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Staff.jpeg)*

## Description

A Furrowed Wake is a stout wooden stick, reinforced and inlaid with [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) decorations. It allows a skilled [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) to twist their magic to throw a target to the ground rather than simply holding them in place. In many ways, this is a superior method of capturing an opponent to the more traditional [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) spell.

[Imperial Orc](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [warcasters](../lore/Imperial_Orcs_magical_traditions.md#warcasters) favour this [staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staff) because it allows them to get up close and personal with their opponents - and place them at a disadvantage at the same time - without them running away. When using the Furrowed Wake they can often hear the voice of an [ancestor](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_religious_beliefs.md#ancestors-and-hero-points) screamingly urging them to brutalise their enemy as they are on the ground, calling it the *Stave of Antecedent Might*. It is also useful for [Thief-takers](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_economic_interests.md#thief-takers) when a group of polearm wielders contains a few warcasters and their tactic involves [knocking](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#mighty-strikedown) their quarry to the ground. It allows the warcasters to be part of the disciplined strategy.

There is debate among nations about which [rune](../lore/Runes.md) this staff should be marked with. Among the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md), [Winterfolk](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [Varushkans](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [Mawrig](../lore/Mawrig.md), the rune of storms, is used, for the ability to surprise and bring down the weak. However in [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) and [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) Mawrig represents disaster and is seen as a particularly ill omen. They instead prefer to mark their *Furrowed Wakes* with [Xun](../lore/Xun.md), rune of transformations, representing the transition from the magical to the physical. For this reason in Urizen it is often decorated with [iridescent butterfly](../../../in-character/world/Mundane_beasts.md#iridescent-butterfly) wings gaining attacks with it the sobriquet *Butterfly Wing Storm*.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staffs). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you cast the [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) spell, you may call [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown) rather than [ENTANGLE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#entangle).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Furrowed Wake requires five ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and five measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
