# Warden's Fists

## Description

*[Image: An Urizen sentinel wielding warden's fists](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Urizen-2655.jpg)*

It is said that the first pair of Warden's Fists was crafted by a [warden](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Warden.md) born of both the [Vard and the Ushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_history.md), she made and named them *Brother and Sister* for the siblings that she never had. However the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) claim that the wardens learnt the secret from their [vates](../lore/Navarr_magical_traditions.md#vates) who claim that they were originally called *[Terunael’s Fists](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_history.md)* and were a common weapon in their war against what came before the [vallorn](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md).

The [Company of the White Stag](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md#warden-fellowships) often refer to Warden's Fists as *Antlers*, and shape the [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) component of the axe and rod into complex tangs often resembling their own, as a maker’s mark.

In [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) they are favourite among the [sentinels](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sentinel.md) who believe that the balance between martial and magical is the best demonstration of [Arete](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Arete.md), a fight that can be altered by quickly removing pivotal opponents is one that you win without wasted lives.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. A pair consisting of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons) and either a [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods) or a [wand](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#wands). You must be wielding both the weapon and the implement to use the set's magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [ambidexterity](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#ambidexterity) and [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skills to bond to these items.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may either use the weapon to call [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) or instead use the implement to cast [repel](../spells/Repel.md) without spending [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) or [mana](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md).
- **Materials:** Crafting a set of Warden's Fists requires two measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), two ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), two ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and two ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make a pair of these items.
