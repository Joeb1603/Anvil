# Blood-sweat Hauberk

## Description

Whether constructed of [mithril](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#common-metals) or tanned leather, the most important step in crafting a Blood-sweat Hauberk is the removal of all impurities. The metal to make the rings, or the leather from which the armour is created, are extensively treated with [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) to ensure the highest possible degree of purity is achieved.

This armour comes with an obvious cost - the wearer is left physically exhausted by the experience of burning the venom out of their system. In the right circumstances, however, it can be a life-saver. The [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) were the first to devise these sets of leathers in response to the poisons of the [vallorn](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md), drawing their tradition of [ritual blood-offering](../lore/Navarr_hearth_magic.md#blood). They were often made with a convenient slit in one sleeve where the wearer could make the cut without ruining the garment. Warriors who often need the power of this armour while battling the vallorn are occasionally nicknamed *Gore-Cuffs* due to the inevitable bloody stains they bear.

When first introduced to this form of armour by Navarr [Brokers](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_economic_interests.md#brokers), most other peoples were leery about making use of them, lacking the Navarr's attitude to blood. However, some [apothecaries](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary) adopted them as protective clothing while at work in case of small explosions and accidental poisonings, leading to the nickname *Herbalist's Hauberk*. Particularly paranoid political figures have been known to wear these almost constantly, including rumours that one [Draughir](../lore/Draughir.md) [Cardinal](../../titles/Cardinal.md) of [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md), Antonia Constanza di Sarvos, took to wearing her Blood-sweat Hauberk as a night-dress.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [light armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#light-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Twice per day you may use 5 seconds of [appropriate roleplaying](../../../out-of-character/rules/Appropriate_roleplaying.md) that includes a self-inflicted cut to remove the [VENOM](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) condition from yourself. Using this power is very painful; it feels as if your blood is on fire and this roleplaying effect takes several seconds to fade.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Blood-sweat Hauberk requires eleven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and five ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make this item.
