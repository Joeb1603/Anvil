# Icon of the Proud

*[Image: This banner by Asenath daughter of Esther shows the proud paragon Richilde inspiring Nicassia, the Urizen scholar who befriended Adelmar the Lion](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Proud_Richilde.jpg)*

## Description

The *Icon of the Proud* is, paradoxically, rarely actually called an Icon of the Proud. Rather, every bearer tends to refer to it by a name that has personal meaning for themselves and their people. In [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), unsurprisingly, it is an *Icon of the [Glorious](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Glory.md)*. In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) an *Icon of the [Hero](../../government/synod/Heroism.md)*; in [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) an *Icon of [Excellence](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Arete.md)*, in [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) an *Icon of [Glass](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Honesty.md)*. It might be an *[Icon of the Resolute](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md)* or *Faithful*, an *[Icon of Sacrifice](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md)*, or an *[Icon of the Calloused Hand](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md)*. And these are just the common names that reflect pride in a nation, rather than the myriad other names that represent more personal pride.

It generally takes the form of a piece of religious art, usually depicting a paragon or exemplar of Pride such as [Richilde](../../../in-character/religion/Empress_Richilde.md). Usually, the image is of a figure that the bearer personally finds inspiring and this may mean that it contains the likeness of someone who is not *technically* a paragon or exemplar. Some [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) priests bear a particular *Icon of the Clever* that bears the image of [Emperor Giovanni](../../../in-character/history/Emperor_Giovanni.md) for example, inspired by his practical politics. The icon is not always a portrait either - even more so that other icons it is common for an Icon of the Proud to take the form of a piece of art particular to a given nation. In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) for example, it often takes the form of a statuette of an eagle, and is known as a *Proud Eagle* or occasionally (and a little paradoxically) a *Wise Eagle*.

The icon is equally valuable to a priest who is dedicated to one of the other six virtues as it is to a priest who is dedicated to none of them. It allows them to [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) ceremonies such as [anointing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md), [consecration](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md), and [hallow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md), and create [auras](../lore/Aura.md) of Pride in place of the auras they would otherwise have access to. It also allows a priest to [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with priests dedicated to Pride who are not part of their [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may perform ceremonial skills other than [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) as if you were dedicated to the virtue of [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md).
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Proud requires 5 measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), 7 ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and 9 measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone); It takes 1 month to make one of these items.
