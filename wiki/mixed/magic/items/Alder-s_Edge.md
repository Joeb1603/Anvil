# Alder's Edge

## Description

*[Image: A Homefire Guardian in the form of a medallion.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:IconAlderman.png)*

These items often take the form of chains of orichalcum and gold, from which hangs an oval medallion inlaid with tempest jade. They are often used by [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [alders](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_economic_interests.md) to bolster their defences, allowing traders and artisans to fight alongside trained bill fighters.

In other parts of the Empire, they are often called *Homefire Guardians* and are used to allow civilians to protect their homes from invaders. These items more commonly take the form of simple rings or bracers of orichalcum and gold, inlaid with tempest jade in the form of symbols of vigilance (such as geese in [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md)), runes such as [Ophis](../lore/Ophis.md), [Feresh](../lore/Feresh.md) or [Jotra](../lore/Jotra.md), or heraldic beasts such as [gryphons](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md#gryphon).

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a piece of [jewellery](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Alder's Edge requires nine ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and three ingots of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
