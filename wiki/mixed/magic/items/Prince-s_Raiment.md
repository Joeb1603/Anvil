# Prince's Raiment

*[Image: The Prince's Raiment in the Freeborn style](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Raiment_Prince.png)*

## Description

The Prince's Raiment is a suit of magical clothing infused with a little of the power of the [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) [persona](../lore/Dramaturgy.md#the-personae) of [the Prince](../lore/The_Prince.md). A [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) who wears this clothing can draw on the power it contains to empower their ability to perform [Autumn magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md). In addition to providing additional magical power, the robes also infuse the wearer with some of the personality of the Prince.

The Prince represents temporal authority and influence, and this magical garment allows a trained magical practitioner to experience a portion of that power and use it to better command the subtle and far-reaching potence of Autumn magic. Through the power of dramaturgy, combined with elements of the potent art of [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding), the magician who dons the Prince's Raiment experiences a little of the archetypal spirit of the persona. The magic of the clothing puts them in touch with their own reserves of personal power and helps strengthen their confidence in using that power to shape the world around them.

The Prince's Raiment is designed to project authority, confidence, and power. Expensive fabrics are used, carefully [bleached](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and dyed with [strong colours](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It is embroidered with [sungold](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) thread and embellished with [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), [polished crystal](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), and delicate [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) fastenings. Typically fashioned after the rich clothing of a [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) [Merchant-Prince](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_leadership.md) or [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [earl](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Earl.md), designs are also available patterned after traditional garb worn by leaders of every nation. The *Dhomiro's Garb*, the *Thane's Attire*, and the *Boyar's Coat* have proved quite popular with [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), and [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) magicians respectively. Work has recently been completed on the *Arbiter's Array*, a breathtakingly beautiful robe sculpted to traditional [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) designs patterned with an incredibly delicate webwork of metal threads and dragonbone-stiffened panels.

At the moment (Winter 380YE), House Bourné is encountering difficulties creating a suitable design for a Prince's Raiment that embodies the aesthetics of the [Imperial Orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md)' leaders. The Guild has expressed an interest in receiving suggestions from orc artisans as to what the leaders of their people would wear and how best these could be incorporated into a new design.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this robe to gain two additional ranks of [Autumn Lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#ritual-magic) for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Roleplaying Effect:** While wearing this robe you feel an urge to take charge of any situation you find yourself in.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Prince's Raiment requires three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), five ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), nine ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and eleven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.

> **OOC Note**  
>
> This is one of six sets of ritual robes designed by *House Bourné*, a [Sarvosan](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) guild formed by the union of [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) master-artisans and a [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [weaver cabal](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weaver_cabal.md). Their unique designs combine the power of [dramaturgy](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) with a deep understanding of the hearth magic of [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding). During Winter 380YE, in conjunction with popular publication The Looking Glass, the Merchant-Prince *Genia Bourné di Sarvos* announced that the guild would allow any Imperial [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) to visit their estate in Sarvos and study the [schemata](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) detailing the six raiments, free of charge. House Bourné is an NPC group and is not represented at Anvil.
>
> Any character can learn how to create this raiment following the normal rules - either by selecting the robe as one of their starting items, or learning it as an [extra item pick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#extra-item). The only restriction is that anyone who knows how to make one of these robes *must* have spent a week or two studying at House Bourné in Sarvos - there is currently no other way to master the patterns which remain the proprietary knowledge of the Bourné guild.
