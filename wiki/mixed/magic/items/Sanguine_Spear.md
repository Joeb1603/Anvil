# Sanguine Spear

*[Image: NebAndPoleArm.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:NebAndPoleArm.jpg)*

## Description

The Sanguine Spear is not named for [blood](../lore/Navarr_hearth_magic.md#blood) (though they do tend to spill a lot of it), but for the burnished ruddy colour of its [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) tip. When made in a form other than the traditional spear they have similar alliterative colour names, such as the *Garnet Glaive* in parts of [Redoubt](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Redoubt.md), the *Blooded Bill* in [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) or the *Crimson Corseque* in [Tassato](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md). Some [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [cataphracts](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md) prefer to have the haft painted or lacquered stark white to [contrast](../../../out-of-character/costume/Highguard_look_and_feel.md#feel) with the red-gold thrusting point.

These weapons are not for the squeamish; they are clearly intended to maim and kill lightly armoured opponents and magicians. The wielder of a Sanguine Spear may consequently receive a cool reception in parts of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), or with anyone who places considerations of cruelty above their respect for the Empire.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may use a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) to call [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) with this polearm.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Sanguine Spear requires five ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron). It takes one month to make one of these items.
