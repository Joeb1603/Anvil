# Mask of the Mountebank

*[Image: The Mask of the Mountebank challenges assumptions about identity.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MonocularMask.jpg)*

## Description

The Mask of the Mountebank empowers a ritualist with the ability to change someone's appearance through the power of [the Twilight Masquerade](../rituals/The_Twilight_Masquerade.md). The ritual is often used to grant the outward features of a [lineage](../lore/Lineage.md) not the target's own - or to disguise and conceal lineage features granting the recipient the appearance of an unlineaged human being. The ritual is often employed in the creation of magical [disguises](../../../out-of-character/rules/Playing_the_game.md#disguises), but also sees some use as a form of entertainment - especially in [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) and some parts of [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md).

With one of these masks, even a reasonably inexperienced practitioner of [Night magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) can perform the ritual without the assistance of a coven, provided they have mastered its intricacies. Furthermore, even a small coven equipped with several of these masks finds it relatively straightforward to enchant [several individuals](../rituals/The_Twilight_Masquerade.md#additional-targets) at once which can prove extremely valuable if time is of the essence.

The mask also provides the bonded wearer with the ability to employ [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) in place of the expensive [crystal mana](../../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) normally required to enact the ritual magic. A ritualist wearing the Mask of the Mountebank often supplements their ritual performance by literally painting new faces onto their subject using the valuable pigments.

These talismans are often crafted as a harlequin mask, a domino mask, or as a full-face mask depicting the [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) [persona](../lore/Dramaturgy.md#the-personae) of [the Mountebank](../lore/The_Mountebank.md) or (despite the common name), [the Witch](../lore/The_Witch.md). Occasionally one of these magical [foci](../../../indexes/by-category/Foci.md) may be encountered in the form of a *Charlatan's Periapt*, a jewel which tricks the eye appearing different from varying angles; or as a *Facedancer's Veil* - a severe white veil that shrouds the head and shoulders of a [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [magister](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Magister.md). Regardless of form, the mask often incorporates either the [Rune of Secrets](../lore/Diras.md) or the [Rune of Transformation](../lore/Xun.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain 2 ranks for the [Twilight Masquerade](../rituals/The_Twilight_Masquerade.md) ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks). When contributing to a performance of the Twilight Masquerade ritual, you may choose to substitute [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) for crystal mana. Every 2 measures of iridescent gloaming spent counts as 1 crystal mana, up to the normal limit determined by your effective rank of night lore.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Mask of the Mountebank requires three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and ten measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
