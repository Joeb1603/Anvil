# Ethereal Manacle

*[Image: Ethereal Manacle.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Ethereal_Manacle.jpg)*

## Description

These staffs are usually made of stout hardwood, treated with [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and bound with [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). Often they are decorated at either end, sometimes with grasping claws. They often have the rune [Queros](../lore/Queros.md) worked into them, and may be carved to look as if they are wound with chains. An Ethereal Manacle allows a magician to wield the power of the [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) incantation to root their opponents in place.

Sometimes called a *magician's retreat*, a little contemptuously, it cannot be denied that rooting an opponent to the spot can give the bearer a good head start. A skilled battle magician, however, is quite capable of rooting an opponent to the spot and then beating them down with the advantage of superior reach - even if the magician starts the fight outnumbered the additional power of this staff can quickly even the odds. Matially-minded [runesmiths](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths) and curmudgeonly [landskeepers](../lore/The_Marches_magical_traditions.md#landskeepers) are both known to make good use of the short time their opponents cannot move to make them regret trying to gang-up on a magician.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staffs). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Three times per day you may cast the [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) spell as if you knew it without spending any mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Ethereal Manacle requires eleven measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and four measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), and three measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
