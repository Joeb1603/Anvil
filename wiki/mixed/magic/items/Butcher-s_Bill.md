# Butcher's Bill

## Description

These polearms are comparatively cheap to craft, and this helps to make them popular among both [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) households and [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) [free companies](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md). They can be bought in moderate numbers, and used to add additional strength to a unit of skilled soldiers, or allow a recruit to fight nearly as well as their more experienced colleagues.

Their extensive use on the battlefield as part of tight formations has given them a brutal reputation that is not entirely deserved. They take their name from the "butcher's bill", the list of casualties after a battle. Several generals have referred to the use of these weapons as "*balancing the butcher's bill*" by ensuring there are more casualties on the enemy side than on the Imperial side.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may call either [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown) or [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) with this polearm.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Butcher's Bill requires eight ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and three ingots of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
