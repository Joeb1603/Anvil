# Captain's Mask

*[Image: A mask that empowers bread-and-butter martial rituals.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:SunMask.jpg)*

## Description

The Captain's Mask empowers a ritualist with the ability to confer additional toughness. The mask is often in the shape of a polished steel mask that covers the forehead and cheeks, reminiscent of a helmet, and is often emblazoned with thunderbolts. Another common form, particularly favoured by [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) magicians, is a full- or half-face sun mask.

The mask often incorporates the [Rune of Battle](../lore/Jotra.md), sometimes openly and sometimes on the inside so that it rests against the brow of the wearer. A martial animal such as a lion, or the [bear and the boar](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_religious_beliefs.md#the-virtuous-animals) popularised as symbols of [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) by [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) [storytellers](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_religious_beliefs.md#storytellers). Likewise, a [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md)-made mask is likely to represent a [hawk or falcon](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_culture_and_customs.md#birds-and-feathers) - birds associated with the virtue of [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md).

While this is most often crafted in the form of a mask, it is sometimes created as a brooch or large amulet depicting crossed swords upon a bright shield. A few [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) magicians favour an orichalcum ring. set with a red or amber stone, and engraved on the inside with a martial motto or inspirational phrase from their [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) oath; such a ring often [specifically represents](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_culture_and_customs.md#rings) their loyalty to their coven.

As with all the magical masks, these [foci](../../../indexes/by-category/Foci.md) are used to grant additional power to perform [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) magic. In this case, it enhances [Splendid Panoply of Knighthood](../rituals/Splendid_Panoply_of_Knighthood.md); [Strength of the Bull](../rituals/Strength_of_the_Bull.md); and [Crown of Phoenix Fire](../rituals/Crown_of_Phoenix_Fire.md). A lone Summer ritualist can use the power of the mask along with the [Imperial regio](../rituals/Regio.md#the-imperial-regio) to [enchant](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) their allies without the aid of a coven, while a committed band of magicians might use the mask to help them enchant large numbers of warriors with a single performance.

While the mask is useful, there are rumours of a much more potent version whose use allows additional warriors to be enchanted with a single ritual. If such a mask exists, the secrets of its construction are not common knowledge.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain 2 ranks for [Splendid Panoply of Knighthood](../rituals/Splendid_Panoply_of_Knighthood.md); [Strength of the Bull](../rituals/Strength_of_the_Bull.md); and [Crown of Phoenix Fire](../rituals/Crown_of_Phoenix_Fire.md), subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Captain's Mask requires eight ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), and five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
