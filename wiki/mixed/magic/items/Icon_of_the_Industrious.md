# Icon of the Industrious

## Description

An Icon of the Industrious allows a priest to perform [ceremonies](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md) as if they were dedicated to the virtue of [Prosperity](../../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md). It generally takes the form of a piece of religious art, usually depicting a paragon or exemplar of Prosperity such as [Zemress](../../../in-character/religion/Zemress.md) or the [Little Mother](../../../in-character/religion/The_Little_Mother.md). The icon is not always a portrait, however - in [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) it is sometimes crafted in the form of a medallion or short rod decorated with the bones and feathers of a magpie, and known as an *Icon of the Magpie*. Often these icons bear the [Rune of Wealth](../lore/Pallas.md), but they are equally as likely to bear the [Rune of Victory](../lore/Tykonus.md). While Pallas is traditionally associated with Prosperity, the idea that Tykonus - which represents struggle and success more than mere wealth and material possessions - is a better fit has gained some purchase in recent decades. In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) it often takes the form of a statuette of bees surrounding a hive, and is known as a *Hive of Industry*. Other less common variants are certainly possible - such as the colourful illuminated scrolls recounting the lives of the exemplars popular in [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md).

This icon is especially popular in the Marches. While all the Virtues are recognised and celebrated, many Marchers feel a strong connection to Prosperity. These icons are often given more down-to-earth names by Marcher priests and lay folk and it is more common to hear talk of an *Icon of the Exhausted*, *Icon of the Aching*, or *Icon of the Sweated Brow* than it is an Icon of the Industrious. A particularly Marcher form of this icon is that of a carefully braided cord, often decorated with tiny silver charms or drops of ambergelt, used to cinch up a handful of corn or wheat stalks to create a *Prosperous Sheaf*.

The icon is equally valuable to a priest who is dedicated to one of the other six virtues as it is to a priest who is dedicated to none of them. It allows them to [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) ceremonies such as [anointing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md), [consecration](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md), and [hallow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md), and create [auras](../lore/Aura.md) of Prosperity in place of the auras they would otherwise have access to. It also allows a priest to [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with priests dedicated to Prosperity who are not part of their [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may perform ceremonial skills other than [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) as if you were dedicated to the virtue of [Prosperity](../../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md).
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Industrious requires 7 measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), 5 measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and 9 measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone); It takes 1 month to make one of these items.
