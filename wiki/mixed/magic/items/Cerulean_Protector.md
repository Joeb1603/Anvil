# Cerulean Protector

*[Image: Leather and Chain Knight-Errant.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Leather_and_Chain_Knight-Errant.jpg)*

## Description

The standard Cerulean Protector is a hauberk or cuirass of sturdy leather. In the [Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) these suits are made of reinforced leather scales, and are often dyed blue or green and called *Cerulean Scale*, after the herb [cerulean mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md). In Dawn it is often used by [knights-errant](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Knight-errant.md) who rely on speed - avoiding the weight of heavy armour leaves one open to the threat of [archers](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#marksman).

The main advantage of this armour comes when the warrior has reduced coverage of arms or legs for some reason, especially among warriors who prefer the freedom granted by minimising the amount of restrictive armour they are wearing. [Archers](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#bows) particularly appreciate both the freedom of movement and the restorative power of the armour, for as it is said in [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) there are few creatures as miserable as a one-armed archer.

[Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md), the [Rune](../lore/Runes.md) of Blood, is fairly commonly employed on this armour for its general healing ability, though [Bravash](../lore/Bravash.md), the Rune of Fertility, is also used to encourage the bones to grow back together rapidly. The runes are often embossed into the leather, but some smiths cut the [mithril](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#common-metals) plates into the shape of the runes, creating a bold form of decoration as well as anchoring the enchantment. Given the use of both [Spring runes](../lore/Runes.md#runes-of-spring) and the association with herbs, some suits bear elaborate floral patterning.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [medium armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#medium-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) to use the [relentless](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#relentless) skill as if you know it.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Cerulean Protector requires three ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), six measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), and three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
