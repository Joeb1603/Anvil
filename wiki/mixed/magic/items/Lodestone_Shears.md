# Lodestone Shears

## Description

These tools allow an [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) to extend their ability to deal with the bonds that all enchanted items require. They most often take the form of a small pair of shears, scissors or snips, depending on the materials the artisan works with. Some traditional crafters - especially in [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) and among the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) - favour rune-carved blades, with which they inflict a tiny nick on a person whose bond they are severing. Often a little of the blood is used to sketch the rune [Evrom](../lore/Evrom.md) on the item, assuming both owner and item are present.

Whatever form they take, Lodestone Shears are usually made of heavy metal and generally marked with the [Rune of Ending](../lore/Evrom.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [tool](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirements:** You need the [Artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** If you have the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill, you may use these shears twice per day to cut a bond connecting to any item you can make as if you were casting the [create bond](../../../out-of-character/rules/Create_bond.md) spell.
- **Materials:** Crafting Lodestone Shears requires seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and six ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade). It takes one month to make one of these items.
