# Mountebank's Guise

*[Image: The Mountebank's Guise in the Dawnish style](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Raiment_Mountebank.png)*

## Description

The Mountebank's Guise is a suit of magical clothing infused with a little of the power of the [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) [persona](../lore/Dramaturgy.md#the-personae) of [the Mountebank](../lore/The_Mountebank.md). A [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) who wears this clothing can draw on the power it contains to empower their ability to perform [Spring magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md). In addition to providing additional magical power, the robes also infuse the wearer with some of the personality of the Mountebank.

The Mountebank is a complex figure in magical dramaturgy. This raiment draws on the power of that persona as an agent of vibrant, energetic change, and unrestrained power. By harnessing this irrepressible energy, the Guise allows a trained ritual magician to tap into a wellspring of power when working Spring magic. As a magical raiment infused with dramaturgical power, the Mountebank's Guise allows a bonded wearer to tap into some of the potent archetypal magical symbolism of the Mountebank, strengthening their will to act and urging them to seek out opportunities to use their power to its best advantage.

The raiment itself is usually woven from hard-wearing cotton or linen, carefully [bleached](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and [dyed](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) in vivid, contrasting colours. The raiment often appears quite rich - it is intended as a dramaturgical costume rather than casual wear after all - with [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) decorations. The clothes tend to favour comfort and movement, reflecting the rough-and-tumble lifestyle of many Spring magicians. They are also often cut specifically to show off the physical attributes of the wearer - the Spring realm understands the power of the physical body to influence perceptions and subtly control social situations...

While the basic design is patterned after [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) fashions, it draws inspiration from the clothing of the street [bravo](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Bravo.md) or the [Free Company](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md), not that of the [merchant prince](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_leadership.md) that it draws inspiration from. Several other designs exist, inspired by other nations. The *Troubadour's Guise* for example, which combines elements of traditional [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [yeofolk](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Yeofolk_of_Dawn.md) garb with the vibrant colours favoured by many [troubadours](../../../in-character/religion/Troubadour.md). The *Woodcutter's Attire* draws on [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) aesthetics, combining a vibrantly embroidered long coat with practical leggings, comfortable boots, and supple gloves - a design that has proved popular not only in Varushka, but also in [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md), [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), and both [Temeschwar](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) and [Holberg](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Holberg.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this robe to gain two additional [ranks](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) of Spring Lore for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Roleplaying Effect:** While wearing this robe you feel a burst of confidence and an urge to speak your mind at all times regardless of the society you are keeping.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Mountebank's Guise requires five ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), twelve measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and eleven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.

> **OOC Note**  
>
> This is one of six sets of ritual robes designed by *House Bourné*, a [Sarvosan](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) guild formed by the union of [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) master-artisans and a [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [weaver cabal](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weaver_cabal.md). Their unique designs combine the power of [dramaturgy](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) with a deep understanding of the hearth magic of [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding). During Winter 380YE, in conjunction with popular publication The Looking Glass, the Merchant-Prince *Genia Bourné di Sarvos* announced that the guild would allow any Imperial [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) to visit their estate in Sarvos and study the [schemata](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) detailing the six raiments, free of charge. House Bourné is an NPC group and is not represented at Anvil.
>
> Any character can learn how to create this raiment following the normal rules - either by selecting the robe as one of their starting items, or learning it as an [extra item pick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#extra-item). The only restriction is that anyone who knows how to make one of these robes *must* have spent a week or two studying at House Bourné in Sarvos - there is currently no other way to master the patterns which remain the proprietary knowledge of the Bourné guild.
