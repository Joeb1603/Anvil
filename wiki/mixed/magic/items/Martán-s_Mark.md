# Martán's Mark

This is a placeholder page for content that PD are actively working on.

## Description

These magical bows have proven to be incredibly useful for skirmishers and scouts. The [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) was forged by **Derowen**, as the [Keeper of the Runegrott](../../titles/Keeper_of_the_Runegrott.md) and disseminated to Imperial artisans at the Summer Solstice 387YE by **Leofa de Courtenay-Wyldrose** as the [Citizens' Voice](../../titles/Citizens-_Voice.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [bow or crossbow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#bows). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [marksman](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#marksman) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use the [relentless](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#relentless) skill as if you know it without spending any hero points.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Martán's Mark requires nine measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and three ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.
