# Boneweaver

## Description

These powerful adjuncts to a magician's healing arts are most often made of bone. They repair arms and legs that have been crippled by [cleaving strikes](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) or [pierced](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) with arrows. They are especially popular with Imperial Orc shaman, who can bring the legion warriors back to full fighting strength quickly while conserving their personal mana for emergency healing or offensive spells. In all nations, they are a common tool of stretcher-bearers, allowing them to get warriors walking again and able to head to the chirurgeons unassisted. This has won the wand the name *Cripple's Delight*.

Finding bones long enough to form the core of these wands can be problematic as the best results are achieved with single bones without any fractures or cracks. [Horse](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Horse.md) leg bones were once used, but since their extinction, the long bones of oxen are the most common materials in many lands. In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), the bones of [bears and boar](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_religious_beliefs.md#the-virtuous-animals) are used due to their size and association with the virtue of [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md), to inspire the injured to overcome hardship and claim victory. Careful application of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) can provide additional length, encasing the bone like a fly in amber. In some examples, tiny fractures appear in the bone every time they are used, taking on the injuries they cure, eventually causing them to shatter at the end of their lifespan.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [wand](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#wands). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Three times per day when you cast the [restore limb](../spells/Restore_limb.md) spell you can do so without spending any mana. You must be able to cast the restore limb spell to use this power.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Boneweaver requires seven measures of [amberbelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), five ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) and five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
