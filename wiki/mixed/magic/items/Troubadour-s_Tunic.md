# Troubadour's Tunic

## Description

*[Image: These robes are often adorned with heraldry in Dawn .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Chain_and_Surcote.jpg)*

A *Troubadour's Tunic*, *Templar's Cuculla*, or a *Sutannir's Coat* (in [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), and [The Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) respectively) is an ornamented priestly garment that helps the wearer connect with their inner reserves of passion and personal power. Both [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) and [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) priests favour a more subdued coat, with a brightly coloured and decorated interior - not because they are modest, or afraid of calling attention to themselves, but because it allows them to move among the people unremarked until it is time for them to take action. Regardless of form, this vestment is heavily reinforced with pieces of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), which help to connect the priest to their own reserves of inner strength.

The vestment generally appeals to two distinct priestly paths. More martial priests wear it beneath their armour when they take the battlefield, sometimes in conjunction with a weapon such as an [Apprentice's Blade](Apprentice-s_Blade.md), [Thresher's Cudgel](Thresher-s_Cudgel.md), or [Butcher's Cleaver](Butcher-s_Cleaver.md). Others draw on the power it offers to help them endure dangerous [urges](../../../out-of-character/rules/Roleplaying_effects.md) or to [inspire](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#get-it-together) and [protect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#stay-with-me) their allies.

As with many such [magic items](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) intended for the use of priests, a Troubadours Tunic is often [hallowed](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md) to offer additional spiritual strength to the wearer. Common choices include [Hallowing of the Shepherd](../lore/Auras_of_Prosperity.md#hallowing-of-the-shepherd), the [Hallowing of Isaella's Resolve](../lore/Auras_of_Courage.md#hallowing-of-isaellas-resolve) or [Ashwine's Rose](../lore/Auras_of_Courage.md#hallowing-of-ashwines-rose), or the [Hallowing of the Unconquered](../lore/Auras_of_Vigilance.md#hallowing-of-the-unconquered).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [vestment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing these vestments to use their magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain two additional [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Troubadour's Tunic requires seven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), four measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and nineteen measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
