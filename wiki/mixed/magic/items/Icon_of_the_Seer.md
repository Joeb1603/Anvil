# Icon of the Seer

*[Image: An icon showing the Paragon Aldones di Sarvos .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:IconAldones.png)*

## Description

An Icon of the Seer, like some other [icons](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md), is generally a ceremonial tool that allows a priest to perform a [Liao](../../religion/Liao.md) ceremony they have not personally mastered. Sometimes called an *Icon of the High Tower*, they often bear symbols associated with [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md) or [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md), or the images of paragons and exemplars who showed great insight or clarity of thought, especially the [sentinel](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md#sentinel). In parts of [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), the item is called an Icon of the High Tower and takes the form of a tall, thin tower carved with eyes, or an *Icon of the Beacon*, which depicts a lighthouse rather than a tower. [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) and [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) alike may wield an *Icon of the Clear Sky*. For various reasons they often take the form of a stout walking stick or rod with a sphere of clear gemstone, polished metal sphere or crystal set into one end.

An Icon of the Seer requires months of work by a skilled [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan), commonly with a set of tools inlaid with [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) used especially for crafting religious items. They often including trace amounts of the magical [materials](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md), especially [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** While you are wielding this icon you can perform the [insight](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#insight) ceremony as if you knew it.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Seer requires no special materials. It takes two months to make one of these items.
