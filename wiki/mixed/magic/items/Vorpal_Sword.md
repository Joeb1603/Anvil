# Vorpal Sword

## Description

A Vorpal Sword is a powerful weapon that grants extensive additional power to a heroic warrior, as well as putting even an unskilled fighter on par with a skilled soldier.

These enchanted blades have a long history, generally cropping in tales of monstrous incursions. The best known name originates from the obscure [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) vale of Vorpa, where according to legend, such a blade was used to slay a poorly defined but vicious monstrosity, the nature of which changes with every teller of the tale. In [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), the stories speak of an axe with similar powers being used to slay an enormous dire ostrich, while the Marchers tell of a magical hammer which was used to crack the skull of an enormous weasel plaguing [Upwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md). The wealth of such stories has made it common practice to work parts of terrible and exotic [beasts](../../../in-character/world/Legendary_beasts.md) into the weapon, possibly as a hilt or haft, to draw upon the blade’s legend.

These fanciful tales mask the very real uses of Vorpal Swords throughout Imperial history. A skilled and spirited warrior armed with a Vorpal Sword can cut through lesser folk with terrifying ease. The Marcher general [Tom Drake](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Life_of_Tom_Drake.md) is said to have wielded one when he confronted and slew the tyrant boyar [Alderei the Fair](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Alderei_the_Fair.md). During the [invasion](../../../in-character/world/Imperial_history.md#2888-ye29-invasion-of-kahraman) of [Kahraman](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md), the Freeborn general Jone i Arco i Erigo wielded a straight-bladed Vorpal Sword at every battle. Those who dislike childish stories call these blades “Conquerors’ Swords” and insist that there is no record of a battle lost by a general bearing one of these blades.

Artisans in [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) especially like to inscribe the rune [Jotra](../lore/Jotra.md) on a Vorpal Sword along with one or both of the runes [Verys](../lore/Verys.md) or [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md). In [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) these swords are often called *Gryphon Talons*.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Twice per day, you may call [CLEAVE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) with this weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Vorpal Sword requires ten units of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), three ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), and three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye). It takes one month to make one of these items.
