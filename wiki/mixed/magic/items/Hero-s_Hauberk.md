# Hero's Hauberk

## Description

This heavy armour is usually fashioned from [mithril](../../economy/Mithril.md) or steel infused with [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). Alloying the base metal with these two materials in the right quantities helps to give the armour its defensive properties, enabling the wearer to keep on fighting even when badly wounded. The [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), although small in volume, is a crucial element of the enchantment, helping to bolster the fortitude and strength of will of the bearer allowing them to endure pain and injury that would stop a bull in its tracks.

After Wintermark joined the Empire it become common for wealth Thanes to present a suit of Hero's Hauberk to a young warrior who has recently passed their [Test of Arms](../../../out-of-character/events/Test_of_Arms.md), or when a warrior gains their name from a scop. The gift is a recognition of a person's worth, an indication that the Thane values their service so much that they want to ensure that the recipient can fight alongside the hall's other warriors even if injured.

[Runesmiths](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths) who craft Hero's Hauberk are usually careful to remind whoever acquires it that the armour won't stop them getting wounded, only from feeling the effect of the wound. There are cautionary tales of warriors who dropped dead suddenly after a battle having failed to visit a grimnir because their Hauberk allowed them to ignore the traumatic wound they had received.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [heavy armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#heavy-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain one additional rank of endurance. The armour counts as a source of spiritual strength for overcoming roleplaying effects caused by traumatic wounds.
- **Materials:** Crafting the Hero's Hauberk requires seven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), two ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), and two measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
