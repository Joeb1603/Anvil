# Dissipating Silks

## Description

*[Image: Bolstered by this armour, a battlemage can push aside the pain of a serious injury until they have time to deal with it.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Dissipating_Silks.png)*

Dissipating Silks are most commonly crafted as a set of pauldrons, vambraces, and a circlet and often see extensive use [runes](../lore/Runes.md). The schema was originally added to Runesmith's law by *Arethas of the Spire of the Stallion's Volition*, following the [consecration](../../../in-character/synod-judgements/388YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-8) of Elos Harbour in recognition of the [Fist of the Mountains'](../../government/military/Fist_of_the_Mountains.md) sacrifice.

Arethas rallied dozens of Urizen magicians to work together to create a copy of the rare schema, making use of the [great foundries in Zenith](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md#crucible-of-fate). This powerful [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour) can be worn by magicians taking the battlefield to provide them with a measure of protection from deadly blows.

Different runesmiths naturally favour different runes, but the most common is the rune of [Purity](../lore/Cavul.md), similar to its use in [Bloodfeather Harness](Bloodfeather_Harness.md). Likewise, the rune of [Battle](../lore/Jotra.md), for its more clearly physical nature. An artisan crafting a set for a [dramaturgist](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) might draw inspiration and use representations of [the Blade](../lore/The_Blade.md) or [the Cloak](../lore/The_Cloak.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may ignore the *roleplaying effects* of any single [traumatic wound](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat.md#traumatic-wounds) until the end of the battle.
- **Materials:** Crafting a suit of Dissipating Silks requires no special materials. It takes two months to make one of these items.

*[Image: Pallas.png](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Pallas.png)*

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
