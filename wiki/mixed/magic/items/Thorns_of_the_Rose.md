# Thorns of the Rose

## Description

These arcane weapons are reasonably rare; they are powerful only in the hands of a warrior who combines a deep understanding of [offensive magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#offensive-spells), including the ability to cast the spell [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) with the puissant drive of the true [hero](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points). In the right hands, however, they are deadly. By binding an opponent in place with her magic, the wielder has several heartbeats in which they can use their advantage to deliver a crippling blow or a mortal strike.

There are more efficient weapons that can deliver [crippling strikes](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) against an enemy - the [Bravo's Blade](Bravo-s_Blade.md) or [Labyrinth's Gate](Labyrinth-s_Gate.md) come to mind - but using these weapons effectively requires a certain discipline and elegance that appeals to some expert warrior-magicians.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. A pair consisting of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons) and either a [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods) or a [wand](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#wands). You must be wielding both the weapon and the implement to use the set's magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [ambidexterity](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#ambidexterity) and [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skills to bond to these items.
- **Effect:** After you successfully [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) a character, you may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) to call [IMPALE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) when you hit the entangled character with this weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting the Thorns of the Rose requires twelve ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), seven measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), five ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make a pair of these items.
