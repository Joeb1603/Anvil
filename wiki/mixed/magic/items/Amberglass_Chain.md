# Amberglass Chain

## Description

*[Image: These staves are often decorated or wound with chains of amber or gold.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Amberglass_Chain.jpg)*

The most cunning [artificers](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artificer) create these staves so that they appear to be rigid chains of translucent amber or glass. The majority however take the form of a shaft of wood or metal wound around with amber or golden chains. They are sometimes favoured by bounty hunters and thief-takers who use them to hold fugitives in place so they can be arrested, or in battles with fast-moving skirmishers to pin them down and speed their eventual defeat.

The [rune](../lore/Runes.md) [Hirmok](../lore/Hirmok.md), the rune of Dominion, is most commonly used on these staves, but some bear [Bravash](../lore/Bravash.md), the rune of Fertility, to command nearby plant-life to grasp at the target. Highly Lineaged [Briars](../lore/Briar.md) have complained that those staves cause their thorns to twitch uncomfortably when they call upon the enchantment, as if also affected by Bravash's call.

Some [magicians](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) keep the power of the Chain in reserve until they have expended all other resources and find themselves needing to flee an opponent, earning the staff the name *Hopeless Headstart*. The most notorious use of this staff was at the loss of the [Mournwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_territories.md#mournwold-the-mourn-lost), where one of the [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) battle-mages used the Chain on their own commander during the rout, distracting the [orcish](../../../in-character/barbarians/Barbarian.md) pursuers long enough to allow the magician's escape. The treacherous mage was arrested and executed after receiving a damning [Testimony](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#testimony) to their lack of [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staffs). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Twice per day you may cast the [entangle](../spells/Entangle.md) spell as if you know it without expending any mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Amberglass Chain requires nine measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
