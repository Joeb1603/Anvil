# Captain's Garb

*[Image: The Captain's Garb in the Varushkan style](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Raiment_Captain.png)*

## Description

The Captain's Garb is a suit of magical clothing with a distinctly martial look, infused with a little of the power of the [dramaturgical](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) [persona](../lore/Dramaturgy.md#the-personae) of [the Captain](../lore/The_Captain.md). A [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) who wears this clothing can draw on the power it contains to empower their ability to perform [Summer magic](../../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md). In addition to providing additional magical power, the robes also infuse the wearer with some of the personality of the Captain.

As a garment infused with the essence of the Captain, the robe also empowers the wearer's awareness of their own heroic qualities, helping them to stand firm and giving a gentle reminder of what is important – their friends.

The raiment itself is generally woven from wool, carefully [bleached](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and dyed with [vibrant colours](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and decorated with [sungold](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) embroidery. They invariably include an amount of both soft leather and [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) incorporated into the embellishments or used to provide additional shape for the costume. The clothes tend to have a martial cut, designed to allow the wearer to move freely, reflecting the role of the Captain as a figure who takes action. The Captain's Raiment tends to reflect the fashions of [the League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) and [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), but designs also exist for an even more practical outfit employing [Marcher](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) style, dubbed the *Mummer's Yeoman*, and a version cut to fit the aesthetics of a wealthy [Steinr](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Steinr.md) dubbed the *Hero's Tunic*.

While the Captain's Garb provides no protection in itself, it is an easy matter for the costume to be created with an eye to being worn under a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour), leaving space to integrate pauldrons, pectoral, belt, circlet, vambraces, gorget, or greaves. In this way, a magician who also fights the enemies of the Empire with [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods) or [staff](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#staff) can continue to draw on the power of the Captain infused into the robe.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [robe](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing this robe to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use this robe to gain two additional ranks of [Summer Lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore) for the purposes of performing a single ritual, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Roleplaying Effect:** While wearing this robe you feel an urge to behave in an honourable fashion, and to risk any danger to aid your friends and allies.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Captain's Garb requires twelve ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), five ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and eleven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.

> **OOC Note**  
>
> This is one of six sets of ritual robes designed by *House Bourné*, a [Sarvosan](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) guild formed by the union of [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) master-artisans and a [Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [weaver cabal](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weaver_cabal.md). Their unique designs combine the power of [dramaturgy](../lore/Dramaturgy.md) with a deep understanding of the hearth magic of [girding](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#girding). During Winter 380YE, in conjunction with popular publication The Looking Glass, the Merchant-Prince *Genia Bourné di Sarvos* announced that the guild would allow any Imperial [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) to visit their estate in Sarvos and study the [schemata](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) detailing the six raiments, free of charge. House Bourné is an NPC group and is not represented at Anvil.
>
> Any character can learn how to create this raiment following the normal rules - either by selecting the robe as one of their starting items, or learning it as an [extra item pick](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#extra-item). The only restriction is that anyone who knows how to make one of these robes *must* have spent a week or two studying at House Bourné in Sarvos - there is currently no other way to master the patterns which remain the proprietary knowledge of the Bourné guild.
