# Grimnir's Hearthfire

## Description

This short ashen wand is polished and coloured, then wound around with weltsilver strands. It is a powerful aid to a battlefield magician, allowing them to more quickly restore those who have taken serious wounds. As the name implies, this wand is closely associated with the grimnir, the dedicated healers of Wintermark. It is rare for a grimnir to commission such an item themselves. More often, grateful Thanes or warriors will present them as gifts to a grimnir who has saved their lives. Traditionally the [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) for the wand will be smelted in a fire begun from the embers of the hearth in the intended grimnir's hall, to strengthen the eventual bond between magician and wand.

In the Marches, the wand is known as *Fletcher's Bane* as it is often used to treat individuals who have been repeatedly hit by arrows. Indeed, the wands themselves are sometimes made of old arrow-shafts with [Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md), the rune of blood, repeated over the entire surface.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [wand](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#wands). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you cast (but not [swift cast](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#swift-casting)) the [heal](../spells/Heal.md) spell, you also [restore](../spells/Restore_limb.md) any and all of the target's limbs which have been crippled with [cleave](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#cleave) or [impale](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Grimnir's Hearthfire requires sixteen ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), nine measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and seven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
