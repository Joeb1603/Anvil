# Mask of Gold and Lead

## Description

*[Image: Transmutation leads to transformation leads to transcendence.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MaskofGold.jpg)*

The Mask of Gold and Lead empowers a ritualist with the ability to transmute raw materials. The mask itself is almost always metallic in nature, often set with jewels. All the materials used to make it are repeatedly purified in baths of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and some of the finest masks are said to be constructed with substances that have been themselves subjected to alchemical transformations. It is rare to find a Mask of Gold and Lead that does not incorporate [Xun](../lore/Xun.md), the Rune of Transformation, often on the forehead of the mask. The rune is often concealed, however - worked into a decorative design, or inscribed on the inside of the mask. While the mask usually covers the entire face, another common design is that of a lower face mask (covering the mouth, nose and chin), often with simple filters incorporated to protect the wearer when they interact with dangerous substances.

In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), the [Kallavesi](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesi.md) favour masks in the form of ravens or magpies (for their connection to the Imperial virtues of [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md) and [Prosperity](../../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md)), while [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) mystics prefer masks in the shape of squirrels or bees - both *Silver Squirrel* or *Golden Bee* are names associated with this magical item. In both [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), specially prepared veils weighted with [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and coloured with [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) are employed, wrapped around the face and head either as a shroud or a tagelmust.

These talismans are sometimes referred to as an *Alchemist's Refrain*. While they usually take the form of masks, a few of these [foci](../../../indexes/by-category/Foci.md) are crafted in the form of a jewelled brooch depicting a pestle and mortar, cauldron or alembic (or a squirrel or bee, for Varushkan ritualists). A few very rare examples of actual tools exist - an inscribed metal flask or set of scales intended for ritual use in measuring or alchemically treating the target materials.

As with all the magical masks, these foci are used to grant additional power to perform [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) magic. In this case, the *Mask of Gold and Lead* empowers [the Eight-spoked Wheel](../../../out-of-character/game-design/Eight-spoked_Wheel.md) and [the Retrograde Wheel](../../../out-of-character/game-design/Retrograde_Wheel.md) rituals - both rituals that deal with the magical transmutation of one material into another. They also provide an additional level of power to the alchemical transmutations worked by the powerful [Amalgamation of Silver and Gold](../rituals/Amalgamation_of_Silver_and_Gold.md) and [Distil the Serpent's Stone](../rituals/Distil_the_Serpent-s_Stone.md) rituals, potentially bringing those powerful magical effects within reach of a smaller coven.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain 1 rank for [the Eight-Spoked Wheel](../../../out-of-character/game-design/Eight-spoked_Wheel.md), [the Retrograde Wheel](../../../out-of-character/game-design/Retrograde_Wheel.md), [Distil the Serpent's Stone](../rituals/Distil_the_Serpent-s_Stone.md), [Amalgamation of Silver and Gold](../rituals/Amalgamation_of_Silver_and_Gold.md), and [Emulsion of Lye and Lightning](../rituals/Emulsion_of_Lye_and_Lightning.md) rituals, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks). In addition, for each contributor to either the Eight-Spoked Wheel or the Retrograde wheel who wears one of these masks, three additional ingots or measures of material can be transmuted without increasing the magnitude of the ritual. All measures or ingots targeted by one of these rituals must be of the same type as usual.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Mask of Gold and Lead requires five measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), and three measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
