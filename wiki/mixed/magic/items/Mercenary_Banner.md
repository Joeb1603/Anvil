# Mercenary Banner

*[Image: Orc March.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Orc_March.jpg)*

## Description

Mercenary Banners are an old [magical item](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) whose invention dates back to the early days of the Empire. When the [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) joined the Empire, it was their [Sentinel Gate](../../../in-character/world/Sentinel_Gate.md) that allowed the elite forces of the Empire to be so effective on the battlefield. The only drawback was the way the essential nature of the Gate made life difficult for mercenary groups and other military bands. Such groups could choose to fight with their nation - or not at all. In response, Alda de Tassato, the infamous leader of the Silver Blades Carta, did a deal with the [eternals](../../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) of the Autumn for a year of service in return for help creating the [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) for the first Mercenary Banner. She named the item the *Gonfalon of Prosperity* - but today most folk simply call them Mercenary Banners.

Centuries after their creation these banners remain most popular among the [Free Companies](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md) of the [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) - but despite the name their use is not solely restricted to mercenary companies. They are also used by companies of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) [sentinels](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Sentinel.md), for example, who embrace the doctrine of offering assistance at the vital moment on foreign battlefields. Some bands may make temporary use of a Mercenary Banner to allow them to fight alongside allies when their nation is committed elsewhere - for example several companies of [Hercynian](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Hercynia.md) thorns fought alongside their [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) allies in [Skarsind](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) before that [territory](../../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) was originally lost to the [Thule](../../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md), even though the rest of the [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) nation was committed against the [Jotun](../../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md) in the west.

## Rules

- **Form:** [Gonfalon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#gonfalon). Takes the form of a gonfalon decorated with the [banner's](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#banners) symbol.
- **Effect:** A [banner](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#band) bonded to this gonfalon may travel to a [battle](../../../out-of-character/events/Battles.md) that their national banner is not attending.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Mercenary Banner requires eighteen measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), seven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), five ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and nine measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.

A Mercenary Banner can only be invoked during the [Muster](../../government/military/Muster.md) of the [Military Council](../../government/military/Military_Council.md). This is the point where the Imperial generals agree which nations will fight on which side of the battle. The enchantment is activated by the band member swearing an oath of service which must be accepted by the [Imperial general](../../titles/General.md) whose nation the band will fight with. Every single character that is a member of the band at the point where the magic is invoked is affected by the enchantment. From that moment they may only use a [major conjunction of the Sentinel Gate](../../../in-character/world/Sentinel_Gate.md#major-conjunctions) with the nation they have sworn to serve for the remainder of that event. A ref is required to activate the enchantment.

A Mercenary Banner allows a band to fight on Saturday rather than Sunday - or vice versa - it does not allow a group to fight in both battles. Regardless of any in-character considerations, all players are always obliged to follow the rule to monster one battle if they play the other.

The power of the Sentinel Gate is limited; to avoid disaster, the generals must balance the sides they will send on each conjunction using the numbers provided for them by the civil service. When a group activates their Mercenary Banner - the civil service will provide all the generals with updated numbers taking account of the *effective* change of nation by the band for that event. The generals can then balance their forces in light of the fact that the Mercenary Banner will be fielded alongside another nation.
