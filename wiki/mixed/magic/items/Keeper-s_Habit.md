# Keeper's Habit

*[Image: Getting into a Habit.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Getting_into_a_Habit.jpg)*

## Description

Modelled after the habits favoured by the [monks](../../../in-character/religion/Monastery.md) and [friars](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_religious_beliefs.md) of [The Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), the Keeper's Habit most often takes the form of a wide-sleeved, hooded robe. Generally simple in design, they are nonetheless woven from delicate lambs' wool and beautifully decorated with [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). These decorations often take the for of symbols associated with the virtues of [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md), [Pride](../../../in-character/religion/Pride.md), and [Prosperity](../../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md).

The vestment empowers the priest who wears it to strengthen their allies, inspiring them to keep fighting regardless of the odds against them. The healing power it provides relies on the ability of a [properly trained priest](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) to briefly connect their own spirit to the spirit of their injured companion.

As with many item intended for use by a priest, these vestments are often [hallowed](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md). Common choices include [Hallowing of the Cicisbeo](../lore/Auras_of_Pride.md#hallowing-of-the-cicisbeo), [Hallowing of the Little Mother](../lore/Auras_of_Prosperity.md#hallowing-of-the-little-mother), or the [Hallowing of the Steward](../lore/Auras_of_Loyalty.md#hallowing-of-the-steward).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [vestment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing these vestments to use their magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Three times per day while wearing this robe, you may use [get it together](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#get-it-together) as if you know it without spending any [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Keeper's Habit requires seven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), four ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), five measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), seven measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), four measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), and four measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
