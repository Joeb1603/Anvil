# Blackwillow Twist

## Description

The Blackwillow Twist is a flexible, resilient weapon whose head is usually made of greensteel (an alloy of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron)), and whose haft is treated with [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). In the hands of even a relatively green recruit it can render an opponent [briefly helpless](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown), allowing the wielder to strike while the enemy is off-guard, or make a swift retreat.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed spear](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-spear). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may call [STRIKEDOWN](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#strikedown) with this one-handed spear.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Blackwillow Twist requires eight ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and five ingots of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt). It takes one month to make one of these items.
