# Hero's Girdle

## Description

*[Image: A Hero's Girdle is a sturdy, reliable piece of mage armour that strengthens the will to fight.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:HeroGirdle.jpg)*

These sturdy suits of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour) help a [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) survive the dangers of the battlefield. In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) this armour has been used for centuries. Both the [icewalkers](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#icewalkers) who ranged across the north alongside the hunters and warriors of the [Suaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) and the warrior-magicians of the [Steinr](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Steinr.md) relied on this simple magical armour to strengthen themselves in dangerous environments. When the Steinr fell from the heavens, their [runesmiths](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths) combined their heavier designs with the lighter, more mobile designs of their new allies. Today's Hero's Girdle usually incorporates a solid leather belt (or a leather pectoral) marked with one of the runes of Summer - most commonly [Verys](../lore/Verys.md), the rune of Might.

These are among the earliest enchanted items mentioned in the annals of [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), as armoursmiths strove to improve their designs for [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour). This was one of their first advances, made from the hides of a hardy breed of goat which lived only on the highest peaks of [Morrow](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_territories.md#morrow), studded with gems in the pattern of constellations. Though other designs for mage armour soon surpassed it, the technique is still taught to apprentices early in their careers as a practice piece.

[Dawnish](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [war witches](../lore/Dawn_magical_traditions.md) often have Hero's Girdle bearing the [heraldry](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_culture_and_customs.md#heraldry) of their [Noble House](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Noble_house.md), while all across the Empire, [covens](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) with a love of battle have matching sets made, emblazoned with imagery symbolising their coven oath.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain one additional rank of [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Hero's Girdle requires no special materials. It takes two months to make one of these items.
