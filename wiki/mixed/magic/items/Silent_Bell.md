# Silent Bell

*This page details the magic item; for the Vigilant association see [Silent Bell (Sodality)](../../government/synod/Silent_Bell_-Sodality.md)*

*[Image: A Silent Bell is rarely a melodious instrument for creating music - it is a weapon for expelling malign spiritual presences .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Silent_Bell.png)*

## Description

This valuable item of priestly regalia is generally crafted in the form of a bell, gong, or chime. In some places, especially [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [The Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) it may be made in the form of a small drum, or even a rattle. Despite the name, it is usually quite capable of making a noise - its common name comes from the idea that the bell is experienced on a spiritual level as well as a gross, physical level. Unlike the [musical instruments](../../../indexes/by-category/Musical_Instruments.md) that many priests use, a Silent Bell is rarely a melodious tool for creating music. It is commonly for creating a discordant racket, intended to drive away malign spirits and corrupting influences.

The Silent Bell allows a lone priest, or a small group of priests, to tackle powerful curses and evil spirits that might otherwise overpower them. It is almost always [hallowed](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md) with an aura that provides [Courage](../../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) or [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md) in the face of spiritual corruption, even when it is intended to be wielded by a priest dedicated to a specific Virtue.

Stories from [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) tell of a particular Silent Bell that is widely believed to be an [artefact](../../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md#creating-artefacts) - or possibly even a [blessed relic](../../government/synod/Signs_of_the_paragon_and_exemplar.md#legacy). Brian of [Heathford](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md#the-heath), the husband of a farmer from the village of the same name, was a renowned cook well known for his [Vigilance](../../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md) and [Wisdom](../../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md). When local gossip suggested that the misfortunes tormenting his fellow villagers were caused by the wicked spirit of a dead sorcerer, he marched down to the spirit's haunt - a burnt out mill - and led his neighbours in a three-day long chorus of the [rough music](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_culture_and_customs.md#rough-music). In the end, despite its best efforts, the ghost was sent howling back into the Labyrinth, but the stout ladle that Brian used to beat on his cook-pot for three solid days and nights without sleeping, eating, drinking, or breaking his dissonant rhythm, was handed down to his children for several generations before being entrusted to the [Threshers](../lore/The_Marches_magical_traditions.md#sorcery-and-the-threshers) of [Upwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md). According to the writings of Ander Thresher in 250YE, it still possessed a potent ability to aid the ceremony of exorcism and was imbued with a "singular aura". Sadly the item was lost to the Empire in 290YE during an altercation with a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) of [Agramant](../../../in-character/eternals/Agramant.md)-inspired sorcerers threatening [King's Stoke](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md#tower-march). Both the Assembly of Vigilance and the Threshers of Upwold would be keen to see the item recovered and put to good use, in the event that it is still in existence.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of [ceremonial regalia](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) or [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with the performance of the [exorcism](../../../out-of-character/rules/Exorcism.md) skill, you may spend up to five additional doses of [liao](../../religion/Liao.md) to increase the [strength](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#ceremony-strength) or the ceremony by the same amount.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Silent Bell requires seven ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), seven measures of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), fourteen measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), eleven measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), and five measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridscent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
