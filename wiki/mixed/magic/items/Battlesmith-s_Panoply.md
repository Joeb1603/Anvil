# Battlesmith's Panoply

## Description

*[Image: This potent mage armour increases both power and durability.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MasterOfIceAndDarkness.jpg)*

A Battlesmith's Panoply is a powerful suit of mage armour, granting both increased arcane power and durability. In [Urizen](../../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), where it is commonly called the *Warlock's Net*, it is said that this mage armour allows any mage to go into a battle unformed, focusing their strengths where they are most needed, being free to adapt and flow as their needs change. In [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md), a [runesmith](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths) wearing a Battlesmith's Panoply is often said to have forged himself for nothing but war, and that those who bond to such an item make a powerful fork in their skein.

These potent forms of mage armour are commonly brightly coloured, with multiple shades created by the extensive use of iridescent gloaming dye, orichalcum and weltsilver threads and the polished and subtly shaded ambergelt inlays used in their construction. It often bears both of the runes [Jotra](../lore/Jotra.md) and [Wyr](../lore/Wyr.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [mage armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#mage-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have both the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) and [battle mage](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#battle-mage) skills to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain two additional points of personal mana and two additional ranks of [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Battlesmith's Panoply requires eleven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#materialsorichalcum), seven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), seven ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), nine measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), five measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) and eleven measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
