# Sungold Basin

## Description

While it usually takes the form of a small bowl, a Sungold Basin is just as likely to be a goblet, cup, or urn. During use, a dose of [liao](../../religion/Liao.md) is poured into the container. When the user stirs the liao with a finger, the magic of the bowl heats it and infuses it with power allowing the priest to use their understanding of the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) ceremony in a manner reminiscent of the techniques used to create [dreams](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md#dreams). Rather than sharing a dream, however, the priest shares a little of their will-to-live with the target, bringing them back from the threshold of the Labyrinth.

The basin is usually metal, made of an [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) alloy with dragonbone inlay, or fired [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) ceramic painted with orichalcum-infused pigments. Commonly the item bears a labyrinth design on the interior, although when crafted by a [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) it will often bear a rune such as [Rhyv](../lore/Rhyv.md), [Lann](../lore/Lann.md), or [Verys](../lore/Verys.md) instead (or as well).

The Sungold Basin is relatively popular among [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) priests who accompany [champions](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md) into dangerous environments, although it also sees some use among those who minister to [League](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) [Free Companies](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md). It is at its most valuable among people who have easy access to liao, but where [chirurgeons](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#chirurgeon) are not common.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of [ceremonial regalia](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** When you use the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) skill on a [dying](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat.md#dying) target you may restore them to 1 hit rather than having the normal effect.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Sungold Basin requires five ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
