# Magistrate's Grasp

## Description

The polearm is very useful for dealing with skirmishers. The superior reach it grants also makes it quite effective for neutralising enemies armed with one-handed weapons.

It is also a common piece of equipment carried by the *badgers* working with the Imperial Magistrates. One of the first [magistrates](../../government/senate/Imperial_law.md#magistrates-and-trials), the [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) Arnost Whitehair, focused his attention on pursuing and capturing criminals. He equipped his lieutenants with these useful polearms, and the name has since stuck. One of the Imperial Magistrates used to take custody of Arnost's artefact version of this weapon, using it as both a badge of office and as a statement that they will commit themselves to the capture of fugitives. During 383YE, the artefact polearm was [cursed](../../../out-of-character/rules/Curse.md) by [Agramant](../../../in-character/eternals/Agramant.md); it has not been seen since although there is rumour that it was ultimately returned to Arnost himself in some fashion.

On occasions where a Magistrate's Grasp is loaned to a group of volunteers, there is often an impromptu footrace to determine who will wield the weapon - the candidate who wins this race (which often includes obstacles to leap over or duck under) in armour and carrying a polearm is usually the one who is bonded to it.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [polearm](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#polearms). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [weapon master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You can use a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero) to call [ENTANGLE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#entangle) with this polearm.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Magistrate's Grasp requires ten ingots of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), five ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and four ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron). It takes one month to make one of these items.
