# Dawn's Glory

*[Image: These potent greatswords help a Dawnish warrior seek glory.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:GleefulMelee.jpg)*

## Description

These great greatswords are very impressive when wielded by a knight in a harness of [full plate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#heavy-armour), striding across the battlefield driving their enemies before them. It is common for them to be granted by Dawnish [knightly orders](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Questing_knight.md#creating-a-knightly-order) to a new member who has just passed their [Test of Mettle](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_culture_and_customs.md#the-test-of-mettle). When given in such a way they are traditionally decorated with the [heraldic symbols](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_culture_and_customs.md#heraldry) of the knight errant’s new order.

They also have some popularity in [the Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) where yeomen with a sense of irony commission great [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) poleaxes called *Dawn’s Folly*.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [great weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#great-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [Weapon Master](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#weapon-master) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain one additional [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Dawn's Glory requires eleven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and three ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.
