# Runebound Ward

*[Image: Emerald Cambion.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Emerald_Cambion.jpg)*

## Description

A traditional set of Runebound Ward is crafted using [mammoth hide](../../../in-character/world/Mundane_beasts.md#mammoths) inlaid with strands of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron). Finished with a trim of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), the armour allows quick movement on the battlefield, protection from savage blows ... and also inspires the wearer, feeding the fire of their soul to perform great deeds. Runes of [strength](../lore/Verys.md) are worked subtly into the overall design of the armour, along with runes of significance to the artisan or the intended wearer of the armour.

Traditionally a [runesmith](../lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#runesmiths) chants a heroic saga while crafting this armour, with the rhythm of the song helps guide the crafting process. In the heat of battle, some Runebound Ward wearers claim that they can hear that song echoing faintly in the music of the battlefield, drawing inspiration from the specific heroic deeds recounted in the tale.

In [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) Runebound Ward is commonly crafted in the form of a leather cuirass. Called *Elfin Guard*, composed of sculpted plates of dyed leather, it is often beautifully decorated and shaped to fit a specific wearer. A fine suit of this armour is popular with troubadours or senators who choose to take the battlefield, even if the magic woven into it has faded.

The [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) may wear fine Runebound Ward in the Wintermark style, but their artisans also create *Runehide* armour, working coiling thorn or vine patterns into layers of thinner leather, using green iron rivets and fastenings to hold the armour snug against the body. As armour that enhances versatility and provides options, without restricting mobility over much, it is unsurprisingly popular with the [thorns](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_military_concerns.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [medium armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#medium-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain one additional [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero).
- **Materials:** Crafting a set of Runebound Ward requires ten ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and four measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
