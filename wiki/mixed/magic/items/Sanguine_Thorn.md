# Sanguine Thorn

## Description

Almost always made of wood or bone, rather than metal, a Sanguine Thorn tends to be a tapering wand stained deep red with iridescent gloaming dyes. The wood itself is shaped and subtly influenced through bathing in beggar's lye, and wound about with orichalcum decorations. It often bears the rune [Cavul](../lore/Cavul.md), representing the power of purity.

Some of these wands, especially those crafted by [Navarr](../../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) or the [Marches](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md), have a sharp point near the base, usually a rose or briar thorn. When the wand is used in conjunction with a [healing](../spells/Heal.md) spell, the magician pricks their thumb on the thorn. This sacrifice is not necessary to use the wand, but rather a reminder that the power to heal rarely comes without a price.

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [wand](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#wands). You must be wielding this implement to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Whenever you cast (but not [swift cast](../../../out-of-character/rules/Spellcasting.md#swift-casting)) the [heal](../spells/Heal.md) spell on a target, you also [remove](../spells/Purify.md) the [venom](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#venom) and/or [weakness](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#weakness) conditions from that target.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Sanguine Thorn requires thirteen measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), ten measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), six ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and three ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.
