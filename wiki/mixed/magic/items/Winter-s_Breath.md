# Winter's Breath

## Description

Crafted from pieces of pale leather, or from thick padded cloth, with [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) fastenings, a jacket of Winter's Breath provides emergency protection on the battlefield. When far from help, more than one wearer has been saved from a cold, lonely death by the burst of energy this armour provides. Some [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) insist that the best suits of Winter's Breath are crafted at the end of Winter, soaked in snowmelt so that they are infused with the promise of the thaw. The harsher the preceding Winter, they say, the more powerful the resulting armour.
It is said that the first five-hundred Winter’s Breaths were made by [Sermersuaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_territories.md#sermersuaq) for her human children from the skins of her drowned children that became seals. [Suaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) seal hunters believe it is [good luck](../lore/Wintermark_hearth_magic.md#animals-and-masks) to wear a seal-skin padded leather Winter’s Breath. These are often painted with designs of swimming seals and the [runes](../lore/Runes.md): [Lann](../lore/Lann.md), the rune of bargains, and [Xun](../lore/Xun.md), the rune of transformations, representing the tale of the Sermersuaq’s children. Therefore they are often referred to as “Seal Coats” or "Mother’s Coats” among the Suaq. The latter makes them popular gifts from parent to child.

The [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) live in warmer climes and thus have less need of protection from the cold, and thus name this item the “Rainbow Coat”. It is traditionally made of 30 layers of silk, the wealthy dye each layer a different colour, with the outer and inner layers being heavily embroidered with weltsilver thread and [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) beads. It is popular among [corsairs](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Corsair.md), as they are able to swim in it should they fall overboard . However should anyone die while wearing a Rainbow Coat then it is considered [unlucky](../lore/The_Brass_Coast_hearth_magic.md#clothing) to handle, let alone wear. Often a [Hakima](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Hakima.md) will ritually cleanse the item before it is burned by a [sutannir](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Sutannir.md) who knows the rite of [Exorcism](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#exorcism).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [light armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#light-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you may use the [unstoppable](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#unstoppable) skill as if you know it without spending any [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Winter's Breath requires six ingots of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt) and five ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.
