# The Eternal Gambit

## Description

This coveted covenstone is especially useful for a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) containing members with a mixture of different lore specialties. It provides the capacity for the coven to perform an extra ritual from any [realm lore](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#realm-lore).

Covenstones can be crafted in a great variety of different forms but are usually large enough to form a decent centre-piece for a ritual. Regardless of the precise form it takes, a coven that uses one is careful to ensure that it plays a central role in their ceremonies. A covenstone is a potent representation of the bonds that tie a coven together and the power that their oaths allow them to wield. They are usually bespoke and thus depict [runes](../lore/Runes.md) and motifs that are relevant to the coven for which they were made.

## Rules

- **Form:** Paraphernalia. Takes the form of a piece of ritual [paraphernalia](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#paraphernalia). The item must be present in the ritual to use its magical properties.
- **Effect:** Once per day your [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) can perform a ritual that does not count towards your daily limit.
- **Materials:** Crafting The Eternal Gambit requires three ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum); seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade); three ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron); three ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver); three measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt); three measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye); seven measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone); three measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming); It takes 1 month to make one of these items.
