# Icewalker's Anvil

## Description

The Icewalker's Anvil is a useful talisman useful to the ritual magicians of [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md). The [schema](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#schema) was purchased from the eternal [Estavus](../../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md) as part of [her offer](../../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Inkpot_gods.md#the-mediators-bargain) to the mediators of Wintermark. Originally known as the *Dripping Ring*, the focus was renamed by the [Suaq](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) Æthwin who passed the schema to [Sulkavaris](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_Egregore.md#sulkavaris) to be added to Runesmith's Law.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per day you can use one personal mana as if it were crystal mana when casting a ritual.
- **Materials:** Crafting the Icewalker's Anvil requires four ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and three measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
