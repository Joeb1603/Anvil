# Isenbrad's Legacy

## Description

This smith's hammer is traditionally made with a stylised white head as if carved from a block of ice, a clear tribute to the Rime Hammer, the legendary tool that was created by [Isenbrad](../../../in-character/religion/Isenbrad.md) centuries ago. The power of the Legacy echoes that of the Rime Hammer itself, though it lacks the potency of its forebear. Even so it is a powerful tool in the hands of an artisan allowing them to recreate Isenbrad's feats in which he would create a item of power in hours rather than the days or weeks it would normally take.

It is often assumed that the Legacy was patterned after the Imperial ritual [Timeless Hammer Rhythm](../rituals/Timeless_Hammer_Rhythm.md), a powerful Autumn magic that produces a similar effect. However Runesmiths claim the opposite is true - that the ritual was created by magicians who sought to imitate Isenbrad's hammer. Given how old a figure Isenbrad is, this is certainly possible.

Artisans who craft a Legacy are often divided on the shape and form the hammer should take. Some prefer to construct a blacksmith's hammer, something with the ideal weight and length for working an anvil, so that they can use the hammer in their regular work. Runesmiths who like to take the battlefield often prefer to create a much bigger heavier hammer, so that it has the right heft to be used as weapon on the battlefield.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [tool](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirements:** You must have the [Artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#artisan) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Once per summit you may cast [Timeless Hammer Rhythm](../rituals/Timeless_Hammer_Rhythm.md) on yourself as if you knew it without expending any mana.
- **Materials:** Crafting an Isenbrad's Legacy requires seven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), seven ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), six measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggar27s-lye), four ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), and four ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.

### Runesmith's Law

This magic item is part of [Runesmith's Law](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_economic_interests.md#runesmith27s-law) rather than Imperial Lore. Any Wintermark character with the [artisan](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) skill can learn to make this item. Before they are able to do so they must swear a powerful *geas*, and if they later leave the nation they lose the ability to make this item.
