# Bear Claws

*[Image: In the hands of a skilled warrior these hatchets devastate enemy weapons and shields.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Kallavesi_Hatchet_Warrior.jpg)*

## Description

Often crafted in the form of two one-handed axes, these destructive weapons are equally effective at tearing a shield apart and smashing the haft of a pike or polearm. [Kallavesi](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesi.md) [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) devised these blades to allow warriors to mimic more closely great predatory beasts and thus more readily draw upon their power and courage. Although Bear Claws are the common styles, others are known as *Wolf Claws*, *Eagle Claws* or even *Boar Tusks* and are usually wielded with the [appropriate mask](../lore/Wintermark_hearth_magic.md#animals-and-masks).

These weapons are also popular in [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), where their power to splinter wood is appreciated in logging as well as in battle. Known as *Felling Hatchets*, bearing these weapons is sometimes seen as a sign that the wielder intends to battle human or at least human-like foes who carry weapons and shields, rather than [wolves](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushkan_Monster.md#wolves) or animals.

They are a popular weapon with a certain foreign nation, where they are often crafted as sickles or curved swords and called *Twin Moon Blades.*

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. A pair consisting of two [one-handed weapons](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding both weapons to use the set's magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [ambidexterity](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#ambidexterity) skill to bond to these items.
- **Effect:** You can call [SHATTER](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#shatter) against an item you hit with both weapons simultaneously by spending a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points).
- **Materials:** Crafting a set of Bear Claws requires fourteen ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), seven measures of [Beggar's Lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), twelve measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and nine ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make a pair of these items.
