# Defiant Steel

*[Image: Defiant Steel helps the wearer survive lucky blows that might otherwise cripple them.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Full_Plate_And_Packing_Steel.jpg)*

## Description

Weltsilver steel is pale and reflective, but also resilient and flexible. Such armour is effective at absorbing the force of a powerful blow that might otherwise cripple or incapacitate the wearer. Reinforced with green iron and orichalcum steel, and inlaid with ambergelt and tempest jade, the armour focuses the will of the wearer to allow them to recover swiftly from attacks that might leave a lesser warrior maimed.

This enchantment is unheard of on full suits of plate, which already protect their wearer from broken bones. Instead, breastplates accompanied by greaves or bracers are the most common form, followed by sets of breastplates and helms. The armour’s power helps to make up for the reduced coverage, a boon for those who prefer a lighter and more flexible suit of plate. Leaders among the [Freeborn](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [Kohan](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_military_concerns.md#kohan) favour this armour in the form of scalemail, calling is “Drake-scale” after the [great reptiles](../../../in-character/world/Mundane_beasts.md#drakes) which plague the region. Occasionally it is also called “Wyvern-scale”, as it is most useful to those without functioning arms.

Arm and leg coverings often double as splints to help the wearer push their limbs back into shape as they draw upon the armour’s power. Some suits are decorated with etchings of [Cerulean Mazzarine](../../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md) to draw upon the knowledge of that herb’s healing sap, the image acting as a substitute for medical treatment. Others bear [Xun](../lore/Xun.md), the rune of transformation, invoked to change a mangled limb to an unbroken one.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [heavy armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#heavy-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Twice each day you may use the [relentless](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#relentless) skill as if you know it without spending any [hero points](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero).
- **Materials:** Crafting a suit of Defiant Steel requires nine ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver), five ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), five measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), and five ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum). It takes one month to make one of these items.
