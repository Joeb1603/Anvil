# Alabaster Cerement

## Description

*[Image: These robes are modelled after the shrouds used to wrap the dead in Highguard .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Alabaster_Cerement.jpg)*

The power of this enchanted robe is to grant a devout wearer a great reserve of inner strength; they bank the fires of mortality, helping the wearer to endure pain and suffering that might overwhelm and kill other people. Also called variously a *martyr's shroud*, a *gravedigger's coat* and a *widow's (or widower's) gown* these robes are modelled after the shrouds used to wrap the dead in [Highguard](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) and several other nations.

In contrast with many other enchanted robes they tend to be sombre, with limited decoration. The cloth is bleached with [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), toughened with [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), reinforced with [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) and finally embroidered with delicate silver threads. Outside the [Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) they are rarely coloured, being commonly left white rather than any other shade.

Among the Freeborn, by stark contrast, they are usually dyed a deep red or yellow colour, and the sleeves and hems are much more likely to be ornamented with tiny gemstones. Here they are called *flickering shrouds*; while their association with death is not denied, it is celebrated rather than mourned.

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a [vestment](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour). You must be wearing these vestments to use their magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain three ranks of [fortitude](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#fortitude).
- **Materials:** Crafting an Alabaster Cerement requires seven measures of [beggar's lye](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye), six measures of [ambergelt](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), and three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes one month to make one of these items.
