# Bravo's Blade

## Description

*[Image: A bravo with a drawn blade is a dangerous sight.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Twilight_bravo.jpg)*

The Bravo’s Blade is a savage weapon, capable of inflicting devastating damage on an opponent. It is said that even those not from the [Free Companies](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md) develop the [bravo](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/Bravo.md#playing-a-bravo) swagger with one of these swords on their belt. However, bravos themselves are unlikely to draw one of these blades in one of their all-to-common tavern brawls because the blade is too lethal. This has led to the belief among warriors in other nations that a bravo only draws such a blade to kill – a story the bravos themselves only encourage!

There are tales in [Sarvos](../../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League_territories.md#the-jewelled-city-of-sarvos) of an infamous mage-assassin who wielded a Bravo’s Blade and [rod](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#rods). They first cast the [venom](../spells/Venom.md) spell then followed up with a blow from the Bravo’s Blade. The assassin wore a [mask](../lore/The_League_hearth_magic.md#masks) and was never identified, and became known as the Maledict, from the [potion](../potions/Master_Medicinal.md#maledicts-medicament) bottle left behind at the scene of every crime.

In some [Varushkan](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) histories the tyrant-boyar [Alderei the Fair](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Alderei_the_Fair.md) was said to have wielded an artefact-quality blackened sword with the properties of a Bravo’s Blade. This had led some [schlacta](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_military_concerns.md) to believe that they should never follow a commander that bears a Bravo’s Blade, lest they play some part in the return of the [dark king](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Alderei_the_Fair.md). This, of course, does not stop them being popular in Varushka. They are particularly valued among the [wardens](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Warden.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [one-handed weapon](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#one-handed-weapons). You must be wielding this weapon to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may spend a [hero point](../../../out-of-character/rules/Heroic_skills.md#hero-points) to call [IMPALE](../../../out-of-character/rules/Calls.md#impale) with this one-handed weapon.
- **Materials:** Crafting a Bravo's Blade requires twenty ingots of [tempest jade](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), seven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), seven ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) and five measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) It takes one month to make one of these items.
