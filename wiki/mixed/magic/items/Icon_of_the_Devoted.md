# Icon of the Devoted

*[Image: An Icon of the Devoted showing Cora Holdfast.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:CoraIcon.png)*

## Description

The Icon of the Devoted allows a priest to perform [ceremonies](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md) as if they were [dedicated](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) to the virtue of [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md). It generally takes the form of a piece of religious art, usually depicting a [paragon or exemplar](../../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of Loyalty such as [Bolstering Bill](../../../in-character/religion/Bolstering_Bill_-Exemplar.md). The icon is not always a portrait, however - in [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) it is sometimes crafted in the form of a medallion or short rod decorated with the bones and feathers of hawks and falcons, generally decorated with the [Rune of Bargains](../lore/Lann.md), which is known as an *Icon of the Hawk*. In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), the icon commonly takes the form of a statuette of a dog and is known as a *Loyal Hound*. Other less common variants are certainly possible - from the beautifully [calligraphed](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_culture_and_customs.md#art) scrolls of [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) to the delicately embroidered [favours](../lore/Dawn_hearth_magic.md#favours) of [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), the only constant is that the Icon of the Devoted be a piece of art that inspire the bearer with the essence of Loyalty.

The icon is equally valuable to a priest who is dedicated to one of the other six virtues as it is to a priest who is dedicated to none of them. It allows them to [perform](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) ceremonies such as [anointing](../../../out-of-character/rules/Anointing.md), [consecration](../../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md), and [hallow](../../../out-of-character/rules/Hallow.md), and create [auras](../lore/Aura.md) of Loyalty in place of the auras they would otherwise have access to. It also allows a priest to [cooperate](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#cooperation) with priests dedicated to Loyalty who are not part of their [sect](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects).

## Rules

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of an [icon](../../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md#overview). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#dedication) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You may perform ceremonial skills other than [dedication](../../../out-of-character/rules/Dedication.md) as if you were dedicated to the virtue of [Loyalty](../../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md).
- **Materials:** Crafting an Icon of the Devoted requires 7 ingots of [green iron](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), 5 measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming), and 9 measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone). It takes 1 month to make one of these items.
