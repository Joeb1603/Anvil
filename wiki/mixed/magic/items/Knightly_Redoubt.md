# Knightly Redoubt

*[Image: Ulric of the Shattered Tower, armoured in righteousness](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:WingedHelmet.jpg)*

## Description

Crafted of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum)-steel with [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) fastenings and decoration, this armour offers powerful protection on the battlefield. It is variously known as *Burnished Steel* or *Orichalcum Harness*, and is worn by serious warriors throughout the Empire. The iconic image of a Dawnish knight is a proud warrior encased in this armour, impenetrable to the brutal blows of barbarians. Such is the reputation of the Knightly Redoubt in [Dawn](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) that only those who are confident that they have attained great [glory](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Glory.md) will dare wear it. [Yeofolk](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Yeofolk_of_Dawn.md) or [knights-errant](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Knight-errant.md) who wear it are often seen as foolish upstarts.

It is therefore slightly aggravating to the artisans of Dawn that one of the things that the Marchers took with them during their [rebellion](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches_history.md) was the design of this armour. It has become a favourite of Marcher generals, though Marcher-made suits tend to consist of several vital pieces, rather than impractically elaborate construction of a complete suit of plate, and are generally less gaudy than the Dawnish equivalents. General Agnes Carver, a key figure in the [conquest](../../../in-character/world/Imperial_history.md#52ye-semmerholm-defeated) of [Semmerholm](../../../in-character/nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md), would tease her Dawnish allies by referring to her armour as *Nightly Doubt*, alluding to her troops' feelings on fighting alongside the Dawnish armies. Occasionally the name is still used but is seen as a slur against Dawn and a sign of disloyalty to the Empire as a whole. More polite Marchers refer to it as *[Mournwold](../../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md) Mail* in reference to the bounteous mines of Mournwold that produce many of the raw materials used in its production.

The armour is also very popular with the [Highborn](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [cataphracts](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_military_concerns.md), who care little for the rivalry between Dawnish and Marcher. Often beautifully lacquered and decorated and almost invariably with a distinctive [winged helmet](../../look-and-feel/Highguard_look_and_feel.md#armour), the armour is sometimes called the *Final Redoubt* or the *Armour of Pride*. It is widely considered the pinnacle of the armoursmith's art, and suits of armour are passed down within a [chapter](../../../in-character/nations/highguard/Chapter.md), being carefully reforged each year. A rare few of these suits ended up being given as gifts to the [Imperial Orcs](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md), who consider them items of great [worth](../../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Worth.md).

## Rules

- **Form:** Armour. Takes the form of a suit of [heavy armour](../../../out-of-character/rules/Weapons_-_armour.md#heavy-armour). You must be wearing this armour to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** Any character can bond to this item.
- **Effect:** You gain two additional ranks of the [endurance](../../../out-of-character/rules/Combat_skills.md#endurance) skill.
- **Materials:** Crafting a suit of Knightly Redoubt requires eleven ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and five ingots of [weltsilver](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver). It takes one month to make one of these items.
