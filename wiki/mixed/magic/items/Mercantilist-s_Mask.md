# Mercantilist's Mask

*[Image: A fish is a fine metaphor for the pursuit of wealth.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MercMurlocMask.jpg)*

## Description

The Mercantilist's Mask provides the wearer with a greater understanding of the tides and rivers of wealth that flow through the Empire and beyond; they learn to better manipulate such forces to benefit business. It is rare for one of these masks not to incorporate a [Wintermark](../../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) rune - most often [Pallas](../lore/Pallas.md) the Rune of Wealth. Some [artisans](../../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) make use of subtle [Queros](../lore/Queros.md) or serendipitous [Ull](../lore/Ull.md). Outside Wintermark, the rune is often inscribed on the interior of the mask, often in such a way that it will rest just above the lips of the wearer.

These masks are seldom opulent or bejewelled, instead preferring a subtle touch of gold trim or silver inlay on a plain and well crafted base. Coins are an obvious motif to include when fashioning such an item; in [the Brass Coast](../../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) a popular form of this mask is a lower-face veil composed of two dozen ring coins bound together with [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) wires called (unsurprisingly) a *Veil of Rings*.

Fish are sometimes used - the scales of a fish can appear metallic, and they navigate literal tides in the same way the magician seeks to navigate metaphoric economic tides (as well as having obvious resonance with the [Winds of Fortune](../rituals/Winds_of_Fortune.md) ritual). In [Varushka](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) the mask may be engraved with [bees](../../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_religious_beliefs.md#the-virtuous-animals), but a mask in the shape of a squirrel may also work well with the magic employed (the squirrel is wise, but it also collects and stores nuts in a way that has obvious resonance to the behaviour of a business owner).

As with all the magical masks, these [foci](../../../indexes/by-category/Foci.md) are used to grant additional power to perform [ritual](../../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) magic. In this case, the Mercantilist's Mask empowers the [Streams of Silver](../rituals/Streams_of_Silver.md), [Rivers of Gold](../rituals/Rivers_of_Gold.md), and [Winds of Fortune](../rituals/Winds_of_Fortune.md) rituals. All three rituals deal with the accumulation of wealth; subtly nudging synchronicity to ensure a steady flow of coins into the pockets of a [business](../../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) owner.

By itself, it will allow even a novice autumn ritualist to perform Streams of Silver without having mastered it and without the assistance of a coven. It is sufficient to empower that simple ritual enough to let a ritualist add one or two additional businesses to a performance depending on the mastery of the wearer. It is not quite so impactful when used to perform Rivers of Gold or Winds of Fortune - in theory, it might allow a master of Autumn magic who is familiar with the ritual to perform it by themselves (perhaps one who has access to an appropriate [potion](../potions/Unguents_of_Falling_Leaves.md#bronze-spider-balm) or [ritual staff](Melancholic_Staff.md)). More likely it will help a [coven](../../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#coven) gather sufficient power to perform the ritual with limited mastery, or [enchant](../../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) an [additional business](../rituals/Rivers_of_Gold.md#additional-targets) or [fleet](../rituals/Winds_of_Fortune.md#additional-targets) that might otherwise be beyond their power with a single performance.

## Rules

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain 1 rank for the [Streams of Silver](../rituals/Streams_of_Silver.md), [Rivers of Gold](../rituals/Rivers_of_Gold.md), and [Winds of Fortune](../rituals/Winds_of_Fortune.md) rituals, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).
- **Materials:** Crafting a Mercantilist's Mask requires five ingots of [orichalcum](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), three measures of [dragonbone](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), and three measures of [iridescent gloaming](../../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming). It takes one month to make one of these items.
