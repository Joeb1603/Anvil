# Keeper of the Golden Fields

## Overview

*[Image: The golden fields of Hay are watched over by a Silent Giant .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:GoldenFields.jpg)*

The Keeper of the Golden Fields is an [Imperial Title](Imperial_title.md) created by a [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_Golden_Fields.md) by the [Marcher](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [Civilian Commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) in Autumn 380YE. The position is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of crystal mana.

Following the Autumn Equinox 385YE **Nicolas Reaper** funded a series of designs incorporating [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) to track the flow of the currents of mana flowing through the Silent Giant as part of the opportunity presented in the **[Shining like fire](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Shining_like_fire.md#asymmetrical-curves)** wind of fortune.

The Keeper of the Golden Fields was [amended](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-landskeeper-titles), along with five other Marcher titles, during the Winter Solstice 387YE to make clear it should be held by a landskeeper. The motion also made it a responsibility of the title to look after the land and people of the Marches.

## Responsibilities

The Keeper of the Golden Fields has a responsibility to look after the land and people of the Marches. In addition, there is some expectation that the mana gathered from the [Silent Giant](#the-silent-giant) will be used to help the war effort - or at least employed in the protection of [Mitwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md). If the Silent Giant were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Keeper to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Mana

The Keeper of the Golden Fields receives an income of 11 crystal mana each season from the basins set around the Silent Giant.

### Landskeeper's Due

If they are a [landskeeper](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Landskeeper.md), the Keeper of the Golden Fields receives eight crowns each season. This represents seasonal gifts of food, warm clothing, second-hand boots, wicker baskets, ironmongery, small chairs and side-tables, and similar marks of approval.

## Appointment

The Keeper is a Marcher [national position appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position). Under normal circumstances it will be appointed by unanimous vote of the Marcher [senators](Senator.md).

Because this is a national position, the title can only be held by a Marcher citizen.

The Keeper has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die, step down or are [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).

The Keeper can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Marcher [national assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and the [Assembly of Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Silent Giant

There are many old standing stones and dolmen in [the Marches](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). The Silent Giant is one of the most famous, and one of the largest - a huge solitary monolith on the [Golden Downs](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#golden-downs) not far from the town of [Hay](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#hay). The old stone itself surmounts a low hill, stark against the skyline, and watches over the golden fields of barley, wheat and corn tended by Marcher [farmers](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md) below.

There are several stories about its origin. One claims it was rolled up the hill and placed there by [Bolstering Bill](../../in-character/religion/Bolstering_Bill_-Exemplar.md) and their mates as part of a cunning ruse to trick some bandits (or [orcs](../world/Orc.md), depending who is telling the story) into thinking that Marchers were giants. Another says it was actually placed by shepherds in the first days of the Marches, to watch over their flocks; these shepherds eventually moved south to the [Mournwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md), and their pastures were turned into fields of golden grain by the settlers at Hay. Another says it is an honest-to-goodness [ogre](../../in-character/world/Ogre.md), or perhaps a giant, turned to stone after losing a contest of riddles with a Marcher [landskeeper](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Landskeeper.md) and smith called Ironfoot Sean. A more prosaic tale says it was placed to mark a site of strong mana flows that served as a meeting place for the first landskeepers of Mitwold. Whatever the truth, the stone is known at least by name across the Marches - the popular song [Silent Giants](../music/Silent_Giants.md) is said to refer specifically to the monolith above Hay.

Since the Senate commission, the standing stone is now surrounded by five wide, shallow basins of black rock lined with mithril. The basins focus the mana flows, which crystallise in their depths. There was some talk of building a fence or low wall around the stone to deter thieves, but the local landskeepers put their feet down very firmly in this regard. Anything that separated the stone from Mitwold, even symbolically, would be a Bad Thing - and anyone who was foolish enough to try and steal from the Silent Giant and the people of Mitwold would find any number of bad things happening to them in pretty short order.
