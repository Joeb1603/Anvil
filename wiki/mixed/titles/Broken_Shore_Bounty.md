# Broken Shore Bounty

*[Image: The corsair families Pride themselves on keeping the Bay of Catazar free of Grendel .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:BrokenShoreBounty.jpg)*

## Overview

The Broken Shore Bounty is technically located near [Oran](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oran) in the [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) of [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md). Custodianship of the Broken Shore Bounty grants an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md); a seat on the [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md); and a seasonal bounty of valuable [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md).

The seat was [reestablished](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#reestablish-broken-shore-bounty) during the Winter Solstice 387YE.

After the Autumn Equinox 383YE, the Broken Shore Bounty ceased to exist, in part due to the [peace treaty](../../in-character/senate-motions/Ratify_treaty_with_the_Grendel_Autumn_383YE.md) with the Grendel. Part of the bounty went to the then [Imperial Fleet Master](Imperial_Fleet_Master.md), **Esteban I Del Rio Blanco I Guerra**, in recognition of his role in opposing the Grendel, whilst we maintained the position.

## The Resource

The [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) represent a powerful threat to the Empire, and there are always a few groups of disgruntled criminals or barbarian orcs who view Imperial boats and ships as easy prey. Only constant vigilance by Imperial [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) owners, especially those among the [corsair](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Corsair.md) families of [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), protect the livelihoods and lives of Imperial boat people and fisherfolk. Without an armed presence in the Bay of Catazar, coastal settlements and foreign trade alike would be endangered by opportunistic pirates. The Broken Shore Bounty is intended to reward the Freeborn ship captains who commit themselves to the defence of local waters, rather than to trade with distant nations.

The ilium that the Seat provides is claimed from the fishing nets of the boats that ply their trades along the coast of the Bay of Catazar; a portion of all such finds are claimed by the Broken Shore Bounty. While some fisherfolk resent the claim, the majority appreciate that the Freeborn are instrumental in helping to keep opportunistic raiders out of Imperial waters - that a portion of any ilium found is a minor loss compared to the complete loss of catch, ship, livelihood, and liberty that an encounter with a Grendel raiding vessel would represent.

The seat comes with a well-stocked villa in the port-town of [Oran](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oran), in [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md). The [corsairs](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Corsair.md) families of the Brass Coast see it as a point of honour to control the Broken Shore Bounty. The ilium is collected and overseen by members of the *i Orieni i Riqueza* family, who have extensive ties with fishing families and mercantile ship owners throughout the Brass Coast, and the southern territories of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md), [Highguard](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md), and [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md). The current dhomiro of the i Orien family is named Riguel.

## The Bourse Seat

### Responsibilities

The Seat has no particular responsibilities; they simply receive a bounty of ilium, which is theirs to do with as they will. If the Broken Shore Bounty were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Seat to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any particular [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were to arise related to the Broken Shore Bounty, the Seat would expect to be consulted as to how that opportunity might be resolved.

### Powers

#### Seat on the Bourse

Custodianship of the Broken Shore Bounty comes with a seat on the Imperial Bourse. For the most part, this Seat comes with no additional responsibilities. It is an Imperial title that grants the right to participate on the rare occasion when the Imperial Bourse as a body is required to make a decision.

#### Bounty of ilium

The Seat has control of a source of ilium, which they receive directly. They can dispense this resource as they choose. Production is 7 rings of ilium every season.

### Appointment

As with all ilium Bourse seats, the holder of the title is determined during the Spring Equinox. Control of the Broken Shore Bounty is assigned to whichever Brass Coast candidate receives the most support from Freeborn [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) owners via the [Tally of the Votes](../economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md). The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Custodianship of the Broken Shore Bounty is retained for a year, or until the citizen holding the seat dies, or steps down. An Imperial Bourse seat cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
