# Stipulator of Stipends

## Overview

The Stipulator of Stipends is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). It was established by the Senate in [Autumn 386YE](../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-title-of-stipulator-of-stipends) with the power to amend [stipends](New_Imperial_titles.md#stipend) of Imperial titles.

## Responsibilities

The Stipulator of Stipends is responsible for managing the stipends of Imperial titles that receive them. There is some expectation that they work alongside the [Auditor of Senatorial Accountability](Auditor_of_Senatorial_Accountability.md), but there is no requirement to do so.

## Power

### Set Stipend

The Stipulator has the power to announce a change in the stipend awarded to an Imperial title once each season.

To use this power, the Stipulator provides an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) to the Civil Service, who present the decision to the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Stipulator. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise their [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Stipulator's decision.

Every Imperial title is considered to have a stipend, though most of them have a stipend of zero thrones. The Stipulator of Stipends is therefore able to amend *any* Imperial title to receive a stipend. There is a season delay, the titleholder does not receive their stipend until the start of the next summit.

## Appointment

The Stipulator of Stipends is an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen can hold this title. The Stipulator serves for a year, and they can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
