# Celesti Lighthouse Keeper

*[Image: The Celesti Lighthouse sits high on the headland overlooking Oran.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Oran_by_the_Sea.jpg)*

### Overview

The Celesti Lighthouse Keeper is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md), originally [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_Celesti_Lighthouse_II.md) by the [Freeborn](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [Civilian Commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) in Winter 380YE. Almost seven years later, at the Autumn Equinox 387YE, **Garviel i Abrira i Erigo**, as [Senator for Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#recent-senate-elections), raised the motion to [repair](../../in-character/senate-motions/Repair_Celesti_Lighthouse.md) the damage done to the Celesti Lighthouse. The [Celesti lighthouse](#the-celesti-lighthouse) itself is built near the town of [Oran](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oranseri), in [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md); a stone tower topped with a massive beacon of polished mithril mirrors.

### Responsibilities

The Lighthouse Keeper is expected to ensure the Celesti Lighthouse remains functional, providing a beacon to sailors along the coast of Feroz. They would also be the responsible for dealing with any threats to the Lighthouse - or in theory to Oran itself - but would also to deal with any [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) related to the Celesti lighthouse.

## Powers

### Crystals for Celesti

The Celesti Lighthouse Keeper is a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md). The Keeper can purchase quantities of crystal mana from mariners and ship-captains that visit Oran, buying at a set price. The Keeper makes their decision how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

### Light in the Storm

*Fotismos*, herald of [Kimus](../../in-character/eternals/Kimus.md), has pledged to provide the Celesti Lighthouse Keeper with a fragment of the material from which the light was made, creating an unbreakable link between the lighthouse and the Keeper. As long as the Celesti Lighthouse Keeper is included as an additional target of a ritual, the effective magnitude of any [divination](../../out-of-character/rules/Divination.md) cast on [Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md) is increased by 10 for the purpose of overcoming shrouds, and any performance of [Piercing Light of Revelation](../magic/rituals/Piercing_Light_of_Revelation.md) has its magnitude increased by 30 for the purposes of removing shrouds or effects that obscure or conceal the territory.

*[Image: A herald of Kimus offers aid for as long as that eternal is not under enmity.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Kimus001.png)*

Furthermore, as long as Kimus is not placed under enmity, a performance of Piercing Light of Revelation specifically intended for that purpose could be used to destroy any curse of storm or ruin targeting Feroz - such as [Foam and Spittle of the Furious Sea](../magic/rituals/Foam_and_Spittle_of_the_Furious_Sea.md) or [Thunderous Deluge](../magic/rituals/Thunderous_Deluge.md) - provided the magnitude of the ritual is at least three times the magnitude of the target curse.

## Appointment

The Celesti Lighthouse Keeper is appointed during the Winter Solstice each year. It is chosen by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Freeborn citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies) of the Brass Coast, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Celesti Lighthouse

The Celesti lighthouse was originally constructed as part of the massive investment in coastal ports and defences during the reign of [Emperor Barabbas](../../in-character/history/Emperor_Barabbas.md). The lighthouse was intended to serve not only as a navigation aid but also to keep a watch for signs of [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) ships. As befitting Barabbas’ grand [ambition](../../in-character/religion/Ambition.md), the structure was significantly larger than was strictly appropriate to the traffic through a moderate-sized coastal town - making it an object of fun for satirists and humourists around the Bay of Catazar.

Unfortunately, when Feroz was conquered by the [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md), and ceded to the [orcs](../world/Orc.md) by the subsequent [peace treaty](../../in-character/senate-motions/Ratify_treaty_with_the_Grendel_Autumn_383YE.md#treaty), the lighthouse came under the dominion of the ruthless and avaricious *Governor Rahab*. Following the liberation of Feroz, the lighthouse was left in remarkable shape; the foundations remain, though the burnished mirrors have long since been looted, and it still serves as an intimidating sight above the cliffs of Oran. This did not prevent the Children of Wrecks seeking to [bring it down](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Send_for_me.md#butchers-yard-feroz) in Winter 386YE - another thumb to the eye of the Freeborn. Fortunately, the [Strategos of the Brass Coast](Strategos.md), **Gael i Guerra**, organised a warband of Imperial heroes to ensure the wreckers were defeated, and so the tower at least still stands.

In Summer 387YE, an [appraisal](../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md) by [Eilian Sweetwater](../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md#eilian-sweetwater) presented a [proposal](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/For_those_in_peril_on_the_sea.md#the-celesti-lighthouse) for restoring the ruined lighthouse to its former glories. The [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) approved [the construction work](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#repair-celesti-lighthouse), which was completed shortly before the Winter Solstice. The beacon now once again shines its beam of brilliant light across the treacherous waters of the Cazar Straits, and the mist-shrouded Bay of Oran. These beams can penetrate even the worst weather conditions, thanks to the inclusion of a [magically infused lamp](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/For_those_in_peril_on_the_sea.md#light-in-the-storm) empowered by the [eternal](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Kimus](../../in-character/eternals/Kimus.md).

The heart of this lamp is the *watchstone*, installed by the [herald](../../in-character/eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md#heralds) *Fotismos*. The light is unquenchable, and provides several other benefits. Everyone in the Bay of Catazar knows exactly where they are in relation to this magical beacon. It serves as a reminder to hostile forces in the bay that the lighthouse - and the Freeborn nation it represents - defies them. Even the Grendel navies know the light is there, and cannot help but be reminded that no matter what they do to the Brass Coast, they will never submit. The light serves to raise the morale of the corsair families, and even has an impact on the pirates and privateers of the Bay.

It also helped restore the confidence of the common folk around the Bay that the Freeborn Corsairs are looking out for them. This allowed the Senate an opportunity to restore the [Broken Shore Bounty](Broken_Shore_Bounty.md), which had previously been lost during the invasion of the Grendel. It also created a unique opportunity for Imperial vessels to [patrol the Bay of Catazar](../economy/Voyage.md#patrol-the-bay-of-catazar), helping anyone who gets into trouble, and also being alert for any signs of threat or peculiar developments at sea or on its shore.

Obviously, if the Lighthouse were destroyed or looted again these benefits would be lost - one reason Fotismos in particular was eager to stress that a [larger, fortified lighthouse](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/For_those_in_peril_on_the_sea.md#a-vigilant-light) was a much better bet than the smaller, less defensible, version.

### History

The original architectural plans were put together in collaboration with the [hakima](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Hakima.md) known as the Celesti, ensuring that it was built with a distinctly Freeborn aesthetic. While the original lighthouse was portrayed outside the Coast as something of a white elephant, the Freeborn appreciated the beautiful structure as much for its aesthetic as for its value as a beacon. The most significant contribution of the Celesti however - and perhaps the real reason for their inclusion in the project - was the beacon. The Celesti’s home since the founding of the Brass Coast is a reclusive monastery near the summit of the [Gilded Peak](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#the-gilded-peak) in [Kahraman](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md). Here they conducted extensive research on the natural phenomenon that gave the summit its name, and the peaks are rich with [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) and mithril with which they crafted fine mirrors to recreate the golden light of the peaks.

Barabbas had originally planned to have the lighthouse lit in a dramatic display as his great flotilla sailed past on its way to conquer the Grendel. It was perhaps an ill omen that due to overspending and delays, construction hadn’t yet started on the complex beacon and only the tower itself was finished. With Barabbas' death just a few days after, funding for coastal projects abruptly stopped, and there was little interest from Senate in finishing what was largely seen as one of the late Throne's great follies. With no further money forthcoming, it proved impossible to complete the lighthouse.

For the past century, a small group of Guerra hakima have kept a large bonfire lit at night in the otherwise empty beacon room to maintain its function as an aid to sailors through the treacherous isles and sandbanks of the southern coast. Though using only a simple bonfire the beacon is nowhere near as clear or bright as was intended. Tourists and pilgrims to the site have provided enough funds to keep the structure itself in fair condition, though never enough to build its complex beacon.

In 380YE, Ahraz i Guerra, one of the remaining hakima of the Celesti, raised the funds necessary to complete the beacon and recovered the plans from the Celesti archives. The work was then authorised by the [Civilian Commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) for the Brass Coast, thus ensuring that golden light of the Gilded Peaks will, after nearly two long centuries of absence, finally be brought to shine out across the bay; welcoming those from near and far to the Brass Coast.

The lighthouse is situated near the farthest tip of the Oran peninsula at one of its highest points, and was engineered so that its beacon sat a square 500 ft above the sea. Making it visible from the mast of a moderately sized ship for some 30 miles, and enabling navigators to quickly calculate their distance from shore taking into account its known elevation. This makes it a useful navigation aid for those attempting to traverse the many isles and sandbanks of Cazar. By no coincidence, this also makes it visible to the taller structures in Siroc, Oran, and Joharra.

To ensure the monument would not fall into disrepair again, the title "Celesti Lighthouse Keeper" was created, to be appointed by the Freeborn senators. Keen to promote trade with the newly opened foreign ports in Rigia and Kantor, the Keeper was given powers of a ministry and empowered to purchase mana directly from ships captains, ensuring guaranteed prices on the docks of Oran for those willing to sell to civil service factors.
