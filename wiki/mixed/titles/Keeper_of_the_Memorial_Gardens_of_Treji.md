# Keeper of the Memorial Gardens of Treji

## Overview

The Keeper of the Memorial Gardens of Treji is an Imperial title [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_herb_gardens_at_Treji.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Autumn 378YE. The position of keeper is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of valuable herbs. Initially a Navarr national position, the title was [amended](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_Memorial_Gardens_of_Treji.md) during the Summer Solstice 382YE to transfer custody to the [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md).

## Responsibilities

The Keeper oversees the gardens that memorialize the Navarr and Imperial Orc heroes who died to retake [Treji](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Hercynia.md#treji) from the [Thule](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md).

## Powers

### Gardener's Bounty

The Keeper of the Memorial Gardens of Treji gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive six drams each of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md) and [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), and two drams each of [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), and [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md) each season.

## Appointment

The Keeper of the Memorial Gardens of Treji is appointed by the [Imperial Orc](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [senators](Senator.md). If the senator cannot reach a decision, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by an Imperial Orcs citizen. The Keeper has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Imperial Orcs [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Memorial Gardens

The gardens contain a thousand flapping pieces of cloth, each piece from a fallen hero from the battle to retake Treji, both Navarr and Summer Storm alike. Beneath each flag is a small herb garden of Bladeroot and scented flowers. The Bladeroot represents the early deaths of those who fought and the scented flowers chosen to remind those that smell them of summer. At the centre of the garden stands a monument to the fallen crafted from weirwood and enchanted ice. Atop the monument is a statue of a Navarr warrior sanding back to back with an Imperial orc from the Summer Storm legion, their bodies covered like tattoos with the names of the dead. Beneath it rests a plaque, memorializing the fallen.

When the gardens were first created, they were placed in the custody of the Navarr. During the Summer Solstice 382YE, however, custodianship was [transferred](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_Memorial_Gardens_of_Treji.md) to the [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). The [Constitutional Court](../../in-character/senate-motions/Scrutiny.md#constitutional-court) ruled that as the title had tenure, the keeper at the time the amendment was made - Ceinwen Eternal of the [Eternal Family](../../out-of-character/groups/Navarr_groups.md#the-eternal-family) - would retain custody until they died, stepped down, or were revoked. After this, the title would be appointed by the Imperial Orcs.
