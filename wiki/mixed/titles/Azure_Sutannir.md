# Azure Sutannir

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Cat's Graces](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Cat%27s_Graces_Sodality.png)*

The Azure Sutannir is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) responsible for overseeing a prestigious temple and performance space in northern [Kahraman](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md). [Construction](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_Azure_Chorus.md) of the Azure Chorus was [commissioned](../commissions/Commission.md) by Lorenzo “La Volpe” Macelliao von Temeschwar as the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) during the Spring Equinox 382YE. Built at [Braydon's Jasse](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#braydon27s-jasse) in [Kahraman](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md), the beautiful temple has been threatened by the [Jotun](../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md) several times, but still stands as a place of respite and succour for pilgrims and secular pilgrims alike. In times of peace, it serves as a venue for musical performances, and regularly attracts artists and audiences from across [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and the neighbouring nations.

As part of a project to protect Kahraman from raiding [orcs](../world/Orc.md), a number of [Faraden](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Faraden.md) *kamkrags* were built across the territory. Construction was assisted by members of the *Bataguin* family, and when one of them was [kidnapped](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Against_the_Jotun.md#the-falcon-kahraman), the Azure Sutannir **Mina i Yashum i Riqueza** stepped up to ensure their release. The Bataguin are not especially wealthy, but they are well respected in Faraden for their building prowess. *Erdene Bataguin*, the rescued architect, was particularly enthusiastic about singing the praises of the [Azure Chorus](#azure-chorus). As the Autumn Equinox 386YE approached, there was a marked increase in Faraden visitors to the area - musicians and other performers keen to explore [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and the south-western Empire in general. Like any Imperial pilgrim, the Faraden pay their hosts for their hospitality, increasing [the funds](#faraden-visitors) available to the Azure Sutannir (for at least as long as relations with the Faraden remain amicable).

Erdene Bataguin also [made a proposal](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Where_five_winds_meet.md#music-and-laughter) that they might help enlarge the Azure Chorus, provided any risk of religious conflict between pilgrims from the two nations could be addressed by the Freeborn assembly. The Freeborn Assembly upheld a statement of principle with a greater majority at the Autumn Equinox 386YE, and the architect laid out [their plans](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/All_the_wind.md#blue-flame-sutannir) shortly after the summit. **Mina i Yashum i Riqueza** funded the construction of the [Parador of Wind and Flame](#parador-of-wind-and-flame) and work was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 387YE.

The title was amended as part of the motion that [established](../../in-character/senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#establish-cats-graces) the [Cat's Graces](Cat-s_Graces.md) during the Spring Equinox 388YE. Before the amendment, the title was previously appointed by the [Freeborn Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies).

## Responsibilities

The Azure Sutannir is responsible for the safety and wellbeing of those who visit the beautiful [Azure Chorus](#azure-chorus) either as guests or performers. In practice, this often means they have a responsibility for looking after pilgrims of [the Way](../../in-character/world/The_Way.md) - whether Imperial citizens or not - travelling through Kahraman.

## Powers

### A Portion of Liao

The sinecure provides 9 doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) to the Azure Sutannir each season.

### A Fair Price

The Freeborn know that everything can have a price, and that includes the services offered by the Azure Chorus. Room, food, and access to the performance space all bring in a small income, and it is traditional to pay a priest for the spiritual support they offer. The Azure Sutannir receives a portion of the money raised at the Chorus, sufficient to provide them with an income of 4 crowns each season.

### Parador of Wind and Flame

Brightly coloured Faraden tents and marquees surround a beautifully designed parador, complementing it and combining elements of Faraden and Freeborn design into a harmonious whole. When it comes to [matters of religion](../../in-character/religion/On_Faraden_faith.md), out of deference to their hosts these western pilgrims avoid conflict. They enthusiastically participate in discussions about the things they have in common with the followers of the Way - their respect for [Courage](../../in-character/religion/Courage.md), [Loyalty](../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md), [Prosperity](../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md), and [Pride](../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) - and avoid talking about the things that create division - [Ambition](../../in-character/religion/Ambition.md), [Wisdom](../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md), [Vigilance](../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md), and of course [Justice](../magic/lore/Malign_spiritual_presences.md#vengeance). Like any Imperial pilgrim, the Faraden pay their hosts for their hospitality. As such, as long as relations between the Empire and Faraden do not deteriorate significantly, the Azure Sutannir receives additional benefits from their [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), amounting to a further 2 crowns, 6 random [ingots](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#metals), and three each of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), and [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) each season.

### Spirit of Cooperation

The Parador of Wind and Flame has cemented in the minds of the Faraden, and to a degree the Freeborn, that the Azure Sutannir represents the spirit of cooperation between the two nations. In conflict between the priests of the Five Flames and the Way, the Azure Sutannir's voice is given additional weight by the Faraden, particularly if they are taking a position of ensuring good faith, recognising the differences and similarities between the two cultures, and resisting calls to precipitous action.

## Appointment

The Azure Sutannir is a [national position](Imperial_title.md#national-position) which is [appointed](../government/conclave/Appointment_by_sodality.md) by members of the Cat's Graces. Only a citizen of the Brass Coast may hold the title.

The Sutannir has tenure and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Freeborn Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Azure Chorus

A beautiful temple of white granite and marble flecked with deep blue veins, the Azure Chorus stands not far from the ruins of Fort Braydon on the foothills of the Gilded Peak. Below the temple is a natural amphitheatre, augmented with carefully sculpted blocks of white granite to produce a curved, acoustically vibrant performance space, surrounded by tiers of seating with long sweeping access points for ease of audience participation. Performers are encouraged to demonstrate their skills, the acoustics not only being superb for the singing but also encourage others to join in.

Following the ringing endorsement of *Erdene Bataguin*, brightly coloured Faraden tents and marquees now join those of Imperial pilgrims, and there have been several well-attended performances in the natural amphitheatre below the temple. When it comes to [matters of religion](../../in-character/religion/On_Faraden_faith.md), out of deference to their hosts these western pilgrims avoid conflict.
