# Curator of the White City Museum

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Highborn Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Highborn.png)*

This title was [established](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_museum.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) during the Summer Solstice 378YE. The White City Museum was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 379YE.

## Responsibilities

The Curator of the White City Museum is responsible for maintaining the museum itself, and for acquiring new exhibits. Indeed, the intention was that the Curator acquire antiquities from across the Empire, establishing the White City Museum as a site of Imperial, rather than only Highborn, [Pride](../../in-character/religion/Pride.md).

In addition, if anything were to threaten the good operation of the museum, it would be the responsibility of the Curator to address those problems.

## Powers

### A Portion of Liao

The sinecure provides 11 doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) to the Curator each season, to be used as they see fit.

### Donations to the Museum

The White City Museum draws pilgrims from across Highguard and beyond. Some visitors wish to make donations to the running of the museum or offer new exhibits to the Curator to encourage a steady stream of visitors. The Curator of the White City Museum receives 4 crowns each season from these donations.

*[Image: Rebekah , Curator of the White City Museum](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Rebekah.jpg)*

## Appointment

Curator of the White City Museum is a national position appointed by the Synod. It is elected by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the Highguard [Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies).

The title can be held by a Highguard citizen. The Curator has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Highguard National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## White City Museum

The museum was established following the destruction of the [Tabernacle](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md#tabernacle) Museum of Antiquities by the [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md), and many of the original parts of the collection were items recovered from the ruins by heroic Imperial action.
