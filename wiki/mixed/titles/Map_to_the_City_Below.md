# Map to the City Below

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the League Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_League.png)*

The Map to the City Below is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). The [motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Chamber_of_Euphony.md) to construct - and cede to [Sung](../../in-character/eternals/Sung.md) - the Chamber of Euphony was raised by **Genoveva Barossa d'Apulian**, as the [Senator for Spiral](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md), during the Spring Equinox 387YE.

The Map to the City Below has a responsibility to encourage the spread of magic, music, and mystery in [Apulian](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md#apulian), and oversees the Chamber of Euphony. The title was created as a result of an opportunity presented in the **[The play that came to Apulian](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_play_that_came_to_Apulian.md#writhing-tails-flashing-wing)** wind of fortune.

## Responsibilities

The Map to the City Below is responsible for encouraging the spread of magic, music, and mystery in Apulian. In addition, if the Chamber of Euphony were to come under threat, it would be the responsibility of the Map to the City Below to respond to those threats.

## Powers

The holder of this Imperial title has two powers that they can employ provided that Sung is not [under enmity](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity).

## Favoured by Sung

The current holder of this title, Sofonisba Amilcara of the City Below, is *favoured* by Sung. As a result of this patronage, she enjoys additional powers above and beyond those granted by the Chamber of Euphony. If the League Assembly elected a new Map to the City Below, the eternal would choose who gained and who maintained these powers.

### Bound to the City

- **Sofonisba is "bound" to the city**
- **This favour *may* grant Sofonisba early warning if the city is threatened**

Sofonisba is now bound to the city and benefits from a unique gift from [Sung](../../in-character/eternals/Sung.md). There is some uncertainty as to what being "*bound*" to the city actually means.

The current belief of Apulian magicians is that the Map will usually be the first to know if the city is threatened by an eternal. Or that the Map will know about any threat as soon as Sung does. Or that Sung will know about any threat as soon as the Map does. At least one of those things is definitely true, possibly all of them. Straight answers are rare with some Night eternals, and none more so than the *Lord of Illusions*.

### A Panoply of Dreams

- **Sofonisba is high in the counsels of Sung**
- **This favour *may* grant Sofonisba additional information each season**

Sofonisba is favoured by Sung who holds the magician in high esteem. The eternal includes Sofonisba in her counsels which *may* provide her with additional information each season.

There is no magical basis for this favour, so it is not possible for magicians to analyse it. The Rainbow Serpent is open about the fact that they hold counsel with Sofonisba in some manner, but only Sofonisba knows what exactly that means.

## The Chamber of Euphony

*[Image: Nothing can be said that can't be sung.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Sung001b.JPG)*

The Chamber of Euphony is an intimate auditorium that was first proposed by [Actius of the Deathless Caves](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_question_of_loyalty.md#spires). It incorporates a stage and seating with strange acoustics that allow for beautiful, evocative performances of music that supporters find transcendent and spellbinding. Sadly not everyone agrees and some music critics have described performances of even the most popular pieces as "weirdly atonal", "disharmonious" and "the musical equivalent of taking too much goosewhisper infusion."

Despite this division, or perhaps because of it, tickets for performances are *always* in high demand. On most nights the mortal performers create something that is at least dramatic and impactful - whatever you think of the Chamber, almost nobody is left indifferent by a recital. On rare nights the music is an accompaniment by a dance by the [Dancer at the End of Time](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_fate_of_the_many.md), a peculiar herald of Sung who lives in the emotion of the music.

## Appointment

The Map to the City Below is a national position which is [appointed](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the [League Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies).

The Map to the City Below is appointed during the Spring Equinox each year. They can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
