# Arcane Envoy

## Overview

The Arcane Envoy is an [Imperial Title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md). It was originally [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-title-of-arcane-envoy) during the Winter Solstice 385YE but was the subject of an [administrative motion](../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-title-of-arcane-envoy) to amend its election method during the Spring Equinox 386YE. The Arcane Envoy serves as part of the Conclave to facilitate the proper endorsement of works of magic and agreements with [eternals](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md).

## Responsibilities

The Arcane Envoy is responsible for ensuring that acts endorsed by the Conclave are properly considered and protected against throughout means both in and out of the Imperial Conclave.

## Powers

### Endorsement

The Arcane Envoy is empowered to raise a single [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Endorsement](../government/conclave/Endorsement.md) each summit to the agenda for debate during a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). The Civil Service will provide the Arcane Envoy with a single writ - to submit their declaration to the Conclave agenda they should complete the writ and hand it to the office of the [Prefect of the Conclave](Prefect_of_the_Conclave.md) at least one hour before the [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md) begins.

Unlike a [grandmaster](Grandmaster.md), the Arcane Envoy is personally responsible for any [presentation](../government/conclave/Declaration.md#presentation). While a [proxy](../government/senate/Proxy.md) may be appointed to deliver the presentation, their declaration is recorded as having come from the Arcane Envoy.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Arcane Envoy is made by the [Declaration of Candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). The Arcane Envoy serves until they are replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).
