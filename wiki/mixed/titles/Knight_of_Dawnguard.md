# Knight of Dawnguard

## Overview

The Knight of Dawnguard is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md). The motion to rebuild Drycastle was [passed](../../in-character/senate-motions/Rebuild_Drycastle.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Summer 386YE. The Knight of Dawnguard is responsible for protecting the region of [Dawnguard](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#dawnguard), the Barrens, from any threats that arise.

## Responsibilities

The Knight of Dawnguard has a responsibility to protect the region of Dawnguard from threats. If the region - or its inhabitants - were threatened, then it would be the responsibility of the Knight of Dawnguard to deal with those threats.

## Powers

> **Suitability**  
> The Knight of Dawnguard is intended to be held by a noble: the task of defending an entire region from threats is not something considered suitable for a yeofolk. If the title is held by a character who is a yeofolk, or they somehow become one during their tenure, then the player must email admin@profounddecisions.co.uk to let us know to turn off production.

### Errant's Wine

The apothecaries of Drycastle have committed to supporting the Knight of Dawnguard with two doses of [Errant's Wine](../magic/potions/Philtres_of_War.md#skops-mead) each season.

### Fireglass

The town of Drycastle has several weavers who are proud of the fact that they have an Imperial title to protect them and their homes. The weavers of Drycastle have committed to providing the holder of the title with a [Fireglass](../magic/items/Fireglass.md) every year at the Autumn Equinox.

## Appointment

The Knight of Dawnguard is appointed during the Summer Solstice each year. It is chosen by unanimous decision of the [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a Dawnish citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Dawnish [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
