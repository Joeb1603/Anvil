# Wise Arborist

## Overview

The Wise Arborist is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of the [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). Construction of the Feldspar Distillery was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Feldspar_Distillery.md) by **Bleakshield Groza** as [Legion Engineer](Legion_Engineer.md) during the Spring Equinox 386YE and construction was completed shortly before the Winter Solstice 386YE. More information on the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) can be found in the **[Standing in the sun](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Standing_in_the_sun.md#herbsheart)** wind of fortune.

## Responsibilities

The Wise Arborist is responsible for aiding Imperial Orc herbalists and apothecaries and helping them prosper. If the distillery, or travellers using the distillery, were to be threatened in any way it would be the responsibility of the Arborist to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any particular [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were to arise related to the distillery, the Arborist would expect to be consulted as to how that opportunity might be resolved.

## Powers

> **Suitability**  
> The Wise Arborist is intended to be held by a [Yerende](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Yerende.md). If the title is held by a character who is not a Yerende, or they change their sept whilst they have the title, then the player must email admin@profounddecisions.co.uk to let us know.

### Precious Cuttings

The sinecure provides three drams each of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), and [realmsroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Realmsroot.md) each season, to be used as they see fit.

### Plentiful Orcsroot

The gardeners of the Yerende mastered the cultivation of [orcsroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Realmsroot.md) centuries ago. When an Imperial Orc upgrades their [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) they can choose for it to produce a single dram of orcsroot each season, rather than two regular [herbs](../magic/potions/Herb.md).

## Appointment

The Wise Arborist is appointed by unanimous decision of the [Imperial Orc](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Imperial Orc citizen. The Arborist has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Imperial Orc [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

### Constitutional Note

- **The appointment method of this title is unconstitutional, it does not follow a well-worn path**
- **The title can operate and be re-elected normally**
- **The title cannot be amended by the Imperial Senate without *also* addressing the issue of the unconstitutional appointment method**

The appointment method of this title has been determined to be unconstitutional following a review carried out by the Constitutional Court. The title ought to be appointed by the members of the [Imperial Apothecaries Guild](Imperial_Apothecaries_Guild.md). This status does not impact the use of any legal powers the position has and the title will be re-elected normally. However the title should not be used as an example of a well-worn path for a position with these responsibilities and it would not be possible to make any legal changes to the title (as the result of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or otherwise) without either addressing the issue of who appoints that title at the same time, or amending the responsibilities of the position so that it was clearly outside the remit of the sodality.
