# Keeper of Aurelie's Garden

## Overview

The Keeper of Aurelie's Garden is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) [commissioned](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Aurelie-s_Garden.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Spring 380YE. The position of keeper is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of valuable herbs. The garden is in the region of [Grovesyard](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md#grovesyard), [Astolat](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md), near the hospital Aurelie's Rest.

During the Summer Solstice 383YE, following some considerable confusion about how the title was appointed, the civil service proposed an [administrative motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Senate_motion.md#administrative-motions) to [Amend Aurelie's Garden](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_Aurelie-s_Garden.md). The method of appointment was changed to unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) and [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), in a similar fashion to the way titles such as the [Spider's Tollkeep](Spider-s_Tollkeep.md) are appointed.

## Responsibilities

The Keeper has no particular responsibilities; they simply receive a bounty of herbs from Aurelie's Garden. If the Garden were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Keeper to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Herbs

The Keeper gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). Each season they receive 10 drams of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), and 2 drams each of [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), and [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md).

## Appointment

The Keeper of Aurelie's Garden is appointed by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) and [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead. The intention is that the senators will ensure that a suitable contest to identify the appropriate candidate is arranged and overseen. Legally, however, all that is required to appoint the title is a unanimous agreement between the senators of Dawn and Navarr.

The title can be held by either a Navarr citizen or a citizen of Dawn.The Keeper serves for a year, until they die, or until they step down.

In accordance with its peculiar method of appointment, the title can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), the National Assembly of Dawn, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
