# Basilisk of the Bourse

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Assembly of Nine](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Nine.png)*

The Basilisk of the Bourse is an Imperial title [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_Basilisk_of_the_Bourse.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Autumn 383YE. The most significant [Imperial Titles of the Bourse](Current_Imperial_titles.md#imperial-bourse) afford their incumbents access to rare and valuable resources - [white granite](../economy/White_granite.md), [mithril](../economy/Mithril.md), [weirwood](../economy/Weirwood.md) and [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md). Whether by [success at auction](../economy/Auction_of_the_Seats.md) or [by gathering sufficient votes](../economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md), these citizens have access to materials necessary for [Senate commissions](../commissions/Commission.md), upgrading [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource), and a variety of other projects. The wains or rings of ilium granted to these title holders are legally their property to dispense with as they wish. Although [constitutionally](../government/senate/Imperial_Constitution.md) the holders of such titles cannot be subject to [Revocation](../government/synod/Revocation.md), they are not immune to the scrutiny of the Synod, including the power of [Inquisition](../government/synod/Inquisition.md).

The title of *Basilisk* is an unusual one - the heraldic beast has a [strong association with Highguard](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_culture_and_customs.md#icons-and-artistry) and the Virtue of [Vigilance](../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md).

## Responsibilities

The Basilisk of the Bourse is expected to advise Bourse seat holders about the [Way of Virtue](../../in-character/world/The_Way.md) and to offer them spiritual advice in their direct involvement in the economy of the Empire. They are also expected to oversee the use of Bourse resources and report to the Synod any concerns that Members of the Bourse are engaging in any activity that might [constitute a religious crime](../government/synod/Religious_crime.md) so that, if necessary, an appropriate [sanction](../government/synod/Sanction.md) can be administered.

## Powers

> **Bourse seat holder**  
> A Bourse seat holder is defined as the incumbent of any Imperial title that receives an income of [white granite](../economy/White_granite.md), [mithril](../economy/Mithril.md), [weirwood](../economy/Weirwood.md) or [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md). For reasons thought to go back to the formation of the Bourse under [Emperor Giovanni](../../in-character/history/Emperor_Giovanni.md), this also includes the [Master of the Imperial Mint](Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md), and would include any other title that explicitly granted a *seat* on the Imperial Bourse.

### Inquisit Member of the Bourse

Once per summit, the Basilisk may issue a [writ of inquisition](../government/synod/Inquisition.md#writ-of-inquisition) against any [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md) seat holder without the need for judgement in an Assembly. They must inform the [Tribune of the Synod](Tribune_of_the_Synod.md) of their intention to issue a writ. The Tribune will update the judgements boards in the Hub so that other priests are aware of the action, but no vote is required. The Basilisk is not required to use their judgement, if any, to employ this power.

The additional writ of judgement is issued under the authority of the Assembly of Nine - it is escalated to a sanction in the Assembly of Nine if required.

## Appointment

The Basilisk is appointed during the Winter Solstice. They are chosen with a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of [appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

The title can be held by any Imperial citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and the Assembly of the Nine.
