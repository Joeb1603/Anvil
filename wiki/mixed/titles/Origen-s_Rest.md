# Origen's Rest

## Overview

Origen was a patron of the [Anvil Hospital](../../in-character/world/Anvil.md#the-anvil-hospital). After he stepped down as [Speaker for the Senate](Speaker_for_the_Senate.md) in 377YE, the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) [created this sinecure](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_conservatory_for_the_Anvil_Hospital.md) to continue the support for the Hospital's work. It was destroyed by the Druj in 381YE.

## Responsibilities

The citizen appointed to oversee Origen's Rest was expected to use the bounty of herbs produced to benefit the hospital and to treat their patients.

## Powers

### Bounty of Herbs

The conservatory produced ten drams of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) and five drams each of [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), and [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md) each season.

## Appointment

The Overseer of Origen's Rest was an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen could hold the title, and the overseer had tenure. They could be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Gardens of Origen's Rest

The beautiful gardens of Origen's Rest were destroyed when the [Druj invaded Zenith in 381YE](../../in-character/winds-of-war/381YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_war.md#valley-of-darkness-28zenith29).

## Recent Elections

There are no records that any citizen was ever appointed to oversee the gardens.
