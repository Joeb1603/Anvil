# Keeper of the Dour Fens

## Overview

The Keeper of the Dour Fens is an [Imperial Title](Imperial_title.md) created by a [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_Dour_Fens.md) by the [Marcher](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [Civilian Commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) in Summer 380YE. The position is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of crystal mana.

The official name given to the site by the commissioner is a nod to the work done by the Circle of Endless Night - a [coven](../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) of magicians well versed in [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) responsible for [protecting the marshes](../magic/rituals/Dripping_Echoes_of_the_Fen.md) during the war with the [Jotun](../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md).

The Keeper of the Dour Fens was [amended](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-landskeeper-titles), along with five other Marcher titles, during the Winter Solstice 387YE to make clear it should be held by a landskeeper. The motion also made it a responsibility of the title to look after the land and people of the Marches.

## Responsibilities

The Keeper of the Dour Fens has a responsibility to look after the land and people of the Marches. If Graven Rock were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Keeper of the Dour Fens to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Mana

The Keeper of the Dour Fens receives an income of 5 mana crystals each season from the caves beneath Graven Rock.

### The Weeping Menhirs

The Keeper receives three vials of [prismatic ink](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#prismatic-ink) each season from the menhirs that anchor the mana flows of Gravenmarch

### Landskeeper's Due

If they are a [landskeeper](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Landskeeper.md), the Keeper of the Dour Fens receives eight crowns each season. This represents seasonal gifts of food, warm clothing, second-hand boots, wicker baskets, ironmongery, small chairs and side-tables, and similar marks of approval.

## Appointment

The Keeper is a Marcher [national position appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position). Under normal circumstances it will be appointed by unanimous vote of the Marcher [senators](Senator.md).

Because this is a national position, the title can only be held by a Marcher citizen.

The Keeper has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die, step down or are [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).

The Keeper can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Marcher [national assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and the [Assembly of Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Beneath Graven Rock

The Keeper of the Dour Fens is a source of some humour to the local inhabitants - since the actual sinecure is located miles from the fens in "*the only dry spot in Bregasland*". In fact, the site is a reclaimed section of one of the old mine-workings that riddle [Graven Rock](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#graven-rock). This vast stone plinth, four miles to a side, stands in [Gravenmarch](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md#gravenmarch) in [Bregasland](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md), not far from the town of Graven itself. The sinecure that the Keeper of the Dour Fens oversees is not an easy matter to locate without a guide who knows the way, or a good map as several of the passages that seem to lead to it have been collapsed, or veer away at the last moment.

The rock contains several unconnected mine workings. This one, which was abandoned in 329YE, had a bad reputation. Workers would talk about hearing odd echoes and seeing peculiar lights - but the [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) ore here was rich and so these concerns were largely ignored. In the years since, this part of Graven Rock has continued to have a reputation for rum doings, given a wide berth, and said to be haunted. The [landskeepers](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Landskeeper.md) of Bregasland know differently of course - the reason this part of the rock has an odd reputation is that flows of mana from the western marshes meet those from the forest to the south and the hills to the east, and create peculiar eddies. These eddies have been harnessed before, and several local landskeepers maintain their own [sources of mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md) in the vicinity.

Taking advantage of these flows, the Graven Rock sinecure consists of several carefully positioned mithril mirrors, and three stone menhirs, all situated in a large, dark cave far beneath the surface. The standing stones are inlaid with carefully carved totems: a leaping salmon in the west, a broad oak tree in the south, and a proud ram in the west symbolising the marshes, the forests, and the hills. The walls of the cave are polished, and several dozen iron lanterns holding lightstones ensure that the cave is never swallowed in darkness.

Visitors to the cave still report odd phenomenon - lantern lights bobbing in the dark passages, the sound of picks and shovels, the coarse voices of miners, and the occasional rumble as of stones falling. Echoes of the time when the mines beneath Graven Rock were still being worked - yet without any suggestion of ghosts *per se*, as if the magically infused rock itself simply remembers a time when human beings (and [orc](../world/Orc.md) slaves) used to occupy the now-empty galleries.
