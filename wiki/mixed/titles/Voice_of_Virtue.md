# Voice of Virtue

This is a placeholder page for content that PD are actively working on.

## Overview

These Imperial titles were established by [a vote](../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#create-titles-of-voices-of-virtue) of the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) during the Spring Equinox 386YE. There are seven voices in total - one for each of the Imperial Virtues.

## Responsibilities

Each voice is responsible for acting as an inspiration to the pilgrims of the faith and laity as to what it means to be virtuous.

## Appointment

The seven Voices are appointed according to a schedule over the course of a year. They are [appointed](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the appropriate Virtue Assembly.

The title can be held by any citizen. The voice serves until the next scheduled election. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the appropriate [Virtue Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

### Schedule

The schedule for annual appointments is:

- Winter - [Ambition](../../in-character/religion/Ambition.md) and [Vigilance](../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md)
- Spring - [Courage](../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) and [Wisdom](../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md)
- Summer - [Loyalty](../../in-character/religion/Loyalty.md) and [Pride](../../in-character/religion/Pride.md)
- Autumn - [Prosperity](../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md)
