# Imperial Spymaster

## Overview

The Imperial Spymaster is an [Imperial Title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). The title was [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Imperial_Spymaster.md) during the Spring Equinox 383YE. The title was later [amended](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-imperial-spymaster) during the Summer Solstice 387YE to include responsibility for espionage and counter-espionage.

## Responsibilities

The Imperial Spymaster is responsible for the creation and decommission of Imperial [spy networks](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) and [smugglers coves](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md), including coordinating their use. The Spymaster is responsible for espionage and counter-espionage. They are expected to liaise and report to both the Senate and the Military Council on matters pertaining to Imperial spy networks and smugglers coves.

## Powers

### Networks and Coves

The Imperial Spymaster can [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) a single [spy network](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md) or [smugglers cove](../../out-of-character/rules/Smugglers_cove.md) each season. To use their power of commission, the Spymaster must make an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) in the Imperial Senate. No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Spymaster.

The Imperial Spymaster can [decommission](../../out-of-character/rules/Spy_network.md#decommissioning-a-spy-network) a single spy network or smugglers cove each season. To use their power, the Spymaster must make an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) in the Imperial Senate. No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Spymaster.

The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md), or [The Throne](The_Throne.md#power-of-veto), may choose to exercise their [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Spymaster's announcement.

### Right of Address

The Imperial Spymaster has the right to make a [address](../government/military/Council_address.md) to the [Military Council](../government/military/Military_Council.md) once per summit. This is intended to allow them to advise the Military Council on matters pertaining to spy networks or smugglers coves. To use this power, the Imperial Spymaster informs the [Herald of the Council](Herald_of_the_Council.md) that they wish to make an address, which will then be added to the agenda for the next [Council session](../government/military/Council_session.md).

## Appointment

The Imperial Spymaster is an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen can hold this title. The Imperial Spymaster serves for a year, and they can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
