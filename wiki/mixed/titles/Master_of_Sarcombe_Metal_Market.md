# Master of Sarcombe Metal Market

## Overview

The Master of Sarcombe Metal Market is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a [Marcher](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) citizen. It is a national position appointed by unanimous decision of the Marcher [senators](Senator.md).

The [Sarcombe Metal Market](#sarcombe-metal-market) was established as part of a wider scheme to revitalise the [market town](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Market_town.md) and the prosperity of the [Mournwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md). In particular, given its location, it is possible that if [Liathaven](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Liathaven.md) were returned to Imperial control there would be additional opportunities to trade metal with the inhabitants of the south-western forest.

## Responsibilities

The Master of Sarcombe Metal Market gets first choice on the materials that pass through the great hardwood doors, and as a consequence is expected to ensure that all materials are safely stored for the market. if anything threatened the vault, or by extension the market town of Sarcombe, they would be expected to deal with the problem.

## Appointment

The Master of Sarcombe Metal Market is appointed during the Winter Solstice each year. It is chosen by unanimous decision of the [Marcher](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Marcher citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Marcher [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Sarcombe Metal Exchange

The grey stone building stands proudly in the centre of Sarcombe. It is entered through a grand double door made of polished hardwood brought all the way from [Alderly](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#alderly), with mithril filigree depicting miners resting under an apple tree. Above the door a mithril plaque reads "Sarcombe Metal Market" and underneath "Courtesy of Applewood".

Much of the building is given over to a large hall that hosts the Sarcombe metal market itself. Underneath the market is a large vault, dug into the stone and made of the finest mithril to allow safe storage of the materials. Whilst normally hidden from public view, the great vault door depicts the Applewood livery etched lightly into the surface.

Miners from all over the Mourn come here to have their ore evaluated, stored, and sold; visitors from [Matilda Stone Quarries](../government/military/Mournwold_spoils_of_war.md#matilda-stone-quarries) and [Jonah's Lament](../government/military/Mournwold_spoils_of_war.md#jonah27s-lament) are particularly regular visitors. Traders from as far afield as [Kalpaheim](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#kalpaheim) and [Tassato](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md) make the journey here to purchase fine Mournwold metal.
