## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Marcher Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Marcher.png)*

The [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) [commissioned](../commissions/Commission.md) the [construction](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Honour-s_Rest.md) of Honour's Rest during the Autumn Equinox 381YE. Built in the [ruins of Overton](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#the-ruins-of-overton) in [Mournwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md), it was funded by the people of [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and given as gift to the people of [the Marches](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md). The Friar of Honour's Rest is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) charged with looking after and preserving the shrine which stands as a memorial to those who fell in the battle to liberate the Mournwold. The position comes with custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but awarded a regular income of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) by the civil service to support the many pilgrims who come to Honour's Rest to meditate on the lessons of the battle for the Mourn.

## Responsibilities

The Friar of Honour's Rest is responsible for the safety and wellbeing of those who traverse the [Greensward](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#greensward) to visit the ruins of Overton.

## Powers

### A Portion of Liao

The sinecure provides 11 doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) to the Friar each season, to be used as they see fit.

### Last of Overton

The *Last of Overton* are a small [sect](../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#sects) of friars who live in [Greensward](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md#greensward) and do what they can to support the Friar of Honour's Rest. The Last of Overton will continue to provide the Friar of Honour's Rest with 4 crowns each season to use as they wish for as long as Greensward is controlled by the Marches.

## Appointment

The Friar of Honour's Rest is a national position appointed by the Synod. It is elected by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the Marcher [Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies). .

The title can be held by any Marcher citizen. The Friar has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Marcher National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Honour's Rest

The church of Honour's Rest stands atop a low hill near Overton on the Greensward. Built mostly in the Marcher style, there are a few clear Wintermark touches including a series of stone Winterfolk warriors that seem to watch over pilgrims and resident priests alike from the white-stone walls of the church.

From the hill, it is only a short walk to the ruins, and pilgrims rest here on their way to visit the great barrow mounds raised by the [Jotun](../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md) in the wake of the fall of Orchard's Watch. Occasionally, the friars who live and work here are called on to address problems with angry or mournful ghosts that haunt the ruins, but for the most part Honour's Rest is an oasis of quiet, calm, and sorrow.
