# Stoat of the Sun

*[Image: The first Stoat of the Sun was appointed in Spring 388YE](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Bartholomew_of_Cantiarch%27s_Hold.jpg)*

## Overview

[Image: Caption2](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Sun_Chamber.png)

[Image: The Sun Chamber](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Sun_Chamber_colour.png)

The Stoat of the Sun is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by members of the [Sun Chamber](Sun_Chamber.md). The title was [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-stoat-of-the-sun) by the Senate during the Winter Solstice 387YE, in a motion raised by **Mel Bleak**, as proxy [Senator of the Mournwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mournwold.md)

## Responsibilities

The Stoat of the Sun has a responsibility to support members of the Sun Chamber in military efforts. They are expected to liaise with the [members of the Military Council](../government/military/Council_session.md#member-of-the-military-council) of their nation.

## Powers

### Address the Council

The Stoat of the Sun has the right to make an [address](../government/military/Council_address.md) to the [Military Council](../government/military/Military_Council.md) once per summit. To use this power, the Stoat of the Sun informs the [Herald of the Council](Herald_of_the_Council.md) that they wish to make an address, which will then be added to the agenda for the next [Council session](../government/military/Council_session.md).

## Appointment

The Stoat of the Sun is [appointed by members of the Sun Chamber](../government/conclave/Powers_of_a_sodality.md#elections) during the Summer Solstice. Only a member of the sodality can present themselves for the title, and only a member of the sodality can vote in the election. The candidate who gains the most votes from the members of the sodality is elected to the title.

The Stoat of the Sun can only be held by a member of the Sun Chamber. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
