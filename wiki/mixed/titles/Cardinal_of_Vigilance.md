# Cardinal of Vigilance

*[Image: The Cardinal guides the Assembly of Vigilance .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Cardinal_Vigilance_3.png)*

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Assembly of Vigilance](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Vigilance.png)*

Each of the eight [virtue assemblies](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) appoints a [Cardinal](Cardinal.md) to represent and lead them. The Cardinal of [Vigilance](../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md) represents the Vigilance Assembly, and is part of what is arguably the post powerful of the Assemblies, the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine). While they are still able to raise only a single [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) for consideration by their peers in the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) each season, they possess a number of specific legal powers that allow them to guide and influence the Assemblies.

The recent [Severin encyclical](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Benevolence-_inspiration-_and_legacy.md) prompted a [great deal of discussion](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Without_a_plea_for_mercy.md) around the role of the cardinals in the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).

## Responsibilities

The Cardinal of Vigilance is assumed to provide leadership to the priests and pilgrims dedicated to that virtue. They are expected to be well versed in matters of [Vigilance](../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md), and of [The Way](../../in-character/world/The_Way.md) in general, so that they can speak with authority on matters related to the study of Vigilance, and of [doctrine](../../in-character/religion/The_Doctrines_of_the_Faith.md). Prominent citizens who are guilty of breaking the law but believe their actions were rooted in Vigilance will often approach the Cardinal to plead [clemency](../government/synod/Member_of_the_Synod.md#clemency) on their behalf, so a familiarity with the law may also prove helpful.

As a member of the Assembly of the Nine, the Cardinal of Vigilance can wield considerable influence over the politics of the Empire. In particular the Assembly has the power to [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) any constitutional motion passed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). This allows the Assembly to uphold their constitutional obligation to "ensure the virtuous behaviour of the Empire".

## Powers

### Cardinal of Vigilance

The Cardinal of Vigilance has the powers common to all [cardinals](Cardinal.md): [membership](Cardinal.md#member-of-the-assembly-of-nine) of the Assembly of the Nine; the ability to call for [further scrutiny](Cardinal.md#further-scrutiny) on a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) raised in any assembly by a [member of the Synod](../government/synod/Member_of_the_Synod.md) (rather than by the [Tribunes](Tribune_of_the_Synod.md)); [appoint](Cardinal.md#appoint-the-gatekeeper) the [gatekeeper](Gatekeeper.md) of Vigilance and any other [titles](Imperial_title.md) designated as [virtuous appointments](Cardinal.md#virtuous-appointments) selected by the Cardinal of the Assembly of Vigilance.

### Appointment

A virtue title might be designed in such a way that it is appointed by the Cardinal alone. Currently they have the power to appoint the [Gatekeeper](Gatekeeper.md) of Vigilance, an important position that controls the distribution of [true liao](../religion/Liao.md#true-liao).

### Additional Powers

The Cardinal of Vigilance may possess additional powers, not shared with their fellow cardinals, either through the action of the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) or through [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented to them. Occasionally, a particularly vigilant Imperial citizen may look specifically to the cardinal for inspiration, such as happened recently when the Silent Bell [sought the approval of the Synod](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/No_evil_shall_escape_our_sight.md#embracing-the-silence). While the Assembly of the Nine ultimatley [went a different way](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/No_evil_shall_escape_our_sight.md#ringing-the-bell), the ability to direct these vigilant investigators *could* have been the [sole province](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/No_evil_shall_escape_our_sight.md#embracing-the-silence) of the Cardinal. Using such powers generally requires them to raise a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) in the Assembly of the Nine.

## Appointment

The Cardinal of Vigilance is appointed during the Spring Equinox by the Assembly of Vigilance, and must be a member of that assembly. Selection is by a judgement of [appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) raised by the Tribune in the standard manner.

The elections for the cardinals are considered to be legally equivalent positions restricted by virtue. This means that if you change your virtue assembly then you must abstain from voting in a election if it occurs less than four events after the previous election in your previous virtue assembly. If you are in any doubt about your eligibility then you should consult the Tribune, or Merlot the civil service overseer of elections who can advise you.

The judgement to appoint a cardinal will typically close at 6pm on the Saturday of the summit. These times may differ if an unscheduled appointment is required but there are always at least three hours for nominations and three hours for voting. After that time, the candidate with the most support is declared cardinal.

The Cardinal of Vigilance serves until the next scheduled election for the title. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), or by the [virtue assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies) of Vigilance.
