# Kennelmaster of Hay

*[Image: The kennels of Hay is home to fine hounds.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:KennelMaster_of_Hay.png)*

## Overview

The Kennelmaster of Hay is a [Marcher](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) created by a [motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Kennels_of_Hay.md) raised by **Sally B-Baker**, [Senator for Mitwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md), and passed by the Senate in Autumn 387YE. The position of Kennelmaster is responsible for ensuring good practice by the kennelmasters of the Golden Downs and protecting the Kennelmaster of Hay's Home for Wayward Dogs.

## Responsibilities

The Kennelmaster of Hay has a responsibility to ensure that good practices are met by the kennelmasters of the [Golden Downs](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Mitwold.md#golden-downs). If any kennelmasters from the Golden Downs, or their customers, had an issue with standards or practices, then they would turn to the Kennelmaster of Hay in the first instance. In addition, if the Kennelmaster of Hay's Home for Wayward Dogs, its workers, its hounds, or its customers were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Kennelmaster to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Profitable Reputation

The Kennelmaster of Hay receives a total of one throne, two crowns, and sixteen rings each season from the kennels.

## Appointment

The Kennelmaster of Hay is appointed by unanimous decision of [the Marches](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a Marcher citizen. The Kennelmaster has tenure and serves until they die, step down, are successfully [challenged](../government/synod/Challenge.md), or are revoked. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Marcher [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
