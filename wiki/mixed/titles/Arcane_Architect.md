# Arcane Architect

## Overview

The Arcane Architect is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md). The title was [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Arcane_Architect.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) during the Autumn Equinox 379YE.

When the title was initially created, it had an unlimited power to [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) the construction of mana structures. The Senate [amended](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_powers_of_the_Arcane_Architect.md) the power to once per season as part of an administrative motion.

In Summer 382YE, the civil service raised [an administrative motion](../../in-character/senate-sessions/382YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#administrative) to confirm that the Arcane Architect could commission sinecures that would produce [vis](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md), as well as those that would produce crystal mana. The Senate confirmed this was their intent.

The Senate voted to further [amend](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_powers_of_the_Arcane_Architect_Autumn_383YE.md) the powers of the Arcane Architect during the Autumn Equinox 383YE by extending their power of commission to ministries and great works.

The Senate further [amended the powers](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-titles-commissioning-sinecures) during Summer 387YE to include the ability to commission [wayhouses](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) and [edifices](../commissions/Edifice.md).

## Responsibilities

The Arcane Architect is responsible for supporting the [ritual](../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) [magicians](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) of the Empire in virtuous application of mana.

## Powers

### Commission

Once per summit, the Arcane Architect can [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) the construction of an edifice, sinecure, ministry, wayhouse, or [great works](../commissions/Great_work.md) that produces crystal mana or vis. In the case of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), [wayhouse](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md), or [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), this would create a new [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md), one that is appointed by [Declaration of Candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md). The Arcane Architect can only announce the construction of an edifice or wayhouse if it produces crystal mana or vis.

These titles must be Imperial titles, rather than national titles (meaning they can be held by any citizen). As with any other title appointed through the Imperial Conclave, these subsidiary titles cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by any assembly of the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).

To use this power, the Arcane Architect provides an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) to the Civil Service, and then presents the announcement to the Senate. No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Arcane Architect. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise their [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Arcane Architect's commission.

### Toad Wand

During the Winter Solstice 379YE, the [eternal](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Sadogua](../../in-character/eternals/Sadogua.md) pledged to provide a [magic item](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) to the Arcane Architect. This item takes the form of a wand surmounted by a toad. Sadogua has pledged to re-empower the wand every year during the Winter Solstice, as long as he enjoys the [amity](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#amity) of the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md). The powers of the wand have not been made public knowledge.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Arcane Architect is made by the [Declaration of Candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md).

The Arcane Architect serves until they are replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).
