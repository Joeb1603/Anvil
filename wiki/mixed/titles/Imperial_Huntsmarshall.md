# Imperial Huntsmarshall

*[Image: Pender Valen 'Archer's Dance' , Imperial Huntsmarshall](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Pender_Valen.jpg)*

## Overview

The Imperial Huntsmarshall is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-title-of-speaker-of-the-magpies) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Summer 385YE. The Imperial Huntsmarshall is recognised as the head of the Gathering of Hunters.

## Responsibilities

The Imperial Huntsmarshall has a responsibility to support the Empire in collecting knowledge and disposing of dangerous beasts which threaten its inhabitants.

## Powers

### Address the Council

The Imperial Huntsmarshall has the right to make an [address](../government/military/Council_address.md) to the [Military Council](../government/military/Military_Council.md) once per summit. To use this power, the Huntsmarshall informs the [Herald of the Council](Herald_of_the_Council.md) that they wish to make an address, which will then be added to the agenda for the next [Council session](../government/military/Council_session.md).

## Appointment

The Imperial Huntsmarshall is [appointed by the Military Council](../government/military/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Military_Council.md#appointment) during the Autumn Equinox. It is elected by simple majority of any members of the Military Council who are eligible to vote who are present when the election takes place. Appointments by the Military Council are traditionally carried out with a show of hands at a [Council session](../government/military/Council_session.md).

The title can be held by any Imperial citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
