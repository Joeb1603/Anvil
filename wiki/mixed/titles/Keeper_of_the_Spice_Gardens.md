# Keeper of the Spice Gardens

*[Image: The gardens themselves as sometimes considered a wonder of the Empire](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Gardens.jpg)*

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Apothecaries Guild](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Apothecaries_Guild.png)*

The Keeper of the Spice-Gardens is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) connected to the [Spice Gardens of Feroz](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#the-spice-gardens-of-feroz). The position of Keeper is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a portion of the finest [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md) grown on the [Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). Additional luxury spices are sold to ensure the upkeep of the Gardens and a comfortable salary for the various farmers, horticulturalists and merchants who work as agents of the Spice Gardens, although production has tailed off a little in recent tumultuous decades.

Spices from the Gardens are sold throughout the Brass Coast, and before the fall of [Segura](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md) were exported, first to the [Faraden](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Faraden.md) and then even farther afield. Regular caravan trains cross the [Spider's Dream](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#the-spider27s-dream) to [Sarvos](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md), and from there travel throughout the Empire being especially sought after in the markets of [Varushka](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) and [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md). While the money from this business is not available to the Keeper, it can often help bring a certain level of prestige to the holder of the title among those who consider themselves gourmands.

The title of Keeper has a prestigious legacy. It was created in 213YE during the reign of [Empress Mariika](../../in-character/history/Empress_Mariika.md), and many scholars believe it was part of an agreed price paid to gain the support of the Freeborn senators for the sweeping financial changes she instituted. The title changes hands with much less regularity than many other Imperial sinecures - the holders tend to be long-lived, and understand the value of maintaining a level of popularity among their peers that makes [revocation](../government/synod/Revocation.md) a very rare occurrence; only one holder has been revoked. In 296YE Inigo i Tamari i Erigo was stripped of the title when he was found guilty of using his position to cover trades in illegal substances with a [Sarcophan](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) beggar-prince. After this, the Erigo tribe would not hold the title for almost a century. During this period several families in Feroz were known to be desperate for the opportunity to claim the title until 382YE when **Sadiq i Isa i Erigo** was appointed.

The title was left without power following the [Autumn 383YE treaty](../../in-character/senate-motions/Ratify_treaty_with_the_Grendel_Autumn_383YE.md) with the Grendel, which saw Morajasse ceded to the orcs of the Broken Shore. The Spice Gardens were reclaimed following the Spring Equinox 388YE, as part of a [treaty](../../in-character/senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#ratify-treaty-with-protectorate-of-morajasse) with the Protectorate of Morajasse.

## Responsibilities

The title holder is expected to ensure that the Gardens remain fertile and continue to produce a wide variety of valuable luxury goods and valuable herbs for use by [physicians](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) and [apothecaries](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick) throughout the Brass Coast and beyond. How the Keeper disposes of the herbs they receive is entirely their own business; while there is often pressure placed on them to donate their bounty to various causes the majority choose to either sell them or have them made into potions for sale or personal use.

While any Freeborn citizen might try to claim the position, the families of Feroz have worked hard over the last several centuries to ensure that it remains in their hands rather than being claimed by someone resident in another territory. The Keeper often uses their position to gain additional prestige. Some citizens of Feroz would rather informally approach the Keeper for help with problems relating to their [farm](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md), [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) or [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) than the senator of their territory.

## Powers

### The Spice of Feroz

The Keeper gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). Each season, they receive twelve drams of [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), and two drams each of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) and [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md).

## Appointment

The Keeper of the Spice Gardens is appointed during the Autumn Equinox each year by members of the Apothecaries Guild.

As **Sadiq i Isa i Erigo** was appointed with tenure, they will remain in position until they step down, die, or are revoked.

Any Freeborn citizen can hold this title. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Freeborn Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
