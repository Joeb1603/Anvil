# Bonesetter of Torfast Trading Post

## Overview

The Bonesetter of the Torfast Trading Post is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to an [Imperial Orc](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) citizen. It is part of the [Northern trade network](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Northern_trade_network.md) which effectively came into being shortly before the Autumn Equinox 380YE, with the construction of the offices of the [Overseer of the Westward Road](Overseer_of_the_Westward_Road.md) in [Delev](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md#delev).

In the past, the Winterfolk maintained trade routes south and west from the town of [Torfast](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#southpine) in southern [Skarsind](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md). In the years since Skarsind was recovered, the roads were repaired and some work went towards re-establishing trade, but the Wintermark focus was more on the routes through the passes into [Hahnmark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) in the north-east. When the Winterfolk withdrew from Skarsind, and the Imperial Orcs claimed it, several of their [bonesetters](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_economic_interests.md#bonesetters) began to look east and south, eager for the opportunity to restore the valuable trade routes with [Hercynia](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Hercynia.md), [Temeschwar](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), and [Karov](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md).

Similar trading posts were established in the [Glen of Shadows](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Hercynia.md#glen-of-shadows), in [Temeschwar](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) city, and eventually in [Delev](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md#delev) in [Karov](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md) (the [Treji Northern Wayhouse](Broker_of_Treji_Wayhouse.md), the [Northern Traders Guild](Northbound_Trademaster.md), and the mercantile office of the [Overseer of the Westward Road](Overseer_of_the_Westward_Road.md) respectively). These improved the opportunities in Torfast, widening the range of goods that could be acquired to include herbs from [Hercynia](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Hercynia.md), metals from [Temeschwar](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), and the bounty of the [forests](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) of [Varushka](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md).

In Summer 381YE, in response to an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) offered by the [Thule](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md) orcs of [Otkodov](../../in-character/barbarians/Otkodov.md), a new partner [joined](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_a_road_connecting_the_Thule_to_the_Torfast_Trading_Post.md) the Northern Trade Network bringing valuable crystal mana to the markets of the northern Empire. This crystal mana, of course, remains available only while the Empire remains at peace with the [Thule](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md). Following the Autumn Equinox 384YE, the activities of dreadful heralds in the [forests of Metri](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md#metri) threatened to sever [Temeschwar](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) from the network, leading to [instability along the trade routes](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Don-t_blame_me.md#broken-hospitality), reducing the options available via the southern trade route. During the Winter Solstice 384YE the Order of the Rod and Shield were [instructed](../../in-character/conclave-sessions/384YE_Winter_Solstice_Conclave_sessions.md#concord-rod-and-shield-and-metri) to go and clear out these heralds which saw the [disruption](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Fade_out_again.md#a-disturbing-encounter) to trade end.

During the Summer of 385YE, following the [construction](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Temeschwar_Otkodov_Mana_Exchange.md) of the [Temeschwar-Otkodov Mana Exchange](Keeper_of_the_Tome.md) far more traders from Otkodov are making the trip to Temeschwar to sell their wares instead. The [completion of the Iron Roads](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Deep_forests_and_dark_hills.md#northern-prosperity) saw an influx of [Varushkan](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) trade goods passing through [Delev Market](Overseer_of_the_Westward_Road.md) in [Karov](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md), providing additional opportunities to purchase goods from them.

## Responsibilities

The Bonesetter is expected to keep the trade flowing along the roads that lead from Skarsind south through Temeschwar, east into Varushka, and west into Hercynia. The close contact with the traders allows ample opportunity for the Bonesetter to acquire supplies at bargain prices. If one of the three existing trade routes were endangered, the production of the Torfast Trading Post would be endangered, and the Bonesetter would likely be responsible for working with representatives of the other nation to restore it.

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the title of Bonesetter of Torfast Trading Post allows the holder to purchase quantities of special materials at a set price. The Bonesetter makes their decision on how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

Unlike a standard ministry, the Torfast Trading Post deals with merchants from four neighbouring territories - [Hercynia](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Hercynia.md), [Temeschwar](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md), [Karov](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md), and [Urdur](../../in-character/barbarians/Otkodov.md#urdur) in [Otkodov](../../in-character/barbarians/Otkodov.md). As such, the Bonesetter has not one but *four* streams from which they can purchase material. The Bonesetter can spend differing amounts on each stream - for example, they could spend 17 crowns on one, 7 on another, and none on the third and fourth.

The Eastern Trade route represents the ability to buy additional goods from Varushkan traders, and thanks to the Iron Roads offers additional opportunities to buy goods. This additional opportunity remains available as long as the Iron Roads continue to support the transportation of valuable materials easily through Delev market.

Following the [imposition](../../in-character/senate-motions/Ratify_Liberty_Pact.md) of [sanctions](../government/senate/Tariffs.md#sanctions) on trade with [Otkodov](../../in-character/barbarians/Otkodov.md) during the Winter Solstice 382YE, the cost to purchase crystal mana increased by roughly two-fifths reflecting the fact that the crystal mana and the orichalcum were *both* subject to tariffs when they crossed the border with the Thule nation. The Imperial Senate [voted](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-trade-with-the-thule-ii) to amend tariffs on the Thule during the Autumn Equinox 384YE to grant them [free trade](../government/senate/Tariffs.md#free-trade) which saw the costs of purchasing from the Thule reduced to the level before sanctions were imposed.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Spring Equinox by Imperial Orcs who control [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) who [supported](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#supporting-an-army) an [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) during the previous year. The larger the military unit an individual has, and the more often they supported the Imperial armies, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only an Imperial Orc character may hold this title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. They cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
