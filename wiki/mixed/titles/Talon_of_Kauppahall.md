# Talon of Kauppahall

## Overview

Talon of Kauppahall is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md), responsible for overseeing the prosperity of the trading hall of the same name. The motion to [construct](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Kauppahall.md) Kauppahall was raised by *Hengest Dun*, Senator for [Hahnmark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md), during the Winter Solstice 383YE, taking advantage of an opportunity [presented](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Apples_from_a_tree.md#a-troll27s-shadow) that season. Construction was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 384YE but election of the title was stalled due to [issues](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Kauppahall.md#constitution) raised by the Constitutional Court. The Imperial Senate voted to [amend](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-talon-of-kaupphall) the position and the first Talon of Kauppahall was elected at the same summit.

## Responsibilities

The Talon of Kauppahall is responsible for ensuring the safety of the Kauppahall, and the trade that passes through it. Merchants from southern Hahnmark and northern Kallavesa come here to sell their wares, and if these merchants or the hall itself were to be threatened, it would be the responsibility of the Talon to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any particular [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were to arise related to the Kauppahall, the Talon would expect to be consulted as to how that opportunity might be resolved.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Spring Equinox each year. The Talon of Kauppahall is appointed, through the [Tally of the Votes](../economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md), by citizens of Wintermark who control [military units](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md). The larger the resource an individual has, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a Wintermark citizen may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).

## Kauppahall

Kaupphall in Valashal, [Valasmark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#valasmark), was constructed on the site of a much older hall, the Runehall. According to legend the Runehall was built by a Troll the scops named *The Torn Light of Night Falling*, but it was said to be cursed. Brigid Gunthermsdottir razed the Runehall to the ground and had fresh timber and stone brought in so that no part of the original remained. Over time the settlement grew and in 383YE a [suaq](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Suaq.md) [icewalker](../magic/lore/Wintermark_magical_traditions.md#icewalkers) named Artja Fivefeet proposed the construction of the Kauppahall, a new trading hall that would allow bring together merchants from southern [Hahnmark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) and northern [Kallavesa](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md).

The hall is actually a pair of twinned buildings, laid out to resemble the curving forms of the [rune](../magic/lore/Runes.md) [Lann](../magic/lore/Lann.md). It particularly facilitates trade in stone from the nearby hills helping to enrich Valasmark as well as creating an opportunity for the Talon to buy small quantities of [white granite](../economy/White_granite.md) when they come up for sale. As well as white granite, the nearby hills produce the odd ring of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md), which might also be available on the market. Fearful children claim that this ilium is Torn Light's final legacy and that it is cursed, but there is no truth to this. Every magician who has ever examined the ilium that is found here confirms that it is pure and unsullied by any curse.

Following the attempt by the *scions* of the [Cold Sun](../../in-character/eternals/Cold_Sun.md) to [destroy Valashal](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-silence-arc) in the Winter of 385YE, stories of the cursed ilium began doing the rounds again. After all, before the Kauppahall was built, and before the ilium was coming into the area with regularity, there had never been a single attack of an army drawn from the Day realm. Could that really be just a coincidence?
