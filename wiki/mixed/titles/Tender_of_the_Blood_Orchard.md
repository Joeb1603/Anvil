# Tender of the Blood Orchard

## Overview

The Tender of the Blood Orchard is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). The Wunderkind Woods were [secured](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#secure-wunderkind-woods), and renamed the Blood Orchard, by the Imperial Senate during the Autumn Equinox 384YE.

## Responsibilities

The Tender of the Blood Orchard is responsible for liaising with the Rebeshi Players, protecting the Blood Orchard, and protecting the people of Holmauer *from* the Blood Orchard should that become necessary.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Winter Solstice. The Tender of the Blood Orchard is selected by unanimous decision of the [League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any League citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Blood Orchard

The trees of the Blood Orchard are twisted unholy things, their naked branches grasping toward the sky like grasping claws. Each tree has a single hollow from which oozes a sweet-smelling, sticky, bright red sap. Those brave enough to venture into the woods have reported sightings of half eaten small animals in the roots of many of the trees, but no sign of any predators - although odd blue-white lights are sometimes seen bobbing among the trees on nights when the sky is particularly clear.

Shortly after the [curse](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/In_the_garden_of_defiance.md) of the [Hag Queen](../../in-character/eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#skathe), the sap was sold to visiting tourists by a [mountebank](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Mountebank.md) as the blood of [Empress Lisabetta](../../in-character/history/Empress_Lisabetta.md). The sap, nicknamed *blood red blood* by the imaginative folk of Holberg's streets, was discovered to be poisonous if consumed but when prepared appropriately serves as a form of winter vis.
