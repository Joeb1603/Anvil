# Scholar of Kragg's Hollow

## Overview

The Scholar of Kragg's Hollow is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) that grants custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of [mana crystals](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals). Construction was announced by **Aleksandr Zoravich Novosad**, in their position as [Arcane Architect](Arcane_Architect.md) during the [Spring Equinox 385YE](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#construct-kragg27s-hollow). Kragg's Hollow is in the hills of Estermark, [Skarsind](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md). It is tucked away from the most common paths and the small cottage is well stocked with a library filled with texts on the creation of arcane projections and the resonances and dissonances of each of the six realms.

## Responsibilities

The Scholar of Kragg's Hollow has a responsibility to further the study of magic through the development of [arcane projections](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md). If the Hollow were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Scholar to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Crystals

The title of Scholar of Kragg's Hollow grants custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). The Scholar receives an income of nine mana crystals each season.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement or removal of the Scholar of Kragg's Hollow is made by the [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md).

The Scholar of Kragg's Hollow serves until they are replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. The Scholar cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
