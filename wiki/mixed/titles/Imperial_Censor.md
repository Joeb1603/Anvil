# Imperial Censor

## Overview

The Imperial Censor is an [Imperial Title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md). It was [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Imperial_Censor.md) at the Summer Solstice summit of 380YE. The intent is that the Censor works as part of the Conclave to protect Imperial citizens from dangerous magic.

## Responsibilities

The Imperial Censor is responsible for safeguarding the Empire from malign magical items, especially those of unknown or unacceptable provenance.

## Powers

### Interdiction

The Imperial Censor is empowered to raise a single [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Interdiction](../government/conclave/Interdiction.md) each summit to the agenda for debate during a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). The Civil Service will provide the Imperial Censor with a single writ - to submit their declaration to the Conclave agenda they should complete the writ and hand it to the office of the [Prefect of the Conclave](Prefect_of_the_Conclave.md) at least one hour before the [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md) begins.

Unlike a [grandmaster](Grandmaster.md), the Imperial Censor is personally responsible for any [presentation](../government/conclave/Declaration.md#presentation). While a [proxy](../government/senate/Proxy.md) may be appointed to deliver the presentation, their declaration is recorded as having come from the Imperial Censor.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Imperial Censor is made by the [Declaration of Candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). The Imperial Censor serves until they are replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).
