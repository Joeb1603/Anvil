# Bright Threadweaver

## Overview

The Bright Threadweaver is a [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) created following an [announcement](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Bright-Threads_Brass_Works.md) during the Autumn Equinox 385YE by **Bleakshield Groza** the [Legion Engineer](Legion_Engineer.md). The brass works were completed shortly before the Winter Solstice 385YE and the title elected for the first time at that summit.

## Responsibilities

The Bright Threadweaver has a responsibility to ensure the safety of the miners of Valasmark, [Hahnmark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md). If the safety of these miners, or the brass works, were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Bright Threadweaver to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Orichalcum

The Bright Threadweaver has custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive 12 ingots of orichalcum each season.

## Appointment

The Bright Threadweaver is appointed by unanimous decision of the [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a Wintermark citizen. The Bright Threadweaver has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Wintermark [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
