# Champion of Prosperity

*[Image: This position is a martial title .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MartialTitle.png)*

*[Image: Glistering, one of the artefact Pilgrim's Shields](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Glistering.jpg)*

## Overview

The Champion of Prosperity is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) that was [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/383YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-titles-of-champions-of-virtue) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Winter 383YE. The title would confer custodianship of the [artefact](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#artefacts) [Pilgrim's Shield](../magic/items/Pilgrim-s_Shield.md) known as *Glistering*, were it to be recovered and gifted for that purpose.

## Responsibilities

The Champion of Prosperity is intended to serve as an inspiration to the Pilgrims of the faith and laity as to what it means to be prosperous, particularly in a military context. They are also charged with bearing and protecting the shield known as *Glistering*. Historically the bearers of *Glistering* formed, along with the bearers of the other Pilgrim's Shields, the “Seven Pilgrims” band. It is a martial title.

The Champion also serves as custodian of A Fair Price, a farm in [Iteri](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md#iteri). If the sinecure, or those who work it, were to be threatened in any way then it would be the responsibility of the Champion to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Regalia

The Champion of Prosperity bears *Glistering*, the unique Pilgrim's Shield. If the Champion of Prosperity loses their position, then the item must be given to the new Champion with all haste. The Champion is encouraged to take all appropriate precautions to keep this item safe and is expected to do everything within their power to recover it if it is lost.

*Glistering* was reclaimed by the Empire and held by the Champion of Prosperity, but was lost to the Druj during the Winter Solstice 386YE.

### Custodian of A Fair Price

A Fair Price, an extensive farm in Iteri, [Zenith](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md), was commissioned by the [Legion Engineer](Legion_Engineer.md) during the [Winter Solstice 385YE](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_A_Fair_Price.md). The farm produces 216 rings in profits each season that are given to the Champion of Prosperity so that they are able to pay the deserving for their work.

## Appointment

The Champion of Prosperity is chosen every Spring Equinox by a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of [appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by the [Assembly of Prosperity](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies).

Any Imperial citizen may hold this title. They can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Prosperity Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## History

The shield *Glistering* was created by Ezkiah the Benefactor, greatest smith of the [Highborn](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) along with six other shields. Each was gifted to a different hero, forming a company known as the “Seven Pilgrims”.

*Glistering* was first carried by Pietro the Golden, captain of a Leagueish [free company](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Free_company.md). Passed from Champion to Champion, or selected by the priests of Prosperity when this was not possible, it remained an enduring inspiration to the faithful of the Empire.

At the battle of [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md) in 367YE the Seven Pilgrims of the day charged an entire [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) army on their own, allowing the outmatched Imperial forces time to quit the field in good order. *Glistering* was lost along with the other six shields.

Historically the Shield was passed from Champion to Champion; the Synod has ended this tradition through the creation of a formal title, following the example set by the [creation](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_Champion_of_Courage.md) of the [Champion of Courage](Champion_of_Courage.md).
