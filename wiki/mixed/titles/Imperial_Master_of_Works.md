# Imperial Master of Works

*[Image: The first Master of Works was appointed at the Winter Solstice 377YE](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Richard_Tunstall.jpg)*

## Overview

The Imperial Master of Works is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). The title was [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Imperial_Master_of_Works.md) at the Autumn Equinox 377YE. The title has been amended several times since its creation. At the Spring Equinox 378YE, the Senate voted to [remove](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_powers_of_Imperial_Master_of_Works.md) the Master of Works' ability to create new [fortifications](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md). They also removed a ten throne [stipend](New_Imperial_titles.md#stipend) the title had initially enjoyed. The Imperial Master of Works has further [amended](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_powers_of_the_Imperial_Master_of_Works.md) in Spring 380YE; it was limited to a single [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) each season in line with other similar titles.

The Master of Works was later [amended](../../in-character/senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-master-of-works) during the Spring Equinox 388YE to grant them both a report on the state of the Empire's fortifications and returned the power to announce the construction of a new fortification.

## Responsibilities

The Master of Works is responsible for overseeing the maintenance and improvement of [fortifications](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md) on behalf of the Senate. When first created, the Master of Works had the ability to [commission](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#creating-a-fortification) new fortifications, but that power was removed by the Imperial Senate in Spring 378YE.

## Powers

The Imperial Master of Works can make one announcement to the Senate at each summit. They can announce the repair of a [bastion](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#bastion), the construction of a new fortification, or the improvement of a single fortification.

### Repair Bastion

The Imperial Master of Works can announce the repair of the [bastion](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#bastion) of a single [fortification](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md). The materials and money required for repair must be provided by the Master of Works or their agents.

To use this power, the Master of Works provides an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) to the Civil Service, who present the decision to the Senate. No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Master of Works. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise its [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Master's decision.

### Maintain Fortification

The Imperial Master of Works can announce the [raising](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#senate-commission) or [improvement](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#improvement) of a single fortification.

To use this power, the Master of Works provides an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) to the civil service, who present the decision to the Senate. No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Master of Works. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise its [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Master's decision.

### State of Fortifications

The Master of Works receives a report on the state of the Empire's [garrisons](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#garrison), and the integrity of [bastions](../../out-of-character/rules/Fortification.md#bastion) each summit at a session of the Military Council. If a new Master of Works is appointed during a summit, they can request a copy of this briefing from the civil service; the [Herald of the Council](Herald_of_the_Council.md) is in the best position to provide one but, any civil servant should be able to help.

## Appointment

The Master of Works is [appointed by the Military Council](New_Imperial_titles.md#the-imperial-military-council). It is elected by simple majority of any members of the Military Council who are eligible to vote who are present when the election takes place. Appointments by the Military Council are traditionally carried out with a show of hands at a [Council session](../government/military/Council_session.md). Any Imperial Citizen may hold this title.

The Imperial Master serves for a year, and they can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
