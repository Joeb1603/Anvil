# Archmage of Autumn

*[Image: The Autumn archmage supports Imperial magicians and serves as a diplomat to the realm of Autumn.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:The_cutest_Autumn_Archmage.png)*

## Overview

[Archmage](Archmage.md) is an Imperial title bestowed upon a [magician](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) by the members of the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md). There are six archmages; one for each of the magical realms. They serve as a focal point for [ritualists](../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) of that realm, as well as helping to guide the use of the Conclave's resources. The six archmages match the six realms into which magic is divided. Where the influence of a [grandmaster](Grandmaster.md) is primarily political, the archmages are responsible for attending to the magical business of the Conclave. They are assumed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md), the [Military Council](../government/military/Military_Council.md), foreign dignitaries and [eternals](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) alike to be well-versed in the capabilities and lore of their realm. The archmages are often seen as the public faces of the Conclave; they are the individuals most likely to be consulted by other Imperial bodies when they wish to know what magical possibilities exist.

Each archmage is addressed by their title and the name of the realm of magic they stand for - *Maurice, Archmage of Autumn* or *Archmage Edmundo of Autumn*. The title archmage comes originally from [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), a development of the title [mage](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#magi).

## Responsibilities

The Archmage of Autumn is responsible for attending to the magical business of the Conclave, and bears [powerful tools](#regalia-of-autumn) to help them do so. They represent the Empire to the eternals of the Autumn realm, and vice versa. They also work with magicians versed in the lore of [Autumn magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md) to expand the Empire's understanding and power in that realm, and employ it to the advantage of Imperial citizens.

## Powers

Each archmage position is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) in the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md).

### Archmage of Autumn

The Archmage of Autumn has the legal powers common to the Archmages. They can participate in the [Arcane Colloquium](../government/conclave/Arcane_Colloquium.md); accept a formal [parley](Archmage.md#parley) with Autumn eternals; raise a [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Alignment](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md) for an Autumn eternal once each summit; and advise the magistrates as the recognised [master of Autumn magic](Archmage.md#master-of-magic).

### Regalia of Autumn

In common with the other archmages, the Archmage of Autumn has legal ownership of three powerful [magic items](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) that make up the [regalia of the archmage](Archmage.md#regalia-of-the-archmage). These include the [Staff of Autumn](Archmage.md#staff-of-the-archmage) which greatly enhances the bearer's prowess with Autumn ritual magic; the [Golden Chain of Aesh](Archmage.md#chain-of-aesh) that can reduce the magnitude of an Autumn ritual; and the [Belt of the Oak](Archmage.md#belt-of-stars) which can allow multiple [covens](../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) from the same nation to cooperate in the performance of a single Autumn ritual.

*[Image: An Autumn archmage will often find themselves engaged in negotiations and bargains, sometimes with unexpected consequences.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Autumn_Archmage.png)*

### Autumn Market

The Autumn Archmage has an additional, somewhat unofficial, use for the [plenipotentiary](Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) power. During the Summer Solstice, the archmage may request of one of the eternals of Autumn that instead of a traditional parley they host an Autumn market during the Autumn Equinox.

Such a market may take any number of forms, but always involves opportunities for Imperial citizens to purchase goods or services from representatives of the realm. In recent years, markets have been hosted by [Estavus](../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md) and [Ephisis](../../in-character/eternals/Ephisis.md) - the former taking the form of a [Sarcophan](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Sarcophan_Delves.md) *[vellingmarkt](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_pleasing_and_deceptive_face.md#estavus)* where representatives of the City of Fire auctioned crafted goods, while the latter was a more [open market](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/We-ve_been_here_forever.md) where representatives of several Autumn eternals offered their wares to mortal shoppers.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Archmage of Autumn is made by [declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). A candidate can be presented for the post of archmage only once a season, and the title can be held by any Imperial citizen.

The Archmage of Autumn serves until they are replaced by another magician using the declaration of candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, an archmage cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).

## The Eternals of Autumn

The Archmage of Autumn is the Empire's formal representative to the inhabitants of the Autumn realm, and often serves as a go-between when those eternals wish to communicate with the Empire. Most eternals will treat the archmage as a peer - although that can mean very different things for different eternals. One of the most potent abilities the Archmage of Autumn possesses is the power of [plenipotentiary](Archmage.md#plenipotentiary), allowing them to communicate with eternals from their realm and potentially arranging a [parley](Archmage.md#parley). A list of previous plenipotentiary results can be found [here](../../indexes/by-category/Plenipotentiary.md)

Of all the archmages, the Archmage of Autumn may be said to have the most difficult job when dealing with the eternals. The Autumn eternals are creatures of bargains, and more than any other inhabitants of the realm they frame their interactions with mortals in terms of *quid pro quo*. Any Archmage planning to deal with the Autumn eternals should be prepared to negotiate, and the more powerful the boon they receive the steeper the price is likely to be. More so than some of the other eternals, the inhabitants of the Autumn realm also have a keen personal philosophy - a belief about how the world works (or should work) - and they will regularly attempt to push that agenda in their negotiations with the Autumn Archmage.

The Archmage of Autumn is often in a good position to present Declarations of [Alignment](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md) to the Conclave when relations between the Empire and an eternal need to be formalised. Indeed, more than any other [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md), the Archmage benefits from staying abreast of the relationships between the inhabitants of the Autumn realm and Imperial citizens. The current status of the Autumn eternals with an interest in the Empire can be found [here](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#autumn).
