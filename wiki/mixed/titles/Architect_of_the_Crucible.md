# Architect of the Crucible

## Overview

The Architect of the Crucible oversees the Crucible of Fate in [Zenith](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md). The Crucible was originally suggested in [Winter 384YE](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Write_your_own_ending.md#the-crucible-of-fate) by Naomi of Virtue's Rest as part of an appraisal to *stabilise and restore the territory of Zenith*. It was commissioned by **Sagua i'Ezmara**, the [Master of the Koboldi](Master_of_the_Koboldi.md), during the [Winter Solstice 384YE](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Crucible_of_Fate.md) and completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 385YE.

## Responsibilities

The Architect of the Crucible is responsible for ensuring the security of the Crucible and the artisans who work there.

The *Keeper of the Bronze Birds*, a herald of [Estavus](../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md), resides at the Crucible with their glass aviary housing five birds fashioned from bronze in the shape of raptors, named Alea, Hona, Roosa, Sind, and Forn. The Keeper can be instructed to unleash their birds; sending them to scour the mountains of Urizen, searching not for prey, but for signs of the remnants of star falls. As soon as they find one, they will bring word to the Crucible, and it will then be possible to gather the precious metal. As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the Architect can purchase rings of ilium at a set price. The Architect spends their money and makes their choices during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and ilium is placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

Following the [mandate](../../in-character/synod-judgements/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-101) enacted by **Bartimaeus of Zenith Ascendant** the Architect of the Crucible has access to three ministries allowing them to purchase [magic items](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) from the artisans and architects who work there. The Architect spends their money and makes their choices during downtime. Money is then removed from, and ribbons are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

The Urizen Assembly can change the theme of one or all of these additional ministries through the use of a [statement of principle](../government/synod/Statement_of_principle.md) that achieved a [greater majority](../government/synod/Judgement.md#greater-majority). If multiple such statements were delivered, the one with the greatest margin would take effect.

### Miracle And Wonder

When the Crucible of Fate was first completed, the Architect was able to commission the artisans of the forge to create bespoke ushabti for an Urizen with a [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md), [forest](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md), [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md), [farm](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md), or [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in [Morrow](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md), [Redoubt](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Redoubt.md), or [Zenith](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md). Shortly before the Summer Solstice 386YE a [proposal](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Metal_and_stone.md#brilliant-star) was made by Izenbra of the Architects of Iron and Stone, a herald of [Estavus](../../in-character/eternals/Estavus.md), that the artisans could instead forge ushabti capable of withstanding the harsh light in the winding tunnels of the [Brilliant Star](Brilliant_Star.md). Following the Summer Solstice **Esteban Ezmara of Zenith Ascendant** ordered the artisans to craft the ushabti of [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) and [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) each season for the mithril mine, increasing production of the Brilliant Star by three wains of mithril each season. An Architect of the Crucible can change the order at any point by emailing [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) which will reduce the production of the Brilliant Star back to 28 wains of mithril and allowing them to upgrade certain personal resources again.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Summer Solstice. The Architect of the Crucible is appointed through [Tally of the Votes](../economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md) by citizens of Urizen who control [mana sites](../../out-of-character/rules/Mana_site.md). The larger the mana site an individual has, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only an Urizen citizen may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
