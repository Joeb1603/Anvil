# Knight-protector

## Overview

*[Image: This position is a martial title .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MartialTitle.png)*

There are four Knight-protector titles, one for each season. The champion of each seasonal [Grand Tourney](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_culture_and_customs.md#the-grand-tourneys) is crowned Knight-protector of that season and serves for a single year. The Knights-protector are expected to help Dawn deal with the realm in question and in particular to be first to stand against any threat that might issue forth from the realm.

The role of Knight-protector is an ancient [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md), a Dawnish national position which had long since lapsed into obscurity in the past. While it is common for settlements across Dawn to host local tourneys, the tradition of a single Grand Tourney to appoint a Knight-protector each season fell into disuse after the death of [Emperor Hugh](../../in-character/history/Emperor_Hugh.md). Following the [changes to Dawnish senatorial](../../out-of-character/rules/Rules_update_2019.md#dawn-elections-and-the-grand-tourneys) elections in autumn 382YE, the [egregores](../../out-of-character/crew/Egregores.md) restored the tradition, to underscore the importance of victory in the tourney - and to provide a mechanism for nobles to commit their support to the tourney winner should they choose.

## Responsibilities

Each knight-protector is expected to help Dawn deal with the realm they represent, and in particular to be first to stand against any threat that might issue forth from the realm. They may also be called upon by the [egregore](../../out-of-character/crew/Egregores.md) to help arrange or marshal a Grand Tourney. The knight-protectors are martial titles.

## Powers

### Banner of the Season

Each Knight-protector receives custody of one of four magical [magic standards](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#magic-standards), created by the [weavers](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weaver_cabal.md) of [Weirwater](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weirwater.md). The bearer is expected to protect this unique [magic item](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) with their life - and to hang it prominently in their tent at Anvil and in their hall at home where all may see their [glory](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Glory.md).

Each banner bears an enchantment that allows a [bonded](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#bonded-items) bearer to pass through any [portal](../../out-of-character/rules/Operate_portal.md) connected with a [regio](../magic/rituals/Regio.md) - including the ability to visit the Hall of the Worlds. There is some variance between the specific usage of each banner; notably the Summer Banner allows a small group to accompany the knight-protector. In addition, the knight-protector is treated by the [eternals](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) of the appropriate realm as an emissary, and granted certain protections from treachery or violence when treating with them - provided they carry the banner.

## Appointment

> **Rules of the Tourney**  
> The Grand Tourney to elect the Knight Protector takes place under rules laid down by the Dawnish egregores. Entry to the tourney is open to any noble of Dawn. They may use any melee weapons they take into the tourney ground with them, or any mage implements - only bows and crossbows are not permitted. Entrants can make use of personal enchantments and heroic skills and anything else they carry with them including any magical items and including potions.
>
> No one may enter the tourney ground once the melee has begun, unless it is to help remove a downed participant from the area. Onlookers may **not** hand potions, mana crystals, or any other items to anyone who is taking part in the tourney, nor cast any spells that affect the tourney.

The winner of each seasonal Grand Tourney gains the title of Knight-protector. All Dawnish nobility are eligible to participate in the tourney; [yeofolk](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Yeofolk_of_Dawn.md), however, may not compete. By long tradition of the grand tourney, a blind eye is turned to the participation of [knights-errant](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Knight-errant.md), who often take part incognito. The tourney takes the form of a grand melee, the winner being the last participant standing when all others are defeated or have yielded. With help from the troubadours, the champion will be crowned as that season's knight-protector.

Each Knight-protector is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md). The winner holds the title for a year, so the Knight-protector of Winter will reign until the start of the next winter tourney, the Knight-protector of Spring will reign until next spring - and so on. A reigning knight may not compete in a grand tourney until a year has passed - they can try to retain their title the following year, but they cannot compete for a different title before then, even if they resign their title or are revoked.

If a character wins the tourney but refuses to take up the position for any reason, then there is no Knight-protector for the coming year. Another legal tourney to elect a knight-protector will not be held until the normal election date comes round again.

Only one grand tourney is held each season. If a knight-protector dies, steps down, or is revoked, the title remains vacant until the next time the grand tourney would be held.

The title can be held by a citizen of Dawn. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Dawn [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
