### The [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md)

Under normal circumstances, all Mithril Bourse Seats would have been re-elected or re-appointed during the Equinox. Due to concerns of economic stability in a time of Imperial crisis, the seats gained at the Winter Equinox 376 will run until the Winter Equinox 377 and then the normal process of re-election will begin again.

#### Changes to Bourse Seats

- The [Brilliant Shore](../economy/Bourse.md#the-brilliant-shore) has been reclaimed from the [Thule](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Thule.md) but the allocation of the Bourse position by the Senate during The Autumn Equinox was [vetoed](../government/synod/Veto.md) by The General Assembly of the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) and hence no one could be appointed to the position. Its allocation is now waiting for a standard Senate motion to be raised (which can occur no earlier than the Saturday evening senate session of The Winter Solstice); the seat continues to accrue mithril until it is successfully allocated.
- The [Vigilant Swan](../economy/Bourse.md#the-vigilant-swan) ([Highguard](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md)) has been lost to the [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) due to their conquest of [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md). The seat will not be auctioned again until it is recovered.
