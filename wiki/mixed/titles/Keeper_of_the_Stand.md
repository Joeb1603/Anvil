# Keeper of the Stand

## Overview

The Keeper of the Stand is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). Construction of Micah's Stand Wayhouse was [commissioned](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Micah-s_Stand.md) by the Imperial Senate during the Winter Solstice 386YE, and construction was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 387YE. More information on the [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) can be found in the **[Wasp nest](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Wasp_nest.md#stand-for-a-dream)** wind of fortune.  
  
As a [Navarr wayhouse](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md), Micah's Stand [supports Navarr](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md#support-navarr-characters) living in [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md). This means that the [civil service](../government/senate/Civil_service.md) can find Navarr characters a [new resource](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource) in the territory for the normal cost; such resources do not suffer the [usual problems](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#resources-in-other-nations) with being established in a different nation; Navarr living here gain all the regular benefits provided by [great works](../commissions/Great_work.md); and they can participate in the [election](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_leadership.md#leading-a-territory) of a specific Navarr [senator](Senator.md).

## Responsibilities

The Keeper of the Stand is responsible for working towards the reclamation of [Vale's Lament](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md#vales-lament) from the vallorn. In addition, they are charged with protecting the wayhouse and the Navarr who live in [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md). If the wayhouse, travellers using the wayhouse, or the Navarr of the territory were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Keeper to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any particular [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were to arise related to the wayhouse, the Keeper would expect to be consulted as to how that opportunity might be resolved.

## Powers

### Bounty of the Gardens

The Keeper of the Stand has custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive 10 drams of random herbs and 10 doses of liao each season.

## Appointment

The Keeper of the Stand is appointed during the Spring Equinox each year. As a [national position](New_Imperial_titles.md#the-imperial-synod), it is elected by unanimous decision of the [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) senators.

The title can only be held by a citizen of Navarr. They can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Micah's Stand Wayhouse

When [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md) was freed from the [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md), and the vile citadel of *Urith Barath* was torn down, the Navarr and Highborn worked together to translate the herbals claimed from the Stone Toad sept. Since then, there have been Navarr living around, and caring for, the [Gardens of High Chalcis](Benefactor_of_High_Chalcis.md#gardens-of-high-chalcis). When the [Last Battle Wayhouse](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Keeper_of_the_Last_Battle.md#last-battle-wayhouse) was built in [the Barrens](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md), the [steading](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Steading.md) of *Micah's Stand* - a small group devoted to the memory of the Highborn who welcomed them in – suggested that a [wayhouse](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md) be built in eastern Reikos. Among other things this would allow the Navarr of Therunin to live close to their home whilst still being relatively safe from the Druj.

Several of the larger [chapters](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Chapter.md) of [Chalcis Mount](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md#chalcis-mount) supported the plan. Micah's Stand, and others like it, had proved excellent neighbours, and they well remember the suffering of knowing that your home is under the terror of the Druj. The proposed building cost a little more than usual, but attracted skilled Navarr herbalists. The exchange of skills with the citizens who tend the Gardens of High Chalcis benefits the Empire directly, increasing the bounty of herbs delivered to the [Benefactor of High Chalcis](Benefactor_of_High_Chalcis.md).

The Wayhouse itself is an open and welcoming building surrounded by beautiful gardens dedicated both to the cultivation of herbs and to presenting plants from all corners of the Empire. Wherever a Navarr striding passes, they bring back cuttings and seeds to Micah's Stand and the gardeners find a way to help the plants prosper. Walking through the gardens provides a sense of just how grand and diverse the Empire is, and how wide the world has become. Visitors of all nations speak of this as a sobering, inspirational experience.

As well as providing the usual benefits of a wayhouse, Micah's Stand also serves as a place for [physicks](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#physick), [apothecaries](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary), herbalists, and adepts of healing magic - especially magicians of the [Silver Chalice](../government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md) - to meet and share their expertise with one another. A number of the healers who escaped the fall of [Peakedge Song](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md#peakedge-song) - Highborn, Navarr, and [Great Forest Orcs](../../in-character/barbarians/Great_Forest_Orcs.md) alike, have taken up permanent residence here to suport the business of the wayhouse.
