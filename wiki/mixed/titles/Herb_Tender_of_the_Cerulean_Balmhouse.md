# Herb Tender of the Cerulean Balmhouse

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Freeborn Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Freeborn.png)*

The Herb Tender of the Cerulean Balmhouse is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) responsible for overseeing the Cerulean Balmhouse in Fontargenta, Feroz. The motion to [construct](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Cerulean_Balmhouse.md) the Cerulean Balmhouse was proposed by **Rahma I Fontanilla I Guerra** as the proxy [Senator for Kahraman](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) during the Spring Equinox 388YE, and drew on [aid](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Forging_links.md#one-link-at-a-time) offered by Sumaash.

The title is a little bit of a mouthful; informally, the holder is sometimes referred to as *Cerulean Herbtender*.

## Responsibilities

The Herb Tender is responsible for ensuring the herbs produced are made available to the "Freeborn healers bloc". There is some expectation that the Herb Tender will ensure that either a [contract](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast_culture_and_customs.md#contracts) is made on the use of the herbs or that they receive an honest price for them.

## Powers

### Bountiful Gardens

The balmhouse produces 22 drams of [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md) and five drams of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) each season for the Herb Tender.

## Appointment

The Cerulean Herbtender is a national position appointed by the Synod. It is elected by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the Freeborn [Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies).

The title can be held by any Freeborn citizen. The Herb Tender has tenure and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Brass Coast National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
