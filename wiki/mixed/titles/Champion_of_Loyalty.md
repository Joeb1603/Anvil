# Champion of Loyalty

*[Image: This position is a martial title .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MartialTitle.png)*

## Overview

The Champion of Loyalty is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) that was [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/383YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-titles-of-champions-of-virtue) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Winter 383YE. The title confers custodianship of the [artefact](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#artefacts) [Pilgrim's Shield](../magic/items/Pilgrim-s_Shield.md) known as *Oath-Heart*.

## Responsibilities

The Champion of Loyalty is intended to serve as an inspiration to the Pilgrims of the faith and laity as to what it means to be loyal, particularly in a military context. They are also charged with bearing and protecting the shield known as *Oath-Heart*. Historically the bearers of *Oath-Heart* formed, along with the bearers of the other Pilgrims shield, the “Seven Pilgrims” band. It is a martial title.

## Powers

### Regalia of the Champion of Loyalty

*[Image: An impression of Oath-Heart, painted shortly before the shield and its wielder were lost to the Druj .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Oath-Heart.png)*

The Champion of Loyalty bears *Oath-Heart*, the unique Pilgrim's Shield. If the Champion of Loyalty loses their position, then the item must be given to the new Champion with all haste. The Champion is encouraged to take all appropriate precautions to keep this item safe, and is expected to do everything within their power to recover it if it is lost.

*Oath-Heart* was recovered by Imperial heroes at a [conjunction at the Summer Solstice 384YE](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/384YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_fortune.md#summer-conjunctions), and was formally vested as regalia by the then-Champion, Goran Sokolov Zaytsev, at the Winter Solstice 384YE.

During a conjunction of the Sentinel Gate [to the ruined fortification of the White Tower](../../in-character/winds-of-war/To_the_last_syllable.md#to-the-white-tower) in [Bastion](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Bastion.md) in Autumn 385YE, *Oath-Heart* was lost in the fight against the *scions* of [Cold Sun](../../in-character/eternals/Cold_Sun.md). The shield fell into the hands of the *scions*, and despite the [attempts of Imperial heroes](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#scouting-and-discoveries-bastion) in Winter 385YE, it was converted into some kind of terrible weapon of Cold Sun by the *grand archon* of its force known as *Winnowers*. Whilst the *Winnowers* were [destroyed](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Eclipse.md#winnowers) in the following season of military action, the corrupted weapon was not recovered.

## Appointment

The Champion of Loyalty is chosen every Winter Solstice by a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of [appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by the [Assembly of Loyalty](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies).

Any Imperial citizen may hold this title. They can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Loyalty Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## History

The shield *Oath-Heart* was created by Ezkiah the Benefactor, greatest smith of the [Highborn](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) along with six other shields. Each was gifted to a different hero, forming a company known as the “Seven Pilgrims”.

*Oath-Heart* was first carried by Drumlin the Sworn, a thrice-geased Winterfolk. Passed from Champion to Champion, or selected by the priests of Loyalty when this was not possible, it remained an enduring inspiration to the faithful of the Empire.

At the battle of [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md) in 367YE the Seven Pilgrims of the day charged an entire [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) army on their own, allowing the outmatched Imperial forces time to quit the field in good order. *Oath-Heart* was lost along with the other six shields.

Historically the Shield was passed from Champion to Champion; the Synod has ended this tradition through the creation of a formal title, following the example set by the [creation](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_Champion_of_Courage.md) of the [Champion of Courage](Champion_of_Courage.md).
