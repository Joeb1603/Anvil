# Master of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar

## Overview

The Master of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). Construction of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_School_of_Hard_Knocks_in_Temeschwar.md) by **D'ardante** as [Bearer of the First Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) during the Autumn Equinox 385YE following an [opportunity](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Schools_and_sawmills.md#school-of-hard-knocks) presented that season. Work was completed shortly before the Winter Solstice 385YE.

While the title is officially Master of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar, many citizens of Temeschwar prefer to use the much pithier *Maestro of Wolves*.

## Responsibilities

The Master of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar is responsible for setting the syllabus for the academy and is expected to invite the best tutors and instructors to teach there. If the academy, the pupils, or the staff were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Master to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Master's Stipend

The Master of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar receives an income of 2 thrones, 3 crowns, and 16 rings each season.

## Appointment

The Master of the Sword Academy of Temeschwar is selected by unanimous decision of the [League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any League citizen. The Master has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
