# Jade Custodian

## Overview

The Jade Custodian is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) that has custodianship of the [Wellspring of Jade](#the-wellspring-of-jade). This idyllic pool is set among light woodlands in eastern [Foracci](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#foracci), at the source of one of the minor tributaries of the Gancio. It was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_The_Wellspring_of_Jade.md) by the then-[Civilian Commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) in Winter 380YE. Work was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 381YE. The title represents a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of crystal mana. Likewise, if any [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) relating to the wellspring presented itself, they would make any decisions needed to take advantage of it.

Following the Autumn Equinox 385YE **Giacomo Corvinoscuro** funded a series of designs incorporating [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) to track the flow of the currents of mana flowing through the Wellspring of Jade as part of the opportunity presented in the **[Shining like fire](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Shining_like_fire.md#asymmetrical-curves)** wind of fortune.

## Responsibilities

The Jade Custodian has no particular responsibilities; they simply receive a bounty of [crystal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) from the [Wellspring](#the-wellspring-of-jade). If the Wellspring were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Jade Custodian to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Mana

The Jade Custodian gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). The Jade Custodian receives an income of 14 crystal mana each season from the Wellspring of Jade.

## Appointment

The Jade Custodian is appointed by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a citizen of the League. The Custodian has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Wellspring of Jade

The Wellspring is found at the source of a minor tributary of the Gancio, near the hamlet of Aversa in [Foracci](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#foracci). The spring from which the tributary rises is part of an idyllic glade where natural flows of mana pool. The spring now rises into a beautiful circular pool, the green marble basin of which is inlaid with plates of mithril, and in whose depths crystals of mana form. It is a simple matter to pluck the formed crystals from the cool, fresh water. On clear days, sunlight causes motes of gold and jade light to dance across the surface of the pool; under the light of the moon, the waters ripple with a gentle silver glow.

During the construction it was discovered that the pooling mana had infused the idyllic spot with a natural [aura](../magic/lore/Aura.md), which has been enhanced by the careful construction work. Anyone who rests beside the pool during the day finds it easy to relax, and their minds naturally turn to pondering philosophical questions or artistic endeavours. By night, travellers camping along the road between [Sarvos](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) and [Casinea](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Casinea.md) have reported vivid, inspirational dreams from which they awaken greatly refreshed. Merchants traveling between the eastern Empire and the City of Masks who know of the Wellspring and its relaxing properties have started favouring the route through Aversa, leading to an unexpected burst of prosperity for the sleepy hamlet.
