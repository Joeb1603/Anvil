# Emerald Arcanist

## Overview

*[Image: Vallornguard](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Vallornguard_sodality.png)*

The Emerald Arcanist is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Vallornguard](Vallornguard.md). Construction of Jonas' Legacy was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Jonas-_Legacy.md) by **Bertrand**, as the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), during the Summer Solstice 387YE, and construction, at Elvette in [Semmerholm](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md), was completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox 387YE.

## Responsibilities

The Emerald Arcanist is responsible for advising the Vallornguard on magical matters. In addition, the Arcanist is expected to defend the border between [Brocéliande](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Brocéliande.md) and [Semmerholm](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Semmerholm.md). Finally, if Jonas' Legacy, or its inhabitants, were to be threatened, it would be the responsibility of the Emerald Arcanist to respond to the threat.

## Powers

*[Image: Llŷr Viridian duLac , Emerald Arcanist](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Viridian_duLac.jpg)*

### Cache of Crystals

The Emerald Arcanist has custodianship of a sinecure. They receive nine mana crystals each season

## Appointment

The Emerald Arcanist is appointed during the Autumn Equinox each year by members of the Vallornguard.

Any Imperial citizen can hold this title. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
