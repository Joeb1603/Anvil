# Merchant to Skoura

## Overview

The Merchant to Skoura is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md). It is a national position appointed by unanimous decision of the Urizen [senators](Senator.md).The Skouran Trade Enclave was commissioned during the [Autumn Equinox 383YE](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Trade_Enclave_in_Axos.md) and work was finished shortly before the Spring Equinox 384YE. Following the Spring Equinox 385YE, the Axou closed the offices of the Merchant to Skoura, angry at both their erstwhile allies and the Empire for their roll in the failure of the [Tsark raid](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Roll_and_fall.md). The title still exists, but receives no access to the ministry established in Axos.

## Responsibilities

The Merchant to Skoura worked alongside Axou traders in the Towers of Kantor in providing [dragonbone](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) for trade with the miners of the Skouran hills.

## Powers

### Wealth of Skoura

The Merchant to Skoura previously had access to a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), allowing the holder to purchase quantities of special materials at a set price.

## Appointment

The Merchant to Skoura is appointed during the Spring Equinox each year. It is chosen by unanimous decision of the [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Urizen citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Urizen [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
