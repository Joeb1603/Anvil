# Reeve of Drycastle

## Overview

The Reeve of Drycastle is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md). The motion to rebuild Drycastle was [passed](../../in-character/senate-motions/Rebuild_Drycastle.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Summer 386YE. The Reeve of Drycastle is responsible for seeing to the Prosperity of the town.

## Responsibilities

The Reeve of Drycastle has a responsibility to ensure the prosperity of the town. They are expected to meet with visiting merchants, help settle disputes, and deal with any rowdiness that comes from trade.

## Powers

### Sizeable Income

The Reeve of Drycastle receives a seasonal income of 25 crowns from rents on buildings in the town and fees paid by visiting merchants.

> **Suitability**  
> The Reeve of Drycastle is intended to be held by a [yeofolk](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Yeofolk_of_Dawn.md); the task of haggling over coins and ensuring good trade is unsuitable for a noble. If the title is held by a character who is a noble, or they become one during their tenure, then the player must email admin@profounddecisions.co.uk to let us know to turn off production.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Summer Solstice. The Reeve of Drycastle is appointed by citizens of Dawn who control a [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) [personal resource](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md). The larger the military unit an individual has the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a Dawnish citizen may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
