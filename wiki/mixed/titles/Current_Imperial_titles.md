# Current Imperial titles

## Overview

This page lists the names of all Imperial citizens who currently hold an Imperial title.
Previous title holders are listed historically below (as recorded at the end of the relevant summit).
For more information on how they were elected, please refer to [imperial elections](../../in-character/elections/Imperial_elections.md) and the [Election schedule](Election_schedule.md)

## The Throne

Uniquely the Imperial Throne wields significant power in the Senate, Synod, Conclave and Military Council.

- [The Throne](The_Throne.md) - Vesna Borkovna Prochnost

### Ambassadors

Ambassadors are usually appointed by the Imperial Senate - they are expected to take the lead in diplomatic relations with the foreign power or entity and all official ambassadors have the right to negotiate a [treaty](Ambassador.md#treaty). A [senator](Senator.md) must use a [Senate motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Senate_motion.md) to submit the treaty for ratification by the Senate - but ratification only requires a single senate motion, regardless of how many legal changes are required to ratify the treaty.

## [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md)

The most significant titles in the Bourse are commonly referred to as Bourse seats. Almost all these seats provide the citizen who controls them with a significant supply of bourse materials each season. Controllers of Imperial seats will usually sell these materials to the highest bidder, controllers of national seats are sometimes expected to provide materials at reduced rates for worthy causes to members of their nation. For historical reasons the [Master of the Imperial Mint](Master_of_the_Imperial_Mint.md) and the [Castellan of Spiral Castle](Castellan_of_Spiral_Castle.md) are both considered to be a Bourse seat even though neither are associated with the production of bourse resources any longer.
