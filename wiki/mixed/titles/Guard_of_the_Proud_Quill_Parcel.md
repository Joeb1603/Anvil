# Guard of the Proud Quill Parcel

## Overview

The Guard of the Proud Quill Parcel is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [senators](Senator.md) of [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md). The Guard has custodianship of the Proud Quill Parcel, a herb garden [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Proud_Quill_Parcel.md) by **Cormack Dun**, as [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md), during the Autumn Equinox 386YE. Construction was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 387YE.

## Responsibilities

The Guard of the Proud Quill Parcel has a responsibility to protect the Proud Quill Parcel and to ensure the safety of those visiting the Proud Quill pub. If the garden or the pub, or the visitors to either, were to be threatened, it would be the responsibility of the Guard to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Produce of the Parcel

The Guard receives an income of herbs each season: 9 drams each of [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md) and [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), and 6 drams of [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md).

## Appointment

The Guard of the Proud Quill Parcel is appointed by unanimous decision of the [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a Wintermark citizen. The Guard has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Wintermark [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Proud Quill Parcel

The Proud Quill is an ale house next door to The Oathwright offices, which trades in herbs and potions. The Proud Quill Parcel is a herb garden in open ground behind the pub. The Proud Quill pub served a clientele of [scops](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_culture_and_customs.md#scops) in central Kalpaheim for several generations until it closed during the war with the Thule. It was reopened by The Oathwright in 383YE and is home to **Cormack Dun** and his hall.
