# Magpies

## The Manifesto

*[Image: Magpies](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Magpie_Sodality.png)*

The sodality is dedicated to the acquisition, documentation and preservation of artefacts for the Empire, and the uncovering of lost Imperial history. It holds the following to be true:

- Knowledge of our past is a key to understanding our present and charting a better future
- Knowledge thrives when shared; hoarding it without good reason is unvirtuous. Research should be distributed for the benefit of all
- A virtuous and victorious Empire upholds, supports, safeguards, and rewards its researchers

The sodality therefore exists to:

- Aid and coordinate Imperial efforts to seek out lost artefacts
- Support the interests of historians and artefact hunters of the Empire
- Ensure accountability, protection, work, and suitable reward for the historians and artefact hunters of the Empire

## Symbol

The Magpies' symbol is a magpie perched on a stylised M.

## Membership

Any Imperial citizen can join the sodality using the [Mark of Fellowship](../magic/rituals/Mark_of_Fellowship.md) ritual.

## Powers

If the title of Speaker of the Magpies is vacant, then the sodality can take a vote to appoint a representative to the position.

The constitutional court identified a range of [titles](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Two_for_joy.md#titles) and [powers](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Two_for_joy.md#powers) that the Senate could grant the Magpies if they chose.

If an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were identified where the sodality could be deployed, then it would be down to a vote of the members.

*[Image: Morgan Drifting Bough , Speaker of the Magpies](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:SpeakerMagpies.jpg)*

## Speaker

The Speaker of the Magpies is an Imperial title awarded to a member of the Magpies. The Speaker is expected to be a mouthpiece for the sodality, with the responsibility to represent their sodality's views to other parts of the Empire, to recruit new members, and to advance the sodality's goals and the members' interests.

#### Appointment

The Speaker is selected by members of the sodality. Only a member of the sodality can present themselves for the title, and only a member of the sodality can vote in the election. The candidate who gains the most votes from the members of the sodality is elected to the title.

They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## History

The Magpies had been active at [Anvil](../../in-character/world/Anvil.md), the Speaker having been created by **Lizzie Stamp**, as the Senator for [Bregasland](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Bregasland.md), during the Summer Solstice 385YE. They were [recognised](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#establish-magpies-sodality) as a formal [Imperial Sodality](../government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) at the start of Spring 387YE. The original fellowship was granted official recognition two years earlier by Senate [establishment](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-title-of-speaker-of-the-magpies) of the [Speaker of the Magpies](Magpies.md#speaker) [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md), whose main responsibility was to serve as the representative of the Magpies as an informal gathering.

This changed in Spring 387YE; the [manifesto](#manifesto) was established as part of an [administrative motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Senate_motion.md#administrative-motions). This saw the amendment of the Speaker of the Magpies title (previously appointed by the Vigilance assembly) to be elected by the members of the sodality. At that time the first Speaker **Morgan Waystone Path** was still serving in the position. In summer of the same year, the civil service presented a set of potential powers for the sodality, and a number of independent entities expressed their interest in the Magpies, as detailed in the **[Two for joy](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Two_for_joy.md)** wind of fortune.

At the first official meeting of the sodality, the [Whisper Gallery](../../in-character/eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md) declared eternal war against the Magpies until their organisation was abrogated or all the members were dead. This was apparently met with applause.

The original manifesto of the sodality included an explicit requirement for them to take responsibility for historical knowledge. The first line read "Aid and coordinate Imperial efforts to seek out lost artefacts and historical knowledge". The Constitutional Court judged that that would require that [all powers of historical research be delegated to the sodality](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Two_for_joy.md#historical-research). The Imperial Senate elected to [abridge the manifesto instead](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-manifesto-of-the-magpies), to avoid this outcome.

The Senate could restore the original manifesto at any time with a successful constitutional vote. If that happened and the [Minister of Historical Research](Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) was left vacant or abrogated, then the [torchbearers of Spider's Vantage](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Two_for_joy.md#spiders-eat-maggots) would provide an income of twenty one mana crystals each season for the sodality's use.
