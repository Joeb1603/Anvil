# Guildmaster of the Artisans

## Overview

The Guildmaster of the Artisans is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) originally [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#construct-guildhouse-of-the-artisans) by the Imperial Senate in Summer 385YE. Originally created as a position with [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) it was [amended](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-the-guildmaster-of-the-artisans) in an administrative motion to instead be elected each year.

## Responsibilities

The Guildmaster of the Artisans has no particular responsibilities. If the [Guildhouse of the Artisans](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#construct-guildhouse-of-the-artisans) were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Guildmaster to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of the Hall

The sinecure provides two measures each of [ambergelt](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#ambergelt), [beggar's lye](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggar27s-lye), [dragonbone](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone), and [iridescent gloaming](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming) and two ingots each of [green iron](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron), [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade), and [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) to the Guildmaster each season, to be used as they see fit.

## Appointment

The Guildmaster of the Artisans is an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen can hold this title. The Guildmaster of the Artisans serves for a year, and they can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Guildhouse of the Artisans

Established in southern [Northspires](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#northspires), in [Hahnmark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md) so as to be close to the [Runegrott](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Hahnmark.md#runegrott), the Guildhouse of the Artisans serves as a central hub for the Guild of Artisans. It is built in a distinctly Wintermark style - unavoidable given it is constructed on the side of a mountain - being of dark wood with peaked slate roofs. From a distance it might be mistaken for a thane's hall, but close up the distinction is very obvious. The grounds around the Guildhouse incorporate a number of forges, smelters, and smithies that are rarely quiet, with interior spaces given over to looms, crafting benches of all kinds, and places for [artisans](../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisans) from across the Empire to meet and talk shop.

At the time of its creation, the Guild of Artisans was an informal sodality - but in Autumn 388YE [Emperor Vesna](../../in-character/history/Emperor_Vesna.md) engaged Magistrate Abraham to [appraise the implications](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Forever_is_composed_of_nows.md#the-abraham-gambit) of their becoming a formal [Imperial sodality](../government/conclave/Imperial_sodality.md). If the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) approves, it is likely the Guildhouse will see a great deal more traffic.
