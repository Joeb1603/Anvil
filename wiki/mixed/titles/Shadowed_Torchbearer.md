# Shadowed Torchbearer

*[Image: The Shadowed Torchbearer enjoys the patronage of Shadowed Fire](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:JanonArt.png)*

## Overview

The Shadowed Torchbearer is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) created by the Imperial Senate during the [Spring Equinox 386YE](../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#create-title-of-shadowed-torchbearer) following an [offer](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Skeleton_key.md#shadowed-torchbearer-janon) presented by [Janon](../../in-character/eternals/Janon.md).

## Responsibilities

The Shadowed Torchbearer has a responsibility to fan the flames of passion in the Empire through whatever means are available (but especially magic), and to serve as a go-between for the [Shadowed Fire](../../in-character/eternals/Janon.md) and the Conclave as they need it.

## Powers

### Gifts of the Night Sword

The Shadowed Torchbearer receives gifts crafted by Janon. The exact details of these are unknown but it is expected that they will take the form of a [focus](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans), a [mage robe](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#armour), and some form of [liquor](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md).

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement or removal of the Shadowed Torchbearer is made by the [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md).

The Shadowed Torchbearer serves until they are replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. The Shadowed Torchbearer cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
