# Preceptor of the Skarsind College of Warcasting

## Overview

The Preceptor of The Skarsind College of Warcasting (often shortened to *Preceptor of Warcasting*, *Preceptor of War*, or even occasionally *The Warcaster*) is an [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) created following an [announcement](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_Skarsind_School_of_Warcasting.md) in Summer 380YE by Bloodcrow Yargol, [Civilian Commissioner](../government/senate/Civilian_commissioner.md) of the [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). Construction was completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox of 380YE.

The College of Warcasting was [expanded](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#expand-college-of-warcasting) following the 384YE Spring Equinox granting the Tamazi access to the classes provided. In return the Tamazi [artisans](../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) offered their own expertise in working with [dragonbone](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#dragonbone) to supply the Preceptor with [magic items](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) useful to [warcasters](../magic/lore/Imperial_Orcs_magical_traditions.md#warcasters).

The position of Preceptor is both a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), offering a regular income of crystal mana, and access to a ministry allowing the purchase of magic items from the Tamazi.

## Responsibilities

The Preceptor is called on to settle any disputes arising between students. The Preceptor is also responsible for ensuring the college runs smoothly, and dealing with anything that might threaten the good functioning of the college. They may also be called upon to advise on the curriculum, but most of the teaching is performed by veteran orc [warcasters](../magic/lore/Imperial_Orcs_magical_traditions.md#warcasters).

### Tools of the Warcasters

The Preceptor of the Skarsind College of Warcasting has access to a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md). The Preceptor can purchase magic items useful for [warcasters](../magic/lore/Imperial_Orcs_magical_traditions.md#warcasters) at a set price. The Preceptor makes their decision how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and ribbons are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

## Appointment

The Preceptor of the Skarsind College of Warcasting is appointed during the Autumn Equinox by Imperial Orcs who control [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) who [supported](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#supporting-an-army) an [Army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) during the previous year. The larger the military unit an individual has, and the more often they supported the Imperial armies, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only an Imperial Orc character may hold this title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. They cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).

## Skarsind College of Warcasting

Built on the northern slopes of Estermark, the College is often plunged into shadow even during the day. A dour, blocky building surrounded by a wooden palisade houses the staff and students dedicated to training [orc](../world/Orc.md) [magicians](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) in the battlefield arts of the [warcaster](../magic/lore/Imperial_Orcs_magical_traditions.md#warcasters).

The college stands in a well defended area, with access to a spring of natural, fresh water, it could easily hold off a small group of attackers for some time. Below the building - and forming part of the cellars of the college itself - is an old mine long since played out at the bottom of which lies a significant mana site that provides crystal mana for use by the orcs studying at the college.
