# Keeper of the Tome

## Overview

The Keeper of the Tome is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). Construction of the Temeschwar-Otkodov Mana Exchange was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Temeschwar_Otkodov_Mana_Exchange.md) by **Elsha Bertolf** as [Chair of the Wolf](Chair_of_the_Wolf.md) during the Spring Equinox 385YE following an opportunity presented to [take advantage](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Don-t_blame_me.md#exploit-the-thule) of the danger presented by the heralds of [Skathe](../../in-character/eternals/The_Thrice-cursed_Court.md#skathe) in the [forests of Metri](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md#metri).

Work was completed shortly before the Summer Solstice 385YE but election of the title was stalled due to [issues](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Temeschwar_Otkodov_Mana_Exchange.md#outcome) raised by the Constitutional Court. An administrative motion was [raised](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-the-keeper-of-the-tome) to the Imperial Senate at the first session of the Solstice and passed, allowing the title to be elected for the first time at the Summer Solstice 385YE.

## Responsibilities

The Keeper of the Tome is responsible for keeping the trading relations with the Thule cordial. In addition if the Temeschwar-Otkodov Mana Exchange were to be threatened, or something were to threaten the trade routes between Otkodov and Temeschwar, it would be the responsibility of the Keeper to deal with those threats.

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the title of Keeper of the Tome allows the holder to purchase quantities of mana crystals traded from the north at a set price. The Keeper makes their decision on how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Summer Solstice by citizens of the League who control [business](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md). The larger the business an individual has, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a League character may hold this title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As an Imperial title appointed through the Bourse, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
