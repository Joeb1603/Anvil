# Spear of the Seven

## Overview

The Spear of the Seven is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). Construction of the Hall of the Seven was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Hall_of_the_Seven.md) by **Bertrand** as [Bearer of the First Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) during the Spring Equinox 385YE following an opportunity presented to [take advantage](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_bones_of_what_you_believe.md#today-is-tomorrow) of the inspiration presented by the knights of Dawn to the thorns of Navarr.

## Responsibilities

The Spear of the Seven is expected to support Imperial heroes fighting the [vallorn](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md), to organise [captains](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) to support the struggle against the vallorn, and to gather resources to empower and assist efforts against the vallorn and vallornspawn.

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the title of Spear of the Seven allows the holder to purchase goods at a set price. The Spear makes their decision on how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

### Spear of Teleri

The Spear of the Seven bears the *Spear of Teleri*, an artefact [Blackwillow Twist](../magic/items/Blackwillow_Twist.md). If the Spear of the Seven loses their position, then the item must be given to the new titleholder with all haste. The Spear of the Seven is encouraged to take all appropriate precautions to keep this item safe and is expected to do everything within their power to recover it if it is lost.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Autumn Equinox by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a Navarr citizen. The Spear of the Seven is appointed annually. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Hall of the Seven

The Hall of the Seven is a dedicated wayhouse in Miaren originally proposed by *Asoli Briarbark*. In the courtyard of the wayhouse stands two plain statues; one of a thorn with a small plaque that names them as **Corwyn Heartsbane** and credits them for funding construction of the wayhouse. The other statue is of a brand with a plaque that reads "*In memory of Amaan Embercast, who fought the vallorn with all seven Virtues, and died protecting the Empire. Though his body may be forever lost, his memory stands here in his dear Miaren.*"

In the wayhouse proper there is a large painting depicting **Arlen Foreman-Smith** of House Tallstag, **Farren Silver Streams**, and Corwyn Heartsbane shaking hands. A small etching notes that this is in recognition of the work done by the Reeve of the Greenforest Fair, Farren Silver Streams, and Corwyn Heartsbane in securing the wayleave required to make this building possible.
