# Medical Officer of the Pegasus Respite

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Assembly of Highguard](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Highborn.png)*

## Overview

The Medical Officer of the Pegasus Respite is a [Highborn](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) created following an [announcement](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Pegasus_Respite.md) during the Autumn Equinox 383YE by **Nathaniel of Balthazar’s Vineyard**, as the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md). The construction was completed shortly before the 384YE Summer Solstice.

## Responsibilities

The Medical Officer of the Pegasus Respite is responsible for ensuring the herbs produced by the sinecure will go to the battle physicks of Highguard to serve the Empire.

In addition, if anything were to threaten the good operation of the infirmary, it would be the responsibility of the Medical Officer to address those problems.

## Powers

### Bounty of herbs

The Medical Officer receives receives an income of herbs each season: 8 doses of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), 4 doses of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), 4 doses of [imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), 4 doses of [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), and 4 doses of [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md)

*[Image: Doctor Lysimachus , Medical Officer of the Pegasus Respite](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Lysimachus.jpg)*

## Appointment

The Medical Officer of the Pegasus Respite is a national position appointed by the Synod. It is elected by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies) of Highguard.

The title can be held by any citizen of Highguard. The Medical Officer has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Highborn National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
