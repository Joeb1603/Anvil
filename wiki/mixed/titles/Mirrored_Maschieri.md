# Mirrored Maschieri

## Overview

The Mirrored Maschieri is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) created by the Imperial Senate as a position at the House Bourné School of Couture.

## Responsibilities

The Mirrored Maschieri is expected to act as a champion of fashion and design right across the Empire, to celebrate true beauty and to support Sarvos’ future.

## Appointment

The Mirrored Maschieri is appointed by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can only be held by a citizen of the League. The Mirrored Maschieri has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
