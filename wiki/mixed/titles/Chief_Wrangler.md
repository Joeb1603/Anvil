# Chief Wrangler

## Overview

The Chief Wrangler is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). The motion to [construct](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Wrangler-s_Market.md) was proposed by **Ana i Taziel i Riqueza**, as Senator for [Segura](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Segura.md), at the Summer Solstice 388YE following an opportunity [presented](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/No_Matter_What.md#break-the-chains) by the Imperial orcs of the [Sunstorm](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Sunstorm.md) sept. The Wrangler's Market presents some opportunity to trade cattle to the Suranni - but secretly also acts as a front to enable spying on the territory of [Arbonne](../../in-character/world/Bay_of_Catazar.md#kalino-or-arbonne).

## Responsibilities

The Chief Wrangler is responsible for ensuring that sufficient cattle are traded to the Suranni to allow [Unshackled](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) scouts to spy on the territory. If the market were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Wrangler to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Cattle Trade

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Cattle Trade** | |
| 7 Crowns | 6 ingots each of orichalcum and weltsilver |
| 17 Crowns | 12 ingots each of orichalcum and weltsilver |
| 30 Crowns | 18 ingots each of orichalcum and weltsilver |

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the position of Chief Wrangler allows the holder to purchase quantities of orichalcum and weltsilver at a set price. The Dhomiro spends their decision about how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money (representing cattle) is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

Operating the ministry allows the spy network in the territory to continue undetected. As long as the ministry is operated each season, the Suranni will have no way at all to discover the network. Indeed, it is in the interests of corrupt officials not to look too closely and even to cover up any evidence of spying that they did discover - because it might disrupt the lucrative trade.

## Appointment

The Chief Wrangler is appointed during the Autumn Equinox. The Wrangler is appointed by citizens of the Brass Coast who control [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md). The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a Freeborn character may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
