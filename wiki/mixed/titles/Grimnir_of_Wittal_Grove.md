# Grimnir of Wittal Grove

## Overview

The Grimnir of Wittal Grove is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) citizen. The motion to construct the Healing House of Wittal Grove was [passed](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Healing_House_of_Wittal_Grove.md) by the Imperial Senate during the Winter Solstice 383YE following an opportunity [presented](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/All_lift_together.md#deep-in-the-marshes-of-kallavesa) by *Elina of Redleaf's Hall*.

## Responsibilities

The Grimnir of Wittal Grove is expected to oversee the Healing House of Wittal Grove. They have a responsibility to purchase surplus herbs from the grimnir who visit and to allow the herbalists of Kallavesa to respond to the needs of the heroes of Wintermark. If the Healing House of Wittal Grove were to be threatened in any way, however, it would be the responsibility of the Grimnir to deal with those threats.

### Ministry

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the title of Grimnir of Wittal Grove allows the holder to purchase quantities of [herbs](../magic/potions/Herb.md) at a set price. The Grimnir spends their money and makes their choices during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and herbs are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

### Bounty of the Marshes

The Healing House of Wittal Grove is also a [great work](../commissions/Great_work.md) that provides herbs to every [herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) in [Kallavesa](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Kallavesa.md) owned by a citizen of Wintermark. As standard, this is a selection of random herbs however, the Grimnir of Wittal Grove can make an announcement in the Imperial Senate to indicate which variety of herb they wish the herb masters to focus on. The Grimnir of Wittal Grove only needs to make an announcement to change the herb, either to a specific type or to return to no particular herbal focus.

No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Grimnir of Wittal Grove. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise their [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Grimnir's decision.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Winter Solstice by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead. The position of Grimnir of Wittal Grove is intended specifically to be held by a [grimnir](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark_military_concerns.md#grimnir). Though there is no legal requirement for such a position, the halls who supported the construction are expected to withdraw support if anyone other than a grimnir is elected to the position.

The title can be held by a citizen of Wintermark. The Grimnir can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Wintermark [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
