# Grandmaster

## Overview

Grandmaster is an Imperial title bestowed upon a [magician](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) who is a member of a [Conclave order](../government/conclave/Conclave_order.md). Each order is represented by a single grandmaster, which means that at this time there are seven grandmasters.

Grandmaster is a political title closely involved with the running of the Conclave. Grandmasters encourage members of their order to participate in Conclave affairs, and represent their order during a Conclave session. Most grandmasters also work to maintain cohesion within their order and to encourage members to pursue and advance the manifesto of the order. A strong grandmaster can organise the magicians that are part of their order and help them wield political and magical power throughout the Empire.

## Responsibilities

Each grandmaster is responsible for supporting the manifesto, advancing the goals, and representing the members of their [Conclave order](../government/conclave/Conclave_order.md). They receive numerous political powers to allow them to do so. Their responsibility is to their order first, to the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md) second, and to the Empire as a whole third.

The grandmaster is also responsible for overseeing the distribution and use of the resources made available to their order. Each order receives a share of the mana from the font, and it is the responsibility of the grandmaster to ensure that the mana they receive on behalf of their order is used in pursuit of their order's goals. Grandmasters have been removed, and in extreme cases [excommunicated](../government/synod/Writ_of_Excommunication.md) for engaging in too much brazen corruption at the expense of their order.

## Powers

Each grandmaster position is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) in the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md).

### Guiding the Conclave

Each Conclave order is entitled to submit a single [declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of any kind to the agenda for *each* [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). An order's declaration must be submitted by a member of the order, but it can be anyone chosen by the grandmaster, including themselves. To support the orders, the Civil Service provide two writs that allow the bearer to submit a declaration, one for each Conclave session.

Although the grandmaster is empowered to choose how to employ their order's declaration, it remains uniquely tied to the order. The Imperial titles with the power to raise a declaration may have their proxy speak instead of them in Conclave, but since you legally cannot proxy your status as a member of a Conclave order, it is not possible to have a proxy present an order declaration on your behalf. This means whoever the grandmaster chooses to submit the declaration to the [Prefect of the Conclave](Prefect_of_the_Conclave.md) is the only person who can be legally recognised as the person presenting the declaration. Whoever signs the writ, must present the declaration to Conclave (the only exception will be where OOC reasons make this impossible).

A writ can be used by any member of an order who has a valid legal claim to own it. If a grandmaster dies, any writs in their possession at the time pass to their legal heirs.

### Guiding an Order

- **A grandmaster can direct their order using a declaration of concord**
- **An order can only support a single situation each season**
- **It is currently only possible to direct an order as part of an opportunity**
- **Sending an order costs 20 mana crystals from the Conclave font**

One of the potential uses of a [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Concord](../government/conclave/Concord.md) is to direct one of the [Conclave orders](../government/conclave/Conclave_order.md) to support or investigate an ongoing situation. It is only possible to direct an order using one of that order's declarations; it is not possible for any other declaration to call an order to action. A declaration guiding an order in this way must clearly state which ongoing situation the order is to support. If the declaration is passed then at the end of the Conclave session twenty [mana crystals](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) are removed from the font to fund the members of the order in supporting whichever situation they have been called to action over.

### Veil of Night

Once per summit, if a grandmaster choses to submit their order [declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) to the Conclave agenda personally (rather than allowing another member of their order to submit it), then they can choose to invoke the Veil of Night. If they do so, they are not required to reveal any detail of a declaration: it is added to the agenda simply as "The Grandmaster of (Order) presents a declaration under the Veil of Night." The grandmaster must still provide the wording of the declaration to the Civil Service when the matter is raised to the agenda and as normal it is the precise wording presented that will be discussed and voted on. After the summit, the details become public knowledge as with any other declaration.

### Power of Precedence

During a Conclave session the ability to speak before the Conclave - to [take part in the debate](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md#debating-a-declaration) - is controlled by the grandmasters. After the presentation of an [agenda item](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md#setting-the-agenda) is complete, the grandmaster may nominate volunteers to speak on the topic being discussed, in order determined by the [Principle of Precedence](../government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence).

Rather than nominate a speaker, the grandmaster may choose to pass (sitting out that round of the debate but allowing them to nominate someone in future rounds) or move to vote. If they move to vote the grandmaster cannot nominate any new speakers, but the debate finishes at the end of the current round once more than half of the grandmasters have moved to vote.

### Share of the Font

At the start of each summit, any [mana that was left in the Font](../government/conclave/Conclave_vault.md#the-font) at the end of the previous summit is divided up between the orders according to the [Principle of Precedence](../government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence), along with a share of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) and any bequests made to their order (such as [materials](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md) or [vis](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md)). The grandmaster is the sole arbiter of how these resources are used, within the remit of their [responsibilities](#responsibilities).

### Funding the Order

The grandmaster has the ability to spend money to buy crystal mana for their order's vault. A grandmaster can spend money each season as if they controlled a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md). The civil service uses the money spent in this manner to buy directly from other Imperial citizens who have access to crystal mana, and provides it to the grandmaster along with their share of the font at the start of the next summit.

During the Spring Equinox 384YE, the Imperial Senate chose to [take advantage](../../in-character/senate-motions/Expand_Mana_Ministries.md) of an opportunity to [improve](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Honey_and_vinegar.md#old-stones) the ability of the grandmasters to purchase mana. Expansions to the mana exchanges in [Tassato](Steward_of_the_Tassato_Mana_Exchange.md), [Temeschwar](Magic_Butcher.md), and [Sarvos](Steward_of_the_Sarvos_Mana_Exchange.md) allow the civil service to purchase more mana without increasing the cost to the grandmaster.

### Stipend

During the Winter Solstice 380YE, the Imperial Senate [voted](../../in-character/senate-motions/Amend_powers_of_Grandmasters.md) to grant the grandmasters a [stipend](New_Imperial_titles.md#stipend) to assist in the purchase of crystal mana. The amount of the stipend was, however, set to 0 crowns. A motion to [increase the stipend](../../in-character/senate-motions/Increase_Grandmasters_stipend.md) during the Spring Equinox 382YE was defeated. Each grandmaster technically receives a stipend from the treasury, but gets no money for it.

## Appointment

- **The Hall of Worlds is officially allocated to the orders who need to elect a grandmaster for thirty minutes before Conclave when an election is due to allow them to hold hustings**
- **The election takes place as soon as the Order indicate they are ready to vote**
- **If a grandmaster retires, dies or is challenged, then an election is held before the start of the next Conclave session, provided the Prefect is given sufficient notice**

The [civil service](../government/senate/Civil_service.md) arrange for hustings to take place half an hour before each Conclave session on Saturday evening. The Hall of Worlds is officially ceded to the orders in question to allow members of the Order to gather and discuss who will be appointed. While members of other orders (and priests of the Imperial Synod exercising the right of [witness](../government/synod/Member_of_the_Synod.md#witness)) can attend the meeting they cannot speak, and the civil service will exclude anyone who attempts to interrupt the proceedings.

If a grandmaster position is up for regular election, or is vacant, a formal vote takes place as soon as the Order indicates that they are ready to vote. If the Order cannot complete the process within half an hour, then the election is the first item of Conclave. There is no opportunity for anyone to speak at that point, members of the orders are expected to use the hustings to make their decision and then to vote once Conclave begins. If there are two or more grandmasters to appoint, the order with the highest [precedence](../government/conclave/Conclave.md#principle-of-precedence) is elected first.

Each grandmaster is selected by members of their Conclave order. Only a member of the order can present themselves for the title, and only a member of the order can vote in a grandmaster election. Once a magician has been eligible to vote in a grandmaster election they cannot vote in the election of the grandmaster of a different order until a year has passed.

A grandmaster *cannot* be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by any chamber of the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md). However, the members of an order may [challenge](../government/conclave/Conclave_order.md#challenging-the-grandmaster) the grandmaster and trigger a new election.

The schedule for annual appointment is:

- Winter - [Celestial Arch](../government/conclave/Celestial_Arch.md) and [Rod and Shield](../government/conclave/Rod_and_Shield.md)
- Spring - [Golden Pyramid](../government/conclave/Golden_Pyramid.md) and [Sevenfold Path](../government/conclave/Sevenfold_Path.md)
- Summer - [Unfettered Mind](../government/conclave/Unfettered_Mind.md)
- Autumn - [Shuttered Lantern](../government/conclave/Shuttered_Lantern.md) and [Silver Chalice](../government/conclave/Silver_Chalice.md)

A grandmaster serves for a year, until a [challenge](../government/conclave/Conclave_order.md#challenging-the-grandmaster) forces their re-election, until they die or step down, or until the start of the next election. If a grandmaster becomes vacant unexpectedly, the [civil service](../government/senate/Civil_service.md) will arrange an election to choose a replacement before the start of the next Conclave session, provided the [Prefect of the Conclave](Prefect_of_the_Conclave.md) is informed at least one hour before the hustings is due to begin. If a grandmaster title become vacant and there is less than an hour before hustings, then the election takes place at the start of the following Conclave session.
