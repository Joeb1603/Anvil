# Archmage of Night

*[Image: The Night archmage supports Imperial magicians and serves as a diplomat to the realm of Night.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Night_Archmage.png)*

## Overview

[Archmage](Archmage.md) is an Imperial title bestowed upon a [magician](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) by the members of the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md). There are six archmages; one for each of the magical realms. They serve as a focal point for [ritualists](../../out-of-character/rules/Rituals.md) of that realm, as well as helping to guide the use of the Conclave's resources. The six archmages match the six realms into which magic is divided. Where the influence of a [grandmaster](Grandmaster.md) is primarily political, the archmages are responsible for attending to the magical business of the Conclave. They are assumed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md), the [Military Council](../government/military/Military_Council.md), foreign dignitaries and [eternals](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) alike to be well-versed in the capabilities and lore of their realm. The archmages are often seen as the public faces of the Conclave; they are the individuals most likely to be consulted by other Imperial bodies when they wish to know what magical possibilities exist.

Each archmage is addressed by their title and the name of the realm of magic they stand for - *Elyssiathain, Archmage of Night* or *Archmage Pavel Denisovich Patriciu of Night*. The title archmage comes originally from [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md), a development of the title [mage](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#magi).

## Responsibilities

The Archmage of Night is responsible for attending to the magical business of the Conclave, and possesses [powerful tools](#regalia-of-night) to help them do so. They represent the Empire to the eternals of the Night realm, and vice versa. They also work with magicians versed in the lore of [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) to expand the Empire's understanding and power in that realm, and employ it to the advantage of Imperial citizens.

## Powers

Each archmage position is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) in the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md).

### Archmage of Night

The Archmage of Night has the legal powers common to the Archmages. They can participate in the [Arcane Colloquium](../government/conclave/Arcane_Colloquium.md); accept a formal [parley](Archmage.md#parley) with Night eternals; raise a [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Alignment](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md) for a Night eternal once each summit; and advise the magistrates as the recognised [master of Night magic](Archmage.md#master-of-magic).

### Regalia of Night

In common with the other archmages, the Archmage of Night has legal ownership of three powerful [magic items](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) that make up the [regalia of the archmage](Archmage.md#regalia-of-the-archmage). These include the [Staff of Night](Archmage.md#staff-of-the-archmage) which greatly enhances the bearer's prowess with Night ritual magic; the [Violet Chain of Aesh](Archmage.md#chain-of-aesh) that can reduce the magnitude of a Night ritual; and the [Belt of the Great Wyrm](Archmage.md#belt-of-stars) which can allow multiple [covens](../../out-of-character/rules/Bands.md#covens) from the same nation to cooperate in the performance of a single Night ritual.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Archmage of Night is made by [declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). A candidate can be presented for the post of archmage only once a season, and the title can be held by any Imperial citizen.

The Archmage of Night serves until they are replaced by another magician using the declaration of candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, an archmage cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).

## The Eternals of Night

The Archmage of Night is the Empire's formal representative to the inhabitants of the Night realm, and often serves as a go-between when those eternals wish to communicate with the Empire. Most eternals will treat the archmage as a peer - although that can mean very different things for different eternals. One of the most potent abilities the Archmage of Night possesses is the power of [plenipotentiary](Archmage.md#plenipotentiary), allowing them to communicate with eternals from their realm and potentially arranging a [parley](Archmage.md#parley). A list of previous plenipotentiary results can be found [here](../../indexes/by-category/Plenipotentiary.md)

Of all the archmages, the Archmage of Night may be said to have the most difficult job when dealing with the eternals. The Night eternals are creatures of passion, rather than reason, and possess an alien intellect quite different to that of the mortals they deal with. They might take actions that make sense only if one understands the complex thought processes of the eternal and otherwise look to be entirely whimsical. They can also be surprisingly single-natured - no archmage has ever managed to sway the eternal [Sadogua](../../in-character/eternals/Sadogua.md) from the belief that magicians are superior to non-magicians for example, and every interaction with that eternal seems to be driven by a desire to exalt magic users over their fellow citizens in some way. The concerns of several Night eternals are also to some degree contradictory - for example [Sung](../../in-character/eternals/Sung.md) and the [Whisper Gallery](../../in-character/eternals/Whisper_Gallery.md) are concerned both with discovering mysteries and secrets and simultaneously with preventing the truth being uncovered. At any given moment, a Night eternal may be one of several sides of a conflict without seemingly suffering any cognitive dissonance.

The Archmage of Night is often in a good position to present declarations of [amity and enmity](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md) to the Imperial conclave when relations between the Empire and an eternal need to be formalised. Indeed, more than any other [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md), the Archmage benefits from staying abreast of the relationships between the inhabitants of the Night realm and Imperial citizens. The current status of the Night eternals with an interest in the Empire can be found [here](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#night).
