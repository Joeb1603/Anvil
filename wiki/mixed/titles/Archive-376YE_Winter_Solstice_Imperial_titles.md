## The [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md)

These seats are usually auctioned at various points throughout the year. Due to concerns of economic stability in a time of Imperial crisis, the seats gained at the Winter Equinox 376 will run until the Winter Equinox 377 and then the normal process of re-election will begin again.
