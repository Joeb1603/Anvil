# Stone Smith

## Overview

The Stone Smith is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a [Marcher](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) citizen. The title was [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Stone_Smith-s_Workshop.md) by the Imperial Senate at the Spring Equinox 387YE. The Stone Smith is responsible for organising the nation's [smiths](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Smith.md), and helping them to create bargains that aid the nation. More information on the opportunity that led to this title's creation can be found on the **[A pinch of salt](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_pinch_of_salt.md)** wind of fortune

## Responsibilities

The Stone Smith has a responsibility to organise the nation's smiths and help them to create bargains that aid the nation. In addition, the Stone Smith is responsible for overseeing the workshop. If the workshop, or those who work there, were to be threatened, then it would be the responsibility of the Stone Smith to deal with those threats.

## Powers

> **Suitability**  
> The Stone Smith is intended to be held by a smith: the smiths who visit, trade, and work at the workshop expect one of their own to be responsible for overseeing it. If the title is held by a character who does not have the [smith](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Smith.md) archetype, then the player must email admin@profounddecisions.co.uk to let us know.

### Seasonal Income

The Stone Smith receives a seasonal income of one throne from the sale of quality goods produced at the workshop.

### Key to Door

The smiths of Mitwold and Upwold have pledged to provide the Stone Smith with a [Pauper's Key](../magic/items/Pauper-s_Key.md) each year at the Spring Equinox, provided that the holder of the title was publicly committed to the role of smith in the Marchers (**OOC Note:** Had the smith archetype when they were appointed).

### Call to the Gull

The Stone Smith is able to send a message to a single eternal during the Autumn Equinox in order to benefit the Marches. This message follows the same restrictions as the [plenipotentiaries](Archmage.md#plenipotentiary) of the archmages. It apparently draws on a very old bargain with a herald named *the Gull*, and there is some speculation that this creature might hail from the [City of Salt and Sail](../../in-character/eternals/Polyptoton.md).

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Summer Solstice. The Stone Smith is appointed by citizens of the Marches who control [farms](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md). The larger the farm an individual has, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a citizen of the Marches may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).

## Stone Smith's Workshop

Built near the [Woldstone](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md#the-woldstone) in [Upwold](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Upwold.md), the Stone Smith's Workshop is a relatively new construction. It acts as a repository of lore for the [smiths](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Smith.md) of the Marches, and a place for them to congregate and discuss matters. The workshop was an expensive proposition, requiring an amount of [ilium](../../out-of-character/rules/Ilium.md) to create the unusual tools and equipment stored there. It serves both as a place for new smiths to be trained, as well as a place for them to discuss their less mundane business with their peers.
