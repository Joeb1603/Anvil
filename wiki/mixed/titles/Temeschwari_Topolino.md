# Temeschwari Topolino

## Overview

The Temeschwari Topolino is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). The [announcement](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Circo_del_Topo.md) was made by  **Lucrezia Corvinascura**, as [Chair of the Wolf](Chair_of_the_Wolf.md), in Winter 387YE. The Temeschwari Topolino is responsible for working with the Rat Catchers guild of Temeschwar and serving as their liaison should the need arise.

## Responsibilities

The Temeschwari Topolino has a responsibility for working with the Rat Catchers guild of Temeschwar to find wider business opportunities to use captured rats. They are also responsible for serving as the liaison to the Rat Catchers guild of Temeschwar if the need should arise. Finally, if the Circo del Topo, its workers, or its punters were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Topolino to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Profitable Business

The Temeschwari Topolino has custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive 216 rings in profits from training the rats captured by the Rat Catchers guild of Temeschwar.

## Appointment

The Temeschwari Topolino is a [national position](Imperial_title.md#national-position) appointed by unanimous decision of the senators of the League.

The Topolino has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die or step down. They can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
