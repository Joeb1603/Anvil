# Dean of the Lyceum

*[Image: The Dean of the Lyceum oversees the most famous college for the study of magic in the Empire.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:EvilWizard.jpg)*

## Overview

The Dean of the Lyceum is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) in the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md) bestowed on a [magician](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician).

The Lyceum was established during the reign of [Empress Aenea](../../in-character/history/Empress_Aenea.md) on an island east of [Siroc](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc). It is a centre of arcane learning in the Empire, and the Dean of the Lyceum maintains sumptuous apartments here. Approach to the island is carefully controlled by the inhabitants, and the most promising magicians are invited to study or teach there. When not pursuing research for the Dean, the masters of the Lyceum dedicate themselves to their own pursuits in comfort and security.

In practice, many members of the Lyceum do not live in the physical college itself, rather they participate in research through exchanges of letters for which they receive a small stipend from the college. There are (for example) [Varushkan](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md) masters of the Lyceum who spend most of their lives with their [cabal](../magic/lore/Varushka_magical_traditions.md#cabals), [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) masters of the Lyceum who rarely leave their dreaming [spires](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Spire.md), and [Freeborn](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) masters who make a great deal of use of [Call Winged Messenger](../magic/rituals/Call_Winged_Messenger.md) to send the results of their researches to their colleagues at the Lyceum while maintaining their mendicant lifestyles.

## Responsibilities

The Dean oversees the Lyceum, and directs the progress of Imperial ritual research. While the Lyceum is often seen as the foremost centre of magical research, there is some rivalry with similar institutions elsewhere in the Empire and beyond.

While the Dean has the final say in the research agenda of the Lyceum, it is common for other magicians to approach her and suggest areas which might benefit one or other of the [orders](../government/conclave/Conclave_order.md), Imperial nations or other interest groups in the Empire. Historically some Deans have taken a scholastic approach to their position while others have used it to enrich themselves.

## Powers

The Dean of the Lyceum is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) in the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md).

### Guiding the magicians of the Lyceum

The Lyceum is a centre of magical learning where ritual magicians from across the Empire come to study, debate, discuss and pursue arcane mastery. In return for maintaining a good standard of living, these magicians are expected to perform ritual research and study for the betterment of the Empire. The Dean is responsible for setting the direction that research takes.

The Dean can direct research towards [formalising](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md) a new ritual so that it can be mastered. The ritual must already exist in the form of an *[arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md#arcane-projection)*, but there is no requirement to select one they have personally commissioned. It is important to note that [creating a new formulaic ritual](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#creating-a-new-formulaic-ritual) is an art not a precise science - the final ritual may differ from the spontaneous magic described in the arcane projection. The Lyceum can work on formalising one ritual at a time, and the research may take anywhere from a season to several years depending on the complexity and the resources available. The amount of time it takes to complete research into a ritual depends on the magnitude of that ritual. It requires at least three months (one season) for every twenty ranks of magnitude the final ritual will have. If research is not completed, the Lyceum keeps notes on their work so far, and may resume it at a later date from where it was left off.

At the completion, the Dean receives a [ritual text](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) suitable for use in mastering the ritual. The Dean may keep the knowledge of the ritual restricted to themselves and their allies, or through a [Declaration of Imperial lore](../government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md) the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md) can have that ritual added to the wider body of [Imperial lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list), making it available to any ritual magician in the Empire.

If the Declaration of Imperial lore passes, the Conclave must raise the ten thrones required to pay for the work involved. A motion requesting funding from the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) is often put forward, if there are good relations between the two houses.

**OOC Note:** The Dean selects the [arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md#arcane-projection) they wish to [formulate](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md) by e-mail to Profound Decisions [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) before downtime closes. The final magnitude of the ritual text is not guaranteed to be the same as that of the arcane projection.

### The Boon of the Black Sloth

Since the completion of the [Quiet Annexe](#the-quiet-annexe), the eternal [Sadogua](../../in-character/eternals/Sadogua.md) functions as a very unofficial patron of the college, encouraging his heralds and magicians seeking his favour to assist the researchers and theoreticians at the college. When [codifying a ritual](#guiding-the-magicians-of-the-lyceum) the Dean can call on the assistance of these heralds and magicians to speed up the process. Doing so requires payment in the form of [iridescent gloaming](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#iridescent-gloaming).

The first five ranks of additional research in a season cost 18 measures of gloaming. The next five ranks will cost a further 44 measures (for a total of 62 measures). The next five ranks will cost *another* 88 measures, and so on doubling the cost for each further additional five ranks of research within the same season.

Unlike the additional benefits provided a college of magic by a [specialisation](../commissions/College_of_magic.md#specialisation), this additional research speed is not restricted to any one magical realm - the Brother of Wizards may favour [Night magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Night_magic.md) but his allies are adept in all six magical lores.

This power cannot be used if the [eternal](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Sadogua](../../in-character/eternals/Sadogua.md) is under the [enmity](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md#enmity) of the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md), however.

### Arbiter of Imperial Lore

The Dean of the Lyceum is empowered to raise a single [Declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [Imperial lore](../government/conclave/Imperial_lore.md) each summit to the agenda for debate during a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). The Civil Service will provide the Dean of the Lyceum with a single writ - to submit their declaration to the Conclave agenda they should complete the writ and hand it to the office of the [Prefect of the Conclave](Prefect_of_the_Conclave.md) at least one hour before the [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md) begins.

Unlike a [grandmaster](Grandmaster.md), the Dean of the Lyceum is personally responsible for any [presentation](../government/conclave/Declaration.md#presentation). While a [proxy](../government/senate/Proxy.md) may be appointed to deliver the presentation, their declaration is recorded as having come from the Dean of the Lyceum.

### Apartments at the Lyceum

The Dean has fine apartments at the heart of the Lyceum itself. These luxurious chambers have been built and rebuilt by the finest architects the Empire has to offer and allow the Dean (and their family if desired) to live a rich lifestyle marked by fine food, access to luxuries and exposure to cultural excellence from across the Empire.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Dean is made by the [Declaration of Candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). Any Imperial citizen may hold the title. A candidate can be presented for the post of Dean only once a season.

The Dean of the Lyceum serves until they are replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md). When a Dean loses their title, research on their projects ends although it is not unknown for a Dean to return to earlier projects of interest and have them completed in their own name.

## The Lyceum

The Lyceum was established during the reign of [Empress Aenea](../../in-character/history/Empress_Aenea.md) on an island east of [Siroc](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#siroc). It is a centre of arcane learning in the Empire, and the Dean of the Lyceum maintains sumptuous apartments here. Approach to the island is carefully controlled by the magicians and professors, and the most promising magicians are invited to study or teach there. The climate is generally warm and pleasant, and the atmosphere on the island tends towards the relaxed and unhurried.

### The Quiet Annexe

Following an [offer](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Mean_what_you_say.md#the-black-sloth-and-the-inclination-of-gratitude) from the [eternal](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Sadogua](../../in-character/eternals/Sadogua.md), a major extension to the Lyceum was [approved](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Lyceum_Annexe.md) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Autumn 382YE. This [commission](../commissions/Commission.md) saw some of the first major construction on the island in over a century. The majority of the new construction took place along the western coast of the island, in a particularly idyllic area popular with students and faculty alike for quiet meditation and private assignations. The orange tree orchard in question surrounds a Night [regio](../magic/rituals/Regio.md), anchored to a set of old ruins of unknown provenance. The area is now a crowded garden featuring a many-coloured assortment of night flowers, subtle wind-chimes, and colourful streamers. The general garden atmosphere has been enhanced with bowers and statues as well as several work rooms, storage for scrolls and books, and the like.

Somewhat controversially, the designs include what is believed to be a discreet statue of the Black Sloth which has of course raised the vague spectre of [idolatry](../government/synod/Religious_crime.md#idolatry). It takes the form of a statue of a crow in black stone with amber eyes that seem to gleam in the half-light. The statue is placed within a grove of orange trees in such a way that whatever the time of day it is always somehow in shadow. Around the crow’s leg is twined a golden chain. This slightly problematic statue sits on a plinth marked with the rune [Diras](../magic/lore/Diras.md) and bears the inscription ‘In Friendship’. Each night it becomes the focus of a spontaneous [enchantment](../../out-of-character/rules/Enchantment.md) that bears all the hallmarks of the [Chamber of Delights](../magic/rituals/Chamber_of_Delights.md) making it a popular place for late-night picnics and nocturnal trysts for many of the residents of the island.

On the opposite side of the regio is a second statue, in the same style, that depicts a veiled and hooded statue of a mysterious Varushkan cabalist, depicts the Dean who oversaw the construction - Simargl, the Empty One. The statue is very slightly larger than that of the crow. It is marked with the runes [Wyr](../magic/lore/Wyr.md), [Xun](../magic/lore/Xun.md), and [Naeve](../magic/lore/Naeve.md). The statue is entwined with climbing moonflower, a plant known for its tendency to attract moths.

### The Lyceum Schloss

A small castle built in [Commonwealth](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Commonwealth.md) style that is used as a residence by wizards from that nation who visit the island to take advantage of their access to Imperial lore.

### The Wards

Following the Autumn Equinox 385YE, the [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) launched a [surprise attack](../../in-character/winds-of-war/A_beacon_of_smoke.md) on [Madruga](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) that saw both the destruction of the city of Siroc and the capture of the Lyceum isle. During the Winter Solstice, Imperial heroes used the [Sentinel Gate](../../in-character/world/Sentinel_Gate.md) to reach the island and [activate its wards](../../in-character/winds-of-war/A_beacon_of_smoke.md#intercede-at-the-lyceum-battle). Specifically, they shrouded the island in darkness, fog, and mist to deny its secrets to the Grendel and to offer protection to the professors, students, and visiting magicians who would otherwise also have fallen into the hands of the orcs. [The wards](../../in-character/winds-of-war/A_beacon_of_smoke.md#objective-raise-the-lyceums-wards), built to exploit the odd properties of two unique [regio](../magic/rituals/Regio.md), had never really been tried before so it is perhaps not a bad thing that the Imperial heroes chose not to activate the Storm wards. As a consequence, however, the Lyceum was cut off from the mortal world - nobody could travel to or from the island - for a whole year.

### The Gates

Shortly before Summer Solstice 387YE, Sadogua provided [plans](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Sound_and_vision.md#a-course-of-shadows) for "*Gates of Shadow*"; a pair of gates that drew inspiration from the [Paths of Lan Thúven](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Liathaven.md#paths-of-lan-thúven-gone). Construction of these gates was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Path_to_the_Lyceum.md) by **Amalia Vicente**, as the [Arcane Architect](Arcane_Architect.md), during the Spring Equinox 388YE and work was completed in [Oranseri](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Feroz.md#oranseri) just three months later.
