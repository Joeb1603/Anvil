# Minister of Historical Research

*[Image: The first Minister of Historical Research was the Urizen former-Senator, Ioseph of Phoenix Reach.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Historical_Minister.jpg)*

## Overview

The Minister of Historical Research is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). It was established by [majority vote of the Senate in Autumn 377](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Minister_of_Historical_Research.md) to help the Senate handle requests for [historical research](../government/senate/Historical_research.md). Prior to the Spring Equinox 384YE, two other titles (the [Advisor on the Vallorn](Advisor_on_the_Vallorn.md) and the [Imperial Advisor for the Feni](Imperial_Advisor_for_the_Feni.md)) also had the power to commission [historical research](../government/senate/Historical_research.md). Their powers [were removed](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-historical-research) following [changes](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_long_December.md#hold-on-to-these-moments) to how the [Department](../government/senate/Historical_research.md#the-department-of-historical-research) was organised. The Minister is now the only title able to instruct the Department to perform research on their behalf.

## Responsibilities

The purpose of the Minister is to authorise research requests, freeing the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) from the need to assess each request directly.

## Power

### Historical Research

The Minister has the power to announce [historical research](../government/senate/Historical_research.md) requests. The civil service is able to process just one research request each season - research requests authorised by the Senate or the Minister all count against this limit.

To use this power, the Minister provides an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) to the Civil Service, who present the decision to the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Minister. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise its [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Minister's decision.

## Appointment

The Minister of Historical Research is an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen can hold this title. The Minister serves for a year, and they can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
