# Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills

*[Image: Estrella of Feroz, a former Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Estrella.jpg)*

## Overview

The Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a [Freeborn](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) citizen. It is a national position appointed through the [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md).

The Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills serves as an arbitrator and mediator charged with keeping peace between the often volatile [families](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Family.md) of [Kahraman](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md). In the process, the Dhomiro encounters plenty of opportunities to trade for [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) from the mines of [Damata](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#serra-damata), [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) from the quarries of the [Jade Range](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#jade-range), and [beggar's lye](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#beggars-lye) gathered from the beggarwood forests of western [Gambit](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#gambit).

Some Dhomiro take their title very seriously, and spend their spare months actively travelling through Kahraman looking for opportunities to improve the lot of the Freeborn who live there. Others leave the job to agents - reaping the rewards of their position by proxy.

Prior to the [popular change](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/384YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_fortune.md#sails-of-fire) prompted by the Brass Coast National Assembly, only those captains of fleets that had engaged in [privateering](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md#take-independent-action) during the previous year could vote for the Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills. This changed shortly before the Spring Equinox 383YE and now every captain of a [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) in the Brass Coast has a say in who will hold the title.

In 385YE around the time of the [appraisal](../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md) into Kahraman, the territory faced significant [problems with raiders](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Time_and_time_again.md#the-kahraman-problem) during which the Dhomiro lost access to the top two ranks of their ministry. This problem was alleviated in Summer 385YE when the Senate opted to [fund caravan guards](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#fund-guards-in-kahraman) to secure the territory.

## Responsibilities

There is a tacit expectation that the Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills will help ensure the economic stability of [Kahraman](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md) - and where possible help increase the [prosperity](../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md) of the [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) owners and [artisans](../../out-of-character/rules/Crafting_skills.md#artisan) that make the hills their home. In practice, they are rarely called on to intervene directly but it is not unknown for inhabitants of Kahraman having purely economic problems to bring them to the Dhomiro before they bother a senator with them - even where the title holder is not themselves a Kahraman native it is assumed they have sufficient self-interest to remain concerned about the general economic welfare of the hill dwellers.

On occasion, the Dhomiro may be asked to help out with problems concerning the [Great Mine of Briante](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#the-great-mine-of-briante) or the [Damatian Cliffs](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md#the-damatian-cliffs), especially where those problems have arisen from conflict between the Freeborn and the individual who holds the Imperial Bourse seat. Some politically astute Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills have invested the wealth they accrue from their title towards placing trusted family members in control of those valuable seats on the Imperial Bourse.

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the title of Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills allows the holder to purchase quantities of special materials at a set price. The Dhomiro makes their decision about how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Autumn Equinox. The Dhomiro of the Cinnabar Hills is appointed by citizens of the Brass Coast who control [fleet](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md). The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a Brass Coast character may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
