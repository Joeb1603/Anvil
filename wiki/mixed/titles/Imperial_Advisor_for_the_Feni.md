# Imperial Advisor for the Feni

*[Image: The first Imperial Advisor for the Feni was appointed by the Imperial Senate in Summer 383YE.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Watt6915.png)*

## Overview

The Imperial Advisor for the Feni is an [Imperial Title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). The title was [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_of_Imperial_Advisor_for_the_Feni.md) during the Spring Equinox 383YE. Prior to the Spring Equinox 384YE the Advisor had the power to commission [historical research](../government/senate/Historical_research.md). That power [was removed](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-historical-research) following [changes](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_long_December.md#hold-on-to-these-moments) to how the [Department](../government/senate/Historical_research.md#the-department-of-historical-research) was organised.

## Responsibilities

The purpose of the Imperial Advisor for the Feni is to diplome with the [Feni](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/Feni.md), and to use the power of historical research in their dealings with them.

#### Constitutional Note

The Constitutional Court have pointed out that while the Advisor has a responsibility to engage in diplomacy with the Feni, they have not been given the legal power associated with an [Ambassador](Ambassador.md). The [Imperial Consul](Imperial_Consul.md) remains responsible for receiving [delegations](Ambassador.md#delegation) and preparing [treaties](Ambassador.md#treaty) with the Feni.

## Appointment

The Imperial Advisor for the Feni is an Imperial position [appointed by the Senate](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#imperial-position). Any Imperial citizen can hold this title. The Imperial Advisor serves for a year, and they can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly) and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
