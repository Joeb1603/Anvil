# Drusilla's Obelus

```
The sinecure associated with this position was destroyed by the vyig in Spring 385YE.
```

## Overview

Drusilla's Obelus was an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) auctioned through the [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md). Any Imperial citizen could hold the title. The position was a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income of [Bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md). It was destroyed by the [vyig](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Vyig.md) in [Spring 385YE](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Fade_out_again.md#were-victims-of-the-night-conjunction).

Following its destruction, evidence emerged that this Imperial sinecure had been shamelessly abused by the vyig. There was ample evidence that Lukash Biessek von Temeschwar, who [commissioned the sinecure](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Drusila-s_Obelus.md) as [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) intentionally meant for it to be used to smuggle the illegal substance *bite* into the Empire; they were ultimately executed for their long-standing role in the trade in Spring 385YE. The Obelus was ultimately burned to the ground by the vyig, in an apparent attempt to cover their trail.

## Responsibilities

The Custodian had no particular responsibilities; they simply received a bounty of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md) from Drusilla's Obelus and were responsible for protecting the Obelus from threats.

## Powers

### Harvest of Bladeroot

The Custodian received an income of 18 drams of bladeroot each season.

## Appointment

Each custodian of Drusilla's Obelus was determined by auction through the [Bourse](../economy/Bourse.md). As an Imperial seat, any Imperial citizen could participate in the auction and hold the title.

The custodian had [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and served until they died or stepped down. As a title in the Bourse, they could not be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md)
