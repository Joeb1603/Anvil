# Pitmaster

## Overview

The Pitmaster is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to an [Imperial Orc](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) citizen. It is a national position appointed by majority vote of the Imperial Orc senators. The position is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income drawn from the fees generated by the Skarsind College of Pit Fighting.

## Responsibilities

As a sinecure, the Pitmaster has few responsibilities. The running and maintenance of the college's facilities is left to trained pit fighters and shamans. If the Skarsind College of Pit Fighting were threatened, it would be the responsibility of the Pitmaster to deal with that threat

## Powers

As a sinecure, the Pitmaster has no legal powers, but they receive an income from the college's fees to the sum of 216 rings a season.

## Appointment

The title is selected by unanimous decision of the [Imperial Orc](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Imperial Orc citizen. The Pitmaster has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Imperial Orc [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Skarsind College of Pit Fighting

The college is made up of a small number of sturdy buildings built near the settlement of [Skogei](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md#skogei-glens). The largest structure by far is the arena, constructed from stone and reinforced with [white granite](../economy/White_granite.md). There are murals painted on the walls of the arena depicting a number of deceased pit fighters.

Beneath the pit of the arena is 'The Gibbet' where fighters prepare for their battles, the name stuck after legionnaires from the two [armies](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs_military_concerns.md#imperial-armies) projected their gallows humour onto the building. Blood often seeps down from the pit above and occasionally spatters the fighters before a fight in the pit.
