# Champion of Vigilance

*[Image: This position is a martial title .](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MartialTitle.png)*

## Overview

The Champion of Vigilance is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) that was [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/383YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-titles-of-champions-of-virtue) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Winter 383YE. The title would confer custodianship of the [artefact](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#artefacts) [Pilgrim's Shield](../magic/items/Pilgrim-s_Shield.md) known as *Bawn-Watch*, were it to be recovered and gifted for that purpose.

## Responsibilities

The Champion of Vigilance is intended to serve as an inspiration to the Pilgrims of the faith and laity as to what it means to be vigilant, particularly in a military context. They are also charged with bearing and protecting the shield known as *Bawn-Watch*. Historically the bearers of *Bawn-Watch* formed, along with the bearers of the other Pilgrims shield, the “Seven Pilgrims” band.

## Powers

### Regalia of the Champion of Vigilance

The Champion of Vigilance bears *Bawn-Watch*, the unique Pilgrim's Shield. If the Champion of Vigilance loses their position, then the item must be given to the new Champion with all haste. The Champion is encouraged to take all appropriate precautions to keep this item safe, and is expected to do everything within their power to recover it if it is lost.

Two judgements of the Imperial Synod, one in [Winter 385YE](../../in-character/synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-104) and one in [Spring 386YE](../../in-character/synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-197) suggest that *Bawn-Watch* has been recovered by the Empire. Its current bearer is not clear, but until it is provided and becomes formal regalia, there is no legal obligation to provide the shield to the title-holder.

## Appointment

The Champion of Vigilance is chosen every Summer Solstice by a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of [appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by the [Assembly of Vigilance](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-virtue-assemblies).

Any Imperial citizen may hold this title. They can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Vigilance Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## History

The shield *Bawn-Watch* was created by Ezkiah the Benefactor, greatest smith of the [Highborn](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) along with six other shields. Each was gifted to a different hero, forming a company known as the “Seven Pilgrims”.

*Bawn-Watch* was first carried by Leia the Hawk, a Navarri known for escorting priests to and from isolated steadings watching over the [vallorn](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md). Passed from Champion to Champion, or selected by the priests of Vigilance when this was not possible, it remained an enduring inspiration to the faithful of the Empire.

At the battle of [Reikos](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Reikos.md) in 367YE the Seven Pilgrims of the day charged an entire [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) army on their own, allowing the outmatched Imperial forces time to quit the field in good order. *Bawn-Watch* was lost along with the other six shields.

Historically the Shield was passed from Champion to Champion; the Synod has ended this tradition through the creation of a formal title, following the example set by the [creation](../../in-character/senate-motions/Create_title_Champion_of_Courage.md) of the [Champion of Courage](Champion_of_Courage.md).
