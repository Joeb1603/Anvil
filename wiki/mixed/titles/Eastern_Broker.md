# Eastern Broker

*The Peakedge Brokerage was destroyed along with the rest of Peakedge Stead by the [Druj](../../in-character/barbarians/Druj.md) after the Summer Solstice 387YE*

## Overview

The Khiklotu Khrysoú are a loose alliance of [Axou](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Axos.md) traders which the civil service suggests are equivalent to a Chamber of Commerce. Shortly before the Summer Solstice 382YE, a trading post was established in [Peakedge Song](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md#peakedge-song), [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Peak_Edge_trading_enclave.md) by **Selenius** as the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md). The Eastern Broker was an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) citizen who oversaw relations between the brokers of [Therunin](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md) and those Axos merchants prepared to make the long trip north from the [Bay of Catazar](../../in-character/world/Bay_of_Catazar.md) to trade with them.

Following the [mandate](../government/synod/Mandate.md) [enacted](../../in-character/synod-judgements/383YE_Summer_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-27) by **Tarquinius Ankarien** in Summer 383YE, encouraging Imperial traders to seek out closer ties with the people of Axos, an influx of traders from the Chambers of Issyk brought [additional opportunities](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/A_mountain_of_vipers.md#traders-from-issyk) to the Eastern Broker. The merchants of Issyk brought cerulean mazzarine, ambergelt, and green iron to trade at the Brokerage. Shortly after the Winter Solstice 384YE, the docks of Issyk were [torched](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_falling_sky.md#trade-with-axos) by the [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md), removing the extra ministry from the Eastern Broker. Likewise, the ongoing issues between the Citadels of Axos and the Empire led to a reduction in goods offered in both materials and mana.

Shortly before the Autumn Equinox 387YE, the Druj succeeded in their efforts when the brokerage was burned to the ground along with the rest of Peakedge Stead.

## Responsibilities

The Broker was expected to ensure trade continues between the Khiklotu Khrysoú and the Navarr. This close contact with the traders allowed ample opportunity for the Broker to acquire supplies at bargain prices from the Axou merchants.

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the title of Eastern Broker allowed the holder to purchase quantities of special materials at a set price. The Broker made their decision on how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money was removed from, and resources were placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime was processed. Unlike a normal ministry, the broker had not one but *two* streams from which they could purchase material. The Broker could spend differing amounts on each stream - for example, they could spend 17 crowns on one, and 7 crowns on the other.

## Appointment

This title was appointed during the Summer Solstice by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). If a unanimous decision could not be reached, the title [could have been appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title could be held by a Navarr citizen. The Broker was appointed annually. They could be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
