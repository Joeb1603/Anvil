# Keeper of the Sapphire Stair

## Overview

The *Keeper of the Sapphire Stair* is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a [League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) citizen. It is a National position, appointed by unanimous decision of the League [senators](Senator.md).

Associated with the [Sapphire Stair](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md#the-sapphire-stair) in [Tassato](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md), the keeper is paid a portion of each toll paid for use of the lock. The majority of the monies raised goes to the maintenance and operation of the marvellous locks, but the keeper is assigned enough to ensure a comfortable lifestyle. Perhaps more importantly, this [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md) also brings with it a certain amount of influence in the politics of the city itself due to the influence it grants the holder over trade along the Vassa. As such, control of the sinecure is hotly contested by guilds on both sides of the river and on the irregular occasions when the sinecure must be re-appointed, competition to influence the League senators is extremely fierce.

The Keeper is *almost always* from Tassato. While there have been incidents where League citizens from other cities have been appointed to the position they have rarely found their tenure to be very satisfying. They either moved to Tassato and became involved in the politics of the city, or they found themselves shunned, vilified, harassed and on one very unfortunate occasion during the reign of [Empress Brannan](../../in-character/history/Empress_Brannan.md), murdered for their temerity in involving themselves directly in a Tassatan matter.

The previous Keeper of the Sapphire Stair was Rebeca Porteiro di Tassato, a [naga](../magic/lore/Naga.md) member of the Gilt Moth *camorra* - a wealthy [guild](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Guild.md) from northern [Madeiras](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md#madeiras) known for, among other things, trade in fine cloth, dark ale and [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) goods. Exiled from the city proper nearly two centuries ago following some scandal or other, the guild stays neutral in political affairs. Rebeca herself is known to be a rake, a [bravo](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Bravo.md), and a hellraiser, and has a reputation for daring amorous adventures and remarkable skill with the sword. Rumour suggests that she was appointed to the position in 366YE following the successful seduction of all three League senators.

During Winter 379YE, Rebecca Porteiro di Tassato suffered a fatal injury, and the title passed to Domitila Sanguineo Rezia di Tassato (after some unfortunate misunderstandings around which of the two beautiful Rezia sisters had been appointed to the position). Following this new appointment there has been an increase in argument and political manoeuvring between those associated with the 'Stair - including some calls for increased political representation of the lockworkers' guilds.

After the Spring Equinox 385YE, a [Jotun](../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md) champion *Eisa Winterborn* [led a force into Tassato](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Shallows_and_miseries.md#meanwhile-in-tassato) to raid Cevia, and to destroy the Sapphire Stair. The locks were all but ruined in the raid. The motion to enact repairs was brought before the Imperial Senate by **Cesare Sanguineo Rezia Di Tassato** in his role as Senator for Tassato. The [motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Repair_the_Sapphire_Stair.md) was passed and work completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox 386YE.

The Red Rock was also defaced, but can be dealt with by the Keeper [as part of their normal duties](#the-red-rock).

## Responsibilities

The title holder is expected to ensure that the Sapphire Stair remains open and in a state of good repair. The actual job of operating the lock falls to civil servants and local labourers, mostly domiciled in the fortified village of [Cevia](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md#cascatas). While the title comes with a comfortable house on the banks of the Vassa, few Keepers actually spend much time there preferring the entertainments of the city of Tassato.

## Powers

### Comfortable Income

As a sinecure, the Keeper of the Sapphire Stair provided an income drawn from the tolls paid by merchants using the lock. The holder of the sinecure receives 20 crowns in income each season.

### The Red Rock

The Keeper of the Sapphire stair is responsible for the [mural](#the-red-rock-mural) that covers the Red Rock - a naturally occurring flat area of stone that rests in the hills above the lock and is visible to travellers passing along the river. They may change the mural if they wish - provided they are prepared to pay the money to employ a team of artists. Altering or replacing the Red Rock Mural costs 5 Thrones.

To use this power, the Keeper should ensure they have 5 thrones in their inventory, and email [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) with the description of their new mural between events. The text should be no more than 200 words, and may be edited before being published on the wiki.

## Appointment

The Keeper of the Sapphire Stair is appointed by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a citizen of the League. The Keeper has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Red Rock Mural

The *Red Rock* is a large, flat, naturally occurring rock surface that stands above the Sapphire Stair and is visible to boats travelling up or down the river. The wounds to the rock are gone; in their place is a resplendent vista showing the fortified manse atop the Sapphire Stair unbroken, the intricate locks restored with Prosperity flowing through the once more. Before the walls of the manse, cambions are depicted in red and gold and standing defiant. Cast down below them are Jotun invaders, their rune of victory broken and splintered beneath a tide of their dead as they fall from the walls. In the distance, the Tassato skyline can be seen once more, larger and greater now. As part of the piece, Annabella di Regario and Aldones di Sarvos are restored to their rightful place upon the red rock, toasting with glasses of wine. Beneath the mural, there is a banner painted upon the rock, proudly displaying the phrase: “*DESPISE THE THIEVING BEGGAR*”.
