# Archivist of the Echoing Obelisk

*[Image: Brother Enoch, first Archivist of the Echoing Obelisk](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Enoch.png)*

## Overview

This title was [established](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-archivist-of-the-echoing-obelisk) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) during the Summer Solstice 384YE following a [suggestion](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Daughters_of_the_Empire.md#archivist-of-the-blank-book) made by Abigail of Ira's Shield.

## Responsibilities

The Archivist of the Echoing Obelisk is responsible for collecting and recording the names of Imperial citizens who fall in our wars who do not attend Anvil but deserve to be remembered with equal virtue. The Archivist has a particular responsibility during the [Day of the Dead](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard_culture_and_customs.md#day-of-the-dead) celebrations; to mark the end of the grand procession at Anvil by calling on all present to spend a moment thinking on the nature of virtue, and what a terrible thing it is to be forgotten. In addition if the Echoing Obelisk was ever threatened it would be the responsibility of the Archivist to deal with those threats.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Autumn Equinox. The Archivist of the Echoing Obelisk is [appointed](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of [Appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) in the Highguard [national assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies).

The title can be held by any Highguard citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Highguard National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
