# Merchant Boyar of the Light Market

## Overview

The Merchant Boyar of the Light Market is an Imperial title [created](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_House_of_Illumination_and_Light_Market.md) by **Dima Vasilyevna Novosad**, as [Master of the Koboldi](Master_of_the_Koboldi.md) during the Winter Solstice 385YE. The inspiration for the Light Market was spearheaded by the Varushkan Assembly's call to action to dedicate the builders of [the Deep Chamber](Bredavoi_Emissary.md#the-deep-chamber).

## Responsibilities

The Merchant Boyar has a responsibility to maintain trade with Urizen, protect and support Varushka's miners, and monitor the [Night Below](Night_Below.md). They are also expected to liaise with other titles, such as the [Warden of the Trees and Hills](Warden_of_the_Trees_and_Hills.md), to help build Varushkan Prosperity. If the Light Market, its workers, or those who trade there were threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Merchant Boyar to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Portion of Trade

The Merchant Boyar gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). Each season, they receive 12 ingots from grateful miners across Varushka who have benefited from the import of lightstones and other goods from the House of Illumination.

The ingots they receive vary from season to season: they receive tempest jade at the Spring Equinox, weltsilver at the Summer Solstice, orichalcum at the Autumn Equinox, and green iron at the Winter Solstice.

### Wealth of the Market

The Light Market is also a [great work](../commissions/Great_work.md) that provides wealth to every [mine](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) in Volodmartz and Miekarova owned by a citizen of Varushka. The season they are appointed, the Merchant Boyar of the Light Market can make an announcement in the Imperial Senate to declare whether the miners of Varushka should employ the lights to seek out precious metals and jewels for trade, or to focus on uncovering deeper veins.

If they announce precious metals and jewels, then the mine owners will receive 36 extra rings each season. If they announce deeper veins, then each mine will receive two additional [ingots](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#metals) each season.

No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been delegated to the Merchant Boyar of the Light Market. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise their [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Merchant Boyar's decision.

### Appointment

This title is appointed during the Autumn Equinox each year. The Merchant Boyar of the Light Market is appointed by citizens of Varushka who control [mines](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forests](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource) through the [Tally of the Votes](../economy/Tally_of_the_Votes.md). The more improved a mine or forest, an individual controls, the more votes they can allocate in support of a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a Varushkan citizen may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
