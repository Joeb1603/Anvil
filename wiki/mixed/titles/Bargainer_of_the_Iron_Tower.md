# Bargainer of the Iron Tower

## Overview

The Iron Roundtower was constructed by [announcement](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Iron_Roundtower.md) of the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) during the Summer Solstice 383YE, following an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) presented during the [Spring Equinox](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_honey_offering.md#proposal-iron-roundtower) of the same year. Construction was completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox 383YE.

## Responsibilities

The Bargainer of the Iron Tower is responsible for ensuring good relations with the Varushkans who offer their mithril in trade. If the Iron Roundtower were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Bargainer to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any particular [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were to arise related to the Iron Roundtower, the Bargainer would expect to be consulted as to how that opportunity might be resolved.

Virtuous mine owners bring their mithril Bourse notes here and exchange them for an agreed amount of money. Those using the exchange do so secure in the knowledge that not only are they enriching themselves, and receiving a just reward for their Prosperity, but at the same time helping their nation.

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the Bargainer can purchase quantities of [mithril](../economy/Mithril.md) at a set price. The Bargainer spends their money and makes their choices during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Autumn Equinox each year. The Bargainer of the Iron Tower is appointed by citizens of Varushka who control [mines](../../out-of-character/rules/Mine.md) and [forests](../../out-of-character/rules/Forest.md) [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource). The larger the holder an individual has, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a Varushkan citizen may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).

## The Iron Roundtower

On the road south-west of [Delev](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Karov.md#delev), not far from the [Eternal Shafts of Time](Eternal_Shafts_of_Time.md), stands an old stone structure called the *Iron Roundtower*. Before the secession of [Temeschwar](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md) it was the heart of a prosperous vale. Its prosperity faltered when the great city became independent, but until Varushka joined the Empire, it was a major trading post on the way to [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) city and the western nations. It also saw the passage of significant amounts of mithril (and salt) from the [Fortress](Fortress_of_Salt.md) in [Metri](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Temeschwar.md#metri) bound for the eastern vales. In the early years of the Empire, its star was eclipsed and it fell practically into ruin. In early 383YE, a [proposal](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_honey_offering.md#proposal-iron-roundtower) was presented, prompted by the [Prosperity](../../in-character/religion/Prosperity.md) assembly, that reimagined the place as a market for Varushkan merchants to deal in Varushkan [mithril](../economy/Mithril.md) for the benefit of Varushkan buyers.

The tower has been rebuilt and a secure vault built beneath it to protect mithril and money alike from opportunistic thieves. Trustworthy guards armed with fine weapons and armour now protect the tower, and ensure the safety of merchants traveling to and from the market. Given its location, its unsurprising that a small vale has quickly grow up around it, supporting the merchants and schlacta alike - a vale that looks to the *Bargainer* for protection and guidance.
