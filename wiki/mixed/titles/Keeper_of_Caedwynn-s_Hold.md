# Keeper of Caedwynn's Hold

## Overview

The Keeper of Caedwynn's Hold is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). The motion to construct the Caedwynn's Hold Wayhouse was [proposed](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Wayhouse_in_the_Barrens.md) by **Lady Gwendoloena de Ordraig**, as the Senator for [the Barrens](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) during the Summer Solstice 388YE, and construction was completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox 388YE.  
  
As a [Navarr wayhouse](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md), Caedwynn's Hold [supports Navarr](../commissions/Navarr_wayhouse.md#support-navarr-characters) living in [the Barrens](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md). This means that the [civil service](../government/senate/Civil_service.md) can find Navarr characters a [new resource](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#personal-resource) in the territory for the normal cost; such resources do not suffer the [usual problems](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md#resources-in-other-nations) with being established in a different nation; Navarr living here gain all the regular benefits provided by [great works](../commissions/Great_work.md); and they can participate in the [election](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_leadership.md#leading-a-territory) of a specific Navarr [senator](Senator.md).

## Responsibilities

The Keeper of Caedwynn's Hold is responsible for protecting the wayhouse and the Navarr who live in [Murderdale](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md#murderdale). If the wayhouse, travellers using the wayhouse, or the Navarr of the region were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Keeper to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any particular [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were to arise related to the wayhouse, the Keeper would expect to be consulted as to how that opportunity might be resolved.

## Powers

### Wealth of the Wilds

The Keeper of Caedwynn's Hold receives one throne, six crowns, and eight rings each season.

## Appointment

The Keeper of Caedwynn's Hold is appointed during the Summer Solstice each year. As a [national position](New_Imperial_titles.md#the-imperial-synod), it is elected by unanimous decision of the [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) senators.

The title can only be held by a citizen of Navarr. They can be revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
