# Challenger in Waiting

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Dawnish Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Dawnish.png)*

The Challenger in Waiting is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md). Construction of the Challenger in Waiting was [announced](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Challenger_in_Waiting.md) by **Valentin Ivarovich Orzel** as [Bearer of the Second Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) during the Summer Solstice 385YE.

The title came with a fair share of controversy, which led to the *Silver Nightingales* requesting the senators of Dawn to make clear one way or another whether this was intended to be a [martial title](Martial_title.md). During the Autumn Equinox 386YE motions for [both sides](../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#senate-motions) were raised, and ultimately it was voted that the title not be made a martial title. For more information on this opportunity, see **[the fayre fight](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_fayre_fight.md#a-challenger-in-waiting)** wind of fortune.

Whilst the Challenger in Waiting has a responsibility to measure the Virtue of the Throne if they challenge to be Monarch of Dawn, it is ultimately the decision of the [nobles of Dawn](../../out-of-character/game-design/Dawn_monarchy_update.md#nobility) during the [tourney](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_monarchy.md#the-monarchy-in-play).

## Responsibilities

The Challenger in Waiting has a responsibility to measure the Virtue of the Throne should they challenge for [Monarch of Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn_monarchy.md#the-monarchy-in-play), and to spread word of what they find. They are responsible for encouraging nobles to stand for or against the Throne on the tourney field. If the chapel were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Challenger in Waiting to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### A Portion of Liao

The sinecure provides 11 doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) to the Challenger in Waiting each season, to be used as they see fit.

### Mark of Virtue

The *Silver Nightingales* are a small group of [troubadours](../../in-character/religion/Troubadour.md) from Hawthorn who have committed to supporting the Challenger for as long as the responsibilities of the title involve scrutinising the Virtue of any potential challengers to the Monarch of Dawn. The Challenger in Waiting receives an [Icon of the Witness](../magic/items/Icon_of_the_Witness.md) every year at the Spring Equinox from the Silver Nightingales so that they might mark the virtue of those they have judged.

## Appointment

The Challenger in Waiting is a national position appointed by the Synod. It is elected by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies) of Dawn each year during the Autumn Equinox.

The title can be held by any citizen of Dawn. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Dawn National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Chapel of the Challenger

The Chapel of the Challenger in Waiting is a tastefully austere villa that overlooks the wild heathland of Hawksmoor. There is a well-stocked gymnasium and tourney square in the grounds where the attendants and mistress-at-arms will put any Dawnish challenger or supplicant to the most exacting trials of their martial arts and virtue.

The attendants of the Chapel provide whatever aid they can to the Challenger in Waiting in preparing their tactics and strategy for providing the fiercest challenge to any potential claimant on the Throne of Dawn.

The feast hall of the Chapel is appointed with beautiful tapestries hung within carved marble alcoves illustrating the woven artistry of **Lady Nineve Orzel** and the Five Silver Needles of the Barrens. Within the shadowed arches are depictions of the heroes of Dawn’s martial past, their deeds, their victories and their glorious defeats: Empress Brannan bearing the Rod of the Sun overlooks countless knights marching war; soldiers fighting fiercely against the Druj; and townsfolk taking up arms against dread husks. The common theme throughout is Dawnish [Pride](../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) despite adversity and [Courage](../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) in the face of overwhelming odds.
