# Election schedule

## Overview

Most significant [Imperial titles](Imperial_title.md) are [reelected](../../in-character/elections/Imperial_elections.md) annually on a specific date; for instance, an election for the title of [Senator](Senator.md) of [Madruga](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) is held every summit at the Winter Solstice. If the title dies, is revoked or steps down, then the title becomes vacant; it is re-elected at the earliest practical opportunity, but an election for the title still takes place at the normal date.

Some Imperial titles appointed by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) serve for a year from the summit where they were appointed. Such a title becomes eligible for reappointment at any time during the summit one year later. The incumbent remains in position until replaced or at the end of the event - whichever comes sooner. If the Senate wishes, they can choose to elect a title to serve for less than a year. This must be explicit in the wording of the Senate motion. It is not possible to extend the term beyond a year, nor to make it shorter than a single season, nor to restrict the length served of a tenured position.
The majority of titles appointed by the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md) can be re-elected at any time through a [declaration](../government/conclave/Declaration.md) of [candidacy](../government/conclave/Candidacy.md).

Some Imperial titles have tenure. The title is held until the citizen dies or steps down.

The schedule below shows the earliest summit at which each Imperial title will become eligible for re-election. A title may become eligible at any point before this if the title holder dies, steps down or is revoked.
