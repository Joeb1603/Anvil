# Shepherd of the Southern Herd

*[Image: The southern herd has alleviated the aridity of Nadir.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:A_well-fed_bull_in_Kuno_WS_AJTJ_P1070807.jpg)*

## Overview

The Shepherd of the Southern Herd is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of the [Imperial Orcs](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Imperial_Orcs.md). The title was [created](../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#establish-cattle-markets) by the Imperial Senate at the Winter Solstice 386YE. The Shepherd of the Southern Herd is responsible for a sinecure that grants them a seasonal income of coin. More information on the opportunity that led to the creation of the title can be found in the **[Million miles of water](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Million_miles_of_water.md#the-cattle-markets)** wind of fortune.

During the Spring Equinox 388YE, **Pathfinder Gaddak**, as [Senator for Mareave](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md), raised a motion to [construct a trading post](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Beoraidh_Trading_Post.md) for the trade of salt beef, tallow, and other cattle-based products from the southern herd in exchange for salt and ores from Beoraidh.

## Responsibilities

The Shepherd of the Southern Herd is responsible for looking after the southern herd, protecting it from the wild drakes around [Rathmonna](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#nadir), and helping it grow to rival the size of the [Great Herd of Skarsind](Shepherd_of_the_Great_Herd.md). If the safety of the herd, or its workers, were threatened, then it would be the responsibility of the Shepherd to deal with those threats.

A total of five Imperial Orcs provided cattle bonds towards the formation of the southern herd. **Bloodcrow Pip** and **Gralka** each provided two cattle bonds; **Ashborn Trosk** provided twenty-eight cattle bonds; and **Sandviper IDRA** and **Bloodcrow Rasp** each provided one hundred and twenty cattle bonds. Sixteen different military unit captains helped drove the herd to Mareave, as detailed in **[Southern Herd Drive](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/387YE_Summer_Solstice_winds_of_fortune.md#southern-herd-drive-cattle-bonds)** and **[Home is Where the Herd Is](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_winds_of_fortune.md#home-is-where-the-herd-is)**.

## Powers

### Substantial Income

The Shepherd of the Southern Herd gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive 43 crowns a season from the ongoing cattle trade in Nadir and trade at the [Twinned Trading Posts](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Mareave.md#the-twinned-trading-posts).

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Winter Solstice. The Shepherd is appointed by Imperial Orcs who control [military unit](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md) [personal resources](../../out-of-character/rules/Resource.md) who [supported](../../out-of-character/rules/Military_unit.md#supporting-an-army) an [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) during the previous year. The larger the military unit an individual has, and the more often they supported the Imperial armies, the more votes they can allocate in support to a candidate. The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only an Imperial Orc may hold this title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As an Imperial title appointed through the Bourse, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
