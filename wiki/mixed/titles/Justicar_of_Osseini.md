# Justicar of Osseini

## Overview

The Justicar of Osseini was an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) [created by the Senate](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#construct-sanctuary-for-justicars) in Spring 384YE. The Justicar oversaw the [Sanctuary of the Justicars](#sanctuary-of-the-justicars) on the Isle of the Osseini in Feroz which was home to a small community of Freeborn priests with a focus on [exorcism](../../out-of-character/rules/Exorcism.md) and dealing with the unquiet dead. The Sanctuary was destroyed by [Asavean warships](../../in-character/foreign-nations/Asavean_Archipelago.md) during the [Spring Equinox 385YE](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Questions_of_virtue.md#last-of-the-justicars)

## Responsibilities

The Justicar was responsible for dealing with the unquiet dead and ghosts in the Brass Coast, although the Freeborn are rarely troubled by such spirits.

## Powers

The Justicar was able to purchase a variety of [magic items](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md) and [potions](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md) from the priests who worked at the Sanctuary with a focus on those that assist with the rite of [exorcism](../../out-of-character/rules/Exorcism.md). The Justicar spent their money and made their choices during downtime from their character page. Money was removed from, and resources were placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime was processed.

## Appointment

This title was appointed during the Summer Solstice. The Justicar was [appointed](../government/synod/Appointment.md) by a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of [Appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) in the [Freeborn](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) [National assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies).

Any Freeborn citizen was able to hold the title. They could have been revoked by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Freeborn Assembly, and the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
