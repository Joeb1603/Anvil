# Churnspire Adjutor

*[Image: MeraudArt.jpg](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:MeraudArt.jpg)*

## Overview

The Churnspire Adjutor is an [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) [commissioned](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#construct-churnspire) during the Spring Equinox 384YE. The initial plans for the Churnspire were [provided](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_Net_of_the_Realms.md#a-spire-for-summer) by Megaris of Glowspire.

[Meraud](../../in-character/eternals/Meraud.md) serves as the patron of the Churnspire Adjutor, and has dominion over the [Churnspire](#churnspire) itself. For some time the eternal was happy for Urizeni [senators](Senator.md) to [appoint](#appointment) the Adjutor and to leave them to their own devices. In Autumn 388YE however, Meraud [proposed](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Towers_of_the_sun.md#churnspire-adjutor) that *he* should pick the Adjutor and that they should serve as an intermediary between himself and the people of Urizen. He requested that the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) recognise his request, and offered to provide additional support to the Adjutor if they did so. As of the end of the Autumn summit, the Senate has declined to do so.

## Responsibilities

The Churnspire Adjutor has no particular responsibilities; they simply receive a set of regalia each year, and a bounty of golden apples each season from [Meraud](../../in-character/eternals/Meraud.md). If the Churnspire were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Adjutor to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Gift of the Enchanter

The Adjutor gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive an income of four [golden apples](../../out-of-character/rules/Vis.md#golden-apples) each season as a gift from Meraud.

### Regalia of the Adjutor

The Churnspire Adjutor receives a set of regalia from the *Golden Magician* in the form of the [Tempestuous Orb](#tempestuous-orb), the [Staff of Storms](#staff-of-storms), and the [Mask of the Geyser](#mask-of-the-geyser). The regalia is imbued by Meraud just before each Summer Solstice. The regalia is legally the property of the Churnspire Adjutor. If the Adjutor were to step down or be revoked then the items should be given to the new Adjutor with all appropriate haste.

#### Tempestuous Orb

- **Form:** Paraphernalia. Takes the form of a piece of ritual [paraphernalia](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#paraphernalia). The item must be present in the ritual to use its magical properties.
- **Effect:** Members of the coven who are performing a Summer ritual of magnitude 30 or higher may use [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum), [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) or [green iron](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#green-iron) in place of crystal mana. Every two measures of suitable materials spent count as one crystal mana.

#### Staff of Storms

- **Form:** Weapon. Takes the form of a [ritual staff](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#weapons). You must be holding this item in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain two ranks of Summer Lore, when casting a ritual of magnitude 40 or higher subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks).

#### Mask of the Geyser

- **Form:** Talisman. Takes the form of a [ritual focus](../../out-of-character/rules/Magic_items.md#talismans). You must be wearing this item or holding it in hand to use its magical properties.
- **Requirement:** You must have the [magician](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician) skill to bond to this item.
- **Effect:** Gain 3 ranks for the [Golden Ramparts](../magic/rituals/Golden_Ramparts.md) and [From the Mouths of Babes](../magic/rituals/From_the_Mouths_of_Babes.md) rituals, subject to the [normal rules for effective skill](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#gaining-additional-ranks). When contributing to a performance of these rituals, you may choose to substitute [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum) or [tempest jade](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#tempest-jade) for crystal mana. Every 2 measures of appropriate materials spent counts as 1 crystal mana, up to the normal limit determined by your effective rank of Summer lore.

## Appointment

The Churnspire Adjutor is selected by unanimous decision of the [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) [senators](Senator.md) during the Summer Solstice. If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Urizen citizen. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Urizen [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Churnspire

The Churnspire was built with a solid foundation of [white granite](../economy/White_granite.md), and the spire towers over the Churn, one of the most volatile geysers located in [Peregro](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md#peregro). The spire is largely dedicated to luxurious apartments for the Adjutor with art depicting a great tower looming over a shrouded lake common throughout. A small turret projects out and overlooks the geyser. The only way to access the turret is through a corridor inlaid with [mithril](../economy/Mithril.md) which glistens during the hours of twilight.
