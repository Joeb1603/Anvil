# Dean of the First Voice Glade

## Overview

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Cat's Graces](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Cat%27s_Graces_Sodality.png)*

The Dean of the First Voice Glade is an Imperial title [created by the Imperial Senate in Autumn 382YE](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_First_Voice_Glade.md). The First Voice Glade School was destroyed by the [Empty Hands](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-empty-hands) shortly before the Winter Solstice 385YE. The Dean lost their seasonal income of herbs, but the title remained and retained its responsibilities. Shortly before the Winter Solstice 387YE, a new school was built in the glade and became an [inspirational location](../government/synod/Inspirational_location.md) dedicated to the teachings of the exemplar [Gerallt Brackensong](../../in-character/religion/Gerallt_Brackensong.md). It currently serves as a [great work](../commissions/Great_work.md) supporting the [herb gardens](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) of [Miaren](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Miaren.md).

The title was amended as part of the motion that [established](../../in-character/senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#establish-cats-graces) the [Cat's Graces](Cat-s_Graces.md) during the Spring Equinox 388YE. Before the amendment, the title was previously appointed by the unanimous decision of the Senators of Navarr.

## Responsibilities

The Dean of the First Voice Glade is expected to uphold the musical and cultural traditions of the Navarr. These responsibilities include encouraging emerging and potential talent, ensuring Navarr musical traditions and history is honoured.

They have little in the way of administrative responsibilities to the school, but it is assumed that they will frequently lecture or run workshops, and so should be at least somewhat familiar with Navarr musical traditions and are expected to be passionate about furthering Navarr culture. If the Glade were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Dean to deal with those threats.

## Appointment

The Dean of the First Voice Glade is a [national position](Imperial_title.md#national-position) which is [appointed](../government/conclave/Appointment_by_sodality.md) by members of the Cat's Graces. Only a citizen of Navarr may hold the title.

The Dean has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## First Voice Glade

*[Image: Artist's impression of the glade's visitors](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Visitors_from_the_Forest_of_Arden.png)*

There have been two schools built at First Voice Glade. The original stood in a glade in [Oakways](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Miaren.md#oakways) that was chosen to mark the memory of the exemplar [Gerallt Brackensong](../government/synod/Known_exemplars.md#gerallt-brackensong), a Navarr bard who fell battling the [vallorn](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) at [Return](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md#upper-tarn-valley). [Splitroot Striding](../../out-of-character/groups/Navarr_groups.md#splitroot-striding), the [Splinterspears](../../out-of-character/groups/Navarr_groups.md#splinterspears) and the Twisted Tree Tradehouse funded and built the small school at the location as part of an effort to ensure that Gerallt continued to provide a lasting legacy for future generations. The school quickly became a gathering place for members of several [stridings](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Striding.md). The music school was strictly focused on Navarr traditions, training students in the musical traditions of that nation as well as other performing arts skills such as dancing, juggling, and fire performance.

When the [eternal](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) known as [Cold Sun](../../in-character/eternals/Cold_Sun.md) sent its *scions* to attack the Empire in 385YE, one of its armies - the Empty Hands - [appeared in Miaren](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-empty-hands). All the armies of Cold Sun are drawn to places of beauty, art, and culture, and this brought their devastating attention down on First Voice Glade. The grand archon of the Empty Hand itself led the force that burned the school, and while most of the staff and students made it to safety, the structure itself was torn apart. The beautiful trees and gardens that surrounded the school were transformed into cursed stone effigies, a painful symbol of the damage being done to the heart of the Navarr nation.

Then in Spring 386YE, Gerallt Brackensong was the subject of one of the first [writs of consecrations](../government/synod/Writ_of_consecration.md) issued by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md). Among the locations proposed for an [inspirational location](../government/synod/Inspirational_location.md) dedicated to his life, the ruins of First Voice Glade were preeminent. Doing so would require that the school be rebuilt; however, a task that took over a [year to complete](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#rebuild-first-voice-glade). Shortly before the Winter Solstice 387YE, however, a new school building was built in First Voice Glade. Brodi Brackensong was [entrusted](../../in-character/synod-judgements/386YE_Spring_Equinox_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-70) by the General Assembly with the solemn task of [consecrating](../../out-of-character/rules/Consecration.md) the new inspirational location.

The new structure stands as a monument to the [Pride](../../in-character/religion/Pride.md) Gerallt inspired in performers and storytellers across his nation. Pilgrims leave with a greater appreciation for Navarr music, and many are inspired to celebrate and explore the songs and stories of their own people. The location also celebrates the kind of [celebration of culture and beauty](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Watercolours_in_the_rain.md#with-tomorrow-in-mind) the Pride Assembly proposed as a response to the hateful destruction of [Cold Sun](../../in-character/eternals/Cold_Sun.md). While the site remains primarily of interest to the Navarr, it also serves as a place of pilgrimage for those who have seen the things they love ruined by the forces of [Oblivion](../../in-character/eternals/Cold_Sun.md), providing reassurance that the beauty of the [orc](../world/Orc.md) and human spirit can never truly be burnt away. The twisted petrified trees created by the scions of Cold Sun still stand in the grounds of the school, but their austerity is counterbalanced by a profusion of brightly coloured flowers and new growth trees that blossom in the surrounding areas.

Shortly after the Summer Solstice 388YE, the glade became home to strange [creatures](../../in-character/eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md) from the [Forest of Arden](../../in-character/eternals/Jaheris.md). A pair of immense spiders have spun webs in the highest boughs between trees to catch resting birds, and at least one feral unicorn has been spotted within the twisted, petrified glade.

- **Restoring the botanical gardens at the First Voice Glade School would allow the Navarr to share their skills with members of the Vallornguard**
- **The Dean would gain 27 herbs each season**
- **The Dean could upgrade a basic herb garden owned by a member of the Vallornguard each season**

The [First Voice Glade School](Dean_of_the_First_Voice_Glade.md#first-voice-glade-ruined) has [just been rebuilt](../../in-character/senate-motions/Rebuild_First_Voice_Glade.md) after it was destroyed by the [Empty Hands](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Things_that_burn.md#the-empty-hands) two years ago. The school itself has now been rebuilt on a much grander scale, and has once again become a scholastic haven, promoting the musical and cultural traditions of the Navarr, sharing them with students from near and far. The school has encouraged emerging talent, ensuring Navarr musical traditions remain vital.

Sadly the associated botanical garden were overlooked in the proposal presented by the civil service, which focused on other benefits. But with scores of visitors coming to the shrine, there is now a chance to recreate the gardens and take advantage of that with the school itself. Whisper it quietly, but the Navarr recognise no equal in herblore in the Empire - no other nation has so many skilled apothecaries and herbalists.

The work proposed is not insignificant, but if the gardens were restored to their former glory and a new classroom built, the Dean would resume receiving a bounty of herbs, but they would also be able to select a member of the Vallornguard to train and study with Navarr masters of herb lore. Provided the student was not already highly proficient, the skills they gained would provide them with permanent benefits, allowing them to grow more herbs each season.

Any member of the Vallornguard would be free to roleplay spending time at the School to learn the history of the Empire's longest war, and to advance their skills in botany. The Dean could select a single member of the Vallornguard with a [basic herb garden](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md) each season - their herb garden would be upgraded by one rank without needing any wains or money. A starting herb garden is one that does not have any existing [upgrades](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md#upgrades).
