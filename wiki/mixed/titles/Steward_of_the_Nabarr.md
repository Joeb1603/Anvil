# Steward of the Nabarr

## Overview

The Steward of the Nabarr is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) [created by the Senate](../../in-character/senate-sessions/384YE_Summer_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#construct-bladeroot-sinecure) in Summer 383YE. The position of Steward is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), producing [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md) each season.

## Responsibilities

The Steward of the Nabarr has no particular responsibilities, they simply receive an income of valuable herbs each season. If the sinecure were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Steward to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Bladeroot

The Steward of the Nabarr gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). Each season they receive 18 drams of bladeroot.

*[Image: Tair Drifting Bough , Steward of the Nabarr](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:StewardNabarr.jpg)*

## Appointment

The title is selected by unanimous decision of the [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [senators](Senator.md) at the Autumn Equinox. If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Navarr citizen. The Steward serves until the next scheduled election. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
