# Doyen of the Spires

## Overview

*[Image: Lights in the darkness, shining across Urizen.](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Three_Lights_in_the_Night.jpg)*

The Doyen of the Spires is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) bestowed upon a citizen of [Urizen](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen.md) by a majority vote of [magicians](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician). The position was created by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Winter 382YE during the work to [repair the Heliopticon network](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_repairs_to_Heliopticon.md). The [Heliopticon](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_culture_and_customs.md#the-heliopticon) is one of the wonders of the Empire, but was significantly damaged by the fighting in [Spiral](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Spiral.md), [Zenith](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Zenith.md), and [Morrow](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Morrow.md). The reconstruction effort replaced the central towers in Morrow and Spiral, and arranged for comprehensive restoration of towers across the nation. At the same time, following the [proposal of Portia the Elder](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Fight_or_flight.md#a-new-hope), a former Provost of the Halls of Knowledge turned Civil Servant, the functionality of the Heliopticon was greatly expanded. With new mirrors, and new methods of enciphering, the towers are now turned to the primary purpose of codifying magical rituals - making Urizen the largest [college of magic](../commissions/College_of_magic.md) the world has ever seen. The Doyen of the Spires guides the magicians and scholars of the nation, creating ritual texts via the unique collaboration of an entire people.

## Responsibilities

The Doyen is responsible for overseeing the codification of a single [arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md), selected when they are appointed. The codification work is undertaken by the entire nation of Urizen, and facilitated by use of the Heliopticon network. Given that the effectiveness of this "college" depends on the number of Urizen magicians and scholars able to commit time to the study, the Doyen by extension has a vested interest in protecting the nation from outside threats.

## Powers

### Codification

The Doyen of the Spires can direct the magicians of a Urizen to research the [formula](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md) of a new ritual so that it can be mastered. The ritual must already exist in the form of an [arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md#arcane-projection). Urizen can work on formalising one ritual at a time, and the research may take anywhere from a season to several years depending on the complexity and the number of people able to commit their time to studying the ritual. At the completion, the Doyen receives a [ritual text](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#ritual-texts) suitable for use in mastering the ritual.

If research is not completed, the college keeps notes on their work so far, and may resume it at a later date from where it was left off.

Due to the nature of the "college", it is not possible to conceal the nature of the arcane projection being worked on from the people of Urizen or, by extension, the Empire.

**OOC Note:** The Doyen should still let Profound Decisions know what [arcane projection](../../out-of-character/rules/Arcane_projection.md#arcane-projection) they are [formulating](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md) by e-mail to [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) before downtime closes. The final magnitude of the ritual text is not guaranteed to be the same as that of the arcane projection.

### Patron of the Spires

The eternal [Prospero](../../in-character/eternals/Prospero.md) serves as [patron of the *college*](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Out_of_the_realm.md#patron-of-the-spires), encouraging his golden spiders - each around the size of a sheep - to assist any scholars who might be stumped by a magical problem. When [codifying a ritual](#codification) the Doyen can call on the assistance of these heralds to speed up the process. Doing so requires payment in the form of [orichalcum](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#orichalcum).

The first five ranks of additional research in a season cost 20 ingots of orichalcum. The next five ranks will cost a further 42 ingots (for a total of 62 ingots). The next five ranks will cost *another* 86 ingots, and so on doubling the cost and adding two ingots for each further additional five ranks of research within the same season.

Unlike the additional benefits provided a college of magic by a [specialisation](../commissions/College_of_magic.md#specialisation), this additional research speed is not restricted to any one magical realm - the Prince of Ties may favour [Autumn magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Autumn_magic.md) but his spiders are able to assist in codification in all six magical lores.

This power cannot be used in the [eternal](../../in-character/eternals/Eternal.md) [Prospero](../../in-character/eternals/Prospero.md) is under the [enmity](../../in-character/eternals/Alignment.md) of the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md), however.

### A People United

The speed at which an arcane projection is codified depends on the existence of large numbers of Urizen magicians and scholars able to commit time to the study. Each [territory](../../out-of-character/rules/Territory.md) that is part of Urizen and connected to the others by the Heliopticon allows the Doyen to codify five ranks of magnitude each season.

As of the time of writing, the Doyen of the Spires and the people of Urizen can codify an arcane projection in one season for every 20 ranks of magnitude it has.

### Extensive Upkeep

Having the whole of Urizen function as a college of magic is expensive. The upkeep on the Heliopticon network, costs the senate the normal amount for a college of magic, and an additional 5 thrones for every Urizen territory contributing to the codification process. As such, with three territories effectively contributing and with the basic cost for a college of magic being 12 thrones, the upkeep cost is 32 thrones each season, paid by the treasury.

The current upkeep as of the Spring Equinox 388YE is 32 thrones; even though the League controls Spiral, sufficient Urizen magicians remain in the territory to support codification.

## Appointment

The Doyen is elected in the Hall of Worlds by a majority vote of [magicians](../../out-of-character/rules/Magical_skills.md#magician). Every Urizen magician present has one vote, regardless of their mastery of ritual magic. Each candidate presents the single arcane projection that they propose to codify, and the one who receives the most votes becomes Doyen of the Spires and work begins on codifying their arcane projection. Alternatively, a candidate may propose to resume work on the codification of a ritual previously presented but on which work has not been completed.

The title can be only held by any Urizen citizen. The Doyen serves until their arcane projection has been codified, at which time the title is reappointed. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Urizen [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

If the Doyen of the Spires is revoked, steps down, or dies, work on their arcane projection ceases. A new Doyen of the Spires may be appointed to continue their work, or a new arcane projection may be presented to the people of Urizen.

## The Studies of Urizen

Due to the nature of the college, it is not feasible to conceal the nature of the work being done via the Heliopticon network from the people of Urizen or, by extension, the Empire.

- **Spring Equinox 388YE**. Able the Taller of Sarangrave Reforged was appointed to codify *Woven Threads of Necessity*, a potent enchantment on an [army](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) of unknown provenance
- **Autumn Equinox 387YE**. Ioseph of Phoenix Reach was appointed to codify [Pillars of Adamant](../magic/rituals/Pillars_of_Adamant.md). This enchantment previously drew the [attention](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Is_there_anybody_out_there.md#enthusiastic-koboldi-adamant) of the *koboldi* of the eternal [Adamant](../../in-character/eternals/Adamant.md), and it was these heralds that provided the arcane projection to be codified. It was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 388YE
- **Summer Solstice 386YE**. Able the Taller was appointed to codify *Crowns and Tankards*. This potent enchantment for [navies](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md) is an Autumn ritual that functions similarly to [Brotherhood of Tian](../magic/rituals/Brotherhood_of_Tian.md); if the navy benefits from [natural resupply](../../out-of-character/rules/Navy.md#resupply) it regains additional fighting strength. It was completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox 387YE.
- **Spring Equinox 386YE**. Cassius was appointed to oversee the codification of [Chalice of Callidius](../magic/rituals/Chalice_of_Callidius.md), a Winter ritual that allows a magician to gain extra personal mana when consuming something that restores mana. It was added to [Urizen lore](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) during the Summer Solstice 386YE.
- **Autumn Equinox 384YE**. Ioseph of Phoenix Reach was appointed to codify *Advent of Majesty* (formerly known as *Advent of Savagery*). This very potent and ambitious ritual creates an [Imperial enchantment](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Stargazer.md#resonance-and-the-omnihedron) flooding the entire Empire with [Summer magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Summer_magic.md). The [stargazers](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Stargazer.md) are particularly excited about this ritual, bringing them as it does one step closer to being able to experiment with the Omnihedron. Given the ritual is magnitude 200, however, it may be some time before anyone has a chance to perform it. Shortly before the Autumn Equinox 385YE, the magnitude of Imperial enchantments [shifted](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Shining_like_fire.md#toward-the-omnihedron), to 210, possibly as a result of [the Barrens](../../in-character/nations/dawn/The_Barrens.md) becoming part of the Empire. Urizen magicians were able to compensate for the changes - even though it may be slightly more difficult to perform, the ritual will take no additional time to codify. In Autumn 385YE, the Conclave voted to [interdict](../government/conclave/Interdiction.md) the [practice](../../in-character/conclave-sessions/385YE_Autumn_Equinox_Conclave_sessions.md#interdiction-empire-wide-enchantments) of "*weaving an enchantment across the entire Empire*", which has obvious implications for this codification. During the Winter Solstice 385YE, Ioseph was [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the Urizen assembly. The [judgement](../../in-character/synod-judgements/385YE_Winter_Solstice_Synod_judgements.md#judgement-109) applauded Ioseph, but deemed his research inauspicious given the Interdiction.
- **Spring Equinox 384YE**. Marcus was chosen to oversee the codification of the ritual [Quick Study](../magic/rituals/Quick_Study.md) (formerly known as *The Wise Master teaches All*), an Autumn ritual that enhances the ability of a College of Magic to formulate ritual texts. It became part of [Urizen lore](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) shortly afterward.
- **Winter Solstice 383YE**. The codification of [Unending Onslaught](../magic/rituals/Unending_Onslaught.md) was managed by Spyridonakes. It became part of Imperial Lore shortly after it was formulated.
- **Summer Solstice 383YE**. The [mage](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#magi) Tenaes was appointed to oversee the codification of [Regrow the Land's Heart](../magic/rituals/Regrow_the_Land-s_Heart.md) an ambitious Spring ritual of magnitude one-hundred-and-twenty intended to infuse the territory of Spiral with [Spring magic](../../out-of-character/rules/Spring_magic.md), encouraging new life and increasing the likelihood of children born in the territory expressing the [briar](../magic/lore/Briar.md) [lineage](../magic/lore/Lineage.md). Several modifications were made to the original arcane projection during codification, with the final ritual having a much lower magnitude. It became part of [Imperial lore](../../out-of-character/rules/Formulaic_ritual.md#imperial-lore-and-the-ritual-list) almost immediately after work was completed
- **Spring Equinox 383YE**. The first person to hold the title of Doyen of the Spires was Ioseph of Phoenix Reach. They directed work on the Summer ritual [Aspect of the Mountain](../magic/rituals/Aspect_of_the_Mountain.md). The work should have taken six months, but the influx of Urizen liberated from Zenith helped to speed the process along. In the end, Aspect of the Mountain was formulated in only a single season. It became part of [Urizen lore](../../in-character/nations/urizen/Urizen_magical_traditions.md#urizen-lore) shortly afterward.
