# Dean of Laroc Cathedral

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Dawnish Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Dawnish.png)*

## Overview

Dedicated to the Imperial virtue of [Courage](../../in-character/religion/Courage.md), the Cathedral at [Laroc](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md#laroc) was [commissioned](../commissions/Commission.md) by a [motion](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Cathedral_of_Courage.md) of the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) in Summer 380YE by **Earl Valentin Orzel**, Senator for [Weirwater](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Weirwater.md). Construction of the Cathedral was completed shortly before the 380YE Autumn Equinox. At the Autumn Equinox 380YE the National Assembly of [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) appointed Lady **Eleanor de Rondell**, the former [Virtue Inquisitor](Virtue_inquisitor.md) of Courage, as the first Dean.

## Responsibilities

The Dean of Laroc Cathedral has no particular responsibilities; they receive a bounty of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) from the civil service and a vial of errant's wine from the *Azure Rose*. If the cathedral were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Dean to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### A Portion of Liao

The Dean of Laroc Cathedral is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) that grants custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). The sinecure provides an income of nine doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) each season, to be used as they see fit.

### Errant's Wine

The *Azure Rose* are a band of questing knights from Laroc and are regular visitors to the Cathedral of Laroc. These fierce followers of Courage have been inspired by the message laid out in the poem composed by Kahendrin Wordsmith. The Dean of Laroc Cathedral will receive three vials of [Errant's Wine](../magic/potions/Philtres_of_War.md#skops-mead) each season from the Azure Rose for as long as the poem of Kahendrin is maintained in the cathedral.

## Appointment

The [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies) of Dawn appoints the Dean of Laroc Cathedral. Only a Dawnish citizen may be Dean of Laroc Cathedral. Despite the sinecure's association with the virtue of Courage, there is no requirement for the recipient to be part of the Courage assembly.

The Dean has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Dawn National Assembly. or the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Cathedral at Laroc

Built of white granite, the cathedral lies close to the tournament grounds in Laroc, [Astolat](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md). Pentagonal in shape, it has five transepts surrounding a central hall, in the centre of which stands a polished marble block inlaid with an epic poem composed by Kahendrin Wordsmith. Four of the transepts are dedicated to paragons and exemplars of Courage, while the fifth is dedicated to specific Imperial heroes.

Although it is officially named a cathedral, the actual structure is more akin to a chapel in size and grandeur. While it is tiny, it is beautifully decorated. Fine works of art painted by artists inspired by the virtue of [Courage](../../in-character/religion/Courage.md) adorn the walls, brought from all the nations of the Empire. It stands in a well-maintained garden.

The statues in particular are noteworthy. One stands in each of the four transepts dedicated to a paragon or an exemplar.

- A Vard with a toothed axe, representing [Korl](../../in-character/religion/Korl.md), Paragon of Courage.
- A Highborn with their hood up, representing [Permion](../../in-character/religion/Permion.md), Exemplar of Courage.
- A Dawnish with a crown of blue roses, representing [Gilda Ashwine](../../in-character/religion/Gilda_Ashwine.md), Exemplar of Courage.
- A Steinr with a two-handed hammer, representing [Inga Tarn](../../in-character/religion/Inga_Tarn.md), Exemplar of Courage

The fifth statue is more a diorama, representing five figures, each a hero of the recent Empire. Two Highborn, a Leaguer, a Marcher and a Dawnish stand side by side, and the inscription on the plinth indicates they represent individuals named Asher, Vulgaris, Tyrus, Andrea Von Holberg, and Henry Talbot.
