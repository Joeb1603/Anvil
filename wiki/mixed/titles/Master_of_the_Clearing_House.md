# Master of the Clearing House

## Overview

The Master of the Clearing House is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). The motion to construct the Clearing House was [proposed](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_apothecary_clearing_house_in_Tassato.md) by **Cesare Sanguineo Rezia Di Tassato**, as the [Senator for Tassato](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md), during the Summer Solstice 379YE, and construction was completed one year later.

The cost to purchase herbs from the Kruidenkenner was increased by roughly a fifth shortly after the Spring Equinox 387YE, as a result of [ongoing issues](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Waiting_for_the_worms.md#passing-costs-along) with Asavean piracy. Shortly before the Summer Solstice 387YE, the Sarcophan put into place the "*Pax Sarcophan*"; a [commitment](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Can-t_give_everything_away.md) to keep the oceans free of piracy on merchant vessels, and an ultimatum to every other nation on the Seven Seas. By the start of the Autumn Equinox 388YE, the price for purchasing Sarcophan goods returned to what it was before the Asaveans started their piracy.

## Responsibilities

The Master is responsible for ensuring that all business taking place at the Tassato Apothecary Clearing House is being undertaken in a scrupulous fashion. The fine quality of herbs passing through the doors of the Clearing House must be guaranteed, and all transactions must be conducted in a civil, legal, and timely fashion. The actual day-to-day running of the Clearing House is handled by a team of well-paid apothecary agents, and for the most part they can get on with their jobs without any interference from the Master.

In the event that the business of the Clearing House were threatened, it would fall to the Master to deal with those threats. At the same time, they might be called on to make decisions about [opportunities](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) relating to the Clearing House.

In return, the agents of the Clearing House keep provide the Master with access to any special bargains they might spot.

## Appointment

The Master of the Clearing House is appointed each year during the Summer Solstice by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a citizen of the League. The Master of the Clearing House is appointed annually. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the League [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

### Constitutional Note

- **The appointment method of this title is unconstitutional, it does not follow a well-worn path**
- **The title can operate and be re-elected normally**
- **The title cannot be amended by the Imperial Senate without *also* addressing the issue of the unconstitutional appointment method**

The appointment method of this title has been determined to be unconstitutional following a review carried out by the Constitutional Court. The title ought to be appointed by the members of the [Imperial Apothecaries Guild](Imperial_Apothecaries_Guild.md). This status does not impact the use of any legal powers the position has and the title will be re-elected normally. However the title should not be used as an example of a well-worn path for a position with these responsibilities and it would not be possible to make any legal changes to the title (as the result of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or otherwise) without either addressing the issue of who appoints that title at the same time, or amending the responsibilities of the position so that it was clearly outside the remit of the sodality.

## Tassato Apothecary Clearing House

The Apothecary Clearing House was [commissioned](../commissions/Commission.md) by Cesare Sanguineo Rezia Di Tassato of the Gilded Horn Carta during the Summer Solstice 379YE, but [construction](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_apothecary_clearing_house_in_Tassato.md) was delayed for some time. It was finally completed nearly a year later, and opened its doors shortly before the Summer Solstice 380YE.

The building itself is fairly unassuming - a large warehouse and offices in southern [Mestra](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md#tassato-mestra) with access to private river docks. Herbs are stored in the secure warehouse until they are sold, carefully protected by well-paid guards. Tassato is not particularly known for its [herb gardens](../../out-of-character/rules/Herb_garden.md), but the Cesare seal-of-approval has attracted interest from herb dealers across the Empire. Now, citizens of Tassato can purchase fine-quality cerulean mazzarine from [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md), prepared bladeroot from [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) and [Varushka](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka.md), and the best true vervain and imperial roseweald [the Marches](../../in-character/nations/the-marches/The_Marches.md) and [Dawn](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md) have to offer - as well as employing the expertise of professional [apothecaries](../../out-of-character/rules/Surgical_skills.md#apothecary). The Clearing House is open to everyone, of course - and its central position along the western borders of the Empire means it has already seen a fair amount of business between the mazzarine-starved physicks of the north and the bladeroot hungry potion-makers of the southern coast.
