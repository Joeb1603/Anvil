# Imperial Apothecaries Guild

## The Manifesto

*[Image: Imperial Apothecaries' Guild](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Apothecaries_Guild.png)*

The sodality is dedicated to the advancement, education, and support of apothecaries and other practitioners of the herbal arts across the Empire. It holds the following to be true:

- Apothecaries are a cornerstone of the Empire.
- Only through unity and shared discovery can we unlock our art's full potential.
- Our expertise should be valued and fairly compensated, not taken for granted.

The sodality therefore exists to:

- Coordinate and support the research, development, and dissemination of ingredients, potions, poisons, antidotes, and drugs to Imperial citizens.
- To serve as the point of contact for bodies of state when apothecary and herbalist advice is required.
- To represent and support the interests of apothecaries and herbalists within the Empire.

## Symbol

The Imperial Apothecaries' symbol is a pestle and mortar.

## Membership

Any Imperial citizen can join the sodality using the [Mark of Fellowship](../magic/rituals/Mark_of_Fellowship.md) ritual.

## Powers

The constitutional court identified [a range of legal powers](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/It-s_what_we_do.md#imperial-lore) that the Senate could grant to the Apothecaries Guild if they chose. The existence of the Guild would make it constitutionally difficult for the Imperial Senate to grant these powers to any other legal body (or title unless that title were appointed by the Guild).

If an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were identified where the sodality could be deployed, then it would be down to a vote of the members.

### Dissemination

- **The sodality has the power to disseminate a herbal**
- **This a legal power of the sodality that must be announced in the Senate**

The Guild can use a resolution to add a [herbal](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md#herbal) to the list of potions that any Imperial citizen can learn to make. If the resolution is upheld, it costs 10 thrones to distribute the knowledge contained in the herbal. The herbal must be handed over to the civil service and is destroyed in the process. If the sodality chooses to make a herbal public knowledge, any character can then learn to make the potions, and the details of the recipes will be added to the wiki. To employ this [legal power](../government/conclave/Powers_of_a_sodality.md#legal-powers), the sodality must [announce](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) the details of the herbal they are disseminating in the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). The Guild Leader is expected to make the announcement after the resolution is passed by the Guild.

[Recent developments](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Lean_and_athirst.md#what-is-a-potion) have seen the Constitutional Court and [Civil service](../government/senate/Civil_service.md) offer some additional guidance. Given not all herb lore is contained in a herbal, the responsibility to "*coordinate and support the research, development, and dissemination*" means that the Guild must implicitly have the ability to spread information related to "*ingredients, potions, poisons, antidotes, and drugs*" to Imperial citizens that is not contained in a herbal.

If the speaker for the sodality does use this ability, their words will be a matter of public record in the same manner as any announcement to the Senate (they will be required to provide a written copy of their announcement as normal). If they choose to include a recommendation, it will have no legal power - the Senate alone has the power to make define laws for concoctions such as this - but members of the Apothecaries Guild outside Anvil will take note, and ensure that their peers among the herbalists and physicks, not only of Varushka but of the rest of the Empire, are aware of it.

### Guild Leader

- **The sodality can elect a Guild Leader to speak on behalf of the sodality**

The Guild Leader of the Imperial Apothecaries is an Imperial title awarded to a member of the Imperial Apothecaries. It is expected to carry out the standard responsibilities of a [Speaker](../government/conclave/Appointment_by_sodality.md#speaker), that is to serve as a mouthpiece for the sodality, to represent their views to other parts of the Empire, to recruit new members, and to advance the sodality's goals and the members' interests.

Though the title is "*Guild Leader*", this represents an oversight by the constitutional court during [scrutiny](../../in-character/senate-motions/Scrutiny.md). Under normal circumstances, the court do not permit the Senate to create Imperial titles with names that imply that they are in charge or have authority over an organisation or group of citizens. The legal position is absolutely clear that nobody can be the "leader" of the sodality.

This status does not impact the use of any legal powers by the position and the title will be re-elected normally. However the title should not be used as an example of a constitutional title. It would not be possible to make any legal changes to the title (as the result of an opportunity or otherwise) without addressing the issue of the name of the title.

#### Donations

Since the manifesto was amended during the Winter Solstice 387YE, the Guild Leader has received four drams each of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md), [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md), [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md), and [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md) from apothecaries in [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md) and [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). These donations will continue as long as the sodality holds that apothecaries' expertise should be valued and fairly compensated.

#### Appointment

The Guild Leader is selected by members of the sodality. Only a member of the sodality can present themselves for the title, and only a member of the sodality can vote in the election. The candidate who gains the most votes from the members of the sodality is elected to the title.

### Imperial Lore

A [herbal](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md#herbal) is a rare, magical item that contains all the details necessary to create a [potion](../../out-of-character/rules/Potion.md) or set of potions. If a member of the guild has possession of a herbal then the Apothecaries Guild can vote to add the herbal to Imperial lore.

If that vote passes, then the civil service take the herbal so that it can be used to disseminate the contents; it is consumed in the process of making the potion or recipe set public knowledge. After the summit is over, any citizen of the Empire will be able to learn how to make the potion or set of potions.

In addition, ten thrones are be required to pay for distribution of the knowledge (**OOC Note:** These funds would need to be placed in the inventory of a character, who should email [plot@profounddecisions.co.uk](mailto:plot@profounddecisions.co.uk) with details of their CID and the herbal being paid for after the event.)

## History

There have been several iterations of a "Guild of Apothecaries" over the Empire's history. Groups of like-minded individuals coming together to organise prices and influence senators and members of the Conclave. Occasionally, these groups managed to demonstrate their value enough to be unofficially recognised by various bodies of state, but the momentum slowed before they could truly establish themselves. The Imperial Apothecaries was created by the Senate during the [Winter Solstice 386YE](../../in-character/senate-sessions/386YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#create-imperial-apothecaries-guild), as the first Imperial sodality since the death of [Empress Britta](../../in-character/history/Empress_Britta.md).

The sodality journeyed to [Therunin](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Therunin.md) in an attempt to neutralise the poison in Lower Tarn Valley using *Ioric's Elixir*; a strange concoction [uncovered](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Wasp_nest.md#isolating-an-antidote) in Anvil. They were prevented from doing so by the Druj, and instead, the majority set to work helping the refugees, providing much-needed medical assistance to those who had been injured. Many moved to bolster the defences of the Upper Tarn, or turned back entirely to help evacuate Peakedge Song. In many ways, the Empire was fortunate - the efforts of the Imperial Apothecaries' Guild have still cost the Senate ten thrones, but the leadership and direction of the sodality prevented the outcome from being much worse.

During the Winter Solstice 387YE, the manifesto of the Guild was amended [not once](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-manifesto-of-the-apothecaries-guild), but [twice](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Winter_Solstice_Senate_sessions.md#amend-manifesto-of-the-apothecaries-guild-ii). This added a set of central tenets, and adjusted the wording on some of their activities. The main change to the latter was to change their focus on apothecary arts to apply only to Imperial citizens.

As of the end of the Spring Equinox 387YE, the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) has [confirmed that the power and authority to disseminate knowledge of potions](../../in-character/senate-sessions/387YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-imperial-apothecaries-guild) across the Empire lies with the sodality. At the Autumn Equinox 388YE, the [Constitutional Court](../../in-character/senate-motions/Scrutiny.md#constitutional-court) reversed a previous decision and [went on record](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Come_together.md#paths-not-taken) as presenting the opinion that the [well worn path](New_Imperial_titles.md#well-worn-paths) for overseeing any Imperial college of apothecary arts would be via the Apothecaries Guild.

- **The opportunity remains for a guildhouse to be built that would draw apothecaries from across the Empire**
- **Such a guildhouse would need to be overseen by a title with the power to direct research**
- **The title would need to be appointed by simple vote of the Apothecaries Guild**

Following the tragic destruction of [Thornsong House](Thornsinger.md#thornsong-house) by the Druj, there is an opportunity for the sodality to establish a guildhouse somewhere in the Empire. The guildhouse would function as a herbal school, with an Imperial title to oversee it and [guide the apothecaries](Thornsinger.md#guide-the-apothecaries) in a similar manner to the Thornsinger. Following the Winter Solstice 387YE, and the rescue of several [fleeing apothecaries](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Crimson_and_green.md#under-an-oak-therunin), the guildhouse will have a reduced labour cost.

The [Yerende](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Yerende.md) put forward a suggestion that it be built in [Skarsind](../../in-character/nations/imperial-orcs/Skarsind.md) when the sodality was [first ratified](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/It-s_what_we_do.md#weeds-and-seeds). If the guildhouse were built in Skarsind, then its upkeep would be reduced to ten thrones a season.
