# Custodian of the Lost

## Overview

The Custodian of the Lost is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) appointed by the [Conclave](../government/conclave/Conclave.md).

## Responsibilities

The Custodian of the Lost has a responsibility to help people harmed by magical curses or by exposure to [celestial forces](../magic/lore/Astronomancy.md).

## Powers

### Bequests

The Custodian of the Lost receives 108 rings each season from bequests made by the families of former patients.

## Appointment

The appointment, replacement, or removal of the Custodian of the Lost is made by the [declaration of candidacy](../government/conclave/Declaration.md#declaration-of-candidacy) as part of a [Conclave session](../government/conclave/Conclave_session.md). Any Imperial citizen may hold the title.

The Custodian of the Lost serves until they are replaced by another magician using the Declaration of Candidacy. As a title in the Conclave, they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md).
