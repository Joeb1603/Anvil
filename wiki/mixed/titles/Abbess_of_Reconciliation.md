# Abbess of Reconciliation

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Assembly of Highguard](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Highborn.png)*

## Overview

The Abbess of Reconciliation was created when the Imperial Senate voted to [construct the Abbey of Reconciliation](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Abbey_of_Reconciliation.md) during the Summer Solstice 385YE. Construction was completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox 385YE. The Abbey forms part of the "Proffered Hand Network", as inspired by the [Lady of the Proffered Hand](Lady_of_the_Proffered_Hand.md) and similar titles.

The Abbey was [raided](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Blood-red_blooms.md#at-the-gardens-necropolis) by the Vorlach Adventurers, a mercenary company of [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md). Following the raid, the gardens were left in a damaged state, reducing the [bounty of herbs](#bounty-of-herbs) produced at the Abbey by half until the damage is repaired.

## Responsibilities

The Abbess of Reconciliation is responsible for supporting ex-slaves and refugees; bringing them into the Way; and providing them with opportunities to work and recover in the Empire. If the Abbey were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Abbess to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any particular [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) were to arise related to the Abbey, the Abbess would expect to be consulted as to how that opportunity might be resolved.

## Powers

### Bounty of Herbs

The Abbess of Reconciliation receives an income of 3 drams of [true vervain](../../out-of-character/rules/True_Vervain.md), 2 drams each of [cerulean mazzarine](../../out-of-character/rules/Cerulean_Mazzarine.md) and [Imperial roseweald](../../out-of-character/rules/Imperial_Roseweald.md), and 1 dram each of [bladeroot](../../out-of-character/rules/Bladeroot.md) and [marrowort](../../out-of-character/rules/Marrowort.md) each season from the Abbey.

### Gift of Gratitude

Donations from the Free Folk of Astolat - former Asavean refugees who found a home in Dawn - provide the Abbess of Reconciliation with 2 doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) each season. These donations from their congregations are intended to help the Abbess continue to do their good works, and are a mark of gratitude for the role Highguard paid in helping them adjust to their new lives in the Empire.

## Appointment

The Abbess of Reconciliation is a [Highborn](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Highguard.md) [appointment](../government/synod/Appointment.md) made by a [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the Highguard [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies). This is a national title and can only be held by a Highborn citizen.

As with most sinecures, this appointment has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure). The assumption is that the Abbess retains the position until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by a lesser majority of the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Assembly of the Nine, or by the Highborn National Assembly.

## Abbey of Reconciliation

The Abbey is modelled somewhat after the [House of the Proffered Hand](Lady_of_the_Proffered_Hand.md#house-of-the-proffered-hand) in [Astolat](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Astolat.md). Built in [Fishguard](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Necropolis.md#fishguard), not far from Myfanwy's Rest, the Abbey itself is a modest structure, in contrast to the extensive gardens that surround it. There are quarters for the Abbess, should they wish to avail themselves of them, as well as for a small staff of teachers and staff. It's common practice for those who live at the Abbey to work in the herb gardens, in between learning about the Empire and the Way. A small village has sprung up near the Abbey, home to a number of former refugees who have chosen to remain to support the work of the Abbess of Reconciliation. It has become a regular stopping-off point for Navarr travellers, who regularly bring with them small numbers of recent arrivals in the Empire who they think would benefit from the aid the Abbey has to offer.

Since the Spring Equinox 386YE, the Abbey has also seen regular visitors from the former Asavean slaves known as the *Free Folk*. Now [Dawnish](../../in-character/nations/dawn/Dawn.md), they do not forget that Highguard offered them support in their own time of need, and [helped train](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Folklore.md#finding-the-way) a number of them in the [ceremonies of the Way](../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md). Following the guidance of Tabris, Son of Armisael, then [Bishop of the Navigators](Bishop_of_the_Navigators.md), they agreed to show their gratitude by supporting the Abbess of Reconciliation both by volunteering at the abbey, and by making small donations of liao from their own congregations each season. It's little enough in the great scheme of things, but the Free Folk feel it is the least they can do for their friends in Highguard.
