# Blackroot Broker

## Overview

The Blackroot Broker is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) citizen. It was [commissioned](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_venom_brokerage.md) in Spring 382YE by order of the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md), and completed shortly before the Summer Solstice. The [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) to construct the brokerage arose as a result of a [meeting of brokers](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Start_a_fire.md#the-brokers-of-navarr) in [Miaren](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Miaren.md) following [successes](../../in-character/winds-of-war/382YE_Spring_Equinox_winds_of_war.md#immolation-28liathaven29) fighting the [vallorn](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md) of [Liathaven](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Liathaven.md).

## Responsibilities

The Broker is expected to gather tools for fighting the vallorn not only from Navarr apothecaries and artisans, but also from crafters across the Empire. How these tools are deployed is at the discretion of the Broker but it is assumed at least some will be made available to those directly opposing the vallorn's wickedness. The brokerage allows the Broker to purchase herbs to combat [vallorn miasma](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Vallorn.md#vallorn-miasma) and [green lung](../../in-character/world/Vallornspawn_husk.md#green-lung), [blade venom](../magic/potions/Legacy_of_Thorns.md#oil-of-blackthorn) inimical to many vallorn creatures, and [Scorpion's Sting](../magic/items/Scorpion-s_Sting.md) daggers whose powers are likewise lethal to creatures such as the [vallornspawn husk](../../in-character/world/Vallornspawn_husk.md).

As a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md), the title of Blackroot Broker allows the holder to purchase specific potions, herbs, and venomous daggers at a set price. The Broker makes their decision on how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

The brokerage is designed to provide several items specifically of use to those fighting the vallorn. As a consequence, the Broker has not one but *three* streams from which they can purchase goods. The Broker can spend differing amounts on each stream - for example, they could spend 7 crowns on herbs, and 17 crowns on potions, and none of the other.

## Appointment

This title is appointed during the Summer Solstice by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a Navarr citizen. The Broker is appointed annually. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

### Constitutional Note

- **The appointment method of this title is unconstitutional, it does not follow a well-worn path**
- **The title can operate and be re-elected normally**
- **The title cannot be amended by the Imperial Senate without *also* addressing the issue of the unconstitutional appointment method**

The appointment method of this title has been determined to be unconstitutional following a review carried out by the Constitutional Court. The title ought to be appointed by the members of the [Vallornguard](Vallornguard.md). This status does not impact the use of any legal powers the position has and the title will be re-elected normally. However the title should not be used as an example of a well-worn path for a position with these responsibilities and it would not be possible to make any legal changes to the title (as the result of an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) or otherwise) without either addressing the issue of who appoints that title at the same time, or amending the responsibilities of the position so that it was clearly outside the remit of the sodality.

## Honest Twyll's Gardening Supplies

This unassuming appearing brokerage is found near the large [steading](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Steading.md) of Holt in [Holtford](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Miaren.md#holtford). It purportedly sells a wide variety of gardening and agricultural supplies, but it also serves as a centre of commerce for Navarr [brokers](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_economic_interests.md#brokers) who deal in herbs, potions, and weapons against the vallorn.
