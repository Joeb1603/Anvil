# Castellan of the Silent Sentinel

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Highborn Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Highborn.png)*

## Overview

Castellan of the Silent Sentinel is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) created by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) during the Winter Solstice 381YE when they [commissioned a place of pilgrimage](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_place_of_pilgrimage_in_Casinea.md) in [Casinea](../../in-character/nations/highguard/Casinea.md) in response to an [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) [made possible](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Sing_your_name.md) through the research of the [Heirs of Lepidus](../../in-character/religion/Lepidus.md#heirs-of-lepidus).

## Responsibilities

The Castellan is responsible not only for the safety and wellbeing of those who visit the Silent Sentinel, but for the fortification itself. They are responsible for facing any threats to the castle, but also for making any decisions related to beneficial opportunities that may arise.

## Powers

### A Portion of Liao

The sinecure provides 15 doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) to the Castellan each season These doses are for the Castellan to use as they see fit, but there is an expectation they will be used to support the [Vigilance](../../in-character/religion/Vigilance.md) of the virtuous against threats within and without.

### Point of Pilgrimage

The Silent Sentinel draws visitors from across the Empire. Some visitors, especially pilgrims of Vigilance, wish to make donations to help support the Castellan. The Castellan of the Silent Sentinel receives one throne each season from these donations.

### Custodian of the Silent Sentinel

The Castellan is the [custodian](New_Imperial_titles.md#custodian) of the Silent Sentinel. While they are not automatically assumed to be involved in the day to day operation of the fortification, they are assumed to offer direction to the garrison where necessary. If the fortification were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Castellan to deal with those threats. Likewise, if any [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) relating to the commission presented itself, they would make any decisions needed to take advantage of that. They cannot, however, take any action that would require a legal power to authorise, such as [abrogating](../government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#abrogation) or [ceding](../government/senate/Powers_of_the_Imperial_Senate.md#concedence) the commission (which are powers of the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md)).

## Appointment

Castellan of the Silent Sentinel is a national position appointed by the Synod. It is elected by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the Highborn [Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies). .

The title can be held by a citizen of Highguard. The Castellan has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Highguard National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Tower of the Sentinel

The Silent Sentinel is one of the oldest (and some say the ugliest) fortifications in the Empire. During their ongoing researches into the nature and identity of the [paragons and exemplars](../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md) of the Way, the Heirs of Lepidus discovered proof that the castle was not only the oldest structure associated with [the Sentinel](../../in-character/religion/The_Sentinel.md), but also without a shadow of a doubt the largest and most extensive. Given its position, they were prepared to venture the theory that the fortification was most likely the abode of the Sentinel itself.

The Sentinel is a proud castle, but as the recognised home of one of the oldest known paragons, it is also significantly more. The Senate commissioned the construction of a grand tower and massive warning bell, incorporated into the fortification itself as a place of pilgrimage. The tower was built using old references to a ruined tower that stood here before the Highborn turned the foundations to their own use, built of black basalt and white granite using as many of the techniques of the original architect as possible.

The tower stands above a grand hall consecrated with [true liao](../religion/Liao.md#true-liao) by Rhesa, Exarch of the Scions of Ravensfell. It serves as a place of pilgrimage not only for Imperial citizens but for followers of the Way across the known world.
