# Tender of the Hearth

*[Image: This title is appointed by the Varushkan Assembly](https://www.profounddecisions.co.uk/empire-wiki/File:Assembly_Varushkan.png)*

## Overview

The [construction](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_Hearth_of_Virtue.md) of the Hearth of Virtue was [commissioned](../commissions/Commission.md) by **Nikolovich Drakov** as the [Bearer of an Imperial Wayleave](../economy/Bearer_of_an_Imperial_Wayleave.md) during the Winter Solstice 382YE. Built in [Perumaki](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Miekarova.md#perumaki), [Miekarova](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Miekarova.md), work was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 383YE.

## Responsibilities

The Tender of the Hearth is responsible for the safety and wellbeing of those who visit, and study, at the [seminary](#hearth-of-virtue). If the Hearth of Virtue were to be threatened, it would be the responsibility of the Tender to face those threats; likewise should any [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) related to the Hearth arise the Tender of the Hearth would decide how to respond.

## Powers

### A Portion of Liao

The sinecure provides 16 doses of [liao](../religion/Liao.md) to the Tender each season, to be used as they see fit.

### Icon of the Pilgrim

The Hearth of Virtue's function as a seminary school is well known - a place for interested citizens to come and discuss the finer points of the many [ceremonies](../../out-of-character/rules/Religious_skills.md#performing-a-ceremony) known by Imperial citizens. The most valuable tools used are the [icons](../../indexes/by-category/Icons.md) known across the Empire, especially those that allow the wielder to perform ceremonies. The Tender of the Hearth receives an [Icon of the Pilgrim](../magic/items/Icon_of_the_Pilgrim.md) at the Spring Equinox each year for as long as the Hearth of Virtue is appointed by the National Assembly of Varushka.

## Appointment

The Tender of the Hearth is a national position appointed by the Synod. It is elected by [judgement](../government/synod/Judgement.md) of the Varushkan [Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies).

The title can be held by any citizen of Varushka. The Tender has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure) and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Varushkan National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## Hearth of Virtue

The Hearth of Virtue stands in the Vale of Strascovia next to a temple dedicated to the virtue of [Wisdom](../../in-character/religion/Wisdom.md). The Hearth of Virtue serves as a seminary school. Within it has several classrooms and dormitories for people to learn from [storytellers](../../in-character/nations/varushka/Varushka_religious_beliefs.md#storytellers) about the [Paragons](../government/synod/Paragons_and_exemplars.md), the Exemplars, and the mysteries of the Way of Virtue. There is a central library, which incorporates many of the writings held in the [Anvil Library](../../in-character/world/Anvil.md#the-anvil-library).

There are seven smaller chapels within the seminary, each dedicated to one of the virtues. At the centre of each chapel stands a hearth fire, which copies the design of the national hearth fire on show during Anvil summits. Each chapel has symbolism throughout showing animals of the appropriate virtue.
