# Spider's Tollkeep

## Overview

The *Spider's Tollkeep* is an Imperial title associated with the [Spider's Dream](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#the-spiders-dream), a unique suspension bridge that links [Madruga](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) and [Sarvos](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md). The position of tollkeeper is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), requiring minimal responsibility but offering a regular income drawn from the tolls paid by merchants crossing the bridge across the furious river Vassa.

During the reign [Emperor Walter](../../in-character/history/Emperor_Walter.md) the sinecure lapsed as the bridge was allowed to fall into an increasing state of disrepair. Eventually, the cost of maintaining the sinecure in the absence of regular tolls resulted in the Senate voting to stop supporting the title. While it still existed in theory - and still had responsibility for ensuring the Spider's Dream was kept in good repair - in practice it ceased to be relevant.

Following the [repairs](../../in-character/senate-motions/Repair_the_Spider-s_Dream.md) overseen by Avisena i Ezmara i Guerra, the sinecure is once again available and was appointed for the first time in five years at the Autumn Equinox 377YE. Historically, the need for six senators from two nations to be in unanimous agreement has made this sinecure particularly difficult to assign... The peculiar method of determining the title's holder stems from the fact that it is connected with a bridge. While it is often considered to be 'in' Madruga, the bridge spans a river and so is legally in *both* Madruga and Sarvos. Needless to say, this sinecure is among one of the most often contested Imperial titles, as consensus between senators from two nations has regularly proved... difficult... to achieve.

In Spring 380YE, [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) [orcs](../world/Orc.md) attached to the forces involved in [the sack of Sarvos](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_sack_of_Sarvos.md) made an attempt to take advantage of unusually high flooding in the river as part of an attempt to destroy the bridge entirely, cutting the Freeborn territories off from the rest of the Empire. They were stopped by the brave action of Imperial heroes, and the bridge preserved - and along with it the ability for armies to pass from the south-western Empire into Sarvos and Tassato, and vice versa.

Following the Spring Equinox 385YE, the [Jotun](../../in-character/barbarians/Jotun.md) sent raiders led by *Stephen of Sarcombe* to attack the bridge. Despite the best efforts of the defenders, it was [seriously damaged](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Shallows_and_miseries.md#meanwhile-in-madruga) to the point where it could [no longer be safely used](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Shallows_and_miseries.md#game-information). With no merchants using it, there was nobody to pay tolls and thus no money coming to the Spider's Tollkeep. The Imperial Senate voted to appraise the [solutions to the bridge](../../in-character/senate-sessions/385YE_Autumn_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#appraise-spider27s-dream-bridge) During the Autumn Equinox 385YE and Eilian Sweetwater presented his [findings](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Dreaming_in_strands.md) before the next summit. During the Winter Solstice 385YE the Senate voted to [repair the damage done](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Spider-s_Dream_Bridge.md) and work was completed shortly before the Spring Equinox 386YE. The bridge was later supplemented with a pair of [simple but effective barbicans](../../in-character/senate-motions/Fortify_Spider-s_Dream_Bridge.md) on either side of the bank.

## Responsibilities

The title holder is expected to ensure that the Spider's Dream remains open and in a state of good repair. The actual job of operating the tollbridge falls to civil servants and locally recruited agents.

## Powers

### Merchants' Crossing

The Spider's Tollkeep gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). Each season the Tollkeep receives an income of 2 thrones drawn from the tolls paid by merchants crossing the bridge.

## Appointment

The Spider's Tollkeep is unusual in that it is appointed by unanimous decision of the [senators](Senator.md) of [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md) and [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by either a Freeborn citizen or a citizen of the League.The Spider's Tollkeep has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down.

In accordance with its peculiar method of appointment, the Spider's Tollkeep can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Freeborn [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), the League National Assembly, and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Spider's Dream

The Spider's Dream is an elegant and unique bridge that spans the tumultuous river Vassa, connecting [Trivento](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md#bocche) in [Sarvos](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) to [Torres](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#torres) in [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). It is a marvellous piece of engineering, a suspension bridge whose cables resemble the strands of a spider's web. It was built in the reign of [Emperor Giovanni](../../in-character/history/Emperor_Giovanni.md) from designs created by the talented engineer Carmen i Dehasa i Erigo. Passage across the bridge requires a toll. The only alternative is to travel the long way round, up through [Tassato](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md).

Despite its strategic importance, the bridge has not always been well maintained. Resources initially used to maintain it were siphoned off in the time of [Emperor Walter](../../in-character/history/Emperor_Walter.md), and the bridge became increasingly dangerous to cross. In Spring 377YE, however, the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) [took steps](../../in-character/senate-motions/Repair_the_Spider-s_Dream.md) to ensure its restoration to full functionality - reinstating the [Imperial Title](Imperial_title.md) of Spider's Tollkeep in the process and heading off potentially unpleasant implications for the people of both [Sarvos](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) and Madruga.

The bridge has been the target of multiple attacks in recent years. In Spring 380YE, [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) [orcs](../world/Orc.md) attached to the forces involved in [the sack of Sarvos](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/The_sack_of_Sarvos.md) made an attempt to take advantage of unusually high flooding in the river as part of an attempt to destroy the bridge entirely, cutting the Freeborn territories off from the rest of the Empire. They were stopped by the brave action of Imperial heroes, and the bridge was preserved - and along with it the ability for armies to pass from the south-western Empire into Sarvos and Tassato, and vice versa.

Then in 385YE, the bridge was heavily damaged by the traitorous *Stephen of Sarcombe* as part of a targeted [raid](../../in-character/winds-of-war/Shallows_and_miseries.md#meanwhile-in-madruga) out of Jotun-held land in [Kahraman](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Kahraman.md). It became impassable, with damage to the bridge [impeding](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md#strategic-considerations) the [Military Council's](../government/military/Military_Council.md) ability to move [armies](../../out-of-character/rules/Army.md) between Madruga and both [Sarvos](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Sarvos.md) and [Tassato](../../in-character/nations/the-league/Tassato.md). It also made it impossible for those [fleeing the invasion](../../in-character/winds-of-war/A_beacon_of_smoke.md) of [Madruga](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md) by the [Grendel](../../in-character/barbarians/Grendel.md) to reach the relative safety of [the League](../../in-character/nations/the-league/The_League.md). The Senate commissioned an [appraisal](../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md) of the steps needed to repair the bridge at the Autumn Equinox 385YE, and the results were delivered by [Eilian Sweetwater](../../in-character/appraisals/Appraisal.md#eilian-sweetwater-of-storm27s-run-striding) at the Winter Solstice. Following [the repairs](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Spider-s_Dream_Bridge.md) and partial fortification the bridge stands once again as a demonstrate of elegant and marvellous engineering.
