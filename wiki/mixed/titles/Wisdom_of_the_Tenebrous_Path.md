# Wisdom of the Tenebrous Path

## Overview

The Wisdom of the Tenebrous Path is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [created by the Senate](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_the_Tenebrous_Path.md) in Winter 383YE. The position of Wisdom is a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md), producing [mana crystals](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals) each season.

Following the Autumn Equinox 385YE **Eurion Firekeeper** funded a series of designs incorporating [weltsilver](../../out-of-character/rules/Materials.md#weltsilver) to track the flow of the currents of mana flowing through the Tenebrous Path as part of the opportunity presented in the **[Shining like fire](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/Shining_like_fire.md#asymmetrical-curves)** wind of fortune.

The title was [amended](../../in-character/senate-sessions/388YE_Spring_Equinox_Senate_sessions.md#amend-wisdom-of-the-tenebrous-path) in a motion raised by **Asthore Autumngale**, as [Senator for Miaren](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Miaren.md), during the Spring Equinox 388YE to give it the responsibility to guide the [thorns](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Thorn.md) of Navarr when dealing with threats posed by inhabitants of the realms.

## Responsibilities

The Wisdom of the Tenebrous Path has the responsibility to guide the Thorns of Navarr in dealing with threats posed by eternals and other [denizens of the realms](../../in-character/eternals/Inhabitants_of_the_realms.md). In addition, if the sinecure were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Wisdom to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Mana

The Wisdom of the Tenebrous Path has custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive an income of 11 mana crystals each season.

## Appointment

The Wisdom of the Tenebrous Path is appointed during the Autumn Equinox each year. It is selected by unanimous decision of the [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [generals](General.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title may be appointed by the [Military Council](../government/military/Military_Council.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Navarr citizen. The Wisdom serves until the next scheduled election. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).

## The Tenebrous Path

The Tenebrous Path is made up of eight small halls clustered in a glade of young oak trees. The main hall is larger than the others and primarily serves to welcome and introduce new visitors. At one end of the hall there is a statue of a [changeling](../magic/lore/Changeling.md) with a small plaque bearing the following "*Our oaths are our choice, given freely to walk the shadowed road to whatever end. Let your oath stand testimony to all that you are. A thorn once, always, until the very end.*" Each of the other halls serve as an academy dedicated to the study and understanding of warfare, with experienced [thorns](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Thorn.md) and [vates](../magic/lore/Navarr_magical_traditions.md#vates) on hand to offer advice and guidance.

The mana crystals that form in the [mithril](../economy/Mithril.md) bowls of the Tenebrous Path are noted for their rich crimson colouration, which some [guides](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr_religious_beliefs.md) suggest should serve as a reminder for the cost of blood in war.
