# Steward of Bear's Rest

## Overview

The Steward of Bears Rest is a [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) [commissioned](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Bears_Rest.md) by the Imperial Senate in Summer 388YE.

## Responsibilities

The Steward of Bears Rest has no particular responsibilities; they simply receive a bounty of [crystal mana](../../out-of-character/rules/Game_items.md#mana-crystals). If Bears Rest were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Steward to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounty of Mana

The Steward of Bears Rest gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). They receive an income of 9 crystal mana each season.

## Appointment

The Steward of Bears Rest is appointed by unanimous decision of the [Wintermark](../../in-character/nations/wintermark/Wintermark.md) [senators](Senator.md). If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by a Wintermark citizen. The Steward has [tenure](New_Imperial_titles.md#tenure), and serves until they die or step down. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Wintermark [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
