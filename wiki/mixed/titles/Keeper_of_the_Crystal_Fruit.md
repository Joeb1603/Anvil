# Keeper of the Crystal Fruit

## Overview

The Keeper of the Crystal Fruit is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md). The commission of the Crystal Fruit Garden was [passed](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Crystal_Fruit_Garden.md) by the Imperial Senate at the Summer Solstice 387YE. The Keeper of the Crystal Fruit is responsible for supporting the prosperity of farm owners in Navarr.

## Responsibilities

The Keeper of the Crystal Fruit has a responsibility for supporting the prosperity of the farmers of Navarr through helping arrange enchantments, trade, and supplies of weirwood for [expansion](../../out-of-character/rules/Farm.md#upgrades). In addition, if the Crystal Fruit Garden, or its tenders, were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Keeper to deal with those threats.

## Powers

### Bounteous Crystals

The Keeper of the Crystal Fruit gains custodianship of a [sinecure](../commissions/Sinecure.md). Each season, they receive ten mana crystals.

## Appointment

The Keeper of the Crystal Fruit is selected by unanimous decision of the [Navarr](../../in-character/nations/navarr/Navarr.md) [senators](Senator.md) at the Spring Equinox. If a unanimous decision cannot be reached, the title [may be appointed](../government/senate/Appointment_by_the_Senate.md#national-position) by the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md) instead.

The title can be held by any Navarri citizen. The Keeper serves until the next scheduled election. They can be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [General Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#general-assembly), the Navarr [National Assembly](../government/synod/Assembly.md#the-national-assemblies), and by the [Assembly of the Nine](../government/synod/Assembly.md#assembly-of-the-nine).
