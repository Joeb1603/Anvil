# Quaymaster of the Municipal Docks

### Overview

The Quaymaster of the Municipal Docks is an [Imperial title](Imperial_title.md) awarded to a citizen of [the Brass Coast](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/The_Brass_Coast.md). Construction of the Municipal Docks was [proposed](../../in-character/senate-motions/Construct_Municipal_Docks.md) by **Eulàlia i Phovas i Riqueza**, as the Senator for [Madruga](../../in-character/nations/the-brass-coast/Madruga.md), during the Winter Solstice 387YE. Construction of the docks, which also function as a [shipyard](../commissions/Shipyard.md), was completed shortly before the Autumn Equinox 388YE, hastened by an [arcane projection](../../in-character/winds-of-fortune/388YE_Summer_Solstice_interlude.md#hammers-of-industry-madruga) being performed on the territory during the Spring Equinox 388YE.

### Responsibilities

The Quaymaster of the Municipal Docks is responsible for overseeing the operation of the docks and protecting them from attack. Traders at the docks, especially from the Commonwealth, will likely look to the Quaymaster for aid in the first instance. If the docks were to be threatened in any way, it would be the responsibility of the Quaymaster to deal with those threats.

The Quaymaster of the Municipal Docks has access to a [ministry](../commissions/Ministry.md). The Quaymaster can purchase [hero stones](../economy/Trade_goods.md#hero-stone) and [Draughts of Clear Headedness](../economy/Trade_goods.md#draught-of-clear-headedness) from the Commonwealth traders of [Volkavaar](../economy/Foreign_ports.md#volkavaar) and [Leerdam](../economy/Foreign_ports.md#leerdam) respectively, for set prices. The Quaymaster makes their decision on how much money to spend during downtime from their character page. Money is removed from, and resources are placed into, the character's inventory when the downtime is processed.

### Redirect the Wealth

The Municipal Dock serves as a [great work](../commissions/Great_work.md) that increases investment in [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md) which undertake trading, questing, or raiding [voyages](../economy/Voyage.md), and [businesses](../../out-of-character/rules/Business.md) in Madruga.

If an appropriate [opportunity](../../out-of-character/game-design/Opportunity.md) has been identified by the Civil Service, the Quaymaster may redirect the wealth of the Municipal Docks towards a specific situation in which the wealth would be of use. To use this power, the Quaymaster provides an [announcement](../government/senate/Senate_announcement.md) to the Civil Service, who present the decision to the [Senate](../government/senate/Senate.md). No voting is possible by the Senate at this point, as the power has been vested in the Quaymaster. The [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md) may, however, choose to exercise their [veto](../government/synod/Veto.md) over the Benefactor's decision.

If the bounty of the docks is redirected, then it will provide no benefit to the fleets and businesses of Madruga, nor will the Quaymaster be able to purchase goods from the Commonwealth traders for as long as the redirection is in place.

## Appointment

The Quaymaster of the Municipal Docks is appointed during the Spring Equinox. The Quaymaster of the Municipal Docks is appointed by citizens of the Brass Coast who control [fleets](../../out-of-character/rules/Fleet.md). The voting is handled by the civil servants in the Bourse.

Only a Freeborn character may hold the title. They do not receive a seat on the Bourse. As a title appointed through the Bourse they cannot be [revoked](../government/synod/Revocation.md) by the [Synod](../government/synod/Synod.md).
